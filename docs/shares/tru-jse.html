About Truworths

Truworths International Limited, an investment holding and management company, engages in the retail of fashion apparel and accessories. It operates through Truworths Africa and Office UK segments. The company offers accessories, bags, bottoms, jackets and coats, jewellery and watches, shoes, sleepwear, tops, and Gifts for women, men and kids. It also provides purses, denim, dresses, lingerie, maternity, formals, and co-ord sets for women; wallets, suiting, and underwears for men; kids multipacks for kids. In addition, the company offers fragrances for men and women, and kids; skincare, such as cleansing, moisturisers, bath and body, gifting, special treatments, mens grooming, and suncare; and makups for eyes, gifting, lips, face, and nails. Further, it provides homeware, including blankets and throws; duvet covers and inners; sheeting; pillowcases; pillows for bedroom; bathmats; bathroom accessories; home fragrances; towels; and bathroom fragrance for bathroom; and curtains, decor, rugs, scatter cushions, dining, the flower shop, and kitchen accessories for living; and soft toys for kids. Additionally, the company offers its products under various ranges of brands through a network of stores, concession outlets, wholesale partnerships, and an ecommerce channel. Truworths International Limited was founded in 1917 and is based in Cape Town, South Africa.
