About Kyivstar Group Ltd.

Kyivstar Group Ltd. provides a range of mobile communication and broadband services for individual and corporate customers in Ukraine and the United States. The company offers a range of connectivity and digital services, including mobile and fixed-line voice and data, ride-hailing, e-health, and digital TV and health; a suite of self-service options; and enterprise solutions, such as Big Data, cloud, and cybersecurity. It also provides mobiles services, such as voice, data, messaging, and wireless internet services; and corporate internet access, fixed-line telephone, data transmission and fixed-mobile convergence, and internet-TV through FTTB network connections. Kyivstar Group Ltd. was founded in 1994 and is headquartered in Dubai, the United Arab Emirates. Kyivstar Group Ltd. operates as a subsidiary of Vimpelcom Amsterdam B.V.
