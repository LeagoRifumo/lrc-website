About RCL Foods Limited

RCL Foods Limited operates as a food manufacturer in South Africa. The company operates through the Groceries, Baking, and Sugar segments. It is involved in culinary products, including mayonnaise, peanut butter, and rusks; milling, specialty, and bakery products; and breads, buns, rolls, and pies, as well as pet food products and beverages, and sugar and molasses-based feed operations. The company sells its products primarily under the 5 Star, Bobtail, bonzo, canine cuisine, catmor, dogmor, Feline Cuisine, MNANDI, Molatek, MONATI, NOLA, Number 1 Megeu, Number 1 Mageu Smooth, Optimizor, OUMA, Pieman, Safari Braaipap, Selati Sprinkle Joy, Sunbake, Supreme Flour, Tafelberg, Ultra Pet, Yapper, and YUMYUM brands. In addition, it engages in treasury, international investments, farming, marketing, and food innovation and research activities. The company was formerly known as Rainbow Chicken Limited and changed its name to RCL Foods Limited in August 2013. The company was founded in 1960 and is based in Durban, South Africa.
