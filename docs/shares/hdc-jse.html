About Hudaco Industries Limited

Hudaco Industries Limited imports and distributes automotive, industrial, and electronic consumable products in South Africa and internationally. It operates in two segments, Consumer-Related Products and Engineering Consumables. The Consumer-Related Products segment distributes clutch kits, automotive ignition leads, oil and hydraulic seals, wheels, brake and clutch hydraulics, mountings, bushes, hydraulic repair kits, cylinders, hoses, CV joints, wheel hubs, suspension components, and vehicle components to the automotive and industrial after-market; batteries, chargers, and related battery management equipment, as well as battery bays; power tools, marine engines, survey instruments, and fasteners; and wireless and data networking, VoIP, and physical security solutions. It also distributes intrusion detection, surveillance, access control, and fire detection equipment; two-way radios and satellite communications; solar power and storage solutions for commercial, industrial and residential markets; and cooking, heating, and outdoor products. The Engineering Consumables segment distributes diesel engines and spares, and support services; bearings, chains, belting, industrial hose, seals, electric and geared motors, and power transmission products; fire detection, containment, and suppression; filtration solutions, kits, and accessories to mining, process, and manufacturing markets; thermoplastic pipes, and fittings and equipment; thermal and electrical insulation materials; and variable speed drives, electric motors, electrical cabling, plugs, sockets, and related products. It also manufactures and distributes hydraulic and pneumatic products; drivetrains; dragline hoses and plastic welding rods; hardening of round, hexagonal and hollow steel bar, key steel, bending steel for dished ends, and manufacture of conveyor drive pulleys and idlers, as well as ferrous and non-ferrous castings. The company was founded in 1891 and is based in Edenvale, South Africa.
