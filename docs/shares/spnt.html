About SiriusPoint Ltd.

SiriusPoint Ltd. provides multi-line reinsurance and insurance products and services worldwide. It operates through four segments: Global P&C Programs, Global Reinsurance, Global Accident & Health, and London Market Specialty. The Global P&C Programs segment offers programs space to clients and distribution partners. The Global Reinsurance segment provides casualty, property, and other specialties, such as proportional and excess of loss, treaty and facultative, aviation and space, marine and energy, and credit to insurance and reinsurance companies, government entities, and other risk bearing vehicles. The Global Accident & Health segment provides accident and health, property and casualty, aviation and space, credit, surety, marine and energy, and mortgage. The London Market Specialty segment provides London casualty, energy, property, and marine leaders services. The company offers medical insurance products, trip cancellation programs, medical management services, and 24/7 emergency medical and travel assistance services. The company was formerly known as Third Point Reinsurance Ltd. and changed its name to SiriusPoint Ltd. in February 2021. SiriusPoint Ltd. was incorporated in 2011 and is headquartered in Pembroke, Bermuda.
