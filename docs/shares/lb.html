About LandBridge Corporation

LandBridge Corporation, together with its subsidiaries, owns and manages land and resources to support and enhance oil and natural gas development in the United States. It owns surface acres in and around the Delaware Basin in Texas and New Mexico. The company holds a portfolio of oil and gas royalties. It sells brackish water and other surface composite materials. The company was formerly known as LandBridge Company LLC and changed its name to LandBridge Corporation in October, 2026. The company was founded in 2021 and is headquartered in Houston, Texas.
