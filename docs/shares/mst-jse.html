About Mustek Limited

Mustek Limited engages in the assembly and distribution of computer products, components, and peripherals in South Africa, East Africa, and Taiwan. It operates through Distribution, Service and Support, Training, and Group segments. The company offers accredited training programs; managed cybersecurity and AI services; and property rental services. It also provides cloud and software solutions; and networking and infrastructure products. Mustek Limited was incorporated in 1987 and is based in Midrand, South Africa.

Mustek Limited is in the Technology · Computer Hardware industry, number 170 of 181 by market value in our JSE universe, based in South Africa. Market value R812M; 52-week range R11.01 – R15.99.
