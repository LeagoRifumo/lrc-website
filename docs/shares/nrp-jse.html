About NEPI Rockcastle

NEPI Rockcastle N.V. is a public limited company domiciled in the Netherlands, with registration number at the Dutch Chamber of Commerce 87488329. The Company's shares are listed on the Main Board of the JSE Limited, Euronext Amsterdam and A2X. NEPI Rockcastle is the premier owner and operator of shopping centres in Central and Eastern Europe. The Group benefits from a highly-skilled internal management team which combines asset management, development, investment, leasing and financial expertise. NEPI Rockcastle N.V. registered office at Strawinskylaan 563, WTC Zuidas, Tower Ten, 5th Floor, 1077 XX Amsterdam. Rockcastle N.V. was established in 2007 and was incorporated in Netherlands.
