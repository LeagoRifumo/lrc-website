About PriceSmart, Inc.

PriceSmart, Inc. owns and operates membership shopping warehouse clubs in the United States, Central America, the Caribbean, and Colombia. The company offers basic consumable merchandise, as well as private label merchandise and consumable and non-consumable products under the Members Selection brand. Its products include groceries, cleaning supplies, and health and beauty aid; meat, produce, deli, seafood, and poultry; electronics, large and small appliances, automotive, hardware, sporting goods, and seasonal products; and clothing, domestics, and home furnishing products. The company also provides food service and bakery; and health services, including optical, audiology, and pharmacy. It sells its products through in-club shopping, mobile app, and online at PriceSmart.com that offers home delivery and curbside pickup under the Click & Go brand service. PriceSmart, Inc. was incorporated in 1994 and is headquartered in San Diego, California.
