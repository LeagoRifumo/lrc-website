About Southern Palladium Limited

Southern Palladium Limited, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the exploration and development of mineral properties in South Africa. The company explores for rutherium, rhodium, palladium, osmium, iridium, platinum, gold deposits, as well as nickel, copper, and chronium oxide. Its flagship property is the Bengwenyama Platinum Group Metals project covering approximately an area of 5,280 hectares located in the Eastern Limb of the Bushveld, South Africa. Southern Palladium Limited was incorporated in 2020 and is based in Sydney, Australia.
