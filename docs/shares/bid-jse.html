About Bid Corporation

Bid Corporation Limited operates as a foodservice distributor in the United Kingdom, Australia, the Netherlands, Italy, Czech Republic, New Zealand, Belgium, South Africa, the People's Republic of China, Hong Kong, and internationally. The company engages in supplying frozen, ambient, and chilled products to caterers and foodservice businesses; fresh food to hospitality, institutional, catering, and retail sectors; premium ice cream and texture-modified meals for people with swallowing difficulties; and proteins, dairy, charcuterie, and specialty products to hotels, international restaurant groups, and fine-dining establishments. It is also involved in buying business; trading and marine services; meat, poultry, and dairy manufacturing and distribution; and bakery ingredients and equipment business. Bid Corporation Limited was founded in 1988 and is headquartered in Johannesburg, South Africa.
