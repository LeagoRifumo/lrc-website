About Costamare Inc.

Costamare Inc. owns and operates containerships and dry bulk vessels worldwide. Its containerships are chartered to liner companies providing transportation of cargoes. The company also charters dry bulk vessels to various customers providing worldwide transportation for dry bulk cargoes. As of February 24, 2026, it had a fleet of 79 containerships and 38 dry bulk vessels. The company was founded in 1974 and is based in Monaco.

Costamare Inc. is in the Industrials · Marine Shipping industry, number 1868 of 2179 by market value in our New York universe, based in Monaco. Market value $1.7B; 52-week range $11.45 – $18.06.
