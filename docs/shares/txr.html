About Torex Resources Inc.

Torex Resources Inc., a precious metals mining company, engages in the acquisition, exploration, and development of mineral properties in Mexico and the United States. The company explores gold, copper, silver, and carbon deposits. Its flagship project is a 100% owned Morelos Complex covering an area of 29,000 hectares located in Guerrero, Mexico. Torex Resources Inc. was formerly known as Torex Gold Resources Inc. and changed its name to Torex Resources Inc. in October 2026. Torex Resources Inc. was incorporated in 1980 and is based in Toronto, Canada.

Torex Resources Inc. is in the Basic Materials · Gold industry, number 1446 of 2432 by market value in our New York universe, based in Canada. Market value $4.1B; 52-week range $32.30 – $62.56.
