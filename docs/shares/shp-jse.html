About Shoprite

Shoprite Holdings Ltd, an investment holding company, engages in the food retail and wholesale business in South Africa and internationally. It operates through the Supermarkets RSA, Supermarkets Non-RSA, and Other Operating segments. The company offers food, clothing, general merchandise, cosmetics, and liquor products under the Checkers, Checkers Hyper, and Usave brands. It also offers a range of perishable and non-perishable food items through supermarket/convenience outlets primarily under the OK brand; and financial services and products, as well as operates retail liquor outlets under the OK Liquor brand; and liquor stores under the Shoprite LiquorShop and Checkers LiquorShop brands. The company engages in fruit and vegetable procurement and distribution under the Freshmark retail outlets; pharmacies and wholesale distribution under the Transpharm brand; wholesale franchise trade under the Megasave brand; and retail buying under the Sentra brand, as well as sells products to franchisees under drop-shipment arrangements. Shoprite Holdings Ltd was incorporated in 1936 and is based in Brackenfell, South Africa.
