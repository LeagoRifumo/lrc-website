The LRC watchlist

Fully automated from the LRC Stock Scanner. After every scanner run the website lists the 5 highest-scoring and 5 lowest-scoring companies for trading (3–12 months) and for investing (5–10 years), in the JSE and in New York. Nobody picks the names by hand. Use the JSE / New York switch to change market.

EDUCATIONAL ONLY This watchlist shows how our scanner ranks companies. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell, and it does not consider your circumstances. Prices and levels can be out of date by the time you read them.
