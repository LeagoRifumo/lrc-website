LRC Stock Scanner

Every stock scored. Every setup planned.

The JSE and New York in one scanner: technicals (breakouts and retests of major support), analyst sentiment and every key ratio judged against its own sector, rolled into one LRC score and an LRC Stock Scanner rating: BUY, HOLD or SELL. Click any company for its one-page analyst summary.

EDUCATIONAL ONLY LRC Stock Scanner ratings are produced by our scoring rules and refreshed automatically. They are general research, not personal advice; where we have completed a Work-sheet, the LRC analyst rating is shown with it. Data can be delayed or wrong — always check the source.
How to read it Every company is checked on 37 readings in seven groups — Valuation, Growth, Profitability, Financial health, Income, Sentiment and Technical. Click a group button to see the exact numbers: the big number is the company, the small number is the sector median (hover for the market median). Each reading scores +1 better / 0 neutral / −1 worse than its sector or our safety lines (Trend, Momentum and Setup count ±2). A BUY also needs upside to the LRC Analyst Target and a SELL needs downside, otherwise HOLD. Valuation verdict: UNDERVALUED = valuation readings net +2 or better and 10%+ upside to the LRC Analyst Target; OVERVALUED = valuation net −2 or worse, or the target 5%+ below the price; otherwise FAIR VALUE.

The LRC Investment track (long-only, 5–10 years) — the quality screen from our workbook: size, ROE, ROA, operating margin, low debt, liquidity, a sensible forward P/E and P/FCF, and multi-year growth in EPS and revenue. Grade A = 8+ of 10 checks passed. Margin of safety compares today's price with the LRC target (covered names) or the model fair value.

Each sector is scored from −10 to +10: the average LRC score of its companies, its sector ETF's technical score and its breadth (the share of names on BUY vs SELL). Sector rotation in one view.

Valuation (7)

Forward P/E, trailing P/E, PEG, price/sales, price/book, price/free cash flow, EV/EBITDA. +1 when 20%+ cheaper than the sector median (PEG below 1); −1 when 25%+ dearer, loss-making or negative cash flow (PEG above 2).

Growth (6)

Revenue and EPS growth for the latest quarter, analyst EPS growth this year and next, and 3-year revenue and EPS growth a year. +1 for strong growth (15–20%+, 10%+ over 3 years); −1 when shrinking.

Profitability (5)

Gross, operating and net margin, return on equity (+1 above 20%, −1 below 8%) and return on assets (+1 above 8%, −1 below 2%). Margins are judged against the sector.

Financial health (5)

Debt/equity (+1 below 0.5, −1 above 1.5), current ratio, quick ratio, free-cash-flow margin and net cash vs market cap. Banks, insurers and property funds skip the balance-sheet ratios.

Income (2)

Dividend yield (+1 above 3% and above the sector; −1 after a dividend cut) and payout ratio (+1 between 20% and 70%; −1 above 100%).

Sentiment (6)

Analyst consensus, upside to the analysts' median target, upgrades minus downgrades, short interest, insider and institutional ownership.

Technical (6)

Trend vs the 50/200-day averages (±2), 6-month momentum vs the market (±2), setup (±2: Breakout or Retest of major support vs Breakdown or Failed retest), stretch/RSI, volume and beta.

The LRC score & rating

Points are added up: score = 50 + 50 × points ÷ possible points. 75+ STRONG BUY, 60–74 BUY, 40–59 HOLD, 25–39 SELL, below 25 STRONG SELL. A BUY needs at least 5% upside to the LRC Analyst Target (10% for STRONG BUY) and a SELL needs downside, otherwise HOLD. Missing data is left out, never counted against a company.

LRC Analyst Target & track record

6-month target: the analyst's own where we have a Work-sheet; otherwise 60% analyst consensus + 40% LRC valuation models, within 20% of the consensus. Every rating is measured on the track record page.
