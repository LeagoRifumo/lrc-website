Long setups — breakouts & retests

Short setups — breakdowns & failed retests

The LRC Investment track (long-only, 5–10 years) — the quality screen from our workbook: size, ROE, ROA, operating margin, low debt, liquidity, a sensible forward P/E and P/FCF, and multi-year growth in EPS and revenue. Grade A = 8+ of 10 checks passed. Margin of safety compares today's price with the LRC target (covered names) or the model fair value.

Each sector is scored from −10 to +10: the average LRC score of its companies, its sector ETF's technical score and its breadth (the share of names on BUY vs SELL). Sector rotation in one view.

1 · Technicals

Trend (−2…+2): price vs the 50- and 200-day averages and the 200-day's slope. Momentum (−2…+2): 6-month relative strength vs the market and RSI(14). Setup (−2…+2): Breakout through the 55-day high or a major resistance on 1.5× volume, or a Retest of a major support (an old resistance broken in the last 40 sessions, or the rising 50-day) that holds = +2; Near breakout = +1; Breakdown or Failed retest = −2. Extension (−1…+1): more than 3 ATR above the 20-day = chasing. Volume (−1…+1): up-day vs down-day volume.

2 · Sentiment

Analysts (−2…+2): consensus rating from 1 (strong buy) to 5 (sell). Target (−1…+1): upside to the median analyst target above 15% = +1, below today's price = −1. Short interest (−1…+1): below 3% of float = +1, above 12% = −1.

3 · Fundamentals

Each −1 / 0 / +1, judged against the median of the same sector in the same market: Forward P/E, TTM P/E, Price/Sales and Price/FCF (20% cheaper = +1, 25% dearer or loss-making = −1), revenue growth, EPS growth, debt/equity, ROE and dividend yield.

4 · The LRC signal

All readings are added up and compared with the maximum possible: ≥45% = STRONG BUY, ≥18% = BUY, between = HOLD, ≤−18% = SELL, ≤−45% = STRONG SELL.

5 · Major support

Swing highs and lows (5-bar pivots) from the last 12 months are clustered; a level touched at least twice is a major support or resistance. The one-page summary draws them on the chart with the entry, stop and targets.

6 · Entry plans

Breakout: buy stop above the breakout-day high, stop below the breakout level. Retest: buy stop above yesterday's high once support holds, stop below support. Shorts mirror this. Size = account × 1% ÷ (entry − stop). Targets 2R and 3R; results within 14 days = event risk.

UPDATES The scanner runs automatically every trading day after the New York close (about 06:30 South African time), using Yahoo Finance prices, fundamentals and analyst data. The JSE universe is our list of large and mid caps; New York covers the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100.
