LRC Stock Scanner

Every stock scored. Every setup planned.

The JSE and New York in one scanner: technicals (breakouts and retests of major support), analyst sentiment and every key ratio judged against its own sector, rolled into one LRC score and an LRC Stock Scanner rating: BUY, HOLD or SELL. Click any company for its one-page analyst summary.

EDUCATIONAL ONLY LRC Stock Scanner ratings are produced by our scoring rules and refreshed automatically. They are general research, not personal advice; where we have completed a Work-sheet, the LRC analyst rating is shown with it. Data can be delayed or wrong — always check the source.
How to read the LRC score (0–100) Every company is compared with its peers — the same sector in the same market — on six factors: Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum, Sentiment and Technical. Each factor is a score from 0 to 100 (grade A = 80+, B = 60+, C = 40+, D = 20+, F = below 20). The LRC score combines them (Value, Growth, Profitability and Momentum 20% each; Sentiment and Technical 10% each) and ranks the result across the market: an LRC score of 85 means the company ranks above 85% of the companies we scan. 80–100 STRONG BUY60–79 BUY40–59 HOLD20–39 SELL0–19 STRONG SELL A BUY also needs upside to the LRC Analyst Target and a SELL needs downside — otherwise the rating is held at HOLD, so the rating and the target never disagree. The LRC Investment track (long-only, 5–10 years) — the quality screen from our workbook: size, ROE, ROA, operating margin, low debt, liquidity, a sensible forward P/E and P/FCF, and multi-year growth in EPS and revenue. Grade A = 8+ of 10 checks passed. Margin of safety compares today's price with the LRC target (covered names) or the model fair value.

Each sector is scored from −10 to +10: the average LRC score of its companies, its sector ETF's technical score and its breadth (the share of names on BUY vs SELL). Sector rotation in one view.

1 · Value (20%)

Forward P/E, trailing P/E, price/sales, price/free cash flow and PEG — each ranked against the same sector in the same market. Cheaper than peers = higher score. Loss-making or negative free cash flow ranks lowest.

2 · Growth (20%)

Revenue growth and EPS growth (latest quarter, this year and next year estimates) against sector peers.

3 · Profitability (20%)

Return on equity and assets, operating and net margins, low debt and liquidity (debt and liquidity are skipped for banks, insurers and property funds, where they do not apply).

4 · Momentum (20%)

Share-price performance over 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, ranked across the whole market.

5 · Sentiment (10%)

Analyst consensus rating, upside to the analysts' median target, short interest and recent upgrades minus downgrades.

6 · Technical (10%)

Trend (price vs the 50- and 200-day averages), setup (Breakout through resistance on 1.5× volume, or a Retest of major support that holds; Breakdown or Failed retest count against), stretch and volume. Major support/resistance = price levels touched at least twice in 12 months.

The LRC score & rating

The six factor scores are weighted and ranked across the market: 80–100 STRONG BUY, 60–79 BUY, 40–59 HOLD, 20–39 SELL, 0–19 STRONG SELL. A BUY needs at least 5% upside to the LRC Analyst Target (10% for STRONG BUY) and a SELL needs downside — otherwise HOLD.

LRC Analyst Target

A 6-month target: 60% analyst consensus and 40% LRC valuation models (P/FCF, PEG, sector P/E), kept within 20% of the consensus and between −35% and +50% of the price. Where we have completed a Work-sheet, the analyst's own target is used.
