Rating checker

Enter a share price, a 6-month target and a checklist score. The tool applies LRC's rating rules: BUY needs 7.5%+ upside, a score of 3.5+ and no critical flag; SELL is −5% or worse, or a score below 2.5.

Tool 3

Position size & risk planner

The rules we use on the live show: risk 1% of the account per trade, stop at 2 × ATR (average true range) and target about 3R (three times the risk). For leveraged CFDs: leverage decides how much margin the trade ties up — not how big the trade should be. Size from your stop, then check the margin and the gap risk.

Find it on any chart: the ATR indicator.
FSCA brokers: usually 1:5 to 1:20 on shares.
Shares can jump past your stop after results or news.
