Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA)

Who we are

Leago Rifumo Capital (“we”, “us”) is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website. Contact: info@leagorifumocapital.co.za.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us — your name, email address and message when you contact us, and the details below if you create a free member account. This website does not run advertising trackers.

Member accounts

If you create a free account we keep your name, email address, a securely hashed password (we never see the password itself), the markets you follow, whether you want email updates, and the date you joined. This is used only to run your account, show you the members-only Top Picks and, if you tick the box, email you about changes. Accounts are stored by our database provider, Supabase, on servers outside South Africa that are bound by data-protection law at least as strict as POPIA. You can change your details, switch email updates off, or delete your account and everything linked to it at any time on the My account page.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry and, where you ask us to, to arrange guest sessions, partnerships or sponsorships. We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

The live market ticker is provided by TradingView and fonts are served by Google Fonts; these providers may receive technical data such as your IP address when the page loads. Links to YouTube, Facebook and TikTok are governed by those platforms’ own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and object to its processing. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Retention & security

We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to deal with them and take reasonable steps to keep them secure.
