The 5 checks before any trade

✓
1. On the listIn Top Picks, or rated BUY / STRONG BUY in the Scanner.
✓
2. Right setupBreakout or Retest, price near support, above its 50- and 200-day averages.
✓
3. Not stretchedRSI between 40 and 70. Above 70: wait for a pullback.
✓
4. Results checkNo results within 5 days for a new trade. Holding into results? Decide first: take profit, reduce or hold.
✓
5. Plan before you buyStop about 2 × ATR below entry or just under support. Target at least 3 × your risk. Risk at most 1% of your account per trade.

All 5 pass = trade idea. One fails = Watchlist, check again tomorrow.

Top Picks: what you need to know

How they are chosenLiquidity first: at least R20m a day traded on the JSE or $50m in New York, market value R5bn/$5bn+. Then the best scores, at most 2 per sector, 5 per market.
Trading picksBUY or STRONG BUY, price trending up, RSI 40–75, no results within 5 days. Usually held for days to a few weeks.
Investment picksQuality grade A or B, not overvalued, healthy balance sheet, at least 15% below our model value, no results within 3 days. Held for months.
How long a pick staysNo fixed time. The list is rebuilt twice a day (06:37 and 17:47 SAST). A company stays while it passes every rule and drops off when one fails or a stronger name replaces it.
When a pick drops offIt is a signal to review your trade, not an automatic sell. Check the snapshot: did the trend break, did RSI overheat, are results close?
Worked example (4 Nov 2026). Life Healthcare shows a Retest of R12.40 support, RSI 56, above both averages, results on 26 Nov (22 days away). Plan: stop about R11.85 (2 × ATR), target about R14.10 (3R), risk 1%. All 5 checks pass, so it is a trade idea. MTN broke out, but RSI 70 fails check 3: it goes on the Watchlist until it pulls back to about R206. Example for education only, not a recommendation.

Golden rules

  • Never risk more than 1% of your account on one trade.
  • Write the stop and target down before you buy.
  • Do not chase: if RSI is above 70, wait.
  • Results can gap past your stop: size down if you hold through them.
  • Keep a trade log: date, ticker, reason, entry, stop, target, result.
  • Trade the plan, not your feelings.
