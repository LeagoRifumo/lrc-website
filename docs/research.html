What a grade means

Five grades — from the LRC score

TOP SCORING · STRONG

A score of 75 or more (Top scoring) or 60 to 74 (Strong): the company's readings are better than most of its sector.

AVERAGE

A score of 40 to 59: the readings are close to the sector average.

WEAK · LOWEST SCORING

A score of 25 to 39 (Weak) or below 25 (Lowest scoring): the readings are worse than most of its sector.

Grades describe the company's data against its sector. They are general information, not a recommendation to buy or sell any share.

What a rating means

BUY, HOLD or SELL — over six months

BUY

Expected gain of 7.5% or more over six months, a checklist score of at least 3.5 out of 5, and no critical red flag.

HOLD

A business worth owning, but the expected gain is smaller — or there is a risk we want to see resolved first.

SELL

Expected fall of 5% or more over six months, or a checklist score below 2.5. Great companies can be SELLs at the wrong price.
