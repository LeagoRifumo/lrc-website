Top Picks explained

What are LRC Top Picks?

A members-only shortlist of JSE and New York shares from the LRC Stock Scanner: up to five trading picks (3–12 months) and five investment picks (5–10 years) per market, chosen by fixed rules.

Are Top Picks free?

Yes. Create a free Leago Rifumo Capital account with your email to see them.

How are the picks chosen?

Liquidity first — at least R20m traded a day on the JSE or $50m in New York — then strong scores, a confirmed price trend and no results in the next 5 days for trading picks (3 days for investment picks), or quality, a healthy balance sheet and a margin of safety for investment picks. At most two per sector.

How often are Top Picks updated?

After every LRC Stock Scanner run — twice a day on weekdays, after the JSE close and after the New York close.

Is this financial advice?

No. Top Picks are general, educational information that shows how our rules rank companies. They do not consider your circumstances; do your own research before you invest.
