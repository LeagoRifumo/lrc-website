Tech sell-off and $112 Brent: what Thursday's session means for the JSE and the rand

The Nasdaq fell 2.6% as chip stocks and Meta sold off, Brent hit $112 and the rand slipped about 1%. Here is what moved, and what we watch next.

Prayer Nkuna, Analyst23 October 2026 · 2 min read
METANVDAAMD

The market in one line

The AI trade cracked and oil revived inflation fears: the Nasdaq fell 2.6% to 26,008.86 and the S&P 500 1.2% to 7,663.43, while Brent jumped to about $112.9 a barrel — its highest since May.

South Africa

  • Rand: about 1% weaker near R16.53/$ — South Africa imports most of its fuel, so a higher oil price hits the currency directly.
  • JSE Top 40: down about 1.2%.
  • Fuel: the mid-year budget brings back temporary fuel levy relief for two months from November, worth about R10bn.

United States

  • Chip stocks led the fall: Lam Research about −9%, Applied Materials and KLA about −6%, AMD and Intel −4% to −5%, NVIDIA about −2%.
  • Meta fell 6.98% to $648.85.
  • Treasuries rallied: the 10-year yield fell about 11bp to near 5.0%.

Commodities

  • Gold slipped for a fifth session to about $4,084/oz as traders priced a possible December Fed hike.
  • Brent rose about 3.7% on Middle East escalation.

What I’m watching

  1. The 28 October FOMC — a hawkish tone with oil above $110 would keep pressure on long-duration tech.
  2. Results season: Alphabet and Amphenol report on 28 October; AMD and Arista on 3 November.
  3. The rand vs Brent: every sustained move in oil shows up in the rand and, a month later, in the petrol price.

The analyst’s view

Our house view on the big platforms has been cautious on valuation, and Thursday showed why: when the market questions AI spending, the most expensive names fall hardest. For South African investors the bigger story is oil — it weakens the rand and keeps inflation, and the SARB, on alert.

Sources: Yahoo Finance market wrap (22 Oct 2026), CNBC Africa (22 Oct 2026). Educational only — not financial advice.
