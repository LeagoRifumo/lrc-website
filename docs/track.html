LRC Track Record

Are our ratings working?

We keep score in public. Every rating the LRC Stock Scanner gives is saved every day and checked later against what the share price actually did. Nothing is back-filled or edited.

How we track our ratings — in 4 steps

1Save

After every scan we save each company's rating (STRONG BUY to STRONG SELL), price and LRC Analyst Target.

2Wait

We let time pass: 1 week, 1 month, 3 months and 6 months.

3Measure

We work out each share's price change since the rating, and group them by rating.

4Compare

We compare each group with the market (the average of every company we scan). BUY-rated shares should beat it and SELL-rated shares should trail it.

The scorecard

Day by day — R100 invested in each group

Imagine R100 split equally across every BUY-rated share, every HOLD, every SELL and every company (the market), rebalanced each day to that day's ratings. If the ratings work, the green BUY line rises above the dashed market line and the red SELL line falls below it.

Results by rating

Average returnThe average price change of every share that had this rating, measured over the period you picked.
vs marketAverage return minus the market's. Green = beat the market, red = trailed it. For SELL ratings, red is good.
Hit rateBUY: share of BUY-rated shares that went up. SELL: share of SELL-rated shares that went down. HOLD: share that moved less than 5%. Above 50% is better than a coin toss.
PeriodsHow many daily snapshots are old enough to be measured. One week of data proves nothing — judge the model on months.
