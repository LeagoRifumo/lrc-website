DNOW Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
HOUSTON, TX, November 5, 2026 – DNOW Inc. (NYSE: DNOW) announced results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Recent Capital Allocation
•
Repurchased $17 million of common stock in the third quarter of 2026 and $92 million year-to-date under the Company's $160 million share repurchase program
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•
Revenue was $1,337 million
•
Gross profit was $265 million, or 19.8% of revenue, and adjusted gross profit was $283 million, or 21.2% of revenue
•
Net income attributable to DNOW Inc. was $26 million, or $0.14 per diluted share, and adjusted net income attributable to DNOW Inc. was $34 million, or $0.18 per diluted share
•
Adjusted EBITDA was $76 million, or 5.7% of revenue
•
Cash flow from operating activities was $120 million
•
Cash and cash equivalents was $112 million and total long-term debt was $377 million at September 30, 2026, resulting in net debt of $265 million, or a net debt leverage ratio of 1.1 times
David Cherechinsky, President and CEO of DNOW Inc., added, “Our team delivered another quarter of outstanding execution, generating third quarter Adjusted EBITDA of $76 million, nearly double our first quarter results, reflecting broad-based strength across the company. Sales increased sequentially in all three geographic segments, with solid growth in the downstream, industrial and midstream businesses, with the gas utilities business achieving its highest quarterly revenue in three years.
Cash flow from operating activities was incredibly strong at $120 million, fueled by substantial improvements in working capital velocity. As a result, we further strengthened our balance sheet, reducing our net debt leverage ratio to 1.1 times, enhancing our financial flexibility to capitalize on future opportunities. We also remained disciplined in our approach to capital allocation, repurchasing $17 million of DNOW shares during the quarter. Year-to-date repurchases totaled $92 million through September, exceeding the combined amount returned through share repurchases over the prior eleven quarters by 24 percent. This demonstrates our confidence in the business and our commitment to delivering value to shareholders.
Most importantly, these results reflect the foundational dedication, expertise and customer focus of our teams across the organization. Their unwavering commitment to supporting our customers, deepening supplier partnerships and executing with excellence every day continues to be a key driver of our success.
With an even stronger balance sheet, improving end-market fundamentals and positive outlook across our business, we believe we are well positioned to continue gaining share, expanding profitability and creating long-term value for our customers, employees and shareholders. I am excited about the opportunities ahead and confident in our ability to build on this momentum as we move forward.”
Prior to the earnings conference call a presentation titled “DNOW Third Quarter 2026 Earnings Presentation” will be available on the Company’s Investor Relations website.
About DNOW
DNOW is a premier energy and industrial solutions provider with a legacy of over 160 years as a leading distributor of pipe, valves, fittings (PVF), gas products, pumps and fabricated equipment. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, with approximately 5,050 employees and a global network of distribution and engineering locations; we provide a broad mix of quality products our customers require to build and maintain essential infrastructure across the upstream, gas utilities, midstream, downstream and industrial markets. We deliver a comprehensive range of value-added supply chain solutions and technical product expertise, supported by advanced digital offerings. Our products and resources enable our customers to run their operations more efficiently and effectively, helping them to meet and exceed their business goals.

U.S. GENERALLY ACCEPTED ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES (GAAP) TO NON-GAAP RECONCILIATIONS
In an effort to provide investors with additional information regarding our results as determined by GAAP, we disclose various non-GAAP financial measures in our quarterly earnings press releases and other public disclosures. The non-GAAP financial measures include: (i) adjusted gross profit, (ii) adjusted gross profit as a percentage of revenue, (iii) adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization and excluding other costs (Adjusted EBITDA), (iv) Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of revenue, (v) Adjusted Net Income attributable to DNOW Inc., (vi) Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share Attributable to DNOW Inc. Stockholders, (vii) Net Debt, (viii) Net Debt Leverage Ratio and (ix) Free Cash Flow. We use these non-GAAP financial measures to evaluate and manage the Company’s operations because we believe they provide useful supplemental information regarding the financial performance of our business. These non-GAAP financial measures are not intended to replace the GAAP financial measures. The Company defines Adjusted Gross Profit as revenue, less cost of products, plus amortization of intangibles, plus inventory-related charges incremental to normal operations, plus transaction related adjustments associated with acquisitions, such as inventory fair value step-up or write-downs and plus or minus the impact of our Last-In, First-Out (“LIFO”) inventory costing methodology. We define Adjusted EBITDA as net (loss) income plus interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization and excluding other costs, such as stock-based compensation, restructuring and exit costs, transaction-related adjustments, net, inventory-related charges incremental to normal operations, long-lived asset impairments (including goodwill and intangible assets) and plus or minus the impact of our LIFO inventory costing methodology. Transaction-related adjustments, net include transaction costs and recoveries, inventory fair value step-up, retention bonus accruals and integration expenses associated with acquisitions. We define Net Debt as total long-term debt, including current portion, minus cash. We define our Net Debt Leverage Ratio as Net Debt divided by trailing twelve months Adjusted EBITDA. The Company believes Net Debt is an indicator of the extent to which the Company’s outstanding debt obligations could be satisfied by cash on hand and a useful metric for investors to evaluate the Company’s leverage position. We believe the Net Debt Leverage Ratio is a commonly used metric that management and investors use to assess the borrowing capacity of the Company. We define Free Cash Flow as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities adjusted for purchases of property, plant and equipment. The Company believes Free Cash Flow is a useful metric for investors to measure a company's financial flexibility and ability to generate cash after considering normal operating expenses and capital expenditures. A reconciliation of each of these non-GAAP financial measures to its most comparable GAAP financial measure is included in the schedules herein. Totals in the schedules herein may not foot due to rounding.
