Hillman Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results; Updates Full-Year Outlook
Q3 2026 Net Sales increased 7.7% to a record $457.5 million
Reiterates FY 2026 Adj. EBITDA and Free Cash Flow guidance; updates Net Sales outlook
CINCINNATI, November 2, 2026 -- Hillman Solutions Corp. (Nasdaq: HLMN) (the “Company” or “Hillman”), a leading provider of hardware and related products, reported financial results for the thirteen and thirty-nine weeks ended September 26, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights (Thirteen weeks ended September 26, 2026)
•Net sales increased 7.7% to $457.5 million compared to $424.9 million in the prior year quarter
•Net income totaled $6.3 million, or $0.03 per diluted share, compared to $23.2 million, or $0.12 per diluted share, in the prior year quarter
•Adjusted diluted EPS1 totaled $0.22 per diluted share compared to $0.22 in the prior year quarter
•Adjusted EBITDA1 increased to $90.2 million compared to $88.0 million in the prior year quarter
•Net cash provided by operating activities increased to $69.9 million compared to $26.2 million in the prior year quarter
•Free Cash Flow1 increased to $51.1 million compared to $9.1 million in the prior year quarter
•Repurchased approximately 0.6 million shares of its common stock at an average price of $7.75 per share, which totaled $5.0 million
•Completed the acquisition of Kanebridge, a leading master distributor of industrial fasteners
Balance Sheet and Liquidity at September 26, 2026
•Successfully refinanced its existing credit facilities, consisting of a new $735 million senior secured term loan B and a $375 million asset-based revolving credit facility; closed a $200 million add-on term loan B for the Kanebridge acquisition
•Gross debt was $967.7 million compared to $693.1 million on December 27, 2025
•Net debt1 was $932.6 million compared to $665.8 million on December 27, 2025

Management Commentary
“The third quarter reflects the continued execution of Hillman’s Blueprint for Strategic Growth," commented Hillman's CEO, Jon Michael Adinolfi. "We continue to deliver net sales growth and strong free cash flow generation that give us the flexibility to invest into attractive opportunities, like our high-margin Robotics and Digital Solutions segment - which had a great quarter, up 19.2% - and accretive acquisitions, like Kanebridge."
"We are winning in Pro, where our channel relationships, sourcing agility, and field sales team are driving new business wins. The Kanebridge acquisition, which is off to a great start, accelerates that momentum, establishing our position as a long-tail master distributor in the U.S. industrial market."
"Given our performance through the first nine months, we are reaffirming our full-year Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow guidance while narrowing down our net sales range, given the macro environment. As we look to the rest of 2026 and beyond, we are well positioned to drive growth and create long-term value for our shareholders.”

About Hillman Solutions Corp.
Founded in 1964 and headquartered in Cincinnati, Hillman is a leading provider of hardware and related products serving retail, pro distribution, and industrial customers. Over the last 60-plus years, Hillman has built a legacy of service and growth by forming strategic partnerships with North America’s leading home improvement, hardware, and farm and fleet retailers. Hillman differentiates itself from the competition with its dedicated field sales team of 1,200+ associates, direct-to-store distribution capabilities, and world class global sourcing and supply chain expertise. The company offers an extensive product portfolio of over 150,000 SKUs, including fasteners (power screws, nuts, and bolts), hardware (builder’s hardware, door locks, rope & chain, accessories), project gear & supplies (gloves, work gear, paint & cleaning sundries), and key and engraving services (key duplication, auto keys, and engraving). Hillman is committed to delivering exceptional customer service, innovative products, and dependable solutions to its customers and regularly earns vendor of the year recognition from top customers. For more information on Hillman, visit www.hillman.com.

Reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA (Unaudited)
(dollars in thousands)
Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure and is the primary basis used to measure the operational strength and performance of our businesses as well as to assist in the evaluation of underlying trends in our businesses. This measure eliminates the significant level of noncash depreciation and amortization expense that results from the capital-intensive nature of our businesses and from intangible assets recognized in business combinations. It is also unaffected by our capital and tax structures, as our management excludes these results when evaluating our operating performance. Our management use this financial measure to evaluate our consolidated operating performance and the operating performance of our operating segments as well as to allocate resources and capital to our operating segments. Additionally, we believe that Adjusted EBITDA is useful to investors because it is one of the bases for comparing our operating performance with that of other companies in our industries, although our measure of Adjusted EBITDA may not be directly comparable to similar measures used by other companies.
