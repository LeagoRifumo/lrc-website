Business Model
Stantec is a global leader in sustainable engineering, architecture, and environmental consulting. Our professionals deliver the expertise, technology, and innovation communities need to manage aging infrastructure, demographic and population changes, the energy transition, and more. Our strategy is guided by our vision: the success of our clients, communities, and people worldwide is our greatest ambition. The diverse perspectives of our partners and interested parties drive us to think beyond what’s previously been done on critical issues like climate change, digital transformation, and future-proofing our cities and infrastructure.
At Stantec, community encompasses everyone connected to the work we do—from our project teams and industry colleagues to our clients and the people our work impacts. The Stantec community unites approximately 34,000 employees working in over 450 locations across six continents. Please see page M-2 of Stantec’s 2025 Annual Report for further details on our business model.

Q3 2026 compared to Q3 2025
We achieved strong third quarter adjusted net income of $212.1 million and adjusted earnings per share of $1.89, reflecting an increase of 23.5%, driven by net revenue growth and strong operational performance.
•Net revenue increased 9.4% or $159.5 million, to $1.9 billion, driven by organic growth of 5.3% and acquisition growth of 2.6%. We achieved organic growth in all of our regions and businesses, most notably in Water with double-digit organic growth.
•Project margin increased 10.5% or $97.0 million, to $1,024.9 million, reflecting an improvement of 60 basis points to 55.0%. This was a result of net revenue growth, solid project execution and favorable project recoveries.
•Adjusted EBITDA increased 16.6% or $53.7 million, to $377.1 million. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 20.2%, an increase of 120 basis points compared to Q3 2025. The growth in margin was primarily due to solid project margin and lower administrative and marketing expenses as a percentage of net revenue, reflecting our focus on efficient management of operations and optimization of discretionary spending.
•Net income increased 19.7% or $29.6 million, to $179.6 million, and diluted EPS increased 21.2%, or $0.28, to $1.60, mainly due to net revenue growth, improvements in project margin, and, as a percentage of net revenue, a 60 basis point reduction in administrative and marketing expenses.

Outlook
The revised guidance that we provided in the Outlook section of our Q2 2026 Interim Report (incorporated herein by reference) was based on our expectations that key demand drivers supporting our industry remain favorable and that we continue to expect to achieve results in line with or exceeding our previously disclosed 2026 targets. Diversified opportunities continue to arise from strong demand for services related to aging infrastructure and urbanization, climate change and the need for resilience to extreme weather events, future technologies and associated energy requirements, advanced manufacturing, and resource security.
Overall, we continue to expect net revenue growth in line with previous expectations. Based on consideration of our financial performance to date in 2026, we have narrowed the range of expected annual net revenue growth from 8.5% to 11.5% to 9.0% to 11.0%. At a regional level, in the fourth quarter, we anticipate the improvement in organic net revenue growth in Canada and the US experienced in the third quarter to continue. This is expected to yield annual organic net revenue growth for 2026 in the mid-single digit range for the US and the low to mid-single digit range in Canada. Our US organic net revenue growth expectations are underpinned by organic growth in backlog across our business lines and continued strength in other customer demand signals. We continue to expect Canada’s organic net revenue growth to be driven primarily by public sector spending plans and higher demand levels in the Water and Buildings business lines. We continue to expect organic net revenue growth in Global to be in the high-single digits, driven by continued high levels of activity in our Water business, ongoing work under Asset Management Programs and frameworks, and strong demand in Energy & Resources.
Our year-to-date results reflect strong margin improvements and effectiveness in managing operating costs, and we have refined our estimates of adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income margin, and adjusted EPS growth accordingly. We expect that adjusted EBITDA margin will reach a record range of 18.3% to 18.5% in 2026, reflecting a narrowing and increase of the range from 17.8% to 18.3%. This will have a positive impact on profitability, and we have therefore adjusted upwards our estimate of adjusted net income margin from 9.5% to 9.8% and narrowed and adjusted upwards the range of our EPS growth target from 15.0% to 18.0% to 17.0% to 19.0%. These improvements reflect strong project margins resulting from effective operational execution, as well as continued focus on enhanced strategies in the management of administration and marketing costs. These initiatives include the continued expansion of our high-value centers, optimization of digital strategies, and increased efficiencies from improved scale in certain key geographies.

Financial Performance
The following sections outline specific factors that affected the results of our operations in Q3 2026 and year-to-date Q3 2026.
Gross and Net Revenue
While providing professional services, we incur certain direct costs for subconsultants, equipment, and other expenditures that are recoverable directly from our clients. Revenue associated with these direct costs is included in gross revenue. Because these direct costs and associated revenue can vary significantly from contract to contract, changes in gross revenue may not be indicative of our revenue trends. Accordingly, we also report net revenue (which is gross revenue less subconsultant and other direct expenses) and analyze results in relation to net revenue rather than gross revenue.
In Q3 2026, we delivered net revenue of $1.9 billion and achieved a net revenue increase of 9.4% compared to Q3 2025. Year to date, we delivered net revenue of $5.3 billion, an overall 10% increase. In both periods, net revenue growth reflects significant contributions from our acquisitions, in particular Page, organic growth in all of our regions, and double digit growth in our Water business. Public infrastructure spending and private investment continue to be key growth drivers in 2026, with strong demand across the water and energy sectors. Other key drivers are the ongoing challenges to build climate resiliency, tackle resource security, and the growing need for power delivery solutions. The focus on Smart Cities and buildings, including hospitals, data centers, and other mission-critical facilities to meet the needs in the civic, healthcare, residential, and industrial markets, also continues to drive growth.
We generate over 75% of our gross revenue in foreign currencies, primarily in US dollars, British pounds (GBP), and Australian (AU) dollars. Fluctuations in these and other currencies had a net $24.3 million positive impact on our net revenue results in Q3 2026 compared to Q3 2025 and a net $8.9 million positive impact on our net revenue results year to date in 2026 compared to 2025:
•The US dollar averaged $1.38 in Q3 2025 and $1.40 in Q3 2026 —a 1.4% increase. Year to date, the US dollar averaged $1.40 in Q3 2025 and $1.39 in Q3 2026 — a 0.7% decrease. The strengthened USD compared to the Canadian dollar had a positive effect on gross and net revenues during the quarter, and had a year-to-date negative impact on gross and net revenues.
•The GBP averaged $1.86 in Q3 2025 and $1.88 in Q3 2026—a 1.1% increase. Year to date, the GBP averaged $1.84 in Q3 2025 and $1.86 in Q3 2026 —a 1.1% increase. The strengthened GBP compared to the Canadian dollar had a positive effect on gross and net revenues.
•The AU dollar averaged $0.90 in Q3 2025 and $0.99 in Q3 2026—a 10.0% increase. Year to date, the AU dollar averaged $0.90 in Q3 2025 and $0.97 Q3 2026—a 7.8% increase. The strengthened AU dollar compared to the Canadian dollar had a positive effect on gross and net revenues.
Fluctuations in other foreign currencies did not have a material impact on our gross and net revenue.

Capital Management
Our objective in managing Stantec's capital is to provide sufficient capacity to cover normal operating and capital expenditures and to have flexibility for financing future growth. We focus our capital allocations on increasing shareholder value through funding accretive acquisitions in pursuit of our growth strategy, while maintaining a strong balance sheet and managing dividend increases to our target payout ratio in a sustainable manner.
Although our priority in deploying capital remains funding accretive acquisitions, as sufficient capital is available, we believe that the repurchase of outstanding common shares is an appropriate use of Company funds when, from time-to-time, the market price of our common shares does not fully reflect the value of our business or future business prospects.
During the quarter, we amended our NCIB, increasing the maximum number of common shares available for repurchase from 2,281,339, originally approved in the first quarter of 2026, to 5,703,349 during the period March 12, 2026 to March 11, 2027. The Automatic Share Purchase Plan (ASPP) was also amended for the increase in the maximum number of common shares available for repurchase. The ASPP permits a designated broker, in its sole discretion and based on the parameters established by the Company, to purchase common shares for cancellation under the NCIB at any time during predetermined trading blackout periods.
We manage our capital structure according to our internal guideline of maintaining a net debt to adjusted EBITDA (actual trailing twelve months) ratio of less than 2.0 to 1.0. There may be occasions when we exceed our target by completing acquisitions that increase our debt level for a period of time.
