EXHIBIT 99.1
Perrigo Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results From Continuing Operations
•Execution of Three-S plan driving sequential improvement in operating results and market share gains across U.S. Store Brand OTC and key European brands
•Infant Formula divestiture expected to simplify portfolio and sharpen focus on higher-return consumer self care categories; strategic review of Oral Care business remains ongoing
•Updated full-year 2026 outlook to reflect divestiture, more measured retailer inventory positioning, and continued softness in Europe
DUBLIN, November 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Perrigo Company plc (NYSE: PRGO) ("Perrigo" or the "Company"), a leading provider of consumer self-care products, today announced financial results from continuing operations for the third quarter ended September 26, 2026.
“Our Three-S plan is delivering continued progress in the areas of the business within our control,” said Albert Manzone, Interim President and Chief Executive Officer. “We gained market share again in U.S. store brand OTC and in key European brands. Category trends improved sequentially, which, combined with disciplined cost management, helped offset a difficult consumer backdrop.
“The sale of Infant Formula is an important step in evolving Perrigo into a more focused consumer self-care company. It is expected to enhance our margin profile, materially reduce expected future capital expenditures, and support continued deleveraging and reinvestment in the business. The result will be a simpler Perrigo, better positioned for profitable growth.
“We have adjusted our full-year outlook to reflect the divestiture, more measured retailer inventory positioning ahead of the cough and cold season, and continued softness in Europe. Our underlying model, combining scaled brands and store brands in categories people need every day, remains unchanged, and the actions we are taking to simplify Perrigo are positioning us for more consistent and profitable growth over the long term.”

Net Sales
•Core net sales were $908 million, declining 1.7% year-over-year, while Core organic net sales decreased 1.1%. Results reflected lower consumption in our Self Care segment, which led to more measured retailer inventory positioning ahead of the cough and cold season within the Upper Respiratory and Pain & Sleep categories. Performance was also impacted by lower contract manufacturing volumes. Partially offsetting those factors were continued share gains across U.S. Store Brand OTC and key European brands, supported by innovation and enhanced consumer marketing initiatives. Pricing decreased 0.4% and volume/mix decreased 0.7%.
•All In reported net sales declined 5.2% year-over-year to $989 million. All In results were primarily driven by the same factors as Core net sales in addition to the year-over-year impact of divestitures and lower Infant Formula net sales.
Gross Margin
•Reported All In gross margin was 32.4%, a decrease of 370 basis points.
•Core adjusted gross margin decreased 140 basis points to 37.9% driven by lower net sales volumes and unfavorable mix. These headwinds were partially offset by the net recognition of a recovery of a portion of previously paid tariffs and continued market share gains.
•All In adjusted gross margin decreased 370 basis points to 36.3%, driven by the same factors impacting Core adjusted gross margin in addition to the impact of divestitures and lower Infant Formula gross margin, which included the impact of planned under absorption.
Operating Margin
•Reported operating margin was 2.3%, a decrease of 460 basis points.
•Core adjusted operating margin decreased 210 basis points to 14.3% due to unfavorable gross profit flow through and higher operating expenses, primarily driven by increased advertising and promotional investments and the normalization of variable employee compensation. This decline was partially offset by benefits from the Operational Enhancement Program.

•All In adjusted operating margin decreased 360 basis points to 13.1%, driven by the same factors impacting Core adjusted operating margin as well as the impact of divestitures and Infant Formula.
Other Items
•Reported interest and other expense decreased $9.0 million to $41.2 million.
•Net adjusted interest and other expense increased $0.5 million to $41.2 million.
•The Company's reported effective tax rate was 21.1%. The Company’s adjusted effective tax rate decreased to 3.5%, reflecting changes in the jurisdictional mix of earnings.
Diluted EPS
•Reported diluted EPS was $(0.10) compared to $0.09 in the prior year.
•Core adjusted EPS declined $0.06 to $0.61, a 9.0% decrease from the prior year. Results were impacted by a reduction in net sales and operating income, partially offset by a lower effective tax rate, which benefited results by $0.10.
•All In adjusted diluted EPS declined $0.20 to $0.60, a 25.0% decrease from the prior year due to the same factors impacting Core adjusted EPS in addition to the impact of divestitures and Infant Formula. Results also reflected approximately $0.12 of unfavorable EPS impact from planned under absorption within Infant Formula.

Self Care
Net sales decreased 3.2% compared to the prior year, inclusive of a 0.2% unfavorable impact of currency translation. Results reflected lower consumption, which led to more measured retailer inventory positioning ahead of the cough and cold season within the Upper Respiratory and Pain & Sleep categories. Performance was also impacted by lower contract manufacturing volumes and the unfavorable impact of pricing. These pressures were partly offset by growth in the Healthy Lifestyle category, driven by continued share gains of store brand nicotine replacement products, and the Digestive Health category, supported by higher volumes of store brand laxative products.
Segment adjusted operating income decreased 19.4%, primarily due to unfavorable gross profit flow through driven by unfavorable production variances, unfavorable mix, and higher advertising and promotional investments. These pressures were partially offset by benefits from the Operational Enhancement Program.
Specialty Care
Net sales decreased 4.6% compared to the prior year, inclusive of a 1.1% unfavorable impact of currency translation. Women’s Health delivered strong growth, led by solid momentum and share gains for ellaOne® and continued growth from Opill®. Within the Skin Health category, strong
in-season performance from Compeed® and Jungle Formula® was more than offset by lower sales of store brand and contract manufactured Minoxidil in addition to lower net sales of Mederma®.
Segment adjusted operating income decreased 24.2% due to unfavorable production variances, unfavorable mix in Skin Health, and higher advertising and promotional investments across the segment. These pressures were partially offset by benefits from the Operational Enhancement Program.
4
Infant Formula
Net sales decreased 9.6% compared to the prior year. Growth in store brand infant formula was more than offset by expected lower contract manufacturing volumes, reflecting the shift of certain contract formula shipments that were recorded in the second quarter.
Segment adjusted operating income decreased primarily due to unfavorable gross profit flow through, including planned under absorption, which was partially offset by lower operating expenses.
All Other
Net sales decreased 13.1%, inclusive of a 1.2% unfavorable impact of currency translation, primarily due to the impact of divestitures.
Segment adjusted operating income increased 18.4% due to the net recognition of a recovery of a portion of previously paid tariffs, in addition to improved productivity and lower operating expenses in the Oral Care category. These factors more than offset the impact of divestitures.
Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
•Net cash from operating activities was $96 million in the third quarter.
•Third quarter capital expenditures were $18 million and the Company returned $40 million to shareholders through dividends.
•Cash and cash equivalents as of September 26, 2026 were $437 million while total debt was $3.3 billion.
•Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company announced an agreement to divest its Infant Formula business for total consideration of up to $175 million. The transaction consideration consists of $40 million in upfront cash at closing and up to an additional $135 million contingent on the achievement of net sales milestones through 2030. Net proceeds are expected to be deployed in accordance with Perrigo's capital allocation priorities. The divestiture is expected to simplify our portfolio and sharpen our focus on higher-return consumer self care categories, while enhancing the Company's margin profile. After considering expected carrying value relative to cash proceeds and an estimate of fair value of the contingent milestones, we expect to recognize an impairment loss between $275.0 million and $375.0 million resulting from the measurement as held for sale during the fourth quarter of 2026.
Fiscal 2026 Outlook
Beginning with the fourth quarter and full-year 2026 reporting, the Infant Formula business will be reported as discontinued operations. Accordingly, the Company's All In outlook no longer includes Infant Formula but continues to include other divested businesses. The Company's Core outlook continues to exclude Infant Formula and previously-announced divestitures. For fiscal 2026, the Company has updated its outlook and related assumptions to reflect the Infant Formula divestiture, more measured retailer inventory positioning ahead of the cough and cold season, and continued softness in the European consumer environment.
