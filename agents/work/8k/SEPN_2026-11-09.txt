Septerna Highlights Business Progress and Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Phase 1 Clinical Trial for PTH1R Agonist SEP-479 Ongoing; SAD / MAD Data Expected in Q1 2027
Plans to Initiate Phase 1 Clinical Trial of Newly Selected Oral TSHR NAM Development Candidate SEP-204 in Q1 2027
Cash Position of $565.2 Million Expected to Support Operating Plans at Least into mid-2029
SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Calif. – November 09, 2026 – Septerna, Inc. (Nasdaq: SEPN), a clinical-stage biotechnology company pioneering a new era of G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) drug discovery, today highlighted pipeline progress and anticipated milestones and reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“We are building momentum across our portfolio, with an upcoming important clinical milestone for SEP-479, our oral PTH1R agonist for hypoparathyroidism, and the selection of SEP-204 as our oral TSHR NAM development candidate for Graves’ disease and thyroid eye disease,” said Jeffrey Finer, M.D., Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer and Co-founder of Septerna. “With Phase 1 data for SEP-479 and the initiation a Phase 1 trial of SEP-204 expected in the first quarter of 2027, we are moving closer to realizing our ambition to translate our GPCR expertise into differentiated oral medicines for patients with significant unmet needs.”
Recent Portfolio Progress and Anticipated Milestones
•
SEP-479 PTH1R Agonist for Hypoparathyroidism:
o
Septerna’s Phase 1 single-ascending dose (SAD) and multiple-ascending dose (MAD) clinical trial of SEP-479, an oral small molecule PTH1R agonist in development for hypoparathyroidism, is ongoing in healthy volunteers, with data expected in the first quarter of 2027.
•
SEP-204 TSHR NAM for Graves’ Disease and Thyroid Eye Disease:
o
In conjunction with last week’s American Thyroid Association (ATA) annual meeting, Septerna announced the selection of SEP-204 as its oral TSHR negative allosteric modulator (NAM) development candidate as a potential disease-modifying treatment for Graves’ disease and thyroid eye disease.
o
Preclinical data presented at ATA demonstrated that SEP-204 potently and selectively inhibited TSHR, restored normal thyroid hormone T4 levels and reduced thyroid enlargement and orbital proptosis in Graves’ disease animal models. SEP-204 was generally well tolerated in 28-day GLP toxicology studies and demonstrated a favorable pharmacokinetic profile anticipated to support once-daily oral dosing in humans. Additional details are available in the data release.
o
Following the successful completion of the remaining preclinical development activities, Septerna anticipates initiating a Phase 1 clinical trial for SEP-204 in healthy volunteers in the first quarter of 2027.
•
SEP-631 MRGPRX2 NAM for Mast Cell-Driven Diseases:
o
Following positive Phase 1 results reported in March 2026, Septerna continues to evaluate capital-efficient, signal-finding clinical studies of SEP-631 in mast cell-driven diseases with high unmet need.
•
Additional Programs:
o
Under its global collaboration with Novo, initiated in July 2025, Septerna and Novo are advancing oral small molecule programs for obesity, type 2 diabetes and other cardiometabolic diseases. Novo recently assumed responsibility for a development candidate delivered by Septerna’s research team and initiated IND-enabling toxicology studies. To date, Septerna has achieved a total of $13 million in research milestones under the collaboration.
o
Septerna also continues to advance wholly owned discovery-stage programs utilizing its Native Complex Platform® across multiple therapeutic areas, including a PTH1R agonist program for osteoporosis.
Corporate Announcements
o
In August 2026, Septerna announced the appointment of Rajiv Patni, M.D., as Chief Medical Officer. Dr. Patni is a physician-scientist and accomplished biopharmaceutical executive with more than 25 years of experience leading research, development, regulatory, and medical affairs organizations.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•
Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $565.2 million as of September 30, 2026. Septerna expects its existing cash runway to fund operations at least into mid-2029.
•
Revenue: Revenue was $18.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $21.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 included the amortization of $2.7 million of the $195.0 million upfront payment from Novo Nordisk, and $5.2 million from the achievement of research milestones, in addition to $11.0 million for research services associated with the collaboration.
•
R&D Expenses: Research and development (R&D) expenses were $36.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $24.3 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
•
G&A Expenses: General and administrative (G&A) expenses were $9.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $7.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
•
Net (Loss) Income: Net loss was $26.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to net income of $8.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
About Septerna
Septerna, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company with a world-class team of GPCR experts and drug developers advancing cutting-edge science to unlock the full potential of GPCR therapies for patients with significant unmet needs. The company’s proprietary Native Complex Platform® is designed to enable new approaches to GPCR drug discovery and has led to the development of a diverse pipeline of novel oral small molecule drug candidates. Septerna is advancing programs in endocrinology, immunology and inflammation, metabolic diseases and additional therapeutic areas, both independently and with partners.
