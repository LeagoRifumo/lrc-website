EXHIBIT 99.1
Teva’s Pivot to Growth Strategy Delivers 33% Growth Across Key Innovative Brands; Raises 2026 Outlook for AUSTEDO®, AJOVY® and UZEDY®; Delivers Substantial Gross Margin Expansion
COMPANY STRENGTHENS NEUROSCIENCE LEADERSHIP WITH WELTRUZA™ LAUNCH AND STRATEGIC ADDITION OF BXCL-501
Q3 2026 revenues of $4.4 billion, a decrease of 2% year-over-year in both U.S. dollars and local currency terms, reflecting lower global generics revenues, primarily due to increased competition for lenalidomide capsules (a generic version of Revlimid®) in the U.S. This decrease was partially offset by continued strong growth across Teva’s key innovative brands.
Strong profitability: Gross profit margin was 55.5% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 51.4% in the third quarter of 2025; non-GAAP gross profit margin increased to the highest quarterly level in the last decade, by 345 basis points to 58.7%, excluding the $500 million development milestone payments received for duvakitug (anti-TL1A) in Q4 2025; operating income was $714 million and non-GAAP operating income was $1,393 million; diluted earnings per share (EPS) was $0.20 and non-GAAP diluted EPS was $0.86; cash flow generated from operating activities was $241 million and free cash flow was approximately $0.5 billion.
Key innovative brands continued to drive Teva’s portfolio transformation, collectively generating approximately $1.1 billion in revenues and growing 33% year-over-year in local currency:
AUSTEDO global revenues were $751 million, increasing 21% year-over-year in local currency. Teva is raising its 2026 revenue outlook to $2.55 billion–$2.65 billion, from $2.45 billion–$2.60 billion.
AJOVY global revenues were $269 million, increasing 61% year-over-year in local currency. Teva is raising its 2026 revenue outlook to $930 million–$960 million, from $850 million–$870 million.
UZEDY revenues were $85 million, increasing 96% year-over-year in local currency. Teva is raising its 2026 revenue outlook to $290 million–$310 million, from $270 million–$290 million.
Building the next wave of neuroscience growth: Teva received FDA approval for WELTRUZA in the U.S. in October 2026, marking a significant expansion for long-acting injectable schizophrenia treatment and highlighting Teva’s established commercial capabilities and deep understanding of the patient journey. The Company continues to target LAI schizophrenia portfolio (UZEDY and WELTRUZA) with peak sales potential of $1.5 billion–$2.0 billion.
Expanding Teva’s growth through strategic business development:
Acquired BXCL-501, from BioXcel Therapeutics Inc., which is under FDA review for potential at-home use for the acute treatment agitation associated with schizophrenia or bipolar I or II disorder in adults;
Deepened collaboration with Samsung Bioepis for the commercialization of two additional biosimilars, infliximab-abda, a biosimilar to Remicade® (infliximab), and etanercept-ykro, a biosimilar to Enbrel® (etanercept), expanding the Company’s burgeoning global pipeline and portfolio of biosimilars.
Generics and biosimilars remain an important foundation: global generics revenues decreased 16% year-over-year in local currency, primarily reflecting lower U.S. revenues from lenalidomide capsules (a generic version of Revlimid®), partially offset by recently launched generic products and growth in biosimilars. Teva’s biosimilars business continued to expand and is currently expected to exceed $0.8 billion in 2026 revenues, achieving our original 2027 revenue objective one year ahead of plan.
Innovative pipeline successes strengthen Teva’s longer-term growth profile with the potential to launch at least one product per year over five years:
The U.S. FDA accepted the ecopipam New Drug Application (NDA) for review, advancing a potential first-in-class selective D1 receptor anti-agonist for Tourette syndrome in the pediatric population, with an action date expected in Q1 2027.
Teva reported positive Phase 2a results for anti-IL-15 (TEV-‘408) in Celiac disease, and is targeting to begin Phase 2b in vitiligo in Q4 2026.
Teva discontinued its phase 2 Multiple System Atrophy (MSA) study of emrusolmin (TEV-‘286), following an interim futility analysis that indicated low probability of the study meeting its primary efficacy endpoint if continued.
Financial strength, with continued value creation for shareholders: the return to an investment grade credit rating, refinancing of ~$4.9 billion in debt during Q3 2026, and the direct listing of Teva's ordinary shares on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE); all reflect Teva's strong execution of its Pivot to Growth strategy, continued financial strength and long-term shareholder value creation.
Finalized Most-Favored-Nation (MFN) agreement with the U.S. Administration, including a prospective pricing commitment with respect to certain future innovative product launches.

TEL AVIV, Israel, Nov. 03, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Teva Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. (NYSE and TASE: TEVA) today reported results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Richard Francis, Teva's President and CEO, said: “Our strong third-quarter performance is another clear demonstration of Pivot to Growth in action and how far Teva has come. The FDA approval of WELTRUZA has further advanced our innovative portfolio, our recently announced biosimilars agreement with Samsung Bioepis has strengthened our generics powerhouse and our return to investment grade reflects the transformation of our financial position. Together, these achievements represent how we are delivering sustained innovative growth and translating that growth into greater profitability, with our three key innovative brands collectively growing 33% in local currency to approximately $1.1 billion year-over-year. This performance, together with continued margin expansion and cash flow generation, gives us the confidence to raise our 2026 revenue outlook for all three brands.”
Mr. Francis added: “At the same time, we are continuously building the next phase of sustainable growth at Teva. The U.S. launch of WELTRUZA expands our schizophrenia franchise, while progress across ecopipam, anti-IL-15 and our broader late-stage pipeline supports our ambition to deliver at least five potential innovative launches over five years. Our return to investment grade and debt refinancing reflect our strengthened financial position.”
Pivot to Growth Strategy
In the third quarter of 2026 and into October 2026, Teva continued to execute on the four pillars of its Pivot to Growth strategy:
Delivering on our growth engines
Teva’s key innovative brands, AUSTEDO, AJOVY and UZEDY, collectively generated approximately $1.1 billion in revenues and grew 33% year-over-year in local currency. Strong performance across all three brands supports increased 2026 revenue outlooks of these products and continues to shift Teva’s portfolio toward innovative medicines. The FDA approval and U.S. launch of WELTRUZA further expand Teva’s long-acting injectable schizophrenia portfolio and builds on the commercial momentum established by UZEDY.
Stepping up innovation
Teva continued to advance a differentiated late-stage pipeline across neuroscience and immunology. The FDA accepted ecopipam's (EBS-101) NDA for review, positive Phase 2a results were reported for anti-IL-15 (TEV-‘408) in Celiac disease, and the anti-IL-15 program is planned to advance into Phase 2b development in vitiligo later this year. Together with the acquisition of BXCL-501 (dexmetomidine sublingual film) from BioXcel Therapeutics, these milestones expand Teva’s capabilities in areas of significant unmet need and support its ambition to deliver a sustained cadence of potential launches.
Sustaining our generics powerhouse
Teva continues to strengthen its global generics business through an increased focus on complex generics and biosimilars. While global generics revenues declined primarily due to lower revenues of lenalidomide capsules (a generic version of Revlimid®) in the U.S., revenues from biosimilars continued to expand. Teva currently expects more than $0.8 billion in biosimilars revenues in 2026, reaching its original 2027 revenue objective one year early, with further portfolio expansion supported by new collaborations, including the recently announced agreement with Samsung Bioepis.
Focusing our business
Teva continued to transform and modernize its business through the Teva Transformation programs, which combined with innovative product growth potential, is expected to support the Company’s objective of achieving a 30% non-GAAP operating income margin and approximately $700 million of net savings, by 2027 including ~two-thirds expected to be realized in 2026. At the same time, Teva continued to maintain disciplined capital allocation and strengthen its financial profile. During Q3 2026, Moody’s and S&P upgraded Teva to investment grade, following Fitch’s earlier upgrade; and Teva refinanced ~$4.9 billion of debt. All these reflect Teva's goal to achieve two times debt leverage by the end of 2027.
Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Results
Revenues in the third quarter of 2026 were $4,395 million, a decrease of 2% in both U.S. dollars and local currency terms compared to the third quarter of 2025. This decrease was mainly due to lower revenues from generic products, primarily lenalidomide capsules (a generic version of Revlimid®) in our U.S. segment, partially offset by higher revenues from our key innovative products, primarily AUSTEDO, AJOVY and UZEDY.
Exchange rate movements in the third quarter of 2026, net of hedging effects, positively impacted revenues by $5 million, compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Gross profit in the third quarter of 2026 was $2,440 million, an increase of 6% compared to $2,304 million in the third quarter of 2025. Gross profit margin was 55.5% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 51.4% in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP gross profit was $2,580 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 4% compared to $2,475 million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP gross profit margin was 58.7% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 55.3% in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in both gross profit margin and non-GAAP gross profit margin was mainly due to a favorable mix of products, primarily higher revenues from our key innovative products, largely AUSTEDO, partially offset by lower revenues from generic products, primarily lenalidomide capsules (a generic version of Revlimid®).
Research and Development (R&D) expenses, net in the third quarter of 2026, were $297 million, an increase of 16% compared to $256 million in the third quarter of 2025. This increase was mainly due to higher expenses in our immunology and neuroscience projects, as well as in our biosimilars pipeline. Our R&D expenses, net in the third quarters of 2026 and 2025, were also impacted by reimbursements and cost sharing from our strategic partnerships and collaborations entered into in recent years.
Selling and Marketing (S&M) expenses in the third quarter of 2026, were $709 million, an increase of 8% compared to the third quarter of 2025. This increase was mainly due to promotional activities related to our key innovative products, primarily AUSTEDO, and other innovative product launch preparation activities in our U.S. segment.
General and Administrative (G&A) expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $298 million, a decrease of 6% compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Other Income in the third quarter of 2026 was $68 million, compared to other loss of $7 million in the third quarter of 2025, mainly related to a capital gain from the sale of a property in Europe.
Operating Income was $714 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to an operating income of $882 million in the third quarter of 2025. Operating Income as a percentage of revenues was 16.2% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 19.7% in the third quarter of 2025. This decrease was mainly due to higher tangible asset impairments and higher legal settlements and loss contingencies, partially offset by an increase in gross profit. Non-GAAP operating income in the third quarter of 2026 was $1,393 million representing a non-GAAP operating margin of 31.7% compared to $1,294 million representing 28.9%, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025. This increase in non-GAAP operating margin in the third quarter of 2026 was mainly due to higher gross profit margin as described above.
Exchange rate movements in the third quarter of 2026, net of hedging effects, had a negative impact of $2 million on our operating income and non-GAAP operating income compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Financial expenses, net in the third quarter of 2026, were $414 million, mainly comprised of a loss of $224 million on extinguishment of debt and net interest expenses of $174 million. In the third quarter of 2025, financial expenses, net were $237 million, mainly comprised of net interest expenses of $209 million.
In the third quarter of 2026, we recognized a tax expense of $66 million on pre-tax income of $300 million. In the third quarter of 2025, we recognized a tax expense of $214 million on pre-tax income of $646 million.
Our tax rate in the third quarter of 2026 was 22.1%, compared to 33.1% in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP tax rate in the third quarter of 2026 was 16.5%, compared to 14.7% in the third quarter of 2025. Our tax rate and non-GAAP tax rate in the third quarter of 2026 were mainly affected by the generation of profits in various jurisdictions in which tax rates are different than the Israeli tax rate and other infrequent or non-recurring items. Our tax rate and non-GAAP tax rate in the third quarter of 2025 were mainly affected by changes in deferred tax balances due to statutory tax rate changes and interest and inflation adjustments related to the agreement with the Israeli Tax Authorities.
Considering the above, we expect our annual non-GAAP tax rate for 2026 to be between 20%-23%, higher than our non-GAAP tax rate for 2025, which was 15.8%.
Net income attributable to Teva and diluted earnings per share in the third quarter of 2026 were $233 million and $0.20, respectively, compared to net income attributable to Teva and diluted earnings per share of $433 million and $0.37, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025. This decrease was mainly due to lower operating income, as discussed above. Non-GAAP net income attributable to Teva and non-GAAP diluted earnings per share in the third quarter of 2026 were $1,013 million and $0.86, respectively, compared to $910 million and $0.78, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA was $1,496 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 7%, compared to $1,394 million in the third quarter of 2025.
As of September 30, 2026 and 2025, the fully diluted share count for purposes of calculating our market capitalization was approximately 1,195 million shares and 1,184 million shares, respectively.

Generic products (including biosimilar products) revenues in our United States segment in the third quarter of 2026 were $718 million, a decrease of 39% compared to the third quarter of 2025. This decrease was mainly driven by lower revenues from lenalidomide capsules (a generic version of Revlimid®) due to increased generic competition in the U.S., partially offset by higher revenues from our portfolio of biosimilar products and from recently launched generic products.
Among the most significant generic products we sold in the United States in the third quarter of 2026 were epinephrine injectable solution (a generic equivalent of EpiPen® and EpiPen Jr®), TRUXIMA® (a biosimilar to Rituxan®), and SIMLANDI® (a biosimilar to Humira®). In the third quarter of 2026, our total prescriptions were approximately 227 million (based on trailing twelve months), representing 5.8% of total U.S. generic prescriptions, compared to approximately 261 million (based on trailing twelve months), representing 6.7% of total U.S. generic prescriptions in the third quarter of 2025, all according to IQVIA data.
On September 28, 2026, we announced that the FDA approved DEGEVMA™ (denosumab-adet) as a biosimilar to Xgeva® (denosumab). DEGEVMA is indicated for the prevention of bone complications in adults with advanced cancer that has spread to the bone, for the treatment of adults and skeletally mature adolescents with giant cell tumor of bone, and for the treatment of hypercalcemia of malignancy.
AJOVY revenues in our United States segment in the third quarter of 2026 were $138 million, an increase of 88% compared to the third quarter of 2025, mainly due to a reduction in sales allowance as well as growth in volume. In the third quarter of 2026, AJOVY’s exit market share in the United States in terms of total number of prescriptions was 32.8% out of the subcutaneous injectable anti-CGRP class, compared to 32.2% in the third quarter of 2025.
AUSTEDO revenues (which include AUSTEDO XR®) in our United States segment in the third quarter of 2026 were $727 million, an increase of 21%, compared to the third quarter of 2025. This increase was mainly due to growth in volume and a favorable business mix including improved net-price realization.
AUSTEDO XR (deutetrabenazine) extended-release tablets was approved by the FDA on February 17, 2023 in three doses of 6, 12 and 24 mg, and became commercially available in the U.S. in May 2023. The FDA approved AUSTEDO XR as a one pill, once-daily treatment option in doses of 30, 36, 42, and 48 mg in May 2024 and in 18 mg in July 2024. AUSTEDO XR is a once-daily formulation indicated in adults for tardive dyskinesia and chorea associated with Huntington’s disease, which is additional to the twice-daily AUSTEDO.
UZEDY (risperidone) extended-release injectable suspension revenues in our United States segment in the third quarter of 2026 were $85 million, an increase of 96% compared to the third quarter of 2025, mainly due to growth in volume.
On October 9, 2026, we announced that the FDA approved WELTRUZA (olanzapine) for extended-release injectable suspension, a new once-monthly, subcutaneous long-acting injectable (LAI) for the treatment of schizophrenia in adults. WELTRUZA is expected to become available in the U.S. in the coming weeks, in three different strengths (318 mg, 425 mg, 531 mg). In May 2026, the European Medicines Agency accepted Teva’s Marketing Authorization Application for olanzapine LAI (TEV-’749). WELTRUZA is protected in the U.S. by four Orange Book listed patents expiring between 2033 and 2044, and in Europe by five granted patents expiring between 2031 and 2044.
BENDEKA® and TREANDA® combined revenues in our United States segment in the third quarter of 2026 were $30 million, a decrease of 15% compared to the third quarter of 2025, mainly due to competition from alternative therapies, as well as from branded and generic bendamustine products.
COPAXONE® revenues in our United States segment in the third quarter of 2026 were $58 million, a decrease of 8% compared to the third quarter of 2025.
