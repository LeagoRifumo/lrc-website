Federal Realty Investment Trust Reports Strong Third Quarter 2026 Results and Guidance Raise
NORTH BETHESDA, Md. (October 30, 2026) – Federal Realty Investment Trust (NYSE: FRT) today reported its results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. For the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, net income available for common shareholders per diluted share was $0.74 and $0.69, respectively. Operating income for the same periods was $116.9 million and $110.7 million, respectively.
Highlights for the third quarter include:
•Generated Nareit-defined funds from operations available to common shareholders (Nareit FFO) and Core funds from operations available to common shareholders (Core FFO) per diluted share of $1.87 each for the quarter, a 5.6% increase year-over-year.
•Signed 117 leases for 532,220 square feet of comparable retail space with rent growth of 14% on a cash basis and 25% on a straight-line basis.
◦On a trailing twelve-month basis, signed 447 leases for 2,601,255 square feet of comparable retail space with rent growth of 14% on a cash basis and 25% on a straight-line basis.
•Generated Comparable Property property operating income (“Comparable Property POI”) growth of 2.5% and Adjusted Comparable Property POI growth (excluding straight-line rents and amortization of in-place leases) of 4.0%.
•Reported overall portfolio occupancy of 93.8% and a leased rate of 96.5% at quarter end, with:
◦Occupancy flat and leased rate up 40 basis points sequentially.
◦Occupancy flat and leased rate up 110 basis points year-over-year.
•Continued strong small shop leased rate, ending the quarter at 94.1% leased, representing an increase of 20 basis points sequentially and 80 basis points year-over-year.
•Issued $460.0 million of 3.50% exchangeable senior notes due August 15, 2031.
•Repurchased 869,748 shares at a weighted average share price of $108.82 for approximately $95 million through October 9, 2026.
•Subsequent to quarter end, acquired The Summit, an 870,000-square-foot dominant retail center in Birmingham, AL, with a diverse mix of approximately 120 tenants for $508.0 million.
•Tightened guidance for 2026 earnings per diluted share to $4.23 to $4.27.
•Raised and tightened guidance for both 2026 Nareit FFO and Core FFO per diluted share to $7.54 to $7.58, representing over 7% Core FFO growth at the midpoint year-over-year.
“This was a really powerful quarter from a company that uses all the tools in its toolbox to make its portfolio more and more valuable,” said Donald C. Wood, President and Chief Executive Officer of Federal Realty. “On the operating side, we delivered strong leasing with materially higher new rents, higher leased occupancy and healthy cash POI growth. On the capital allocation side, we stayed disciplined: share repurchases, incremental development on our existing land and an irreplaceable acquisition,” Mr. Wood continued. “The established skill sets of our team and the strength of our balance sheet were on full display this quarter.”

Financial Results
Net Income
For the third quarter of 2026, net income available for common shareholders was $64.2 million and earnings per diluted share was $0.74 versus $59.6 million and $0.69, respectively, for the third quarter of 2025.
FFO
Nareit FFO and Core FFO were each $163.0 million, or $1.87 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $153.0 million, or $1.77 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025, a 5.6% per share increase.
Nareit FFO is a non-GAAP supplemental earnings measure which the Trust considers meaningful in measuring its operating performance. Core FFO adjusts Nareit FFO to exclude the impact of certain items that management considers are not indicative of the company’s ongoing operating and financial performance. See attachments for a reconciliation of Nareit FFO and Core FFO and a full definition of Core FFO.
Operational Update
Occupancy
The following operational metrics for the commercial portfolio are as of September 30, 2026:
•Overall portfolio occupancy was 93.8%, flat sequentially and year-over-year.
•Overall portfolio leased rate was 96.5%, up 40 basis points sequentially and up 110 basis points year-over-year.
•Small shop leased rate was 94.1%, up 20 basis points sequentially and up 80 basis points year-over-year.
The residential leased rate for comparable properties was 95.7% as of September 30, 2026.
Leasing Activity
During the third quarter of 2026, Federal Realty signed 123 leases totaling 589,726 square feet of retail space. On a comparable space basis, the company signed 117 leases for 532,220 square feet at an average rent of $40.01 per square foot, compared to $35.07 under prior leases, representing a 14% increase on a cash basis and a 25% increase on a straight-line basis.
On a trailing twelve-month basis, Federal Realty signed 447 comparable leases totaling 2,601,255 square feet, representing 14% rent spreads on a cash basis and 25% on a straight-line basis.
Transaction Activity
•On October 1, 2026, the company acquired The Summit, Alabama's premier retail destination, an approximately 870,000-square-foot open-air property in Birmingham, AL, for $508.0 million.
◦Acquired from the original developer, The Summit is a 100-acre property developed over more than 30 years. It is the most-visited retail destination in Alabama, drawing nearly 9 million visits annually from a trade area reaching 100 miles in every direction, according to Placer.ai.
◦The tenant roster includes Apple, Trader Joe’s, RH, Nordstrom Rack, ALO, Sephora and North Italia, many of which operate their only Alabama location at the property. Recent commitments from Aritzia, Reformation and LoveShackFancy reinforce The Summit’s position as the entry point for retailers expanding into Alabama.
◦Additional information is available in the Investors section of www.federalrealty.com, including the company’s October 1, 2026 press release and a detailed acquisition overview.*

About Federal Realty
Federal Realty is a recognized leader in the ownership, operation and redevelopment of high-quality retail-based properties located primarily in major coastal markets and select underserved regions with strong economic and demographic fundamentals. Founded in 1962, Federal Realty's mission is to deliver long-term, sustainable growth through investing in communities where retail demand exceeds supply. This includes a portfolio of open-air shopping centers and mixed-use destinations—such as Santana Row, Pike & Rose, and Assembly Row—which together reflect the company's ability to create distinctive, high-performing environments that serve as vibrant destinations for their communities. Federal Realty's 102 properties include approximately 3,700 tenants in 28.8 million commercial square feet, and approximately 2,700 residential units.
Federal Realty has increased its quarterly dividends to its shareholders for 59 consecutive years, the longest record in the REIT industry. The company is an S&P 500 index member and its shares are traded on the NYSE under the symbol FRT. For additional information about Federal Realty and its properties, visit www.federalrealty.com.

Comparable Property Information
