Skydance Studios
Skydance Studios include our motion picture and television production businesses. Our motion
picture group brings together two of the most iconic studios in Hollywood’s history, Warner
Bros. Pictures and Paramount Pictures, as well as the highly acclaimed New Line Cinema, DC
Studios and Skydance Animation. Our television group includes well-established industry
leaders Warner Bros. Television Group, CBS Studios, Paramount Television Studios and
Skydance Sports Entertainment. In addition, Skydance owns a deep library of more than
15,000 films and hundreds of thousands of series hours, which we distribute on our own
platforms and license to third parties.
Our goal is to establish Skydance as the premier global content engine and the home for the
world’s leading storytellers. Our strategy is anchored by the strength of our franchises and
quality of the content made by our world class studios, and we expect that demand for
premium content and global franchises will continue to increase. Collectively, our studios have
created approximately forty franchises that have generated at least $1 billion in revenue
across platforms over the past 15 years and continue to resonate with audiences today. We
are now one of the largest producers and suppliers of original live-action TV content to both
our platforms and third-parties in the world.
Paramount Pictures continues to perform well and is expected to double its output in 2026
with 15 releases. In the third quarter, PAW Patrol: The Dino Movie grossed more than $160
million worldwide and drove revenue across multiple lines of business, including consumer
products. David Ayer’s original film Heart of the Beast, starring Brad Pitt, has grossed more
than $180 million globally.
In the final two months of the year, we have six film releases across Paramount Pictures and

Skydance TV
Skydance TV brings together broadcast and cable networks, underpinned by a leading
portfolio of live news and sports programming, serving audiences in 200+ countries and
territories. Our offering spans CBS and more than 50 cable networks, among them MTV,
Comedy Central, Nickelodeon and BET, and WBD’s CNN, TNT, TBS, HGTV, Food Network and
Discovery. TNT, TBS, TLC, Food Network and CNN were five of the top 10 ad-supported cable
networks among adults 25-54 last year. We distribute marquee sports, including the NFL and,
via Eurosport, the Olympics in Europe, and we provide trusted news through CBS News and
CNN. Skydance TV is a highly profitable, cash-generative business, anchored by the enduring
strength of CBS, the crown jewel of U.S. broadcast television.
Our goal is to manage the combined portfolio to optimize long-term profitability and cash flow
as the industry shifts toward streaming. To achieve that, we have established a unified
leadership structure across TV and DTC to ensure we fully align distribution, advertising sales,
and content sales as part of a holistic strategy. This will allow us to follow one globalized
commercial approach to maximize the full potential of our scale across TV and DTC.
CBS is having another strong year and currently leads the new fall season with the #1 show
on television, all five of the top series and seven of the top 10, and 11 shows are up year over
year. Excluding sports, CBS is the #1 broadcast network in primetime and up 18% year-over-
year among total viewers. Live sports add to that strength: the NFL on CBS is posting its
highest viewership through Week 8 on record, averaging 19.6 million viewers, up 5% from
last year’s record-setting pace. It has also brought us the season’s most-watched game on
any network and the most-watched international NFL game ever.
Our scale in sports is also a competitive advantage. With CBS Sports and TNT Sports under
one roof, we have a premium sports offering that includes NFL, NHL, UFC, MLB, NASCAR,
March Madness, as well as the Olympics in Europe and the Champions League in key markets.
Advertisers can purchase media across the sports portfolio through a single partner spanning
broadcast, cable, and streaming. Sports advertising commitments have risen over 20% year-
over-year for current and upcoming sports seasons leading into 2027.
U.S. pay TV subscriber declines moderated sequentially in the third quarter as genre-based
packages gained traction with the start of the NFL season. Our sports and news networks,
including CBS, drive a majority of the U.S. Skydance TV revenues and EBITDA, giving us a
durable, live-driven foundation. We believe more consumer choice supports the long-term
health of the pay TV ecosystem, and we will continue partnering with distributors to optimize
the experience across linear and streaming.
With the CBS Television Network, premier live sports and some of the most beloved cable
brands in the world, Skydance TV is well positioned to give viewers more of what they love
across broadcast, cable and streaming, and to create lasting value for our shareholders.

Transformation Program
We expect to achieve at least $6 billion in run-rate synergies, net of reinvestment across our
cost structure within three years. More than 20 workstreams are already underway, as we
evaluate and rightsize every aspect of our organization and cost structure. But this is more
than cutting costs: it is a tech-forward transformation that will make us more efficient and
agile, create flexibility to reinvest for long-term growth, and establish a healthy margin profile
and free cash flow trajectory.
By combining two global streaming services, two film and TV studio groups, two television
portfolios, and overlapping corporate functions, we can reduce duplicative spend across an
expense base of more than $50 billion. We have identified multi-billion dollar opportunities in
key areas, including non-labor and labor spend where we expect to deliver across these
areas:
•Corporate overhead, real estate & facilities (2026 ~$8 billion): At least $2.2
billion of cost reduction, by consolidating redundant back-office and corporate
functions, reducing management layers, using shared services, implementing a single
ERP, scaling AI workflows, and shifting to lower-cost support functions. In addition, we
are rationalizing our office footprint (while keeping our production lots).
•Marketing (2026 ~$5 billion): At least $1.0 billion in savings by streamlining and
optimizing our enterprise-wide spend, as well as strategic utilization of our expanded
global portfolio to promote our key projects and initiatives. This includes unifying media
planning and buying across business units to eliminate audience overlap, coordinate
cross-BU campaigns, and maximize media efficiency at scale.
•Technology (2026 ~$4 billion): At least $1.4 billion reduction to our annual spend
by unifying our enterprise tech stack, streaming ecosystem, and broadcast architecture,
merging parallel technology functions, leveraging AI coding tools, and integrating
platforms and systems.
•Procurement and vendor management (2026 ~$9 billion): At least $1.4 billion in
savings by optimizing third-party spend across vendors spanning hundreds of services.
Our combined purchasing levels will give us economies of scale by consolidating
purchasing decisions into one centralized group.
We also see opportunity in content, which is both an expense for us but also an investment in
building valuable, long-term assets for the company. We start from an industry-leading base
of more than $30 billion in annual content spend and a deep library built over a combined
200-plus years of storytelling, and we will keep investing in the content engine that drives our
growth. With the reach of our combined platforms across streaming, broadcast and cable,
each title can work harder for us, and as one company we can make smarter choices about
how we distribute and monetize our slate.

Paramount Skydance Q3’26
In Q3, total revenue of $7.0 billion increased 5% versus revenue of $6.7 billion in Q3'25 for
Paramount Skydance, led by growth in Studios and DTC revenues, partially offset by a decline
in TV Media. Operating income was $368 million (5.2% margin) including $187 million of
restructuring and transaction-related costs. Adjusted EBITDA was $1.0 billion, a 14.2%
margin, with profitability stable year-over-year.
Direct-to-Consumer
•DTC revenue growth improved to 11% year-over-year to $2.5 billion, led by
accelerating Paramount+ revenue growth of 21% year-over-year, reflecting subscriber
growth of approximately 6% and ARPU growth of approximately 14% as our
monetization improved in the quarter driven by previous price adjustments and
accelerating advertising growth. As previously noted, we completed our integration of
BET+ into Paramount+ in Q2 and realized a full quarter of impact in Q3, which along
with Showtime declines, amounted to a six-percentage-point headwind to total DTC
growth in the quarter.
•DTC adj. EBITDA was $225 million (9.0% margin), as we recognized higher costs from
planned investment in our programming schedule in the quarter, including the UFC and
ongoing investment in Originals.
Studios
•Studios revenue increased 23% year-over-year to $1.5 billion, reflecting a strong
quarter for secondary market licensing and content produced for third parties.
•Studios adj. EBITDA was $129 million (8.6% margin), reflecting the increase in
licensing revenues.
TV Media
•TV Media revenue declined 7% year-over-year to $3.0 billion. Advertising revenue was
down 8% year-over-year, including an approximate three-percentage-point headwind
from the absence of Telefe and Chilevisión, which were sold in October 2025 and
January 2026, respectively, offset by higher political advertising revenues. Affiliate
revenue decreased 5% year-over-year, consistent with continued pay TV subscriber
erosion while rates remain resilient.
•TV Media adj. EBITDA was $1 billion, a 34.0% margin, benefiting from cost savings
initiatives.

Skydance Outlook
We will provide our full-year outlook for 2027 during our Q4 2026 earnings when we will share
our full year financial results.
Going forward, we will provide guidance on an annual basis, as we prioritize long-term value
creation, which we believe will be more consistent with our strategic approach. We will
continue to provide quarterly updates, including certain forward-looking commentary where
applicable, which will give investors visibility into our near-term progress of the business.
Based on year-to-date results, Paramount Skydance as a standalone would remain on track
for our prior 2026 guidance, including achieving the high end of our prior profitability range at
$3.9 billion.
In Q4, we expect to see continued strong growth in DTC revenue, and consistent TV revenue
trends to prior quarters, adjusting for seasonality around politics and sports. And while we are
incredibly excited about the shape of the combined Studio business and our Paramount
theatrical slate continues to perform, the near-term performance of the Warner Bros. film
slate in Q4, including films already released quarter-to-date, has underperformed. Based on
our current forecasts for the rest of the year for Studios, we expect slower revenue growth
and a profit impact of up to $200 million vs. prior expectations for Q4.
Looking ahead to 2027, we expect continued strong growth in DTC revenue, higher Studios
revenue, and an increase in pro forma total company adj. EBITDA year-over-year based on
underlying growth and the ramp of our $6 billion-plus synergies program. 2027 is the first
step in working towards achieving our medium-term financial goals.
Capital Structure & Capital Allocation
Paramount Skydance ended the quarter with $1.4 billion in cash and cash equivalents and
$15.0 billion in gross debt, prior to the acquisition. Free cash flow was $31 million, inclusive of
$174 million of restructuring, transaction-related, and transformation costs. On October 6, we
completed the Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition funded with $47 billion of new equity and
$57 billion of new secured debt. Gross debt following closing and related debt activity was $87
billion, with cash of approximately $10 billion, part of which is earmarked for payment of
cash-converted equity to WBD employees who joined the company and also to pay down
WBD’s securitization program.
We are restructuring our cost base so we can invest for growth while achieving at least $6
billion of run-rate synergies within three years and strengthening our balance sheet. Growing
our business and paying down debt are our top priorities, and we strongly believe we can
achieve both simultaneously. We expect to reduce net leverage to 3.0x by the end of 2029,
reaching investment-grade credit metrics within approximately three years of closing. To get
there, we expect to ramp our annual free cash flow to more than $10 billion in 2030. One-
time cash costs to achieve the synergies are expected to be roughly $1.5 to $2 billion in 2027
and roughly $4 billion throughout our transformation, 60% to 70% of which will impact 2027
and 2028, and have been contemplated in our financial and leverage outlook.
Until we reach our leverage target, we will use excess cash, beyond our reinvestment into the
business and our current dividend, to repay debt.

and useful for investors because they allow investors to view our performance in a manner
consistent with the method used by our management; and because they exclude items that
are not representative of our normal operations, they provide a clearer perspective on
underlying performance, and make it easier for investors, analysts and peers to compare
our operating performance to other companies in the industry and to compare our results
across reporting periods.

applicable, as indicators of operating performance and undue reliance should not be placed
