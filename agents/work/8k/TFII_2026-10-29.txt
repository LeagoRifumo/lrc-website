Montreal, Quebec, October 29, 2026 – TFI International Inc. (NYSE and TSX: TFII), a North American leader in the transportation and logistics industry, today announced its results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. All amounts are shown in U.S. dollars.
“Through our talented team’s impressive efforts we again generated a sub-90 firmwide operating ratio which, along with a remarkably strong performance from our Truckload operations, helped TFI International record 17% operating income growth and nearly 40% adjusted EPS growth. These results are a direct reflection of our initiatives in recent years to strategically invest and pursue attractive M&A, resulting in a superior focus on specialized freight with industrial end market diversification across multiple modes of transportation,” said Alain Bédard, Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Our Truckload performance is particularly gratifying, with double digit top line growth despite fewer trucks and operating income that nearly doubled. Our Logistics segment was also very strong, with operating income up nearly 60% and a 15% return on invested capital, while our pricing initiatives within LTL began to benefit results late in the quarter and into October. Overall, TFI International has now produced $440 million of year-to-date free cash flow, improving our leverage ratio both sequentially and year-over-year, all while rewarding our shareholders with the return of excess capital. This solid financial foundation will allow us to remain highly strategic going forward regardless of the cycle, in our drive to further enhance shareholder value.”

THIRD QUARTER RESULTS
Total revenue of $2.23 billion increased 13% from $1.97 billion in the prior year period and revenue before fuel surcharge of $1.86 billion increased 8% from $1.72 billion in the prior year period. The increase is due to contributions from business acquisitions of $59.3 million, and with $73.7 million related to improving market conditions.
Operating income increased 17% to $180.0 million from $153.3 million in the prior year period. The increase is primarily organic, attributable to the increase in revenues, margin improvements and contributions from business acquisitions of $12.2 million, partially offset by an office-related non-cash building impairment charge of $14.4 million.
Net income increased 25% to $105.7 million from $84.7 million in the prior year period, and net income of $1.28 per diluted share increased 25% from $1.02 in the prior year period. Adjusted net income, a non-IFRS measure, was $137.3 million, or $1.67 per diluted share, a 38% and 39% increase, respectively, over $99.1 million, or $1.20 per diluted share, in the prior year period.
Total revenue increased by 22% for the Logistics segment, 15% for the Truckload segment, and 7% for the Less-Than-Truckload segment. Operating income increased by 91% in the Truckload segment, 59% in the Logistics segment, and decreased by 43% in the Less-Than-Truckload segment, which included the non-cash impairment charge of $14.4 million. Corporate expenses were $14.1 million, up from $8.0 million in the prior year, that benefited from a $5.0 million reversal on accident reserves.
NINE-MONTH RESULTS
Total revenue of $6.47 billion increased from $5.97 billion in the prior year period and revenue before fuel surcharge of $5.46 billion increased from $5.23 billion in the prior year period. The increase is due to contributions from business acquisitions of $169.1 million, and with $58.1 million related to improving market conditions.
Operating income grew to $496.9 million from $438.1 million in the prior year period. The increase is primarily attributable to the increase in revenues, margin improvements, and contributions from business acquisitions of $32.3 million, partially offset by incremental accident related reserves of $23.4 million, that includes a $5.0 million reserve reversal in the prior year, and a non-cash office-related building impairment of $14.4 million.
Net income grew to $285.2 million compared to $238.9 million in the prior year period, and net income of $3.46 per diluted share compared to $2.86. Adjusted net income, a non-IFRS measure, was $347.0 million, or $4.21 per diluted share, compared to $275.4 million, or $3.29 per diluted share in the prior year period.
Total revenue increased by 11% for the Logistics segment, 10% for the Truckload segment, and 6% for the Less-Than-Truckload segment. Operating income increased by 52% in the Truckload segment and 34% in the Logistics segment, and decreased by 19% in the Less-Than-Truckload segment, which included the non-cash impairment charge of $14.4 million. Corporate expenses were $59.1 million, up from $32.2 million in the prior year, primarily due to increased accident reserves.

CASH FLOW
Net cash flow from operating activities decreased to $235.2 million compared to $255.4 million in the prior year period. The change was due in part to a reduction in non-cash working capital as the increase in accounts receivable outpaced the increase in accounts payable as fuel costs have shorter payment periods and more income tax payments, and was offset by an increase in income and less cash requirements for provisions and employee benefits.
Net cash from investing activities decreased by $61.9 million, primarily due to an increase in the purchase of property and equipment of $60.6 million, as planned capital expenditure-related deliveries were received during the quarter.
Net cash used in financing activities decreased by $61.3 million, primarily due to not having repurchases of shares for cancellation in the quarter as compared to $67.4 million in the same prior year period.
On September 15, 2026, the Board of Directors of TFI International declared a quarterly dividend of $0.47 per outstanding common share, paid on October 15, 2026, representing a 4% increase over the $0.45 quarterly dividend declared in Q3 2025.
OUTLOOK
Assuming no significant positive or negative change in the operating environment, the Company expects fourth quarter 2026 adjusted diluted EPS to be in the range of $1.30 to $1.40. The Company expects full-year net capital expenditures excluding real estate; to be in the range of between $225 million and $250 million. The Company expects year-over-year adjusted operating ratio, a non-IFRS measure, improvement in the quarter of 400 to 500 basis points for the Truckload segment, 100 to 200 basis points for the Logistics segment, and to have a 0 to 100 basis points decline in the Less-Than-Truckload segment.

Management’s Discussion and Analysis
ABOUT TFI INTERNATIONAL
Services
TFI International is a North American leader in the transportation and logistics industry, operating across the United States ("U.S."), Canada and Mexico through its subsidiaries. TFI International creates value for shareholders by identifying strategic acquisitions and managing a growing network of wholly-owned operating subsidiaries. Under the TFI International umbrella, companies benefit from financial and operational resources to build their businesses and increase their efficiency. TFI International companies service the following reportable segments:
•
Less-Than-Truckload (“LTL”);
•
Truckload (“TL”);
•
Logistics.
Seasonality of operations
The activities conducted by the Company are subject to general demand for freight transportation. Historically, demand has been relatively stable with the weakest generally occurring during the first quarter. Furthermore, during the winter months, fuel consumption and maintenance costs tend to rise.
Human resources
As at September 30, 2026, the Company had 25,680 employees throughout TFI International’s various business segments across North America. This compares to 26,092 employees as at September 30, 2025. The year-over-year decrease of 412 employees is attributable to 1,021 employees added through business acquisitions and offset by rationalizations affecting 1,433 employees. The Company believes that it has a relatively low turnover rate among its employees in Canada, and a normal turnover rate in the U.S. comparable to other U.S. carriers, and that its employee relations are very good.
Equipment
The Company is a significant transportation provider throughout North America. As at September 30, 2026, the Company had 12,125 trucks, 40,356 trailers and 5,879 independent contractors. This compares to 13,322 trucks, 42,060 trailers and 6,280 independent contractors as at September 30, 2025.
Facilities
TFI International’s head office is in Montréal, Québec and has executive offices in Etobicoke, Ontario, Dallas, Texas and Palm Beach Gardens, Florida. As at September 30, 2026, the Company had 617 facilities, as compared to 623 facilities as at September 30, 2025. Of these 617 facilities, 371 are located in the United States and 246 are located in Canada. In the last twelve months, 16 facilities were added from business acquisitions while terminal consolidation decreased the total number of facilities by 22, across all segments.
Customers
The Company has a diverse customer base across a broad cross-section of industries with no single client accounting for more than 5% of consolidated revenue. Net foreign exchange gain or loss and net investment hedge
The Company designates as a hedge a portion of its U.S. dollar denominated debt held against its net investments in U.S. operations. This accounting treatment allows the Company to offset the designated portion of foreign exchange gain (or loss) of its debt against the foreign exchange loss (or gain) of its net investments in U.S. operations and present them in other comprehensive income. Net foreign exchange gains or losses recorded in income or loss are attributable to the translation of the U.S. dollar portion of the Company’s credit facilities not designated as a hedge and to the translation of other financial assets and liabilities denominated in currencies other than the functional currency. For the three-month period ended September 30, 2026, a gain of $6.0 million of foreign exchange variations (a gain of $5.4 million net of tax) was recorded to other comprehensive income as it relates to the translation of the debt in the net investment hedge. For the three months ended September 30, 2026, revenue increased by $68.9 million, or 10%, to $753.0 million, compared to $684.1 million for the same period in 2025. This increase was mainly attributable to higher revenue from existing operations of $57.6 million and contributions from business acquisitions of $11.3 million. Revenue before fuel surcharge increased 10.1%, primarily reflecting a $59.1 million, or 49%, increase in brokerage revenue. Revenue excluding brokerage and fuel surcharge increased 1.8%, despite a 12.0% reduction in the average truck count, reflecting improved fleet productivity, as revenue per truck per week, excluding fuel surcharge, increased 13.2%.

Management’s Discussion and Analysis
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Truckload revenue increased by $127.4 million, or 6%, from $2,059.2 million in 2025 to $2,186.6 million in 2026. There was an increase in revenue from existing operations of $93.6 million and contributions from business acquisitions of $33.8 million.
Operating expenses
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, operating expenses, net of fuel surcharge, increased by $21.3 million to $652.8 million, compared to $631.5 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily driven by $11.5 million of operating expenses from business acquisitions, and $9.8 million from existing operations. Personnel expenses decreased by $6.2 million, or 3%, reflecting lower workforce requirements and improved operating efficiencies. As a percentage of revenue, personnel expenses decreased to 26.5% from 30.1%, also benefiting from the higher proportion of brokerage revenue in the revenue mix. Fleet right-sizing initiatives reduced average trucks by 12.0% and average trailers by 6.4%, contributing to an $8.3 million, or 16%, decrease in depreciation of property and equipment compared to the same period in 2025.
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, operating expenses, net of fuel surcharge, increased by $37.5 million, or 2%, from $1,887.3 million in 2025 to $1,924.8 million in 2026. This is mainly due to an increase of $32.7 million from business acquisitions, and a $4.8 million increase from existing operations.
Operating income
Operating income was $100.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $47.6 million, or 91%, compared to $52.6 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was organic, with a $47.8 million improvement in operating income from existing operations. Adjusted operating ratio1, a non-IFRS measure, improved to 86.7% from 92.3%, driven by rate increases, fleet right-sizing, and better utilization.
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, operating income in the segment increased by $89.8 million, or 52%, from $171.9 million in 2025 to $261.8 million in 2026. The increase was due to an $88.8 million improvement in existing operations, and contributions from business acquisitions of $1.1 million.
