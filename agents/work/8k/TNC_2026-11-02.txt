Highlights
•Net sales of $322.3 million increased 6.2% year over year, as volume grew 4.1 percentage points, led by North America and robotics, and pricing added 1.6 percentage points, for organic growth of 5.7%.
•Orders grew 9.5% year over year, supported by broad-based demand across most regions, while backlog remained elevated at approximately $118 million, providing a solid foundation for fourth quarter net sales.
•Robotics momentum accelerated during the quarter, with robotics sales of approximately $37.3 million increasing 71% year over year, supported by growth across equipment and autonomy subscriptions and continued customer adoption of autonomous cleaning solutions. We also expanded our innovation portfolio with the recent launch of the P1 autonomous vacuum, addressing more customer applications and price points to maximize market coverage.
•Adjusted EBITDA(a) of $48.8 million, or 15.1% of net sales, increased by 420 bps sequentially, but declined compared to the prior-year period, as higher volume, pricing and tariff refunds were more than offset by inflationary cost pressures and part availability constraints in cost of sales, and by higher S&A and R&D, including ERP-related costs and robotics investment.
•Adjusted diluted EPS(a) of $1.40 declined compared to the prior year, reflecting the operating results referenced above coupled with higher interest expense, partially offset by the benefit of share repurchases.

Operating Results
Gross profit margin was 42.3% in the third quarter of 2026, down 40 basis points from the third quarter of 2025. Included in the quarter was a one-time benefit of approximately $6.1 million, or 190 basis points, related to refunds of previously paid tariffs following changes to IEEPA-related regulations. Excluding this benefit, gross margin was approximately 40.4%, down 230 basis points from the prior year. Roughly two-thirds of the decline came from North America and one-third from EMEA, driven by inflationary pressures mostly tied to the Middle East conflict and by part availability constraints that led to production inefficiencies and under-absorption, only partly offset by pricing.
Selling and administrative ("S&A") expense totaled $92.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, a $3.9 million decrease compared to the third quarter of 2025. Period-over-period changes included higher compensation and benefit expenses, higher technology spending, and incremental investments in our TNC Robotics venture. These increases were more than offset by lower ERP implementation costs and the absence of legal contingency costs incurred in the prior year period. S&A expense as a percentage of sales was 28.8% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 31.8% in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted S&A(a) as a percentage of net sales increased to 27.8% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 27.5% in the third quarter of 2025.
Research and development ("R&D") expense totaled $12.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $10.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by continued investment in innovation, including robotics and autonomous solutions.
Adjusted EBITDA(a) was $48.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $49.8 million in the prior-year period. Higher volume and pricing, together with approximately $6.1 million of tariff refunds, were more than offset by higher cost of sales, primarily inflationary freight and material costs and production inefficiencies from part availability constraints, and by higher S&A and R&D, primarily ERP-related technology and employee costs and robotics investment. Adjusted EBITDA margin(a) for the third quarter of 2026 was 15.1%, down 130 basis points compared to 16.4% in the prior-year period..
Net income was $19.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $14.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income(a) was $24.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $3.2 million compared to the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily driven by gross margin pressures coupled with increased S&A.
Cash Flow, Liquidity and Capital Allocation
Tennant generated $11.9 million of cash flow for operating activities during the third quarter of 2026, a $16.8 million decrease compared to the prior‑year period, driven by lower operating performance and increased working capital requirements, including higher accounts receivable and inventory balances and lower accounts payable. While working capital and cash conversion were affected by inefficiencies from the North America ERP implementation, we are making steady progress in addressing them, and working capital efficiency remains a key focus for management.
Liquidity remained strong with a balance of $75.3 million in cash and cash equivalents at the end of the third quarter, and $289.4 million of unused borrowing capacity under the Company's revolving credit facility.
The Company continues to strategically deploy cash flow to meet operational capital requirements and to return capital to shareholders in alignment with its capital allocation priorities. During the third quarter of 2026, the Company invested $6.7 million in capital expenditures and returned $5.2 million to shareholders through dividends. The Company remains diligent in managing its debt and maintaining a strong balance sheet. The Company had a net leverage ratio (Adjusted Net Debt(a) / trailing twelve months (TTM) Adjusted EBITDA(a)) of 2.0 times as of September 30, 2026.

Company Profile
Founded in 1870, Tennant Company (TNC), headquartered in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, is a world leader in the design, manufacture and marketing of solutions that help create a cleaner, safer and healthier world. Its products include equipment for maintaining surfaces in industrial, commercial and outdoor environments; detergent-free and other sustainable cleaning technologies; and cleaning tools and supplies. Tennant's global field service network is the most extensive in the industry. Tennant Company had sales of $1.20 billion in 2025 and has approximately 4,500 employees. Tennant has manufacturing operations throughout the world and sells products directly in more than 21 countries and through distributors in more than 100 countries. For more information, visit www.tennantco.com and www.ipcworldwide.com. Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This news release and the related conference call include presentation of Non-GAAP measures that include or exclude special items of a nonrecurring and/or nonoperational nature (hereinafter referred to as “special items”). Management believes that the Non-GAAP measures provide useful information to investors regarding the Company’s results of operations and financial condition because they permit a more meaningful comparison and understanding of Tennant Company’s operating performance for the current, past or future periods. Management uses these Non-GAAP measures to monitor and evaluate ongoing operating results and trends and to gain an understanding of the comparative operating performance of the Company.
The Company believes that disclosing S&A expense – as adjusted, S&A expense as a percent of net sales – as adjusted, operating income – as adjusted, operating margin – as adjusted, income before income taxes – as adjusted, income tax expense – as adjusted, net income – as adjusted, net income per diluted share – as adjusted, EBITDA – as adjusted, and EBITDA margin – as adjusted (collectively, the “Non-GAAP measures”), excluding the impacts from special items, is useful to investors as a measure of operating performance. The Company uses these measures to monitor and evaluate operating performance. The Non-GAAP measures are financial measures that do not reflect United States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP). The Company calculates the Non-GAAP measures by adjusting for legal contingency costs, ERP modernization costs, ERP amortization costs, legal and financial advisory costs, restructuring-related costs, transaction and integration-related costs, equity method losses and amortization expense. The Company calculates income tax expense – as adjusted by adjusting for the tax effect of these Non-GAAP measures. The Company calculates net income per diluted share – as adjusted by adjusting for the after-tax effect of these Non-GAAP measures and dividing the result by the diluted weighted average shares outstanding. The Company calculates EBITDA margin – as adjusted by dividing EBITDA – as adjusted by net sales.
