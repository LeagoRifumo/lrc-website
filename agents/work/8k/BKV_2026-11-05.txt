BKV Reports Q3 2026 Results, Cost Reimbursement Agreement with Leading Frontier AI Lab, and Verra Carbon Credit Certification
DENVER, Colorado – November 5, 2026 – BKV Corporation (“BKV” or the “Company”) (NYSE: BKV) today reported financial and operational results for the third quarter of 2026, along with guidance for the fourth quarter of 2026. In addition, the Company is announcing an expanded scope for its Phase 1 modular power development in Temple, TX, supported by a Cost Reimbursement Agreement with a Leading Frontier AI Lab, and Verra carbon credit certification.

•Completed $575.0 million Convertible Senior Notes Offering at an annual rate of 1.625%
Cost Reimbursement Agreement with a Leading Frontier AI Lab for Modular Power Development
BKV-BPP Power (“Power JV”) entered into a cost reimbursement agreement (“CRA”) with a Leading Frontier AI Lab supporting an increase in planned modular power generation at the Temple Energy Complex to 400 MW from 200 MW. If mutually agreeable offtake arrangements are not agreed to by November 18, 2026, the CRA covers up to approximately $179 million. The agreement contemplates an extension to December 21, 2026, and the ability to extend further, each on mutual agreement. The intended offtaker may elect to take assignment of the equipment supply contract, if not retained by the Power JV, in which case it would reimburse Power JV for eligible costs, subject to an aggregate cap of $374.1 million. The CRA allows BKV to maintain its development schedule while advancing commercial discussions toward a long-term purchase price agreement (“PPA”).
Equipment Supply Agreement Backed by Agreement with a Leading Investment-Grade Hyperscaler
As previously announced in October, a wholly owned subsidiary of BKV entered into an equipment supply agreement with a Tier 1 supplier for the purchase of combined-cycle gas turbine (“CCGT”) power generation equipment for one of the Company’s prospective power generation projects in Texas representing approximately 1.2 GW. BKV also announced a backstop agreement with a leading investment-grade hyperscaler, under which the hyperscaler has agreed to reimburse a portion of BKV’s costs associated with the equipment and related project work. This investment-grade hyperscaler is also the intended offtaker for the prospective power generation project.
Verra Carbon Credit Certification Unlocks Carbon Sequestered Gas
BKV became the first company to receive carbon credit certification from Verra for CO₂ permanently sequestered through its CCUS operations. This milestone enables the Company to monetize carbon credits through its Carbon Sequestered Gas (“CSG”), a first-of-its-kind, third-party-verified carbon-neutral natural gas product. With verification complete, BKV can begin delivering CSG under its previously announced arrangements with Kiewit and Gunvor, while pursuing additional opportunities to market CSG to customers seeking reliable, lower-carbon energy solutions.

Strategic Barnett Shale Acquisition Highlights
As previously announced, on September 15, 2026, BKV completed the acquisition of upstream, midstream and carbon capture and sequestration (“CCS”) assets in the Barnett Shale, further strengthening its position as one of the basin’s leading operators. The acquisition added approximately 65 MMcfe/d of production (more than 50% liquids) and approximately 0.35 Tcfe of proved developed producing reserves. The acquired assets also include a cryogenic natural gas processing plant with 180 MMcf/d of capacity, approximately 340 miles of owned natural gas gathering infrastructure, 225 miles of owned water gathering infrastructure and two saltwater disposal wells. Additionally, the acquisition included an operating CCS facility that sequestered greater than 100,000 metric tons of CO₂ during the twelve months ended September 30, 2026.
“Our upstream business delivered another strong quarter, with continued operational outperformance providing a solid foundation as we advanced our strategic growth initiatives,” said Chris Kalnin, Chief Executive Officer of BKV. “We also made meaningful commercial progress in our power business, securing a cost reimbursement agreement for modular generation equipment and a previously announced backstop agreement with an investment-grade hyperscaler supporting our CCGT equipment commitments.”
“These milestones reflect our deliberate, disciplined approach to developing additional power generation capacity,” continued Kalnin. “We are encouraged by the progress of our commercial discussions and remain focused on securing long-term power purchase agreements that support our growth objectives. Combined with continued execution across our upstream and carbon capture businesses, we believe this progress positions BKV to advance its closed-loop strategy and create sustainable, long-term shareholder value.”

Segment Operational Results - Third Quarter 2026
Power Segment
BKV has made tangible commercial progress toward securing long-term PPAs across its power development platform. The Company announced an equipment supply agreement for approximately 1.2 GW of CCGT capacity, supported by a backstop agreement with an investment-grade hyperscaler. The Power JV also entered into a cost reimbursement agreement with a leading Frontier AI Lab, supporting an increase in planned modular generation at the Temple Energy Complex to 400 MW from 200 MW. These agreements represent important commercial milestones as BKV advances discussions toward long-term PPAs. With the additional modular generation equipment, the Company's existing and planned power generation assets now represent approximately 3.3 GW of potential capacity.
The Temple I and II facilities continued to demonstrate strong operational reliability during the quarter, achieving a combined capacity factor of 71.7% and total generation of 2,317 GWh. Plant availability remained high with an average equivalent availability factor of 96% reflecting continued operational execution across both facilities.
Despite record power demand across ERCOT during the quarter, wholesale power prices and spark spreads were below expectations, as increased renewable generation and battery storage moderated peak pricing. BKV remains confident in the long-term fundamentals of the ERCOT market, supported by a substantial backlog of large-load interconnection requests and anticipated demand growth.

Carbon Capture Utilization and Sequestration (“CCUS”)
During the third quarter, BKV expanded its operational CCUS portfolio to four projects following the acquisition of an additional injection facility as part of its upstream Barnett acquisition. Together, these projects represent an annual CO₂ sequestration run rate of approximately 350,000 metric tons. Since commencing initial injection at Barnett Zero in November 2023, BKV has significantly expanded its operating footprint and sequestration capacity in less than three years.
Total injection volumes for the third quarter were 55,200 metric tons of CO₂, including 15 days of injection volumes of 5,400 metric tons from the acquired CCS injection well.
The Company remains focused on developing its broader CCUS pipeline in collaboration with its joint venture partner, CIP, and working closely with the State of Louisiana on its Class VI permit applications. The Company remains on track to achieve its targeted annual injection run rate of 1.5 million metric tons in 2028.

About BKV Corporation
Headquartered in Denver, Colorado, BKV Corporation is a forward-thinking, growth-driven energy company focused on creating value for its stockholders. BKV's core business is to produce natural gas from its owned and operated upstream assets. BKV’s overall business is organized into four business lines: natural gas production; natural gas gathering, processing and transportation; power generation; and carbon capture, utilization and sequestration. BKV (and its predecessor entity) was founded in 2015, and BKV and its employees are committed to building a different kind of energy company. BKV is one of the top 15 gas-weighted natural gas producers in the United States and the largest natural gas producer by gross operated volume in the Barnett Shale. BKV Corporation is the parent company for the BKV family of companies. For more information, visit the BKV website at www.bkv.com.

Adjusted Free Cash Flow before Power Growth reflects cash flow available to fund the Company's capital program, excluding strategic power growth capital expenditures, while Adjusted Free Cash Flow before Power Growth attributable to BKV further adjusts for noncontrolling interests to reflect amounts attributable to the Company's common shareholders. The Company believes these measures are useful indicators of liquidity because they facilitate period-over-period comparisons of cash flow provided by operating activities and the Company's ability to internally fund its capital program (including acquisitions), reduce leverage, fund acquisitions and return capital to shareholders. Adjusted Free Cash Flow before Power Growth and Adjusted Free Cash Flow before Power Growth attributable to BKV should not be considered alternatives to, or more meaningful than, net income (loss) or net cash provided by (used in) operating activities determined in accordance with GAAP.
