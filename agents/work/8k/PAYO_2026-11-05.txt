Payoneer Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
13% volume growth led by B2B acceleration, up 59% year-over-year
Stockholders approved the Nuvei merger agreement proposal on September 14, 2026
NEW YORK – November 5, 2026 – Payoneer Global Inc. (“Payoneer” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: PAYO), the global financial technology company powering business growth across borders, today reported financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Payoneer’s Q3 results show our upmarket strategy is working: B2B volume growth accelerated to 59% year-over-year, ARPU ex. interest expanded 20%+ year-over-year for a 9th consecutive quarter, and the percentage of our revenue from larger customers continues to grow. Additionally, we obtained payment licenses in Canada and Israel, extending our global regulatory infrastructure to more markets for customers.
​
We continue to work towards a targeted mid-2027 close of the Nuvei transaction. We received shareholder approval in September and are making progress with obtaining necessary regulatory approvals. Our customer relationships and distribution, regulatory infrastructure and wallet and payout capabilities are highly complementary with Nuvei’s capabilities and together, we will build the end-to-end money movement infrastructure designed for the world’s largest digital commerce ecosystems.”
​
John Caplan, Chief Executive Officer

Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights (unless otherwise noted)
​
● | Revenue excluding interest income of $222 million grew 5% year-over-year

● | Volume of $25.3 billion increased 13% year-over-year, reflecting:

o | SMBs that sell on marketplaces volume of $12.6 billion up 1% year-over-year.

o | B2B volume of $5.0 billion, up 59% year-over-year driven by accelerating growth across all major regions and continued momentum with larger customers, particularly in China and EMEA.

o | Checkout volume of $330 million, up 48% year-over-year.

o | Enterprise payouts volume of $7.4 billion, up 14% year-over-year.

● | SMB customer revenue of $199 million grew 4% year-over-year, reflecting:

o | SMBs that sell on marketplaces revenue of $115 million, down 5% year-over-year.

o | B2B SMBs revenue of $70 million, up 13% year-over-year.

o | Checkout revenue of $14 million, up 60% year-over-year.

● | 18% growth in ARPU, and 21% growth in ARPU excluding interest income, the ninth consecutive quarter of 20%+ growth in ARPU excluding interest income.

● | $7.5 billion of customer funds (including both short-term and long-term funds) as of September 30, 2026. Customer funds growth of 6% year-over-year partially offsetting the impact of lower interest rates on year-over-year interest income.

● | Obtained shareholder approval of the Nuvei transaction on September 14, 2026.

● | Payoneer continues to strengthen its global regulatory infrastructure with payment licenses in Canada and Israel.

About Payoneer
​
Payoneer is the financial platform for cross-border business and global payments. Payoneer empowers millions of businesses with the financial tools and services they need to grow and transact globally with confidence. Payoneer makes it easier for businesses, particularly in emerging markets, to connect to the global economy, pay and get paid across borders, manage their funds across multiple currencies, and grow their businesses.

Additional Information and Definitions
​
In this earnings release, we reference volume, which is an operational metric. Volume refers to the total dollar value of transactions successfully completed or enabled by our platform, not including orchestration transactions. For a customer that both receives and later sends payments, we count the volume only once. Note: orchestration transactions ceased in 2024 and were related to our 2020 acquisition of optile GmbH.
​
We also reference ARPU (Average Revenue Per User), which is defined as the Revenue from Active Customers divided by the number of Active Customers over the period in which the Revenue was earned. Active Customers for these purposes are defined as Payoneer accountholders with at least 1 financial transaction over the period. Revenue from Active Customers represents revenue attributed to Active Customers based on their use of the Payoneer platform, including interest income earned from their balances, and excluding revenues unrelated to their activities.
