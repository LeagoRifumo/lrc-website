Genmab Announces Financial Results for the Nine Months of 2026
November 5, 2026 Copenhagen, Denmark;
Interim Report for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026
Highlights
•Epcoritamab approved in Europe (as TEPKINLY®) and Japan (as EPKINLY®) in combination with R² for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory FL
•Initiation of two new Phase 3 trials for petosemtamab in first- and second-line CRC
•Genmab revenue increased 22% compared to the first nine months of 2025, to $3,238 million
“The third quarter of 2026 reinforced the breadth of our late-stage portfolio. TEPKINLY plus R2 became the first bispecific-based therapy approved in Europe for second-line FL, extending epcoritamab's reach as a core therapy across B-cell malignancies. We also broadened our petosemtamab program with new Phase 3 studies in CRC, underscoring our confidence in this asset beyond head and neck cancer. As anticipated, we are now in our data-rich fourth quarter, where we have delivered pivotal readouts across our three late-stage assets, epcoritamab, Rina-S® (rinatabart sesutecan) and petosemtamab, supporting multiple regulatory submissions and potential launches in 2027,” said Jan van de Winkel, Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer of Genmab.
Financial Performance First Nine Months of 2026
•Revenue was $3,238 million for the first nine months of 2026 compared to $2,662 million for the first nine months of 2025. The increase of $576 million, or 22%, was primarily driven by higher DARZALEX® and Kesimpta® royalties and higher EPKINLY® net product sales.
•Royalty revenue was $2,699 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $2,219 million in the first nine months of 2025, an increase of $480 million, or 22%. The increase in royalties was driven by higher net sales of DARZALEX and Kesimpta.
•Net sales of DARZALEX by J&J were $12,410 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $10,448 million in the first nine months of 2025, an increase of $1,962 million or 19%.
•Global net sales of EPKINLY/TEPKINLY® were $491 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $333 million in the first nine months of 2025, an increase of $158 million or 47%.
•Cost of product sales were $234 million for the first nine months of 2026 compared to $157 million for the first nine months of 2025. The increase of $77 million, or 49%, was primarily driven by the profit-sharing amounts payable to AbbVie related to EPKINLY sales.
•Acquisition and integration related charges related to the integration of Merus were $87 million in the first nine months of 2026.
•Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $35 million for the first nine months of 2026 compared to $9 million for the first nine months of 2025. The increase of $26 million, was primarily driven by the amortization of the Merus technology platform.
•Operating expenses were $2,018 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $1,498 million in the first nine months of 2025, an increase of $520 million or 35%.
•Adjusted operating expenses, which exclude Acquisition and integration related charges and Amortization of acquired intangible assets, were $1,896 million for the first nine months of 2026 compared to $1,489 million for the first nine months of 2025. The increase of $407 million, or 27%, was primarily driven by investment in our product pipeline, including the advancement of Rina-S and petosemtamab, and our global commercialization capabilities in preparation for their anticipated launches.
•Operating profit was $986 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $1,007 million in the first nine months of 2025. Adjusted operating profit, which excludes Acquisition and integration related charges and Amortization of acquired intangible assets, was $1,108 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $1,016 million in the first nine months of 2025.

Key Company Events Post-Quarter End
•October: Positive topline results from the Phase 3 EPCORE® DLBCL-2 trial evaluating fixed treatment duration epcoritamab in combination with standard of care rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin hydrochloride, vincristine, and prednisone (R-CHOP), compared to R-CHOP alone, in newly diagnosed patients with diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) with an International Prognostic Index (IPI) score of 2 to 5. Data will be submitted for presentation at a future medical meeting. Genmab and AbbVie Inc. (AbbVie) will engage global regulatory authorities to determine next steps.
•October: Positive topline results from the global Phase 3 RAINFOL™-02 trial of Rina-S in patients with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer (PROC) regardless of Folate Receptor Alpha (FRα) expression level or prior treatment with mirvetuximab. The trial met its primary endpoint of progression-free survival (PFS), with Rina-S demonstrating a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement compared with investigator’s choice chemotherapy in the intent-to-treat (ITT) and in the primary analysis set (PAS) populations. Based on these results, Genmab plans to engage with regulatory authorities worldwide regarding potential next steps. The data will also be submitted for presentation at a future medical meeting.
•October: Positive topline results from the global Phase 3 LiGeR-HN1 trial (NCT06525220) evaluating petosemtamab in combination with pembrolizumab versus pembrolizumab in patients with first-line (1L) PD-L1 positive recurrent/metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC). The trial met both of its primary endpoints of overall survival (OS) and objective response rate (ORR), regardless of HPV status. Based on these positive topline results, Genmab plans to engage global regulatory authorities and will submit the full results from LiGeR-HN1 for presentation at a future medical meeting.

EPKINLY/TEPKINLY (epcoritamab) – bispecific antibody approved to treat multiple B-cell malignancies in the U.S., Europe and Japan

•In 2025, EPKINLY plus R2 became the first bispecific antibody combination regimen available in the U.S. as a treatment option for patients with relapsed/refractory FL
•More than 40 clinical trials across different treatment settings, lines of therapy and in combination regimens across histologies, including five Phase 3 trials

Epcoritamab is a proprietary bispecific antibody created using Genmab’s DuoBody technology platform. Epcoritamab targets CD3, which is expressed on T-cells, and CD20, a clinically validated target on malignant B-cells. Genmab used technology licensed from Medarex Inc. (Medarex) to generate the CD20 antibody forming part of epcoritamab. Epcoritamab is marketed as EPKINLY in the U.S., Japan, and other regions, and as TEPKINLY in Europe and other regions. See local prescribing information for specific indications and safety information. In 2020, Genmab entered into a collaboration agreement with AbbVie to jointly develop and commercialize epcoritamab. The companies share commercialization responsibilities in the U.S. and Japan, with AbbVie responsible for further global commercialization.
Genmab records sales in the U.S. and Japan and receives tiered royalties between 22% and 26% on remaining global sales outside of these territories, subject to certain royalty reductions. The companies have a broad clinical development program for epcoritamab including five Phase 3 trials. Tivdak (tisotumab vedotin) – First ADC for recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer after disease progression in the U.S., Europe and Japan
•An ADC directed to TF, a protein prevalent on cervical cancer cells, which is associated with poor prognosis
•Tisotumab vedotin, approved as Tivdak, is the first ADC approved in multiple territories including the U.S., Europe and Japan for the treatment of recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer after prior therapy and is the only ADC with demonstrated OS data in this setting compared to chemotherapy
•Co-developed globally and co-promoted in the U.S. in collaboration with Pfizer, exclusively by Genmab outside of the U.S. and China
Tisotumab vedotin is an ADC composed of Genmab’s human monoclonal antibody directed to TF and Pfizer’s ADC technology that utilizes a protease-cleavable linker that covalently attaches the microtubule-disrupting agent monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE) to the antibody. Genmab used technology licensed from Medarex to generate the TF antibody forming part of tisotumab vedotin. Tisotumab vedotin, marketed as Tivdak, is the first ADC approved for the treatment of adult patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer after prior therapy in multiple territories including the U.S., Europe and Japan. Tisotumab vedotin is being co-developed by Genmab and Pfizer. Under a joint commercialization agreement, Genmab is co-promoting Tivdak in the U.S. and is leading commercial operational activities in Japan, Europe and all other regions globally, excluding the U.S. and China. Pfizer is leading commercial operational activities in the U.S. and in China in connection with the sublicense of its rights to develop and commercialize tisotumab vedotin in China to Zai Lab.

Rinatabart Sesutecan (Rina-S, GEN1184) – ADC with FDA Fast Track and Breakthrough Therapy Designations
•FRα-directed Type I Topoisomerase (TOPO1) inhibitor ADC being evaluated for potential treatment of FRα-expressing cancers
•FDA granted Fast Track Designation (FTD) for FRα-expressing cancers, BTD for recurrent or progressive endometrial cancer
•Five Phase 3 clinical trials active or announced in ovarian and endometrial cancers
•Phase 2 signal-seeking trials in additional indications recruiting
Rina-S is a novel FRα-directed TOPO1 ADC being evaluated for the potential treatment of ovarian cancer and other FRα-expressing cancers. Data suggests that Rina-S has robust single agent activity in various cancers across a broad range of FRα expression levels. In January 2024, Rina-S was granted FTD by the FDA for the treatment of FRα-expressing high-grade serous or endometrioid PROC. In August 2025, the FDA granted BTD for recurrent or progressive endometrial cancer. Five Phase three trials are active or have been announced: RAINFOL™-02 (NCT06619236) in 2L+ PROC completed enrollment in March 2026; RAINFOL-03 (NCT07166094) in 2L+ endometrial cancer, RAINFOL-04 (NCT07225270) in 2L PSOC maintenance and RAINFOL-07 (NCT07564141) in 2L PSOC platinum-doublet chemo replacement are recruiting; RAINFOL-08 in 1L pMMR endometrial cancer has been announced. A Phase 2 trial (RAINFOL-05, NCT07288177) in NSCLC and a Phase 2 trial in advanced GI cancers (RAINFOL-09, NCT07539311) are also recruiting.

•FDA granted FTD for recurrent/metastatic HNSCC and BTD for both 1L and 2L+ recurrent/metastatic HNSCC indications
