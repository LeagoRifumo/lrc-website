EXHIBIT 99.1
BioCryst Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
— Total net revenue of $180.3 million and ORLADEYO® net revenue of $174.8 million —
— Operating profit of $53.3 million and Non-GAAP operating profit of $86.5 million —
— Maintained Full Year 2026 ORLADEYO revenue guidance of $625 to $645 million and increased total revenue guidance to $715 million to $730 million —
— Improved Non-GAAP operating expense guidance to $415 to $425 million —
— Received FDA approval for ORLADEYO 150 mg oral pellets for patients aged 12 and older —
RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK, N.C., Nov. 04, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- BioCryst Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq:BCRX) today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.
"In the third quarter, we delivered continued growth of ORLADEYO and strong profitability while broadening the foundation for our long-term success," said Charlie Gayer, President and Chief Executive Officer of BioCryst. “We successfully launched ORLADEYO pellets in pediatric patients, received FDA approval for their use in adolescents and adults in October, and implemented our specialty pharmacy transition to CareMed, all of which will make ORLADEYO accessible for more patients. Looking to our pipeline, BCX17725 remains on track for the early clinical data by the end of this year, and we look forward to navenibart pivotal results in the third quarter of 2027. This progress across our business increases our confidence in our ability to drive sustainable growth and lasting value for patients and shareholders."
Business & Corporate Updates
The launch of ORLADEYO oral pellets in patients with hereditary angioedema (HAE) aged 2 to <12 years began in August. Early reimbursement is strong and new prescriptions continue to be filled ahead of the company’s expectations.
In October, the FDA approved the company’s supplemental New Drug Application for the use of the oral pellet formulation of once-daily ORLADEYO for patients with HAE aged 12 years and older. This approval enables prescribers to offer their patients a more personalized treatment choice with ORLADEYO in either capsule or pellet form.
In August, the company commenced operations with CareMed as its sole-source specialty pharmacy partner for ORLADEYO shipments to patients. CareMed’s deep rare disease experience, high-touch patient services, and operational scale make it a strong partner to drive continued growth of ORLADEYO across patient segments and formulations.
The company is studying navenibart, an investigational, long-acting plasma kallikrein inhibitor, in ALPHA-ORBIT, the ongoing and fully enrolled pivotal study in hereditary angioedema with every three-month and every six-month subcutaneous dosing, and ORBIT-EXPANSE, the long-term extension study. The program remains on track to report top-line results at six- and twelve-month endpoints in Q3 2027.
The company is studying BCX17725, an investigational KLK5 inhibitor for the treatment of Netherton syndrome (NS), in a Phase 1 trial. The company has enrolled Part 4 of this trial and expects to report initial data in patients with NS by the end of 2026.
The company's Birmingham site closure activities are on track to be substantially complete by year-end. The company expects the site closure and associated restructuring to result in cash costs of approximately $10 million total and annualized cost savings of approximately $30 million to $35 million once fully realized, not including additional savings from other program-related expenses.
In October, the company announced the formation of a Scientific Advisory Board comprising seven accomplished scientific and industry leaders to provide independent guidance and expertise for the successful execution of the external R&D strategy.
The company has discontinued development of STAR-0310, the early-stage atopic dermatitis program acquired through the acquisition of Astria Therapeutics, to focus resources on rare diseases. The company previously paused investment in the program while it explored partnering opportunities, and it has now concluded that effort.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total revenues were $180.3 million (+13% y-o-y; +24% y-o-y on a comparable basis excluding European revenue). Revenue growth in the quarter benefited from the transition to CareMed as the company's sole-source specialty pharmacy partner for shipments to patients. As part of its distribution model, CareMed built an initial inventory of ORLADEYO to support patient demand and efficient dispensing, consistent with customary specialty pharmacy practices. The company expects that inventory level to remain relatively stable over time. ORLADEYO revenue, excluding European revenue, grew year-over-year when removing the effect of the initial inventory build.
Research and development expenses, excluding stock-based compensation expense, were $52.4 million for the third quarter of 2026. Research and development expenses for the quarter include one-time impairment charges associated with the closure of the Birmingham site and employee termination costs. Total restructuring costs in research and development included cash costs of $6.3 million and non-cash costs of $9.3 million.
On a non-GAAP basis, R&D expenses were $37.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $38.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. While costs increased due to navenibart pivotal program, overall expenses decreased due to lower expenses related to BCX17725 and the termination of programs related to the Birmingham facility closure.
Sales and marketing expenses, excluding stock-based compensation expense, were $37.7 million (-15% y-o-y) on a GAAP basis and $36.0 million (+4% y-o-y) on a non-GAAP basis. Fees related to the specialty pharmacy transition contributed to the increase. General and administrative expenses, excluding stock-based compensation expense, were $21.0 million (-22% y-o-y) on a GAAP basis and $19.1 million (-8% y-o-y) on a non-GAAP basis.
The company recorded an operating profit of $53.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $29.6 million for the third quarter of 2025.
On a non-GAAP basis, operating profit was $86.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $50.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.
The company recorded net income of $37.9 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $12.9 million for third quarter of 2025.
On a non-GAAP basis, net income was $71.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $34.7 million for the third quarter of 2025.
During Q3 2026, the company generated positive cash flow. As a result, cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash and investments totaled $376.4 million on September 30, 2026.
The accompanying tables provide GAAP and non-GAAP financial information for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. Non-GAAP measures include adjustments, as applicable, for the sale of the European ORLADEYO business on October 1, 2025 (including transaction-related costs), stock-based compensation, expenses incurred in connection with the acquisition of Astria on January 23, 2026, employee termination and impairment charges related to the Birmingham site closure, and workforce reduction expenses. Management believes that the presentation of these non-GAAP figures provides greater transparency into the financial results of core, ongoing operations and improves comparability across reporting periods by excluding items that are non-recurring or other items that may vary significantly from period to period.
Financial Outlook for 2026
The company maintained its outlook for full year 2026 global net ORLADEYO revenue of $625 million to $645 million and increased its full year 2026 total revenue outlook, including revenue from RAPIVAB® (peramivir injection) and the licensing of navenibart European rights, to $715 million to $730 million. The increase was primarily driven by higher revenues from the licensing of navenibart rights, as well as higher RAPIVAB orders from international partners.
The company improved its outlook for full year 2026 non-GAAP operating expenses, excluding stock-based compensation, restructuring, and transaction-related costs, to $415 million to $425 million. The decrease was primarily driven by continued progress on the Birmingham site closure.

About BioCryst Pharmaceuticals
BioCryst is a global biotechnology company focused on developing and commercializing medicines for hereditary angioedema (“HAE”) and other rare diseases, driven by its deep commitment to improving the lives of people living with these conditions. BioCryst has commercialized ORLADEYO® (berotralstat), the first oral, once-daily plasma kallikrein inhibitor, and is advancing a pipeline of potential first-in-class or best-in-class therapeutics for rare diseases. For more information, please visit www.biocryst.com or follow us on LinkedIn.
