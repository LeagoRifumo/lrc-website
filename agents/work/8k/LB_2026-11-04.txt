LandBridge Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
Delivers record third quarter revenue of $70.9 million, representing growth of 39% year-over-year and 6% quarter-over-quarter
Increases full year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance to $220 to $235 million
Completes the acquisition of ~25,000 acres across the Delaware Basin, increasing LandBridge's contiguity and scale, undeveloped pore space portfolio, and contracted royalty cash flows
Announces PowerBridge's conversion of its previously announced option to lease on a portion of the Alpha Digital Campus acreage and commencement of construction on the first phase of the powered data center campus project
Declares quarterly cash dividend of $0.12 per share
HOUSTON—(BUSINESS WIRE)—LandBridge Corporation (NYSE: LB; NYSE TX: LB) (the “Company,” or “LandBridge”) today announced its financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•
Revenues of $70.9 million, representing an increase of 39% year-over-year and 6% quarter-over-quarter
•
Net income(1) of $34.0 million, representing an increase of 68% year-over-year and 10% quarter-over-quarter
•
Net income margin(1) of 48%
•
Adjusted EBITDA(2) of $63.7 million, representing an increase of 42% year-over-year and 7% quarter-over-quarter
•
Adjusted EBITDA Margin(2) of 90%
•
Cash flows from operating activities of $49.6 million, representing an increase of 42% year-over-year and 20% quarter-over-quarter
•
Free Cash Flow(2) of $49.0 million, representing an increase of 45% year-over-year and 22% quarter-over-quarter
•
Operating cash flow margin of 70%

•
Free Cash Flow Margin(2) of 69%
•
Completed the Company's conversion and redomestication from a Delaware limited liability company to a Texas corporation, enabling potential participation in broader market indices

Increased liquidity through the previously announced $100 million revolving credit facility upsize on August 4, 2026, and a $125 million tack-on debt offering on October 1, 2026

Announced quarterly cash dividend of $0.12 per share
Management Commentary
Jason Long, Chief Executive Officer of LandBridge, stated, “LandBridge delivered another strong quarter, with revenue growing 39% year-over-year, and we are increasing our full year 2026 guidance based on the continued organic and M&A momentum across our acreage. Our acquisition of approximately 25,000 acres during the quarter perpetuates our strategy of acquiring strategic acreage at accretive multiples, with the goal of blending that multiple down over time through our dedicated commercial efforts. Alongside the continued strength of our acquisition pipeline, PowerBridge’s conversion of its option to a long-term lease and the start of construction on Alpha Phase I demonstrate the growing momentum of our commercial strategy across the footprint."
Scott McNeely, Chief Financial Officer of LandBridge, said, “Our third quarter results underscore the scalability of our high-margin, asset-light business model. Growth across every revenue stream, together with the expected contribution from recent acquisitions beginning in the fourth quarter, supports our decision to raise the midpoint of our full year Adjusted EBITDA guidance to $227.5 million. We also recently completed our conversion to a Texas corporation, a milestone that supports broader index eligibility and reinforces our focus on long-term shareholder value creation."
Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Financial Information
Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $70.9 million as compared to $66.8 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $50.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The sequential increase was attributable to growth across all key revenue streams, including increases of $1.4 million in resource sales and royalties, $2.1 million in surface use royalties and revenues, $0.5 million in oil and gas royalties. Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $34.0 million as compared to $31.0 million in the second quarter of 2026 and a net income of $20.3 million in the third quarter of 2025.(1)
Adjusted EBITDA was $63.7 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $59.8 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $44.9 million in the third quarter of 2025.(2)
Net income margin was 48% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to 46% in the second quarter of 2026 and a net income margin of 40% in the third quarter of 2025.(1) Adjusted EBITDA margin was 90% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to 89% in the second quarter of 2026 and 88% in the third quarter of 2025.(2)

Diversified Revenue Streams
Surface Use Royalties and Revenue: Generated revenues of $54.3 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $52.2 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $35.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Surface Use Royalties and Revenue increased $2.1 million sequentially, primarily driven by an increase in produced water handling volumes across our acreage, including increased volumes from the ramping Speedway Phase I pipeline, as well as an increase in pipeline easement revenue.
Resources Sales and Royalties: Generated revenues of $12.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $11.1 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $10.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. Revenue from Resource Sales and Royalties increased $1.4 million sequentially, primarily driven by increases in water and caliche sales as well as sand royalties on our acreage.
Oil and Gas Royalties: Generated revenues of $4.1 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $3.6 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $3.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. Revenue from Oil and Gas Royalties increased $0.5 million sequentially, primarily driven by net royalty production increasing from 773 boe/d in the second quarter of 2026 to 917 boe/d in the third quarter of 2026.
Free Cash Flow Generation
Cash flow from operations for the third quarter of 2026 was $49.6 million as compared to $41.4 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $34.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. Free Cash Flow for the third quarter of 2026 was $49.0 million as compared to $40.2 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $33.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.(2)
Capital expenditures for the third quarter of 2026 were $0.6 million and net cash used in investing activities during the third quarter of 2026 was $267.4 million, which included approximately $266.8 million of acquisition expenditures in the third quarter. Third Quarter 2026 Acquisitions
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company acquired approximately 25,000 acres, representing numerous individual strategic transactions across the Company's existing footprint. The acquired acreage includes fee surface and mineral classified surface interests, which enables LandBridge to participate in the upside of minerals production as the surface owner. The acquisitions include material upside tied to further commercialization of the surface estate access and provide access to highly-contiguous surface acreage with attractive, undeveloped pore space, strategically positioned outside of the over-pressured injection zones close to the Stateline region, offering large, active E&P operators a responsible, long-term alternative for produced water disposal. LandBridge is engaged in active discussions with operators, providing strong confidence in its ability to develop and commercialize this pore space, helping to further diversify the asset's cash flows while preserving their long-term, recurring nature.
PowerBridge Alpha Lease Phase I
During the third quarter of 2026, PowerBridge exercised its option to lease an initial 825-acre portion of the approximately 3,400 total acreage under option for the previously announced Alpha Digital Campus on LandBridge's acreage in Reeves County, Texas (“Alpha Phase I”). In September 2026, PowerBridge and LandBridge entered into a long-term ground lease for Alpha Phase I and concurrently entered into a power royalty agreement for power generated by PowerBridge on the Alpha Phase I campus. PowerBridge subsequently commenced construction on Alpha Phase I in September 2026, which is expected to include more than 300 megawatts of the planned 2 gigawatt powered data center campus project on the Alpha Digital Campus, with first power delivery date anticipated in the fourth quarter of 2027.
Third Quarter 2026 Dividend
The Board declared a dividend on our Class A common stock of $0.12 per share, payable on December 10, 2026 to shareholders of record as of November 25, 2026, and a corresponding required cash distribution to OpCo unitholders.

Comparison of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Free Cash Flow and Free Cash Flow Margin are supplemental non-GAAP measures that we use to evaluate current, past and expected future performance. Although these non-GAAP financial measures are important factors in assessing our operating results and cash flows, they should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for net income, gross margin or any other measures presented under GAAP.
Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin are used to assess the financial performance of our assets over the long term to generate sufficient cash to return capital to equity holders or service indebtedness. We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest; taxes; depreciation, amortization, and depletion; share-based compensation; non-recurring transaction-related expenses and other non-cash or non-recurring expenses. We define Adjusted EBITDA Margin as Adjusted EBITDA divided by total revenues.
We believe Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin are useful because they allow us to more effectively evaluate our operating performance and compare the results of our operations from period to period, and against our peers, without regard to our financing methods or capital structure. We exclude the items listed above from net income (loss) in arriving at Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin because these amounts can vary substantially from company to company within our industry depending upon accounting methods, book values of assets, capital structures and the method by which the assets were acquired.
