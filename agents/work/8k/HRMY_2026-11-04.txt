PLYMOUTH MEETING, Pa., November 4, 2026 /Globe Newswire/ -- Harmony Biosciences Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: HRMY), today reported third quarter 2026 financial results and preliminary data from its multiple-ascending dose (MAD) clinical study for its potential best-in-class orexin agonist BP-205. WAKIX® (pitolisant) delivered third quarter net revenue of $269 million, driven by strong demand of 400 net patient adds, and the Company remains on track to achieve full-year WAKIX net revenue guidance of $1.0 billion to $1.04 billion.
​
​
​
​
​
The encouraging preliminary results from the MAD study with BP-205, following 14 days of repeated once-daily dosing, reinforce the favorable safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetic profile observed in the single-ascending dose (SAD) portion of the study. No liver or visual adverse events were reported, and no clinically significant cardiovascular events or EKG abnormalities were recorded. Harmony remains on track to report Phase 1b data in sleep-deprived healthy volunteers in the first quarter of 2027, followed by the initiation of Phase 2 studies across multiple CNS indications by mid-2027.
​
Additionally, as announced on October 12, 2026, Peter Anastasiou, Senior Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer, will become President and Chief Executive Officer effective January 1, 2027, succeeding Jeffrey Dayno, MD, who will retire on December 31, 2026. This planned leadership transition does not change Harmony's previously communicated strategic priorities or the expected timing of key commercial and pipeline milestones.
​
“As I prepare to transition the President and CEO role to Peter on January 1, I believe Harmony is operating from a position of strength, with a durable and growing WAKIX franchise, the financial flexibility to invest in innovation, conviction to pursue strategic business development opportunities, and a pipeline led by BP-205 that has the potential to generate significant long-term value,” said Jeffrey Dayno, MD, President and Chief Executive Officer of Harmony Biosciences. “I am proud of the talented team and passionate organization we have built, which position Harmony for continued success in delivering innovative treatments to patients and sustained value for our shareholders.”

BP-205 (Orexin-2 receptor agonist)
​
BP-205 MAD Study Design and Preliminary Results
● | 14-day double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled study that evaluated once-daily doses of BP-205 at 0.1 mg, 0.6 mg, 1 mg, and 3 mg across six cohorts (4 male and 2 female); a total of 94 healthy volunteers participated in this study

● | BP-205 was rapidly absorbed, with a mean T(max) of approximately 60 minutes (range 30 to 90 minutes) supportive of the potential for rapid onset of efficacy

● | The mean effective half-life (T1/2) after 14-day repeat dosing was observed to be approximately 29 hours; no clinically meaningful change compared to the half-life in the SAD study and supportive of once-daily dosing

● | BP-205 was generally well tolerated across all doses evaluated: headache was the most common adverse event occurring in approximately 25% of participants

● | On-target adverse events of insomnia were reported in less than 20% of participants at all doses evaluated, were mild in severity and did not lead to discontinuation

● | On-target adverse events of urinary frequency and urgency were reported in less than 20% of participants at the 1mg and 3mg doses only; these events were mild in severity and did not lead to discontinuation

● | No liver or visual adverse events were reported, and no clinically significant cardiovascular events or EKG abnormalities were recorded

● | There were no serious or severe adverse events

● | The complete MAD study results will be presented at an upcoming medical meeting

● | Next steps in BP-205 development:

o | Phase 1b study in sleep-deprived healthy volunteers initiated in Q3 in the US; data expected in Q1 2027

o | Expect to initiate Phase 2 studies to evaluate multiple CNS indications by mid-2027

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
​
Harmony Biosciences reported WAKIX net revenue of $268.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $239.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, representing a 12% increase over the same period. This performance reflects continued strong demand for WAKIX within the large narcolepsy market opportunity (approximately 80,000 diagnosed patients in the U.S.), disciplined commercial execution across the franchise and the product's broad clinical utility. Based on our results for the first nine months of 2026, WAKIX remains on track to achieve full-year net revenue of $1.0 billion to $1.04 billion.
​
Cost of product sold was $80.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, or 30% as a percentage of WAKIX net revenue, as compared to $59.7 million, or 25%, for the same quarter in 2025, representing a 35% increase. The increase in cost of product sold as a percentage of net product revenue was primarily driven by new royalties related to the Novitium license agreement.
​
Net income for the third quarter was $48.2 million, or $0.81 per diluted share, compared to $50.9 million, or $0.87 per diluted share, for the same quarter in 2025. The decline in earnings was primarily driven by an increase in milestone charges and the new MSN royalty charges during the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the same quarter in 2025.
​
Harmony’s operating expenses include the following:
● | Research and Development expenses were $71.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $55.0 million for the same quarter in 2025, representing a 30% increase; this was primarily driven by a $25.0 million charge related to a milestone achieved for the MSN license agreement, partially offset by a $15.0 million charge related to a clinical milestone achieved for ZYN002 in the prior year period;

● | Sales and Marketing expenses were $34.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $29.5 million for the same quarter in 2025, representing an 18% increase, primarily driven by the expansion of our field-based teams;

● | General and Administrative expenses were $27.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $29.8 million for the same quarter in 2025, representing a 7% decrease; primarily driven by a decline in stock-based compensation expense; and

● | Total Operating expenses were $134.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $114.3 million for the same quarter in 2025, representing a 17% increase.

About Harmony Biosciences
Harmony Biosciences is developing and commercializing innovative medicines for both rare and broad CNS disorders, and we are becoming an enduring leader in neuroscience. Our first medicine, WAKIX® (pitolisant), is the first and only FDA-approved non-scheduled treatment for excessive daytime sleepiness or cataplexy in patients 6 years of age and older with narcolepsy. Its launch ranks among the most successful in the history of orphan drugs. Building on our success and profitability, we are continuing to advance our novel clinical-stage pipeline, led by BP-205, an investigational orexin-2 receptor agonist with best-in-class potential across a broad range of CNS disorders. With a strong balance sheet, we have the capacity and conviction to pursue strategic business development opportunities. Harmony is guided by an unwavering commitment to patients and driven to create long-term value for shareholders. For more information, visit: www.harmonybiosciences.com.
