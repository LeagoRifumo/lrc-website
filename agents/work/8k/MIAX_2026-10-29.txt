Miami International Holdings Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results • Q3 Net revenue of $131 million (+20% YoY) • Q3 GAAP diluted EPS of $0.47; Adjusted diluted EPS of $0.45 • Q3 Adjusted EBITDA of $71 million (+47% YoY); Adjusted EBITDA margin of 54% (+>1,000 bps YoY) • Updates full-year 2026 expense guidance PRINCETON, N.J. and MIAMI – October 29, 2026 – Miami International Holdings, Inc. (MIAX or MIH) (NYSE: MIAX), a technology-driven leader in building and operating regulated financial markets across multiple asset classes, today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026. MIAX delivered strong financial results in Q3 2026. Total net revenue grew 20% year-over-year to $131.0 million, adjusted EBITDA increased 47% to $70.8 million, and adjusted EBITDA margin was 54%. Adjusted diluted earnings per share was $0.45. The company’s options business posted a 15% year-over-year increase in average daily volume to 11.1 million contracts. “MIAX’s lean, high-margin operating model enabled us to deliver strong revenue growth and margin expansion in the third quarter,” said Thomas P. Gallagher, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of MIAX. “Net revenue grew 20% year-over-year and options market share increased to 17.4%. Adjusted expenses came in below last year’s level and adjusted EBITDA margin expanded by more than 1,000 basis points.” Mr. Gallagher added: “Our next growth phase is underway, with Bloomberg index futures now available on NinjaTrader and four fertilizer futures contracts recently launched in response to clear member interest. With a strong balance sheet and the technology, licenses and customer relationships to compete in a fast-changing market, MIAX remains well-positioned to deliver long-term value for our shareholders.” Third Quarter 2026 Highlights All figures are compared to the third quarter of 2025 unless otherwise stated. • Net revenue, defined as revenues less cost of revenues, grew 20%, or $21.5 million, to $131.0 million, compared to $109.5 million in the prior-year period. The increase was primarily driven by strong options business performance, including increased industry volumes and higher non-transaction revenue. • Total operating expenses were $83.8 million, compared to $109.8 million in the prior-year period. The decrease was primarily due to initial public offering (IPO) related expense in 2025. • Total adjusted operating expenses of $60.3 million decreased 2% compared to the prior-year period. • Operating income was $47.2 million, compared to an operating loss of $0.3 million in the prior-year period. • GAAP net income was $52.5 million, compared to a net loss of $102.1 million in the prior-year period. • Adjusted earnings increased 25% to $50.1 million, compared to $39.9 million in the prior-year period. • Adjusted EBITDA increased 47% to $70.8 million, compared to $48.0 million in the prior-year period, driven primarily by strong growth in net revenues. • Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 54% from 44% in the prior-year period. • Diluted EPS of $0.47 compared to $(1.46) in the prior-year period. • Adjusted diluted EPS of $0.45 compared to $0.42 in the prior-year period. Non-GAAP Financial Information Adjusted earnings, a non-GAAP financial measure, is defined as net income (loss) attributable to MIH adjusted for share-based compensation, investment gain/loss, litigation costs and settlement, acquisition-related costs, change in fair value of puttable warrants issued with debt, change in fair value of puttable common stock, gain on sale of business, unrealized gain/loss on derivative and digital assets, loss on sale of intangible asset, impairment charges, warrant modifications, one time IPO payments, loss on extinguishment of debt, provision for notes receivable, and non-GAAP tax adjustments. Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, is defined as net income (loss) attributable to MIH adjusted for interest expense and amortization of debt discount costs, interest income, income tax provision and depreciation and amortization, share-based compensation, investment gain/loss, litigation costs and settlement, acquisition- related costs, change in fair value of puttable warrants issued with debt, change in fair value of puttable common stock, gain on sale of business, unrealized gain/loss on derivative and digital assets, loss on sale of intangible asset, impairment charges, warrant modifications, one time IPO payments, loss on extinguishment of debt, and provision for notes receivable. Adjusted EBITDA margin, a non-GAAP financial measure, is defined as adjusted EBITDA divided by revenues less cost of revenues. Adjusted diluted EPS, a non-GAAP financial measure, is defined as adjusted earnings divided by diluted weighted average shares outstanding used for adjusted diluted earnings per share (which includes the impact of anti- dilutive securities on a GAAP basis). Certain components of the guidance given in this presentation with respect to our financial performance for the full year of 2026 are provided on a non-GAAP basis only without providing the most comparable guidance on a GAAP basis or a quantitative reconciliation to guidance provided on a GAAP basis. Information is presented in this manner because the preparation of such guidance on a GAAP basis and such reconciliation could not be accomplished without unreasonable efforts. The company does not have access to certain information that would be necessary to provide such guidance on a GAAP basis or such reconciliation, including non-recurring items that are not indicative of the company’s ongoing operations. The company does not believe that this information is likely to be significant to an assessment of the company’s ongoing operations.
