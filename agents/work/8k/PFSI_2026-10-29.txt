PennyMac Financial Services, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and
Announces Changes to its Leadership Team
WESTLAKE VILLAGE, Calif. – October 29, 2026 – PennyMac Financial Services, Inc. (NYSE: PFSI) today reported net income of $29 million, or $0.54 in diluted earnings per share (EPS), on total net revenues of $469 million for the third quarter of 2026. Adjusted net income was $56 million, or $1.05 in adjusted diluted EPS, on adjusted net revenues of $502 million1. PFSI’s Board of Directors declared a third quarter cash dividend of $0.30 per share, payable on November 27, 2026, to common stockholders of record as of November 16, 2026.
Additionally, PFSI today announced that Doug Jones, President and Chief Mortgage Banking Officer, will be retiring on June 30, 2027 after a storied career in the mortgage banking industry including more than 15 years of leadership at Pennymac. His roles and responsibilities will transition to other senior leaders within the company over the next several months.
Also, effective January 1, 2027, Kevin Ryan, currently Chief Strategy Officer, will assume the role of Chief Financial Officer (CFO). Current CFO, Daniel Perotti, will remain in his current role through the remainder of this year and will continue to support the company in an advisory role through the first quarter of 2027. Mr. Ryan will continue to lead the strategy and business development teams while in his new role.
“I am extraordinarily grateful to both Doug and Dan for their long service at Pennymac and their ongoing commitment to and passion for our business,” said Chairman and Chief Executive Officer David Spector. “Both served in various roles and helped to shape Pennymac’s significant growth and transformation into a leading mortgage company. They have made invaluable contributions to the company, and will be greatly missed.”
Mr. Spector continued, “We are very fortunate to have an exceptional leadership team and a deep bench of talent to lead Pennymac into the next chapter, and drive long-term value for our stockholders.”
CEO Commentary
“Our servicing business delivered strong profitability, while our production segment reported a pretax loss due an industry-wide contraction in origination activity coupled with margin pressure near the end of the quarter,“ said Chairman and CEO David Spector. “We expect mortgage demand will remain subdued due to higher interest rates, and as a result we have taken aggressive and proactive steps to right-size our platform appropriately. By the first quarter of 2027 we expect to have reduced our annualized operating expenses by $160 million from the second quarter of 2026.”
Mr. Spector continued, “Looking forward, we are executing on several high-impact strategic catalysts to optimize returns on capital. Subsequent to quarter-end, we agreed to sell $29 billion in UPB of low-rate conventional MSRs where recapture opportunities are limited. We plan to redeploy the $490 million in proceeds to reduce higher-cost debt and repurchase common shares while our stock trades at a sizable discount to book value. In addition, we expect to close the acquisition of Cenlar’s subservicing business in early 2027, scaling our high-margin, fee-based servicing platform. Coupled with the accelerated deployment of AI across our production workflows, we are well-positioned to capture significant operating leverage when market volumes rebound.”

Servicing revenues excluding MSR fair value changes net of hedge results totaled $401 million, up from $369 million in the prior quarter and down slightly from $405 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase from the prior quarter was primarily due to lower realization of MSR cash flows and payoff-related expenses, reflecting lower prepayment speeds, and an increase in loan servicing fees due to growth in the MSR portfolio. The decrease from the third quarter of 2025 was primarily due to lower EBO-loan related income due to higher mortgage rates and recent changes to FHA and VA loss mitigation programs, which changed the timing and volume of buyout and redelivery opportunities.
Servicing expenses excluding MSR fair value changes net of hedge results were $268 million, down slightly from $270 million in the prior quarter and up from $250 million in the third quarter of 2025. The slight decrease from the prior quarter was primarily driven by a decline in payoff-related expenses as a result of lower prepayments, partially offset by higher interest expense from larger outstanding financing balances for MSR due to higher interest rates. The increase from the third quarter of 2025 was primarily due to higher interest expense and losses and provisions for defaulted loans.
MSR fair value changes net of hedge results was a loss of $34 million, compared to $77 million in the prior quarter and $3 million of net gains in the third quarter of 2025. The losses in the quarter included $18 million in hedge costs, compared to $52 million in the prior quarter and $4 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Corporate and Other
Pretax loss from corporate and other was $49 million, compared to $29 million in the prior quarter and $44 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Revenues were $12 million, down from $23 million in the prior quarter and essentially unchanged from the third quarter of 2025. The prior quarter included a non-recurring gain from an increase in the value of our minority equity interest in Vesta.
Expenses were $61 million, up from $52 million in the prior quarter and $56 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase from the prior quarter was driven primarily by higher compensation expenses, while the increase from the third quarter of 2025 was driven primarily by higher technology expenses.

About PennyMac Financial Services, Inc.
PennyMac Financial Services, Inc. (NYSE: PFSI) is a national financial services firm focused on the production and servicing of U.S. mortgage loans and the management of mortgage-related investments. Founded in 2008 and headquartered in Westlake Village, California, it is one of the nation's largest mortgage producers and servicers, and one of the few lenders operating at scale across all three production channels. The company combines advanced technology with significant operational scale to serve home buyers, existing homeowners, and its institutional partners nationwide. Additional information about PennyMac Financial Services, Inc. is available at pfsi.pennymac.com.
