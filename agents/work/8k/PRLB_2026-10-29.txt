Protolabs Reports Financial Results for the Third Quarter of 2026
Record Quarterly Revenue of $154.3 Million, a 14.0% Increase Year-Over-Year
GAAP Earnings Per Share of $0.48, up 61% YoY; Non-GAAP Earnings Per Share of $0.63, up 33% YoY
Raising Full Year 2026 Revenue Growth Guidance from 8% - 10% to 10% - 11%
MINNEAPOLIS – October 29, 2026 – Proto Labs, Inc. ("Protolabs" or the "Company") (NYSE: PRLB), the world’s leading provider of digital manufacturing services, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:
•Revenue was a record $154.3 million, a 14.0% increase over the third quarter of 2025.
◦Total revenue per customer contact increased 16.6% year-over-year.
◦CNC machining revenue grew 16.4% year-over-year.
◦Injection molding revenue grew 12.5% year-over-year.
◦3D printing revenue grew 11.4% year-over-year.
•GAAP operating margin was 9.4%, up from 6.5% in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP operating margin was 11.9%, up from 10.0% in the third quarter of 2025.
•GAAP net income was $0.48 per diluted share, compared to $0.30 per diluted share in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net income was $0.63 per diluted share, compared to $0.47 per diluted share in the third quarter of 2025.
Note: See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below.
"Protolabs delivered another record quarter, with double-digit revenue growth for the fourth consecutive quarter," said Suresh Krishna, President and Chief Executive Officer. "Our momentum reflects strong execution and growing demand from industries that value speed, quality, and manufacturing agility. As reindustrialization, advanced hardware innovation, and the need for resilient supply chains reshape manufacturing, our digital model enables customers to move quickly from idea to production — positioning us to deliver sustained profitable growth and long-term shareholder value."
Additional Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:
•GAAP net income was $11.8 million, or 7.6% of revenue, up 63.0% compared to $7.2 million, or 5.3% of revenue in the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA was $26.2 million, or 17.0% of revenue, up 23.8% compared to $21.1 million, or 15.6% of revenue, in the third quarter of 2025. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below.
•Cash generated from operations was $22.8 million.
•Cash and investments balance was $175.0 million as of September 30, 2026.
"We delivered another quarter of strong financial performance, with broad-based revenue growth, meaningful operating leverage, and continued earnings growth," said Dan Schumacher, Chief Financial Officer. "We remain focused on balancing investments in our strategic priorities with disciplined cost management, while generating strong cash flow and positioning the business for sustained profitable growth."
Financial Guidance and Outlook:
•For full year 2026, Protolabs is raising its guidance and now expects revenue growth between 10% and 11%.
•For the fourth quarter of 2026, the Company expects revenue between $143.0 million and $151.0 million.
•For the fourth quarter of 2026, the Company expects GAAP diluted net income per share between $0.28 and $0.36, and non-GAAP diluted net income per share between $0.46 and $0.54. See "Non-GAAP Financial Measures" below.
Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The Company has included non-GAAP revenue growth by region and by product line that excludes the impact of changes in foreign currency exchange rates (collectively, “non-GAAP revenue growth”). Management believes these metrics, when viewed in conjunction with the comparable metrics calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States ("GAAP"), are useful in evaluating the underlying business trends and ongoing operating performance of the Company.
The Company has included earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (“EBITDA”) and EBITDA, adjusted for stock-based compensation expense, unrealized loss (gain) on foreign currency, trade refunds and charges, net, restructuring and transformation costs, costs related to exit and disposal activities and CEO transition costs (collectively, “Adjusted EBITDA”), in this press release to provide investors with additional information regarding the Company’s financial results. The Company has also included earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization margin (“EBITDA margin”) and EBITDA margin, adjusted for stock-based compensation expense, unrealized loss (gain) on foreign currency, trade refunds and charges, net, restructuring and transformation costs, costs related to exit and disposal activities and CEO transition costs (“Adjusted EBITDA margin”), in this press release to provide investors with additional information regarding the Company’s financial results.
The Company has included non-GAAP gross margin, adjusted for stock-based compensation expense, amortization expense and trade refunds and charges, net ("non-GAAP gross margin"), in this press release to provide investors with additional information regarding the Company’s financial results.
The Company has included non-GAAP operating margin, adjusted for stock-based compensation expense, amortization expense, trade refunds and charges, net, restructuring and transformation costs, costs related to exit and disposal activities and CEO transition costs (“non-GAAP operating margin”), in this press release to provide investors with additional information regarding the Company’s financial results.
The Company has included non-GAAP net income and non-GAAP net income per share, in each case, adjusted for stock-based compensation expense, amortization expense, unrealized loss (gain) on foreign currency, trade refunds and charges, net, restructuring and transformation costs, costs related to exit and disposal activities and CEO transition costs (“non-GAAP net income” and "non-GAAP net income per share", respectively), in this press release to provide investors with additional information regarding the Company’s financial results.
The Company has provided below reconciliations of GAAP to non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP net income per share, non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP revenue growth by region and by product line, EBITDA, EBITDA margin, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin to the most directly comparable measures calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. These non-GAAP measures are used by the Company’s management and board of directors to understand and evaluate operating performance and trends, provide useful measures for period-to-period comparisons of the Company’s business, and in determining executive and senior management incentive compensation. Accordingly, the Company believes that these non-GAAP measures provide useful information to investors and others in understanding and evaluating operating results in the same manner as the Company's management and board of directors. These non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP. These non-GAAP financial measures exclude significant expenses and income that are required by GAAP to be recorded in the Company's condensed consolidated financial statements and are subject to inherent limitations. Investors should review the reconciliations of non-GAAP financial measures to the comparable GAAP financial measures that are included in this press release.
