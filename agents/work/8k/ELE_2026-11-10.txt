ELEMENTAL ROYALTY DELIVERS STRONG QUARTERLY PERFORMANCE
November 10, 2026 - Denver, Colorado: Elemental Royalty Corporation (NASDAQ: ELE) (TSX: ELE) ("Elemental" or the "Company") is pleased to report results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. For the quarter, Elemental delivered revenue of US$20.8 million, quarterly GEOs1 of 4,861, and adjusted EBITDA1 of US$16.6 million.
Q3 2026 Financial Highlights
• Quarterly revenue of US$20.8 million, up 203% compared to revenue in Q3 2025;
• Quarterly revenue plus attributable share of Caserones1 of US$20.8 million, up 153% compared to Q3 2025;
• Gold Equivalent Ounces ("GEOs") of 4,861 for Q3 2026 (2,362 in Q3 2025), and record 15,092 GEOs for Q3 year to date 2026 (10,136 in 2025);
• Adjusted EBITDA1 of US$16.6 million, up 168% compared to adjusted EBITDA in Q3 2025;
• Adjusted operating cash flow1 of US$13.2 million, up 162% compared to the comparative period;
• Cash and cash equivalents of US$23.0 million as of September 30, 2026; and
• On track to meet updated GEO and revenue guidance of 19,500-22,000 GEOs for 2026, driven by significant contributions from Karlawinda, Bonikro, Timok, and Caserones.
Frederick Bell, Elemental Chief Executive Officer, commented: "Elemental delivered another strong quarter, reflecting the strength of our expanded portfolio, robust commodity prices and solid contributions from our key producing assets. Our performance reflects the increasing quality and maturity of the portfolio, with a growing base of cash-generating assets providing both resilience and a platform for further investment.
The third quarter also marked important milestones in our growth strategy, underpinned by the acquisition of Vizsla Royalties and our recently announced streaming and royalty acquisitions. Together, these transactions further strengthen our portfolio, adding exposure to producing assets and development projects with material growth potential. Importantly, we continue to see substantial embedded growth across the portfolio, supported by expansion projects, exploration success and investment by established operators, providing opportunities to increase future cash flow without additional capital commitments from Elemental.
Our recently announced strategic divestment of the royalty generation business represents the next stage of Elemental's evolution, creating a simpler, leaner and more focused royalty and streaming company, while retaining exposure to the long-term upside of the generation model. Looking ahead, our focus remains on growing the portfolio through accretive acquisitions while maintaining a lean and efficient corporate structure. Increasing cash flow from our producing assets, together with our enhanced financial flexibility puts us in a strong position to fund future growth, reduce leverage and minimize shareholder dilution. Our priority is to translate this growth into increasing cash flow and value per share, building a larger, higher-quality business and delivering sustainable long-term returns for shareholders."

Elemental has updated its 2026 guidance for its existing portfolio to 18,000 - 20,500 GEOs, compared to guidance of 17,000 - 21,000 GEOs reported in Q2 2026.
In addition, the Company expects an economic contribution equivalent to approximately 1,500 GEOs from the Orion portfolio for the period August 1 to December 31, 2026, bringing total expected contributions to 19,500 - 22,000 GEOs on an economic basis. Under the terms of the Orion acquisition, Elemental is entitled to all royalty and stream economics from August 1, 2026. However, for financial reporting purposes, the Company will only recognize revenue and GEOs generated subsequent to the closing date of the acquisition on October 26, 2026.
Based on performance during the first three quarters of 2026 and current expectations for the underlying assets, the Company remains on track to achieve its updated 2026 GEO sales guidance. The Company's updated guidance assumes a commodity price of $4,000/oz gold and $6.00/lb copper for the remaining three months of 2026.
Guidance in 2026 is based on public forecasts, other disclosure by the owners and operators of our assets, historical performance, and management's understanding of the underlying producing assets.
Key Strategic Developments
During the third quarter and subsequent to quarter-end, Elemental continued to execute its growth strategy through significant royalty and streaming acquisitions, portfolio optimization and initiatives to strengthen its financial flexibility. Key developments included:
• Acquisition of Vizsla Royalties: Completed the acquisition of Vizsla Royalties in September 2026, adding a life-of-mine 2.0%-3.5% net smelter return ("NSR") royalty over the Panuco silver-gold project in Mexico, which is expected to become a cornerstone asset within Elemental's portfolio.
• Orion Portfolio Acquisition: Announced the acquisition of a portfolio of five precious metals streams and royalties from funds managed by Orion Mine Finance for total consideration of US$290 million. Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company completed the acquisition of four of the five assets, significantly increasing its exposure to producing and development-stage assets with substantial growth potential. The remaining asset is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027.

• Strategic Divestment of Royalty Generation Business: Announced a proposed divestment of the Company's royalty generation business to Carlin East, representing an important step toward a more streamlined, cash-flow-focused royalty and streaming business, with an anticipated reduction in corporate general and administrative expenses of more than 25%.
• Expanded Credit Facility: Subsequent to quarter-end, increased the Company's revolving credit facility from US$150 million to US$250 million, with an additional US$50 million accordion feature, providing greater financial flexibility to support acquisitions and future growth.

Asset update
Cornerstone and Key Producing Assets
Caserones - Copper and Molybdenum - Effective 1.304% NSR
• Effective revenue from Caserones of US$3.1 million and US$16.6 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 respectively.
• Copper production for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 was 15,015 and 87,531 tonnes, respectively.
• During the third quarter of 2026, production was significantly impacted by severe winter storms, resulting in prolonged power outages and approximately 30 days of operational suspensions. The impacts continued into the fourth quarter, with snowmelt affecting haulage rates and ore deliveries to the mill. As a result, Lundin Mining reduced its 2026 copper production guidance to 105,000-115,000 tonnes, noting that the production shortfall is expected to be recovered during 2027.
Karlawinda - Gold - 2% NSR
• Royalty revenue from Karlawinda of US$2.9 million and US$8.8 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 respectively.
• Gold production for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 was 31,218 ounces and 90,013 ounces, respectively.
• Capricorn completed construction and commissioning of the expanded processing plant, which commenced continuous operations in September 2026, increasing capacity to approximately 6.5 Mtpa and targeting annual gold production of 150,000 ounces.
• Capricorn announced an increase in Mineral Reserves at Karlawinda, supporting an approximately 10-year mine life at the expanded processing capacity.
• Together with ongoing mining activities and regional exploration, these developments support stable production and incremental upside over time, and increased royalty revenue for Elemental.
Timok - Copper and Gold - 0.3625% NSR
• Royalty revenue from Timok of US$2.6 million and US$7.0 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 respectively.
• Expansion and optimization activities continue at the Upper Zone, including advancement of multi-level mining, while Phase 1 of the Lower Zone remains on track, including shaft and conveyor decline development.
• Exploration drilling at the Malka Golaja Project is progressing as planned, with preliminary works and infrastructure development underway.

Caserones Reclassification
Effective November 13, 2025, the shareholders of SLM California executed an amendment to the entity's shareholder agreement, resulting in the Company reassessing the classification of its interest in SLM California, which holds the Company's Caserones royalty. As a result of the amendment to the shareholder agreement, the Company determined that the revised arrangement constituted a joint operation in accordance with IFRS 11 Joint Arrangements. Consequently, on November 13, 2025, the Company discontinued equity accounting under IAS 28 Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures and began recognizing its proportionate share of the assets, liabilities, revenues, and expenses of SLM California as a joint operation. As a result, there are no adjustments in the current period related to the reclassification for revenue plus attributable share of Caserones, depletion plus attributable share of Caserones or tax expense plus attributable share of Caserones.

Reconciliation of Revenue, Depletion and Tax expense plus Attributable Share of Caserones:
Revenue plus attributable share of Caserones is a non-IFRS financial measure, which is defined as including gross royalty revenue from associated entities holding royalty interests related to Elemental's effective royalty on the Caserones copper mine. Management uses revenue plus attributable share of Caserones to evaluate the underlying operating performance of the Company for the reporting periods presented, to assist with the planning and forecasting of future operating results, and to supplement information in its financial statements. Management believes that in addition to measures prepared in accordance with IFRS Accounting Standards such as revenue, investors may use revenue plus attributable share of Caserones to evaluate the results of the underlying business, particularly as the revenue plus attributable share of Caserones may not typically be included in operating results. Management believes that revenue plus attributable share of Caserones is a useful measure of the Company performance because it adjusts for items which management believes reflect the Company's core operating results from period to period. Revenue plus attributable share of Caserones is intended to provide additional information to investors and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for measures of performance prepared in accordance with IFRS Accounting Standards. It does not have any standardized meaning under IFRS Accounting Standards and may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other issuers.
Depletion plus attributable share of Caserones and tax expense plus attributable share of Caserones are non-IFRS measures which include depletion and tax expense from the Caserones royalty asset respectively, consistent with the recognition of revenue plus attributable share of Caserones as described above.
