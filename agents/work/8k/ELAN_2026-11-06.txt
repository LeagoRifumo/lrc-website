Elanco Animal Health Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Accelerated Price, Organic Constant Currency Revenue Growth, and Gross Margin Expansion; Raising Full Year Outlook and Innovation Target; Achieved 2.9x Net Leverage Ratio Milestone
•Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results:
◦Revenue of $1.265 billion, an increase of 11% year-over-year; 9% organic constant currency growth
◦Reported Net Income of $18 million, Adjusted Net Income of $136 million
◦Adjusted EBITDA of $243 million; Adjusted EBITDA Margin of 19.5%
◦Reported EPS of $0.04, Adjusted EPS of $0.27
◦Net leverage ratio of 2.9x Adjusted EBITDA
•Full Year 2026 Guidance:
◦Raising innovation revenue target to $1.275 billion
◦Raising revenue guidance to $5.155 billion to $5.18 billion, or approximately 8% organic constant currency growth
◦Raising Adjusted EBITDA guidance to $1.05 billion to $1.065 billion, a year-over-year increase of 17% at midpoint

◦Building on expectations for full year 2026 outperformance, with continued expectation for 2027 results in line with December 2025 Investor Day annual algorithm: mid-single digit organic constant currency revenue growth, high-single digit Adjusted EBITDA growth, and low-double digit Adjusted EPS growth
Indianapolis, IN (November 6, 2026) - Elanco Animal Health Incorporated (NYSE: ELAN) today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026, provided guidance for the fourth quarter of 2026, updated guidance for the full year 2026, and provided an early outlook for the full year 2027.
"Third quarter results demonstrate Elanco is a consistent, reliable growth company, delivering our diverse portfolio of innovation along with accelerating cash flow," stated Jeff Simmons, President and CEO of Elanco. "Organic constant currency revenue growth of 9% was led by International Pet Health up 17% and U.S. Pet Health up 13%, as we saw strong veterinarian and pet owner demand for what we believe is 'best medicine' innovation for our 'Big 6' growth led by Zenrelia and Credelio Quattro. Price accelerated to 4% growth, exceeding our expectations, while volume remained robust at 5%. Our durable top-line is fueled by a significant runway for share gains off a small base in large, growing, global markets. Both Zenrelia and Credelio Quattro reached new market share highs in September, with competitive activity having no fundamental impact on the trajectory of our innovation. At the same time, adjusted gross margin expanded 300 basis points, we invested in our commercial execution, and we drove our net leverage ratio to the 2.9x milestone which accelerates our capital allocation flexibility. As we are raising full-year guidance to fully pass through the third quarter beat, Elanco is well positioned with momentum heading into 2027. We are recommitting to our annual algorithm of mid-single digit organic constant currency revenue growth, high-

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was $1,265 million, an increase of 11% on a reported basis, or 9% on an organic constant currency basis, compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Pet Health revenue was $608 million, an increase of 14% on both a reported and an organic constant currency basis. The 5% increase from price exceeded the company's expectation for acceleration. The year-over-year volume increase of 9% in the third quarter was primarily driven by strong demand for Zenrelia, Credelio Quattro and AdTab™. The Advantage® Family of products and Seresto® Family of products contributed revenue of $115 million and $49 million, respectively; Seresto was negatively impacted by U.S. retailer inventory draw down, with underlying performance in line with expectations.
Farm Animal revenue was $631 million, an increase of 6% on a reported basis, or 4% on an organic constant currency basis. The year-over-year reported increase was primarily driven by a 3% increase in price during the period and a 2% contribution from AHV.
Gross profit was $720 million and gross margin percentage was 56.9% in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 350 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025. On an adjusted basis, gross profit was $699 million and gross margin percentage was 56.1% in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 300 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increase in gross margin percentage on both a reported and adjusted basis, which was ahead of the company's expectations, was primarily driven by increased pricing and favorable mix from the strong performance in U.S. Pet Health and global ruminants.
Total operating expenses were $492 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 12% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Marketing, selling and administrative expenses increased 12% to $393 million, driven by higher compensation expense and strategic investments in the global launches of new products. Research and development expenses increased 11% to $99 million.
Asset impairment, restructuring and other special charges were $3 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $25 million in the third quarter of 2025. Charges recorded in the third quarter of 2026 primarily related to the company's 2025 restructuring plan as well as costs associated with the acquisition of AHV.
Reported net interest expense was $59 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $7 million compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increase was principally due to the maturity of interest rate swaps in September 2025, which generated a favorable impact to interest expense in the prior year period, as well as imputed interest on our liability for sale of future revenue. These increases were partially offset by lower average debt balances. Adjusted net interest expense, which excludes this imputed interest, was $41 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $7 million compared to the third quarter of 2025.
The reported effective tax rate was 25.7% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 30.9% in the third quarter of 2025. This decrease was primarily due to a more favorable jurisdictional mix of earnings and the absence of prior-year discrete tax changes. The adjusted effective tax rate was 19.4% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 26.5% in the third quarter of 2025.
Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $18 million, or $0.04 per diluted share on a reported basis, compared with net loss of $34 million, or $0.07 per diluted share, for the same period in 2025. On an adjusted basis, net income for the third quarter 2026 grew 45% compared to the same period in 2025 to $136 million, representing $0.27 per diluted share.
Adjusted EBITDA was $243 million in the third quarter of 2026, a 23% increase compared to the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 19.5% compared with 17.5% for the third quarter of 2025.
Working Capital and Balance Sheet
Cash provided by operations was $216 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to cash provided by operations of $219 million in the third quarter of 2025.
As of September 30, 2026, Elanco’s net leverage ratio was 2.9x adjusted EBITDA, a decrease of 0.7x compared to December 31, 2025.

ABOUT ELANCO
Elanco Animal Health Incorporated (NYSE: ELAN) is a global leader in animal health dedicated to innovating and delivering products and services to prevent and treat disease in farm animals and pets, creating value for farmers, pet owners, veterinarians, stakeholders and society as a whole. Gross and Net Debt and Net Leverage Ratio
We define gross debt as the sum of the current portion of long-term debt and long-term debt excluding unamortized debt issuance costs. We define net debt as gross debt less cash and cash equivalents and finance lease liabilities on the balance sheet. We define our net leverage ratio as net debt divided by our trailing twelve month adjusted EBITDA. We believe our net debt and net leverage ratio are important measures to monitor our financial flexibility, liquidity and capital structure and may enhance investors' understanding of our ability to meet future financial obligations. In addition, a net leverage ratio is a financial measure that is frequently used by investors and creditors.
