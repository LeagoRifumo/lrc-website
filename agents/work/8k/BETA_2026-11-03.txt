BETA Technologies, Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
Company delivers record quarterly revenue, achieves landmark FAA market survey authorization and surpasses key year-end goals ahead of schedule.
SOUTH BURLINGTON, Vt. – November 3, 2026 – BETA Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: BETA) (“BETA” or the “Company”), an aerospace and defense company, today announced its financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“This was our strongest quarter of revenue since inception, and we’re exceeding the targets we set at the beginning of 2026,” said Kyle Clark, founder and chief executive officer of BETA. “We’ve now flown more than 250,000 nautical miles, advanced commercial operations through our eIPP launches, and secured a historic FAA authorization to fly prospective customers aboard our A250 electric VTOL aircraft. Each of these milestones builds on years of engineering and real-world operating experience. Our vertically integrated, stepwise approach allows us to take what we're learning in the field and apply it directly to improving our aircraft. We’re demonstrating that our partnerships and strategy are working, and we are carrying that momentum into scaling production as we advance toward commercial certification.”
Business Highlights
•Achieved Historic FAA Authorization: BETA’s A250 became the first electric vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) aircraft to receive a Federal Aviation Administration (“FAA”) Special Airworthiness Certificate for market surveys, enabling demonstration flights with customers onboard ahead of type certification. The authorization builds on years of rigorous A250 flight testing and BETA’s existing CX300 market survey authorization, broadening opportunities to demonstrate both aircraft in real-world operating environments.
•Delivered Record Revenue: Generated record quarterly revenue of $17.7 million, representing 98% year-over-year growth and exceeding the Company’s guidance range. Revenue is entirely organic.
•Surpassed Year-End Flight Target: Exceeded 250,000 nautical miles flown across BETA’s aircraft fleet ahead of schedule, building operational experience across a range of missions and environments. This milestone reflects the continued expansion of flight operations across both CTOL and VTOL aircraft, generating valuable real-world performance data to support ongoing development and certification efforts.
•Expanded Charging Network to 168 Sites: Increased the reach of BETA’s open, interoperable charging infrastructure, supporting aircraft operations across the United States and providing the infrastructure for broader electric aviation adoption.
•Increased Aircraft Backlog: Increased commercial aircraft backlog to just over $4.0 billion, surpassing the Company’s year-end target. The backlog represents 1,040 aircraft in firm and option orders supported by financial commitments.
1
•Broadened Electric Aviation Operations Across the United States: BETA has launched 6 of the 7 selections in the FAA’s eVTOL Integration Pilot Program (eIPP). Select missions included transporting medical supplies in North Carolina, delivering emergency response equipment in Texas and moving time-sensitive cancer medicines in Utah.

About BETA Technologies, Inc.
BETA (NYSE: BETA) is an aerospace and defense company designing, manufacturing and selling high-performance electric aircraft, advanced electric propulsion systems, components and charging systems to top operators worldwide. BETA’s intentional approach to developing the enabling technologies necessary to electrify aviation unlocks lucrative aftermarket revenue opportunity over the life of each aircraft. These highly scalable enabling technologies allow BETA to serve a customer base across cargo and logistics, defense, passenger and medical end markets and unlock cost-effective and safe missions. Visit www.beta.team for more information about BETA and its products.
