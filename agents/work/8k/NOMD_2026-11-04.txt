Nomad Foods Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Organic revenue increased 1.6% year-on-year on stable volume and positive price/mix
Strong cash flow with lower exceptional item adjustments
2026 guidance and 2027 targets reiterated
WOKING, England - November 4, 2026 - Nomad Foods Limited (NYSE: NOMD), today reported financial results for the three and nine month periods ended September 30, 2026.
Key operating metrics and financial performance for the third quarter 2026, when compared to the third quarter 2025, include:
•Reported Revenue increased 2.3% to €770 million; Organic revenue increased 1.6% with volume stable at (0.2%) and a positive price-mix contribution of 1.8%.
•Gross profit was €207 million and gross margin decreased 70 bps; Adjusted gross profit declined 1.7% and rose low-single-digits excluding a year-on-year incentive compensation increase and reversal of timing benefits noted in 2Q 2026. Adjusted gross margin decreased 110 bps.
•Profit for the period increased 16% to €67 million, Adjusted EBITDA decreased 1.5% to €141 million including a mid-single-digit impact from higher incentive compensation.
•Reported Diluted EPS increased 21% to €0.47; Adjusted EPS decreased 4.1% to €0.47.
Management Comments
Dominic Brisby, Chief Executive Officer, commented “Our third quarter results reflect the progress we discussed at our analyst day and mark an important milestone as we returned to organic revenue growth, with volume flat for the first time since 2024. We are seeing improving momentum across the business as the actions we have taken to strengthen execution, simplify the organization, enhance accountability, and improve our commercial effectiveness continue to gain traction. We are encouraged by the progress achieved during the quarter and it reinforces our confidence in the growth framework we outlined for 2027 and beyond.”
Third Quarter 2026 results compared to Third Quarter of 2025
•Revenue increased 2.3% to €770 million. Organic revenue increased by 1.6% and was driven by stable volume of (0.2%) partly offset with positive price-mix of 1.8%.
•Adjusted gross profit decreased 1.7% to €208 million. Adjusted gross profit increased low-single-digits in the quarter excluding higher year on year incentive compensation expense and the reversal of timing benefits noted in the second quarter. Adjusted gross margin decreased 110 basis points to 27.0%.
•Adjusted operating expenses were comparable with prior year at €93 million as productivity savings offset the majority of the year on year increase in incentive compensation.
•Adjusted EBITDA decreased 1.5% to €141 million including a mid-single digit headwind from incentive compensation. Adjusted Profit for the period decreased 7% to €67 million.
•Adjusted EPS decreased €0.02 to €0.47. Reported Diluted EPS increased €0.08 to €0.47.
First Nine Months of 2026 results compared to the First Nine Months of 2025
•Revenue decreased 2.2% to €2,209 million. Organic revenue decreased by 2.2% and was driven by a volume decline of 3.5% and an improvement in price/mix of 1.3%.
•Adjusted gross profit decreased 4.8% to €601 million. Adjusted gross margin decreased 70 basis points to 27.2%, due to supply chain inflation headwinds, partially offset by pricing and supply chain productivity.
•Adjusted operating expenses increased 2.2% to €320 million due to the rebuild of the company's employee performance incentive scheme partly offset by a reduction in Advertising and Promotion expense.
•Adjusted EBITDA decreased 9.0% to €357 million due to the aforementioned factors. Adjusted Profit for the period decreased 17.1% to €154 million.
•Adjusted EPS decreased by €0.13 to €1.09. Reported Diluted EPS increased €0.05 to €1.02.

About Nomad Foods
Nomad Foods (NYSE: NOMD) is Europe’s leading frozen food company. The Company’s portfolio of iconic brands, which includes Birds Eye, Findus, iglo, Ledo and Frikom, have been a part of consumers’ meals for generations, standing for great tasting food that is convenient, high quality and nutritious. Nomad Foods is headquartered in the United Kingdom. Additional information may be found at www.nomadfoods.com.
Non-IFRS Financial Information
Nomad Foods is presenting Adjusted and Organic financial information, which is considered non-IFRS financial information, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 and for comparative purposes, the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025.
Adjusted financial information for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 presented in this press release reflects the historical reported financial statements of Nomad Foods, adjusted primarily for, when they occur, share based payment expenses and related employer payroll taxes, non-operating M&A related costs, acquisition purchase price adjustments, exceptional items and foreign currency translation charges/gains.
Adjusted EBITDA is profit or loss for the period before taxation, net financing costs, depreciation and amortization, adjusted to exclude, when they occur, the impacts of exited markets, acquisition purchase price adjustments and exceptional items such as restructuring charges, goodwill and intangible asset impairment charges and other unusual or non-recurring items. In addition, we exclude other adjustments such as the impact of share based payment expenses and related employer payroll taxes, and non-operating M&A related costs, because we do not believe they are indicative of our normal operating costs, can vary significantly in amount and frequency, and are unrelated to our underlying operating performance. The Company believes Adjusted EBITDA provides important comparability of underlying operating results, allowing investors and management to assess operating performance on a consistent basis.
Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as an alternative to profit/(loss) for the period, determined in accordance with IFRS, as an indicator of the Company’s operating performance.
Adjusted Profit for the period is defined as profit for the period excluding, when they occur, the impacts of exited markets, acquisition purchase price adjustments and exceptional items such as restructuring charges, goodwill and intangible asset impairment charges, net financing income/(cost) on amendment of terms of debt, interest cost on tax relating to legacy tax audits, foreign exchange translation gains/(losses), foreign exchange gains/(losses) on derivatives, hedge ineffectiveness on cross currency and interest rate swaps, as well as certain other items considered unusual or non-recurring in nature. In addition, we exclude other adjustments such as the impact of share based payment expenses and related employer payroll taxes, and non-operating M&A related costs, because we do not believe they are indicative of our normal operating costs, can vary significantly in amount and frequency, and are unrelated to our underlying operating performance. The Company believes Adjusted Profit for the period provides important comparability of underlying operating results, allowing investors and management to assess operating performance on a consistent basis.

Organic revenue growth/(decline) is an adjusted measurement of our operating results. The comparison for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 presented in this press release takes into consideration only those activities that were in effect during both time periods. Organic revenue growth/(decline) reflects reported revenue adjusted for currency translation and non-comparable trading items such as expansion, acquisitions, disposals, closures, trading day impacts or any other event that artificially impacts the comparability of our results period over period.
Adjusted Gross Profit and adjusted gross margin exclude accelerated depreciation associated with restructuring programs within cost of goods sold.
Adjustments for currency translation are calculated by translating data of the current and comparative periods using a budget foreign exchange rate that is set once a year as part of the Company's internal annual forecast process.
Adjusted Free Cash Flow is the amount of cash generated from operating activities less cash flows related to exceptional items (as described above), non-operating M&A related costs and working capital movements on employer taxes associated with share based payment awards, plus capital expenditure (on property, plant and equipment and intangible assets), net interest paid, proceeds/(payments) on settlement of derivatives where hedge accounting is not applied and payments of lease liabilities. Adjusted free cash flow reflects cash flows that could be used for payment of dividends, repayment of debt or to fund acquisitions or other strategic objectives.
Adjusted Free Cash flow conversion is Adjusted Free Cash Flow as a percentage of Adjusted Profit for the period.

Key Factors Affecting Our Financial Condition and Results of Operations
Our results of operations and financial performance are influenced by a variety of factors. These factors include macroeconomic conditions impacting our costs and consumer behavior, the effectiveness of our commercial strategies, our ability to manage our supply chain and manufacturing operations, and our execution of strategic initiatives such as business transformation programs.
Macroeconomic and Geopolitical Environment
Our business is significantly influenced by the broader macroeconomic environment, particularly in our key European markets. Factors such as inflation, interest rates and geopolitical conflicts affect consumer confidence, household spending, and our input costs.
•Inflation and Pricing: Increased inflation within several of the markets in which we operate has caused volatility in the cost of raw materials, energy, packaging, and logistics. For example, prices for certain fish species have increased in recent years due to several factors, including quota reductions, increased transportation and logistics expense, contributing to increased cost of goods sold. Our results of operations are affected by our ability to offset increased input costs through pricing actions, productivity initiatives and procurement strategies. To the extent that higher costs cannot be recovered or that price increases adversely affect volumes, our margins and profitability may be negatively impacted. In addition, there is often a time lag between our cost increases and the implementation of price increases, which can negatively impact our profit margins in the short term.
•Consumer Spending and Channel Mix: Increased cost of living has resulted in greater value-seeking behavior by consumers throughout Europe and we expect this trend to continue given inflationary dynamics. Continued value-seeking behavior may support demand for frozen food products, which generally offer consumers an affordable alternative to fresh food and reduce food waste for consumers. However, value-seeking behavior may also accelerate growth in hard-discount channels, where our market share is lower than in the traditional grocery channel, increase demand for private-label products or limit the price premium that we are able to command for certain products, each of which could adversely affect our sales mix, pricing and margins.
•Geopolitical Conflicts: The conflict in Ukraine has created volatility, impacting the availability and cost of key raw materials such as fish, wheat, and energy. Our business could be materially affected by sanctions, tariffs, or other restrictions on supply from the region. We are actively working to diversify our supply sources and mix of species, but this process will take time.
Management discussion on goodwill
In Q3, the Company updated its forecasts as part of its Value Creation Plan, which was shared as part of its Analyst and Investor day on October 6, 2026. The forecasts are a notable change to those used in our impairment test as presented within the 20-F for the year ended December 31, 2025.
