Equinor ASA: Share buy-back – fourth tranche for 2026
Please see below information about transactions made under the fourth of the 2026 share buy-back programme for Equinor ASA (OSE:EQNR, NYSE:EQNR, CEUX:EQNRO, TQEX:EQNRO).
Date on which the buy-back tranche was announced: 28 October 2026.
The duration of the buy-back tranche: 29 October 2026 to no later than 1 February 2027.
Further information on the tranche can be found in the stock market announcement on its commencement dated 28 October 2026, available here: https://newsweb.oslobors.no/message/684948
From 29 October to 30 October 2026, Equinor ASA has purchased a total of 265,300 own shares at an average price of NOK 441.0372 per share.
