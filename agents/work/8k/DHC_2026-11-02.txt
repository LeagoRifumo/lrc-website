About Diversified Healthcare Trust:
DHC is a real estate investment trust focused on owning high-quality healthcare properties located throughout the United States. DHC’s portfolio is anchored by a strategically curated mix of senior housing, medical office and life science assets that combine high quality care, modern technology and amenity rich environments to meet rising demand across the healthcare continuum. As of September 30, 2026, DHC’s approximately $6.3 billion portfolio included 285 properties in 33 states and Washington, D.C., with 23,773 senior living units, approximately 5.6 million square feet of medical office and life science properties and occupied by approximately 250 tenants. DHC is managed by The RMR Group (Nasdaq: RMR), a leading U.S. alternative asset management company with approximately $37 billion in assets under management as of September 30, 2026 and 40 years of institutional experience in buying, selling, financing and operating commercial real estate. DHC is headquartered in Newton, MA. For more information, visit www.dhcreit.com.

RETURN TO TABLE OF CONTENTS Q3 2026 3 “Our third quarter results reflect the continued momentum DHC is experiencing from the strategic initiatives we have implemented over the past two years. This has translated into stronger operating and financial performance, as our same property cash basis NOI increased 25.6% year over year to $77.9 million and Adjusted EBITDAre increased 28.4% to $73.4 million. Normalized FFO increased to $0.13 per share, from $0.02 last year, keeping us on track to achieve our updated full year 2026 guidance. Within our SHOP segment, same property occupancy increased 200 basis points year over year to 84.4%, while average monthly rates grew 5.2%, driving a 50.4% increase in same property NOI and a 470 basis point expansion in NOI margins to 15.8%. These results demonstrate the continued success of our operator transition strategy and strong execution by our operating partners. We also completed the transition of seven skilled nursing facilities to a new 15-year triple net lease with operating subsidiaries of The Ensign Group, Inc., which we expect to generate first year annual rent of $8.0 million while significantly reducing our future capital expenditure requirements. Across our Medical Office and Life Science portfolio, we leased approximately 130,000 square feet at rents that were 25.2% higher than prior levels with a weighted average lease term of 9.7 years. We believe that these results reflect healthy tenant demand and the continued strength of the portfolio.
