PRESS RELEASE
Versigent Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Raises Full-Year 2026 Guidance Midpoints for Net Sales and Free Cash Flow1,
Tightens All Guidance Ranges, Raises Upper End of Free Cash Flow1 Guidance; Declares Quarterly Dividend
SCHAFFHAUSEN, Switzerland, November 4, 2026 – Versigent PLC (NYSE: VGNT), a global leader in the design and manufacture of low‑ and high‑voltage electrical architectures, today reported results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Highlights
Third Quarter 2026
•Net sales of $2,450 million, up 7.2% year-over-year
◦Adjusted Net Sales Growth1 of approximately 1% year-over-year
•Net income attributable to Versigent of $102 million
•Diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) of $1.41, Adjusted Diluted EPS1 of $1.91
•Adjusted EBITDA1 of $275 million, up 10.9% year-over-year
◦Adjusted EBITDA1 margin of 11.2%, up 40 basis points year-over-year
•Net cash provided by operating activities of $225 million, up slightly year-over-year
•Free Cash Flow1 of $170 million, compared to $179 million in Q3 2025, reflecting higher capital expenditures
•Board of Directors declared quarterly cash dividend of $0.13 per share
Full Year 2026 Guidance
•Raised midpoint and tightened net sales guidance range on higher commodity pass-throughs
•Tightened Adjusted EBITDA1 guidance range
•Raised midpoint and upper end of Free Cash Flow1 guidance, while tightening range
“Versigent's strong third-quarter performance reflects the resilience of our business model and disciplined execution amid significant launch activity, lower global automotive production and a volatile macroeconomic environment,” said Joseph Liotine, Chief Executive Officer, Versigent. “During the quarter, we successfully executed a high volume of complex launches across multiple manufacturing facilities, secured $2.1 billion of bookings and continued investing in our adjacent markets commercial and go-to-market capabilities. Together, these actions reinforce our competitive position and support long-term growth and value creation.”
“Our third-quarter results demonstrate the earnings and cash-generating power of the business, highlighted by continued growth over market, year-over-year net sales growth, double-digit Adjusted EBITDA1 growth and margin expansion, as well as strong Free Cash Flow1 generation,” said Doug Ostermann, Chief Financial Officer, Versigent. “Based on our year-to-date results and current macroeconomic outlook, we are raising the midpoint and tightening our full-year net sales and Free Cash Flow1 guidance ranges, as well as tightening our Adjusted EBITDA1 range. Consistent with our commitment to returning capital to shareholders and our disciplined approach to capital allocation, our Board of Directors has declared a quarterly dividend.”

Third Quarter 2026 Results
Versigent delivered third quarter 2026 net sales of $2,450 million, an increase of 7.2% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted Net Sales Growth1, which excludes the impact of year-over-year commodity and foreign currency movements, was approximately 1% despite lower global automotive production.
Net income attributable to Versigent was $102 million, compared to $109 million in the prior-year quarter. The quarter included higher interest expense associated with the Company’s first quarter 2026 debt financing, partially offset by lower income tax expense. Net income margin was 4.2%, down 60 basis points year-over-year. Diluted EPS was $1.41 and Adjusted Diluted EPS1 was $1.91 for the third quarter of 2026.
Adjusted EBITDA1 increased 10.9% year-over-year to $275 million. Adjusted EBITDA1 margin was 11.2%, up 40 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025, reflecting continued strong operational execution amid a significant number of program launches during the quarter, together with favorable net pricing and foreign currency impacts, partially offset by higher commodity costs.
Interest expense was $36 million in the quarter, compared to de minimis for the third quarter of 2025, principally due to the Company’s senior notes and credit facility issued in the first quarter of 2026.
Income tax expense was $35 million, compared to $54 million in the prior-year period. The decrease was primarily driven by a lower effective tax rate in the third quarter of 2026, as the prior-year quarter included higher net discrete tax expenses, primarily related to valuation allowance adjustments.
Net cash provided by operating activities was $225 million, up slightly year-over-year. Capital expenditures increased to $55 million from $44 million in the prior-year quarter. Free Cash Flow1 was $170 million, compared to $179 million in the prior-year quarter, primarily reflecting increased capital expenditures associated with higher launch activity in the second half of 2026. Free Cash Flow1 for the third quarter of 2026 included $11 million of separation-related costs, compared to $7 million in the prior-year quarter.
Dividend
On November 3, 2026, the Company's Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.13 per ordinary share, payable on December 17, 2026 to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 4, 2026.
The declaration and payment of future dividends are subject to the discretion of the Company’s Board of Directors and will depend on the Company’s financial condition, results of operations, cash requirements, and other factors deemed relevant by the Board of Directors.

About Versigent
Versigent is a global leader in the purposeful design and advanced manufacturing of low- and high-voltage electrical architectures. Building on a legacy of engineering excellence and trusted partnerships, Versigent delivers versatile, intelligent solutions engineered to unlock greater capabilities for our customers. Powering one in six passenger vehicles in production today, Versigent’s high performance signal, power, and data distribution systems are trusted by industry leaders across automotive, commercial vehicles, agriculture and energy storage. With engineering and manufacturing centers on four continents and operations in more than 25 countries, Versigent’s approximately 138,000 employees match global scale with regional responsiveness to deliver consistent quality and reliable performance connecting the world to faster, smarter and safer experiences. Visit www.versigent.com.

Our management utilizes Adjusted Net Sales Growth in its financial decision-making process, to evaluate performance of the Company and for internal reporting, planning and forecasting purposes. Adjusted Net Sales Growth is defined as the change in reported net sales relative to the comparable period, excluding the impact on net sales from foreign currency and commodity movements.
