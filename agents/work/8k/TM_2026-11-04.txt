November 5, 2026
TMC Announces Changes to Organizational Structure and Executive
and Senior Professional/Senior Management Employee Positions
Toyota City, Japan, November 5, 2026—Toyota Motor Corporation (TMC) today announced the following organizational and executive and senior professional/senior management employee changes, effective January 1, 2027.
1. | Organizational changes

(1) | Developing people and strengthening the management foundation

● | Toyota Philosophy and Education Group (newly established)

- | Returning to the fundamental principle that “monozukuri (manufacturing) is about developing people,” we will establish a new Toyota Philosophy and Education Group, recognizing people development as a key management priority that will shape Toyota’s future.

- | Akio Toyoda, Chairman of the Board of Directors, will spearhead this initiative as head of the group, personally engaging in education to pass on to the next generation—through hands-on, practical experience—the lessons learned amid numerous challenges, as well as the Toyota philosophy, skills, and conduct inherited from our predecessors.

- | We will transform real-world work into a learning environment, harnessing each individual’s unique qualities to foster talent capable of thinking independently, grappling with challenges, taking on new endeavors, and acting “for the sake of others.”

● | Kanri Group (newly established)

● | General Administration & Human Resources Group (abolished)

● | Accounting Group (abolished)

- | Toyota will integrate and reorganize the functions of the General Administration & Human Resources Group and the Accounting Group to establish the Kanri Group.

The name “Kanri” reflects a shift away from the traditional concept of management. The Japanese character “kan” (看), meaning “to watch over,” combines the elements for “hand” and “eye,” reflecting the philosophy of closely observing, standing alongside, supporting, and at times leading colleagues at the genba.
- | Rather than simply managing people, resources, and finances in the traditional sense, the Kanri Group aims to make greater use of expertise in general administration, human resources, and accounting at the genba. As a service-oriented organization supporting the company, the genba, and its people, the group aims to be a cheering squad that helps colleagues find fulfillment in their work and supports them in taking on new challenges.

● | External & Public Affairs Group (reorganized)

- | To enhance corporate value, some public relations and IR functions will be integrated to centralize information provided to the media and investors, thereby strengthening corporate communications capabilities.

- | Some of the public relations functions of Toyota, Lexus International Company, and GAZOO Racing Company will be integrated to consolidate and optimize product and technology communications shared across the brands.

- | External affairs and research functions will be integrated to strengthen corporate intelligence capabilities to address an increasingly uncertain global environment.

(2) | Strengthening Execution Capabilities in Engineering and Business Development

● | Toyota Robotics (newly established)

- | Building on the engineering expertise and genba capabilities developed through its automotive business, Toyota will position robotics as a key pillar of its future business, enabling it to continue creating what people need in each era. Toyota aims to introduce robots into society not as machines that take away people’s jobs, but as partners that stand alongside them and expand their potential.

1
- | To accelerate these efforts, Toyota will establish Toyota Robotics as a company-wide organization under direct corporate oversight.

- | Takefumi Shiga, Operating Officer and Chief Production Officer, will also head Toyota Robotics, bringing together robotics-related expertise, personnel, and functions spread across the company. Working closely with development departments and drawing on the challenges and needs of the manufacturing genba, the organization will accelerate the practical application, commercialization, and deployment of robots on the front lines, while creating new value toward a society in which people and robots coexist.

● | Carbon Neutral Engineering Development Center (abolished)

- | The Carbon Neutral Engineering Development Center will be abolished, and its functions will be integrated into the Powertrain Company. The technical expertise, activities, and human resources developed by the center will be integrated into powertrain planning, advanced development, and mass production development, with a focus on hybrid systems.

- | This reorganization will accelerate efforts to achieve carbon neutrality by taking an integrated approach from technology exploration and development through to implementation in products and regions, helping Toyota provide customers with a diverse range of options.

● | Frontier Research Center (abolished)

- | The Frontier Research Center will be abolished, and its functions will be integrated into the Advanced R&D and Engineering Company and the Software Development Center. This will more closely connect the center’s research and exploration of future technologies with the organizations responsible for technology development and implementation in society.

- | By pursuing these research and exploration activities in conjunction with related technologies and businesses, rather than within a separate organization, Toyota will accelerate efforts to translate research outcomes into new forms of mobility and social value.

(3) | Further Advancing Regional Management

- | Guided by the philosophy of “management based on products and regions,” leaders who know their regions best will further advance regional management by putting customers and the genba first, thinking independently, making swift decisions, and taking action.

- | Toyota will appoint new CEOs for the North America, China, and Asia regions, as well as a new Business Planning & Operation President. The next generation of leaders will lead Toyota’s transformation into a mobility company in each region, while the Business Planning & Operation functions will bring together expertise and strengths across regions to support regional initiatives. This approach aims to achieve the best outcomes for each region while strengthening Toyota’s overall competitiveness.

● | India & Middle East Region (newly established)

● | East Asia & Oceania Region (newly established)

● | India-Middle East-East Asia & Oceania Region (abolished)

- | The India, Middle East, East Asia & Oceania Region will be divided into two new regions: the India & Middle East Region and the East Asia & Oceania Region. This will replace the existing structure covering a broad geographic area with one that allows each region to engage more closely with its local operations, taking into account differences in market conditions, stages of growth, and business challenges. The new structure will enable swift decision-making at the genba and accelerate efforts to become the best-in-town.

- | The India & Middle East Region will strengthen its business foundation for future growth through initiatives such as launching a new plant in India and coordinating businesses and functions within the region.

- | The East Asia & Oceania Region will work closely with customers and the sales and production genba in each country and region, promoting products, services, and business operations tailored to each market.
