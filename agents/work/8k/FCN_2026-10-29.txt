FTI Consulting Reports Record Third Quarter 2026 Revenues of $1.013 Billion, With Growth Across All Five Business Segments
•
Record Quarterly Revenues of $1.013 Billion, Up 6%, Compared to $956.2 Million in Prior Year Quarter
•
EPS of $2.35, Down 10%, and Record Adjusted EPS of $3.84, Up 48%, Compared to EPS and Adjusted EPS of $2.60 in Prior Year Quarter
•
Company Reaffirms Revenue Guidance, Updates EPS Guidance Range to Between $7.55 and $8.05 and Raises Adjusted EPS Guidance Range to Between $9.50 and $10.00
Washington, D.C., October 29, 2026 — FTI Consulting, Inc. (NYSE: FCN) today released financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third quarter 2026 record revenues of $1.013 billion increased $56.4 million, or 5.9%, compared to revenues of $956.2 million in the prior year quarter. The increase was driven by revenue growth across all business segments. Net income of $63.4 million compared to $82.8 million in the prior year quarter. The decrease in net income was primarily due to a $46.0 million special charge and higher direct costs, selling, general and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses and interest expense, which more than offset revenue growth and a lower income tax provision. The special charge related to a plan to align staffing with demand included $45.5 million in salary- and benefits-related costs and $0.5 million of non-cash real estate-related costs.
Adjusted EBITDA of $153.7 million, or 15.2% of revenues, compared to $130.6 million, or 13.7% of revenues, in the prior year quarter. Third quarter 2026 Adjusted EBITDA excludes the aforementioned special charge and $4.5 million of Extraordinary Litigation-Related Expenses.1 Third quarter 2026 EPS of $2.35 compared to $2.60 in the prior year quarter. Third quarter 2026 EPS included the special charge and Extraordinary Litigation-Related Expenses, which reduced EPS by $1.36 and $0.13, respectively. Third quarter Adjusted EPS of $3.84 compared to EPS of $2.60 in the prior year quarter, where Adjusted EPS was equal to EPS.
Steven H. Gunby, CEO and Chairman of FTI Consulting, commented, “Our record results this quarter reflect the strength and breadth of our global platform. Though our results included certain favorable non-recurring items, such as record success fees and a lower effective tax rate, at their core, they were driven by the fact that in a world facing major change and disruption, our clients are increasingly turning to us for the depth and breadth of our capabilities.”

Cash Position and Capital Allocation
Net cash provided by operating activities of $190.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to $201.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The year-over-year decrease in net cash provided by operating activities was primarily due to an increase in operating expense and compensation payments, which was partially offset by higher cash collections and a decrease in forgivable loan issuances.
During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company repurchased 523,588 shares of its common stock at an average price per share of $151.55 for a total cost of $79.4 million. As of September 30, 2026, approximately $264.7 million remained available for common stock repurchases under the Company’s stock repurchase program.
Cash and cash equivalents of $88.1 million at September 30, 2026 compared to $146.0 million at September 30, 2025 and $163.7 million at June 30, 2026. Total debt of $852.0 million at September 30, 2026 compared to $510.0 million at September 30, 2025 and $1.020 billion at June 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Segment Results
Corporate Finance
Revenues in the Corporate Finance segment increased $19.5 million, or 4.8%, to $424.4 million in the quarter compared to $404.9 million in the prior year quarter. The increase in revenues was primarily due to higher success fees and realized bill rates, which was partially offset by lower demand for turnaround & restructuring and transactions services. Segment operating income of $83.4 million compared to $93.0 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $102.8 million, or 24.2% of segment revenues, compared to $96.4 million, or 23.8% of segment revenues, in the prior year quarter. The increase in Adjusted Segment EBITDA was primarily due to higher revenues, which was partially offset by an increase in compensation, which includes the impact of a 6.3% increase in billable headcount.
Forensic and Litigation Consulting
Revenues in the Forensic and Litigation Consulting segment increased $0.4 million, or 0.2%, to $195.1 million in the quarter compared to $194.7 million in the prior year quarter. The increase in revenues was primarily due to higher realized bill rates, which was partially offset by lower demand for risk & investigations and data & analytics services. Segment operating income of $20.2 million compared to $40.5 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $38.0 million, or 19.5% of segment revenues, compared to $42.6 million, or 21.9% of segment revenues, in the prior year quarter. The decrease in Adjusted Segment EBITDA was primarily due to higher SG&A expenses.
Economic Consulting
Revenues in the Economic Consulting segment increased $16.2 million, or 9.3%, to $189.3 million in the quarter compared to $173.1 million in the prior year quarter. The increase in revenues was primarily due to higher demand for non-merger and acquisition (“M&A”)-related antitrust and M&A-related antitrust services, which was partially offset by lower demand for financial economics services. Segment operating income of $10.7 million compared to segment operating loss of $5.8 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $14.0 million, or 7.4% of segment revenues, compared to a loss of $4.6 million in the prior year quarter. The increase in Adjusted Segment EBITDA was primarily due to higher revenues and lower SG&A expenses.
Technology
Revenues in the Technology segment increased $9.1 million, or 9.7%, to $103.2 million in the quarter compared to $94.1 million in the prior year quarter. The increase in revenues was primarily due to higher demand for M&A-related “second request” services. Segment operating income of $8.8 million compared to $9.3 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $16.0 million, or 15.5% of segment revenues, compared to $13.6 million, or 14.5% of segment revenues, in the prior year quarter. The increase in Adjusted Segment EBITDA was primarily due to higher revenues, which was partially offset by an increase in compensation, which includes higher as-needed consultant costs, and higher SG&A expenses.
Strategic Communications
Revenues in the Strategic Communications segment increased $11.2 million, or 12.5%, to $100.6 million in the quarter compared to $89.4 million in the prior year quarter. The increase in revenues was primarily due to higher demand for public affairs and corporate reputation services. Segment operating income of $14.1 million compared to $15.9 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $20.4 million, or 20.3% of segment revenues, compared to $16.9 million, or 18.9% of segment revenues, in the prior year quarter. The increase in Adjusted Segment EBITDA was primarily due to higher revenues, which was partially offset by an increase in compensation, which includes the impact of a 5.5% increase in billable headcount, and higher SG&A expenses.
Fourth Quarter 2026 Special Charge and Expected Annualized Savings
The Company expects that actions taken in the third and fourth quarters of 2026 to align staffing with demand will result in annualized cost savings of approximately $80 million in salary- and benefits-related and real estate-related costs. In connection with these actions, the Company expects to record a fourth quarter 2026 special charge of approximately $7 million, which includes approximately $2 million in salary- and benefits-related costs associated with targeted headcount reductions and approximately $5 million in real estate-related costs. Together with the $46.0 million special charge recorded in the third quarter of 2026, the estimated combined special charge for full year 2026 is expected to be approximately $53 million.
2026 Guidance
The Company is reaffirming its full year 2026 revenue guidance range of between $3.940 billion and $4.100 billion. The Company now estimates EPS for full year 2026 will range between $7.55 and $8.05, which compares to the prior range of between $8.70 and $9.30. The Company estimates Adjusted EPS will range between $9.50 and $10.00, which compares to the prior range of between $9.10 and $9.70. The variance between EPS and Adjusted EPS guidance for full year 2026 is related to the third and fourth quarter of 2026 special charges, which the Company estimates will be approximately $1.55, and an estimated $0.40 of Extraordinary Litigation-Related Expenses.

We have included the definition of Segment Operating Income (Loss), which is a GAAP financial measure, below in order to more fully define the components of certain non-GAAP financial measures in the accompanying analysis of financial information. We define Segment Operating Income (Loss) as a segment’s share of consolidated operating income. We use Segment Operating Income (Loss) for the purpose of calculating Adjusted Segment EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure. We define Adjusted Segment EBITDA as Segment Operating Income (Loss) before depreciation, amortization of intangible assets, remeasurement of acquisition-related contingent consideration, special charges and goodwill impairment charges. We use Adjusted Segment EBITDA as a basis to internally evaluate the financial performance of our segments because we believe it reflects core operating performance and provides an indicator of the segment’s ability to generate cash.
We define Adjusted EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure, as consolidated net income before income tax provision, other non-operating income (expense), depreciation, amortization of intangible assets, remeasurement of acquisition-related contingent consideration, special charges, goodwill impairment charges, gain or loss on sale of a business, losses on early extinguishment of debt and Extraordinary Litigation-Related Expenses (as defined below). We define Adjusted EBITDA Margin, which is a non-GAAP financial measure, as Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of total revenues. We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures, when considered together with our GAAP financial results and GAAP financial measures, provide management and investors with a more complete understanding of our operating results, including underlying trends. Many of our competitors use alternative measures of operating performance. Non-GAAP financial measures are used by investors, financial analysts, rating agencies and others to value and compare the financial performance of companies in our industry. Therefore, we also believe that our non-GAAP financial measures, considered along with corresponding GAAP financial measures, provide management and investors with useful supplemental information.
We define Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Earnings per Diluted Share ("Adjusted EPS"), which are non-GAAP financial measures, as net income and EPS, respectively, excluding the impact of remeasurement of acquisition-related contingent consideration, special charges, goodwill impairment charges, the gain or loss on sale of a business, losses on early extinguishment of debt and Extraordinary Litigation-Related Expenses (as defined below). We use Adjusted Net Income for the purpose of calculating Adjusted EPS. Management uses Adjusted EPS to assess total Company operating performance on a consistent basis. We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures, when considered together with our GAAP financial results and GAAP financial
measures, provide management and investors with useful supplemental information on our business operating results, including underlying trends.
“Extraordinary Litigation-Related Expenses” represent expenses related to the Company’s litigation in the case captioned FTI Consulting, Inc. et al., v. Jonathan M. Orszag et al., 8:23-cv-03200-BAH-AAQ (D.Md.) (together with ancillary proceedings, “FTI vs. Orszag, et al”). In May 2026, the United States District Court for the District of Maryland (the “Court”) allowed the Company to file a third amended complaint to an existing proceeding against Jonathan Orszag, adding Econic Partners LLC, a competitor of the Company, and Dr. Mark Israel, a former Company employee, as defendants. The third amended complaint also added additional claims, including for theft of Company trade secrets and conspiracy to unlawfully compete. This litigation was originally filed in November 2023 against Mr. Orszag, a former Company employee, to enforce the terms of his employment agreement. As a result of the Court’s allowance of the third amended complaint, in the Company’s judgment, beginning in the second quarter of 2026, FTI vs. Orszag, et al became non-recurring and outside of the ordinary course of business based on the following considerations: (i) the magnitude of the proceedings, (ii) the complexity of the proceedings, (iii) the counterparties involved and (iv) the Company’s overall litigation strategy. No non-GAAP financial measures for prior periods presented have been adjusted for litigation expenses related to FTI vs. Orszag, et al because the proceedings did not become extraordinary until the second quarter of 2026.
