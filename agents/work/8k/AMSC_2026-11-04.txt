AMSC Reports Second Quarter Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Results and Business Outlook
Second Quarter Financial Highlights:
• Increased Revenue by More than 40% Year-Over-Year Exceeding $90 Million • Reported Total Orders Above $95 Million Driven by Energy Markets • More Than Quadrupled Operating Cash Flow to $33 Million
Company to host conference call tomorrow, November 5th, at 10:00 am ET
Ayer, MA – November 4, 2026 – AMSC (Nasdaq: AMSC), a leading provider of power control solutions that harmonize an increasingly complex energy system and enable customers to scale their operations without added complexity or size, today reported financial results for its second quarter ended September 30, 2026 of fiscal year 2026.
Revenues for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 were $94.0 million compared with $65.9 million for the same period of fiscal 2025. The year-over-year increase was driven by organic growth and the acquisition of Comtrafo.
AMSC reported net income for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 of $3.0 million, or $0.06 per share, compared to $4.8 million, or $0.11 per share, for the same period of fiscal 2025. The Company’s non-GAAP net income for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 was $9.1 million, or $0.20 per share, compared with a non-GAAP net income of $8.9 million, or $0.20 per share, in the same period of fiscal 2025. Please refer to the financial table below for a reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP results.
Cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash on September 30, 2026, totaled $181.9 million, compared with $147.6 million at March 31, 2026.
"We delivered over $90 million in second quarter revenue, up more than 40% year-over-year, reflecting continued customer-requested accelerations in project deliveries through the first half of our fiscal year. During the second quarter, our power solutions saw excellent traction with orders exceeding $95 million, driven primarily by traditional and renewable energy as well as demand from materials and utility customers. We also generated over $45 million in operating cash during the first half of the year. Our momentum along with our quarter-on-quarter gross margin expansion and significant operating cash flow provides a high degree of flexibility for our long-term strategic investments in growth,” said Daniel P. McGahn, Chairman, President, and CEO, AMSC. “Looking forward, we expect revenue to average approximately $90 million for each of our first three quarters. We anticipate strong demand to continue through the second half, ending the fiscal year on a high note and delivering significant year-over-year growth for the full fiscal year."

About AMSC (Nasdaq: AMSC)
Guided by a belief in the power of next, AMSC is a leading provider of power controls solutions that apply innovation and creativity to address today's challenges and enable a more resilient and sustainable energy future. Driven by the purpose "to power progress," the Company integrates future-facing technologies to balance the global demand for clean energy with reliable, efficient power delivery. AMSC delivers advanced grid systems and engineering services to optimize network reliability, provides ship protection and power management solutions to enhance fleet efficiency and safety, and supplies electronic controls and designs that reduce wind energy costs. Beyond these systems, the Company provides capabilities in industrial process and control alongside environmental and emission control to ensure operational efficiency across the entire energy infrastructure. The Company's solutions are optimizing power network, increasing the safety of navy fleets, and powering gigawatts of renewable energy globally. Founded in 1987, AMSC is headquarters near Boston, Massachusetts with operations in Asia, Australia, Brazil, Europe, and North America. For more information, please visit www.amsc.com.
