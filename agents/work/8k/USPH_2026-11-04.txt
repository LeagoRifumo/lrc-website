USPH Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results,
Record High Visits and Revenue Per Visit
Houston, TX, November 4, 2026 – U.S. Physical Therapy, Inc. (“USPH” or the “Company”) (NYSE, NYSE Texas: USPH), a national operator of outpatient physical therapy clinics and provider of industrial injury prevention services (“IIP”), today reported results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
•
Total net revenue was $223 million for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 (“Q3 2026”), a 13% increase over the third quarter ended September 30, 2025 (“Q3 2025”).
•
Net income attributable to USPH shareholders was $11 million for Q3 2026 compared to $13 million for Q3 2025. The Company reported loss per share of $1.04 in Q3 2026 compared to earnings per share of $0.48 in Q3 2025. Included in pretax income for Q3 2026 was a $0.1 million loss on change in fair value of contingent earn out consideration compared to a gain of $6 million in Q3 2025. Under GAAP, changes in the value of redeemable noncontrolling interests, representing our partners’ ownership stakes in subsidiaries not fully owned by USPH, are excluded from net income but are included in the calculation of earnings per share. Improving performance increases the value of these ownership interests, which has a dilutive effect on earnings per share.
•
Adjusted net income (1)(2) was $12 million for Q3 2026 compared to $10 million for Q3 2025. Adjusted earnings per share (1)(2) was $0.80 in Q3 2026 compared to $0.66 in Q3 2025.
•
Adjusted EBITDA (1) was $28 million for Q3 2026 compared to $24 million for Q3 2025.
•
The Company’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.46 to be payable on December 11, 2026, to shareholders of record on November 16, 2026, consistent with the Company’s longstanding practice of delivering value to shareholders.
•
Through the end of Q3 2026, the Company successfully integrated all 72 clinics under the two previously announced hospital affiliation agreements.

Chris Reading, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer commented, “We are pleased with the strong financial performance delivered in the third quarter 2026. The year-over-year EBITDA improvement reflects the initial benefit of the completed rollout of our first two hospital affiliations. The 72 clinics operating under hospital affiliation agreements generated $25 million in revenue in Q3, and we expect this number to increase significantly as referral volumes rise, additional clinics are incorporated into existing agreements, and our pipeline of hospital prospects results in new affiliations.”

Q3 2026 compared to Q3 2025
•
Physical therapy net revenue was $190 million for Q3 2026, a 13% increase compared to Q3 2025, including a 6% increase in mature revenue (1).
•
Patient visits (1) were 1.7 million for Q3 2026, an 8% increase compared to Q3 2025, with average daily visits per clinic (1) of 33 for Q3 2026 compared to 32 for Q3 2025.
•
Physical therapy net revenue per patient visit (1) was $111 for Q3 2026, a $5 increase compared to Q3 2025.
•
Physical therapy margin was 19% in each of Q3 2026 and Q3 2025. Adjusted physical therapy margin (2) was 19% in Q3 2026, up 150 basis points compared to Q3 2025. Q3 2026 results reflect higher company-provided health benefit costs compared to a favorable experience in Q3 2025, representing approximately 150 basis points of margin headwind.
•
IIP revenue was $33 million for Q3 2026, a 14% increase compared to Q3 2025. Excluding the IIP acquisition on January 31, 2026, IIP revenue increased 8%.
•
IIP margin was 22% for Q3 2026 compared to 20% for Q3 2025.
•
Corporate expense as a percentage of total revenue was 8% in Q3 2026 compared to 9% in Q3 2025. Adjusted corporate expense (2) as a percentage of total revenue was 8% in Q3 2026 compared to 9% in Q3 2025.
•
The Company added 21 and closed three locations during Q3 2026, bringing the location count (1) to 799 as of September 30, 2026.
Nine Months ended September 30, 2026, compared to Nine Months ended September 30, 2025
•
Total net revenue was $635 million for nine months ended September 30, 2026 (“YTD 2026”), a 10% increase over the nine months ended September 30, 2025 (“YTD 2025”).
•
Physical therapy net revenue was $540 million for YTD 2026, a 10% increase compared to YTD 2025, including a 4% increase in mature revenue (1).
•
Patient visits (1) were 4.9 million for the YTD 2026, a 7% increase compared to YTD 2025, with average daily visits per clinic (1) of 33 for YTD 2026 compared to 32 for the YTD 2025.
•
Physical therapy net revenue per patient visit (1) was $108 for YTD 2026, a $3 increase compared to YTD 2025.
•
Physical therapy margin and adjusted physical therapy margin (2) were 18% for YTD 2026 compared to 19% for YTD 2025, respectively. YTD 2026 results include the unfavorable impact of company-provided health benefit costs compared to a favorable impact in YTD 2025, impacting margins by approximately 100 basis points.
•
IIP revenue was $95 million for YTD 2026, a 12% increase compared to YTD 2025. Excluding the IIP acquisition made on January 31, 2026, IIP revenue increased 7%.
•
IIP margin was 21% for YTD 2026 compared to 20% for YTD 2025.
•
Corporate expense as a percentage of total revenue was 9% in each of YTD 2026 and YTD 2025. Adjusted corporate expense (2) as a percentage of total revenue was 8% for YTD 2026 compared to 9% for YTD 2025.
•
Net income attributable to USPH shareholders was $26 million for YTD 2026 compared to $35 million for YTD 2025 with loss per share of $0.90 compared to earnings per share of $1.85 for the same periods, respectively. Included in pretax income for Q3 2026 was a loss on change in fair value of contingent earn out consideration of $3 million versus a gain of $11 million in Q3 2025.
•
Adjusted net income (2)(3) was $30.2 million for YTD 2026 compared to $29.7 million for YTD 2025, with adjusted earnings per share (2)(3) of $2.01 compared to $1.96 for the same periods, respectively.
•
Adjusted EBITDA (2) was $75 million for YTD 2026 compared to $70 million for YTD 2025.

GLOSSARY OF TERMS
Mature revenue includes revenues from owned and hospital affiliated locations as well as homecare which were operational prior to January 1, 2025, and are still operating as of the balance sheet date. This metric excludes other management contracts.
Physical therapy revenue per patient visit is net revenue from owned and hospital affiliated clinics as well as homecare divided by total number of patient visits (defined below) during the periods presented. This metric excludes other management contracts.
Patient visits is the number of unique patient visits at the Company’s owned and hospital affiliated clinics as well as homecare for the periods presented. This metric excludes other management contracts.
Average daily visits per clinic is patient visits at the Company’s owned and hospital affiliated clinics, divided by the number of days in which normal business operations were conducted during the periods presented and further divided by the average number of owned and hospital affiliated clinics in operation during the periods presented. This metric excludes homecare and other management contracts.
Location count includes owned and hospital affiliated clinics as well as other management contracts. This metric excludes homecare.
ABOUT U.S. PHYSICAL THERAPY, INC.
Founded in 1990, U.S. Physical Therapy, Inc. owns and/or manages 799 outpatient physical therapy locations in 45 states. USPH locations provide preventative and post-operative care for a variety of orthopedic-related disorders and sports-related injuries, treatment for neurologically-related injuries and rehabilitation of injured workers. USPH also has an industrial injury prevention business which provides onsite services for clients’ employees including injury prevention and rehabilitation, performance optimization, post-offer employment testing, functional capacity evaluations, and ergonomic assessments.
More information about U.S. Physical Therapy, Inc. is available at www.usph.com. The information included on that website is not incorporated into this press release.

RECONCILIATION OF NON-GAAP MEASURES
TO THE MOST DIRECTLY COMPARABLE GAAP MEASURE
The following tables provide details of the basic and diluted earnings per share computation and reconcile net income attributable to USPH shareholders calculated in accordance with GAAP to non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA, non-GAAP adjusted net income and non-GAAP adjusted earnings per share. The tables also provide a reconciliation of additional non-GAAP measures to the most comparable GAAP measure. Management believes providing adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income and adjusted earnings per share to investors is useful for comparing the Company's period-to-period results as well as for comparing with other similar businesses since most do not have redeemable instruments and therefore have different equity structures. Management uses adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income and adjusted earnings per share, which eliminate certain items described above that can be subject to volatility and unusual costs, as the principal measures to evaluate and monitor financial performance period over period.
Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income attributable to USPH shareholders before interest income, interest expense, taxes, depreciation, amortization, change in fair value of contingent earn-out consideration, changes in revaluation of put-right liability, equity-based awards compensation expense, clinic closure costs, business acquisition related costs, costs related to a one-time financial and human resources systems upgrade, gain or loss on sale of a partnership, settlement of a payor dispute, other income and related portions for non-controlling interests, and other non-recurring items as applicable.
Non-GAAP adjusted net income equals net income attributable to USPH shareholders less changes in revaluation of a put-right liability, clinic closure costs, gain or loss on sale of a partnership, changes in fair value of contingent earn-out consideration, business acquisition related costs, costs related to a one-time financial and human resources systems upgrade, settlement of a payor dispute, any allocations to non-controlling interests, all net of taxes, and other non-recurring items as applicable. Non-GAAP adjusted earnings per share also excludes the impact of the revaluation of redeemable non-controlling interest and the associated tax impact.
Adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income and adjusted earnings per share are not measures of financial performance under GAAP. Adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income, adjusted earnings per share and other non-GAAP measures should not be considered in isolation or as an alternative to, or substitute for, net income attributable to USPH shareholders presented in the consolidated financial statements.
