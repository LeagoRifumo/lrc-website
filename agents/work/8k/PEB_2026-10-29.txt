“One of the most encouraging developments in the third quarter was the continued improvement in pricing power across our portfolio,” noted Mr. Bortz. “Healthy business and leisure transient demand supported an 8.5% increase in transient ADR, while group ADR also increased despite softer convention calendars in several markets. Importantly, demand remained strong throughout the third quarter, reaccelerating in September. Together, these trends reinforce the broad strength of underlying travel demand beyond the early-quarter major events.”
Pebblebrook’s hotel operators and asset management team continued to convert stronger revenues into greater profitability through disciplined expense control and comprehensive operating efficiency initiatives. Same-Property Total Revenues increased 6.9%, well above the high end of the Company’s outlook, while Same-Property Total Expenses rose just 4.0%, or 2.7% on a per-occupied-room basis. This drove a robust 15.1% increase in Same-Property Hotel EBITDA and 205 basis points of margin expansion. The improvement reflects stronger pricing from increased confidence and more compression days, better demand mix, less discounting, healthy on-property spending, and continued productivity gains across the portfolio.
Year-to-date, Same-Property RevPAR climbed 8.7% and Total Revenues increased 7.1%, compared with expense growth of just 4.4%, driving 14.7% growth in Same-Property Hotel EBITDA and approximately 190 basis points of margin expansion. These year-to-date results demonstrate that the sustained benefits of improving demand, stronger rate realization, and the Company’s strategic operating efficiency initiatives extend well beyond any single quarter.

Update on Strategic Dispositions
On October 8, 2026, Pebblebrook completed the sale of The Hotel Zags, a 174-room hotel in Portland, Oregon, for $10.0 million. The property generated negative EBITDA of $0.6 million for the trailing-twelve-months ending August 31, 2026. The sale reduces the Company’s exposure to Portland to just one hotel and provides additional liquidity for debt reduction and opportunistic common and preferred share repurchases.
In connection with The Hotel Zags sale and the Company’s ongoing evaluation of additional dispositions, $77.3 million of non-cash impairment charges were recorded in the third quarter.
Over the past year, the Company has completed nearly $170 million of property dispositions at an aggregate 17.4x EBITDA multiple and a 3.9% net operating income capitalization rate based on trailing-twelve-month performance prior to each respective sale and a capital reserve of 4.0% of total hotel revenues. These sales provide transaction-based support for the portfolio’s underlying private-market valuation and enable the Company to recycle capital into debt reduction and share repurchases at attractive prices. The valuation metrics do not include additional capital investments that buyers may undertake following acquisition.
The Company has also increased its estimated net asset value (“NAV”) per common share by $1.00, to a range of $23.50 to $27.50, reflecting improved property performance, recent transaction evidence, and the benefits of common and preferred share repurchases.
Capital Investments
During the third quarter, the Company invested $14.8 million in capital improvements across its portfolio. Year-to-date, capital investments across the portfolio totaled $39.3 million.
For the full year, the Company anticipates investing $60 to $70 million, a $5 million reduction at the midpoint from its prior outlook of $65 to $75 million, primarily reflecting project timing, with a portion of planned spending shifting into 2027. With the Company’s multi-year redevelopment program largely completed, capital investing has settled at a lower, more normalized level that supports higher discretionary free cash flow for debt reduction and opportunistic repurchases of common and preferred shares. The Company expects this lower level of capital investment to continue into 2027.
Balance Sheet and Share Repurchases
The Company’s deleveraging trend continued in the third quarter, driven by stronger operating results and lower net debt. Net debt-to-trailing 12-month corporate EBITDA declined to 5.1x as of September 30, 2026, from 5.9x at December 31, 2025.
As of September 30, 2026, cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash had grown to $298 million, with an additional $641 million of available capacity on the Company’s $650 million senior unsecured revolving credit facility and $90 million of available capacity on its senior unsecured 2031 term loan. Combined with the Company’s lower normalized capital investments, this enhanced balance sheet position increases financial flexibility for capital allocation decisions. The Company’s consolidated debt and convertible notes currently carry an estimated weighted-average interest rate of 4.2% and a weighted-average debt maturity of 2.5 years, with 89% of the debt effectively fixed and 98% unsecured.
Repayment of the Company’s $350 million of convertible notes maturing in December 2026 is expected to be fully funded by existing cash, expected free cash flow, and available term loan capacity. There are no other debt maturities until 2028.
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company repurchased 1.2 million common shares for $20.9 million, at an average share price of $18.12, bringing year-to-date 2026 repurchases to 2.1 million shares at an average price of $16.09 per share. Since October 2022, the Company has repurchased 20.6 million common shares, approximately 16% of outstanding shares, at an average price of $13.64, representing a 47% discount to the midpoint of the Company’s updated NAV range. The reduced share count is visible in per-share results: third-quarter Adjusted FFO per diluted share increased 23.5%, compared with 18.9% growth in Adjusted FFO.
The Company also repurchased 0.4 million preferred shares during the third quarter for $7.2 million at an attractive 23% average discount to liquidation preference, reducing total outstanding preferred equity securities to $707.8 million, bringing 2026 total repurchases through the third quarter up to 1.9 million preferred shares.

2026 Outlook
Third-quarter results reinforced Pebblebrook’s key 2026 earnings drivers: healthy premium business and leisure travel, strengthening pricing power, resilient guest spending beyond the room, well-controlled expense growth, and continued recovery across urban markets led by San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Boston. The sustained momentum through September, beyond early-quarter major events and the ongoing benefits of the Company’s strategic operating initiatives, further supports the portfolio’s earnings growth outlook.
The Company raised its 2026 Outlook to reflect both stronger-than-expected third-quarter results and improved expectations for the fourth quarter. Of the $16.3 million increase in the Adjusted EBITDAre midpoint, $13.4 million reflects third-quarter results above the prior third-quarter outlook midpoint, and $2.9 million reflects higher fourth-quarter expectations. The change in the full-year GAAP net loss outlook primarily reflects the third-quarter non-cash impairment charges, partially offset by the improved operating outlook.
Current demand and booking trends remain constructive, supported by resilient business and leisure travel activity, while the Company remains mindful of shorter booking windows and macroeconomic, policy, and geopolitical uncertainties. The updated Outlook reflects the sale of The Hotel Zags Portland and assumes no acquisitions and no additional dispositions.

Considerations Regarding Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release includes certain non-GAAP financial measures. Non-GAAP financial measures are measures of the Company’s historical or future financial performance that are different from measures calculated and presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. The Company reports FFO, Adjusted FFO, EBITDA, EBITDAre, Adjusted EBITDAre, and Hotel EBITDA, which are non-GAAP financial measures that it believes are useful to investors as key measures of its operating performance.Adjusted FFO is defined as FFO, as adjusted for transaction costs, non-cash ground rent on operating and finance lease liabilities, management/franchise contract transition costs, interest expense adjustment for acquired liabilities, finance lease adjustment, non-cash amortization of acquired intangibles, gain on insurance settlement, early extinguishment of debt, amortization of share-based compensation expense, issuance costs of redeemed preferred shares, hurricane-related costs, noncash interest expense, unrealized loss on investment, and deferred tax asset provision (benefit). The Company believes Adjusted FFO provides useful supplemental information regarding its ongoing operating performance. EBITDA is defined as earnings before interest, income taxes, depreciation and amortization. The Company calculates EBITDAre in accordance with standards established by Nareit. EBITDAre is defined as EBITDA as adjusted for gain on sale of hotel properties and impairment loss. Adjusted EBITDAre is defined as EBITDAre, as adjusted for transaction costs, non-cash ground rent on operating and finance lease liabilities, management/franchise contract transition costs, non-cash amortization of acquired intangibles, gain on insurance settlement, amortization of share-based compensation expense, unrealized loss on investment, and hurricane-related costs. Hotel EBITDA is defined as Adjusted EBITDAre plus corporate general and administrative expenses less interest income, business interruption insurance income, and other. The Company believes that EBITDA, EBITDAre, Adjusted EBITDAre, and Hotel EBITDA provide investors useful financial measures to evaluate its operating performance, excluding the impact of its capital structure (primarily interest expense) and asset base (primarily depreciation and amortization).The Company believes that Same-Property Hotel EBITDA provides investors an additional useful financial measure to evaluate hotel-level operating performance of hotels that were owned and fully operating at any time during the period presented. Same-Property Hotel EBITDA is calculated by excluding from Hotel EBITDA the Hotel EBITDA attributed to hotels that were neither owned nor fully operating (whether due to significant redevelopment or disruption) at any time during the period presented.FFO, Adjusted FFO, EBITDA, EBITDAre, Adjusted EBITDAre, and Hotel EBITDA do not represent cash generated from operating activities as determined by U.S. GAAP and should not be considered as alternatives to U.S. GAAP net income (loss), as indications of the Company’s financial performance, or to U.S. GAAP cash flow from operating activities, as measures of liquidity. In addition, FFO, Adjusted FFO, EBITDA, EBITDAre, Adjusted EBITDAre, and Hotel EBITDA are not indicative of funds available to fund cash needs, including the ability to make cash distributions.The Company presents weighted-average number of basic and fully diluted common shares and units by excluding the dilutive effect of shares issuable upon conversion of convertible debt.
