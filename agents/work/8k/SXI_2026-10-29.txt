STANDEX REPORTS FISCAL FIRST QUARTER 2027 FINANCIAL RESULTS
● | Record Sales of $232.6 Million, Up 12.4% YOY Organically; Electronics Up 29.5% YOY Organically

Commenting on the quarter’s results, President and Chief Executive Officer David Dunbar said, “We are off to a strong start to fiscal 2027. Our strategy with multiple independent growth drivers is accelerating growth and expanding margins. We delivered record sales of $232.6 million, driven by 12.4% organic growth. Strong end market demand driven by electrification, data center and defense needs, new products from our ongoing growth investments, and a recovery in general industrial market contributed to this growth. Our Electronics segment led the way with approximately 30% organic growth and a book-to-bill of 1.23. Sales from fast growth markets were approximately $78 million this quarter, and we are now on track to exceed $315 million in fiscal year 2027. Adjusted operating margin rose 110 basis points year over year to 20.2% with margin expansion across each of our segments. We ended the quarter with net leverage of 2.0x, providing flexibility to continue investing for future growth.
1
Considering first quarter results and our expanding backlog, we are raising our fiscal year 2027 outlook based on multiple growth engines.”
Fiscal Second Quarter 2027 Outlook
In fiscal second quarter 2027, on a year-on-year basis, the Company expects moderately higher revenue and adjusted operating margin.
On a sequential basis, the Company expects slightly higher revenue and similar adjusted operating margin.
Fiscal Year 2027 Outlook
For fiscal year 2027, the Company is raising its outlook to high-single digits total sales growth, from mid-to-high single digits previously. The Company now anticipates organic sales to increase by more than $100 million in fiscal year 2027. The Company expects higher sales and adjusted operating margin expansion to drive high teens growth in adjusted earnings per share.
The Company is on track to release more than 20 new products, which are expected to contribute more than 300 bps of incremental growth. Sales from fast growth markets are now expected to grow greater than 20% year-on-year to greater than $315 million.

Capital Allocation
● | Interest: In fiscal second quarter 2027, the Company expects interest expense between $7.0 million and $7.5 million.

● | Share Repurchase: The Company did not repurchase shares under its share repurchase program during the fiscal first quarter of 2027. On October 27, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors authorized the repurchase of up to $100 million of the Company’s common stock, replacing and superseding the approximately $23.5 million that remained available under the prior authorization.

● | Capital Expenditures: In fiscal first quarter 2027, the Company’s capital expenditures were $6.6 million compared to $6.4 million in the fiscal first quarter of 2026. The Company expects fiscal year 2027 capital expenditures between $45 million and $55 million. The increase over fiscal year 2026 is primarily due to capacity expansion within Standex Electronics Grid. Capital expenditures were $25.2 million in fiscal year 2026.

● | Dividend: On October 22, 2026, the Company declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.36 per share, an approximately 5.9% year-on-year increase. The dividend is payable November 20, 2026, to shareholders of record on November 6, 2026.

Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Highlights
● | Net Debt: Standex had net (cash) debt of $401.2 million on September 30, 2026, compared to $446.0 million at the end of fiscal first quarter 2026. Net (cash) debt for the first quarter of 2027 consisted primarily of long-term debt of $518.1 million and cash and equivalents of $116.8 million.

● | Cash Flow: Net cash provided by continuing operating activities for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $15.7 million compared to $16.8 million in the prior year’s quarter. Free cash flow after capital expenditures was $9.1 million compared to free cash flow after capital expenditures of $10.4 million in the fiscal first quarter of 2026.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to the financial measures prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), the Company uses certain non-GAAP financial measures, including non-GAAP adjusted income from operations, non-GAAP adjusted net income from continuing operations, free operating cash flow, EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA to net debt, and adjusted earnings per share. The attached financial tables reconcile non-GAAP measures used in this press release to the most directly comparable GAAP measures. The Company believes that the use of non-GAAP measures which exclude the impact of restructuring charges, purchase accounting, amortization from acquired intangible assets, insurance recoveries, discrete tax events, gain or loss on sale of a business unit, acquisition costs, and litigation costs help investors to obtain a better understanding of our operating results and prospects, consistent with how management measures and forecasts the Company's performance, especially when comparing such results to previous periods. An understanding of the impact in a particular quarter of specific restructuring costs, acquisition expenses, or other gains and losses, on net income (absolute as well as on a per-share basis), operating income or EBITDA can give management and investors additional insight into core financial performance, especially when compared to quarters in which such items had a greater or lesser effect, or no effect. Non-GAAP measures should be considered in addition to, and not as a replacement for, the corresponding GAAP measures, and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies.
