STMicroelectronics Announces Updates to its Organization and
Leadership Appointments
Geneva, October 29, 2026 – STMicroelectronics (NYSE: STM), a global
semiconductor leader serving customers across the spectrum of electronics
applications, announced today a new organization to further strengthen its
positioning for the next phase of growth and innovation.
Remi El-Ouazzane is confirmed President, member of the Executive Committee,
Head of Microcontrollers, Digital ICs, RF and Optical Communication Products. In
addition to his current responsibilities, he will also take on leadership of the Imaging
Sensors Sub-Group and will be appointed ST Chief Technology Officer (CTO).
In his new CTO role El-Ouazzane will take on leadership of R&D for digital CMOS
and derivative technologies such as RF mixed-signal, embedded flash, and imaging,
advanced packaging and test and characterization, as well as Global System
Research & Applications and System Engineering. This evolution is intended to
enhance coordination across product development, research, and technology
activities across the Company to ultimately boost innovation and time-to-market.
Marco Cassis is confirmed President, member of the Executive Committee, head of
Analog, Power & Discrete and MEMS Products. In addition to his current
responsibilities, he will take on leadership of R&D for Analog, Power & Discrete and
MEMS technologies. This evolution is intended to accelerate the profitable growth of
this Product Group.
Cassis’ responsibilities as head of ST strategy remain unchanged and he will be
appointed ST Chief Strategic Officer (CSO). In his new CSO role he will align ST’s
vision and business objectives with evolving market dynamics, identifying growth,
partnership and bolt-on M&A opportunities while anticipating the competitive
landscape.
Jean-Marc Chery, ST President & CEO, said: “The CTO and CSO responsibilities are
strongly interdependent, and in their respective roles Remi and Marco, together with
ST’s Managing Board and Executive Committee, will ensure stronger coordination
between the Company’s strategy and its advanced technology and product
roadmaps, to support ST’s long-term direction and growth opportunities”.
The remainder of the organization is unchanged.
The new organization will take effect on January 1, 2027, with implementation to be
completed by the end of March 2027.
The Company will adjust its reportable segments from January 1, 2027.
About STMicroelectronics
At ST, we are 49,000 creators and makers of semiconductor technologies mastering
the semiconductor supply chain with state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities. An
integrated device manufacturer, we work with more than 200,000 customers and
thousands of partners to design and build products, solutions, and ecosystems that
address their challenges and opportunities, and the need to support a more
sustainable world. Our technologies enable smarter mobility, more efficient power
and energy management, and the wide-scale deployment of cloud-connected
autonomous things. We are on track to be carbon neutral in all direct and indirect
emissions (scopes 1 and 2), product transportation, business travel, and employee
commuting emissions (our scope 3 focus), and to achieve our 100% renewable
electricity sourcing goal by the end of 2027. Further information can be found at
www.st.com
