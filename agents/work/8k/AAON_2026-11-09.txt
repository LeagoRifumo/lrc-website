TULSA, Okla., November 9, 2026 - AAON, INC. (NASDAQ-AAON) ("The Aaon Group" or the "Company"), a leader in high-performance HVAC and thermal management solutions, today announced its results for the third quarter of 2026 and raised its full-year outlook.

Third Quarter 2026 Results
Net sales for the third quarter of 2026 increased 84.1% to a record $707.4 million, compared with $384.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. Growth was driven by strong demand for both the AAON and BASX brands, increased manufacturing throughput, and greater utilization of recently expanded production capacity.
AAON-branded sales increased to a record level, growing 19.6% to $310.2 million, benefitting from a healthy backlog and continued production throughput gains. BASX-branded sales grew 218.3% to a record $397.2 million, reflecting strong momentum in data center demand, continued production output gains, and increased utilization of recently added manufacturing capacity. Booking activity remained solid across both brands, contributing to market share gains and elevated backlog levels. AAON‑branded backlog increased 18.0% from the prior-year period while BASX-branded products ended the quarter with backlog up 51.1%.
Gross profit increased 76.4% to $188.5 million, compared with $106.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. Gross profit margin was 26.7%, compared with 27.8% in the prior-year period, but increased 60 basis points sequentially, marking the second consecutive quarter of improvement. Year over year, margin was impacted primarily by a higher proportion of BASX revenue generated from less mature, lower-utilization facilities, partially offset by improved price/cost and volume leverage. The sequential improvement reflects progress in mitigating these headwinds, and through a combination of pricing, sourcing, productivity, and other operational initiatives, margins continue to trend positively.
Selling, general and administrative expenses as a percent of sales declined 400 basis points to 12.5%, reflecting continued operating leverage and disciplined cost management as revenue growth outpaced overhead investments.
Operating income increased 129.3% to $100.0 million, compared with $43.6 million in the prior-year period. Diluted earnings per share were $0.83, an increase of 124.3% from $0.37 in the third quarter of 2025.

Restatement of Previously Issued Quarterly Financial Statements & Material Weakness
The Company has restated its previously issued financial statements for the first and second quarters of 2026. In aggregate, the restatements resulted in increases to first-half 2026 net sales, gross margin, and GAAP diluted EPS of $10.7 million, 71 basis points, and $0.09, respectively.
Management concluded that restatements to previously issued interim financial statements were appropriate and that a material weakness existed in internal control over financial reporting. The Company has implemented corrective actions and is executing additional measures designed to further strengthen our control environment and internal controls over financial reporting. The Company found no evidence of fraud or misconduct in connection with these matters and believes the identified errors do not alter the underlying strength of the business or its long-term growth outlook.
The impact of the restated first and second quarter of 2026 financial statements to correct for the identified errors can be seen at the end of this release or in Footnote 2 of the restated 10-Q filings for the first and second quarters of 2026, being filed today. Additional information regarding the material weakness can be found in Item 4 of the third quarter 10-Q filing, also being filed today.

EBITDA
EBITDA (as defined below) is presented herein and reconciled from the GAAP measure of net income because of its wide acceptance by the investment community as a financial indicator of a company's ability to internally fund operations. The Company defines EBITDA as net income, plus (1) depreciation and amortization, (2) interest expense (income), net and (3) income tax expense. EBITDA is not a measure of net income or cash flows as determined by GAAP. EBITDA margin is defined as EBITDA as a percentage of net sales.
The Company’s EBITDA measure provides additional information which may be used to better understand the Company’s operations. EBITDA is one of several metrics that the Company uses as a supplemental financial measurement in the evaluation of its business and should not be considered as an alternative to, or more meaningful than, net income, as an indicator of operating performance. Certain items excluded from EBITDA are significant components in understanding and assessing a Company's financial performance. EBITDA, as used by the Company, may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies. The Company believes that EBITDA is a widely followed measure of operating performance and is one of many metrics used by the Company’s management team and by other users of the Company’s consolidated financial statements.
