November 9, 2026 Life360 Reports Q3 2026 Results Record third quarter global net Paying Circle additions of 188 thousand, reaching 3.4 million Monthly Active User additions of 5.3 million, reaching 107.7 million Total revenue grew 40% year-over-year to $174.1 million SAN FRANCISCO, California. Life360, Inc. (Life360 or the Company) (NASDAQ: LIF, ASX: 360), the provider of the market-leading family connection and safety mobile application, today announced unaudited financial results for the third quarter (Q3'26) ended September 30, 2026. Life360 delivered strong quarterly results, with its highest-ever net Paying Circle additions for a third quarter, and international Monthly Active Users (MAU) crossing over the 50 million mark. “Life360 continues to demonstrate its invaluable place in everyday family life and translate that into meaningful value for our shareholders. Our impressive third quarter operating and financial results are a direct reflection of the trust families place in us to keep them connected and safe,” said Life360 Chief Executive Officer Lauren Antonoff. “We saw healthy MAU growth in Q3, with 5.3 million net new active members bringing us to 107.7 million MAU globally, and we added a third-quarter record of 188,000 net new Paying Circles. Our subscription engine continues to get even stronger, and early results from our U.S. price changes are encouraging. In advertising, we’re building the foundation for a much larger business, with growing proof points from leading brands providing positive signals for 2027. Meanwhile, as consumers adopt personal AI assistants, we’re making Life360’s family intelligence available to third-party AI agents to simplify everyday coordination through the tools families already use. Members decide whether to share their Circle’s information with an agent. We believe AI agents are a meaningful opportunity to increase the value we deliver.” "As foreshadowed in our Q2’26 results, Q3’26 delivered revenue growth acceleration, along with moderating operating expense growth following a period of investment in the first half," said Chief Financial Officer Russell Burke. "Quarterly revenue grew 40% year-over-year to $174.1 million. Looking ahead to Q4’26, we expect continued revenue momentum underpinned by recent subscription price increases and the seasonal uplift in advertising, combined with cost efficiency and seasonally lower growth media spend. We expect strengthening operating leverage as we continue to balance growth investment with our commitment to margin expansion. "We ended Q3’26 with $465.0 million in cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and short-term investments, and we generated operating cash flows of $27.2 million, up 3% year-over-year. Following the announcement of our share repurchase program in May 2026, we have repurchased shares of common stock for an aggregate purchase consideration of $44.4 million, leaving approximately $180.6 million available for future repurchases under the program. This is in line with our commitment to return value to our shareholders and reflects the same disciplined approach we outlined when the Board authorized the program. We plan to offset dilution measured by stock-based compensation expense on a rolling 12 month basis from the launch of the program. We will remain opportunistic and retain the flexibility to adjust the pace and timing to market conditions.” Q3'26 Financial Highlights • Total Q3'26 revenue of $174.1 million, a YoY increase of 40%, with total subscription revenue of $124.2 million, up 29% YoY and core subscription revenue1 of $119.9 million, up 32% YoY. • Advertising revenue of $25.4 million, up 250% YoY. • Annualized Monthly Revenue (AMR) of $578.0 million, up 29% YoY. • Net income of $22.5 million, up 130% YoY including a $17.5 million net gain on the fair value of investments. • Adjusted EBITDA2 of $34.6 million, an increase of 41% from $24.5 million in Q3'25. • Positive Operating Cash Flow of $27.2 million, up 3% YoY. • Quarter-end cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash and short-term investments of $465.0 million, an increase of $7.8 million from Q3'25, after investing in the Nativo acquisition and returning capital through share repurchases. Adjusted EBITDA To supplement our consolidated financial statements prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP, we use certain non-GAAP financial measures, as described below, to facilitate analysis of our financial and business trends and for internal planning and forecasting purposes. For more information, see the “Supplementary and Non-GAAP Financial Information” section below. Non-GAAP financial measures include adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (Adjusted EBITDA) and Adjusted EBITDA Margin. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income, excluding (i) gain on change in fair value of investments, (ii) provision for (benefit from) income taxes, (iii) depreciation and amortization, (iv) interest income, (v) other expense, net, (vi) acquisition-related transaction and integration costs, (vii) stock-based compensation, (viii) channel restructuring costs, (ix) workplace restructuring costs, and (x) warehouse relocation costs. These items are excluded from Adjusted EBITDA because they are non-cash in nature, because the amount and timing of these items are unpredictable, or because they are not driven by core results of operations and render comparisons with prior periods and competitors less meaningful.
