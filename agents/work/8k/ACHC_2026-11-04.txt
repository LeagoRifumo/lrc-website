ACADIA HEALTHCARE ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS AND RAISES FULL YEAR 2026 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE
FRANKLIN, Tenn. (November 4, 2026) – Acadia Healthcare Company, Inc. (“Acadia” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: ACHC) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Results
● | Revenue totaled $895.3 million, an increase of 5.1% compared with the third quarter of 2025

● | Same-facility revenue increased 5.2% compared with the third quarter of 2025, as patient days increased 1.1% and revenue per patient day increased 4.0%

● | Net income attributable to Acadia totaled $34.5 million, or $0.38 per diluted share, compared with $36.2 million, or $0.40 per diluted share, in the prior-year period

● | Adjusted net income attributable to Acadia totaled $54.6 million, or $0.60 per diluted share, compared with $64.8 million, or $0.72 per diluted share, in the prior-year period

● | Adjusted EBITDA was $176.4 million, compared with $173.0 million in the prior-year period

● | Operating cash flows were $97.9 million and capital expenditures were $50.7 million

● | Added 220 licensed beds during the third quarter, including 144 beds at a new facility and 76 beds at existing facilities

Adjusted net income attributable to Acadia, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted earnings per diluted share are non-GAAP financial measures. A reconciliation of all non-GAAP financial measures in this press release begins on page 9.
“Acadia delivered strong results in the third quarter, and our positive momentum is driving our increased full-year guidance. We remain focused on our key priorities, including expanding access to quality patient care and consistent operational execution with financial discipline," said Debbie Osteen, Chief Executive Officer of Acadia. "We've expanded our footprint by opening 16 new acute facilities over the last four years, and based on the progress we've made so far this year, we now expect to deliver approximately $60 million of incremental EBITDA from these facilities in 2027 relative to 2026. Overall, we are confident that we have the capabilities in place to continue to drive Acadia forward and deliver additional value for our patients, our partners, and our investors.”

Discussion of Third Quarter Results
Total revenue in the third quarter was $895.3 million, which was an increase of 5.1% compared with the prior-year period. Facilities closed over the last twelve months represented a negative 1.3% impact to reported revenue growth in the quarter.
Same-facility revenue increased 5.2% compared with the prior-year period. Same-facility patient days increased 1.1%, and same-facility revenue per patient day increased 4.0%.
Acute inpatient psychiatric facility (“Acute”) revenue was $524.8 million, which was an increase of 11.3% compared with the prior-year period. Third quarter Acute inpatient volumes increased 7.5% compared with the prior-year period, driven primarily by expanded capacity from both newly constructed and existing facilities.
Specialty treatment facility (“Specialty”) revenue was $136.0 million, a decrease of 8.2% compared with the prior-year period. The revenue decline was related to Specialty facilities in Pennsylvania and the impact from having closed several Specialty facilities over the last 12 months.
Comprehensive treatment center (“CTC”) revenue was $141.1 million, which was a decrease of 2.3% compared with the prior-year period. Residential treatment center (“RTC”) revenue was $93.4 million, an increase of 6.7% compared with the prior-year period.
Total operating expenses were $728.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 6.4% compared with the prior-year period.
Salaries, wages and benefits were $490.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 6.1% compared with the prior-year period. The increase is primarily due to new facility openings, as well as routine annual wage increases. Same-facility salaries, wages and benefits increased by 4.3%.
Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter was $176.4 million, compared with $173.0 million in the prior-year period. Third quarter 2026 Adjusted EBITDA includes a favorable pre-tax benefit related to an accrual for expected payments from the Florida supplemental payment program year ended September 30, 2026, which was $13.4 million compared to $5.6 million included in the Company's previous guidance.
Development Activity
The Company added 220 licensed beds during the third quarter, including a 144-bed de novo facility near Jacksonville, Florida and 76 beds added to existing facilities. Additionally, the Company opened one new CTC location and closed one existing CTC location during the quarter.
Cash and Liquidity
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $163.3 million in cash and cash equivalents and $710.1 million available under its $1.0 billion revolving credit facility. As of September 30, 2026, Acadia’s net leverage ratio was 3.9x Adjusted EBITDA, calculated in accordance with its Credit Agreement as disclosed in the Company’s latest periodic reports and other filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”).

About Acadia
Acadia Healthcare is the largest stand-alone behavioral healthcare company in the United States, providing mental health and addiction treatment services across a comprehensive continuum of care that includes inpatient psychiatric hospitals, specialty treatment facilities, RTCs, and outpatient clinics. Acadia operates 280 behavioral health facilities across 40 states and Puerto Rico and serves more than 85,000 patients daily. Guided by its mission, Acadia provides paths to healing through evidence-based treatment and compassionate care that inspires hope, improves lives and elevates communities.

We have included certain financial measures in this press release, including those listed below, which are “non-GAAP financial measures” as defined under the rules and regulations promulgated by the SEC. These non-GAAP financial measures include, and are defined, as follows:
• EBITDA: net income attributable to Acadia Healthcare Company, Inc. adjusted for net income attributable to noncontrolling interests, provision for income taxes, net interest expense and depreciation and amortization.
• Adjusted EBITDA: EBITDA adjusted for equity-based compensation expense, transaction, legal and other costs, debt extinguishment costs, legal settlements expense, loss on impairment and gain on sale of property, net.
• Adjusted income before income taxes attributable to Acadia Healthcare Company, Inc.: net income attributable to Acadia Healthcare Company, Inc. adjusted for transaction, legal and other costs, debt extinguishment costs, legal settlements expense, loss on impairment, gain on sale of property, net, and provision for income taxes.
• Adjusted income attributable to Acadia Healthcare Company, Inc.: Adjusted income before income taxes attributable to Acadia Healthcare Company, Inc. adjusted for the income tax effect of adjustments to income.
• Total facility adjusted EBITDA: Adjusted EBITDA adjusted for general and administrative costs related to our corporate functions. General and administrative costs directly related to the facilities are included in total facility results.
• Same facility adjusted EBITDA: Adjusted EBITDA for facilities and services to those facilities operated in both the current and prior year. These metrics exclude the operating results associated with facilities under operation for less than one year and facilities acquired, divested or removed from service during the current or prior year.

Total facility results include operating results for all of our facilities and services but exclude general and administrative costs related to our corporate functions. Such costs related to our corporate functions include, amongst others, costs for accounting and finance, information systems, human resources, legal and operational and executive leadership. General and administrative costs directly related to the facilities are included in facility results. Such costs directly related to our facilities include, amongst others, labor at the facility level, insurance, including property, professional, legal and general liability insurance, hospital supplies, including medication, utilities and food service, and general maintenance costs for the facility. We determine which general and administrative costs to exclude and include in total facility results by ensuring those costs directly associated with facility operations are captured at the facility level for reporting. Note that total facility costs include those related to new facilities and the cost of closure and run-out costs related to facilities we have closed. We believe that providing results on a total facility basis is helpful to our investors as a measure of our financial and operating performance because it neutralizes the impact of corporate-level items that do not arise out of our core operations at our facilities.
Same facility results include operating results only for facilities and services operated in both the current and prior year. These metrics exclude the operating results associated with facilities under operation for less than one year and facilities acquired during the current or prior year, as well as facilities divested or removed from service. We believe that providing results on a same facility basis is helpful to investors because it neutralizes the impact of new facilities that are in early stages of operation and facilities that we no longer operate, each of which may distort investors’ understanding of the Company’s underlying performance at our existing and continuing facilities. Further, we believe that providing same facility information is helpful to our investors as a measure of the financial and operating performance of our existing and continuing facilities on a comparable basis, and same facility results provide investors with information useful in understanding underlying organic growth in such facilities. For these reasons, we believe that same facility results are particularly useful during periods of significant expansion or contraction.
Total facility results reflect adjustments that are intended to provide the specific presentation described above, and same facility results reflect adjustments that may be irregular in timing from period to period related to newly opened or acquired facilities or facilities that we no longer operate, and may omit certain results that investors may view as important. Total facility and same facility results may therefore not be indicative of the overall performance of our business and should be not be considered as alternatives for net income or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP.
