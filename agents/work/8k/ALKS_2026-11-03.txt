Alkermes plc Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
— Third Quarter Revenues of $480.1 Million —
— GAAP Net Income of $16.3 Million and Adjusted EBITDA of $120.1 Million —
— Company Reiterates 2026 Financial Expectations —
DUBLIN, Nov. 3, 2026 — Alkermes plc (Nasdaq: ALKS) today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
“We delivered solid commercial and financial performance in the third quarter while continuing to advance our portfolio of orexin 2 receptor agonists in central disorders of hypersomnolence and other neuropsychiatric conditions,” said Blair Jackson, President and Chief Executive Officer of Alkermes. “The rapid expansion of our orexin development programs, highlighted by our recent clinical proof-of-concept data in ADHD and initiation of a phase 2 study in major depressive disorder, further strengthens our leadership position in orexin-based research. As we continue to advance our orexin development portfolio, we are growing the body of evidence supporting the potential of orexin biology across multiple neuropsychiatric disorders and expect several important clinical data readouts over the year ahead. With a portfolio of development candidates in one of the most exciting new therapeutic categories in neuroscience and a steady cadence of clinical data readouts ahead, we believe we are well positioned to drive long-term value for shareholders.”

About Alkermes plc
Alkermes plc (Nasdaq: ALKS), a mid-cap growth and value equity, is a global biopharmaceutical company that seeks to develop innovative medicines in the field of neuroscience. The company has a portfolio of proprietary commercial products for the treatment of alcohol dependence, opioid dependence, schizophrenia, bipolar I disorder and narcolepsy. Alkermes has established a leadership position in orexin biology and is advancing a portfolio of investigational orexin 2 receptor agonists for narcolepsy, idiopathic hypersomnia and other psychiatric and neurological disorders, including attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), major depressive disorder (MDD) and fatigue associated with multiple sclerosis and Parkinson’s disease. Headquartered in Ireland, Alkermes also has a corporate office and research and development center in Massachusetts and a manufacturing facility in Ohio. For more information, please visit Alkermes’ website at www.alkermes.com.
Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release includes information about certain financial measures that are not prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the U.S. (GAAP), including EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA. These non-GAAP measures are not based on any standardized methodology prescribed by GAAP and are not necessarily comparable to similar measures presented by other companies.
EBITDA represents earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA excludes share-based compensation expense and non-recurring gains or losses in addition to the components of EBITDA from earnings.
The company’s management and board of directors utilize these non-GAAP financial measures to evaluate the company’s performance. The company provides these non-GAAP financial measures of the company’s performance to investors because management believes that these non-GAAP financial measures, when viewed with the company’s results under GAAP and the accompanying reconciliations, are useful in identifying underlying trends in ongoing operations. However, EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are not measures of financial performance under GAAP and, accordingly, should not be considered as alternatives to GAAP measures as indicators of operating performance. Further, EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered measures of the company’s liquidity.
A reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP financial measures has been provided in the tables included in this press release.
