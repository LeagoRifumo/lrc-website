GeneDx Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $123.1 million, including $110.4 million in exome and genome revenue
•Reported exome and genome volume growth of 25% year-over-year with accession growth of 28% year-over-year
•Expanded adjusted gross margin(1) to 72% and adjusted net income(1) to $8.6 million
•Reiterating full year 2026 revenue guidance of $475 to $490 million
•Hosting conference call today at 4:30 p.m. ET
GAITHERSBURG, Md., November 9, 2026 — GeneDx Holdings Corp. (Nasdaq: WGS), the leader in rare disease diagnosis and improving health through the power of genomic data, today reported its financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
“Our Q3 results reflect profitable execution in a growing market, as we translate the rising demand for exome and genome testing into durable revenue growth,” said Katherine Stueland, CEO of GeneDx. “Our priorities are clear: improve the economics of our testing, expand utilization, and deliver leading products at scale. By uniting these priorities with our clinical expertise and differentiated exome and genome capabilities, we are bringing life-changing answers to more families.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results (Unaudited)(1)
Revenue
•Revenue grew to $123.1 million, an increase of 5% year-over-year.
•Exome and genome test revenue grew to $110.4 million, an increase of 12% year-over-year.
Exome and genome volume
•Exome and genome test result volume grew to 32,134, an increase of 25% year-over-year, with accession growth of 28% year-over-year.
Gross margin
•Adjusted gross margin of 72% in the third quarter of 2026 is up from 70% in the second quarter of 2026 and compares to 74% in the third quarter of 2025.
◦GAAP gross margin was 70%.
Operating expenses
•Adjusted total operating expenses were $79.4 million, representing 65% of revenue.
◦Total GAAP operating expenses were $96.5 million.
Net income
•Adjusted net income was $8.6 million, up $8.2 million from the second quarter of 2026 and compared to adjusted net income of $16.1 million in the third quarter of 2025.
◦GAAP net loss was $12.3 million.
Cash position
•Cash, cash equivalents, marketable securities and restricted cash was $171.1 million as of September 30, 2026.
(1)Adjusted gross margin, adjusted total operating expenses and adjusted net income are non-GAAP financial measures. See appendix for a reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP figures presented.

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Business Highlights
Strategic Expansion and Market Leadership
•Announced genome testing portfolio enhancement with long-read whole genome sequencing, reflecting GeneDx's continued focus on genome innovation to expand diagnostic capabilities and deliver more answers to more patients.
•Launched additional customer experience features, including simplified test reports, easy ordering, and report guides to simplify genomic testing from ordering through understanding and communicating results.
•Launched a new online offering designed to expand accessibility of exome testing for pediatric patients with global developmental delay (GDD), intellectual disability (ID) or epilepsy by enabling families to initiate genetic testing online and connect with licensed healthcare providers who can review medical information, order testing, and deliver results without requiring traditional in-person visits.
•Collaborated with Mirum Pharmaceuticals to launch a sponsored testing program, providing no-cost exome testing for eligible patients with unexplained cholestasis, early onset cataracts, or suspected or confirmed peroxisome biogenesis disorder–Zellweger spectrum disorder (PBD-ZSD).
•Partnered with Beren Therapeutics P.B.C. to launch the NPC GenomeComplete Sponsored Testing Program, providing no-cost comprehensive genome sequencing for eligible pediatric patients with clinical findings, family history, or biomarker evidence suggestive of Niemann-Pick Disease Type C (NPC).
Expanded Coverage for Exome and Genome
•Expanded the number of state Medicaid programs covering whole genome sequencing to 25 with the addition of Arkansas and Utah Medicaid.
•Appointed Nicole Maassen, J.D. as Chief Patient Access Officer to lead Market Access and Government Affairs with a focus on advancing payer partnerships, building upon government relations efforts, and translating positive coverage policies into timely access for children who need testing.
Innovation and Clinical Leadership
•Published new data in collaboration with the clinical genetics team at Seattle Children’s demonstrating that implementing first-tier rapid genome sequencing (rGS) broadly across inpatient pediatric care units improves diagnostic rates, patient outcomes, and operational efficiency within health systems.
•Presented 27 scientific abstracts at the American Society of Human Genetics (ASHG) 2026 Annual Meeting, highlighting how exome and genome sequencing are advancing scientific discovery, earlier diagnosis and more precise care. GeneDx research highlighted:
◦Scaling genomic interpretation with AI and machine learning: A GeneDx-led platform presentation explores machine learning to prioritize relevant copy number variants (CNVs), reflecting the company’s human-first, human-last approach to leveraging AI decision support tools in exome and genome sequencing analysis.
◦Advancing exome and genome sequencing as standard of care: Research from more than 100,000 individuals with autism examines factors influencing molecular diagnostic yield, adding evidence to support broad genomic testing for complex neurodevelopmental conditions.
◦Translating clinical data into actionable insights: Studies explore genotype-first reanalysis, emerging genotype-phenotype associations, rare variant detection and the health-economic value of first-line exome and genome sequencing.
◦Accelerating gene discovery: Nine abstracts using GeneDx Infinity™, GeneMatcher and global research collaborations to identify novel disease-associated genes and expand understanding of developmental and neurodevelopmental disorders.
•Demonstrated progress toward making genomic newborn screening a scalable and accessible part of proactive healthcare at the International Consortium on Newborn Sequencing (ICoNS) conference, including new findings from the GUARDIAN study, which has enrolled 30,000 newborns with a 72.3% parental participation rate, demonstrating the feasibility and clinical value of genomic newborn screening across diverse populations.
•Partnered with the BRIDGES-NBS initiative to begin enrollment in Oregon as part of the nation's first coordinated, multi-state genomic newborn screening study. GeneDx serves as the integrated laboratory partner, providing genome sequencing and interpretation to screen up to 30,000 newborns for more than 700 actionable genetic conditions over two years.

About GeneDx
GeneDx’s (Nasdaq: WGS) mission is to empower everyone to live their healthiest life through genomics. GeneDx combines unmatched clinical expertise, advanced technology, and the power of GeneDx Infinity™ – the world’s largest rare disease genomic dataset. This unparalleled foundation powers GeneDx’s ExomeDx™ and GenomeDx™ tests – ranked #1 by expert geneticists and granted FDA Breakthrough Device designation – enabling clinicians to deliver precise, fast, and actionable diagnoses. GeneDx Infinity also fuels discovery for biopharma, with the most powerful AI-driven genomic intelligence. A genomics pioneer over the last 25 years, diagnosing more than 4,800 genetic diseases and publishing more than 1,100 research publications, GeneDx is building the network that will drive the future of genomic precision medicine. For more information, visit genedx.com and connect with us on LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram.
