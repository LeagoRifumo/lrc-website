Interface Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Disciplined execution drives earnings growth; Company reaffirms full year earnings guidance
ATLANTA – November 6, 2026 – Interface, Inc. (Nasdaq: TILE), the global flooring and sustainability leader, today announced results for the third quarter ended October 4, 2026.
Third quarter highlights (all comparisons are year-over-year):
•Net sales totaled $366.7 million, up 0.6% and up 0.4% currency neutral.
•GAAP earnings per diluted share of $0.67, a 14.1% decrease; Adjusted earnings per diluted share of $0.68, an 11.5% increase.
•One Interface strategy continues to drive shareholder value.
“Our third quarter results reflect the strength of our diversified portfolio and the effectiveness of our One Interface strategy. Global billings grew across all three primary market segments, led by Healthcare, which increased 5% on top of 29% growth in the prior-year quarter, while Education and Corporate Office each grew in the low single digits," commented Laurel Hurd, CEO of Interface.
"We continue to outperform the broader industry. Momentum in the Americas, disciplined execution, and continued progress on our One Interface strategy helped offset softer conditions in EMEA in the quarter. With our ongoing investments in innovation, productivity, and operational efficiency, we remain confident in our ability to drive growth, expand margins, and create value for our stakeholders,” continued Hurd.
“Our operating discipline enabled us to drive adjusted earnings growth ahead of our expectations, even with modest net sales growth in the quarter as certain Retail and nora deliveries shifted into future periods. We are reaffirming our full-year earnings outlook and remain confident in the strength of our business. Our backlog is solid, our balance sheet is healthy, and we remain focused on margin expansion and cost discipline. Together, these position us to deliver long-term, sustainable growth," added Bruce Hausmann, CFO of Interface.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Interface provides adjusted earnings per share, adjusted net income, adjusted operating income ("AOI"), adjusted gross profit, adjusted gross profit margin, adjusted SG&A expenses, currency- neutral sales and currency-neutral sales growth, net debt, and adjusted EBITDA as additional information regarding its operating results in this press release. These non-GAAP measures are not in accordance with – or alternatives to – GAAP measures, and may be different from non-GAAP measures used by other companies. Adjusted EPS, adjusted net income, and AOI exclude restructuring, asset impairment, severance, and other, net and the nora purchase accounting amortization. Adjusted EPS and adjusted net income also exclude the warehouse fire recovery and deferred taxes - rate changes and other. Adjusted gross profit and adjusted gross profit margin exclude the nora purchase accounting amortization. Adjusted SG&A expenses exclude restructuring, asset impairment, severance, and other, net. Currency-neutral sales and currency-neutral sales growth exclude the impact of foreign currency fluctuations.
Net debt is total debt less cash on hand. Adjusted EBITDA is GAAP net income excluding interest expense, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization, share-based compensation expense, restructuring, asset impairment, severance, and other, net, the nora purchase accounting amortization, and a warehouse fire recovery. This news release should be read in conjunction with the Company's Current Report on Form 8-K furnished today to the U.S. Securities & Exchange Commission, which explains why Interface believes presentation of these non-GAAP measures provides useful information to investors, as well as any additional material purposes for which Interface uses these non-GAAP measures.
About Interface
Interface is a global flooring and sustainability leader dedicated to rethinking how spaces work for people and the planet. Our portfolio includes Interface® carpet tile and LVT, nora® rubber flooring, and FLOR® premium area rugs. Across every brand, we innovate in a way that combines design, performance, and sustainability—without compromise.
Trusted by architects, designers, and building professionals worldwide, we help bring bold visions to life with solutions that deliver real, measurable impact. Building on more than 30 years of sustainability progress and industry‑first innovation, we remain ‘all in’ on our goal of becoming carbon negative by 2040, without the use of offsets.
Learn more about Interface (NASDAQ: TILE) and our brands at interface.com and FLOR.com. Join us on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Pinterest.
