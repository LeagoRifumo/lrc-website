Progressing commercial initiatives and advancing value unlock strategy in the Benelux
Denver, Colorado: November 3, 2026 - Liberty Global Ltd. announces its Q3 2026 financial results.
CEO Mike Fries stated, “In the third quarter, we made significant progress against our key priorities:
•Ziggo Group spin-off: During Q3, we completed several important milestones to advance the structural formation of Ziggo Group1 ahead of our planned Q2 2027 spin-off. Most notably, we closed on the purchase of Vodafone's 50% stake in VodafoneZiggo, announced the senior executive management team and engaged external advisors. Operating and financial synergies are being finalized and are currently expected to exceed our previous NPV estimate of €1 billion. We also reached an agreement to sell VodafoneZiggo's mobile towers for €669m (16.2x 2025 EBITDA), with the proceeds to be used for deleveraging at VodafoneZiggo, as part of the wider €1.2-€1.4B Ziggo Group non-core asset disposals target to further reduce leverage. We currently intend to make initial confidential submissions to the SEC and the AFM in December in connection with the proposed spin-off transaction. Meanwhile, both operating businesses in the Netherlands and Belgium reported strong quarterly results.
•Liberty Telecom: Our Telecom operations continued to focus on improving commercial performance. In the Netherlands, VodafoneZiggo delivered a second consecutive quarter of positive broadband additions and the best performance since Q2 20192, while total revenue returned to growth for the first time since Q2 2024. VodafoneZiggo also became the first major provider globally to offer 8Gbps on DOCSIS 4.0, demonstrating the potential of a network that already reaches millions of households across the Netherlands. In Belgium, Telenet grew the broadband customer base for the sixth consecutive quarter, driven by continued strength in BASE FMC. Virgin Media Ireland continued to drive strong postpaid mobile performance, with positive net adds for the seventh consecutive quarter, while positive broadband net adds3 were supported by continued strong wholesale performance.
•Virgin Media O2, in the UK, outlined the direction for a new strategic plan, with the goal of simplifying the business and driving sustainable growth and cash generation. The program calls for £800 million of run-rate cost reductions by 2029, supported, in part, by a 30% reduction in people cost. Against the backdrop of a challenging macro-economic climate and an intensely competitive UK telecom market, Virgin Media O2 will prioritize capital spending on fewer, more measurable projects, and will halt its own fiber upgrade plan, while continuing to support nexfibre. These initiatives, and more, will benefit the broader goal of reducing leverage over the coming years.

•Liberty Global: We ended Q3 with ~$2.0B of corporate cash and completed $100m of share buybacks during the quarter. We anticipate ending 2026 with ~$1.8B4 of corporate cash, with a further $100m of share buybacks expected in Q4. We remain intensely focused on disciplined capital allocation and executing our unlock strategy to deliver value directly to shareholders."

Virgin Media O2 outlines direction of new strategic plan with a focus on simplifying the business and driving growth
Virgin Media O2 announced substantial cost reduction measures and set out the strategic direction for the company's next chapter, which will lead to greater customer satisfaction, sustainable growth and increased cash generation. The full plan, to be detailed at a strategic update around the time of the full year results, will set clear priorities for a simpler and more focused company, optimizing and redirecting investment toward initiatives with a clear customer benefit and near-term returns.
Operationally during the quarter, fixed performance remained under pressure, although fixed-line net losses improved year-over-year. Mobile net losses also improved YoY, with a focus on value management driving growth in consumer mobile contract ARPU. VMO2 remains on-track for all of its full-year financial guidance metrics9 and we are reviewing our options with respect to VMO2's planned cash distributions to shareholders in 2026 and will provide an update in Q1 2027 when we announce full-year results and know the outcome of the Netomnia transaction.
Highlights for Q3
•Fixed network expansion: Full-fiber footprint reached 9.4 million10 premises, including the nexfibre network, with 5G+ outdoor population coverage of 86%
•Customer service transformation: Launched a new voice AI agent to handle broadband fault calls, building on continued improvements in service quality, with VMO2 recording the lowest level of broadband complaints among major UK providers in Ofcom’s latest data
•Brand reinforcement: Secured a long-term extension of The O2 naming rights agreement through 2038, strengthening O2's premium brand positioning and customer proposition through Priority
Q3 Financial Highlights (in U.S. GAAP, as reported by Liberty Global)11
•Revenue of $3,262.7 million, -5.0% YoY on a reported basis and -5.7% YoY on a rebased7 basis
◦Primarily driven by (i) the reduction in nexfibre construction revenue, (ii) lower consumer fixed and moderately lower consumer mobile revenue where ARPU growth partially offset the lower base and (iii) lower business revenue as O2 Business streamlines its product portfolio, despite business service revenue growth improvement compared to H126
•Adjusted EBITDA12 of $1,207.8 million, -3.4% YoY on a reported basis and -3.9% on a rebased basis
◦Primarily driven by lower consumer service revenue, partially offset by (i) cost efficiency programs, (ii) a net benefit of ~$34m relating to legal matters, and (iii) ~$13m reduction in sales commission expense reflecting a change in the estimated amortization period for sales commissions implemented in Q2 2026
•Property and equipment additions of $678.7 million, +4.8% YoY on a reported basis and +5.9% on a rebased basis
•Adjusted EBITDA less P&E additions12 of $529.1 million, -12.2% YoY on a reported basis and -14.2% on a rebased basis
•Cash flows from operating activities of $749.1 million, cash flows from investing activities of -$326.8 million and cash flows from financing activities of -$559.6 million

Ziggo Group delivered strong commercial momentum and further progress towards planned spin-off
Q3 2026 marked the formal creation of the Ziggo Group, bringing together VodafoneZiggo and Telenet as the leading Benelux connectivity champion. Combining the strengths of two market-leading operators creates greater scale, stronger shared capabilities and increased capacity to invest, innovate and create long-term value. Ziggo Group continued to demonstrate commercial momentum, with VodafoneZiggo delivering its best quarterly broadband performance since 2019 and Telenet enhancing its customer proposition with the launch of Belgium's most complete sports offering. VodafoneZiggo and Telenet remain on track to meet their respective full-year guidance.
Highlights for Q3
•Commercial momentum: VodafoneZiggo and Telenet maintained strong commercial momentum with further initiatives growing the customer base in both markets. At VodafoneZiggo, the continued success of the How We Win plan supported a return to revenue growth for the first time in two years
•Full management team announced: The full Ziggo Group executive leadership team was announced in Q3, led by CEO Stephen van Rooyen and CFO Jany Fruytier, with Rocío Lorenzo announced as Telenet's new CEO; the planned Ziggo spin-off remains on track for Q2 2027
•VodafoneZiggo towers sale: The sale of VodafoneZiggo's tower assets was agreed for a total consideration of €669 million, with the proceeds to be used for deleveraging at VodafoneZiggo
Q3 Financial Highlights (in U.S. GAAP)1
•Revenue of $1,906.5 million, -1.9% YoY on a reported basis and +1.4% on a rebased basis
◦Primarily driven by a return to positive revenue growth at VodafoneZiggo, supported by price indexation and increased handset sales, as well as revenue at Telenet relating to the Wyre TSS, higher handset sales and Jupiler Pro League (JPL) programming revenue
•Adjusted EBITDA of $674.8 million, -6.3% YoY on a reported basis and -6.8% on a rebased basis
◦Primarily driven by (i) higher costs at VodafoneZiggo relating to marketing and sales, handsets, programming and network resilience investment, and (ii) lower subscription revenue and programming costs associated with the new DAZN deal at Telenet
Q3 Financial Highlights (in U.S. GAAP) in local currency
•Revenue of €1,656.6 million, -0.3% YoY on a reported basis and +1.4% on a rebased basis
•Adjusted EBITDA of €586.3 million, -4.9% YoY on a reported basis and -6.8% on a rebased basis
Further standalone financial and operating information for VodafoneZiggo and Telenet can be found in their respective Bond Updates.

Wyre advancing fiber deployment and network cooperation
In the third quarter, Wyre continued to advance its network cooperation plans and accelerated its fiber investment, including pre-investment capital to support future deployment. Adj EBITDA continued to reflect the costs of establishing Wyre's standalone operating model and additional network optimization support, while the topline continued to reflect the impact of its new wholesale pricing model introduced in Q4 2025. Wyre remains on track to meet its medium-term rollout targets, while scaling an efficient and disciplined deployment model.
Highlights for Q3
•Network cooperation progresses: Following regulatory approval in July, Wyre continues with the implementation of its network cooperation agreement
•Fiber investment accelerated: Investment in the FTTH mass-rollout program increased, including additional investment to support future deployment

Virgin Media Ireland expands broadband coverage nationwide, supporting strong customer base growth
Virgin Media Ireland maintained commercial momentum in Q3, with positive aggregate broadband net additions, and its first positive month of Consumer net adds in 12 months. Fixed ARPU continued to improve sequentially, benefitting from the successful implementation of a price rise earlier in the year. Postpaid net adds were positive for the seventh consecutive quarter, supported by ongoing promotional offers. Virgin Media also announced its Superfast broadband footprint expansion, covering more Irish homes and businesses than any other provider.
Highlights for Q3
•Network quality: Virgin Media Ireland’s network leadership was recognized for a second consecutive quarter and was named the fastest major broadband provider in Ireland by SpeedGeo
•Expanding coverage nationwide: Through continued network expansion and off-net partnerships with SIRO, NBI and, more recently, OpenEir, Virgin Media is now the number one provider of 1Gbps+ connectivity in Ireland
•Momentum in fixed supported by wholesale: Virgin Media Ireland delivered its strongest aggregate broadband and wholesale net additions performance in seven quarters, reflecting sustained wholesale growth and improving broadband trends
Q3 Financial Highlights (in U.S. GAAP)
•Revenue of $118.6 million, -2.9% YoY on a reported basis and -1.5% on a rebased basis
◦Primarily driven by lower consumer fixed revenue, specifically relating to VMTV
•Adjusted EBITDA of $28.5 million, -31.8% YoY on a reported basis and -30.8% on a rebased basis
◦Primarily driven by (i) a one-time ~$10 million change in estimates relating to the amortization of VMTV content rights across multiple years and (ii) the flow through impact of lower revenue
•Cash flows from operating activities of $0.5 million, cash flows from investing activities of -$38.4 million and cash flows from financing activities of $22.1 million
Q3 Financial Highlights (in U.S. GAAP) in local currency
•Revenue of €103.0 million, -1.5% YoY on both a reported and rebased basis
•Adjusted EBITDA of €24.7 million, -30.8% YoY on both a reported and rebased basis
Q3 Operating Highlights
•Broadband net losses of 2,000 improving sequentially, with the first positive monthly net additions in 12 months achieved in September, despite persistent competitive intensity
•Postpaid net adds of 2,700 due to successful promotional offers
•Wholesale broadband net adds of 6,900, driven by sustained momentum in wholesale strategy execution, including expanding wholesale partnerships

Additional General Notes to Tables:
Most of our broadband communications subsidiaries provide broadband, telephony, data, video or other B2B services. Certain of our B2B revenue is derived from SOHO subscribers that pay a premium price to receive enhanced service levels along with broadband, video or telephony services that are the same or similar to the mass marketed products offered to our residential subscribers. All mass marketed products provided to SOHOs, whether or not accompanied by enhanced service levels and/or premium prices, are included in the respective RGU and customer counts of our broadband communications operations, with only those services provided at premium prices considered to be “SOHO RGUs” or “SOHO customers”. To the extent our existing customers upgrade from a residential product offering to a SOHO product offering, the number of SOHO RGUs or SOHO customers will increase, but there is no impact to our total RGU or customer counts. With the exception of our B2B SOHO subscribers and mobile subscribers at medium and large enterprises, we generally do not count customers of B2B services as customers or RGUs for external reporting purposes.
While we take appropriate steps to ensure that subscriber statistics are presented on a consistent and accurate basis at any given balance sheet date, the variability from country to country in (i) the nature and pricing of products and services, (ii) the distribution platform, (iii) billing systems, (iv) bad debt collection experience and (v) other factors add complexity to the subscriber counting process.
