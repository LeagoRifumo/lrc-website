HAWTHORNE, Calif. — (BUSINESS WIRE) — October 29, 2026—OSI Systems, Inc. (the “Company” or “OSI Systems”) (NASDAQ: OSIS) today announced its financial results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027.
Ajay Mehra, OSI Systems’ President and Chief Executive Officer, stated, “We are pleased to begin fiscal 2027 with record first quarter revenues and earnings per share, led by the performance of our Security and Optoelectronics and Manufacturing divisions. Bookings of $583 million represented a book-to-bill ratio of 1.4 in the quarter, and backlog increased to a record of $2.1 billion from $1.9 billion as of June 30, 2026, providing meaningful visibility into future opportunities. We remain committed to disciplined execution and innovation, and converting our expanding opportunity pipeline into long-term growth.”

Mr. Mehra further commented, “The Security division delivered a solid quarter despite continued geopolitical headwinds. Strong bookings led to record divisional backlog and reflected robust customer demand for our security inspection and surveillance products, integrated solutions, and services. The operating margin was impacted by a less favorable mix of revenues; however, we expect this to improve over the remainder of the fiscal year as our revenue mix stabilizes and service revenues increase. We are enthusiastic about the outlook for this division over the balance of fiscal 2027 and beyond.”
Mr. Mehra continued, “Our Optoelectronics and Manufacturing division delivered strong fiscal first-quarter results with revenues increasing 9% year-over-year including intersegment sales and 16% year-over-year from sales to external customers, while also achieving adjusted operating margin expansion. The division continued to benefit from its vertically integrated capabilities, broad customer relationships, and diversified manufacturing footprint leading to a strong book-to-bill ratio resulting in record backlog for this division. We remain focused on supporting growth while maintaining disciplined operational execution.”
Mr. Mehra concluded, “The performance of the Healthcare division was soft in the quarter, but we are focused on improving performance and anticipate a return to growth this fiscal year.”
Alan Edrick, OSI Systems’ Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, stated, “Operating cash flow more than doubled in the first fiscal quarter over the prior year Q1 to $37 million, and we finished the quarter with approximately $302 million of cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments. In August 2026, our board of directors authorized an additional 1 million shares to the existing share repurchase program. The Company repurchased 305,559 shares during the first quarter of fiscal 2027 resulting in total capital returned to shareholders through repurchases of approximately $62 million. As of September 30, 2026, there are 773,172 shares remaining under our repurchase authorization.”

About OSI Systems
OSI Systems designs and manufactures specialized electronic systems and components for critical applications. The Company operates through three business segments: Security, Optoelectronics and Manufacturing, and Healthcare. The Company’s Security division delivers advanced inspection systems, turnkey screening solutions, and comprehensive support services to protect people and infrastructure. The Optoelectronics and Manufacturing segment serves as a global supplier of high-performance optoelectronic solutions and precision manufacturing services for leading OEMs. The Healthcare segment focuses on patient monitoring and diagnostic cardiology and related services with the goal of enhancing clinical care and patient outcomes. Serving customers in over 170 countries, OSI Systems strategically positions its sales, service, R&D, and manufacturing capabilities worldwide to provide fast and efficient delivery and support. This earnings release includes a presentation of non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP diluted earnings per share, non-GAAP operating income (loss) by segment and non-GAAP operating margin, all of which are non-GAAP financial measures. Although we exclude amortization of acquired intangible assets from our non-GAAP figures, revenue generated from such intangibles is included in determining non-GAAP financial performance of the Company. Non-GAAP financial measures should not be assessed in isolation or as a substitute for measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with GAAP. Our non-GAAP financial measures may not be the same as measures used by other companies due to possible differences in methods and in the items or events for which adjustments are made.
