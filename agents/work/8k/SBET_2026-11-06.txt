Sharplink Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results
Strong Financial Performance Reflects Continued Execution of Active
Treasury Management and Disciplined Capital Allocation
Revenue up 24% YoY to $13.5 Million; Net Income of $751.4 Million or $3.45 Per Diluted Share
MIAMI, FL — November 9, 2026 — Sharplink, Inc. (Nasdaq: SBET) (“Sharplink” or the “Company”), one of the world’s largest corporate holders of Ether (“ETH”) and prominent industry advocate of Ethereum adoption, today reported financial and operating results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, along with an update on its ETH treasury strategy.
“Our strong third quarter performance reflects our progress in executing our Ethereum treasury strategy and further strengthening our institutional-grade treasury platform,” said Joseph Chalom, Chief Executive Officer of Sharplink. “We are focused on maximizing the productivity of our ETH holdings, maintaining a disciplined approach to capital allocation and supporting the broader Ethereum ecosystem. Our relentless focus translates into tangible financial results, highlighted by double-digit revenue growth, improved operating leverage and a material increase in net income. We believe these results reinforce the strength of our strategy and the opportunities ahead as Ethereum continues to gain traction across global financial markets.”
Continuing, Chalom noted, “During the quarter, we made meaningful progress putting our ETH treasury to work, completing our $200 million Lido staking allocation and beginning capital deployment through the Galaxy Sharplink Onchain Yield Fund. These milestones reflect our belief that ETH should be managed as a productive operating asset rather than simply held for potential appreciation. Our permanent capital base enables us to pursue longer-duration treasury deployments and differentiated yield opportunities, while maintaining a disciplined approach to risk management. As Ethereum’s institutional adoption continues to accelerate, we believe Sharplink is well-positioned to leverage our scale, institutional expertise and ecosystem relationships to generate incremental ETH, grow ETH-per-share and create sustainable long-term value for shareholders.”

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
Income Statement and Balance Sheet
● | Total revenue increased 24% to $13.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $10.8 million in the prior-year quarter. Staking revenue grew 27% to $13.1 million from $10.3 million, reflecting Sharplink’s ongoing ETH treasury management activities.
● | SG&A expenses decreased 8% to $11.0 million, compared with $12.0 million in the prior-year quarter, as the Company continued to carefully manage the operating costs of its treasury platform.
● | Net income increased to $751.4 million, or $3.45 per diluted share, compared with net income of $104.3 million, or $0.62 per diluted share, in the prior year quarter. The increase was driven principally by unrealized and realized gains on crypto assets.

○ | Unrealized gains: $676.7 million primarily reflecting ETH market conditions in the third quarter of 2026.
○ | Net realized gain: $75.2 million primarily related to ETH treasury activities, including liquid staking transactions.
○ | Unrealized gains reflect changes in the fair value of the Company’s ETH holdings and do not represent cash proceeds or a change in the number of ETH and ETH-equivalent tokens held; however, impairment charges reduce the carrying value of LsETH, weETH, and wstETH under U.S. GAAP and are not reversed for subsequent market recoveries.

● | Cash and cash equivalents totaled $47.9 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with $28.5 million as of December 31, 2025.

Joseph Lubin, Chairman of Sharplink and Co-Founder of Ethereum, added, “Ethereum is rapidly evolving into foundational infrastructure for the next generation of global finance, supported by accelerating institutional adoption and continued advancements in its underlying technology. From JPMorgan’s expansion of its institutional deposit token to Ethereum mainnet to the growing tokenization of financial assets by leading global asset managers, these developments underscore Ethereum’s expanding role across payments, asset management and financial-market infrastructure. Combined with its ongoing technological advancements, deep liquidity and commitment to security, we believe Ethereum is uniquely positioned to support the next generation of global financial activity. As institutional adoption accelerates and more economic activity moves onchain, Sharplink is well-positioned to participate in Ethereum’s continued growth through its actively managed ETH treasury and commitment to supporting the broader ecosystem.”

● | Crypto assets totaled approximately $2.1 billion as of September 30, 2026, valued in accordance with U.S. GAAP.

About Sharplink, Inc.
Sharplink (Nasdaq: SBET) is a leading institutional-grade Ethereum treasury platform designed to give public market investors smarter, more productive exposure to ETH. Ethereum underpins the majority of global stablecoin, tokenized real-world assets and decentralized finance settlement. Sharplink was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Miami, Florida.
