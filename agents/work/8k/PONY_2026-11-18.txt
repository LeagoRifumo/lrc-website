Pony AI Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results:
Total Revenues Up 75.2% YoY to US$44.6 million with Robotaxi Services Revenues Growing by 165.4% YoY
· | Strong Robotaxi growth momentum. Robotaxi services revenues grew by 165.4% year-over-year in the third quarter, accounting for approximately 40% of total revenues. Our Robotaxi fleet reached 2,768 vehicles as of September 30, 2026, advancing toward our full-year target of 3,500 vehicles.
· | Broader coverage in China. Our previously separate operating areas in downtown Guangzhou and Nansha District now form a single contiguous operating area spanning more than 1,000 square kilometers,1 nearly nine times the size of the city of San Francisco. In addition, we integrated the newly permitted driverless vehicles into our ongoing operations in Pudong New Area in Shanghai. We have also begun deployment in Hangzhou, Chongqing and Hengqin in Zhuhai.
· | Expanding international partnerships. We began Europe’s first fully-driverless test rides with invited passengers in Zagreb, Croatia, in September 2026. We continue to advance our expansion in key European cities, including London and Manchester, with a focus on high-fare markets, building on our previously announced pipeline of over 4,000 Robotaxi vehicles under agreements in negotiation across international markets.
· | Safety record translating into better unit economics. Insurance premiums per seventh-generation (“Gen-7”) Robotaxi decreased by 19% year-over-year upon renewal.

NEW YORK, Nov. 18, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Pony AI Inc. (“Pony.ai” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: PONY; HKEX: 2026), a global leader in achieving large-scale mass production and commercialization of autonomous driving technology, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Dr. James Peng, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Pony.ai, commented, “Our recent progress demonstrates how our long-standing commitment to safety and R&D is translating into broader market access, scalable operations and cost advantages. In China, we have expanded Robotaxi service coverage in tier-one cities and at major transportation hubs, with our contiguous operating area in Guangzhou now spanning more than 1,000 square kilometers. Globally, we are advancing overseas partnerships in European cities including Zagreb, London and Manchester through the joint deployment model. We have also broadened our fourth-generation (“Gen-4”) Robotruck lineup. Together, these developments across Robotaxis and Robotrucks reinforce our confidence in scaling our autonomous driving businesses across different markets as we head into next year.”
Dr. Tiancheng Lou, Chief Technology Officer of Pony.ai, commented, “Our proprietary PonyWorld supports our AI driver’s post-training through reinforcement learning (“RL”), enabling it to train on diverse and complex traffic scenarios repeatedly in simulation and refine its decisions based on the outcomes. Post-training is critical in autonomous driving, as pre-training can build basic driving skills, but only RL can push the AI driver beyond the limits of human driving data and solve long-tail safety problems. This capability supports our faster deployment in China and overseas while improving safety in a more efficient way. It also creates cross-platform generalization across Robotaxi and Robotruck and a closed-loop moat that is hard to replicate.”
Dr. Leo Wang, Chief Financial Officer of Pony.ai, commented, “In the third quarter, strong growth in Robotaxi and Robotruck drove a 75.2% year-over-year increase in total revenues, with Robotaxi fare-charging revenues growing by nearly 400% from the prior-year period. Higher gross profit helped narrow our GAAP loss from operations to US$68.7 million, compared with US$69.7 million in the same period last year. The improvement was more pronounced on a non-GAAP basis, with non-GAAP loss from operations decreasing by 7.6% year-over-year, reflecting improving operating leverage. We remain on track to achieve our 2026 targets: expanding our Robotaxi fleet to 3,500 vehicles deployed in 20 cities worldwide by year-end, while growing full-year Robotaxi services revenues to 3.5 times the 2025 level. We will continue investing in the deployment of Gen-7 Robotaxi vehicles and core technology, while remaining disciplined in our spending.”
Scaling Robotaxi Commercialization across Domestic and International Markets
· | Broadening Robotaxi Operations in China’s Tier-One Cities and Beyond. 1) All three of our Gen-7 Robotaxi models, developed with the Beijing Automotive Industry Corporation (“BAIC”), Guangzhou Automotive Corporation (“GAC”) and Toyota, are now in fully-driverless commercial operation. 2) In Guangzhou, under the city-wide commercial permit obtained in April 2026, our Robotaxi services now cover more of the city’s core urban areas, with the contiguous operating area exceeding 1,000 square kilometers,1 nearly nine times the size of the city of San Francisco. Users can book long-distance rides between Nansha District and the core urban areas through our PonyPilot app. 3) In Shenzhen, we are the only operator providing fully-driverless commercial Robotaxi services at Bao’an International Airport, which is the only airport among China’s tier-one cities offering such services. 4) In Shanghai, we integrated the newly permitted driverless vehicles into our ongoing operations in Pudong New Area. We expect to expand service coverage to key sub-districts including Zhangjiang, Beicai and Kangqiao. 5) We have begun deployment in Hangzhou, Chongqing and Hengqin in Zhuhai. 6) Our safety record continued to improve. Upon renewal, per-vehicle insurance premiums for our Gen-7 Robotaxi fleet were down to approximately 40% of those for human-driven taxis, compared with approximately 50% in 2025.

· | Advancing Overseas Robotaxi Commercialization through the Joint Deployment Model. 1) In Zagreb, Croatia, we launched ride-hailing operations in April 2026 and progressed to Europe’s first fully-driverless test rides with invited passengers in September 2026. We have accumulated over 200,000 kilometers of operations and completed several thousand passenger trips.2 2) Building on our previously announced overseas deployment pipeline of over 4,000 Robotaxi vehicles under agreements in negotiation, we are advancing deployments in London and Manchester, with road testing already underway in Manchester. 3) We signed a memorandum of understanding with inDrive and NVIDIA to explore potential Robotaxi cooperation in Kazakhstan.

Building Momentum in Robotruck Commercialization
· | Expanding Robotruck Deployment and Commercial Applications. 1) Our Gen-4 Robotrucks entered mass production on schedule. We also unveiled a new Gen-4 heavy-duty model with GAC Commercial Vehicle, expanding our product lineup. 2) We are deploying Robotrucks at Jiangmen Port and Shenzhen Mawan Port, with regular commercial operations planned. 3) Our fully-driverless light-duty trucks have entered production, and initial deployment is now underway in China’s tier-one cities with logistics clients.

Total revenues were US$44.6 million (RMB298.8 million) in the third quarter of 2026, up 75.2% from US$25.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was mainly driven by strong growth in Robotaxi services revenues and Robotruck services revenues.
· | Robotaxi services revenues were US$17.8 million (RMB119.1 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 165.4% from US$6.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by higher fare-charging revenues as we expanded commercial operations with our Gen-7 Robotaxi vehicles, as well as increased revenues from higher deliveries of Robotaxi vehicles under our joint deployment model in China and overseas. In particular, fare-charging revenues increased by nearly 400% year-over-year.

· | Robotruck services revenues were US$18.0 million (RMB120.4 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 76.3% from US$10.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to growth in freight transportation services, supported by our collaboration with Sinotrans.

· | Intelligent solutions revenues were US$8.9 million (RMB59.4 million) in the third quarter of 2026, broadly flat compared to US$8.6 million in the third quarter of 2025.

For financial reporting purposes, our revenues are classified into service revenues and product revenues based on the nature of the underlying revenue streams. Service revenues were US$25.2 million (RMB168.9 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 64.8% from US$15.3 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily attributable to growth in Robotaxi services revenues and Robotruck transportation services revenues. Product revenues were US$19.4 million (RMB129.9 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 90.9% from US$10.2 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily attributable to higher deliveries of Robotaxi vehicles under our joint deployment model.
Cost of Revenues
· | Total cost of revenues was US$36.7 million (RMB245.8 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 76.5% from US$20.8 million in the third quarter of 2025, broadly in line with revenue trends.

Gross Profit and Gross Margin
· | Gross profit was US$7.9 million (RMB53.1 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 69.5% from US$4.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.

· | Gross margin was 17.8% in the third quarter of 2026, broadly flat compared to 18.4% in the third quarter of 2025.

Operating Expenses
Operating expenses were US$76.6 million (RMB513.9 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 3.1% from US$74.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP3 operating expenses were US$66.2 million (RMB443.8 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing a decrease of 2.3% from US$67.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.
· | Research and development expenses were US$60.1 million (RMB402.7 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$60.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP research and development expenses were US$53.1 million (RMB356.2 million), representing a decrease of 5.4% from US$56.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease primarily reflected quarterly fluctuations in customized development fees, which were higher in the third quarter of 2025 related to Gen-7 development, partially offset by the increase in R&D personnel expenses.

· | Selling, general and administrative expenses were US$16.6 million (RMB111.2 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$14.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP selling, general and administrative expenses were US$13.1 million (RMB87.6 million), representing an increase of 12.6% from US$11.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was mainly attributable to higher personnel expenses.

Loss from Operations
· | Loss from operations was US$68.7 million (RMB460.8 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$69.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP loss from operations was US$58.3 million (RMB390.8 million), representing a decrease of 7.6% from US$63.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was mainly attributable to higher gross profit, coupled with lower non-GAAP operating expenses.

· | Operating loss margin was 154.2% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 273.9% in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP operating loss margin was 130.8% in the third quarter of 2026, narrowing from 247.9% in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year reduction in non-GAAP operating loss margin primarily reflected improved operating leverage as our operations continued to scale.

Net Loss
· | Net loss was US$136.3 million (RMB914.1 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$61.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in net loss was primarily attributable to fair value losses on trading securities. Non-GAAP net loss was US$48.9 million (RMB327.7 million), compared to US$56.8 million in the third quarter of 2025.

· | Net loss margin was 305.9% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 242.0% in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net loss margin was 109.6% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 223.1% in the third quarter of 2025. The reduction in non-GAAP net loss margin primarily reflected improved operating leverage as our operations continued to scale.

Net Loss Attributable to Pony AI Inc.
· | Net loss attributable to Pony AI Inc. was US$105.6 million (RMB707.7 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$61.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. The difference between total net loss and net loss attributable to Pony AI Inc. reflected US$30.8 million of net loss attributable to non-controlling interests during the quarter. Non-GAAP net loss attributable to Pony AI Inc. was US$48.5 million (RMB325.4 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$56.5 million in the third quarter of 2025.

Basic and Diluted Net Loss per Ordinary Share
· | Basic and diluted net loss per ordinary share was both US$0.24 (RMB1.63) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$0.16 in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP basic and diluted net loss per ordinary share was both US$0.11 (RMB0.75) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$0.15 in the third quarter of 2025. Each American depositary share (“ADS”) represents one Class A ordinary share.

Balance Sheet
· | Cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, short-term and long-term debt instruments for wealth management were US$1,154.0 million (RMB7,737.3 million) as of September 30, 2026, compared to the balance of US$1,206.1 million as of June 30, 2026. The decrease primarily reflected operating cash outflows and capital expenditures during the period.

Cash Flows
· | Net cash used in operating activities was US$60.5 million (RMB405.8 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$56.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was mainly attributable to increased cash used in working capital. The increased cash used in working capital was generally in line with our business expansion, and we expect our working capital requirements to continue to reflect the growth of our operations.

· | Capital expenditures were US$17.7 million (RMB118.7 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to US$22.8 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily reflecting a disciplined pacing of investment, while we continue to support the mass production and deployment of the Gen-7 Robotaxi fleet.

· | Free cash flows were outflows of US$78.2 million (RMB524.6 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to outflows of US$79.6 million in the third quarter of 2025.

About Pony AI Inc.
Pony AI Inc. (NASDAQ: PONY; HKEX: 2026), founded in 2016, is a global leader in achieving large-scale mass production and commercialization of autonomous driving technology.
