Roblox has an unparalleled data set that supports hundreds of​ ​proprietary models for safety, discovery, and creation​ ​Roblox has a proprietary data set, including more than 20 years of gameplay data, hundreds of​ ​thousands of daily active creators, and an average of more than 350 million hours of human​ ​interaction data per day. We believe no other company can replicate these unique bodies of data:​ ​how games are built, how worlds and assets come together, which mechanics keep players​ ​engaged, and what maximizes player lifetime value. We have nearly 400 models that power​ ​creation, safety and social communication, discovery, economy and infrastructure. These models​ ​use a combination of open-weight and frontier models with self-hosted models handling 77% of​ ​requests. We use our unique data to fine-tune open weight models for the tasks that matter most​ ​to our creators, such as generating game logic, building worlds, creating assets, and incorporating​ ​player signals that allow them to build businesses.​ ​Creation.​​Our data on human gameplay and spend is a unique asset. By capturing gameplay as 4D​ ​spatial data down to the instance and frame level, our Data Model Recording (DMR) platform​ ​provides richer, more granular ground truth. Models trained on this data can learn precise 3D state​ ​changes over time from any perspective, learning complex behaviors directly from exact telemetry​ ​like line of sight, weapon usage, and hit locations. This rich data allows training models for​ ​human-like playtesters and NPCs, at higher quality and lower inference costs than frontier models.​ ​We are adding purpose-built models for 3D asset generation via diffusion, NPC models trained on​ ​our human gameplay data to playtest and give feedback to the coding agent, and scene​ ​verification models. We are also building art agents for UI, UX, and visual assets to avoid generic​ ​AI-generated output.​ ​Safety and Social Communication.​​We are also using AI models to detect violative content in real​ ​time. Earlier this year, we released RM3, our real-time multimodal moderation system, which​ ​proactively detects and addresses violative content. RM3 contextually evaluates entire scenes —​ ​including avatars, text, emotes, and 3D objects — dramatically reducing the time it takes for​ ​violative content to be detected and addressed. Our Bad Avatar Moderation (BAM) system scans​ ​assembled avatars to prevent individually compliant items from being combined into inappropriate​ ​outfits. We also expanded our safety systems to include an AI-powered chat filtering system,​ ​which uses an LLM-based model to rewrite certain messages in real time into age-appropriate​ ​versions.​ ​8​
​Discovery.​​Our discovery systems are trained on billions of daily signals: games users see, what​ ​they play, how long they stay, who they play with, and what they come back to. These systems​ ​help match each of our 138 million DAUs with the right game from a growing catalog, including new​ ​and niche titles that might otherwise go unseen. As AI brings more games onto Roblox, we believe​ ​our discovery systems will become even more important to connecting great content with its​ ​audience, and to the health of our creator ecosystem.​ ​Economy.​​Roblox’s fully integrated economy produces​​a rich, proprietary record of how value is​ ​created in games: when users buy items, how to price them, at what point in a play session to put​ ​them in front of a user, and how spending relates to long-term engagement. We are able to utilize​ ​these signals to improve our AI creation tools, letting agents learn not only how to help creators​ ​build a game, but also which design choices could lead to successful games.​ ​4. Our work​ ​on import and Universal Player also creates an iterative feedback loop, identifying technological​ ​gaps in our engine and allowing us to deploy AI-enabled engineering resources to close them.​ ​Expanding 3D Capabilities.​​We are advancing our terrain system and runtime technologies,​ ​including SLIM and dynamic mesh and texture streaming, to deliver complex 3D geometry and​ ​high-resolution textures across any device. ​Non-GAAP Financial Measures​ ​This letter contains the following non-GAAP financial measures: bookings, Adjusted EBITDA, and​ ​free cash flow.​ ​We use this non-GAAP financial information to evaluate our ongoing operations and for internal​ ​planning and forecasting purposes. We believe that this non-GAAP financial information may be​ ​helpful to investors because it provides consistency and comparability with past financial​ ​performance. However, non-GAAP financial measures have limitations in their usefulness to​ ​investors because they have no standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP and are not prepared​ ​under any comprehensive set of accounting rules or principles. In addition, other companies,​ ​including companies in our industry, may calculate similarly titled non-GAAP financial measures​ ​differently or may use other measures to evaluate their performance, all of which could reduce the​ ​usefulness of our non-GAAP financial information as a tool for comparison. As a result, our​ ​non-GAAP financial information is presented for supplemental informational purposes only and​ ​should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, financial information presented in​ ​accordance with GAAP.​ ​Reconciliation tables of the most comparable GAAP financial measure to each non-GAAP financial​ ​measure used in this letter are included at the end of this letter. We encourage investors and​ ​others to review our business, results of operations, and financial information in their entirety, not​ ​to rely on any single financial measure, and to view these non-GAAP measures in conjunction with​ ​the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures.​ ​Bookings​​represent the sales activity in a given period​​without giving effect to certain non-cash​ ​adjustments, as detailed below. Substantially all of our bookings are generated from sales of virtual​ ​currency, which can ultimately be converted to virtual items on the Roblox platform. Sales of virtual​ ​currency reflected as bookings include one-time purchases or monthly subscriptions purchased​ ​via payment processors or through prepaid cards. Bookings are initially recorded in deferred​ ​revenue and recognized as revenues over the estimated period of time the virtual items purchased​ ​with the virtual currency are available on the Roblox platform (estimated to be the average lifetime​ ​of a paying user) or as the virtual items purchased with the virtual currency are consumed.​ ​Bookings also include an insignificant amount from advertising and licensing arrangements. We​ ​believe bookings provide a timelier indication of trends in our operating results that are not​ ​necessarily reflected in our revenue as a result of the fact that we recognize the majority of​ ​revenue over the estimated average lifetime of a paying user. The change in deferred revenue​ ​constitutes the vast majority of the reconciling difference from revenue to bookings. By removing​ ​these non-cash adjustments, we are able to measure and monitor our business performance​ ​based on the timing of actual transactions with our users and the cash that is generated from these​ ​transactions. Over the long term, the factors impacting our revenue and bookings trends are the​ ​same. However, in the short term, there are factors that may cause revenue and bookings trends to​ ​differ.​ ​20​

We believe that, when considered together with reported GAAP amounts, Adjusted​ ​EBITDA is useful to investors and management in understanding our ongoing operations and​ ​ongoing operating trends.
