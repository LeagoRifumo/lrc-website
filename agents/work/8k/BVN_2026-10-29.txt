Buenaventura Announces
Third Quarter and Nine-Month 2026 Results
Lima, Peru, October 29, 2026 – Compañía de Minas Buenaventura S.A.A. (“Buenaventura” or “the Company”) (NYSE: BVN; Lima Stock Exchange: BUE.LM), Peru’s largest publicly-traded precious metals mining company, today announced results for the third quarter (3Q26) and nine months ended September 30, 2026 (9M26). Financial results are prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), except for certain non-IFRS financial measures identified herein. All amounts are stated in U.S. dollars (US$), unless otherwise indicated.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:
· | Direct operations copper production increased by 7% year-over-year (YoY), primarily driven by higher ore throughput at El Brocal. Direct operations gold production decreased by 6% YoY, while equity-attributable gold production, including associated companies, increased by 7% YoY, primarily reflecting a full quarter of operations at nameplate capacity at Coimolache, partially offset by lower production at La Zanja, Orcopampa and Tambomayo. Direct operations silver production decreased by 21% YoY, primarily due to lower planned silver grades at Yumpag. Lead and zinc production decreased by 12% and 1% YoY, respectively, with the decline in lead production primarily reflecting lower grades at Uchucchacua.
· | 3Q26 EBITDA from direct operations was US$ 261.2 million, compared with US$ 195.3 million reported in 3Q25. EBITDA from direct operations for the first nine months of 2026 was US$ 882.0 million, compared with US$ 434.1 million reported in the corresponding period of 2025.
· | 3Q26 net income was US$ 268.4 million, compared with US$ 179.0 million reported in 3Q25. Net income for the first nine months of 2026 was US$ 825.4 million, compared with US$ 424.2 million in net income for the corresponding period of 2025.
· | Processing at San Gabriel remained constrained for most of the quarter as the first phase of reinforcement work on the tailings filtration system was completed. Processing resumed during the final week of September. Additional reinforcement work is planned to be carried out progressively over the next six months. Mining activities continued throughout the quarter, resulting in an ore stockpile of approximately 100,000 metric tons at quarter-end.
· | Buenaventura reported a cash balance of US$ 1,000.6 million as of September 30, 2026, and negative net debt of US$ 307.3 million, representing a net cash position. The Company’s reported leverage ratio was negative 0.25x.
· | Buenaventura’s Board of Directors approved a dividend of US$ 0.5508 per share or American Depositary Shares (ADS).

Company Description
Compañía de Minas Buenaventura S.A.A. is Peru’s largest publicly traded precious and base metals mining company and a major holder of mining rights in Peru. The Company engages in the exploration, development, mining, processing and sale of gold, silver and base metals through its wholly-owned operations and interests in joint ventures. Buenaventura currently operates several mines in Peru (Orcopampa*, Uchucchacua*, Julcani*, Tambomayo*, La Zanja*, San Gabriel*, El Brocal and Coimolache).
The Company holds a 19.58% ownership interest in Sociedad Minera Cerro Verde, a major Peruvian copper producer, alongside Freeport-McMoRan Inc. and Sumitomo Corporation.
(*) Wholly owned operations.
