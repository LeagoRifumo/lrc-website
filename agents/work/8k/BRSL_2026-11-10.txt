NEWS RELEASE
BRIGHTSTAR LOTTERY PLC REPORTS
THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
•Third quarter revenue of $607 million impacted by higher non-cash service revenue amortization
•Operational growth drivers accelerated, driving 4% expansion, led by global product sales and other service revenue
•Income from continuing operations of $45 million; Adjusted EBITDA of $289 million in line with expectations bolstered by improved product sales mix and cost efficiencies
•Over $200 million deployed for cash dividends and share repurchases year-to-date through November 9
•Updating revenue and Adjusted EBITDA outlook for the year; maintaining cash from operations outlook
LONDON – November 10, 2026 – Brightstar Lottery PLC (NYSE:BRSL) (“Brightstar” or the "Company") today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Today, at 8:00 a.m. EST, management will host a conference call and webcast to present the results; access details are provided below.
"Our third quarter and year-to-date performance reflect the breadth of the products and services we provide around the world and the progress we are making against our strategic priorities,” said Vince Sadusky, CEO of Brightstar. “Revenue and profit were in line with our expectations, even as we invest to strengthen our contract portfolio, expand our technology and service offerings, and accelerate game and product innovation. We remain confident in our ability to leverage our leading market positions, technology capabilities, and customer relationships to drive sustainable growth and shareholder value."
“Operational growth drivers are contributing at an accelerated pace, reinforcing our medium-term growth outlook,” said Max Chiara, CFO of Brightstar. “Our profitability is strong and resilient as we remain focused on disciplined execution and cost efficiencies. Leverage is within our targeted range and we are committed to returning capital to shareholders."

Financial Highlights
Revenue of $607 million, down 4% from $629 million in the prior year
•Key drivers of growth include:
•Product sales of retail hardware, software, and instant tickets
•Higher other service revenue, primarily from software services for new contracts and extensions outside the U.S. and Italy
•Italy B2C expansion
•Offsets to growth include:
•Higher service revenue amortization related to the new Italy Lotto license
•Lower same-store sales, primarily due to U.S. multi-state jackpot performance
•U.K. service contract transition
•Foreign currency translation
Revenue was $1.78 billion in the year-to-date period of 2026, down 4% versus $1.84 billion in 2025 as increases in product sales and other service revenue were more than offset by increased service revenue amortization and the transition of the UK contract.
Income from continuing operations was $45 million compared to $95 million in the prior-year period, primarily resulting from:
•Higher service revenue amortization related to the new Italy Lotto license

•Items listed as drivers of growth and offsets to growth in Adjusted EBITDA below
•Increased net interest expense, primarily due to lower interest income
•Year-over-year decrease was partially offset by a reduced provision for income taxes
Income from continuing operations for the year-to-date 2026 period was $164 million versus $43 million in 2025 primarily driven by:
•Non-cash impact of fluctuations in the EUR/USD FX rate on the Company’s debt balances
•Improvement in the effective tax rate and benefit of a discrete tax item in the second quarter of 2026
•Key drivers of and offsets to year-over-year AEBITDA increase described below
Adjusted EBITDA of $289 million versus $294 million in the prior-year period
•Key drivers of growth include:
•Higher global product sales and improved margin mix
•Profit flow-through from other service revenue
•Cost efficiencies
•Offsets to growth include:
•Lower same-store sales, primarily due to U.S. multi-state jackpot performance
•Investments in growth initiatives
•U.K. service contract transition
•One-time expense recoveries recognized in prior year
•Foreign currency translation
Adjusted EBITDA was $861 million in the year-to-date period of 2026, up 5% versus $818 million in 2025 due to:
•Higher product sales and improved margin mix on sales
•Increased other service revenue and associated profit flow-through
•OPtiMa-driven cost efficiencies & expense recoveries
•Partial offsets to growth include the UK contract transition and investments in growth initiatives
Diluted net income per share from continuing operations for the third quarter was $0.12 compared to $0.29 in the prior year due to drivers of the year-over-year change in income from continuing operations described above. Adjusted EPS from continuing operations for the third quarter was $0.10 compared to $0.36 in the prior year, primarily driven by the non-cash impact of higher service revenue amortization.
•Diluted net income per share and Adjusted EPS from continuing operations include $43 million ($0.23 per share) of Brightstar's portion, net of tax, of service revenue amortization compared to $23 million ($0.12 per share) in 2025.
Diluted net income per share from continuing operations for the year-to-date period of 2026 was $0.49 versus a loss of $0.31 in the same period of 2025 due to drivers of year-over-year improvement in income from continuing operations described above as well as net income attributable to non-controlling interests. Adjusted EPS from continuing operations for the year-to-date period of 2026 was $0.34 versus $0.55 for 2025 primarily due to the non-cash impact of higher service revenue amortization.
•Diluted net income per share and Adjusted EPS from continuing operations for the year-to-date-period include $130 million ($0.70 per share) of Brightstar's portion, net of tax, of service revenue amortization compared to $68 million ($0.34 per share) in 2025.
Net debt was $3.8 billion compared to $2.7 billion at December 31, 2025, reflecting the final Italy Lotto payment made earlier this year. Net debt leverage was 3.30x.
Cash and Liquidity Update
Total liquidity was $1.9 billion as of September 30, 2026, including approximately $0.5 billion in unrestricted cash and $1.3 billion in additional borrowing capacity from undrawn credit facilities.

About Brightstar Lottery PLC
Brightstar Lottery PLC (NYSE: BRSL) is a global leader in lottery focused on innovation and forward-thinking strategies, building on our renowned expertise in delivering secure technology and producing reliable, comprehensive solutions for our customers. As a premier pure play global lottery company, our best-in-class lottery operations, retail and digital solutions, and award-winning lottery games enable our customers to achieve their goals, entertain players and distribute meaningful benefits to communities. Brightstar has a well-established local presence and is a trusted partner to governments and regulators around the world, creating value by adhering to the highest standards of service, integrity, and responsibility. Brightstar serves nearly 90 lottery customers and their players on six continents. It is the primary technology provider to 26 of the 46 lottery jurisdictions in the U.S. and eight of the world’s 10 largest lotteries with central systems. Brightstar has approximately 6,000 employees. For more information, please visit www.brightstarlottery.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Management supplements the reporting of financial information, determined under GAAP, with certain non-GAAP financial information. Management believes the non-GAAP information presented provides investors with additional useful information, but it is not intended to, nor should it be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the related GAAP measures. Moreover, other companies may define non-GAAP measures differently, which limits the usefulness of these measures for comparisons with such other companies. The Company encourages investors to review its financial statements and publicly filed reports in their entirety and not to rely on any single financial measure.
Adjusted EBIT represents net income (loss) from continuing operations (a GAAP measure) before income taxes, interest expense, net, foreign exchange gain (loss), net, other expenses (e.g., gains/losses on extinguishment and modifications of debt, etc.), net, impairment losses, restructuring expenses, transformation costs, stock-based compensation, litigation expense (income), and certain other non-recurring items. Other non-recurring items are infrequent in nature and are not reflective of ongoing operational activities. Management believes that Adjusted EBIT is useful in providing period-to-period comparisons of the results of the Company's ongoing operational performance.
Adjusted EBIT margin represents Adjusted EBIT divided by revenue.
Adjusted EBITDA represents net income (loss) from continuing operations (a GAAP measure) before income taxes, interest expense, net, foreign exchange gain (loss), net, other expenses (e.g., gains/losses on extinguishment and modifications of debt, etc.), net, depreciation, impairment losses, amortization (service revenue, purchase accounting, and non-purchase accounting), restructuring expenses, transformation costs, stock-based compensation, litigation expense (income), and certain other non-recurring items. Other non-recurring items are infrequent in nature and are not reflective of ongoing

operational activities. Management believes that Adjusted EBITDA is useful in providing period-to-period comparisons of the results of the Company's ongoing operational performance.
Adjusted EBITDA margin represents Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.
Adjusted EPS represents diluted earnings per share (a GAAP measure), excluding the effects of foreign exchange, impairments, amortization from purchase accounting, discrete tax items, and other significant non-recurring adjustments that are not reflective of on-going operational activities (e.g., gains/losses on sale of business, gains/losses on extinguishment and modifications of debt, etc.). Adjusted EPS is calculated using diluted weighted-average number of shares outstanding, including the impact of any potentially dilutive common stock equivalents that are anti-dilutive to GAAP net income (loss) per share but dilutive to Adjusted EPS. Management believes that Adjusted EPS is useful in providing period-to-period comparisons of the results of the Company's ongoing operational performance.
Net debt is a non-GAAP financial measure that represents debt (a GAAP measure, calculated as long-term obligations plus short-term borrowings) minus capitalized debt issuance costs and cash and cash equivalents, including cash and cash equivalents classified as held for sale. Cash and cash equivalents, including cash and cash equivalents held for sale, are subtracted from the GAAP measure because they could be used to reduce the Company’s debt obligations. Management believes that net debt is a useful measure to monitor leverage and evaluate the balance sheet.
Net debt leverage is a non-GAAP financial measure that represents the ratio of Net debt as of a particular balance sheet date to Adjusted EBITDA for the last twelve months prior to such date. Management believes that net debt leverage is a useful measure to assess Brightstar's financial strength and ability to incur incremental indebtedness when making key investment decisions.
Free cash flow is a non-GAAP financial measure that represents cash flow from operations (a GAAP measure) less capital expenditures. Management believes free cash flow is a useful measure of liquidity and an additional basis for assessing Brightstar’s ability to fund its activities, including debt service and distribution of earnings to shareholders.
Constant currency is a non-GAAP adjustment to certain financial measures that expresses current financial data using the prior-year/period exchange rate (i.e., the exchange rate used in preparing the financial statements for the prior year). Management believes that constant currency is a useful measure to compare period-to-period results without regard to the impact of fluctuating foreign currency exchange rates.

Management believes that net debt is a useful measure to monitor leverage and evaluate the balance sheet. Management believes that net debt leverage is a useful measure to assess the Company’s financial strength and ability to incur incremental indebtedness when making key investment decisions. Free cash flow is a non-GAAP financial measure that represents cash flow from operations (a GAAP measure) less capital expenditures. Management believes free cash flow is a useful measure of liquidity and an additional basis for assessing the Company’s ability to fund its activities, including debt service and distribution of earnings to shareholders. Constant currency is a non-GAAP adjustment to certain financial measures that expresses the current financial data using the prior-year/period exchange rate (i.e., the exchange rate used in preparing the financial statements for the prior year).
