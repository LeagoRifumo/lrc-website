Dexcom Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
SAN DIEGO - (BUSINESS WIRE-October 29, 2026) - DexCom, Inc. (Nasdaq: DXCM) today reported its financial results as of and for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:
•Revenue grew 11% year-over-year to $1.344 billion on a reported basis and 11% year-over-year on an organic1 basis.
•U.S. revenue grew 14% and international revenue grew 5% on a reported basis and 5% on an organic1 basis, all on a year-over-year basis.
•GAAP operating income of $327.2 million or 24.3% of revenue, an increase of 420 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP operating income* of $339.5 million or 25.3% of reported revenue, an increase of 270 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Third Quarter 2026 Strategic Highlights:
•Subsequent to the quarter, published the CONNECT randomized clinical trial in The New England Journal of Medicine, showcasing outcomes driven by Dexcom G7 that redefine the standard of care for people with Type 2 diabetes not on insulin.
•Launched the fully redesigned Stelo experience, with enhanced glucose insights, simplified meal logging, and more detailed nutritional information.
•Advanced the rollout of the Dexcom G7 15 Day System in the U.S. and secured first two G7 15 Day international regulatory clearances, positioning for continued momentum in the transition of the global base to the extended wear platform.
“This quarter, we continued to build momentum across the business, delivering strong revenue growth and margin progression, strengthening our clinical evidence, and advancing key product and operational initiatives,” said Jake Leach, Dexcom’s president and CEO. “This performance enabled us to again raise our full-year revenue and operating margin guidance and reinforces our confidence in our ability to deliver on the long-term objectives we outlined at Investor Day.”
2026 Annual Guidance
Dexcom is raising the midpoint of fiscal year 2026 Revenue guidance, reiterating fiscal year 2026 Non-GAAP Gross Profit Margin guidance, and increasing fiscal year 2026 guidance for Non-GAAP Operating Margin and Adjusted EBITDA Margin to the following levels:
•Revenue of $5.20 - $5.25 billion (approximately 12-13% growth)
•Non-GAAP Gross Profit Margin of approximately 64%
•Non-GAAP Operating Margin of approximately 24%
•Adjusted EBITDA Margin of approximately 32%
The company also announced a share repurchase authorization of up to $1.0 billion in conjunction with third quarter results.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Revenue: In the third quarter of 2026, worldwide revenue grew 11% to $1.344 billion on a reported basis, up from $1.209 billion in the third quarter of 2025.
Gross Profit: GAAP gross profit totaled $864.4 million or 64.3% of revenue for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $731.4 million or 60.5% of revenue in the third quarter of 2025.
1 Third quarter 2026 organic revenue was $1.346 billion and excludes $1.7 million of foreign exchange impact and $0.7 million of acquired non-CGM revenue.
Non-GAAP gross profit* totaled $875.3 million or 65.1% of reported revenue for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $741.3 million or 61.3% of reported revenue in the third quarter of 2025.
Operating Income: GAAP operating income for the third quarter of 2026 was $327.2 million or 24.3% of revenue, compared to GAAP operating income of $242.5 million or 20.1% of revenue for the third quarter of 2025.
Non-GAAP operating income* for the third quarter of 2026 was $339.5 million or 25.3% of reported revenue, compared to non-GAAP operating income of $272.9 million or 22.6% of reported revenue for the third quarter of 2025.
Net Income and Diluted Net Income Per Share: GAAP net income was $275.1 million, or $0.72 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to GAAP net income of $283.8 million, or $0.70 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.
Non-GAAP net income* was $275.0 million, or $0.73 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to non-GAAP net income of $242.5 million, or $0.61 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. The third quarter 2026 non-GAAP net income excludes $11.9 million of amortization of intangible assets, $0.4 million of business transition and other significant items, and $12.4 million of tax adjustments.
Cash and Liquidity: As of September 30, 2026, Dexcom held $1.82 billion in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities and our revolving credit facility remains undrawn. The cash balance represents significant financial and strategic flexibility as Dexcom continues to expand production capacity and explore new market opportunities.

ABOUT NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES
The accompanying press release dated October 29, 2026 contains non-GAAP financial measures. These non-GAAP financial measures include organic revenue, non-GAAP gross profit margin, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP diluted net income per share, and non-GAAP diluted weighted average shares outstanding, as well as Adjusted EBITDA.
We report non-GAAP financial measures in addition to, and not as a substitute for or as superior to, measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with GAAP. We use these non-GAAP financial measures for financial and operational decision making and period-to-period comparisons. We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures provide useful information about operating results, enhance the overall understanding of our operating performance and future prospects, and allow for greater transparency with respect to key metrics used by senior management in our financial and operational decision making. Our non-GAAP financial measures exclude amounts that we do not consider part of ongoing operating results when planning and forecasting and when assessing the performance of the organization and our senior management. While we compute non-GAAP financial measures using a consistent method from quarter to quarter and year to year, we may consider whether other significant items that arise in the future should be excluded from our non-GAAP financial measures.
These non-GAAP financial measures are not based on any comprehensive set of accounting rules or principles, differ from GAAP measures with the same names, and may differ from non-GAAP financial measures with the same or similar names that are used by other companies.
We believe that non-GAAP measures have limitations in that they do not reflect all of the amounts associated with our results of operations as determined in accordance with GAAP and that these financial measures should only be used to evaluate our results of operations in conjunction with the corresponding GAAP financial measures. We encourage investors to carefully consider our results under GAAP, as well as our supplemental non-GAAP information and the reconciliations between these presentations, to more fully understand our business.
Management believes organic revenue is a meaningful metric to investors as it provides a more consistent comparison of Dexcom’s revenue to prior periods as well as to industry peers. We exclude the following items from organic revenue:
•The effect of non-CGM revenue acquired or divested in the trailing twelve months; and
•The effect of foreign currency fluctuations
Management believes that the presentation of operating results that exclude these items provides useful supplemental information to investors and facilitates the analysis of our core operating results and comparison of operating results across reporting periods. Management believes that this supplemental non-GAAP information is therefore useful to investors in analyzing and assessing our past and future operating performance.

Our policy is to exclude the following items from non-GAAP financial measures for non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP net income, and non-GAAP diluted net income per share:
•Amortization of acquired intangible assets;
•Business transition and related costs associated with acquisition and divestiture, integration and business transition activities, including severance, relocation, consulting, leasehold exit costs, third-party merger and acquisition costs, and other non-recurring significant items;
•Income or loss from equity investments, which includes realized and unrealized gains or losses from marketable and non-marketable equity securities. These amounts may reflect changes in value due to observable price changes or impairments;
•Third-party intellectual property litigation costs in connection with Dexcom’s patent infringement litigation against Abbott Diabetes Care, Inc.;
•Litigation settlement costs;
•Gain or loss on extinguishment of debt; and
•Adjustments related to taxes for the excluded items above, as well as excess benefits or tax deficiencies from share-based compensation, and the quarterly impact of other discrete items
Adjusted EBITDA excludes non-cash operating charges for share-based compensation (including equity-related charges associated with severance, restructuring, or other business transition activities), depreciation and amortization as well as non-operating items such as interest income, interest expense, gain or loss on extinguishment of debt, income or loss from equity investments, and income tax expense or benefit. For the reasons explained above, Adjusted EBITDA also excludes business transition and other significant items, litigation settlement costs, and intellectual property litigation costs.
