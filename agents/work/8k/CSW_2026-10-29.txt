CSW Industrials Reports Impressive Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter with Record Results; Contractor Solutions Segment Delivers 13% Organic Growth
DALLAS, October 29, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) - CSW Industrials, Inc. (NYSE: CSW or the "Company") today reported results for the fiscal 2027 second quarter period ended September 30, 2026.
Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter Highlights (comparisons to fiscal 2026 second quarter)
•Total revenue increased 31% to a record of $361 million, driven by both acquisitions and organic growth
•Contractor Solutions Segment delivered strong organic revenue growth of 13%
•Earnings per diluted share ("EPS") of $2.90 increased 20% compared to $2.41, driven primarily by increased revenue
•Adjusted EPS, which excludes the amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets and nonrecurring expenses, was a record second quarter $3.66 and increased 24% compared to $2.96
•Net income attributable to CSW of $47 million increased 16% compared to $41 million
•Adjusted EBITDA increased 33% to a record second quarter $97 million, capitalizing on increased revenue
•Cash flows from operations were a record $95 million, increasing 54%
•Net debt of $734 million at the end of the quarter, reflecting a debt paydown of $91 million during the quarter and resulting in a net leverage ratio (Net Debt to EBITDA), in accordance with our credit facility, of 2.10x, within our stated target range of 1-3x, and improved from our first fiscal quarter-end
Fiscal 2027 First Half Highlights (comparisons to fiscal 2026 first half)
•Total revenue increased 32% to a record half of $712 million, driven by acquisitions during the last twelve months and organic growth
•Contractor Solutions Segment delivered strong organic revenue growth of 9%
•EPS improved 23% to a record half of $5.94, compared to $4.84
•Adjusted EPS of $7.50, increased 29% when compared to $5.81
•Net income attributable to CSW increased 19% to $97 million, compared to $82 million
•Adjusted EBITDA increased 40% to a record half of $198 million, leveraging revenue growth
•Cash flows from operations were a record $171 million, increasing 40%
•Repaid $105 million of debt since fiscal year-end due to strong operating cash flows
•Returned total cash of $39 million to shareholders through share repurchases of $29 million and dividends of $10 million
Comments from the Chairman, President, and Chief Executive Officer
Joseph B. Armes, CSW Industrials’ Chairman, President, and Chief Executive Officer, commented, "Today we reported all-time-record revenue, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EPS, and
operating cash flow for the first half of fiscal 2027 underscoring the wisdom of our strategy, the excellence of our execution, and the resilience of our business. Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter Consolidated Results
Fiscal second quarter revenue was $361.4 million, an $84.5 million or 30.5% increase over the prior year period. Total revenue growth included $56.3 million or 20.3% inorganic growth contributed by acquisitions completed over the last twelve months, which are reported within the Contractor Solutions and Specialized Reliability Solutions segments, as well as an increase in organic revenue of $28.2 million or 10.2%, comprised of 12.9% growth in Contractor Solutions and 12.7% growth in Specialized Reliability Solutions, offset by lower revenue in Engineered Building Solutions.
Gross margin contracted 100 bps to 42.0%, or 90 bps to 42.1% as adjusted, compared to 43.0% in the prior year period. The adjusted gross margin decrease was primarily a result of increased ocean and domestic freight expense as well as rising raw materials costs. Gross profit in the fiscal second quarter was $151.9 million, or $152.3 million adjusted, representing 27.5%, or 27.8% adjusted, growth over $119.2 million in the prior year period. Adjustments made to gross profit and gross margin in the quarter included acquisition-related integration expenses.
Operating expenses were $76.4 million, or $75.6 million adjusted. Adjustments in the quarter were primarily comprised of $0.7 million in nonrecurring expenses related to integration of completed acquisitions. Operating expenses in the prior period were $62.4 million, or $60.6 million adjusted. Operating expenses were higher in the current period due to acquired companies' additional expenses, including amortization of intangible assets. Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue were 21.1%, or 20.9% adjusted, lower than the prior year period of 22.5%, or 21.9% adjusted, leveraging increased revenue, as well as restructuring savings and synergy realization.
Operating income in the current period was $75.5 million, or $76.8 million adjusted, compared to $56.8 million, or $58.5 million adjusted in the prior year period. Operating income as a percentage of revenue was 20.9%, or 21.2% adjusted, compared to 20.5%, or 21.1% adjusted in the prior year period. The adjusted operating income margin was comparable to the prior year period.
Interest expense, net of interest income, was $12.5 million, as compared to $1.3 million in the prior year period. Interest expense in the quarter resulted from the Term Loan A and increased borrowings under our revolving line of credit, due to our acquisitions and share repurchases in the last year.

In the current period, reported net income attributable to CSW improved 16.4% to $47.3 million, compared to $40.7 million in the prior year period. EPS was $2.90 per diluted share, an increase of 20.3% as compared to $2.41 per diluted share in the prior year period, driven by both contributions from recent acquisitions and organic growth, partially offset by higher interest expense and intangible amortization, as well as lower share count. Excluding the amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets and nonrecurring expenses, adjusted EPS increased 23.6% to a record $3.66 per diluted share, compared to $2.96 per diluted share in the prior year period.
Fiscal 2027 second quarter adjusted EBITDA increased 32.6% to a record $96.7 million, up from $72.9 million in the prior year period. Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 50 bps to 26.8%, compared to 26.3% in the prior year period, due to the previously mentioned improvement in operating expense leverage, offset somewhat by gross margin contraction.
The Company’s effective tax rate for the fiscal second quarter was 24.1%, or 24.5% adjusted, as compared to 26.4% in the prior year period. The adjusted effective tax rate was favorably impacted by the discount on an energy tax credit purchase.
The quarterly cash flows from operations were $95.4 million, as compared to $61.8 million in the prior year period, an increase of 54.4%. Free cash flows, defined as cash flows from operations minus capital expenditures, was $91.9 million, compared to $58.7 million in the prior year period, an increase of $33.2 million, or 56.5%. The increases were primarily due to strong working capital management and higher earnings.
Including cash on hand, our net debt according to our credit facility covenant calculation was $734 million, resulting in a Net Debt-to-EBITDA leverage ratio of 2.10x. The Company ended the quarter with $766.5 million outstanding on the revolving line of credit and the Term Loan A. The revolving line of credit and Term Loan A will have an interest rate of one-month SOFR plus 175 basis points following the fiscal second quarter covenant calculation submission, per terms of the credit facility. As a reminder, in the third quarter of fiscal 2026, the Company entered into a three-year syndicated interest rate hedge to lock in the SOFR rate at 3.416% for the first $300.0 million borrowing under the Term Loan A.
Following quarter-end, the Company announced a regular quarterly cash dividend in the amount of $0.30 per share, to be paid on November 13, 2026, to shareholders of record on October 30, 2026. This represents the Company's thirty-first consecutive quarterly dividend.
Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter Segment Results
Contractor Solutions segment revenue was $285.8 million, a $77.3 million or 37.1% increase over the prior year period, comprised of inorganic growth of 24.2% or $50.5 million from the MARS Parts acquisition and a 12.9% or $26.8 million increase in organic revenue from both pricing actions and volume growth. Segment operating income was $69.7 million, or $70.5 million adjusted to exclude $0.8 million of nonrecurring expenses related to acquisition integration, compared to $53.4 million, or $54.9 million adjusted, in the prior year period. The increase in operating profit resulted from the inclusion of the MARS Parts acquisition, organic volume growth, and pricing actions, partially offset by raw materials cost inflation and increased ocean and domestic freight expense. Segment operating income margin for the fiscal second quarter was 24.4%, or 24.7% adjusted, as compared to 25.6%, or 26.3% adjusted, primarily due to increased costs of materials and freight expenses. Segment adjusted EBITDA in the fiscal second quarter increased 31.5% to $88.9 million, or 31.1% of revenue, compared to $67.6 million, or 32.4% of revenue, in the prior year period. Fiscal 2027 First Half Consolidated Results
Fiscal first half revenue was a record $712.1 million, representing 31.7% growth from $540.6 million in the prior year period, with growth coming from the Contractor Solutions and Specialized Reliability Solutions reporting segments. Of the $171.5 million total growth, $129.3 million was inorganic from acquisitions in the last twelve months, complemented with a $42.2 million or 7.8% increase in organic revenue.
Gross profit in the fiscal first half was $309.3 million, or $310.3 million adjusted, representing $75.7 million or 32.3% growth from $234.6 million in the prior year period, with the incremental profit resulting predominantly from revenue growth, partially offset by increases in material costs and freight expense. Gross profit as a percentage of sales was 43.4%, or 43.6% adjusted, which was comparable to 43.4% in the prior year period. Adjustments made to gross profit and gross margin in the first half include acquisition-related integration expenses and costs related to the Greco Plan.

Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue were 21.6%, or 21.3% adjusted, which was an improvement compared to the prior year period of 22.7% or 22.4% adjusted. Adjustments in the first half were comprised of $1.8 million in nonrecurring expenses related to integration of completed acquisitions, and $0.1 million related to the Greco Plan. Operating expenses in the current year period were $153.9 million or $152.0 million adjusted as compared to $123.0 million or $121.2 million in the prior year period. The additional expenses were related to recent acquisitions including amortization of intangible assets.
In the current period, operating income was $155.4 million or $158.3 million adjusted to exclude the aforementioned adjustments, as compared to $111.7 million, or $113.4 million adjusted, in the prior year period. The incremental operating income resulted primarily from increased revenue. Operating income margin in the current period increased to 21.8% or 22.2% adjusted, compared to the prior year period of 20.7% or 21.0% adjusted due to the improved leverage of operating expenses.
Interest expense, net was $25.2 million, compared to $2.3 million in the prior year period. The increase of $22.9 million was a result of a higher debt balance throughout the first half of the year resulting from the Term Loan A and increased borrowings against the revolver, as well as elevated interest rate.
Other expense, net was $0.2 million, compared to other income, net of $0.5 million in the prior year period. The change of $0.7 million was primarily related to losses/gains arising from transactions in currencies other than functional currencies, as well as a non-cash $0.3 million tax indemnification asset related to the Falcon acquisition that was released and recognized in other expense.
Net income attributable to CSW increased to $97.1 million, compared to the prior year period of $81.6 million primarily as a result of higher revenue. EPS was $5.94, compared to $4.84 in the prior period. Excluding the amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets and the aforementioned nonrecurring expenses, adjusted EPS was $7.50 vs. $5.81, an increase over the prior year period of 29.1%, driven by contributions from recent acquisitions and organic growth, partially offset by higher interest expense and lower share count.
Fiscal 2027 first half adjusted EBITDA increased 40.0% to $198.3 million from $141.7 million in the prior year period. Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of revenue increased 160 bps to 27.8%, compared to 26.2%, in the prior year period, leveraging our increased revenue.
Net cash provided by operating activities for the fiscal 2027 first half was $171.0 million, an increase of 39.7% compared to $122.5 million in the prior year's first half due to increased profit and strong working capital management. Free cash flows, defined as cash flows from operations minus capital expenditures, was $161.5 million compared to $116.5 million in the prior year period, an increase of $45.1 million, or 38.7%. The Company paid down $105.0 million of debt in the first half, utilizing our strong operating cash flow.
The Company’s effective tax rate for the fiscal first half was 24.9%, or 25.1% adjusted, which remained in line with 25.4% in the prior year.

Fiscal 2027 First Half Segment Results
Contractor Solutions segment revenue was $561.8 million, a $156.6 million or 38.6% increase from the prior year period. Revenue growth was comprised of inorganic growth from acquisitions during the last twelve months ($118.2 million, or 29.2%, of growth), and organic growth of $38.4 million or 9.5% due to increased pricing and unit volumes. Organic unit volumes were up in the first half of the current fiscal year due to end market improvement and leveraging our extended product portfolio. Segment operating income in the current year period was $144.7 million, or $146.4 million adjusted to exclude $1.7 million in acquisition-related expenses, compared to $106.1 million, or $107.7 million adjusted in the prior year period. The incremental profit resulted primarily from the inclusion of recent acquisitions, pricing actions, and organic volume growth, partially offset by increased raw materials costs and higher ocean and domestic freight. Segment operating income margin was 25.8%, or 26.1% adjusted, compared to 26.2%, or 26.6% adjusted, in the prior year period, driven primarily by increased raw material costs and higher freight costs. Segment adjusted EBITDA in the current period was $183.3 million, or 32.6% of revenue, compared to $132.6 million, or 32.7% of revenue in the prior year period.
Specialized Reliability Solutions segment revenue grew to $97.7 million, a $22.1 million or 29.2% increase from the prior year period of $75.6 million, with growth in all end markets served. In the current year period, segment operating income was $17.0 million, or $17.8 million adjusted, compared to the prior year period of $10.3 million. Segment operating income margin was 17.4% or 18.2% adjusted, compared to 13.7% in the prior year period. The improvement in segment operating income resulted from the addition of higher margin acquisitions, pricing actions, savings from restructuring actions, and synergy realization. Segment adjusted EBITDA in the current period was $20.7 million, or 21.2% of revenue, compared to $12.9 million, or 17.1% of revenue in the prior year period.
Engineered Building Solutions segment revenue was $57.3 million, a $6.5 million or 10.2% decrease over the prior year period of $63.8 million, driven primarily by the Greco Plan. Segment operating income was $8.0 million, or $8.4 million adjusted. This represents a 5.1% decrease from prior year period operating income of $8.8 million, or 13.8% of revenue. Segment adjusted EBITDA in the current period was $8.7 million, or 15.1% of revenue, compared to $9.7 million, or 15.1% of revenue in the prior year period.
Excluding the Greco businesses, Engineered Building Solutions segment revenue was $46.1 million, a 4.7% increase compared to $44.0 million in the prior year period. Segment adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA margin in the fiscal first half were $10.5 million and 22.8%, respectively, compared to $8.7 million and 19.8%, respectively, in the prior year period.

About CSW Industrials, Inc.
CSW Industrials is a diversified industrial growth company with industry-leading operations in three segments: Contractor Solutions, Specialized Reliability Solutions, and Engineered Building Solutions. CSW provides niche, value-added products with two essential commonalities: performance and reliability.
