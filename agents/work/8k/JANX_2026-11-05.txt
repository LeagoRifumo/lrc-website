Initial clinical data from the Phase 1 healthy-volunteer study of JANX011 to be presented during an investor webcast on November 9, 2026

JANX007 Phase 1b development continues across taxane-naïve monotherapy and darolutamide combination cohorts

Strong balance sheet with $945.7 million in cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments supports advancement of multiple clinical-stage programs
SAN DIEGO, November 5, 2026 – Janux Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: JANX) (Janux), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing a broad pipeline of novel immunotherapies, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.
“During the third quarter, we continued to execute across our clinical portfolio, including dosing the first patient in the Phase 1 study of JANX013 in combination with JANX007,” said David Campbell, Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of Janux. “We are also preparing to present initial clinical data from JANX011 on November 9. JANX011 was designed to achieve deep B-cell depletion while limiting cytokine release. The upcoming clinical update will provide an initial look at how this differentiated approach translates into humans.”
BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS AND RECENT DEVELOPMENTS:
Clinical and Pipeline Progress
•
JANX007 (PSMA-TRACTr) continues to advance through Phase 1b development in metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC), with ongoing dose optimization and expansion in taxane-naïve patients, including an actively enrolling cohort evaluating JANX007 in combination with darolutamide.
•
JANX014 (PSMA-TRACTr), a double-masked, PSMA-targeted tumor-activated T-cell engager, continues enrollment in its Phase 1 clinical study evaluating safety, pharmacokinetics and preliminary antitumor activity in patients with mCRPC.
•
JANX011 (CD19-ARM) continues to be evaluated in a Phase 1 clinical study in healthy volunteers. JANX011 is Janux's first clinical candidate from its Adaptive Immune Response Modulator (ARM) platform and was designed to achieve deep and durable B-cell depletion while limiting cytokine release. Initial clinical data from the study will be presented during an investor webcast on November 9, 2026.
•
Janux recently disclosed that JANX011 selectively engages TRBV6-expressing T cells rather than broadly engaging the CD3+ T cell population, a differentiated approach intended to focus immune activation on a defined T cell subset.
•
JANX013 (PSMA-TRACIr) The first patient has been dosed in the Phase 1 study evaluating JANX013 in combination with JANX007 in patients with mCRPC, representing the first clinical evaluation of Janux's TRACIr platform.
Upcoming Milestones
•
Initial clinical data from the Phase 1 healthy-volunteer study of JANX011 will be presented during an investor webcast, providing the first clinical evaluation of Janux's ARM platform.
•
Additional clinical data from the Phase 1b study of JANX007 are expected to be presented at a medical congress in the first half of 2027.
THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS:
•
Cash and cash equivalents and short-term investments: As of September 30, 2026, Janux reported cash and cash equivalents and short-term investments of $945.7 million, compared to $966.6 million as of December 31, 2025.
•
Research and development expenses: Research and development expenses were $30.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $34.6 million for the comparable period in 2025.
•
General and administrative expenses: General and administrative expenses were $10.0 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $10.6 million for the comparable period in 2025.
•
Net loss: Net loss was $21.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $24.3 million for the comparable period in 2025.
About Janux Therapeutics
Janux is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing a broad pipeline of novel immunotherapies by applying its proprietary technology to its Tumor Activated T Cell Engager (TRACTr), Tumor Activated Immunomodulator (TRACIr), and Adaptive Immune Response Modulator (ARM) platforms.
Janux’s lead clinical candidate, JANX007, is a PSMA-targeted TRACTr being investigated in a Phase 1 clinical trial in patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC). The Company is also advancing additional PSMA-targeted programs, including JANX014, a double-masked PSMA-TRACTr being evaluated in a Phase 1 clinical trial, and JANX013, a CD28 co-stimulatory TRACIr designed for combination with JANX007 and being evaluated in a Phase 1 clinical trial.
Beyond its prostate cancer programs, Janux’s pipeline includes JANX011, a CD19-targeted ARM being evaluated in a Phase 1 clinical trial in healthy adult volunteers for potential treatment of autoimmune diseases.
Janux continues to generate additional TRACTr, TRACIr, and ARM programs for potential future development.
