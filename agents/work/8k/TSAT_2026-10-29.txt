Telesat reports results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026
OTTAWA, CANADA – October 29, 2026 – Telesat (Nasdaq and TSX: TSAT), one of the world’s largest and most innovative satellite operators, today announced its financial results for the three- and nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026. All amounts are in Canadian dollars and reported under IFRS® Accounting Standards unless otherwise noted.
“I’m pleased with the strong progress we made last quarter and throughout the year on our key strategic objectives,” commented Dan Goldberg, Telesat’s President and CEO. “The third quarter marked the signing of our landmark $2.7 billion initial agreement with the Government of Canada for the ESCP-P program, a major milestone in validating the large and growing opportunity for Telesat Lightspeed in the sovereign satellite communications space. We see this initial ESCP-P contract as just the first step in a very attractive pipeline of government and defence opportunities with Canada and allied countries, and we’re moving in a focused way to secure those opportunities and turn them into contracted backlog. In parallel, we’re making steady progress on the initial Telesat Lightspeed constellation, now expanded to a planned 225 satellites, and the ground infrastructure that will support it. Telesat Lightspeed is fully funded and we expect to commence high latitude commercial service around the end of Q1 2028.”
“In our GEO business, we continue to be laser-focused on maximizing the revenue and cash flow from our existing GEO satellite fleet while at the same time taking a highly disciplined approach to cost management. Our GEO business has performed consistent with our expectations in the first nine months of the year, and we are reiterating our 2026 GEO revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance.”
“Finally, we remain focused on refinancing the Telesat GEO debt, as it begins to mature later this year. Our advisors continue to seek good-faith engagement with the Telesat GEO debtholders and to evaluate other alternatives as well, with the goal of reaching an outcome that is fair to all stakeholders.”
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Telesat reported consolidated revenue of $81 million, a decrease of 20% ($20 million) compared to the same period of the prior year, and adjusted EBITDA1 of $12 million, a decrease of 74% ($35 million) from the third quarter of 2025. Foreign exchange did not materially impact these year-on-year comparisons. Excluding the impact of higher expenses related to our debt refinancing process, adjusted EBITDA decreased 41%. Telesat net loss for the quarter was $157 million compared to a $121 million loss in the prior year. The increased net loss was primarily due to lower adjusted EBITDA and a larger loss associated with increases in the fair value of the Telesat Lightspeed Financing Warrants, partially offset by a foreign exchange gain in the third quarter of 2026 compared to a foreign exchange loss in the third quarter of 2025.
In our GEO segment, revenue for the quarter was $79 million, a decrease of 21% ($22 million) from the same period in 2025. The revenue decline was driven primarily by non-renewal of a broadcast contract in 2025 and a reduction in contracted capacity for a broadcast customer in 2026, combined with a reduction in service to a North American broadband customer and the removal from service of certain geostationary satellites. GEO segment adjusted EBITDA for the quarter was $36 million, a 43% ($27 million) decline from the comparable period in 2025, reflecting lower revenue and higher expenses related to our debt refinancing process. Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 included $25 million in expenses relating to our debt refinancing process. Excluding these costs, adjusted EBITDA for the GEO segment was 23% lower than the prior period and the adjusted EBITDA margin1 was 78%, compared to 79% in the same period of 2025.
In our LEO segment, we invested $368 million in the Telesat Lightspeed program in the third quarter of 2026, comprising $24 million in operating expense and $344 million in capital expenditure.
For the nine-month period ending September 30, 2026, Telesat reported consolidated revenue of $247 million, a decrease of 24% ($77 million) compared to the prior year, and adjusted EBITDA1 of $69 million, a decrease of 60% ($104 million) from the first nine months of 2025. Foreign exchange does not materially impact these year-on-year comparisons. Excluding the impact of higher expenses related to our debt refinancing process, adjusted EBITDA decreased 42%. Telesat net loss for the first nine months of 2026 was $867 million, compared to a $97 million net loss in the prior year. The variance was primarily due to a loss associated with an increase in the fair value of the Telesat Lightspeed Financing Warrants, a GEO goodwill impairment charge, lower adjusted EBITDA, and a foreign exchange loss.
GEO segment revenue in the nine months ending September 30, 2026 was $243 million, a 24% decline ($79 million) from the same period in 2025. GEO segment adjusted EBITDA for the nine months ending September 30, 2026 was $135 million, a 40% decline from the comparable period in 2025. Excluding expenses related to our debt refinancing process, adjusted EBITDA for the GEO segment was 28% lower than the prior period, and the adjusted EBITDA margin was 74% in the first nine months of 2026, compared to 78% in the same period of 2025.
Telesat invested $705 million in the Telesat Lightspeed program in the first nine months of 2026, of which $64 million was recorded as operating expense and $641 million as capital expenditure.
As of September 30, 2026, backlog2 for our GEO segment totaled approximately $779 million and LEO backlog2 totaled approximately $5.6 billion. GEO satellite utilization was approximately 59% at September 30, 2026.
Business Highlights
● | In August, Telesat announced a $2.7 billion agreement, including option periods, with Canada’s Defence Investment Agency to deliver Telesat Lightspeed Military Ka-band services to the Canadian Armed Forces for the Enhanced Satellite Communications Project – Polar (ESCP-P) program. The contract is for a period of 15 years, including option years, with service under the contract beginning once Telesat Lightspeed enters commercial service. The contract includes milestone-based payments totaling $2.0 billion which are expected to be received between Q3 2026 and Q4 2028. The milestone payments will principally be used to fund the expansion of the Telesat Lightspeed constellation from a planned 156 to 225 satellites.

About Telesat
Telesat Corporation (Nasdaq and TSX: TSAT) is a global satellite operator and leader in advanced satellite communications. Through its Telesat Lightspeed Low Earth Orbit (LEO) network, Telesat is delivering a new generation of high-performance connectivity for telecom, enterprise and government customers. Designed around open standards, Telesat Lightspeed enables seamless integration of high-throughput, low latency satellite services into existing terrestrial networks and operations, extending and augmenting network capabilities with greater control, visibility and assurance. Its advanced, software-defined architecture operates in both commercial and military Ka-band spectrum, enabling secure, resilient broadband communications with protection against evolving threats. Backed by nearly 60 years of innovation and engineering excellence, Telesat is uniquely positioned to help customers solve their most complex communications challenges with scalable and future-ready solutions. End Notes
1 | Non-IFRS Accounting Standards Measures – Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin are non-IFRS Accounting Standards measures. EBITDA is defined as “Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization.” Adjusted EBITDA is used to measure Telesat’s financial performance. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as operating income (less certain operating expenses such as share-based compensation expenses and unusual and non-recurring items, including restructuring related expenses) before interest expense, taxes, depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA margin is used to measure Telesat’s operating performance. Adjusted EBITDA margin is defined as the ratio of Adjusted EBITDA to revenue.

Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin are not standardized financial measures under IFRS Accounting Standards and might not be comparable to similar financial measures disclosed by other issuers. Adjusted EBITDA allows investors and Telesat to compare Telesat’s operating results with that of competitors exclusive of depreciation and amortization, interest and investment income, interest expense, taxes and certain other expenses. Financial results of competitors in the satellite services industry have significant variations that can result from timing of capital expenditures, the amount of intangible assets recorded, the differences in assets’ lives, the timing and amount of investments, the effects of other income (expense), and unusual and non-recurring items. The use of Adjusted EBITDA assists investors and Telesat to compare operating results exclusive of these items. Competitors in the satellite services industry have significantly different capital structures. Telesat believes that the use of Adjusted EBITDA improves comparability of performance by excluding interest expense.
Telesat believes that the use of Adjusted EBITDA and the Adjusted EBITDA margin along with IFRS Accounting Standards measures enhances the understanding of our operating results and is useful to investors and us in comparing performance with competitors, estimating enterprise value and making investment decisions. Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin as used here may not be the same as similarly titled measures reported by competitors. Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin should be used in conjunction with IFRS Accounting Standards measures and are not presented as a substitute for cash flows from operations as a measure of our liquidity or as a substitute for net income (loss) as an indicator of our operating performance.
