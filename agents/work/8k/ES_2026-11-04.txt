Eversource Energy Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
HARTFORD, Conn. and BOSTON, Mass. (November 4, 2026) – Eversource Energy (“Eversource” or the “Company”) (NYSE: ES) today reported GAAP and non-GAAP earnings of $346.0 million, or $0.92 per share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared with GAAP earnings of $367.5 million, or $0.99 per share and non-GAAP recurring earnings of $442.5 million1, or $1.19 per share1, in the third quarter of 2025. There were no non-GAAP adjustments in the third quarter of 2026.
For the first nine months of 2026, Eversource reported GAAP earnings of $1.01 billion, or $2.67 per share, compared with $1.27 billion, or $3.44 per share, for the first nine months of 2025. Non-GAAP recurring earnings totaled $1.33 billion1, or $3.52 per share1, in the first nine months of 2026, compared with $1.35 billion1, or $3.64 per share1, in the first nine months of 2025.
GAAP results for the first nine months of 2026 include a non-cash, after-tax charge of $111.4 million, or $0.30 per share, related to the sale of Aquarion Water Company on June 30, 2026, an after-tax charge of $164.0 million, or $0.43 per share, related to an increase in Eversource's expected future payments related to its offshore wind divestiture as part of the September 30, 2024, sale of South Fork Wind and Revolution Wind, and an after-tax charge of $43.9 million, or $0.12 per share, related to estimated refunds associated with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) decision of March 19, 2026, that reduced the return on equity (ROE) rate for New England transmission owners from 10.57% to 9.57%. GAAP results for the first nine months of 2025 include an aggregate net after-tax loss of $75.0 million, or $0.20 per share, related to an increase in Eversource’s offshore wind contingent liability, net of tax benefits associated with the tax losses on the sale of the offshore wind projects. These charges were excluded from non-GAAP results in their respective quarters.
“We continued to execute well across our key strategic initiatives and our employees continued to deliver safe and reliable energy service and advance the investments necessary to support our customers and communities,” said Joe Nolan, Eversource Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. “We were pleased to receive a constructive decision from PURA on previously deferred storm costs, which provides a clear path to securitization that ultimately will benefit our Connecticut customers. This outcome demonstrates the importance of constructive, transparent engagement with our regulators as we work collaboratively to balance customer affordability, system reliability, and the investments needed to maintain and strengthen the energy infrastructure for our customers. Looking ahead, our focus remains on disciplined execution and successfully delivering on the operational, regulatory, and financial objectives we established for 2026.”
The Company is updating its 2026 non-GAAP recurring earnings guidance to be in the range of $4.62 per share1 and $4.70 per share1 reflecting an increased midpoint of $4.66 per share1. It also reaffirms its cumulative long-term earnings per share growth rate within the range of 5 to 7 percent through 2030, using the aforementioned revised 2026 non-GAAP earnings guidance midpoint of $4.66 per share1 as the base year. Eversource expects annual earnings growth towards the upper half of its long-term guidance by 2028.
Electric Transmission
Eversource Energy’s transmission segment, excluding the non-recurring FERC ROE refund charge noted previously, earned $182.0 million in the third quarter of 2026 and $590.0 million1 in the first nine months of 2026, compared with earnings of $185.5 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $593.0 million in the first nine months of 2025. Transmission segment results in both 2026 periods reflect the impact from the reduction to the allowed ROE mentioned previously, as well as higher interest expense, partially offset by continued investment in Eversource’s electric transmission system.
Electric Distribution
Eversource Energy’s electric distribution segment earned $200.9 million in the third quarter of 2026 and $574.1 million in the first nine months of 2026, compared with earnings of $221.6 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $571.6 million in the first nine months of 2025. Third quarter and year-to-date results were unfavorably impacted by disallowed storm costs from PURA's July 29, 2026, final decision on CL&P's storm prudency proceeding, as well as higher depreciation, interest expense and property taxes. These unfavorable earnings impacts were partially offset in both periods by higher revenues from base distribution rate increases at Eversource’s Massachusetts and New Hampshire electric businesses, and continued investments in the Company’s distribution system.
Natural Gas Distribution
Eversource Energy’s natural gas distribution segment had a loss of $43.4 million in the third quarter of 2026 and earned $281.6 million in the first nine months of 2026, compared with a loss of $16.8 million in the third quarter of 2025 and earnings of $236.9 million in the first nine months of 2025. Lower results in the third quarter are due primarily to lower revenues at Eversource's Massachusetts natural gas businesses driven by a change in the seasonal rate design that shifted the recovery of quarterly revenues, as well as higher depreciation expense. Improved results in the first nine months were due primarily to base distribution rate increases at Eversource’s Massachusetts and Connecticut natural gas businesses, effective November 1, 2025, to recover continued investment in the Company’s natural gas infrastructure, partially offset by higher depreciation expense, operations and maintenance expense, property taxes, interest expense, a higher effective income tax rate, and the impact from timing of certain cost recovery items.
Water Distribution
Lower year-to-date results for Eversource Energy’s water distribution segment are due to the absence of earnings in the third quarter of 2026 due to the sale of the Aquarion water business on June 30, 2026.The water segment, excluding the charge related to the sale of Aquarion noted previously, earned $17.9 million1 in the first nine months of 2026, compared with earnings of $36.8 million in the first nine months of 2025.
Eversource Energy Parent and Other Companies
Eversource Energy parent and other companies, excluding the increases in the offshore wind contingent liability noted previously, earned $6.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 and had a loss of $137.8 million1 in the first nine months of 2026, compared with earnings of $33.3 million1 in the third quarter of 2025 and a loss of $92.2 million1 in the first nine months of 2025. Lower results in 2026 for both periods are driven by a higher effective income tax rate and higher interest expense.
