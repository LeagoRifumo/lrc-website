Alvotech Announces Financial Results for the Nine Months to September 30, 2026, with a Business Update
REYKJAVIK, Iceland, Nov. 11, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Alvotech (NASDAQ: ALVO; ALVO-SDB) (“Alvotech” or the “Company”), a global biotechnology company specializing in the development and manufacture of biosimilar medicines for patients worldwide, today announced financial results for the nine months to September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.
Supplemental information, including the management presentation providing additional details and a business update, is available on our website: https://alvotech.com/financials.1

Recent Business Highlights
The FDA accepted Alvotech’s Biologics License Application (BLA) for AVT80, a proposed biosimilar to Entyvio® (vedolizumab) in prefilled syringe and autoinjector presentations for subcutaneous administration. Alvotech is responsible for development and manufacturing, while Teva has licensed the U.S. commercial rights.
Alvotech and its commercial partner Advanz Pharma submitted the first known regulatory submission worldwide for a proposed biosimilar to Eylea HD (aflibercept 8 mg). The marketing application was submitted to the European Medicines Agency.
Alvotech signed an agreement with Lotus Pharmaceuticals covering the commercialization rights in the U.S. and selected Asian markets for AVT34, a proposed biosimilar to Imfinzi® (durvalumab) and AVT87, a proposed biosimilar to Hemlibra® (emicizumab). Alvotech retains the right to also commercialize both products directly in the U.S. alongside Lotus.
Alvotech and LOTTE Biologics entered a long-term partnership under which LOTTE biologics will manufacture drug substance for multiple biosimilars at its facility in Syracuse, New York. The arrangement expands Alvotech’s U.S. manufacturing footprint, adding additional capacity and flexibility to its global supply network.
The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approved a new process for manufacturing Simlandi®, Alvotech’s biosimilar to Humira®, in the Reykjavik manufacturing facility. The new process more than doubles output per batch, allowing the same quantity of Simlandi to be produced in fewer batches and thus freeing up manufacturing capacity for other products.
The FDA also approved the production of drug substance for Simlandi® in a second production suite at Alvotech’s Reykjavik site, resulting in added flexibility and increased manufacturing capacity.
Lisa Graver, Chief Executive Officer, comments:
“Our commercial performance strengthened in the third quarter, with product and service revenue reaching $58 million, up 6% sequentially and 78% year over year. Following the completion of the manufacturing improvement program in the first half of the year, we are continuing to increase supply to meet strong underlying demand across our markets.
“The two recent FDA approvals for Simlandi at our Reykjavík facility represent significant milestones. The addition of a second approved manufacturing suite and an enhanced process that more than doubles output per batch give us greater capacity and operational flexibility to support our expanding commercial portfolio.
“We have also made important advances in our pipeline, including regulatory milestones for our proposed biosimilars to Entyvio and high-dose Eylea. Our agreement with Lotus creates an opportunity for Alvotech to participate directly in U.S. commercialization, while our partnerships with LOTTE Biologics and Fujifilm strengthen our future manufacturing network. Our priority now is to translate this progress into planned product deliveries in the fourth quarter, while continuing to build the platform for long-term growth.”
Outlook for the Full Year 2026
Alvotech maintains its full-year 2026 guidance for total revenue of $650-$700 million and EBITDA of $180-$220 million, with performance now expected toward the lower end of the range. The Company remains focused on executing planned product deliveries in the fourth quarter, as production and commercial supply continue to ramp up following the manufacturing improvement program completed in the first half of the year.

About Alvotech
Alvotech is a biotechnology company, founded by Robert Wessman, focused solely on the development and manufacture of biosimilar medicines for patients worldwide. Alvotech seeks to be a global leader in the biosimilar space by delivering high-quality, cost-effective products and services, enabled by a fully integrated approach and broad in-house capabilities. Five biosimilars are already approved and marketed in multiple global markets, including biosimilars to Humira® (adalimumab), Stelara® (ustekinumab), Simponi® (golimumab), Eylea® (aflibercept) and Prolia®/Xgeva® (denosumab). The current development pipeline includes disclosed biosimilar candidates aimed at treating autoimmune disorders, eye disorders, and cancer. Alvotech has formed a network of strategic commercial partnerships to provide global reach and leverage local expertise in markets that include the United States, Europe, Japan, China, and other Asian countries and large parts of South America, Africa and the Middle East. For more information, please visit https://www.alvotech.com. None of the information on the Alvotech website shall be deemed part of this press release.
