ReNew Announces Results for the Second Quarter
of Fiscal 2027 (Q2 FY27) and First Half of Fiscal 2027 (H1 FY27),
both ended September 30, 2026
November 2, 2026: ReNew Energy Global Plc (“ReNew”, “the Company”, “we” or “our”) (Nasdaq: RNW), a leading decarbonization solutions company, today announced its unaudited consolidated IFRS results for Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27.
Operating Highlights:
•
As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s portfolio consisted of ~20.5 GW (including 1.7 GW/6.2 GWh of BESS). Additionally, ReNew has 6.4 GW of solar module and 2.5 GW of solar cell manufacturing capacities and is expanding its solar cell manufacturing capacity by another 4 GW, which is expected to commence commercial operations in Q4 FY27.
•
The Company’s commissioned capacity has increased 19.5% year-over-year to ~13.7 GW (including 100 MW/250 MWh of BESS) as of September 30, 2026. Subsequently, the Company commissioned 112 MW of solar, increasing commissioned capacity as of date to ~13.8 GW.
•
Total Income (or total revenue) for H1 FY27 was INR 97,483 million (US$ 1,017 million), compared to INR 79,715 million (US$ 832 million) for H1 FY26. Net profit for H1 FY27 was INR 12,375 million (US$ 129 million) compared to INR 9,806 million (US$ 102 million) for H1 FY26. Adjusted EBITDA for H1 FY27 was INR 63,980 million (US$ 668 million), as against INR 53,459 million (US$ 558 million) for H1 FY26.
•
Total Income (or total revenue) for Q2 FY27 was INR 49,619 million (US$ 518 million), compared to INR 38,557 million (US$ 402 million) for Q2 FY26. Net profit for Q2 FY27 was INR 6,422 million (US$ 67 million) compared to INR 4,675 million (US$ 49 million) for Q2 FY26. Adjusted EBITDA for Q2 FY27 was INR 33,588 million (US$ 350 million), as against INR 26,240 million (US$ 274 million) in Q2 FY26.
•
Revenue from sale of power for H1 FY27 was INR 60,524 million (US$ 632 million) compared to INR 51,548 million (US$ 538 million) for H1 FY26. Revenue from sale of power for Q2 FY27 was INR 33,775 million (US$ 352 million), compared to INR 26,076 million (US$ 272 million) for Q2 FY26.
•
Total income (or total revenue) for H1 FY27 includes external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounting to INR 29,239 million (US$ 305 million) compared to INR 23,351 million (US$ 244 million) for H1 FY26. Net profit and Adjusted EBITDA for H1 FY27 from external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations were INR 6,572 million (US$ 69 million) and INR 9,732 million (US$ 102 million) compared to INR 5,767 million (US$ 60 million) and INR 8,621 million (US$ 90 million) for H1 FY26 respectively.
•
Total income (or total revenue) for Q2 FY27 from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations was INR 12,462 million (US$ 130 million) compared to INR 10,128 million (US$ 106 million) for Q2 FY26. Net profit and Adjusted EBITDA for Q2 FY27 from external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations were INR 2,658 million (US$ 28 million) and INR 4,081 million (US$ 43 million) compared to INR 2,206 million (US$ 23 million) and INR 3,329 million (US$ 35 million) respectively for Q2 FY26.
Note: the translation of Indian rupee amounts into U.S. dollars has been made at INR 95.83 to US$ 1.00. See note below for more information.
Key Operating Metrics
In Q2 FY27, we commissioned 628 MW, which included 516 MW of solar and 112 MW of wind capacity. In H1 FY27, we commissioned 1,244 MW, of which 1,112 MW was solar and 132 MW was wind. Subsequent to the end of the quarter, the Company commissioned 112 MW of solar in October 2026, increasing commissioned capacity as of date to ~13.8 GW.
As of September 30, 2026, our total portfolio consisted of ~20.5 GW (including 1.7 GW/6.2 GWh of BESS) and commissioned capacity was ~13.7 GW (including 100 MW/250 MWh of BESS), of which ~5.7 GW were wind, ~7.8 GW were solar and 99 MW were hydro. Our commissioned capacity increased by 19.5% year over year, net of the 100 MW of assets sold as part of our capital recycling strategy.
Electricity Sold
Total electricity sold in Q2 FY27 was 8,989 million kWh, an increase of 29.0% over Q2 FY26. Electricity sold in Q2 FY27 from wind assets was 5,459 million kWh, an increase of 30.6% from Q2 FY26. Electricity sold in Q2 FY27 from solar assets was 3,327 million kWh, an increase of 28.4% over Q2 FY26. Electricity sold for Q2 FY27 from hydro assets was 203 million kWh, an increase of 3.6% over Q2 FY26.
Total electricity sold in H1 FY27 was 16,366 million kWh, an increase of 18.6% over H1 FY26. Electricity sold in H1 FY27 from wind assets was 9,355 million kWh, an increase of 21.1% over H1 FY26. Electricity sold in H1 FY27 from solar assets was 6,739 million kWh, an increase of 16.9% over H1 FY26. Electricity sold in H1 FY27 from hydro assets was 272 million kWh, a decrease of 11.4% from H1 FY26.
Plant Load Factor
Our weighted average PLF for Q2 FY27 for wind assets was 43.9%, compared to 37.3% for Q2 FY26. The PLF for Q2 FY27 for solar assets was 19.8%, compared to 19.4% for Q2 FY26.
Our weighted average PLF for H1 FY27 for wind assets was 38.0%, compared to 35.1% for H1 FY26. The PLF for H1 FY27 for solar assets was 21.0%, compared to 21.9% for H1 FY26.
Total Income
Total Income for Q2 FY27 was INR 49,619 million (US$ 518 million) compared to INR 38,557 million (US$ 402 million) in Q2 FY26. Total income benefited from higher revenue driven by an increase in operational capacity, higher PLF and external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations, partially offset by revenue forgone on divested assets. Total Income includes finance income and fair value change in warrants of INR 1,136 million (US$ 12 million).
Total income for Q2 FY27 from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations was INR 12,462 million (US$ 130 million) compared to INR 10,128 million (US$ 106 million) in Q2 FY26, an increase of 23% primarily due to higher sales.
Total Income for H1 FY27 was INR 97,483 million (US$ 1,017 million) compared to INR 79,715 million (US$ 832 million) in H1 FY26. Total income benefited from higher revenue driven by an increase in operational capacity and external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations and gains from asset sales, partially offset by lower solar PLF and revenue forgone on divested assets. Total Income includes finance income and fair value change in warrants of INR 2,320 million (US$ 24 million).
Total income for H1 FY27 includes external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounting to INR 29,239 million (US$ 305 million) compared to INR 23,351 million (US$ 244 million) in H1 FY26.
Raw Materials and Consumables Used (net of change in inventory)
Raw materials and consumables used for Q2 FY27 were INR 6,546 million (US$ 68 million) compared to INR 5,607 million (US$ 59 million) for Q2 FY26, a change of 16.7%. Raw materials and consumables used for Q2 FY27 are primarily attributable to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations.
Raw materials and consumables used for H1 FY27 were INR 16,884 million (US$ 176 million) compared to INR 12,298 million (US$ 128 million) for H1 FY26. Raw materials and consumables used for H1 FY27 are primarily attributable to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations.
Employee Benefits Expense
Employee benefits expense for Q2 FY27 was INR 1,937 million (US$ 20 million), compared to INR 1,420 million (US$ 15 million) for Q2 FY26, an increase of 36.4%, primarily due to increased headcount supporting our solar module and cell manufacturing operations and other employee costs related to 3E.
Employee benefits expense for Q2 FY27 includes expense attributable to our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounting to INR 443 million (US$ 5 million) compared to INR 429 million (US$ 4 million) for Q2 FY26.
Employee benefits expense for H1 FY27 was INR 3,927 million (US$ 41 million), compared to INR 3,038 million (US$ 32 million) for H1 FY26, an increase of 29.3% primarily due to increased headcount supporting our solar module and cell manufacturing operations and other employee costs related to 3E.
Employee benefits expense attributable to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations for H1 FY27 was INR 1,042 million (US$11 million) compared to INR 875 million (US$ 9 million) for H1 FY26.
Other Expenses
Other Expenses for Q2 FY27 were INR 6,737 million (US$ 70 million), compared to INR 4,331 million (US$ 45 million) for Q2 FY26. The increase was primarily due to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations, capacity-linked higher operations and maintenance costs since Q2 FY26 and provisions created for contractual obligations in Q2 FY27.
Other Expenses for Q2 FY27 include expenses attributable to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounting to INR 1,339 million (US$ 14 million) compared to INR 577 million (US$ 6 million) for Q2 FY26.
Other Expenses for H1 FY27 were INR 11,069 million (US$ 116 million), compared to INR 8,947 million (US$ 93 million) for H1 FY26. The increase was primarily related to our solar module and cell manufacturing operations, capacity-linked higher operations and maintenance costs, higher provisions created for contractual obligations and travel-related expenditure.
Other Expenses for H1 FY27 include expenses attributable to our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounting to INR 2,151 million (US$ 22 million) compared to INR 1,332 million (US$ 14 million) for H1 FY26.
Finance Costs and Fair Value Change in Derivative Instruments
Finance costs and fair value change in derivative instruments for Q2 FY27 were INR 16,936 million (US$ 177 million), compared to INR 15,326 million (US$ 160 million) for Q2 FY26. The increase in finance costs was primarily due to an increase in operational assets from Q2 FY26, and finance costs associated with manufacturing operations.
Finance costs and fair value change in derivative instruments for Q2 FY27 include expense attributable to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounting to INR 514 million (US$ 5 million) compared to INR 332 million (US$ 3 million) for Q2 FY26.
Finance costs and fair value change in derivative instruments for H1 FY27 were INR 32,465 million (US$ 339 million) compared to INR 29,779 million (US$ 311 million) for H1 FY26. The increase in finance costs was primarily due to an increase in operational assets from H1 FY26.
Finance costs for our solar module and cell manufacturing operations for H1 FY27 were INR 1,018 million (US$ 11 million) compared to INR 874 million (US$ 9 million) for H1 FY26.
Net Profit
The net profit for Q2 FY27 was INR 6,422 million (US$ 67 million) compared to INR 4,675 million (US$ 49 million) for Q2 FY26, with the increase primarily driven by higher operating revenues and external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations, partially offset by higher scale-linked financing costs and depreciation related to projects commissioned since Q2 FY26.
Net profit for Q2 FY27 attributable to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounted to INR 2,658 million (US$ 28 million) compared to INR 2,206 million (US$ 23 million) for Q2 FY26.
The net profit for H1 FY27 was INR 12,375 million (US$ 129 million) compared to net profit of INR 9,806 million (US$ 102 million) for H1 FY26, with the increase primarily driven by higher operating revenues, external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations and gains from asset sales, partially offset by higher scale linked financing costs and depreciation related to projects commissioned since Q2 FY26.
Net profit for H1 FY27 attributable to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounted to INR 6,572 million (US$ 69 million) compared to INR 5,767 million (US$ 60 million) for H1 FY26.
Adjusted EBITDA
Adjusted EBITDA for Q2 FY27 was INR 33,588 million (US$ 350 million), compared to INR 26,240 million (US$ 274 million) in Q2 FY26.
Adjusted EBITDA for Q2 FY27 attributable to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounted to INR 4,081 million (US$ 43 million) compared to INR 3,329 million (US$ 35 million) for Q2 FY26.
Adjusted EBITDA for H1 FY27 was INR 63,980 million (US$ 668 million) compared to INR 53,459 million (US$ 558 million) for H1 FY26.
Adjusted EBITDA for H1 FY27 attributable to external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations amounted to INR 9,732 million (US$ 102 million) compared to INR 8,621 million (US$ 90 million) for H1 FY26.
Adjusted EBITDA is a non-IFRS measure. For more information, see “Use of Non-IFRS Financial Measures” elsewhere in this release. IFRS refers to International Financial Reporting Standards as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board. In addition, reconciliations of non-IFRS measures to IFRS financial measures, and operating results are included at the end of this release.
FY 27 Guidance
The Company continues to expect to complete the construction of 1.6 to 2.4 GW by the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027 (“FY27”). The Company’s Adjusted EBITDA and Cash Flow to Equity guidance for FY27 are subject to weather and resource availability being similar to FY26. The Company continues to anticipate net gains from asset sales, which are part of ReNew’s capital recycling strategy, and has included INR 1–2 billion related to asset sales in the Adjusted EBITDA guidance. The Company continues to expect external sales from our solar module and cell manufacturing operations and has included INR 12–14 billion of Adjusted EBITDA against such sales in this guidance.

For Cash Flow to Equity, they also include the amount and timing of interest and tax payments and refunds, normalized loan repayments and other non-cash items. These items depend on future events and market conditions outside the Company's control, such as interest and exchange rates, and could have a significant effect on the Company's future IFRS results.

The increase was primarily driven by higher operating profit, partially offset by higher working capital deployment. Use of Non-IFRS Financial Measures
Adjusted EBITDA
Adjusted EBITDA is a non-IFRS financial measure. We present Adjusted EBITDA as a supplemental measure of our performance. This measurement is not recognized in accordance with IFRS and should not be viewed as an alternative to IFRS measures of performance. The presentation of Adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as an inference that our future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items.
The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as profit/(loss) for the period plus (a) current and deferred tax, (b) finance costs and fair value changes on derivative instruments, (c) change in fair value of warrants (if recorded as expense), (d) depreciation and amortization, (e) listing expenses, (f) share based payment and other expense related to listing, less (g) share in profit/(loss) of jointly controlled entities, (h) finance income and fair value change in derivative
instruments, and (i) change in fair value of warrants (if recorded as income). We believe Adjusted EBITDA is useful to investors in assessing our ongoing financial performance and provides improved comparability on a like-to-like basis between periods through the exclusion of certain items that management believes are not indicative of our operational profitability and that may obscure underlying business results and trends. However, this measure should not be considered in isolation or viewed as a substitute for net income or other measures of performance determined in accordance with IFRS. Moreover, Adjusted EBITDA as used herein is not necessarily comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies due to potential inconsistencies in the methods of calculation.
Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider it in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under IFRS. Some of these limitations include:
•
it does not reflect cash expenditures or future requirements for capital expenditures or contractual commitments or foreign exchange gain/loss;
•
it does not reflect changes in, or cash requirements for, working capital;
•
it does not reflect significant interest expense or the cash requirements necessary to service interest or principal payments on outstanding debt;
•
it does not reflect payments made or future requirements for income taxes; and
•
although depreciation, amortization and impairment are non-cash charges, the assets being depreciated and amortized will often have to be replaced or paid in the future and Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect cash requirements for such replacements or payments.
Investors are encouraged to evaluate each adjustment and the reasons we consider it appropriate for supplemental analysis. For more information, please see the reconciliations of profit/(loss) for the period to Adjusted EBITDA towards the end of this earnings release.
Cash Flow to Equity (CFe)
CFe is a Non-IFRS financial measure. We present CFe as a supplemental measure of our performance. This measurement is not recognized in accordance with IFRS and should not be viewed as an alternative to IFRS measures of performance. The presentation of CFe should not be construed as an inference that our future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items.
We define CFe as Adjusted EBITDA plus non-cash expense and finance income and fair value change in derivative instruments, less interest expense paid, tax paid/(refund) and normalized loan repayments. Normalized loan repayments are repayments of scheduled payments as per the loan agreement. Ad hoc payments and refinancing (including planned arrangements/borrowings in previous periods) are not included in normalized loan repayments. The definition also excludes changes in net working capital and investing activities.
We believe IFRS metrics, such as net income (loss) and cash from operating activities, do not provide the same level of visibility into the performance and prospects of our operating business as a result of the long-term capital-intensive nature of our businesses, non-cash depreciation and amortization, cash used for debt servicing as well as investments and costs related to the growth of our business.
