INTRODUCTION
The following Management’s Discussion and Analysis (“MD&A”) of IAMGOLD Corporation (“IAMGOLD” or the “Company”), dated November 5, 2026, should be read in conjunction with IAMGOLD’s unaudited condensed consolidated interim financial statements and related notes for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 ("consolidated interim financial statements"). This MD&A should be read in conjunction with IAMGOLD's audited annual consolidated financial statements and related notes as at and for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, and the related MD&A included in the 2025 annual report. All figures in this MD&A are in U.S. dollars and tabular dollar amounts are in millions, unless stated otherwise. Additional information on IAMGOLD can be found at www.iamgold.com. However, the information on the website is not in any way incorporated in or made a part of this MD&A.
ABOUT IAMGOLD
IAMGOLD is an intermediate gold producer and developer based in Canada with operating mines in North America and West Africa, including Côté Gold (Canada), Westwood (Canada) and Essakane (Burkina Faso). The Côté Gold Mine (“Côté” or “Côté Gold”) is among the largest gold mines in production in Canada, which IAMGOLD operates in a 70|30 partnership with Sumitomo Metal Mining Co. Ltd. (“SMM”). In addition, the Company has an established portfolio of early stage and advanced exploration projects within high potential mining districts, including the large-scale Nelligan Mining Complex located in Quebec, Canada.
IAMGOLD employs approximately 3,900 people and is committed to maintaining its culture of accountable mining through high standards of Environmental, Social and Governance (“ESG”) practices. IAMGOLD is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE:IAG) and the Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX:IMG).

Health and Safety

IAMGOLD continues to advance its critical risk management program and is currently on track to achieve our target of all sites achieving verification objectives.
Environmental

Social Performance
During the third quarter of 2026, IAMGOLD continued engagements with local communities and supported community-based initiatives. Notably, Essakane supported Sahelian Arts and Culture Days for Peace in Dori, an event that brought people together through music, art, and cultural expressions. Westwood hosted Westwood en Ville in partnership with the Abitibiwinni First Nation, showcasing cultural activities to foster broader community engagement.
Indigenous Relations
As a Canadian business committed to responding to the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada’s Calls to Action2, IAMGOLD is continuing to advance a company-wide initiative to articulate how it works with Indigenous peoples beyond reconciliation, towards a future that builds upon the Company’s experiences and reflects its values.
Earlier this year, IAMGOLD launched a 5-pathway Reconciliation Plan, along with new mandatory awareness training for all its Canada-based employees titled “Indigenous Peoples of Canada: An Introduction to History and Relationship”. By the end of September 2026, 95% of Canadian employees had completed the training. Site-specific reconciliation plans aligned with IAMGOLD’s corporate reconciliation framework will reflect the distinct operating contexts and priorities of partner Indigenous communities. In September, Côté Gold published their plan and Westwood is continuing to advance their planning.
Culture and Inclusion
IAMGOLD includes annual objectives to support its efforts in integrating culture and inclusion into the strategy and corporate scorecard, for the annual objectives, and tracks metrics in site and corporate reports for visibility and measurement. As of September 30, 2026, women accounted for 33% of the Company’s executive leadership team.
Governance
The Board of Directors of IAMGOLD (the “Board”) maintains diversity and renewal guidelines that reflect governance best practices. Membership should comprise, at a minimum, the greater of (i) two women directors, or (ii) 30% women directors. The average tenure of the Board should not exceed ten years, and no director should serve as the chair of the Board or the chair of any committee for more than ten consecutive years.
On August 6, 2026, the Company announced that Ms. Catherine McLeod-Seltzer was appointed to the Company’s Board of Directors effective September 1, 2026. Ms. McLeod-Seltzer brings more than four decades of mining industry experience as both a senior executive and public-company director. In 2026, Ms. McLeod-Seltzer was inducted into the Canadian Mining Hall of Fame.
With the inclusion of Ms. McLeod-Seltzer, the Board has returned to meeting all its diversity guidelines. The Board maintains an active commitment to governance best practices in respect of membership composition.
The average tenure of directors on the Board is approximately three years.
