Tenaris Announces 2026 Third Quarter Results

Luxembourg, November 4, 2026. - Tenaris S.A. (NYSE and Mexico: TS and EXM Italy: TEN) (“Tenaris”) today announced its results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 in comparison with its results for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

*EBITDA in the third quarter of 2025 includes a $34 million gain recorded for the return of U.S. antidumping deposits paid on OCTG imports from Argentina for which the duty rate had been revised downwards. If this gain was not included EBITDA would have amounted to $719 million, or 24.1% of sales.
In the third quarter, our sales recovered 5% sequentially on higher demand in North America and an increase in shipments to customers in the Middle East. Our EBITDA margin also recovered sequentially, despite higher raw material, energy and logistic costs, reflecting a favorable product mix and efficient industrial performance, as well as the postponement of maintenance programs at some of our facilities. Net income increased 6% sequentially to $521 million, as the improvement in operating income was partially offset by a higher income tax charge and lower financial results and earnings from non-consolidated companies.
During the quarter, our free cash flow amounted to $268 million, which included an increase of $270 million in working capital, and our net cash position amounted to $3.8 billion at September 30, 2026.
Market Background and Outlook
Oil and gas drilling activity has been increasing in the USA, Canada and Argentina. In addition, customers around the world are moving forward with investments in cost-competitive offshore projects, as the industry increases its focus on security and diversification of supply.
In the United States, OCTG prices have been responding to the increase in demand and higher raw material and logistic costs, while OCTG inventories are gradually drawn down.
In the Middle East, the conflict continues to cause disruption to shipping through the strait of Hormuz, although flows of oil have increased using ship-to-ship transfers. Drilling activity in the region has slowly started to recover as oil producers increase production and exports from the region.
In the fourth quarter, we expect an increase in our sales and EBITDA reflecting higher OCTG demand and prices in North America and an increase in shipments to offshore projects. The fourth quarter should set a new quarterly baseline for our sales and EBITDA going into 2027, assuming no significant change in the geopolitical context.

Net sales of tubular products and services increased 4% sequentially and 2% year on year. Overall volumes increased 3% sequentially with a 2% increase in seamless volumes and a 10% increase in welded. Average selling prices increased 2% sequentially, excluding services performed on third party pipes. Sequentially, sales increased in North America, thanks to higher sales of OCTG in the region and higher sales of line pipe to distributors in the United States. In Europe, sales declined due to lower sales of OCTG in Turkey and seasonally lower sales of mechanical pipe to distributors. In Asia Pacific, Middle East and Africa, sales increased due to deliveries to Kuwait and Iraq and higher sales in North Africa.
Operating results from tubular products and services amounted to a gain of $557 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to a gain of $465 million in the previous quarter and a gain of $592 million in the third quarter of 2025. Operating income in the quarter improved sequentially following the improvement in sales, with higher absorption of fixed costs, lower maintenance expenses and a more efficient industrial performance offsetting an increase in raw material, logistic and energy costs.

Exhibit I – Alternative performance measures
Alternative performance measures should be considered in addition to, not as substitute for or superior to, other measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with IFRS.
EBITDA, Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization
EBITDA provides an analysis of the operating results excluding depreciation and amortization and impairments, as they are recurring non-cash variables which can vary substantially from company to company depending on accounting policies and the accounting value of the assets. EBITDA is an approximation to pre-tax operating cash flow and reflects cash generation before working capital variation. EBITDA is widely used by investors when evaluating businesses (multiples valuation), as well as by rating agencies and creditors to evaluate the level of debt, comparing EBITDA with net debt.
EBITDA is calculated in the following manner:
EBITDA = Net income for the period + Income tax charges +/- Equity in Earnings (losses) of non-consolidated companies +/- Financial results + Depreciation and amortization +/- Impairment charges/(reversals).
EBITDA is a non-IFRS alternative performance measure.

Free cash flow
Free cash flow is a measure of financial performance, calculated as operating cash flow less capital expenditures. FCF represents the cash that a company is able to generate after spending the money required to maintain or expand its asset base.
Free cash flow is calculated in the following manner:
Free cash flow = Net cash (used in) provided by operating activities - Capital expenditures.
Free cash flow is a non-IFRS alternative performance measure.

Net cash / (debt) is calculated in the following manner:
Net cash = Cash and cash equivalents + Other investments (Current and Non-Current)+/- Derivatives hedging borrowings and investments - Borrowings (Current and Non-Current).

Operating working capital days
Operating working capital is the difference between the main operating components of current assets and current liabilities. Operating working capital is a measure of a company’s operational efficiency, and short-term financial health.
Operating working capital days is calculated in the following manner:
Operating working capital days = [(Inventories + Trade receivables – Trade payables – Customer advances) / Annualized quarterly sales ] x 365.
Operating working capital days is a non-IFRS alternative performance measure.
