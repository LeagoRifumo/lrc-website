Manchester United PLC Reports First Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results
Key Points
· | Achieved total revenues of £146.1 million and adjusted EBITDA of £27.5 million, compared to £140.3 million and £26.9 million respectively in the first quarter of fiscal 2026;
· | Operating loss for the quarter was £3.0 million, compared with an operating profit of £13.0 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2026;
· | Operating loss before profit on disposal of player registrations was £23.7 million, compared with £32.0 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2026;
· | We announced that Mauricio Pochettino has joined as Head Coach of our men’s first team after Michael Carrick left his position earlier this month;
· | Our men’s first team returned to the UEFA Champions League this season and is currently participating in the League Stage, having won 2 of our opening 4 matches;
· | Our women’s first team is currently 4th in the Women’s Super League, unbeaten in their last 13 games;
· | We continue to make progress on our ambition to build a new stadium and transform the wider Old Trafford area;
· | For Fiscal 2027, the company reiterates its prior guidance of total revenues of £740 million to £760 million and adjusted EBITDA of £205 million to £225 million

MANCHESTER, England – 19 November 2026 – Manchester United (NYSE: MANU; the “Company” and the “Group”) today announced financial results for the 2027 fiscal first quarter ended 30 September 2026.
Management Commentary
Omar Berrada, Chief Executive Officer, commented, “Our revenue remains strong and on track, while we continue to grow sustainably. We are pleased to welcome Mauricio Pochettino and his staff to the club and our priority is to support them as we look continuously to improve our performances on the pitch. We are pleased with the progress Eva Olid has made with our women’s team and how well the team has adapted to her approach.”
Outlook
For fiscal 2027, the Company reiterates its full year revenue guidance of £740 million to £760 million and adjusted EBITDA guidance of £205 million to £225 million. The club remains committed to, and in compliance with, both the Premier League’s Sustainability and Systemic Resilience Rules and Squad Cost Ratio and UEFA’s Club Licensing and Financial Sustainability Regulations.

Revenue Analysis
Commercial
Commercial revenue for the quarter was £84.1 million, a decrease of £0.1 million, or 0.1%, over the prior year quarter.
· | Sponsorship revenue was £45.6 million, a decrease of £1.4 million, or 3.0%, over the prior year quarter, primarily due to the men’s first team undertaking a smaller pre-season tour for the 2026/27 season compared to the 2025/26 season due to the timing of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, mostly offset by our new Training Kit partnership with Betway.

· | Retail, Merchandising, Apparel & Product Licensing revenue was £38.5 million, an increase of £1.3 million, or 3.5%, over the prior year quarter.

Broadcasting
Broadcasting revenue for the quarter was £35.2 million, an increase of £5.3 million, or 17.7%, over the prior year quarter, due to our men’s first team participating in the UEFA Champions League in the current year, with no UEFA competition in the prior year, partially offset by the phasing of Premier League matches with 5 taking place in the current year quarter, compared to 6 in the prior year quarter.
Matchday
Matchday revenue for the quarter was £26.8 million, an increase of £0.6 million, or 2.3%, over the prior year quarter. This was driven primarily by the current year quarter including 1 home UEFA Champions League and 1 domestic cup home match, partially offset by one fewer Premier League home match compared to the prior year quarter.
Other Financial Information
Operating expenses
Total operating expenses for the quarter were £169.8 million, a decrease of £2.5 million, or 1.5%, over the prior year quarter. This decrease is explained by category below.
Employee benefit expenses
Employee benefit expenses for the quarter were £79.9 million, an increase of £6.3 million, or 8.6%, over the prior year quarter, primarily due to the men’s first team participating in the UEFA Champions League in the current year.
Other operating expenses
Other operating expenses for the quarter were £38.7 million, a decrease of £1.1 million, or 2.8%, over the prior year quarter.
Depreciation and amortization
Depreciation for the quarter was £4.6 million, a decrease of £0.2 million, or 4.2%, over the prior year quarter. Amortization for the quarter was £46.6 million, a decrease of £7.5 million, or 13.9%, over the prior year quarter, due to changes in the make-up of the first team squad. The unamortized balance of registrations at 30 September 2026 was £601.9 million, compared to £624.1 million at 30 September 2025.
Profit on disposal of intangible assets
Profit on disposal of intangible assets for the quarter was £20.7 million, a decrease of £24.3 million, or 54.0%, from £45.0 million in the prior year quarter.
3
Net finance costs
Net finance costs for the quarter were £14.5 million, compared to net finance costs of £21.4 million in the prior year quarter. This is primarily due to a minor favorable swing in foreign exchange rates resulting in unrealized foreign exchange gains on unhedged USD borrowings in the current year quarter (£0.2 million), compared to a large unfavorable swing resulting in unrealized foreign exchange losses in the prior year quarter (£5.1 million).
Income tax
The income tax expense for the quarter was £0.6 million, compared to an income tax credit of £1.8 million in the prior year quarter.
Cash flows
Overall cash and cash equivalents (including the effects of exchange rate movements) decreased by £11.8 million in the quarter to 30 September 2026 compared to the cash position at 30 June 2026.
Net cash inflow from operating activities for the quarter was £47.7 million, compared to net cash outflow of £1.3 million in the prior year quarter.
Net capital expenditure on property, plant and equipment for the quarter was £4.1 million, a decrease of £12.9 million over the prior year quarter, primarily due to the prior year quarter containing expenditure relating to the finalisation of the redevelopment of our men’s first team facility at Carrington.
Net capital expenditure on intangible assets for the quarter was £143.1 million, an increase of £43.4 million over the prior year quarter, primarily due to decreased proceeds received from player sales in the current year quarter.
Net cash inflow from financing activities for the quarter was £89.7 million, compared to a net cash inflow of £102.7 million in the prior year quarter. This is due to a net drawdown of £90.0 million on our revolving facility in the current year quarter compared to a net drawdown of £105.0 million in the prior year quarter.
Balance sheet
Our USD non-current borrowings as of 30 September 2026 were $775.0 million, an increase of $125.0 million compared to 30 September 2025 as a result of the refinancing of our Senior Secured Notes in June 2026. Taking account of the year-on-year change in the USD/GBP exchange rate from 1.3449 at 30 September 2025 to 1.3279 at 30 September 2026, our non-current borrowings when converted to GBP were £577.4 million, compared to £481.2 million at the prior year quarter.
In addition to non-current borrowings, the Group maintains a revolving facility which varies based on seasonal flow of funds. Current borrowings, inclusive of accrued interest, at 30 September 2026 were £207.1 million compared to £268.0 million at 30 September 2025. As of 30 September 2026, cash and cash equivalents were £55.5 million compared to £80.5 million at the prior year quarter end.

About Manchester United
Manchester United is one of the most popular and successful sports teams in the world, playing one of the most popular spectator sports on Earth. Through our 148-year football heritage we have won 69 trophies, including a record 20 English league titles, enabling us to develop what we believe is one of the world’s leading sports and entertainment brands with a global community of 1.1 billion fans and followers, per latest available survey data from 2019. Our large, passionate and highly engaged fan base provides Manchester United with a worldwide platform to generate significant revenue from multiple sources, including sponsorship, merchandising, product licensing, broadcasting and matchday initiatives which in turn, directly fund our ability to continuously reinvest in the club.

Non-IFRS Measures: Definitions and Use
1. | Adjusted EBITDA

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as profit/(loss) for the period before depreciation, amortization, profit on disposal of intangible assets, net finance income/costs, exceptional items and tax.
Adjusted EBITDA is useful as a measure of comparative operating performance from period to period and among companies as it is reflective of changes in pricing decisions, cost controls and other factors that affect operating performance, and it removes the effect of our asset base (primarily depreciation and amortization), material volatile items (primarily profit on disposal of intangible assets and exceptional items), capital structure (primarily finance income/costs), and items outside the control of our management (primarily taxes). Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider it in isolation, or as a substitute for an analysis of our results as reported under IFRS as issued by the IASB. A reconciliation of profit/(loss) for the period to adjusted EBITDA is presented in supplemental note 2.
2. | Adjusted profit/(loss) for the period (i.e. adjusted net profit/(loss))

Adjusted profit/(loss) for the period is calculated, where appropriate, by adjusting for charges related to exceptional items, foreign exchange losses/gains on unhedged US dollar denominated borrowings and fair value movements on embedded foreign exchange derivatives, subtracting/adding the actual tax credit/expense for the period, and adding the adjusted tax credit/expense for the period (based on a normalized tax rate of 25%; 2025: 25%). The normalized tax rate of 25% is the current UK corporation tax rate. A reconciliation of profit/(loss) for the period to adjusted profit/(loss) for the period is presented in supplemental note 3.
3. Adjusted basic and diluted earnings/(loss) per share
Adjusted basic and diluted earnings/(loss) per share are calculated by dividing the adjusted profit/(loss) for the period by the weighted average number of ordinary shares in issue during the period. Adjusted diluted earnings/(loss) per share is calculated by adjusting the weighted average number of ordinary shares in issue during the period to assume conversion of all dilutive potential ordinary shares. There is one category of dilutive potential ordinary shares: share awards pursuant to the 2012 Equity Incentive Plan (the “Equity Plan”). Share awards pursuant to the Equity Plan are assumed to have been converted into ordinary shares at the beginning of the financial year. Adjusted basic and diluted earnings/(loss) per share are presented in supplemental note 3.
