Tempus Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
CHICAGO, November 2, 2026 — Tempus AI, Inc. (NASDAQ: TEM), a technology company leading the adoption of AI to advance precision medicine, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Q3 was another record quarter, as we delivered $411.5 million of revenue, led by accelerating unit growth of 32% in our oncology business and increasing momentum of 33% revenue growth in Data & Applications,” said Eric Lefkofsky, founder and CEO of Tempus. “These results reinforce the durability of our model as our best-in-class diagnostic platform enhances our data and AI offerings, distinguishes our business from our many competitors, and helps drive our performance quarter after quarter.”
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
For the three-month period ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the same period in 2025:
• Total revenue of $411.5 million, an increase of 23%
• Oncology volume growth accelerated to 32%
• Minimal Residual Disease (MRD) volume of ~12,000 tests, up 300%
• Data & Apps revenue increased 33%, with Insights increasing 29%
• Increasing full year 2026 revenue guidance to $1.60 to $1.605 billion and expect full year Adjusted EBITDA of ~$65 million
• Announced multi-year strategic collaboration with Merck and Moderna to support commercialization of their individualized neoantigen therapy (INT) class of drugs
• In October, announced a strategic collaboration with BMS bringing Tempus’ total data licensing bookings to >$550 million year to date
Third Quarter 2026 Summary Results
For the three-month period ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the same period in 2025:
• Total revenue increased 23% to $411.5 million.
• Diagnostics generated $303.5 million of revenue, representing 20% growth, driven by Oncology volume growth of 32%, offset by Hereditary revenue growth of 3%.
• Data and Applications generated $108.1 million of revenue, representing 33% growth, with Insights growing 29%.
• Gross profit increased 27% to $266.7 million, led by growth in Data and Applications.
• Net loss was $(14.9) million, which included $50.8 million of stock-based compensation expense and related employer payroll taxes and $42.8 million in unrealized gains on marketable securities, compared to a net loss of $(80.0) million in Q3 of 2025.
• Adjusted EBITDA was $25.6 million, compared to $1.5 million in Q3 of 2025.
• Ended the quarter with cash and marketable securities of $854.4 million.
Detailed Operational Highlights
• Announced a strategic collaboration with BMS in October through which Tempus is directly licensing to BMS oncology data and models for up to $137.5 million over a five-year term. In addition, BMS and Pathos are licensing data for patients with Alzheimer's or dementia for $55 million over a five-year term, extending Tempus’ data and modeling platform beyond oncology.
• Announced a multi-year strategic collaboration with Merck and Moderna to support the potential commercialization of intismeran autogene, a novel, potential first-in-class individualized neoantigen therapy (INT) being evaluated in combination with KEYTRUDA (pembrolizumab), Merck’s anti-PD-1 therapy, in patients with completed resected stage IIB-IV melanoma and other cancer types.
• Cleared regulatory review of pending acquisition of Personalis (Nasdaq: PSNL). Personalis' shareholder meeting to approve the acquisition is planned for November 30, 2026.
• Received two 510(k) clearances from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for Tempus ECG-PH and ECG-MR, expanding our non-invasive AI cardiology footprint.
• Announced an initiative to build a research platform containing 100,000 whole genomes linked to longitudinal clinical information over the next several years.

About Tempus
Tempus is a technology company making precision medicine a reality through the practical application of artificial intelligence in healthcare. With one of the world’s largest libraries of de-identified multimodal data and an intelligent operating system that makes that data accessible and actionable, Tempus connects AI, diagnostics and clinical workflows to deliver more personalized patient care and accelerate the discovery, development and delivery of better medicines. By continuously transforming data into insights, Tempus
is building a learning healthcare ecosystem in which every patient can benefit from the treatment and experiences of those who came before.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to the financial information presented in this release in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (GAAP), Tempus also presents adjusted non-GAAP financial measures.
Non-GAAP gross profit is defined as GAAP gross profit, excluding stock-based compensation expense and employer payroll tax related to stock-based compensation (collectively, the “stock-based compensation adjustments”). Non-GAAP gross margin is defined as gross profit, excluding the stock-based compensation adjustments, as a percentage of revenue. Non-GAAP operating expenses are calculated as the sum of technology research and development expense, research and development expense, and selling, general and administrative expense, excluding the stock-based compensation adjustments, acquisition-related expenses, amortization of intangibles due to acquisition, and franchise taxes related to IPO. Non-GAAP income (loss) from operations is defined as loss from operations, adjusted to exclude (i) the stock-based compensation adjustments, (ii) acquisition-related expenses, (iii) franchise taxes related to IPO, and (iv) amortization of intangibles due to acquisition. Non-GAAP net income (loss) is defined as net loss, adjusted to exclude (i) changes in fair value of our marketable equity securities and indemnity-related holdback liabilities, (ii) the stock-based compensation adjustments, (iii) acquisition-related expenses, (iv) amortization of intangibles due to acquisition, (v) losses (gains) from equity method investments, (vi) provision for (benefit from) income taxes, (vii) franchise taxes related to IPO, (viii) amortization of deferred other income from our IP License Agreement with SB Tempus, and (ix) loss on debt extinguishment. Non-GAAP net income (loss) per share is defined as non-GAAP net income (loss) divided by weighted average common shares outstanding, basic and diluted.
Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net loss, adjusted to exclude (i) interest income, (ii) interest expense, (iii) depreciation and amortization, (iv) provision for (benefit from) income taxes, (v) losses (gains) from equity method investments, (vi) changes in fair value of our marketable equity securities and indemnity-related holdback liabilities, (vii) the stock-based compensation adjustments, (viii) acquisition-related expenses, (ix) amortization of deferred other income from our IP License Agreement with SB Tempus, (x) franchise taxes related to our IPO, and (xi) loss on debt extinguishment.
Tempus believes these non-GAAP financial measures are useful to investors and others because they allow for additional information with respect to financial measures used by management in its financial and operational decision-making and they may be used by institutional investors and the analyst community to help them analyze the health of Tempus’ business. In particular, Adjusted EBITDA is a key measurement used by Tempus management to make operating decisions, including those related to analyzing operating expenses, evaluating performance, and performing strategic planning and annual budgeting. However, there are a number of limitations related to the use of non-GAAP financial measures, and these non-GAAP measures should be considered in addition to, not as a substitute for or in isolation from, our financial results prepared in accordance with GAAP. Other companies, including companies in our industry, may calculate these non-GAAP financial measures differently or not at all, which reduces their usefulness as comparative measures.
Tempus does not provide guidance for net loss, the most directly comparable GAAP measure to Adjusted EBITDA, and similarly cannot provide a reconciliation between Tempus’ forecasted Adjusted EBITDA and net loss without unreasonable effort due to the unavailability of reliable estimates for certain components of net loss and the respective reconciliations. These forecasted items are not within Tempus’ control, may vary greatly between periods, and could significantly impact future financial results.
