Corporate Profile
Digital Realty Trust, Inc. (“Digital Realty” or the “company”) owns, acquires, develops, and operates data centers through its operating partnership subsidiary, Digital Realty Trust, L.P. (the “operating partnership”). The company is focused on providing data center, colocation, and interconnection solutions for domestic and international customers across a variety of industry verticals ranging from cloud and information technology services, communications and social networking to financial services, manufacturing, energy, healthcare, and consumer products. As of September 30, 2026, the company’s 311 data centers, including 88 data centers held as investments in unconsolidated entities, contain applications and operations critical to the day-to-day operations of technology industry and corporate enterprise data center customers. Digital Realty’s portfolio is comprised of approximately 3.2 gigawatts of IT capacity, as well as approximately 9.8 gigawatts of buildable IT capacity under active development and held for future development, located throughout North America, Europe, South America, Asia, Australia, and Africa. For additional information, please visit the company’s website at digitalrealty.com.

Digital Realty Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Austin, TX — October 29, 2026 — Digital Realty (NYSE: DLR), the world’s largest cloud- and carrier-neutral data center platform, announced today its financial results for the third quarter of 2026. All per share results are presented on a fully diluted basis.
Highlights
◾ | Reported net income available to common stockholders of $0.48 per share in 3Q26, compared to $0.15 in 3Q25

◾ | Reported FFO per share of $1.97 in 3Q26, compared to $1.65 in 3Q25

◾ | Reported Core FFO per share of $2.10 in 3Q26, compared to $1.89 in 3Q25

◾ | Signed record total bookings during 3Q26 that are expected to generate $855 million of annualized GAAP base rent at 100% share; at Digital Realty’s share, bookings were a record $627 million, including a record $152 million within the 0-1 megawatt plus interconnection category

◾ | Reported rental rate increases on renewal leases of 11.8% on a cash basis in 3Q26

◾ | Reported a record total backlog of $2.5 billion of annualized GAAP base rent at 100% share at the end of 3Q26; at Digital Realty’s share, the backlog was a record $1.8 billion

◾ | Raised 2026 Core FFO per share (excluding net promote) outlook to $8.30 - $8.35 and 2026 Constant-Currency Core FFO per share (excluding net promote) outlook to $8.25 - $8.30

Financial Results
Digital Realty reported total revenues of $1.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, 10% higher than the previous quarter when excluding $201 million of promote income in the second quarter and 21% above the same quarter last year.
The company delivered net income of $194 million in the third quarter of 2026, as well as net income available to common stockholders of $185 million and $0.48 per share, compared to $1.21 per share in the previous quarter and $0.15 per share in the same quarter last year.
Digital Realty generated Adjusted EBITDA of $978 million in the third quarter of 2026, which was in line with the previous quarter and a 13% increase over the same quarter last year.
The company reported Funds From Operations (FFO) of $742 million in the third quarter of 2026, or $1.97 per share, compared to $2.73 per share in the previous quarter and $1.65 per share in the same quarter last year.
Digital Realty delivered Core FFO per share (excluding net promote) of $2.10 in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $2.13 per share in the previous quarter and $1.89 per share in the same quarter last year. Digital Realty delivered Core FFO per share (excluding net promote) of $6.27 for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026. Digital Realty delivered Constant-Currency Core FFO per share (excluding net promote) of $2.10 in the third quarter of 2026 and $6.18 per share for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026.
“Digital Realty extended its track record of execution in the third quarter with record leasing, an expanding backlog, and another quarter of double-digit earnings growth,” said Digital Realty President and Chief Executive Officer Andy Power. “These results underscore the global strength of customer demand for connectivity-rich environments, trusted execution, and a well-capitalized partner with the reach and capabilities to support their evolving requirements across the digital infrastructure landscape.”
Leasing Activity
In the third quarter, Digital Realty signed total bookings that are expected to generate $855 million of annualized GAAP rental revenue, at 100% share; at Digital Realty’s share, total bookings were $627 million, including a $130 million contribution from the 0-1 MW category and a $22 million contribution from interconnection.
The weighted-average lag between new leases signed during the third quarter of 2026 and the contractual commencement date was 17 months. The backlog of signed-but-not-commenced leases at quarter-end was $2.5 billion of annualized GAAP base rent at 100% share, and $1.8 billion at Digital Realty’s share. In addition, Digital Realty also signed renewal leases representing $261 million of annualized cash rental revenue during the quarter. Rental rates on renewal leases signed during the third quarter of 2026 increased 11.8% on a cash basis and 17.0% on a GAAP basis.
