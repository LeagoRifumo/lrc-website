EXHIBIT 99.1
Rhythm Pharmaceuticals Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Update
-- Strong U.S. launch momentum for IMCIVREE® (setmelanotide) for acquired hypothalamic obesity (HO) continues with more than 700 patient start forms as of September 30, 2026 --
-- Third quarter 2026 net product revenue from global sales of IMCIVREE of $92.1 million, an increase of 29% from the prior quarter --
-- Commercial launch of IMCIVREE in Japan for patients with acquired HO underway --
-- Bivamelagon to advance into pivotal development in Prader-Willi syndrome --
-- Rhythm and LG Chem amend bivamelagon licensing agreement --
-- Five-year term loan facility secured, providing access to up to $750 million --
-- Management to host conference call today at 8:00 a.m. ET --
BOSTON, November 3, 2026 – Rhythm Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: RYTM), a global commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on transforming the lives of patients living with rare neuroendocrine diseases, today reported financial results and provided a business update for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Rhythm continues to make strong progress in bringing IMCIVREE® (setmelanotide) to patients with acquired hypothalamic obesity (HO), highlighted by continued commercial momentum in the United States. Our commercial launch in Japan represents another important milestone in a market with the potential to become our second largest,” said David Meeker, M.D., Chairman, Chief Executive Officer and President of Rhythm Pharmaceuticals. “The strength of the U.S. launch through two quarters reinforces both the significant unmet need in acquired HO and our opportunity to transform care for patients living with this serious disease.”
Dr. Meeker added, “We are excited about advancing our next-generation MC4R agonists, beginning with bivamelagon. Strong clinical results have reinforced our confidence in its potential across multiple indications, and we look forward to initiating pivotal trials in acquired HO and Prader-Willi syndrome. Our restructured bivamelagon royalty agreement with LG Chem reflects our strong belief in the future potential of the portfolio. With a strong global foundation built on IMCIVREE, bivamelagon entering Phase 3 development and further development planned for RM-718, there is a clear and compelling path to long-term growth and value creation for Rhythm.”
Recent Business and Development Highlights
•Today, the Company announced it has received more than 700 patient start forms for IMCIVREE for acquired HO from more than 500 prescribers as of September 30, 2026, following approval by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) on March 19, 2026;
•Revenue from global sales of IMCIVREE was $92.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 29% on a sequential basis from the second quarter of 2026. The number of patients on reimbursed therapy globally increased by 20% as compared to the second quarter of 2026.
•U.S.: Revenue of $67.5 million, or 73% of product revenue, was generated in the United States in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $16.5 million or 32% compared to the second quarter of 2026. The sequential quarter-over- quarter growth in U.S. revenue was driven primarily by increased patient demand for IMCIVREE in acquired HO, as well as continued growth in demand in Bardet-Biedl syndrome (BBS).
•Ex-U.S.: Revenue of $24.7 million, or 27% of product revenue, was generated outside the United States, a sequential increase of $4.4 million or 22% compared to the second quarter of 2026. The number of reimbursed patients on IMCIVREE continued to increase in the third quarter of 2026;
•Today, the Company announced that five presentations have been accepted for presentation at ObesityWeek® 2026, taking place November 14-17, 2026. Presentations include:
•Definition and Diagnostic Criteria for Acquired Hypothalamic Obesity: Global Expert Consensus;
•Patient-Reported Outcomes From the TRANSCEND Trial in Acquired Hypothalamic Obesity;
•Efficacy and Safety of Setmelanotide in Acquired Hypothalamic Obesity: Final Phase 3 Trial Results;
•Two-Year Phase 3 Trial Results of Setmelanotide in Patients With Acquired Hypothalamic Obesity; and
•Weight Category Improvement Following Setmelanotide in Patients With Acquired Hypothalamic Obesity.
•On November 2, 2026, the Company announced an amendment to its exclusive license agreement with LG Chem for bivamelagon. Under the terms of the amendment, Rhythm has agreed to pay LG Chem $350 million in fixed payments over four years in order to reduce the low-to-mid single digit royalty in the original agreement to 1% royalty on net product revenue generated by Rhythm’s MC4R portfolio;
•On November 2, 2026, the Company entered into a five-year term loan facility led by KKR's HealthCare Royalty ("Healthcare Royalty") in partnership with Blackstone Life Sciences, providing access to up to $750 million of capital. In connection with entry
into this loan facility the Company entered into an amended and restated Revenue Interest Financing Agreement with Health Care Royalty;
•On October 13, 2026, the Company announced the commercial launch of IMCIVREE in Japan for patients with acquired HO;
•On September 22, 2026, the Company announced Health Canada approval of IMCIVREE for weight management in adult and pediatric patients 4 years of age and older with acquired HO;
•On September 10, 2026, the Company announced the presentation of six abstracts, including three oral presentations and three poster presentations, at the 64th Annual European Society for Paediatric Endocrinology (ESPE) Meeting. Presentations included:
•Real-world data demonstrating reductions in BMI and hunger for up to 18 months in paediatric patients with acquired HO treated with setmelanotide through France's early-access program;
•A Phase 3 post-hoc analysis demonstrating improvements across multiple cardiometabolic risk indices with setmelanotide in paediatric patients with acquired HO;
•Long-term weight outcomes with setmelanotide in paediatric patients with BBS, POMC deficiency and LEPR deficiency; and
•Additional analyses evaluating metabolic syndrome severity and risk in acquired HO and clinical characteristics associated with BBS.
•On August 24, 2026, the Company announced that Japan’s Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) approved IMCIVREE for patients with acquired HO in Japan;
•On August 17, 2026, the Company announced the appointment of Manher (“AJ”) Joshi, M.D., as Chief Medical Officer;
•On August 11, 2026, the Company announced that IMCIVREE received expanded Marketing Authorization in the United Kingdom for the treatment of obesity and control of hunger in patients with acquired HO due to hypothalamic injury or impairment;
Anticipated Upcoming Milestones
Rhythm expects to achieve the following near-term milestones:
•Complete enrollment in the Phase 1/2, Part D trial evaluating RM-718 in PWS in the second half of 2026;
•Initiate a pivotal Phase 3 trial evaluating bivamelagon in acquired HO by year-end 2026;
•Complete enrollment in the setmelanotide substudy in congenital HO in 2027;
•Begin country-level launches of IMCIVREE for acquired HO in Europe beginning with Germany in 2027; and

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Cash Position: As of September 30, 2026, cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments were approximately $320.9 million, as compared to $388.9 million as of December 31, 2025. Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments as of September 30 do not include the $250 million initial draw on the term loan facility or the $70 million payment due to LG Chem under the terms of the amendment to the licensing agreement for bivamelagon.
Revenue: Net product revenues from global sales of IMCIVREE were $92.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $51.3 million for the third quarter of 2025.
R&D Expenses: R&D expenses were $52.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $46.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year increase was primarily due to an increase in costs in chemistry, manufacturing, and controls (CMC) activity and clinical trial costs, as well as personnel costs, and costs associated with our achievement of a development milestone for our program focused on developing therapies for congenital hyperinsulinism (CHI).
SG&A Expenses: SG&A expenses were $66.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $52.4 million for the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year increase was primarily due to increased personnel costs, including stock-based compensation, related to expanded business operations.
Other income (expense), net: Other expense, net was $(1.5) million for the third quarter of 2026, as compared to other expense, net of $(0.1) million for the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year increase in other expense, net was primarily due to less interest income earned in the third quarter of 2026 based on decreasing average cash and short-term investments.
Net Loss attributable to common stockholders: Net loss attributable to common stockholders was $(42.4) million for the third quarter of 2026, or a net loss per basic and diluted share of $(0.61), as compared to a net loss attributable to common stockholders of $(54.3) million for the third quarter of 2025, or a net loss per basic and diluted share of $(0.82).

Revenue: Net product revenues relating to sales of IMCIVREE were $223.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $137.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

About Rhythm Pharmaceuticals
Rhythm is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company committed to transforming the lives of patients and their families living with rare neuroendocrine diseases. Rhythm’s lead asset, IMCIVREE® (setmelanotide), an MC4R agonist designed to treat hyperphagia and severe obesity, is approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to reduce excess body weight and maintain weight reduction long term in adult and pediatric patients aged 4 years and older with acquired hypothalamic obesity, adult and pediatric patients 2 years of age and older with syndromic or monogenic obesity due to Bardet-Biedl syndrome (BBS) or genetically confirmed pro-opiomelanocortin (POMC), including proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 1 (PCSK1), deficiency or leptin receptor (LEPR) deficiency. Both the European Commission (EC) and the UK’s Medicines & Healthcare Products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) have authorized setmelanotide for the treatment of obesity and the control of hunger associated with genetically confirmed BBS or genetically confirmed loss-of-function biallelic POMC, including PCSK1, deficiency or biallelic LEPR deficiency in adults and children 2 years of age and above. The European Commission (EC) has authorized setmelanotide for the treatment of obesity and control of hunger in patients 4 years of age and above with acquired hypothalamic obesity. Additionally, Rhythm is advancing a broad clinical development program for setmelanotide in other rare diseases, as well as investigational MC4R agonists bivamelagon and RM-718, and a preclinical suite of small molecules for the treatment of congenital hyperinsulinism. Rhythm’s headquarters is in Boston, MA.
Setmelanotide Indication
In the United States, setmelanotide is indicated to reduce excess body weight and maintain weight reduction long term in adult and pediatric patients aged 4 years and older with acquired HO, and in adult and pediatric patients aged 2 years and older with syndromic or monogenic obesity due to Bardet-Biedl syndrome (BBS) or Pro-opiomelanocortin (POMC), proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 1 (PCSK1), or leptin receptor (LEPR) deficiency as determined by an FDA-approved test demonstrating variants in POMC, PCSK1, or LEPR genes that are interpreted as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, or of uncertain significance (VUS).
In the European Union and the United Kingdom, setmelanotide is indicated for the treatment of obesity and the control of hunger associated with genetically confirmed BBS or loss-of-function biallelic POMC, including PCSK1, deficiency or biallelic LEPR deficiency in adults and children 2 years of age and above. In the European Union and the United Kingdom, setmelanotide should be prescribed and supervised by a physician with expertise in obesity with underlying genetic etiology.
Limitations of Use
Setmelanotide is not indicated for the treatment of patients with the following conditions as setmelanotide would not be expected to be effective:
•Obesity due to suspected POMC, PCSK1, or LEPR deficiency with POMC, PCSK1, or LEPR variants classified as benign or likely benign
•Other types of obesity not related to BBS or POMC, PCSK1, or LEPR deficiency, including obesity associated with other genetic syndromes and general (polygenic) obesity
Contraindication
Prior serious hypersensitivity to setmelanotide or any of the excipients in IMCIVREE. Serious hypersensitivity reactions (e.g., anaphylaxis) have been reported.
WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS
Disturbance in Sexual Arousal: Spontaneous penile erections in males and sexual adverse reactions in females have occurred. Inform patients that these events may occur and instruct patients who have an erection lasting longer than 4 hours to seek emergency medical attention.
Depression and Suicidal Ideation: Depression, suicidal ideation and depressed mood have occurred. Monitor patients for new onset or worsening depression or suicidal thoughts or behaviors. Consider discontinuing IMCIVREE if patients experience suicidal thoughts or behaviors, or clinically significant or persistent depression symptoms occur.
Hypersensitivity Reactions: Serious hypersensitivity reactions (e.g., anaphylaxis) have been reported. If suspected, advise patients to promptly seek medical attention and discontinue IMCIVREE.
Skin Hyperpigmentation, Darkening of Pre-existing Nevi, and Development of New Melanocytic Nevi: Generalized or focal increases in skin pigmentation, darkening of pre-existing nevi, development of new melanocytic nevi and increase in size of existing melanocytic
nevi have occurred. Perform a full body skin examination prior to initiation and periodically during treatment to monitor pre-existing and new pigmented lesions.
Risk of Serious Adverse Reactions Due to Benzyl Alcohol Preservative in Neonates and Low Birth Weight Infants: IMCIVREE is not approved for use in neonates or infants. Serious and fatal adverse reactions including “gasping syndrome” can occur in neonates and low birth weight infants treated with benzyl alcohol preserved drugs.
ADVERSE REACTIONS
Most common adverse reactions (incidence ≥20%) included skin hyperpigmentation, injection site reactions, nausea, headache, diarrhea, abdominal pain, vomiting, depression, and spontaneous penile erection.
USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS
Treatment with IMCIVREE is not recommended when breastfeeding. Discontinue IMCIVREE when pregnancy is recognized unless the benefits of therapy outweigh the potential risks to the fetus.
