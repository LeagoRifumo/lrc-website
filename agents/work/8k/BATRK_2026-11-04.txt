ATLANTA BRAVES HOLDINGS REPORTS
THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
​
Atlanta, Georgia, November 4, 2026 – Atlanta Braves Holdings, Inc. (“ABH”) (Nasdaq: BATRA, BATRK) today reported results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
​
Highlights include:
● | The Braves returned to the postseason and saw a 14% increase in average attendance per regular season home game in the third quarter from the prior year period.

● | BravesVision completed its first regular season and led the league in in-market direct-to-consumer subscribers.

● | Total revenue grew to $325 million in the third quarter of 2026, up 4% from the prior year period.

o | Baseball revenue increased 4% from the prior year period to $296 million driven by increases in baseball event revenue.

o | Mixed-Use Development revenue increased 7% from the prior year period to $29 million primarily due to an increase in rental income.

● | On a year-to-date basis, total revenue grew to $702 million, up 5% from the prior year period.

o | Baseball revenue increased 3% from the prior year period to $618 million.

o | Mixed-Use Development revenue increased 19% from the prior year period to $84 million.

● | Total Adjusted OIBDA(1) of $41 million in the third quarter of 2026.

o | Baseball Adjusted OIBDA declined to $26 million from $50 million in the prior year period driven mainly by increases in player salaries.

o | Mixed-Use Development Adjusted OIBDA declined to $18 million from $20 million in the prior year period.

● | Operating income declined to $9 million in the third quarter of 2026 from $39 million in the prior year period.

Baseball revenue increased 4% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the prior year primarily driven by increases in baseball event revenue and retail and licensing revenue. Baseball event revenue increased due to contractual rate increases on season tickets, increased attendance at regular season home games, favorable single-game ticket sales, and increases in regular and postseason home games. Retail and licensing revenue increased compared to the prior year due to demand for City Connect apparel following its April 2026 launch, higher league-wide revenue, increased attendance at regular season home games and the impact of two postseason home games. Media related revenue declined due to the timing of revenue recognition under BravesVision linear distribution agreements compared to our previous long-term local broadcasting arrangement, as well as changes in certain national media rights arrangements, including the increased commercialization of digital media rights through MLB Advanced Media, L.P. (“MLBAM”). Other revenue decreased due to fewer special events held at Truist Park.
Mixed-Use Development revenue increased 7% for the third quarter of 2026 primarily due to an increase in rental income driven by increases in tenant recoveries and new lease agreements.
Operating income (loss) and Adjusted OIBDA(1) decreased in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the prior year primarily due to increases in operating costs and selling, general, and administrative expenses. Baseball operating costs increased primarily due to increases in major league player salaries, expenses associated with the production of BravesVision, variable concessions and retail operating expenses and postseason game operating costs. Selling, general and administrative expenses increased due to increases in sales, marketing and administrative costs associated with BravesVision, additional marketing costs, personnel costs, property taxes, insurance costs and other professional fees.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES AND SUPPLEMENTAL DISCLOSURES
SCHEDULE 1: Reconciliation of Adjusted OIBDA to Operating Income (Loss)
To provide investors with additional information regarding our financial results, this press release includes a presentation of Adjusted OIBDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure, for ABH together with reconciliations to operating income, as determined under GAAP. ABH defines Adjusted OIBDA as operating income (loss) plus stock-based compensation, depreciation and amortization, separately reported litigation settlements, restructuring, acquisition and impairment charges. However, ABH’s definition of Adjusted OIBDA may differ from similarly titled measures disclosed by other companies.
ABH believes Adjusted OIBDA is an important indicator of the operational strength and performance of its businesses by identifying those items that are not directly a reflection of each business’ performance or indicative of ongoing business trends. In addition, this measure allows management to view operating results and perform analytical comparisons and benchmarking between businesses and identify strategies to improve performance. Because Adjusted OIBDA is used as a measure of operating performance, ABH views operating income as the most directly comparable GAAP measure. Adjusted OIBDA is not meant to replace or supersede operating income or any other GAAP measure, but rather to supplement such GAAP measures in order to present investors with the same information that ABH management considers in assessing the results of operations and performance of its assets.
