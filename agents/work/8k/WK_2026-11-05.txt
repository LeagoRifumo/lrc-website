Workiva Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Subscription and support revenue increased by 17% year-over-year
Total revenue was $263 million, up 17% year-over-year
GAAP operating margin was 6.8%, non-GAAP operating margin was 18.3%
NEW YORK - November 5, 2026 – Workiva Inc. (NYSE: WK), a leading, audit-ready platform for trust, transparency, and accountability, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Our third quarter results reflect continued momentum across the Workiva platform as organizations navigate growing complexity,” said Julie Iskow, President & Chief Executive Officer. “AI is fundamentally changing enterprise software, from helping people do the work to increasingly doing the work itself. This shift plays directly to Workiva’s strengths. As software takes on more complex, regulated, and high-stakes work, the need for accuracy, transparency, and trust becomes even more critical. Workiva is uniquely positioned to meet these needs and expand the role our platform plays across the enterprise.”
“In Q3, we continued to deliver healthy top-line growth while expanding our operating margins, reinforcing that growth and profitability go hand-in-hand at Workiva,” said Barbara Larson, Chief Financial Officer. "We beat the high end of our revenue guidance with 17% growth in both subscription and total revenue. At the same time, we expanded non-GAAP operating margin by 560 basis points year-over-year. This expanding operating leverage generated healthy free cash flow, providing the financial agility to fund our technology roadmap as we build toward our 2030 targets.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Revenue: Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $263 million, an increase of 17% from $224 million in the third quarter of 2025. Subscription and support revenue was $246 million, up 17% from the third quarter of 2025. Professional services revenue was $17 million, up 15% from the third quarter of 2025.
•Operating Margin: GAAP operating margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 6.8% compared to (1.5)% in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP operating margin was 18.3% compared to 12.7% in the third quarter of 2025.
•GAAP Net Income: GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $19 million compared with net income of $3 million in the third quarter of 2025. GAAP net income per basic share and diluted share was $0.35, compared with net income per basic share and diluted share of $0.05 in the third quarter of 2025.
•Non-GAAP Net Income: To better reflect our long-term expected tax profile and enhance period-to-period comparability, beginning with our third quarter of 2026, non-GAAP net income includes a non-GAAP provision for income taxes using a fixed long-term projected rate of 22%. This change in methodology is also retrospectively reflected in comparable prior periods presented to provide better comparability. Guidance issued prior to this quarter was prepared under the previous methodology which reflected a non-GAAP provision for income taxes that equaled our GAAP provision for income taxes. Non-GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $41 million compared with non-GAAP net income of $27 million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net income per basic share and diluted share for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.75 and $0.72, respectively, compared with non-GAAP net income per basic share and diluted share of $0.47 and $0.46, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025.
•Operating Cash Flow and Free Cash Flow: Operating cash flow for the third quarter of 2026 was $70 million compared with $46 million for the third quarter of 2025. Free cash flow for the third quarter of 2026 was $69 million compared with $46 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•Liquidity: As of September 30, 2026, Workiva had cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaling $817 million, compared with $892 million as of December 31, 2025. The remaining obligation associated with our 1.125% convertible senior notes due in 2026 was fully settled upon maturity on August 15, 2026, resulting in a $71 million cash outflow from financing activities during the period. Workiva had $702 million aggregate principal amount of 1.250% convertible senior notes due in 2028 and $13 million of finance lease obligations outstanding as of September 30, 2026.
Key Metrics and Recent Business Highlights
•Customers: Workiva had 6,840 customers as of September 30, 2026, a net increase of 299 customers from September 30, 2025.
•Retention Rate: As of September 30, 2026, Workiva's gross retention rate was 97%, and the net retention rate was 110%. Net retention includes changes in both solutions and pricing for existing customers.
•Large Contracts: As of September 30, 2026, Workiva had 2,853 customers with an annual contract value (“ACV”) of more than $100,000, up 20% from 2,372 customers at September 30, 2025. Workiva had 708 customers with an ACV of more than $300,000, up 31% from 541 customers at September 30, 2025. Workiva had 283 customers with an ACV of more than $500,000, up 20% from 236 customers at September 30, 2025.
•Share Repurchase Plan: During the third quarter of 2026, Workiva purchased approximately 32.6 thousand shares for approximately $2 million under our previously disclosed $350 million repurchase plan. As of September 30, 2026, approximately $104 million remained available under the repurchase plan for future share repurchases.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The non-GAAP adjustments referenced herein relate to the exclusion of stock-based compensation, amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, the income tax effect of non-GAAP adjustments, and in certain cases, interest expense. A reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP historical financial measures has been provided in Table I at the end of this press release. A reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP guidance has been provided in Table II at the end of this press release.
Workiva believes that the use of, among other non-GAAP measures presented in this press release, non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP income from operations and non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP net income per share, free cash flow and free cash flow margin is helpful to its investors. These measures, which are referred to as non-GAAP financial measures, are not prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States, or GAAP. Workiva’s management uses these non-GAAP financial measures as tools for financial and operational decision making and for evaluating Workiva’s own operating results over different periods of time.
Non-GAAP gross profit is calculated by excluding stock-based compensation expense and amortization expense for acquisition-related intangible assets attributable to cost of revenues from gross profit. Non-GAAP income from
operations is calculated by excluding stock-based compensation expense and amortization expense for acquisition-related intangible assets from income (loss) from operations. Non-GAAP operating margin is the ratio calculated by dividing non-GAAP income from operations by total revenues. Non-GAAP net income is calculated by excluding stock-based compensation expense and amortization expense for acquisition-related intangible assets from net income (loss) and adjusting for the estimated income tax effects of non-GAAP adjustments. Non-GAAP net income per basic share is calculated by dividing non-GAAP net income by basic weighted- average shares outstanding. Non-GAAP net income per diluted share is calculated by dividing non-GAAP net income, adjusted for interest expense on our convertible senior notes when dilutive and the related income tax effect, by non-GAAP diluted weighted-average shares outstanding.
To better reflect our long-term expected tax profile and enhance period-to-period comparability, beginning with the three months and nine months ended September 30, 2026, non-GAAP net income includes a non-GAAP provision for income taxes of 22%, which was not included in our previously-issued guidance. This change is also retrospectively reflected in comparable prior periods presented. Previously, our non-GAAP provision for income taxes equaled our GAAP provision for income taxes. The rate presented is based on a three-year financial projection that excludes the direct impact of non-GAAP adjustments, assumes no benefit from available U.S. net operating loss or tax credit carryforwards or valuation allowances, considers our operating structure, jurisdictional tax positions and key tax legislation, and differs from our GAAP effective tax rate. We re-evaluate the rate periodically for significant events, tax law changes, and material changes in geographic earnings mix.
Moreover, beginning with the three months and six months ended June 30, 2026, we are adding back interest expense to the numerator of our non-GAAP diluted earnings per share in accordance with the if-converted method of calculating the dilutive impact of our convertible senior notes with the related income tax effect presented separately within the income tax effect of non-GAAP adjustments. Prior to this change, potentially dilutive shares associated with our convertible senior notes were included in the denominator, but the numerator was not adjusted, as our convertible senior notes had historically been anti-dilutive for GAAP purposes. As Workiva has achieved GAAP profitability, our convertible senior notes may now be dilutive, and we are aligning our non-GAAP calculation with the if-converted method used for GAAP diluted earnings per share. Prior periods have been recast for comparative purposes.
Because of varying available valuation methodologies, subjective assumptions and the variety of equity instruments that can impact a company’s non-cash expenses, Workiva believes that providing non-GAAP financial measures that exclude stock-based compensation expense allows for more meaningful comparisons between its operating results from period to period. For business combinations, we generally allocate a portion of the purchase price to intangible assets. The amount of the allocation is based on estimates and assumptions made by management and is subject to amortization. The amount of purchase price allocated to intangible assets and the term of its related amortization can vary significantly and are unique to each acquisition and thus we do not believe they are reflective of ongoing operations.
Free cash flow, a non-GAAP measure, represents cash flow from operating activities less purchases of property and equipment. Free cash flow margin is calculated by dividing free cash flow by total revenue. We consider free cash flow and free cash flow margin to be liquidity measures that provide useful information to investors about the amount of cash generated or used by the business. The utility of free cash flow and free cash flow margin as measures of our liquidity is limited because these measures do not represent the total increase or decrease in our cash balance for a given period.
Non-GAAP financial measures may not provide information that is directly comparable to that provided by other companies in Workiva’s industry, as other companies in the industry may calculate non-GAAP financial results differently. In addition, there are limitations in using non-GAAP financial measures because the non-GAAP financial measures are not prepared in accordance with GAAP, may be different from non-GAAP financial measures used by other companies and exclude expenses that may have a material impact on Workiva’s reported financial results. Further, stock-based compensation expense has been and will continue to be for the foreseeable future a significant recurring expense in Workiva’s business and an important part of the compensation provided to its employees. The presentation of non-GAAP financial information is not meant to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the directly comparable financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. Investors should review the reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to the comparable GAAP financial measures included below and not rely on any single financial measure to evaluate Workiva’s business.
