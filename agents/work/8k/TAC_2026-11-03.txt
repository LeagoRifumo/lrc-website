Description of the Business
TransAlta Corporation is one of Canada’s largest publicly traded power generators, with operations across Canada, the United States (U.S.) and Western Australia. Our diversified fleet includes hydro, wind, solar, battery storage and thermal generation, complemented by asset optimization and energy marketing activities. As one of Canada’s largest producers of wind and thermal generation and Alberta’s largest producer of hydroelectric power, TransAlta remains committed to a diverse generation mix. With strong cash flows underpinned by a high-quality portfolio, TransAlta strives to deliver sustainable long-term shareholder value in an evolving energy landscape. We have four generation segments including Hydro, Wind and Solar, Gas and Energy Transition, along with two non-generation segments including Energy Marketing and Corporate.
Our diversified portfolio consists of both high-quality contracted and merchant cash flows. Our merchant cash
flow streams are derived from our unique hydro portfolio, legacy and peaking thermal assets and wind assets. Our merchant exposure is primarily in Alberta, where 61 per cent of our generating capacity is located and 77 per cent is exposed to the merchant market.
In Alberta, the Company manages its merchant exposure by executing hedging strategies that include a significant base of commercial and industrial customers, supplemented with financial hedges. A significant portion of our thermal and hydro generation capacity in Alberta may be hedged to enhance cash flow predictability while maintaining exposure to market upside.
Refer to the "2026 Outlook" and the "Optimization of the Alberta Portfolio" sections of this MD&A for further details.

Significant and Subsequent Events - Third Quarter 2026
Mothballing of Battle River Units 4 and 5 and Extension of the Mothballing of Sundance 6
On Nov. 2, 2026, the Company provided notice to the Alberta Electric System Operator (AESO) that Battle River Units 4 and 5 will be mothballed effective March 1, 2027, for a period of time up to Jan. 1, 2029 and extended the mothballing of Sundance 6 for a period of time up to April 1, 2029. The Company maintains the flexibility to return the mothballed units to service when market fundamentals improve or contracting opportunities are secured. Battle River Units 4 and 5 will remain available and fully operational through to March 1, 2027.
Sale of minority interest in Oklahoma wind portfolio
On Oct. 22, 2026, the Company completed the sale of a 49 per cent minority ownership interest in its 500 MW Oklahoma wind portfolio to Ares Infrastructure funds for cash proceeds of US$305 million, before customary closing adjustments. The portfolio comprises the 100 MW White Rock West, 200 MW White Rock East and 200 MW
Horizon Hill wind facilities. The Company will continue to provide operational oversight under a long-term asset management agreement.
Acquisition of Mountain Peak Power and Canyon Peak Power and associated public offering of common shares
On Oct. 22, 2026, the Company closed the acquisition of Mountain Peak Power and Canyon Peak Power, two fully contracted natural gas-fired peaking facilities totaling 318 MW near Denver, Colorado from an indirect subsidiary of Blackstone, Inc. (the Acquisition). The purchase price for the Acquisition was US$1 billion, including the assumption of US$750 million of project debt.
On June 9, 2026 the Company closed a public offering of 18,230,000 common shares at a price of $19.20 per share through a syndicate of underwriters, for total gross proceeds of approximately $350 million, which was used to fund the cash portion of the purchase price for the Acquisition. The proceeds received in advance of the Acquisition were partially used to repay the drawn amounts under the syndicated credit facility.
