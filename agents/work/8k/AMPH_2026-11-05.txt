Amphastar Pharmaceuticals Reports Financial Results for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026
​
- | Net revenues of $207.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026

​
- | GAAP net income of $37.8 million, or $0.87 per share, for the third quarter

​
- | Adjusted non-GAAP net income of $49.3 million, or $1.14 per share, for the third quarter

​
- | Company to hold a conference call today at 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time

​
RANCHO CUCAMONGA, CA – November 5, 2026 – Amphastar Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMPH) (“Amphastar” or the “Company”), a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing, manufacturing, and commercializing technically challenging generic and proprietary injectable, inhalation, and intranasal products, today reported results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
​
“Our third quarter results demonstrate the strength of Amphastar’s diversified portfolio and the commercial platform we have built. Record sales of BAQSIMI® and Primatene MIST®, together with contributions from recently launched products, supported continued revenue growth and further strengthened the foundation for our long-term strategy. During the quarter, we continued to advance programs across our complex generic, biosimilar, and proprietary pipelines while investing in the manufacturing, quality and development capabilities needed to support future growth. We remain focused on disciplined execution, strengthening our operations, and deploying capital in ways that we believe can create sustainable long-term value for our shareholders,” said Dr. Jack Zhang, Amphastar’s President and Chief Executive Officer.

Changes in net revenues as compared to the third quarter of the prior year were primarily driven by:
​
● | BAQSIMI® sales increased primarily due to an increase in unit volumes, contributing $7.4 million in sales driven by our continued marketing efforts. This increase was partially offset by a lower average selling price, as a result of a change in gross-to-net discounts due to changes in chargebacks and rebates and changes to the customer mix, impacting sales of approximately $2.2 million.

● | Primatene MIST® sales increased due to a higher average selling price, contributing $1.6 million in sales, as a result of a price increase that went into effect in the second quarter of 2026, and an increase in unit volumes, contributing $1.0 million in sales, as in-store demand for Primatene MIST® continues to grow.

● | Lidocaine sales increased primarily due to an increase in unit volumes as a result of an increase in demand caused by other supplier shortages.

● | Ipratropium bromide continued to have strong sales during the quarter, following our launch in the second quarter of 2026.

● | Epinephrine sales decreased primarily due to lower unit volumes of our pre-filled syringe product, resulting from slower product releases associated with ongoing remediation efforts at our subsidiary, International Medication Systems, Limited (“IMS”).

● | Glucagon sales decreased primarily due to a lower average selling price, as a result of increased competition.

● | Other pharmaceutical product sales increased primarily due to sales of teriparatide of $6.4 million, which we launched in December 2025. Additionally, API sales from our subsidiary, Amphastar Nanjing Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (“ANP”), contributed $6.2 million. These increases were partially offset by a decrease in sales of several products manufactured at our IMS facility due to ongoing remediation activities.

Share Buyback Program
​
On November 4, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors authorized a $50 million increase to the Company’s share buyback program, which is expected to continue for an indefinite period of time. The primary goal of the program is to offset dilution created by the Company’s equity compensation programs.
​
Purchases may be made through the open market and private block transactions pursuant to Rule 10b5-1 plans, privately negotiated transactions, or other means, as determined by the Company’s management and in accordance with the requirements of the Securities and Exchange Commission and applicable laws.
​
The timing and actual number of shares repurchased will depend on a variety of factors including price, corporate and regulatory requirements, and other conditions.

Pipeline Information
​
The Company currently has one abbreviated new drug application (“ANDA”) and one biosimilar insulin candidate filed with the FDA for products targeting a combined market size exceeding $1.6 billion, along with two biosimilar products in development targeting a market size exceeding $3.5 billion, and three generic products in development targeting a market size of over $1.2 billion. This market information is based on IQVIA data for the 12 months ended September 30, 2026, supplemented by data provided by the branded company for one of the generic targets. The Company is developing multiple proprietary products with injectable, topical and intranasal dosage forms.
​
The Company’s proprietary pipeline also includes four in-licensed products including three proprietary peptides targeting oncology and ophthalmology indications, and a fully synthetic corticotropin compound designed to address inflammatory and autoimmune conditions.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
​
To supplement its consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), the Company is disclosing non-GAAP financial measures when providing financial results. The Company believes that an evaluation of its ongoing operations (and comparisons of its current operations with historical and future operations) would be difficult if the disclosure of its financial results were limited to
financial measures prepared only in accordance with GAAP. As a result, the Company is disclosing certain non-GAAP results, including (i) Adjusted non-GAAP net income (loss) and (ii) Adjusted non-GAAP diluted EPS, which generally exclude amortization expense, share-based compensation, impairment charges, certain debt issuance costs, legal settlements, and other one-time events in order to supplement investors’ and other readers’ understanding and assessment of the Company’s financial performance because the Company’s management uses these measures internally for forecasting, budgeting, and measuring its operating performance. Whenever the Company uses such non-GAAP measures, it will provide a reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures. Investors and other readers are encouraged to review the related GAAP financial measures and the reconciliation of non-GAAP measures to their most directly comparable GAAP measures set forth below and should consider non-GAAP measures only as a supplement to, not as a substitute for or as a superior measure to, measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with GAAP.

About Amphastar Pharmaceuticals, Inc.
​
Amphastar is a biopharmaceutical company that focuses on developing, manufacturing, and commercializing technically challenging generic and proprietary injectable, inhalation, and intranasal products. Additionally, the Company sells active pharmaceutical ingredient, or API products. Most of the Company’s finished products are contracted and distributed through group purchasing organizations, drug wholesalers, and drug retailers. More information and resources are available at www.amphastar.com.
