Caris Life Sciences Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Increases 2026 Revenue Guidance
Revenue growth of 27% driven by strong performance in molecular profiling services, including approximately 61,500 clinical cases, consisting of over 358,000 total oncology tests
Raises 2026 revenue guidance to $1.04 to $1.05 billion, representing growth of 28% to 29%
IRVING, Texas, November 04, 2026— Caris Life Sciences, Inc. (Nasdaq: CAI), a leading TechBio company actively developing and commercializing solutions to transform healthcare, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Reported total revenue of $275.4 million, an increase of 27% over the corresponding prior year period.
•Completed approximately 61,500 clinical cases, an increase of approximately 21% over the corresponding prior year period.
•Reported gross margin of 66%.
•Reported net income of $15.3 million.
•Reported positive Adjusted EBITDA of $44.4 million.
•Reported net cash used in operating activities of $22.2 million, and free cash flow of $(39.7) million.
“This was another record quarter, with approximately 61,500 clinical cases, up 21% year over year,” said David Dean Halbert, Founder, Chairman and CEO of Caris Life Sciences. “Every case we run carries real molecular depth, whether Whole Exome, Whole Transcriptome, Whole Genome, or AI-driven digital pathology, and that depth is what allows us to move across the entire cancer journey. Caris Assure® is now available for therapeutic response monitoring, and Caris Confirm, our Whole Genome, tumor-informed assay for minimal residual disease, is targeted to launch before year-end. It all comes back to making precision medicine a reality for every patient through a comprehensive, data-driven approach.”
Recent Operating Highlights
•Launched Caris Assure Therapy Response Monitoring, extending the Caris Assure blood-based platform from therapy selection to serial monitoring of treatment response.
•Launched enhanced Caris Assure for Therapy selection with therapy focused sequencing depth reaching 40,000x and increasing RNA reads to ~600M.
•Published validation of the Caris AI Insights™ pancreatic cancer signature, demonstrating that an AI-based molecular signature can predict first-line treatment benefits.
•Share repurchase program of up to $100.0 million, of which approximately $72.1 million remains available for repurchase under the existing Board authorization.
•Launched two national advertising campaigns featuring Kevin Costner and Jordan Spieth, spotlighting the importance of early-stage cancer detection and introducing Caris Detect™ to a consumer audience.
•Surpassed 1,200,000 total profiles and 845,000 total matched profiles through September 30, 2026. More than 849,000 Whole Transcriptome and 796,000 Whole Exome profiles through September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total revenue was $275.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $216.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, an increase of $58.5 million, or 27%.
The increase in total revenue was driven primarily by a 27% growth in molecular profiling services revenue, which was $262.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $207.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in molecular profiling services revenue was primarily driven by an increase in total clinical case volume and ASP improvements.
Gross profit, calculated as total revenue less cost of services, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, was $182.3 million and $147.5 million, respectively, representing a gross margin of 66% and 68%, respectively.
Operating expenses were $163.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $114.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, an increase of $48.1 million, or 42%. The increase was primarily driven by headcount-related costs, increased research and development investment, and marketing expenditures.
Net income was $15.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to a net income of $24.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Net income per share attributable to common shareholders, basic and diluted, was $0.05 and $0.05, respectively, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to a net income per share attributable to common shareholders, basic and diluted, of $0.09 and $0.08, respectively, for the three months ended September 30, 2025.
Net cash used in operating activities was $22.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to net cash provided by operating activities of $62.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, a 136% decrease. The decrease was driven by continued investment in commercial expansion and marketing expenditures, inventory build-up for new solution launches, and research and development efforts.
2026 Financial Outlook and Guidance
Caris Life Sciences now expects full year 2026 revenue to be in the range of $1.04 to $1.05 billion, representing growth of 28% to 29% compared to full year 2025 and reaffirms its guidance for clinical therapy selection volume growth of approximately 20% compared to full year 2025.

About Caris Life Sciences
Caris Life Sciences® (Caris) is a leading TechBio company actively developing and commercializing innovative solutions to transform healthcare. Through comprehensive molecular profiling (Whole
Genome, Whole Exome and Whole Transcriptome Sequencing), advanced AI and machine learning, Caris has created the large-scale, multimodal clinico-genomic database and computing capability needed to analyze and further unravel the molecular complexity of disease. This convergence of next-generation sequencing, AI and machine learning technologies and high-performance computing provides a differentiated platform for developing the latest generation of advanced precision medicine diagnostic solutions for early detection, diagnosis, monitoring, therapy selection and drug development.
Caris was founded with a vision to realize the potential of precision medicine to improve the human condition. Headquartered in Irving, Texas, Caris has offices in Phoenix, New York, Cambridge (MA), Tokyo, Japan and Basel, Switzerland. Caris or its distributor partners provide services in the U.S. and other international markets.

Non-GAAP Measures
We use Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow, financial measures not calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”), to supplement our condensed consolidated financial statements, which are presented in accordance with GAAP. We believe the non-GAAP financial measures we use, are useful in evaluating our performance and liquidity. Our non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools, however, and you should not consider them in isolation or as substitutes for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. Other companies, including other companies in our industry, may not use these measures or may calculate these measures differently than as presented herein, limiting their usefulness as comparative measures.
We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss), adjusted to exclude interest income, interest expense, changes in fair value of financial instruments, other expense, net, the provision for (benefit from) income taxes, depreciation and amortization, and stock-based compensation expense. We use Adjusted EBITDA in conjunction with GAAP measures as part of our overall assessment of our performance, including the preparation of our annual operating budget and quarterly forecasts, to evaluate the effectiveness of our business strategies, and to communicate with our board of directors concerning our financial performance. We believe Adjusted EBITDA provides useful information to investors and others in understanding and evaluating our operating results in the same manner as our management team and board of directors. Adjusted EBITDA provides a useful measure for period-to-period comparisons of our business, as it removes the effect of certain non-cash expenses and certain variable charges. We define free cash flow as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less capitalized software and purchases of property and equipment. Our method of calculating free cash flow is unchanged from prior periods; the caption has been updated to identify capitalized software as a component of our investing capital expenditures. We believe free cash flow is a useful measure of liquidity that provides an additional basis for assessing our ability to generate cash.
