Riot Platforms Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Strategic Highlights
Executed a 15-year, 910 critical IT MW triple-net powered land lease with a leading Frontier AI Lab at Corsicana, representing approximately $28 billion in total contract value and approximately $1.8 billion in average annual rent over the initial term
​
Riot has now leased 1,151 MW of critical IT capacity, representing approximately $38 billion of long-term contracted revenue
​
Sold 20,264 bitcoin year-to-date for approximately $1.5 billion primarily to fund data center development; ended the quarter with $950.2 million in cash and bitcoin, and no equity raised in 2026
​
Quarterly revenue of $181.4 million, including $18.4 million of Data Center revenue
​
CASTLE ROCK, Colo., November 4, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) - Riot Platforms, Inc. (NASDAQ: RIOT) (“Riot” or “the Company”), a vertically-integrated industry leader in digital infrastructure, specializing in the development and operation of large-scale data centers, reported financial results for the three-month period ended September 30, 2026. The accompanying presentation materials are available on Riot’s website.
“Today, we are excited to announce the largest lease in Riot’s history: a 15-year triple-net powered land lease with a leading Frontier AI Lab for the entirety of our Corsicana campus, representing 910 megawatts of critical IT capacity and approximately $28 billion of contracted revenue over the initial term,” said Jason Les, CEO of Riot. “Together with our AMD contract and the lease signed with the same Frontier AI Lab at Rockdale earlier this year, Riot has contracted 1,151 megawatts and approximately $38 billion of data center revenue, which we expect will generate more than $2 billion of average annual net operating income at full deployment, firmly establishing Riot as a leading developer of large-scale digital infrastructure.
“The structure of this new lease matters as much as the scale. With total capital costs of approximately $6 million per IT megawatt, the Corsicana development will require about half the capital of a Tier 3 build while significantly limiting procurement, development, and ongoing operational risk to Riot. That lower capital requirement, combined with our strong balance sheet, means that we do not currently anticipate issuing equity to fund our announced projects.
“As these significant contracted cash flows come online, we will allocate our capital with discipline to whichever use creates the most value per share, whether that is our development pipeline or returning capital to shareholders.”
910 IT MW Powered Land Lease With a Leading Frontier AI Lab at Corsicana
Riot has signed a 15-year triple-net powered land lease with a leading Frontier AI Lab for the entirety of the Company's Corsicana campus, covering 910 MW of critical IT capacity on a contiguous 332-acre parcel.
Transaction highlights:
● | Capacity Leased: Leased the entirety of the Corsicana campus representing 910 MW of critical IT capacity.

● | Total Contract Value: Approximately $28 billion of total contract value and approximately $1.8 billion of average annual rent, including contractual escalators. Approximately $54 billion if both five-year extension options are exercised.

● | NOI Contribution: Estimated NOI margin of approximately 99% under the triple-net structure, or approximately $1.8 billion of average annual net operating income over the initial term.

● | Capital Requirement: $6 million per IT MW, or $5.5 billion overall, approximately half the capital intensity of a typical Tier 3 build, implying an unlevered yield on cost of approximately 33%. Riot will fund $2 million per IT MW for site work and electrical infrastructure, while the remaining $4 million per IT MW will be reimbursed to the tenant in phases, after each phase is delivered and paying rent.

● | Delivery Schedule: 400 MW in September 2027, 200 MW in January 2028 and 310 MW in July 2028, with rent on each phase commencing no later than 60 days after delivery of its powered pads.

● | Financing: Reimbursements to the tenant are expected to be funded with non-recourse project debt sized to contracted rent. If financing on comparable market terms is not available, the tenant will directly fund the reimbursements, which will be recovered as a credit against rent over the remaining lease term. Riot’s cash contribution is expected to be funded from existing liquidity, with no requirement to issue equity currently anticipated.

The lease is structured as a triple-net powered land lease, under which Riot delivers the land, the power infrastructure, and the powered pads, while continuing to own and maintain the high- and medium-voltage infrastructure that will serve them. The tenant will procure, install, own, and operate the modular compute deployment on the powered pads and will bear substantially all operating costs, including power costs as a pass-through. Rent is payable on the full 910 MW irrespective of utilization.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operational Highlights
Key financial and operational highlights for the quarter include:
• | Total revenue of $181.4 million, as compared to $180.2 million for the same three-month period in 2025, a 1% increase year-over-year as growth in Data Center and Engineering revenue offset lower Bitcoin Mining revenue.

• | Data Center revenue of $18.4 million for the quarter, comprised of $7.7 million in base rent, $0.5 million in variable lease revenue, and $10.2 million in tenant fit-out reimbursement revenue, reflecting the first full quarter of the stabilized 25 MW delivered to AMD.

• | Bitcoin Mining revenue of $116.0 million for the quarter, as compared to $160.8 million for the same three-month period in 2025, primarily driven by a lower average bitcoin price of $70,855, as compared to $114,361 in the prior-year period, partially offset by a 16% increase in production.

• | Produced 1,637 bitcoin, as compared to 1,406 during the same three-month period in 2025.

• | The average cost to mine bitcoin, excluding depreciation, was $39,265 in the quarter, as compared to $46,324 per bitcoin in the same three-month period in 2025, driven by lower power costs and higher production, with $24.7 million of power curtailment credits, equivalent to $15,067 per bitcoin mined.

• | Engineering revenue of $47.0 million for the quarter, as compared to $19.1 million for the same three-month period in 2025, driven by strong third-party data center demand for custom electrical equipment.

• | Ended the quarter with $950.2 million in cash and bitcoin, including $748.2 million in cash, of which $79.2 million is restricted, and 2,418 bitcoin, equating to approximately $202.0 million based on the market price for one bitcoin on September 30, 2026 of $83,554. During the first nine months of 2026, the Company sold 20,264 bitcoin for proceeds of approximately $1.5 billion, primarily to fund data center development, and repaid in full and terminated its $200 million Coinbase credit facility, releasing all pledged bitcoin.

Non-U.S. GAAP Measures of Financial Performance
In addition to financial measures presented under generally accepted accounting principles in the United States of America (“GAAP”), we consistently evaluate our use of and calculation of non-GAAP financial measures such as “Adjusted EBITDA.” EBITDA is computed as net income before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA is a performance measure defined as EBITDA, adjusted to eliminate the effects of certain non-cash and/or non-recurring items that do not reflect our ongoing strategic business operations, which management believes results in a performance measurement that represents a key indicator of the Company’s core business operations. The adjustments include fair value adjustments such as derivative power contract adjustments, equity securities fair value changes, and non-cash stock-based compensation expense, in addition to financing and legacy business income and expense items. We believe Adjusted EBITDA can be an important financial performance measure because it allows management, investors, and our board of directors to evaluate and compare our operating results, including our return on capital and operating efficiencies from period-to-period by making such adjustments. Additionally, Adjusted EBITDA is used as a performance metric for share-based compensation.
Adjusted EBITDA is provided in addition to, and should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, net income, the most comparable measure under GAAP to Adjusted EBITDA. Further, Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as an alternative to revenue growth, net income, diluted net income per share or any other performance measure derived in accordance with GAAP, or as an alternative to cash flow from operating activities as a measure of our liquidity. Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider this financial measure either in isolation or as a substitute for analyzing our results as reported under GAAP.
