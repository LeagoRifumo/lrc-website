SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION
Dear Sirs,
Decision to summon Ordinary General Shareholders’ Meeting to be held on December 15th, 2026
I am writing to you as Attorney in fact of Telecom Argentina S.A. to inform you that the Company’s Board of Directors, in a meeting held today, resolved to summon an Ordinary General Shareholders’ Meeting to be held on December 15th, 2026, at 11:00 a.m. on the first call, and at 12:00 p.m. on the second call, in order to consider the appointment of two (2) directors to serve from this Shareholders´ Meeting and until the end of the fiscal year 2026 and the performance of two resigning directors.
The Shareholders’ Meeting will be held by personal attendance.
We will submit in due time and manner the additional documentation required by Section 4, Chapter II, Title II of the ‘Comisión Nacional de Valores’ Rules (N.T. 2013).
