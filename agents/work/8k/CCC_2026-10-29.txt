CCC Intelligent Solutions Holdings Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
October 29, 2026 – CCC Intelligent Solutions Holdings Inc. (“CCC” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: CCC), a leading SaaS and AI platform provider for the multi-trillion-dollar insurance economy, today announced its financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
“CCC delivered strong third quarter results, with revenue growth of 10% and adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 44%. Our performance reflects the durability of our business, the trust customers place in us, and the increasing role our platform plays in helping participants across the insurance economy operate more efficiently and achieve better outcomes,” said Githesh Ramamurthy, Chairman & CEO of CCC.
“The pending transaction with GTCR and Elliott marks an exciting next chapter for CCC and our customers,” continued Ramamurthy. “Together, we expect to accelerate the AI and agentic capabilities our customers want from us, expand the network at the center of our platform, and make long-term investments in innovation that help customers navigate growing complexity across the claims lifecycle. We believe this positions CCC to deliver even greater value to the insurance economy in the years ahead.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
Revenue
•
Total revenue was $294.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 10.1% from $267.1 million for the third quarter of 2025.
Profitability
•
GAAP gross profit was $218.0 million, representing a gross margin of 74%, for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $193.0 million, representing a gross margin of 72%, for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross profit was $224.6 million, representing an adjusted gross profit margin of 76%, for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $199.5 million, representing an adjusted gross profit margin of 78%, for the third quarter of 2025.
•
GAAP operating income was $57.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with GAAP operating income of $30.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted operating income was $115.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with adjusted operating income of $93.0 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•
GAAP net income was $30.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with GAAP net loss of $2.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income was $75.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $59.4 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•
Adjusted EBITDA was $130.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, up 18.5% compared with adjusted EBITDA of $110.1 million for the third quarter of 2025.
Liquidity
•
CCC had $202.9 million in cash and cash equivalents and $1.28 billion of total debt as of September 30, 2026. The Company generated $100.4 million in cash from operating activities and had free cash flow of $82.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $94.8 million in cash generated from operating activities and free cash flow of $78.6 million for the third quarter of 2025.
3rd Quarter and Recent Business Highlights
•
GTCR and Elliott to acquire CCC. On October 15, 2026, CCC announced a definitive agreement to be acquired by a consortium of private investment firms led by GTCR and including Elliott Investment Management in an all-cash transaction in which shareholders will receive $7.75 per share, representing an enterprise value of approximately $6.1 billion. Subject to regulatory and stockholder approvals and other customary closing conditions, the proposed transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027. Following the close, CCC will become a privately held company.
•
Insurers expand AI adoption across core claims workflows. CCC’s AI-related revenue reached approximately $130 million in annualized run-rate in the third quarter of 2026, representing growth of 40% year over year and increasing from an annualized run rate of approximately $120 million in the second quarter of 2026. This growth reflects a continuing shift in customer behavior from AI experimentation to AI deployment at scale.
•
Leading insurer is largest to adopt Medhub for Auto Casualty. During the third quarter of 2026, a top-five insurer (based on 2025 direct premium written) became the largest insurer to adopt CCC’s Medhub for Auto Casualty, which uses AI to extract critical
insights from complex medical records and help claims professionals make faster, more informed decisions grounded in auditable claim data. The deployment marks the largest application of EvolutionIQ’s technology within the P&C auto market and demonstrates customers’ willingness to adopt adjacent workflows from CCC, underscoring the substantial growth opportunity beyond the traditional APD business.
Business Outlook and Conference Call Information
In light of the proposed transaction, the Company is suspending its practice of providing guidance and will not be hosting a conference call or webcast to discuss the Company’s financial results.
About CCC Intelligent Solutions
CCC Intelligent Solutions Inc. (CCC), a subsidiary of CCC Intelligent Solutions Holdings Inc. (NASDAQ: CCC), is a leading SaaS and AI platform provider powering the multi-trillion-dollar insurance and repair economy. CCC connects more than 35,000 businesses, including insurers, repairers, automakers and parts suppliers, through the workflows where claims and repair decisions are made every day. With AI built into those workflows for over a decade and informed by more than 40 years of industry data, CCC helps customers manage growing complexity and move work forward with confidence. Through purposeful innovation and the strength of its network, CCC helps keep people’s lives moving forward when it matters most. Learn more about CCC at www.cccis.com.
