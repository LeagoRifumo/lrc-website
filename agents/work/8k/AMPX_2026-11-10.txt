Amprius Technologies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Recent Business Highlights
•Q3 2026 revenue increased 83% year-over-year to $39.1 million
•Net loss of $3.5 million, a 10% year-over-year improvement
•Increasing 2026 revenue outlook to $145 million
•U.S. revenues more than doubled quarter over quarter (QoQ)
FREMONT, Calif. — November 10, 2026 – Amprius Technologies, Inc. ("Amprius" or the "Company") (NYSE: AMPX), a leader in silicon anode lithium-ion batteries, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and discussed recent business developments.
Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $39.1 million, up 83% from $21.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net loss attributable to common stockholders was $3.5 million, compared to a net loss of $3.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. GAAP net loss per common share was $0.02, compared to net loss per share was $0.03 in the third quarter of 2025.
Q3 2026 Financial Highlights
•Record revenue of $39.1 million, up 15% sequentially and 83% year-over-year (YoY)
•Gross profit of $10.5 million, up $7.2 million and 217% YoY
•GAAP gross margin of 27%, improving from 15% in Q3 2025
•Net loss attributable to common stockholders of $3.5 million, a $0.4 million improvement YoY
•Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA of $(1.0) million, a decrease from $0.2 million in Q3 2025
•U.S. revenues increased by 2.3X quarter-over-quarter (QoQ) to $12.2 million
“Our third quarter results demonstrate the strength of our company’s technology leadership and our ability to convert growing customer demand into meaningful financial performance," said Amprius Technologies CEO Tom Stepien. "We delivered record revenue, as we continued to deepen our relationships with customers across the drone, aviation, defense, and advanced mobility markets. Amprius introduced SiCore 500, a new battery model, with industry-leading energy density. The Company was selected for a competitive and significant contract to increase production in the U.S. in 2028. As our guidance suggests, Amprius enters the fourth quarter with strong momentum, driven by an expanding pipeline, and increased manufacturing scale.”

Business Highlights
•Secured a $75 million U.S. Department of War competitive contract to produce cells in the U.S., $22.3 million of which is obligated, by supporting the conversion of an existing battery production line with planned output of up to 12 million NDAA-compliant cells annually in 2028.
•Introduced SiCore® 500, a second-generation silicon anode cell delivering 500 Wh/kg on conventional lithium-ion manufacturing equipment, enabling a scalable path to large-scale commercial production to support aviation customers.
•Expanded our relationship with Quantum Systems, a leading German developer of autonomous unmanned systems, with increased deployment of Amprius cells across its Vector and Twister ISR platforms.
•Began supplying SiCore cells to a U.S. aerospace company producing a solar-powered stratospheric platform. Our cell supports extended-duration missions where energy density and weight are critical performance requirements.
•Secured a design win with a global leader in maritime and defense technology, for an autonomous deep-sea underwater vehicle platform, extending the application of Amprius' SiCore technology into subsea defense and maritime markets.
Updated 2026 Financial Outlook
Amprius updates its 2026 financial outlook as follows:
•Revenue is expected to be $145 million, raised from at least $140 million
•Gross margin is expected to be at least 28%
•Net loss is expected to be less than $10 million, including the Q2 $1.9 million non-cash fair value charge impact of the Warrant Exchange
•Net loss per share is expected to be under $0.07
•Adjusted EBITDA is expected to be at least $2.5 million
•Capital expenditure is expected to be under $10 million
The Company's 2026 outlook assumes depreciation and amortization of $3.0 million, stock-based compensation expense of $9.5 million, $1.2 million of Colorado facility expenses, interest income of $1.7 million, government grant income of $8.0 million and weighted average shares outstanding of 143.4 million for the full year.
Amprius’ CFO Ricardo C. Rodriguez added: “The third quarter once again proved the scalability of our fabless technology and process-owning strategy. Our team continues to meet growing demand with an expanding family of high-performance cells, built in the right place at the right time, without the need for capital investment for manufacturing. The agreements and work that we are executing to establish additional NDAA-compliant production extend this model to the next levels of demand.”

About Amprius Technologies, Inc.
Amprius Technologies, Inc. is a leader in advanced lithium-ion battery technology, delivering high-energy and high-power silicon-anode batteries with up to twice the energy density, range, and flight time of conventional graphite-based cells. Headquartered in Fremont, California, Amprius operates an R&D lab and pilot manufacturing facility for silicon anodes and cells. To support scalable production, the Company employs a contract manufacturing strategy, enabling rapid capacity expansion with minimal capital investment. Committed to driving innovation in energy storage, Amprius powers next-generation applications in aerospace, defense, and mobility. For additional information, please visit amprius.com and the Company’s LinkedIn page.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
To supplement our financial results presented on a basis in conformity with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”), we use the non-GAAP measures: Adjusted Net Loss, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin, which excludes from our GAAP net loss, interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, as well as other significant expenses including stock-based compensation that we believe are helpful in understanding our past financial performance. Our non-GAAP financial measures are not meant to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for comparable GAAP measures and should be read only in conjunction with our consolidated financial statements prepared in accordance with GAAP.
Management believes that these non-GAAP financial measures reflect our ongoing business in a manner that allows for meaningful comparisons and analysis of trends in its business, as it excludes expenses and gains not reflective of ongoing operating results or that may be infrequent and/or unusual in nature. We exclude the non-cash impact of the increase in fair value related to the warrant exchange, as the exchange of warrants for common shares is not related to our ongoing operations. We exclude the operating costs for our former facility in Colorado, as these costs were recurring in the past but with the termination of the lease in January 2026, they are no longer indicative of our ongoing operational results. We also adjust for the effect of stock-based compensation expenses noting that such expenses will recur in future periods. Although stock-based compensation is a key incentive offered to our employees, we continue to evaluate our business performance internally excluding stock-based compensation expenses.
Management also believes that these non-GAAP financial measures provide useful information to investors in understanding and evaluating our operating results and future prospects in the same manner as management and in comparing financial results across accounting periods and to those of peer companies. These non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures presented by other companies. In this press release, we provided reconciliations of non-GAAP adjusted net loss and non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA to GAAP net loss, the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure.
