Almonty Strengthens Board with Two Independent Directors and Announces Intention to Pursue a Secondary Listing on the Korean Exchange
Veteran U.S. Diplomat Dr. Eugene Bae and Seasoned Public Company CFO Jim Rudyk Join the Board; David Hanick and Andrew Frazer to Step Down Effective December 31, 2026
DILLON, Montana – November 17, 2026 – Almonty Industries Inc. (“Almonty” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: ALM) (Frankfurt: ALI1), a leading global producer of tungsten concentrate, today announced the appointment of Dr. Eugene Bae, Ph.D., and James (Jim) Rudyk, CPA,CA, to its Board of Directors (the “Board”) as independent directors, effective immediately, and that David Hanick and Andrew Frazer will step down from the Board effective December 31, 2026. The Company also announced its intention to pursue a listing on the Korea Exchange (“KRX”) in South Korea, home to Almonty’s flagship Sangdong Tungsten Mine (“Sangdong”).
The appointments advance the Company’s governance evolution, including its commitment to strengthen the Board’s independence and broaden its skills as Almonty’s scale and prominence in U.S. capital markets continue to grow. Following the departures of Mr. Hanick and Mr. Frazer, the Board will consist of seven directors, five of whom will be independent. Mr. Hanick steps down but will continue with Almonty as the General Counsel and Mr. Frazer steps down after the delisting on the ASX but remains as an executive consultant going forward.
Eugene Bae, Ph.D.
Dr. Bae is a veteran U.S. diplomat with 25 years of experience advancing American commercial and strategic interests in Asia, including 16 years posted in Seoul, Beijing and New Delhi, India. He most recently served as Director of Technology Policy in the U.S. Department of State’s Bureau of Cyberspace and Digital Policy, where he advised the White House and interagency partners on the design and implementation of the Technology Prosperity Deals with the Republic of Korea, Japan and the United Kingdom, and engaged with congressional committees on export controls and technology partnerships. He previously served as Chief of Staff of the Department’s Bureau of Oceans and International Environmental and Scientific Affairs.
Dr. Bae holds a Ph.D. in Nuclear Engineering and an M.S. in Technology and Policy from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Missouri University of Science and Technology. He has professional proficiency in Korean and has received ten Superior Honor Awards and nine Meritorious Honor Awards, among them awards in 2016 and 2018 for advancing the U.S.-Republic of Korea bilateral relationship.
Jim Rudyk, CPA, CA, ICD.D
Mr. Rudyk is a senior finance and operations executive with more than 35 years of experience across public companies, private equity-backed businesses and global multinationals, with deep expertise in financial reporting, capital markets, mergers and acquisitions, and governance. He currently serves as Chief Financial Officer of Mevotech L.P., a TorQuest Partners portfolio company that engineers and designs aftermarket driveline, steering and suspension parts. From 2020 to 2026, he served as Chief Financial Officer of Ag Growth International Inc. (TSX: AFN), a global provider of equipment and solutions for the storage, transport and processing of food, with manufacturing operations in Canada, the United States, Brazil, India, France and Italy.
Mr. Rudyk has served on the board of directors of Cronos Group Inc. (NASDAQ: CRON) since 2018, where he is Lead Director and Chair of the Audit Committee. He is a Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA, CA), holds the ICD.D designation from the Institute of Corporate Directors, and earned a Master of Accounting and a Bachelor of Arts from the University of Waterloo.
Intention to Pursue Korea Exchange Listing
Almonty also announced its intention to pursue a listing on the KRX, reflecting its long-standing presence in South Korea, where the Company has been developing Sangdong for more than a decade. A KRX listing would complement the Company’s existing listing on Nasdaq, and Almonty’s common shares will continue to trade on Nasdaq under the symbol “ALM”.
Any listing would be subject to, among other things, review and approval by the KRX and applicable Korean regulatory authorities, market conditions and compliance with applicable securities laws. There can be no assurance that a listing on the KRX will be completed on any particular timeline. The Company will provide further updates as appropriate.
Management Commentary
Lewis Black, Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer of Almonty, said: “Ahead of our annual meeting this year, we told shareholders that the composition of this Board would evolve to reflect the ambition and sophistication of the business that Almonty has become. Today’s appointments put that commitment into action. Eugene has spent more than two decades advancing U.S. economic and technology interests across Asia, including extensive work in South Korea, and that perspective will be invaluable as Sangdong ramps toward full Phase I capacity, expansion into level 2 and the move ahead with the Tungsten oxide plant all as our presence in South Korea continues to grow. Jim is a seasoned public company CFO and audit committee chair whose financial discipline, capital markets experience and governance leadership will strengthen the Board’s oversight as our business grows in scale and complexity. My expectation is we will continue to strengthen our Board in the run up to the next shareholders meeting.
“On behalf of the Board, I want to thank David and Andrew for their service during a period in which Sangdong reached commercial operation and Almonty listed on Nasdaq. With these changes, the Board is well positioned to oversee Almonty’s next phase of growth on behalf of my fellow shareholders. I am very happy they are both remaining with us at Almonty in an active capacity given how many years we have worked together.”
“Turning to the KSX, South Korea is home to our flagship asset and has been central to Almonty’s story since we acquired Sangdong in 2015. We see a listing on the Korea Exchange as a natural complement to our Nasdaq listing and affords the Korean investment base an opportunity to share in the success of Sangdong. We look forward to updating shareholders as that progresses. The appointment of a lead banker in Korea is expected to be made shortly.”
About Almonty
Almonty (NASDAQ: ALM) (Frankfurt: ALI1) is a leading supplier of conflict-free tungsten, a strategic metal critical to the defense and advanced technology sectors. As geopolitical tensions heighten, tungsten has become essential for armor, munitions, and electronics manufacturing. Almonty’s flagship Sangdong Tungsten Mine in South Korea, historically one of the world’s largest and highest-grade tungsten deposits, is expected to be a major contributor to the global non-China tungsten supply chain upon reaching full capacity, directly addressing critical supply vulnerabilities highlighted by recent U.S. defense procurement bans and export restrictions by China. With established operations in Portugal and additional projects in Spain and the United States, Almonty is strategically aligned to meet rapidly rising demand from Western allies committed to supply-chain security and defense readiness. To learn more, please visit https://almonty.com.
