Target Hospitality Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results Reflecting Execution at Scale and Building Momentum on Continued Strategic Transformation
​
Since January 2026, secured over $1.7 billion of multi-year contract awards representing more than 10,000 beds, scaling the strategic Workforce Hospitality Solutions segment to more than 6,200 utilized beds
​
THE WOODLANDS, Texas, November 9, 2026 (PRNewswire) – Target Hospitality Corp. (“Target Hospitality,” “Target” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: TH), one of North America’s largest providers of vertically integrated modular accommodations and value-added hospitality services, today reported results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
​
Financial and Operational Highlights for the quarter ended September 30, 2026
​
● | Revenue of $129.1 million, up 30% year-over-year and 51% sequentially.

● | Net loss of $9.2 million, representing basic and diluted loss per share of $0.09.

● | Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $34.2 million, up 59% year-over-year, with margin expanding approximately 480 bps to 27%, driven by significant growth in the Company’s Workforce Hospitality Solutions (“WHS”) segment.

● | Generated year-to-date Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities of $154.2 million and Discretionary Cash Flow(1) (“DCF”) of $150.0 million led by a significant increase in advance payments from customers associated with WHS segment contract awards.

● | Ended the quarter with total liquidity of approximately $440 million and a total net leverage ratio of 1.4x.

● | Secured multiple scope expansions across recent WHS contract awards, increasing total contract value by more than 10% over initial terms and reflecting customers’ continued project investment and confidence in Target’s execution.

● | Target Hyper/Scale’s highly customizable solutions continue to gain traction with prospective customers amid accelerating demand across data center, power generation and other critical infrastructure development, supporting a geographically diverse growth pipeline exceeding 25,000 beds of potential opportunities, a portion of which is in advanced contract discussions.

● | Advancing opportunities to expand into value-added adjacencies and incremental service offerings, positioning Target to capture a greater share of customers’ total development spend.

​
Executive Commentary
​
“Our third-quarter results reflect a strategic transformation that is delivering meaningful revenue growth and margin expansion. Since January, we have secured multi-year contract awards representing more than $1.7 billion of revenue and over 10,000 beds. With WHS utilized beds now exceeding 6,200 and additional contracted capacity coming online, we are building a foundation for continued growth into 2027 and beyond. Strong execution and growing contributions from these contracts give us significant momentum,” stated Brad Archer, President and Chief Executive Officer.
​
“For customers developing data centers, power generation facilities and other critical infrastructure, attracting and retaining skilled workers is essential to project success. Our vertically integrated, scalable Target Hyper/Scale platform and speed-to-market capabilities differentiate us as a preferred sole-source partner. As adoption grows, we see meaningful potential to expand into value-added adjacencies and capture a greater share of our customers’ total development spend. These advantages underpin the deepest commercial pipeline in our history, with advanced discussions underway on multiple large-scale industrial projects. With a diversified contract portfolio and meaningful financial flexibility, we believe Target is uniquely positioned to capitalize on a multi-decade critical infrastructure build-out. We are firmly committed to converting that opportunity into compounding growth and long-term shareholder value,” concluded Mr. Archer.

Adjusted EBITDA(1) increased 59% to $34.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, from $21.5 million for the same period in 2025, while Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded approximately 480 basis points to 27% of revenue.

As the previously announced multi-year WHS contracts progress through their phased developments over the coming quarters, the combination of strong unit economics and increasing operational leverage is expected to drive further Adjusted EBITDA(1) growth.

Capital Management
​
The Company invested approximately $246 million in capital expenditures for the three months ended September 30, 2026, primarily to support long-term customer contracts in its expanding WHS segment, funded in part by customer advance payments. These contracts are expected to generate significant revenue and operating cash flow as they scale, supporting attractive returns on invested capital.
​
On July 24, 2026, the Company closed on a new $660 million asset-based revolving credit facility (“ABL”), significantly enhancing the Company’s financial flexibility through expanded liquidity and reducing borrowing costs by up to 250 basis points.
​
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had approximately $3 million of cash and cash equivalents and approximately $223 million of borrowings under its $660 million ABL, with approximately $437 million of undrawn ABL commitments, subject to borrowing base availability and other conditions, and a total net leverage ratio of 1.4x. The total net leverage ratio is calculated on a last-quarter-annualized basis, as permitted under the ABL through the quarter ending June 30, 2028.

Business Update
​
Since January 2026, Target has secured more than $1.7 billion in multi-year contract awards representing over 10,000 beds, validating the Company’s pivot into high-value end markets. These awards span a diverse customer base across large-scale industrial projects, including AI data centers and power generation infrastructure. Target has prioritized opportunities anchored by substantial customer investment and tangible development activity. The long duration of these projects supports minimum take-or-pay revenue commitments and, in many cases, significant advance payments. These terms enhance revenue visibility and tie Target’s capital deployment directly to contracted customer demand. By aligning contract terms with customers’ multi-phase project plans, Target is laying the foundation for lasting strategic partnerships.
​
Target’s vertical integration and the unique speed-to-market advantages of its Target Hyper/Scale platform underpin these partnerships and are translating into execution at scale, highlighted by more than 6,200 utilized beds as of September 30, 2026. Target’s comprehensive offering has also driven multiple scope expansions on existing contracts, increasing total contract value by more than 10% over initial terms. This momentum reflects the Company’s multi-decade operating track

Full Year 2026 Financial Outlook
​
Target’s year-to-date performance and continued execution across its rapidly expanding WHS contract portfolio reinforce the Company’s confidence in its full-year 2026 outlook, which it is reaffirming today. The outlook reflects the growing contributions of recently awarded WHS contracts as these communities scale, supported by strong unit economics and disciplined operational execution across the broader portfolio.
​
● | Total revenue between $435 and $445 million

● | Adjusted EBITDA(1) between $105 and $115 million

● | Total Capital Expenditures between $490 and $510 million, excluding acquisitions

​
As Target’s growing WHS contract portfolio comes online and scales through 2026 and into 2027, the Company anticipates continued revenue and Adjusted EBITDA(1) growth. Long-term contractual commitments provide clear revenue visibility, while greater operating scale and strong unit economics are expected to drive further margin expansion. Together, these factors position the Company to achieve annualized revenue exceeding $750 million and annualized Adjusted EBITDA(1) above $300 million exiting 2027. This projection is based entirely on Target’s existing contract portfolio and assumes no contribution from the Company's commercial pipeline.

About Target Hospitality
​
Target Hospitality is one of North America’s largest providers of vertically integrated specialty rental modular accommodations and full-service value-added hospitality solutions in the United States. Target builds, owns and operates a customized and growing network of communities for a range of end users through a full suite of value-added solutions including premium catering and food services, maintenance, housekeeping, grounds-keeping, concierge, laundry services, logistics, security, recreational facilities services, community management, and community design and construction.

Utility and Purposes:
​
EBITDA reflects Net income (loss) excluding the impact of interest expense and loss on extinguishment of debt, provision for income taxes, depreciation, and amortization. We believe that EBITDA is a meaningful indicator of operating performance because we use it to measure our ability to service debt, fund capital expenditures, and expand our business. We also use EBITDA, as do analysts, lenders, investors, and others, to evaluate companies because it excludes certain items that can vary widely across different industries or among companies within the same industry. For example, interest expense can be dependent on a company’s capital structure, debt levels, and credit ratings. Accordingly, the impact of interest expense on earnings can vary significantly among companies. The tax positions of companies can also vary because of their differing abilities to take advantage of tax benefits and because of the tax policies of the jurisdictions in which they operate. As a result, effective tax rates and provision for income taxes can vary considerably among companies. EBITDA also excludes depreciation and amortization expense because companies utilize productive assets of different ages and use different methods of both acquiring and depreciating productive assets. These differences can result in considerable variability in the relative costs of productive assets and the depreciation and amortization expense among companies.
​
Target Hospitality also believes that Adjusted EBITDA is a meaningful indicator of operating performance. Our Adjusted EBITDA reflects adjustments to exclude the effects of additional items, including certain items, that are not reflective of the ongoing operating results of Target Hospitality. In addition, to derive Adjusted EBITDA, we exclude gains or losses on the sale or disposal of depreciable assets and impairment losses because including them in EBITDA is inconsistent with reporting the ongoing performance of our remaining assets. Additionally, the gain or loss on sale or disposal of depreciable assets and impairment losses represents either accelerated depreciation or excess depreciation in previous periods, and depreciation is excluded from EBITDA.
​
Target Hospitality also presents Discretionary cash flows because we believe it provides useful information regarding our business as more fully described below. Discretionary cash flows indicate the amount of cash available after maintenance capital expenditures for specialty rental assets for, among other things, investments in our existing business.
Adjusted gross profit, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Discretionary cash flows are not measurements of Target Hospitality’s financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered as alternatives to Gross profit, Net income (loss), or other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP, or as alternatives to Cash flow from operating activities as measures of Target Hospitality’s liquidity. Adjusted gross profit, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, and Discretionary cash flows should not be considered as discretionary cash available to Target Hospitality to reinvest in the growth of our business or as measures of cash that is available to it to meet our obligations. In addition, the measurement of Adjusted gross profit, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, and Discretionary cash flows may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. Target Hospitality’s management believes that Adjusted gross profit, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, and Discretionary cash flows provides useful information to investors about Target Hospitality and its financial condition and results of operations for the following reasons: (i) they are among the measures used by Target Hospitality’s management team to evaluate its operating performance; (ii) they are among the measures used by Target Hospitality’s management team to make day-to-day operating decisions, (iii) they are frequently used by securities analysts, lenders, investors and other interested parties as a common performance measure and to compare results across companies in Target Hospitality’s industry.
