Spire reports fiscal 2026 results
ST. LOUIS (November 17, 2026) – Spire Inc. (NYSE: SR) today reported results for its fiscal 2026 year ended September 30. Highlights include:
•
Completed acquisition of the Piedmont Natural Gas Tennessee business and divestitures of Spire Marketing and Spire Storage businesses
•
Fiscal 2026 net income from continuing operations of $168.4 million, or $2.62 per diluted share, compared to $199.5 million, or $3.14 per share, a year ago
•
Adjusted earnings* from continuing operations of $243.6 million, or $4.02 per share, compared to $211.1 million, or $3.34 per share a year ago, an increase of 20%
•
Fiscal 2027 adjusted earnings per share (EPS) guidance range lowered to $5.05–$5.25
•
Introduced fiscal 2028 adjusted EPS guidance range of $6.10–$6.30
•
Raised long-term adjusted EPS compound annual growth rate target t0 6%–8% for fiscal 2028-2031, using fiscal 2028 guidance midpoint as a base
•
Raised common stock dividend by 4.8%, marking 24 years of consecutive growth
Following the divestitures of the Spire Marketing and Spire Storage businesses, results discussed in this release reflect continuing operations for the gas utilities, excluding Spire Tennessee, unless otherwise noted. Results for fiscal 2026 reflect solid performance across the utilities supported by infrastructure investment and disciplined cost management. Gas Utility earnings improved year-over-year, driven by new rates at Spire Missouri and Spire Alabama and increased off-system sales. These favorable items were partially offset by lower Spire Missouri usage, net of weather mitigation.
“Fiscal 2026 was a transformational year for Spire as we completed our transition to a focused, fully regulated utility platform,” said Scott Doyle, president and chief executive officer of Spire. “Our fiscal 2026 results reflect strong execution across the business, supported by disciplined cost management and continued investment in infrastructure to serve our customers. Our fiscal 2028 guidance and increased long-term EPS growth target of 6%–8% through 2031 reflect the improved business profile and earnings visibility of our regulated platform. Spire is well positioned to deliver sustainable long-term earnings and dividend growth, while continuing to safely deliver reliable and affordable natural gas to our customers.”

Continuing operations
During fiscal 2026, Spire reported consolidated net income from continuing operations of $168.4 million, or $2.62 per diluted share, compared to $199.5 million, or $3.14 per diluted share in the prior year. Adjusted earnings from continuing operations reflected earnings of $243.6 million, or $4.02 per share, compared to $211.1 million, or $3.34 per share last year.
Gas Utility
Gas Utility fiscal 2026 adjusted earnings were $286.5 million, an increase from $231.4 million in the prior year, reflecting improvement at both Spire Missouri and Spire Alabama.
Contribution margin increased $159.0 million, driven primarily by new Spire Missouri rates effective October 2025, higher Missouri Infrastructure System Replacement Surcharge (ISRS) revenues, and Spire Alabama rates under the Rate Stabilization and Equalization (RSE) mechanism that were effective December 2025. Earnings also benefited from higher off-system sales at Spire Missouri and Spire Alabama. These favorable items were partially offset by lower Spire Missouri usage, net of weather mitigation.
Operation and maintenance expense was $16.6 million higher compared to prior year. After adjusting for the impact of a pension reclass and bad debt expense, operation and maintenance expense increased $3.5 million, as the higher operating expenses were partially offset by a reduction in employee-related costs.
Depreciation expense increased $44.0 million year over year, driven by capital investment and updated depreciation schedules implemented under Spire Missouri’s new rates. Taxes other than income taxes increased $21.7 million primarily reflecting revised property tax amortization included in new rates at Spire Missouri. Interest expense increased $8.0 million due to higher long-term debt balances.
Other
Spire’s other activities reported an adjusted loss from continuing operations of $42.9 million compared to an adjusted loss of $20.3 million in the prior year. The variance in earnings is primarily due to higher corporate costs and interest expense.
Discontinued operations
Spire’s earnings from discontinued operations were $325.4 million during fiscal 2026, which includes an after-tax gain on sale of $257.6 million.

Continuing operations
Due to the seasonal nature of natural gas demand and the timing of regulatory recovery in our gas utility business, we typically incur a loss in our fiscal fourth quarter ended September 30. For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Spire reported a consolidated net loss from continuing operations of $94.4 million, or $(1.60) per diluted share, compared to prior-year net loss of $48.6 million, or $(0.89) per diluted share. Adjusted earnings from continuing operations reflected a loss of $58.2 million, or $(0.99) per share, compared to a prior-year loss of $37.0 million, or $(0.69) per diluted share.
Gas Utility adjusted earnings reflect higher Spire Missouri rates and ISRS revenues and Spire Alabama rates under the RSE mechanism. These favorable items were more than offset by lower CCM benefits in Spire Alabama in addition to higher operation and maintenance expense, depreciation expense and taxes other than income taxes compared to the prior year period. Interest expense also increased year over year.
Spire’s other activities reflect higher corporate costs and interest expense in the current quarter.
Guidance and Outlook
Spire expects fiscal 2027 adjusted earnings to be in the range of $5.05–$5.25 per share, compared with its previous guidance range of $5.40–$5.60 per share. The updated outlook primarily reflects recent regulatory outcomes in Tennessee and Alabama, as well as updated assumptions for weather-normalized margin recovery in Missouri, including continued exposure to variability in fiscal 2027.
Looking ahead, Spire expects fiscal 2028 adjusted earnings per share of $6.10–$6.30 and has increased its long-term adjusted EPS compound annual growth rate target to 6%–8% through fiscal 2031, using the fiscal 2028 guidance midpoint of $6.20 per share as a base. The long-term growth outlook includes earnings from the Spire MoGas expansion project, which are expected to begin during construction, ahead of the project's targeted in-service date in fiscal 2030.
Spire has also increased its 10-year capital investment plan to $11.8 billion through fiscal 2036, supporting continued investment in its regulated utility infrastructure and long-term growth. Capital expenditures in fiscal 2027 are expected to total $880 million.
Dividend
Reflecting solid performance in fiscal 2026 and expectations for future growth, the Spire board of directors increased the quarterly common stock dividend to $0.865 per share, an increase of 4.8%. This raises the annualized rate by $0.16 per share to $3.46. The dividend is payable January 5, 2027, to shareholders of record on December 11, 2026. Spire has continuously paid a cash dividend since 1946, and 2027 will mark the 24th consecutive year of a dividend increase.

About Spire
At Spire (NYSE: SR), our vision is to deliver a stronger energy future as an industry-leading natural gas provider. We safely and reliably serve the natural gas needs of close to 2 million homes and businesses through gas utilities in Alabama, Mississippi, Missouri and Tennessee, making us one of the largest publicly traded natural gas companies in the country. We are committed to transforming our business through growing organically, investing in infrastructure and driving continuous improvement. Learn more at SpireEnergy.com.
