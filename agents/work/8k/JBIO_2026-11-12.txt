Jade Biosciences Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update
• | JADE101 Phase 3 registrational trial in IgA nephropathy (EVERGREEN) expected to initiate in the fourth quarter of 2026

• | JADE101 Phase 2 clinical trial in IgA nephropathy (JUNIPER) ongoing; interim data expected in the second quarter of 2027

• | JADE201 Phase 1 clinical trial in rheumatoid arthritis ongoing; interim data expected in 2027

• | JADE301 Phase 1 clinical trial in dermatomyositis expected to initiate in the fourth quarter of 2026, with interim data expected in the second half of 2027

• | Cash, cash equivalents, and investments of $476 million as of September 30, 2026 expected to provide runway into the fourth quarter of 2028

San Francisco and Vancouver, British Columbia – November 13, 2026 – Jade Biosciences, Inc. (“Jade” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: JBIO), a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing best-in-class therapies for autoimmune diseases, today announced financial results for the third quarter ending September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.
“The third quarter marked another period of meaningful progress for Jade as we advanced each of our development programs toward important clinical milestones,” said Tom Frohlich, Chief Executive Officer of Jade. “We are preparing to initiate the Phase 3 EVERGREEN trial of JADE101 ahead of schedule, reflecting both our rapid execution and our continued conviction in JADE101’s potential best-in-class profile. We also expect to advance JADE301 into the clinic this quarter, which, alongside JADE101 and JADE201, will give us three clinical-stage programs heading into 2027. With multiple clinical catalysts expected across our pipeline next year, we see significant opportunities to further demonstrate the differentiated profiles of our programs and ultimately deliver meaningful new therapies for patients with autoimmune disease.”
Pipeline and Corporate Updates
JADE101: anti-APRIL monoclonal antibody for IgA nephropathy (IgAN)
• | Jade expects to initiate EVERGREEN, a Phase 3 registrational trial of JADE101 in IgAN, in the fourth quarter of 2026, reflecting the Company’s rapid execution as JADE101 advances into registrational development. A Trial-in-Progress poster describing EVERGREEN was presented at the American Society of Nephrology (ASN) Kidney Week 2026.

• | Enrollment in the Phase 2 JUNIPER trial of JADE101 in IgAN is ongoing, with interim data expected in the second quarter of 2027. A Trial-in-Progress poster describing JUNIPER was presented at ASN Kidney Week 2026.

JADE201: anti-BAFF-R monoclonal antibody for autoimmune diseases
• | The Phase 1 clinical trial evaluating JADE201 in participants with rheumatoid arthritis is ongoing, with interim biomarker-rich data expected in 2027 to inform indication prioritization.

JADE301: anti-IFN-b monoclonal antibody for dermatomyositis
• | Jade presented preclinical data at the 2026 European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology (EADV) Congress supporting the potentially differentiated profile of JADE301 in dermatomyositis. Key results included:

• | JADE301 demonstrated selective, high-affinity binding to IFN-β and greater functional potency than a dazukibart comparator in disease-relevant human cells.

• | Following subcutaneous (SC) administration in non-human primates, JADE301 demonstrated an approximately 25-day half-life and was well tolerated at all dose levels evaluated, supporting the potential for infrequent SC dosing.

• | Jade expects to initiate a Phase 1 trial of JADE301 in healthy volunteers in the fourth quarter of 2026, with interim data anticipated in the second half of 2027.

Corporate
• | Expanded Brad Dahms’ role and title to Chief Financial Officer and Chief Business Officer. Mr. Dahms will continue to oversee the Company’s finance and investor relations functions and will now also lead business development and corporate strategy as Jade advances its pipeline toward multiple clinical readouts in 2027.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
• | Cash Position: As of September 30, 2026, Jade had cash, cash equivalents, and investments of $475.7 million. Based on its current operating plans, Jade believes that its existing cash, cash equivalents and investments will enable it to fund its operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements into the fourth quarter of 2028.

• | Research and Development (R&D) expenses: R&D expenses were $30.8 million for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $8.8 million compared to the third quarter of 2025, and included $3.6 million in stock-based compensation expense. R&D expenses increased primarily due to increased costs around chemistry, manufacturing and controls, clinical activity, and personnel-related costs and related stock-based compensation expense.

• | General and Administrative (G&A) expenses: G&A expenses were $8.8 million for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $3.4 million compared to the third quarter of 2025, and included $3.5 million in stock-based compensation expense. The increase in G&A expenses was primarily due to higher personnel-related costs and related stock-based compensation expense.

• | Net loss: Net loss totaled $35.1 million for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $25.2 million for the third quarter of 2025. Net loss increased primarily due to higher R&D and G&A expenses, as described above.

About Jade Biosciences, Inc.
Jade Biosciences is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing best-in-class therapies that address critical unmet needs in autoimmune diseases. Jade’s lead candidate, JADE101, targets the cytokine APRIL, and is currently being evaluated for the treatment of immunoglobulin A nephropathy. Jade’s pipeline also includes JADE201, an afucosylated anti-BAFF-R monoclonal antibody, as well as JADE301, an anti-IFN-b monoclonal antibody. Jade was launched based on assets licensed from Paragon Therapeutics, an antibody discovery engine founded by Fairmount. For more information, visit JadeBiosciences.com and follow the Company on LinkedIn.
