Maximus Reports Fourth Quarter and Full Year Results for Fiscal Year 2026
Completes Agile Defense acquisition, delivers earnings above guidance, and establishes fiscal year 2027 guidance
(Tysons, Va. - November 19, 2026) - Maximus (NYSE: MMS), a leading provider of government services, reported financial results for the three months and year ended September 30, 2026. The acquisition of Agile Defense, LLC (Agile Defense) was completed on November 18, 2026.
Highlights for fiscal year 2026 include:
•Full year revenue of $5.23 billion, landing within the guidance range.
•Full year operating margin of 11.7% and adjusted EBITDA margin of 14.2%, along with diluted earnings per share of $7.27 and adjusted diluted earnings per share of $8.24, which exceeded the top end of guidance and increased 12.0% over the prior year on an adjusted earnings per share basis.
•Full year cash flows from operating activities of $112 million and free cash flow of $69 million. Subsequent to fiscal year-end, collections totaled more than $330 million from a major customer.
•Repurchases of Maximus common stock totaling approximately $192 million during fiscal year 2026.
•Establishes fiscal year 2027 guidance of $5.45 billion to $5.65 billion of revenue, adjusted diluted earnings per share of $7.50 to $8.00, and free cash flow of $700 million to $800 million; fiscal year 2027 guidance reflects the absence of an estimated $1.05 per share of benefit recognized in fiscal year 2026 attributable to a specific program, which is discussed further below.
•A quarterly cash dividend of $0.33 per share payable on November 30, 2026, to shareholders of record on November 13, 2026.
"Fiscal year 2026 reflected the benefits of disciplined execution, technology-enabled productivity enhancements, and thoughtful capital allocation," said Bruce Caswell, President and Chief Executive Officer. "Throughout the year, we continued deploying automation and AI-enabled solutions across our operations, improving efficiency, strengthening profitability, and delivering meaningful outcomes for customers. These efforts contributed to adjusted diluted earnings per share of $8.24, representing 12.0% growth over the prior year and performance above the top end of our most recently updated guidance range."
Caswell continued, "Subsequent to fiscal year-end, we completed the acquisition of Agile Defense, which significantly expands our position in defense and national security markets and enhances our portfolio of technology-enabled capabilities. As we enter fiscal year 2027, we remain focused on integrating Agile Defense, growing existing customer relationships, executing on opportunities associated with recent policy initiatives, and further advancing our leadership in AI-enabled solutions and technology innovation."
Agile Defense Acquisition Close
On November 18, 2026, Maximus completed its acquisition of Agile Defense, a provider of mission-focused technology solutions supporting customers across the Department of War, Intelligence Community, homeland security, and civilian agencies.
1
The acquisition expands Maximus' presence in defense and national security markets while adding differentiated capabilities in cybersecurity, digital modernization, software and analytics, cloud solutions, and AI-enabled mission support services. The transaction further diversifies the U.S. Federal Service's portfolio, enhances access to strategically important customers, and increases exposure to attractive markets with favorable long-term growth characteristics.
Agile Defense is expected to contribute approximately ten months of revenue during fiscal year 2027 and is included in the fiscal year 2027 guidance.
Consolidated Fiscal Year 2026 Results
Revenue for fiscal year 2026 was $5.23 billion, compared to $5.43 billion reported for the prior fiscal year. As previously discussed, fiscal year 2025 benefited from elevated natural disaster support activities and temporary clinical program volume surges that did not recur at the same level during fiscal year 2026. Revenue finished within the fiscal year 2026 guidance range and was consistent with our expectations throughout the year.
For fiscal year 2026, the operating margin was 11.7% and adjusted EBITDA margin was 14.2%, compared to 9.7% and 12.9%, respectively, in the prior fiscal year. The improvement reflects technology-enabled productivity enhancements, including automation initiatives, disciplined cost management efforts, and strong operational performance.
Diluted earnings per share were $7.27 and adjusted diluted earnings per share were $8.24 for fiscal year 2026. This compares to $5.51 and $7.36, respectively, in the prior fiscal year. Adjusted diluted earnings per share exceeded the top end of the most recently updated guidance range for fiscal year 2026 and increased 12.0% over the prior year. Earnings growth was driven principally by improved operating performance reflected in the margin improvement and the benefit of capital deployment actions, including share repurchases.
U.S. Federal Services Segment
U.S. Federal Services Segment revenue for fiscal year 2026 was approximately $2.99 billion. Prior fiscal year revenue of $3.07 billion benefited from nearly $100 million of elevated natural disaster support activity and temporary clinical volume surges that did not recur at the same level during fiscal year 2026.
The segment operating margin for fiscal year 2026 was 18.1%, compared to 15.3% reported for the prior fiscal year. Margin expansion was driven by technology-enabled efficiencies, productivity improvements, strong execution across core program areas, and favorable operating leverage generated through automation initiatives deployed throughout the fiscal year. Together, these factors helped create opportunities to efficiently process higher volumes and improve profitability, such as incentive payments on the Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Disability Examination (VA MDE) program during the first three quarters of the fiscal year 2026.
Fourth quarter results of fiscal year 2026 reflected the previously disclosed customer-directed modification to the VA MDE program regarding performance incentives. The fourth quarter also benefited from strong performance across the clinical portfolio, which is expected to moderate in fiscal year 2027.
U.S. Services Segment
U.S. Services Segment revenue for fiscal year 2026 was $1.68 billion, compared to $1.76 billion reported for the prior fiscal year. Revenue performance was generally consistent with expectations and reflected varying volumes across several contract areas. Revenue trends improved throughout fiscal year 2026, culminating in positive organic growth in the fourth quarter.
The segment operating margin for fiscal year 2026 was 9.1%, compared to 9.7% reported in the prior fiscal year. Fiscal year 2026 results included a $6.9 million non-cash impairment charge related to a software asset developed and capitalized for a specific customer.
We continue to see encouraging demand indicators associated with Medicaid community engagement requirements, program integrity activities, eligibility administration, and other state government initiatives that are expected to support future growth opportunities.
2
Outside the U.S. Segment
Outside the U.S. Segment revenue for fiscal year 2026 was $557 million, compared to $600 million reported for the prior fiscal year. The revenue decrease was attributable to previously divested businesses as well as lower revenue across several employment services and clinical programs amidst certain customer-specific challenges.
The segment reported a modest operating loss, primarily reflecting the lower revenue across those employment services and clinical programs. Maximus continues to focus on driving profitable growth through disciplined bid selection, operational improvements, and successful conversion of its sales pipeline.
Sales and Pipeline
Signed contract awards for fiscal year 2026 totaled $2.51 billion, and contracts pending (awarded but unsigned) totaled $1.86 billion at September 30, 2026. The book-to-bill ratio at September 30, 2026, was 0.5x on a trailing twelve-month basis and the quarterly book-to-bill ratio improved to approximately 1.0x, representing the strongest quarterly award performance of fiscal year 2026.
The fourth quarter book-to-bill ratio was enabled by a signed contract award for a significant state Medicaid contract renewal representing more than $800 million of total contract value over a ten-year period. Included in the contracts pending balance are two recently awarded state Medicaid contract renewals totaling approximately $528 million of total contract value.
The total sales pipeline at September 30, 2026, was $44.8 billion (comprised of approximately $4.01 billion in proposals pending, $1.61 billion in proposals in preparation, and $39.2 billion in opportunities tracking). New work opportunities represent approximately 61% of the total sales pipeline, and U.S. Federal Services Segment opportunities represent approximately 58% of the total sales pipeline.
During fiscal year 2026, Maximus achieved a recompete win rate of approximately 89%, reflecting the strength of its customer relationships, operational performance, and technology-enabled service delivery model.
Balance Sheet and Cash Flows
At September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents totaled $280 million, and gross debt was $1.61 billion. The ratio of debt, net of allowed cash, to consolidated EBITDA for the full year ended September 30, 2026, as calculated in accordance with our credit agreement, was 1.8x.
Following the completion of the Agile Defense acquisition, our pro forma leverage ratio is expected to be approximately 2.3x, remaining within Maximus' targeted leverage range of 2x to 3x and preserving financial flexibility for future capital allocation priorities.
For fiscal year 2026, cash flows from operating activities totaled $112 million and free cash flow was $69 million, enabled by $278 million of free cash flow for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. This compares to $429 million and $366 million, respectively, in the prior fiscal year.
Days sales outstanding, or DSO, were 93 days at September 30, 2026, compared to 98 days at June 30, 2026. Subsequent to fiscal year-end and through November 13, 2026, we collected more than $330 million from a major customer that had been anticipated in our fiscal year 2026 guidance. As a result, a portion of cash generation expected during fiscal year 2026 shifted into fiscal year 2027 guidance due to timing-related factors rather than collection concerns.
During fiscal year 2026, we repurchased approximately 2.6 million shares of Maximus common stock totaling approximately $192 million. As of November 13, 2026, the full $400 million balance from the most recent Board of Directors authorization remains available.
On October 5, 2026, our Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.33 for each share of our common stock outstanding. The dividend is payable on November 30, 2026, to shareholders of record on November 13, 2026.
Fiscal Year 2027 Guidance
Maximus is establishing fiscal year 2027 guidance.
3
We expect revenue to range between $5.45 billion and $5.65 billion. The outlook includes approximately ten months of contribution from Agile Defense and reflects expectations for continued growth opportunities across defense and national security markets, state government programs, and technology-enabled services.
Maximus expects adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 12.8% for fiscal year 2027 and adjusted diluted earnings per share to range between $7.50 and $8.00 per share.
This guidance assumes no incentive contribution related to the VA MDE contracts during fiscal year 2027. Fiscal year 2026 included incentive-related revenue and profit equal to an estimated $75 million and $1.05 adjusted earnings per share, respectively, total contribution across the first three quarters of the fiscal year 2026. Excluding the incentive contribution related to the VA MDE contracts to the fiscal year 2026, the midpoint of our adjusted diluted earnings per share guidance represents growth of approximately 8%.
We expect free cash flow to range between $700 million and $800 million. The outlook reflects DSO returning to the historical range of 60-70 days and includes the benefit of collections that shifted beyond fiscal year-end.
For fiscal year 2027, Maximus expects amortization of intangible assets of approximately $96 million, depreciation and amortization of property, equipment and capitalized software of approximately $55 million, interest expense of approximately $110 million, an effective tax rate of approximately 25%, and weighted average shares outstanding of approximately 52.1 million.
