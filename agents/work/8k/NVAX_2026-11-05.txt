Press Release
Novavax Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Operational Highlights
•Reports total revenue of $68 million for the third quarter of 2026
•Raises full-year 2026 Non-GAAP Adjusted Total Revenue by $145 million to $400 million at mid-point and improves GAAP Combined R&D and SG&A expense guidance by $5 million at mid-point
•In October, Novavax earned a $125 million milestone upon Sanofi’s launch of Phase 3 influenza-COVID-19 combination vaccine trial in Europe
•Sanofi confirms their intent to initiate a U.S. Phase 3 influenza-COVID-19 combination vaccine trial in 2027 to support U.S. approval
•Differentiated C. difficile vaccine program anticipated to advance into the clinic as early as 2027
•Multiple Novavax and partner-led experiments with Matrix-M® currently underway in a wide array of infectious disease targets and oncology models
•Company to host conference call today at 8:30 a.m. ET
GAITHERSBURG, Md., November 5, 2026 – Novavax, Inc. (Nasdaq: NVAX) today announced its financial results and operational highlights for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“This quarter, we continued to execute across all three pillars of our strategy,” said John C. Jacobs, President and Chief Executive Officer, Novavax. “Our partner Sanofi initiated their Phase 3 influenza-COVID-19 combination vaccine trial vaccine trial in Europe, triggering a $125 million milestone payment. Our focused R&D efforts are building a growing body of data on what our technology can do in infectious disease, advancing our C. difficile candidate towards the clinic and exploring oncology as a potential new area of application. And we have been doing this while continuing to reduce expenses and operate with financial discipline. Over time, we believe this strategy can build a robust and profitable Novavax, with multiple partners and products, diversified revenue streams and a meaningful impact on global health.”
Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights
Key Business Highlights
oIn October 2026, Novavax earned a $125 million milestone payment with the initiation of Sanofi’s Phase 3 influenza-COVID-19 combination vaccine trial in Europe
oSanofi confirmed to Novavax their intent to initiate a U.S. Phase 3 CIC trial in 2027 to support U.S. approval. Novavax is eligible to receive an additional milestone of $225 million upon a U.S. commercial launch of a CIC vaccine following successful completion by Sanofi of clinical and U.S. FDA regulatory requirements.
oNovavax is eligible to receive royalties in the high single digits to sub-teens as a percentage of Sanofi’s global net sales on a CIC product(s).
oManufacturing technology transfer to Sanofi for Nuvaxovid™ is expected to be completed in mid-2027, which would trigger a $75 million milestone payment to Novavax.
oIn August 2026, Novavax’s commercial partners received approvals for the XFG-adapted Nuvaxovid formulation in major markets, including the U.S., the EU and Japan.
oSanofi initiated commercial activities for the 2026–2027 COVID-19 vaccination season in the U.S. and select international markets.
oSanofi is expanding Nuvaxovid’s reach through physician engagement, pharmacy activation and direct-to-consumer outreach.
oNovavax is eligible to receive royalties in the high teens to low twenties as a percentage of Sanofi’s global net sales for Nuvaxovid.
oTakeda initiated commercial activities for the 2026–2027 COVID-19 vaccination season in Japan.
oNovavax is eligible to receive an annual milestone payment and mid-to-high teens percentage of Takeda’s net sales in Japan each year.
oDifferentiated C. difficile (C. diff) vaccine program is anticipated to advance into the clinic as early as 2027.
oNovavax presented preclinical data from its C. diff program showing robust immune responses and protection in lethal challenge models at the 9th International C. Difficile Symposium in September 2026.
oMultiple scientific evaluations by Novavax, partners and material transfer agreement collaborations are underway. These are expected to add to the growing body of data relating to Matrix-M’s utility in infectious disease and its potential in oncology, which could enable future licensing opportunities.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $68 million, a 3% decrease compared to $70 million in the same period in 2025. Product sales of $26 million in the third quarter of 2026 were 91% higher than the same period in 2025 and consisted of Novavax’s supply sales of Nuvaxovid to Sanofi and Matrix-M to license partners. Licensing royalties and other revenue of $43 million in the third quarter of 2026 were 25% lower than the same period in 2025 driven primarily by a $42 million reduction year-over-year in research and development (R&D) reimbursement revenue as Novavax nears completion of its support of Sanofi Nuvaxovid clinical support for postmarketing commitments. The third quarter of 2026 includes $11 million in non-cash deferred royalty recognition related to prior Nuvaxovid sales by SK bioscience to the South Korean government.
•Cost of sales for the third quarter of 2026 was $23 million, compared to $21 million in the same period in 2025.
•R&D expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $52 million, compared to $98 million in the same period in 2025. GAAP and Non-GAAP R&D expenses, decreased by 47% and 7%, respectively compared to the same period in 2025. R&D expenses reimbursed by partners in the third quarter of 2026 were $4
3
million. The lower R&D expenses were driven by the near completion of Sanofi clinical support for Nuvaxovid, decreased seasonal strain change support for Nuvaxovid and ongoing Novavax cost reduction program.
•Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $24 million, a 25% decrease compared to $32 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily due to the transition of lead commercial activities to Sanofi and the elimination of commercial infrastructure plus the ongoing general administrative cost reduction program.
•Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $31 million, compared to a net loss of $202 million in the same period in 2025.
•Cash, cash equivalents, marketable securities and restricted cash (Cash) were $627 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $751 million as of December 31, 2025.

About Novavax
Novavax, Inc. (Nasdaq: NVAX) tackles some of the world’s most pressing health challenges with its scientific expertise in vaccines and its proven technology platform, including its Matrix-M adjuvant and protein-based nanoparticles. The Company’s corporate growth strategy is designed to deliver value via three key strategic pillars: partnering its technology, targeted and capital-efficient R&D innovation and a lean and efficient operating model. This includes maximizing impact through partnerships for its marketed products (Nuvaxovid, R21™/Matrix-M), Matrix technology and R&D assets. Please visit novavax.com and LinkedIn for more information.
Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The Company presents the following non-GAAP financial measures in this press release: Non-GAAP Combined R&D and SG&A Expenses, Adjusted Total Revenue and Adjusted Licensing, Royalties and Other Revenue. Non-GAAP financial measures refer to financial information adjusted from financial measures prepared in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (GAAP). The Company believes that the presentation of these adjusted financial measures is useful to investors as they provide additional information on comparisons between periods by including certain items that affect overall comparability. The Company uses these non-GAAP financial measures for business planning purposes and to consider underlying trends of its business. Non-GAAP financial measures should be considered in addition to, and not as an alternative for, the Company’s reported results prepared in accordance with GAAP. Our use of non-GAAP financial measures may differ from similar measures reported by other companies and may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures. The Company is unable to reconcile these revenue forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measures without unreasonable effort because the Company is reliant on partner sales forecasts for certain revenue categories, which are not available.
