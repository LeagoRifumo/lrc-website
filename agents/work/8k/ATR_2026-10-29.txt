Aptar Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Crystal Lake, Illinois, October 29, 2026 -- AptarGroup, Inc. (NYSE:ATR), a global leader in drug delivery, dosing and protection technologies, and consumer product dispensing, today reported the following third quarter results for the period ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the corresponding period of the last fiscal year.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
(Compared to the prior year quarter; see Non-GAAP section for full definitions; see reconciliation for Non-GAAP measures)
•Reported sales increased 6% and core sales increased 5%
•Reported net income was $33 million and reported earnings per share were $0.51, impacted by deconsolidation of the Russian subsidiary
•Adjusted earnings per share were $1.52, at the higher end of the guidance range, compared to $1.62 in the prior year period at comparable exchange rates
•Adjusted EBITDA margin was 21.3%, compared to 23.2% in the prior year quarter
•Returned $61 million in the quarter and $272 million year-to-date to shareholders through share repurchases and dividends
•Subsequent to the quarter, the Board of Directors approved an increase in the quarterly cash dividend from $0.48 to $0.50
“We delivered broad-based revenue growth across all three segments. Pharma delivered impressive growth in central nervous system therapies, pulmonary, injectables and consumer healthcare, while maintaining profitability at the high end of our long-term target range. Our Pharma pipeline remains strong, with continued focus on building the pipeline and converting opportunities into future growth. New technologies and development programs are expanding the role we can play with customers across the drug development cycle. Beauty and Closures also delivered strong top-line growth, including double-digit core sales growth in Closures, where innovation in beverage dispensing continues to gain momentum. As I begin my tenure as CEO, our direction is clear: deepen our position with customers, accelerate innovation, execute with greater consistency and allocate capital thoughtfully. These priorities will guide how we drive profitable growth and create long-term value for Aptar and our shareholders,” said Gael Touya, Aptar President and CEO.
Third Quarter Results
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, reported sales increased 6% to $1.02 billion compared to $961 million in the prior year and core sales increased 5% compared to the prior year period.

Pharma’s reported sales increased 2% compared to the prior year period and core sales increased 1%. Adjusting for emergency medicine destocking, Pharma delivered 8% core sales growth in the quarter. Performance was supported by continued growth across a number of end markets, including central nervous system and pulmonary therapies, nasal decongestants, eye care and dermal drug delivery solutions, and demand related to biologics, vaccines and GLP-1 therapies. These growth drivers were partially offset by the anticipated reduction in emergency medicine sales. Pharma delivered adjusted EBITDA margin of 36.4% a decline of 80 basis points, remaining above our long-term target despite a short-term unfavorable product mix related to destocking. Royalties and productivity improvements continued to positively impact margins.
Beauty’s reported sales increased 9% when compared to the prior year period, with core sales growth of 8%. There was increased demand for prestige fragrance dispensing, color cosmetics, as well as hair care applications, and pass throughs of higher input costs also contributed positively. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 11.4%, a decline of 70 basis points, including unfavorable mix and ongoing challenges in North American operations.
Closures’ reported sales rose 11% from the prior year period, with core sales growth of 11%. Beverage and food sales grew significantly, led by strong demand for bottled water and concentrates, continued momentum from our latest beverage dispensing closure innovation, as well as strong condiment sales. Pass throughs of higher input costs contributed positively to core sales growth, however, the dilutive impact drove the decline of 60 basis points in Adjusted EBITDA margin to 15.5%.
During the third quarter, Aptar deconsolidated its Russian subsidiary following a governmental decree placing the subsidiary under external administration and the resulting loss of control. Aptar recorded a $58.0 million loss on deconsolidation which included approximately $30.0 million of cash held by the subsidiary. The combined impact was $61.2 million before tax, including approximately $2.6 million of related receivable reserves and $0.5 million of related legal costs; and $60.4 million after tax, or $0.94 per diluted share.
Reported third quarter earnings per share were $0.51 compared to $1.92 reported a year ago. Adjusted earnings per share were $1.52, compared to the prior year period’s adjusted earnings per share of $1.62, including comparable exchange rates. The third quarter reported effective tax rate was 45.6%, reflecting the non-deductible tax impact of the deconsolidation of the Russian subsidiary. The adjusted effective tax rate was 23.4%, compared to the prior year period’s reported effective tax rate of 17.1% and adjusted effective tax rate of 20.8%.

Outlook
Regarding Aptar’s outlook, Touya stated, “We expect Aptar to finish the year strong, supported by continued Pharma growth, particularly in consumer healthcare and injectables and without the headwind from emergency medicine destocking. Our Pharma pipeline remains strong, with new technologies and development programs expanding our reach across therapeutic areas and advancing opportunities for future growth. Closures continues its great momentum, with innovation helping drive increased demand, particularly for our beverage technologies. In Beauty, we remain focused on improving our long-term North American trajectory, while we continue to see pockets of healthy demand in areas, such as prestige fragrance, globally.”
Aptar currently expects adjusted earnings per share for the fourth quarter of 2026 to be in the range of $1.39 to $1.47. This guidance assumes an effective tax rate range of 17.5% to 19.5%. The earnings per share guidance range is assuming a 1.12 Euro to USD exchange rate.
Cash Dividends and Share Repurchases
During the third quarter, Aptar repurchased approximately 234 thousand shares for $30 million. Aptar may repurchase shares through the open market, privately negotiated transactions or other programs, subject to market conditions. As previously announced, Aptar’s Board of Directors approved a quarterly cash dividend of $0.50 per share. The payment date is December 3, 2026, to stockholders of record as of November 12, 2026.

About Aptar
Aptar is a global leader in drug delivery, dosing and protection technologies, and consumer product dispensing. Aptar partners with the world’s top healthcare and consumer brands to deliver medicines and create exceptional user experiences. Serving diverse markets, from pharmaceutical to beauty to food and beverage, Aptar combines market expertise with proprietary design, engineering and science to develop innovative solutions that help improve lives worldwide. Headquartered in Crystal Lake, Illinois, Aptar employs 14,000 dedicated people across 20 countries. Learn more at http://www.aptar.com.

Presentation of Non-GAAP Information
This press release refers to certain non-GAAP financial measures, including current year adjusted earnings per share and adjusted EBITDA, which exclude the impact of restructuring initiatives, acquisition-related costs, certain purchase accounting adjustments related to acquisitions and investments, gains from remeasurement of equity method investments and net unrealized investment gains and losses related to observable market price changes on equity securities, Russia deconsolidation impacts, and other special items. Core sales and adjusted earnings per share also neutralize the impact of foreign currency translation effects when comparing current results to the prior year. Core sales also exclude material acquisitions and, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Russian subsidiary’s net sales from both periods. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as earnings before net interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization, restructuring initiatives, acquisition-related costs, purchase accounting adjustments related to acquisitions and investments, gains from remeasurement of equity method investments, net unrealized investment gains and losses related to observable market price changes on equity securities, Russia deconsolidation impacts, and other special items. Russia deconsolidation impacts comprise the loss on deconsolidation of our Russian subsidiary and related receivable reserves and legal costs described above, which we exclude because they resulted from a government action rather than from the operations of our businesses and therefore do not reflect the underlying performance of our remaining operations. For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, “Other special items” include costs incurred related to non-ordinary-course litigation, specifically: lawsuits between Aptar and ARS Pharmaceuticals, Inc., involving Aptar’s claims of trade-secret misappropriation and contractual breaches and ARS’s lawsuit against Aptar under U.S. antitrust laws; and patent infringement actions filed by Nemera La Verpillière SAS in Germany and France relating to certain of Aptar’s ophthalmic products. These costs are excluded because they do not reflect our core operating performance. Please refer to “Legal Proceedings” within Note 13 - Commitments and Contingencies within Aptar’s Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025 and subsequent SEC filings for more information. Adjusted EBITDA margin is adjusted EBITDA divided by reported net sales. Non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to similarly titled non-GAAP financial measures provided by other companies. Aptar’s management believes these non-GAAP financial measures provide useful information to our investors because they allow for a better period over period comparison of operating results by removing the impact of items that, in management’s view, do not reflect Aptar’s core operating performance. These non-GAAP financial measures also provide investors with certain information used by Aptar’s management when making financial and operational decisions. Free cash flow is calculated as cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditures plus proceeds from government grants related to capital expenditures. We use free cash flow to measure cash flow generated by operations that is available for dividends, share repurchases, acquisitions and debt repayment. We believe that it is meaningful to investors in evaluating our financial performance and measuring our ability to generate cash internally to fund our initiatives. These non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for GAAP financial results but should be read in conjunction with the unaudited condensed consolidated statements of income and other information presented herein. A reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measures is included in the accompanying tables. Our outlook is provided on a non-GAAP basis because certain reconciling items are dependent on future events that either cannot be controlled, such as exchange rates and changes in the fair value of equity investments, or reliably predicted because they are not part of our routine activities, such as restructuring initiatives, acquisition-related costs and other special items.
