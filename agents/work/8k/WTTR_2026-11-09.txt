Select Water Solutions Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial, Operational and Strategic Updates
Generated third quarter 2026 consolidated revenue of $412 million, an increase of $90 million or 28% year-over-year, as compared to the third quarter of 2025
Produced net income of $23 million during the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $21 million as compared to the third quarter of 2025
Generated Adjusted EBITDA of $93 million during the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $34 million or 57% year-over-year, as compared to the third quarter of 2025
Achieved record Water Infrastructure revenue of $110 million, an increase of $31 million or 40% year-over-year, as compared to the third quarter of 2025
Completed recapitalization and sale of a majority interest in Peak Rentals in the third quarter of 2026
Subsequent to quarter end, closed the acquisition of Pilot Water Solutions, expanding Select’s leading water midstream position across the Delaware Basin
Gainesville, TX – November 9, 2026 – Select Water Solutions, Inc. (NYSE: WTTR) (“Select,” the “Company,” “we” or “us”), a leading provider of sustainable water and chemical solutions, today announced its financial and operating results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
John Schmitz, Chairman of the Board, President and CEO, stated, “During the third quarter of 2026, we delivered consolidated revenue and gross profit growth, resulting in $23 million of net income and $93 million of Adjusted EBITDA. The third quarter was a strong quarter for Select across all three operating segments, with our Water Infrastructure segment producing another quarter of record revenue and gross profit in the period. Importantly, we have also made significant progress on advancing our key strategic objectives through the completed divestment of a majority interest in Peak Rentals and the subsequent acquisition of Pilot Water Solutions.
“During the third quarter of 2026, we increased total produced water volumes handled to 1.7 million barrels per day, an increase of more than 30% relative to the third quarter of 2025. These volume gains drove accretive financial contributions, resulting in Water Infrastructure segment revenues growing by 40% on a year-over-year basis to a record level of $110 million for the third quarter of 2026. We anticipate continued strength in our Water Infrastructure segment, with incremental revenue growth expected in the fourth quarter for the base business before including any partial fourth quarter contributions from the recently closed acquisition of Pilot Water.
“We are increasingly excited about the addition of Pilot Water and the additional opportunities it enables, and we expect strong growth in our Water Infrastructure segment as we look ahead. We continue to win new contracted organic growth projects that underwrite scaled network expansion and we believe our combined infrastructure asset base will provide for incremental, critically needed solutions to help our customers solve the complex water dynamics in the Delaware basin.
“Elsewhere, our Water Services and Chemical Technologies segments also generated strong performance relative to guided expectations for the third quarter. In the Chemical Technologies segment, we generated revenue of $90 million, at the high end of our $85 to $90 million guided range, while the Water Services segment increased revenues 7% sequentially, outpacing macro activity and our guided expectation of a flat sequential quarter. Additionally in the Water Services segment, we completed the recapitalization and divestment of a 55% majority interest in our Peak Rentals business late in the third quarter and we are pleased to retain long-term exposure to Peak Rentals’ future growth through a minority ownership position in the business.
“Taking into consideration the divestment and deconsolidation of Peak Rentals and a partial quarter contribution from Pilot Water, we anticipate Adjusted EBITDA in the fourth quarter growing to an estimated $95 - $100 million. Additionally, as we begin integrating the combined asset base and finish out Pilot Water’s ongoing capital projects during the fourth quarter, we now anticipate net capital expenditures for 2026 of approximately $300 – $310 million. Select is well positioned to fund the continued growth opportunities ahead with growing earnings, a strong balance sheet and one of the leading credit ratings and leverage profiles amongst our water midstream peer group. Subsequent to the end of the third quarter, Select closed on an upsized $900 million inaugural senior notes offering to fund the Pilot Water acquisition and repayment of our existing term loan. Furthermore, in conjunction with the closing of the Pilot Water acquisition, we also enhanced our liquidity position with our entry into a new $500 million revolving credit facility, more than doubling our available liquidity compared to our prior facility.
“In summary, I am proud of the 2026 performance to date for Select and am increasingly excited about the Company’s future. We have significantly enhanced our Water Infrastructure business with the acquisition of Pilot Water and continue to add organic project wins that drive more contracted produced water volumes on our system. Our recent growth initiatives and Water Services streamlining efforts, combined with our strong balance sheet and resilient contracted earnings profile, position Select well to deliver ongoing value creation for our shareholders,” concluded Schmitz.

Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Financial Information
Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $412 million as compared to $396 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $322 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $22.9 million as compared to net income of $22.6 million in the second quarter of 2026 and net income of $2.3 million in the third quarter of 2025.
For the third quarter of 2026, gross profit was $81.4 million, as compared to $76.8 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $43.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. Total gross margin was 19.7% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to 19.4% in the second quarter of 2026 and 13.5% in the third quarter of 2025. Gross profit before D&A was $129.4 million for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $124.1 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $87.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin before D&A for the third quarter of 2026 was 31.4% as compared to 31.3% for the second quarter of 2026 and 27.0% for the third quarter of 2025.
SG&A during the third quarter of 2026 was $53.6 million as compared to $41.2 million during the second quarter of 2026 and $41.7 million during the third quarter of 2025. SG&A during the third and second quarters of 2026 was impacted by transaction costs of $7.4 million and $0.6 million, respectively, while SG&A during the third quarter of 2025 was impacted by non-recurring severance and transaction costs of $2.2 million.
Adjusted EBITDA was $93.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $92.7 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $59.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA during the third quarter of 2026 was adjusted for $7.4 million of non-recurring transaction costs, $4.2 million related to a gain on the deconsolidation of Peak Rentals, $0.4 million of non-cash losses in equity investments, and $4.8 million in other adjustments primarily consisting of non-recurring gains related to a historical insurance claim. Non-cash compensation expense accounted for an additional $8.3 million adjustment during the third quarter of 2026. Please refer to the end of this release for reconciliations of gross profit before D&A (non-GAAP measure) to gross profit and of Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP measure) to net income.
Business Segment Information
The Water Infrastructure segment generated revenues of $110 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $102 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $79 million in the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin before D&A for Water Infrastructure was 57.2% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to 58.3% in the second quarter of 2026 and 53.1% in the third quarter of 2025. Water Infrastructure revenues increased 8.4% sequentially relative to the second quarter of 2026, driven primarily by increases in total produced water volumes handled to 1.7 million barrels per day during the third quarter of 2026 from 1.5 million barrels per day during the second quarter of 2026. Looking ahead, the Company anticipates Water Infrastructure revenues of $150 - $160 million in the fourth quarter of 2026, driven by a partial quarter contribution from Pilot Water, with gross margins before D&A in the 52% – 56% range in the fourth quarter of 2026.
3
The Water Services segment generated revenues of $212 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $198 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $167 million in the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin before D&A for Water Services was 23.8% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to 23.0% in the second quarter of 2026 and 18.0% in the third quarter of 2025. Water Services segment revenues increased 7.1% sequentially, driven primarily by revenue gains in the Permian Basin region. Normalizing for the divestment of Peak Rentals, which contributed approximately 21% of the segment’s revenues year-to-date, the Company anticipates typical fourth quarter seasonality for the segment resulting in mid single-digit percent declines sequentially for the remaining segment. This is expected to result in fourth quarter 2026 revenues of $150 - $160 million, with gross margins before D&A in the 21% – 24% range during the fourth quarter.
The Chemical Technologies segment generated revenues of $90 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $96 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $77 million in the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin before D&A for Chemical Technologies was 17.8% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to 20.2% in the second quarter of 2026 and 19.9% in the third quarter of 2025. For the fourth quarter of 2026, the Company anticipates revenues of $75 – $80 million, driven by the timing of key customer activity levels and fourth quarter seasonality, with gross margins before D&A remaining steady in the 17% – 18% range for the segment.
Cash Flow and Capital Expenditures
Cash flow provided by operating activities for the third quarter of 2026 was $84 million as compared to $87 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $72 million in the third quarter of 2025. Free cash flow in the third quarter of 2026 and the second quarter of 2026 was ($3.6) million and $17.0 million, respectively and ($19.4) million in the third quarter of 2025. Please refer to the end of this release for reconciliations of free cash flow (non-GAAP measure) to cash flow provided by operating activities.
Net capital expenditures for the third quarter of 2026 were $88.1 million, comprised of $89.5 million of capital expenditures partially offset by $1.5 million of cash proceeds from asset sales. Cash flows from investing activities also included $2.3 million of outflows primarily due to two bolt-on asset acquisitions in the Northeast region related to multiple water disposal and solids management facilities.
Cash flows provided by financing activities during the third quarter of 2026 were $12.0 million, primarily reflecting $39.4 million of net proceeds related to the deconsolidation of Peak Rentals partially offset by the payment of $9.5 million of quarterly dividends and distributions and $15.8 million of debt payments primarily related to the term loan component of our sustainability-linked credit facility.

Balance Sheet and Capital Structure
Total cash and cash equivalents were $34.1 million as of September 30, 2026, as compared to $33.4 million and $56.0 million as of June 30, 2026, and March 31, 2026, respectively. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $234.4 million of borrowings outstanding under the term loan component of its sustainability-linked credit facility, $12.8 million of other borrowings and no amounts drawn on the revolving credit facility, compared to $250.0 million of outstanding borrowings under the term loan, $12.9 million of other borrowings, and no amounts drawn on the revolving credit facility as of June 30, 2026 and $250.0 million of outstanding borrowings under the term loan and no other borrowings and no amounts drawn on the revolving credit facility as of March 31, 2026.
As of September 30, 2026, the borrowing base under the Company’s sustainability-linked credit facility was $256.2 million, compared to $264.0 million and $271.3 million as of June 30, 2026, and March 31, 2026, respectively. Available borrowing capacity under the sustainability-linked credit facility was approximately $237.5 million as of September 30, 2026, after giving effect to outstanding borrowings and letters of credit totaling $18.7 million. As of June 30, 2026, available borrowing capacity under the credit facility was approximately $244.4 million, after accounting for outstanding borrowings and letters of credit totaling $19.6 million.
Total liquidity was $271.6 million as of September 30, 2026, as compared to $277.8 million as of June 30, 2026, and $307.7 million as of March 31, 2026. The Company had 127,774,689 weighted average shares of Class A common stock and 8,190,743 weighted average shares of Class B common stock outstanding during the third quarter of 2026. The Company had 131,352,190 Class A shares and 7,107,701 Class B shares issued and outstanding as of September 30, 2026.
Subsequent to quarter end, Select issued $900 million aggregate principal amount of 7.625% Senior Notes due November 1, 2034 (the “Notes”) in a private placement. The majority of the net proceeds were used to fund the cash consideration of the Pilot Water Acquisition and repay the outstanding obligations under Select’s sustainability-linked credit facility. In connection with the closing of the Pilot Water acquisition, Select fully repaid and terminated the sustainability-linked credit facility and entered into a new five-year $500 million senior secured cash flow revolving credit facility. The revolving credit facility remains undrawn as of today, November 9, 2026, with approximately $483.4 million of available borrowing capacity after giving effect to outstanding letters of credit.

Peak Rentals Recapitalization and Divestment
On September 23, 2026, certain subsidiaries of Select sold an indirect 55% interest in Peak Rentals HoldCo, LLC (“Peak”), pursuant to a securities purchase agreement and related transaction documents (“Peak Transaction”). Select received approximately $76.8 million of consideration including approximately $41.5 million in cash proceeds, subject to customary purchase price adjustments, and a $35.3 million seller note. Concurrently with the Peak Transaction, Peak entered into its own standalone financing agreements, such that Peak is not expected to require ongoing capital investment from Select. Following the Peak Transaction, Select retained a 45% indirect interest in Peak, to be reflected on the balance sheet as an equity method investment, and any future results of the Peak business arising from Select’s 45% interest will be reflected in equity in earnings (losses) of unconsolidated entities on a go forward basis.

About Select Water Solutions, Inc.
Select is a leading provider of sustainable water and chemical solutions to the energy industry. These solutions are supported by the Company’s critical water infrastructure assets, chemical manufacturing and water treatment and recycling capabilities. As a leader in sustainable water and chemical solutions, Select places the utmost importance on safe, environmentally responsible management of water throughout the lifecycle of a well. Additionally, Select believes that responsibly managing water resources throughout its operations to help conserve and protect the environment is paramount to the Company’s continued success. Comparison of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, gross profit before depreciation, amortization and accretion (“D&A”), gross margin before D&A and free cash flow are not financial measures presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the U.S. (“GAAP”). We define EBITDA as net income (loss), plus interest expense, income taxes and depreciation, amortization and accretion. We define Adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA, plus any impairment and abandonment charges or asset write-offs pursuant to GAAP, plus non-cash losses on the sale of assets or subsidiaries, non-cash compensation expense, and non-recurring or unusual expenses or charges, including severance expenses, transaction costs, or facilities-related exit and disposal-related expenditures, plus/(minus) foreign currency losses/(gains), plus/(minus) losses/(gains) on unconsolidated entities plus tax receivable agreements expense and less remeasurement gain on business combination and gain on deconsolidation of subsidiary. We define gross profit before D&A as revenue less cost of revenue, excluding cost of sales D&A expense. We define gross margin before D&A as gross profit before D&A divided by revenue. We define free cash flow as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less purchases of property and equipment, plus proceeds received from sale of property and equipment. EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, gross profit before D&A, gross margin before D&A and free cash flow are supplemental non-GAAP financial measures that we believe provide useful information to external users of our financial statements, such as industry analysts, investors, lenders and rating agencies because it allows them to compare our operating performance on a consistent basis across periods by removing the effects of our capital structure (such as varying levels of interest expense), asset base (such as depreciation, amortization and accretion) and non-recurring items outside the control of our management team. We present EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, gross profit before D&A, gross margin before D&A and free cash flow because we believe they provide useful information regarding the factors and trends affecting our business in addition to measures calculated under GAAP. Our board of directors, management and investors use EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA to assess our financial performance because it allows them to compare our operating performance on a consistent basis across periods by removing the effects of our capital structure (such as varying levels of interest expense), asset base (such as depreciation, amortization and accretion) and items outside the control of our management team.
Net income (loss) is the GAAP measure most directly comparable to EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA. Gross profit and gross margin are the GAAP measures most directly comparable to gross profit before D&A and gross margin before D&A, respectively. Net cash provided by (used in) operating activities is the GAAP measure most directly comparable to free cash flow. Our non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered as alternatives to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure. Each of these non-GAAP financial measures has important limitations as an analytical tool due to exclusion of some but not all items that affect the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures. You should not consider EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, gross profit before D&A, gross margin before D&A or free cash flow in isolation or as substitutes for an analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. Because EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, gross profit before D&A, gross margin before D&A and free cash flow may be defined differently by other companies in our industry, our definitions of these non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies, thereby diminishing their utility.
For forward-looking non-GAAP measures, the Company is unable to provide a reconciliation of the forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measure as the information necessary for a quantitative reconciliation, including potential acquisition-related transaction costs as well as the purchase price accounting allocation of the recent acquisitions and the resulting impacts to depreciation, amortization and accretion expense, among other items is not available to the Company without unreasonable efforts due to the inherent difficulty and impracticability of predicting certain amounts required by GAAP with a reasonable degree of accuracy at this time.
