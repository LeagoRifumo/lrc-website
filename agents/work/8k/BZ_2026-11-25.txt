KANZHUN LIMITED Announces Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results
BEIJING, November 25, 2026 – KANZHUN LIMITED (“BOSS Zhipin” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: BZ; HKEX: 2076), a leading online recruitment platform in China, today announced its unaudited financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
· | Total paid enterprise customers1 in the twelve months ended September 30, 2026 were 7.2 million, an increase of 5.9% from 6.8 million in the twelve months ended September 30, 2025.

· | Average monthly active users2 for the third quarter of 2026 were 73.0 million, an increase of 14.4% from 63.8 million for the same quarter of 2025.

· | Revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB2,416.2 million (US$360.4 million), an increase of 11.7% from RMB2,163.3 million for the same quarter of 2025.

· | Income from operations for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB844.8 million (US$126.0 million), an increase of 23.0% from RMB687.1 million for the same quarter of 2025. Adjusted3 income from operations for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB1,076.2 million (US$160.5 million), an increase of 19.1% from RMB903.5 million for the same quarter of 2025.

· | Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB249.2 million (US$37.2 million), compared with net income of RMB775.4 million for the same quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB1,065.1 million (US$158.9 million), an increase of 12.3% from RMB948.5 million for the same quarter of 2025.

Mr. Jonathan Peng Zhao, Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of the Company, remarked, “This quarter, the Company continued its steady growth momentum, with the number of users served continuing to grow. In the third quarter, the average monthly active users on our app reached 73 million, with blue-collar workers, small-to-medium enterprises and users from lower-tier cities continuing to serve as significant growth drivers. As a national strategic partner and the officially designated human resources service provider, the Company participated in and sponsored the 48th WorldSkills Competition, steadily enhancing its brand influence and recognition. The Company has consistently adhered to a user-first philosophy and firmly made long-term, sustained investments in technology and service capabilities. With the in-depth application of AI technology in the Company’s algorithms and products, the Company has made tangible progress in a number of areas, including improving algorithmic matching accuracy and the efficiency of communication between job seekers and recruiters, and has continued to provide users with higher-value services while achieving robust, high-quality growth in a volatile market environment.
The Board of the Company announced a new US$500 million share repurchase program, demonstrating the Board’s confidence in long-term, high-quality growth and its commitment to rewarding the shareholders for their support.”
Ms. Wenbei Wang, Deputy Chief Financial Officer of the Company, added, “We are pleased to report that the Company achieved high-quality growth with increases in both revenue and operating margin in the third quarter, benefiting from our continuously expanding user base and the strong operating leverage brought by our highly efficient business model. At the same time, we remain committed to investing in research and development to consistently enhance the Company’s monetization capabilities by providing more efficient, high-value services to users.
The Company actively utilizes its ample cash reserves and sound operating cash flow to provide continuous returns to shareholders. Since the beginning of 2026, the aggregate returns to shareholders provided by the Company through share repurchases and dividends have exceeded US$530 million, surpassing the adjusted net income for the entire previous year. The new US$500 million share repurchase authorization further reflects the determination of management and the Board to continue over-delivering on their commitments to shareholder returns.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Revenues
Revenues were RMB2,416.2 million (US$360.4 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 11.7% from RMB2,163.3 million for the same quarter of 2025.
· | Revenues from online recruitment services to enterprise customers were RMB2,400.9 million (US$358.1 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 11.8% from RMB2,146.8 million for the same quarter of 2025. This increase was mainly driven by the paid enterprise customer growth.

· | Revenues from other services, consisting primarily of paid value-added services offered to job seekers, were RMB15.3 million (US$2.3 million) for the third quarter of 2026, decreasing slightly from RMB16.4 million for the same quarter of 2025.

Operating cost and expenses
Total operating cost and expenses were RMB1,574.7 million (US$234.9 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 6.7% from RMB1,476.2 million for the same quarter of 2025. Total share-based compensation expenses were RMB231.4 million (US$34.5 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 6.9% from RMB216.4 million for the same quarter of 2025.
· | Cost of revenues was RMB323.6 million (US$48.3 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 5.1% from RMB307.8 million for the same quarter of 2025, primarily due to increases in server and bandwidth cost and employee-related expenses.

· | Sales and marketing expenses were RMB585.0 million (US$87.3 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 48.6% from RMB393.6 million for the same quarter of 2025, primarily driven by an increase in advertising and marketing expenses.

· | Research and development expenses were RMB424.8 million (US$63.4 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 4.1% from RMB408.0 million for the same quarter of 2025, primarily due to an increase in cloud service fees.

· | General and administrative expenses were RMB241.3 million (US$36.0 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing a decrease of 34.2% from RMB366.8 million for the same quarter of 2025, primarily due to an intangible asset impairment recognized in the third quarter of 2025.

Income from operations and adjusted income from operations
Income from operations was RMB844.8 million (US$126.0 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 23.0% from RMB687.1 million for the same quarter of 2025.
Adjusted income from operations was RMB1,076.2 million (US$160.5 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 19.1% from RMB903.5 million for the same quarter of 2025.
2
Interest and investment income/loss, net
Interest and investment loss was RMB1,277.9 million (US$190.6 million) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with interest and investment income of RMB228.0 million for the same quarter of 2025. The loss was primarily attributable to an investment loss of RMB1,443.9 million recognized in the quarter from fair value changes in investments in an investee company that completed its initial public offering in January 2026.
Income tax expenses/benefit
Income tax benefit was RMB161.4 million (US$24.1 million) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with income tax expenses of RMB172.2 million for the same quarter of 2025, a shift primarily due to a deferred income tax benefit of RMB361.0 million recognized in the quarter related to the investment loss described above.
Net income/loss and adjusted net income
Net loss was RMB249.2 million (US$37.2 million) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with net income of RMB775.4 million for the same quarter of 2025. The change was primarily driven by the investment loss described above, despite the year-on-year growth in income from operations.
Adjusted net income was RMB1,065.1 million (US$158.9 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 12.3% from RMB948.5 million for the same quarter of 2025, primarily driven by an increase in adjusted income from operations, partially offset by an increase in income tax expenses.
Net income/loss per American depositary share (“ADS”) and adjusted net income per ADS
Basic and diluted net loss per ADS attributable to ordinary shareholders for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB0.55 (US$0.08) and RMB0.55 (US$0.08), respectively, compared with basic and diluted net income per ADS of RMB1.75 and RMB1.70 for the same quarter of 2025.
Adjusted basic and diluted net income per ADS attributable to ordinary shareholders for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB2.38 (US$0.36) and RMB2.34 (US$0.35), respectively, compared with adjusted basic and diluted net income per ADS of RMB2.13 and RMB2.06 for the same quarter of 2025.
Net cash provided by operating activities
Net cash provided by operating activities was RMB1,262.6 million (US$188.3 million) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 7.6% from RMB1,173.8 million for the same quarter of 2025.
Cash position
As of September 30, 2026, the balance of cash and cash equivalents, short-term time deposits and short-term investments (excluding investments in equity securities) was RMB19,602.1 million (US$2,923.7 million).

Shareholder Return Plan
On March 18, 2026, the Company announced that, subject to the final determination of the Company’s board of directors (the “Board”) and the prevailing market conditions, the Company expects to allocate no less than 50% of the Company’s adjusted net income (a non-GAAP financial measure) of the preceding fiscal year for distribution of dividend under the annual dividend policy and share repurchases for each of the next three years starting from 2026.
Share Repurchase Program
On March 18, 2026, the Board approved amendments to the share repurchase program, increasing the total authorization under the program to repurchase up to US$400 million of the Company’s shares (including ADSs) over the extended term of the program through August 28, 2027.
On November 25, 2026, the Board authorized a new share repurchase program effective from November 25, 2026 for a 12-month period, under which the Company may repurchase up to US$500 million of the Company’s shares (including ADSs).
Outlook
For the fourth quarter of 2026, the Company currently expects its total revenues to be between RMB2.19 billion and RMB2.23 billion, representing a year-on-year increase of 5.3% to 7.3%. This forecast reflects the Company’s current views on the market and operational conditions in China, which are subject to change and cannot be predicted with reasonable accuracy as of the date hereof.
