Protagonist Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update
MIMRYLO™ (rusfertide) Approved by FDA for the Treatment of Erythrocytosis in Adults with Polycythemia Vera; Approval Triggered $275 Million in Payments to Protagonist
Comprehensive Phase 2b Program with PN-881, an Oral IL-17 Antagonist for Psoriasis, Planned to Initiate in Early Q1 2027
$300 Million Share Repurchase Program Announced in September 2026
Cash, Cash Equivalents and Marketable Securities of $834.6 Million as of September 30, 2026
NEWARK, Calif., November 5, 2026 – Protagonist Therapeutics (Nasdaq: PTGX) (“Protagonist” or “the Company”) today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.
“The third quarter was a defining period for Protagonist, with FDA approval of MIMRYLO, our second approval this year for a first-in-class peptide drug, and the continued strong commercial launch of ICOTYDE under our collaboration with Johnson & Johnson,” said Dinesh V. Patel, PhD, President and Chief Executive Officer of Protagonist Therapeutics. “MIMRYLO reflects years of disciplined scientific dedication and clinical execution, and together with ICOTYDE, provides immensely important validation both of our innovative expertise in peptide therapeutics and of the successful pharma-biotech partnership model we have built around these two approved drugs. The partnerships are fueling an accelerating revenue engine, which provides the flexibility to fund the next generation of wholly owned programs emerging from our platform, and to return meaningful capital to shareholders through a share repurchase program.”
Third Quarter 2026 Recent Developments and Upcoming Milestones
MIMRYLO™ (rusfertide): Subcutaneous Injectable Hepcidin Mimetic for Polycythemia Vera
· | On August 28, Protagonist announced that its partner Takeda received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval of MIMRYLO for the treatment of erythrocytosis in adults with polycythemia vera (PV). MIMRYLO is the first and only hepcidin mimetic peptide therapy approved for this indication. On September 30, 2026, MIMRYLO was also approved in the United Arab Emirates. Protagonist discovered MIMRYLO and had primary responsibility for its clinical development through its New Drug Application (“NDA”) filing in December 2025.

· | The FDA approval of MIMRYLO triggered $275 million in payments to Protagonist, consisting of a $200 million opt-out payment and a $75 million approval milestone.

· | Following Protagonist’s April 2026 opt-out election, Takeda holds exclusive worldwide development and commercialization rights to MIMRYLO. Protagonist is eligible to receive tiered royalties ranging from 14% to 29% on worldwide net sales, corresponding to an approximately 21% weighted-average royalty rate at $1.5 billion in annual net sales. Protagonist is eligible for up to an additional $875 million in potential future milestone payments.

· | On October 13, 2026, results from the pivotal Phase 3 VERIFY study were published in the Journal of Clinical Oncology. The article is available here.

ICOTYDE® (Icotrokinra): Oral IL-23 Receptor Antagonist
· | During the third quarter, ICOTYDE received regulatory approvals in China, Japan and the European Union for the treatment of psoriasis, following U.S. FDA approval in March 2026. ICOTYDE is the first and only targeted oral peptide designed to precisely block the IL-23 receptor.

· | On October 14, 2026, results from the Phase 2 ANTHEM-UC study were published in the New England Journal of Medicine. The article is available here.

· | The third quarter of 2026 represents ICOTYDE’s second full quarter of U.S. commercial sales. Under the collaboration with Johnson & Johnson, Protagonist receives tiered royalties ranging from 6% to 10% on global net sales, with an approximate 7.25% weighted-average royalty rate at $4 billion in annual net sales and a 10% tier applying to incremental annual sales above $4 billion. Protagonist is eligible for up to an additional $580 million in potential future milestone payments.

· | In September and October 2026, Johnson & Johnson presented two-year Phase 3 results from the ICONIC-TOTAL and ICONIC-LEAD studies. The results demonstrated sustained responses through Week 112 and a continued favorable safety profile in adults and adolescents with plaque psoriasis, including patients with disease affecting high-impact and difficult-to-treat areas.

· | ICOTYDE is also in development for additional immune-mediated diseases, including psoriatic arthritis, ulcerative colitis and Crohn’s disease.

Wholly-Owned Clinical and Discovery Programs
· | PN-881 (oral IL-17 antagonist peptide for immune-mediated disease): The Phase 1 study of PN-881 has been completed. PN-881 achieved 24-hour trough concentrations several-fold above prospectively targeted IC90 values for IL-17AA, IL-17FF and IL-17AA+FF with once-daily oral dosing and was generally well tolerated, with no serious adverse events. Protagonist plans to initiate a comprehensive Phase 2b dose range-finding study in patients with plaque psoriasis in early Q1 2027. Phase 1 data are expected to be presented at a future medical meeting.

· | PN-477 (triple GLP-1/GIP/GCG agonist peptide for obesity): In the subcutaneously administered PN-477sc Phase 1 SAD study we observed positive dose-related pharmacokinetic, pharmacodynamic and clinical activity. Based on these highly encouraging findings, the Company has made a strategic decision to prioritize the orally administered PN-477o clinical development program. Initiation of a Phase 1 study of oral PN-477o is anticipated in the first half of 2027.

· | PN-458o (dual GLP-1/GIP agonist peptide for obesity): IND-enabling studies are ongoing, with Phase 1 initiation anticipated in the second half of 2027.

· | PN-8047 (oral small molecule hepcidin functional mimetic for PV): IND-enabling studies are ongoing, with Phase 1 initiation anticipated in Q1 2027.

· | Discovery: The Company continues to advance high-priority discovery programs for oral peptides, including an oral IL-4Rα antagonist and amylin-based mono- and poly-agonists.

Share Repurchase Program
· | On September 8, Protagonist announced that its Board of Directors authorized a share repurchase program under which the Company may repurchase up to $300 million of its outstanding common stock. The Company intends to fund repurchases from its cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities, as well as royalties and other payments received under its collaboration arrangements with Takeda related to MIMRYLO and Johnson & Johnson related to ICOTYDE. The timing and amount of any repurchases will depend on market conditions, alternative capital deployment opportunities and other factors.

About Protagonist
Protagonist Therapeutics is a biopharmaceutical company built on a proprietary peptide technology platform that has now produced two FDA-approved, first-in-class medicines, generating royalties and milestones from global commercial partnerships with Johnson and Johnson and Takeda Pharmaceutical Company Limited. ICOTYDE® (icotrokinra), licensed to Johnson & Johnson company Janssen Biotech, Inc., is the first and only targeted oral peptide that precisely blocks the Interleukin-23 (IL-23) receptor. ICOTYDE was launched in the U.S. in March 2026 for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults and pediatric patients 12 years of age or older and is in Phase 3 development for psoriatic arthritis, ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease. ICOTYDE was jointly discovered by Protagonist and Johnson & Johnson scientists, with Protagonist having primary responsibility for the development of ICOTYDE through Phase 1, and Johnson & Johnson assuming responsibility for further clinical and regulatory development and commercialization. Protagonist also discovered and led research and development through Phase 3 for MIMRYLO™ (rusfertide), a first-in-class hepcidin mimetic peptide licensed to Takeda Pharmaceutical Company Limited for worldwide commercialization. MIMRYLO is approved in the U.S. for the treatment of erythrocytosis in adults with PV. The Company also has a number of clinical and preclinical programs based on clinically and commercially validated targets and addressing unmet medical needs, including an oral IL-17 antagonist peptide, anti-obesity dual and triple agonists, an oral hepcidin functional mimetic, and the recently announced IL-4 and amylin discovery programs.
More information on Protagonist, its pipeline drug candidates, and clinical studies can be found on the Company's website at https://www.protagonist-inc.com.
