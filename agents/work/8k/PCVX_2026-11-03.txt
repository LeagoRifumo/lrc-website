Vaxcyte Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update
VAX-31 Adult Indication: Announced Positive Topline Data from OPUS-1 Pivotal Phase 3 Trial; VAX-31 Met All Prespecified Primary Endpoints, Was Well Tolerated and Demonstrated a Safety Profile Similar to PCV20 and PCV21 Across All Ages Studied
VAX-31 Adult Indication: Company Expects to Report Results from OPUS-2 and OPUS-3 Phase 3 Trials in First Half of 2027, Supporting Planned Biologics License Application Submission in First Half of 2028
VAX-31 Infant Indication: Topline Safety, Tolerability and Immunogenicity Data from Primary Immunization Series and Booster Dose of Phase 2 Dose-Finding Study Expected Either Sequentially or Together by End of First Half of 2027
Phase 1 Study of VAX-A1, the Company's Vaccine Candidate Designed to Provide Broad Protection Against Disease Caused by Group A Streptococcus, Advancing to Stage 2 in Adults
Pro Forma Cash, Cash Equivalents and Investments of Approximately $3.4 Billion as of September 30, 2026, Including Approximately $1.1 Billion in Net Proceeds from October 2026 Equity and Convertible Senior Notes Offerings
SAN CARLOS, Calif., November 4, 2026 – Vaxcyte, Inc. (Nasdaq: PCVX), a clinical-stage vaccine innovation company, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.
“The positive topline results from OPUS-1 represent a significant milestone for Vaxcyte and our broad-spectrum pneumococcal conjugate vaccine (PCV) franchise,” said Grant Pickering, Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder of Vaxcyte. “VAX-31 met all prespecified primary endpoints in this pivotal Phase 3 trial, which inaugurated a more stringent noninferiority standard, and we believe the results further support its potential best-in-class profile. With OPUS-1 serving as the foundation of our planned Biologics License Application (BLA), we remain on track to announce results from the OPUS-2 and OPUS-3 Phase 3 trials in the first half of next year and, together with a manufacturing consistency study, we expect to submit a BLA in the first half of 2028. For the VAX-31 infant indication, we expect to announce topline data from the primary immunization series and booster dose of the ongoing Phase 2 study, either sequentially or together, by the end of the first half of 2027.”
“Following our positive OPUS-1 results, we completed concurrent equity and convertible senior notes offerings, raising approximately $1.1 billion in net proceeds, to further strengthen our balance sheet and extend our cash runway,” said Andrew Guggenhime, President and Chief Financial Officer of Vaxcyte. “With approximately $3.4 billion in pro forma cash, cash equivalents and investments as of September 30, 2026, we are well positioned to support the potential U.S.
commercial launch of VAX-31 for adults and advance our other key initiatives, including the VAX-31 OPUS-2 and OPUS-3 adult Phase 3 studies, the VAX-31 infant Phase 2 study, scale-up of our manufacturing and supply chain, and our earlier stage pipeline including VAX-A1 and VAX-XL.”
Key Third Quarter and Recent Highlights
PCV Franchise Adult Indication:
Reported Positive Topline Data from OPUS-1, the Pivotal Adult Phase 3 Trial of VAX-31: In October 2026, the Company announced positive topline results from OPUS-1, which evaluated the safety, tolerability and immunogenicity of VAX-31, its next-generation 31-valent PCV candidate, for the prevention of invasive pneumococcal disease (IPD) and pneumococcal pneumonia. The randomized, double-blind, active-controlled trial evaluated VAX-31 in 4,047 healthy, pneumococcal-naïve1 U.S. adults, including 3,572 aged 50 years and older and 475 aged 18-49. The results will serve as the cornerstone of a planned BLA submission, which will be subject to U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) review.
In the OPUS-1 trial, VAX-31 met all prespecified co-primary immunogenicity endpoints in adults 50 and older:
•All 28 VAX-31 serotypes shared with Prevnar 20® (PCV20) and/or Capvaxive® (PCV21) met the prespecified noninferiority criteria based on opsonophagocytic activity (OPA) geometric mean ratios (GMRs), using a lower bound of the 95% confidence interval (LBCI) >0.667.2
•For the 11 serotypes shared by VAX-31, PCV20 and PCV21, VAX-31 met all 11 primary noninferiority assessments based on OPA GMRs. For each serotype, the prespecified analysis required noninferiority against at least one comparator, with prespecified multiplicity adjustment.2
•For the nine serotypes only in VAX-31 and PCV20, all nine met the prespecified OPA GMR noninferiority criterion.3
•For the eight serotypes only in VAX-31 and PCV21, all eight met the prespecified OPA GMR noninferiority criterion.3
•The three serotypes unique to VAX-31 and cross-reactive serotype 20B met the OPA GMR superiority criterion (LBCI >2.0) with prespecified multiplicity adjustment.2
For the prespecified primary immunobridging endpoint, VAX-31 met the noninferiority criterion (LBCI >0.667) for all 324 serotype OPA GMR comparisons in adults aged 18-49 compared to adults aged 50-64.
In the study, VAX-31 was well tolerated and demonstrated a safety profile similar to PCV20 and PCV21 at all ages studied. No serious adverse events were considered to be related to study vaccines, and there were no study discontinuations due to adverse events.
In the prespecified individual comparator analyses, VAX-31 met the noninferiority criterion (LBCI >0.667)3 for 20 of 20 serotypes shared with PCV20 and 17 of 19 shared with PCV21. Compared to PCV21, serotypes 3 and 12F did not meet the LBCI >0.667 noninferiority criterion, but did meet the historical LBCI >0.5 threshold; compared to PCV20, VAX-31 met the LBCI >0.667 noninferiority criterion.5 Given current U.S. epidemiology, with the serotypes unique to PCV21 accounting for a larger share of adult disease than those unique to PCV20, the Company expects the comparison of VAX-31 to PCV21 to be a particular focus of FDA review.
OPUS-2 and OPUS-3 Phase 3 Results Expected in First Half of 2027, Supporting Planned BLA Submission in First Half of 2028: The OPUS-2 and OPUS-3 Phase 3 trials, which are fully enrolled, are designed to generate a broad and robust safety, tolerability and immunogenicity dataset as part of the OPUS Phase 3 program evaluating VAX-31 for the prevention of IPD and pneumococcal pneumonia. In addition, the Company is planning a manufacturing consistency study as the final Phase 3 study to enable the submission of the BLA.
•The OPUS-2 Phase 3 study, in which 1,390 participants were dosed, is evaluating the safety, tolerability and immunogenicity of VAX-31 when administered either concomitantly with or one month following administration of a licensed seasonal influenza vaccine in healthy, pneumococcal-naïve U.S. adults aged 50 years and older. The results of this descriptive study are intended to inform the design of a potential post-licensure outcomes study that further evaluates VAX-31 in concomitant use with an influenza vaccine and to provide supportive evidence as part of the broader Phase 3 dataset.
•The OPUS-3 Phase 3 descriptive study, in which 752 participants were dosed, is evaluating the safety, tolerability and immunogenicity of VAX-31 in healthy U.S. adults aged 50 years and older who have previously received pneumococcal vaccination. Among other objectives, the study will evaluate responses to serotypes included in the lower-valency pneumococcal vaccines and the ability of VAX-31 to expand the breadth of serotype-specific immune responses.
Early-Stage Pipeline:
VAX-A1 Phase 1 Study in Adults Advancing to Stage 2: VAX-A1 is an investigational prophylactic vaccine candidate designed to provide broad protection against disease caused by Group A Streptococcus. The VAX-A1 Phase 1 study initiated in June 2026 is advancing to Stage 2 following the completion of an independent Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) review of Stage 1 safety and tolerability data. The Company is completing plans to initiate Stage 2 including an updated study design that incorporates the DSMB’s recommendations for sequential enrollment, beginning with the Low and Mid Dose cohorts. Enrollment of the High Dose cohort will be part of a new Stage 3 and will follow completion of the prespecified safety assessments and further DSMB review and approval. The study design has also been updated to add a third vaccination at the Low and Mid Dose levels, while the High Dose regimen remains two vaccinations. These updates are consistent with the stepwise evaluation of safety, tolerability and dosing regimens in first-in-human vaccine development, which is designed to identify the dose that best balances immune response and tolerability.
The randomized, observer-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-escalation study is designed to enroll approximately 80 healthy adults aged 18 to 40 years in Australia. The primary objective is to assess safety and tolerability, with a secondary objective of evaluating immune responses. Participants will be followed for safety through six months after their last vaccination.

About Vaxcyte
Vaxcyte is a vaccine innovation company engineering high-fidelity vaccines to protect humankind from the consequences of bacterial diseases. VAX-31, a 31-valent PCV candidate being evaluated in the OPUS Phase 3 adult clinical program and in a Phase 2 infant clinical program, is being developed for the prevention of IPD and is the broadest-spectrum PCV candidate in the clinic today. VAX-24, a 24-valent PCV candidate, has generated positive Phase 2 clinical results in both adults and infants and is designed to cover more serotypes than any PCV on-market. VAX-31 and VAX-24 are designed to improve upon standard-of-care PCVs by covering the serotypes in circulation that cause a significant portion of IPD and are associated with high case-fatality rates, antibiotic resistance and meningitis, while maintaining coverage of previously circulating strains. VAX-XL, in earlier-stage development, also leverages the Company’s carrier-sparing, site-specific conjugation technology with the aim of further expanding coverage to deliver the broadest-spectrum candidate in the Company’s PCV franchise.
VAX-A1 is a prophylactic vaccine candidate designed to provide broad, strain-independent protection against disease caused by Group A Strep and is currently being evaluated in a Phase 1 clinical study in adults. Group A Strep remains a significant global cause of morbidity and mortality across both adult and pediatric populations and is a leading driver of antibiotic use, underscoring the substantial public health burden.
Vaxcyte is re-engineering the way highly complex vaccines are made through XpressCF®, its cell-free protein synthesis platform exclusively licensed from Sutro Biopharma, Inc. Unlike conventional cell-based approaches, the Company’s system for producing difficult-to-make proteins and antigens is intended to develop and deliver high-fidelity vaccines with enhanced immunological benefits. Vaxcyte’s pipeline also includes VAX-GI, a vaccine candidate designed to prevent Shigella. For more information, visit www.vaxcyte.com.
