Bakken East Pipeline Update
MDU Resources reached an important milestone Sept. 30, 2026, when the company’s board of directors made a positive final investment decision to proceed with WBI Energy’s Bakken East Pipeline. The company has since narrowed the project’s estimated capital investment range to $2.8 billion to $3.1 billion from the previous estimate of $2.7 billion to $3.2 billion.
The approximately 350-mile pipeline will extend from the Bakken region in western North Dakota to a location near Fargo in eastern North Dakota, creating a new pathway for Bakken natural gas to serve customers in central and eastern North Dakota. The company has finalized the pipeline design to include segments of 42-inch, 36-inch and 30-inch-diameter pipe, moving from west to east. The project is subject to regulatory approval and is being designed with an initial capacity of 1.4 billion cubic feet of natural gas per day with flexibility to accommodate anticipated future growth. The company is actively evaluating financing structures to fund the project, including potential participation of financial or strategic investors, while maintaining its expectation that WBI Energy will retain majority ownership and operational control.
For North Dakota, Bakken East represents a multibillion-dollar investment in infrastructure intended to support the state’s continued economic development. By creating additional transportation capacity between western North Dakota’s abundant natural gas supply and growing demand across the state, the project is designed to strengthen energy infrastructure that can support a range of industries, communities and economic activity well into the future. Project development continues to advance, including progress on materials procurement, human capital deployment and discussions with prospective construction contractors.
Construction is expected to occur during the 2028-2030 construction seasons, with the facilities placed in service in two phases. The first phase, from the Bakken to central North Dakota, is expected to be completed in late 2029. The second phase, extending to an existing WBI Energy compressor station near Mapleton, North Dakota, has a target completion date of late 2030. The project will include additional compression at two existing WBI Energy compressor stations and construction of four new compressor stations.
Electric Utility Segment
Implementation of new rates, Badger Wind Farm recovery and increased volumes contributed to increased earnings, compared to the same quarter in 2025
•New rates contributed $7.1 million in revenue for the quarter
•Badger Wind Farm contributed $3.2 million in earnings for the quarter
•Retail sales volumes increased 9.3%
The electric utility segment earned $26.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase from $21.5 million in the third quarter of 2025, or 23.6%. The segment benefited from $7.1 million in additional revenue from new rates approved in North Dakota, Montana and Wyoming, $3.2 million in earnings from Badger Wind Farm and higher retail volumes across all customer classes.
Total retail electric sales increased 9.3%, reflecting growth across all customer classes. Sales to data center customers increased 18.4% compared with the same quarter in 2025.
Growing demand from large-load customers, including data centers, continues to provide opportunities for increased electric sales. MDU Resources’ approach to serving these customers remains grounded in protecting existing customers and ensuring new growth creates value for the electric system and the communities it serves. The company follows the principle of cost causation, meaning the customer that creates a cost should pay that cost. Infrastructure and service costs that are specifically needed to serve large-load customers are assigned appropriately through contracts, tariffs and regulatory approvals. Benefits associated with these agreements may also flow back to customers through approved regulatory mechanisms.

Natural Gas Distribution Segment
New rates and higher retail sales volumes more than offset by higher operation and maintenance expense
•New rates contributed $6.5 million in additional revenue
•Retail sales volumes increased 7.1%
•Higher operating and maintenance expense, including higher payroll-related costs and a
$2.1 million net of tax loss related to the discontinued Knott Landfill renewable natural gas project
•Customer growth of 1.5% year over year
The natural gas distribution segment recorded a seasonal net loss of $20.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with a net loss of $18.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. Results benefited primarily from $6.5 million in new rates approved in Idaho and Washington as well as higher sales volumes. Retail sales volumes increased 7.1% across all customer classes, while the number of customers increased 1.5% compared with the prior year.
Benefits were more than offset by higher operation and maintenance expense, higher interest expense and lower returns on nonqualified benefit plan investments. Operation and maintenance expense included higher payroll-related costs and a $2.1 million net of tax loss related to Cascade Natural Gas Corporation’s decision to discontinue development of the Knott Landfill renewable natural gas project in Bend, Oregon.

The pipeline segment earned $13.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $16.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. Strategic Project Updates:
•Bakken East Pipeline: MDU Resources reached a positive final investment decision on Sept. 30, 2026, to proceed with the project based on secured customer commitments and the anticipated strategic and economic benefits for North Dakota and the broader region. The FERC Section 7(c) application for the project is anticipated to be filed during the fourth quarter of 2026.
•Line Section 32 Expansion Project: The FERC Section 7(c) application was filed in March 2026 and noticed in April. The project continues to target a late-2028 in-service date, subject to regulatory approvals.
•Minot Industrial Project: Development activities continue for a potential project that could include approximately 90 miles of 20-inch pipeline, additional compression and ancillary facilities. An agreement is in place to provide cost recovery protections during the development phase, with the agreement currently extended through late 2026.
Regulatory Update:
•FERC: WBI Energy filed a rate case May 29, 2026. The filing seeks a $31 million annual revenue increase. Approximately 30% of the requested revenue increase is due to proposed new depreciation and amortization rates. FERC accepted and suspended the proposed rates on June 30, 2026, with rates to become effective Dec. 1, 2026, subject to refund and the outcome of hearing procedures if a settlement with our customers and FERC is not reached.
Guidance
MDU Resources is reaffirming guidance and expects earnings per share to be in the range of $0.93 to $1.00.
The expected 2026 results are based on several assumptions, including normal weather, economic and operating conditions for the remainder of the year, continued customer growth, successful execution of approved capital investment programs and constructive regulatory outcomes.
The company's long-term earnings-per-share growth objective remains 6% to 8%.

About MDU Resources Group, Inc.
MDU Resources Group, Inc., a member of the S&P SmallCap 600 index, strives to deliver safe, reliable, cost-effective and environmentally responsible electric utility and natural gas distribution services to more than 1.2 million customers across the Pacific Northwest and Midwest. In addition to its utility operations, the company’s pipeline business operates a more than 3,800-mile natural gas pipeline network and storage system, ensuring reliable energy delivery across the Northern Plains. With a legacy spanning over a century, MDU Resources remains focused on energizing lives for a better tomorrow.
