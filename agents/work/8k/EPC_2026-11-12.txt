Edgewell Personal Care Announces Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results
Fourth Quarter Net Sales Increased 3.0%, or 3.1% Organic; GAAP EPS $0.23, Adjusted EPS $0.78, or +50.0%
Fiscal 2026 Net Sales Increased 1.8%, or 0.3% Organic; GAAP EPS $(0.05), Adjusted EPS $1.93, or +6.6%
Provides Fiscal 2027 Outlook; Expects Growth in Organic Net Sales, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EPS
Shelton, Conn - November 12, 2026 - Edgewell Personal Care Company (NYSE: EPC) today announced results for its fourth fiscal quarter 2026 and fiscal year ended September 30, 2026.
Executive Summary
•Fourth quarter net sales were $484.1 million, an increase of 3.0% compared to the prior year quarter. Full year net sales were $1,996.5 million, an increase of 1.8% compared to the prior year.
•Organic net sales increased 3.1% for the quarter and increased 0.3% for the full year. (Organic basis excludes the impact from currency movements).
•GAAP Diluted Net Earnings (Loss) from Continuing Operations Per Share ("EPS") were $0.23, compared to $0.26 in the prior year quarter and $(0.05) for the full fiscal year compared to $0.93 for the prior year.
•Adjusted EPS were $0.78 for the quarter, compared to $0.52 in the prior year quarter and $1.93 for the full fiscal year compared to $1.81 for the prior year.
•Ended the fourth quarter with $375.6 million in cash on hand, plus access to an additional $418.8 million under the Company’s U.S. revolving credit facility.
•Returned $6.9 million to shareholders in the form of dividends in the fourth quarter.
•The Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.15 per common share on November 12, 2026, for the fourth quarter.
“Fiscal 2026 was a year of meaningful progress and change for Edgewell, culminating in a strong fourth quarter that reflected continued growth in North America, a return to growth in International and stronger performance across our focus brands,” said Rod Little, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Our second-half performance enabled us to deliver the top- and bottom-line outlook we established at the beginning of the year on an adjusted basis, providing further evidence that our investments in innovation, brand building and commercial execution are gaining traction. We enter fiscal 2027 as a more focused business, with confidence in our plans to build on this progress, increase support behind our brands and improve profitability. While significant cost and competitive headwinds persist, we believe the broader transformation of our operating model is creating greater capacity to invest and supporting more consistent growth and margin expansion.”

Fiscal 4Q 2026 Operating Results (Unaudited)
Net sales were $484.1 million in the quarter, an increase of $14.2 million, or 3.0%, including a $0.5 million, or 0.1% unfavorable impact from currency movements. Organic net sales increased $14.7 million, or 3.1%, reflecting broad-based growth across the business, including growth in both North America and International. North America organic sales increased 3.3%, driven by growth in Grooming and Wet Shave, reflecting improved execution, increased distribution and continued strength across several of the Company's focus brands. International organic sales increased 3.0%, reflecting growth across all segments.
Gross profit was $209.6 million, as compared to $188.4 million in the prior year quarter. Gross margin as a percent of net sales was 43.3%, an increase of 320-basis points. Adjusted gross margin increased 510-basis points, to 45.7%, driven by productivity savings, favorable volume absorption, favorable currency, and the lapping of transitory costs in the prior year period, partially offset by higher inflation, net of tariffs, and unfavorable mix.
Advertising and sales promotion expense ("A&P") was $56.1 million, or 11.6% of net sales, an increase of $10.2 million, compared to $45.9 million, or 9.8% of net sales in the prior year quarter. The increase reflects continued investment behind the Company's focus brands and growth priorities, including expanded media support and brand-building initiatives.
Selling, general and administrative expense ("SG&A") was $107.2 million, or 22.1% of net sales, as compared to $105.5 million, or 22.5% of net sales in the prior year quarter. Adjusted SG&A was 20.6% of net sales, compared to 21.9% in the prior year quarter. The improvement primarily reflects lower corporate and people-related expenses.
The Company recorded pre-tax restructuring and related costs in support of cost efficiency and effectiveness programs of $29.9 million in the quarter.
Operating income was $16.9 million, or 3.5% of net sales, inclusive of $4.0 million favorable impact from currency movements, compared to income of $6.9 million, or 1.5% of net sales in the prior year quarter. Adjusted operating income was $51.1 million, or 10.6% of net sales, compared to $27.7 million, or 5.9% of net sales in the prior year quarter.
Interest expense associated with debt was $15.0 million, compared to $14.8 million in the prior year quarter. The increase in interest expense was the result of lower capitalized interest for projects with capital expenditures, partially offset by lower borrowing levels on the Company’s U.S. revolving credit facility due to the paydown of the facility with the proceeds of the Feminine Care divestiture.
Other (income) expense, net was income of $8.3 million compared to expense of $2.1 million in the prior year quarter. The current year quarter included $7.2 million of Transition Services Agreement (“TSA”) income. Additionally, the prior year quarter included $0.8 million of other project gains. Currency hedge and remeasurement gains were $0.3 million in the current quarter, compared to a loss of $2.0 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted other (income) expense, net was income of $8.7 million compared to expense of $2.9 million in the prior year quarter.
The effective tax rate for fiscal 2026 was (175.3)% compared to (19.1)% in the prior year period. The current year period reflects a tax expense on a loss while fiscal 2025 reflects a tax benefit on income. The fiscal 2026 effective tax rate reflects less favorable discrete and unusual items compared to fiscal 2025. The adjusted effective tax rate for fiscal 2026 was 23.0%, compared to 11.0% in the prior year period.
GAAP net earnings from continuing operations were $10.7 million of income, or $0.23 per diluted share, compared to income of $12.1 million, or $0.26 per diluted share, in the prior year quarter. Adjusted net earnings from continuing operations were $36.8 million, or $0.78 per share, inclusive of a $0.11 favorable currency impact, compared to $24.3 million, or $0.52 per share, in the prior year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA was $76.8 million, inclusive of a $6.3 million favorable currency impact, compared to $43.0 million in the prior year quarter.
Net cash provided by operating activities on a consolidated basis, inclusive of continuing and discontinued operations was $49.6 million for the year ended September 30, 2026, compared to $118.4 million in the prior year period. The decrease in cash provided by operating activities was largely driven by one-time operating cash flow impacts associated directly with the Feminine Care divestiture including tax, working capital, and deal related fees and expenses. The fourth quarter ended with $375.6 million in cash on hand and access to $418.8 million under the Company’s U.S. revolving credit facility. Adjusted net debt leverage ratio at the end of the fourth quarter was 3.4x.

Fiscal 4Q 2026 Operating Segment Results (Unaudited)
Wet Shave (Men's Systems, Women's Systems, Disposables, and Shave Preps)
Net sales increased $3.9 million, or 1.2%. Organic net sales increased $5.2 million or 1.6%, as growth in the branded business more than offset lower Private Label sales related to temporary supply constraints in North America and certain international markets. Segment profit increased $5.7 million, or 10.8%. Organic segment profit, excluding the favorable impact from currency, increased $2.5 million, or 4.8%, as the benefits of higher gross profit were partially offset by higher marketing and SG&A expenses.
Sun and Skin Care (Sun Care, Men’s and Women’s Grooming Products, and Wet Ones)
Net sales increased $10.3 million, or 7.0%. Organic net sales increased $9.5 million, or 6.5%, driven by strong global Grooming performance, partially offset by declines in North America Sun Care and Skin Care. Segment profit increased $14.7 million, or 294.0%, including a favorable impact from foreign currency of $0.8 million, or 16.0%. Organic segment profit increased $13.9 million, or 278.0%, as the benefits of higher gross profit and lower SG&A expense were partially offset by increased investment behind the Company's brands.
Fiscal 2026 Operating Results (Unaudited)
Net sales were $1,996.5 million, an increase of $34.5 million, or 1.8%. Organic net sales increased 0.3%. North America organic net sales increased 0.4%, primarily attributable to higher volumes in Grooming and Sun Care, partially offset by declines in Wet Shave and Skin Care. International organic net sales increased 0.2%, driven by pricing, partially offset by lower volumes.
Gross Profit was $830.0 million, compared to $847.8 million in the prior year. Gross margin as a percent of net sales was 41.6%, a decrease of 160-basis points compared to the prior year period. Adjusted gross margin decreased 10-basis points, to 43.4%, as 230 basis points of productivity savings, favorable currency and slightly favorable net pricing, were more than offset by 250 basis points of core inflation, inclusive of tariffs, and 60 basis points of unfavorable mix and other.
A&P was $243.5 million, or 12.2% of net sales, an increase of $15.6 million from the prior year, compared to 11.6% in the prior year, reflecting increased investment behind the Company's focus brands and growth priorities in North America and International markets.
Selling, general and administrative expense ("SG&A") was $428.9 million, or 21.5%, of net sales, as compared to $408.6 million, or 20.8%, of net sales in the prior year. Adjusted SG&A as a percent of net sales was 20.5% as compared to 20.4% in the prior year, due to higher corporate expenses and consulting costs.
The Company recorded pre-tax restructuring and related costs of $101.8 million, consisting largely of severance, project implementation and other exit costs in support of transformation and cost efficiency programs. These costs included $80.0 million related to the consolidation of its Mexico facilities and Wet Shave operations and $21.8 million related to global transformation actions to strengthen its operating model and support the Company’s new growth strategy and commercial ambitions.
Operating income was $41.4 million, or 2.1% of net sales, inclusive of $11.7 million favorable impact from currency movements, compared to $110.2 million, or 5.6% of net sales, in the prior year. Adjusted operating income was $161.6 million, or 8.1% of net sales, compared to $173.3 million, or 8.8%, of net sales in the prior year. The decrease in adjusted operating income margin was attributable to higher brand investment, lower gross margin, and higher SG&A.
Other (income) expense, net was income of $26.7 million in fiscal 2026 compared to income of $0.2 million in the prior year period. The current year included $21.5 million of Transition Services Agreement (“TSA”) income. The impact was partially offset by currency hedge and remeasurement losses of $0.8 million in fiscal 2026 compared to losses of $0.6 million in fiscal 2025. Adjusted other (income) expense, net was income of $24.9 million compared to expense of $3.0 million in the prior year period. The current year period included $0.7 million of other project gains, compared to $2.3 million of other project gain in fiscal 2025.
GAAP net (loss) earnings from continuing operations were $(2.2) million or $(0.05) per diluted share compared to $44.3 million or $0.93 per diluted share in the prior year period. Adjusted net earnings were $90.5 million or $1.93 per share, inclusive of a $0.20 favorable impact from currency movements, compared to $86.3 million or $1.81 per share in the prior year period. Adjusted EBITDA was $254.5 million, inclusive of an $11.5 million favorable currency impact, compared to $239.7 million in the prior year.

About Edgewell
Edgewell is a leading pure-play consumer products company with an attractive, diversified portfolio of established brand names such as Schick®, Wilkinson Sword® and Billie® men's and women's shaving systems and disposable razors; Edge and Skintimate® shave preparations; Banana Boat®, Hawaiian Tropic®, Bulldog®, Jack Black®, and CREMO® sun and skin care products; and Wet Ones® products. The Company has a broad global footprint and operates in more than 50 markets, including the U.S., Canada, Mexico, Germany, Japan, the U.K. and Australia, with approximately 6,500 employees worldwide.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures. While the Company reports financial results in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP") in the U.S., this discussion also includes non-GAAP measures. These non-GAAP measures are referred to as "adjusted" or "organic" and exclude items which are considered by the Company as unusual, non-recurring, or outside of ordinary operations and which may have a disproportionate positive or negative impact on the Company’s financial results in any particular period. Reconciliations of non-GAAP measures, including reconciliations of measures related to the Company's fiscal 2027 financial outlook, are included within the Notes to Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements included with this release.
This non-GAAP information is provided as a supplement to, not as a substitute for, or as superior to, measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with GAAP. The Company uses this non-GAAP information internally to make operating decisions and believes it is helpful to investors because it allows more meaningful period-to-period comparisons of ongoing operating results. The information can also be used to perform analysis and to better identify operating trends that may otherwise be masked or distorted by the types of items that are excluded. This non-GAAP information is a component in determining management's incentive compensation. Finally, the Company believes this information provides a higher degree of transparency. The following provides additional detail on the Company's non-GAAP measures:
•The Company utilizes “adjusted” non-GAAP measures including gross margin, SG&A, operating income, operating margin, effective tax rate, net earnings, earnings per share, EBITDA, and other (income) expense to internally make operating decisions.
◦Constant currency measures are calculated by removing the impact of translational and transactional foreign currencies changes, net of foreign currency hedges compared to the prior year. Transactional foreign currency changes are driven by foreign legal entities’ transactions not denominated in local currency.
•The Company analyzes its net sales and segment profit on an organic basis to better measure the comparability of results between periods. Organic net sales and organic segment profit exclude the impact of changes in foreign currency.
◦Segment profit is impacted by fluctuations in translation and transactional foreign currency. The impact of currency was applied to segments using management’s best estimate.
•The Company presents certain metrics on a consolidated and continuing operations basis to help with comparability.
•Free cash flow is defined as net cash from operating activities, less capital expenditures plus collections of deferred purchase price of accounts receivable sold and proceeds from sales of fixed assets. Adjusted free cash flow is defined as free cash flow, adjusted for the following: the one-time operating cash flow impacts associated directly with Feminine Care divestiture including tax, working capital, and deal related fees and expenses.
•Net debt is defined as Gross debt less cash and cash equivalents. Net debt leverage ratio is defined as net debt divided by trailing twelve month adjusted EBITDA. Adjusted net debt leverage ratio is defined as net debt divided by continuing operations trailing twelve month adjusted EBITDA, which includes Transition Services Agreement income realized in fiscal 2026 Q2, Q3 and Q4 (eight months), plus $9 million of pro forma Transition Services Agreement income (four months).
