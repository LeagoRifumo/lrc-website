Revenues
Three Months Ended
The decrease in Revenues was due to decreases in strategic advisory, restructuring, and private capital solutions revenues, partially offset by an increase in primary fundraising revenues.
Nine Months Ended
The increase in Revenues was due to increases in strategic advisory, restructuring, and private capital solutions revenues.
Compensation and Benefits Expense
Three Months Ended
GAAP Compensation and Benefits Expense was $258 million for the current quarter compared with $303 million in the prior year. Adjusted Compensation and Benefits Expense was $256 million compared with
2
$302 million in the prior year. The decrease in Compensation and Benefits Expense was driven by the combination of lower revenues and a lower accrual rate compared with the prior year.
Nine Months Ended
GAAP Compensation and Benefits Expense was $864 million compared with $801 million in the prior year. Adjusted Compensation and Benefits Expense was $858 million compared with $795 million in the prior year. The increase in Compensation and Benefits Expense was driven by higher revenues compared with the prior year, partially offset by a lower accrual rate.
Non-Compensation Expense
Three Months Ended
GAAP Non-Compensation Expense was $58 million for the current quarter compared with $53 million in the prior year. Adjusted Non-Compensation Expense was $57 million for the current quarter compared with $51 million in the prior year.
The increases in Non-Compensation Expense were driven by: (i) Continued investments in technology infrastructure and higher market data expense, which resulted in increased Communications and Information Services expense; (ii) Expansion of our global office footprint and the associated build-out, which resulted in increased Occupancy and Related, and Depreciation and Amortization expense, respectively; and (iii) Elevated business-related activity and higher travel costs, which resulted in increased Travel and Related expense.
Nine Months Ended
GAAP Non-Compensation Expense was $174 million compared with $157 million in the prior year. Adjusted Non-Compensation Expense was $170 million compared with $153 million in the prior year.
The increases in Non-Compensation Expense were driven by: (i) Expansion of our global office footprint and the associated build-out, which resulted in increased Occupancy and Related, and Depreciation and Amortization expense, respectively; (ii) Elevated business-related activity and higher travel costs, which resulted in increased Travel and Related expense; and (iii) Continued investments in technology infrastructure and higher market data expense, which resulted in increased Communications and Information Services expense.
Provision for Taxes
As of September 30, 2026, the Company owned 64.6% of PJT Partners Holdings LP. The Company is subject to U.S. federal and state corporate income tax while PJT Partners Holdings LP and its operating subsidiaries are subject to certain state, local and foreign income taxes. Refer to Note 11. “Stockholders’ Equity” in the “Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements” in “Part II. Item 8. Financial Statements and Supplementary Data” of the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025 for further information about the corporate ownership structure. The effective tax rate for GAAP Net Income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 was 18.6% and 17.6%, respectively. The effective tax rate for GAAP Net Income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 was 9.8% and 4.3%, respectively.
The effective tax rate for Adjusted Net Income, If-Converted for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was 19.5% compared with 14.1% for the full year 2025.

Balance Sheet and Capital Management
As of September 30, 2026, the Company held Cash, Cash equivalents and Short-term investments of $766 million and had no funded debt.
During the third quarter 2026, the Company repurchased 0.2 million shares and share equivalents at an average price of $156.74 per share. During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company repurchased 2.3 million shares and share equivalents at an average price of $154.07 per share.
As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s remaining repurchase authorization was $739 million.
The Company intends to exchange 75 thousand Partnership Units for cash at an amount to be determined by the volume-weighted average price per share of the Company’s Class A common stock on November 5, 2026, subject to approval by the Board of Directors.
Dividend
The Board of Directors of the Company has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.25 per share of Class A common stock. The dividend will be paid on December 16, 2026 to Class A common stockholders of record as of December 2, 2026.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The following represent additional performance measures that management uses in making resource allocation and/or compensation decisions. These measures should not be considered substitutes for, or superior to, financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP.
Management believes the following non-GAAP measures, when presented together with comparable GAAP measures, are useful to investors in understanding the Company’s operating results: Adjusted Pretax Income; Adjusted Net Income, If-Converted, in total and on a per-share basis (referred to as “Adjusted EPS”); Adjusted Compensation and Benefits Expense; and Adjusted Non-Compensation Expense. These non-GAAP measures, presented and discussed in this earnings release, remove the impact of: (a) acquisition-related compensation expense; (b) acquisition-related intangible asset amortization; and (c) the net change to the amount the Company has agreed to pay Blackstone Inc. (our “former Parent”) related to the net realized cash benefit from certain compensation-related tax deductions. Reconciliations of the non-GAAP measures to their most directly comparable GAAP measures and further detail regarding the adjustments are provided in the Appendix.
