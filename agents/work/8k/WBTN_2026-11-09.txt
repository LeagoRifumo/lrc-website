WEBTOON Entertainment Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Delivered Reported Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA Within Guidance Ranges
Third Quarter Revenue Decline of 4.0%; Revenue Growth on a Constant Currency Basis of 0.2%
Net Loss of $7.5 million; Adjusted EBITDA of $2.2 million
Strong Balance Sheet With Cash and Cash Equivalents of Approximately $548.4 million and No Debt
LOS ANGELES, November 9, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- WEBTOON Entertainment Inc. (Nasdaq: WBTN) (“WEBTOON Entertainment” or “the Company”), a leading global entertainment company and home to some of the world’s largest storytelling platforms, today announced results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights (vs. Third Quarter 2025)
•Total revenue of $362.8 million decreased 4.0%, driven by declines in Paid Content and IP Adaptations, partially offset by growth in Advertising.
•Revenue on a constant currency basis was $378.6 million, increasing 0.2%, driven by growth in Paid Content and Advertising, partially offset by a decline in IP Adaptations.
•Net Loss was $7.5 million, compared to $11.1 million in the prior year. The improvement was driven primarily by higher gross profit and an income tax benefit, partially offset by increased marketing investment and higher foreign currency losses.
•Adjusted EBITDA was $2.2 million, compared to $5.1 million in the prior year, primarily due to higher foreign currency losses. Adjusted EBITDA Margin was 0.6%, compared to 1.4% in the prior year.
•Diluted loss per share was $0.06, compared to diluted loss per share of $0.09 in the prior year.
•Adjusted Earnings Per Share was $0.02, compared to $0.04 in the prior year.
•Cash and cash equivalents of approximately $548.4 million plus another $13.3 million of short-term deposits included in prepaid expenses and other current assets, net.
•Net cash provided by operating activities was $6.2 million for the quarter, compared to $16.5 million in the prior year quarter.
Junkoo Kim, Founder and CEO, said, “We delivered third quarter reported revenue and Adjusted EBITDA within our guidance ranges while continuing to invest with discipline in our next phase of growth, and we are confident in our return to double-digit revenue growth in the fourth quarter.”
Kim continued, “Our strategy is working across the business. On-platform, we are strengthening how readers discover and engage with stories; Off-platform, we are turning our IP into global franchises across diverse formats; and through collaborations, we are extending our reach to new audiences. Each reinforces the other, and together they position us for durable, long-term growth. This is the future of storytelling, and WEBTOON is building it.”
Fourth Quarter 2026 Outlook
For the fourth quarter 2026, the Company expects:
•Revenue growth on a constant currency basis in the range of 9.0%-12.0%. This represents revenue in the range of $371-$381 million, based on current FX rates.
•Adjusted EBITDA in the range of $0.0-$5.0 million, representing an Adjusted EBITDA Margin in the range of 0.0%-1.3%.
Our fourth quarter 2026 outlook includes expected revenue and contributions from RI Games Holdings Inc. following the anticipated second closing of the transaction.

About WEBTOON Entertainment Inc.
WEBTOON Entertainment is a leading global entertainment company and home to some of the world's largest storytelling platforms. As the global leader and pioneer of the mobile webcomic format, WEBTOON Entertainment has transformed comics and visual storytelling for fans and creators.
With its CANVAS UGC platform empowering anyone to become a creator, and a growing roster of superstar WEBTOON Originals creators and series, WEBTOON Entertainment’s passionate fandoms are the new face of pop culture. WEBTOON Entertainment adaptations are available on Netflix, Prime Video, Crunchyroll, and other screens around the world, and the company’s content partners have included Warner Bros. Animation, Discord, HYBE, and Duolingo, among many others.
With approximately 159 million monthly active users, WEBTOON Entertainment’s IP & Creator Ecosystem of aligned brands and platforms include WEBTOON, Wattpad--the world’s leading webnovel platform--WEBTOON Productions, Studio N, Studio LICO, WEBTOON Unscrolled, LINE MANGA, and eBookJapan, among others.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures & Definitions
This release contains certain financial information that is not presented in conformity with U.S. GAAP. These non-GAAP measures include Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Earnings Per Share (Adjusted EPS), revenue on a constant currency basis and revenue growth on a constant currency basis.
We believe that these non-GAAP measures provide users of the Company’s financial information with additional meaningful information to assist in understanding financial results and assessing the Company’s performance from period to period. Management believes these measures are important indicators of operations because they exclude items that may not be indicative of our core operating results and provide a better baseline for analyzing trends in our underlying businesses, and they are consistent with how business performance is planned, reported and assessed internally by management and the board of directors of the Company. Our non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered in isolation, or as substitutes for, financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP. Non-GAAP measures have limitations as they do not reflect all the amounts associated with our results of operations as determined in accordance with GAAP, and should only be used to evaluate our results of operations in conjunction with the corresponding or the most directly comparable GAAP measures. We strongly encourage investors and shareholders to review our financial statements and publicly filed reports in their entirety and not to rely on any single financial measure.
A reconciliation is provided at the end of this release for each historical non-GAAP financial measure to the most directly comparable financial measure stated in accordance with U.S. GAAP. We encourage investors and shareholders to review the related U.S. GAAP financial measures and the reconciliation of these non-GAAP financial measures to their most directly comparable U.S. GAAP financial measures, and not to rely on any single financial measure to evaluate our business. We do not provide a reconciliation of forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP financial measures on a forward-looking basis because we are unable to predict with reasonable certainty or without unreasonable effort non-recurring items that may arise in the future.
Adjusted EBITDA: We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss), adjusted to remove the impact of interest income, interest expense, income tax expense (benefit) and depreciation and amortization, with further adjustments to eliminate the effects of (gain) loss on equity method investments, effect of applying the valuation method of fair value through profit or loss, impairment of goodwill, non-cash stock-based compensation expense and certain other non-recurring costs.
Adjusted EBITDA Margin: We define Adjusted EBITDA Margin as Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.
Adjusted Earnings Per Share (Adjusted EPS): We define Adjusted Earnings Per Share as Earnings Per Share before interest expense, interest income, income tax expense (benefit) and depreciation and amortization with further adjustments to eliminate the effects of (gain) loss on equity method investments, effect of applying the valuation method of fair value through profit or loss, impairment of goodwill, non-cash stock-based compensation and certain other non-recurring costs. We calculate Adjusted Earnings Per Share by making the adjustments described herein from Net Income (Loss) and dividing by basic and diluted weighted average shares of common stock outstanding, respectively, for the applicable period.
Revenue on a Constant Currency Basis: We define revenue on a constant currency basis as revenue adjusted to remove the impact of foreign currency rate fluctuations and the impact of deconsolidated and transferred operations. We calculate revenue on a constant currency basis in a given period by applying the average currency exchange rates in the comparable period of the prior year to the local currency revenue in the current period. We calculate revenue on a constant currency basis in each of our revenue streams – Paid Content, Advertising and IP Adaptations – and each region – Korea, Japan and Rest of World – using the same method as laid out herein.
Revenue Growth on a Constant Currency Basis: We define revenue growth on a constant currency basis as period-over-period growth rates of revenue, adjusted to remove the impact of foreign currency rate fluctuations and the impact of deconsolidated and transferred operations. We calculate revenue growth (as a percentage) on a constant currency basis by determining the increase in current period revenue over prior period revenue, where current period foreign currency revenue is translated using prior period average currency exchange rates.

4 Auto-Translation: One Story, Multiple Languages Since its May beta, we’ve steadily expanded access to Auto-Translation for eligible English-language CANVAS creators, with the phased rollout progressing as planned. With this expansion, more English-language series are now available across our other languages, including Spanish, French, Indonesian, Thai, German, and Traditional Chinese. The early results are encouraging. Comparing average weekly results for the eight weeks just before the beta launch with the eight weeks that ended in August, we saw CANVAS episodes read by our non-English readers grow almost 40% and CANVAS episodes read per reader grow close to 30%. These early stats suggest that readers aren’t trading their usual reading for translated CANVAS series — they’re adding them on top. Auto-Translation goes beyond just simply translating text, using our proprietary technology to understand a series’ context and characters in order to keep translations consistent across languages and authentic to the original story. Over time, we aim to extend Auto-Translation to more languages, giving creators additional avenues to access new audiences while further accelerating our global flywheel. Short Animation: A New Way to Experience Our Stories We see animation as one of the most genuine ways for users to fall in love with a story by bringing a character they've only read about to life. Built by our in-house creative team and supported by AI-assisted tools, our Short Animation feature complements the animated series we develop with top-tier partners, another way for our fans to enjoy stories and for our creators to reach their fans. While it is early days, we plan to roll out Short Animation thoughtfully, and we are excited about the new pathways our technology will allow us to tap into in partnership with our creators, growing the value of our proven IP. Digital Characters: From Reading to Engaging Fans want opportunities beyond just reading stories to interact more deeply with the content they love. byUs, our AI interactive story-chat service, lets fans talk with their favorite characters and even become storytellers themselves. With just a sketch of the core plot, AI can create a complete story in about five minutes. These stories are shared with the user community on our platform, creating entirely new IP inspired by the original and imagined by fans. Early results are strong. Since launching byUs in Korea in June, we have steadily expanded our library of IP and characters, including Operation: True Love, Special Civil Servant, and Garbage Time. In its first 10 weeks, byUs passed 14 million cumulative conversations, including more than 3.2 million around Ctrl+Alt+Resign, the service's first title. By week 17, total cumulative conversations roughly doubled to 28.4 million as weekly conversations grew about 1.5x, with fan-created stories driving about 64% of all conversations. More importantly, that engagement is driving fans back to the source. Building on the results we saw with Ctrl+Alt+Resign last quarter, byUs is now reigniting fandoms around completed series. Garbage Time, which concluded in January 2024, drew 820,000 conversations within 12 days of launching on byUs, with 95% of users coming from the original’s readership, a powerful demonstration of how Digital Characters can deepen engagement and re-engage existing audiences. We are now scaling byUs beyond Korea. In October, we launched byUs in Japanese on LINE Manga, building on our proven Korea playbook while tailoring the experience to Japanese fans, with more markets to follow. Reading has always been the beginning of a fan’s relationship with a story, and we are building what comes next.
