Natera Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
AUSTIN, Texas, November 5, 2026 /BUSINESS WIRE/ — Natera, Inc. (NASDAQ: NTRA), a global leader in cell-free DNA and precision medicine, today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Recent Financial Highlights
•Generated total revenues of $794.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $592.2 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 34.1%.
•Generated a gross margin1 of 64.7% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to a gross margin1 of 64.9% in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding revenue true-ups, third quarter non-GAAP gross margin2 increased by 137 basis points over the second quarter of 2026.
•Processed approximately 1,093,100 tests in the third quarter of 2026, compared to approximately 893,600 tests in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 22.3%.
•Processed approximately 339,500 oncology tests in the third quarter of 2026, compared to approximately 211,000 in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 60.9%. Clinical molecular residual disease (MRD) oncology units grew 32,700 units over the second quarter of 2026.
•Increased cash by approximately $5.7 million3 as of September 30, 2026 compared to June 30, 2026.
•Raised 2026 annual revenue guidance by $140 million at the midpoint, from $2.850 billion - $2.910 billion to $3.005 billion - $3.035 billion.
Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Results
Total revenues were $794.0 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $592.2 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 34.1%. The increase in revenues was driven by an increase in volume and average selling price improvements.
Natera processed approximately 1,093,100 tests in the third quarter of 2026, including approximately 1,080,300 tests accessioned in its laboratory, compared to approximately 893,600 tests processed, including approximately 879,700 tests accessioned in its laboratory, in the third quarter of 2025.
In the third quarter of 2026, Natera recognized revenue on approximately 1,024,800 tests for which results were reported to customers in the period (tests reported), including approximately 1,012,100 tests reported from its laboratory, compared to approximately 832,900 tests reported, including approximately 819,900 tests reported from its laboratory, in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 23.0% from the prior period.
Gross profit1 for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 was $513.5 million and $384.4 million, respectively, representing a gross margin1 of 64.7% and 64.9%, respectively. This was primarily as a result of higher revenues and continued progress in reducing cost of revenues, offset by a smaller benefit from changes in estimates. Changes in estimates for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 increased revenue and, as a result, increased gross margin1 by 1.5% and 3.6%, respectively, and increased gross profit1 by $32.3 million and $55.1 million, respectively. The decrease in the change in estimate reflects the continued accumulation of historical collection experience, which has resulted in our estimates of variable consideration at the time of revenue recognition more closely approximating subsequent cash collections.
Gross margin1 for the three months ended June 30, 2026 was 64.5%. Excluding the change in estimates in revenue accruals of approximately $32.3 million and $52.3 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, respectively, Natera's non-GAAP gross margin2 increased by approximately 1.4% sequentially.
Total operating expenses, representing research and development expenses and selling, general and administrative expenses, for the third quarter of 2026 was $576.1 million, compared to $482.0 million in the same period of the prior year, an increase of 19.5%. The increase was primarily driven by headcount growth to support new product offerings as well as increases in clinical trial expenses. Amortization of acquired intangible assets for the third quarter of 2026 was $5.7 million. No such amortization occurred in the third quarter of 2025.
Loss from operations for the third quarter of 2026 was $68.3 million compared to $97.6 million for the same period of the prior year.
Natera’s net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $59.9 million, or ($0.42) per diluted share, compared to a net loss of $87.5 million, or ($0.64) per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Weighted average shares outstanding were 144.1 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 137.2 million for the same period in the prior year.
At September 30, 2026, Natera held approximately $1,097.2 million in cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash, compared to $1,076.1 million as of December 31, 2025. As of September 30, 2026, Natera had a total outstanding debt balance of $80.3 million, including accrued interest under its line of credit with UBS at a variable interest rate of 30-day SOFR plus 50 bps.
Financial Outlook
Natera anticipates 2026 total revenue of $3.005 billion to $3.035 billion; gross margin1 to be approximately 64% to 66%; selling, general and administrative costs to be approximately $1.225 billion to $1.300 billion; research and development costs to be $825 million to $925 million; and cash flow to be positive.

About Natera
Natera™ is a global leader in cell-free DNA and precision medicine, dedicated to oncology, women’s health, and organ health. We aim to make personalized genetic testing and diagnostics part of the standard-of-care to protect health and inform earlier, more targeted interventions that help lead to longer, healthier lives. Natera’s tests are supported by more than 400 peer-reviewed publications that demonstrate excellent performance. Natera operates ISO 13485-certified and CAP-accredited laboratories certified under the Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments (CLIA) in Austin, Texas, San Carlos, California, and Boulder, Colorado. For more information, visit www.natera.com.
