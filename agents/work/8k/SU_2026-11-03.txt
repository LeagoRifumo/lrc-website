Suncor Energy reports third quarter 2026 results
Unless otherwise noted, all financial figures are unaudited, presented in Canadian dollars (Cdn$), and derived from the company’s condensed consolidated financial statements which are based on Canadian generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), specifically International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB), and are prepared in accordance with International Accounting Standard (IAS) 34 Interim Financial Reporting. Production volumes are presented on a working-interest basis, before royalties, except for production values from the company's Libya operations, which are presented on an economic basis. Certain financial measures referred to in this news release (adjusted funds from operations, adjusted operating earnings, free funds flow, and net debt) are not prescribed by GAAP. See the Non-GAAP Financial Measures section of this news release. References to Oil Sands operations exclude Suncor Energy Inc.’s ownership of Fort Hills and interest in Syncrude.
Calgary, Alberta (November 3, 2026) Suncor Energy (TSX: SU) (NYSE: SU)
Third Quarter Highlights
• | Generated record quarterly adjusted funds from operations of $6.7 billion and free funds flow of $5.2 billion.

• | Returned more than $2.0 billion to shareholders, with nearly $1.4 billion in share repurchases and nearly $700 million in dividends.

• | Leveraged unparalleled integration through record bitumen transfers, supporting strong upstream production of 853,000 bbls/d.

• | Achieved record third quarter refined product sales of 677,000 bbls/d, backed by strong refining throughput of 488,000 bbls/d.

• | Subsequent to the quarter, monthly share repurchases increased by 50% to $750 million.

• | Subsequent to the quarter, increased the quarterly dividend by 10% to $0.66 per share.

• | Agreement reached to divest non-core offshore assets including Terra Nova, White Rose and West White Rose.

“The third quarter was highlighted by record adjusted funds from operations and the ingenuity of our teams, who continue to find new ways to create value through our unparalleled integration, most recently by sustaining Edmonton refinery throughput during a turnaround,” said Rich Kruger, Chief Executive Officer. “Our confidence in the continued earnings power and resiliency of our business has allowed us to increase our dividend and our monthly share repurchases, while also returning tremendous value to Canadians with significantly higher royalty payments and income taxes.”

• | Suncor’s adjusted operating earnings increased to $4.631 billion ($3.97 per common share) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.794 billion ($1.48 per common share) in the prior year quarter, primarily due to higher upstream price realizations, increased downstream margins and increased high-value global export sales, partially offset by a corresponding increase in royalties and tax expense.

• | Net earnings increased to $3.814 billion ($3.27 per common share) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.619 billion ($1.34 per common share) in the prior year quarter. In addition to the factors impacting adjusted operating earnings, net earnings for the third quarter of 2026 and the prior year quarter were impacted by the items shown in the table above, which included a non-cash asset impairment of $1.080 billion recorded in the E&P segment related to the planned sale of the company’s non-core offshore assets Terra Nova, White Rose and West White Rose.

• | Adjusted funds from operations increased to $6.705 billion ($5.75 per common share) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $3.831 billion ($3.16 per common share) in the prior year quarter, and were primarily influenced by the same factors impacting adjusted operating earnings. Adjusted funds from operations benefited from the strength of Suncor’s integrated operations, as the upstream captured strong benchmark pricing and the downstream benefited from higher cracking margins.

• | Cash flow provided by operating activities, which includes changes in non-cash working capital, was $6.196 billion ($5.31 per common share) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $3.785 billion ($3.13 per common share) in the prior year quarter.

• | Free funds flow increased to $5.196 billion ($4.46 per common share), compared to $2.347 billion ($1.94 per common share) in the prior year quarter, and was primarily influenced by the same factors impacting adjusted funds from operations.

• | Operating, selling and general (OS&G) expenses were $3.552 billion in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $3.270 billion in the prior year quarter, with the increase primarily due to increased mining activity, higher share-based compensation expense and higher commodity input costs.

• | Strong net earnings translated to third quarter current income tax and royalties expenses totalling $3.518 billion, predominantly attributable to Canada. Current income tax and royalties expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, totalled $8.067 billion.

• | Total Oil Sands bitumen production was 948,000 bbls/d in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 958,300 bbls/d in the prior year quarter, with the decrease primarily due to lower mining production at Syncrude related to increased maintenance activities, partially offset by record third quarter Firebag production.

• | The company’s net synthetic crude oil (SCO) production decreased to 528,100 bbls/d with upgrader utilization of 98% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 544,100 bbls/d and 102%, respectively, in the prior year quarter, primarily due to the planned upgrader maintenance activities at Syncrude.

• | Non-upgraded bitumen production was 260,400 bbls/d in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 268,100 bbls/d in the prior year quarter, consistent with lower bitumen production.

• | Exploration and Production (E&P) production increased to 64,000 bbls/d in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 57,800 bbls/d in the prior year quarter, primarily due to higher production at Terra Nova.

• | Refinery crude oil throughput of 487,700 bbls/d with refinery utilization(1) of 95% was comparable to 491,700 bbls/d and 96%, respectively, in the prior year quarter. Strong current quarter operating performance was offset by increased planned maintenance activities at the Montreal refinery. The company was able to fully mitigate the expected throughput impacts of planned maintenance at its Edmonton refinery by leveraging its regional integration with Oil Sands. In Eastern Canada, the impacts of a third-party refinery supply pipeline outage were largely mitigated through the company's feedstock optionality and logistics capabilities.

• | Refined product sales increased to a third quarter record of 676,900 bbls/d, compared to 646,800 bbls/d in the prior year quarter, as Suncor leveraged prior debottlenecking efforts to increase high-value export capacity, and delivered more domestic volumes through high-value retail channels, including Canada’s number one retail brand.

Corporate and Strategy Updates
• | Share repurchases increased to $750 million per month. Beginning in October 2026, Suncor increased planned monthly share repurchases to $750 million, from $500 million, projecting total 2026 share repurchases of $5.5 billion.

• | Quarterly dividend increase. Subsequent to the third quarter, Suncor’s board of directors approved a quarterly dividend of $0.66 per share, an increase of 10% over the prior quarter dividend, reflecting Suncor’s confidence in the earnings power and resiliency of its business.

• | Suncor to divest non-core offshore assets. Subsequent to the quarter, Suncor executed an agreement to sell its 48% interest in Terra Nova, 40% interest in White Rose and 38.6% interest in West White Rose offshore assets for $1.2 billion (US$860 million) and an additional contingent payment of up to $350 million (US$250 million), based on future oil prices. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, total production of these assets was 17,000 bbls/d. All investment commitments and future liabilities associated with the assets will be transferred to the purchaser, including a $500 million regulatory well compliance program starting in 2027 at Terra Nova and the total present value of estimated future abandonment and lease liabilities of $1.4 billion. The transaction has an effective date of July 1, 2026, and is expected to close in early 2027, subject to customary closing conditions, regulatory approvals and partner consents.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Certain financial measures in this news release – namely adjusted funds from operations, adjusted operating earnings, free funds flow, net debt, and related per share or per barrel amounts – are not prescribed by GAAP. These non-GAAP financial measures are included because management uses the information to analyze business performance, leverage and liquidity, as applicable, and it may be useful to investors on the same basis. These non-GAAP financial measures do not have any standardized meaning and, therefore, are unlikely to be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies. Therefore, these non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for measures of performance prepared in accordance with GAAP. Except as otherwise indicated, these non-GAAP financial measures are calculated and disclosed on a consistent basis from period to period. Specific adjusting items may only be relevant in certain periods.
Adjusted Operating Earnings
Adjusted operating earnings is a non-GAAP financial measure that adjusts net earnings for significant items that are not indicative of operating performance. Management uses adjusted operating earnings to evaluate operating performance because management believes it provides better comparability between periods. Adjusted operating earnings are reconciled to net earnings in the news release above.
​
Adjusted Funds From (Used In) Operations
Adjusted funds from (used in) operations is a non-GAAP financial measure that adjusts a GAAP measure – cash flow provided by operating activities – for changes in non-cash working capital, which management uses to analyze operating performance and liquidity. Changes to non-cash working capital can be impacted by, among other factors, commodity price volatility, the timing of offshore feedstock purchases and payments for commodity and income taxes, the timing of cash flows related to accounts receivable and accounts payable, and changes in inventory, which management believes reduces comparability between periods.

Free Funds Flow (Deficit)
Free funds flow (deficit) is a non-GAAP financial measure that is calculated by taking adjusted funds from operations and subtracting capital expenditures, including capitalized interest. Free funds flow reflects cash available for increasing distributions to shareholders and reducing debt. Management uses free funds flow to measure the capacity of the company to increase returns to shareholders and to grow Suncor’s business.
