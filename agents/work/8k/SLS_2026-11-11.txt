SELLAS Life Sciences Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update
- Topline Primary Endpoint Results of the Phase 3 REGAL Trial of Galinpepimut-S (GPS) in Acute Myeloid Leukemia Following the 80th Event to Report this Month -
- 40 Patients Enrolled in Phase 2 Trial of SLS009 in Newly Diagnosed First-Line AML; Preliminary Topline Data Expected in Q4 2026 -
- $134.7 Million in Cash and Cash Equivalents as of September 30, 2026 -
NEW YORK, Nov. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SELLAS Life Sciences Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: SLS) (“SELLAS’’ or the “Company”), a late-stage clinical biopharmaceutical company focused on the development of novel therapies for a broad range of cancer indications, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.
“We continue to execute across both of our clinical programs as we approach several important milestones,” said Angelos Stergiou, MD, ScD h.c., President and Chief Executive Officer of SELLAS Life Sciences. “In our ongoing Phase 3 REGAL trial of GPS, we are focused on completing the final analysis following the announcement of 80th event, and we expect to announce topline primary endpoint results this month. If successful, we believe GPS could be a transformative new immunotherapy for AML patients in second complete remission. In parallel, enrollment and dosing continue in our Phase 2 trial of tambiciclib (SLS009) in newly diagnosed AML, with preliminary topline data expected in the fourth quarter. As announced earlier this month, we look forward to presenting our Phase 2 data in relapsed/refractory AML at the upcoming ASH in December. We were also encouraged by the preclinical data presented at AACR in pancreatic cancer models, including in resistance to a RAS inhibitor, and we are continuing our preparations for clinical development in that indication. With $134.7 million in cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, we are well positioned to further advance these programs.”
Recent Corporate Highlights:
Phase 3 REGAL Trial of GPS: Ongoing Phase 3 trial in AML patients who have achieved complete remission following second-line salvage therapy. In October 2026, the Company announced the required pre-specified 80th event occurred, triggering the customary database lock, blinded data review procedures before statistical analysis, unblinding, and disclosure of topline results, which the Company expects to report this month.
Ongoing dosing of SLS009 in earlier-line AML: 40 patients have been enrolled, and enrollment and dosing continue in the ongoing 80-patient Phase 2 trial in newly diagnosed AML patients, including those who become refractory early to AZA/VEN treatment identified through extensive transcriptomics, genomics, and proteomics models. Preliminary topline data is expected in Q4 2026. Additional information about the trial can be found at clinicaltrials.gov (NCT04588922).
Phase 2 Data of Tambiciclib (SLS009) in Combination with Azacitidine and Venetoclax in Relapsed/Refractory AML to be presented at upcoming 2026 American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting: In November, the Company announced that it will present data from its completed Phase 2 study of tambiciclib (SLS009) for the treatment of relapsed or refractory acute myeloid leukemia (r/r AML) at the 68th American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting and Exposition, being held December 12–15, 2026, in New Orleans, Louisiana. Patients with relapsed/refractory monocytic AML achieved a 54% ORR with a median overall survival (mOS) of 5.8 months and patients with TP53-mutated AML achieved a 57% ORR and a mOS of 8.8 months, comparing favorably to historical outcomes in these adverse-risk settings of ~2.5 months.
The Phase 2 trial evaluated SLS009, a highly selective CDK9 inhibitor, in combination with azacitidine (AZA) and venetoclax (VEN) for the treatment of patients with r/r AML after prior VEN-based treatment.
Presented Preclinical Data Demonstrating SLS009 Activity in Pancreatic Cancer Models at the 2026 AACR Conference on Pancreatic Cancer: In September, the Company presented preclinical data from studies evaluating SLS009 (tambiciclib), its highly selective cyclin-dependent kinases 9 (CDK9) inhibitor, in patient-derived organoid models of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). The data were presented at the American Association for Cancer Research (AACR) Conference on Pancreatic Cancer: New Frontiers in Biology and Therapeutic Development. The studies, conducted in collaboration with researchers at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, evaluated SLS009 in MYC-amplified PDAC models, including a model resistant to the recently approved RAS inhibitor daraxonrasib (RMC-6236), as well as in combination with the BET inhibitor ZEN3694. MYC amplification is a biologically recognized mechanism of RAS targeting resistance.
The findings support further investigation of CDK9 inhibition as a strategy to enhance RAS-directed therapy and potentially address MYC-associated resistance in pancreatic cancer. The BET combination data provide additional mechanistic support for SLS009-based approaches designed to disrupt MYC-dependent transcriptional programs and suggest the potential to enhance BET inhibition at lower drug exposures.
Financial Results for the Third Quarter 2026:
Research and Development Expenses: Research and development expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, were $7.0 million, compared to $4.2 million for the same period in 2025. Research and development expenses were $18.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $11.3 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily due to increases in manufacturing costs, clinical and regulatory consulting, and clinical trial expenses in preparation for a potential Biologics License Application (BLA) for GPS following the final analysis of the REGAL study.
General and Administrative Expenses: General and administrative expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $4.3 million, as compared to $2.9 million for the same period in 2025. General and administrative expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $12.8 million compared to $8.7 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily due to increases in non-cash stock-based compensation and professional fees.
Net Loss: The net loss was $10.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, or a basic and diluted loss per share of $0.05, as compared to a net loss of $6.8 million for the third quarter of 2025, or
a basic and diluted loss per share of $0.06. The net loss was $28.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, or a basic and diluted loss per share of $0.15, as compared to a net loss of $19.2 million for the same period in 2025, or a basic and diluted loss per share of $0.20.
Cash Position: As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents totaled approximately $134.7 million.
About SELLAS Life Sciences Group, Inc.
SELLAS is a late-stage clinical biopharmaceutical company focused on the development of novel therapeutics for a broad range of cancer indications. SELLAS’ lead product candidate, GPS, is licensed from Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center and targets the WT1 protein, which is present in an array of tumor types. GPS has the potential as a monotherapy and combination with other therapies to address a broad spectrum of hematologic malignancies and solid tumor indications. The Company is also developing SLS009 (tambiciclib) – potentially the first and best-in-class differentiated small molecule CDK9 inhibitor with reduced toxicity and increased potency compared to other CDK9 inhibitors. Data suggests that SLS009 demonstrated a high response rate in AML patients with unfavorable prognostic factors including ASXL1 mutation, commonly associated with poor prognosis in various myeloid diseases. For more information on SELLAS, please visit www.sellaslifesciences.com.
