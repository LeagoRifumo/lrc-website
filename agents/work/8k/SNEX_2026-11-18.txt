EXHIBIT 99.1
StoneX Group Inc. Reports Fiscal 2026 Fourth Quarter Financial Results
Record Fiscal Year Results, with Net Income of $525.4 million, up 72%
Quarterly Net Operating Revenues of $655.2 million, up 12%
Quarterly Net Income of $84.2 million, Quarterly ROE of 11.6%
Quarterly Diluted EPS of $0.66 per share, Fiscal Year of $4.15 per share
NEW YORK – November 18, 2026 – StoneX Group Inc. (the “Company”; NASDAQ: SNEX), a leading financial services franchise connecting clients to global markets, today announced its financial results for the fiscal fourth quarter and year ended September 30, 2026.
“We are proud to close out fiscal 2026 with record annual results, highlighted by $2.9 billion in net operating revenues and $525.4 million in net income,” said Philip Smith, the Company’s Chief Executive Officer. “These results reflect the momentum we have built across StoneX, with continued double-digit year-over-year growth in both net operating revenues and earnings, with record annual performance in our Commercial, Institutional and Payments segments. The strength of our unique non-bank ecosystem continues to deepen our relevance to a growing global client base and gives us confidence in the opportunities ahead. Just as importantly, alongside delivering record earnings, we successfully integrated our largest acquisition to date, on schedule whilst achieving our capital and cost synergy targets—demonstrating both the resilience of our platform and our ability to execute with discipline,” Mr. Smith concluded.

Operating Revenues
Operating revenues increased $258.2 million, or 21%, to $1,460.5 million in the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $1,202.3 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025. The acquisition of RJO, which was effective July 31, 2025, contributed $195.8 million in operating revenue in the three months ended September 30, 2026 as compared to $141.0 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025, which included two months of post-acquisition activity.
Operating revenues derived from listed derivatives increased $82.4 million, principally driven by the acquisition of RJO which contributed $143.3 million in operating revenue from listed derivatives in the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $89.5 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025, which included two months of post-acquisition activity. Operating revenues derived from listed derivatives in our Commercial and Institutional segments were up $48.4 million and $34.0 million, respectively.
Operating revenues derived from OTC derivatives increased $51.4 million, principally driven by a 97% increase in OTC derivative contract volumes, which was partially offset by a 6% decrease in the average RPC.
Operating revenues derived from securities transactions increased $84.5 million, principally due to a 17% increase in securities ADV along with an increase in interest income, as carried interest on fixed income securities is a component of operating revenues. When calculating securities RPM, we deduct interest expense associated with our fixed income activities from the operating revenues in order to provide a more useful measure of the financial performance of our securities business, which is more comparable to the amount of net operating revenues derived from securities transactions, shown further below, which decreased $21.0 million, principally from a 23% decline in securities RPM, primarily as a result of the narrowing of spreads across both equity and fixed income products.
Additionally, during the three months ended September 30, 2026, we refined our disaggregation of operating revenues by separately reporting operating revenues related to “Prime services”, which previously had been reported within “Securities” and “Other” disaggregation lines. All prior periods presented have been revised retroactive to October 1, 2023, and the impact of this change resulted in a lower calculated securities RPM than as previously shown for all periods reflected in the table above.
Operating revenues derived from prime services decreased $4.4 million, or 6%, to $72.1 million in the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $76.5 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025.
Operating revenues derived from FX/CFD contracts decreased $7.4 million, as a result of a $7.7 million decrease in our Self-Directed/Retail segment primarily driven by a decline in ADV, partially offset by a $0.3 million increase in Commercial segment FX contracts operating revenues, principally as a result of an increase in RPM. Our FX & Interest Rates business previously reported in our Institutional segment has been moved into the Commercial segment, and all segment information herein has been revised retroactive to October 1, 2023.
Operating revenues derived from payments increased $9.8 million, principally driven by a 28% increase in payments ADV, partially offset by a 9% decline in payments RPM.
Operating revenues derived from physical contracts increased $58.5 million, principally driven by a $46.4 million increase in operating revenues in our physical precious metals business, along with a $12.4 million increase in physical supply and trading operating revenues. Precious metals related operating revenues were favorably impacted by $2.1 million of realized gains on the sale of physical inventories carried at the lower of cost or net realizable value for which losses on related derivative positions were recognized in prior periods, while the prior year period was unfavorably impacted by unrealized losses of $5.0 million on derivative positions related to physical inventories carried at the lower of cost or net realizable value.
Interest and fee income earned on client balances, which is associated with our listed and OTC derivative businesses, as well as our Correspondent Clearing, Independent Wealth Management, and Prime Services businesses, decreased $12.3 million, principally as a result of a $17.9 million unrealized marked-to-market loss on U.S. Treasury and Agency positions in our investment portfolio for the three months ended September 30, 2026 as compared to a $9.0 million unrealized gain on U.S. Treasury and Agency positions for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Average client equity balances increased 26% in the three months ended September 30, 2026 as compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The acquisition of RJO contributed $48.8 million to interest and fee income earned on client balances in the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $50.0 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025.

EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure used to measure operating performance, is defined as net income plus interest expense, depreciation and amortization, and income tax expense. Adjusted EBITDA represents EBITDA plus amortization of share-based compensation and other non-recurring losses and less interest expense attributable to trading activities, including the credit facilities of our subsidiaries, and non-recurring gains from class action settlements.
Each of the EBITDA-based measures described above is not a presentation made in accordance with GAAP and should not be considered as an alternative to net income or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP as a measure of operating performance or to cash flows as a measure of liquidity. Additionally, each such measure is not intended to be a measure of free cash flows available for management’s discretionary use, as it does not consider certain cash requirements such as interest payments, tax payments and debt service requirements. Such measures have limitations as analytical tools, and you should not consider any of such measures in isolation or as substitutes for our results as reported under GAAP. Management compensates for the limitations of using non-GAAP financial measures by using them to supplement GAAP results to provide a more complete understanding of the factors and trends affecting the business than GAAP results alone. Because not all companies use identical calculations, these EBITDA-based measures may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies.
The Company believes EBITDA is helpful in highlighting the business’s trends because EBITDA excludes the results of decisions that are outside the control of management and can differ significantly from company to company depending on long-term strategic decisions regarding capital structure, the tax jurisdictions in which companies operate and capital investments. In addition, EBITDA may provide more comparability between the historical operating results that reflect purchase accounting and the new capital structure.
