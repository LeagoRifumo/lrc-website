Piper Sandler Companies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results; Declares Quarterly Dividend of $0.20 Per Share;
Announces New Share Repurchase Authorization
MINNEAPOLIS—October 30, 2026—Piper Sandler Companies (NYSE: PIPR), a leading investment bank, today announced its results for the third quarter of 2026.
"We delivered solid results for the third quarter, supported by 18% year-over-year growth in advisory services revenues and increased client activity in fixed income," said Chad Abraham, chairman and chief executive officer. "As we build on our record year-to-date revenues, we remain focused on supporting our clients across dynamic markets, finishing the year strong and continuing to return capital to our shareholders."

Financial & Business Highlights
•Net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $443 million, and adjusted net revenues totaled $456 million.
◦Corporate investment banking generated record third quarter revenues of $307 million driven by strong results from our financial services, healthcare and energy, power & infrastructure groups.
◦Fixed income services activity was robust, generating revenues of $60 million, the best quarter since 2021.
•For the first nine months of 2026, both net revenues and adjusted net revenues were a record, totaling $1.4 billion, an increase of 15% and 14%, respectively, over last year driven by 21% growth in corporate investment banking revenues.
Strategic Updates
•In September 2026, we launched equities trading in Europe through Piper Sandler Europe, expanding our reach and bringing our leading U.S. equities franchise to European clients.
Talent
•Established a London-based debt advisory team within our energy, power & infrastructure investment banking group consisting of four professionals, including one managing director, focused on advising infrastructure companies.
•Continued to strengthen our private capital advisory group with the addition of a managing director focused on secondary capital advisory.
Capital
•Declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.20 per share of the company's common stock on October 30, 2026 to be paid on December 11, 2026 to shareholders of record as of November 24, 2026.
•Authorized the repurchase of up to $200 million of the company's outstanding common stock, effective immediately, which will expire on December 31, 2028.
•Returned an aggregate of $268 million to shareholders during the first nine months of 2026 through dividends paid and repurchases of 1.8 million shares of the company's common stock at an average price of $77.80 per share.

NET REVENUES
For the third quarter of 2026, net revenues of $443.0 million decreased 11% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and 8% compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Investment banking revenues of $331.7 million for the third quarter of 2026 decreased 8% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and were essentially flat compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Advisory services revenues of $250.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 decreased 9% compared to the second quarter of 2026 driven by a lower average fee, but increased 18% compared to the third quarter of 2025 resulting from a higher average fee. Performance was led by our financial services group and our energy, power & infrastructure team, which generated a record quarter, and included a solid contribution from our healthcare franchise.
•Corporate financing revenues of $56.6 million for the third quarter of 2026 increased 50% compared to the second quarter of 2026 as higher revenue per deal more than offset a decline in completed financings. Corporate financing revenues decreased 29% compared to the strong third quarter of 2025 driven primarily by fewer completed transactions. Our performance during the quarter was centered around capital raising activity for our healthcare and financial services clients.
•Municipal financing revenues of $24.8 million for the third quarter of 2026 decreased 50% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and 36% compared to the third quarter of 2025 as higher interest rates negatively impacted issuance activity.
3
Institutional brokerage revenues of $114.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 increased 2% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and 3% compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Equity brokerage revenues of $54.5 million for the third quarter of 2026 decreased 13% compared to the strong second quarter of 2026 resulting from lower client activity, but revenues were flat compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Fixed income services revenues of $59.8 million for the third quarter of 2026 increased 23% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and 5% compared to the third quarter of 2025 as the elevated interest rate environment drove increased client activity.
Investment income/(loss) for the third quarter of 2026 was a loss of $13.5 million compared to income of $14.1 million for the second quarter of 2026 and income of $30.6 million for the third quarter of 2025. For the current and prior periods, investment income/(loss) includes amounts attributable to noncontrolling interests primarily related to the alternative asset funds we manage.
NON-INTEREST EXPENSES
For the third quarter of 2026, non-interest expenses of $376.4 million decreased 5% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and were essentially flat compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Compensation ratio of 64.7% for the third quarter of 2026 was higher compared to 62.3% for the second quarter of 2026 and 60.3% for the third quarter of 2025 resulting from lower net revenues, which was exacerbated by the investment loss attributable to noncontrolling interests.
•Non-compensation expenses of $89.8 million for the third quarter of 2026 increased 4% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and 8% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Non-compensation expenses were higher during the current quarter primarily due to incremental occupancy and equipment costs in connection with our New York City office move and increased outside services expenses resulting from higher legal fees as well as increased professional fees associated with technology consulting services.
PRE-TAX INCOME
For the third quarter of 2026, we recorded pre-tax income of $66.6 million compared to $100.4 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $107.4 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•Pre-tax margin of 15.0% for the third quarter of 2026 declined compared to 20.3% for the second quarter of 2026 and 22.4% for the third quarter of 2025 driven primarily by losses on our investments, including amounts attributable to noncontrolling interests in the alternative asset funds we manage. In addition, pre-tax margin for the current quarter was impacted by higher non-compensation expenses.
EFFECTIVE TAX RATE
For the current and prior periods, the effective tax rate is impacted by the level of noncontrolling interests, the amount of non-deductible expenses, and the vesting of restricted stock awards. For the third quarter of 2026, the effective tax rate of 39.7% was elevated due to the net loss attributable to noncontrolling interests. The effective tax rate for the second quarter of 2026 was 30.2%. For the third quarter of 2025, the effective tax rate of 23.2% was impacted by the higher net income attributable to noncontrolling interests.
NET INCOME & EARNINGS PER SHARE
For the third quarter of 2026, we generated net income of $55.4 million, or $0.78 per diluted common share.
Results for the current quarter decreased compared to net income of $67.8 million, or $0.95 per diluted common share, for the second quarter of 2026 and net income of $60.3 million, or $0.85 per diluted common share, for the third quarter of 2025 resulting from lower net revenues, a decreased pre-tax margin and a higher effective tax rate.

ADJUSTED NET REVENUES
For the third quarter of 2026, adjusted net revenues of $456.0 million decreased 7% compared to the second quarter of 2026 driven by lower municipal financing, advisory services and equity brokerage revenues which were offset in part by increased corporate financing and fixed income services revenues. Adjusted net revenues were modestly higher compared to the third quarter of 2025, as increased advisory services revenues were offset by lower corporate and municipal financing activity.
ADJUSTED OPERATING EXPENSES
For the third quarter of 2026, adjusted operating expenses of $365.7 million decreased 5% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and increased 2% compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted compensation ratio of 61.5% for the third quarter of 2026 was flat compared to the second quarter of 2026 and declined compared to 61.7% for the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted non-compensation expenses of $85.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 increased 4% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and 9% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted non-compensation expenses were higher during the current quarter primarily due to incremental occupancy and equipment costs in connection with our New York City office move and increased outside services expenses resulting from higher legal fees as well as increased professional fees associated with technology consulting services.
ADJUSTED OPERATING INCOME
For the third quarter of 2026, adjusted operating income of $90.3 million decreased 16% compared to the second quarter of 2026 and 6% compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted operating margin of 19.8% for the third quarter of 2026 decreased compared to 21.8% for the second quarter of 2026 resulting primarily from lower adjusted net revenues. Adjusted operating margin decreased compared to 21.2% for the third quarter of 2025 primarily due to increased adjusted non-compensation expenses.
ADJUSTED EFFECTIVE TAX RATE
For the third quarter of 2026, our adjusted effective tax rate was 31.7%, compared to 30.5% for the second quarter of 2026 and 28.8% for the third quarter of 2025.
ADJUSTED NET INCOME & ADJUSTED EARNINGS PER SHARE
For the third quarter of 2026, we generated adjusted net income of $61.7 million, or $0.87 of adjusted earnings per diluted common share.
Results for the current quarter decreased compared to adjusted net income of $74.5 million, or $1.04 of adjusted earnings per diluted common share, for the second quarter of 2026 resulting primarily from lower adjusted net revenues, higher adjusted non-compensation expenses and a higher adjusted effective tax rate. Results for the current quarter decreased compared to adjusted net income of $68.6 million, or $0.96 of adjusted earnings per diluted common share, for the third quarter of 2025 resulting primarily from higher adjusted non-compensation expenses and a higher adjusted effective tax rate.

Capital
DIVIDENDS
On October 30, 2026, our Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend on the company's common stock of $0.20 per share. This dividend will be paid on December 11, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on November 24, 2026.
During the third quarter of 2026, we paid a quarterly cash dividend of $0.20 per share of common stock, for an aggregate of $13.6 million.
During the first nine months of 2026, we paid quarterly cash dividends of $0.575 per share of common stock and a special cash dividend of $1.25 per share of common stock, which was paid in the first quarter of 2026. Total dividends paid year-to-date, including accrued forfeitable dividends paid on restricted stock awards that vested, were $128.2 million.
SHARE REPURCHASES
On October 30, 2026, our Board of Directors authorized the repurchase of up to $200 million of the company's outstanding common stock. This authorization is effective immediately and will expire on December 31, 2028. The share repurchase program will be used as part of our capital allocation strategy, which includes returning capital to our shareholders, and to offset the dilutive effect of employee equity-based compensation. Our existing share repurchase authorization has $20.3 million remaining and will expire on December 31, 2026.
During the third quarter of 2026, we repurchased 0.5 million shares of the company's common stock, at an average price of $74.53 per share, pursuant to our existing share repurchase authorization. We also repurchased 23 thousand shares of the company's common stock, at an average price of $75.91 per share, from restricted stock award recipients selling shares upon the award vesting to meet their employment tax obligations. The aggregate amount of 0.5 million shares, or $39.4 million of the company's common stock, were repurchased at an average price of $74.59 per share.
During the first nine months of 2026, we repurchased 1.3 million shares of the company's common stock, at an average price of $76.94 per share, pursuant to our existing share repurchase authorization. We also repurchased 0.5 million shares of the company's common stock, at an average price of $80.10 per share, from restricted stock award recipients selling shares upon the award vesting to meet their employment tax obligations. The aggregate amount of 1.8 million shares, or $140.3 million of the company's common stock, were repurchased at an average price of $77.80 per share.
