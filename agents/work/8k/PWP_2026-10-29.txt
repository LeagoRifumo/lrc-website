Revenues
For the third quarter of 2026, revenues were $251.4 million, an increase of 53% from $164.6 million reported in the third quarter of 2025, driven by a higher number of fee-paying clients and a greater share of fee events exceeding $5 million. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, revenues were $556.8 million, an increase of 5% from $531.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was driven by higher M&A revenue, reflecting a greater number of fee events, partially offset by lower financing and capital solutions revenue.

Three Months Ended
GAAP total compensation and benefits were $175.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $116.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted total compensation and benefits were $157.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $110.3 million for the same period a year ago. The increase in total compensation and benefits was due to higher revenues, partially offset by the impact of a lower adjusted compensation margin versus the third quarter of 2025, and, on a GAAP basis only, included acquisition-related compensation and previously disclosed business realignment costs.
GAAP non-compensation expenses were $41.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $39.4 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted non-compensation expenses were $37.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $36.8 million for the same period a year ago. The increase in GAAP non-compensation expenses was primarily driven by increased amortization expenses related to acquisitions as well as higher technology expenses.

Nine Months Ended
GAAP total compensation and benefits were $413.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $373.9 million for the prior year period. Adjusted total compensation and benefits were $373.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $356.3 million for the same period a year ago. The increase in total compensation and benefits was due to higher revenues and, on a GAAP basis only, higher acquisition-related compensation and previously disclosed business realignment costs. The adjusted compensation margin was 67% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, consistent with the prior year period.
GAAP non-compensation expenses were $116.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $128.4 million for the prior year period. Adjusted non-compensation expenses were $105.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $122.4 million for the same period a year ago. The decrease in non-compensation expenses was largely driven by a decrease in professional fees, partially offset by a modest increase in technology spend and, on a GAAP basis only, increased transaction and amortization expenses related to acquisitions.
Provision for Income Taxes
As of September 30, 2026, Perella Weinberg Partners owned 81.5% of the operating partnership (“PWP OpCo”) and is subject to U.S. federal and state corporate income tax on its allocable share of earnings. Income earned by PWP OpCo is subject to certain state, local, and foreign income taxes. The GAAP income tax expense for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $1.7 million, which included $10.9 million of tax benefit from restricted stock units (“RSUs”) that vested at a share price higher than the grant price.
For purposes of calculating adjusted if-converted net income, we present our results as if all partnership units had been converted to shares of Class A common stock and as if all of our adjusted results were subject to U.S. corporate income tax. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, adjusted if-converted net income included $11.5 million of tax benefit from the vesting of RSUs at a share price higher than the grant price.

Balance Sheet and Capital Management
As of September 30, 2026, we had $150 million of cash with no outstanding indebtedness and an undrawn revolving credit facility.
During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, we returned $88.3 million in aggregate to our equity holders through: (i) the net settlement of 3,018,699 share equivalents at an average price per share of $19.85, (ii) the repurchase of 127,328 shares at an average price per share of $15.73 in open market transactions pursuant to PWP’s Class A common stock repurchase program, (iii) the payment of aggregate dividends of $22.7 million to Class A common stockholders and (iv) the payment of $3.6 million in distributions to limited partners.
At September 30, 2026, there were 79.5 million shares of Class A common stock and 18.0 million partnership units outstanding.
During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Perella Weinberg made $7.2 million of cash payments related to the business realignment. Currently, we are estimating future cash payments of approximately $7.0 million related to the business realignment, substantially all of which is expected to be paid by or soon after December 31, 2026.
The Board of Directors has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.07 per share of Class A common stock. The dividend will be paid on December 8, 2026 to Class A common stockholders of record on November 24, 2026.
