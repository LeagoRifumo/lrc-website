Anterix Inc. Reports
Second Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Results
Woodland Park, NJ – November 10, 2026 – Anterix (NASDAQ: ATEX) today announced its second quarter fiscal 2027 results and filed its Form 10-Q for the three and six months ended September 30, 2026.
Financial and Operational Highlights
–Approximately $33.1 million of contracted proceeds outstanding with approximately $9.6 million expected to be received during the remainder of fiscal 2027
–Exchanged narrowband for broadband licenses in 6 counties and recorded a $6.1 million gain on exchange of broadband licenses
–Invested $5.4 million in spectrum clearing costs
–Announced that iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo are the first iPhone models to operate on dedicated private LTE networks using Anterix 900 MHz (Band 106) spectrum
–Subsequent to quarter end, on October 29, 2026, the Federal Communications Commission advanced a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking to modify its Supplemental Coverage from Space (“SCS”) rules, which includes consideration of adding the 900 MHz band to the list of SCS bands
Liquidity and Balance Sheet
At September 30, 2026, the Company had no debt and cash and cash equivalents of $107.5 million. In addition, the Company had a restricted cash balance of $3.2 million in escrow deposits.
The Company has an authorized share repurchase program for up to $250.0 million of the Company’s common stock on or before September 22, 2029. In fiscal 2027 second quarter, Anterix had no share repurchase activity. As of September 30, 2026, $250.0 million remained available under the share repurchase program.

About Anterix Inc.
Anterix is transforming how critical infrastructure stays connected. As the market leader in mission-critical private wireless broadband spectrum for the utility sector, Anterix delivers more secure, private 900 MHz licensed spectrum and advanced intelligent infrastructure solutions that enhance efficiency, strengthen resilience, and accelerate digital transformation. Backed by a growing ecosystem of industry-leading partners, Anterix provides the connectivity foundation that powers a more resourceful and resilient future. Learn more at www.anterix.com.
