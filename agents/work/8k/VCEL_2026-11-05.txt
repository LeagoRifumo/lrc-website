Vericel Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Raises Full-Year Financial Guidance
Total Revenue Growth of 26% to $85.4 Million
Net Income Increased 179% to $14.2 Million
Adjusted EBITDA Margin of 30%
Raised Full-Year Revenue Guidance to $344 to $346 Million and Adjusted EBITDA Margin Guidance to 28%
Conference Call Today at 8:30am Eastern Time
BURLINGTON, Mass., November 5, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Vericel Corporation (NASDAQ:VCEL), a leader in advanced therapies for the sports medicine and severe burn care markets, today reported financial results and business highlights for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Total net revenue growth of 26% to $85.4 million
•MACI® net revenue growth of 21% to $67.4 million
•Burn Care net revenue growth of 52% to $18.0 million, with Epicel® revenue of $12.7 million and NexoBrid® revenue of $5.3 million
•Gross margin of 73%
•Net income increased 179% to $14.2 million, or $0.27 per diluted share
•Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA increased 48% to $25.2 million, or 30% of revenue
•Operating cash flow of $17.7 million
•Free cash flow of $15.7 million
•$235.6 million in cash and investments, and no debt
Year-to-Date 2026 Financial Highlights
•Total net revenue growth of 26% to $231.3 million
•MACI net revenue growth of 22% to $189.3 million
•Burn Care net revenue growth of 50% to $42.0 million
•Adjusted EBITDA growth of 48% to $49.6 million
•Operating cash flow of $50.3 million
•Free cash flow of $45.1 million

2026 Full-Year Financial Guidance
•Raised total revenue guidance to $344 to $346 million, compared to $330 to $340 million previously

Third Quarter 2026 Results
Total net revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 increased 26% to $85.4 million, compared to $67.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. Total net product revenue for the quarter included $67.4 million of MACI (autologous cultured chondrocytes on porcine collagen membrane) net revenue, $12.7 million of Epicel (cultured epidermal autografts) net revenue, $2.3 million of NexoBrid (anacaulase-bcdb) commercial net revenue and $3.0 million of NexoBrid BARDA procurement revenue, compared to $55.7 million of MACI net revenue, $10.4 million of Epicel net revenue, and $1.5 million of commercial NexoBrid net revenue, respectively, in the third quarter of 2025.

Gross profit for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $62.6 million, or 73% of net revenue, compared to $49.6 million, or 73% of net revenue, for the third quarter of 2025.
Total operating expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $50.4 million, compared to $46.1 million for the same period in 2025. The increase in operating expenses was primarily due to increased headcount and related employee expenses, including the MACI sales force expansion, and an increase in marketing programs.

Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $25.2 million, or 30% of net revenue, compared to $17.0 million, or 25% of net revenue, for the third quarter of 2025. A table reconciling non-GAAP measures is included in this press release for reference.

About Vericel Corporation
Vericel is a leading provider of advanced therapies for the sports medicine and severe burn care markets. The Company combines innovations in biology with medical technologies, resulting in a highly differentiated portfolio of innovative cell therapies and specialty biologics that repair injuries and restore lives. Vericel markets three products in the United States. MACI (autologous cultured chondrocytes on porcine collagen membrane) is an autologous cellularized scaffold product indicated for the repair of symptomatic, single or multiple full-thickness cartilage defects of the knee with or without bone involvement in adults. Epicel (cultured epidermal autografts) is a permanent skin replacement for the treatment of patients with deep dermal or full thickness burns greater than or equal to 30% of total body surface area. Vericel also holds an exclusive license for North American rights to NexoBrid (anacaulase-bcdb), a biological orphan product containing proteolytic enzymes, which is indicated for eschar removal in adults and pediatric patients with deep partial-thickness and/or full-thickness thermal burns. For more information, please visit www.vcel.com.
