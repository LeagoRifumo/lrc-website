Tencent Music Entertainment Group Announces Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results
SHENZHEN, China, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Tencent Music Entertainment Group (“TME,” or the “Company”) (NYSE: TME and HKEX: 1698), the leading all-in-one music and audio entertainment platform in China, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
· | Total revenues were RMB8.94 billion (US$1.33 billion), representing a 5.6% year-over-year increase. Revenues from music related services1 were RMB7.68 billion (US$1.15 billion), representing 10.2% year-over-year growth. Revenues from membership services2 were RMB4.99 billion (US$744 million), representing 9.2% year-over-year growth. Revenues from marketing and consumption services3 were RMB2.70 billion (US$402 million), representing 12.1% year-over-year growth, driven by robust growth in revenues from artist-related merchandise and offline performance related services.

· | On an IFRS basis:

Ø | Net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company was RMB2.15 billion (US$321 million) and remained stable compared with the same period of 2025.

Ø | Diluted earnings per ADS was RMB1.36 (US$0.20), compared with RMB1.38 in the same period of 2025.

· | On a non-IFRS basis:

Ø | Adjusted EBITDA4 was RMB3.02 billion (US$450 million), representing 2.7% year-over-year growth.

Ø | Non-IFRS net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company4 was RMB2.45 billion (US$365 million), compared with RMB2.41 billion in the same period of 2025.

Ø | Non-IFRS diluted earnings per ADS was RMB1.55 (US$0.23), compared with RMB1.54 in the same period of 2025.

· | Total cash, cash equivalents, term deposits and short-term investments as of September 30, 2026, were RMB51.49 billion (US$7.68 billion).

· | In the third quarter of 2026, the Company repurchased 34.7 million ADSs for an aggregate consideration of approximately US$300.0 million in cash.

Mr. Cussion Pang, Executive Chairman of TME, commented, “We delivered healthy growth in the third quarter, supported by the resilience of our music business and strong momentum from a broader range of IP experiences. Against an evolving competitive landscape, we are strengthening our music and audio ecosystem by leveraging our broad IP portfolio and growing pan-IP operating capabilities. Anchored in music, we are bringing IP to life across music, audio, live entertainment, and beyond. Ximalaya further enriches these experiences and creates more ways for users to engage. Each touchpoint reinforces the others, creating richer experiences for users while broadening and extending the reach and longevity of our IP.”

Third Quarter 2026 Operational Highlights
IP-Centric Content Ecosystem – Deepening and diversifying our premium music and audio catalogue to serve a broader range of users and listening needs.
· | Strengthened high-demand music offerings across K-pop, Mandopop, and OSTs to meet evolving listening preferences. 1) Bolstered K-pop catalogue by renewing our contract with HYBE and securing 30-day early release arrangements for selected major releases, including global icon JISOO’s latest single. 2) Reinforced Mandopop catalogue, a key driver of music consumption, through our renewed partnership with YH Entertainment Group, securing premium music IP assets and paving the way for future merchandise collaborations. 3) Produced bespoke soundtracks for blockbuster Tencent Video drama series and introduced trending OSTs from popular dramas, including Against the Current and The Early Spring, further enriching our premium content library.

· | Broadened our audience reach with a richer selection of premium audio content spanning entertainment, knowledge, children’s, and parenting categories. 1) Collaborated with studios to produce multiple audiobook adaptations of The Grave Robbers’ Chronicles, bringing fresh appeal to the classic IP and driving stream growth. 2) Expanded our original audio content in popular categories such as history and children's programming, attracting new audience and supporting membership conversion.

Holistic IP Capabilities – Leveraging our distinctive IP operations to amplify artist influence, deepen user engagement, and extend IP value across touchpoints.
· | Expanded artists’ reach through integrated promotion and fan engagement. 1) Advanced collaboration with Weixin Video Accounts by bringing leading artists such as JENNIE, YUQI, Silence Wang, Jane Zhang, and Ziyu5 onto the platform, driving artist-fan interaction and higher average daily video views. 2) Celebrated BIGBANG’s 20th anniversary with integrated online campaigns and offline exhibitions across dozens of cities in mainland China, boosting catalogue streams and merchandise sales. 3) Empowered JJ Lin, Lil Ghost and TIA RAY to perform at the Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix, elevating their global influence.

· | Unlocked deeper IP value through live concerts, scaling operations from domestic to overseas markets. 1) Staged four stadium concerts for rapper Zhou Yan (GAI), attracting over 120,000 attendees, and hosted concerts for artists and groups such as Will Pan, Shan Yichun, Steven Zhang, and TREASURE. 2) Supported TIA RAY’s first world tour across Australia, Canada, Malaysia, and the United States, as well as her performance at the GRAMMY Museum, expanding her international reach. 3) Co-produced the 2026 Pepsi Music Festival, featuring Ashin of Mayday, Jackson Wang, and TransfOrm Project, enhancing Pepsi’s brand visibility among younger audiences.

Products & Services – Deepening engagement and driving monetization across everyday listening, premium memberships, and broader IP consumption.
· | Enhanced user experience across music and audio to reinforce loyalty and broaden user reach. 1) Main music apps: Simplified music discovery through contextualized recommendations and swipe-based feeds, leading to higher daily time spent per user. · | Rolled out more pan-IP offerings, driving strong growth in paying users and average spending across IP consumption services. This momentum was supported by solid live performance results and strong fan demand for merchandise spanning artist and literary IP.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Review
Total revenues increased by RMB478 million, or 5.6%, to RMB8.94 billion (US$1.33 billion) from RMB8.46 billion in the same period of 2025.
· | Revenues from music related services increased by 10.2% to RMB7.68 billion (US$1.15 billion), compared with RMB6.97 billion in the same period of 2025. Revenues from membership services were RMB4.99 billion (US$744 million), representing 9.2% year-over-year growth, compared with RMB4.56 billion in the same period of 2025. Revenues from marketing and consumption services were RMB2.70 billion (US$402 million), representing 12.1% year-over-year growth, compared with RMB2.41 billion in the same period of 2025. The increase of revenues from music related services was due to consolidation of Ximalaya, as well as robust growth in revenues from artist-related merchandise sales and offline performance related services, primarily driven by collaborations with Silence Wang, one of our strategic artists. Our SVIP membership program continued to expand and contributed to our membership revenue growth.

· | Revenues from social entertainment services and others decreased by 15.7% to RMB1.26 billion (US$188 million) from RMB1.49 billion in the same period of 2025.

3
Cost of revenues increased by 7.0% year-over-year to RMB5.12 billion (US$763 million), mainly due to increased costs related to long-form audio content costs and artist-related merchandise, as well as higher costs related to offline performances related services.
Gross margin was 42.8% compared with 43.5% in the same period of 2025, mainly due to the shift of revenue mix, as revenues from artist-related merchandise and offline performance related services continued to grow, whereas revenues from social entertainment services and others decreased.
Total operating expenses increased by 16.9% year-over-year to RMB1.53 billion (US$229 million). Operating expenses as a percentage of total revenues increased to 17.1% from 15.5% in the same period of 2025. The increase was primarily due to the consolidation of Ximalaya, including the amortization of intangible assets arising from the acquisition.
On an IFRS basis, net profit and net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB2.22 billion (US$331 million) and RMB2.15 billion (US$321 million), respectively. Basic and diluted earnings per American Depositary Shares (“ADS”) for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB1.37 (US$0.20) and RMB1.36 (US$0.20), respectively. The Company had weighted averages of 1.57 billion basic and 1.58 billion diluted ADSs outstanding, respectively. Each ADS represents two of the Company’s Class A ordinary shares.
On a non-IFRS basis, adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB3.02 billion (US$450 million). Non-IFRS net profit was RMB2.53 billion (US$377 million) and non-IFRS net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company was RMB2.45 billion (US$365 million). Non-IFRS basic and diluted earnings per ADS were RMB1.56 (US$0.23) and RMB1.55 (US$0.23), respectively. Please refer to the section in this press release titled “Non-IFRS Financial Measures” for details.
As of September 30, 2026, the combined balance of the Company’s cash, cash equivalents, term deposits and short-term investments amounted to RMB51.49 billion (US$7.68 billion), compared with RMB44.22 billion as of June 30, 2026. In September 2026, we issued senior unsecured notes with aggregate principal amount of US$1 billion, consisting of US$500 million due 2031 and US$500 million due 2036.
Share Repurchase Program
Under our previously announced share repurchase program, during the three months ended September 30, 2026, we repurchased a total of 34.7 million ADSs in the open market with cash for an aggregate consideration of approximately US$300.0 million at an average price of US$8.6 per ADS.
Environmental, Social, and Governance (“ESG”)
Our commitment to sustainability extends to our physical music products. We have optimized multiple stages of our production process, from the use of eco-friendly materials and resource-efficient packaging to the recycling and reuse of manufacturing materials. To date, these practices have been adopted across a growing portfolio of physical albums and related merchandise.

Non-IFRS Financial Measures
The Company uses non-IFRS financial measures for the period, including non-IFRS net profit, adjusted EBITDA (inc.SBC) and adjusted EBITDA, in evaluating its operating results and for financial and operational decision-making purposes. TME believes that non-IFRS financial measures help identify underlying trends in the Company’s business that could otherwise be distorted by the effect of certain expenses that the Company includes in its profit for the period. TME believes that non-IFRS financial measures for the period provide useful information about its results of operations, enhances the overall understanding of its past performance and future prospects and allows for greater visibility with respect to key metrics used by its management in its financial and operational decision-making.
Non-IFRS financial measures for the period should not be considered in isolation or construed as an alternative to operating profit, net profit for the period or any other measure of performance or as an indicator of its operating performance. Investors are encouraged to review non-IFRS financial measures for the period and the reconciliation to its most directly comparable IFRS measure. Non-IFRS financial measures for the period presented here may not be comparable to similarly titled measures presented by other companies. Other companies may calculate similarly titled measures differently, limiting their usefulness as comparative measures to the Company’s data. TME encourages investors and others to review its financial information in its entirety and not rely on a single financial measure.
Adjusted EBITDA (inc.SBC) for the period represents net profit for the period excluding income tax expense, finance cost, share of profit/loss of associates and joint ventures, other gains/losses, interest income, depreciation of property, plant and equipment and right-of-use assets, and amortization of intangible assets.
Adjusted EBITDA for the period represents net profit for the period excluding income tax expense, finance cost, share of profit/loss of associates and joint ventures, other gains/losses, interest income, depreciation of property, plant and equipment and right-of-use assets, amortization of intangible assets, and share-based compensation expenses.
Non-IFRS net profit for the period represents profit for the period excluding amortization of intangible and other assets arising from business acquisitions or combinations, share-based compensation expenses, net losses/gains from investments and related income tax effects.
