EXHIBIT 99.1
Adaptive Biotechnologies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
SEATTLE, Nov. 04, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Adaptive Biotechnologies Corporation (“Adaptive Biotechnologies”) (Nasdaq: ADPT), a commercial stage biotechnology company that aims to translate the genetics of the adaptive immune system into clinical products to diagnose and treat disease, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Adaptive achieved total company profitability ahead of expectations in Q3 and delivered 44% growth in core MRD revenue,” said Chad Robins, chief executive officer and co-founder of Adaptive Biotechnologies. “This momentum supports another increase in our full year outlook. Updated guidelines and a new definition of cure for multiple myeloma amplify the need for MRD testing and bolster clonoSEQ’s long-term potential.”
Recent Highlights
•
Achieved total company profitability in the third quarter of 2026 with adjusted EBITDA of $14.3 million and free cash flow of $7.7 million.
•
Generated revenue of $83.9 million in the third quarter of 2026. MRD revenue increased 28%, or 44% excluding milestones, versus the third quarter of 2025.
•
Delivered 39,587 clonoSEQ® tests in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 46% versus the third quarter of 2025.
•
Submitted a technical assessment to MolDX seeking an additional Medicare bundle for maintenance testing in multiple myeloma.
•
NCCN Guidelines® established a dedicated framework for MRD assessment in multiple myeloma, including annual testing during maintenance.
•
The International Myeloma Working Group introduced the first definition of cure for multiple myeloma, requiring a five-year treatment-free period with sustained MRD negativity assessed at a sensitivity of one myeloma cell among one million cells.
•
Recognized revenue from successful data delivery under our target discovery agreement and are progressing with the Immune Medicine separation process.
•
Adaptive raised full year 2026 MRD revenue guidance to a range of $276 million to $286 million, representing annual growth of 30% to 35%.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Revenue was $83.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, representing an 11% decrease from the third quarter in the prior year. Excluding revenue recognized under the Genentech Agreement, which did not generate revenue in the quarter ended September 30, 2026, revenue for the current quarter increased 39% from the third quarter in the prior year. MRD revenue was $72.6 million for the quarter, representing a 28% increase from the third quarter in the prior year. Immune Medicine revenue was $11.3 million for the quarter, representing a 70% decrease from the third quarter in the prior year. Excluding revenue generated from the Genentech Agreement, Immune Medicine revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 increased 229% from the third quarter in the prior year.
Operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $84.4 million, compared to $83.7 million in the third quarter of the prior year, representing an increase of 1%.
Interest and other income, net was $3.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $2.2 million in the third quarter of the prior year. Interest expense was $0.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $3.0 million in the third quarter of the prior year.
Net income was $2.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $9.5 million for the same period in 2025. Excluding revenue generated from the Genentech Agreement, net loss was $24.2 million for the third quarter of 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) was $14.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $28.0 million for the third quarter of the prior year. Excluding revenue generated from the Genentech Agreement, Adjusted EBITDA was a loss of $5.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.
Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities was $400.2 million as of September 30, 2026, inclusive of $13.8 million of cash and cash equivalents held by Digital Biotechnologies, Inc.
2026 Updated Financial Guidance
Adaptive Biotechnologies expects full year revenue for the MRD business to be between $276 million and $286 million, updated from the previous range between $268 million and $278 million. No revenue guidance is provided for the Immune Medicine business.
We expect full year total company operating expenses, including cost of revenue, to be approximately $350 million, updated from the previous range between $350 million and $355 million.
Management will provide further details on the outlook during the conference call.

About Adaptive Biotechnologies
Adaptive Biotechnologies (“we” or “our”) is a commercial-stage biotechnology company focused on harnessing the inherent biology of the adaptive immune system to transform the diagnosis and treatment of disease. We believe the adaptive immune system is nature’s most finely tuned diagnostic and therapeutic for most diseases, but the inability to decode it has prevented the medical community from fully leveraging its capabilities. Our proprietary immune medicine platform reveals and translates the massive genetics of the adaptive immune system with scale, precision and speed. We apply our platform to partner with biopharmaceutical companies, inform drug development, and develop clinical diagnostics across our two business segments: Minimal Residual Disease (MRD) and Immune Medicine. Our commercial products and clinical pipeline enable the diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment of diseases such as cancer and autoimmune disorders. Our goal is to develop and commercialize immune-driven clinical products tailored to each individual patient.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measure
To supplement our unaudited condensed consolidated statements of operations and unaudited condensed consolidated balance sheets, which are prepared in conformity with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States of America (“GAAP”), this press release also includes references to Adjusted EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure that we define as net income (loss) attributable to Adaptive Biotechnologies Corporation adjusted for interest and other income, net, interest expense, income tax (expense) benefit, depreciation and amortization expense, impairment costs for long-lived assets, restructuring expense, share-based compensation expense and revenue interest liability extinguishment loss. We define our segment Adjusted EBITDA in the same way to the extent the net income (loss) attributable to Adaptive Biotechnologies Corporation and adjustments are allocable to each segment. We have provided reconciliations of net income (loss) attributable to Adaptive Biotechnologies Corporation, the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure, to Adjusted EBITDA at the end of this press release.
Management uses Adjusted EBITDA, including segment Adjusted EBITDA, to evaluate the financial performance of our business and segments and to evaluate the effectiveness of our strategies. We present these figures because we believe they are frequently used by analysts, investors and other interested parties to evaluate companies in our industry and they facilitate comparisons on a consistent basis across reporting periods. Further, we believe it is helpful in highlighting trends in our operating results because it excludes items that are not indicative of our core operating performance.
Adjusted EBITDA, including segment Adjusted EBITDA, has limitations as an analytical tool and you should not consider it in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. We may in the future incur expenses similar to the adjustments we make. In particular, we expect to incur meaningful share-based compensation expense in the future. Other limitations include that Adjusted EBITDA, including segment Adjusted EBITDA, does not reflect:
•
all expenditures or future requirements for capital expenditures or contractual commitments;
•
changes in our working capital needs;
•
interest income and interest expense, which is an ongoing element of our costs to operate;
•
income tax (expense) benefit, which may be a necessary element of our costs and ability to operate;
•
the costs of replacing the assets being depreciated and amortized, which will often have to be replaced in the future;
•
the noncash component of employee compensation expense;
•
long-lived assets impairment costs; and
•
the impact of earnings or charges resulting from matters we consider not to be reflective, on a recurring basis, of our ongoing operations, such as our restructuring activities, reductions in workforce and our revenue interest liability extinguishment loss.
In addition, Adjusted EBITDA, including segment Adjusted EBITDA, may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies in our industry or across different industries.
