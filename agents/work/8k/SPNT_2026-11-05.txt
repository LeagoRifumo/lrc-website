SiriusPoint Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Announces LPT on Remaining Run-Off Reserves
HAMILTON, Bermuda, November 5, 2026 - SiriusPoint Ltd. (“SiriusPoint” or the “Company”) (NYSE:SPNT), a global specialty underwriter, today announced results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and entered into a loss portfolio transfer (LPT) agreement to close the chapter on its legacy portfolio.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Net income available to SiriusPoint common shareholders of $87 million, or $0.74 per diluted common share
•Operating earnings per share increased 19% to $0.88
•Return on equity of 15.4%, with operating return on equity of 17.6%
•Core gross written premium increased 14%, driven by 20% growth in Insurance & Services
•Core combined ratio of 90.1%
•Book value per diluted common share (ex. AOCI) increased 4% from June 30, 2026 to $20.21
•Balance sheet remains strong with BSCR estimate of 232%
Nine Months 2026 Highlights
•Net income available to SiriusPoint common shareholders of $255 million, up 25% versus prior year
•Diluted earnings per common share of $2.14, with operating earnings per share up 17% to $2.29
•Return on equity of 15.1%, with operating return on equity of 15.8%
•Insurance & Services gross written premium growth of 14%; continued discipline in Reinsurance with premiums decreasing 6%
•Core combined ratio of 90.1% improved 1.3 points versus prior year
•Book value per diluted common share (ex. AOCI) increased 12% from December 31, 2025 to $20.21
SiriusPoint’s LPT agreement with RiverStone covers substantially all of the remaining run-off reserves and is supported by significant limit and strong collateral. The deal is expected to unlock over $200 million of capital currently supporting the run-off portfolio. The deal is subject to regulatory approval.
Scott Egan, Chief Executive Officer, said: “Our third quarter results represent continued strong performance, with our diversified, low-volatility portfolio again delivering on both growth and profitability. Our Insurance & Services segment grew by 20% and our core combined ratio was 90.1% both in the quarter and year to date. With an operating return on equity of 17.6% for the quarter and 15.8% year to date, the business continues to outperform against our 12-15% target.
“We continue to create value for our shareholders as our book value per share (ex. AOCI) grew by 4% in the quarter and 12% year to date. As we head into the final quarter, we believe our underwriting credentials and positive momentum position us well to close out the year with confidence. Our balance sheet and capital are very strong, and we have the agility to deploy capital where we see the best opportunities.
“We have also announced another important milestone in the quarter linked to our legacy run-off portfolio, agreeing a loss portfolio transfer with RiverStone. This will allow us to effectively close the door on this historical book of reserves, further simplifying our business and unlocking significant capital.”

Third Quarter and Nine Months 2026 Summary
Consolidated underwriting income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $103.5 million compared to $91.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The improvement was primarily driven by lower attritional loss ratios and increased favorable prior year loss reserve development, slightly offset by higher expense ratios.
Consolidated underwriting income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $254.7 million compared to $235.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The improvement was primarily driven by a decrease in catastrophe losses as the prior period included losses from the California wildfires and increased favorable prior year loss reserve development, partially offset by higher expenses. Increased acquisition costs primarily resulted from profit commission accruals related to favorable loss experience and increased other underwriting expense is largely driven by expenses related to incentive compensation award outperformance.
Reportable Segments
The determination of our reportable segments is based on the manner in which management monitors the performance of our operations, which consist of two reportable segments - Insurance & Services and Reinsurance. Collectively, the sum of our two segments, Insurance & Services and Reinsurance, constitute our “Core” results. Core underwriting income, Core net services income, Core income and Core combined ratio are non-GAAP financial measures. See reconciliations in “Segment Reporting.” We believe it is useful to review Core results as they reflect how management views the business and reflects our decision to exit the run-off business. The sum of Core results and Corporate results are equal to the consolidated results of operations.
In the fourth quarter of 2026, SiriusPoint plans to realign its reportable segments to reflect how management now views and manages the business. Over recent years, SiriusPoint has evolved into a specialty underwriter, and management increasingly evaluates performance and allocates capital across its specialty markets. Our external financial reporting will be aligned with that approach.
Under the new structure, SiriusPoint plans to report two segments. Accident & Health, which now has over $1 billion of premiums, will become a standalone segment, reflecting its distinct product, risk, earnings and capital characteristics. The remaining specialty businesses will be reported in a new Specialty P&C segment, which management evaluates on a combined basis, allocating capital and resources and assessing underwriting performance across the portfolio. These segments will replace our previous Insurance & Services and Reinsurance segments, and together they will continue to make up our Core results.

Three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025
Core Premium Volume
Gross written premium increased by $119.0 million, or 13.7%, to $990.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $871.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Net written premium increased by $13.8 million, or 2.1%, to $678.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $664.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Net earned premium increased by $121.3 million, or 18.9%, to $764.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $643.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increases in written premium were driven by our Insurance & Services segment, including growth in Surety business as well as an increase in our estimated premium for Professional Liability business, slightly offset by the exit of an International A&H program. The increase in net earned premium was primarily related to increases in our estimate of the earned premium in Professional Liability and A&H businesses. The Professional Liability and A&H premium estimate changes were largely offset by corresponding loss and acquisition costs, with no significant impact on underwriting income.
Core Underwriting Results
Core results for the three months ended September 30, 2026 included income of $87.8 million compared to $79.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 consists of underwriting income of $75.9 million (90.1% combined ratio) and net services income of $11.9 million, compared to underwriting income of $69.6 million (89.1% combined ratio) and net services income of $10.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The improvement in underwriting income was primarily driven by lower attritional loss ratios and increased favorable prior year loss reserve development, slightly offset by higher expense ratios. For the three months ended September 30, 2026, favorable prior year loss reserve development was $18.3 million compared to $9.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by favorable development on Property and A&H business.
The increase in net services income was due to growth in the IMG travel business and the acquisition of Assist America, partially offset by the deconsolidation of Armada. Service margin, which is calculated as Net service fee income as a percentage of services revenues, increased to 19.7% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from 16.3% for the three months ended September 30, 2025, when adjusted to exclude Armada, driven by the acquisition of Assist America.
Nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025
Core Premium Volume
Gross written premium increased by $184.3 million, or 6.6%, to $2,975.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $2,791.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Net written premium decreased by $31.7 million, or 1.5%, to $2,085.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $2,116.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Net earned premium increased by $127.0 million, or 6.6%, to $2,041.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $1,914.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increases in gross written premium and net earned premium were driven by our Insurance & Services segment, primarily driven by an increase in premium estimates on Professional Liability and A&H businesses, new program growth, mainly in General Liability, as well as continued organic growth in existing programs and growth in A&H. These increases were partially offset by decreases in our Reinsurance segment, mainly in Casualty, Property Catastrophe, and Other Specialties. The Professional Liability and A&H premium estimate changes were largely offset by corresponding loss and acquisition costs, with no significant impact on underwriting income. The decrease in net written premium was primarily driven by the decreases in our Reinsurance segment and the ceded premium related to the inception of an aggregate reinsurance program in 2026.
Core Underwriting Results
Core results for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 included income of $232.0 million compared to $203.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 consists of underwriting income of $201.8 million (90.1% combined ratio) and net services income of $30.2 million, compared to underwriting income of $165.7 million (91.4% combined ratio) and net services income of $37.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The improvement in net underwriting results was primarily driven by decreased catastrophe losses, partially offset by higher acquisition costs and other underwriting expense. Catastrophe losses were $6.7 million, or 0.3 percentage points on the combined ratio, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $67.4 million, or 3.5 percentage points on the combined ratio, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by the California wildfires in the prior period. Increased acquisition costs primarily resulted from profit commission accruals related to favorable loss experience and increased other underwriting expense is largely driven by expenses related to incentive compensation award outperformance.

Insurance & Services Segment
Three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025
Insurance & Services gross written premium were $673.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $111.0 million, or 19.8%, compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by growth in Surety business as well as an increase in premium estimate on Professional Liability business, slightly offset by the exit of an International A&H program. The Professional Liability premium estimate change was largely offset by corresponding loss and acquisition costs, with no significant impact on underwriting income.
Insurance & Services generated income of $44.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $47.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 consists of underwriting income of $33.0 million (92.8% combined ratio) and net services income of $11.9 million, compared to underwriting income of $37.7 million (90.1% combined ratio) and net services income of $10.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease in underwriting income was primarily a result of higher acquisition and other underwriting expense ratios, slightly offset by lower attritional loss ratios.
Nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025
Insurance & Services gross written premium were $2,002.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $244.7 million, or 13.9%, for the nine months September 30, 2026 compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by an increase in premium estimate on Professional Liability business, Surety and new program growth, mainly in General Liability, as well as continued organic growth in existing programs and growth in North America A&H. The Professional Liability premium estimate change was largely offset by corresponding loss and acquisition costs, with no significant impact on underwriting income.
Insurance & Services generated income of $128.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $135.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 consists of underwriting income of $98.5 million (92.0% combined ratio) and net services income of $30.2 million, compared to underwriting income of $97.3 million (91.1% combined ratio) and net services income of $37.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The improvement in underwriting income was primarily driven by increased favorable prior year loss reserve development, partially offset by increased expenses. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, favorable prior year loss reserve development was $40.3 million compared to $21.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by favorable development in A&H. Increased acquisition costs primarily resulted from profit commission accruals related to favorable loss experience and increased other underwriting expense is largely driven by expenses related to incentive compensation award outperformance.
Reinsurance Segment
Three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025
Reinsurance gross written premium were $317.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $8.0 million, or 2.6%, compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by increases in certain Other Specialties lines, specifically Property and Marine & Energy.
Reinsurance generated underwriting income of $42.9 million (86.0% combined ratio) for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $31.9 million (87.9% combined ratio) for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in underwriting income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025 was primarily driven by increased favorable prior year reserve development in Property and Energy.
Nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025
Reinsurance gross written premium were $973.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, a decrease of $60.4 million, or 5.8%, compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025, primarily driven by deliberate reductions in Casualty, rate and exposure reductions in Property Catastrophe, and reductions in Other Specialties.
Reinsurance generated underwriting income of $103.3 million (87.4% combined ratio) for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $68.4 million (91.7% combined ratio) for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in underwriting income was primarily driven by decreased catastrophe losses, as the nine months ended September 30, 2025 included losses of $62.6 million, or 7.6 percentage points on the combined ratio, primarily from the California wildfires. This was partially offset by lower favorable prior year loss reserve development of $26.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $35.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, mainly from reserve releases in Property relating to prior year’s catastrophe events.

Investments
Three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025
Net investment income decreased to $65.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $66.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025 driven by lower yields on cash and short-term investments, as well as portfolio repositioning. Net investment gains (losses) decreased to $3.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $6.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily related to realized losses on sales of TPOC and other AFS debt securities, slightly offset by higher gains on sales of other long-term investments.
Nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025
Net investment income decreased to $197.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $205.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 driven by lower yields on cash and short-term investments, as well as portfolio repositioning. Net investment gains (losses) increased to $22.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $6.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 primarily due to gains from fair value changes in the Company's investments managed by related parties, which are included in Other long-term investments, partially offset by the reversal of inception to date unrealized gains.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Core Results
Collectively, the sum of the Company's two segments, Insurance & Services and Reinsurance, constitute "Core" results. Core underwriting income, Core net services income, Core income and Core combined ratio are non-GAAP financial measures. We believe it is useful to review Core results as they reflect how management views the business and reflects our decision to exit the run-off business. The sum of Core results and Corporate results are equal to the consolidated results of operations.
Core underwriting income - calculated by subtracting loss and loss adjustment expenses incurred, net, acquisition costs, net, and other underwriting expenses from net premiums earned.
Core net services income - consists of services revenues which include commissions, brokerage and fee income related to consolidated MGAs, and other revenues, as well as services expenses which include direct expenses related to consolidated MGAs and services noncontrolling income which represent minority ownership interests in consolidated MGAs. Net services income is a key indicator of the profitability of the Company's services provided.
Core income - consists of two components, core underwriting income and core net services income. Core income is a key measure of our segment performance.
Core combined ratio - calculated by dividing the sum of Core loss and loss adjustment expenses incurred, net, acquisition costs, net and other underwriting expenses by Core net premiums earned. Accident year loss ratio and accident year combined ratio are calculated by excluding prior year loss reserve development to present the impact of current accident year net loss and loss adjustment expenses on the Core loss ratio and Core combined ratio, respectively. Attritional loss ratio excludes catastrophe losses from the accident year loss ratio as they are not predictable as to timing and amount. These ratios are useful indicators of our underwriting profitability.
