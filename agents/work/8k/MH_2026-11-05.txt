McGraw Hill, Inc. Reports Fiscal Second Quarter 2027 Results
Advances Agentic AI in Education while Driving Digital Revenue Growth
Exceeded Revenue Expectations with Momentum Building for FY 2028 Growth Acceleration
COLUMBUS, Ohio— November 5, 2026—(BUSINESS WIRE)— McGraw Hill, Inc. (NYSE: MH) (“McGraw Hill” or the “Company”), a leading global provider of education solutions for preK-12, higher education and professional learning, today announced financial results for the fiscal second quarter 2027 ended September 30, 2026.
“McGraw Hill's first half fiscal year 2027 performance underscores our continued higher education market share gains, scale and deep position of trust serving over 100 million active curriculum licenses across the global education ecosystem,” said Philip Moyer, President, Chief Executive Officer of the Company and a member of the Company’s Board of Directors. “McGraw Hill has established an early-mover advantage as one of the first education companies to advance an agentic AI strategy to connect our trusted proprietary educational content directly to Microsoft Copilot, Anthropic’s Claude for Teachers and leading academic institutions, unlocking the next generation of learning and expanding our addressable market.”
Fiscal Second Quarter 2027 Key Financial Highlights
•Total revenue of $632.2 million, a decline of 5.5% year-over-year, favorable to expectations, as market share gains and strong capture rates across most markets partially offset anticipated lower capture rates in select math markets; year-to-date revenue decline of 1.9% year-over-year, with leading indicators gaining momentum.
•Re-occurring revenue of $414.1 million, a decline of 2.0% year-over-year, reached 65.5% of total revenue; year-to-date re-occurring revenue growth of 3.7% year-over-year underscoring subscription model durability.
•Digital revenue of $361.4 million, an increase of 2.6% year-over-year; year-to-date growth of 5.6% driven by strong retention, robust learning platform adoption and interactions across AI learning tools embedded in our solutions such AI Reader in Connect.
•Remaining performance obligation (RPO) of $1,790.5 million, demonstrating strong revenue predictability and future growth visibility.
•GAAP gross profit of $500.5 million, representing a GAAP gross profit margin of 79.2%, consistent with prior year.
•GAAP net income of $86.4 million, compared to $105.3 million in the prior-year period; year-to-date GAAP net income of $144.2 million, compared to $105.8 million in the prior-year period.
•Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $250.6 million, representing an Adjusted EBITDA margin(1) of 39.6%, which supports the ongoing execution focus with confidence for fiscal year margin expansion.
•Continued commitment to gross debt reduction, with $50 million term loan paydown in the quarter, bringing total gross debt reduction to approximately $700 million since our initial public offering with net leverage tracking toward the 2.0–2.5x net leverage target; proactively extended near-dated maturities subsequent to quarter end.
•Credit rating upgrades from S&P Global Ratings and Moody's Ratings in the quarter provide independent, external validations of the Company's financial trajectory.
“Our second quarter results demonstrated digital revenue growth, a strong margin profile and commitment to gross debt reduction. Our first-half performance, including robust re-occurring revenue growth, combined with strengthening indicators of our ability to deliver accelerating growth in fiscal year 2028, reinforces our confidence in our long-term strategy,” said Bob Sallmann, the Company’s Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer. “Our financial profile, scale, innovation and service remain differentiators in the global education market as we focus on the growth opportunities ahead of us.”
Fiscal Second Quarter 2027 Strategic Highlights
•Grew active AI users double-digits across nine AI learning tools embedded in our solutions, with additional AI tool launches planned for the remainder of the fiscal year.
1
•Reached over 80 million AI Reader learning interactions across approximately 3.4 million students since inception through October 2026, including 62 disciplines and 715 titles, a meaningful acceleration from 47 million learning interactions and 2.2 million students in fiscal year 2026.
•Became one of the first major education companies to integrate proprietary content and curriculum into leading Microsoft Copilot and Anthropic’s Claude for Teachers via Model Context Protocol, advancing agentic AI in education and expanding reach across education markets.
•Advanced agentic AI strategy with a Model Context Protocol integration with the University of California, San Francisco School of Medicine, delivering precision medical learning at institutional scale, while also doubling the number of ongoing institutional agentic AI pilots.
•Expanded K-12 AI capabilities through the acquisitions of TeachFX and Teachally, adding AI-enabled teacher coaching and curriculum customization solutions designed to strengthen educator effectiveness and expand McGraw Hill’s reach across classroom and professional learning markets.
•Expanded the Evergreen content delivery model in Higher Education with direct updates to over 800 titles, fueling stronger retention and market share gains.
Leadership Updates
•Robin Lucas has been appointed President of McGraw Hill's Higher Education business, effective October 19, 2026. Previously Chief Sales Officer of McGraw Hill’s Higher Education business, Ms. Lucas succeeds Michael Ryan, who will be retiring before year-end. With 25 years in education sales and marketing leadership roles and a proven record of driving market share gains, Ms. Lucas is well-positioned to lead Higher Education's next phase of global growth. She joined McGraw Hill's Higher Education marketing team in 2015.
•Jana Thompson has been appointed President of McGraw Hill's K-12 business, effective October 1, 2026. Previously serving as Interim President and Chief Product Officer of McGraw Hill’s School group. Ms. Thompson has over 25 years of education technology experience. She has been with McGraw Hill for 13 years in various technology and product leadership roles, leading some of the biggest product launches in the company’s history and helping shape the personalized, data-driven learning experiences redefining K-12 education.
“As we increasingly align our organization to capture expanding growth drivers, I am thrilled to appoint Robin Lucas and Jana Thompson to lead our Higher Education and K-12 businesses,” said Philip Moyer, President, Chief Executive Officer of the Company and a member of the Company’s Board of Directors. “With decades of sector experience and proven internal track records as former heads of sales and product, respectively, their leadership ensures seamless continuity, a more unified global commercial vision and the execution speed needed to shape the future of education. I also want to thank Michael Ryan for his 26 years of service to McGraw Hill, including seven years leading our Higher Education business. His leadership has been instrumental in strengthening our customer relationships and positioning the business for continued growth.”

Fiscal Second Quarter 2027 Segment Highlights
Higher Education
•Revenue totaled $213.5 million, an increase of 0.2% year-over-year, notwithstanding a strong prior-year comparison; year-to-date growth of 4.5% versus prior year reflects continued market share gains, value-based pricing momentum, and sustained Higher Education market leadership.
•Re-occurring revenue totaled $170.6 million, an increase of 5.5% year-over-year; year-to-date growth of 9.8% driven by continued Inclusive Access momentum and deepening Evergreen content delivery penetration.
•Continued Higher Education market share gains, including ~5 points of market share gained over the past four fiscal years from competitors, according to MPI.
•Evergreen adoption driving a 35% reduction in sales touchpoints and 20% higher competitive win rates while NPS scores remain at record high levels.
K-12
•Revenue totaled $324.6 million, down 9.6% year-over-year, as capture rate strength in Science and ELA was offset by lower anticipated capture rates in select Math markets; first half revenue of nearly $600 million, down 4.9% year-over year.
•Re-occurring revenue totaled nearly $200.0 million, down 7.8% year-over-year; year-to-date re-occurring revenue relatively flat versus prior year, reflecting the predictability of our business.
•Emerge, Summit, and Soar continue to exceed early capture rate expectations, with leading indicators strengthening in open territory markets, ahead of the multi-year ELA super-cycle beginning in fiscal year 2028.
•Strong California Math feedback, with four times the number of pilots versus prior year, ahead of year two of the fiscal year 2028 adoption.
Global Professional and International
•Global Professional delivered 5.5% re-occurring revenue growth year-over-year driven by 7% medical portfolio growth, with structural tailwinds and agentic integrations poised to accelerate momentum.
•International revenue increased 1.6% year-over-year, with delayed Middle East shipments being fulfilled.

EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin
“EBITDA” is defined as net income (loss) from continuing operations plus interest expense (income), net, income tax provision (benefit), depreciation and amortization.
“Adjusted EBITDA” is defined as net income (loss) from continuing operations plus interest expense (income), net, income tax provision (benefit), depreciation and amortization, restructuring and cost savings implementation charges, the effects of the application of purchase accounting, advisory fees paid to Platinum Advisors pursuant to the Advisory Agreement (which was terminated on July 25, 2025 in connection with the consummation of our initial public offering), impairment charges, transaction and integration costs, stock-based compensation, (gain) loss on extinguishment of debt and the impact of earnings or charges resulting from matters that we do not consider indicative of our ongoing operations.
Further, although not included in the calculation of Adjusted EBITDA below, we may at times add estimated cost savings and operating synergies related to operational changes ranging from acquisitions or dispositions to restructurings, and exclude one-time transition expenditures.
“Adjusted EBITDA Margin” is calculated by dividing Adjusted EBITDA by total revenue.

LTM is defined as the twelve-month period ended on the last day of the most recently completed fiscal quarter and is calculated by adding the results for the six months ended September 30, 2026, to the results of the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, and subtracting the six months ended September 30, 2025.
