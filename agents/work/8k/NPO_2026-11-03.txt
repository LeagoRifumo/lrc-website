Enpro Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results; Raises Full-Year Guidance
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
(All results reflect comparisons to prior-year period, unless otherwise noted)
•Sales of $350.8 million, up 22.4%
•AST sales up 25.8%; Sealing Technologies sales up 20.5%
•Net income of $40.3 million versus $21.6 million
•Adjusted EBITDA* of $91.3 million versus $69.3 million
•GAAP diluted earnings per share of $1.88 versus $1.01
•Adjusted diluted earnings per share* of $2.66 versus $1.99
•Raising full-year 2026 guidance: Revenue expected to be in the range of $1.33 billion to $1.34 billion, adjusted EBITDA* in the range of $340 million to $345 million and adjusted diluted earnings per share* in the range of $9.70 to $9.95
CHARLOTTE, N.C., November 3, 2026 -- Enpro Inc. (NYSE: NPO) today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Our strong third quarter performance reflects our disciplined execution and success of Enpro 3.0 as we continue to accelerate profitable growth across the organization," said Eric Vaillancourt, President and Chief Executive Officer. Increased semiconductor industry demand drove AST sales up nearly 26%, while Sealing Technologies delivered nearly 12% organic sales growth — and more than 20% including acquisitions. We are raising our outlook for the balance of the year to reflect robust demand across the portfolio and our strong momentum as we head into 2027."
"We remain focused on reinvesting in attractive organic growth opportunities across both segments to deliver on our Enpro 3.0 growth targets. Strong cash generation and a healthy balance sheet provide us with significant flexibility to invest in areas of the portfolio with higher growth rates and superior profitability and pursue strategic acquisitions that expand our capabilities and align with our disciplined financial criteria," Mr. Vaillancourt continued.

Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Results
Sales of $350.8 million increased 22.4% compared to last year. Excluding foreign exchange translation and contributions from the AlpHa Measurement Solutions and Overlook Industries acquisitions completed in the fourth quarter of 2025, sales increased 16.9%. Improved demand across AST for our semiconductor products and solutions, strength in aerospace, domestic general industrial and compositional analysis applications, as well as strategic pricing initiatives more than offset slow European general industrial and food and biopharmaceutical demand. Nuclear and commercial vehicle OEM revenue improved during the third quarter compared to last year.
Corporate expense of $13.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased from $10.2 million last year primarily due to higher incentive compensation accruals and restructuring costs.
Net income of $40.3 million increased 86.6% compared to $21.6 million last year. Diluted earnings per share were $1.88, compared to $1.01 in the prior year. Operating leverage from strong sales growth was partially offset by increased expenses supporting growth initiatives across both segments.
Adjusted net income* of $57.0 million increased 34.4% compared to the third quarter of 2025 and adjusted diluted earnings per share* increased 33.7% to $2.66, versus $1.99 last year.
Adjusted EBITDA* of $91.3 million, or 26.0% of total sales, increased 31.7% year-over-year. Higher sales drove the increase, offset in part by increased operating expenses supporting growth initiatives.

Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and Capital Allocation
During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the company generated $172.8 million of cash flow from operating activities and $129.2 million of free cash flow, net of $43.6 million in capital expenditures. This compares to $138.5 million of cash flow from operating activities, or $104.9 million of free cash flow, net of $33.6 million in capital expenditures last year. Higher net income and lower cash taxes were the primary drivers of the strong increase in free cash flow, which also includes investments in working capital supporting strong demand and higher capital expenditures supporting growth initiatives.
During the third quarter, the company paid a regular quarterly dividend of $0.32 per share, with dividend payments totaling $20.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Enpro ended the third quarter with total debt of $520.7 million and cash and cash equivalents of $83.4 million and reduced outstanding revolving debt by $55.0 million during the third quarter, and $135.0 million year-to-date, resulting in a net leverage ratio of 1.4x to trailing twelve month adjusted EBITDA.
Share Repurchase Authorization
Enpro’s Board of Directors approved a new share repurchase authorization in October 2026, replacing the previous $50 million authorization that recently expired. No shares were purchased under the prior repurchase program. Under the replacement authorization, which is identical to the prior authorization, the company may repurchase up to $50 million of shares in both open market and privately negotiated transactions. The company’s management is authorized to determine the timing and amount of any such repurchases based on its evaluation of market conditions, capital alternatives, and other factors. Repurchases may also be made under Rule 10b5-1 plans, which permit the company to repurchase shares when it otherwise would be precluded from doing so under insider trading laws. The renewed share repurchase authorization expires in October 2028.

2026 Guidance Increase
Enpro is raising guidance for full-year 2026 and now expects revenue to be in the range of $1.33 billion to $1.34 billion, or a 16% to 17% increase year over year, adjusted EBITDA* in the range of $340 million to $345 million and adjusted diluted earnings per share* in the range of $9.70 to $9.95.
This compares to the prior guidance of revenue growth of 14%-16%, adjusted EBITDA* in the range of $330 million to $340 million and adjusted diluted earnings per share* in the range of $9.30 to $9.80 per share.

Segment Operating Performance Measure
The segment profitability metric used by management to allocate resources and assess segment performance is adjusted segment EBITDA, which is segment revenue reduced by operating expenses and other costs identifiable with the segment, excluding acquisition and divestiture expenses, restructuring costs, impairment charges, non-controlling interest compensation, amortization of the fair value adjustment to acquisition date inventory, and depreciation and amortization. Segment non-operating expenses and income, corporate expenses, net interest expense, and income taxes are not included in the computation of adjusted segment EBITDA. Under U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), the segment profitability metric used by management to allocate resources and assess segment performance is required to be disclosed in financial statement footnotes, and accordingly such metric as presented for each segment is not deemed to be a non-GAAP measure under applicable regulations of the Securities and Exchange Commission.
