CALL FOR AN ORDINARY GENERAL SHAREHOLDERS’ MEETING OF TELECOM ARGENTINA S.A.
The Shareholders of Telecom Argentina S.A. (“Telecom Argentina” or “the Company”) are summoned to an Ordinary General Shareholders’ Meeting to be held on December 15, 2026, at 11:00 a.m. Buenos Aires time on the first call, and at 12:00 p.m. Buenos Aires time on the second call, by personal attendance at the corporate offices at General Hornos No. 690, Ground Floor, City of Buenos Aires, in order to consider the following items:
AGENDA
1) Appointment of two shareholders to sign the Minutes of the Meeting.
2) Appointment of a regular director to replace resigning director Diego Miguel Bianchi, to serve from the date of this Shareholders’ Meeting until the end of fiscal year 2026.
3) Appointment of a regular director to replace resigning director Luca Luciani, to serve from the date of this Shareholders’ Meeting until the end of fiscal year 2026.
4) Consideration of the performance of resigning regular directors Diego Miguel Bianchi and Luca Luciani.
THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
Note 1: Within the regulatory deadline, the documents related to the Shareholders’ Meeting may be consulted on the Comisión Nacional de Valores’ website and on Telecom Argentina’s website: www.telecom.com.ar.
Note 2: Pursuant to Section 22 of Chapter II, Title II of the CNV Rules, at the time of registration to participate in the Shareholders’ Meeting and upon actual attendance thereat, shareholders must provide all required information concerning the holders of the shares and their representatives (full name or full corporate name; type and number of identity document or registration details, expressly identifying the applicable registry and its jurisdiction; and address, indicating its capacity. The same information must also be provided with respect to the representative(s) of the holder of the shares who will attend the Shareholders’ Meeting). Legal entities or other legal structures must provide the information and submit the documentation required under Sections 24, 25 and 26 of Chapter II, Title II of the CNV Rules.
Note 3: Persons registering to participate in the Shareholders’ Meeting as custodians or administrators of third-party shareholdings are reminded that they must comply with the requirements of Section 9, Chapter II, Title II of the CNV Rules in order to cast votes in different directions.
Note 4: To attend the Shareholders’ Meeting, holders of Class B and Class C shares must deposit the book-entry shareholding certificates issued for such purpose by Caja de Valores S.A. no later than three business days prior to the date set for the Shareholders’ Meeting, at General Hornos 690, 8th Floor, City of Buenos Aires, from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. and from 3:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m., or notify their attendance by email to AsuntosSocietarios@teco.com.ar, attaching the corresponding shareholding certificate, within the same period and no later than 5:00 p.m. Holders of Class A and Class D book-entry shares must notify their attendance in the same manner and within the same period. The deadline is December 9, 2026, at 5:00 p.m.
Note 5: Shareholders are requested to arrive at least 15 minutes prior to the scheduled commencement time of the Shareholders’ Meeting.
PROPOSALS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS TO THE ORDINARY GENERAL SHAREHOLDERS’ MEETING
Proposal for the First Item on the Agenda:
The proposal to the Shareholders’ Meeting is that “the shareholders propose the persons who will sign the Meeting Minutes.”
Proposal for the Second and Third Items on the Agenda:
The Board of Directors abstains from making a proposal with respect to these items. Shareholders proposing the appointment of regular directors are reminded of the need to inform the Shareholders’ Meeting whether the nominees qualify as “independent” or “non-independent” directors under the rules of the Argentine Securities Commission (Comisión Nacional de Valores).
Proposal for the Fourth Item on the Agenda:
No proposal is made with respect to this item, leaving the performance of the resigning regular directors for consideration by the Shareholders’ Meeting.
