PagSeguro Digital Ltd. Announces Cash Dividend
São Paulo, November 16, 2026 – PAGSEGURO DIGITAL LTD., or the Company (NYSE: PAGS), hereby announces that its Board of Directors has approved the payment of a cash dividend of US$0.26 per common share of the Company, expected to be paid on December 30, 2026 to shareholders of record as of December 14, 2026. Any future declaration of dividends and the amount thereof will be at the discretion of the Company’s Board of Directors.
