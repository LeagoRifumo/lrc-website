Trinity Capital Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Return on Average Equity climbs to 15.5% in Q3
Quarterly Net Investment Income reaches a record $49.9 million, up 34.9% year-over-year
Net Asset Value per share increases to $13.61
PHOENIX, November 4, 2026 – Trinity Capital Inc. (NYSE: TRIN) (“the Company”), a leading international alternative asset manager, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•
Total investment income of $98.4 million, an increase of 30.2% year-over-year.
•
Net investment income (“NII”) of $49.9 million, or $0.52 per basic share. NII grew 34.9% year-over-year.
•
Net increase in net assets resulting from operations of $46.9 million, or $0.49 per basic share.
•
15.5% Return on Average Equity “ROAE” (NII/Average Equity).
•
7.0% Return on Average Assets “ROAA” (NII/Average Assets).
•
Net Asset Value (“NAV”) of $1.33 billion, or $13.61 per share at the end of Q3.
•
Total gross investment commitments of $880.5 million.
•
Total gross investments funded of $614.1 million, which was comprised of $431.9 million to 15 new portfolio companies, $117.7 million to 24 existing portfolio companies and $64.5 million to multi-sector holdings.
•
Total gross investment exits and repayments of $494.8 million, which was comprised of $237.2 million from investments sold, $194.3 million from early debt repayments and refinancings, $57.3 million from scheduled/amortizing debt payments and $6.0 million from warrant and equity sales.
•
The Company announced $0.17 monthly distributions for each of October, November and December 2026, totaling $0.51 for the fourth quarter and marking the 28th consecutive quarter of a consistent regular dividend.
“The third quarter was another step in building a platform designed to perform across market environments,” said Kyle Brown, Trinity Capital CEO. “We funded $614 million, grew platform AUM to $3.4 billion, and continued to expand our managed funds offering, enhancing returns for TRIN beyond the income generated by our BDC portfolio. We like the foundation we’ve built, and we’re excited about what’s next for TRIN and our shareholders.”
Third Quarter 2026 Operating Results
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, total investment income was $98.4 million, compared to $75.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The effective yield on the average debt investments at cost was 15.2% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 15.0% for the third quarter of 2025. Effective yields generally include the effects of fees and income accelerations attributed to early loan repayments and other one-time events, and may also fluctuate quarter-to-quarter depending on the amount of prepayment activity.
Total operating expenses and excise taxes, excluding interest expense, for the third quarter of 2026 were $20.2 million, compared to $17.6 million during the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to higher compensation costs partially offset by higher allocated expenses to the Company’s registered investment adviser subsidiary.
Interest expense for the third quarter of 2026 was $28.3 million, compared to $21.0 million during the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to the increase in weighted average debt outstanding.
Net investment income was approximately $49.9 million, or $0.52 per share based on 95.1 million basic weighted average shares outstanding for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $37.0 million or $0.52 per share for the third quarter of 2025 based on 71.5 million basic weighted average shares outstanding.
During the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company’s net unrealized appreciation totaled approximately $4.5 million, which included net unrealized appreciation of $4.8 million from its equity investments, net unrealized appreciation of $0.3 million from its debt investments, partially offset by net unrealized depreciation of $0.7 million from its warrant investments. Additionally, there was $0.1 million net unrealized appreciation attributable to foreign currency forward contracts.
Net realized loss on investments was approximately $7.5 million, primarily due to the realized losses of two loan positions and one equipment financing position, partially offset by the realized gain of one warrant position.
Net increase in net assets resulting from operations was $46.9 million, or $0.49 per share, based on 95.1 million basic weighted average shares outstanding. This compares to a net increase in net assets resulting from operations of $27.6 million, or $0.39 per share, based on 71.5 million basic weighted average shares outstanding for the third quarter of 2025.
Net Asset Value
Total net assets at the end of the third quarter of 2026 increased by 4.6% to $1.33 billion, compared to $1.27 billion at the end of the second quarter of 2026. The increase in total net assets was primarily due to accretive ATM issuances, partially offset by net portfolio performance. NAV per share increased to $13.61 per share in the third quarter from $13.47 per share as of June 30, 2026.
Portfolio and Investment Activity
As of September 30, 2026, the Company's investment portfolio had an aggregate fair value of approximately $2.9 billion and was comprised of approximately $2.0 billion in secured loans, $470.7 million in equipment financings, and $369.5 million in equity and warrants, across 198 portfolio companies. The Company’s debt portfolio is comprised of 89.2% first-lien loans and 10.8% second-lien loans, with 78.4% of the debt portfolio at floating rates based on principal outstanding.
During the third quarter, the Company originated approximately $880.5 million of total new commitments. Third quarter gross investments funded totaled approximately $614.1 million, which was comprised of $431.9 million of investments in 15 new portfolio companies, $117.7 million of investments in 24 existing portfolio companies and $64.5 million to multi-sector holdings. Gross investment fundings during the quarter for secured loans totaled $377.9 million, equipment financings totaled $159.1 million and warrant and equity investments totaled $77.1 million.

Gross proceeds received from exits and repayments of the Company’s investments during the third quarter totaled approximately $494.8 million, which included $237.2 million from investments sold, $194.3 million from early debt repayments and refinancings, $57.3 million from scheduled/amortizing debt payments and $6.0 million from warrant and equity sales. The investment portfolio increased by $121.1 million on a cost basis, an increase of 4.5%, and $125.4 million on a fair value basis, an increase of 4.6%, each as compared to June 30, 2026.
As of the end of the third quarter, loans to three portfolio companies were on non-accrual status with a total fair value of approximately $17.2 million, or 0.7% of the Company’s debt investment portfolio at fair value.

Liquidity and Capital Resources
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had approximately $225.0 million in available liquidity, including $20.7 million in unrestricted cash and cash equivalents. About Trinity Capital Inc.
Trinity Capital Inc. (NYSE: TRIN) is an international alternative asset manager that seeks to deliver consistent returns for investors through access to private credit markets. Trinity Capital sources and structures investments in well-capitalized growth-oriented companies. With five distinct business verticals — Sponsor Finance, Equipment Finance, Tech Lending, Asset-Based Lending, and Healthcare & Life Sciences — Trinity Capital stands as a long-term trusted partner for innovative companies seeking tailored debt solutions. Headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona, Trinity Capital's dedicated team is strategically located across the United States and in Europe. For more information on Trinity Capital, please visit trinitycapital.com and stay connected to the latest activity via LinkedIn.
