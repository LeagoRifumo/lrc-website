Definium Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Recent Highlights
Reported positive topline Phase 3 results from two Phase 3 studies of DT120 ODT in generalized anxiety disorder (GAD)
Received FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation for DT120 ODT in major depressive disorder (MDD)
On track for DT120 ODT NDA filing in first half of 2027
DT402 demonstrated positive initial results in a Phase 2a trial in adults with autism spectrum disorder (ASD); clinical study evaluating multiple dosing paradigms planned for 2027
Approximately $1.05 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and investments as of September 30, 2026
Conference call scheduled today at 4:30 p.m. EST
NEW YORK, November 9, 2026 -- Definium Therapeutics, Inc. (“Definium” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: DFTX), a late-stage clinical biopharmaceutical company developing a new generation of therapeutics intended to address underlying causes of psychiatric and neurological disorders, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and recent highlights. The Company also reported positive initial results from its Phase 2a trial of DT402 in adults with ASD, with improvements observed across several measures of socialization, communication and other functional outcomes.
“Our positive Phase 3 topline results from Voyage and Panorama this quarter further strengthened the evidence supporting DT120 ODT. Together with our previously reported results, these findings demonstrate consistent, clinically meaningful results across three Phase 3 studies in GAD and MDD, reinforcing our confidence in DT120 ODT’s potential to address significant unmet needs across both conditions,” said Rob Barrow, Chief Executive Officer. “With Breakthrough Therapy designation in MDD and Ascend progressing, we continue to advance our late-stage program while building the capabilities needed to prepare for potential approval and commercialization. Beyond DT120, we believe DT402 represents an exciting opportunity and its initial results support continued development as we work to deliver differentiated therapies for patients with significant unmet needs.”
Third Quarter and Recent Highlights
▪
DT120 ODT in GAD: Reported positive topline results from Phase 3 Voyage study in GAD with 214 participants randomized 1:1 to receive DT120 ODT 100 µg or placebo. Key findings included:
o
Met the primary endpoint and all key secondary efficacy endpoints with a high degree of statistical significance.
o
Demonstrated a statistically significant and clinically meaningful placebo-adjusted improvement of 5.4 points in Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A) total score from baseline at Week 12 (p<0.0001, Cohen’s d=0.81), the study’s primary endpoint.
o
DT120 ODT was generally well tolerated, with no drug-related serious adverse events and no suicidality signal observed in Part A.
▪
DT120 ODT in GAD: Reported positive topline results from Phase 3 Panorama study in GAD with 245 participants randomized 2:1:2 to receive DT120 ODT 100 µg, DT120 ODT 50 µg control, or placebo. Key findings included:
o
Met the primary endpoint and all key secondary efficacy endpoints with a high degree of statistical significance.

DT120 ODT in MDD:
o
Received Breakthrough Therapy designation for DT120 ODT in MDD following positive topline results from Phase 3 Emerge study, which met the primary endpoint and all key secondary efficacy endpoints with a high degree of statistical significance.
o
Continuing enrollment for the Phase 3 Ascend study in MDD. The trial is expected to enroll approximately 165 participants randomized 2:1:2 to receive DT120 ODT 100 µg, DT120 ODT 50 µg control, or placebo. Topline data is expected in 2027.
▪
DT120 ODT in PTSD: Planning to initiate the Phase 3 Haven study in PTSD in 2027 with approximately 200 participants randomized 1:1 to receive DT120 ODT 100 µg or placebo. The planned primary endpoint in the study is the Clinician-Administered PTSD Scale for DSM-5 (CAPS-5) at Week 8.
▪
DT402 in ASD: The Company’s Phase 2a trial of DT402 in five adults with ASD demonstrated positive initial results. The 200 mg single-dose, single arm, open-label study was designed to characterize the pharmacodynamic and clinical effects of DT402 and explore changes across measures of socialization, communication and other functional outcomes. The study endpoints were considered exploratory and were not powered to draw statistical conclusions. Following treatment, improvements were observed across several measures, including reduced rigidity and fixation and increased engagement in social settings, as well as increases in empathy and closeness to others and reductions in anxiety and irritability. DT402 was well tolerated, with the most common adverse events being euphoria, fatigue and enhanced relaxation. Based on these initial findings, the Company plans to further evaluate DT402 in a study that will investigate the safety and clinical activity of multiple dosing paradigms expected to begin in 2027.
▪
Scientific engagement: Presented clinical and scientific data at several major medical meetings, including Psych Congress 2026, the 39th ECNP Congress and the 2026 NEI Fall Congress. Presentations included topline results from the Phase 3 Emerge, Voyage and Panorama studies of DT120 ODT, an overview of the DT120 ODT Phase 3 safety data, and the DT120 ODT Phase 3 development program. At Psych Congress, the Emerge and Voyage posters were named finalists, with Voyage receiving the Top Poster award and Emerge selected for a podium presentation during the Latest Discoveries & Emerging Trends in MDD session.
▪
Commercial readiness and organizational growth: Advancing commercial readiness activities and expanding organizational capabilities to support the Company’s transition toward commercialization. The Company is also planning its first Commercial Day in the first half of 2027 to highlight the market opportunity for DT120 ODT and provide greater detail on its commercialization strategy and launch preparations.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Cash, Cash Equivalents and Investments. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash, cash equivalents and investments of approximately $1.05 billion compared to $411.6 million as of December 31, 2025. Based on the Company’s current operating plan and anticipated milestones, the Company believes that its cash, cash equivalents and investments as of September 30, 2026 will be sufficient to fund the Company’s operations into 2030.

Research and Development (R&D). R&D expenses were $46.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $31.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, an increase of $15.1 million. The increase was primarily attributable to an $8.8 million increase in personnel-related costs associated with the expansion of the Company’s research and development organization, a $5.9 million increase in expenses related to the DT120 development program, and a $0.6 million increase in preclinical and other program expenses, partially offset by a $0.2 million decrease in DT402 program expenses.
General and Administrative (G&A). G&A expenses were $26.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $14.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, an increase of $11.4 million. The increase was primarily due to a $3.5 million increase in corporate and government affairs expenses, a $3.4 million increase in commercial-preparedness related expenses, a $3.3 million increase in personnel-related expenses as the Company expanded its organizational capabilities, a $0.7 million increase in legal and patent expenses, a $0.4 million increase in other miscellaneous administrative expenses, and a $0.1 million increase in stock-based compensation expenses.
Net loss. Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $51.0 million, compared to $67.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease in net loss was primarily attributable to a gain from the change in fair value of the Company’s 2022 USD financing warrants and higher interest income, partially offset by increased operating expenses.

About DT120 Orally Disintegrating Tablet (ODT)
DT120 ODT is an ergoline derivative belonging to the group of classic serotonergic psychedelics, which acts as a partial agonist at 5-hydroxytryptamine serotonin-2A (5-HT2A) receptors. DT120 ODT is Definium's proprietary and pharmaceutically optimized formulation of Lysergide (LSD). DT120 ODT is an advanced formulation incorporating Catalent's Zydis® ODT fast-dissolve technology, designed to deliver several unique advantages, including faster absorption and onset of transient cognitive, perceptual, and affective changes, improved bioavailability, and a lower incidence of gastrointestinal side effects. Definium is developing DT120 ODT, the tartrate salt form of lysergide, for generalized anxiety disorder (GAD), major depressive disorder (MDD), and posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD), and is exploring its potential applications in other serious brain health disorders. DT120 has received Breakthrough Therapy designation from the FDA for GAD and MDD. Definium maintains a strong foundation to protect and extend the long-term value of the DT120 ODT franchise through a multi-layered intellectual property strategy spanning composition, formulation, and methods-of-use patents.
About DT402
DT402 is the Company’s proprietary form of R(-)-MDMA (rectus-3,4 methylenedioxymethamphetamine), being developed for the treatment of core symptoms of autism spectrum disorder (ASD). MDMA is a synthetic molecule that is often referred to as an empathogen because it is reported to increase feelings of connectedness and compassion. Preclinical and early-stage clinical studies of R(-)-MDMA demonstrate its acute pro-social and empathogenic effects, while its diminished dopaminergic activity suggest that it has the potential to exhibit less stimulant activity, neurotoxicity, hyperthermia and abuse liability compared to racemic MDMA or the S(+)-enantiomer.
