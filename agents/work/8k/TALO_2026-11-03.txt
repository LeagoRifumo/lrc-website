Talos Energy Announces Third Quarter 2026 Operational and Financial Results
Houston, Texas, November 3, 2026 – Talos Energy Inc. (“Talos” or the “Company”) (NYSE: TALO) today announced its operational and financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Talos also provided revised full-year 2026 guidance.
Third Quarter Operational and Financial Highlights
•
Production of 68.5 thousand barrels of oil per day (“MBo/d”) and 91.5 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day (“MBoe/d”) exceeded guidance, driven by strong base business performance, no storm downtime and contributions from the completed acquisition.
•
Reported net cash provided by operating activities of $325.8 million.
•
Generated Adjusted Free Cash Flow(1)(2) of $186.3 million.
•
Premium oil realizations of ~$1.60 above WTI supported peer-leading Adjusted EBITDA margins of ~$52/BOE.
•
Repurchased 1.4 million shares for $23.6 million; returned $61.8 million to shareholders through the first three quarters of 2026.
•
Recorded Net Loss(2) of $81.2 million or $0.49 Net Loss(2) per diluted share; Adjusted Net Income(1)(2) of $99.3 million or $0.58 Adjusted Net Income per diluted share(1)(2).
•
Generated Adjusted EBITDA(1)(2) of $406.1 million.
•
Invested $127.9 million of capital expenditures, excluding plugging and abandonment and settled decommissioning obligations.
•
Maintained a strong balance sheet with $410.2 million of cash, an undrawn credit facility and Net Debt to Last Twelve Months (“LTM”) Adjusted EBITDA(1)(2) of 0.6x as of September 30, 2026.
•
Achieved first oil at CPN late in the third quarter, with the well performing in line with expectations.
•
Commenced drilling operations at Monument #4 in early September.
•
Drilled the Daenerys appraisal well to target depth, meeting appraisal objectives.
Key Strategic Highlights
•
Completed the acquisition of deepwater assets from Shell on September 22, 2026; final net cash purchase price of $420 million.
•
Increased midpoint of full-year 2026 production guidance to 69 MBo/d and 93.5 MBoe/d, inclusive of the completed Gulf of America acquisition, non-core shelf divestment and impact from Hurricane Isaias in early October.
•
Delivered greater than 100% of the $100 million Optimal Performance Plan 2026 target ahead of schedule.
•
Commenced a deepwater 3D seismic campaign in Honduras in October, less than three months after signing the agreements.
•
Awarded both leases bid on at the August 2026 BBG3 Lease Sale.
“The third quarter demonstrated the strength of the business we are building,” said Paul Goodfellow, President and Chief Executive Officer of Talos. “With the completion of our Gulf of America bolt-on acquisition, we have added high-quality, oil-weighted assets that increase free cash flow, improve margins and create additional infrastructure-led growth opportunities. Combined with strong performance from our base business, we are executing our strategy with greater scale, financial flexibility and a deeper portfolio of development and exploration opportunities to drive long-term value creation.
Our teams delivered another quarter of consistent operational execution, disciplined cost management and strong financial outcomes. Looking ahead, we are focused on fully integrating the new deepwater assets, advancing development and exploration opportunities across the portfolio, and continuing to build the scale and portfolio depth to position Talos as the leading pure-play offshore E&P.”

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS AND OPERATIONS UPDATE
Operations Update:
Production Update: During the third quarter, oil and total equivalent production exceeded guidance, driven by continued strong base business performance, the absence of storm-related downtime and contributions from acquired assets following the September 22, 2026 closing.
CPN: The Company achieved first oil in September 2026, with the well performing in line with expectations. Talos, as operator, holds a 65% W.I., Walter Oil & Gas Corp. holds a 25% W.I., and Houston Energy holds a 10% W.I.
Exploration, Development and Appraisal Update:
Monument: The first Monument development well reached target depth in June, after which the operator redeployed the rig to another asset in which Talos does not hold a working interest before returning to Monument to spud the second development well in early September. Completion operations on both Monument wells are expected to commence after the second well reaches target depth. First oil is now expected in the first quarter of 2027, with gross production expected to reach 20–30 MBoe/d. Monument is a large Wilcox oil discovery in Walker Ridge blocks 271, 272, 315, and 316 and is being developed as a subsea tie-back to the Shenandoah production facility, with committed firm gross capacity of 20 MBbl/d. There is a prospective drilling location that could extend the resource beyond the base development case. Beacon Offshore Energy LLC, as operator, holds a 41.7% W.I., Talos holds a 29.7% W.I., and Navitas Petroleum LP holds a 28.6% W.I.
Brutus Program: The Brutus development program commenced during the third quarter with operations from the platform-based rig. The development plan has been optimized to incorporate batch drilling and completions, improving execution efficiency and project returns, with first oil expected to be phased from the second quarter through the fourth quarter of 2027. The program has also expanded from four to five wells to include an additional sidetrack opportunity.
Daenerys: The Daenerys appraisal well was spud in early July and drilled to target depth in late August, meeting the Company’s appraisal objectives and further informing its understanding of reservoir and fluid properties. The results support continued appraisal activity while advancing the evaluation of development concepts ranging from a tie-back to a standalone host. The Company expects additional appraisal activity at Daenerys in 2027, including a sidetrack designed to further evaluate the discovery. The Company also expects to drill an exploration well in late 2027 or early 2028 targeting a nearby prospect acquired in the December 2025 BBG1 lease sale.
Offshore Mexico: During the third quarter, Talos announced a farm-in transaction into Block 29, a Repsol-operated pre-FID development located in the Salinas-Sureste Basin in the southern Gulf of Mexico. Upon closing, Talos will hold a 50% working interest alongside Repsol. Block 29 contains the Polok and Chinwol oil discoveries, which together are estimated to contain more than 200 MMBoe of gross recoverable resource, with a further 200 MMBoe of estimated gross resource potential across multiple on-block exploration prospects. The partners expect to progress the project toward FID in 2027.
Offshore Honduras: During the third quarter, Talos executed definitive agreements to acquire an 80% operated working interest in an offshore Honduras block spanning more than 4 million gross acres. The Company closed on a 45% working interest and assumed operatorship, with the acquisition of the remaining 35% working interest subject to approval by Honduras's Secretaría de Energía (SEN). Talos commenced a 3D seismic campaign in October, initially targeting a portion of the deepwater acreage.
Share Repurchase Program:
During the third quarter of 2026, Talos repurchased 1.4 million shares for $23.6 million. Since announcing its current return of capital framework in the second quarter of 2025, Talos has returned approximately $160 million to shareholders through share repurchases, reducing shares outstanding by approximately 8%.
The remaining share repurchase authorization as of September 30, 2026, was approximately $176 million. Under Talos's capital allocation framework, management expects to allocate up to 50% of annual free cash flow to share repurchases. The timing and amount of any repurchases under the Company's share repurchase program will depend on market conditions, share price, legal requirements, and other factors, and may be made from time to time in accordance with Rule 10b-18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.
Credit Facility Update:
In conjunction with the completed Gulf of America bolt-on acquisition, the Company's borrowing base increased to $850 million, with the credit facility remaining undrawn as of September 30, 2026.

Reconciliation of Net Income (Loss) attributable to Talos Energy Inc. to EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA attributable to Talos Energy Inc.
“EBITDA,” “Adjusted EBITDA” and "Adjusted EBITDA attributable to Talos Energy Inc." provide management and investors with (i) additional information to evaluate, with certain adjustments, items required or permitted in calculating covenant compliance under our debt agreements, (ii) important supplemental indicators of the operational performance of our business, (iii) additional criteria for evaluating our performance relative to our peers and (iv) supplemental information to investors about certain material non-cash and/or other items that may not continue at the same level in the future. EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA attributable to Talos Energy Inc. have limitations as analytical tools and should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP or as alternatives to net income (loss), net income (loss) attributable to Talos Energy Inc., operating income (loss) or any other measure of financial performance presented in accordance with GAAP. We define these as the following:
EBITDA. Net income (loss) plus interest expense; income tax expense (benefit); depreciation, depletion and amortization; and accretion expense.
Adjusted EBITDA. EBITDA plus non-cash impairment of oil and natural gas properties, transaction and other (income) expenses, decommissioning obligations, the net change in fair value of derivatives (mark-to-market effect, net of cash settlements and premiums related to these derivatives), (gain) loss on debt extinguishment, non-cash write-down of other well equipment and non-cash equity-based compensation expense.
Adjusted EBITDA attributable to Talos Energy Inc. Adjusted EBITDA, less adjustments for noncontrolling interest.
Adjusted EBITDA attributable to Talos Energy Inc. excluding hedges. We have historically provided as a supplement to—rather than in lieu of—Adjusted EBITDA including hedges, provides useful information regarding our results of operations and profitability by illustrating the operating results of our oil and natural gas properties without the benefit or detriment, as applicable, of our financial oil and natural gas hedges. By excluding our oil and natural gas hedges, we are able to convey actual operating results using realized market prices during the period, thereby providing analysts and investors with additional information they can use to evaluate the impacts of our hedging strategies over time.
