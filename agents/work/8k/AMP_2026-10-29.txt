•Return on equity remains distinguished in the industry at
56 percent(1)(2).

$28 billion of client assets transferring through early 2027. We are pleased to welcome an additional 29 advisors managing nearly $4 billion of client assets that joined Huntington through their acquisition of Cadence Bank and
will come onto our platform in mid-2027.
•Ameriprise continues to be recognized in the marketplace, including on the Forbes 2027 Most Trusted Companies in America list.

Advice & Wealth Management generated strong performance with pretax adjusted operating earnings of
$989 million, up 12 percent, and a margin of 29.3 percent. Core distribution earnings increased more than
20 percent and represented the majority of segment earnings.
Adjusted operating net revenues increased 13 percent to $3.4 billion, primarily driven by 8 percent growth in client assets and higher transactional activity. Strong wrap net inflows and market appreciation contributed to growth in fee-based revenues, while increased sales of annuity products and brokerage transactions drove strong transactional activity.
Adjusted operating expenses increased 13 percent to $2.4 billion, primarily driven by distribution expenses, which grew in line with advisor-driven revenues. General and administrative expenses increased 4 percent
to $455 million, primarily driven by volume-related expenses and investments for growth.
Our underlying business fundamentals coupled with strong operating and technology capabilities are foundational in driving sustained profitable growth.
•Adjusted operating net revenue per advisor on a trailing 12-month basis reached a new high of $1.2 million, up 13 percent. The company added 79 experienced advisors in the quarter.
•Total client assets were $1.2 trillion, up $93 billion, or 8 percent, and wrap assets were $729 billion, up
$79 billion, or 12 percent, driven by organic growth and market appreciation.
•Excluding Comerica, client net inflows were up 56 percent to $5.3 billion and wrap net inflows were up
38 percent to $6.6 billion, reflecting continued solid underlying organic activity.
•We anticipate approximately $32 billion of Huntington assets transitioning to our platform through mid-2027.
•Transactional activity continued to grow, up 6 percent, driven by our planning model.
•Cash sweep balances were $27.0 billion compared to $28.8 billion in the prior quarter, primarily reflecting
a $1.5 billion impact from the Comerica transition, with stable underlying balances.
•Bank assets increased 2 percent to $24.7 billion and earnings were up in the mid-single-digit percent range.

Asset Management adjusted operating net revenues increased 9 percent to $990 million and pretax adjusted operating earnings increased 18 percent to $308 million. Net pretax adjusted operating margin increased 360 basis points to 45.7 percent, reflecting our discipline in pursuing growth that enhances shareholder value. The underlying fee rate remained stable.
Adjusted operating expenses increased 6 percent. General and administrative expenses increased 2 percent driven by higher compensation expense for Seligman from AUM growth and performance, as well as higher volume-related expenses. We continue to demonstrate expense discipline, with full-year general and administrative expenses expected to be flat excluding Seligman and other performance fee compensation, while investing in opportunities to drive growth.
Assets under management and advisement increased 4 percent to $743 billion. Investment performance remained strong with 78 percent of retail funds above median versus peers on an asset-weighted basis for the 3-year period, 74 percent for the 5-year period, and 87 percent for the 10-year period. In addition, 92 Columbia Threadneedle funds globally earned four- or five-star ratings from Morningstar.
We remain focused on meeting clients’ needs with a broad range of investment capabilities and product structures, including ETFs, SMAs and Model Delivery offerings, while further expanding our alternative strategies. In the quarter, net outflows were $9.3 billion, reflecting:
•Institutional net outflows of $5.5 billion primarily related to client asset allocation decisions.
•Retail net outflows of $2.9 billion, which included approximately $0.8 billion of outflows from VP/VIT funds associated with our closed block of variable annuities with living benefit riders. Remaining flows reflect industry pressure on active managers.
•Generation of net inflows in areas of strategic focus within retail and institutional, including $1.3 billion of net inflows into technology and healthcare strategies and $2.4 billion of net inflows into ETFs, SMAs and Model Delivery offerings.
