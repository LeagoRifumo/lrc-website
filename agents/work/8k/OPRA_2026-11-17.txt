Opera Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results at the Top End of Guidance Ranges
Revenue increased 20% year-over-year to $182.8 million, toward the top end of the guidance range
Adjusted EBITDA was $43.2 million, representing a 24% margin and 19% year-over-year growth, exceeding the guidance range
Raised full-year revenue guidance to $735–742 million (20–21% growth) with adjusted EBITDA of $172–175 million (23.5% margin)
Announced replacement of ADS program with Nasdaq listing of ordinary shares
OSLO, Norway, November 17, 2026 — Opera Limited (NASDAQ: OPRA), a leading global browser and AI agent company, today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“We delivered another quarter of strong performance across our business, reflecting the resilience of our model and consistent execution in key markets. We continue to execute on our strategy of integrating advanced AI, local inference, and agentic tools into the browser to serve evolving user preferences,” said Lin Song, CEO. “By maintaining our focus on differentiated product experiences, we continue to capture high-intent engagement across our platforms and in key geographies. Our partners benefit by leveraging our advertising platform to reach customers across channels and devices. Looking ahead, we remain focused on scaling AI tools throughout our products and creating more reasons for users to continue choosing Opera browsers.”

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Business Highlights
•
Advertising revenue: Grew 22% year-over-year to $116.8 million, representing 64% of total revenue. Strength in advertising was broad-based, with especially strong growth across gaming and travel in Q3.
•
Query revenue: Grew 17% year-over-year to $65.0 million, accounting for 36% of total revenue and benefiting from the ongoing favorable shift in user geographic mix, as well as increased ARPU due to the evolution of opportunities to address user queries.
•
User base & ARPU: Average monthly active users (“MAUs”) was 279 million across all products and services, with annualized average revenue per user (“ARPU”) increasing 22% year-over-year to $2.60. MAU growth was strong in high-ARPU markets and products, including 1 million MAUs added in Western markets, and more than 4 million MAUs added for Opera GX year-over-year in Q3.
•
Opera GX: Averaged 37 million MAUs in the quarter across PC and mobile, up 13%, or more than 4 million, year-over-year.
•
MiniPay: Reached over 20 million cumulative activated wallets as of Q3 2026. Since MiniPay’s launch three years ago, users have completed approximately 600 million peer-to-peer transactions and over $2 billion of cumulative transaction volume.
•
Board of Directors: Mr. Tian Jin resigned from the Opera board to focus on other pursuits. Mr. Jin served on Opera’s board for over five years, in two separate terms, including from 2019 to 2021 and from 2022 to 2026.
•
Transition from ADSs to ordinary shares on Nasdaq: Opera announced it will replace its American Depositary Shares (ADSs) with its ordinary shares listed on The Nasdaq Global Select Market. Each ADS will be exchanged automatically for one ordinary share, with no action required by holders. The ADSs will trade until the close on Friday, December 4, 2026, and the ordinary shares will begin trading on Monday, December 7, 2026, under the existing ticker “OPRA.” Removing the depositary simplifies Opera’s listing structure and ends the depositary fees currently paid by ADS holders. For further information, please see the separate press release and investor FAQ issued today.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
All comparisons in this section are relative to the third quarter of 2025 unless otherwise stated.
Revenue increased 20% to $182.8 million.
•
Advertising revenue increased 22% to $116.8 million.
•
Query revenue increased 17% to $65.0 million.
•
Other revenue was $1.1 million.
Operating expenses increased 15% to $148.9 million.
•
The total amount of technology and platform fees, content cost and cost of inventory sold, all being costs of revenue, was $74.1 million, or 41% of revenue, primarily reflecting the continued scaling of the Opera Ads platform in line with 22% year-over-year advertising revenue growth.
•
Personnel expenses excluding share-based compensation decreased 1% to $20.7 million. Sequentially, these expenses decreased 10% compared to the second quarter of 2026, which included accelerated bonus accruals.
•
Share-based compensation expenses decreased 23% to $6.9 million, primarily from having passed the first vesting period for multi-year equity awards issued in 2025.
•
Marketing and distribution expenses remained flat at $36.0 million compared to both the third quarter of 2025 and the second quarter of 2026, reflecting disciplined user acquisition focused on audiences with higher lifetime value and stronger return on investment.
•
Depreciation and amortization increased 17% to $5.7 million, predominantly reflecting increased product development activities.
•
All other operating expenses decreased 13% to $5.6 million, driven mainly by the reversal of the prior quarter impairment of non-financial assets.
Operating profit was $34.0 million, representing a 19% margin, compared to an operating profit of $22.4 million and a margin of 15% in the third quarter of 2025.
Net finance loss was $0.4 million, reflecting a foreign exchange loss of $1.0 million, partially offset by net interest income of $0.6 million.
2
Income tax expense was $5.5 million, corresponding to an effective tax rate of 17%, and representing 13% of adjusted EBITDA both in the quarter and year-to-date. This compares to a full-year ratio of income tax expense to adjusted EBITDA of 12% in 2025.
Net income was $27.6 million, representing a 15% margin, compared to net income of $18.6 million and a margin of 12% in the third quarter of 2025.
Adjusted net income was $31.0 million, representing a 17% margin and an increase of 15% relative to $26.8 million and an 18% margin in the third quarter of 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA was $43.2 million, representing a 24% margin and an increase of 19% relative to $36.3 million and a 24% margin in the third quarter of 2025.
Diluted earnings per share was $0.30, whereas adjusted diluted earnings per share was $0.34.
Net cash flow from operating activities was $37.4 million, or 87% of adjusted EBITDA. Free cash flow from operations was $32.6 million, or 75% of adjusted EBITDA. Cash and cash equivalents were $109.5 million at quarter-end, compared to $145.2 million as of June 30, 2026, reflecting cash used for share repurchases and dividends in the quarter, as described in the section below.

Non-IFRS Financial Measures
In addition to financial measures presented in accordance with IFRS Accounting Standards, we use the non-IFRS performance measures adjusted net income, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted diluted earnings per share, as well as the non-IFRS liquidity measure free cash flow from operations, to manage our business, evaluate performance, support planning and decision-making, and allocate resources. The non-IFRS performance measures are intended to provide supplemental information by excluding items that we believe are not representative of core business operating performance. While free cash flow from operations does not represent residual cash available for discretionary uses, we believe that it provides useful supplemental information regarding our ability to generate cash from ongoing operations to fund investments, including acquisitions, and to support capital allocation decisions.
Adjusted net income is defined as net income adjusted to exclude (i) profit (loss) from discontinued operations, (ii) gain (loss) on investments in unconsolidated entities, (iii) non-recurring expenses, (iv) impairment of non-financial assets, (v) amortization of acquired intangible assets, (vi) share-based compensation expenses, and (vii) the income tax effect of these adjustments. Adjusted net income margin is calculated as adjusted net income divided by revenue. Adjusted diluted earnings per share is calculated as adjusted net income divided by the diluted weighted average number of shares outstanding.
Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income adjusted to exclude (i) profit (loss) from discontinued operations, (ii) income tax expense, (iii) net finance income (expense), (iv) gain (loss) on long-term investments in unconsolidated entities, (v) non-recurring expenses, (vi) impairment of non-financial assets, (vii) depreciation and amortization, (viii) share-based compensation expenses, and (ix) other operating income. Adjusted EBITDA margin is calculated as adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.
Free cash flow from operations is defined as net cash flows from (used in) operating activities less (i) purchases of fixed and intangible assets, (ii) development expenditure and (iii) payment of lease liabilities.
We believe these non-IFRS financial measures are useful to investors because they facilitate period-to-period comparisons of operating performance and are consistent with how management evaluates the business. These measures should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for, or superior to, the financial information prepared in accordance with IFRS Accounting Standards. Our definitions of adjusted net income, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted diluted earnings per share and free cash flow from operations may differ from similarly titled measures used by other companies. In addition, these measures may be limited in their usefulness because they do not present the full economic effects of certain items of income, expenses and cash flows. We address the limitations of these non-IFRS financial measures by providing reconciliations from the most closely comparable IFRS financial measures in the section titled “Reconciliations of Non-IFRS Financial Measures” included at the end of this earnings press release. Investors are encouraged to review these reconciliations and to consider non-IFRS financial measures together with our IFRS results.
