FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
CAMTEK ANNOUNCES RESULTS
FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2026
Q3 record revenues of $161.3 million; Expects revenues of ~$180 million in Q4 2026;
Initial outlook of over $800 million in 2027
MIGDAL HAEMEK, Israel – November 4, 2026 – Camtek Ltd. (NASDAQ: CAMT; TASE: CAMT), today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
2026 Third Quarter Financial Highlights
· | Record revenues of $161.3 million, a 21% QoQ increase;

· | GAAP gross margin of 50.0% and non-GAAP gross margin of 51.1%;

· | GAAP operating income of $39.8 million and non-GAAP operating income of $47.5 million, representing operating margins of 24.7% and 29.5%, respectively;

· | GAAP net income of $42.8 million and non-GAAP net income of $50.3 million; GAAP diluted EPS of $0.84 and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $0.99; and

· | Strong positive operating cash flow of $71.1 million in the quarter.

Forward-Looking Expectations
Management reiterates the guidance it provided in conjunction with its investor and analyst breakfast event held in October. Management expects continued growth in the fourth quarter with revenues of approximately $180 million, representing approximately 40% year-over-year growth and bringing full-year 2026 revenue to approximately $595 million.
As previously announced, management provided initial outlook for 2027 revenue to exceed $800 million, representing growth of approximately 35% over 2026, supported by strong order backlog and sales pipeline.
Management Comment
Rafi Amit, Camtek’s CEO, commented: “Our record third-quarter results reflect the exceptional strength of our business and the sustained momentum that continues into the fourth quarter of 2026 and extends well into 2027.
These results further reinforce the key messages we shared at our recent Investor and Analyst Breakfast event. Camtek operates in some of the fastest-growing segments of the semiconductor industry, particularly high-end advanced packaging driven by the rapidly expanding demand for AI.
Since the event, our business momentum continued across all key indicators, further reinforcing our confidence in achieving our business targets.”
Concluded Mr. Amit, “For the first time in our history, we have visibility a year ahead giving us great confidence in our outlook and trajectory.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $161.3 million. This compares to third quarter 2025 revenues of $126.0 million, a year-over-year growth of 28%.
Gross profit on a GAAP basis in the quarter totaled $80.6 million (50.0% of revenues), an increase of 28% compared to $63.0 million (50.0% of revenues) in the third quarter of 2025.
Gross profit on a non-GAAP basis in the quarter totaled $82.3 million (51.1% of revenues), an increase of 27% compared to $64.9 million (51.5% of revenues) in the third quarter of 2025.
Operating income on a GAAP basis in the quarter totaled $39.8 million (24.7% of revenues), an increase of 25% compared to $31.8 million (25.3% of revenues) in the third quarter of 2025.
Operating income on a non-GAAP basis in the quarter totaled $47.5 million (29.5% of revenues), an increase of 26% compared to $37.6 million (29.9% of revenues) in the third quarter of 2025.
Net income on a GAAP basis in the quarter totaled $42.8 million, or $0.84 per diluted share, compared to a net loss of $53.2 million, or $1.16 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025.
Net income on a non-GAAP basis in the quarter totaled $50.3 million, or $0.99 per diluted share, an increase of 23% compared to a non-GAAP net income of $40.9 million, or $0.82 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025.
Cash and cash equivalents, short-term deposits and marketable securities, as of September 30, 2026, were $877.1 million compared to $815.8 million as of June 30, 2026. During the third quarter, the Company generated an operating cash flow of $71.1 million.

ABOUT CAMTEK LTD.
Camtek is a developer and manufacturer of high-end inspection and metrology equipment for the semiconductor industry. Camtek's systems inspect IC and measure IC features on wafers throughout the production process of semiconductor devices, covering the front and mid-end and up to the beginning of assembly (Post Dicing). Camtek's systems inspect wafers for the most demanding semiconductor market segments, including Advanced Interconnect Packaging, Heterogeneous Integration, Memory and HBM, CMOS Image Sensors, Compound Semiconductors, MEMS, and RF, serving numerous industries’ leading global IDMs, OSATs, and foundries.
With manufacturing facilities in Israel and Germany, and eight offices around the world, Camtek provides state of the art solutions in line with customers' requirements.
