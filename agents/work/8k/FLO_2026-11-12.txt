FLOWERS FOODS, INC. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
THOMASVILLE, Ga., November 12, 2026 – Flowers Foods, Inc. (NYSE: FLO) today reported financial results for the company’s 12-week third quarter ended October 10, 2026.
Third Quarter Summary:
Compared to the prior year third quarter where applicable
•
Net sales(1) decreased 4.7% to $1.169 billion as favorable price/mix was more than offset by lower volume.
•
Net income decreased 63.6% to $14.4 million, representing 1.2% of sales, a 200-basis point decrease, primarily due to lower sales volume, increased marketing investment, higher logistics costs, and greater outside purchases of product. These factors were partially offset by lower incentive compensation, lower ingredient costs, and lower distributor distribution fees. Adjusted net income(2) decreased 56.0% to $21.4 million.
•
Adjusted EBITDA(2) decreased 33.2% to $78.9 million, representing 6.8% of net sales, a 280-basis point decrease.
•
Diluted EPS decreased $0.12 to $0.07. Adjusted diluted EPS(2) decreased $0.13 to $0.10.
Chairman and CEO Remarks:
“Our third-quarter results reflect a continued challenging operating environment, driven by pressure on household budgets, evolving consumer behavior and heightened promotional activity, as well as areas where we need to move faster with focused execution,” said Ryals McMullian, chairman and CEO of Flowers Foods. “Following the second quarter, we expected our earlier pricing actions and the relaunch of Nature's Own with fewer, simpler ingredients to help stabilize performance as we worked to introduce new innovation to market. We are seeing encouraging signs for Nature’s Own, including improving sequential trends and growing awareness of the relaunch, although the pace of improvement has been slower than anticipated, primarily in loaf, where category trends remain challenging. We are accelerating actions already underway to strengthen customer execution, close innovation gaps, and better align our portfolio with evolving consumer preferences.”
“While it will take time for our initiatives to translate more fully into results, we believe they are putting Flowers on a stronger path forward. We expect the environment to remain challenging in the near term, but we remain confident in the long-term potential of the business. We participate in large, attractive markets across fresh packaged bread, cake, better-for-you snacking, and foodservice, and our leading brands, extensive network, and deep customer relationships provide a strong foundation to drive improved performance and long-term growth. Given our third-quarter performance and current conditions, we are adjusting our 2026 guidance.”
Revised Outlook: 52-week Fiscal 2026, the Company Expects:
•
Net sales of approximately $5.016 billion to $5.063 billion, representing a -4.6% to -3.7% change compared to the prior year. Prior guidance called for net sales of approximately $5.070 billion to $5.142 billion.
•
Adjusted EBITDA(3) in the range of approximately $415 million to $430 million, compared to prior guidance of $453 million to $481 million.
•
Adjusted diluted EPS(2) of approximately $0.63 to $0.68 per share, compared to prior guidance of $0.75 to $0.85 per share.

•

Weighted average diluted share count for the year of approximately 213.5 million shares.

Consolidated Third Quarter Operating Highlights
Compared to the prior year third quarter where applicable
•
Net sales decreased 4.7% to $1.169 billion. Pricing/mix(4) increased 2.3% and volume(5) declined 7.0%.
•
Branded Retail net sales decreased $41.1 million, or 5.1%, to $772.1 million due to volume declines partially offset by favorable pricing/mix. Pricing/mix(4) rose 3.5% and volume(5) decreased 8.6%.
•
Other net sales decreased $16.4 million, or 4.0%, to $397.0 million due to lower sales of store branded products, partially due to discontinued business. This was partially offset by improved non-retail volume and favorable pricing/mix, largely from growth in foodservice sales. Pricing/mix(4) increased 0.9% and volume(5) declined 4.9%.
•
Materials, supplies, labor, and other production costs (exclusive of depreciation and amortization) were 53.3% of net sales, a 120-basis point increase. The increase as a percentage of sales primarily reflected the impact of lower production volumes on workforce-related costs and greater outside purchases of product (sales with no associated ingredient costs), partially offset by lower ingredient costs.
•
Selling, distribution, and administrative (SD&A) expenses were 40.2% of net sales, a 140-basis point increase. The increase as a percentage of net sales reflected greater marketing investments and higher logistics costs, partially offset by lower distributor distribution fees. Excluding matters affecting comparability, adjusted SD&A(2) was 39.9% of net sales, a 160-basis point increase.
•
Restructuring charges, impairment of assets, and recovery on inferior ingredients together totaled $5.4 million, approximately flat with the prior-year period restructuring charges of $5.5 million.
•
Depreciation and amortization (D&A) expenses were $38.3 million or 3.3% of net sales, approximately flat with last year's third quarter as a percentage of sales.
•
Net interest expense decreased approximately $1.0 million primarily due to lower debt outstanding and increased interest income.
•
Net income decreased 63.6% to $14.4 million, representing 1.2% of sales, a 200-basis point decrease, and diluted EPS decreased $0.12 to $0.07. Adjusted net income(2) decreased 56.0% to $21.4 million and adjusted diluted EPS(2) decreased $0.13 to $0.10.
•
Adjusted EBITDA(2) decreased 33.2% to $78.9 million, representing 6.8% of net sales, a 280-basis point decrease.
Cash Flow, Capital Allocation, and Capital Return
Year-to-date, cash flow from operating activities decreased $0.9 million to $319.9 million, capital expenditures decreased $15.8 million to $64.5 million, and dividends paid to shareholders decreased $49.4 million to $107.6 million. Cash and cash equivalents were $72.2 million at quarter end.

About Flowers Foods
Headquartered in Thomasville, Ga., Flowers Foods, Inc. (NYSE: FLO) is one of the largest producers of packaged bakery foods in the United States with 2025 net sales of $5.3 billion. Flowers operates bakeries across the country that produce a wide range of bakery products. Among the company’s top brands are Nature’s Own, Dave’s Killer Bread, Canyon Bakehouse, Simple Mills, Wonder, and Tastykake. Learn more at www.flowersfoods.com.

Information Regarding Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The company prepares its consolidated financial statements in accordance with U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP). However, from time to time, the company may present in its public statements, press releases and SEC filings, non-GAAP financial measures such as, EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income, adjusted diluted EPS, adjusted income tax expense, adjusted selling, distribution and administrative expenses (SD&A), and gross margin excluding depreciation and amortization. The company’s definitions of these non-GAAP measures may differ from similarly titled measures used by others. These non-GAAP measures should be considered supplemental to, and not a substitute for, financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP.
The company defines EBITDA as earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. Earnings are net income. The company believes that EBITDA is a useful tool for managing the operations of its business and is an indicator of the company’s ability to incur and service indebtedness and generate free cash flow. The company also believes that EBITDA measures are commonly reported and widely used by investors and other interested parties as measures of a company’s operating performance and debt servicing ability because EBITDA measures assist in comparing performance on a consistent basis without regard to depreciation or amortization, which can vary significantly depending upon accounting methods and non-operating factors (such as historical cost). EBITDA is also a widely-accepted financial indicator of a company’s ability to incur and service indebtedness.
EBITDA should not be considered an alternative to (a) income from operations or net income (loss) as a measure of operating performance; (b) cash flows provided by operating, investing and financing activities (as determined in accordance with GAAP) as a measure of the company’s ability to meet its cash needs; or (c) any other indicator of performance or liquidity that has been determined in accordance with GAAP.
The company defines adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income, adjusted diluted EPS, adjusted income tax expense and adjusted SD&A, respectively, to exclude additional costs that the company considers important to present to investors to increase the investors’ insights about the company’s core operations. These costs include, but are not limited to, the costs of closing a plant or costs associated with acquisition and integration-related activities, restructuring activities, certain impairment charges, legal settlements, costs to implement an enterprise resource planning system and enhance bakery digital capabilities (business process improvement costs) to provide investors direct insight into these costs, and other costs impacting past and future comparability. The company believes that these measures, when considered together with its GAAP financial results, provide management and investors with a more complete understanding of its business operating results, including underlying trends, by excluding the effects of certain charges. Adjusted EBITDA is used as a performance measure in the company’s incentive compensation program.
Presentation of gross margin includes depreciation and amortization in the materials, supplies, labor and other production costs according to GAAP. Our method of presenting gross margin excludes the depreciation and amortization components, as discussed above.
