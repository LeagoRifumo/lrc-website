Madrigal Pharmaceuticals Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Updates
•Third-quarter 2026 Rezdiffra® (resmetirom) net sales of $431.3 million, representing year-over-year growth of 50%; achieved first quarter of profitability
•Rezdiffra reaches a major milestone, achieving blockbuster status with year-to-date net sales of $1.1 billion
•As of Sept. 30, 2026, more than 55,850 patients on Rezdiffra, nearly doubling from 3Q25, reflecting continued strong prescriber adoption and high patient demand
•U.S. F2-F3 MASH market is accelerating, growing to more than 530,000 addressable patients through June 30, 2026, on track for more than 30% growth by YE26
•Madrigal strengthens its leadership position in well-compensated MASH cirrhosis (F4c) with new MAESTRO-CSPH outcomes clinical trial; MAESTRO-NASH OUTCOMES on track, data expected late 2027
•Reports cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash and marketable securities of $961.8 million as of Sept. 30, 2026

•Company to host conference call today, Nov. 3, 2026, at 8 a.m. EST
CONSHOHOCKEN, Pa., Nov. 3, 2026 – Madrigal Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: MDGL), a biopharmaceutical company focused on delivering novel therapeutics for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH), today reports third-quarter 2026 financial results and provides corporate updates.
Bill Sibold, Chief Executive Officer of Madrigal, stated: “Madrigal delivered another strong quarter, driven by continued Rezdiffra demand, deepening prescriber adoption and accelerating market growth. Rezdiffra surpassed $1 billion in year-to-date net sales, achieving blockbuster status less than three years after launch, and we are still early in a rapidly growing market. Our latest analysis shows the addressable U.S. F2-F3 market is on track to increase more than 30 percent by year-end 2026, reinforcing how quickly this category is developing and the significant runway ahead.”
Sibold continued: “Madrigal’s next major opportunity is in well-compensated cirrhosis, or F4c, an urgent unmet need for patients. Our objective in F4c is clear: be first and best. Our ongoing MAESTRO-NASH OUTCOMES trial positions us to deliver the first approved therapy to patients with this advanced stage of MASH. MAESTRO-CSPH is our new, Phase 3, practice-shaping trial that we expect will extend our competitive advantage and strengthen our long-term leadership in this rapidly growing market.”
Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Corporate Updates
•Strengthens leadership in well-compensated MASH cirrhosis (F4c) with new MAESTRO-CSPH Phase 3 outcomes trial
oMadrigal plans to initiate MAESTRO-CSPH, a second Phase 3 event-driven outcomes trial that builds on its ongoing MAESTRO-NASH OUTCOMES study. The trial is expected to enroll more than 2,000 F4c patients, with a population significantly enriched for clinically significant portal hypertension (CSPH). It is designed to generate the broadest dataset in F4c and support validation of noninvasive biomarkers for Rezdiffra and future pipeline programs. Trial initiation is expected in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory discussions.
•Madrigal to have strong presence at upcoming AASLD Liver Meeting
oMadrigal will have a significant presence at the upcoming American Association for the Study of Liver Diseases (AASLD) Liver Meeting taking place Nov. 5-9 in Denver, CO. Resmetirom will be featured in more abstracts than any other MASH therapy at the conference. Eighteen Madrigal abstracts have been accepted, including 13 abstracts based on real-world evidence where the largest data set spans nearly 12,000 patients, underscoring the breadth of Madrigal’s evidence-generation program for Rezdiffra and MASH.
•Journal of Hepatology publication demonstrates Rezdiffra’s consistent efficacy across common MASH genetic risk variants
oSecondary analysis of the Phase 3 MAESTRO-NASH trial demonstrated Rezdiffra improved histology and MRI-PDFF across common genetic variants associated with MASH progression including PNPLA3, HSD17B13, TM6SF2, MTARC1 and MBOAT7, reinforcing Rezdiffra as a foundational treatment.
•Appointed John C. Reed, M.D., Ph.D., to Madrigal’s Board of Directors
oIn August, Dr. John Reed was appointed to Madrigal’s Board of Directors. Currently Dr. Reed serves as Executive Vice President, Innovative Medicine, Research & Development at Johnson & Johnson where he leads research and development across oncology, immunology and neuroscience. He previously held executive leadership positions at both Sanofi and Roche.
Third-Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Total Revenues: Third-quarter 2026 net revenues were $431.3 million, an increase of 50% compared to $287.3 million in the comparable prior year period, driven by increased demand for Rezdiffra in the U.S. in 2026.
•Operating Expenses: Third-quarter 2026 operating expenses were $375.3 million, inclusive of $38.9 million in non-cash stock-based compensation expense, compared to $401.2 million, inclusive of $26.3 million in non-cash stock-based compensation expense, in the comparable prior year period.
oCost of Sales: Third-quarter 2026 cost of sales was $37.8 million compared to $18.1 million in the comparable prior year period. The increase in cost of sales was primarily driven by higher royalties payable to Roche as a result of increased net sales of Rezdiffra in 2026.
oR&D Expenses: Third-quarter 2026 R&D expenses were $71.8 million compared to $174.0 million in the comparable prior year period, both inclusive of non-cash stock-based compensation. The decrease in R&D expenses was primarily due to the one-time,
upfront business development expense of $117.3 million in the comparable prior year period.
oSG&A Expenses: Third-quarter 2026 SG&A expenses were $265.7 million compared to $209.1 million in the comparable prior year period, both inclusive of non-cash stock-based compensation. The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily due to continued investment in commercial activities for Rezdiffra, including headcount for the endocrinology field force expansion that began in the fourth quarter of 2025, and marketing efforts, including a direct-to-consumer (DTC) campaign.
•Net Income: Third-quarter 2026 net income was $55.4 million, or $1.90 per basic share and $1.83 per diluted share, compared to a net loss of $114.2 million, or $4.01 per basic and diluted share, in the comparable prior year period.
•Cash, Cash Equivalents, Restricted Cash and Marketable Securities: As of Sept. 30, 2026, Madrigal had cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and marketable securities of $961.8 million compared to $988.6 million as of Dec. 31, 2025.

About MASH
Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) is a serious liver disease that can progress to cirrhosis, liver failure, liver cancer, the need for liver transplantation, and premature mortality. MASH is the leading cause of liver transplantation in women and the second leading cause of all liver transplantation in the U.S. It is the fastest-growing indication for liver transplantation in Europe.
Once patients progress to MASH with moderate to advanced liver fibrosis (consistent with stages F2 to F3 fibrosis), the risk of adverse liver outcomes increases dramatically: these patients have a 10 to 17 times higher risk of liver-related mortality as compared to patients without fibrosis. Patients with MASH who progress to cirrhosis face a 42 times higher risk of liver-related mortality, underscoring the need to treat MASH before complications of cirrhosis develop. MASH is also an independent driver of cardiovascular disease, the leading cause of mortality for patients.
As disease awareness improves and disease prevalence increases, the number of diagnosed patients with F2 to F4c MASH is growing.
About Madrigal
Madrigal Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: MDGL) is a biopharmaceutical company focused on delivering novel therapeutics for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH), a liver disease with high unmet medical need. Madrigal’s medication, Rezdiffra (resmetirom), is a once-daily, oral, liver-directed THR-β agonist designed to target key underlying causes of MASH. Rezdiffra was the first medication approved by both the FDA and European Commission for the treatment of MASH with moderate to advanced fibrosis (F2 to F3). An ongoing Phase 3
outcomes trial is evaluating Rezdiffra for the treatment of compensated MASH cirrhosis (F4c). For more information, visit www.madrigalpharma.com.
