THIRD QUARTER 2026 UPDATE
Highlights
•U.S. GAAP net income attributable to shareholders of Teekay of $59.8 million, or $0.68 per share, in the third quarter of 2026.
•Teekay Tankers’ spot rates have remained at historic highs, with the third quarter of 2026 Suezmax and Aframax / LR2 spot rates averaging $106,400 per day and $67,600 per day, respectively. So far in the fourth quarter of 2026, Suezmax and Aframax/LR2 spot rates are setting new records, averaging $272,000 per day and $208,300 per day for approximately 63% and 53% of fourth quarter spot days booked to date, respectively.
•As part of Teekay Tankers' fleet renewal plan, the following transactions were entered into: (1) ordered two Aframax / LR2 tankers at a Chinese yard with expected deliveries in 2030; (2) ordered two Suezmax tankers at a Korean yard with expected deliveries in 2030, which include options for two additional Suezmax tankers with expected deliveries in 2030; and (3) agreed to acquire one 2020-built MR tanker with expected delivery by mid-January 2027.
•Consistent with Teekay Tankers’ dividend policy, Teekay Tankers declared its regular fixed quarterly cash dividend of $0.25 per share for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, payable in December 2026.
Hamilton, Bermuda, November 11, 2026 - Teekay Corporation Ltd. About Teekay
Teekay is a leading provider of international crude oil marine transportation and other marine services. Teekay provides these services through its controlling ownership interest in Teekay Tankers, a leading owner and operator of mid-sized crude tankers. Teekay Tankers has a fleet of 38 double-hull crude oil and product tankers (which includes committed newbuildings) and has three chartered-in oil tankers. In addition, Teekay Tankers manages and operates vessels for the Australian government and Australian energy companies as part of the marine services provided by Teekay Tankers and owns a ship-to-ship transfer business that performs full-service lightering and lightering support operations in the U.S. Gulf and Caribbean.
Teekay’s common shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol “TK”.
