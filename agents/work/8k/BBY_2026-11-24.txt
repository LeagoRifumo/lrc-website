“Our team delivered strong third quarter performance, with comparable sales growth of 6.1% which was ahead of our expectations and our highest in five years,” said Jason Bonfig, Best Buy CEO. “Disciplined execution, a compelling product cycle and continued market share gains helped drive broad-based topline momentum across nearly all product categories, our services offerings, and both our digital and store channels. We also continued to scale Marketplace and Best Buy Ads, which are contributing to topline growth and supporting gross profit rate expansion.”
Bonfig continued, “The strength and breadth of our results give us confidence to raise our full-year guidance. I am grateful to our teams for the dedication they bring to our customers every day, and for the energy I know they will bring to this year’s holiday season.”
FY27 Financial Guidance
The company’s FY27 financial guidance is as follows:
•Revenue of $43.0 billion to $43.3 billion, compared to prior guidance of $42.3 billion to $42.8 billion
•Comparable sales % change1 of 3.4% to 4.0%, compared to prior guidance of 1.9% to 3.0%
•Adjusted operating income rate2 of approximately 4.5%, compared to prior guidance of 4.4% to 4.5%
•Adjusted effective income tax rate2 of approximately 25.5%, which is unchanged
•Adjusted diluted EPS2 of $6.90 to $7.05, compared to prior guidance of $6.70 to $6.90
•Capital expenditures of approximately $750 million, which is unchanged
The company expects Q4 FY27 comparable sales to be in the range of 2.0% to 4.0% and adjusted operating income rate to be in the range of 4.9% to 5.1%.
Domestic Segment Q3 FY27 Results
Domestic Revenue
Domestic revenue of $9.4 billion increased 6.1% versus last year, primarily driven by comparable sales growth of 6.2%.
From a category perspective, the company generated comparable sales growth across most of its categories, with the largest drivers on a weighted basis being computing, mobile phones, and a collection of emerging categories such as AI glasses and trading cards.
Domestic online revenue of $3.1 billion increased 10.8% on a comparable basis, and as a percentage of total Domestic revenue, online revenue was 33.2% versus 31.8% last year.
Domestic Gross Profit Rate
Domestic gross profit rate was 24.1% versus 23.3% last year. The higher gross profit rate was primarily driven by IEEPA tariff refunds of $41 million and growth in Marketplace and Best Buy Ads. The previous items were partially offset by lower product margin rates.
Domestic Adjusted Selling, General and Administrative Expenses (“SG&A”)
Domestic adjusted SG&A was $1.8 billion, or 19.6% of revenue, versus $1.7 billion, or 19.2% of revenue, last year. The higher adjusted SG&A rate was primarily driven by increased incentive compensation and investments in the company's Marketplace and Best Buy Ads initiatives, partially offset by leverage from higher sales volume.
International Segment Q3 FY27 Results
International Revenue
International revenue of $820 million increased 3.3% versus last year. The revenue increase was primarily driven by comparable sales growth of 4.9%, which was partially offset by the negative impact of foreign exchange rates.
International Gross Profit Rate
International gross profit rate was 21.6% versus 22.8% last year. The lower gross profit rate was primarily due to lower product margin rates.
International Adjusted SG&A
International adjusted SG&A was $153 million, or 18.7% of revenue, versus $153 million, or 19.3% of revenue, last year. The lower adjusted SG&A rate was primarily driven by leverage from higher sales volume.

Share Repurchases and Dividends
In Q3 FY27, the company returned a total of $350 million to shareholders through dividends of $201 million and share repurchases of $149 million. On a year-to-date basis, the company has returned a total of $791 million to shareholders through dividends of $606 million and share repurchases of $185 million. The company expects to spend approximately $300 million on share repurchases during FY27.
Today, the company announced its board of directors has authorized the payment of a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.96 per common share. The regular quarterly dividend is payable on January 5, 2027, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on December 15, 2026.
Conference Call
Best Buy is scheduled to conduct an earnings conference call at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time (7:00 a.m. Central Time) on November 24, 2026. A webcast of the call is expected to be available at www.investors.bestbuy.com, both live and after the call.
