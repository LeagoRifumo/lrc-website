XPEL Reports Revenue of $137.8 million; Revenue Growth of 9.9% in Third Quarter 2026
San Antonio, TX – November 4, 2026 – XPEL, Inc. (Nasdaq: XPEL) (the "Company"), a global provider of protective films and coatings, announced consolidated results1 for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Overview:
•Revenue increased 9.9% to $137.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $125.4 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•Gross margin of 44.6% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 41.8% in the third quarter last year.
•Net income attributable to stockholders of the Company increased 26.3% to $16.3 million, or $0.59 per basic and diluted share, versus net income attributable to stockholders of the Company of $12.9 million, or $0.47 per basic and diluted share in the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted net income attributable to stockholders increased 31.7% to $17.0 million. Adjusted earnings per share was $0.62 per basic and diluted share. Adjusted net income attributable to stockholders and adjusted earnings per share excludes costs related to the start-up and ramp-up of the Company’s San Antonio manufacturing facility investment incurred prior to reaching full operational capacity.2
•EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization) increased 28.3% to $25.6 million, or 18.6% of revenue, compared to $19.9 million, or 15.9% of revenue in the third quarter of 2025.2
•Adjusted EBITDA grew 30.7% to $26.1 million or 18.9% of revenue. Adjusted EBITDA excludes costs related to the start-up and ramp-up of the Company’s San Antonio manufacturing investments incurred prior to reaching full operational capacity.2
First Nine Months 2026 Overview:
•Revenue increased 12.5% to $398.2 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to $353.9 million in the same period in 2025.
•Gross margin of 44.2% in the first nine months of 2026 compared to 42.3% in the first nine months last year.
•Net income attributable to stockholders of the Company increased 18.3% to $44.7 million, or $1.62 per basic and diluted share, versus net income attributable to stockholders of the Company of $37.8 million, or $1.37 per basic and diluted share in the first nine months of 2025.
•Adjusted net income attributable to stockholders increased 22.2% to $46.2 million. Adjusted earnings per share was $1.67 per basic and diluted share. Adjusted net income attributable to stockholders and adjusted earnings per share exclude acquisition related expenses associated with the purchase of the China manufacturer and costs related to the start-up and ramp-up of the Company’s San Antonio manufacturing investments incurred prior to reaching full operational capacity.2
•EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization) increased 21.3% to $70.1 million, or 17.6% of revenue, compared to $57.8 million, or 16.3% of revenue in the first nine months of 2025.2
•Adjusted EBITDA grew 23.4% to $71.3 million or 17.9% of revenue. Adjusted EBITDA exclude acquisition related expenses associated with the purchase of the China manufacturer and costs related to the start-up and ramp-up of the Company’s San Antonio manufacturing investments incurred prior to reaching full operational capacity.2
Ryan Pape, President and Chief Executive Officer of XPEL, commented, "We delivered a solid third quarter in a challenging environment, growing revenue nearly 10% and expanding gross margin for the third consecutive quarter. Our manufacturing investments in San Antonio and China remain on track, and we are confident they will drive meaningful margin expansion in the years ahead."

About XPEL, Inc.
XPEL is a leading provider of protective films and coatings, including automotive paint protection film, surface protection film, automotive and architectural window films, and ceramic coatings. With a global footprint, a network of trained installers and proprietary DAP software, XPEL is dedicated to exceeding customer expectations by providing high-quality products, leading customer service, expert technical support and world-class training. Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) plus interest expense, net, plus income tax expense plus depreciation expense and amortization expense. EBITDA should be considered in addition to, not as a substitute for, or superior to, financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP. It is not a measurement of our financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered as alternatives to revenue or net income, as applicable, or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP and may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other businesses. EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider it in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our operating results as reported under GAAP. EBITDA does not reflect the impact of certain cash charges resulting from matters we consider not to be indicative of ongoing operations and other companies in our industry may calculate EBITDA differently than we do, limiting its usefulness as a comparative measure.
We use adjusted EBITDA, adjusted earnings per share attributable to stockholders and adjusted net income attributable to stockholders, together with financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP to assess our historical and prospective operating performance and to enhance our understanding of our core operating performance. We believe these measures are useful for investors to assess the operating performance of our business without the effect of costs related to the start-up and ramp-up of our manufacturing operations.
