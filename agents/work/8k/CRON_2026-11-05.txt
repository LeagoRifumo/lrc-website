Cronos Group Reports 2026 Third Quarter Results
Organically achieved record net revenue, gross profit, and Adjusted EBITDA while reducing share count
Net revenue in Q3 2026 increased by 73% year-over-year on an organic, constant-currency basis
Generated record net revenue in Canada, with Spinach® maintaining #1 market share in vapes and edibles1
Eleventh consecutive quarter of record net revenue in Israel, where PEACE NATURALS® remains the #1 cannabis brand2
Delivered record net revenue in Germany
TORONTO, November 5, 2026 - Cronos Group Inc. (NASDAQ: CRON) (TSX: CRON) (“Cronos” or the “Company”), today announced its 2026 third quarter business results.
“Cronos organically achieved records across net revenue, gross profit, and Adjusted EBITDA for the second consecutive quarter, while at the same time reducing shares outstanding. In Canada, the Spinach® brand continued to gain significant market share, maintaining its #1 position in vapes for the third consecutive quarter and its #1 position in edibles for the ninth consecutive quarter,1 while also making notable gains in pre-rolls and flower. Cronos Israel delivered its eleventh consecutive quarter of record net revenue, with PEACE NATURALS® maintaining its position as the leading cannabis brand in the country.2 Outside of Israel, our international business also delivered another record net revenue quarter, led by strong demand in Germany,” said Mike Gorenstein, Chairman, President and CEO of Cronos.
“Our strategic focus on the development and production of market-leading borderless products continues to pay off, reflected in another record quarter of results. With the closing of the CanAdelaar acquisition in October 2026, we’ve established a leadership position in Europe’s largest adult-use cannabis market. Integration, including IP transfer, is well underway and we’re excited to work with the CanAdelaar team to continue to bring innovative market-leading products to Dutch consumers. We maintain an industry-leading balance sheet, which, coupled with positive operating cash flow for the first nine months of 2026, enables us to invest in our growth strategy while returning capital to shareholders and maintaining flexibility to execute on attractive acquisition opportunities as they arise. We remain active under our share repurchase program and continue to believe the repurchases represent an attractive use of capital.”

Third Quarter 2026
•Net revenue of $63.5 million in Q3 2026 increased by $27.1 million from Q3 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher cannabis flower sales in Israel and other countries, specifically Germany, which carry no excise taxes, and higher cannabis flower and extract sales in the Canadian market. In addition, net revenue benefited from the strengthening of the New Israeli Shekel versus the U.S. dollar in the current period.
•Gross profit of $32.3 million in Q3 2026 increased by $14.0 million from Q3 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher average sales prices, largely driven by a mix shift to Israel and other countries, which carry no excise taxes, and higher sales volumes. Higher sales volumes led to higher net revenue and efficiencies as fixed overhead costs were spread over greater volumes.
•Net income was $17.8 million in Q3 2026, compared to net income of $28.3 million in Q3 2025. The lower net income was primarily due to lower other income, higher operating expenses, and income tax provision compared to a benefit in the prior year, partially offset by higher gross profit.
•Adjusted EBITDA of $17.1 million in Q3 2026 improved by $11.4 million from Q3 2025. The improvement was primarily driven by higher gross profit, partially offset by higher operating expenses due to higher sales and marketing and general and administrative costs.
Business Updates
Brand and Product Portfolio
Spinach®3
The Spinach® brand remained Canada’s #2 overall cannabis brand in Q3 2026, with national market share expanding to 6.3%. During the quarter, Canadian retail sales of Spinach® branded products increased by 41% year-over-year, relative to nationwide retail sales growth of 1%.
In edibles, Spinach® remained the #1 brand in Canada for the ninth consecutive quarter, with market share rising to 21.8%. In Q3 2026, four SOURZ by Spinach® gummies products ranked among the top 7 edibles nationally, including the top-selling edibles SKU in Canada, the Fully Blasted Blue Raspberry Watermelon 10 Pack.
In September 2026, Cronos expanded the SOURZ by Spinach® product lineup with the launch of the SOURZ by Spinach® Fully Blasted Blue Raspberry Lemonade gummies in Canada, the brand’s first gummy infused with 100% live resin. In October 2026, Cronos brought the seasonal Caramel Green Apple flavor to the SOURZ by Spinach® Fully Blasted portfolio for the first time, with a limited-edition offering available in Canada this fall.
Spinach® remained the #1 vape brand in Canada for the third consecutive quarter, with total vape market share across all formats expanding to 11.4%. Within vape cartridges specifically, Spinach® remained #1 for the fourth consecutive quarter, with market share expanding to 12.1%. In all-in-one vapes, Spinach® continued to gain share following the late 2025 launch of Spinach® PUFFERZ™, with market share increasing to 10.2%. Notably, during Q3 2026, six of the eight best-selling vape SKUs nationwide across all formats were Spinach® vape products.
In Q3 2026, Cronos strengthened its market-leading vape portfolio with the launch of two new flavors in Canada, the Spinach® Strawberry Banana 1.2g vape and the Spinach® Sweet Citrus Punch 1g liquid diamonds vape. Additionally, in October 2026, Cronos re-introduced the fan-favorite seasonal limited-edition Sweet Green Apple 1g vape.
In flower, Spinach® remained #3 in Canada, with market share expanding to 6.1%. During Q3 2026, two Spinach® flower products ranked among the top 7 flower SKUs nationally.
In pre-rolls, Spinach® rose to #5 in Canada, with market share increasing to 3.3%, as the renovation of the brand’s pre-roll assortment continues to gain traction. In the third quarter of 2026, Spinach® ranked #6 in infused pre-rolls, with market share increasing to 3.8%, and ranked #6 in traditional pre-rolls, with market share increasing to 3.0%, led by demand for Spinach® 2x1g offerings.
Medical Cannabis (including PEACE NATURALS® and LIT™)4
Cronos Israel delivered its eleventh consecutive quarter of record net revenue in Q3 2026, with net revenue growing 33% year-over-year (19% growth on a constant-currency basis), as the PEACE NATURALS® brand continued to expand its lead in the Israeli medical cannabis market.
Cronos continued to generate strong growth internationally in Q3 2026, with the Company’s international business ex-Israel delivering record net revenue, increasing 614% year-over-year, led by strong demand for the Company’s flower products, particularly in Germany. The robust growth in sales of Cronos flower products reflects the benefits of Cronos’ advanced genetic breeding program and industry-leading cultivation capabilities, strengths discussed at Cronos’ September 24, 2026 Investor Day, the detailed presentation and replay of which are available on our website at https://ir.thecronosgroup.com/events-presentations.

Transactions
Following the end of the third quarter, the Company completed the acquisition of CanAdelaar B.V. (“CanAdelaar”) on October 15, 2026. Please refer to the associated press release and the Company’s filings on EDGAR and SEDAR+ for additional information. In connection with the acquisition, Christopher Martens, former CEO of CanAdelaar, was appointed General Manager of Cronos Netherlands. Since 2018, Christopher had served as Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder of CanAdelaar, which grew to be the leading operator in the Wietexperiment, the Netherlands’ adult-use cannabis pilot program. Prior to founding CanAdelaar, Christopher held professional roles at Red Bull.

Non-GAAP Measures
Cronos reports its financial results in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles in the United States (“U.S. GAAP”). This press release refers to measures not recognized under U.S. GAAP (“non-GAAP measures”). These non-GAAP measures do not have a standardized meaning prescribed by U.S. GAAP and are therefore unlikely to be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies. Rather, these non-GAAP measures are provided as a supplement to corresponding U.S. GAAP measures to provide additional information regarding our results of operations from management’s perspective. Accordingly, non-GAAP measures should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. All non-GAAP measures presented in this press release are reconciled to their closest reported U.S. GAAP measure. Reconciliations of historical adjusted financial measures to corresponding U.S. GAAP measures are provided below.
Adjusted EBITDA
Management reviews Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, which excludes non-cash items and items that do not reflect management’s assessment of ongoing business performance. Management defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest, tax expense (benefit), depreciation and amortization adjusted for: share of (income) loss from equity method investments; impairment loss on goodwill and intangible assets; impairment loss on long-lived assets; (gain) loss on revaluation of derivative liabilities; (gain) loss on revaluation of financial instruments; gain on revaluation of loan receivable; gain on revaluation of equity method investment; transaction costs related to strategic projects; loss on held-for-sale assets; impairment loss on other investments; foreign currency transaction (gain) loss; other, net; loss from discontinued operations; change in allowance for credit loss on non-operating loan and interest-bearing deposits; restructuring costs; inventory write-downs resulting from restructuring actions; share-based compensation; costs related to the Israel Ministry of Economy and Industry dumping inquiry; purchase accounting adjustment-related inventory step-up adjustments recorded through cost of sales; and restatement litigation costs and reserves related to the restatements of our 2019 and 2021 interim financial statements (the “Restatements”), including the costs related to the settlement of the SEC’s and the OSC’s investigations of the Restatements and legal costs of defending shareholder class action complaints brought against us as a result of the 2019 restatement (see Note 10(b) “Contingencies,” to the condensed consolidated financial statements under Item 1 of our Quarterly Report for a discussion of the shareholder class action complaints relating to the restatement of the 2019 interim financial statements and the settlement of the SEC’s and the OSC’s investigations of the Restatements). Results are reported as total consolidated results, reflecting our reporting structure of one reportable segment.
Management believes that Adjusted EBITDA provides useful insight into underlying business trends and results and facilitates comparison of period-over-period results. Management uses Adjusted EBITDA for planning, forecasting and evaluating business and financial performance, including allocating resources and evaluating results relative to employee compensation targets.
Beginning in 2025, the Company modified the composition of Adjusted EBITDA to exclude the impact of the provision for expected credit losses recognized under ASC 326 with respect to the High Tide Loan (see Note 4 “Loans Receivable, net” to the condensed consolidated financial statements under Item 1 of our Quarterly Report for further information). Management believes that this change provides additional information regarding the Company’s ongoing operational results and enhances comparability with peers that do not routinely extend credit to third parties. Beginning in the third quarter of 2026, the Company modified the composition of Adjusted EBITDA to exclude the impact of the provision for expected credit losses recognized under ASC 326 related to the Company’s guaranteed investment certificates held in cash equivalents, short-term investments, and non-current interest-bearing deposits (see Note 1 “Background, Basis of Presentation, and Summary of Significant Accounting Policies” to the condensed consolidated financial statements under Item 1 of our Quarterly Report for further information). Management determined that excluding these non-cash provisions provides investors with additional insight into period-over-period operating performance by isolating credit-risk movements unrelated to the Company’s core operations.
These changes do not affect the Company’s GAAP financial statements.
