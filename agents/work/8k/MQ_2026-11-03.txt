MARQETA REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
The global modern card issuer reported Total Processing Volume growth of 29%
and Gross Profit growth of 7% in the third quarter of 2026.
OAKLAND, Calif. – November 3, 2026 - Marqeta, Inc. (NASDAQ: MQ), the global modern card issuing platform, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
The Company reported Total Processing Volume (TPV) of $126 billion, representing a year-over-year increase of 29%. Marqeta reported Net Revenue of $176 million and Gross Profit of $122 million, growing 8% and 7% year-over-year, respectively. GAAP Net Income for the quarter was $115 million, which includes a $107 million discrete non-cash income tax benefit from the release of valuation allowances on certain deferred tax assets. Adjusted EBITDA was $39 million, growing 28% year-over-year.
“Our third quarter results demonstrate the durable, differentiated business Marqeta is building, as well as our focus on enhancing the platform that customers trust to build and scale their payment programs,” said Mike Milotich, CEO of Marqeta. “TPV surpassed a $500 billion annualized run rate for the first time, Adjusted EBITDA margin set a new high-water mark, and we delivered our third consecutive quarter of GAAP profitability. From Square's credit card on the American Express Network to Google Wallet for kids, the programs we are launching show how the range, flexibility, and scale of our platform are helping leading companies deliver innovative payment experiences.”
Marqeta has been at the forefront of modern issuer processing for over a decade, enabling growth and innovation for customers across diverse use cases and geographies. Marqeta highlighted several recent updates that demonstrate its current business momentum, including:
•Marqeta now enables the Square Credit Card on the American Express Network, extending a relationship that already includes Square's debit card program. Marqeta's portfolio migration capabilities allowed Square to move existing accounts and card credentials onto Marqeta's platform without disruption to the cardholder experience, giving Square a single platform on which to build and iterate card products for sellers. This program is Marqeta's first on the American Express Network.
•Marqeta expanded its longstanding collaboration with Google to provide a new offering in Google Wallet for kids. Marqeta's tokenization and spend control capabilities enable a safer, digital-first way for kids and teens to receive and spend money without a traditional bank account, with parents able to set daily spending limits, monitor activity, and lock or unlock the balance.
•Marqeta enabled Satispay, a leading payments company in Italy with more than 6.5 million users and 450,000 merchants, to launch its consumer debit card program, allowing users to pay with Satispay anywhere in the world, in-store and online.
•Marqeta is partnering with Coinbase to give its enterprise customers a single platform for payment offerings across fiat and digital asset markets, expanding a relationship in which Marqeta already issues Coinbase’s crypto-backed cards. Coinbase’s infrastructure and global reach strengthen Marqeta’s ecosystem of stablecoin infrastructure partners, which also includes zerohash and BVNK. These partnerships give customers a comprehensive solution for launching multinational, stablecoin-backed cards that connect directly to existing card rails, so stablecoins can be used anywhere a card is accepted.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results:
Total Processing Volume increased by 29% year-over-year, from $98 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to $126 billion for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Net Revenue of $176 million increased by $13 million, or 8%, year-over-year, primarily driven by higher volumes, partially offset by unfavorable mix due to faster growth of card programs where we provide processing services with minimal or no program management.
Gross Profit increased by 7% year-over-year to $122 million from $115 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in Gross Profit was largely driven by our TPV growth. Gross Margin was 69% in the third quarter of 2026.
Net Income of $115 million in the quarter, compared to a Net Loss of $4 million in the same period in the prior year, a year-over-year improvement of $119 million. This includes a $107 million discrete non-cash income tax benefit from the release of valuation allowances on certain deferred tax assets. Net income margin in the quarter was 66%, an increase of 68 percentage points versus last year, which includes the impact of the discrete income tax benefit.

About Marqeta, Inc.
Marqeta (NASDAQ: MQ) enables payments innovation through its enterprise-grade modern card issuing platform. Spanning debit and credit offerings for both consumer and commercial, it provides card issuing and processing, credit services, accounts and money movement, risk and fraud, and user experience capabilities. The Company’s solutions and expertise provide customers with greater control and agility in issuing payment credentials and allow them to build and embed financial services into their branded experience. With compliance and security built-in, Marqeta’s end-to-end configurable platform is proven at scale, processing nearly $400 billion in annual payments volume in 2025 and certified to operate in more than 40 countries. Information Regarding Non-GAAP Measures
In addition to the financial measures prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”), this press release contains certain non-GAAP financial measures. Marqeta considers Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Growth, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted EBITDA Margin based on Gross Profit, Net Income (Loss) Margin based on Gross Profit, and Adjusted operating expenses as supplemental measures of the Company’s performance that are not required by, nor presented in accordance with GAAP.
We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) adjusted, as applicable, to exclude depreciation and amortization; share-based compensation expense; payroll tax related to share-based compensation; restructuring and other one-time costs; non-recurring litigation expense; acquisition-related expenses which consist of due diligence costs, transaction costs and integration costs related to potential or successful acquisitions, and cash and non-cash postcombination compensation expenses; income tax expense (benefit); and other income (expense), net, which primarily consists of interest income from our short-term investments and cash deposits, and realized foreign currency gains and losses. We believe that Adjusted EBITDA is an important measure of operating performance because it allows management and our board of directors to evaluate and compare our core operating results, including our operating efficiencies, from period to period. Additionally, we utilize Adjusted EBITDA as an input into our calculation of our annual employee bonus plans and performance-based restricted stock units.
Adjusted EBITDA Margin is calculated as Adjusted EBITDA divided by net revenue. Adjusted EBITDA Margin based on Gross Profit is calculated as Adjusted EBITDA divided by Gross Profit, and Net Income (Loss) Margin based on Gross Profit is calculated as Net Income (Loss) divided by Gross Profit. Adjusted EBITDA growth represents the year-over-year percentage change in Adjusted EBITDA. These measures are used by management and our board of directors to evaluate our operating efficiency.
We define Adjusted operating expenses as total operating expenses adjusted, as applicable, to exclude depreciation and amortization; share-based compensation expense; payroll tax related to share-based compensation; restructuring and other one-time costs; non-recurring litigation expense; and acquisition-related expenses which consist of due diligence costs, transaction costs and integration costs related to potential or successful acquisitions, and cash and non-cash postcombination compensation expenses. We believe that Adjusted operating expenses is an important measure of operating performance because it allows management and our board of directors to evaluate and compare our core operating results, including our operating efficiencies, from period to period.
Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Growth, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted EBITDA Margin based on Gross Profit, Net Income (Loss) Margin based on Gross Profit, and Adjusted operating expenses should not be considered in isolation, or construed as an alternative to net loss, or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP, or as an alternative to cash flow from operating activities or as a measure of the Company's liquidity. In addition, other companies may calculate Adjusted EBITDA differently than Marqeta does, which limits its usefulness in comparing Marqeta’s financial results with those of other companies.
