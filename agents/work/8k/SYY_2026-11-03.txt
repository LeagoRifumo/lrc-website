SYSCO REPORTS FIRST QUARTER 2027 RESULTS;
REITERATES FY27 ADJUSTED EPS AND LOCAL VOLUME GUIDANCE
HOUSTON, November 3, 2026 - Sysco Corporation (NYSE:SYY) (“Sysco” or the “company”) today announced financial results for its 13-week first fiscal quarter ended September 26, 2026.
Key financial results for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 include the following (comparisons are to the same period in fiscal year 2026):
•Sales increased 2.8%; U.S. Foodservice volume increased 2.6%; U.S. Foodservice local volume increased 2.7%;
•Gross profit increased 3.8% to $4.1 billion;
•Operating income decreased 10.4% to $717 million, and adjusted operating income increased 3.2% to $927 million1;
•Net earnings increased 10.5% to $526 million, and adjusted net earnings increased 4.2% to $574 million1;
•EBITDA increased 20.4% to $1.2 billion2, and adjusted EBITDA increased 5.4% to $1.1 billion1,2; and
•EPS3 increased 10.1% to $1.09, and adjusted EPS1 increased 3.5% to $1.19.
“We are pleased to report a strong start to fiscal 2027. Our Q1 adjusted EPS performance was in-line with our expectations, fueled by positive volume growth, expanding gross margins, and disciplined expense control. We delivered profitable growth across the US and International segments despite continued softness in restaurant foot traffic, higher than expected fuel costs, and lower than expected U.S. food cost inflation. Sysco remains firmly focused on three priorities for the year: a) delivering with excellence for our customers, as well as our core Sysco financial commitments, b) improving our operating margins through AI-powered business transformation, and c) preparing for the anticipated closure of the Jetro Restaurant Depot transaction. I want to thank our entire team for their customer focus, the improvement in our business that they are delivering, and express how excited everyone at Sysco is for the year ahead,” said Kevin Hourican, Sysco’s Chair of the Board and Chief Executive Officer.
“Our teams delivered 2.7% local growth in our U.S. segment and grew adjusted operating income across our USFS and International segments. Our company specific initiatives kept us focused on the elements of performance within our control within a choppy operating environment. Looking ahead, we remain confident in our net cost savings targets enabled by AI technology which include our expectation for $100 million in savings included in our FY27 guidance, with benefits accelerating in the second half. We remain confident in delivering on our adjusted EPS growth guidance of 9%-11%, reflecting unchanged local volume growth assumptions of approximately 2.5%, while full year sales

First Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Results (comparisons are to the same period in fiscal year 2026)
Total Sysco
Sales for the first quarter increased 2.8% to $21.7 billion.
Gross profit increased 3.8% to $4.1 billion, and gross margin increased 18 basis points to 18.6%. Product cost inflation was 0.3% at the total enterprise level, as measured by the estimated change in Sysco’s product costs, primarily in the meat category. The increase in gross profit for the first quarter was primarily driven by growth from local volumes, benefits associated with tariffs, strategic sourcing efficiencies, and Sysco Brand penetration.
Operating expenses increased 7.5%, driven by acquisition-related costs, higher volumes, and investments in higher growth areas of the business. Adjusted operating expenses increased 4.0%1.
Operating income decreased 10.4% to $717 million, driven by acquisition costs related to Jetro Restaurant Depot. Adjusted operating income increased 3.2% to $927 million1.
U.S. Foodservice Operations
The U.S. Foodservice Operations segment results were driven by balanced volume growth, positive Sysco Brand penetration rates, effective margin management, and benefits associated with tariffs.
Sales for the first quarter increased 2.4% to $15.1 billion. Total case volume within U.S. Foodservice increased 2.6% for the first quarter, and local case volume within U.S. Foodservice increased 2.7%.
Gross profit increased 3.2% to $2.9 billion, and gross margin increased 14 basis points to 19.2%.
Operating expenses increased 3.9%, and adjusted operating expenses increased 3.4%1.
Operating income increased 1.6% to $894 million, and adjusted operating income increased 2.7% to $941 million1.
International Foodservice Operations
The International Foodservice Operations segment continued to deliver strong top-line growth, including local volume growth, expanded margins, and double-digit adjusted profit growth.
Sales for the first quarter increased 5.1% to $4.2 billion. On a constant currency basis4, sales for the first quarter increased 6.0% to $4.2 billion. Foreign exchange rates decreased both International Foodservice Operations sales by 0.9% and total Sysco sales by 0.2% during the quarter.
Gross profit increased 7.4% to $887 million, and gross margin increased 45 basis points to 21.3%. On a constant currency basis4, gross profit increased 8.1% to $893 million. Foreign exchange rates decreased both International Foodservice Operations gross profit by 0.7% and total Sysco gross profit by 0.2% during the quarter.
Operating expenses increased 13.1%, and adjusted operating expenses increased 6.3%1. On a constant currency basis4, adjusted operating expenses increased 7.1%. Foreign exchange rates decreased both International Foodservice Operations operating expenses by 0.8% and total Sysco operating expenses by 0.2% during the quarter.

About Sysco
Sysco is the global leader in selling, marketing and distributing food and related products to customers who prepare meals away from home. This includes restaurants, healthcare and educational facilities, lodging establishments, entertainment venues, and more. Sysco operates 333 distribution centers, in 10 countries, with 75,000 colleagues serving approximately 670,000 customer locations. The company generated sales of more than $84 billion in fiscal year 2026 that ended June 27, 2026.
As the world’s largest food-away-from-home distributor, Sysco offers customized supply chain solutions, bespoke specialty product offerings, and culinary support to drive customers to innovate and optimize their operations. We act as a trusted business partner to our customers, helping them grow through our industry-leading portfolio that includes fresh produce, premium proteins, specialty products, sustainably focused items, equipment and supplies, and innovative culinary solutions.
For more information, visit www.sysco.com. For important news and key information for Sysco investors, visit the Investor Relations section of the company’s website at investors.sysco.com.

Sysco Corporation and its Consolidated SubsidiariesNon-GAAP Reconciliation (Unaudited)Impact of Certain Items
The discussion of our results includes certain non-GAAP financial measures, including EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA, that we believe provide important perspective with respect to underlying business trends. Other than EBITDA and free cash flow, any non-GAAP financial measures will be denoted as adjusted measures to remove (1) restructuring charges; (2) expenses associated with our various transformation initiatives; (3) severance charges; and (4) acquisition-related costs consisting of: (a) intangible amortization expense and (b) acquisition costs and due diligence costs related to our acquisitions. Adjustments provided herein for fiscal 2027 results of operations also remove amortization expense associated with debt issuance costs on a bridge loan facility and a gain on deal contingent rate lock transactions entered into to mitigate interest rate risk on permanent debt issued to finance the JRD Acquisition Transactions. No similar charges were applicable in the first quarter of fiscal 2026.
The results of our operations can be impacted due to changes in exchange rates applicable in converting local currencies to U.S. dollars. We measure our results on a constant currency basis. Constant currency operating results are calculated by translating current-period local currency operating results with the currency exchange rates used to translate the financial statements in the comparable prior-year period to determine what the current-period U.S. dollar operating results would have been if the currency exchange rate had not changed from the comparable prior-year period.
Management believes that adjusting its operating expenses, operating income, operating margin, interest expense, other (income) expense, net earnings and diluted earnings per share to remove these Certain Items, presenting its results on a constant currency basis, provides an important perspective with respect to our underlying business trends and results. It provides meaningful supplemental information to both management and investors that (1) is indicative of the performance of the company’s underlying operations and (2) facilitates comparisons on a year-over-year basis.
Sysco has a history of growth through acquisitions and excludes from its non-GAAP financial measures the impact of acquisition-related intangible amortization, acquisition costs and due-diligence costs for those acquisitions. We believe this approach significantly enhances the comparability of Sysco’s results for fiscal 2027 and fiscal 2026.

SYSCO NAMES BRANDON SEWELL CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER
Company reaffirms fiscal 2027 adjusted EPS and local volume guidance
HOUSTON, November 3, 2026 - Sysco Corporation (NYSE:SYY) (“Sysco” or the “company”) today announced that Brandon Sewell, the Company’s Interim Chief Financial Officer since March 2026, has been named Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, effective November 1, 2026.
“Brandon has distinguished himself as a trusted and highly effective leader throughout his twelve years with the company, and when asked to step in as Interim Chief Financial Officer, he performed at a high level,” said Kevin Hourican, Sysco’s Chair of the Board and Chief Executive Officer. “Brandon commands a deep knowledge of our business, broad financial expertise, and strong understanding of how Sysco creates shareholder value. During Brandon’s interim assignment, Sysco delivered strong financial results, launched a multi-year Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Technology business modernization, and successfully financed the Jetro Restaurant Depot acquisition. With these accomplishments, Brandon has earned the trust and confidence of our leadership team, colleagues, Sysco’s board of directors and our investors.”
“I’m honored to be appointed Chief Financial Officer and grateful for the confidence that Kevin and the Board have placed in me,” said Sewell. “Having spent more than a decade at Sysco, I have tremendous conviction in our business, our people and the opportunities ahead. I look forward to building on our strong foundation, maintaining the financial discipline that has supported our industry-leading balance sheet, and deploying our resources to advance our strategic priorities while delivering long-term value for our shareholders.”
About Brandon Sewell
Brandon Sewell has served as Interim Chief Financial Officer of Sysco since March 2026. He joined Sysco in 2014 and has held a series of finance leadership roles with increasing responsibility across the organization. Prior to being named interim CFO, Sewell served as Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of Sysco’s U.S. business, the company’s largest operating segment, where he oversaw financial strategy, planning and performance management. Previously, he led Sysco’s global Financial Planning & Analysis function and held finance leadership roles across the company’s supply chain and merchandising organizations. Sewell brings more than a decade of experience at Sysco and deep knowledge of the company’s operations, financial strategy and business. Brandon has an undergraduate degree from Brigham Young University, and an MBA from Duke’s Fuqua School of Business.
In a separate press release issued today, Sysco announced its earnings results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027, confirming it remained confident in delivering on its previously stated local volume growth of approximately 2.5% and adjusted EPS growth guidance of 9%-11% for the full fiscal year.
