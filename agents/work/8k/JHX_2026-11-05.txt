James Hardie Reports Second Quarter FY27 Results; Delivers Strong Growth and Reaffirms Fiscal 2027 Outlook
Second Quarter Net Sales of $1.564 Billion, including discontinued operations
Second Quarter Highlights (Continuing Operations)
Net Sales of $1.428 Billion, up 24% Year Over Year, at the High End of Guidance
Siding & Trim Net Sales of $880.3 Million, Above the High End of Guidance, Led by Continued Growth in Fiber Cement
Net Income margin of 10.5%; Adjusted EBITDA Margin of 31.8%, Above the High End of Guidance and up 450 Basis Points
Cost Synergies Ahead of Schedule; Revenue Synergies On Track
$111 Million of Term Loan Debt Repaid in the Quarter, Reached FY27 Leverage Target Ahead of Schedule
Consolidated FY27 Net Sales and Adjusted EBITDA Guidance Reaffirmed, Free Cash Flow Target Raised to More than $650 Million
James Hardie Industries plc (NYSE / ASX : JHX) ("James Hardie" or the "Company"), a leading provider of exterior home and outdoor living solutions, today announced results for its second quarter ended September 30, 2026.
In August 2026, the Company announced the strategic divestiture of its European operations and the intended closure of its European fiber cement operations. Europe is therefore presented within discontinued operations, and unless otherwise noted, results and the outlook in this release are on a continuing operations basis, excluding Europe. Beginning in the first quarter FY27, the Company revised the definitions of certain non-GAAP financial measures, including Adjusted EBITDA, to exclude share-based compensation costs.
Aaron Erter, CEO of James Hardie, said, "We delivered a strong second quarter, with sales from continuing operations up 24%, well above the market and at the high end of our guidance range. Adjusted EBITDA was above the guidance range, with margins expanding 450 basis points. North America fiber cement and Deck, Rail & Accessories both delivered double-digit sell-through growth, driven by consistent execution against our sales initiatives, material conversion opportunities and commercial synergies. Australia & New Zealand also delivered strong growth, driven by continued share gains."
Mr. Erter added, "We're focused on what we can control: accelerating material conversion, executing our growth initiatives, and driving continuous improvement through the Hardie Operating System. Our synergy momentum continues to build, with commercial synergies on track to reach our fiscal 2027 target and cost synergies ahead of schedule.
Mr. Erter concluded, "Halfway through the year, we are delivering on the commitments we set for fiscal 2027. While the U.S. housing market remains challenging, particularly new construction, our plan does not depend on a near-term recovery. Based on our broad set of self-help growth levers and our confidence in continuing to outperform the market, we are reaffirming our consolidated fiscal 2027 sales and Adjusted EBITDA outlook and raising our free cash flow target."

Siding & Trim net sales increased 15% compared to the quarter ended September 30, 2025, led by continued fiber cement volume growth, favorable price/mix, and traction from our strategic growth initiatives.
Fiber cement volumes increased mid-single digits in the quarter, with multi-family up high-teens. Growth was driven by share gains against vinyl and other materials, strategic initiatives such as ColorPlus®, trim-over and the rollout of Hardie ProLabs, and the resilience of multi-family. Total exterior volumes grew in nearly every region, even as new construction remained soft across many of the Company’s markets. Fiber cement sell-through increased low double digits in the quarter, well ahead of declining end markets.
Second quarter operating income margin was 26.4%, up 670 basis points year-over-year. Adjusted EBITDA Margin, which excludes inventory fair value adjustments, acquisition and divestiture related restructuring expenses, and amortization of acquired AZEK intangibles, was 34.6%, up 540 basis points year-over-year. The increase was primarily driven by favorable net price and mix realization, volume leverage, and lower manufacturing costs, reflecting favorable input costs and continued savings from the Hardie Operating System ("HOS") and footprint optimization. Disciplined SG&A management contributed further. These gains were partially offset by higher freight and diesel costs.

DR&A net sales increased 51%, or $131.2 million, compared to the quarter ended September 30, 2025, reflecting strong sell-through, the completion of the planned channel inventory normalization in the first quarter and the impact of the initial load-in of our new business with Boise Cascade.

Outlook
Q3 FY27 Guidance and Full Year Planning Assumptions (Continuing Operations)
Ryan Lada, CFO said, “Our second quarter sales growth reflected share gains, continued traction of our growth initiatives and the early benefits of our expanded distribution partnerships. Higher Net Income Margin and the 450 basis point increase in Adjusted EBITDA Margin was driven by favorable volume, price and mix and HOS savings; offset by general inflation and higher freight costs, with Net Income Margin also benefiting from lower acquisition-related expenses.
We are reaffirming our consolidated full-year fiscal 2027 net sales and Adjusted EBITDA guidance and increasing our free cash flow target to more than $650 million. We expect Adjusted EBITDA to be lower in the third quarter and second half driven by DR&A seasonality in Q3, incremental freight costs and the weaker macro environment.
Strong cash generation is accelerating our deleveraging. First-half free cash flow was $535 million and we repaid $111 million of term loan debt in the quarter. Together with the expected proceeds from the Europe divestiture, we remain on track to reach net leverage below 2.0x by the end of the second quarter of fiscal 2028."
We provide certain elements of our outlook on a non-GAAP basis, as we cannot predict some elements that are included in reported GAAP results, including the impact of actuarial estimates on asbestos-related assets and liabilities in future periods. Cash Flow, Capital Investment & Allocation
First half operating cash flow totaled $693.7 million, up from $254.3 million a year ago, driven by net income adjusted for non-cash items of $682.5 million and a $99.2 million working capital inflow, partially offset by $65.2 million of asbestos claims and handling costs paid. Capital expenditures totaled $158.3 million, resulting in Free Cash Flow of $535.4 million for the six month period versus $58.4 million for the prior year period.
Our capital allocation priorities for FY2027 are unchanged: invest in organic growth and reduce leverage. We expect to apply approximately $600 million of proceeds from the Europe divestiture, which is expected to close in the first half of calendar 2027, toward debt repayment. Together with continued free cash flow generation, this is expected to support our target of net leverage below 2.0x by the end of the second quarter of FY2028.
For FY2027, we continue to expect capital expenditures to be in the range of approximately 6% to 7% of net sales, reflecting maintenance and targeted growth investments across the manufacturing network. Our footprint today is well positioned and equipped to support demand across both fiber cement and decking, and we do not anticipate the need for significant new capacity in the near term.
The previously announced closures of our Fontana, California and Summerville, South Carolina facilities are expected to generate approximately $25 million in annualized cost savings for Fiscal Year 2027.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
To supplement our Earnings Release and condensed consolidated financial statements prepared and presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States ("GAAP"), we use certain non-GAAP financial measures, as described in this earnings release, to provide investors with additional useful information about our financial performance, to enhance the overall understanding of our past performance and future prospects and to allow for greater transparency with respect to important metrics used by our management for financial and operational decision-making. We are presenting these non-GAAP financial measures to assist investors in seeing our financial performance and liquidity from management’s view and because we believe they provide an additional tool for investors to use in comparing our core financial performance and liquidity over multiple periods with other companies in our industry.
•Adjusted Net Income: Defined as net income from continuing operations before legacy items such as asbestos related expenses and adjustments, AICF interest income, and significant non-recurring items, such as restructuring gain or expenses, acquisition and divestiture related expenses, pre-close financing costs, non-cash gain on foreign currency forward contracts, inventory fair value adjustment, amortization of intangible assets resulting from AZEK acquisition, as well as share-based compensation expense and adjustments to tax expenses.
•Adjusted EBITDA: Defined as net income from continuing operations before interest, net, other expense (income), net, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization, legacy items such as asbestos related expenses and adjustments, share-based compensation expense, and significant non-recurring items, such as restructuring gain and expenses, acquisition and divestiture related expenses and inventory fair value adjustment. Adjusted EBITDA Margin is equal to Adjusted EBITDA divided by net sales.
•Adjusted Diluted EPS: Defined as Adjusted Net Income divided by weighted average common shares outstanding – diluted, to reflect the conversion or exercise, as applicable, of all outstanding shares of restricted stock awards, restricted stock units and options to purchase shares of our common stock.
•Adjusted Segment EBITDA: Defined as segment operating income before depreciation and amortization and significant non-recurring items such as restructuring expenses, acquisition and divestiture related expenses, inventory fair value adjustment and amortization of intangible assets resulting from AZEK acquisition. The Company does not calculate net income by segment, therefore, Adjusted Segment EBITDA is reconciled to the closest GAAP measure of segment profitability, Segment operating profit.
•Adjusted General Corporate and Unallocated R&D EBITDA: Defined as General Corporate and Unallocated R&D costs before depreciation and amortization, share-based compensation expense, legacy items such as asbestos related expenses and adjustments, and significant non-recurring items such as restructuring gain or expenses, and acquisition and divestiture related expenses. The Company does not calculate net income for General Corporate and Unallocated R&D costs, therefore, Adjusted General Corporate and Unallocated R&D EBITDA is reconciled to the closest GAAP measure of profitability, General Corporate and unallocated R&D costs.
•Adjusted Income Before Income Taxes: Defined as Income before income taxes before share-based compensation expense, legacy items such as asbestos related expenses and adjustments and AICF interest income, and significant non-recurring items such as restructuring gains or expenses, pre-close financing costs, non-cash gain on foreign currency forward contracts, acquisition and divestiture related expenses, inventory fair value adjustment and amortization of intangible assets resulting from AZEK acquisition.
•Adjusted Income Tax Expense: Defined as income tax expense before tax adjustments.

•Net Leverage: Defined as gross debt less cash and cash equivalents, divided by trailing twelve month Adjusted EBITDA.
•Free Cash Flow: Defined as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less purchases of property, plant and equipment plus proceeds from sale of property, plant and equipment.
These non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools, and you should not consider them in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. Non-GAAP financial measures may be calculated differently from, and therefore may not be directly comparable to, similarly titled measures used by other companies. See the accompanying earnings tables for a reconciliation of these non-GAAP measures to their most directly comparable GAAP measures.
The Company is unable to forecast the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP financial measure for future periods due to, amongst other factors, uncertainty regarding the impact of actuarial estimates on asbestos-related assets and liabilities in future periods. Such reconciling items that impact Adjusted EBITDA and Free Cash Flow have not occurred, are outside of our control or cannot be reasonably predicted. Accordingly, a reconciliation of each of Adjusted EBITDA and Free Cash Flow to its most directly comparable GAAP measure is not available without unreasonable effort. However, it is important to note that material changes to these reconciling items could have a significant effect on our Adjusted EBITDA and Free Cash Flow planning assumptions and future GAAP results.
