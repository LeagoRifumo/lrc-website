ANI Pharmaceuticals Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Quarterly net revenues of $290.8 million, an increase of 27.6% year-over-year
•Purified Cortrophin® Gel net revenues of $138.7 million, an increase of 36.1% year-over-year
•Quarterly GAAP net income available to common shareholders of $35.1 million; quarterly adjusted non-GAAP EBITDA of $83.3 million, an increase of 39.8% year-over-year
•Diluted GAAP income per share of $1.51 and adjusted non-GAAP diluted earnings per share of $2.70
•Narrows 2026 total company revenue guidance to $1,085 - $1,125 million, revises Cortrophin Gel revenue guidance to $480 - $500 million and raises both adjusted non-GAAP EBITDA to $290 - $300 million, and adjusted non-GAAP diluted earnings per share to $9.37 to $9.71
•Announced offer to acquire Egetis Therapeutics, expanding the scope and scale of ANI’s Rare Disease business
•Repurchased $20 million in common stock year-to-date
PRINCETON, N.J., November 5, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ANI Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: ANIP) (ANI or the Company) today announced financial results and business highlights for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“In the third quarter, ANI delivered strong financial results, while advancing our transformation into a leading Rare Disease company,” said Nikhil Lalwani, President and CEO of ANI. “Cortrophin Gel delivered solid growth, driven by momentum from the gout expansion and continued progress in our existing specialties, while our Generics business continued to outperform. We also executed on our capital allocation strategy by repurchasing shares and expanding the scope and scale of our Rare Disease business through our offer to acquire Egetis Therapeutics. This acquisition would add an FDA-approved rare disease therapy with significant long-term growth potential, that is poised to address the unmet need of patients with MCT8 deficiency who have no other approved treatment options.”
“As we head into the end of the year, we are focused on delivering our 2026 financial guidance and advancing the proposed acquisition of Egetis toward closing. We are also executing multiple operational and strategic initiatives to achieve approximately 40% year-over-year growth in 2026 for Cortrophin and capture the significant multi-year growth opportunity ahead,” he concluded.
Third Quarter and Recent Business Highlights:
Rare Disease
•Cortrophin Gel:
▪Cortrophin Gel net revenues were $138.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 36.1% over the same period in 2025. Year-over-year growth in the third quarter of 2026 was driven by momentum from the gout expansion and continued progress in the existing specialty areas of Nephrology, Neurology, Rheumatology, Ophthalmology, and Pulmonology.
▪Leading indicators of demand in the gout expansion remained strong, with new gout patient cases doubling quarter-over-quarter and an approximately 67% increase in the number of new prescribers.
▪However, revenue conversion was slower than expected in both existing specialties and gout, due to capacity constraints in the Company’s specialty pharmacy network. The Company is expanding its specialty pharmacy network and patient support capabilities to accelerate the conversion of strong demand into patients on therapy.
•ILUVIEN:
▪ILUVIEN® net revenues were $22.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 34.1% over the same period in 2025 due to increased volumes and phasing of international shipments.
▪The Company announced 6- and 24-month topline data from the Phase 4 open-label single-arm SYNCHRONICITY clinical trial in chronic non-infectious uveitis affecting the posterior segment of the eye (NIU-PS) and presented select secondary endpoint analyses highlighting durable efficacy at the American Academy of Ophthalmology annual conference in October 2026.
Generics
•Generics net revenues were $104.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 10.7% over the same period in 2025, driven by strong market demand for our existing products and new product launches in 2025 and 2026.
•Launched 16 new Generics products year-to-date.
Brand Royalties and Other Revenues
•The Company recognized $11.5 million of revenue under the Harmony Agreement, comprised of $9.5 million of royalties on net sales of pitolisant-based products for the third quarter of 2026 and $2.0 million of revenue associated with the achievement of certain development milestones. In addition, the Company will continue to earn low single digit royalties on net sales of pitolisant-based products.
Brands
•Brands net revenues were $10.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 6.5% compared to the same period in 2025, reflecting a net decrease in demand for certain products.
Corporate Highlights
Offer to Acquire Egetis Therapeutics AB
On November 5, 2026, ANI announced a recommended public offer to acquire the shares in Egetis Therapeutics AB (‘Egetis’) for SEK 11.25 per share, representing a 27% premium to the 60-day VWAP, as well as the convertible debt and the warrants issued by Egetis. The Offer, if consummated, would add EMCITATE® (tiratricol), the first and only approved treatment in the U.S. and Europe for peripheral thyrotoxicosis associated with MCT8 deficiency, a rare genetic disorder, supported by Orphan Drug exclusivity and patent protection into 2045. The value of the Offer, including outstanding incentive programs, amounts to approximately SEK 5.6 billion or approximately $5601 million.
1 Calculated utilizing 30 trading day average USD to SEK exchange rate of 9.9471 as of November 3, 2026.
On November 4, 2026, Egetis announced that it had entered into a definitive asset purchase agreement to sell its Rare Pediatric Disease Priority Review Voucher (PRV) for gross proceeds of $190.0 million (USD).
Upon the closing of the PRV sale, former owners of Rare Thyroid Therapeutics International AB (RTTI) will receive an earn-out of their share under agreements governing Egetis’ acquisition of RTTI. The consideration in the Offer fully reflects the net value of the PRV on the terms of that sale. When adjusted for the estimated PRV net proceeds, the enterprise value reflected in the deal is approximately $4341 million.
The Board of Directors of Egetis unanimously recommends that Egetis’ shareholders and the holder of the convertible and the warrants accept the Offer. In addition, shareholders representing in total approximately 42 percent of the total number of outstanding shares in Egetis, have undertaken to accept the Offer, under certain conditions.
The Offer is subject to customary closing conditions, including that the Offer is accepted by shareholders representing at least 90 percent of the outstanding shares in Egetis, and that ANI receives necessary regulatory approvals.
The Offer is financed in full through a combination of cash on hand and new debt financing provided by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., on terms customary for financing of public offers on the Swedish market.
The acceptance period in the offer is expected to commence on or around November 20, 2026 and to expire on or around December 18, 2026. For information in relation to the Offer, please refer to the formal offer announcement available on www.biopharma-offer.com.

Total net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $290.8 million, an increase of 27.6% over the prior year period.
Net revenues for Rare Disease, which includes Cortrophin Gel and ILUVIEN, increased 35.8% to $160.9 million. Cortrophin Gel net revenues increased 36.1% to $138.7 million and ILUVIEN net revenues increased 34.1% to $22.3 million. Growth for Cortrophin Gel was driven primarily by increased volume.
Net revenues for Brands decreased 6.5% to $10.0 million driven by a net decrease in demand for certain products.
Net revenues from Brand royalties and other revenues include $2.0 million of revenue associated with the achievement of development milestones and royalties of approximately $9.5 million, related to the Harmony Agreement.
Net revenues for Generic pharmaceutical products increased 10.7% to $104.5 million driven by strong market demand for our existing products and new product launches in 2025 and 2026.
On a GAAP basis, gross margin increased from 59.0% to 61.6%, while on a non-GAAP basis, gross margin increased from 59.2% to 61.8%. Both increases were primarily due to a favorable shift in product mix year over year in conjunction with revenue recognized under the Harmony Agreement, tempered by an increase in sales of products that bear a royalty payable.
On a GAAP basis and a non-GAAP basis, research and development expenses increased 5.6% to $13.0 million and 4.7% to $12.3 million, respectively, driven by timing associated with ongoing and new projects to support future growth of our Rare Disease and Generics businesses.
On a GAAP basis, selling, general, and administrative expenses increased 23.8% to $94.9 million, while on a non-GAAP basis, selling, general, and administrative expenses increased 32.4% to $84.2 million. Both comparisons include expense related to expansion of the Rare Disease team, which is targeting opportunities in acute gouty arthritis, and an overall increase in activities to support the growth of our business.
On a GAAP basis, the Company reported net income available to common shareholders of $35.1 million, or $1.51 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $26.3 million, or $1.13 per diluted share, in the prior year period. On a non-GAAP basis, the Company reported adjusted diluted earnings per share of $2.70 for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $2.04 in the prior year period.
Adjusted non-GAAP EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was $83.3 million, an increase of 39.8% from the third quarter of 2025, driven by increased net revenues and gross profit.
