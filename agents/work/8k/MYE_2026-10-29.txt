Myers Industries Announces 2026 Third Quarter Results
Grew Revenue 8.1% as Focused Transformation Initiatives Drive Commercial Excellence Across Growth Platforms
Spike in Resin Costs and Unfavorable Product Mix Pressured Third Quarter Margins; Anticipate Significant Sequential Fourth Quarter Margin Improvement Based on Pricing Actions and Resin Stabilization
Reduced SG&A Expense as a Percent of Revenue Year-over-year, Indicating Sustained Focused Transformation Savings and Disciplined Investment with Growth
Myers Tire Supply Divestiture Simplifies Portfolio and Enables Focus on Growth Platforms to Drive Sustainable Value Creation
October 29, 2026, Akron, Ohio - Myers Industries Inc. (NYSE: MYE), a leading manufacturer of Products that Protect™, today announced results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Myers Industries President and CEO Aaron Schapper commented, “Our Focused Transformation initiatives continued to drive commercial excellence across our growth platforms, resulting in a year-over-year increase in revenue. At the same time, a significant spike in resin cost and unfavorable product mix pressured gross margins in the quarter. Resin costs have since declined and stabilized from their recent peak, while our pricing actions remain in place and continue to support margin recovery. With the completion of our sale of Myers Tire Supply, we have further focused our portfolio on our core specialty engineered products and are driving operational productivity and disciplined cost control to offset the inflationary environment. We expect significant sequential margin improvement in the fourth quarter and the outlook for our end markets remains favorable.”

About Myers Industries
Myers Industries Inc., based in Akron, Ohio, is a leading manufacturer of sustainable plastic and metal Products that Protect™ for Consumer, Vehicle, Food & Beverage, Industrial, and Infrastructure end markets. Myers Industries has a rich history that is built on strong brands and innovative products. Through years of continuous product development and strategic acquisitions, we have established ourselves as a leading diversified industrial company. We provide critical solutions to our customers, delivering exceptional value. Visit www.myersindustries.com to learn more.
