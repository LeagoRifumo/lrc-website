Alliance Laundry Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

All results are for the third quarter of fiscal 2026, and comparisons are year-over-year unless otherwise noted
•Net revenues of $461 million

___________________________________________________________________
RIPON, Wis., November 10, 2026 – Alliance Laundry Holdings Inc. (NYSE: ALH) (“Alliance” or the
“Company”), the global leader in commercial laundry equipment, today announced results for its third
quarter ended September 30, 2026 and updated full-year guidance.
“Our third quarter results reflect our deep commitment to customers and the strength, resilience and
non-discretionary nature of our business model,” said Michael Schoeb, CEO of Alliance. “We
continue to invest in our business by expanding our connected equipment platform, deepening our
distributor partnerships, and supporting the businesses, operators and people who count on our
products every day. In the quarter, North America delivered strong growth, and our International
segment expanded profitability with mixed top-line performance across regions. Demand for our
laundry solutions remains durable, and diversity by product, end market, and geography helps to drive
long term consistent results. Our focus on operational excellence and disciplined capital allocation
positions us well to compound value for shareholders over the long term. Additionally, we are raising
our full-year Adjusted EBITDA guidance to the high end of our previous range and pleased to be
approaching our year-end Net Leverage target even when factoring in the share repurchase completed
in the third quarter.”

THIRD QUARTER 2026 CONSOLIDATED RESULTS
Net revenues increased 5% to $461 million compared to $438 million in the prior year quarter.
Volume and mix benefits accounted for half of this increase in the quarter with pricing contributing to
the balance. Growth in North America was slightly offset by lower International segment revenue. The
majority of international markets performed as expected, with particular strength in the Latin America
region. Foreign currency fluctuation had an approximate one percent negative impact on International
revenue compared to the prior year.
Gross profit increased 12% to $182 million, representing a gross margin of 39.4%, which is an
approximate 240 basis points increase from the prior year quarter. Volume and mix provided a
meaningful benefit, helping to offset ongoing inflationary pressures, alongside pricing actions already
in place. A discrete benefit from tariff refunds in the quarter also helped absorb higher current year
inflationary cost increases. Our local-for-local manufacturing footprint continues to provide a
meaningful structural cost advantage, particularly given the tariff environment.
Net income was $65 million compared to $33 million in the prior year quarter, with Net income
margin of 14.1%. Adjusted Net income increased approximately 57% to $76 million versus $48
million in the prior year quarter, reflecting the growth in operating earnings and approximately $20
million in lower interest expense.
Adjusted EBITDA increased 14% to $127 million, with Adjusted EBITDA Margin of 27.5%, an
increase of approximately 220 basis points versus the prior year quarter. Volume leverage, operational
excellence, and supply chain efficiency drove the margin expansion, as well as the discrete benefit
from tariff refunds in the quarter.
CASH FLOW AND BALANCE SHEET
Operating cash flow for the quarter was $84 million, up from $66 million in the prior year quarter,
reflecting strong operating cash conversion and continued working capital efficiency, consistent with
the Company’s historical performance. During the quarter, the Company paid down $45 million of
debt, bringing total year-to-date debt reduction to $160 million, and repurchased $75 million of
common stock. As of September 30, 2026, total debt was $1.21 billion and net debt was $1.07 billion.
Net Leverage decreased to 2.25x, down from approximately 2.4x at June 30, 2026, and approximately
2.8x as of the prior year end.
THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS BY REPORTABLE SEGMENT
North America revenues increased 8% to $356 million, with Adjusted EBITDA of $115 million and
Adjusted EBITDA margin increasing over 300 basis points versus the prior year. North America
revenue growth was led by Commercial In-Home and On-Premise Laundry, both driven by higher
volume. Commercial In-Home results were driven by consumers choosing the reliability, longevity,
and durability that our commercial heritage delivers. On-Premise Laundry had a strong quarter,
reflecting the nature of its steady, replacement-driven demand. In Vended, which includes laundromat
and multi-housing customers, results reflected the timing of new store projects, including longer
permitting and construction lead times, while customers continued the trend of retooling with larger-
capacity machines. Significant cost pressures persisted during the quarter, led by steel and freight, but
a tariff refund and pricing actions taken late last year helped to offset that inflation, and our U.S.
manufacturing footprint and supply chain remain a structural advantage.
International revenues decreased 1% to $105 million, mainly due to foreign currency, and Adjusted
EBITDA increased to $27 million with Adjusted EBITDA margin of 25.3%. International grew
profitability despite varied top-line performance across regions. Europe was steady, with continued
support from the ongoing replacement and upgrade cycle, plus vended growth across its end markets.
Latin America showed solid growth while Asia Pacific results reflect timing of projects following
strength in prior quarters, with year-to-date revenue up versus the prior year. Middle East & Africa
stabilized in the quarter, supported by the hospitality market and growth in areas not directly affected
by the conflict in the Middle East. Adjusted EBITDA and margin expansion reflected favorable
product and end market mix and positive price versus cost, net of inflation and tariffs. Our local-for-
local manufacturing model also helped mitigate the impact of currency on profitability. While regional
and product mix will continue to make international results variable from quarter to quarter,
underlying financial trends have been positive beyond a single quarter even as we continue to invest in
people and products in emerging markets to support future growth.
UPDATED 2026 FULL YEAR GUIDANCE
The Company’s outlook includes Adjusted EBITDA and Net Leverage, which are non-GAAP
measures. The Company does not provide certain estimated future results for Adjusted EBITDA and
Net Leverage on a GAAP basis because the Company is unable to predict, with reasonable certainty,
certain items that are excluded from Adjusted EBITDA, including but not limited to stock-based
compensation, strategic transaction costs, refinancing and debt-related costs, foreign exchange gain/
loss on intercompany loans, plus interest, taxes, and depreciation and amortization. These items are
uncertain and will depend on several factors, including industry conditions, and could be material to
the Company’s results computed in accordance with GAAP. The Company has not provided
reconciliations between the Company’s 2026 guidance and the most directly comparable GAAP
measures because it would be too difficult to prepare a reliable U.S. GAAP quantitative reconciliation
without unreasonable effort.
Based on the strength of our year-to-date performance and our visibility into the balance of the year,
the Company is raising Adjusted EBITDA guidance for 2026 to the high end of the previous range and
firming up its Net Leverage guidance. Revenue guidance remains unchanged.
Revenue growth guidance versus prior year remains at +6% to 7%. Adjusted EBITDA growth has
been increased to ~10%, the high end of the prior range of +8% to 10%, as the Company realizes the
benefit of volume and mix contributions as well as pricing impact alongside the realization of
continued cost-down initiatives and tariff refunds that have helped address the rising input cost
environment. The Company anticipates Net Leverage of approximately 2.1x by the end of 2026,
absent any other capital allocation opportunities, and includes the $75 million share repurchase
completed during the third quarter. Interest is now expected to total approximately $75 million for
2026 and share count is now expected to be approximately 203 million for fiscal year 2026. Our
Capital Expenditures and Effective Tax Rate assumptions remain unchanged.

ABOUT ALLIANCE LAUNDRY
Alliance Laundry makes the world cleaner as a provider of the highest quality commercial laundry
systems. Our laundry solutions are available under five respected brands, sold and supported by a
global network of select distributors. We serve approximately 150 countries with a team of more than
4,000 employees. Our brands include Speed Queen®, UniMac®, Huebsch®, Primus® and IPSO®.
Together, they present a full line of commercial washing machines, dryers, and ironers (with load
capacities from 20–400 lb. or 9–180 kg.) and support service. You can also enjoy the superior wash
and fabric care of commercial-grade laundry equipment in your home through our legendary Speed
Queen® washers and dryers.
For more information, visit www.alliancelaundry.com.
NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES
We regularly review non-GAAP measures to evaluate our business, measure our performance and
manage our operations, including identifying trends affecting our business, formulating business plans
and making strategic decisions. We believe that non-GAAP measures provide an additional way of
viewing aspects of our operations that, when viewed together with our GAAP results, provide a more
complete understanding of our results of operations and the factors and trends affecting our business.
These non-GAAP financial measures are also used by our management to evaluate financial results
and to plan and forecast future periods. Non-GAAP financial measures should be considered a
supplement to, and not a substitute for, or superior to, the corresponding measures calculated in
accordance with GAAP. Non-GAAP financial measures used by us may differ from the non-GAAP
measures used by other companies, including our competitors.
“Adjusted EBITDA” represents Net income before provision for income taxes, interest expense,
depreciation and amortization and is further adjusted to exclude certain expenses not representative of
our ongoing operations and other charges not involving cash outlays and “Adjusted EBITDA Margin”
represents Adjusted EBITDA divided by Net revenues.
“Adjusted Net income” represents Net income adjusted to exclude certain expenses not
representative of our ongoing operations and other charges. These adjustments include, but are not
limited to, refinancing and debt related costs, stock-based compensation, strategic transaction costs,
intangible amortization, foreign exchange on intercompany loans and other non-recurring items.
“Net Debt” represents our total debt less Cash and cash equivalents.
“Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA” or “Net Leverage” represents total debt less Cash and cash
equivalents divided by Adjusted EBITDA for the relevant period.
SEGMENT INFORMATION
Our business is organized into two reportable segments, North America and International. The
Company uses Segment net revenues, Segment Adjusted EBITDA and Segment Adjusted EBITDA
Margin as its measures of performance. The Company allocates certain costs including manufacturing
variances, customer support expenses and selling and general expenses which are incurred in our
global operations to the reportable segments in determining Segment Adjusted EBITDA.
We define “Segment Adjusted EBITDA” as, on a segment basis, net income excluding interest
income/expense, income taxes, depreciation and amortization. Segment Adjusted EBITDA is also
adjusted for the discrete items that management excluded in analyzing the segments’ operating
performance, such as refinancing and debt related costs, stock-based compensation, strategic
transaction costs, foreign exchange on intercompany loans and other non-recurring items which
management believes are not indicative of the Company’s ongoing operating performance. Segment
Adjusted EBITDA is a measure of operating performance of our reportable segments and may not be
comparable to similar measures reported by other companies.
