Elastic Reports Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results
Continued revenue acceleration during Q2 FY27
cRPO growth of 19% as reported and 20% on a constant currency basis
Expect ongoing GAAP operating margin profitability
SAN FRANCISCO, Nov 19, 2026 -- Elastic (NYSE: ESTC) announced financial results for its second quarter of fiscal 2027 ended October 31, 2026.
Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Highlights
•Total revenue was $496 million, an increase of 17% year-over-year, as reported and on a constant currency basis
•Total subscription revenue was $465 million, an increase of 17% year-over-year, as reported and on a constant currency basis
•Sales-led subscription revenue (calculated as subscription revenue excluding Monthly Elastic Cloud) was $415 million, an increase of 19% year-over-year, as reported and on a constant currency basis
•Current remaining performance obligations were $1.157 billion, an increase of 19% year-over-year, or 20% on a constant currency basis
•Remaining performance obligations were $1.858 billion, an increase of 23% year-over-year, or 24% on a constant currency basis
•GAAP operating income was $22 million; GAAP operating margin was 4.4%; GAAP diluted earnings per share was $0.00
•Non-GAAP operating income was $110 million; non-GAAP operating margin was 22.2%; non-GAAP diluted earnings per share was $0.94
•Operating cash flow and adjusted free cash flow were $44 million
•Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $1.423 billion as of October 31, 2026
“Elastic delivered another strong quarter, beating guidance across all key metrics,” said Ash Kulkarni, CEO of Elastic. “In Q2, we accelerated our AI innovation with new capabilities such as AlertZero, Elastic® nightshift, and Elastic Context Engine, and I’m personally very excited about our agreement to acquire HoneyHive, which will allow us to expand our business in the area of agent observability and evaluations. We are also pleased with the continued growth of our $1M+ customer cohort, similar to the solid net additions we saw during the first quarter. Our strong results reflect expanding customer adoption and position us well to continue our strong execution in the back half of fiscal 2027.”
Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Key Metrics and Recent Business Highlights
Key Customer Metrics
•Total customer count with Annual Contract Value (ACV) greater than $100,000 was over 1,830 compared to over 1,800 in Q1 FY27, and over 1,600 in Q2 FY26
•Net Expansion Rate was approximately 111%
Product Innovations and Updates
•Unveiled Elastic© nightshift AI SRE in private preview, an AI agent built into Elastic Observability that continuously detects and investigates issues across the entire stack, delivering evidence-backed root cause analysis and recommended fixes
•Announced AlertZero in technical preview, a team of specialized AI agents built into Elastic Security to automate alert triage, threat hunting, investigation, detection tuning and forensic analysis, helping security teams accelerate response to evolving cyber threats
•Announced a new optimized serverless offering for Elasticsearch Vector Database for large-scale vector search and AI applications, enabling developers to deploy vector search in minutes and scale affordably to hundreds of billions of vectors
•Made cross-project search generally available for Elastic Cloud Serverless, enabling customers to query data across projects, regions, and cloud providers without moving or duplicating data
•Released jina-ocr-v1, an end-to-end optical character recognition model that converts complex documents, including tables, handwriting, and mathematical notation into structured, machine-readable text across more than 100 languages
•Announced plans to integrate OpenAI GPT cyber models into Elastic Security, bringing specialized cyber reasoning capabilities into existing security workflows to accelerate threat investigation and remediation
•Organizations now face threats that move at AI-speed. We’re announcing that we have joined Anthropic’s Cyber Verification Program to give our security teams an advantage against this new attack surface.
Other Business Highlights
•Appointed Julia Liuson, former president of Microsoft's Developer Division, to Elastic's board of directors, bringing more than three decades of leadership experience across AI, developer platforms, and enterprise technology
•Recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Enterprise AI Search, positioned highest for Ability to Execute and furthest for Completeness of Vision
•Achieved the only perfect protection score in the 2026 AV-Comparatives Endpoint Prevention and Response Test, stopping all 50 attack scenarios at the first phase of compromise with zero false positives
•Announced an agreement to acquire HoneyHive, Inc., an emerging leader in agent observability and evaluation, to help our customers observe, evaluate, and trust the agents they run in production
•Announced a multi-year partnership with Audi Revolut F1® Team, building on the team's existing use of Elastic's search and observability technology to support its engineering software operations
Share Repurchase Program
In October 2025, Elastic announced a share repurchase program pursuant to which the Company may repurchase up to $500 million of the Company’s outstanding ordinary shares. As part of this program, during the second quarter of fiscal 2027, Elastic repurchased approximately 0.6 million ordinary shares at an average price per share of $86.31 on the open market, representing an aggregate value of approximately $55 million. The Board has authorized a new $500 million repurchase program (including approximately $45 million remaining availability under the existing program).
Financial Outlook
During our last guidance, we anticipated FX rates of 1.17 against the Euro and 1.36 against the Pound. The dollar has subsequently strengthened to 1.09 against the Euro, and 1.31 against the Pound. The stronger dollar will result in a total revenue headwind of $4.5 million in Q3, and $5.0 million in Q4, a total of $9.5 million of currency-related headwind in the second half of fiscal 2027.
Against this backdrop, the Company is providing the following guidance:
For the third quarter of fiscal 2027 (ending January 31, 2027):
•Total revenue is expected to be between $507.5 million and $508.5 million, representing 12.9% year-over-year growth at the midpoint (13.9% year-over-year constant currency growth at the midpoint). This guidance incorporates a $4.5 million FX headwind to the midpoint.
•Sales-led subscription revenue is expected to be between $432.0 million and $433.0 million, representing 15.1% year-over-year growth at the midpoint (16.2% year-over-year constant currency growth at the midpoint). This guidance incorporates a $4.0 million FX headwind to the midpoint.
•GAAP operating margin is expected to be positive
•Non-GAAP operating margin is expected to be approximately 21.0%
•Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share is expected to be between $0.90 and $0.92, assuming between 108.5 million and 109.5 million diluted weighted average ordinary shares outstanding
For fiscal 2027 (ending April 30, 2027):
•Total revenue is expected to be between $2.000 billion and $2.008 billion, representing 15.2% year-over-year growth at the midpoint (15.9% year-over-year constant currency growth at the midpoint). This guidance incorporates a $9.5 million FX headwind to the midpoint.
•Sales-led subscription revenue is expected to be between $1.682 billion and $1.690 billion, representing 17.2% year-over-year growth at the midpoint (18.0% year-over-year constant currency growth at the midpoint). This guidance incorporates a $9.0 million FX headwind to the midpoint.
•GAAP operating margin is expected to be positive
•Non-GAAP operating margin is expected to be approximately 19.5%
•Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share is expected to be between $3.35 and $3.41, assuming between 108.0 million and 109.0 million diluted weighted average ordinary shares outstanding
•Adjusted free cash flow margin is expected to be approximately 21.5%, excluding any acquisitions or other one-time charges
The diluted weighted average ordinary shares outstanding reflect only share buybacks completed as of October 31, 2026.
The midpoint of the guidance range assumes the following exchange rates, among others: 1 Euro = 1.09 US Dollars; and 1 Great British Pound = 1.31 US Dollars.

Statement Regarding Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to our results determined in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), we believe the non-GAAP measures discussed below are useful in evaluating our operating performance. We use these non-GAAP financial measures to evaluate our ongoing operations and for internal planning and forecasting purposes. We believe that non-GAAP financial information, when taken collectively, may be helpful to investors because it provides consistency and comparability with past financial performance. However, non-GAAP financial information is presented for supplemental informational purposes only, has limitations as an analytical tool and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with GAAP. In addition, other companies, including companies in our industry, may calculate similarly-titled non-GAAP measures differently or may use other measures to evaluate their performance, all of which could reduce the usefulness of our non-GAAP financial measures as tools for comparison. Investors are cautioned that there are a number of limitations associated with the use of non-GAAP financial measures and key metrics as analytical tools. Investors are encouraged to review the differences between GAAP financial measures and the corresponding non-GAAP financial measures, and not to rely on any single financial measure to evaluate our business and financial results.
Reconciliations of historical GAAP financial measures to their respective historical non-GAAP financial measures are included below. In relation to constant currency non-GAAP financial measures, the only reconciling item between GAAP financial measures and non-GAAP financial measures is the effect of foreign currency rate fluctuations. Further details on how we calculate such effects can be found in the definition of “Constant Currency” below.
Sales-led Subscription Revenue
Sales-led subscription revenue is a non-GAAP financial measure that we calculate as total subscription revenue excluding Monthly Elastic Cloud. We believe sales-led subscription revenue provides management and our investors with a consistent metric with which to measure the health of our business.
Non-GAAP Gross Profit and Non-GAAP Gross Margin
We define non-GAAP gross profit and non-GAAP gross margin as GAAP gross profit and GAAP gross margin, respectively, excluding stock-based compensation expense and related employer taxes, and amortization of acquired intangible assets. We believe non-GAAP gross profit and non-GAAP gross margin provide our management and investors consistency and comparability with our past financial performance and facilitate period-to-period comparisons of operations, as these metrics generally eliminate the effects of certain variables from period to period for reasons unrelated to overall operating performance.
Non-GAAP Operating Income and Non-GAAP Operating Margin
We define non-GAAP operating income and non-GAAP operating margin as GAAP operating income/(loss) and GAAP operating margin, respectively, excluding stock-based compensation expense and related employer taxes, amortization of acquired intangible assets, acquisition-related expenses, and restructuring and other related charges. We believe non-GAAP operating income and non-GAAP operating margin provide our management and investors consistency and comparability with our past financial performance and facilitate period-to-period comparisons of operations, as these metrics generally eliminate the effects of certain variables from period to period for reasons unrelated to overall operating performance.
Non-GAAP Net Income and Non-GAAP Earnings Per Share
We define non-GAAP net income as GAAP income/(loss), excluding stock-based compensation expense and related employer taxes, amortization of acquired intangible assets, acquisition-related expenses, restructuring and other related charges, and the income tax benefit from the release of any valuation allowance against deferred tax assets. Additionally, non-GAAP net income and non-GAAP earnings per share are adjusted for an assumed provision for income taxes based on a projected non-GAAP annual effective tax rate in fiscal 2027 and 2026 of 12% and 13%, respectively. We define non-GAAP earnings per share, basic, as non-GAAP net income divided by weighted average shares outstanding and non-GAAP earnings per share, diluted, as non-GAAP net income divided by weighted average diluted shares outstanding, which includes the potentially dilutive effect of the Company’s employee equity incentive plan awards. We believe non-GAAP earnings per share provides our management and investors consistency and comparability with our past financial performance and facilitates period-to-period comparisons of operations, as this metric generally eliminates the effects of certain variables from period to period for reasons unrelated to overall operating performance.
Adjusted Free Cash Flow and Adjusted Free Cash Flow Margin
Adjusted free cash flow is a non-GAAP financial measure that we define as net cash provided by operating activities adjusted for cash paid for interest on long-term debt less cash used for investing activities for purchases of property and equipment. Adjusted free cash flow margin is calculated as adjusted free cash flow divided by total revenue. Adjusted free cash flow does not represent residual cash flow available for discretionary expenditures since, among other things, we have mandatory debt service requirements.
Constant Currency
We compare the percent change in certain results from one period to another period using constant currency information to provide a framework for assessing how our business performed excluding the effect of foreign currency rate fluctuations. In presenting this information, current and comparative prior period results are converted into United States dollars at the exchange rates in effect on the last day of our prior fiscal year, rather than the actual exchange rates in effect during the respective periods.
