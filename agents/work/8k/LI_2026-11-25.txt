Quarterly total revenues reached RMB28.8 billion (US$4.3 billion)1

Recent Developments
Delivery Update
· | In October 2026, the Company delivered 29,025 vehicles. As of October 31, 2026, in China, the Company had 481 retail stores in 159 cities, 527 servicing centers and Li Auto-authorized servicing shops operating in 217 cities, and 4,206 super charging stations in operation equipped with 23,163 charging stalls.

Product Lineup Update
· | In September and October 2026, the Company launched the new Li MEGA, a high-tech flagship MPV, Li i9, a pioneering flagship family SUV, and 2026 Li i6, a pioneering five-seat SUV. These models feature the Company’s latest proprietary technologies, including the MACH M100 chip and Lianshan Battery, together with other premium configurations. The new Li MEGA, Li i9, and 2026 Li i6 are priced at RMB509,800, RMB369,800, and RMB249,800, respectively. Deliveries of the new Li MEGA and Li i9 began in September, and deliveries of the 2026 Li i6 began in October.

Equity Financing for Chip Business
· | On October 16, 2026, the Company announced the equity financing for Shanghai Mahe Xin Technology Co., Ltd. (“MHX Technology”), a subsidiary of the Company dedicated to its chip and domain controller business. The Company, through a wholly-owned subsidiary, and external investors will invest RMB1.0 billion and RMB625 million respectively in MHX Technology. Further details are available in the Company’s announcement published on the website of HKEX on October 16, 2026.

US$1.0 Billion Share Repurchase Program
· | Pursuant to its US$1.0 billion share repurchase program announced on March 24, 2026, the Company repurchased a total of 10,638,200 Class A ordinary shares at an aggregate consideration of HK$493.3 million on the HKEX and a total of 13,562,312 ADSs (representing 27,124,624 Class A ordinary shares) at an aggregate consideration of US$169.7 million on the Nasdaq in the third quarter of 2026. As of the date of this press release, the Company has repurchased a total of approximately 102.3 million Class A ordinary shares (including approximately 23.7 million ADSs) for an aggregate consideration of approximately US$694.4 million.

Global Expansion
· | The Company reached new globalization milestones, launching its all-new Li L9 in the UAE in September 2026, making its European premiere at the Paris Motor Show in October 2026, and signing agreements with partners across Hong Kong, Thailand, Singapore, the Philippines, and Vietnam in November 2026 to advance its Asia-Pacific expansion. The Company plans to debut the right-hand-drive Li MEGA at the Thailand International Motor Expo and launch it in Hong Kong in December 2026, followed by the start of deliveries of Li 6 (the Europe-spec Li i6) in the Netherlands and Norway in the first quarter of 2027.

Power Day
· | In October 2026, the Company hosted Power Day to introduce its proprietary Lianshan Electric Technology System, covering battery, electric drive, and charging infrastructure. The Company also launched the Lianshan Battery Warranty Program at the event, further underscoring its commitment to user experience and confidence in its proprietary electric technologies.

Financial Results for the Third Quarter of 2026
Revenues
· | Total revenues were RMB28.8 billion (US$4.3 billion) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 5.4% from RMB27.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of 12.3% from RMB25.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

· | Vehicle sales were RMB27.1 billion (US$4.0 billion) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 4.7% from RMB25.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of 12.6% from RMB24.1 billion in the second quarter of 2026. The increase in revenue from vehicle sales over the third quarter of 2025 was primarily due to the increase in vehicle deliveries, partially offset by a lower average selling price due to a different product mix. The increase in revenue from vehicle sales over the second quarter of 2026 was primarily attributable to a higher average selling price due to a different product mix.

· | Other sales and services were RMB1.7 billion (US$259.6 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 16.2% from RMB1.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of 8.8% from RMB1.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026. The increase in revenue from other sales and services over the third quarter of 2025 and the second quarter of 2026 was mainly due to increased provision of services and sales of accessories, which is in line with higher accumulated vehicle sales.

Cost of Sales and Gross Margin
· | Cost of sales was RMB24.0 billion (US$3.6 billion) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 4.9% from RMB22.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of 5.2% from RMB22.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026. The increase in cost of sales over the third quarter of 2025 was primarily due to the increase in vehicle deliveries and a higher average cost of sales due to a different product mix, partially offset by estimated costs related to the recall of Li MEGA in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in cost of sales over the second quarter of 2026 was primarily attributable to a higher average cost of sales due to a different product mix.

· | Gross profit was RMB4.8 billion (US$718.3 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 7.8% from RMB4.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of 69.8% from RMB2.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

· | Vehicle margin was 15.3% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 15.5% in the third quarter of 2025 and 9.4% in the second quarter of 2026. The decrease in vehicle margin over the third quarter of 2025 was mainly due to a different product mix, partially offset by estimated costs related to the recall of Li MEGA in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in vehicle margin over the second quarter of 2026 was mainly attributable to a different product mix.

· | Gross margin was 16.7% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 16.3% in the third quarter of 2025 and 11.0% in the second quarter of 2026.

Operating Expenses
· | Operating expenses were RMB5.5 billion (US$822.7 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing a decrease of 2.3% from RMB5.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of 7.4% from RMB5.1 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

· | Research and development expenses were RMB3.1 billion (US$464.9 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 4.8% from RMB3.0 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of 12.3% from RMB2.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026. Research and development expenses remained relatively stable over the third quarter of 2025. The increase in research and development expenses over the second quarter of 2026 was mainly due to increased costs to support expanding product portfolios and technologies.

· | Selling, general and administrative expenses were RMB2.4 billion (US$352.2 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing a decrease of 14.7% from RMB2.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and an increase of 3.7% from RMB2.3 billion in the second quarter of 2026. The decrease in selling, general and administrative expenses over the third quarter of 2025 was primarily due to decreased employee compensation and rental and other expenses associated with sales and servicing network. The selling, general and administrative expenses remained relatively stable over the second quarter of 2026.

Operating margin was negative 2.4% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with negative 4.3% in the third quarter of 2025 and negative 9.0% in the second quarter of 2026. Net Loss and Net Loss Per Share
· | Net loss was RMB309.7 million (US$46.2 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing a decrease of 50.4% from RMB624.4 million in the third quarter of 2025 and a decrease of 81.8% from RMB1.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026. Non-GAAP net loss was RMB112.9 million (US$16.8 million) in the third quarter of 2026, representing a decrease of 68.6% from RMB359.7 million in the third quarter of 2025 and a decrease of 92.5% from RMB1.5 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

· | Basic and diluted net loss per ADS attributable to ordinary shareholders were both RMB0.32 (US$0.05) in the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB0.62 in the third quarter of 2025 and RMB1.69 in the second quarter of 2026. Non-GAAP basic and diluted net loss per ADS attributable to ordinary shareholders were both RMB0.12 (US$0.02) in the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB0.36 in the third quarter of 2025 and RMB1.49 in the second quarter of 2026.

The change in net cash provided by operating activities over the third quarter of 2025 was mainly due to the decrease in payments made for inventory purchases. The change in net cash provided by operating activities over the second quarter of 2026 was mainly due to the increase in cash received from customers mainly resulting from a higher average selling price.

About Li Auto Inc.
Li Auto Inc. is a leader in China’s new energy vehicle market. The Company designs, develops, manufactures, and sells premium smart electric vehicles. Its mission is: Be Proactive, Change the World. Through innovations in product, technology, and business model, the Company provides families with safe, convenient, and comfortable products and services. Li Auto is a pioneer in successfully commercializing extended-range electric vehicles in China. While firmly advancing along this technological route, it builds platforms for battery electric vehicles in parallel. The Company leverages technology to create value for users. It concentrates its in-house development efforts on proprietary range extension systems, innovative electric vehicle technologies, and smart vehicle solutions. The Company started volume production in November 2019. It offers high-tech flagship family MPVs, Li L series extended-range electric SUVs, and Li i series battery electric SUVs. The Company will continue to expand its product lineup to target a broader user base.
