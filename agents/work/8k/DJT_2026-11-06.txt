Trump Media & Technology Group Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
~ Total Assets of $2.5 Billion and Over $2.3 Billion in Financial Assets* ~
~ Form S-4 Registration Statement for Proposed Merger with TAE Technologies Declared Effective by SEC ~
~ Third Quarter Revenue of $3.0 Million, Up 205%Year-Over-Year ~
~ Company to Host Conference Call Today at 5:00 pm E.T. ~
SARASOTA, Fla., Nov. 9, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Trump Media & Technology Group Corp. (Nasdaq, NYSE Texas: DJT) ("TMTG" or the "Company"), operator of the social media platform Truth Social, the video streaming service Truth+, and the financial services and FinTech brand Truth.Fi, is announcing its financial results for the fiscal quarter ended on September 30, 2026.
TMTG closed the third quarter of 2026 with total assets of $2.5 billion and financial assets* of approximately $2.3 billion comprising cash, restricted cash, short-term investments, equity securities, note receivable and accrued interest, digital assets, and digital assets pledged.
Bolstered by its strong balance sheet, the Company believes it is well positioned to achieve its key strategic priorities, including:
● | Completing its proposed merger with TAE Technologies, Inc. (“TAE”) in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to customary regulatory and closing conditions, including approval by TMTG shareholders;

● | Implementing a more disciplined institutional management approach to the digital asset component of the Company’s treasury framework to preserve long-term strategic exposure while managing volatility and improving the productivity of its balance sheet;

● | Continuing to enhance the Truth Social and Truth+ platforms;

● | Scaling its marketing strategy to leverage the total addressable market for Truth Social and Truth+; and

● | Monetizing its proprietary assets via long-term data licensing product offerings.

TMTG filed a registration statement on Form S-4 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) on September 30, 2026, in connection with the proposed merger with TAE, with amendments filed October 28 and November 3, 2026. The SEC declared the registration statement effective on November 5, 2026, a key milestone toward completion of the merger. TMTG has filed a definitive proxy statement/prospectus and will hold a special meeting on December 14, 2026 (as may be postponed or adjourned from time to time) for shareholders of record as of November 2, 2026 to vote on merger-related matters. Completion of the merger remains subject to required shareholder, stockholder, and regulatory approvals and other closing conditions. In October 2026, TAE drew the remaining $100.0 million available under its amended and restated convertible promissory note with TMTG.
The Company posted $3.0 million in revenue in the third quarter of 2026, up 205% from $1.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Revenue growth was primarily driven by $1.8 million of subscription growth in the Media segment from Truth API, TMTG's first data licensing product, which launched on August 1, 2026, and Truth+, along with $0.2 million in management fees from Truth.Fi ETF offerings.
With legacy legal matters now resolved, legal fees declined to $2.4 million in the third quarter of 2026 from $20.3 million in the third quarter of 2025.
"We’ve remained committed to capitalizing on our business fundamentals, and that focus is paying off,” said Kevin McGurn, Interim CEO of TMTG. “Revenue grew 205% year-over-year on recurring subscription revenue, our cost structure is inherently leaner, and our balance sheet gives us real flexibility. With our S-4 effective and our special meeting set for December 14th, we are advancing an exciting new chapter for our shareholders. Our commitment to building independent, uncancellable media technology remains unwavering, while TAE’s groundbreaking innovations represent an extraordinary opportunity to advance American energy independence. Both reflect the power of American innovation and our commitment to creating long-term shareholder value."
The Company reported $409.7 million of net income for the third quarter of 2026. The bulk of net income reflected non-cash items, including net realized and unrealized gains on digital assets, digital assets pledged, and equity securities ($496.1 million), partially offset by realized and unrealized loss on derivatives ($54.0 million), accreted interest expense ($11.9 million), stock-based compensation ($9.4 million) and investment expenses ($4.0 million), resulting in $12.3 million of Adjusted EBITDA* loss. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company used $28.4 million of cash in operating activities, primarily driven by legal expenses tied to the recently concluded legacy legal matters.
*Financial Assets and Adjusted EBITDA are Non-GAAP Financial Measures, the definitions of which can be found in the Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures section at the end of this release. A reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA to the most comparable GAAP measure can also be found in the Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures section at the end of this release.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The Company uses certain Non-GAAP financial measures, including Financial Assets and Adjusted EBITDA, as we believe these measures can provide meaningful information regarding our operating performance. These Non-GAAP measures should be evaluated in addition to and not as a substitute for our financial results presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP.
Financial Assets is a non-GAAP financial measure that is our Total Assets comprised solely of cash, restricted cash, short-term investments, equity securities, note receivable and accrued interest, digital assets, and digital assets pledged. Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure defined as net income plus realized and unrealized gain/loss on digital assets and digital assets pledged, investment income/loss, interest expense, interest income, provision for income taxes, depreciation and amortization, and stock-based compensation. The Company presents Financial Assets because management believes that it can be a useful metric in understanding the Company’s [financial position] in conjunction with GAAP metrics. The Company presents Adjusted EBITDA because management believes that it can be a useful financial metric in understanding the Company’s earnings from operations. Financial Assets and Adjusted EBITDA are not measures of the Company’s financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered as an alternative to total assets or net income or any other performance measure derived in accordance with GAAP, nor should they be considered measures of liquidity.
