Karat Packaging Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
— Continued Strengthening of Pipeline and Solid Topline Growth —
CHINO, Calif., November 5, 2026 – Karat Packaging Inc. (Nasdaq: KRT) ("Karat Packaging" or the "Company"), a specialty distributor and manufacturer of environmentally friendly, disposable foodservice products and related items, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Quarterly net sales of $134.7 million, up 8.2 percent, from $124.5 million in the prior-year quarter.
•Gross profit of $45.1 million, up 5.2 percent, from $42.9 million in the prior-year quarter.
•Gross margin of 33.5 percent, compared with 34.5 percent in the prior-year quarter.
•Net income of $4.7 million, compared with $7.6 million in the prior-year quarter.
•Net income margin of 3.5 percent versus 6.1 percent in the prior-year quarter.
•Adjusted EBITDA of $8.9 million versus $13.1 million in the prior-year quarter.
•Adjusted EBITDA margin of 6.6 percent versus 10.5 percent in the prior-year quarter.
Guidance
•Net sales for the 2026 fourth quarter expected to increase by 9 to 11 percent from the prior-year quarter.
•Gross margin for the 2026 fourth quarter expected to be between 33 to 35 percent.
•Adjusted EBITDA margin for the 2026 fourth quarter expected to be in the high-single digits.
•Net sales for full-year 2026 expected to increase by 9 to 11 percent from the prior year.
•Gross margin for full-year 2026 expected to be between 39 and 41 percent, including IEEPA tariff refunds recorded during the first half of 2026.
•Adjusted EBITDA margin for full-year 2026 expected to be approximately mid-teens, including IEEPA tariff refunds recorded during the first half of 2026.
“We delivered solid top-line growth in the third quarter, with sales increasing 8.2 percent, and we saw continued strong momentum in our e-commerce business, where online sales grew nearly 38 percent over the prior-year quarter,” said Alan Yu, Chief Executive Officer. “While increased import as well as domestic shipping costs and elevated resin prices pressured margin and contributed to results below our expectations, we remain encouraged by the strength of our sales performance, especially the continued momentum in our e-commerce business expansion.”
“With continued success in new account wins and the strength in our pipeline, we remain confident in our long-term growth prospects. In October, we began shipping select products to the four new chain accounts secured in the second quarter. More notably, we successfully built on the relationships we started with these customers and won additional new business with them across multiple product lines in the third quarter, with shipments expected to commence in early 2027. In addition, we gained four additional chain accounts in the third quarter, with shipments expected to commence in the first half of 2027. These wins further broaden our distribution footprint and strengthen confidence in our expansion strategy.

“Against a backdrop of elevated oil prices and ocean freight costs, we continue to realign Karat’s global supply chain and diversify sourcing toward markets with more favorable trade conditions and freight costs, including Indonesia, Malaysia and South America. We are also implementing price increases on select items this month and in January 2027 to help offset higher costs. We believe these efforts, combined with the continued strength of our online channel, new chain account wins, and the opening of our new Florida distribution center expected in November 2026, position us well to accelerate revenue growth and improve operating performance in the quarters ahead,” Yu added.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Net sales for the 2026 third quarter increased 8.2 percent to $134.7 million, from $124.5 million in the prior-year quarter. The increase was primarily driven by $10.7 million in volume growth and product mix, as well as a $0.4 million favorable year-over-year pricing comparison, partially offset by a decrease of $0.8 million in shipping and logistics revenue.
Cost of goods sold for the 2026 third quarter increased 9.8 percent to $89.6 million, from $81.6 million in the prior-year quarter. The increase was primarily driven by a $7.2 million increase in product costs due to higher sales volume and resin prices, coupled with higher import costs. Ocean freight costs rose $2.4 million, mainly reflecting an 11.7 percent increase in the number of containers imported and a 17.0 percent increase in the average container rate compared with the prior-year quarter, while duty and tariff costs decreased $1.1 million compared with the prior-year quarter.
Gross profit for the 2026 third quarter increased 5.2 percent to $45.1 million, from $42.9 million in the prior-year quarter. Gross margin was 33.5 percent in the 2026 third quarter, compared with 34.5 percent in the prior-year quarter. The decline was primarily driven by higher product costs, which increased to 50.6 percent of net sales from 48.9 percent in the prior-year quarter, partially offset by a decrease in depreciation expense as a percentage of net sales from 1.3 percent in the prior-year quarter to 1.0 percent.
Operating expenses for the 2026 third quarter increased 17.2 percent to $40.3 million, from $34.3 million in the prior-year quarter. The increase was primarily driven by $2.8 million in higher shipping and transportation costs due to increased online shipping costs of $2.2 million resulting from online sales growth and elevated shipping costs, $0.9 million higher online platform fees, and $0.7 million higher online marketing expenses. Other increases included a $0.9 million increase in salaries and benefits, a $0.4 million increase in warehouse expense, and a $0.4 million increase in stock-based compensation expense.
Other income, net, for the 2026 third quarter was $1.0 million, compared with $1.3 million in the prior-year quarter. The decrease was primarily due to a $0.5 million decline in gains on foreign currency transactions.
Net income for the 2026 third quarter was $4.7 million, compared with $7.6 million in the prior-year quarter. Net income margin was 3.5 percent in the 2026 third quarter, compared with 6.1 percent in the prior-year quarter.
Net income attributable to Karat Packaging for the 2026 third quarter was $4.4 million, or $0.22 per diluted share, compared with $7.3 million, or $0.36 per diluted share, in the prior-year quarter.
Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure defined below, was $8.9 million for the 2026 third quarter, compared with $13.1 million for the prior-year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA margin, a non-GAAP measure defined below, was 6.6 percent of net sales for the 2026 third quarter, compared with 10.5 percent for the prior-year quarter.
Adjusted diluted earnings per common share, a non-GAAP measure defined below, was $0.24 per share for the 2026 third quarter, compared with $0.37 per share for the prior-year quarter.
Nine-Month 2026 Financial Results
2
Net sales for the first nine months of 2026 increased 10.2 percent to $388.0 million, from $352.1 million in the same period of 2025. The increase was primarily due to an increase of $36.5 million in volume and changes in product mix, as well as a $2.2 million favorable year-over-year pricing comparison, partially offset by a decrease of $2.8 million in shipping and logistics revenue.
Cost of goods sold for the first nine months of 2026 was $224.2 million, compared with $219.3 million in the same period of 2025. The increase was mainly due to increased product costs of $19.3 million driven by higher sales volume and resin prices, and an increase of $12.0 million in import costs, including duty, tariffs and ocean freight, primarily as a result of an increase in the number of imported containers and a higher average container rate. These increases were partially offset by a decrease of $0.7 million in logistics costs compared with the same period of 2025 and IEEPA tariff refunds of $25.8 million recognized during the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Gross profit for the first nine months of 2026 increased 23.4 percent to $163.8 million, compared with $132.8 million in the same period of 2025. Gross margin was 42.2 percent for the first nine months of 2026, including a 660-basis-point benefit from the IEEPA tariff refunds, compared with 37.7 percent in the same period of 2025. This benefit was partially offset by higher product costs and import costs as a percentage of net sales. Product costs increased to 49.4 percent for the first nine months of 2026 from 49.0 percent, and import costs increased to 13.0 percent from 11.0 percent, compared with the same period of 2025.
Operating expenses for the first nine months of 2026 increased 13.1 percent to $112.9 million, from $99.8 million in the same period of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by a $5.5 million increase in shipping and transportation costs due to higher shipping volume and increased shipping rates, as well as higher salaries and benefits, warehouse expense, online marketing expenses, online platform fees and stock-based compensation expense. Additionally, a net loss of less than $0.1 million was recognized on disposal of property and equipment during the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with a $0.7 million gain in the same period of 2025.
Other income, net, for the first nine months of 2026 was $3.3 million, compared with $0.4 million in the same period of 2025. The increase was mainly driven by a gain on foreign currency transactions of $0.3 million, compared with a loss of $1.9 million during the same period of 2025. In addition, interest income increased $0.3 million due to $0.9 million interest income related to IEEPA tariff refunds recognized in the nine months ended September 30, 2026, partially offset by a decrease of $0.6 million in interest income from investment in certificates of deposit, compared with the same period of 2025.
Net income increased to $41.5 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared with $25.5 million in the same period of 2025. The increase included a contribution of $20.2 million from the IEEPA tariff refunds. Net income margin was 10.7 percent in the first nine months of 2026, compared with 7.2 percent in the same period of 2025.
Net income attributable to Karat Packaging was $40.5 million, or $2.01 per diluted share in the first nine months of 2026, compared with $24.7 million, or $1.22 per diluted share, in the same period of 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure defined below, was $63.0 million in the first nine months of 2026, including a contribution of $25.8 million from the IEEPA tariff refunds, compared with $42.7 million in the same period of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA margin, a non-GAAP measure defined below, was 16.2 percent in the first nine months of 2026, including a contribution of 660 basis points from the IEEPA tariff refunds, compared with 12.1 percent in the same period of 2025.
Adjusted diluted earnings per common share, a non-GAAP measure defined below, was $2.07 per share in the first nine months of 2026, including a contribution of $1.00 per share from the IEEPA tariff refunds, compared with $1.27 per share in the same period of 2025.

About Karat Packaging Inc.
Karat Packaging Inc. is a specialty distributor and manufacturer of a wide range of disposable foodservice products and related items, primarily used by national and regional restaurants and in foodservice settings throughout the United States. Its products include food and take-out containers, bags, tableware, cups, lids, cutlery, straws, specialty beverage ingredients, equipment, gloves and other products. The Company’s eco-friendly Karat Earth® line offers quality, sustainably focused products that are made from renewable resources. Karat Packaging also offers customized solutions, including new product development and design, printing, and logistics services. To learn more about Karat Packaging, please visit the Company’s website at www.karatpackaging.com.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Karat Packaging utilizes certain financial measures and key performance indicators that are not defined by, or calculated in accordance with, GAAP to assess our financial and operating performance. A non-GAAP financial measure is defined as a numerical measure of a company’s financial performance that (i) excludes amounts, or is subject to adjustments that have the effect of excluding amounts, that are included in the comparable measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP in the statement of operations; or (ii) includes amounts, or is subject to adjustments that have the effect of including amounts, that are excluded from the comparable GAAP measure so calculated and presented. The following non-GAAP measures are presented in this press release:
•Adjusted EBITDA is a financial measure calculated as net income excluding (i) interest income, (ii) interest expense, (iii) provision for income taxes, (iv) depreciation and amortization, (v) stock-based compensation expense, and (vi) secondary offering transaction costs.
•Adjusted EBITDA margin is calculated by dividing Adjusted EBITDA by net sales.
•Adjusted diluted earnings per common share is calculated as diluted earnings per common share, plus the per share impact of stock-based compensation and secondary offering transaction costs, and adjusted for the related tax effects of these adjustments.
•Free Cash Flow is calculated as cash from operating activities less cash used in (i) purchases of property and equipment, and (ii) deposits paid for property and equipment.
We believe the above-mentioned non-GAAP measures, which are used by management to assess the core performance of Karat Packaging, provide useful information and additional clarity of our operating results to our investors in their own evaluation of the core performance of Karat Packaging and facilitate a comparison of such performance from period to period. These are not measurements of financial performance or liquidity under GAAP and should not be considered in isolation or construed as substitutes for net income or other cash flow data prepared in accordance with GAAP for purposes of analyzing our financial performance or liquidity. These measures should be considered in addition to, and not as a substitute for, revenue, net income, earnings per share, cash flows or other measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with GAAP. In addition, these non-GAAP financial measures may not provide information that is directly comparable to that provided by other companies, as other companies may calculate such financial results differently.
With respect to our financial targets for the 2026 fourth quarter and 2026 full year adjusted EBITDA margin, a reconciliation of these non-GAAP measures to the corresponding GAAP measures is not available without unreasonable effort due to the variability and complexity of the reconciling items described above that we exclude from these non-GAAP target measures. The variability of these items may have a significant impact on our future GAAP financial results and, as a result, we are unable to prepare the forward-looking statements of income and cash flows prepared in accordance with GAAP, that would be required to produce such a reconciliation.
