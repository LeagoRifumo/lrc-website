Reynolds Consumer Products Reports
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Gross Profit Growth Despite Commodity Inflation
Productivity Gains Fueling Reinvestment
Maintaining Full Year 2026 Revenue & Adjusted EBITDA Outlook
LAKE FOREST, Ill., November 4, 2026 – (BUSINESSWIRE) – Reynolds Consumer Products Inc. (the “Company”) (Nasdaq: REYN) today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“We delivered a solid third quarter in a volatile operating environment, acting decisively to address significant commodity inflation and delivering earnings in-line with our guidance,” said Scott Huckins, President and Chief Executive Officer. “We made further progress on our productivity initiatives, allowing us to continue investing behind our brands, innovation and other strategic initiatives. Disciplined execution remains critical as we navigate the balance of the year, and we remain focused on delivering against our full-year 2026 outlook and establishing a strong foundation for future growth.”
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Net Revenues of $944 million compared to $931 million in Q3 2025
◦Retail Net Revenues of $865 million compared to $864 million in Q3 2025
◦Non-Retail Net Revenues1 of $79 million compared to $67 million in Q3 2025
•Net Income of $58 million compared to $79 million in Q3 2025, and Adjusted Net Income of $83 million compared to $88 million in Q3 2025
•Adjusted EBITDA of $163 million compared to $168 million in Q3 2025
•Earnings Per Share of $0.28 compared to $0.38 in Q3 2025, and Adjusted Earnings Per Share of $0.39 vs. $0.42 in Q3 2025
Net Income decreased to $58 million from $79 million in Q3 2025. Adjusted Net Income, which excludes certain items affecting comparability in each period as shown in the reconciliation tables at the end of this release, was $83 million, at the high end of our outlook, and compares to $88 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA decreased $5 million to $163 million compared to the prior year period, primarily driven by the impact of 7% lower retail volumes and increased SG&A costs, as we continue to reinvest in the business.

Third Quarter Key Business Segment Results
As outlined in our first quarter earnings release, effective January 1, the Company realigned its former Hefty Waste & Storage and Presto Products operating segments to enhance efficiency, sharpen its innovation focus, and better support future expansion into adjacent categories. These changes did not impact previously reported consolidated results. All prior periods are recast to conform with current year presentation.
Reynolds Cooking & Kitchen Essentials
•Net Revenues increased $29 million, or 9%, to $337 million, reflecting increases in both Retail and Non-retail Revenues, and included an 18% price increase to recover input cost increases.
•Adjusted EBITDA decreased $4 million to $49 million, as pricing actions fully offset higher input costs, but lower volumes resulted in the year-over-year decline.
Hefty® Waste & Clean-Up
•Net Revenues increased $4 million to $248 million and included a 5% price increase to recover input cost increases.
•Adjusted EBITDA increased $4 million to $76 million, due to productivity gains across the supply chain.
Hefty® Home & Tableware
•Net Revenues decreased $14 million to $186 million, due to the impact of lower volumes driven by elasticities and continued foam declines.
•Adjusted EBITDA decreased $4 million to $25 million, primarily due to the impact of lower volumes.
Hefty® Storage & Organization
•Net Revenues decreased $8 million to $172 million, reflecting volume timing after strong Q2 promotions.
•Adjusted EBITDA decreased $6 million to $30 million, driven primarily by the impact of lower volumes.
Year to Date 2026 Highlights
•Net Revenues of $2,765 million compared to $2,687 million in the comparable prior year period
◦Retail Net Revenues of $2,556 million compared to $2,517 million in the comparable prior year period
◦Non-Retail Net Revenues of $209 million compared to $170 million in the comparable prior year period
•Net Income of $206 million compared to $184 million in the comparable prior year period, and Adjusted Net Income of $231 million compared to $220 million in the comparable prior year period
•Adjusted EBITDA of $464 million compared to $447 million in the comparable prior year period
•Earnings Per Share of $0.97 compared to $0.87 in the comparable prior year period, and Adjusted Earnings Per Share of $1.09 vs. $1.05 in the comparable prior year period
For the first nine months of 2026, Net Income increased to $206 million from $184 million in the comparable period of 2025. Adjusted Net Income, which excludes certain items affecting comparability in each period as shown in the reconciliation tables at the end of this release, was $231 million compared to Adjusted Net Income of $220 million in the comparable period of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA was $464 million compared to $447 million in the comparable period of 2025, primarily attributable to lower operational costs and the timing of pricing actions in relation to input cost increases, partially offset by the impact of 3% lower retail volumes and higher SG&A costs, as we continue to reinvest across the business.
Owl’s Head Alloys Acquisition
Subsequent to quarter end, we completed the acquisition of Owl’s Head Alloys ("OHA") for $110 million on a cash-free, debt-free basis, subject to customary post-closing adjustments. The acquisition was paid for in cash provided by borrowings under our Revolving Credit Facility. OHA adds specialized aluminum processing capabilities that complement our manufacturing network and expands our vertically integrated capabilities.
Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Highlights
Cash and cash equivalents were $25 million at September 30, 2026 and debt was $1,531 million, resulting in Net Debt of $1,506 million. Net Debt to Trailing Twelve Months Adjusted EBITDA1 was 2.2x on September 30, 2026 and at the lower end of the Company’s target leverage range.
“Through the first nine months of the year, we have grown Adjusted EBITDA to $464 million, improving our profitability through strong productivity gains,” said Nathan Lowe, Chief Financial Officer. “With leverage at the low end of our target range, we are well positioned to continue investing across the business to strengthen our capabilities and extend our competitive position, while supporting long-term cash generation and returns to shareholders.”

Full Year 2026 and Fourth Quarter Outlook
The Company is reiterating its full year 2026 Net Revenues outlook of a range of +1% to +3%, compared to 2025 Net Revenues of $3,721 million. Given the adjustments made during the third quarter, the Company expects Net Income in the range of $307 million to $320 million, with EPS of $1.45 to $1.51. The Company now expects Adjusted Net Income in the range of $332 million to $345 million. Full-year Adjusted EPS guidance is unchanged at $1.57 to $1.63. The Company is reiterating its full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA outlook of $660 million and $675 million.
Based on the full-year outlook, the implied fourth quarter 2026 Net Revenues range is expected to be -3% to +3% compared to fourth quarter 2025 Net Revenues of $1,034 million. Net Income and Adjusted Net Income are expected to be in the range of $101 million to $114 million in the fourth quarter, with EPS and Adjusted EPS expected to be in the range of $0.48 to $0.54. The Company expects fourth quarter Adjusted EBITDA to be in the range of $196 million to $211 million.
Quarterly Dividend
The Company’s Board of Directors has approved a quarterly dividend of $0.23 per common share. The Company expects to pay this dividend on November 30, 2026, to shareholders of record as of November 16, 2026.

About Reynolds Consumer Products Inc.
Reynolds Consumer Products is a leading provider of household essentials designed to simplify daily life, so consumers can enjoy what matters most. Found in 95% of U.S. homes, the Company offers trusted solutions for cooking, serving, clean-up, and storage. Its portfolio features iconic brands like Reynolds® and Hefty®, along with store brand products tailored to retail partners. Reynolds holds the No. 1 or No. 2 market share in most of the categories it serves. Learn more at: investors.reynoldsconsumerproducts.com

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
We use non-GAAP financial measures “Adjusted EBITDA,” “Adjusted Net Income,” “Adjusted Earnings Per Share,” “Net Debt,” and “Net Debt to Trailing Twelve Months Adjusted EBITDA” in evaluating our past results and future prospects. We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income calculated in accordance with GAAP, adjusted for certain items described in the reconciliation tables such as income tax expense, net interest expense, depreciation and amortization and certain unusual items that we do not consider representative of our ongoing operating performance. We define Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Earnings Per Share (“Adjusted EPS”) as Net Income and Earnings Per Share (“EPS”) calculated in accordance with GAAP, adjusted for certain unusual items that we do not consider representative of our ongoing operating performance. We define Net Debt as the current portion of long-term debt plus long-term debt less cash and cash equivalents. We define Net Debt to Trailing Twelve Months Adjusted EBITDA as Net Debt (as defined above) as of the end of the period to Adjusted EBITDA (as defined above) for the period.
We present Adjusted EBITDA because it is a key measure used by our management team to evaluate our operating performance, generate future operating plans and make strategic decisions. In addition, our chief operating decision maker uses Adjusted EBITDA of each reportable segment to evaluate the operating performance of such segments. We use Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted EPS as supplemental measures to evaluate our business’ performance in a way that also considers our ability to generate profit without the impact of certain items. We use Net Debt as we believe it is a more representative measure of our liquidity. We use Net Debt to Trailing Twelve Months Adjusted EBITDA because it reflects our ability to service our debt obligations. Accordingly, we believe presenting these measures provide useful information to investors and others in understanding and evaluating our operating results in the same manner as our management team and board of directors.
Non-GAAP information should be considered as supplemental in nature and is not meant to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the related financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP. In addition, our non-GAAP financial measures may not be the same as or comparable to similar non-GAAP financial measures presented by other companies.
