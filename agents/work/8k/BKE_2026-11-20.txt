THE BUCKLE, INC. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER NET INCOME
KEARNEY, NE -- (BUSINESS WIRE) -- The Buckle, Inc. (NYSE: BKE) announced today that net income for the fiscal quarter ended October 31, 2026 was $48.0 million, or $0.95 per share ($0.94 per share on a diluted basis).
Net sales for the 13-week fiscal quarter ended October 31, 2026 increased 2.6 percent to $329.2 million from net sales of $320.8 million for the prior year 13-week fiscal quarter ended November 1, 2025. Comparable store net sales for the 13-week fiscal quarter ended October 31, 2026 increased 0.6 percent from comparable store net sales for the prior year 13-week period ended November 1, 2025. Online sales decreased 1.4 percent to $52.2 million for the 13-week fiscal quarter ended October 31, 2026, compared to net sales of $53.0 million for the 13-week fiscal quarter ended November 1, 2025.
Net sales for the 39-week fiscal period ended October 31, 2026 increased 4.3 percent to $937.8 million from net sales of $898.7 million for the prior year 39-week fiscal period ended November 1, 2025. Comparable store net sales for the 39-week period ended October 31, 2026 increased 2.5 percent from comparable store net sales for the prior year 39-week period ended November 1, 2025. Online sales increased 1.1 percent to $144.5 million for the 39-week period ended October 31, 2026, compared to net sales of $142.9 million for the 39-week period ended November 1, 2025.
Net income for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 was $48.0 million, or $0.95 per share ($0.94 per share on a diluted basis), compared with net income of $48.7 million, or $0.97 per share ($0.96 per share on a diluted basis) for the third quarter of fiscal 2025.
Net income for the 39-week fiscal period ended October 31, 2026 was $139.3 million, or $2.75 per share ($2.73 per share on a diluted basis), compared with net income of $128.9 million, or $2.57 per share ($2.55 per share on a diluted basis) for the 39-week period ended November 1, 2025.

About Buckle
Buckle is a specialty retailer focused on delivering exceptional service and style through unforgettable experiences. Offering a curated mix of high-quality, on-trend apparel, accessories, and footwear, Buckle is for those living the styled life. Known as a denim destination, each store carries a wide selection of fits, styles, and finishes from leading denim brands, including the Company’s exclusive brand, BKE. Headquartered in Kearney, Nebraska, Buckle currently operates 452 retail stores in 42 states, which includes the opening of two new stores located in Round Rock, Texas and Elizabethtown, Kentucky in fiscal November (after the fiscal quarter ended). As of the end of the fiscal quarter, it operated 450 stores in 42 states compared with 442 stores in 42 states at the end of the third quarter of fiscal 2025.
