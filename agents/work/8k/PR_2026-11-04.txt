Permian Resources Announces Strong Third Quarter 2026 Results and Updated Full Year Guidance
MIDLAND, Texas – November 4, 2026 (BUSINESS WIRE) -- Permian Resources Corporation (“Permian Resources” or the “Company”) (NYSE: PR) today announced its third quarter 2026 financial and operational results and revised 2026 guidance.
Recent Financial and Operational Highlights
•Reported average daily oil production of 208.0 MBbls/d, NGLs of 118.3 MBbls/d and natural gas of 787.1 MMcf/d, or total production of 457.5 MBoe/d
◦Increased oil and total production volumes by 5% and 22% quarter-over-quarter, respectively
•Announced cash provided by operating activities of $1,255 million, cash capital expenditures of $518 million and adjusted free cash flow1 of $846 million
•Demonstrated continued cost control, decreasing quarter-over-quarter LOE by 8% to $5.11 per Boe and cash G&A by 16% to $0.73 per Boe
•Completed ~175 acquisitions, totaling ~24,000 net acres, ~9,000 NRAs and ~200 net 10,000’ locations in the core of the New Mexico Delaware Basin for ~$1.15 billion
•Raised mid-point of full year guidance for oil production to 202.5 MBbls/d and updated mid-point of capital expenditures guidance to $2.025 billion
•Declared quarterly base dividend of $0.16 per share
•Maintained strong balance sheet with leverage1 of ~0.5x
Management Commentary
“This marks another outstanding quarter from the Permian Resources team, with continued execution across the board. During the third quarter, we responded to a strong price environment with higher production across all three commodity streams. Importantly, we were able to generate production growth while maintaining cost discipline, delivering record low total controllable cash costs per Boe,” said Will Hickey, Co-CEO of Permian Resources. “As a result, we generated adjusted free cash flow of $846 million, representing a significant increase compared to the second quarter despite lower oil prices.”
“Throughout the year our team has reacted quickly to higher commodity prices, growing production in order to maximize value for shareholders. Importantly, we have been able to accomplish this through rapid payback initiatives such as increasing working interest, accelerating workover activity and reducing cycle times, while maintaining our current rig and fleet counts,” said James Walter, Co-CEO of Permian Resources. “As a result of our team's relentless focus on driving higher free cash flow, our current plan is expected to generate an incremental $300 million of free cash flow this year compared to our original plan in February, normalized for commodity prices. This is a true testament to our team, and I would like to thank them for their hard work and dedication.”
Financial and Operational Results
During the third quarter, average daily crude oil production was 208,029 barrels of oil per day (“Bbls/d”), a 5% increase compared to the prior quarter. Reported NGL and natural gas volumes increased 37% to 118,312 Bbls/d and 42% to 787,125 Mcf/d, respectively, compared to the prior quarter. Total production was 457,528 barrels of oil equivalent per day (“Boe/d”), a 22% increase compared to the prior quarter. Due to continued elevated pricing,
Permian Resources sought to maximize oil production wherever economically feasible, while maintaining low unit costs. Natural gas and NGL volumes were further enhanced by the Company placing high-GOR wells back online that were curtailed during the second quarter due to negative regional natural gas prices.
Realized prices for the quarter were $85.73 per barrel of oil, $24.22 per barrel of NGL and $0.74 per Mcf of natural gas. Notably, the Company increased the amount of its natural gas volumes priced at Gulf Coast and DFW markets, as incremental firm transportation agreements came into effect. Through the combination of higher prices and higher production volumes, Permian Resources’ total natural gas revenue increased by $141 million to $53 million during the quarter. Similarly, the Company’s NGL revenue increased by $81 million quarter-over-quarter to $264 million.
The Company also demonstrated strong cost control in the third quarter, delivering the lowest controllable cash costs (LOE, GP&T and cash G&A) in Company history of $7.05 per Boe. Third quarter LOE was $5.11 per Boe, GP&T was $1.21 per Boe and cash G&A was $0.73 per Boe. Lower LOE per Boe was driven by the Production Operations team's continued optimization of power and compression projects, which delivered lower costs and record runtimes. Cash G&A per Boe also decreased quarter-over-quarter through continued cost discipline.
Permian Resources remains focused on identifying ways to increase efficiencies, reduce costs and increase recoveries. With over a decade of operating experience and as one of the most active operators in the Delaware Basin, the Company has access to significant historical and leading-edge data that provides proprietary insights. The Company's flat organizational structure results in improvements being deployed rapidly and effectively across its operations. As a result, the Company continues to establish new benchmarks in the field. Permian Resources’ Drilling team drilled the fastest well in Company history during the quarter, averaging over 2,700 feet per day. Additionally, technology continues to positively impact our operations. The Production Operations team has deployed an AI-powered artificial lift monitoring system to increase runtime and optimize well performance. The Company is integrating its proprietary technical and subsurface data with large language models to improve development strategy, evaluate wellbore placement and optimize drilling parameters in real-time. Permian Resources continues to test the potential for increasing recoveries in the Permian Basin. While early, the Company is encouraged by the potential for improved recoveries through a combination of surfactant and chemical deployment, better subsurface data and secondary and tertiary recovery.
Total cash capital expenditures for the third quarter were $518 million, in line with the prior quarter. Permian Resources generated third quarter net cash provided by operating activities of $1,255 million, adjusted operating cash flow1 of $1,364 million and adjusted free cash flow1 of $846 million. Diluted weighted average shares outstanding were 858.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
Continued Ground Game and Bolt-On Acquisition Success
Permian Resources continues to enhance its core New Mexico Delaware Basin position through the addition of high-quality assets at attractive valuations. Since its last update, Permian Resources executed approximately 175 transactions, predominantly located directly offset the Company’s existing core Northern Delaware position. In aggregate, these transactions added approximately 24,000 net acres, 9,000 net royalty acres (“NRAs”) and 13,000 Boe/d of production for upfront cash consideration of $1.15 billion, subject to customary post-closing adjustments, plus potential contingent cash payments tied to future oil prices. These transactions were executed for an acquisition value of $22,000 per net leasehold acre, $14,000 per NRA and $3 million per net 10,000' location at an 80% 8/8ths net revenue interest (“NRI”).
The aforementioned transactions include the acquisition of Ridge Runner Resources II, LLC and its affiliated royalty company (collectively “Ridge Runner”), both portfolio companies of EnCap Investments L.P. (“EnCap”), for initial cash consideration of approximately $1.025 billion. The Ridge Runner business consists of high-quality acreage and high-margin production directly adjacent to Permian Resources’ core New Mexico operating areas, with high-NRI, long lateral locations that immediately compete for capital.
The Ridge Runner transaction highlights the Company’s ability to execute high-quality bolt-ons while continuing to generate attractive full cycle returns at mid-cycle commodity prices. Permian Resources has a long-standing relationship with both the Ridge Runner and EnCap teams, enabling the parties to tailor the transaction to achieve a “win-win” outcome. The final transaction included Ridge Runner's affiliated royalty company that was not originally included in the sale and excluded certain non-core assets. To address continued commodity price volatility, the parties agreed to a commodity price earnout that is further detailed below. The Ridge Runner transaction was finalized when the full year 2027 WTI strip price was approximately $72 per barrel.
Permian Resources continues to execute upon its disciplined acquisition strategy, which leverages its Midland-based Business Development and Land teams, proprietary technical data and peer-leading cost structure to drive returns. In addition to the Ridge Runner acquisition, the Company executed additional bolt-ons and ground game transactions primarily in New Mexico for approximately $120 million.
“Our primary objective is to create shareholder value, and that starts with a sound M&A strategy which delivers full cycle returns and makes our business better not only today but also into the future. The Ridge Runner bolt-on is a perfect example, adding core inventory offset our existing position and driving free cash flow per share accretion over the near, medium and long-term,” said James Walter, Co-CEO. “I’d also like to thank Scott Germann and the entire Ridge Runner team. They built an extraordinary business, and we are excited to get this deal to the finish line. The Ridge Runner management team has been good friends and partners to us over the past ten plus years, and we are excited to find additional ways to work with them in the future.”
Ridge Runner Potential Contingent Payments
The Ridge Runner acquisition includes potential contingent cash payments of up to $125 million, based on quarterly WTI prices over the two-year period starting January 1, 2027. For each quarter in 2027, Permian Resources will pay $9.375 million if the average WTI price exceeds $70 per barrel or $18.75 million if the average WTI price exceeds $80 per barrel. For each quarter in 2028, Permian Resources will pay $6.25 million if the average WTI price exceeds $70 per barrel or $12.5 million if the average WTI price exceeds $80 per barrel.

2026 Operational Plan Update
Permian Resources increased its 2026 oil production target to 202.5 MBbls/d, based on the mid-point of guidance. The increase in full year production guidance is primarily driven by strong new well and base performance, increased working interest and the continuation of higher workover activity. The Company’s updated full year oil guidance represents an increase of 13.5 MBbls/d compared to its initial oil guidance in February. The Ridge Runner bolt-on was producing approximately 6 MBbls/d of oil at closing on October 30, 2026 and is expected to contribute approximately 4 MBbls/d in the fourth quarter.
The Company is also adjusting its cash capital expenditures range to $1.975 – $2.075 billion due to higher working interest associated with its successful ground game efforts, increased workovers and approximately $20 million associated with the Ridge Runner bolt-on. For the fourth quarter of 2026, Permian Resources expects oil production of over 210 MBbls/d with a similar level of cash capital expenditures compared to the third quarter.

PR's Investment Grade Balance Sheet
Permian Resources continues to maintain a strong financial position and low leverage profile. As previously announced, during the quarter the Company redeemed $325 million in principal of legacy Earthstone 9.875% Senior Notes due 2031. Combined with the $550 million legacy Earthstone notes redeemed in the second quarter, these redemptions reduce annual cash interest expense by approximately $75 million.
Subsequent to the third quarter, Permian Resources closed the Ridge Runner bolt-on, which was funded with cash on hand and the issuance of commercial paper. Through continued strong free cash flow generation, the Company expects to have less than 0.5x leverage1 at year-end 2026.
Shareholder Returns
Permian Resources announced today that its Board of Directors declared the Company’s fourth quarter 2026 base dividend of $0.16 per share of Class A common stock or $0.64 per share on an annualized basis. The base dividend is payable on December 31, 2026 to shareholders of record as of December 17, 2026. The Company’s base dividend represents an annualized yield of 2.7% as of November 3, 2026.

About Permian Resources
Headquartered in Midland, Texas, Permian Resources is an independent oil and natural gas company focused on driving peer-leading returns through the acquisition, optimization and development of high-return oil and natural gas properties. The Company’s assets are located in the Permian Basin, with a concentration in the core of the Delaware Basin. Through its position of approximately 560,000 net acres in West Texas and Southeast New Mexico, Permian Resources is the second largest Permian Basin pure-play E&P. For more information, please visit www.permianres.com.

In addition to disclosing financial results calculated in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), our earnings release contains non-GAAP financial measures as described below.
Adjusted EBITDAX
Adjusted EBITDAX is a supplemental non-GAAP financial measure that is used by management and external users of our consolidated financial statements, such as industry analysts, investors, lenders and rating agencies. We define Adjusted EBITDAX as net income attributable to Class A Common Stock before net income attributable to noncontrolling interest, interest expense, income taxes, depreciation, depletion and amortization, impairment and abandonment expense, gains or losses on extinguishment of debt, non-cash gains or losses on derivatives, stock-based compensation, exploration and other expenses and other non-recurring items. Adjusted EBITDAX is not a measure of net income as determined by GAAP.
Our management believes Adjusted EBITDAX is useful as it allows them to more effectively evaluate our operating performance and compare the results of our operations from period to period and against our peers, without regard to our financing methods or capital structure. We exclude the items listed above from net income in arriving at Adjusted EBITDAX because these amounts can vary substantially from company to company within our industry depending upon accounting methods and book values of assets, capital structures and the method by which the assets were acquired. Adjusted EBITDAX should not be considered as an alternative to, or more meaningful than, net income as determined in accordance with GAAP or as an indicator of our operating performance or liquidity. Certain items excluded from Adjusted EBITDAX are significant components in understanding and assessing a company’s financial performance, such as a company’s cost of capital and tax structure, as well as the historic costs of depreciable assets, none of which are components of Adjusted EBITDAX. Our presentation of Adjusted EBITDAX should not be construed as an inference that our results will be unaffected by unusual or nonrecurring items. Our computations of Adjusted EBITDAX may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies.

Adjusted Operating Cash Flow and Adjusted Free Cash Flow
Adjusted operating cash flow and adjusted free cash flow are supplemental non-GAAP financial measures used by management and external users of our consolidated financial statements, such as industry analysts, investors, lenders and rating agencies. We define adjusted operating cash flow as net cash provided by operating activities adjusted to remove changes in working capital and estimated tax distributions to our non-controlling interest owners prior to its elimination during the three months ended March 31, 2026. Adjusted operating cash flow is reduced by total cash capital expenditures to arrive at adjusted free cash flow.
Our management believes adjusted operating cash flow and adjusted free cash flow are useful indicators of the Company’s ability to internally fund its future exploration and development activities, to service its existing level of indebtedness or incur additional debt, without regard to the timing of settlement of either operating assets and liabilities or estimated tax distributions to noncontrolling interest owners after funding its capital expenditures paid for the period. The Company believes that these measures, as so adjusted, present meaningful indicators of the Company’s actual sources and uses of capital associated with its operations conducted during the applicable period. Our computation of adjusted operating cash flow and adjusted free cash flow may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies. Adjusted operating cash flow and adjusted free cash flow should not be considered as alternatives to, or more meaningful than, net cash provided by operating activities as determined in accordance with GAAP or as indicators of our operating performance or liquidity.
