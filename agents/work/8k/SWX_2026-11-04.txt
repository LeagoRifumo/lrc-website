Reaffirms Full-Year 2026 Guidance

Company Completes Nevada and California Rate Cases as Arizona Rate Case Progresses
Great Basin 2028 Expansion Project Remains on Track for 4Q CPCN Filing
LAS VEGAS – November 4, 2026 – Southwest Gas Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: SWX) (“Southwest Gas Holdings” or “Company”) today reported results for its third quarter and nine-months ended September 30, 2026. This earnings press release should be read in conjunction with the Form 10-Q and earnings slides, which are concurrently being posted at www.swgasholdings.com.
“Our strong results give us confidence that we are on track to achieve our full-year guidance. We achieved several important regulatory milestones, completing our Nevada and California rate cases, which resulted in approximately $80 million of combined annual revenue increases, reinforcing regulatory support for infrastructure investments and enhancing earnings visibility,” said Justin Brown, President and CEO of Southwest Gas Holdings. “In Arizona, Commission Staff has recommended $58 million of incremental annual revenue, a 9.75% return on equity and a transition to a formula-rate model. The hearing is scheduled for December and we continue to anticipate new rates becoming effective in April 2027.
“In addition, we advanced our Great Basin 2028 expansion project by filing key resource reports and a draft Fixing America's Surface Transportation Act (“FAST”) 41 application and remain focused on our commitment to project delivery by fourth quarter 2028. Together, these accomplishments demonstrate the disciplined execution of our strategy and position the company to deliver sustainable growth while maintaining our dedication to operational excellence.”

Recent Operational and Financial Highlights
•Delivered strong quarter-over-quarter growth in earnings per share from continuing operations compared with the prior-year period, reflecting balance sheet improvement resulting in lower interest expense, constructive regulatory outcomes, continued infrastructure investment recovery, and disciplined operational execution;
•Maintained a highly flexible and well-capitalized balance sheet, ending the quarter with nearly $0.7 billion in available liquidity to support, among other items, organic growth initiatives and infrastructure investment programs;
•Southwest Gas Corporation (“Southwest Gas”, “Utility”, or “Natural Gas Distribution” segment) delivered Utility return on period-end equity of 8.0% and adjusted Utility return on period-end equity of 8.0% over the 12 months ended September 30, 2026;
•Southwest Gas received a final decision in the Nevada general rate case, with rates effective in October 2026, providing nearly $48 million of incremental annual revenue and supporting recovery of costs and investments made on behalf of customers. •Southwest Gas achieved gross margin of $115.1 million and $571.1 million, and operating margin of $278.1 million and $1.1 billion, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Great Basin 2028 Expansion Project Status
Preparation of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (“FERC”) application for a certificate of public convenience and necessity (“CPCN”) remains on track for filing by year-end. Recent progress includes filing key draft resource reports, submitting a draft FAST-41 application, conducting field surveys and public outreach, and advancing engineering and design.
Shippers have executed binding precedent agreements (“BPAs”) and posted surety supporting 1 billion cubic feet (“Bcf”) per day of contracted demand. Upon FERC approval of the CPCN, these shippers are required to execute Transportation Service Agreements with a minimum term of 20 years. Based on this contracted demand:
•Margin: The project’s estimated $2.3 billion investment is expected to generate approximately $270 million to $300 million of incremental annual margin beginning at the expected fourth quarter 2028 in-service date. Because the project is cost-of-service, the 1 Bcf per day already under contract fully supports this margin.
•Capacity: The 48-inch pipe design serves the contracted 1 Bcf per day and can accommodate nearly 2 Bcf per day with relatively modest additional investment depending on shipper specifications. Additional demand served within this design capacity is expected to lower the rate for all shippers subscribed to the 2028 Expansion Project.
Capital spending is expected to be approximately 15% in 2026, 25% in 2027 and 60% in 2028. Allowance for funds used during construction (“AFUDC”) is expected to accrue until the project is placed in service. The Company does not expect the updated project estimate to materially affect 2026 capital expenditure guidance, and it will update long-term guidance with its annual five-year plan in February 2027.
Demand beyond 2028: Continued economic growth in Northern Nevada has generated approximately 1.8 Bcf per day of additional non-binding expressions of interest for in-service dates from 2029 through 2035, which the Company is working to convert into BPAs. With added compression, the 48-inch pipeline can be expanded to approximately 4 Bcf per day. Contracted demand beyond the current design capacity could create future expansion and capital investment opportunities, subject to posted surety, regulatory approvals and construction schedules. These additional expressions of interest are not expected to affect the current FERC filing schedule.

Southwest Gas / Natural Gas Distribution - Third Quarter 2026
In the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the same period in 2025, the increase in net income of $6.9 million was primarily due to:
•$4.9 million lower Net interest deductions primarily due to lower over-collected purchased gas adjustment (“PGA”) balances for Nevada, lower net deferrals and recoveries associated with the Southern Nevada variable interest expense recovery (“VIER”) mechanism driven by a lower recovery rate in 2026 when compared to the same period in 2025, and higher capitalization of borrowing costs related to debt AFUDC;
•$3.8 million higher Operating margin primarily driven by updated rates that better align with Southwest Gas’ cost of service and capital investments across California adding approximately $3.2 million. Also, there was a $1.8 million increase in recovery on regulatory account balances, with a comparable increase in Depreciation and amortization expense in regulatory account balances, $1.6 million attributable to customer growth for Arizona and Nevada, and $1.5 million increase related to Arizona System Integrity Mechanism (“SIM”). Partially offsetting these increases were $3.9 million decrease attributable to nondecoupled billed margin across Arizona and Nevada, and $1.3 million decrease related to the Southern Nevada VIER mechanism, with a comparable decrease in Net interest deductions; and
•$3.4 million higher Income tax benefit primarily due to a $3.4 million higher deferred state income tax benefit recognized in the current year’s quarter related to a change in state apportionment rates when compared to the prior year’s quarter combined with higher amortization of excess accumulated deferred income taxes.
Partially offset by:
•$2.0 million higher Operations and maintenance expense primarily due to increases in employee-related labor costs of $2.7 million, inclusive of a one-time incentive award of $2.0 million that has been adjusted as a non-GAAP measure, and bad debt expense of $1.7 million. These increases were partially offset by lower net insurance cost of $2.1 million and lower leak survey and line locating expenses;
•$1.8 million, or 2%, higher Depreciation and amortization expense reflecting a $788.2 million, or 7%, increase in gas plant in service since the corresponding third quarter of 2025. The increase in plant was attributable to pipeline capacity reinforcement work, franchise requirements, scheduled pipe replacement activities, and new infrastructure. Also contributing to the increase was a $1.8 million higher amortization of regulatory account balances which is offset in Operating margin. This increase was partially offset by a $5.8 million cumulative adjustment related to certain regulatory assets;
•$1.3 million higher Taxes other than income taxes primarily due to an increase in property taxes in California and Nevada jurisdictions.

Management uses these non-GAAP measures internally to evaluate performance and in making financial and operational decisions. Management believes that its presentation of these measures provides investors greater transparency with respect to its results of operations and that these measures are useful for a period-to-period comparison of results. Management also believes that providing these non-GAAP financial measures helps investors evaluate the Company’s operating performance, profitability, and business trends in a way that is consistent with how management evaluates such performance.
Management also uses the non-GAAP measure, operating margin, related to its natural gas distribution operations. Southwest Gas recognizes operating revenues from the distribution and transportation of natural gas (and related services) to customers. Gas cost is a tracked cost, which is passed through to customers without markup under purchased gas adjustment mechanisms, impacting revenues and net cost of gas sold on a dollar-for-dollar basis, thereby having no impact on Southwest Gas’ profitability. Therefore, management routinely uses operating margin, defined by management as regulated operations revenues less the net cost of gas sold, in its analysis of Southwest Gas’ financial performance. Operating margin also forms a basis for Southwest Gas’ various regulatory decoupling mechanisms. Management believes supplying information regarding operating margin provides investors and other interested parties with useful and relevant information to analyze Southwest Gas’ financial performance in a rate-regulated environment.
