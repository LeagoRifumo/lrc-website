“During the third quarter, we continued to make progress across our Vistance Networks business, supported by ongoing DOCSIS 4.0 deployments and adoption of our next-generation intelligent amplifiers. While memory chip pressures and customer deployment timing continue to impact near-term performance, we remain confident in the long-term demand for our products and opportunities to expand our portfolio. We are also pleased with the significant value we have returned to shareholders following the divestitures of our CCS and RUCKUS businesses. Including our recently announced $1.50 per share special distribution, we will have distributed $16.50 per share in 2026. As of October 30, 2026, we repurchased $225 million of stock under our existing stock repurchase programs. With a strong, debt-free balance sheet, we are well positioned to continue investing in our business and creating additional value,” said Chuck Treadway, President and Chief Executive Officer.
“Aurora Networks delivered $320 million of revenue and $57 million of Adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter. This was generally aligned with our expectations as memory chip pricing and stranded costs were more than offset by a lower variable compensation. Looking ahead, we are revising our full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance to $180 million to $200 million, primarily resulting from customer demand being pushed out into 2027. We remain confident in the underlying demand for our products and are focused on managing costs, maintaining financial flexibility and positioning the business to capitalize on longer-term network upgrade opportunities," said Kyle Lorentzen, Chief Financial Officer.
Through October 30th, the Company has utilized $225 million of the authorization to buy back 28.9 million shares or approximately 13% of the outstanding shares at the end of the second quarter, inclusive of shares that vested with the Ruckus transaction in the third quarter.
Third Quarter Results and Comparisons
Net sales in the third quarter of 2026 decreased 5.4% year-over-year to $319.7 million with decreases in the United States (U.S.), the Caribbean and Latin America (CALA) region and Canada, partially offset by increases in the Europe, Middle East and Africa (EMEA) region and the Asia Pacific (APAC) region.
Income from continuing operations of $54.7 million, or $0.24 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2026, increased compared to loss from continuing operations of $18.9 million, or $(0.16) per diluted share in the same prior year period. Non-GAAP adjusted net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $53.0 million, or $0.23 per diluted share, increased compared to $17.7 million, or $0.06 per diluted share, in the same prior year period.
Aurora operating income decreased $2.5 million or 9.8% to $23.0 million in the third quarter compared to $25.5 million in the same prior year period.
Aurora non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA increased 5.0% to $56.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $54.1 million in the same prior year period. Aurora non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of net sales increased to 17.8% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 16.0% in the same prior year period. Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA increased 128.2% to $56.6 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $24.8 million in the same prior year period. Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of net sales increased to 17.7% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 7.3% in the same prior year period.

Net Sales, Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
•
Aurora net sales of $319.8 million decreased 5.3% compared with the same prior year period driven by a decrease in the legacy business, partially offset by an increase in the Access Technologies business.
•
Aurora orders increased 41% compared with the same prior year period, while backlog totaled $410 million, up 3% compared with the same prior year period.
•
GAAP cash flow used in operations in the third quarter of 2026 was $57.5 million.
•
Free cash flow used in the third quarter of 2026 was $57.7 million after adjusting operating cash flow for $0.2 million of additions to property, plant and equipment. The cash flows related to discontinued operations have not been segregated. Accordingly, this cash flow information includes the results of continuing and discontinued operations.
•
The Company ended the quarter with $683.1 million in cash and cash equivalents.
•
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had no outstanding borrowings under its asset-based revolving credit facility and had availability of $158.9 million, after taking into account the borrowing base limitations and outstanding letters of credit. The Company ended the quarter with total liquidity of approximately $842.0 million.
Stock Repurchase Plan
In addition, the Company announced that its Board of Directors has approved a share repurchase program authorizing the Company to repurchase up to an aggregate of $100 million of its outstanding shares of common stock. The $100 million repurchase authorization is in addition to the $250 million in aggregate repurchase authorizations that were previously announced by the Company. The Company may repurchase shares in the open market, in privately negotiated transactions or in such other manners as determined by the Company, including through repurchase plans complying with the rules and regulations of the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”). The method, timing and amount of any repurchases made under the share repurchase program will depend on a variety of factors, including capital and liquidity requirements, market conditions and alternative uses for cash. The share repurchase program does not obligate the Company to repurchase any dollar amount or number of shares of common stock, and the program may be modified, suspended or discontinued at any time.
