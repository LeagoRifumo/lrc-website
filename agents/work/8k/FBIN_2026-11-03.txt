EXHIBIT 99.1
Fortune Brands Announces Third Quarter Results,
Taking Action to Revitalize the Company's Performance
Highlights:
•
Q3 2026 sales were $1.1 billion, a decrease of 3.1 percent versus Q3 2025; sales excluding the impact of China decreased 2.4 percent
•
Q3 2026 GAAP EPS was $0.42, inclusive of a ($0.47) asset impairment charge
•
Q3 2026 EPS before charges / gains was $1.14, inclusive of a $0.28 net tax benefit and $0.14 of net tariff refunds1
•
Took portfolio optimization actions related to Fiberon, the Company's China business and EMEA operating footprint
•
Recently implemented leadership changes to strengthen execution and enable greater focus on individual business performance
•
Updating full year 2026 guidance, reflecting actions to set up the Company for future success
DEERFIELD, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--November 3, 2026--Fortune Brands Innovations, Inc. (NYSE: FBIN) (“Fortune Brands” or the “Company”), an industry-leading home, security and digital products company, today announced third quarter 2026 results.
“Fortune Brands is in the early stages of a multi-year turnaround plan to get Back to Business. We are building from a strong foundation of brands that consumers and pros know and trust. Third quarter results were in line with our outlook excluding the tax and tariff benefits, but not where they need to be. Our biggest challenge has been our own execution, and we are taking aggressive steps to drive improvement,” said Fortune Brands Chief Executive Officer Jesse Singh.
“Service levels improved 10 percentage points in the third quarter, with fill rates in the high-90% range entering the fourth quarter. We have realigned the organization around customer-focused segments and expect to have more than 80% of our salaried workforce directly supporting a specific business by year end, up from roughly 40% at the start of the year. In addition, we have strengthened the leadership team with a new Chief Financial Officer, Chief Information Officer and Chief Legal Officer, and new leaders within each of our businesses. We remain on track for $70 million of annualized SG&A savings by the first quarter of 2027. We also entered into a purchase agreement to sell Fiberon, are exiting our commercial operations in China, which we expect to be completed by the third quarter of 2027 and are rationalizing the EMEA operating footprint of a business in our Water segment, which we expect to be completed by the fourth quarter of 2027. Together these businesses generate an annualized operating loss of approximately $50 million to $55 million on revenue of $320 million to $330 million.
“In the fourth quarter, we expect incrementally lower revenue, driven by three things: lower sales from the businesses involved in the portfolio optimization actions, incremental softening in single-family new construction, and a reduction in channel inventory. We are using this quarter’s net tax benefit to fund additional portfolio and alignment actions in the fourth quarter. The work ahead is substantial, but the opportunity is large: we have leading brands, customers and channel partners who want to win with us, and a team that is engaged and moving with urgency. I am confident that our actions will build a stronger, simpler and more customer-focused company that delivers consistent growth, margin expansion and shareholder value over the long term.”

2026 Full-Year Guidance
“Looking ahead, we see a softer environment for single-family new construction and expect this part of the market to be down mid to high single digits, with R&R down low single digits for the year. Customers are signaling greater caution and we are taking a more conservative approach to channel inventory given the uncertain macroeconomic environment. We have also made important decisions on dilutive parts of our portfolio, which we expect will help improve future profitability. Those businesses will continue to have an impact on our results for the fourth quarter and the first half of next year. With these factors in mind, we are prudently updating our guidance for net sales and EPS before charges / gains. Fourth quarter and full year net sales are expected to be in the range of $925 million to $995 million and $4,205 million to $4,275 million, respectively. Fourth quarter and full year EPS before charges / gains is expected to be in the range of $0.19 to $0.29 and $3.20 to $3.30, respectively. We also expect full year operating cash flow and free cash flow to be in the range of $480 million to $500 million and $380 million to $410 million, respectively," said Fortune Brands Chief Financial Officer Pete Clifford. "While these actions will weigh on our near-term results, they position us to enter 2027 on stronger footing."

Definitions of Terms: Non-GAAP Financial Measures
(a) Operating income (loss) before charges/gains is calculated as operating income (loss) derived in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), excluding restructuring, other charges/gains, asset impairment charges, manufacturing facility fire, transformation costs and governance advisory services and leadership transitions. Operating income (loss) before charges/gains is a measure not derived in accordance with GAAP. Management uses this measure to evaluate the returns generated by the Company and its business segments. Management believes this measure provides investors with helpful supplemental information regarding the underlying performance of the Company from period to period. This measure may be inconsistent with similar measures presented by other companies.
(b) Free cash flow is cash flow from operations calculated in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP") less capital expenditures. Free cash flow does not include adjustments for certain non-discretionary cash flows such as mandatory debt repayments. Free cash flow is a measure not derived in accordance with GAAP. Management believes that free cash flow provides investors with helpful supplemental information about the Company's ability to fund internal growth, make acquisitions, repay debt and related interest, pay dividends and repurchase common stock. This measure may be inconsistent with similar measures presented by other companies.
(c) EBITDA before charges/gains is calculated as net income (loss) in accordance with GAAP, excluding depreciation, amortization of intangible assets, restructuring and other charges/gains, interest expense, asset impairment charge, manufacturing facility fire, transformation costs, governance advisory services and leadership transitions, and income taxes. EBITDA before charges/gains is a measure not derived in accordance with GAAP. Management uses this measure to assess returns generated by the Company. Management believes this measure provides investors with helpful supplemental information about the Company's ability to fund internal growth, make acquisitions and repay debt and related interest. This measure may be inconsistent with similar measures presented by other companies.
(d) Net debt is calculated as long-term debt less cash and cash equivalents. Net debt is a measure not derived in accordance with GAAP. Management believes this supplemental measure is useful as it reflects the Company's debt obligations after considering cash and cash equivalents available to repay such obligations.
(e) Net debt-to-EBITDA before charges/gains ratio is calculated as net debt divided by EBITDA before charges/gains for the trailing 52 weeks. Management believes net debt-to-EBITDA before charges/gains is a useful measure of the Company's leverage position because it provides investors with helpful supplemental information about the Company's ability to service and repay outstanding debt using earnings from the underlying performance of the Company. This measure may be inconsistent with similar measures presented by other companies.
(f) Diluted earnings (loss) per share before charges/gains is calculated as net income on a diluted per-share basis, excluding restructuring, other charges/gains, asset impairment charges, manufacturing facility fire, transformation costs, governance advisory services and leadership transitions and a discrete tax impact of the China commercial exit. This measure is not in accordance with GAAP. Management uses this measure to evaluate the Company's overall performance and believes it provides investors with helpful supplemental information about the Company's underlying performance from period to period. However, this measure may not be consistent with similar measures presented by other companies.
(g) Operating margin is calculated as operating income in accordance with GAAP, divided by the GAAP net sales. The operating margin before charges/gains is calculated as operating income, excluding restructuring, other charges/gains, asset impairment charges, manufacturing facility fire, transformation costs, governance advisory services and leadership transitions, divided by GAAP net sales. The operating margin before charges/gains is not a measure derived in accordance with GAAP. Management uses this measure to evaluate the returns generated by the Company and its business segments. Management believes that this measure provides investors with helpful supplemental information about the Company's underlying performance from period to period. However, this measure may not be consistent with similar measures presented by other companies.
