GRAHAM HOLDINGS COMPANY REPORTS
THIRD QUARTER EARNINGS

ARLINGTON, VA - Graham Holdings Company (NYSE: GHC) today reported its financial results for the third quarter of 2026. The Company also filed its Form 10-Q today for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 with the Securities and Exchange Commission.
Division Operating Results
Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $1,326.0 million, up 4% from $1,278.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. The Company reported operating income of $85.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $67.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. The Company reported adjusted operating cash flow (non-GAAP) of $121.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $110.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. Revenues, operating results and adjusted operating cash flow increased at television broadcasting, healthcare, manufacturing, automotive and other businesses, partially offset by a decline at education. Capital expenditures totaled $39.6 million and $20.2 million for the third quarter of 2026 and 2025, respectively.
Revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was $3,864.5 million, up 6% from $3,660.5 million in the first nine months of 2025. The Company reported operating income of $227.0 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared to $187.4 million for the first nine months of 2025. Revenues and operating results increased at television broadcasting, healthcare, manufacturing, automotive and other businesses, partially offset by a decline at education. The Company reported adjusted operating cash flow (non-GAAP) of $354.5 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared to $309.5 million for the first nine months of 2025. Adjusted operating cash flow increased at television broadcasting, manufacturing, automotive and other businesses, partially offset by declines at education and healthcare. Capital expenditures totaled $80.2 million and $54.1 million for the first nine months of 2026 and 2025, respectively.
Disposition of Business
In the third quarter of 2026, Kaplan entered into an agreement to sell Dublin Business School (DBS) to China Chunlai Education Group Co., Ltd., a provider of private higher education in China. At September 30, 2026, the Company classified the assets and liabilities of DBS as held for sale. The transaction closed on October 27, 2026. Under the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement, Kaplan sold all of the outstanding shares of Accountancy & Business College (Ireland) Limited, which operates DBS, together with certain intellectual property, for $127.5 million.
Debt, Cash and Marketable Equity Securities
At September 30, 2026, the Company had $853.4 million in borrowings outstanding at an average interest rate of 5.8%, including $186.2 million outstanding on its $400 million revolving credit facility. Cash, marketable equity securities and other investments totaled $1,246.2 million at September 30, 2026, excluding DBS cash classified as held for sale.
Overall, the Company recognized $15.0 million in net losses and $17.9 million in net gains on marketable equity securities in the third quarter and first nine months of 2026, compared to $84.8 million and $117.0 million in net gains on marketable equity securities in the third quarter and first nine months of 2025.
Common Stock Repurchases
During the first nine months of 2026, the Company purchased a total of 176,529 shares of its Class B common stock at a cost of $198.6 million. At September 30, 2026, there were 4,185,665 shares outstanding. On September 12, 2024, the Board of Directors authorized the Company to acquire up to 500,000 shares of its Class B common stock; the Company has remaining authorization for 285,953 shares as of September 30, 2026.
Overall Company Results
The Company reported net income attributable to common shares of $75.1 million ($17.60 per share) for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $122.9 million ($27.91 per share) for the third quarter of 2025.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL INFORMATION
GRAHAM HOLDINGS COMPANY
(Unaudited)
In addition to the results reported in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (GAAP) included in this press release, the Company has provided information regarding Adjusted Operating Cash Flow and Net income excluding certain items described below, reconciled to the most directly comparable GAAP measures. Management believes that these non-GAAP measures, when read in conjunction with the Company’s GAAP financials, provide useful information to investors by offering:
•the ability to make meaningful period-to-period comparisons of the Company’s ongoing results;
•the ability to identify trends in the Company’s underlying business; and
•a better understanding of how management plans and measures the Company’s underlying business.
Adjusted Operating Cash Flow and Net income, excluding certain items, should not be considered substitutes or alternatives to computations calculated in accordance with and required by GAAP. These non-GAAP financial measures should be read only in conjunction with financial information presented on a GAAP basis.
The gains and losses on marketable equity securities relate to the change in the fair value (quoted prices) of its portfolio of equity securities. The mandatorily redeemable noncontrolling interest represents the ownership portion of a group of minority shareholders at a subsidiary of the Company's Healthcare business. The Company measures the redemption value of this minority ownership on a quarterly basis with changes in the fair value recorded as interest expense or income, which is included in net income for the period. The effect of gains and losses on marketable equity securities and net interest expense related to fair value adjustments of the mandatorily redeemable noncontrolling interest are not directly related to the core performance of the Company’s business operations since these items do not directly relate to the sale of the Company’s services or products. GAAP requires that the Company include the gains and losses on marketable equity securities and net interest expense related to fair value adjustments of the mandatorily redeemable noncontrolling interest in net income on the Condensed Consolidated Statements of Operations. The Company excludes the gains and losses on marketable equity securities and net interest expense related to fair value adjustments of the mandatorily redeemable noncontrolling interest from the non-GAAP adjusted net income because these items are independent of the Company’s core operations and not indicative of the performance of the Company’s business operations.
