Everus Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results, Raises 2026 Guidance
BISMARCK, N.D. — Nov. 3, 2026 — Everus Construction Group (NYSE: ECG) today reported financial results for third quarter 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Summary
(All comparisons versus the prior-year period unless otherwise noted, and results denoted with * are quarterly records.)
•Revenues of $1.38 billion*, up 40.3%.
•Net income of $91.9 million*, up 61.2%; net income margin of 6.6%.
•Diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $1.79*, up 61.3%.
•Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA) of $140.2 million*, up 57.5%; EBITDA margin of 10.1%.
•Backlog of $5.48 billion*, up 69.8% from Dec. 31, 2025, and up 86.1% from Sept. 30, 2025.
•Net leverage of 0.7x.
•On Sept. 1, 2026, Everus completed the acquisition of Epsilon Industries.
See the Non-GAAP Measures sections for definitions and reconciliations of the non-GAAP financial measures used in this news release.

EBITDA increased 58%, while EBITDA margin expanded 110 basis points, reflecting our team’s ongoing commitment to the Everus operational playbook.

Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Results
Revenues increased 40.3% to $1.38 billion in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $986.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding the contribution from 2026 acquisitions, revenues increased 35.8% on an organic basis in the third quarter of 2026. Electrical and mechanical (E&M) revenues expanded $368.4 million, or 48.0%, and transmission and distribution (T&D) revenues were up $32.2 million, or 14.4%.
1
Gross profit increased 65.1% to $205.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $124.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily from revenue growth and gross margin improvement due to increased workloads, solid project execution, project timing and a dispute resolution on prior billings that drove a $7.6 million net benefit. Gross margin was 14.8% in the third quarter of 2026, up compared to 12.6% in the third quarter of 2025.
Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses increased 55.8% to $80.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $51.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was driven by higher incentive compensation of $13.4 million, primarily resulting from increase business performance; $6.7 million of acquisition-related costs; and higher amortization expenses of $5.7 million from acquisitions.
Net income increased 61.2% to $91.9 million, or diluted EPS of $1.79, in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $57.0 million, or diluted EPS of $1.11, in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily from higher gross profit, partially offset by higher SG&A expenses, higher income taxes on greater pretax income, and lower other income. Net income margin was 6.6% in the third quarter of 2026, up compared to 5.8% in the third quarter of 2025.
EBITDA increased 57.5% to $140.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $89.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily from higher gross profit, partially offset by higher SG&A expenses. EBITDA margin was 10.1%, up compared to 9.0% in the third quarter of 2025.
Backlog increased to $5.48 billion as of Sept. 30, 2026, up 69.8% compared to $3.23 billion as of Dec. 31, 2025, and up 86.1% compared to $2.95 billion as of Sept. 30, 2025. Acquisitions completed during the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, contributed estimated backlog of $742.3 million.
Third Quarter 2026 Segment Results
Electrical and Mechanical
E&M segment revenues increased 48.0% to $1.14 billion in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $767.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding the contribution from 2026 acquisitions, E&M segment revenues increased 42.3% on an organic basis in the third quarter of 2026. The increase in revenues was primarily driven by the commercial and industrial end markets, particularly continued growth in the data center and high-tech submarkets, partially offset by lower revenues in the renewables end market.
E&M segment net income increased 73.3% to $88.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $50.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by segment revenue growth and gross profit from gross margin improvement, project timing and efficient project execution, partially offset by higher SG&A expenses, particularly incentive compensation and labor; amortization from the acquisitions; higher income taxes on greater pretax income; and lower other income. E&M segment net income margin was 7.8%, up compared to 6.6% in the third quarter of 2025.
E&M segment EBITDA increased 79.1% to $119.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $66.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by segment revenue growth and higher gross profit due to gross margin improvement, project timing and efficient project execution, partially offset by higher SG&A expenses and lower other income as previously mentioned. E&M segment EBITDA margin was 10.5%, up compared to 8.7% in the third quarter of 2025.
E&M backlog increased to $5.11 billion as of Sept. 30, 2026, up 79.7% compared to $2.84 billion as of Dec. 31, 2025, and up 98.9% compared to $2.57 billion as of Sept. 30, 2025. Acquisitions completed during the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, contributed estimated backlog of $743 million.
Transmission and Distribution
T&D segment revenues increased 14.4% to $255.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $223.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by higher revenues in the utility end market, particularly in the transmission, distribution and underground submarkets, partially offset by lower revenues in the transportation end market.
T&D segment net income increased 13.3% to $23.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $20.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by higher segment revenues and gross profit, partially offset by higher income taxes on greater pretax income. T&D segment net income margin was 9.0%, consistent with the third quarter of 2025.
T&D segment EBITDA increased 13.3% to $38.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $33.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by higher segment revenues and gross profit. T&D segment EBITDA margin was 15.0%, consistent with the third quarter of 2025.
T&D backlog decreased to $371.3 million as of Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $384.5 million as of Dec. 31, 2025, and compared to $375.8 million as of Sept. 30, 2025.

Nine Months Ended Sept. 30, 2026, Consolidated Results
Revenues increased 33.6% to $3.65 billion for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $2.73 billion for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025. Excluding the contribution from 2026 acquisitions, revenues increased 30.7% on an organic basis for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026. E&M revenues rose $852.1 million, or 40.0%, and excluding the contribution from 2026 acquisitions, E&M revenues increased 36.4% on an organic basis. T&D revenues grew $66.6 million, or 10.7%.
Gross profit increased 54.1% to $518.6 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $336.6 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025. The increase was primarily from revenue growth and gross margin improvement due to increased workloads, solid project execution, project timing, and a dispute resolution on prior billings which drove a $7.6 million net benefit. Gross margin was 14.2% for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, up compared to 12.3% for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025.
SG&A expenses increased 45.6% to $204.9 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $140.7 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025. The increase was driven primarily by higher incentive compensation of $28.2 million, primarily resulting from increased business performance; acquisition-related costs of $10.2 million; higher amortization expenses of $8.9 million from acquisitions; and increased labor costs, excluding incentive compensation, of $8.4 million to support continued operational growth.
Net income increased 59.8% to $234.1 million, or diluted EPS of $4.57, for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $146.5 million, or diluted EPS of $2.87, for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025. The increase was primarily from increased gross profit, partially offset by higher SG&A expenses and higher income taxes on greater pretax income. Net income margin was 6.4% for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, up compared to 5.4% for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025.
EBITDA increased 52.2% to $357.7 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $235.0 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025. The increase was primarily from increased gross profit, partially offset by higher SG&A expenses. EBITDA margin was 9.8% for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, up compared to 8.6% for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025.
Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Commentary
Balance Sheet
As of Sept. 30, 2026, the company had $244.8 million of unrestricted cash and cash equivalents and $571.5 million of gross debt, compared to $152.7 million and $285.0 million, respectively, as of Dec. 31, 2025.
During the third quarter of 2026, the company entered into an amended credit agreement that increased the revolving credit facility by $125.0 million. In connection with the amended credit agreement, the company drew $100.0 million under the credit facility, which was still outstanding as of Sept. 30, 2026. As a result, the company had $247.8 million available under the revolving credit facility, net of $2.2 million of outstanding standby letters of credit. As of Dec. 31, 2025, the company had $222.8 million available under the revolving credit facility, net of $2.2 million of outstanding standby letters of credit.
Net leverage, defined as net debt-to-trailing 12-month EBITDA, was 0.7x as of Sept. 30, 2026, compared to 0.4x as of Dec. 31, 2025.
Working capital, defined as current assets minus current liabilities, was $612.3 million as of Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $560.2 million as of Dec. 31, 2025. The working capital changes were driven by the 2026 acquisitions, project timing, workload activity and billing fluctuations, primarily from increased receivables and cash, partially offset by increased net contract liabilities, accounts payables and accrued compensation.
Cash Flow
Operating cash flows were $267.3 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $108.6 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025. The increase was primarily from favorable changes in operating assets and liabilities to support company growth, including revenue growth, and increased operating results with higher non-cash items, including amortization expenses from the acquisitions.
Capital expenditures were $53.4 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $42.1 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025. The increase was primarily from increased vehicle and equipment investments to support the company's growth, partially offset by a prefabrication-related investment made during the first quarter of 2025.
Everus had free cash flow of $221.2 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, compared to $74.8 million for the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2025. The increase was primarily from higher operating cash flows, partially offset by higher net capital expenditures.
Investing cash flows included net cash outflows of $380.5 million related to acquisitions completed during the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026.

Forecast for 2026
Because of the strong results for the first three quarters of the year and the acquisitions completed during 2026, Everus is raising its guidance and now expects:
•Revenues to be in the range of $5.0 billion to $5.1 billion, updated from $4.5 billion to $4.7 billion.
•EBITDA to be in the range of $485 million to $495 million, updated from $410 million to $425 million.
Everus still expects gross capital expenditures to be in the range of $90 million to $100 million, representing between 1.8% to 2.0% of forecasted revenues.

About Everus Construction Group
Everus Construction Group, Inc., a member of the S&P SmallCap 600® index, is Building America's Future® by providing a full spectrum of construction services through its electrical and mechanical, and transmission and distribution specialty contracting services across the United States. These specialty contracting services are provided to commercial, industrial, institutional, renewables, service, transportation, utility and other customers. Its E&M contracting services include construction and maintenance of electrical and communication wiring and infrastructure, fire suppression systems, and mechanical piping and services. Its T&D contracting services include construction and maintenance of overhead and underground electrical, gas and communication infrastructure, as well as the manufacture and distribution of transmission line construction equipment. For more information about Everus, visit everus.com or email investors@everus.com.

Backlog at any given point in time may not accurately represent revenue or net income realized in any period, and backlog as of the end of the year may not be indicative of revenue or net income expected to be realized in the following year. EBITDA and EBITDA Margin
Everus utilizes EBITDA and EBITDA margin to consistently assess its operating performance and as a basis for strategic planning and forecasting since the company believes EBITDA closely correlates to long-term enterprise value. Everus believes that measuring performance on an EBITDA basis is useful to investors because it enables a more consistent evaluation of its period-to-period operational performance. Everus also believes these non-GAAP financial measures, in addition to the corresponding GAAP measures of net income and net income margin, are useful to investors and provide meaningful information about operational efficiency by excluding the impacts of differences in tax jurisdictions and structures, debt levels and capital investment. Investors also may use EBITDA to calculate leverage as a multiple of EBITDA. Management uses EBITDA and EBITDA margin, in addition to GAAP metrics, to evaluate the company's operating results, calculate compensation packages and determine leverage as a multiple of EBITDA to establish the appropriate funding of operations.
EBITDA is calculated by adding back interest expense, net of interest income, income taxes, and depreciation and amortization to net income. EBITDA margin is calculated by dividing EBITDA by operating revenues.

Net Debt and Net Leverage
Everus uses net debt and net leverage as measures of assessing its borrowing capacity and achieving its optimal capital structure. The company believes these non-GAAP financial measures, in addition to the corresponding GAAP measures of total debt and gross leverage, are useful to investors because they provide insight into how long it would take the company to pay back its debt if net debt and EBITDA were constant.
Net debt is calculated by adding unamortized debt issuance costs to the total debt balance on the balance sheet, less any unrestricted cash. Net leverage is calculated by dividing net debt by trailing 12-month EBITDA.

Free Cash Flow
Everus uses free cash flow as a measure of liquidity that indicates how much cash the company can produce after taking cash outflows from operations and assets into consideration. The company believes this non-GAAP financial measure, in addition to the corresponding GAAP measure of cash provided by (used in) operating activities, is useful to investors because it provides meaningful information about the company’s financial health and ability to generate cash, support additional debt obligations, pay potential future dividends and fund growth. Free cash flow does not represent residual cash flow available for discretionary purposes.
Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less net capital expenditures.
