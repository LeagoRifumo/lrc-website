Rapport Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financials and Provides Business Update
Positive topline results from Phase 2 trial of RAP-219 in bipolar mania; initiation of two Phase 3 trials in bipolar mania expected in 2H 2027
Data from the Phase 2 FOS open-label long-term safety trial expected in 4Q 2026; Phase 3 FOS trials enrolling patients
Well-capitalized with $815.4 million1 in cash and cash equivalents as of November 3, 2026, and runway through the end of 2029
BOSTON and SAN DIEGO, November 4, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Rapport Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: RAPP) (“Rapport” or the “Company”), a clinical-stage biotechnology company dedicated to the discovery and development of small molecule precision medicines for patients with neurological and psychiatric disorders, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update, including a summary of the recently announced positive topline results from its Phase 2 trial of RAP-219 in adults with acute bipolar mania and updates across the RAP-219 development program and pipeline.
“We continue to deliver on our priorities with strong execution across the portfolio,” said Abraham N. Ceesay, chief executive officer of Rapport. “Our positive Phase 2 results in bipolar mania are transformational for Rapport, accelerating our path to becoming an industry-leading precision neuroscience company. RAP-219 now has clinical proof-of-concept in two indications across neurology and psychiatry, giving us the opportunity to serve patients in two multibillion-dollar markets. We are building on this momentum, with a steady cadence of catalysts ahead. With the additional capital from our recent financing, we are well funded to deliver on the catalysts ahead and remain committed to delivering sustained value for both patients and shareholders.”

RAP-219 in Bipolar Mania
•
Positive Phase 2 Topline Results. On October 20, 2026, the Company reported topline results from its randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 2 trial of RAP-219 in adults with bipolar I disorder (BD) (N=253) experiencing an acute manic episode.
Exhibit 99.1
–
The trial met its primary endpoint, with a statistically significant 3.1-point placebo-adjusted least squares (LS) mean improvement in Young Mania Rating Scale (YMRS) total score at Week 3 (p=0.045), and its pre-specified secondary endpoint, with a statistically significant 0.4-point improvement over placebo in Clinical Global Impression-Bipolar Version Severity of Mania score at Week 3 (p=0.024).
–
RAP-219-treated patients had a 15.4-point LS mean reduction in YMRS total score from baseline at Week 3 (vs 12.3 points with placebo), consistent with the magnitude of improvement reported in recent trials of approved acute mania treatments.
–
All 11 YMRS items improved on RAP-219 compared to placebo, with improvements in irritability, thought content, disruptive-aggressive behavior, and sexual interest reaching nominal significance (p<0.05).
–
RAP-219 was well tolerated; all treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) were mild or moderate and there were no serious adverse events. 3.2% of patients on RAP-219 discontinued due to a TEAE (vs 0.8% on placebo).
•
Regulatory Path and Phase 3 Plans. Rapport plans to meet with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in the first quarter of 2027 to discuss the Phase 2 data and registrational trial design for RAP-219 in adults experiencing acute mania associated with BD, with initiation of two Phase 3 trials in acute bipolar mania expected in the second half of 2027.

About RAP-219
RAP-219 is an investigational and potential first-in-class, clinical-stage TARPγ8-specific AMPA receptor (AMPAR) negative allosteric modulator (NAM). Whereas AMPARs are distributed widely in the central nervous system, the receptor associated protein (RAP) TARPγ8 is expressed only in discrete brain regions, including the hippocampus and neocortex, which are implicated in neurological and psychiatric disorders. By contrast, TARPγ8 has minimal expression in the hindbrain, where drug effects are often associated with intolerable adverse events. With this precision approach, the Company believes RAP-219 has the potential to provide a differentiated profile as compared to traditional neuroscience medications. The Company believes RAP-219’s selective targeting of TARPγ8 and the role of AMPA biology across multiple disorders give it pipeline-in-a-product potential. RAP-219 has demonstrated proof-of-concept in both focal onset seizures and bipolar mania, with Phase 3 trials ongoing and planned, respectively. A Phase 3 trial in primary generalized tonic-clonic seizures is also planned. A long-acting injectable formulation of RAP-219 is also in development and has potential to transform the treatment landscape for patients across indications and extend the product’s lifecycle.
About Rapport Therapeutics
Rapport Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company dedicated to discovering and developing small molecule precision medicines for patients with neurological and psychiatric disorders. The Company’s founders made pioneering discoveries related to the function of receptor associated proteins (RAPs) in the brain, which form the basis of Rapport’s RAP technology platform. The platform enables a differentiated approach to generate precision small molecule product candidates with the potential to overcome many limitations of conventional neurology drug discovery. Rapport’s precision neuroscience pipeline includes the Company’s lead investigational drug, RAP-219, which is designed to achieve neuroanatomical specificity through selective targeting of a RAP expressed only in discrete regions of the brain. The pipeline is anchored by the Company’s epilepsy portfolio, including FOS and primary generalized tonic-clonic seizures, as well as bipolar mania. The Company is also advancing additional discovery and preclinical programs leveraging its platform, including in chronic pain and migraine and in hearing and vestibular disorders.
