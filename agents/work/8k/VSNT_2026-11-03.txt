Versant Media Reports Third Quarter 2026 Operating and Financial Results
•Revenue of $1.59 Billion
•Net Income Attributable to Versant of $47 Million
•Adjusted EBITDA of $353 Million1
•Declared Fourth Quarterly Cash Dividend of $0.375 Per Share
•Completed $100 Million Accelerated Share Repurchase (ASR) Transaction
•Announced an Additional Planned $100 Million ASR in the Fourth Quarter of 2026
NEW YORK, NY – November 3, 2026. Versant Media Group, Inc. (Nasdaq: VSNT) (the “Company”) today reported operating and financial results for the third quarter of 2026, highlighted by strong performance across its businesses, including favorable network audience trends, double-digit Platforms revenue growth and significant cash flow generation.
“Versant’s momentum continues, with audience growth and the completion of all planned pay television distribution renewals for the year, underscoring the value and enduring appeal of our brands,” said Mark Lazarus, Chief Executive Officer. “In the third quarter, we advanced Fandango’s evolution into a comprehensive entertainment platform, integrated Full Swing into our golf ecosystem, and launched MS NOW Membership to connect directly with our audiences. We’re building deeper consumer relationships, expanding our reach and creating new opportunities for growth and monetization across our four core markets.”
“Our third quarter performance demonstrates the financial discipline and flexibility that underpins Versant’s strategy,” said Anand Kini, Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer. “We delivered strong Platforms revenue, continued operating efficiencies and significant cash flow. Looking ahead, we are reaffirming our full-year revenue and Adjusted EBITDA1 outlook, and we expect Free Cash Flow1 to exceed our prior $1.0 to $1.2 billion forecast. We believe that our ability to invest in growth, maintain a healthy balance sheet, and return capital to shareholders is a distinct advantage. With $457 million already returned to shareholders to date, this morning’s announcement of an additional planned $100 million ASR, along with the declaration of our quarterly dividend, underscores our commitment to delivering shareholder value.”

Business Highlights: In the third quarter of 2026, Versant’s networks reached more than 120 million viewers monthly, while aggregate viewership grew double digits year over year. The Company expanded its digital offerings, launching MS NOW Membership and advancing development of CNBC’s direct-to-consumer platform. Fandango recorded significant growth across ticketing, with home entertainment sales and Fandango1 also contributing to third-quarter performance, while the Company continued to expand its golf ecosystem with the addition of Full Swing.
Business News and Personal Finance: CNBC reinforced its position as the leading global business news brand, ranking among the top 10 most-watched cable networks during market hours for the eighth consecutive month across key demographics. The network remained the destination for CEOs and business leaders to share their perspectives on the issues shaping markets and the economy. CNBC also advanced plans to launch its new digital offering early next year.
Political News and Opinion: MS NOW delivered double-digit year-over-year viewership growth across key demographics, ranking as the second-most-watched cable network during the quarter and recording its eleventh consecutive month of audience growth in September. Digital engagement remained healthy, with MS NOW ranking as the No. 1 news organization on YouTube during the quarter and generating more than 105 million podcast downloads year to date. The network also launched its direct-to-consumer membership platform ahead of the midterm elections, creating new ways to engage audiences beyond television.
Golf: Golf Channel continued to build on its leadership position in the third quarter, delivering its most-watched PGA TOUR season since 2018. Audience reach, time spent viewing and viewing frequency for the PGA TOUR on Golf Channel all increased year over year, reflecting solid demand for premium live golf and the continued strength of the Golf Channel brand. Versant continued to integrate Full Swing into its golf ecosystem during the quarter, creating more ways to engage golfers across consumer and commercial markets.
Sports and Genre Entertainment: Live sports continued to drive robust audience engagement, with USA Network ranking among the top five cable entertainment networks across key demographics. Viewership of the WNBA's inaugural season on USA increased 25% compared to the prior year's cable average. Entertainment programming also delivered strong viewership among key demographics throughout the quarter.
Platforms: Platforms revenue grew 10% in the third quarter, or 17% excluding the impacts of the SportsEngine divestiture and Full Swing acquisition. Fandango delivered significant growth in ticketing, while home entertainment sales and Fandango1 also contributed to its third-quarter performance.

Total Revenue was $1,594 million, representing a decline of 4.2% compared to the same period in 2025. Excluding the impact of SportsEngine and Full Swing, revenue decreased 3.5%.
•Linear Distribution revenue declined 5.8% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the same period in 2025, primarily due to subscriber declines, which were partially offset by contractual rate increases.
•Advertising revenue declined 5.3% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the same period in 2025, reflecting the event timing impact of the Ryder Cup in the prior year. The current quarter continues to reflect improvements in recent trends including favorable ratings at the networks as well as incremental revenue from a recent acquisition.
•Platforms revenue increased 9.6% in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the same period in 2025, or 17.4% excluding the impact of SportsEngine and Full Swing. The increase was primarily due to higher revenue at Fandango, largely driven by movie ticket purchases and sales from our new cinema operating platform.
•Content licensing and other revenue decreased in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the same period in 2025, primarily due to the timing of content licensing agreements.
Net income attributable to Versant was $47 million, a decrease of $33 million compared to the prior year quarter, primarily due to lower revenue and public company costs and interest expense following the separation from Comcast, partially offset by a prior year impairment loss on one of our investments, which also resulted in a higher provision for income taxes in the prior year period.
Adjusted EBITDA2 was $353 million, a decrease of 20.9% compared to the prior year quarter. Compared to Standalone Adjusted EBITDA in the prior year quarter, Adjusted EBITDA decreased 7.4%. This decrease reflects lower revenue, partially offset by lower entertainment programming costs, and reduced selling, general and administrative costs. Programming costs in the current year reflect costs related to additional sports rights compared to the prior year period, which are generally more significant in the third and fourth quarters due to the timing of events.
Net cash provided by operating activities was $346 million and Free Cash Flow2 was $302 million in the third quarter of 2026.

Dividends and Share Repurchase Program
On November 3, 2026, the Board of Directors declared the fourth quarterly cash dividend of $0.375 per share, payable on January 25, 2027, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on January 4, 2027.
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company repurchased $100 million of its Class A common stock under an accelerated share repurchase (ASR) agreement, representing approximately 2.6 million shares. Approximately $700 million remained available under the Company's stock repurchase program as of September 30, 2026.
The Company expects to enter into an ASR agreement commencing November 4, 2026, to repurchase $100 million of its Class A common stock under the Company's stock repurchase program. The Company anticipates completing the transaction during the fourth quarter of 2026.

We evaluate our operating performance based on several factors, including the following non-GAAP financial measures:
Adjusted EBITDA
Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure and is a key measure used to assess the operational strength and performance of our business as well as to assist in the evaluation of underlying trends in our business. This measure eliminates the significant level of noncash depreciation and amortization expense that results from property and equipment and intangible assets recognized in business combinations. It is also unaffected by our capital and tax structures, and by our investment activities, including the impacts of entities that we do not consolidate, as our management excludes these results when evaluating our operating performance. Our management and Board of Directors use this financial measure to evaluate our operating performance and to allocate resources. It is also a significant performance measure in our annual incentive compensation programs. Additionally, we believe that Adjusted EBITDA is useful to investors because it is one of the bases for comparing our operating performance with that of other companies in our industries, although our measure of Adjusted EBITDA may not be directly comparable to similar measures used by other companies.
We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income attributable to Versant before net income (loss) attributable to noncontrolling interests, income tax expense, investment and other income (loss), net, interest expense, depreciation and amortization expense, and other operating gains and losses (such as impairment charges related to fixed and intangible assets and gains or losses on the sale of long-lived assets), if any. From time to time, we may make adjustments to Adjusted EBITDA for the impact of certain other events, gains, losses or other charges that affect the period-to-period comparability of our operating performance. Reconciliations of Adjusted EBITDA to the nearest GAAP measures for historical periods are presented below. A reconciliation for full-year 2026 outlook cannot be provided without unreasonable efforts because of the inherent difficulty in forecasting certain amounts that are necessary for such reconciliations due to the uncertainty and potential variability of reconciling items, which are dependent on future events and often outside of management’s control and which could be significant. Forward-looking estimates are made in a manner consistent with the relevant definitions noted herein.
Standalone Adjusted EBITDA
Standalone Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure used in periods prior to the Separation to measure the operational strength and performance of our business as well as to assist in the evaluation of underlying trends in our business. Consistent with Adjusted EBITDA, this measure eliminates noncash depreciation and amortization expense and is unaffected by our capital and tax structures and by our investment activities, as our management excludes these results when evaluating our operating performance. Standalone Adjusted EBITDA also includes estimated incremental costs of operating as a standalone company following the Separation. We use Standalone Adjusted EBITDA and believe this measure is useful to investors because it provides an estimate of our operating performance giving effect to the Separation and related transactions and additional costs that we expect to incur as a standalone company for the periods presented, and is one of the bases for comparing our operating performance with that of other companies in our industries, although our measure of Standalone Adjusted EBITDA may not be directly comparable to similar measures used by other companies.
We define Standalone Adjusted EBITDA as net income attributable to Versant before net income (loss) attributable to noncontrolling interests, income tax expense, investment and other income (loss), net, interest expense, depreciation and amortization expense, and other operating gains and losses (such as impairment charges related to fixed and intangible assets and gains or losses on the sale of long-lived assets), if any, and further adjusted to give effect to estimated incremental costs of commercial agreements with Comcast and estimated additional costs we expect to incur as a standalone company in certain of our corporate administrative, facilities and support functions. From time to time, we may make adjustments to Standalone Adjusted EBITDA for the impact of certain events, gains, losses or other charges that affect the period-to-period comparability of our operating performance. Standalone Adjusted EBITDA is presented for informational purposes only and does not purport to represent what our results of operations actually would have been had we operated as a standalone company for the periods presented or to project our financial performance for any future period. Standalone Adjusted EBITDA is based on available information, estimates and assumptions, which we believe are reasonable, although actual future results will differ from the amounts presented. Free Cash Flow is a non-GAAP financial measure that we believe provides a meaningful measure of liquidity and a useful basis for assessing our ability to repay debt, make strategic acquisitions and investments, and return capital to investors through stock repurchases and dividends. Free Cash Flow has certain limitations, including that it does not represent the residual cash flow available for discretionary expenditures since other non-discretionary payments, such as mandatory debt repayments, are not deducted from the measure.
Free Cash Flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities, reduced by capital expenditures. Non-GAAP Financial Measures In addition to financial measures included in our combined financial statements, we use certain non-GAAP financial measures as defined below. We provide reconciliations of these non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP in the following pages. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income attributable to Versant before net income (loss) attributable to noncontrolling interests, income tax expense, investment and other income (loss), net, interest expense, depreciation and amortization expense, and other operating gains and losses (such as impairment charges related to fixed and intangible assets and gains or losses on the sale of long-lived assets), if any. From time to time, we may make adjustments to Adjusted EBITDA for the impact of certain other events, gains, losses or other charges that affect the period-to-period comparability of our operating performance. Standalone Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income attributable to Versant before net income (loss) attributable to noncontrolling interests, income tax expense, investment and other income (loss), net, interest expense, depreciation and amortization expense, and other operating gains and losses (such as impairment charges related to fixed and intangible assets and gains or losses on the sale of long-lived assets), if any, and further adjusted to give effect to estimated incremental costs of commercial agreements with Comcast and estimated additional costs we expect to incur as a standalone company in certain of our corporate administrative, facilities and support functions. From time to time, we may make adjustments to Standalone Adjusted EBITDA for the impact of certain events, gains, losses or other charges that affect the period-to-period comparability of our operating performance. Standalone Adjusted EBITDA is presented for informational purposes only and does not purport to represent what our results of operations actually would have been had we operated as a standalone company for the periods presented or to project our financial performance for any future period. Standalone Adjusted EBITDA is based on available information, estimates and assumptions, which we believe are reasonable, although actual future results will differ from the amounts presented. 2
