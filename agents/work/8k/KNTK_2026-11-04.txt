Kinetik Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
HOUSTON and MIDLAND, Texas, November 4, 2026 – Kinetik Holdings Inc. (NYSE: KNTK) (“Kinetik” or the “Company”) today reported financial and operating results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Kinetik reported net income including noncontrolling interest of $50.9 million and $168.9 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively. Kinetik generated Adjusted EBITDA1 of $278.1 million and $810.1 million, Distributable Cash Flow1 of $213.7 million and $589.5 million, and Free Cash Flow1 of $87.5 million and $294.1 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively.
Highlights
•Record processed gas volumes of 1.94 Bcf/d
•Upsized Kings Landing II processing capacity by more than 25% to 380 MMcf/d
•Reached final investment decision on phase 1 of the ECCC Pipeline expansion, which is expected to increase throughput capacity by roughly 50% to approximately 225 MMcf/d
•Ordered long-lead equipment for the Company's next processing expansion beyond Kings Landing II
•Executed new and amended gathering and processing agreements in New Mexico and Texas
•Full year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA1 expected to be at the high end of the $1.04 billion to $1.1 billion guidance range
•Anticipate full year 2026 Capital Expenditures2 to be approximately 5% below guidance of $560 million
CEO Commentary
“Kinetik delivered another strong quarter with record processed gas volumes underscoring the strength and increasing scale of our business,” said Jamie Welch, Kinetik’s President & Chief Executive Officer. “As our strategic growth projects come online and processed gas volumes continue to increase, our financial performance is increasingly driven by durable, repeatable earnings.”
“The momentum we have built throughout the year has continued into the fourth quarter. Producer activity remains strong, processed gas volumes are tracking toward our approximately 2.2 Bcf/d fourth quarter 2026 target, and margins and costs continue to outperform our internal budget expectations.”
Welch added, “Our confidence in the long-term outlook for the Permian Basin has never been stronger. Producers are demonstrating success delineating new benches — which tend to have higher gas-to-oil ratios — making plays like the Woodford, Avalon, First Bone Spring, and Penn Shale more expansive throughout the Delaware Basin, a trend directly playing to Kinetik's strengths. Demand for gathering, processing, and treating capacity is outpacing existing infrastructure in many areas, reinforcing the strategic value of integrated systems. At the same time, more than 13 Bcf/d of incremental takeaway capacity is expected to come online between 2028 and 2030, which we believe will unlock the next wave of producer activity, substantially improve residue gas egress, and reduce Waha price volatility.”

Segment Insights
The Midstream Logistics segment generated Adjusted EBITDA1 of $203.6 million, a 34% increase year-over-year for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Kinetik’s processed natural gas volumes increased to 1.94 Bcf/d in the third quarter of 2026, with an additional estimated 25 MMcf/d of Waha price-related gas volume that was shut-in. Third quarter 2026 results also benefited from continued strength in system operating performance and favorable commodity margins realized, which were partially offset by a reduction in residue gas marketing contributions. With all Delaware North processing facilities, including Kings Landing (“KL”), operating at full capacity for much of the third quarter of 2026, the ECCC Pipeline performed as designed, transporting surplus volumes to the Delaware South system.
The Pipeline Transportation segment generated Adjusted EBITDA1 of $78.5 million, an 18% decrease year-over-year for the three months ended September 30, 2026, primarily due to the Company’s EPIC Crude Holdings, LP divestiture, and to a lesser extent, higher fuel costs at Permian Highway Pipeline and lower throughput volumes at Shin Oak.
2026 Guidance & Outlook
Kinetik now expects its 2026 Adjusted EBITDA1 to be at the high end of its $1.04 billion to $1.1 billion guidance range, reflecting (i) gas gathering and processing fee benefiting from stronger-than-expected well results; (ii) robust operating performance that has yielded stronger-than-expected equity volumes; and (iii) commodity prices outperforming the Company’s guidance assumptions set forth in August 2026.
Kinetik anticipates 2026 Capital Expenditures2 (including maintenance) to be approximately 5% below its August 2026 guidance of $560 million. The capital reduction is largely driven by disciplined execution of the Company’s growth capital program and timing of project spend.
Strategic Projects & Commercial Activity
The Company advanced its portfolio of strategic growth projects, supporting increasing producer activity and long-term volume growth across its footprint.
2
In September, Kinetik increased the planned processing capacity of KLII by more than 25% to 380 MMcf/d. The expected capital cost remains unchanged at approximately $260 million, as several critical equipment components were under budget, and the project is on track for a mid-2028 in-service date.
Construction activities on the acid gas injection (“AGI”) and sour gas conversion project at KL progressed during the quarter, with the AGI well reaching total depth and acid gas compression equipment delivered on site. This project is expected to be completed by year-end.
The Company reached final investment decision (“FID”) on phase 1 of the ECCC Pipeline expansion, which should increase throughput capacity by roughly 50% to approximately 225 MMcf/d and unlock additional Delaware South processing access for Delaware North customers. Right-of-way acquisition activities were underway during the third quarter of 2026. Pipeline procurement and construction activities on the partial looping of the pipeline should commence in early 2027, with in-service anticipated mid-2027.
Commercial activity remained strong as Kinetik executed new and amended low-pressure gathering and processing agreements with customers across both New Mexico and Texas. Collectively, over 30,000 gross acres were dedicated to Kinetik since the beginning of August. Notably, certain of the dedicated acreage and committed volumes are prospective for the Woodford formation, an emerging play in the Delaware Basin that Kinetik believes will support additional long-term volume growth across its system. First flow for these agreements is anticipated in the second and third quarters of 2027. Importantly, one of these agreements includes a firm, minimum volume commitment from the customer, which provides enhanced cash flow visibility and further de-risks Kinetik’s pipeline and processing investments. The Company is procuring additional compression equipment to accommodate the expected producer volume growth.
The new agreements also provide support toward an FID of the Company’s next processing expansion beyond KLII. Long-lead residue compression equipment has been ordered, and procurement of front-end amine treating and the 400 MMcf/d cryogenic unit is in process. The exact location, in either Texas or New Mexico, has not yet been finalized, and Kinetik will provide further details in the future.
With the Company’s increased volume outlook and rising CO2 concentrations from Loving and Lea Counties, Kinetik is undertaking an expansion of its front-end amine treating capacity and overall processing capability at its Pecos Bend facility. The Company is also making cost-effective plant modifications to further boost processing capacity and recoveries at the East Toyah, Pecos, and Diamond Cryo processing complexes.
Kinetik also further strengthened its downstream commercial position with two new agreements. First, the Company committed to additional transport capacity on a new long-haul residue gas pipeline connecting the Waha Hub to the Gulf Coast, with the agreement expected to commence in 2029. Second, the Company secured firm access to additional Gulf Coast residue gas pricing, commencing in 2027. Kinetik believes incremental residue gas takeaway capacity will be required to support long-term Permian production growth, and these agreements represent a continuation of its long-term commercial strategy to expand Gulf Coast market access for the residue natural gas of its producer customers.
The Company reached an important milestone in the evolution of its natural gas liquids (“NGL”) transportation and fractionation agreements portfolio as the first of the Lone Star NGL contracts in Texas expired on October 31, 2026, and the remaining contract with Lone Star NGL in Texas will expire at year-end. As these agreements roll off, Kinetik now forecasts more meaningful margin contributions from system optimization in 2027 than previously expected.
Construction-related activities continued at Diamond Volt, the behind-the-meter power generation project at Diamond Cryo, which is expected to be in-service by mid-2027.

About Kinetik Holdings Inc.
Kinetik is a fully integrated, pure-play, Permian-to-Gulf Coast midstream C-corporation operating in the Delaware Basin. Kinetik is headquartered in Houston and Midland, Texas. Kinetik provides comprehensive gathering, transportation, compression, processing and treating services for companies that produce natural gas, natural gas liquids, crude oil and water. Kinetik posts announcements, operational updates, investor information and press releases on its website, www.kinetik.com.
