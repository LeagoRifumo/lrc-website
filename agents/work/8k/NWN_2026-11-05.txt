NW Natural Holdings Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Raises 2026 EPS Guidance
PORTLAND, ORE. — Northwest Natural Holding Company (NYSE: NWN) (NW Natural Holdings or the Company) reported financial results and highlights including:
Third Quarter and Year-to-Date 2026 Highlights
•Reported net loss per share of $0.71 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a net loss per share of $0.73 for the same period in 2025
•Reported earnings per share (EPS) of $1.62 for the first nine months of 2026, compared to EPS of $1.36 and adjusted EPS1 of $1.52 for the same period in 2025
•Constructive regulatory outcomes for NW Natural in Washington and Oregon and reached settlement in SiEnergy rate case in Texas
•Added 17,000 gas and water utility connections over the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026 for a growth rate of 1.8%
•Invested $373 million in the first nine months of 2026 in our gas and water systems to support growth and reliability
•Announced a dividend increase for the 71st consecutive year, our largest increase since 2012
2026 EPS Guidance Raised and Long-term Growth Targets Reaffirmed
•Raised 2026 EPS guidance range to $3.10 – $3.20 (previously the upper half of the guidance range of $2.95 – $3.15)
•Reaffirmed rate base growth expectation of 6% – 8% through 2030 driven by planned capital expenditures of $2.6 – $2.9 billion from 2026 – 2030
•Reaffirmed long-term EPS growth rate target of 4% – 6%2 and potential to increase to 5% – 7%2 with MX3 gas storage project
"We are delivering another strong year, and I am encouraged by the progress we are making across the Company," said Justin Palfreyman, president and chief executive officer. "Our performance reflects disciplined cost management, constructive regulatory outcomes, and continued customer growth. Based on our results through the first nine months of 2026 and the momentum we see across the business, we are raising our earnings guidance and have declared the largest dividend increase since 2012. These actions underscore our confidence in both our near-term outlook and our ability to drive long-term value. As we head into the heating season, we remain focused on safe and reliable operations, cost discipline, and customer affordability."

KEY EVENTS
Washington Commission Approved New Rates for NW Natural
On July 29, 2026, the Washington Utilities and Transportation Commission (WUTC) issued an order approving the multi-party settlement in Northwest Natural Gas Company's (NW Natural) multi-year general rate case. The order increases the annual revenue requirement over three years, consisting of a $20.1 million revenue increase in the first year beginning Aug. 1, 2026, a $7.5 million revenue increase in the second year, and a $7.4 million revenue increase in the third year. The order included a capital structure of 50% common equity and 50% long-term debt, a return on equity of 9.5%, and an overall cost of capital of 7.15% beginning in the first year and growing to 7.22% in the third year. Rate base is $328 million in the first year, or an increase of $81 million since the last rate case. New rates were effective Aug. 1, 2026.
Oregon Commission Approved Alternative Rate Mechanism for NW Natural
On Oct. 13, 2026, the Public Utility Commission of Oregon (OPUC) issued an order approving the multi-party settlement in NW Natural's alternative rate mechanism (ARM) docket. The order provides an increase to the annual revenue requirement of $13.0 million, compared to the original request of $15.6 million. The ARM covers certain capital expenditures including safety investments and information technology projects over $1 million. New rates took effect on Oct. 31, 2026.
Reached Settlement with Key Parties in SiEnergy's General Rate Case
On Sept. 22, 2026, SiEnergy filed a settlement with the Texas Railroad Commission (RRC) that resolves all items in the general rate case. The settlement provides an $8.6 million revenue requirement increase (compared to the requested $12.0 million), a return on equity of 9.8%, an overall cost of capital of 8.0%, and a 59.75% equity layer. The filing includes rate base of $343 million, a $177 million increase since the last rate case.
The settlement establishes the factors necessary for SiEnergy’s future participation in the Gas Reliability Infrastructure Program (GRIP), which enables interim rate adjustments based upon the change in net plant investments during the prior year. It also permits the consolidation of SiEnergy Gas, LLC and its gas distribution affiliates. If approved, new rates are expected to be effective in November 2026.

ABOUT NW NATURAL HOLDINGS
NW Natural Holding Company (NYSE: NWN) is headquartered in Portland, Oregon and has operated for more than 167 years. It owns Northwest Natural Gas Company (NW Natural), the Company's long-standing natural gas utility serving the Pacific Northwest; SiEnergy Operating, LLC (SiEnergy), a fast-growing natural gas utility serving key Texas markets; NW Natural Water Company (NW Natural Water), an expanding water and wastewater utility; and additional business interests. Together, NW Natural Holdings provides essential energy and water services to nearly one million customers across seven states. The Company has a longstanding commitment to safety, environmental stewardship and supporting its employees and communities, and consistently leads the industry in J.D. Power customer satisfaction.
