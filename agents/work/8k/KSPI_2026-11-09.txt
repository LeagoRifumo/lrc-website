Kaspi.kz 3Q & 9M 2026 Financial Results
Almaty, Kazakhstan, 9 November 2026 – Joint Stock Company Kaspi.kz (“Kaspi.kz”) (Nasdaq:KSPI) today published its unaudited consolidated IFRS financial results for the quarter and nine months ended 30 September 2026 (“3Q and 9M 2026”).
Letter from Mikheil Lomtadze
We remain confident in the strength of our businesses and their long-term growth potential.
Our focus has always been on developing innovative products and services that improve people's lives. This mission has enabled us to build highly engaged and profitable businesses, and continues to create exciting opportunities for future growth.
I would like to share three reasons why I am excited about our future.
First, our consumers are shopping more frequently and buying more products
Across our e-Commerce business, annualized purchases per consumer increased 27% year-over-year.
Our Marketplace connects consumers with merchants who set their own prices. A stronger tenge reduces the local-currency cost of foreign-currency-priced imports, creating opportunities for merchants to offer more competitive prices.
We believe more frequent purchases and more competitive prices strengthen our customer proposition and support long-term growth, even as lower average selling values and easing inflation moderate GMV growth today.
Importantly, monetization continues to improve. Marketplace revenue grew 20% year-over-year on a constant-currency basis, compared with GMV growth of 8%, while adjusted EBITDA increased 8%.
Second, we see opportunities for stronger earnings growth in 2027
Our Payments and Fintech businesses continue to provide a strong foundation for future earnings growth. We have reduced our three-month deposit rate by 100 basis points in both August and October, and expect the benefits to become increasingly visible in 2027.
We also expect investments in Türkiye to moderate significantly next year, supporting our consolidated earnings outlook.
1
Third, our track record of innovation gives us confidence in our ability to build new businesses
In just five years, we built one of Kazakhstan's leading grocery businesses. Today, our e-Grocery serves 1.7 million consumers and grew GMV by 39% in the first nine months of 2026.
We are now leveraging this infrastructure, technology and data to expand into Kazakhstan's estimated $9 billion neighbourhood grocery market through Magnum Daily.
We have successfully completed our initial 42-store pilot in Almaty, achieving approximately 15% EBITDA margin. Magnum Daily stores are supplied by our existing e-Grocery darkstores and operated by local partners, enabling us to expand rapidly without building a separate supply chain.
We also see opportunities to leverage Magnum Daily stores for 30-minute e-Commerce delivery, making shopping faster and more convenient for our consumers.
Encouraged by these results, we are ready to scale to around 300 stores by the end of 2027, with a medium-term ambition of 3,000 stores.
In Türkiye, our strategy is to combine a strong e-Commerce platform with innovative Fintech products.
Consumer engagement at Hepsiburada continues to improve, with order frequency increasing 11% year-over-year in 3Q 2026.
Having launched our consumer shopping loan, we are now building our Fintech platform. In October, Hepsicredit financed approximately 8% of Hepsiburada's smartphone GMV. We see significant opportunities to develop innovative Fintech products for consumers and merchants, creating another source of long-term growth.
We believe AI will fundamentally transform how consumers interact with mobile applications. Our ambition is for Kasper to become a trusted personal assistant for everyday tasks across the Kaspi Super App.
We started with e-Commerce, where Kasper has already been used by 1.4 million consumers since launching in July. Early results are encouraging, with purchase conversion 1.5 times higher and average GMV per order twice that of traditional text search.
Later this year, we plan to introduce a new generation of Kasper, making shopping more intuitive, personal and effortless.
Imagine showing Kasper a photo of something you like, describing what you need in your own words and receiving recommendations tailored specifically to you. Kasper will remember your preferences, learn from previous interactions and understand your personal context, becoming more helpful every time you use it.

We believe this is only the beginning of what Kasper can do for our consumers.
I see significant opportunities ahead to keep innovating, building new businesses and creating long-term value for our customers and shareholders.
Reflecting this confidence and our commitment to shareholder returns, our Board has proposed another quarterly dividend of KZT1,000 per ADS, subject to shareholder approval.
Thank you for your continued trust and support.

Update on key strategic initiatives
From successful pilot to scaling: We’re entering the $9bn neighbourhood grocery market
e-Grocery is our fastest-growing category and one of our largest, with GMV increasing 40% year-over-year in 3Q 2026. Today we serve just under 2 million consumers, with GMV of $447 million in the first nine months of this year.
Building on this success, we are entering Kazakhstan's estimated $9 billion neighborhood grocery market through Magnum Daily.
Over the last year, we have piloted Magnum Daily convenience stores. We partner with existing operators, rebranding and refitting their current locations to our specifications.
Magnum Daily leverages the infrastructure, technology and data we have built through e-Grocery.
Stores are supplied by our existing darkstore network, while Kaspi funds store development and manages assortment, inventory and pricing. Local partners are responsible for day-to-day operations and customer service. This standardized model allows us to expand rapidly, city by city, without building a separate supply chain.
Our initial 42-store pilot has delivered encouraging results, with stores EBITDA-positive from the first month, approximately 15% store-level EBITDA margins and an estimated 18-month payback period. Capex per store is around $227,000 per store depending on the format.
6
Looking at more mature grocery markets, we estimate that the top 3 leading chains can comfortably reach 5 stores per 10,000 of the population, implying the opportunity for around 10,000 modern stores in Kazakhstan.
7
Our medium-term ambition is to build a network of approximately 3,000 Magnum Daily stores across Kazakhstan. We estimate 3,000 stores would be equivalent to $3.3 billion of GMV. Assuming an EBITDA margin of 15%, consistent with current results, the uplift to annual EBITDA is around $500 million.
By the end of 2027, we expect to reach around 300 Magnum Daily stores across nine cities in Kazakhstan. This would require partnering with fewer than 1% of existing independent stores in these cities.
With e-Grocery still growing fast, Magnum Daily will ensure we can keep delivering fast growth for longer. It will also allow us to more efficiently leverage our existing e-Grocery infrastructure.
Meanwhile, our network of local convenience stores can be the infrastructure for 30-minute e-Commerce delivery. This will allow us to offer consumers greater convenience, faster delivery and a better shopping experience.
Kasper: Kaspi’s personal AI assistant
In July, we began rolling out Kasper, our personal AI assistant, marking the first step toward transforming how consumers interact with the Kaspi Super App. We started with shopping, with the ambition of bringing AI-powered assistance to everyday tasks.
Following its initial launch, Kasper has demonstrated strong consumer adoption and is resulting in more relevant search results. Average GMV per order being 2x that of traditional text search, illustrates this.
8
Kasper is transforming how consumers discover and choose products, turning shopping into a natural conversation.
Instead of searching through thousands of products, consumers can simply explain what they need, ask questions, compare options and receive personalized recommendations.
Later this year, we plan to introduce significant new capabilities for Kasper, including visual understanding, memory and more personalized shopping assistance.
Consumers will be able to share photos, describe what they are looking for in natural language and receive relevant product recommendations.
By better understanding their preferences, previous purchases and interactions, Kasper will be able to provide more personalized assistance over time.
We also plan to make Kasper accessible directly from the home screen of the Kaspi Super App, making it easier for consumers to use AI-powered assistance as part of their everyday experience.
These developments represent another step toward expanding Kasper beyond shopping and into a personal AI assistant for everyday tasks across the Kaspi Super App.

Rollout of Hepsicredit in Türkiye
As part of our fintech strategy in Türkiye, we’re now ramping up our consumer shopping loan.
Hepsicredit is seamlessly integrated with Hepsiburada and is relevant for large ticket e-Commerce categories.
In October, for example, around 8% of smartphone GMV was already funded by consumers using this product.
With initial feedback positive and risk indicators acceptable, we’re ready to scale further.
As we progress through 2027, we plan to launch other fintech products for both consumers and merchants.
Combined, we expect these to be positive for consumer engagement, growth and unit economics.
Our ambition is to create more value for both consumers and merchants through innovative Fintech products. We want to make shopping easier and more affordable for consumers, while helping merchants attract more customers, increase sales and grow their businesses.

Kaspi.kz 3Q 2026 Financials
Total revenue increased 18% year-over-year on a constant currency basis to KZT1.1 trillion ($2.5 billion). Growth was mainly driven by improving order frequency and take-rate expansion in Marketplace, strong Fintech loan portfolio growth, along with robust growth from our Payments Platform. On a reported basis, revenue growth was broadly flat due to 32% year-over-year depreciation of the Turkish lira versus the Kazakhstan tenge, which mainly impacts reported e-Commerce and Marketplace growth.
Adjusted EBITDA increased 1% year-over-year to KZT408 billion ($925 million). Consolidated net income was stable at KZT281 billion ($637 million). Net income profitability remains impacted by higher deposit funding costs and ongoing investments at Hepsiburada.
In October, we lowered the interest rate offered on our 3-month deposit product by 100bps. This followed a 100bps reduction in August. The result will be more modest growth in interest expense in the fourth quarter of this year, with a more meaningful benefit in 2027.
Once again, our Board has proposed a quarterly dividend of KZT1,000 per ADS.
11
Marketplace Platform
Within Marketplace, constant-currency e-Commerce GMV increased 17% year-over-year to KZT1.1 trillion ($2.6 billion), during the third quarter of 2026.
In both Kazakhstan and Türkiye, we’re prioritising improving consumer engagement; this is illustrated by e-Commerce purchases per consumer increasing to 16.3 from 12.8 in the third quarter of 2025. In Kazakhstan demand for smartphones is seeing a strong rebound following last year’s disruption.
However, strong growth in e-Commerce items sold of 24% year-over-year is being temporarily offset by easing inflation and imported items being priced more competitively as merchants pass the cost savings resulting from the sharp increase in the tenge onto their consumers.
12
During the third quarter of 2026, the average e-Commerce selling price declined by 8% year-over-year. Assuming a more stable tenge, we would expect price deflation to be less pronounced next year and GMV growth to reaccelerate.
We’re seeing good results growing revenue from value-added services, namely advertising and delivery. During 3Q 2026, constant-currency VAS revenue increased 42% year-over-year.
As a result, e-Commerce 3P take rate increased 210 bps year-over-year to 16.3%, and e-Commerce revenue grew significantly faster than GMV. During 3Q 2026, e-Commerce revenue increased 23% year-over-year on a constant currency basis to KZT343 billion ($779 million).
13
Marketplace constant-currency GMV increased 8% year-over-year, driven by e-Commerce growth in Kazakhstan and Türkiye. Take rate improved 90 bps year-over-year to 11.9%, resulting in constant-currency Marketplace revenue growing 20% year-over-year to KZT466 billion ($1.1 billion).
14
Adjusted EBITDA grew 8% year-over-year to KZT124 billion ($281 million). Adjusted EBITDA growth was impacted by ongoing investments into Hepsiburada, which remains around breakeven.
15
Payments Platform
During 3Q 2026, constant-currency TPV increased 14% year-over-year to KZT13.6 trillion ($30.8 billion).
Payments revenue increased 6% year-over-year to KZT182 billion ($413 million). Revenue growth below TPV growth reflects take-rate decline to 1.01% from 1.03% in 3Q 2025. This is due to changing product mix and is consistent with long-run trends. Interest revenue accounted for 24% of total revenue and increased 12% year-over-year.
Adjusted EBITDA was down 1% year-over-year at KZT105 billion ($238 million), impacted by higher technology costs related to Kaspi Alaqan. In Payments, adjusted EBITDA excludes interest revenue.
16
Fintech Platform
During 3Q 2026, our average net loan portfolio increased by 14% year-over-year, to KZT7.5 trillion ($17.1 billion).
We are deliberately shifting our lending mix toward longer-duration, higher-revenue products. Average net loan portfolio duration was 9.1 months in 3Q 2026, compared with 7.9 months in 3Q 2025. Portfolio yield was stable year-over-year at 6%. As a result, Fintech revenue growth remained strong and increased 19% year-over-year to KZT489 billion ($1.1 billion).
Interest rate increases in Kazakhstan last year resulted in our average cost of funding increasing 150bps year-over-year to 14.9% in 3Q 2026.
In August, we lowered the interest rate offered on our 3-month deposit product, which accounts for around a third of deposits, by 100bps, with a further 100bps cut in October. With deposit rates looking to have peaked during 3Q 2026, consumers initially locked in higher rates, resulting in 21% growth in savings well ahead of average net loan portfolio growth. That said, our cost of funding is expected to fall in the final quarter of this year and again in 2027.
Cost of risk was 0.6% in 3Q 2026 and stable year-over-year. Our underlying credit trends remain strong and consistent.
Our NPL ratio of 6.9% in 9M 2026 was higher than the 6.1% seen in full-year 2025 but down slightly compared to 2Q 2026. Lower coverage reflects the growing share of lower risk car

and merchant loans in our portfolio, a decreasing share of BNPL loans and ongoing improvements in our collection processes.
Higher deposit costs and deposit growth ahead of loan growth negatively impacted 3Q 2026 Fintech adjusted EBITDA which declined 1% year-over-year to KZT179 billion ($406 million), compared with revenue growth of 19% to KZT489 billion ($1.1 billion).
The rollout of Hepsi Bank in Türkiye will have no meaningful impact on Fintech financials in 2026.
Full-year 2026 Guidance
Our Payments TPV and Fintech average balance sheet growth guidance remains on track and unchanged.
Marketplace GMV growth is now expected to be around 12% year-over-year.
Even with softer GMV trends, lower Fintech funding costs mean we are able to once again reiterate our guidance of around 5% Kaspi.kz consolidated adjusted EBITDA growth year-over-year in 2026.
Our guidance assumes constant-currency exchange rates as of January 1, 2026.

About Kaspi.kz
Kaspi.kz’s mission is to improve people’s lives by developing innovative mobile products and services.
Kaspi.kz operates a unique two-sided Super App model, serving more than 27 million consumers and 907 thousand merchants across Kazakhstan and Türkiye. In Kazakhstan, our Super App seamlessly integrates payments, e-Commerce, e-Grocery, fintech, travel, classifieds and government services. This comprehensive offering is deeply relevant to users’ daily lives, driving exceptional engagement with 77 monthly transactions per active consumer. In Türkiye, Kaspi.kz owns a 93.5% stake in Hepsiburada, one of the country’s leading e-Commerce platforms.
Kaspi.kz has been listed on Nasdaq since January 2024.

Use of key financial & operating metrics
Certain parts of this press release contain our key financial and operating metrics, which we do not consider to be non-IFRS financial measures. We use these metrics to evaluate our business, measure our performance, identify trends affecting our business, formulate financial projections and make strategic decisions. Our key operating metrics may be calculated in a manner different than similar key financial and operating metrics used by other companies. For definitions of our key financial and
operating metrics please refer to “Other Key Financial and Operating Metrics” section of our latest 20-F.
Additional/updated key financial and operating metrics used in this Release:
(1)
Adjusted EBITDA (Kaspi.kz Consolidated) is Profit or loss for the period less interest revenue from other operations, plus interest expense and fees from other operations, share-based compensation expense, other gains (losses), less income tax expense, depreciation and amortization expenses.
(2)
Marketplace Active consumers is The total number of consumers, including consumers in Türkiye, that completed at least one purchase of goods and services within Marketplace during the prior 12 months
(3)
e-Commerce Active Consumers is The total number of consumers, including consumers in Türkiye, that completed at least one purchase of goods and services within e-Commerce during the prior 12 months
(4)
Total Active Consumers is The total number of consumers, including consumers in Türkiye, that completed at least one transaction with Kaspi or Hepsiburada during the prior 12 months
(5)
Loan Duration (months) is Average effective maturity of loans measured in months
(6)
Cost of Funding is Average effective rate paid on term deposits of individuals and legal entities
(7)
e-Commerce GMV is The total transaction value of goods and services sold within the e-Commerce business of Marketplace (on an aggregate, “third-party” or “first-party” basis, as applicable). Our “first-party” e-Commerce GMV includes e-Grocery’s GMV. Starting from January 2026 e-Commerce GMV also includes GMV of Hepsiburada
(8)
Constant-currency growth is calculated by using end of period exchange rate for previous year reporting period and applying it to the corresponding current year reporting period, so as to calculate what our results would have been had exchange rates remained stable from one year to the next
(9)
Pro-forma growth is Includes full 1Q’25 result of Hepsiburada including the amounts which were prior to acquisition of Hepsiburada on 28 January 2025, for comparability purposes in 1Q’26
Non-IFRS financial measures
To supplement our results presented in accordance with IFRS, we present Adjusted EBITDA for both the Company and Hepsiburada on a standalone basis. The Company provides a reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA, a non-IFRS financial measure for historical periods. However, the Company does not provide guidance on net income and is unable to provide a reconciliation for its Adjusted EBITDA guidance range to net income without unreasonable efforts due to high variability and complexity with respect to estimating certain forward-looking amounts, the probable significance of which cannot be determined. The adjustments from net income for these forward-looking amounts include, for Adjusted EBITDA of the Company, earnings before interest revenue from other operations, interest expenses and fees from other operations, share-based compensation expense, other gains (losses), income tax expense, and depreciation and amortization expenses, and for Adjusted EBITDA of Hepsiburada on a standalone basis, taxation on income, financial income, financial expenses, depreciation and amortization, and monetary gain/(loss).
