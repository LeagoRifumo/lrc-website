USA Rare Earth Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
STILLWATER, Okla. - November 9, 2026 – USA Rare Earth, Inc. (Nasdaq: USAR) (the “Company” or “USA Rare Earth”), an integrated rare earth, critical minerals and advanced materials company, today announced its financial and operational results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Executive Commentary
“The third quarter marked a series of significant operational and commercial milestones, reflecting the growing positive momentum at USA Rare Earth. This progress was further reinforced by the closing of the Serra Verde combination in September, which coincided with the completion of Stage 1 of Serra Verde’s optimization and expansion program, followed by the commencement of commercial-scale shipments in October and sales of MREC in November” said Thras Moraitis, CEO of USA Rare Earth. “Our magnet facility in Stillwater, Oklahoma, achieved a major milestone with its first commercial magnet sales. In July we produced our first samples of light and heavy rare earth oxide at Wheat Ridge, Colorado, followed by our first Yttrium oxide samples in October. In addition, we broke ground on our new metals and magnets facility in Blacksburg, South Carolina. Every link in our integrated value chain is beginning to gain momentum as we focus on executing against our operational, commercial and strategic objectives.”
Mr. Moraitis continued, “I am grateful to Barbara Humpton for the strong foundation she has built, and proud to lead this team as we build on that foundation, transforming it into an integrated platform that delivers meaningful results for our customers and investors. Demand for a secure and responsible supply of rare earth products is becoming increasingly acute across a broad range of technology-driven sectors. We have customers for products at every stage of our value chain, from rare earth carbonate and oxides to metals, alloys and specialist powders, and from our integrated permanent magnet value chain. Our task is to deliver these critical elements and products independently and reliably. My focus is on bringing our businesses together under a clear integrated operating model, with accountability for results at every level and a consistent commitment to delivering reliably for our customers. The milestones achieved over the last quarter are only the beginning. With an unwavering focus on customer needs, disciplined execution, operational excellence and responsible capital allocation, we are well positioned to become the multinational partner of choice for customers and governments seeking an independent and secure supply of rare earth products, while creating superior long-term value for our shareholders.”

Third Quarter Business Updates
•Acquisition of Serra Verde completed, creating one of the few fully integrated rare earth and permanent magnet platforms outside Asia: In September 2026, the Company completed its combination with Serra Verde, creating a global rare earths leader and a partner of choice for the supply of advanced materials and products that underpin Western national security and technological innovation. Serra Verde is the only scaled producer outside Asia of all four key rare earths used in NdFeB magnets – neodymium (“Nd”), praseodymium (“Pr”), and the scarce heavy rare earths dysprosium (“Dy”) and terbium (“Tb”) – as well as yttrium and other critical heavy rare earth elements.
•Serra Verde completed Stage 1 of its optimization and expansion program and is producing commercial scale Mixed Rare Earth Carbonate (MREC), which is being shipped to its offtake customer. In September 2026, Serra Verde’s mining and processing operation in Goiás, Brazil, successfully completed Stage 1 of its optimization and commissioning program on time and on budget. In October 2026, Serra Verde began commercial-scale shipments to its offtake customer and recorded sales in November. This Stage 1 expansion is now ramping up to 4,000 metric tonnes per annum (“tpa”) of total rare earth oxide (TREO), while Stage 2, which is targeting to add a further 2,400 tpa of capacity by the end of 2027, is expected to complete construction and commence commissioning in Q3 2027.
•Commenced NdFeB magnet sales from Stillwater facility: In September 2026, the Company sold the first NdFeb magnets produced at its 600 tpa magnet manufacturing facility in Stillwater, OK. The initial sales comprised sintered NdFeB block magnets, sold to a high-performance materials manufacturing company. The Company has installed the first magnet finishing line at Stillwater, with a second finishing line expected to be installed by the end of 2026, which will broaden the Company’s product offering. These initial magnet sales position USA Rare Earth among the few commercial-scale magnet manufacturers outside Asia, marking an important milestone in the execution of the Company’s mine-to-magnet strategy.
•Successfully produced first light and heavy rare earth oxide samples for qualification: In July 2026, the Company announced that its hydrometallurgical facility in Wheat Ridge, Colorado, had successfully produced commercial-grade dysprosium (Dy) oxide and neodymium-praseodymium (NdPr) oxide samples from recycled rare earth magnet scrap, known in the industry as “swarf.” This positions USA Rare Earth as one of the few producers outside Asia capable of executing this technically demanding process. This capability broadens the Company’s feedstock options and enhances the circularity of its own value chain, with swarf projected to support up to 30% of future magnetic rare earth oxide feedstock needs.
•Broke ground on advanced rare earth metal and magnet manufacturing facility in Blacksburg, South Carolina: In September 2026, the Company broke ground on its new rare earth metal and magnet manufacturing facility in Blacksburg, South Carolina, marking a major step forward in the Company’s effort to build a secure, integrated rare earth supply chain for the United States and its allies. Located on a 124-acre site in Bailey Industrial Park in Cherokee County, the approximately 800,000-square-foot facility represents an approximately $1.2 billion investment and is expected to create roughly 490 high-skilled, high-wage manufacturing jobs in South Carolina’s Upstate. Once operational, the facility is targeting production capacity of 6,400 tpa of strip cast metal and alloy feeding 6,400 tpa of sintered NdFeB permanent magnets.
•Announced upsized capitalization of the U.S. government-backed special purpose vehicle (“SPV”) for Serra Verde offtake: In August 2026, the Company announced that US SIIE, LLC, the SPV that will purchase 100% of Serra Verde’s Phase 1 production of rare earth materials, completed an upsized capitalization totaling $1.55B in funding. The funding consists of: a $750 million investment in the SPV by the U.S. Department of War (“DoW”) - $250 million more than the $500 million originally contemplated; a commitment letter from a Tier-1 institutional bank for a senior secured borrowing-base revolving credit facility of up to $500 million; and a forward purchase contract from the U.S. government of not less than $300 million of rare earth payable products over five years.
•Finalized definitive agreements to acquire minority stake in Carester: In July 2026, the Company announced that it had entered into definitive agreements to acquire a strategic minority stake representing approximately 13.6% in Carester SAS, a French leader in rare earth processing and separation. InfraVia, acting through its Critical Metals Fund, seeded by the French State as an anchor investor alongside private institutional capital, is acquiring a similar stake in Carester alongside the Company. The Company and its subsidiary Less Common Metals – Europe are expected to have the ability to purchase a portion of Carester’s oxide output from its Caremag facility, which is under construction. The Company will have access to Carester’s engineering capabilities and related intellectual property for separation, processing, and recycling. In turn, Carester is expected to have access to a portion of the Company’s feedstock sources.
•Closed Acquisition of Texas Mineral Resources Corp. (“TMRC”): In August 2026, the Company closed the acquisition of TMRC. The Company is now the sole operator and 100% economic beneficiary of the Round Top project, which is expected to streamline operations, governance and decision-making at the Round Top project.
•Announced retirement of CEO Barbara Humpton, succeeded by Thras Moraitis, CEO of Serra Verde: In July 2026, the Company announced that Barbara Humpton will retire as Chief Executive Officer and Board Director on October 1, 2026. The Company’s Board of Directors named Thras Moraitis, CEO of Serra Verde and a highly experienced operator in the rare earths and global mining and processing industries, as Ms. Humpton’s successor. Mr. Moraitis assumed the CEO role on October 1, 2026. Mr. Moraitis was formerly President of USA Rare Earth following the completion of the Company’s combination with Serra Verde on September 3, 2026.
Recent Developments
Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company announced the following achievements and milestones:
•Commenced commercial-scale shipments and sale of heavy rare earth MREC from Serra Verde to US SIIE, LLC: In October 2026, the Company commenced the shipping and sales of heavy mixed rare earth carbonate (“MREC”) to US SIEE, LLC under a 15-year, 100% take-or-pay offtake agreement. Serra Verde is now actively producing and supplying product that meets the customer’s quality requirements in accordance with the contract. These deliveries are a defining milestone for the Company and for the broader effort to build a secure, reliable and responsible non-China supply chain for the materials that power tomorrow’s modern economy. The products are priced in accordance with the offtake agreement, which is based on non-China prices. Recent non-China prices for Dy oxide are 14-16x higher than China prices, while non-China prices for Tb oxide are 6-8x higher than China prices.
•Delivered first commercial-grade yttrium oxide samples to major customer: In October 2026, the Company announced that its Upstream business unit had achieved first production and delivery of yttrium oxide samples from its 24/7 continuous solvent extraction demonstration plant in Wheat Ridge, CO. This milestone positions USA Rare Earth as one of the only companies outside China with capabilities spanning the yttrium supply chain from mining to metal. The samples were delivered to a major integrated defense, medical and specialty coatings company.
•Completed CEO transition and set integration, execution and growth priorities: In October 2026, the Company completed its previously announced CEO succession plan. Thras Moraitis, who was previously President of USA Rare Earth, assumed the CEO position and will focus on integrating the Company’s rare-earth businesses, strengthening execution, focusing on responsible capital investment and shareholder returns, while reliably serving customers seeking secure supplies of critical materials. The Company organized its operations into three business units designed to support faster decision-making, accountability and disciplined execution while maintaining coordination across the platform: Upstream, covering mining, processing and separation; Midstream, covering metals, alloys and powders; and Downstream, covering magnets and derivative products.

About USA Rare Earth
USA Rare Earth, Inc. (Nasdaq: USAR) is building a fully integrated rare-earth and permanent-magnet value chain across the United States, Brazil and the United Kingdom. Through its ownership of Less Common Metals (LCM), one of the world’s leading producers of rare-earth metals and alloys, its development of magnet manufacturing capacity in Stillwater, Oklahoma, its Pela Ema mine in Brazil and the Round Top deposit in Texas, USA Rare Earth is pursuing capabilities across the value chain from mining to metal-making, alloy production and neodymium magnet manufacturing. USA Rare Earth is establishing a secure, Western-aligned supply of materials essential to the aerospace and defense, semiconductor, energy, data center, physical AI, mobility, healthcare and industrial sectors.
