PRESS RELEASE
CN Delivers Strong Third Quarter Performance
•Delivered diluted earnings per share (EPS) of C$2.00, an increase of 9% and adjusted diluted EPS of C$2.16, an increase of 18%, or 17% at constant currency (1)
•Generated free cash flow of C$2,918 million, an increase of 25% for the first nine months of 2026 (consisting of net cash provided by operating activities of C$4,780 million and net cash used in investing activities of C$1,862 million)(1)
•Realized record third quarter fuel efficiency performance and on track for annual record
•Repurchased approximately 3.9 million shares for C$676 million
MONTREAL, October 30, 2026 — CN (TSX: CNR) (NYSE: CNI) today reported its financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
"I am very proud of CN’s dedicated railroaders for delivering another strong quarter. Their relentless focus on safely executing the plan across our unique network continues to strengthen service, enhance productivity, and support our customers’ success. Together with our disciplined financial management, these rigorous efforts position CN to deliver profitable growth, continue to create long-term value for our shareholders, and capitalize on the opportunities ahead."
–Tracy Robinson, President and Chief Executive Officer, CN
Third-Quarter 2026 Results Highlights
CN's commercial momentum and service performance were supported by a 4% increase in gross ton miles (GTMs) to 116,264 (millions) and a 4% increase in RTMs to 59,383 (millions). The Company delivered diluted EPS of C$2.00, an increase of 9%, and adjusted diluted EPS of C$2.16, an increase of 18%, or C$2.14 on an adjusted basis at constant currency, an increase of 17%. (1)
The quarter’s operating performance reflects CN’s commitment to strong service execution, enabling its ability to meet customer demand in key markets including grain and energy, while continuing to advance operational improvements through Fast Track.
Quarterly Financial Results Highlights
Third-quarter 2026 compared to third-quarter 2025
•Revenues of C$4,771 million, an increase of C$606 million, or 15%.
•Operating income of C$1,730 million, an increase of C$124 million, or 8%, and adjusted operating income of C$1,862 million, an increase of C$256 million, or 16%. (1)
•Operating ratio, defined as operating expenses as a percentage of revenues, of 63.7%, an increase of 230 basis points, and adjusted operating ratio of 61.0%, an improvement of 40 basis points. (1)
•Net income of C$1,209 million, an increase of C$70 million, or 6%, and adjusted net income of C$1,307 million, an increase of C$168 million, or 15%. (1)
•Diluted EPS of C$2.00, an increase of 9% and adjusted diluted EPS of C$2.16, an increase of 18%, or C$2.14 on an adjusted basis at constant currency, an increase of 17%. (1)
•Free cash flow for the first nine months of 2026 was C$2,918 million, an increase of C$577 million, or 25%. (1)
•Adjusted EBITDA reported for the twelve months ended September 30, 2026 of C$9,080 million, an increase of 5%. (1)
•Adjusted debt-to-adjusted EBITDA of 2.61 times as at and for the twelve months ended September 30, 2026. (1)
•Repurchased approximately 3.9 million shares in the third quarter of 2026 for C$676 million.

Dividends
CN's Board of Directors has approved a fourth-quarter 2026 dividend on the Company’s common shares outstanding. A quarterly dividend of ninety-one and a half cents (C$0.9150) per common share will be paid on December 30, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 9, 2026.
2026 financial guidance (1)(2)
The Company continues to assume to deliver low single-digit RTM growth in 2026. The Company continues to expect adjusted diluted EPS growth in the mid-to-high single-digit range and is currently tracking more towards the upper end of this range.
In 2026, CN is still on track to invest approximately C$2.8 billion in its capital program, net of amounts reimbursed by customers. The Company also expects to continue improving its free cash flow conversion throughout 2026.

About CN
CN powers the economy by safely transporting more than 300 million tons of natural resources, manufactured products, and finished goods throughout North America every year for its customers. With its nearly 20,000-mile rail network and related transportation services, CN connects Canada’s Eastern and Western coasts with the U.S. Midwest and the U.S. Gulf Coast, contributing to sustainable trade and the prosperity of the communities in which it operates since 1919.

In this supplementary schedule, the "Company" or "CN" refers to Canadian National Railway Company, together with its wholly-owned subsidiaries. Financial information included in this schedule is expressed in Canadian dollars, unless otherwise noted.
CN reports its financial results in accordance with United States generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP). The Company also uses non-GAAP measures that do not have any standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP, including adjusted performance measures, free cash flow, constant currency and adjusted debt-to-adjusted EBITDA multiple. These non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies. From management's perspective, these non-GAAP measures are useful measures of performance and provide investors with supplementary information to assess the Company's results of operations and liquidity. These non-GAAP measures should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP.
Adjusted performance measures
Adjusted net income, adjusted diluted earnings per share, adjusted operating income, adjusted operating expenses and adjusted operating ratio are non-GAAP measures that are used to set performance goals and to measure CN's performance and may include the following adjustments:
i.operating expense adjustments: workforce reduction program, advisory costs related to rail consolidation matters, depreciation expense on the deployment of a replacement system, advisory fees related to shareholder matters, asset impairments and held-for-sale recoveries, business acquisition-related costs;
ii.non-operating expense adjustments: business acquisition-related financing fees, merger termination income, gains and losses on disposal of property; and
iii.the effect of changes in tax laws including rate enactments and changes in tax positions affecting prior years.
These non-GAAP measures do not have any standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP and therefore, may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies.
For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company's adjusted net income was $1,307 million, or $2.16 per diluted share, and $3,670 million, or $6.03 per diluted share, respectively. The adjusted figures for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 exclude:
•asset impairments related to CN's decision to discontinue the development of certain components of its dispatching and back office information technology projects before completion of $118 million, or $87 million after tax ($0.14 per diluted share) recorded in the third quarter in Asset impairments within the Consolidated Statements of Income, representing the full carrying amount of the affected software components, which had no alternative use and were no longer expected to provide future economic benefits;
•advisory costs related to the analysis and advocacy for the U.S. Surface Transportation Board (STB) review of the impacts to fair competition pertaining to the potential merger between Union Pacific and Norfolk Southern, including participation in ongoing merger-related regulatory proceedings, discovery activities and efforts to secure competitive access conditions, of $14 million, or $11 million after-tax ($0.02 per diluted share) and $48 million, or $36 million after-tax ($0.05 per diluted share), respectively, recorded in Purchased services and material within the Consolidated Statements of Income; and
•the sale of a portion of the Newmarket subdivision located in Washago and Sundridge, Ontario, Canada, together with rail fixtures, for cash proceeds of $84 million, which resulted in a gain of $66 million, or $57 million after-tax ($0.09 per diluted share) recorded in the first quarter in Other income within the Consolidated Statements of Income.
For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, the Company's net income was $1,139 million, or $1.83 per diluted share, and $3,472 million, or $5.54 per diluted share, respectively. There were no adjustments in the third quarter and the first nine months of 2025.
Adjusted net income is defined as Net income in accordance with GAAP adjusted for certain significant items. Management believes that adjusted net income provides additional insight to management and investors into the Company's operations and underlying business trends as well as facilitates period-to-period comparisons, as it excludes certain significant items that are not reflective of CN's underlying business operations and could distort the analysis of trends in business performance. Adjusted diluted earnings per share is defined as adjusted net income divided by the weighted-average diluted shares outstanding. This measure helps management and investors evaluate the Company's profitability on a per-share basis, facilitating the assessment of period-over-period performance by removing the impact of significant, non-recurring items.

Free cash flow
Free cash flow is a useful measure of liquidity as it demonstrates the Company's ability to generate cash for debt obligations and for discretionary uses such as payment of dividends, share repurchases, and strategic opportunities. The Company defines its free cash flow measure as the difference between net cash provided by operating activities and net cash used in investing activities, adjusted for the impact of (i) business acquisitions and combinations; and (ii) merger transaction-related payments, cash receipts and cash income taxes, which are items that are not indicative of operating trends. Free cash flow does not have any standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP and therefore, may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies.

This ASU introduces comprehensive accounting guidance for government grants received by business entities by expanding Topic 832 beyond disclosure-only requirements to include recognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure.
