Zai Lab Announces Third Quarter 2026
Financial Results and Recent Corporate Updates
Zai Lab anticipates major catalysts in 2027 across multiple global programs with transformative potential:
–Zoci 1.6 mg/kg plus atezolizumab showed compelling 1L SCLC data at ESMO: 81.8% confirmed ORR, an estimated 81.9% six-month PFS rate and 9.1% Grade ≥3 zoci-related AEs; advancing toward a global Phase 3 study in 1L SCLC
–Registrational global Phase 3 DLLEVATE study evaluating zoci in 2L+ SCLC is ongoing, with a potential interim analysis in the second half of 2027, followed by a subsequent U.S. accelerated approval submission
–Completed enrollment in zoci’s potentially registrational 2L epNECs expansion cohort, with data expected in the first half of 2027, followed by a subsequent U.S. accelerated approval submission
–ZL-1503, a long-acting IL-13/IL-31Rα bispecific antibody, is advancing toward key clinical milestones including initial first-in-human data at 2026 ACAAI and atopic dermatitis patient data expected in the first half of 2027
–Total revenues of $108.2 million in the third quarter of 2026 with 2% sequential net product revenue growth; positioned for a return to meaningful growth in 2027 as patient access expands for key products
Conference call and webcast today, November 5, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. ET (9:00 p.m. HKT)
SHANGHAI & CAMBRIDGE, Mass., November 5, 2026 -- Zai Lab Limited (NASDAQ: ZLAB; HKEX: 9688), an innovation-driven, global biopharma, today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026, along with recent product highlights and corporate updates.
“We are approaching a defining period for Zai Lab, as we advance toward bringing our first internally developed innovative medicines to patients globally,” said Dr. Samantha Du, Founder, Chairperson and Chief Executive Officer of Zai Lab. “The strong first-line combination data for zocilurtatug pelitecan (zoci) presented at ESMO strengthen our confidence in its potential to become an ADC backbone of choice in small cell lung cancer. 2027 is set to be a pivotal year, with a potential first U.S. BLA submission for zoci and continued advancement of our first-line registrational development strategy in small cell lung cancer. Beyond zoci, ZL-1503 adds a second global program, targeting atopic dermatitis, one of the largest opportunities in immunology. Together with our commercially profitable China business, which we expect to return to growth in 2027, these advancements position Zai Lab to create substantial value for patients and shareholders.”
“In first-line small cell lung cancer, zoci 1.6mg/kg plus atezolizumab demonstrated an 81.8% confirmed response rate and an encouraging 81.9% six-month progression-free survival rate, with a favorable tolerability profile,” said Rafael G. Amado, M.D., President, Head of Global Research and Development at Zai Lab. “This profile supports taking a standard chemotherapy-free regimen into a global Phase 3 study and gives us a strong foundation to build on with T-cell engager combinations. Our registrational programs in SCLC and neuroendocrine carcinomas are advancing toward readouts that could support our first U.S. BLA submission in 2027. In atopic dermatitis, ZL-1503 is designed to block two drivers of disease at once: IL-13 for inflammation and IL-31Rα for itch, with a half-life that could support less frequent dosing. We will present first-in-human data at ACAAI in November and initial patient data in the first half of 2027, and we expect two additional oncology and immunology programs to enter the clinic in 2027.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Total revenue was $108.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $116.1 million for the same period in 2025. Product revenue, net was $107.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $115.4 million for the same period in 2025. Net product revenue increased 2% versus the second quarter of 2026, primarily driven by NUZYRA, supported by increased market coverage and penetration, and KarXT, which was launched in June 2026, partially offset by a decrease in OPTUNE due to evolving competition in the glioblastoma market.
•Research and Development (R&D) expenses were $49.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $47.9 million for the same period in 2025.
•Selling, General and Administrative (SG&A) expenses were $70.1 million in both the third quarter of 2026 and the same period in 2025. R&D and SG&A both remained relatively flat year over year, reflecting continued efforts to streamline the organization, optimize resource allocation, and enhance operating efficiency as the company advances its next phase of growth.
•Loss from operations was $63.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, $47.6 million when adjusted to exclude certain non-cash expenses including depreciation, amortization, and share-based compensation. A reconciliation of loss from operations (GAAP) to adjusted loss from operations (non-GAAP) is included at the end of this release.
•Net loss was $46.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, or a loss per ordinary share attributable to shareholders of $0.04 (or loss per American Depositary Share (ADS) of $0.41), compared to a net loss of $36.0 million for the same period in 2025, or a loss per ordinary share of $0.03 (or loss per ADS of $0.33). The increase in net loss was primarily due to a $13.6 million in inventory write-downs in 2026.
•Cash and cash equivalents, current restricted cash, and short-term investments totaled $659.7 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $717.5 million as of June 30, 2026.

Recent Pipeline Highlights
Below are key product candidate updates since our last earnings release:
Oncology Pipeline
•Zocilurtatug Pelitecan (zoci, DLL3-Targeting ADC) (formerly ZL-1310):
•First-Line Extensive-Stage Small Cell Lung Cancer (1L ES-SCLC): In October 2026, Zai Lab presented promising data at the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) Congress 2026 from the ongoing Phase 1 study evaluating zoci in combination with atezolizumab as a first-line treatment for ES-SCLC. At the selected zoci dose of 1.6 mg/kg, the zoci plus atezolizumab doublet achieved a confirmed objective response rate (ORR) of 81.8% and an estimated 6-month progression-free survival (PFS) rate of 81.9% among 33 efficacy evaluable patients in 1L ES-SCLC. Among the first 15 patients with at least six months of follow-up, who had a median follow-up of 10.0 months, median PFS had not been reached. The doublet regimen was generally well tolerated,
2
with 9.1% of patients experiencing Grade 3 or higher adverse events attributed to zoci and only one reported case of Grade 1 interstitial lung disease (ILD) or pneumonitis in this cohort. Based on the encouraging efficacy and favorable safety profile, Zai Lab is advancing the zoci 1.6 mg/kg plus PD-L1 inhibitors doublet into a global Phase 3 study in 1L ES-SCLC.
•Combinations with T-Cell Engagers (TCEs): Zai Lab continues to evaluate zoci in combination with TCEs including tarlatamab and obrixtamig, in collaboration with partners Amgen and Boehringer Ingelheim, respectively. These combinations are designed to pair zoci’s rapid tumor-debulking activity and favorable tolerability profile with T-cell-mediated killing, with the goal of deepening and extending responses in SCLC.
•Second-Line+ Extrapulmonary Neuroendocrine Carcinomas (2L+ epNECs): Zai Lab completed enrollment in the Phase 2 expansion portion of the ongoing global Phase 1b/2 study evaluating zoci in patients with 2L+ epNEC. Based on feedback from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), Zai Lab will pursue a potential accelerated approval pathway based on this single-arm expansion study with topline data expected in the first half of 2027.
Immunology, Neuroscience, and Infectious Disease Pipeline
•ZL-1503 (IL-13/IL-31Rα):
•In October 2026, Zai Lab announced that initial first-in-human data from the single ascending dose (SAD) portion of the global Phase 1/1b study in healthy volunteers is expected to be shared for the first time at the American College of Allergy, Asthma & Immunology (ACAAI) 2026 Annual Scientific Meeting, taking place November 12–16, 2026, in Phoenix, AZ. Findings will include pharmacokinetic (PK) data following single-dose administration, as well as pharmacodynamic (PD) data on atopic dermatitis (AD)-related biomarkers across a range of ZL-1503 doses. The presentation will also include initial safety and immunogenicity data.
•In October 2026, Zai Lab completed enrollment for the global Phase 1 intravenous-to-subcutaneous (IV-to-SC) bioavailability bridging study.
•VYVGART (FcRn):
•Generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG) and chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy (CIDP): In October 2026, the Center for Drug Evaluation (CDE) accepted the Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls (CMC) variation application for the addition of pre-filled syringe presentation of efgartigimod subcutaneous (SC) for the treatment of gMG and CIDP.
•Myositis: In August 2026, Zai Lab and partner argenx announced positive topline results from the Phase 3 ALKIVIA study evaluating VYVGART® Hytrulo (efgartigimod alfa and hyaluronidase-qvfc) in adults with autoimmune myositis. The study met its primary endpoint of mean Total Improvement Score (TIS) at week 52 in the combined study population of immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy (IMNM) and dermatomyositis (DM) patients (p=0.0011). Patient improvements were observed early and sustained throughout the study, with a consistent treatment effect across IMNM and DM. Zai Lab participated in the ALKIVIA study in Greater China.
•Sjögren’s Disease: In October 2026, argenx announced that it will discontinue the Phase 3 UNITY study of efgartigimod SC in adults with moderate-to-severe Sjögren’s disease. The decision is based on a recommendation from an Independent Data Monitoring Committee to stop the study for futility following an interim analysis, having concluded that the UNITY study is unable to meet its primary endpoint. Safety was consistent with efgartigimod’s established profile, and no new safety signals were identified.
•Elegrobart (Anti-IGF-1R, subcutaneous): Zai Lab completed enrollment in the Phase 3 registrational study for the treatment of thyroid eye disease in China.
