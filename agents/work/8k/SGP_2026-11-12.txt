SpyGlass Pharma Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Updates
•Acquired Advanced Vision Science, Inc. (AVS), securing scalable commercial intraocular lens (IOL) manufacturing capacity for its Phase 3 lead product candidate the Bimatoprost Drug Pad-IOL System (BIM-IOL System) and accelerating premium IOL portfolio expansion capability.
•Announced groundbreaking four-year first-in-human (FIH) efficacy and safety follow-up data of the BIM-IOL System, with a 37% reduction in mean intraocular pressure (IOP) across all dose levels and 86% of patients remaining off all topical IOP-lowering drops.
•Enrollment remains on track in the registrational Phase 3 trials of the BIM-IOL System, with completion expected in 2027.
•Announces completion of first implantations in the FIH study of the Bimatoprost-Drug Ring System (BIM-DRS), a non-IOL-based sustained drug delivery system for glaucoma designed to deliver up to seven years of bimatoprost.
•Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $215.8 million on September 30, 2026, are expected to fund planned operations through 2028.
IRVINE, Calif., Nov. 11, 2026 – SpyGlass Pharma, Inc. (Nasdaq: SGP) (“SpyGlass Pharma” or “Company”), a late-stage biopharmaceutical company, today reported recent business highlights and financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“SpyGlass Pharma continued to execute our objectives over the course of the quarter as we aim to alter the treatment paradigm for glaucoma patients. Our underlying sustained drug delivery platform delivered four years of safety and efficacy results in the FIH trial of the BIM-IOL System, demonstrating that it can control IOP with unprecedented durability. In parallel, enrollment in our Phase 3 trials of the BIM-IOL System remains on track, and we have secured long-term commercial manufacturing supply of the IOL for the BIM-IOL System and have become a supplier of other world-class IOLs,” stated Patrick Mooney, chief executive officer of SpyGlass Pharma. “We are pleased to announce that our BIM-DRS is now in the clinic, with successful implantations of the next-generation drug delivery system in glaucoma patients who have had prior cataract surgery.”
Corporate Highlights
Acquisition of Advanced Vision Science, Inc. In October, SpyGlass Pharma acquired all the outstanding shares of common stock of AVS, an ophthalmic medical device company and manufacturer of IOLs and lens materials, for approximately $13 million in cash. This acquisition secures scalable commercial IOL manufacturing capacity for SpyGlass Pharma. AVS was a wholly owned subsidiary of Santen Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. and is the current supplier of SpyGlass Pharma’s IOL in its two Phase 3 registrational trials evaluating the BIM-IOL System.
Announced Four-Year BIM-IOL FIH Efficacy and Safety Follow-Up Data. In October, SpyGlass Pharma announced groundbreaking four-year follow-up data of its FIH study of the BIM-IOL System, which demonstrated that 86% of patients were free from using topical IOP-lowering drops and showed a 37% reduction in mean IOP across all doses. Additionally, 90% of patients maintained ≥20/25 best corrected distance visual acuity (BCDVA). SpyGlass Pharma designed the BIM-IOL System in the FIH trial to deliver bimatoprost for up to seven years. No product-related adverse events were reported to date.
Presentation of Detailed Phase 1/2 Data from the BIM-IOL System Clinical Program at the American Academy of Ophthalmology (AAO) Annual Meeting. In October, Jeffrey Kammer, M.D., associate professor of ophthalmology at Vanderbilt University, presented topline 12-month Phase 1/2 data at AAO in New Orleans. The detailed 12-month safety and efficacy results reinforced the sustained IOP reduction at that timepoint, and the elimination of topical eye drops for 98% of patients who received the intended commercial dose of the BIM-IOL System. Additionally, the BIM-IOL System demonstrated improved visual performance, with mean BCDVA of 86 letters, which is equivalent to 20/20 vision. Lastly, Dr. Kammer presented new data showing endothelial cell count loss following BIM-IOL System implantation was consistent with routine cataract surgery.
Completed First Implantations of the BIM-DRS in FIH Trial. In October, SpyGlass Pharma successfully completed first implantations of its next-generation drug delivery platform in the FIH trial of the BIM-DRS. BIM-DRS is a non-IOL-based system that is implanted in the ciliary sulcus and available to all ophthalmic surgeons, designed to deliver bimatoprost for up to seven years with removal and replacement capability to deliver lifetime IOP control without the burden of daily eye drops. Importantly, BIM-DRS represents the expansion of SpyGlass Pharma’s drug delivery technology beyond traditional cataract surgery and has the potential to provide continuous care over the lifetime of a patient. SpyGlass Pharma anticipates providing preliminary safety and IOP-lowering efficacy data for the BIM-DRS FIH trial in 2027.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Cash, Cash Equivalents and Short-Term Investments totaled $215.8 million as of September 30, 2026.
Research and Development Expenses were $10.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $8.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to the hiring of clinical personnel and continued enrollment in the Phase 3 registrational trials for the BIM-IOL System.
General and Administrative Expenses were $16.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $3.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher professional service fees and personnel costs.
Net Loss was $24.3 million, or ($0.73) per basic and diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $9.8 million, or ($4.53) per basic and diluted share, for the same period of 2025.
About the Bimatoprost Drug Pad-IOL System
SpyGlass Pharma’s lead product candidate, the BIM-IOL System, comprising novel, proprietary non-bioerodible drug pads attached to its intraocular lens, was designed to be implanted during routine cataract surgery to reduce elevated IOP in patients who have either open-angle glaucoma (OAG) or ocular hypertension (OHT). The BIM-IOL System being evaluated in ongoing Phase 3 clinical trials is designed to consistently deliver three years of bimatoprost, a prostaglandin analog approved for topical use by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in 2001, for the reduction of elevated IOP in patients with OAG or OHT.
SpyGlass Pharma continues long-term follow-up of patients in both the FIH trial and Phase 1/2 trial as well as patient enrollment in ongoing Phase 3 clinical trials. SpyGlass Pharma plans to work with the FDA to advance the program through 505(b)(2) New Drug Application (NDA) submission and ultimately to potential commercial approval.
About the Bimatoprost-Drug Ring System
BIM-DRS is a novel, non-bioerodible, non-IOL-based, sustained-release implant, designed to consistently deliver up to seven years of bimatoprost. BIM-DRS is implanted in a standalone procedure in the ciliary sulcus space, offering extended care for pseudophakic eyes with OAG or OHT and enabling retreatment of patients who have previously received the BIM-IOL System. BIM-DRS is engineered to be a removable and replaceable system, potentially allowing for continuous care over a patient’s lifetime.
About SpyGlass Pharma
SpyGlass Pharma is a late-stage biopharmaceutical company dedicated to transforming the treatment paradigm for patients living with chronic eye conditions through long-acting, sustained drug delivery of approved medicines. The Company’s mission is to significantly improve the lives of patients with chronic eye conditions by developing durable drug delivery solutions that can empower patients and surgeons with confidence in long-term disease control and vision preservation.
The SpyGlass Pharma platform, a novel, non-bioerodible drug delivery technology, is designed to be used with various well-established, approved medicines, including bimatoprost and other small molecules, providing flexibility to potentially treat a range of conditions in the front and back of the eye.
The Company was founded in 2019 by Malik Y. Kahook, M.D. and Glenn Sussman to solve the lack of ophthalmic innovations that capitalize on durable treatment options. The SpyGlass Pharma platform was originally developed in the Sue Anschutz-Rodgers Eye Center at the University of Colorado Anschutz School of Medicine.
