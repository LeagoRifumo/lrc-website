Ceribell Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Sunnyvale, Calif. – November 3, 2026 – CeriBell, Inc. (Nasdaq: CBLL) (“Ceribell”), a medical technology company focused on transforming the diagnosis and management of patients with serious neurological conditions, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 & Recent Highlights
•
Reported total revenue of $29.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, a 30% increase compared to the same period in 2025
•
Ended the quarter with 747 total active accounts, adding a record 35 net new customers in the quarter
•
Drove double-digit year-over-year same-store utilization growth
•
Achieved gross margin of 90%
•
Announced the full launch of the Company’s delirium monitoring solution following the conclusion of its successful commercial pilot
•
Received U.S. Food and Drug Administration 510(k) clearance for Quantitative EEG Trending and Amplitude-Integrated EEG Analysis
“Our third quarter results reflect new highs in account additions and utilization within our existing account base, demonstrating the strength of our core seizure business as a durable growth engine,” said co-founder and CEO Jane Chao, Ph.D. “With the full launch of our delirium monitoring solution and the coming introduction of our new hardware platform, we are well positioned for a pivotal 2027 as we advance our mission to establish EEG as a new vital sign.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was $29.3 million, a 30% increase from $22.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by the success of the Company’s same-store growth strategy, which delivered record utilization within its existing account base, in addition to increased adoption of the Ceribell System across new and existing accounts. Product revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $22.2 million, representing an increase of 31% from $17.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Subscription revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $7.1 million, representing an increase of 28% from $5.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, reflecting continued growth in the Company’s installed base of Clarity subscriptions.
Gross profit in the third quarter of 2026 was $26.3 million, compared to $19.9 million for the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 90%, compared to 88% for the same period in 2025. Gross margin strength was driven by manufacturing cost reduction initiatives and the impact of the Company’s introduction of a fully operational manufacturing line in Vietnam.
Operating expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $46.9 million, compared to $34.6 million for the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 36%. The increase in operating expenses was primarily attributable to continued investments in the Company’s commercial organization, including infrastructure in advance of the delirium launch, investments in research and development to support its next-generation hardware platform, algorithm development and clinical studies, stock-based compensation expense, and legal costs associated with intellectual property enforcement activities.
Net loss in the third quarter of 2026 was $21.4 million, or $0.55 net loss per share, compared to a net loss of $13.5 million, or $0.37 net loss per share, for the same period in 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA loss (a non-GAAP measure) for the third quarter of 2026 was $12.1 million, compared to $9.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. Reconciliations of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 are in the financial schedules that are included with this press release.
Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $117.5 million as of September 30, 2026. In August 2026, the Company further strengthened its balance sheet by entering into a new credit facility providing access to up to $60 million in committed capital, comprising a $30 million term loan and a $30 million revolving credit facility, with an additional $25 million of uncommitted capital available at the lender’s discretion.
2026 Financial Outlook
Ceribell is raising its revenue guidance for the full year 2026 to a range of $115 million to $117 million, representing growth of approximately 29% to 31% over the Company’s prior year revenue. This update reflects the Company’s continued momentum in its core business, driven by both new account additions and increased utilization within its established account base. The updated range does not include material contributions from the Company’s neonate, pediatric, or delirium products.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are non-GAAP financial measures. Ceribell defines EBITDA as GAAP net loss adjusted to exclude (i) provision for income taxes, (ii) depreciation and amortization expense, and (iii) interest income and interest expense, net. EBITDA is then adjusted to exclude (iv) stock-based compensation expense, (v) legal fees and related professional services costs incurred in connection with the patent infringement action we filed against Natus Medical Incorporated and certain of its subsidiaries in July 2025, as further described in Part II, Item 1 — Legal Proceedings of Ceribell's Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, and (vi) the loss on debt extinguishment to arrive at Adjusted EBITDA. Management uses EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA to evaluate ongoing operations and for internal planning and forecasting purposes. Ceribell believes EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA provide investors with meaningful supplemental information regarding its performance by excluding certain items that may not be indicative of its business, results of operations, or outlook. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation, as a substitute for, or superior to GAAP net loss, and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies. Reconciliations between U.S. GAAP and non-GAAP results are presented in the accompanying tables of this release.
About CeriBell, Inc.
Ceribell is a medical technology company focused on transforming the diagnosis and management of patients with serious neurological conditions. Ceribell has developed the Ceribell System, a novel point-of-care electroencephalography (“EEG”) platform specifically designed to address the unmet needs of patients in the acute-care setting. By combining proprietary, highly portable, and rapidly deployable hardware with sophisticated artificial intelligence (“AI”)-powered algorithms, the Ceribell System enables rapid diagnosis and continuous monitoring of patients with neurological conditions. The Ceribell System is FDA-cleared for use in detecting seizure and monitoring delirium in intensive care units and emergency rooms across the U.S. Ceribell is headquartered in Sunnyvale, California. For more information, please visit https://www.ceribell.com or follow the company on LinkedIn.
