São Paulo, November 5, 2026 – Suzano S.A. (“Company” or “Suzano”) (B3: SUZB3 | NYSE: SUZ), in accordance with the provisions of Law No. 6,404, of December 15, 1976, as amended, CVM Resolution No. 44, of August 23, 2021, and CVM Resolution No. 80, of March 29, 2022, as amended, hereby informs its shareholders, investors, and the market in general as follows:
1. Net debt estimates
Further to the Material Fact disclosed on May 11, 2026, and in continuation of the information provided, the Company estimates that its net debt will close fiscal year 2026 below US$14.0 billion (fourteen billion U.S. dollars), based on an assumed year-end 2026 exchange rate (BRL/USD) of R$5.40.
The other projections and assumptions previously disclosed by the Company regarding its net debt and leverage estimates, which do not include the effects arising from the acquisition of Arbex on July 1, 2026, remain unchanged, namely, that its net debt and leverage targets in U.S. dollars, expected to be reached over fiscal years 2027 and 2028, are, respectively: (a) US$11.0 billion and (b) below 2.5x.
2. Pulp cash production cost estimates – 2026 annual average
Suzano also informs that, due to the revision of the assumptions regarding the average exchange rate (BRL/USD), the Brent price and the result impact on input costs associated with the conflict scenario in the Middle East, its estimated average pulp cash production cost for 2026, excluding the effects of scheduled maintenance downtimes, has been updated from R$800 per tonne to approximately R$825 per tonne. The new estimate is based on the following assumptions: i) an average exchange rate (BRL/USD) of R$5.20 for the year (compared to R$5.07 in the previous estimate); and ii) a Brent price (ICE Brent Crude) of US$94 per barrel (compared to US$84 per barrel in the previous estimate).
3. Update of Reference Form
The Company also clarifies that item 3 of the Reference Form will be duly updated within the deadline set in CVM Resolution 80/22.
By disclosing the information contained in this Material Fact notice, the Company reaffirms its commitment to transparency with its shareholders, investors and the market and will keep them adequately informed of any significant change in the estimated long-term operational performance announced.
