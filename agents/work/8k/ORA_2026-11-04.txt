“Our strong third-quarter performance was driven by record Energy Storage profitability, supported by newly added capacity, high asset availability and favorable merchant pricing in PJM. Revenue increased 10.2%, net income attributable to stockholders rose 32.2%, and Adjusted EBITDA grew 11.1%. Our year-to-date performance and fourth-quarter outlook support an increase in the midpoints of our full-year revenue and Adjusted EBITDA guidance,” said Doron Blachar, Chief Executive Officer of Ormat Technologies. “While we continue to add new capacity and expand our Electricity segment portfolio, segment revenue slightly declined 2.1% year-over-year in the third quarter. This was primarily due to lower generation at Puna following a hurricane-related forced outage, partially offset by the July commencement of commercial operation at our 10 MW Dominica geothermal plant and improved performance at our Olkaria plant.”
“We remain focused on executing the growth plan outlined at our recent Investor Day, doubling our YE 2025 capacity and achieving annual revenue and Adjusted EBITDA run rates exceeding $1.5 billion and $1 billion, respectively, by the end of 2030. These targets exclude potential contributions from EGS. With growing demand for reliable, around-the-clock power and an expanding portfolio of contracted projects, we believe Ormat is well positioned to deliver sustainable long-term growth and shareholder value.”
Blachar added, “Ormat’s six decades of geothermal experience and integrated capabilities position the Company to participate in EGS as a developer, owner, operator and technology provider, while pursuing a diversified approach that extends beyond a single emerging technology. We are advancing two pilot projects with SLB and Sage and negotiating power purchase agreements for Dixie Valley, our first planned commercial EGS project, and for other potential projects. Since the start of the year, we have also secured approximately 40,000 acres of BLM leases for EGS development and identified additional acreage with EGS potential within our existing portfolio. Our total EGS land position now stands at approximately 170,000 acres, which we estimate could support 5 to 6 gigawatts of potential future EGS development, a significant opportunity for long-term growth.”
FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS
• | Net income attributable to stockholders for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $31.9 million, or $0.51 per diluted share, compared to $24.1 million, or $0.39 per diluted share, in the prior year period. The 32.2% increase was mainly driven by the increase in the Energy Storage revenue and gross profit.

• | Adjusted net income for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $34.8 million, or $0.56 per diluted share, compared to $24.9 million, or $0.41 per diluted share, in the prior year period.

• | Adjusted EBITDA for the three months ended September 30, 2026, increased 11.1% to $153.8 million, driven by strong contributions from the Energy Storage segment, which benefited from higher merchant prices, high availability and portfolio expansion.

• | Electricity segment revenues declined 2.1% in the third quarter compared to the prior year period, primarily driven by lower generation at Puna due to a hurricane-related forced outage, partially offset by the commencement of commercial operation at our power plant in Dominica and improved performance at our Olkaria plant.

• | Energy Storage revenues for the three months ended September 30, 2026, increased 145.2% compared to the prior year period. Growth was driven by the high availability of our assets, which allowed us to capitalize on strong merchant pricing in the PJM market, as well as new portfolio capacity additions over the past 12 months.

• | Product segment revenues for the three months ended September 30, 2026, declined 1.2% compared to the prior year period due largely to the timing of manufacturing and construction progress.

• | Product segment backlog stands at approximately $183 million as of November 4, 2026.

BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS:
• | In August 2026, Ormat decided to proceed with the development of the 100 MW / 400 MWh Denali energy storage facility in California. Upon completion, expected by the end of 2028, the project is anticipated to provide energy storage services under a 20-year tolling agreement with Clean Power Alliance.

• | In July 2026, Ormat achieved commercial operation of its 10 MW Dominica geothermal power plant, reflecting continued execution across the Company's global development pipeline.

EGS UPDATE:
• | Ormat continues to advance the two EGS pilot programs with SLB and Sage, with drilling expected to commence late 2026 or early 2027 in both locations.

• | Ormat is negotiating PPAs for Dixie Valley, Nevada, its first planned commercial EGS project, and for other potential EGS projects.

• | In September 2026, Ormat was selected by the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) for up to $35 million in funding, alongside SLB and the University of Utah, to advance enhanced geothermal systems at Dixie Valley, Nevada, and hydrothermal development at Cove Fort, Utah.

• | Since its Investor Day, Ormat has significantly expanded its EGS land position, from approximately 70,000 acres to approximately 170,000 acres. This includes approximately 16,000 acres of new BLM leases acquired in Nevada’s October 2026 lease sale and significant existing land portfolio that have been identified as having EGS potential. Ormat estimates that this expanded land position could support approximately 5 to 6 GW of potential future EGS development, compared with the approximately 3 to 4 GW estimate presented at Investor Day.

ABOUT ORMAT TECHNOLOGIES
With over six decades of experience, Ormat Technologies, Inc. is a leading geothermal company, and the only vertically integrated company engaged in geothermal and recovered energy generation (“REG”), with robust plans to accelerate long-term growth in the energy storage market and to establish a leading position in the U.S. energy storage market. The Company owns, operates, designs, manufactures and sells geothermal and REG power plants primarily based on the Ormat Energy Converter – a power generation unit that converts low-, medium- and high-temperature heat into electricity. The Company has engineered, manufactured and constructed power plants, which it currently owns or has installed for utilities and developers worldwide, totaling approximately 3,600MW of gross capacity. Ormat has leveraged its core capabilities in the geothermal and REG industries and its global presence to expand the Company’s activity into energy storage services, solar Photovoltaic (PV) and energy storage plus Solar PV. Ormat’s current total generating portfolio is 1,853MW with a 1,358MW geothermal and solar generation portfolio that is spread globally in the U.S., Kenya, Guatemala, Indonesia, Honduras, Guadeloupe, and Dominica, and a 495MW energy storage portfolio that is located in the U.S.
Ormat is the only single-solution company with tangible and proven end-to-end capabilities that span subsurface exploration, project development, power plant technology, construction and operations. The Company is leveraging this experience to advance EGS pilot programs and commercial development opportunities. As electricity demand accelerates, driven by AI data centers, electric vehicles, industrial reshoring, and the electrification of heating and cooling, Ormat's geothermal and EGS capabilities position the Company to help meet this growing demand and lead technological advancement in the market. The Company's industry-leading business model and strong reputation are built on long-standing expertise, operational excellence, high-performance teams, and delivering reliable long-term energy generation.

Reconciliation of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA
We calculate EBITDA as net income before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization and accretion. We calculate Adjusted EBITDA as net income before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization and accretion, adjusted for (i) mark-to-market gains or losses from accounting for derivatives not designated as hedging instruments; (ii) stock-based compensation; (iii) merger and acquisition transaction costs; (iv) gain or loss from extinguishment of liabilities, including induced conversion expense; (v) cost related to a settlement agreement; (vi) non-cash impairment charges; (vii) write-off of unsuccessful exploration and storage activities; (viii) allowance for bad debts; (ix) bargain purchase gains; and (x) other unusual or non-recurring items. We adjust for these factors as they may be non-cash, unusual in nature and/or are not factors used by management for evaluating operating performance. We believe that presentation of these measures will enhance an investor’s ability to evaluate our financial and operating performance. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are not measurements of financial performance or liquidity under accounting principles generally accepted in the United States, or U.S. GAAP, and should not be considered as an alternative to cash flow from operating activities or as a measure of liquidity or an alternative to net earnings as indicators of our operating performance or any other measures of performance derived in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Our Board of Directors and senior management use EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA to evaluate our financial performance. However, other companies in our industry may calculate EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA differently than we do.
