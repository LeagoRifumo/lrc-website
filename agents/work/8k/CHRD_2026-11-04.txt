Chord Energy Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results, Declares Base Dividend, and Updates 2026 Outlook
Houston, Texas — November 4, 2026 — Chord Energy Corporation (NASDAQ: CHRD) (“Chord,” “Chord Energy,” or the “Company”) today reported financial and operating results for the third quarter 2026.
Key Takeaways and Updates:
•Operational Strength: Cash Flow from Operations and Adjusted Free Cash Flow(1) exceeded expectations in 3Q26, supported by production volumes above the high-end of guidance and strong price realizations;
•Raising Volumes & Adjusted Free Cash Flow Estimates: Strong operational performance led Chord to increase FY26 oil volumes ~1% to 162.5 MBopd and FY26 Adjusted Free Cash Flow expectations increased by ~$200MM since the August outlook;
•Shareholder Returns: Returned capital through the $1.30/share base dividend and $205MM of share repurchases at an average price of $135.74/share during 3Q26 with another $78MM repurchased in 4Q26 as of October 30, 2026 at an average price of $146.84/share;
•Marketing Optimization: Executed numerous third-party marketing agreements YTD, which are expected to deliver $35MM-$55MM annualized Adjusted Free Cash Flow savings over time, and also identified additional small-scale midstream investment opportunities. See “Marketing Update” below for additional information;
•Executing 4-Mile Laterals: Successfully executed and turned in line (“TIL”) 24 additional 4-mile wells across five pads;
•Enhancing Base Production: Continuing to benefit from strength in base production performance which is driving volumes above the initial 2026 plan;
•Driving Run-Rate Savings: Expected annual run-rate savings now $225MM, more than double the amount expected in the initial 2025 update, with the recent increase driven by base production optimization and further marketing improvements; and
•Marcellus Divestiture: Announced divestiture of non-operated Marcellus assets for total gross consideration of $550.0 million, including $55.0 million deposit received during 3Q26. Closing is expected in 4Q26.
3Q26 Operational and Financial Highlights:
•Strong Volumes: Oil volumes of 165.5 MBopd were above the top-end of guidance. Natural gas and NGL volumes were also above the top-end of guidance;
•Capital Discipline: CapEx of $371MM, after excluding $4.5MM of reimbursable non-op CapEx and $6.0MM of reimbursable Marcellus CapEx that exceeded expectations, was slightly below midpoint of guidance;
•Realizations: Oil differentials were favorable to expectations as were natural gas and NGL realizations;
•Profitability: Net income was $181.6MM ($3.29/diluted share) and Adjusted Net Income(1) was $332.9MM ($6.08/diluted share); and
•Cash Generation: Net cash provided by operating activities was $781.4MM, Adjusted EBITDA(1) was $871.3MM and Adjusted Free Cash Flow(1) was $442MM (excluding $4.5MM of reimbursable non-op CapEx and $6.0MM of reimbursable Marcellus CapEx that exceeded expectations).
(1) Non-GAAP financial measure. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below for a reconciliation to the most directly comparable financial measures under United States generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”).

Marketing Update:
Chord has executed numerous third-party marketing agreements year-to-date, which are expected to deliver $35MM-$55MM of annualized Adjusted Free Cash Flow savings. These agreements encompass crude oil, natural gas and water marketing and midstream services across the Company’s Williston Basin acreage position, reflecting Chord’s ongoing commitment to continuous improvement, cost efficiency and value creation.
Additionally, Chord has identified additional small-scale midstream investment opportunities with competitive economics. These projects are largely focused on water gathering and disposal and are expected to lead to total FY26 midstream CapEx of $50MM vs. $30MM in February guidance.
Operations Update:
•4-Mile Laterals: As of 3Q26, the Company has turned in line 47 total 4-mile wells. Execution and production results continue to be in line with expectations, supported by multi-well efficiencies. Chord has scaled its 4-mile program throughout 2026, with 70% of its lateral footage drilled as 4-mile wells, as of September 30, 2026.
•Execution: Chord continues to demonstrate strong operational execution and improved cycle times across its Williston Basin program. The Company continues to drive 4-mile efficiencies, with 70% of its laterals being drilled in a single run. As compared to 2025, Chord’s 2026 frac productivity has improved by over 20%, achieving a mile per day of completed lateral length in 3Q26. Cleanouts continue to reach total depth, ensuring contribution from the toe.
•Production/LOE: Continued execution improvements on base production helped drive production above the high-end of guidance. This has been driven by a broad set of initiatives across logistics, mechanical optimization, returning shut-in wells to production, and various chemical treatments. The team continues to generate new ideas, aided by AI screening tools, and Chord is testing multiple new approaches within its chemical workover program.
2026 Outlook Update:
Chord is updating its 2026 guidance to reflect 3Q26 performance and its latest forecasts, which assumes the Marcellus divestiture closes mid-4Q26. In 2026, Chord expects to generate approximately $3.4B of Adjusted EBITDA and $1.6B of Adjusted Free Cash Flow, including the impact of derivatives ($90/Bbl WTI and $3.00/MMBtu Henry Hub for 4Q26).
Key Update Summary:
•Volumes: FY26 oil volume midpoint guidance increased ~1% from 161.0 MBopd to 162.5 MBopd;
•4Q26 oil volume midpoint guidance of 161.0 MBopd reflects continued strong operational performance from production optimization and the development program;
•Capital: FY26 CapEx midpoint guidance increasing from $1.4B to $1.46B;
•FY26 CapEx range includes ~$20MM in incremental midstream spending vs. August guidance;
•4Q26 CapEx midpoint guidance of $330MM reflects higher midstream spending and higher operated and non-operated activity;
•FY26 CapEx excludes ~$10MM of 2H26 Marcellus CapEx that exceeded expectations vs. original guidance, which will be fully reimbursed at closing;
•Realizations: Adjusting realization guidance to reflect the current market outlook and for the Marcellus divestiture;
▪LOE: Adjusting for impacts of the Marcellus divestiture;
•Cash GPT: Lowering Cash GPT guidance reflecting the impact of marketing optimization discussed above and the Marcellus divestiture;
•Cash Taxes: Adjusting FY26 for YTD performance. Cash Tax (% of Adjusted EBITDA) FY26 midpoint guidance remains essentially unchanged vs. August guidance; and
•Activity: Chord plans to TIL 149–155 gross operated wells in FY26 with an average working interest of ~75%. 4Q26 volumes and 4Q26 CapEx reflect a sequential decline in activity as a result of dropping the second frac crew in July, with plans to reinstate the second crew in late 2026 or early 2027.

Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share
Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share are supplemental non-GAAP financial measures that are used by management and external users of the Company’s financial statements, such as industry analysts, investors, lenders and rating agencies. The Company defines Adjusted Net Income as net income after adjusting for (1) the impact of certain non-cash items, including non-cash changes in the fair value of derivative instruments, non-cash changes in the fair value of the Company’s investment in equity securities, impairment, loss on debt extinguishment and other similar non-cash charges (2) merger and acquisition costs and (3) the impact of taxes based on an estimated tax rate applicable to those adjusting items in the same period. Adjusted Net Income is not a measure of net income as determined by GAAP.
The Company calculates earnings per share under the two-class method in accordance with GAAP. The two-class method is an earnings allocation formula that computes earnings per share for each class of common stock and participating security according to dividends declared (or accumulated) and participation rights in undistributed earnings. Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share is calculated as (i) Adjusted Net Income (ii) less distributed and undistributed earnings allocated to participating securities (iii) divided by the weighted average number of diluted shares outstanding for the periods presented.
