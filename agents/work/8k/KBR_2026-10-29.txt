HOUSTON, TX - October 29, 2026 - KBR, Inc. (NYSE: KBR) today announced its third quarter fiscal 2026 results.
“We delivered a strong third quarter, with profitable growth and solid execution across both businesses reinforcing our confidence in the full-year outlook,” said Stuart Bradie, President and Chief Executive Officer. “We also achieved important separation milestones during the quarter, including publication of the Form 10 and the successful implementation of key standalone systems and infrastructure. With leadership teams in place, strong customer demand across both businesses and Investor Days approaching, KBR and Trinzic are well positioned to launch as independent companies in January.”

Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Consolidated Results Review
(All comparisons against the third quarter fiscal 2025 unless noted.)
Revenues were $2.0 billion, up 5% or $97 million, primarily due to strong execution of engineering and professional services projects within Sustainable Technology Solutions, partially offset by anticipated reductions in NASA programs within Mission Technology Solutions.
Operating income was $186 million, down 3% or $5 million, as higher gross profit driven by increased revenues was more than offset by increased spin-off costs associated with the planned separation, higher selling, general and administrative expenses and lower equity in earnings from unconsolidated joint ventures.
Net income attributable to KBR was $133 million, up 16% or $18 million, reflecting strong operating performance, lower interest expense and a lower effective tax rate driven by interest income associated with a discrete tax benefit, partially offset by higher spin-off costs associated with the planned separation.

Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Segment Results Review
(All comparisons against the third quarter fiscal 2025 unless noted.)
Mission Technology Solutions (MTS)
Revenues were $1.3 billion, down 5% or $60 million, primarily due to lower U.S. Government Federal Civilian customer volumes associated with anticipated NASA headwinds, partially offset by growth across Defense & Intelligence and International Government customers.
Operating income was $115 million, up 2% or $2 million, reflecting higher equity in earnings from unconsolidated joint ventures, favorable portfolio mix and strong project execution, partially offset by lower revenues and higher selling, general and administrative expenses. Operating income margin was 9.1%.
Adjusted EBITDA2 was $145 million, up 5% or $7 million, reflecting higher unconsolidated joint venture adjusted EBITDA contributions and favorable portfolio mix. Adjusted EBITDA2 margin was 11.5%.
Backlog and options as of the quarter end totaled $17.3 billion. Book-to-bill1 was 0.6x for the quarter. Excluding the NASA insourcing debooking, book-to-bill¹ was 0.8x for the quarter.
The following new business awards were announced:
•Re-awarded the $8 billion ceiling Antarctic Science and Engineering Support Contract (ASESC) for the U.S. National Science Foundation, supporting U.S. Antarctic Program stations and research camps under a 20-year single-award IDIQ contract following resolution of the GAO protest. This award is not yet reflected in backlog or book-to-bill.
•Awarded the $208 million U.S. Army Tactical Aviation and Ground Munitions (TAGM) task order to provide engineering, technical and programmatic support for precision strike and aviation modernization programs over a five-year period of performance.
•Awarded the $60 million NATO PATRIOT air and missile defense support contract to provide technical and engineering services supporting the PATRIOT system and its integration with the Integrated Battle Command System (IBCS).
•Awarded the UK Ministry of Justice Workplace Services Management contract to provide integrated workplace services across the Ministry's estate, extending a longstanding relationship spanning more than eight years.
•Awarded a three-year contract to provide radio frequency engineering and modernization services for a U.S. government customer supporting mission-critical national security systems.
During the quarter, we reached a settlement agreement with U.S. TRANSCOM related to the Global Household Goods Contract, resolving outstanding matters and converting the government's prior termination for default to termination for convenience.

Sustainable Technology Solutions (STS)
Revenues were $762 million, up 26% or $157 million, driven by the conversion of strong bookings secured over the past several quarters into revenue growth.

Planned Spin-Off of Mission Technology Solutions and Investor Days
KBR continues to advance the planned separation of its Mission Technology Solutions business, which will become Trinzic upon completion of the spin-off. The transaction is targeted to be completed on January 4, 2027, subject to final approval by KBR's Board of Directors and other customary conditions, including the effectiveness of the registration statement on Form 10 filed with the SEC on September 30, 2026. Upon completion, KBR and its shareholders are expected to benefit from ownership in two independent public companies, each with enhanced strategic focus, operational flexibility and distinct capital allocation priorities.
To provide investors with a deeper look at the strategies, growth opportunities and financial outlooks of both businesses, KBR and Trinzic will host separate Investor Day events in New York City on November 11, 2026 and November 12, 2026, respectively. The events will feature presentations from each company's leadership team and will be webcast live.
Additional information regarding the planned spin-off, including registration details for KBR's and Trinzic's Investor Day events, is available on the Investor Relations section of KBR's website at investors.kbr.com/news-and-events/spin-off-information.

About KBR
KBR is a global, capital-light lifecycle solutions company serving customers in high-complexity industrial, energy and infrastructure markets. Through its advisory, technical, engineering and operating expertise, KBR helps customers shape investments, reduce risk, deploy complex technologies, improve performance and deliver reliable outcomes across the asset lifecycle.
Following the planned separation of the Mission Technology Solutions business, KBR will operate as a focused standalone company with differentiated customer relationships, global execution capabilities and a capital-efficient business model. The company is positioned to benefit from long-term secular growth trends across energy security, energy transition, industrial modernization, and infrastructure investment. KBR's 16,000 employees operate across more than 30 countries.
Visit www.kbr.com
About Trinzic
KBR’s Mission Technology Solutions business is expected to be spun off as an independent public company in January 2027 and will then operate under the new name Trinzic. The name is inspired by the word intrinsic, reflecting the essential capabilities, deep expertise, speed and trusted performance that have defined the business for decades. Trinzic will enter the market as a global company and partner to customers supporting some of the highest priority missions across national security, human performance, global operations and space. Trinzic will launch with more than $5 billion in annual revenue, established partnerships and contracts, 20,000 employees and a global footprint.
Visit www.trinzic.com

Adjusted EBITDA can also be defined as Operating income less Net income attributable to noncontrolling interests from continuing operations; plus D&A; and certain discrete items as identified by Management to be non-recurring in nature as set forth below. Adjusted EBITDA margin is calculated as Adjusted EBITDA divided by Revenues.
