Ibotta Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Ibotta’s third quarter financial results exceeded the upper end of the guidance range for both revenue and adjusted EBITDA
Revenue grew by 17% year-over-year to $97.8 million
Total redemption revenue grew by 23% year-over-year to $88.5 million and third-party publisher redemption revenue grew by 43% to $70.7 million
Generated net loss of $3.5 million, representing net loss as a percent of revenue of (3.6)%, and adjusted EBITDA of $23.8 million, representing a 24.3% adjusted EBITDA margin
DENVER, November 2, 2026 (Business Wire) -- Ibotta, Inc. (NYSE: IBTA), the performance marketing platform for promotions, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“We delivered accelerating revenue growth in the third quarter, with revenue up 17% and Adjusted EBITDA up 43% year-over-year, both exceeding our expectations,” said Ibotta CEO and Founder, Bryan Leach. “Redemption revenue grew 23% and our redeemer base grew 30% to 23.6 million, a direct outcome of improving advertiser offer supply, consistently strong execution by our team, and significant consumer demand for value.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:
•Total revenue of $97.8 million, a year-over-year increase of 17%.
•Total redemption revenue of $88.5 million, an increase of 23% year-over-year, driven by increased offer supply.
•During the quarter, the IPN had 23.6 million redeemers, compared to 18.2 million redeemers in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 30% year-over-year, primarily driven by growth with existing publishers
•Third-party publisher redemptions of 97.6 million, compared to 62.1 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 57% year-over-year.
•Generated net loss of $3.5 million, representing net loss as a percent of revenue of (3.6)%, and non-GAAP net income of $16.0 million, representing non-GAAP net income as a percent of revenue of 16.4%.
•Delivered adjusted EBITDA of $23.8 million, representing an adjusted EBITDA margin of 24.3%.
•Generated cash from operating activities of $22.0 million and free cash flow of $16.8 million.
•Repurchased 0.4 million shares for a total of $14.4 million at an average price per share of $36.39, exclusive of broker commissions and excise tax.

Adjusted EBITDA margin is calculated as adjusted EBITDA as a percent of revenue. Non-GAAP net income excludes stock-based compensation, restructuring charges, and the related income tax effects. Non-GAAP diluted net income per share is calculated as non-GAAP net income divided by non-GAAP diluted weighted average common shares outstanding. Free cash flow is defined as cash provided by operating activities, less additions to property and equipment and additions to capitalized software development costs.

Key Business Terms and Notes
Ibotta Performance Network (IPN): A platform that allows clients to deliver digital promotions to consumers via a network of publishers, consisting of our owned properties and third-party publishers.
Redeemer: A consumer who has redeemed at least one digital offer within the time period specified. If a consumer were to redeem on more than one publisher during that period, they would be counted as multiple redeemers. Year-to-date redeemers are calculated as the average of current year quarter-to-date redeemers.
Redemption: A verified purchase of one or more items qualifying for an offer by a client on the IPN.
Redemption Revenue: The Company’s customers promote their products and services to consumers through rewards offered on the IPN. The Company earns a fee per redemption which is recognized in the period in which the redemption occurred. The Company may also charge fees to set up a redemption campaign which are deferred and recognized over the average duration of historical redemption campaigns.
About Ibotta ("I bought a...")
Ibotta (NYSE: IBTA) is the leading provider of digital promotions for CPG brands, reaching over 200 million consumers through a network of publishers called the Ibotta Performance Network (IPN). The IPN allows marketers to influence what people buy, and where and how often they shop – all while paying only when their campaigns directly result in a sale. American shoppers have earned over $3.1 billion through the IPN since 2012. Ibotta is headquartered in Denver, and has been listed as a top place to work by The Denver Post and Inc. Magazine.
