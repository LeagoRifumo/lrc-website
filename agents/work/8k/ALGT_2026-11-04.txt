* Year-to-date results include the financial performance of Sun Country only from and after the date the acquisition closed on May 13, 2026
LAS VEGAS. November 4, 2026 — Allegiant Travel Company (NASDAQ: ALGT) today reported the below financial results for third quarter 2026, as well as comparisons to the prior year.
"Allegiant delivered another quarter of operating profitability, expanding our adjusted operating margin by 340 basis points year over year on a pro forma basis in our seasonally slowest quarter, despite a more than 55 percent surge in fuel costs per gallon," stated Greg Anderson, chief executive officer of Allegiant Travel Company. "These results underscore robust demand from our customer base as well as continued momentum in our commercial initiatives that drove a 28.3 percent year-over-year increase in TRASM, on a pro forma basis. They also demonstrate our flexible business model in action as we reduced pro forma capacity by nearly six percent year over year to protect margins in a high-fuel environment.
"I am particularly pleased with the momentum across our commercial initiatives, highlighted by co-brand remuneration growth of more than 23 percent, pricing and product enhancements that lifted ancillary revenue per passenger, and our new Expedia partnership that is introducing new customers into our ecosystem. Continued execution, combined with our expanding scale, creates multiple pathways to realize the underlying earnings power of the company.
"Looking ahead to the fourth quarter, 72 percent of our ASMs are scheduled on high-demand peak days, and fuel-efficient MAX aircraft are expected to account for 20 percent of our combined capacity. With strong booking momentum, we remain confident in our 2026 full-year adjusted earnings per share guidance of greater than $6.00, despite elevated fuel costs.
"I want to thank our dedicated teams for their exceptional execution and continued integration progress. Together, we are building the nation’s leading leisure carrier, and we look forward to sharing our integration milestones and long-term financial framework at our Investor Day on December 7."

Third Quarter 2026 Results and Highlights
•Consolidated total operating revenue of $961.6M
•Record third-quarter TRASM of 14.18 cents, up 28.3 percent year over year on 5.7 percent less capacity, both on a pro forma basis
•Total fixed fee revenue of $83.6M, up 7.9 percent year over year on a pro forma basis
•Total ancillary revenue per passenger of $76.64, up 5.8 percent from the prior year on a pro forma basis
•Adjusted operating income(1)(2) of $29.8M, yielding an adjusted operating margin of 3.1 percent, a 3.4 percentage point improvement year over year on a pro forma basis despite a 55.5 percent increase in pro forma fuel cost per gallon
•Adjusted loss before income taxes(1)(2)(3) of $1.7M, yielding an adjusted pre-tax margin of (0.2) percent
•Adjusted EBITDA(1)(2) of $110.9M, yielding an adjusted EBITDA margin of 11.5 percent, a 1.6 percentage point improvement over the prior year on a pro forma basis
•Adjusted operating CASM, excluding fuel, special charges, and cargo expenses(2) of 9.23 ¢, up 9.1 percent year over year on a pro forma basis
•$42.1M in total Allegiant Air co-brand credit card remuneration received, up 23.6 percent year over year (excluding Sun Country)
•Named 2026's Best Low-Cost Airline in North America by Skytrax, the international air transport rating organization, marking the third consecutive year Allegiant has won the award
•Earned the top spot in USA TODAY's 10Best 2026 Readers' Choice Awards, being recognized as Number 1 for Best Airline Credit Card and Best Frequent Flyer Program
•In October, exercised six Boeing 737-8 aircraft purchase options for delivery in 2028, bringing total firm aircraft orders under the Boeing purchase agreement to 56, of which 22 have been delivered to date, with 74 options remaining
On a "pro forma basis" means that we are comparing 2026 actual results to pro forma 2025 results calculated as if the acquisition of Sun Country and the sale of Sunseeker Resort were completed on January 1, 2025. See pro forma numbers in our Form 8-K filed with the SEC on November 2, 2026.

Allegiant Travel Company
Las Vegas-based Allegiant (NASDAQ: ALGT) is an integrated travel company with an airline at its heart, focused on connecting customers with the people, places and experiences that matter most. Through Allegiant Air and Sun Country Airlines, the Company serves approximately 22 million annual customers across scheduled passenger, charter and cargo operations. Together, the airlines operate more than 650 routes serving nearly 175 cities throughout the United States and select international destinations. Allegiant is committed to providing affordable travel options, operational excellence and long-term value for customers, employees, communities and shareholders. Consolidated and airline-only earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization ("Consolidated EBITDA" and "Airline EBITDA"), adjusted Consolidated EBITDA, adjusted Airline EBITDA, and estimated adjusted earnings per share, as presented in this press release, are supplemental measures of our performance that are not required by, or presented in accordance with, accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). These are not measurements of our financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered in isolation or as an alternative to net income or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP or as an alternative to cash flows from operating activities as a measure of our liquidity.
We define “EBITDA” as earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. The adjusted EBITDA measures also exclude special charges and gains and losses on the extinguishment of debt. We caution investors that amounts presented in accordance with this definition may not be comparable to similar measures disclosed by other issuers, because not all issuers and analysts calculate EBITDA in the same manner.
We use EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA to evaluate our operating performance and liquidity, and these are among the primary measures used by management for planning and forecasting of future periods. We believe these presentations of EBITDA are relevant and useful for investors because they allow investors to view results in a manner similar to the method used by management and make it easier to compare our results with other companies that have different financing and capital structures. EBITDA has important limitations as an analytical tool. These limitations include the following:
•EBITDA does not reflect our capital expenditures, future requirements for capital expenditures or contractual commitments to purchase capital equipment;
•EBITDA does not reflect interest expense or the cash requirements necessary to service principal or interest payments on our debt;
•although depreciation and amortization are non-cash charges, the assets that we currently depreciate and amortize will likely have to be replaced in the future, and EBITDA does not reflect the cash required to fund such replacements; and
•other companies in our industry may calculate EBITDA differently than we do, limiting its usefulness as a comparative measure.
