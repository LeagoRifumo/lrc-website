Avista Corp. Reports Q3 2026 Financial Results, Confirms 2026 Utility Earnings Guidance
SPOKANE, Wash. – Nov. 4, 2026, 4:05 a.m. PST: Avista Corp. (NYSE: AVA) today reported the following results for the third quarter of 2026 and the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026 as compared to the respective periods in 2025 (dollars in millions, except per-share amounts):1

Avista Corp. is confirming its 2026 non-GAAP utility earnings guidance2 with a range of $2.52 to $2.72 per diluted share.
CEO Perspective
“Utility earnings for the third quarter remain on track for the year. Through our ongoing focus on strategic execution and cost management, we continue to expect to be at the midpoint of our non-GAAP utility earnings guidance for 2026,” said Heather Rosentrater, President and CEO of Avista.

Analysis of Non-Regulated Other Business Income
Income at our non-regulated other businesses was $29 million in the first three quarters of 2026, compared to losses of $13 million in the first three quarters of 2025. The fluctuation in results is primarily related to net investment gains in the second and third quarters of 2026 from our equity method investments, compared to net investment losses in 2025.
Liquidity and Capital Resources
Liquidity
As of Sept. 30, 2026, we had $168 million of available liquidity under the Avista Corp. committed line of credit and $57 million of available liquidity under our letter of credit facility.
Subject to market conditions, we expect to issue up to $15 million of additional common stock in 2026. In the first three quarters of 2026, we issued $58 million of common stock. In 2027, we expect to issue up to $110 million of common stock.
We issued $230 million of long-term debt during the first three quarters of 2026. We don't expect further issuances of long-term debt in 2026. Due to increased regulatory deferrals and delayed recovery, we plan to obtain a short-term loan of up to $100 million by the end of the fourth quarter of 2026. In 2027, we expect to issue approximately $220 million of long-term debt.

2026 Utility Earnings Guidance and Outlook
Avista Corp. is confirming its 2026 non-GAAP utility earnings guidance with a range of $2.52 to $2.72 per diluted share.
This non-GAAP utility earnings guidance is based on the following assumptions:
•
Normal weather for the remainder of the year
•
A negative impact from the ERM of ($0.09) cents per diluted share within the 90% customer, 10% company sharing band
•
An effective tax rate of 12 percent
•
Capital expenditures of $615 million for the year
Over the long term, we expect non-GAAP utility earnings to grow 4 to 6 percent from the midpoint of our 2025 earnings guidance.
Our guidance does not include the effect of unusual or non-recurring items until the effects are probable. Various factors could cause actual results to differ materially from our expectations. Please refer to our 10-K for 2025, our 10-Q for the third quarter of 2026, and the cautionary statements below for a full discussion of these factors.

Avista Corp. is an energy company involved in the production, transmission and distribution of energy as well as other energy-related businesses. Avista Utilities is our operating division that provides electric service to approximately 430,000 customers and natural gas to 385,000 customers. Our service territory covers 34,000 square miles in eastern Washington, northern Idaho and parts of southern and eastern Oregon, with a population of 1.5 million. AERC is an Avista subsidiary that, through its subsidiary AEL&P, provides retail electric service to 18,000 customers in the city and borough of Juneau, Alaska.
