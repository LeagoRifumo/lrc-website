JOYY Reports Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results
SINGAPORE, November 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- JOYY Inc. (NASDAQ: JOYY) (“JOYY” or the “Company”), a global technology company, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights1
· | Net revenues were US$624.6 million, an increase of 15.6% from US$540.2 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and an increase of 5.7% from US$590.8 million in the second quarter of 2026.

o Social Entertainment net revenues increased by 8.8% to US$441.0 million from US$405.5 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and by 4.3% from US$422.7 million in the second quarter of 2026.
o BIGO Ads net revenues increased by 39.6% to US$145.7 million from US$104.4 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and by 9.0% from US$133.7 million in the second quarter of 2026.
o Shopline net revenues increased by 24.7% to US$37.8 million from US$30.3 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and by 10.0% from US$34.4 million in the second quarter of 2026.
· | Operating income was US$13.5 million, compared with US$19.6 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$13.8 million in the second quarter of 2026.
· | Non-GAAP EBITDA2 was US$55.7 million, an increase of 10.1% from US$50.6 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and compared with US$56.9 million in the second quarter of 2026.
· | Net income from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest of JOYY3 was US$59.6 million, compared with US$62.0 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$51.8 million in the second quarter of 2026.
· | Non-GAAP net income from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest and common shareholders of JOYY4 was US$67.1 million, compared with US$72.4 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$63.5 million in the second quarter of 2026.
· | Net Cash5 as of September 30, 2026 was US$2,940.5 million.
· | Net Cash from operating activities was US$102.2 million, compared with US$73.4 million in the corresponding period of 2025.

Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights
Global community:
· | Global average mobile MAUs6 reached 278.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, up by 4.7% from 266.2 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and up by 0.6% from 277.1 million in the second quarter of 2026. The Company continued to optimize its marketing strategies to focus on return on investment (ROI) and high-value users.

Social Entertainment:
· | In the third quarter, Social Entertainment revenues increased by 8.8% year over year to US$441.0 million, with live streaming revenues reaching US$419.8 million, an 8.1% increase from the corresponding period of 2025. By region, live streaming revenues showed year-over-year growth across all four geographical areas.
· | In the third quarter, core live streaming paying users7 increased by 8.1% year over year to 1.64 million, while ARPPU8 was US$218.7.
· | Bigo Live continued to make enhancements across content operations, streamer ecosystem, and AI-driven capabilities. In content operations, the Company refined its cross-regional content algorithms, expanded live translation coverage and noise-resilient speech recognition, enabling users to discover diverse content and interact with streamers across regions. Improved onboarding and guidance for newly signed streamers contributed to a 4.7-percentage-point sequential increase in the share of newly signed streamers completing their first livestream. The Company also continued to expand AI-generated interactive content and virtual gifts. In September 2026, AI-generated interactive virtual gifts accounted for 35.4% of total virtual gift consumption on Bigo Live.

B2B Initiatives: Advertising Technology and Smart Commerce
· | Beginning in 2022, the Company ramped up efforts to diversify its revenue streams, cultivating its new initiatives in advertising technology and smart commerce. The Company has made steady progress advancing towards its strategic positioning as a global tech company powered by multiple growth engines. In the third quarter, total non-live streaming revenues reached US$204.7 million, up by 34.9% year over year, representing 32.8% of total net revenues of the Company, compared with 28.1% in the corresponding period of 2025.

BIGO Ads:
· | BIGO Ads is a global AI-powered programmatic advertising platform. Launched to provide one-stop marketing and monetization solutions, it leverages deep learning, real-time bidding, and smart bidding models to enable brands to scale user acquisition and app developers to effectively unlock monetization potential through connecting premium global demand.
· | In the third quarter, BIGO Ads’ total revenues grew by 39.6% year over year to US$145.7 million. In particular, BIGO Audience Network, which includes third-party advertising revenues generated on network partners’ traffic properties, maintained robust growth, with revenues increasing by 52.3% year over year.
· | BIGO Ads has access to a vast traffic pool, comprising the Company’s own global average mobile MAU base and an extensive network of third-party traffic through seamless integration of developer traffic across major channels. During the third quarter, Software Development Kit (SDK) traffic scale continued to expand, increasing by 55.6% year over year.
· | Broader traffic coverage, multi-vertical advertiser expansion, and ongoing algorithm optimization continued to fuel growth. Web-based demand grew approximately 328% year over year and 30% quarter over quarter, alongside solid performance from in-app demand.
· | BIGO Ads continued to invest in its algorithm and engineering infrastructure during the quarter. As its cross-channel attribution systems improved and advertiser conversion data accumulated, BIGO Ads broadened its data inputs for model training, improving predictions of conversion probability and user lifetime value. The platform also upgraded its model architecture to support different conversion objectives and traffic types, while refining its bidding algorithm to better assess the value of each ad impression and allocate advertiser budgets more effectively.

Shopline:
· | Shopline serves as a global AI-powered operating system for modern retail. Beyond storefront creation, Shopline offers a deeply integrated suite of merchant services across payments, logistics, marketing, and data analytics. It is an open, extensible omnichannel platform that enables merchants to manage the full commerce value chain from store setup and transactions to fulfillment, customer acquisition, and lifecycle engagement. Shopline has helped merchants in diverse industries across multiple markets to launch and scale their businesses.
· | Shopline currently generates revenues from recurring software subscription fees and a suite of transaction-based value-added services, including localized payment processing (Shopline Payments) and marketing solutions.
· | In the third quarter, Shopline generated revenues of US$37.8 million, up 24.7% year over year and 10.0% quarter over quarter. Notably, cross-border merchants revenue increased by 77.8% year over year. The Company continued to enhance Shopline Payments with more local payment options, stronger multi-currency payment support, and improved transaction management, helping merchants manage cross-border payments more easily and efficiently.
· | As AI platforms and agents emerge as new channels for product discovery and purchasing, Shopline continued to strengthen its AI Commerce capabilities. On the ecosystem front, Shopline expanded its integrations with leading AI platforms and AI agents, and enhanced its AI tools and interfaces for developers. On the merchant operations front, the Company rolled out its AI-powered commerce assistant, Shopline Omi, spanning product management, customer segmentation, promotional events, multilingual support, and detailed store performance analysis.

Ms. Ting Li, Chairperson and Chief Executive Officer of JOYY, commented, “This quarter, we made further progress toward becoming a global technology company powered by multiple growth engines. Total net revenues reached US$624.6 million, up 15.6% year over year and 5.7% quarter over quarter. Our Social Entertainment business remained on a steady course, while BIGO Ads and Shopline sustained robust performances, driving solid topline expansion and strong operating cash flow generation. We remain steadfast in our commitment to shareholder returns, having returned a total of US$536.0 million year-to-date through November 13, 2026, comprising US$216.2 million in dividends and US$319.8 million in share repurchases.
Social Entertainment revenues grew 8.8% year over year to US$441.0 million, supported by continued enhancements to Bigo Live’s content operations, streamer ecosystem, and AI-driven capabilities. Live streaming revenues and core live streaming paying users both increased 8.1% year over year. BIGO Ads revenues rose 39.6% year over year to US$145.7 million, led by 52.3% year-over-year growth in BIGO Audience Network as we expanded our traffic network, diversified advertiser demand, and improved our algorithms and ad delivery. Shopline revenues reached US$37.8 million, up 24.7% year over year, supported by continued expansion among cross-border merchants and strong momentum in Shopline Payments. We also advanced our AI Commerce initiatives to help merchants reach customers through emerging AI-powered shopping channels and strengthen Shopline’s role as an omnichannel e-commerce infrastructure platform.
Looking ahead to 2027, we believe JOYY will enter a pivotal phase in which our established strategy translates into stronger operating results. With clearer strategic trajectories across our three businesses, we are well positioned to deliver diversified and sustainable growth while creating long-term shareholder value.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
NET REVENUES
Net revenues were US$624.6 million, representing an increase of 15.6% from US$540.2 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and an increase of 5.7% from US$590.8 million in the second quarter of 2026.
Social Entertainment net revenues increased by 8.8% to US$441.0 million from US$405.5 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and by 4.3% from US$422.7 million in the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter increases were primarily driven by higher live streaming revenues, as expanded content categories and enhanced localized operations contributed to stronger user engagement and paying conversion across key markets.
BIGO Ads net revenues increased by 39.6% to US$145.7 million from US$104.4 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and by 9.0% from US$133.7 million in the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter increases were primarily driven by expansion of traffic, elevated advertiser demand across regions and verticals, and enhanced algorithm performance that resulted in improved advertisement delivery efficiency and higher advertiser spending.
Shopline net revenues increased by 24.7% to US$37.8 million from US$30.3 million in the corresponding period of 2025, and by 10.0% from US$34.4 million in the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter increases were primarily driven by continued merchant adoption and deeper penetration of value-added services.
COST OF REVENUES AND GROSS PROFIT
Cost of revenues was US$407.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$347.1 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$389.2 million in the second quarter of 2026.
Social Entertainment’s cost of revenues increased by 7.4% year over year to US$274.3 million, and by 3.7% from US$264.6 million in the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter increases were primarily attributable to higher revenue-sharing fees and content costs of US$28.4 million and US$7.9 million, respectively.
BIGO Ads’ cost of revenues increased by 47.9% year over year to US$112.2 million, and by 5.6% from US$106.3 million in the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter increases were primarily attributable to higher traffic acquisition costs paid to third-party partners in relation to the expansion of BIGO Audience Network.
Shopline’s cost of revenues increased by 30.8% year over year to US$20.9 million, and by 13.9% from US$18.3 million in the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter increases were primarily attributable to higher payment processing costs, reflecting an increased contribution from payment services to Shopline’s revenue mix.
Gross profit was US$217.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$193.1 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$201.6 million in the second quarter of 2026. Gross margin was 34.8% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 35.8% in the corresponding period of 2025 and 34.1% in the second quarter of 2026.
OPERATING EXPENSES AND INCOME
Operating expenses were US$204.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$174.2 million in the same period of 2025 and US$188.2 million in the second quarter of 2026. Among the operating expenses, sales and marketing expenses were US$86.7 million, compared with US$72.1 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$79.6 million in the second quarter of 2026. Research and development expenses were US$59.0 million, compared with US$63.1 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$53.0 million in the second quarter of 2026. General and administrative expenses were US$58.4 million, compared with US$39.1 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$55.6 million in the second quarter of 2026.
Operating income was US$13.5 million, compared with US$19.6 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$13.8 million in the second quarter of 2026.
Non-GAAP operating income9 was US$46.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$40.7 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$49.1 million in the second quarter of 2026. Non-GAAP operating income margin10 was 7.5% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 7.5% in the corresponding period of 2025 and 8.3% in the second quarter of 2026.
Non-GAAP EBITDA was US$55.7 million, compared with US$50.6 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$56.9 million in the second quarter of 2026. Non-GAAP EBITDA margin11 was 8.9%, compared with 9.4% in the corresponding period of 2025 and 9.6% in the second quarter of 2026.
NET INCOME
Net income from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest of JOYY was US$59.6 million, compared with US$62.0 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$51.8 million in the second quarter of 2026. Net income margin was 9.5% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 11.5% in the corresponding period of 2025 and 8.8% in the second quarter of 2026.
Non-GAAP net income from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest and common shareholders of JOYY was US$67.1 million, compared with US$72.4 million in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$63.5 million in the second quarter of 2026. Non-GAAP net income margin12 was 10.7% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 13.4% in the corresponding period of 2025 and 10.7% in the second quarter of 2026.
NET INCOME PER ADS
Diluted net income from continuing operations per ADS13 was US$1.20 in the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$1.15 in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$1.01 in the second quarter of 2026.
Non-GAAP diluted net income from continuing operations per ADS14 was US$1.36 in the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$1.36 in the corresponding period of 2025 and US$1.24 in the second quarter of 2026.
BALANCE SHEET AND CASH FLOWS
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had net cash of US$2,940.5 million, compared with US$3,258.0 million as of December 31, 2025. For the third quarter of 2026, net cash from operating activities was US$102.2 million.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The unaudited condensed consolidated financial information is prepared in conformity with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“U.S. GAAP”). JOYY uses non-GAAP operating (loss) income, non-GAAP operating income (loss) margin, non-GAAP EBITDA, non-GAAP EBITDA margin, non-GAAP net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest and common shareholders of JOYY, non-GAAP net income (loss) margin attributable to controlling interest and common shareholders of JOYY, and basic and diluted non-GAAP net income (loss) from continuing operations per ADS, all of which are non-GAAP financial measures adjusted from the most comparable U.S. GAAP results. Non-GAAP operating income (loss) is operating income (loss) excluding share-based compensation expenses, impairment of goodwill and investments, amortization of intangible assets from business acquisitions, and gain (loss) on deconsolidation and disposal of subsidiaries and business. Non-GAAP operating income (loss) margin is non-GAAP operating income as a percentage of net revenues. Non-GAAP net income (loss) from continuing operations is net income (loss) from continuing operations excluding share-based compensation expenses, impairment of goodwill and investments, amortization of intangible assets from business acquisitions, gain (loss) on deconsolidation and disposal of subsidiaries and business, gain (loss) on disposal and deemed disposal of investments, gain (loss) on fair value change of investments, reconciling items on the share of equity method investments (referring to share of income (loss) from equity method investments resulting from non-recurring or non-cash items of the equity method investments), interest expenses related to the convertible bonds’ amortization to face value, and income tax effects of the above non-GAAP reconciling items. Non-GAAP EBITDA is non-GAAP operating income (loss) added back depreciation and amortization (other than amortization of intangible assets resulting from assets and business acquisitions), and non-GAAP EBITDA margin is non-GAAP EBITDA as a percentage of net revenues. Non-GAAP net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest and common shareholders of JOYY is net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest of JOYY excluding share-based compensation expenses, impairment of goodwill and investments, amortization of intangible assets from business acquisitions, gain (loss) on deconsolidation and disposal of subsidiaries and business, gain (loss) on disposal and deemed disposal of investments, gain (loss) on fair value change of investments, reconciling items on the share of equity method investments, interest expenses related to the convertible bonds’ amortization to face value, income tax effects of the above non-GAAP reconciling items and adjustments for non-GAAP reconciling items for the net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to non-controlling interest shareholders. Non-GAAP net income (loss) margin is non-GAAP net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest and common shareholders of JOYY as a percentage of net revenues. Non-GAAP net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest and common shareholders of JOYY is net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to common shareholders of JOYY excluding share-based compensation expenses, impairment of goodwill and investments, amortization of intangible assets from business acquisitions, gain (loss) on deconsolidation and disposal of subsidiaries and business, gain (loss) on disposal and deemed disposal of investments, gain (loss) on fair value change of investments, reconciling items on the share of equity method investments, interest expenses related to the convertible bonds’ amortization to face value, accretion, cumulative dividend and deemed dividend to subsidiaries’ preferred shareholders, gain on repurchase of redeemable convertible preferred shares of a subsidiary and income tax effects of above non-GAAP reconciling items and adjustments for non-GAAP reconciling items for the net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to non-controlling interest shareholders. Basic and diluted non-GAAP net income (loss) from continuing operations per ADS is non-GAAP net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to common shareholders of JOYY divided by weighted average number of ADS used in the calculation of basic and diluted net income (loss) per ADS. The Company believes that separate analysis and exclusion of the non-cash impact of above reconciling items adds clarity to the constituent parts of its performance. The Company reviews these non-GAAP financial measures together with GAAP financial measures to obtain a better understanding of its operating performance. It uses the non-GAAP financial measures for planning, forecasting and measuring results against the forecast. The Company believes that non-GAAP financial measures is useful supplemental information for investors and analysts to assess its operating performance without the non-cash effect of (i) share-based compensation expenses, amortization of intangible assets from business acquisitions, and interest expenses related to the convertible bonds’ amortization to face value, which have been and will continue to be significant recurring expenses in its business, (ii) impairment of goodwill and investments, gain (loss) on deconsolidation and disposal of subsidiaries and business, gain (loss) on disposal and deemed disposal of investments, gain (loss) on fair value change of investments, reconciling items on the share of equity method investments, accretion, cumulative dividend and deemed dividend to subsidiaries’ preferred shareholders and gain on repurchase of redeemable convertible preferred shares of a subsidiary which may not be recurring in its business, and (iii) income tax expenses and non-GAAP adjustments for net income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to non-controlling interest shareholders, which are affected by the above non-GAAP reconciling items. However, the use of non-GAAP financial measures has material limitations as an analytical tool. One of the limitations of using non-GAAP financial measures is that they do not include all items that impact the Company’s net income (loss) for the period. In addition, because non-GAAP financial measures are not measured in the same manner by all companies, they may not be comparable to other similar titled measures used by other companies. In light of the foregoing limitations, you should not consider non-GAAP financial measures in isolation from or as an alternative to the financial measures prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP.
