Jefferson Capital Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
​
Record Collections Grow 35% to $319.2 Million and Deployments grow 111% to $318.0 Million
Record Estimated Remaining Collections (“ERC”) up 23% to $3.6 Billion
Pre-tax Income of $58.9 Million with Net Income of $46.9 Million and EPS of $0.76
Adjusted Pre-tax Income of $63.3 Million with Adjusted Net Income of $51.3 Million and Adjusted EPS of $0.83
Board of Directors Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend of $0.24 per Share
​
​
MINNEAPOLIS, November 12, 2026 /GLOBE NEWSWIRE/ -- Jefferson Capital, Inc. (“Jefferson Capital, the “Company”), a leading analytically driven purchaser and manager of charged-off, insolvency and active consumer accounts, today announced its third quarter 2026 financial results.
​
Third quarter performance was excellent and we continue to build significant momentum in all key aspects of the business,” said David Burton, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. “We are confident in our ability to generate attractive shareholder returns in the near and long term.”
​
“We delivered the largest third quarter deployments in the Company’s history, with triple digit growth year-over-year in the United States, the United Kingdom and Latin America – a testament to our leadership position in hard-to-value and hard-to-service asset classes, the growing geographic breadth of our platform, and the strength of our client relationships. We are well positioned to take advantage of the opportunities ahead with low leverage and ample capital resources.”
​

Revenues
● | Total revenues increased $34.6 million for the quarter, or 22.9%, to $185.5 million compared to $150.8 million for the third quarter 2025. The growth was primarily the result of strong deployments in prior periods

Operating Expenses
● | Total operating expenses increased $18.2 million, or 22.7% to $98.4 million compared to $80.2 million for the third quarter 2025. The increase was primarily due to a $20.1 million increase in servicing expenses, including $6.3 million in higher court costs, $8.5 million in agency and legal commission expense including $3.1 million related to the Bluestem portfolio purchase, partially offset by a $2.4 million decrease in salaries and benefits (including $0.7 million on lower stock-based compensation) and $0.6 million lower depreciation and amortization

​
● | For the third quarter 2026, the Company recognized portfolio revenue of $13.4 million and net operating income of $10.3 million related to the Bluestem portfolio purchase

Leverage Ratio, Liquidity and Capital Resources
● | Leverage ratio* was 1.73x at September 30, 2026 compared to 1.59x at September 30, 2025 primarily reflecting increased borrowings to fund portfolio purchases and continued business growth

Dividend

About Jefferson Capital, Inc.
Founded in 2002, Jefferson Capital is an analytically driven purchaser and manager of charged-off, insolvency and active consumer accounts with operations in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Latin America. It purchases and services both secured and unsecured assets, and its growing client base includes Fortune 500 creditors, banks, fintech origination platforms, telecommunications providers, credit card issuers and auto finance companies. Jefferson Capital is headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota with additional offices and operations located in Sartell, Minnesota, Denver, Colorado and San Antonio, Texas (United States); Basingstoke, England; London, England and Paisley, Scotland (United Kingdom); London, Ontario and Toronto, Ontario (Canada); as well as Bogota (Colombia).
