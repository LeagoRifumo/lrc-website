EARNINGS RELEASE
COUSINS PROPERTIES REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
Raises 2026 Earnings Guidance
ATLANTA, GA (October 29, 2026) - Cousins Properties (NYSE:CUZ) today reported its results of operations for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
"Our third quarter was exceptionally strong. We increased our FFO guidance, completed strategic transactions that will enhance the quality of our portfolio, and had robust leasing and operating metrics," said Colin Connolly, President and Chief Executive Officer of Cousins Properties. "Given strong office fundamentals, we remain on track to achieve our 90% occupancy target by year end."
Financial Results
For third quarter 2026:
•
Net income available to common stockholders was $17.0 million, or $0.10 per share, compared to net income of $8.6 million, or $0.05 per share, for third quarter 2025.
•
Funds From Operations ("FFO") was $123.8 million, or $0.75 per share, compared to $116.5 million, or $0.69 per share, for third quarter 2025.
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026:
•
Net income available to common stockholders was $18.3 million, or $0.11 per share, compared to net income of $44.0 million, or $0.26 per share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The decline in net income is primarily attributable to an impairment charge.
•
Funds From Operations ("FFO") was $371.3 million, or $2.23 per share, compared to $358.9 million, or $2.13 per share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
Operations and Leasing Activity
For third quarter 2026:
•
Same property net operating income ("NOI") on a cash-basis increased 7.4%.
•
Second generation net rent per square foot on a cash-basis increased 19.8%.
•
Executed 546,000 square feet of office leases, including 220,000 square feet of new and expansion leases, representing 40.3% of total leasing activity.
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026:
•
Same property net operating income ("NOI") on a cash-basis increased 6.2%.
•
Second generation net rent per square foot on a cash-basis increased 14.2%.
•
Executed 2.4 million square feet of office leases, including 1.1 million square feet of new and expansion leases, representing 45.7% of total leasing activity.

Investing & Financing Activity
For third quarter 2026:
•
Acquired a preferred equity interest in 5th & Walsh, a 199,000 square foot office building under development in Austin. Our funding commitment of $31.5 million is expected to be invested in the second half of 2027 and will earn a 10% preferred return.
•
Sold One Eleven Congress, a 519,000 square foot office property in Austin, for a gross sales price of $208.0 million.
•
Sold 917,000 shares under our ATM program, on a forward basis, at an average price of $31.37 per share. This brings our total unsettled common shares sold on a forward basis to 3.9 million at an average price of $30.66 per share.
•
Formed a joint venture to acquire 2.3 acres at 2401 McKinney in Uptown Dallas, consisting of fee and leasehold interests. We hold a 97.5% equity interest in the venture. At formation, we contributed $50.0 million, including $42.2 million for land and $7.8 million for predevelopment costs.
•
Repaid a $99.2 million mortgage note, secured by Colorado Tower in Austin, at par on the September 1, 2026 maturity date.
•
Subsequent to quarter end, repaid a $115.9 million mortgage note, secured by 201 North Tryon in Charlotte, at par on the October 1, 2026 maturity date.
Earnings Guidance
For year ending December 31, 2026:
•
Net income between $0.14 and $0.18 per share, up from $0.08 and $0.14 per share.
•
FFO between $2.95 and $2.99 per share, up from $2.92 and $2.98 per share.
•
The increase in FFO per share is primarily driven by leasing activity and fee income that exceeded our prior forecast.
•
Guidance excludes the settlement of 3.9 million common shares previously sold on a forward basis.
•
Guidance assumes the sale of our 303 Tremont land parcel during the fourth quarter.
•
Guidance does not include any speculative property acquisitions, dispositions, or development starts.
•
Guidance reflects management’s current plans and assumptions as of the date of this earnings release and is subject to change as well as the risks and uncertainties more fully described in our SEC filings. Actual results could differ materially from this guidance.

COMPANY INFORMATION
THE COMPANY
Cousins Properties Incorporated ("Cousins" or the "Company") is a fully integrated, self-administered, and self-managed real estate investment trust (REIT). The Company, based in Atlanta, GA and acting through its operating partnership, Cousins Properties LP, primarily invests in Class A office buildings located in high-growth Sun Belt markets. Founded in 1958, Cousins creates shareholder value through its extensive expertise in the development, acquisition, leasing, and management of high-quality real estate assets. The Company has a comprehensive strategy in place based on a simple platform, trophy assets, and opportunistic investments. For more information, please visit www.cousins.com.
