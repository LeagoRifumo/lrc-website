LATTICE SEMICONDUCTOR REPORTS RECORD 3Q26 REVENUE, UP 118% YOY
Record FPGA Revenue, Up Approximately 90% YOY, Led by Compute and Communications Demand
Expects Approximately 130% YOY Revenue Growth in 4Q26, with Industrial and Embedded Revenue Growth Accelerating to 70% YOY
HILLSBORO, Ore. – Nov. 2, 2026 – Lattice Semiconductor Corporation (Nasdaq: LSCC), the low power programmable and platform firmware leader, announced financial results today for the fiscal third quarter ended October 3, 2026.
Lattice completed the acquisition of AMI on July 27, 2026 (the “acquisition date”). Lattice’s results for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 include AMI’s results from the acquisition date, while the prior periods presented do not.
Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $291.0 million, with GAAP gross margin of 67.4%, and GAAP net income per diluted share of $0.08. On a non-GAAP basis, gross margin was 70.9%, with net income per diluted share of $0.72. GAAP net income and GAAP net income margin for the third quarter of 2026 were $11.2 million and 3.9%, respectively, with adjusted EBITDA of $131.0 million, which is a 45.0% adjusted EBITDA margin for the third quarter of 2026. GAAP net cash flow from operating activities and operating cash flow margin for the third quarter of 2026 were $95.0 million and 32.7%, respectively, which corresponds to non-GAAP free cash flow and free cash flow margin of $77.6 million and 26.7%, respectively.
Ford Tamer, Chief Executive Officer, said, "We delivered record third quarter revenue of $291 million and non-GAAP EPS of $0.72, up 118% and approximately 160% year-over-year, respectively. FPGA revenue grew approximately 90%, fueled by accelerating data center AI demand and continued momentum across Industrial & Embedded applications. Strong bookings, backlog and design wins support our fourth-quarter revenue outlook of approximately $335 million and reinforce our confidence in growth into 2027 and beyond. The addition of AMI meaningfully expands our opportunity in security, control and management solutions. Early commercial momentum across security retrofit, rack boot, plug and play, and power and cooling demonstrates the value of our combined portfolio, positioning Lattice to capture greater content per platform, deepen strategic customer relationships and drive sustainable long-term growth."
Lorenzo Flores, Chief Financial Officer, said, "Lattice’s operating leverage was evident in the third quarter as we translated accelerating demand into faster year-over-year earnings growth. We continue to execute against our growth strategy by investing to strengthen our leadership in small and mid-range FPGAs, expanding our combined Lattice and AMI solutions portfolio, and advancing our companion chip opportunity. Our focus remains on driving profitable growth, expanding free cash flow, reducing debt, and delivering long-term shareholder value."

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:
• | Achieved Record New Product Revenue Growth: Revenue from new products grew more than 70% year over year and is tracking to reach 25% of FPGA revenue in 2026, led by AI-related server demand and Industrial and Embedded accelerated recovery.
• | Hosted Two Technical Summits in Taipei and Shenzhen with Lattice and AMI: Over 700 customers and partners attended the two events, with presentations from Advantech, Inovance, Intel, Mitsubishi Electric, NVIDIA, NXP Semiconductors, Panasonic, Qualcomm and Sony. Demonstrated over 20 solutions, showing the power of Lattice companion chip and AMI companion firmware ecosystem.
• | Advanced Secure Control and Management at OCP Global Summit with Other Industry Leaders: Announced the Secure Control and Management Plane (SCMP), with AMD, Arm, ASPEED, Intel, Jabil, Microsoft, and NXP Semiconductor. Brought Lattice and AMI together with keynote, technical presentation and demonstrations.
• | Expanded AI Infrastructure Collaboration with Arm: Announced a collaboration combining Arm AGI CPU server platforms, Lattice FPGAs and AMI firmware to deliver secure control, management and connectivity for next-generation AI data centers.
• | Accelerated FPGA Development with AI: Launched Lattice Prompt, enabling natural-language access across the FPGA design flow and productivity gains of 10X or more on common design tasks.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights (continued):
• | Extended Secure Control FPGA Leadership: Introduced the Lattice Mach-N2 family, combining integrated flash, hardware Root of Trust and post-quantum cryptography with greater logic density and connectivity. Samples have shipped to leading Compute & Communications customers.
• | Expanded Quantum-Resistant Platform Security: Released Lattice Sentry 5.0, the industry’s first PQC-enabled platform firmware resilience and attestation solution, combining Lattice FPGAs with AMI software to protect AI infrastructure and simplify secure deployment.
• | Recognized for Post-Quantum Cryptography Security: Lattice MachXO5-NX TDQ FPGAs won the 2026 CyberSecurity Breakthrough Award for Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) Solution of the Year.
• | Broadened AI Infrastructure Support with AMD: Announced fully validated AMI Rack Manager support for AMD Helios rackscale solutions and Aptio V and MegaRAC OneTree firmware support for 6th Gen AMD EPYC processors, simplifying deployment and enabling secure boot, monitoring and management for next-generation AI data centers.
• | Launched Rack-Scale AI Infrastructure Management: Introduced AMI Rack Manager 1.0 at AMI Tech Summit 2026 in Taipei, providing centralized monitoring, management and automation across diverse rack infrastructure. Expanded visibility into rack health, power and thermal conditions to simplify deployment and optimize resource utilization.
• | Strengthened Data Center Memory Resilience with SK Hynix: Announced production-ready support for the SK Hynix RAS Library in AMI MegaRAC OneTree, integrating proactive memory error management, runtime repair and page-offlining to improve server reliability, simplify maintenance and reduce total cost of ownership.
• | Expanded Data Center Management Capabilities: Released AMI Data Center Manager 6.2, combining fleet-wide BIOS configuration, enhanced firmware provisioning and advanced GPU diagnostics with expanded power and thermal monitoring to simplify large-scale infrastructure operations and improve efficiency across AI and enterprise data centers.
