Zenas BioPharma Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update
- PDUFA target action date of May 27, 2027, following FDA acceptance of Obexelimab BLA submission for the treatment of IgG4-RD in second quarter 2026 -
- Obexelimab MAA submitted to the EMA for the treatment of IgG4-RD -
- New data presented at 6th International Symposium on IgG4-Related Disease supports obexelimab’s potential role as a reversible, steroid-sparing, B-cell inhibitor for the treatment of IgG4-RD -
- Obexelimab INDIGO open-label extension data to be featured in late-breaking poster at ACR Convergence 2026 -
- Obexelimab global Phase 2 SunStone SLE trial topline results on track for fourth quarter 2026 -
- ZB021 (oral IL-17AA/AF inhibitor) dosing ongoing in Phase 1 trial; initial clinical data expected fourth quarter 2026 -
- Orelabrutinib 24-week efficacy and safety data and pharmacokinetic analysis from Phase 2 trial in RRMS presented at MSToronto2026 -
- Jerald Korn appointed as Chief Legal and Strategy Officer -
- Cash, cash equivalents and investments of $647.1 million as of September 30, 2026 -
WALTHAM, Mass., November 5, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Zenas BioPharma, Inc. (“Zenas” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: ZBIO), a clinical-stage global biopharmaceutical company advancing therapies for patients living with autoimmune and inflammatory diseases, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided recent corporate updates.
“This quarter we continued to execute across our portfolio as we prepare for the potential commercialization of obexelimab for the treatment of IgG4-RD. Following the FDA’s acceptance of our BLA in the second quarter of this year, and the recent submission of our MAA to the EMA, we remain focused on commercial readiness ahead of the potential launch. We continue to generate meaningful data supporting the unique inhibitory mechanism of obexelimab, including open-label extension data, data demonstrating the reduction in glucocorticoid-associated toxicity, and data from the INDIGO vaccine sub-study presented at the IgG4ward! 6th International Symposium on IgG4-RD, and we look forward to the presentation of open-label extension data from INDIGO in a late-breaking poster at ACR Convergence 2026 next week,” said Lonnie Moulder, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Zenas. “Across our broader pipeline, we remain on track to report topline Phase 2 SunStone data in SLE and initial Phase 1 data for our oral IL-17 inhibitor, ZB021, this quarter. We look forward to closing out a very productive year as we turn our attention to 2027 and continue to build toward our vision of becoming a fully integrated, global development and commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company.”
Corporate highlights
Obexelimab, inhibitor of B cell function targeting CD19 and FcγRIIb
· | The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) accepted the Company’s Biologics License Application (BLA) for obexelimab for the treatment of Immunoglobulin G4-Related Disease (IgG4-RD): The BLA, which was accepted in August, is under active review by the FDA and has been assigned a Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) target action date of May 27, 2027. Clinical data reported in the second quarter of 2026 confirmed bioequivalence between obexelimab delivered via prefilled syringe and single-dose prefilled pen, which has the potential to offer a more convenient delivery option for patients. Zenas plans to submit the bioequivalence data in a supplemental application to the obexelimab IgG4-RD BLA, if approved.

· | The European Medicines Agency (EMA) validated the recently submitted Marketing Authorization Application (MAA) for the treatment of IgG4-RD: EMA validation confirms that all essential regulatory elements required for scientific assessment are included in the application prior to the start of the assessment. The application is based on the results of the global Phase 3 INDIGO trial, as well as clinical data confirming bioequivalence between obexelimab delivered via prefilled syringe and single-dose prefilled pen.

· | Three abstracts accepted at ACR Convergence 2026, including a late-breaking poster with open-label extension data from the Phase 3 INDIGO trial of obexelimab in IgG4-RD:

· | Durable Disease Control with Obexelimab in IgG4-Related Disease: Phase 3 INDIGO Open-Label Extension

· | Obexelimab Reduced Glucocorticoid-Associated Toxicity in Patients with IgG4-Related Disease: Results from the Phase 3 Randomized, Placebo-Controlled INDIGO Trial

· | Clinically Meaningful Influenza and SARS-CoV-2 Vaccine Responses Following Treatment-Free Window of Obexelimab in Patients with IgG4-RD

· | Presented additional positive Phase 3 INDIGO data at the IgG4ward! 6th International Symposium on IgG4-RD: Phase 3 INDIGO results were presented at IgG4ward! highlighting long-term efficacy and safety data from the open-label extension. Additionally, data was presented on reduced glucocorticoid-associated toxicity in patients with IgG4-RD, as well as influenza and SARS-CoV-2 vaccine seroprotection data following a treatment-free window of obexelimab in patients with IgG4-RD.

· | Phase 2 SunStone trial in Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE) topline results expected in the fourth quarter 2026: Zenas anticipates reporting topline results, including results from a biomarker subset from the Phase 2 SunStone trial in the fourth quarter of 2026. More information on the Phase 2 SunStone trial (NCT06559163) is available at clinicaltrials.gov.

· | Hosted a virtual Obexelimab IgG4-RD Investor Event featuring Zenas’ management team and Dr. Guy Katz, M.D., Division of Rheumatology, Inflammation, and Immunity, Massachusetts General Hospital, Boston, MA, USA, in September. The virtual event provided an overview of IgG4-RD, including current management strategies and the significant unmet need for patients. The event also covered the Company's prelaunch preparedness strategies and the potential commercial opportunity for obexelimab in IgG4-RD, if approved.

· | Hosted a virtual R&D event featuring Zenas’ management team and Dr. Joseph Merola, MD, MMSc, Dermatologist, Rheumatologist, in October. The virtual event provided additional context ahead of the upcoming data for both obexelimab in SLE and the initial Phase 1 data from ZB021, as well as an overview of the broader pipeline. The event also included a fireside chat between Haley Laken, Ph.D., Chief Scientific Officer, and Dr. Joseph Merola MD, MMSc, Dermatologist, Rheumatologist, focused on IL-17 as a target for the treatment of multiple diseases and the relevance of oral therapy.

Orelabrutinib, a highly selective CNS-penetrant Bruton’s Tyrosine Kinase (BTK) inhibitor
· | Orelabrutinib Phase 3 PriMroSe Primary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis (PPMS) trial and Phase 3 Monarch non-active Secondary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis (naSPMS) trial ongoing: PriMroSe, a Phase 3, global, registration-directed, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy and safety of orelabrutinib in patients with PPMS, and Monarch, a Phase 3, global, registration-directed, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy and safety of orelabrutinib in patients with naSPMS are both ongoing. More information on the Phase 3 PriMroSe trial (NCT07067463) and on the Phase 3 Monarch trial (NCT07299019) is available at clinicaltrials.gov.

· | Seven posters on orelabrutinib and obexelimab were presented at MSToronto2026 including data in Relapsing Remitting Multiple Sclerosis (RRMS), clinical trial designs in Progressive Multiple Sclerosis and other multiple sclerosis portfolio data: There were four posters included at the conference that focused on orelabrutinib in multiple sclerosis, including pharmacokinetic data in a Phase 2 study in RRMS, 24-week efficacy and safety data from the Phase 2 study in RRMS, and the designs of the Phase 3 Monarch and PriMroSe randomized controlled trials in naSPMS and PPMS, respectively. Additionally, three posters highlighted additional data from the Phase 2 MoonStone study of obexelimab in RRMS, including its effect on serum biomarkers and the MS Disease Activity Score, data on the reduction in disease activity at 24 weeks, and obexelimab’s pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics in the MoonStone trial. The Company hosted an industry-supported symposium, chaired and moderated by Amit Bar-Or, MD, FRCP, FAAN, FANA, Chief of the Multiple Sclerosis Division, Department of Neurology, Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania, that highlighted the evolving science and clinical potential of BTK inhibition in progressive MS.

ZB021, a novel oral, IL-17AA/AF inhibitor
· | Dosing ongoing in Phase 1 trial of ZB021 in healthy volunteers: Dosing is ongoing in the single ascending dose (SAD) and now the multiple ascending dose (MAD) parts of the Phase 1 trial designed to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetic profile of ZB021 in healthy volunteers. The trial is being conducted in partnership with InnoCare Pharma. Zenas expects to report data from the Phase 1 trial in the fourth quarter of 2026. Upon completion and evaluation of the SAD and MAD study, Zenas plans to initiate a proof-of-concept (POC) trial to evaluate clinical activity and safety with data anticipated in 2027.

About Obexelimab
Obexelimab is a bifunctional monoclonal antibody designed to bind both CD19 and FcγRIIb, which are broadly present across B cell lineage, to inhibit the activity of cells that are implicated in many autoimmune diseases without depleting them. This unique inhibitory mechanism of action and self-administered, subcutaneous injection regimen may broadly and effectively modulate the pathogenic role of the B cell lineage in chronic autoimmune and inflammatory diseases. Obexelimab has been evaluated in nine clinical trials in a total of 667 subjects, including INDIGO. Obexelimab was well tolerated and demonstrated clinical activity across these clinical trials.
About Orelabrutinib
Orelabrutinib is a late-stage, potentially best-in-class, highly selective central nervous system (CNS)-penetrant, oral, small molecule Bruton’s Tyrosine Kinase (BTK) inhibitor. Orelabrutinib’s mechanism of action targets pathogenic B cells in both the periphery and the CNS. Additionally, it directly modulates macrophages and microglial cells in the CNS, with the potential to address compartmentalized inflammation and disease progression in multiple sclerosis (MS). In MS, Zenas is advancing PriMroSe, a Phase 3 trial in Primary Progressive MS (PPMS), and Monarch, a Phase 3 trial in non-active Secondary Progressive MS (naSPMS). Orelabrutinib is approved for B cell malignancies in mainland China and Singapore, marketed by our partner InnoCare.
About ZB021
ZB021 is a novel potentially best-in-class oral small molecule IL-17AA/AF inhibitor being developed by Zenas BioPharma in partnership with InnoCare Pharma. ZB021 is designed to selectively block the signal transduction pathways of both the IL-17AA homodimer and IL-17AF heterodimer, inhibiting downstream pro-inflammatory cytokine and chemokine release. Preclinical studies have demonstrated potent anti-inflammatory activity, a favorable safety profile, and excellent Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, and Excretion (ADME) properties. The IL-17 pathway has demonstrated broad utility across many rheumatic and dermatologic indications. Currently, no oral IL-17 inhibitors have been approved or are in late-stage development globally. ZB021's oral, small molecule profile may offer meaningful advantages over currently approved biologic IL-17 therapies in terms of convenience, compliance, and accessibility. Zenas licensed the exclusive rights from InnoCare Pharma to develop, manufacture, and commercialize ZB021 in all fields of use worldwide, excluding greater China and Southeast Asia.
About Zenas BioPharma
Zenas is a clinical-stage global biopharmaceutical company focused on the development and commercialization of therapies for autoimmune diseases and inflammatory conditions. Zenas combines our experienced leadership team with a disciplined global product candidate acquisition approach to identify, acquire and develop product candidates with the potential to deliver clinically meaningful benefits to patients. Zenas is advancing two late-stage, potential franchise molecules, obexelimab and orelabrutinib. Obexelimab, Zenas’ lead product candidate, is a bifunctional monoclonal antibody designed to bind CD19 and FcγRIIb to inhibit the activity of B cells implicated in many autoimmune diseases without depleting them. Zenas believes that the unique mechanism of action of obexelimab and its self-administered, subcutaneous injection regimen may enable sustained control across multiple chronic autoimmune diseases. Orelabrutinib is a potentially best-in-class, highly selective CNS-penetrant, oral, small molecule BTK inhibitor. Orelabrutinib’s mechanism of action targets pathogenic B cells not only in the periphery but also within the CNS. Additionally, it directly modulates macrophages and microglial cells in the CNS, with the potential to address compartmentalized inflammation and disease progression in MS. Zenas’ earlier stage programs include ZB021, a novel, potentially best-in-class, oral, IL-17AA/AF inhibitor, ZB022, a preclinical, potentially best-in-class, oral, brain-penetrant, TYK2 inhibitor, and ZB014, a preclinical, half-life extended anti-CD19 and FcγRIIb monoclonal antibody.
