Suburban Propane Partners, L.P.
Announces Full Year and Fourth Quarter Results
Whippany, New Jersey, November 12, 2026 -- Suburban Propane Partners, L.P. (NYSE:SPH), today announced earnings for its full year and fiscal fourth quarter ended September 26, 2026.
Fiscal Year 2026 Results
Net income for fiscal 2026 was $109.0 million, or $1.63 per Common Unit, compared to $106.6 million, or $1.64 per Common Unit, in fiscal 2025. Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA,” as defined and reconciled below) increased $6.0 million, or 2.2%, to $284.0 million for fiscal 2026, compared to $278.0 million in the prior year.
In announcing these results, President and Chief Executive Officer Michael A. Stivala said, “Fiscal 2026 was another strong year for Suburban Propane with solid results in our propane operations, the completion of capital projects to increase the scale of our renewable natural gas (“RNG”) business, and continued debt reduction to further strengthen our financial position. Our propane segment benefited from strong heat-related demand across the eastern half of the United States during the 2026 heating season, as well as continued growth in our counter-seasonal customer base. I am proud of how our dedicated employees continued to deliver outstanding service to our customers while effectively managing selling prices and expenses in a volatile commodity and cost environment. We also continued to invest in our propane platform, acquiring two well-run businesses in strategic markets in California for $24.0 million and, subsequent to the end of the fiscal year, a high-quality business in Florida for $6.2 million.”
Mr. Stivala continued, “In our RNG business, the capital investments and operational enhancements that we have implemented at our Stanfield, Arizona facility resulted in more stable and reliable production and increased facility uptime. Combined with the contribution from our new anaerobic digester facility in Upstate New York, which was placed into service in July, average daily RNG injection for fiscal 2026 increased 13% compared to the prior year. We also completed the installation of gas upgrading equipment at our existing anaerobic digestion facility in Columbus, Ohio, and we are awaiting final commissioning of the injection site by the local utility. During fiscal 2026, we recognized $12.4 million of benefits from production tax credits (“PTCs”) related to RNG injections for the period from January 2025 through September 2026 and generated $11.3 million of investment tax credits (“ITCs”) associated with qualifying expenditures for the construction of our facility in Upstate New York. We monetized approximately $3.0 million of the PTCs during fiscal 2026 and expect to monetize the remaining PTCs and the ITCs during fiscal 2027. With these investments in place, we enter fiscal 2027 with a larger, more reliable RNG platform, opportunities to increase production through capacity optimization and higher feedstock intake, and the ability to enhance our earnings profile from the recognition of additional PTCs.”
Concluding his remarks, Mr. Stivala stated, “During fiscal 2026, we successfully refinanced $350.0 million of senior notes that were due in March 2027 with the issuance of $350.0 million of 6.5% senior notes due in December 2035, extending these maturities by nearly nine years. We also reduced total debt by more than $25.0 million, which, combined with the $6.0 million increase in Adjusted EBITDA, improved our leverage metric to 4.13x at the end of fiscal 2026. With the progress we have made in strengthening our financial position, the substantial completion of our RNG growth capital

Retail propane gallons sold in fiscal 2026 of 402.9 million gallons increased approximately 1% compared to the prior year. The impact of colder temperatures during the heating season across much of the eastern half of the United States on heat-related demand, together with contributions from recent acquisitions, was partially offset by considerably warmer temperatures in the West, near record warm temperatures during the spring and the absence of incremental volumes experienced in the prior year in the aftermath of hurricanes in the Southeast. Average temperatures (as measured by heating degree days) across all of the Partnership’s service territories for fiscal 2026 were 8% warmer than normal and 1% cooler than the prior year. Notably, average temperatures in the East were 4% warmer than normal and 6% cooler than the prior year, whereas average temperatures in the West were 23% warmer than normal and 14% warmer than the prior year.
Average propane prices (basis Mont Belvieu, Texas) for fiscal 2026 decreased 7.1% compared to the prior year. Total gross margins of $893.4 million for fiscal 2026 increased $34.7 million, or 4.0%, compared to the prior year. Gross margins for fiscal 2026 included an unrealized gain of $4.5 million attributable to the mark-to-market adjustment for derivative instruments used in risk management activities, compared to an unrealized gain of $2.4 million in fiscal 2025. These non-cash adjustments, reported in cost of products sold, were excluded from Adjusted EBITDA for both periods. Excluding the impact of these unrealized mark-to-market adjustments, gross margin for fiscal 2026 increased $32.7 million, or 3.8%, compared to the prior year, primarily due to an increase in propane unit margins of $0.05 per gallon, or 2.5%, higher propane volumes sold, and the benefit of $12.4 million from PTCs recognized during fiscal 2026 on RNG injections.
Combined operating and general and administrative expenses of $603.1 million for fiscal 2026 increased $22.8 million, or 3.9%, compared to the prior year. The increase was primarily attributable to higher payroll and benefit-related expenses, increased fuel and vehicle maintenance costs, and higher accruals for self-insurance claims, offset to an extent by an insurance recovery related to the partial settlement of certain claims associated with the Partnership’s acquisition of RNG production assets in December 2022.
During fiscal 2026, the Partnership utilized cash flows from operating activities and net proceeds of $23.5 million from the issuance of Common Units under its at-the-market equity issuance program to fund its long-term strategic growth initiatives, including the acquisition of propane businesses for total consideration of $24.0 million, growth capital expenditures of $34.6 million to advance construction activities and operational enhancements at its RNG production facilities, and repayment of outstanding borrowings under its revolving credit facility of $25.4 million. The Total Consolidated Leverage Ratio, as defined in the Partnership’s credit agreement, for the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026, improved to 4.13x, compared to 4.29x for the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025.
Fourth Quarter of Fiscal Year 2026 Results
Consistent with the seasonal nature of the propane business, the Partnership typically reports a net loss for its fiscal fourth quarter. Net loss for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was $56.8 million, or $0.85 per Common Unit, compared to a net loss of $35.1 million, or $0.53 per Common Unit, in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025. Adjusted EBITDA for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 increased to $7.3 million, compared to $0.7 million in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025. Retail propane gallons sold were 60.5 million gallons for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, which were essentially flat compared to the prior year fourth quarter. Adjusted EBITDA for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 included a benefit of $7.8 million from PTCs.
As of September 26, 2026, the Partnership modified the composition of its operating and reportable segments to provide greater transparency into the operating and financial performance of its businesses and better align its financial reporting with how management evaluates performance and allocates resources. As a result, the Partnership’s two reportable segments are Propane and Renewable Natural Gas. The Propane segment includes activities associated with the distribution of propane, renewable propane, other fuels and related services. The Renewable Natural Gas segment includes activities associated with the production and injection of RNG. The change in reportable segments does not affect the Partnership’s consolidated financial position, results of operations or cash flows. All prior-period segment information presented herein has been recast to conform to the current segment presentation.

About Suburban Propane Partners, L.P.
Suburban Propane Partners, L.P. (“Suburban Propane”) is a publicly traded master limited partnership listed on the New York Stock Exchange. Headquartered in Whippany, New Jersey, Suburban Propane has been in the customer service business since 1928 and is a nationwide distributor of propane, renewable propane, renewable natural gas (“RNG”), fuel oil and related products and services, as well as a marketer of natural gas and electricity and producer of and investor in low carbon fuel alternatives, servicing the energy needs of approximately 1 million residential, commercial, governmental, industrial and agricultural customers through approximately 750 locations across 42 states.
Suburban Propane is supported by three core pillars: (1) Suburban Commitment to Excellence – showcasing Suburban Propane’s almost 100-year legacy, and ongoing commitment to the highest standards for dependability, flexibility, and reliability that underscores Suburban Propane’s commitment to excellence in customer service; (2) SuburbanCares – highlighting continued dedication to giving back to local communities across Suburban Propane’s national footprint; and (3) Go Green with Suburban Propane – promoting propane and renewable propane as versatile, low-carbon energy solutions and investing in the next generation of innovative, renewable energy alternatives.
For additional information on Suburban Propane, please visit www.suburbanpropane.com.
