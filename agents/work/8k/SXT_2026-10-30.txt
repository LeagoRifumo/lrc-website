Sensient Technologies Corporation
Reports Results for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
MILWAUKEE— October 30, 2026 — Sensient Technologies Corporation (NYSE: SXT), a leading provider of flavors and colors for the food, pharmaceutical, and personal care markets, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter Consolidated Results
• | Reported revenue increased 10.6% to $455.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 versus last year’s third quarter results of $412.1 million. On a local currency basis(1), revenue increased 10.2%.

• | Reported operating income increased 25.5% to $72.4 million compared to $57.7 million recorded in last year’s third quarter. In the third quarter of 2025, the Company recorded $3.3 million of costs related to its Portfolio Optimization Plan versus no costs recorded in the third quarter of 2026. Local currency adjusted operating income(1) and local currency adjusted EBITDA(1) were up 18.6% and 15.4%, respectively, in the third quarter.

• | Reported earnings per share increased 29.9% to $1.13 in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 87 cents in the third quarter of 2025. Local currency adjusted diluted EPS(1) increased 17.7% in the third quarter.

“Sensient delivered an exceptional third quarter, with results that reflect the strength of our strategy and unwavering focus on our customers. As we look ahead, I am encouraged by the momentum across our business, the opportunities in our markets, and the investments we are making to support growth. I remain very confident about our performance in 2026 and beyond,” said Paul Manning, Sensient’s Chairman, President, and Chief Executive Officer.

ABOUT SENSIENT TECHNOLOGIES
Sensient Technologies Corporation is a leading global manufacturer and marketer of colors, flavors, and other specialty ingredients. Sensient uses advanced technologies and robust global supply chain capabilities to develop specialized solutions for food and beverages, as well as products that serve the pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, and personal care industries. Sensient’s customers range in size from small entrepreneurial businesses to major international manufacturers representing some of the world’s best-known brands. Sensient is headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

Fruity cereals that maintained balanced color ratios (equal mix of warm and cool hues) through a natural color transition significantly outperformed unbalanced color mixes, because consumers perceived the intact ratio as a guarantee of consistent taste. Maximizing visual similarity between artificial to natural cereal bridges the gap between wellness and indulgence, driving optimal taste and quality perceptions. Willingness to Pay Consumers associate natural colors with higher quality and demonstrate a clear willingness to pay a premium.
