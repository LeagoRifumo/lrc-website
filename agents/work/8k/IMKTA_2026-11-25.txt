Ingles Markets, Incorporated Reports
Results for Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026
ASHEVILLE, N.C. - Ingles Markets, Incorporated (NASDAQ: IMKTA) today reported results for the quarter and year ended September 26, 2026.
Robert P. Ingle II, Executive Chairman of the Board, stated, “Thank you to our associates for the care they show our customers every day. As we close the fiscal year, I am grateful for their hard work and for the loyalty of the families who shop with us.”
Fourth Quarter Results
Net sales totaled $1.374 billion for the quarter ended September 26, 2026, compared with $1.368 billion for the quarter ended September 27, 2025. Fourth quarter results for fiscal 2026 and 2025 each consisted of 13 weeks of operations.
Gross profit for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 totaled $328.5 million, or 23.9% of sales. Gross profit for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025 was $334.7 million, or 24.5% of sales.
Operating and administrative expenses for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 totaled $303.9 million compared with $299.0 million for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025.
Interest expense totaled $5.0 million in both the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 and the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025. Total debt at the end of fiscal 2026 was $497.3 million compared with $514.8 million at the end of fiscal 2025.
Net income totaled $18.5 million for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 compared with net income of $25.7 million for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share for Class A Common Stock were $1.00 and $0.97, respectively, for the quarter ended September 26, 2026, compared with $1.38 and $1.35, respectively, for the quarter ended September 27, 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share for Class B Common Stock were each $0.91 for the quarter ended September 26, 2026, and $1.26 for the quarter ended September 27, 2025.

Annual Results
Net sales totaled $5.42 billion for the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026, compared with $5.33 billion for the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025. The fiscal years ended September 26, 2026, and September 27, 2025, each consisted of 52 weeks of operations.
Gross profit for the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026, totaled $1.32 billion, or 24.4% of sales. Gross profit for the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025, totaled $1.27 billion, or 23.9% of sales.
Operating and administrative expenses totaled $1.19 billion for the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026, and $1.16 billion for the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025.
Interest expense was $18.6 million for the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026, compared with $19.7 million for the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025.
Net income totaled $96.8 million for the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026, compared with $83.6 million for the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share for Class A Common Stock were $5.21 and $5.10, respectively, for the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026, as compared with $4.50 and $4.40, respectively, for the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share for Class B Common Stock were each $4.73 for the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026, as compared with basic and diluted earnings per share of $4.09 for the fiscal year ended September 27, 2025.
Capital expenditures for the 2026 fiscal year totaled $100.6 million compared with $114.5 million for the 2025 fiscal year.
As of September 26, 2026, the Company had only a single letter of credit in the amount of $900,000 outstanding under its $150.0 million line of credit and otherwise had no borrowings outstanding thereunder.
About Ingles Markets, Incorporated
Ingles Markets, Incorporated is a leading grocer with operations in six southeastern states. Headquartered in Asheville, North Carolina, the Company operates 194 supermarkets. At September 26, 2026, three stores temporarily closed due to damage sustained in Hurricane Helene remained closed but are expected to reopen at various times in 2027 or in 2028. In conjunction with its supermarket operations, the Company operates neighborhood shopping centers, most of which contain an Ingles supermarket. The Company also owns a fluid dairy facility that supplies Ingles supermarkets and unaffiliated customers. To learn more about Ingles Markets visit ingles-markets.com.
