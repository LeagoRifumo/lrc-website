GDS Holdings Limited Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Shanghai, China, November 25, 2026 – GDS Holdings Limited (“GDS Holdings”, “GDS” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: GDS; HKEX: 9698), a leading developer and operator of high-performance data centers in China, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
· | Net revenue increased by 10.1% year-over-year (“Y-o-Y”) to RMB3,180.1 million (US$474.3 million) in the third quarter of 2026 (3Q2025: RMB2,887.1 million).

· | Net income was RMB28.5 million (US$4.2 million) in the third quarter of 2026 (3Q2025: RMB728.6 million).

· | Net income margin was 0.9% in the third quarter of 2026 (3Q2025: 25.2%).

· | Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) increased by 2.8% Y-o-Y to RMB1,380.2 million (US$205.9 million) in the third quarter of 2026 (3Q2025: RMB1,342.2 million).

· | Adjusted EBITDA margin (non-GAAP) was 43.4% in the third quarter of 2026 (3Q2025: 46.5%).

Third Quarter 2026 Operating Highlights
· | Total area committed and pre-committed increased by 25.5% Y-o-Y to 823,876 sqm as of September 30, 2026 (September 30, 2025: 656,729 sqm).

· | Area utilized increased by 15.3% Y-o-Y to 560,957 sqm as of September 30, 2026 (September 30, 2025: 486,607 sqm).

· | Area in service increased by 5.6% Y-o-Y to 690,529 sqm as of September 30, 2026 (September 30, 2025: 653,762 sqm).

· | Utilization rate (area utilized divided by area in service) was 81.2% as of September 30, 2026 (September 30, 2025: 74.4%).

“We delivered another quarter of solid results and operational progress, underpinned by our disciplined execution,” said Mr. William Huang, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of GDS. “During the quarter, we secured strong new bookings while continuing to deliver our backlog, with customer move-ins driving higher utilization.”
2
“In the third quarter, our revenue increased by 10.1% and adjusted EBITDA grew by 2.8% year-over-year,” added Mr. Dan Newman, Chief Financial Officer. “With a solid financial position and liquidity, we are well positioned to support our business and deliver sustainable, long-term value for our stakeholders.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Net revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was RMB3,180.1 million (US$474.3 million), a 10.1% increase over the same period last year of RMB2,887.1 million. The Y-o-Y increase was mainly due to the continued ramp-up of our data centers.
Cost of revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was RMB2,554.1 million (US$381.0 million), a 13.6% increase over the same period last year of RMB2,247.9 million. The Y-o-Y increase was in line with the continued ramp-up of our data centers.
Gross profit was RMB626.0 million (US$93.4 million) in the third quarter of 2026, a 2.1% decrease over the same period last year of RMB639.2 million. The Y-o-Y decrease was mainly due to an increase in utility costs.
Gross profit margin was 19.7% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 22.1% in the same period last year. The Y-o-Y decline was mainly due to a higher level of utility costs as a percentage of net revenue.
Adjusted Gross Profit (“Adjusted GP”) (non-GAAP) is defined as gross profit excluding depreciation and amortization, operating lease cost relating to prepaid land use rights, accretion expenses for asset retirement costs and share-based compensation expenses allocated to cost of revenue. Adjusted GP was RMB1,497.1 million (US$223.3 million) in the third quarter of 2026, a 1.7% increase over the same period last year of RMB1,472.6 million. See “Non-GAAP Disclosure” and “Reconciliations of GAAP and non-GAAP results” elsewhere in this earnings release.
3
Adjusted GP margin (non-GAAP) was 47.1% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 51.0% in the same period last year. The Y-o-Y decrease was mainly due to a higher level of utility costs as a percentage of net revenue.
Selling and marketing expenses, excluding share-based compensation expenses of RMB11.0 million (US$1.6 million), were RMB25.8 million (US$3.8 million) in the third quarter of 2026, a 17.3% decrease over the same period last year of RMB31.1 million (excluding share-based compensation of RMB9.1 million). The Y-o-Y decrease was mainly due to a lower level of personnel costs.
General and administrative expenses, excluding share-based compensation expenses of RMB37.6 million (US$5.6 million), depreciation and amortization expenses of RMB49.8 million (US$7.4 million) and operating lease cost relating to prepaid land use rights of RMB12.7 million (US$1.9 million), were RMB95.9 million (US$14.3 million) in the third quarter of 2026, a 12.2% decrease over the same period last year of RMB109.2 million (excluding share-based compensation expenses of RMB43.8 million, depreciation and amortization expenses of RMB59.9 million and operating lease cost relating to prepaid land use rights of RMB15.5 million). The Y-o-Y decrease was mainly due to savings on corporate expenses. The depreciation and amortization expenses included in general and administrative expenses were RMB49.8 million (US$7.4 million) in the third quarter of 2026, a 16.9% decrease over the same period last year of RMB59.9 million. The Y-o-Y decrease was primarily attributable to lower amortization of intangible assets, specifically customer contracts, which had been fully amortized upon expiration.
Research and development costs were RMB11.7 million (US$1.7 million) in the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB8.3 million in the same period last year.
Net interest expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB369.2 million (US$55.1 million), a 1.7% decrease over the same period last year of RMB375.5 million. The Y-o-Y decrease was mainly due to lower interest rates as well as higher interest income.
Foreign currency exchange loss for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB3.1 million (US$0.5 million), compared with RMB0.6 million in the same period last year.

Others, net for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB17.0 million (US$2.5 million), compared with RMB16.1 million in the same period last year.
Income tax expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB36.4 million (US$5.4 million), compared with RMB181.9 million in the same period last year. The Y-o-Y decrease was mainly due to the income tax expenses incurred for the C-REIT transaction during the third quarter last year.
Share of results of equity method investees for the third quarter of 2026 was an income of RMB38.6 million (US$5.8 million), mainly arising from the equity pick-up of our investment in DayOne Data Center Ltd., which we hold a minority interest in, compared with a loss of RMB461.1 million in the same period last year.
Net income in the third quarter of 2026 was RMB28.5 million (US$4.2 million), compared with RMB728.6 million in the same period last year. The Y-o-Y decrease was mainly due to the gain on deconsolidation of subsidiaries as the result of the C-REIT transaction in the same period last year, partially offset by an improvement in the share of results of equity method investees.
Net loss available to GDS Holdings Limited ordinary shareholders in the third quarter of 2026 was RMB5.5 million (US$0.8 million), compared with net income of RMB712.3 million in the same period last year.
Basic loss per ordinary share in the third quarter of 2026 was RMB0.003 (US$0.001), compared with basic income of RMB0.46 in the same period last year.
Diluted loss per ordinary share in the third quarter of 2026 was RMB0.005 (US$0.001), compared with diluted income of RMB0.40 in the same period last year.
Basic loss per American Depositary Share (“ADS”) in the third quarter of 2026 was RMB0.03 (US$0.004), compared with basic income of RMB3.70 in the same period last year.
Diluted loss per ADS in the third quarter of 2026 was RMB0.04 (US$0.006), compared with diluted income of RMB3.21 in the same period last year.

Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) is defined as net income (loss) excluding net interest expenses, income tax expenses (benefits), depreciation and amortization, operating lease cost relating to prepaid land use rights, accretion expenses for asset retirement costs, share-based compensation expenses, share of results of equity method investees and gain on deconsolidation of subsidiaries. Adjusted EBITDA was RMB1,380.2 million (US$205.9 million) in the third quarter of 2026, a 2.8% increase over the same period last year of RMB1,342.2 million.
Adjusted EBITDA margin (non-GAAP) was 43.4% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 46.5% in the same period last year. The Y-o-Y decrease was mainly due to a higher level of utility costs as a percentage of net revenue, which was partially offset by cost savings at the corporate level.

Third Quarter 2026 Operating Results
Sales
Total area committed and pre-committed at the end of the third quarter of 2026 was 823,876 sqm, compared with 656,729 sqm at the end of the third quarter of 2025 and 784,802 sqm at the end of the second quarter of 2026, an increase of 25.5% Y-o-Y and an increase of 5.0% quarter-over-quarter (“Q-o-Q”), respectively. In the third quarter of 2026, net additional total area committed was 39,074 sqm.
Data Center Resources
Area in service at the end of the third quarter of 2026 was 690,529 sqm, compared with 653,762 sqm at the end of the third quarter of 2025 and 684,977 sqm at the end of the second quarter of 2026, an increase of 5.6% Y-o-Y and an increase of 0.8% Q-o-Q, respectively.
Area under construction at the end of the third quarter of 2026 was 191,930 sqm, compared with 72,764 sqm at the end of the third quarter of 2025 and 170,355 sqm at the end of the second quarter of 2026, an increase of 163.8% Y-o-Y and an increase of 12.7% Q-o-Q, respectively.
Commitment rate for area in service was 93.9% at the end of the third quarter of 2026, compared with 92.3% at the end of the third quarter of 2025 and 92.4% at the end of the second quarter of 2026. Pre-commitment rate for area under construction was 91.3% at the end of the third quarter of 2026, compared with 73.0% at the end of the third quarter of 2025 and 89.2% at the end of the second quarter of 2026.
Move-In
Area utilized at the end of the third quarter of 2026 was 560,957 sqm, compared with 486,607 sqm at the end of the third quarter of 2025 and 542,236 sqm at the end of the second quarter of 2026, an increase of 15.3% Y-o-Y and an increase of 3.5% Q-o-Q, respectively. In the third quarter of 2026, net additional area utilized was 18,721 sqm.
Utilization rate for area in service was 81.2% at the end of the third quarter of 2026, compared with 74.4% at the end of the third quarter of 2025 and 79.2% at the end of the second quarter of 2026.

Non-GAAP Disclosure
Our management and board of directors use Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Adjusted GP and Adjusted GP margin, which are non-GAAP financial measures, to evaluate our operating performance, establish budgets and develop operational goals for managing our business. We believe that the exclusion of the income and expenses eliminated in calculating Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted GP can provide useful and supplemental measures of our core operating performance. In particular, we believe that the use of Adjusted EBITDA as a supplemental performance measure captures the trend in our operating performance by excluding from our operating results the impact of our capital structure (primarily interest expense), asset base charges (primarily depreciation and amortization, operating lease cost relating to prepaid land use rights, accretion expenses for asset retirement costs and impairment losses of long-lived assets), other non-cash expenses (primarily share-based compensation expenses), and other income and expenses which we believe are not reflective of our operating performance (primarily gain or loss on deconsolidation of subsidiaries and share of results of equity method investees), whereas the use of adjusted gross profit as a supplemental performance measure captures the trend in gross profit performance of our data centers in service by excluding from our gross profit the impact of asset base charges (primarily depreciation and amortization, operating lease cost relating to prepaid land use rights and accretion expenses for asset retirement costs) and other non-cash expenses (primarily share-based compensation expenses) included in cost of revenue. In addition, we exclude the income (loss) from discontinued operations from our Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin to measure our financial performance from continuing operations, which will be consistent with our future financial performance disclosure.
We note that depreciation and amortization is a fixed cost which commences as soon as each data center enters service. However, it usually takes several years for new data centers to reach high levels of utilization and profitability. The Company incurs significant depreciation and amortization costs for its early-stage data center assets. Accordingly, gross profit, which is a measure of profitability after taking into account depreciation and amortization, does not accurately reflect the Company’s core operating performance.
We also present these non-GAAP measures because we believe these non-GAAP measures are frequently used by securities analysts, investors and other interested parties as measures of the financial performance of companies in our industry.

About GDS Holdings Limited
GDS Holdings Limited (NASDAQ: GDS; HKEX: 9698) is a leading developer and operator of high-performance data centers in China. The Company’s facilities are strategically located across the key hubs where demand for high-performance data center services is concentrated. The Company’s data centers have large net floor area, high power capacity, density and efficiency, and multiple redundancies across all critical systems. The Company is carrier- and cloud-neutral, which enables its customers to access the major telecommunications networks, as well as the largest PRC and global public clouds, which are hosted in many of its facilities. The Company has a 26-year track record of service delivery, successfully fulfilling the requirements of some of the largest and most demanding customers for outsourced data center services in China. The Company’s customer base consists predominantly of hyperscale cloud service providers, large internet companies, financial institutions, telecommunications carriers, IT service providers, and large domestic private sector and multinational corporations. The Company also holds a minority equity interest in DayOne Data Centers Limited, an independent Singapore-headquartered hyperscale data center platform.
