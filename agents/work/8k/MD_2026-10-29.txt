Pediatrix Medical Group Reports Third Quarter Results
FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., October 29, 2026 - Pediatrix Medical Group, Inc. (NYSE: MD), a leading provider of physician services, today reported earnings of $0.59 per share for the three months ended September 30, 2026. On a non-GAAP basis, Pediatrix reported Adjusted EPS of $0.72.
For the 2026 third quarter, Pediatrix reported the following results:
•
Net revenue of $501 million;
•
Net income of $47 million; and
•
Adjusted EBITDA of $85 million.
“We are pleased with our results this quarter, driven by both volume and pricing performing ahead of our expectations,” said Mark S. Ordan, Chief Executive Officer of Pediatrix Medical Group. “We continue to push operating efficiency paired with sector leading care quality, while considering high-value opportunities in adjacent businesses, as they arise.”
Operating Results– Three Months Ended September 30, 2026
Pediatrix’s net revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $501.4 million, compared to $492.9 million for the prior-year period. This increase of $8.5 million, or 1.7%, reflects growth in non-same unit activity of 1.1 percent, driven by recent acquisitions, partially offset by practice dispositions, and growth in same-unit revenue of 0.6 percent.
Same-unit revenue from net reimbursement-related factors increased by 0.3 percent for the 2026 third quarter as compared to the prior-year period. This increase primarily reflects a favorable shift in payor mix, an increase in administrative fees from our hospital partners and higher patient acuity, primarily in neonatology, partially offset by the benefit from strong improvement in cash collections in the prior year. The percentage of services reimbursed by commercial and other non-government payors increased by 70 basis points compared to the prior-year period.
Same-unit revenue attributable to patient volume increased by 0.3 percent for the 2026 third quarter as compared to the prior-year period. For the 2026 third quarter, practice salaries and benefits expense was $341.5 million, compared to $332.3 million for the prior-year period. This increase primarily reflects increases in clinical salaries expense, partially offset by lower benefits expense, at our existing units.
For the 2026 third quarter, general and administrative expenses were $62.7 million, as compared to $60.8 million for the prior-year period. This increase primarily reflects higher incentive-based compensation based on financial results, partially offset by a decrease in information technology-related expenses.
For the 2026 third quarter, transformational and restructuring related expenses were $6.1 million, compared to $6.0 million for the prior-year period. The expenses in both periods were primarily related to position eliminations and revenue cycle management transition activities.
Adjusted EBITDA, which is defined as earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, transformational and restructuring related expenses, and net gain on investments in divested businesses was $85.5 million for the 2026 third quarter, compared to $87.3 million for the prior-year period. The decrease in Adjusted EBITDA was primarily due to higher expenses as compared to revenue growth at our existing units, partially offset by net favorable impacts from recent acquisitions.
Depreciation and amortization expense was $6.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $5.6 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily related to capital expenditures at our existing units and from capital expenditures and amortization of intangible assets from recent acquisitions.
Interest expense was $8.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $8.9 million for the third quarter of 2025, reflecting a reduction in interest expense from modestly lower interest rates and borrowings.

Investment and other income was $5.9 million for the third quarter of 2026 and 2025.
During the third quarter of 2025, the Company recognized a net gain on investments in divested businesses of $20.9 million.
Pediatrix generated net income of $47.0 million, or $0.59 per diluted share, for the 2026 third quarter, based on a weighted average shares outstanding of 79.7 million. This compares with net income of $71.7 million, or $0.84 per diluted share, for the 2025 third quarter, based on a weighted average shares outstanding of 85.6 million. The decrease in weighted average shares outstanding is primarily due to the impact of shares repurchased under the Company’s repurchase program, partially offset by issuances of restricted stock.
For the third quarter of 2026, Pediatrix reported Adjusted EPS of $0.72, compared to $0.67 for the third quarter of 2025. For these periods, Adjusted EPS is defined as diluted income per common and common equivalent share excluding non-cash amortization expense, stock-based compensation expense, transformational and restructuring related expenses, net gain on investments in divested businesses, and impacts from discrete tax events.
Operating Results – Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Pediatrix generated revenue of $1.47 billion, compared to $1.42 billion for the prior-year period. Pediatrix generated net income of $116.4 million, or $1.43 per share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, based on a weighted average 81.3 million shares outstanding, which compares to net income of $131.7 million, or $1.54 per share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, based on a weighted average 85.6 million shares outstanding. Adjusted EBITDA for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $220.1 million, compared to $209.7 million for the prior year. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Pediatrix reported Adjusted EPS of $1.78, compared to $1.54 for the same period of 2025.
Financial Position and Cash Flow – Continuing Operations
Pediatrix had cash and cash equivalents of $311.9 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $375.2 million at December 31, 2025, and net accounts receivable at September 30, 2026 were $231.8 million.
For the third quarter of 2026, Pediatrix generated cash from continuing operations of $117.7 million, compared to $138.1 million during the third quarter of 2025. Additionally, during the third quarter of 2026, the Company used $83.0 million to fund share repurchases and $4.4 million to fund capital expenditures.
At September 30, 2026, Pediatrix had total debt outstanding of $578 million, consisting of its $400 million in 5.375% Senior Notes due 2030 and $178 million in borrowings under its Term A Loan. At September 30, 2026, the Company had no outstanding borrowings under its $450 million revolving line of credit.

ABOUT PEDIATRIX MEDICAL GROUP
Pediatrix® Medical Group, Inc. (NYSE:MD) is a leading provider of physician services. Pediatrix-affiliated clinicians are committed to providing coordinated, compassionate and clinically excellent services to women, babies and children across the continuum of care, both in hospital settings and office-based practices. Specialties include obstetrics, maternal-fetal medicine and neonatology complemented by multiple pediatric subspecialties. The group’s high-quality, evidence-based care is bolstered by significant investments in research, education, quality-improvement and safety initiatives. The physician-led company was founded in 1979 as a single neonatology practice and today provides its highly specialized and often critical care services through approximately 4,300 affiliated physicians and other clinicians. To learn more about Pediatrix, visit www.pediatrix.com or follow us on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and the Pediatrix blog. Investment information can be found at www.pediatrix.com/about/investors.
