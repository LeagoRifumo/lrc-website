MBX Biosciences Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Corporate Highlights
Enrollment in pivotal Phase 3 oPTimize trial of once-weekly canvuparatide underway
12-week MAD (Part C, Cohort 1) data for MBX 4291, a GLP-1/GIP co-agonist prodrug for obesity designed for once-monthly dosing, on track for Q4 2026
Preclinical data for MBX 6180, a GLP-1/GIP/GCG triple agonist prodrug with potential for once-monthly dosing, accepted for presentation at ObesityWeek 2026
$481.6 million in cash and investments expected to support operations into 2029
CARMEL, Ind., and BURLINGTON, Mass., Nov. 09, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MBX Biosciences, Inc. (Nasdaq: MBX), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on the discovery, development and commercialization of novel precision peptide therapies for the treatment of endocrine and metabolic disorders, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and highlighted recent corporate progress.
“The third quarter marked an important milestone for MBX with the initiation of our pivotal Phase 3 oPTimize trial of once-weekly canvuparatide, a potential best-in-class PTH replacement therapy,” said Steve Hoerter, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of MBX Biosciences. “We remain on track to report unblinded 12-week MAD Phase 1 data for MBX 4291, which is designed to deliver true once-monthly dosing and we believe has the potential to offer improved gastrointestinal tolerability. Meanwhile, we continue to advance multiple preclinical obesity programs all enabled by our clinically-validated and differentiated Precision Endocrine Peptide (PEP™) platform. With a strong balance sheet and cash runway into 2029, we believe we are well positioned to advance canvuparatide through pivotal development while continuing to invest in our growing obesity portfolio.”
Third Quarter 2026 and Corporate Highlights
Once-Weekly Canvuparatide for Hypoparathyroidism (HP)
•
Pivotal Phase 3 oPTimize trial of once-weekly canvuparatide underway: In August 2026, MBX announced the dosing of the first patient in its pivotal Phase 3 oPTimize trial. The global, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial is expected to enroll approximately 160 adults with chronic hypoparathyroidism. The trial builds on positive Phase 2 Avail™ results and sustained clinical benefit demonstrated through one year of treatment in the open-label extension study.
MBX 4291 and Expanding Obesity Portfolio
•
12-week MAD (Part C) data for MBX 4291 on track for Q4 2026: Following initial Phase 1 data supporting the potential for once-monthly dosing, MBX remains on track to report unblinded data from the first cohort of the 12-week multiple ascending dose (MAD, Part C, Cohort 1) portion of its ongoing Phase 1 trial of MBX 4291. Cohort C1 is evaluating a titration regimen of 30 mg once weekly for four weeks, followed by 120 mg and 180 mg once monthly, in adult participants with a BMI ≥30 kg/m2.
•
IND-enabling studies ongoing for MBX 5765: MBX continues to advance MBX 5765, its amycretin prodrug candidate combining GLP-1, GIP, glucagon and dual amylin/calcitonin receptor agonist (DACRA) activity in a single peptide construct. The differentiated mechanism of MBX 5765 is designed for once-monthly dosing, with the potential for superior efficacy and improved tolerability.
•
Preclinical data for MBX 6180 accepted for presentation at ObesityWeek 2026: MBX expects to present preclinical pharmacokinetic data for MBX 6180, its GLP-1/GIP/GCG triple agonist prodrug designed for once-monthly dosing, in a poster presentation at ObesityWeek 2026, taking place November 14–17, 2026, in Washington, D.C. IND-enabling studies for MBX 6180 are ongoing.
Corporate
•
Appointed Peter Radovich to Board of Directors: In October 2026, MBX appointed Peter Radovich, President and Chief Operating Officer of Mirum Pharmaceuticals, to its Board of Directors. Mr. Radovich brings more than 20 years of biopharmaceutical industry experience spanning commercial operations, corporate development and program leadership. Prior to Mirum, he held senior leadership positions at Global Blood Therapeutics (now Pfizer Inc.) and Onyx Pharmaceuticals (now Amgen, Inc.), bringing valuable experience in advancing and commercializing innovative therapies.
•
Appointed Jeff Held as Chief Legal Officer: In September 2026, MBX appointed Jeff Held, J.D., as Chief Legal Officer. Mr. Held brings more than 30 years of legal, compliance and executive leadership experience across the biotechnology, life sciences and healthcare industries, including prior leadership roles at Zenas BioPharma and Deciphera Pharmaceuticals.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•
Cash and Cash Equivalents and Marketable Securities: As of September 30, 2026, MBX had cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of $481.6 million, which reflects net proceeds of $97.6 million from the sale of common stock under the Company’s at-the-market (ATM) sales agreement. Based on its current operating plan, the Company expects the combined cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities balance to fund operations into 2029.
•
R&D Expenses: Research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $32.0 million, compared to $19.3 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $12.7 million was driven by an increase in start-up costs related to the canvuparatide Phase 3 clinical trial which was initiated in the third quarter of 2026, ongoing
conduct of the canvuparatide Phase 2 open-label extension clinical trial, ongoing conduct of the MBX 4291 Phase 1 clinical trial and timing of preclinical and IND-enabling activities related to MBX 5765 and MBX 6180.
•
G&A Expenses: General and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $34.3 million, compared to $4.7 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $29.6 million was driven by higher personnel-related costs, including $20.8 million of stock-based compensation expense related to the modification of awards for the Company’s former chief executive officer, and other higher personnel-related costs resulting from continued expansion of both the Company’s executive leadership team and general infrastructure to support growth in the Company’s operations.
•
Net Loss: Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $62.4 million compared to a net loss of $21.6 million for the same period in 2025.
About MBX Biosciences
MBX Biosciences is a biopharmaceutical company focused on the discovery, development and commercialization of novel precision peptide therapies based on its proprietary PEP™ platform, for the treatment of endocrine and metabolic disorders. The Company is advancing a pipeline of novel candidates for endocrine and metabolic disorders with clinically validated targets, established endpoints for regulatory approval, significant unmet medical needs and large potential market opportunities. The Company’s pipeline includes canvuparatide for the treatment of chronic hypoparathyroidism in Phase 3 development; and an obesity portfolio that includes MBX 4291 in Phase 1 development and MBX 5765 and MBX 6180 in preclinical development, as well as additional discovery candidates. The Company is based in Carmel, Indiana and Burlington, Massachusetts. To learn more, please visit the company website at www.mbxbio.com and follow it on LinkedIn.
About MBX’s Proprietary Precision Endocrine Peptide (PEP™) Platform
MBX was founded by global leaders with a transformative approach to peptide drug design and development. Leveraging this expertise, the Company designed its proprietary Precision Endocrine Peptide™ (PEP™) platform to overcome the key limitations of unmodified and modified peptide therapies and to improve clinical outcomes and simplify disease management for patients. PEPs are selectively engineered to have optimized pharmaceutical properties, including extended time-action profiles and consistent drug concentrations with low peak-to-trough concentration ratios, consistent exposure to target tissues, and less frequent dosing.
