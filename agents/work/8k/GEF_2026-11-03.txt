Greif Reports Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Results
DELAWARE, Ohio (November 3, 2026) – Greif, Inc. (NYSE: GEF, GEF.B), a world leader in industrial packaging products and services, today announced fourth quarter and fiscal 2026 results.
On June 30, 2025, we entered into a definitive agreement to divest our Containerboard Business, including our CorrChoice sheet feeder system. Beginning in the third quarter of fiscal 2025, the Containerboard Business has been reported as discontinued operations. Unless otherwise noted, all financial results and discussions in this press release relate to continuing operations. Additional information regarding the basis of presentation and changes in reportable segments is provided under "Basis of Presentation and Comparability".
Fiscal Fourth Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:
(all results compared to the fourth quarter 2025 and both periods reflect only continuing operations unless otherwise noted)
•Net loss decreased to $6.5 million or $0.11 per diluted Class A share compared to net loss of $24.3 million or $0.42 per diluted Class A share.
•Net income, excluding the impact of adjustments(1), increased 46.1% to $49.1 million or $0.87 per diluted Class A share compared to net income, excluding the impact of adjustments, of $33.6 million or $0.58 per diluted Class A share.
•Adjusted EBITDA(2) decreased 3.1% to $163.0 million compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $168.2 million. The current quarter's results were $18.2 million unfavorable due to timing of incentive accrual changes in the prior year quarter.
•Net cash provided by operating activities increased by $314.5 million to a source of $126.0 million, as prior year operating activities included an unfavorable tax impact from the sale of our Containerboard Business of approximately $381.0 million. Adjusted free cash flow(3) decreased by $89.4 million to $83.3 million. Adjusted free cash flow in the prior year quarter includes contribution from the Containerboard Business and is not directly comparable to current quarter results.
Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Highlights:
(all results compared to the fiscal year 2025 and both periods reflect only continuing operations unless otherwise noted):
•Net income increased to $261.5 million or $4.54 per diluted Class A share compared to net income of $31.6 million or $0.55 per diluted Class A share.
•Net income, excluding the impact of adjustments, increased 73.3% to $231.9 million or $4.04 per diluted Class A share compared to net income, excluding the impact of adjustments, of $133.8 million or $2.31 per diluted Class A share.
•Adjusted EBITDA increased 11.7% to $625.7 million compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $560.0 million.
•Net cash provided by operating activities increased by $198.4 million to a source of $296.0 million, as prior year operating activities included an unfavorable tax impact from the sale of our Containerboard Business of approximately $381.0 million. Adjusted free cash flow decreased by $101.0 million to $279.3 million, below our most recent guidance range, primarily due to elevated inventory balances and higher resin costs, reflecting our strategic decision to ensure continuity of supply for customers amid Middle East supply disruptions. Adjusted free cash flow in the prior year includes contribution from the Containerboard Business and is not directly comparable to current year results.
•Total debt decreased by $106.2 million to $1,096.3 million primarily due to repayment of debt in connection with the sale of our timberlands business. Net debt(4) decreased by $144.9 million to $800.9 million. The Company's leverage ratio(5) decreased to 1.2x from 1.7x.
Strategic Actions and Announcements
•Announced strategic exit of Coated Recycled Board business with the closure of our Sweetwater paperboard mill in Austell, GA. This decision reflects our continued focus on businesses where we have leading market positions and

Commentary from CEO Ole Rosgaard
“We delivered a strong fiscal 2026, growing adjusted EBITDA by nearly 12% despite continued weakness in global industrial markets,” said Ole Rosgaard, Chief Executive Officer. “Our performance reflects the strength of our businesses and the actions our teams are taking to improve profitability, rather than relying on a market recovery.
We achieved $105 million in annual run-rate cost savings, exceeding our original commitment a year ahead of schedule. This success gives us the confidence to increase our original cost optimization target by 50% to $150 million by the end of fiscal 2027.
Our sharpened portfolio is focused on core businesses where we hold leading market positions and see attractive opportunities for profitable growth. With a lower cost base, stronger balance sheet and further improvements ahead, we are well positioned to grow earnings, increase cash flow and deliver lasting value to our shareholders, even without a meaningful recovery in industrial demand.”

Basis of Presentation and Comparability
On June 30, 2025, we entered into a definitive agreement to divest our Containerboard Business, including our CorrChoice sheet feeder system, in an all-cash transaction for $1.8 billion to Packaging Corporation of America. Beginning in the third quarter of 2025, the Containerboard Business was reported as discontinued operations. The transaction closed as of August 31, 2025.
Effective fiscal 2025, we changed our fiscal year end from October 31 to September 30. As a result, prior-year comparative information has been recast to reflect a twelve-month period ended September 30, 2025, for comparability with fiscal 2026. The results for the twelve-month period recast for fiscal 2025 is unaudited and not included in our audited financial statements for that fiscal year.
Effective October 1, 2025, our Integrated Solutions reportable segment was renamed Innovative Closure Solutions. Additionally, activities related to the purchase and sale of recycled fiber and the production and sale of adhesives used in paperboard products, which were previously reported within the Integrated Solutions reportable segment, are now reported within the Sustainable Fiber Solutions reportable segment. Likewise, activities related to production and sale of complementary packaging products and services such as paints, linings and filling, that are used in or relate to our steel products and were previously reported within the Integrated Solutions reportable segment, are now reported within the Durable Metal Solutions reportable segment.

Tax Summary
During the fourth quarter, we recorded an income tax rate of 118.6 percent and a tax rate excluding the impact of adjustments of 42.2 percent. Note that the application of accounting for income taxes often causes fluctuations in our quarterly effective tax rates. For the full year, we recorded an income tax rate of 28.0 percent and a tax rate excluding the impact of adjustments of 28.1 percent.

About Greif
Founded in 1877, Greif is a global leader in performance packaging located in over 35 countries. The company delivers trusted, innovative, and tailored solutions that support some of the world’s most demanding and fastest-growing industries. With a commitment to legendary customer service, operational excellence, and global sustainability, Greif packages life’s essentials – and creates lasting value for its colleagues, customers, and other stakeholders.
