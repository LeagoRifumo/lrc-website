EXHIBIT 99.1
ImmunityBio Reports Label Expansion and Additional Global Approval with Q3 2026 Net Product Revenue of $48.7 Million, Up 53% Year-Over-Year and First Nine Months U.S. Revenue Up 92% to $143.6 Million
Label Expansion and Global Progress
•FDA Expanded Approval: On October 14, 2026, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approved our supplemental Biologics License Application (sBLA) expanding the approved indication for ANKTIVA® in combination with Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) to include adult patients with BCG-unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC). This expanded indication now includes adult patients with BCG-unresponsive NMIBC with high-grade papillary-only tumors.
•BCG Naïve Primary Endpoint Met: In November 2026, we reported that the primary endpoint for Cohort A was met in the randomized QUILT-2.005 trial, and we remain on track to submit an sBLA to the FDA in the fourth quarter of 2026 for ANKTIVA in combination with BCG in patients with BCG-naïve NMIBC.
•ANKTIVA Approval in Israel: In November 2026, we announced approval in Israel of ANKTIVA in combination with BCG for BCG-unresponsive NMIBC with CIS, with or without papillary tumors, bringing the number of global approvals to 35.
•First Patient Treated in Europe: In November 2026, we announced that the first patients in Europe have received treatment with ANKTIVA in combination with BCG for BCG-unresponsive NMIBC with CIS, with or without papillary tumors, expanding access to ANKTIVA across the region.
92% Nine Month Revenue Growth
•Q3 2026 net product revenue of $48.7 million, up 53% year-over-year, driven by continued adoption among U.S. urologists and strong market access
•2026 year-to-date net product revenue of $143.6 million, up 92% year-over-year
•Over $334.3 million cash and cash equivalents, and marketable securities on hand as of September 30, 2026
CULVER CITY, Calif. November 9, 2026 — ImmunityBio, Inc. (NASDAQ: IBRX), a commercial-stage immunotherapy company, today announced continued global expansion, expanded FDA approval, and financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
ImmunityBio continued to advance a broad clinical pipeline spanning bladder cancer, non-small cell lung cancer, and hematologic malignancies with expanded FDA approval and global expansion of ANKTIVA in nonmuscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC). ANKTIVA is now approved or authorized in six regulatory jurisdictions, expanding its global footprint to 35 countries, while multiple ongoing clinical and regulatory programs continue to support the Company’s long-term growth strategy.

The Company advanced several key regulatory and clinical programs across its immunotherapy pipeline. It received an expanded label for ANKTIVA in combination with BCG to include adults with BCG-unresponsive NMIBC, including those with papillary-only tumors. In addition, Cohort A of the randomized QUILT-2.005 trial evaluating ANKTIVA in combination with BCG in patients with BCG-naïve NMIBC met its primary endpoint. The Company also initiated its first sales in Europe in early Q4 and obtained approval in Israel in collaboration with its partner, Rafa Laboratories Ltd.
In this quarter (October 2026), ImmunityBio received FDA approval for expanded use of ANKTIVA plus BCG in subjects with BGC-unresponsive nonmuscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC). This expanded FDA approval has already been authorized by the Emirates Drug Establishment (United Arab Emirates) and the Company will now pursue global filings for this expanded indication.
The company today announced that the primary endpoint for BCG naïve nonmuscle invasive bladder (NMIBC) cancer has been reached in subjects receiving ANKTIVA plus BCG versus those receiving BCG alone. With this positive result the supplemental BLA (sBLA) for BCG naïve NMIBC patients is on track for submission in this quarter.
Global expansion and operational implementation of the launch of ANKTIVA plus BCG in BCG unresponsive NMIBC continues with the approval of ANKTIVA plus BCG in NMIBC in Israel and the first commercial dose administered in Europe.
On October 16, 2026, The Journal of Urology published long-term outcomes from our Phase 2/3 QUILT-3.032 trial of ANKTIVA in combination with BCG in 100 patients with BCG-unresponsive NMIBC with carcinoma in situ (CIS), with or without papillary tumors. Among responders, cystectomy avoidance was 84% at 36 months. The median duration of complete response (CR) was 26.6 months, with some responses extending beyond 53 months—emphasizing support for ANKTIVA by a mature dataset.
“The FDA’s recent approval of ANKTIVA plus BCG for papillary-only disease, together with data published in The Journal of Urology showing that more than half of responding patients maintained complete responses beyond 3.5 years, further reinforces the clinical potential of our chemotherapy-free immunotherapy approach and ANKTIVA’s novel mechanism of action,” said Patrick Soon-Shiong, M.D., Founder, Executive Chairman and Global Chief Scientific and Medical Officer of ImmunityBio. “We look forward to reviewing the final results from our study in BCG-naïve patients and initiating real-world evidence studies to further characterize ANKTIVA’s clinical impact in NMIBC. We also continued to advance our clinical program in metastatic non-small cell lung cancer, GBM, and hematologic malignancies during the quarter.”
Quarterly Financial Highlights
“We continue to see more physicians prescribing ANKTIVA to multiple patients and growing physician interest in our chemotherapy-free approach in the U.S. and Europe,” said Richard Adcock, President and CEO of ImmunityBio. “The recent label expansion represents an important opportunity to broaden access to ANKTIVA for a significantly larger patient population. Looking ahead, our QUILT-2.005 trial in BCG-naïve patients offers another potential avenue for expanding the eligible patient population and supporting long-term growth, subject to positive clinical and regulatory approval.”
Net product revenue reached $48.7 million, representing 53% year-over-year growth. For the first nine months of 2026, net product revenue totaled $143.6 million, up 92% compared with the first nine months of 2025, and building on full-year 2025 net product revenue of $113.0 million. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $334.3 million in cash and cash equivalents, and marketable securities.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Summary
Product Revenue, Net
Product revenue, net increased $17.0 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, due to an increase in sales of ANKTIVA.
Research and Development Expense
Research and development (R&D) expense increased $20.3 million to $71.5 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $51.2 million during the three months ended September 30, 2025, mainly due to increased clinical trial expenses and personnel-related costs, partially offset by decreased manufacturing expenses.
Selling, General and Administrative Expense
Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expense increased $17.7 million to $54.0 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $36.3 million during the three months ended September 30, 2025, mainly due to increased professional services expenses, personnel-related costs, and commercial-related expenses.
Other Expense, Net
Other expense, net increased $94.9 million to $106.6 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $11.7 million during the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily due to non-cash changes in the fair value of a related-party convertible note, warrant liabilities, and other derivative liabilities mainly driven by an increase in our common stock price during the quarter, and an increase in interest expense related to the revenue interest liability due to additional funding received in Q1 2026. These changes were partially offset by an increase in interest and investment income combined with a decrease in interest expense due to a lower related-party convertible note balance after a partial loan conversion in Q1 2026.
Net Loss Attributable to ImmunityBio Common Stockholders (Net Loss)
Net loss attributable to ImmunityBio common stockholders was $184.6 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $67.3 million during the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in net loss was mainly driven by changes in the fair value of a related-party convertible note, and warrant and derivative liabilities due to an increase in our common stock price during the quarter, higher interest expense related to the revenue interest liability, and higher R&D and SG&A expenses described above, which were partially offset by higher product revenue.

About ImmunityBio
ImmunityBio is a biotechnology company focused on innovating, developing, and commercializing next-generation immunotherapies designed to activate the patient’s immune system and deliver durable protection against cancer and infectious diseases. Our approach harnesses both the adaptive and innate immune systems with the goal of restoring immune function and generating lasting immunological memory in patients. At the core of our strategy is the Cancer BioShield™ platform, which is designed to stimulate critical lymphocytes, including natural killer (NK) cells, cytotoxic T cells, and memory T cells via our proprietary IL-15 receptor superagonist, ANKTIVA® (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept). Our Cancer BioShield platform is anchored by this antibody-cytokine fusion protein and is complemented by a portfolio that includes adenovirus-vectored vaccines, allogeneic (off-the-shelf) and autologous NK-cell therapies, and additional immunomodulators intended to promote immunogenic cell death and support durable immune responses while potentially reducing reliance on high-dose chemo-radiation therapy.
