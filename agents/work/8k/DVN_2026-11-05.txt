Devon Energy Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Results
HOUSTON, TX – November 5, 2026 – Devon Energy Corporation (NYSE: DVN) today reports third-quarter 2026 results and has published a letter to shareholders from President and CEO Clay Gaspar. The shareholder letter, supplemental financial tables, and forward-looking guidance are available on the company’s website at www.devonenergy.com.
KEY FINANCIAL, OPERATIONAL & STRATEGIC HIGHLIGHTS
• | Delivering on 2026, Providing Initial 2027 Outlook: Maintaining full-year 2026 guidance. Expect 2027 oil production growth of 1-3%, with approximately 525,000 barrels per day of oil and 1,600,000 Boe (barrels of oil equivalent) per day for total capital of approximately $5.3 billion, pro forma for the Eagle Ford sale

• | Robust Cash Flow Generation: Operations generated $2.8 billion of operating cash flow, adjusted operating cash flow of $3.5 billion, and $2.0 billion of adjusted free cash flow during the third quarter

• | Eagle Ford Divestiture: As announced on October 8, agreed to sell the Eagle Ford assets for $4.2 billion in cash; closing expected in early 2027

• | Accelerating Share Repurchases: Ramping to greater than $1 billion in the fourth quarter with further acceleration in early 2027, post-Eagle Ford sale proceeds

• | Shareholder Returns and Balance Sheet: Returned $1,530 million during the quarter through the $0.32 per share dividend ($366 million), $414 million of share repurchases and the retirement of the remaining $750 million term loan, bringing debt reduction since merger close to $1.25 billion

• | Synergy Outlook Raised: Raised the expected year-end 2026 synergy run rate to approximately $350 million, 75% above the plan laid out in August, with approximately $700 million of average synergies expected in 2027, up $100 million from our prior outlook, on the way to at least $1.0 billion of expected annual synergies by year-end 2027

• | Solitude Pipeline Investment: Acquired a 25% interest in the Solitude Pipeline System with firm transportation capacity, connecting Permian natural gas to Gulf Coast and LNG-linked pricing

OPERATING RESULTS
Devon’s operational activity in the third quarter averaged 32 operated drilling rigs and 8 completion crews. This level of activity resulted in 124 net operated wells being placed online, with an average lateral length of 11,400 feet. Capital expenditures, excluding acquisitions, totaled $1,496 million.
Production averaged 1,668,000 Boe per day in the third quarter, within guidance. Oil totaled 553,000 barrels per day in the quarter, with natural gas liquids of 382,000 barrels per day and natural gas of 4.4 Bcf (billion cubic feet) per day. Eagle Ford oil volumes were below expectations due to the timing of completions during the quarter, offset by solid performance across the rest of the portfolio, led by Permian Basin oil production of 389,000 barrels per day.
For the third quarter, Devon’s oil, gas, and NGL sales totaled $5.9 billion. The company’s realized price during the period, including commodity hedges, was $79.54 per barrel of oil, $23.81 per barrel of NGL and $2.16 per Mcf (thousand cubic feet) of natural gas. Unhedged natural gas realizations improved to 66% of Henry Hub, above the guidance range, as additional Permian takeaway capacity commenced service and narrowed Waha differentials.
Cash costs, including production expenses, general and administrative expenses, and financing costs, totaled $12.89 per Boe in the third quarter, approximately 2% below the midpoint of guidance. Gathering, processing, and transportation expense of $3.20 per Boe and general and administrative expense of $1.18 per Boe both came in below their guidance ranges, while lease operating expenses were slightly above the quarterly guidance range due to higher workover activity and third-party water disposal.

FINANCIAL RESULTS
Devon reported net earnings of $1.0 billion, or $0.88 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2026. Adjusting for items analysts typically exclude from estimates, the company’s core earnings were $1.6 billion, or $1.42 per diluted share.
Devon’s operating cash flow totaled $2.8 billion in the third quarter, the company’s first full quarter of combined operations. The company funded its capital requirements and had $2.0 billion in adjusted free cash flow for the quarter.
During the quarter, Devon retired the remaining $750 million of its term loan, bringing total debt reduction since merger close to $1.25 billion. Supported by free cash flow and the Eagle Ford sale proceeds, the company plans to retire an additional $1.5 billion of long-term debt, reaching its $9 billion gross debt target in early 2027.
At the end of the third quarter, Devon had a cash balance of $0.4 billion and an undrawn credit facility of $3.0 billion. Outstanding debt totaled $10.6 billion and net debt totaled $10.2 billion, 0.9 times trailing twelve-month EBITDAX.
RETURN OF CAPITAL
Devon’s board of directors declared a fixed quarterly cash dividend of $0.32 per share for the fourth quarter, payable on December 30, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 15, 2026, consistent with the company’s strategic priority of delivering value to shareholders through a sustainable, annually growing fixed dividend. The third-quarter dividend totaled $366 million and was paid on September 30, 2026.
The company also returned capital to shareholders through its $8.0 billion share repurchase program. During the third quarter, the company repurchased 8.9 million shares for $414 million, bringing repurchases since the program began in May to 13.3 million shares for $617 million, leaving $7.4 billion of remaining capacity on the authorization, which extends through mid-2029. The company expects share repurchases to step up to more than $1 billion in the fourth quarter and to rise further in 2027, fueled in part by the net proceeds from the Eagle Ford sale.
ACCELERATING SYNERGIES
Merger integration is running ahead of plan. Devon now expects to exit 2026 at an annualized pre-tax synergy run rate of approximately $350 million, 75% above the $200 million exit rate outlined in August, with the increase spread across capital optimization, operating margin improvements, and corporate cost reductions. The company now expects approximately $700 million of average synergies in 2027, up from $600 million, and continues to expect at least $1.0 billion of annual pre-tax run-rate synergies by year-end 2027. Shared best practices and technology applied across the combined portfolio are strengthening margins and increasing capital efficiency.
OUTLOOK
The company is reaffirming its full-year 2026 production and capital guidance with the Eagle Ford included through closing. For the fourth quarter of 2026, total production is expected to average between 1,660,000 and 1,700,000 Boe per day and oil production is expected to average between 550,000 and 560,000 barrels per day. Capital spending in the fourth quarter is expected to be between $1,290 million and $1,395 million.
Devon also provided an initial view of 2027. On a pro forma basis, adjusted for the Eagle Ford sale, the company expects 2027 oil production of approximately 525,000 barrels per day and total production of approximately 1,600,000 Boe per day on total capital of approximately $5.3 billion. Full 2027 guidance will be provided with fourth-quarter results in February.
Additional details of Devon’s forward-looking guidance are available on the company’s website at www.devonenergy.com.

EAGLE FORD DIVESTITURE
As announced on October 8, 2026, Devon has entered into a definitive agreement to sell its Eagle Ford assets for total consideration of $4.2 billion in cash, subject to customary closing adjustments. The transaction has an effective date of July 1, 2026 and is expected to close in early 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions. After taxes and effective-date adjustments, net proceeds are expected to be approximately $3.5 billion and will be used to accelerate share repurchases and to reduce debt.
SOLITUDE PIPELINE INVESTMENT
During the third quarter, Devon acquired a 25% interest in the Solitude Pipeline System, a joint venture constructing two natural gas pipelines from the Permian Basin to the Texas Gulf Coast and secured firm transportation capacity on the system. The investment, funded by cash flow, connects a portion of Devon’s Permian natural gas production to Gulf Coast and LNG-linked pricing and is expected to improve realized natural gas prices once the system enters service.
CONFERENCE CALL WEBCAST AND SUPPLEMENTAL EARNINGS MATERIALS

ABOUT DEVON ENERGY
Devon Energy is a leading oil and gas producer in the U.S. with a diversified multi-basin portfolio headlined by a world-class acreage position in the Delaware Basin. Devon’s disciplined cash-return business model is designed to achieve strong returns, generate free cash flow and return capital to shareholders, while focusing on safe and sustainable operations.
