Teradata Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Cash flow from operations of $91 million and Adjusted Free Cash Flow of $96 million(3)
•Third quarter GAAP diluted EPS of $0.40 and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $0.70(2)
•Reiterate full year outlook for Total ARR and Adjusted Free Cash Flow; narrowing the Total Revenue and Recurring Revenue outlook to the high-end of the range
SAN DIEGO – November 5, 2026 -- Teradata (NYSE: TDC) today announced its third quarter 2026 financial results.
“In Q3, Teradata marked another quarter of progress against the goals we set at the start of the year, driving total ARR growth, improving profitability and delivering strong free cash flow," said Steve McMillan, president and CEO, Teradata. "The increased pace of new product innovation and new AI service capabilities is driving positive interest from customers.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights Compared to Third Quarter 2025
•Total ARR increased to $1.512 billion from $1.490 billion, an increase of 1% as reported and 2% in constant currency(1)
•Public cloud ARR increased to $700 million from $633 million, an increase of 11% as reported and 11% in constant currency(1)
•Total revenue was $399 million versus $416 million, a decrease of 4% as reported and 4% in constant currency(1)
•Recurring revenue was $359 million versus $366 million, a decrease of 2% as reported and 2% in constant currency(1)
•Recurring revenue was 90% of total revenue versus 88%
•GAAP gross margin was 59.6% versus 60.8%
•Non-GAAP gross margin was 60.7% versus 62.3%(2)
•GAAP operating margin was 13.5% versus 14.7%
•Non-GAAP operating margin was 21.8% versus 23.6%(2)
•GAAP diluted EPS was $0.40 versus $0.42 per share
•Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $0.70 versus $0.72 per share(2)
•Cash flow from operations was $91 million compared to $94 million
•Free cash flow was $85 million compared to $88 million(3)
•Adjusted free cash flow was $96 million compared to $88 million(3)
Q3 2026 Product and Innovation Highlights
•Released Teradata Factory for general availability, an integrated GPU system combining analytics, AI, and governance that keeps data within an organization’s own walls.
•Evolved Tera into an agentic coworker for enterprise data work, letting anyone analyze data, build AI applications, operate infrastructure, and automate workflows in natural language.
•Announced Tera Context Engine, a vendor-neutral layer that connects databases, data platforms, pipelines, catalogs, models, and AI agents, giving AI governed business knowledge wherever data lives.
•Launched Tera Harness, an execution layer that routes work to the right skills, tools, data, and models, improving AI accuracy, speed, and efficiency.
•Made Teradata’s Data Analyst Agent available in AWS Marketplace and integrated Teradata Autonomous Knowledge Platform with Microsoft OneLake, so customers can run Teradata AI on OneLake data without moving or copying it.
•Tera delivered 53% lower cost per reliably solved task than Snowflake Cortex Code, compared to Snowflake’s own published benchmark data on the data-eng-bench. This internal test used a public benchmark of data pipeline engineering created by Snowflake Labs and Bespoke Labs.
Outlook
For the full-year 2026, Teradata increases the following ranges:
•GAAP diluted EPS is now expected to be in the range of $4.63 to $4.67
•Non-GAAP diluted EPS is now expected to be in the range of $2.86 to $2.90 per share(2)
For the full-year 2026, Teradata narrows to the high-end of the previous ranges:
•Recurring revenue in the range of 1% to 2% year-over-year
•Total revenue in the range of -1% to flat year-over-year
For the full-year 2026, Teradata reaffirms the following ranges:
•Total ARR growth of 2% to 4% year-over-year
•Cash flow from operations of $665 million to $685 million, which includes an after-tax net benefit of $315 million related to a settlement with SAP
•Adjusted free cash flow of $330 million to $350 million(3)
For the fourth quarter of 2026:
•Recurring revenue in the range of -2% to -4% year-over-year
•Total revenue in the range of -4% to -6% year-over-year
•GAAP diluted EPS is expected to be in the range of $0.26 to $0.30 per share
•Non-GAAP diluted EPS is expected to be in the range of $0.58 to $0.62 per share(2)

3.As described below, the Company believes that free cash flow and adjusted free cash flow are useful non-GAAP measures for investors. Free cash flow and adjusted free cash flow do not have a uniform definition under GAAP in the United States and therefore, Teradata's definitions may differ from other companies' definitions of this measure. Teradata defines free cash flow as cash provided by/used in operating activities, less total capital expenditures and adjusted free cash flow as free cash flow less the gross proceeds from the SAP settlement, plus the non-recurring legal and other expenses incurred in connection with the SAP litigation and resulting settlement, and taxes paid specific to the settlement agreement. Teradata’s management uses free cash flow and adjusted free cash flow to assess the financial performance of the Company and believes they are useful for investors because they relate the operating cash flow of the Company to the capital that is spent to continue and improve business operations. In particular, free cash flow indicates the amount of cash generated after capital expenditures which can be used for among other things, investments in the Company's existing businesses, strategic acquisitions, strengthening the Company’s balance sheet, repurchase of Company stock and repay the Company’s debt obligations and adjusted free cash flow adjusts the impact of the SAP settlement. Neither free cash flow or adjusted free cash flow represent the residual cash flow available for discretionary expenditures since there may be other non-discretionary expenditures that are not deducted from these measures. These non-GAAP measures should not be considered as a substitute for, or superior to, cash flows from operating activities under GAAP.
