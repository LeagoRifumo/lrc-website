• Total reported revenues for the third quarter were $2.0 billion, compared with $1.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 13.5%. Excluding the impact of foreign currency exchange ("Fx"), total reported revenues increased 13.3% compared to the prior year, driven by an 11.2% increase in storage rental revenue and a 16.3% increase in service revenue. Year to date, total reported revenues increased 17.7%, or 16.5% excluding the impact of Fx. • Net Income for the third quarter was $128.9 million, compared with $86.2 million in the third quarter of 2025, driven primarily by increased Operating Income. Year to date, Net Income was $384.0 million, compared with $59.1 million in 2025. • Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter was $748.0 million, compared with $660.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 13.3%. On a constant currency basis, Adjusted EBITDA increased by 13.0% in the third quarter, compared to the third quarter of 2025, driven by increased revenue and Adjusted EBITDA across each of our segments and improved operating leverage from our continued transformation activities. Year to date, Adjusted EBITDA increased 16.8%, or 15.7% excluding the impact of Fx. • FFO (Normalized) per share was $1.05 for the third quarter, compared with $0.93 in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 12.9%. Year to date, FFO (Normalized) per share was $3.03, compared with $2.59 in 2025, or an increase of 17.0%. • AFFO was $458.4 million for the third quarter, compared with $393.3 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 16.6% driven by improved Adjusted EBITDA. Year to date, AFFO was $1,317.3 million compared with $1,111.5 million, or an increase of 18.5%. • AFFO per share was $1.53 for the third quarter, compared with $1.32 in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 15.9%. Year to date, AFFO per share was $4.40, compared to $3.73 in 2025, or an increase of 18.0%. Funds From Operations, or FFO (Nareit), and FFO (Normalized) Funds from operations ("FFO") is defined by the National Association of Real Estate Investment Trusts as net income (loss) excluding depreciation on real estate assets, losses and gains on sale of real estate, net of tax, and amortization of data center leased-based intangibles (“FFO (Nareit)”). We calculate our FFO measure, including FFO (Nareit), adjusting for our share of reconciling items from our unconsolidated joint ventures. FFO (Nareit) does not give effect to real estate depreciation because these amounts are computed, under GAAP, to allocate the cost of a property over its useful life. Because values for well-maintained real estate assets have historically increased or decreased based upon prevailing market conditions, we believe that FFO (Nareit) provides investors with a clearer view of our operating performance. Our most directly comparable GAAP measure to FFO (Nareit) is net income (loss). We modify FFO (Nareit), as is common among REITs seeking to provide financial measures that most meaningfully reflect their particular business ("FFO (Normalized)").
