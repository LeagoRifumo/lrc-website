Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results Compared With Third Quarter 2025
Net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $1,344.1 million, or $14.49 per diluted share, compared with net income in the third quarter of 2025 of $26.3 million, or $0.24 per diluted share (Diluted EPS). The third quarter of 2026 included a number of special items, including gains on the sale of real estate assets and contingent consideration associated with the CSP acquisition, partially offset by expenses associated with mergers and acquisitions, expenses associated with debt repayments, and settlement compensation associated with the executive transition. Excluding these special items, Adjusted Net Income was $45.7 million, or $0.49 per diluted share (Adjusted Diluted EPS), compared with Adjusted Net Income in the third quarter of 2025 of $26.2 million, or $0.24 per diluted share. Special items are presented in detail in the calculation of Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted EPS in the Supplemental Financial Information following the financial statements presented herein.
The increases in Diluted EPS and Adjusted Diluted EPS compared with the prior year quarter resulted from continued occupancy increases at previously idle facilities resulting from new contract awards in the CoreCivic Residential segment at our 600-bed West Tennessee Detention Facility (West Tennessee Facility), the 2,560-bed California City Detention Facility (California City Facility), our 2,160-bed Diamondback Correctional Facility (Diamondback Facility), and the 1,033-bed Midwest Regional Reception Center (Midwest Facility). These four facilities are expected to generate total annual revenue of approximately $320.0 million once fully activated. Increases in Diluted EPS and Adjusted Diluted EPS also resulted from higher federal and state populations across the remaining Residential portfolio, and the acquisition on April 1, 2026 of Clinical Solutions Pharmacy (CSP) in the CoreCivic Services segment. Occupancy levels in our Residential segment increased to 84.0% in the third quarter of 2026 from 76.7% in the third quarter of 2025. Finally, Diluted EPS and Adjusted Diluted EPS benefited from lower interest expense, net, reflecting debt repayments and higher interest income on larger cash balances, as well as a 13.8% reduction in weighted average diluted shares outstanding primarily resulting from our share repurchase program, including most notably the $500.0 million ASR Agreement executed on August 10, 2026.

The solutions we provide for our federal customers, including primarily ICE and the U.S. Marshals Service (USMS), continue to be a significant component of our business. The federal customers in our Residential segment generated 53% and 55% of our total revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively, increasing $71.3 million, or 22.2%, during the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared with the same period in 2025. The increase in federal revenue was primarily a result of increased occupancy at numerous facilities, particularly those where we have contracts with ICE, and per diem increases.
Operating margin in the CoreCivic Residential segment was relatively flat at 22.3% versus 22.5% in the prior year quarter despite an increase in occupancy as the increase in occupancy was substantially attributable to the four facilities under activation. Because these four facilities continue to be in various stages of activation, they are not yet contributing at a fully activated margin percentage. During the three months ended September 30, 2026, these four facilities generated net operating income, or revenue less operating expenses, of $19.9 million.
Net operating income in the CoreCivic Services segment increased $8.5 million, to $9.6 million in the third quarter of 2026 from $1.1 million in the prior year quarter. The Services segment includes the operating results of CSP, a newly acquired subsidiary that provides mail order pharmacy services to over 600 correctional facilities, including correctional facilities owned or operated by CoreCivic, across 32 states. CoreCivic Services also includes the operating results of TransCor America, LLC, a subsidiary that provides transportation services to governmental agencies and CoreCivic, as well as the operating results of Recovery Monitoring Solutions, a subsidiary that provides electronic monitoring and case management services. The increase in net operating income in this segment was primarily attributable to the acquisition of CSP on April 1, 2026.
Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA) was $1,899.9 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $89.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in EBITDA from the prior year quarter was attributable to the special items, including most notably the gain on sale of real estate assets. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes special items, was $117.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $88.8 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 32.6%. The increase in EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA was primarily driven by the activation of four previously idle facilities and the acquisition of CSP, partially offset by an increase in general and administrative expenses resulting from higher incentive compensation, transitional expenses related to executive leadership changes, and expenditures in technology.
Funds From Operations (FFO), which excludes the after-tax impact of the gain on sale of real estate assets, for the third quarter of 2026 was $78.3 million, or $0.84 per diluted share, compared with $51.5 million, or $0.48 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Normalized FFO, which excludes the remaining special items, was $69.9 million, or $0.75 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $52.1 million, or $0.48 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Normalized FFO per share was positively impacted by the same factors that affected Adjusted EBITDA, as well as the reduction in interest expense and a 13.8% reduction in weighted average diluted shares outstanding compared with the prior year quarter primarily due to the ASR Agreement.

Facility Sales & Continuing Management
As previously announced, we completed the sales on July 2, 2026, of our California City Facility and our Otay Mesa Detention Center (Otay Mesa Facility), both located in California. On August 4, 2026, after announcing a new management contract with ICE to activate the Appleton Detention Facility, formerly known as the Prairie Correctional Facility (Appleton Facility), as further described hereinafter, we completed the sales of our Appleton Facility and our Midwest Facility. The gross sales price for these four facility sales totaled $2.2 billion, resulting in an aggregate gain on sales of approximately $1.8 billion. After federal and state income taxes of approximately $0.5 billion and transaction costs, our net proceeds from the facility sales were approximately $1.6 billion. All of these facilities were sold to the United States of America and its assigns, by and through the Department of Homeland Security (DHS).
We currently expect to continue to manage these four facilities under existing management contracts with ICE, although we expect the terms of the management contracts to be modified to reflect the change in ownership. However, since all of our contracts with ICE provide it with the ability to terminate our contracts for non-appropriation of funds or for convenience, we can provide no assurance that we will continue to manage these facilities in the future, or that the terms of the management agreements will remain the same. The management contract for the California City Facility expires in August 2027 and the management contract for the Otay Mesa Facility expires in December 2029 and contains a five-year extension option. The management contract for the Midwest Facility expires in August 2027, and the management contract for the Appleton Facility expires in August 2031.
In addition to the facility sales completed to-date, we have been in discussions with ICE about the potential acquisition of additional detention facilities from us. These discussions are in preliminary stages, and we can provide no assurance that any additional sales will occur.
Capital Strategy
Accelerated Share Repurchase Agreement. On August 4, 2026, our board of directors (BOD) authorized an increase to our existing share repurchase program pursuant to which we may purchase up to an additional $500.0 million of CoreCivic’s outstanding common stock. On August 10, 2026, we entered into the ASR Agreement with a financial institution (Dealer) to repurchase $500.0 million of the Company’s common stock.

Following entry into the ASR Agreement and using a portion of the net proceeds from the facility sales, we made a payment of $500.0 million to the Dealer and received 12.4 million shares of common stock from the Dealer, representing 80% of the $500.0 million divided by the August 7, 2026 closing trading price of the common stock of $32.27 per share. The final number of shares we will repurchase and the average price per share of such repurchases will be determined on the completion of the transactions contemplated by the ASR Agreement and will be based on the average of the daily volume-weighted average price of the common stock during the term of the ASR Agreement, less a discount and subject to adjustments pursuant to the ASR Agreement. At settlement, the Dealer may be required to deliver additional shares of common stock to the Company, or under certain circumstances, the Company may be required to deliver shares of common stock or to make a cash payment, at its election, to the Dealer. The final settlement of the transactions is scheduled to occur prior to the end of the second quarter of 2027.
Increase in Share Repurchase Authorization. On November 3, 2026, the BOD authorized an increase to its existing share repurchase program pursuant to which CoreCivic may purchase up to an additional $500.0 million of CoreCivic’s outstanding common stock. As a result of the increase, the aggregate authorization under CoreCivic’s repurchase program increased from $1.2 billion of CoreCivic’s outstanding common stock to $1.7 billion of CoreCivic’s outstanding common stock. Including the additional authorization, we have $755.8 million of repurchase authorization available under the share repurchase program, before any additional repurchase settlements occur under the ASR Agreement. Additional repurchases of common stock will be made in accordance with applicable securities laws and may be made at management’s discretion within parameters set by the BOD from time to time in the open market, through privately negotiated transactions, accelerated share repurchases or otherwise, subject to restricted payment limitations in our debt agreements. The share repurchase program currently has no time limit and, other than the ASR Agreement, does not obligate us to purchase any particular amount of our common stock. The authorization for the share repurchase program may be terminated, suspended, increased or decreased by the BOD in its discretion at any time.
Debt Repayments. As previously announced, during the third quarter of 2026, a portion of the net proceeds from the facility sales was used to pay down the then outstanding balances under our Bank Credit Facility, including all of the $270.0 million outstanding on the Revolving Credit Facility, which has a borrowing capacity of $575.0 million and can be redrawn, the $100.0 million outstanding balance of the Incremental Term Loan, and the $107.8 million outstanding balance on the Initial Term Loan. Additionally, we redeemed in full the 4.75% senior unsecured notes issued in October 2017 with an original principal amount of $250.0 million (2027 Notes) that remained outstanding, which amounted to $238.5 million, on August 12, 2026 (Redemption Date). The 2027 Notes were otherwise scheduled to mature on October 15, 2027. The 2027 Notes were redeemed at a redemption price, including the applicable make-whole premium, equal to $1,002.26 per $1,000.00 of the principal amount of the then outstanding 2027 Notes, or approximately $239.0 million, plus accrued and unpaid interest to, but not including, the Redemption Date. We reported charges totaling $4.0 million associated with these debt repayments. Following these debt repayments, the Company’s total debt outstanding as of September 30, 2026 was $629.6 million.

Other Business Developments
CEO Transition. On September 24, 2026, the BOD appointed Lucibeth N. Mayberry, who most recently served as the Company’s Executive Vice President and Chief Strategy Officer, as President and Chief Executive Officer of the Company. Patrick D. Swindle stepped down as President and Chief Executive Officer due to health reasons. Mr. Swindle had served as President and Chief Executive Officer since January 1, 2026, following his appointment as President and Chief Operating Officer on January 1, 2025. In addition, Mr. Swindle resigned from CoreCivic’s Board, and Ms. Mayberry was appointed to fill the vacancy.
Activation of the Appleton Facility. On August 3, 2026, we were awarded a new management contract with ICE to utilize the Appleton Facility. The new contract commenced on August 11, 2026, for a term of five years. The agreement provides for a fixed monthly payment plus an incremental per diem payment based on detainee populations. Taking into account start-up activities and the phased commencement of intake operations, we currently expect an immaterial impact to earnings for the remainder of 2026. Once the facility is fully activated, we expect this facility to generate total annual revenue of approximately $75.0 million. We expect to begin receiving detainees in the fourth quarter of 2026, with the activation estimated to be complete in the second quarter of 2027.

About CoreCivic
CoreCivic is a diversified, government-solutions company with the scale and experience needed to solve tough government challenges in flexible, cost-effective ways. We provide a broad range of solutions to government partners that help build safer, healthier, and more productive communities one person at a time through residential corrections, detention, and reentry management, complementary service offerings to the corrections industry that include pharmaceutical, transportation, and alternatives to incarceration, and government real estate solutions. We are the nation’s largest owner of partnership correctional, detention and residential reentry facilities, and one of the largest operators of such facilities in the United States. We have been a flexible and dependable partner for government for more than 40 years. Our employees are driven by a deep sense of service, high standards of professionalism and a responsibility to help government better the public good. Learn more at www.corecivic.com.

NOTE TO SUPPLEMENTAL FINANCIAL INFORMATION
Adjusted Net Income, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, FFO, and Normalized FFO, and, where appropriate, their corresponding per share metrics are non-GAAP financial measures. The Company believes that these measures are important operating measures that supplement discussion and analysis of the Company’s results of operations and are used to review and assess operating performance of the Company and its properties and their management teams. The Company believes that it is useful to provide investors, securities analysts, and other interested parties disclosures of its results of operations on the same basis that is used by management.
FFO, in particular, is a widely accepted non-GAAP supplemental measure of performance of real estate companies, grounded in the standards for FFO established by the National Association of Real Estate Investment Trusts (NAREIT). NAREIT defines FFO as net income computed in accordance with GAAP, excluding gains (or losses) from sales of property and extraordinary items, plus depreciation and amortization of real estate and impairment of depreciable real estate and after adjustments for unconsolidated partnerships and joint ventures calculated to reflect funds from operations on the same basis. As a company with extensive real estate holdings, we believe FFO and FFO per share are important supplemental measures of our operating performance and believe they are frequently used by securities analysts, investors and other interested parties in the evaluation of REITs and other real estate operating companies, many of which present FFO and FFO per share when reporting results. EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, and FFO are useful as supplemental measures of performance of the Company’s properties because such measures do not take into account depreciation and amortization, or with respect to EBITDA, the impact of the Company’s tax provisions and financing strategies. Because the historical cost accounting convention used for real estate assets requires depreciation (except on land), this accounting presentation assumes that the value of real estate assets diminishes at a level rate over time. Because of the unique structure, design and use of the Company’s properties, management believes that assessing performance of the Company’s properties without the impact of depreciation or amortization is useful. The Company may make adjustments to FFO from time to time for certain other income and expenses that it considers non-recurring, infrequent or unusual, even though such items may require cash settlement, because such items do not reflect a necessary or ordinary component of the ongoing operations of the Company. Normalized FFO excludes the effects of such items. The Company calculates Adjusted Net Income by adding to GAAP Net Income expenses associated with the Company’s debt repayments and refinancing transactions, and certain impairments and other charges that the Company believes are unusual or non-recurring, and by deducting the gain on sale of real estate assets and certain other gains, which the Company believes are not indicative of its ongoing operating performance to provide an alternative measure of comparing operating performance for the periods presented.
Other companies may calculate Adjusted Net Income, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, FFO, and Normalized FFO differently than the Company does, or adjust for other items, and therefore comparability may be limited. Adjusted Net Income, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, FFO, and Normalized FFO and, where appropriate, their corresponding per share measures are not measures of performance under GAAP, and should not be considered as an alternative to cash flows from operating activities, a measure of liquidity or an alternative to net income as indicators of the Company’s operating performance or any other measure of performance derived in accordance with GAAP. This data should be read in conjunction with the Company’s consolidated financial statements and related notes included in its filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission.
The Company’s operating and reportable segments are CoreCivic Residential, CoreCivic Services, and CoreCivic Properties. The operating performance of each segment is measured based on its net operating income, which the Company defines as a reportable segment’s revenue less operating expenses. Segment net operating income is the measure used by the Company’s chief operating decision maker to assess segment performance and allocate resources, and is presented in accordance with Accounting Standards Codification Topic 280, Segment Reporting (ASC 280).
