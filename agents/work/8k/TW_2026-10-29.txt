DISCUSSION OF RESULTS
Rates – Revenues of $324.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased 18.2% compared to prior year period (increased 18.9% on a constant currency basis). Rates ADV was up 33.8% from prior year period. U.S. government bond ADV was up 28.0% from prior year period, driven by robust institutional and wholesale activity, with September 2026 marking the most active month for U.S. government bond trading on our platform this year. Swaps/swaptions ≥ 1-year ADV was up 42.4% from prior year period, driven by global central bank policy changes, continued geopolitical tensions and uncertainty surrounding inflation. European government bonds ADV was up 24.5% from prior year period led by strong volumes across our institutional client channel and a growing number of clients trading across a diverse set of trading protocols. Mortgages ADV was down 3.6% from prior year period, against exceptionally strong prior-year performance. The 10.5% decline in rates derivatives ≥ 1-year average variable fees per million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to prior year period was primarily driven by higher compression activity which carries a comparatively lower fee per million as well as lower duration.
Credit – Revenues of $133.0 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased 9.6% compared to prior year period (increased 10.0% on a constant currency basis). U.S. credit ADV was up 23.9% from prior year period, as client adoption continued across trading protocols, most notably in Portfolio Trading ("PT"), Request-for-Quote ("RFQ") and Tradeweb AllTrade®. European credit ADV was up 8.4% from prior year period, driven by a diverse set of protocols, particularly Tradeweb Automated Intelligent Execution ("AiEX"), which reached a record in September 2026 as clients increasingly turned to automated execution. Credit derivatives ADV was up 61.3% from prior year period, driven by increased hedge fund and systematic account activity year-over-year ("YoY") which led to increased swap execution facility ("SEF") and multilateral trading facility ("MTF") credit default swaps activity. Municipal bond ADV was up 9.7% from prior year period, relative to the broader market which was up 13% from prior year period, based on the Municipal Securities Rulemaking Board ("MSRB"), driven by elevated new issuance which Tradeweb does not participate in. We reported 19.9% share of fully electronic U.S. high grade TRACE(3), up 130 basis points (bps) from prior year period and 7.9% share of fully electronic U.S. high yield TRACE, up 40 bps from prior year period. We also reported 27.9% total share of U.S. high grade TRACE, up 170 bps from prior year period and 9.9% total share of U.S. high yield TRACE, up 10 bps from prior year period. The 10.6% decline in cash credit average variable fees per million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to prior year period was primarily driven by a mix shift away from higher fee per million municipal bonds and retail U.S. credit and towards PT which carries a comparatively lower fee per million.
Equities – Revenues of $38.4 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased 28.8% compared to prior year period (increased 29.8% on a constant currency basis). Equities ADV was up 14.4% from prior year period, driven by robust activity in our institutional and wholesale channels, including strong activity in U.S. and international exchange traded funds ("ETFs"), which included an expanded client base and clients' increased adoption of Tradeweb AiEX YoY.
Money Markets – Revenues of $44.2 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased 3.0% compared to prior year period (increased 3.0% on a constant currency basis). Money Markets ADV was up 6.0% from prior year period, driven by strong global repurchase agreement ADV as a result of increased client activity across the platform, as well as Tradeweb ICD Portal activity, which was driven by continued activity from existing clients and new client additions. This was partially offset by less client demand for commercial paper and discount notes YoY.
Market Data – Revenues of $36.9 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased 19.6% compared to prior year period (increased 19.7% on a constant currency basis), primarily due to amendments to our LSEG market data license agreement which were effective as of November 2025. The amended market data license agreement included higher overall fees and a change in the timing of the delivery of periodic historical data sets, with more frequent deliveries scheduled under the amended agreement and a corresponding increase in revenue during the third quarter of 2026.
Other – Revenues of $7.6 million in the third quarter of 2026 decreased 17.3% compared to prior year period (decreased 17.3% on a constant currency basis), primarily due to lower digital asset revenue earned for performing validation services on the Canton Network, driven by scheduled changes in the Canton Network’s minting curve and its allocation of rewards to Super Validators and lower software development and implementation revenue on behalf of certain clients.
Operating Expenses of $331.1 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased 11.0% compared to $298.2 million in prior year period, primarily due to (i) an increase in employee compensation and benefits primarily due to an increase in incentive compensation expense tied to our financial performance and an increase in headcount and related salaries, benefits and stock-based compensation associated with our continued growth, (ii) an increase in technology and communication expense primarily due to increased investment in our artificial intelligence ("AI") and data strategy and infrastructure, as well as higher data and clearance fees driven primarily by higher trading volumes period-over-period and (iii) an increase in professional fees primarily due to higher technology consulting expenses and professional fees primarily related to mergers and acquisitions and other strategic investing activities.
Adjusted Expenses of $288.1 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased 13.9% (increased 15.1% on a constant currency basis) compared to $253.1 million in prior year period primarily due to (i) an increase in employee compensation and benefits primarily due to an increase in incentive compensation expense tied to our financial performance and an increase in headcount and related salaries, benefits and stock-based compensation associated with our continued growth and (ii) an increase in technology and communication expense due to increased investment in our AI and data strategy and infrastructure, as well as higher data and clearance fees driven primarily by higher trading volumes period-over-period. Please see "Non-GAAP Financial Measures" below for additional information.

ABOUT TRADEWEB MARKETS
Tradeweb Markets Inc. (Nasdaq: TW) is a leading, global operator of electronic marketplaces for rates, credit, equities and money markets. Founded in 1996, Tradeweb provides access to markets, data and analytics, electronic trading, straight-through-processing and reporting for more than 50 products to clients in the institutional, wholesale, retail and corporates markets. Advanced technologies developed by Tradeweb enhance price discovery, order execution and trade workflows while allowing for greater scale and helping to reduce risks in client trading operations. Tradeweb serves more than 3,000 clients in more than 85 countries. On average, Tradeweb facilitated more than $3.0 trillion in notional value traded per day over the past four fiscal quarters. We make use of non-GAAP financial measures in evaluating our past results and future prospects. We present these non-GAAP financial measures because we believe they assist investors and analysts in comparing our operating performance across reporting periods on a consistent basis by excluding items that we do not believe are indicative of our core operating performance.
Management and our board of directors use Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted EBIT margin to assess our financial performance and believe they are helpful in highlighting trends in our core operating performance, while other measures can differ significantly depending on long-term strategic decisions regarding capital structure, the tax jurisdictions in which we operate and capital investments. Further, our executive incentive compensation is based in part on components of Adjusted EBITDA.
We use Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted EPS as supplemental metrics to evaluate our business performance in a way that also considers our ability to generate profit without the impact of certain items. Each of the normal recurring adjustments and other adjustments included in Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted EPS help to provide management with a measure of our operating performance over time by removing items that are not related to day-to-day operations or are non-cash expenses.

We use Adjusted Expenses as a supplemental metric to evaluate our underlying operating performance over time by removing items that are not related to day-to-day operations or are non-cash expenses.

We present certain changes on a “constant currency” basis. We use constant currency change as a supplemental metric to evaluate our underlying performance between periods by removing the impact of foreign currency fluctuations. We present certain constant currency change information because we believe it provides investors and analysts a useful comparison of our results and trends between periods.
