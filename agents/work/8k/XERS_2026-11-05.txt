XERIS BIOPHARMA REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS AND BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS
Total revenue increased 44% year-over-year to $107 million
Recorlev net revenue increased 93% year-over-year to more than $71 million
Raises full-year 2026 total revenue guidance to $395 million to $405 million
Raises 2030 total revenue outlook to more than $1 billion, up from approximately $750 million
Conference call and webcast today at 8:30 a.m. ET
CHICAGO — November 5, 2026 – Xeris Biopharma Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: XERS), a leading endocrine and rare disease biopharmaceutical company committed to helping people realize better lives through the development and commercialization of innovative medicines, today announced its financial results and business highlights for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“As a biopharmaceutical leader in endocrine and rare diseases, our strong third quarter performance puts us on a new growth curve that is accelerating our Company’s near-term outlook, and we are now on a path to reach more than $1 billion in total revenue in 2030," said John Shannon, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Xeris. "This new trajectory reflects growth from the full enterprise. Net product revenue in the third quarter grew more than 43% year-over-year, led by Recorlev, where our expanded commercial team moved from build to yield and delivered record referrals and new patient starts. We now see Recorlev on a path to nearly $1 billion in annual net revenue three to four years sooner than previously expected.”
Shannon continued, “We also continued to advance XP-8121 following our comprehensive program overview in September. We remain on track to initiate Cornerstone, our pivotal Phase 3 study, by year-end. Given the strong momentum across Recorlev, Gvoke, and Keveyis, we are raising our full-year total revenue guidance to $395–$405 million.”

Key Business Highlights
•2028 Convertible Notes Fully Retired: On July 15, 2026, Xeris completed the retirement of all of its 8.00% Convertible Senior Notes due 2028 through a combination of cash and the issuance of shares of common stock, eliminating approximately $34 million of debt and approximately $3 million in annual interest expense. No 2028 Convertible Notes remain outstanding, and there were no charges related to the notes in the third quarter of 2026.
•XP-8121 Program Overview: On September 9, 2026, Xeris hosted a program overview detailing the comprehensive clinical development program for XP-8121, its investigational once-weekly subcutaneous levothyroxine for hypothyroidism. The pivotal Phase 3 Cornerstone study is designed to demonstrate that XP-8121 is therapeutically equivalent to daily oral levothyroxine and is expected to enroll 500 adults and adolescents with well-controlled hypothyroidism at more than 50 U.S. sites, randomized 1:1 for 54 weeks. The program also includes Capstone, a pediatric study; Touchstone, a study in patients with inconsistent thyroid control despite oral therapy; and a real-world evidence program. Xeris remains on track to initiate Cornerstone by year-end 2026, with topline data expected in late 2028 and potential approval and launch in 2030. A replay and accompanying materials are available under the “Events” section of the Xeris Investor Relations website.
•Third U.S. Patent Granted for XP-8121: On October 6, 2026, the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) granted Xeris U.S. Patent No. 12,751,937, entitled “Stable Levothyroxine Compositions In Aprotic Polar Solvents,” which is expected to expire on October 19, 2043. This is the third U.S. patent issued for XP-8121, the Company’s investigational drug product candidate for hypothyroidism.
•New Patent Issued Covering Keveyis: On October 13, 2026, the USPTO issued U.S. Patent No. 12,758,502 from U.S. Patent Application No. 17/151,405, entitled “Compositions and Methods of Use.” The claims cover the use of the Company’s KEVEYIS® (dichlorphenamide) product, and the patent is expected to provide intellectual property protection for Keveyis through at least 2043.
•Scientific Presentations: Xeris presented real-world data at the 2026 Breakthrough T1D Clinical & Research Congress on October 9, examining healthcare provider awareness of American Diabetes Association guidelines for prescribing ready-to-use glucagon versus actual prescribing practices. The presentation, titled “Ensuring Readiness: A Case For Proactive Glucagon Prescribing For Patients At Increased Risk of Severe Hypoglycemia,” highlighted the unmet clinical need and opportunity to increase adoption of ready-to-use glucagon products. At the 21st Annual Cardiometabolic Health Congress on October 15–16, Xeris presented a case study titled “Discontinuation of Antihypertensives and Insulin in a Patient with Difficult-to-Control Type 2 Diabetes and Hypertension After Treatment With Levoketoconazole,” demonstrating cortisol normalization accompanied by improvements in multiple cardiometabolic parameters.
•Menassie M. Taddese appointed to the Xeris Biopharma Board of Directors: Mr. Taddese joined the Xeris Board of Directors as an independent director, effective November 2, 2026. Mr. Taddese is an accomplished biopharmaceutical executive with nearly 30 years of global biopharmaceutical leadership experience, with a track record spanning commercial operations, corporate finance, business transformation and corporate governance.

Note Regarding Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release includes financial results prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (GAAP) and also certain historical non-GAAP financial measures, namely Adjusted EBITDA. This non-GAAP financial measure is not meant to be considered in isolation and should be read in conjunction with the Company's consolidated financial statements prepared in accordance with GAAP, and was not prepared under any comprehensive set of accounting rules or principles. Non-GAAP financial measures are not an alternative for financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP, and the calculation of the non-GAAP financial measure included herein may differ from similarly titled measures used by other companies. The Company believes that the presentation of Adjusted EBITDA, when viewed in conjunction with actual GAAP results, provides investors with a more meaningful understanding of the Company's ongoing and projected operating performance, exclusive of factors that do not directly affect what the Company considers to be its core operating performance, as well as unusual events. The Company believes this non-GAAP financial measure helps indicate underlying trends in the Company’s business and is important in comparing current results with prior period results and understanding expected operating performance. Also, management uses this non-GAAP financial measure to establish budgets and operational goals, and to manage the Company’s business and evaluate its performance. In addition, management believes that Adjusted EBITDA is important in evaluating the administrative costs of operating the Company’s business.
Adjusted EBITDA is GAAP net income (loss) before income tax (benefit) expense, less interest and other income, plus depreciation and amortization, interest expense, share-based compensation and debt refinancing fees or extinguishment losses.
About Xeris
Xeris Biopharma Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: XERS) is a leading endocrine and rare disease biopharmaceutical company committed to helping people realize better lives through the development and commercialization of innovative medicines for serious diseases with significant unmet medical need. Xeris has three commercially available products: Recorlev®, a cortisol synthesis inhibitor for the treatment of endogenous Cushing’s syndrome; Gvoke®, a ready-to-use liquid glucagon for the treatment of severe hypoglycemia; and Keveyis®, a proven therapy for primary periodic paralysis. Xeris also has a pipeline of development programs led by XP-8121, a Phase 3-ready, once-weekly subcutaneous injection for the treatment of hypothyroidism, as well as multiple early-stage programs leveraging Xeris’ technology platforms, XeriSol® and XeriJect®, for Xeris and its partners.
Xeris Biopharma Holdings is headquartered in Chicago, IL. For more information, visit www.xerispharma.com, or follow us on X, LinkedIn, or Instagram.
