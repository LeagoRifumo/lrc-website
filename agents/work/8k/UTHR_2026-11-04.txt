United Therapeutics Corporation Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
SILVER SPRING, Md. and RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK, N.C., November 4, 2026: United Therapeutics Corporation (Nasdaq: UTHR), a public benefit corporation, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Total revenues in the third quarter of 2026 increased by four percent year-over-year to $832.5 million, compared to $799.5 million in the third quarter of 2025.
“United Therapeutics is entering a period of extraordinary growth. We are advancing a portfolio of new therapies designed to exponentially increase the number of patients we can serve,” said Martine Rothblatt, Ph.D., Chairperson and Chief Executive Officer of United Therapeutics.
“Our record third quarter revenue performance reflects a business operating at a very high level, driven by strong commercial momentum and focused execution across our organization,” said Michael Benkowitz, President and Chief Operating Officer of United Therapeutics. “Our strategies are delivering, securing an increasingly strong position to leverage our commercial operations. With a durable and robust foundation combined with significant new opportunities across cardiopulmonary and respiratory medicine rapidly approaching, we believe we have the strongest growth outlook in our history.”

Interest income. Interest income was $33.7 million and $46.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively. The decrease in interest income was primarily due to a decrease in marketable investments due to the sale of securities to fund our three accelerated share repurchase agreements with Citibank, N.A. (Citi): two agreements executed in March 2026 to repurchase approximately $1.5 billion of our common stock (the March 2026 ASRs) and one agreement executed in September 2026 to repurchase approximately $477.6 million of our common stock (the September 2026 ASR).
4
Other income, net. Other income, net was $34.2 million and $6.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively. The increase in other income was primarily due to net realized and unrealized gains on equity securities.
Income tax expense. Income tax expense for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 was $114.2 million and $99.3 million, respectively. Our effective income tax rate (ETR) for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 was 31 percent and 23 percent, respectively. Our ETR for the three months ended September 30, 2026 increased compared to our ETR for the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily due to nondeductible IPR&D expense related to the acquisition of Thymmune, which was capitalized for tax purposes into the basis of the stock acquired.
Share repurchases. In March 2026, our Board of Directors approved a share repurchase program authorizing up to $2.0 billion in aggregate repurchases of our common stock. In connection with the repurchase program, we entered into three accelerated share repurchase agreements with Citi: the March 2026 ASRs and the September 2026 ASR. During the three months ended September 30, 2026, we received an additional 215,948 shares of our common stock upon final settlement of the second of the March 2026 ASRs and received an initial delivery of 719,376 shares of our common stock under the September 2026 ASR. The final settlement of the September 2026 ASR is expected to occur in the fourth quarter of 2026.
During the three months ended September 30, 2026, we adopted a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan and repurchased 45,070 shares of our common stock at an aggregate cost of $22.4 million under this share repurchase authorization. The September 2026 ASR utilized the remaining $477.6 million of the share repurchase program authorization, and no amount remained available under the share repurchase program authorized by our Board in March 2026.
During the three months ended September 30, 2026, we repurchased a total of 980,394 shares of our common stock that we currently hold as treasury stock in our consolidated balance sheets as of September 30, 2026.
In October 2026, our Board of Directors approved a share repurchase program authorizing up to $2.0 billion in aggregate repurchases of our common stock through October 21, 2027.

About United Therapeutics
Founded by CEO Martine Rothblatt to discover a cure for her daughter's life-threatening rare disease, pulmonary arterial hypertension, United Therapeutics transforms the treatment of rare diseases and pioneers alternatives to expand the supply of transplantable organs. From our innovative therapies to our groundbreaking manufactured organs, we are bold and unconventional. We move quickly from scientific theory to practical technologies that can save lives. As a public benefit corporation, even our legal structure reflects our commitments. We serve patients, act with integrity, create long-term shareholder value, and operate with sustainable practices that protect the future we are working to build.
