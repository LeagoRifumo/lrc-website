Graphic Packaging Holding Company Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Net Sales of $2,192 million were slightly higher year-over-year, with volumes up 1%.
•Net Income of $22 million; Adjusted EBITDA of $289 million, with Adjusted EBITDA margin expanding sequentially for the second consecutive quarter.
•Year-to-date September 2026 Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities of $295 million; Adjusted Cash Flow of $314 million increased $646 million from the first nine months of 2025 with significant contribution in Q3, enabling debt reduction from year-end 2025.
•Full-year 2026 expectations for Net Sales, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EPS of approximately $8.6 billion, $1.05 billion, and $0.65, respectively. Reaffirming full-year 2026 Adjusted Cash Flow of $600 to $700 million, an increase from $169 million in 2025.
ATLANTA, November 3, 2026 - Graphic Packaging Holding Company (NYSE: GPK) ("Graphic Packaging" or the "Company"), a global leader in sustainable consumer packaging, today reported third quarter 2026 results.
Net Sales in third quarter 2026 were $2,192 million, versus $2,190 million in third quarter 2025. Net Income was $22 million, or $0.07 per diluted share, versus Net Income of $142 million, or $0.48 per diluted share in third quarter 2025. Third quarter 2026 and 2025 Net Income were impacted by net charges from non-recurring and special items and amortization of purchased intangibles of $45 million and $30 million, respectively. Excluding non-recurring and special items and amortization of purchased intangibles, Adjusted Net Income for the third quarter of 2026 was $67 million, or $0.23 per diluted share, and $172 million, or $0.58 per diluted share in third quarter 2025.
President and CEO Robbert Rietbroek commented, “In the third quarter, we demonstrated progress on our near-term strategic priorities. We drove year-over-year revenue and volume growth and for the second consecutive quarter, expanded Adjusted EBITDA margin sequentially. Our strong relative performance despite elevated inflation reflects the resilience of our business and the benefits of our structural cost reduction and productivity initiatives. Importantly, these actions have created positive operating leverage and better position us to capture benefits from our commercial excellence initiatives and price improvements in the fourth quarter and into 2027. Our decisive actions to enhance and optimize the business this year support continued margin improvement, strong cash flow generation, and a return to EBITDA growth next year."
“Since the beginning of the year, we have strengthened our leadership team, sharpened our focus on commercial and operational excellence and delivered structural cost reductions and productivity improvements. I am proud of what our team has accomplished in a short period of time and the alignment we are creating between our strategy, investments and the most attractive market opportunities. We remain focused on delivering profitable growth, generating sustained cash flow and creating long-term value for shareholders.”
Financial and Operating Results
Net Sales
Third quarter 2026 Net Sales increased to $2,192 million, versus $2,190 million in the same quarter last year. The $2 million increase in Net Sales was driven by a 1% increase, or $19 million, in volume/mix, partially offset by a 1% decrease, or $15 million, of price and $2 million of unfavorable foreign exchange/other impact. Innovation Sales Growth in the second quarter was $42 million.
EBITDA
Third quarter 2026 EBITDA decreased 30% to $254 million from $361 million in the same quarter last year. Excluding the impact of business combinations and other non-recurring and special items, Adjusted EBITDA was $289 million versus $383 million in the same quarter last year. The $94 million decline in Adjusted EBITDA was driven by $83 million of increased commodity input and operating cost inflation, $15 million of unfavorable price impact, $3 million of lower volume/mix, partially offset by positive $2 million of Net Performance and $5 million of favorable foreign exchange. Third quarter Adjusted EBITDA Margin was 13.2% in 2026, down from 17.5% in the third quarter of 2025.
Other Results
Total Debt (Long-Term, Short-Term and Current Portion) was $5,520 million in third quarter 2026 compared to $5,592 million in fourth quarter 2025 and $5,688 million in second quarter 2026. Net Debt (Total Debt less Cash and Cash Equivalents) was $5,183 million in third quarter 2026, a decline of $148 million and $300 million, as compared to $5,331 million in fourth quarter 2025 and $5,483 million in second quarter 2026. The Company's third quarter 2026 Net Leverage Ratio was 4.8x compared to 3.8x in fourth quarter 2025.
Capital expenditures in third quarter 2026 were $64 million, versus $267 million in the same quarter last year.
The Company returned approximately $98 million to stockholders during the first nine months of 2026 through regular dividends.

About Graphic Packaging Holding Company
Graphic Packaging Holding Company (NYSE: GPK), headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, designs and produces consumer packaging made primarily from renewable or recycled materials. An industry leader in innovation, the Company is committed to reducing the environmental footprint of consumer packaging. Graphic Packaging operates a global network of design and manufacturing facilities serving the world's most widely recognized brands in food, beverage, foodservice, household, and other consumer products. Learn more at www.graphicpkg.com.
