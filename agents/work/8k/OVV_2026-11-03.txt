Ovintiv Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results
Track Record of Strong Profitability Continues, Significant Additions Made to Premium Return Drilling Inventory
Highlights:
•
Generated third quarter net earnings of $549 million, cash from operating activities of $1.1 billion, Non-GAAP Cash Flow of $1.2 billion and Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow of $680 million after capital expenditures of $563 million
•
Produced average third quarter volumes of 631 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day (“MBOE/d”), including oil and condensate volumes of 204 thousand barrels per day (“Mbbls/d”), along with 83 Mbbls/d of other NGLs (C2 to C4) and 2,062 million cubic feet per day (“MMcf/d”) of natural gas
•
Announced agreement to acquire Logan Energy Corp. for C$1.40 per share in cash for total transaction value of approximately $890 million (C$1.26 billion) including the assumption of Net Debt
•
Completed ground game acreage acquisitions in the third quarter totaling $457 million, adding approximately 120 net well locations in Montney, and approximately 120 net well locations in Permian for an average cost of $1.3 million to $1.7 million per 10,000 foot equivalent drilling location
•
Net Debt of $3.4 billion as of September 30, 2026, Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA of 0.7x
•
Returned approximately 69% ($467 million) of third quarter Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow to shareholders via share repurchases of approximately $386 million (6.3 million shares) and dividend payments of $81 million
•
Full year 2026 shareholder returns expected to exceed 60% of Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow
•
Maintained full year 2026 guidance, generating approximately 6% oil and condensate production per share growth over the last six months and on pace for 8% to 9% per share growth by year end
DENVER, November 3, 2026 – Ovintiv Inc. (NYSE, TSX: OVV) (“Ovintiv” or the “Company”) today announced its third quarter 2026 financial and operating results. The Company plans to hold a conference call and webcast at 8:00 a.m. MST (10:00 a.m. EST) on November 4, 2026. Please see dial-in details within this release, as well as additional details on the Company's website at www.ovintiv.com under Presentations and Events – Ovintiv.
“Our third quarter results demonstrate the ongoing commitment of our team to create value across the business, and we are on track to achieve record annual free cash flow,” said Ovintiv President and CEO, Brendan McCracken. “We have continued to enhance our drilling inventory and future growth optionality in both the Permian and the Montney at an industry leading low-cost per undeveloped location in the core of the best basins. Bolstering our positions in these two basins where we have an operating advantage and demonstrated top performance means we are set to continue delivering superior and durable returns."
Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results
•
Reported third quarter net earnings of $549 million, or $1.99 per share diluted
•
Recognized net gains on risk management in revenues of $27 million, before tax
•
Generated cash from operating activities of $1.1 billion and Non-GAAP Cash Flow of approximately $1.2 billion
•
Third quarter average total production volumes were approximately 631 MBOE/d, including 204 Mbbls/d of oil and condensate, 83 Mbbls/d of other NGLs (C2 to C4) and 2,062 MMcf/d of natural gas

•

Shareholder Returns
Ovintiv’s shareholder return framework commits to returning 50% to 100% of annual Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow to shareholders via the combination of base dividend payments and share buybacks.
Third quarter shareholder returns totaled approximately $467 million, or approximately 69% of Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow, consisting of share buybacks of approximately $386 million, or approximately 6.3 million shares of common stock, and base dividend payments of approximately $81 million.
As of September 30, 2026, year-to-date shareholder returns totaled approximately $1.1 billion, or approximately 53% of Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow, consisting of share buybacks of approximately $815 million, or approximately 13.9 million shares of common stock, and base dividend payments of approximately $250 million. Ovintiv expects full year 2026 shareholder returns to total more than 60% of Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow.

Continued Balance Sheet Focus
As of September 30, 2026, Ovintiv’s Net Debt was $3.4 billion and Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA was approximately 0.7 times. The Company had approximately $3.8 billion in total liquidity, which included available credit facilities of $3.5 billion, available uncommitted demand lines of $141 million, and cash and cash equivalents of $176 million.
During the quarter, Ovintiv repurchased in the open market approximately $109 million in principal amount of the Company’s senior notes.
Dividend Declared
On November 3, 2026, Ovintiv’s Board declared a quarterly dividend of $0.30 per share of common stock payable on December 31, 2026, to shareholders of record as of December 15, 2026.
Asset Highlights
Permian
Permian production averaged 235 MBOE/d (77% liquids) in the third quarter with 31 net wells turned in line. Full year 2026 capital investment is expected to total approximately $1.375 billion to $1.425 billion in the play to run approximately 5 rigs and bring on an expected 130 to 140 net wells. In the fourth quarter, oil and condensate production is expected to average approximately 125 Mbbls/d to 130 Mbbls/d and natural gas production is expected to average 300 to 320 MMcf/d.
Montney
Montney production averaged 395 MBOE/d (27% liquids) in the third quarter with 28 net wells turned in line. Full year 2026 capital investment is expected to total approximately $825 million to $875 million in the play to run approximately 6 rigs and bring on an expected 120 to 130 net wells. In the fourth quarter, oil and condensate production is expected to average 75 to 80 Mbbls/d, reflecting lower volumes due to higher than originally expected royalty rates as a result of higher commodity prices, and an extended third-party midstream outage in the play, and natural gas production is expected to average 1.7 to 1.8 billion cubic feet per day (“Bcf/d”).

NI 51-101 Exemption
The Canadian securities regulatory authorities have issued a decision document (the “Decision”) granting Ovintiv exemptive relief from the requirements contained in Canada’s National Instrument 51-101 Standards of Disclosure for Oil and Gas Activities (“NI 51-101”). As a result of the Decision, and provided that certain conditions set out in the Decision are met on an on-going basis, Ovintiv will not be required to comply with the Canadian requirements of NI 51-101 and the Canadian Oil and Gas Evaluation Handbook. The Decision permits Ovintiv to provide disclosure in respect of its oil and gas activities in the form permitted by, and in accordance with, the legal requirements imposed by the U.S. Securities Exchange Commission (“SEC”), the Securities Act of 1933, the Securities and Exchange Act of 1934, the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 and the rules of the NYSE. The Decision also provides that Ovintiv is required to file all such oil and gas disclosures with the Canadian securities regulatory authorities on www.sedarplus.ca as soon as practicable after such disclosure is filed with the SEC.
NOTE 1: Non-GAAP Measures
Certain measures in this news release do not have any standardized meaning as prescribed by U.S. GAAP and, therefore, are considered non-GAAP measures. These measures may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies and should not be viewed as a substitute for measures reported under U.S. GAAP. These measures are commonly used in the oil and gas industry and/or by Ovintiv to provide shareholders and potential investors with additional information regarding the Company’s liquidity and its ability to generate funds to finance its operations. For additional information regarding non-GAAP measures, see the Company’s website. This news release contains references to non-GAAP measures as follows:
•
Non-GAAP Cash Flow is a non-GAAP measure defined as cash from (used in) operating activities excluding net change in other assets and liabilities, and net change in non-cash working capital.
•
Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow is a non-GAAP measure defined as Non-GAAP Cash Flow in excess of capital expenditures, excluding net acquisitions and divestitures.
•
Non-GAAP Adjusted Earnings is a non-GAAP measure defined as net earnings (loss) excluding non-cash items that management believes reduce the comparability of the Company's financial performance between periods. These items may include, but are not limited to, unrealized gains/losses on risk management, impairments, non-operating foreign exchange gains/losses, and gains/losses on divestitures. Income taxes includes adjustments to normalize the effect of income taxes calculated using the estimated annual effective income tax rate. In addition, valuation allowances and the effect of non-recurring discrete transactions are excluded in the calculation of income taxes.
•
Net Debt is defined as long-term debt, including the current portion, less cash and cash equivalents.
Ovintiv Inc. | 8

•
Adjusted EBITDA, Debt to EBITDA, Debt to Adjusted EBITDA (Leverage Target/Ratio) and Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA are non-GAAP measures. EBITDA is defined as trailing 12-month net earnings (loss) before income taxes, depreciation, depletion and amortization, and interest. Adjusted EBITDA is EBITDA adjusted for impairments, accretion of asset retirement obligation, unrealized gains/losses on risk management, foreign exchange gains/losses, gains/losses on divestitures and other gains/losses. Debt to EBITDA is calculated as long-term debt, including the current portion, divided by EBITDA. Debt to Adjusted EBITDA is calculated as long-term debt, including the current portion, divided by Adjusted EBITDA. Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA is calculated as Net Debt, divided by Adjusted EBITDA. Debt to Adjusted EBITDA and Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA are non-GAAP measures monitored by management as indicators of the Company’s overall financial strength.
