Digital Turbine Reports Strong Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter Financial Results
Second Quarter Net Revenue Totaled $178.7 Million, Representing Year-over-Year Growth of 27%
Second Quarter GAAP Net Income of $10.3 Million and diluted GAAP EPS of $0.06; Second Quarter Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income1 of $23.4 Million and Non-GAAP Adjusted EPS1 of $0.18
Second Quarter Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA2 Totaled $39.8 Million, Representing Year-over-Year Growth of 46%
Austin, TX – November 9, 2026 – Digital Turbine, Inc. (Nasdaq: APPS) announced financial results for the fiscal second quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Recent Financial Highlights:
•Fiscal second quarter of 2027 revenue totaled $178.7 million, representing an increase of 27% year-over-year as compared to the fiscal second quarter of 2026.
•GAAP net income for the fiscal second quarter of 2027 was $10.3 million, or $0.06 per diluted share. Non-GAAP adjusted net income1 for the fiscal second quarter of 2027 was $23.4 million, or $0.18 per share, as compared to non-GAAP adjusted net income1 of $16.5 million, or $0.15 per share, in the fiscal second quarter of 2026.
•Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA2 for the fiscal second quarter of 2027 was $39.8 million, representing an increase of 46% year-over-year as compared to non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA2 of $27.2 million in the fiscal second quarter of 2026.
•Non-GAAP free cash flow3 totaled $10.5 million in the fiscal second quarter of 2027.
“Our strong second quarter results are further testament to the positive momentum underlying the business,” said Bill Stone, CE O. “Our team’s focused execution continues to accelerate the flywheel effect, whereby data- and AI-driven improvements are self-sustaining greater overall platform performance across all aspects of the business, which in turn is attracting higher levels of partner adoption and advertiser investment across the platform. The resultant 20+% revenue growth in both our ODS segment and AGP segment during the second quarter, along with operating leverage efficiencies, enabled us to achieve 46% adjusted EBITDA growth in the quarter. The stronger-than-expected second quarter performance and continuing flywheel momentum provide us with greater conviction that Digital Turbine has the right team, platform, market position, and vision to fully exploit the wealth of opportunities directly in front of us.”

About Digital Turbine, Inc.
Digital Turbine empowers superior mobile consumer experiences and results for the world’s leading telcos, advertisers, and publishers. Its end-to-end platform uniquely simplifies its partners’ abilities to supercharge awareness, acquisition, and monetization – connecting them with more consumers, in more ways, across more devices. Digital Turbine is headquartered in North America, with offices around the world. For additional information visit www.digitalturbine.com.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
To supplement the Company’s consolidated financial statements presented in accordance with GAAP, Digital Turbine uses non-GAAP measures of certain components of financial performance. These non-GAAP measures include non-GAAP adjusted net income and earnings per share (“EPS”), non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA, non-GAAP free cash flow and non-GAAP gross profit. Reconciliations to the nearest GAAP measures of all non-GAAP measures included in this press release can be found in the tables below.
Non-GAAP measures are provided to enhance investors’ overall understanding of the Company’s current financial performance, prospects for the future and as a means to evaluate period-to-period comparisons. The Company believes that these non-GAAP measures provide meaningful supplemental information regarding financial performance by excluding certain expenses and benefits that may not be indicative of recurring core business operating results. The Company believes the non-GAAP measures that exclude such items when viewed in conjunction with GAAP results and the accompanying reconciliations enhance the comparability of results against prior periods and allow for greater transparency of financial results. The Company believes non-GAAP measures facilitate management’s internal comparison of its financial performance to that of prior periods as well as trend analysis for budgeting and planning purposes. The presentation of non-GAAP measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP.

Revision of Prior Period Financial Statements
The Company has revised, during the current quarter, its previously issued financial statements as it identified immaterial adjustments resulting in incremental costs related to its contractual revenue share obligations that originated in prior periods. This resulted in an understatement of revenue share expense in the amount of $3.5 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026 and a corresponding understatement of its accrued revenue share liabilities. This adjustment did not impact the Company's previously reported net cash provided from operations or compliance with debt covenants and was not material to the previously reported interim results of operations. The adjusted financial statements for the impacted periods noted will be included in the Company’s Form 10-Q as of September 30, 2026.
