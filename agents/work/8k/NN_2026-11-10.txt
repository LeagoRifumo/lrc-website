NextNav Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Operational Highlights
RESTON, VA., November 10, 2026 -- NextNav Inc. (NASDAQ: NN) a leader in next generation positioning, navigation, and timing (PNT) and 3D geolocation, today reported its financial results and operational updates for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“The third quarter was productive on all fronts, marked by continued regulatory progress, important technical milestones, new partnerships, and perhaps most notably, highly visible market validation of the increasing value and strategic importance of low-band spectrum assets,” said Mariam Sorond, CEO of NextNav. “We continue to demonstrate the value of uniquely combining low-band spectrum with 5G-capable PNT and sensing technologies to support a growing range of mission-critical applications, including those serving national security, critical infrastructure and public safety.”
Operational Highlights
 On October 5, 2026, NextNav announced a strategic partnership with AiRANACULUS to advance integrated sensing and communications (ISAC) across its lower 900 MHz spectrum, expanding long-range, early warning counter-UAS detection while reducing the infrastructure needed for wide-area sensing coverage. Details can be found here.
 On September 22, 2026, NextNav announced that Marissa Mitrovich joined as Vice President of Government Affairs and Vijay Venkateswaran was appointed Chief Business Development Officer, strengthening the company’s policy engagement and business development leadership. Details can be found here.
 On September 15, 2026, NextNav announced real-world field validation, both indoors and outdoors, achieving single-digit-meter horizontal positioning accuracy using PRS signals transmitted from its 5G PNT Network in Santa Clara County, California. Details can be found here.
 On August 24, 2026, NextNav announced Tiami Networks as a sensing ecosystem partner to advance 5G-powered, counter unmanned aircraft system (UAS) detection solutions. Details can be found here.
Financial Highlights
· Balance Sheet: As of September 30, 2026, the Company held approximately $290.9 million in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term and long-term investments, and had no outstanding debt.

About NextNav Inc.
NextNav Inc. (Nasdaq: NN) is a leader in next-generation 3D Positioning, Navigation, and Timing (PNT) solutions. As the nation’s largest license holder in a spectrum band expressly designated for terrestrial positioning services, NextNav is uniquely positioned to enable a widescale terrestrial complement and backup to GPS. Leveraging licensed low-band spectrum and the global 5G ecosystem, NextNav is focused on delivering an accurate, reliable, and resilient 3D PNT solution to protect national security, public safety, and the economy. Learn more at www.nextnav.com.
For more information, please visit https://nextnav.com/ or follow NextNav on X at https://x.com/NextNav or LinkedIn at https://www.linkedin.com/company/nextnav/.
