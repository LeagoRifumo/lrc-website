Patria Reports Third Quarter 2026 Earnings Results Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands, November 3, 2026 – Patria Investments Limited (Nasdaq:PAX) reported today its unaudited results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The full detailed presentation of Patria's third quarter 2026 results can be accessed on the Shareholders section of Patria’s website at https://ir.patria.com/. “Our third quarter results demonstrate the strength and resilience of Patria’s increasingly diversified platform. We raised $2.4 billion in the quarter and $6.9 billion year to date, effectively reaching our full- year fundraising target one quarter ahead of schedule, and we are on track for another record year.” said Alex Saigh, Chief Executive Officer at Patria. “Our differentiated investment performance, with over 85% of our Fee-Earning AuM, excluding SMAs and third-party managed funds, in funds at or above their benchmarks since inception, and our ability to design tailored solutions for the world’s leading institutional investors are driving this momentum. With Fee-Related Earnings per share up 16% year to date and $4.8 billion in pending Fee-Earning AuM, we are confident in delivering on our 2026 guidance and in our path toward our 2027 targets.” Financial Highlights (reported in $ USD) IFRS results included $7.2 million of net income attributable to Patria in Q3 2026. Patria generated Fee Related Earnings of $52.8 million in Q3 2026, up 7% from $49.5 million in Q3 2025, with an FRE margin of 53.7%. Distributable Earnings were $46.9 million for Q3 2026, or $0.30 per share. Dividends Patria declared a quarterly dividend of $0.1625 per share to record holders of common stock at the close of business on November 13th, 2026. This dividend will be paid on December 11th, 2026. Conference Call Patria will host its third quarter 2026 earnings conference call via public webcast on November 3rd, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. ET. To register and join, please use the following link: https://edge.media-server.com/mmc/p/m9aemh23/lan/en/ For those unable to listen to the live broadcast, there will be a webcast replay on the Shareholders section of Patria’s website at https://ir.patria.com/ shortly after the call’s completion.
