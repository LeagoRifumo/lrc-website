Trulieve Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
•Third quarter revenue of $230 million, with 64% gross margin
•Cash flow from operations of $183 million and free cash flow of $120 million* in first nine months of 2026
•Received final DEA registrations pursuant to final order rescheduling state licensed medical marijuana to Schedule III under the Controlled Substances Act
Tallahassee, FL – November 5, 2026 – Trulieve Cannabis Corp. (NYSE: TRLV) (“Trulieve” or “the Company”), a leading and top-performing medical cannabis company in the U.S., today announced its results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Results are reported in accordance with U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP), unless otherwise indicated. Numbers may not sum perfectly due to rounding.
Q3 2026 Financial and Operational Highlights*
•Revenue of $230 million, with 95% of revenue from retail sales.
•Achieved gross margin of 64%, with gross profit of $148 million.
•Reported positive net income attributable to common shareholders of $1 million. Adjusted net income of $18 million* excludes non-recurring charges, asset impairments, disposals, and unconsolidated entity.
•Achieved adjusted EBITDA of $89 million*, or 39% of revenue.
•Generated cash flow from operations of $74 million and free cash flow of $45 million*.
•Cash at quarter end was $327 million.
•Realized seven-fold increase in traffic in Georgia following medical cannabis program expansion on July 1 with new product launches including Modern Flower and Roll One.
•Completed corporate domestication to Delaware from British Columbia.
•Rang The Closing Bell at the New York Stock Exchange to commemorate NYSE listing.
•Added to the S&P Total Market Index (TMI), S&P Completion Index (CI) and Morningstar Total Stock Market Index.
•Closed perpetual royalty free license for Alien Labs and Connected branded cannabis products in Florida and Texas that includes genetics, trademarks, and proprietary know-how and a right of first refusal for exclusive licensing rights in additional expansion markets.
•Opened two dispensaries in Marco Island, Florida, and Dunwoody, Georgia.
*See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below for additional information and a reconciliation to GAAP for Non-GAAP metrics.
Recent Developments
•Received final registrations from the Drug Enforcement Agency for state licensed medical marijuana dispensaries as part of federal regulatory framework for qualified medical marijuana businesses under the Controlled Substances Act.
•Repaid $65 million mortgage notes payable ahead of maturity in 2028 secured by cultivation site in Jefferson County, Florida to reduce leverage and interest expense.
•Currently operate 208 retail dispensaries and 3.5 million square feet of cultivation and processing capacity in the United States.
Management Commentary
"State licensed medical marijuana rescheduling unlocked major milestones including DEA registration, removal of 280E tax, and uplisting to the NYSE,” said Kim Rivers, Trulieve CEO. “Third quarter results demonstrate the strength of our medical cannabis platform underscored by revenue, margins, and cash generation. With meaningful growth expansion opportunities in Georgia and Texas, we are in the early stages of an accelerated growth cycle.”

Non-GAAP Financial Measures (Unaudited)
Our management uses financial measures that are not in accordance with GAAP, in addition to financial measures in accordance with GAAP to evaluate our operating results. These non-GAAP financial measures should be considered supplemental to, and not a substitute for, our reported financial results prepared in accordance with GAAP. EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, EBITDA margin, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income (loss), adjusted net income (loss) per diluted share, and free cash flow are financial measures that are not defined under GAAP. Our management uses these non-GAAP financial measures and believes they enhance an investor’s understanding of our financial and operating performance from period to period because they exclude certain material non-cash items and certain other adjustments management believes are not reflective of our ongoing operations and performance. These non-GAAP measures are not a measure of liquidity, cash generated by the Company, or cash available for discretionary expenditures.
EBITDA is calculated as net income (loss) before net interest expense, interest income, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization; adjusted EBITDA is calculated as net income (loss) before net interest expense, interest income, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization and also excludes certain contributions, such as cash expenditures related to campaign and political initiatives, items that we do not believe represent the operations of the core business such as cash and non-cash acquisition, disposition and specified transaction costs and certain other cash and non-cash costs including major system changes, impairments and disposals of long-lived assets including goodwill, discontinued operations, share-based compensation, other income and expense items, equity in net loss of Harvest and loss on Deconsolidation Transaction; EBITDA margin is calculated as EBITDA as % of revenue; adjusted EBITDA margin is calculated as adjusted EBITDA as % of revenue; adjusted net income (loss) is calculated as net income (loss) less certain contributions, such as cash expenditures related to campaign and political initiatives, items that we do not believe represent the operations of the core business such as cash and non-cash acquisition, disposition and specified transaction costs and certain other cash and non-cash costs including major system changes, impairments and disposals of long-lived assets including goodwill, discontinued operations, share-based compensation, other income and expense items, equity in net loss of Harvest and loss on Deconsolidation Transaction; adjusted EPS is calculated as adjusted net income (loss) divided by diluted shares outstanding; and free cash flow is calculated as cash flow from operations less capital expenditures. Some of the items added back to, or removed from, these non-GAAP measures have occurred in prior periods and may occur in future periods.
We report these non-GAAP measures to help investors assess the operating performance of the Company’s business. The financial measures noted above are metrics that have been adjusted from GAAP net income (loss), net income (loss) per share, and cash flow from operating activities in an effort to provide readers with normalized metrics in making comparisons more meaningful across the cannabis industry as competitors commonly disclose similar performance measures; however, our calculation of EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, EBITDA margin, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income (loss), adjusted net income (loss) per diluted share, and free cash flow may not be comparable to other similarly titled performance measures of other companies, as well as to remove items that may otherwise distort the GAAP net income (loss), net income (loss) per share or cash flow from operating activities measure.
As noted above, our EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, EBITDA margin, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income (loss), adjusted net income (loss) per diluted share, and free cash flow measures are not prepared in accordance with GAAP, and should not be considered in isolation of, or as an alternative to, measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. There are a number of limitations related to the use of these non-GAAP measures rather than net income (loss), net income (loss) per share and cash flow from operating activities, which are the most directly comparable financial measures calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Because of these limitations, we consider, and you should consider, these non-GAAP measures together with other operating and financial performance measures presented in accordance with GAAP. A reconciliation of these non-GAAP measures to the most directly comparable financial measure calculated in accordance with GAAP can be found below.

www.trulieve.com 3 Management’s Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures Our management uses financial measures that are not in accordance with GAAP, in addition to financial measures in accordance with GAAP to evaluate our operating results. These non-GAAP financial measures should be considered supplemental to, and not a substitute for, our reported financial results prepared in accordance with GAAP. Adjusted SG&A, adjusted SG&A margin, EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, EBITDA margin, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income (loss), adjusted net income (loss) per diluted share, and free cash flow are financial measures that are not defined under GAAP. Our management uses these non-GAAP financial measures and believes they enhance an investor’s understanding of our financial and operating performance from period to period because they exclude certain material non-cash items and certain other adjustments management believes are not reflective of our ongoing operations and performance. These non-GAAP measures are not a measure of liquidity, cash generated by the Company, or cash available for discretionary expenditures. Adjusted SG&A is calculated as SG&A less extraordinary expenses; adjusted SG&A margin is calculated as SG&A as % of revenue; EBITDA is calculated as net income (loss) before net interest expense, interest income, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization; adjusted EBITDA is calculated as net income (loss) before net interest expense, interest income, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization and also excludes certain contributions, such as cash expenditures related to campaign and political initiatives, items that we do not believe represent the operations of the core business such as cash and non-cash acquisition, disposition and specified transaction costs and certain other cash and non-cash costs including major system changes, impairments and disposals of long-lived assets including goodwill, discontinued operations, share-based compensation, other income and expense items, equity in net loss of Harvest and loss on Deconsolidation Transaction; EBITDA margin is calculated as EBITDA as % of revenue; adjusted EBITDA margin is calculated as adjusted EBITDA as % of revenue; adjusted net income (loss) is calculated as net income (loss) less certain contributions, such as cash expenditures related to campaign and political initiatives, items that we do not believe represent the operations of the core business such as cash and non-cash acquisition, disposition and specified transaction costs and certain other cash and non-cash costs including major system changes, impairments and disposals of long-lived assets including goodwill, discontinued operations, share-based compensation, other income and expense items, equity in net loss of Harvest and loss on Deconsolidation Transaction; adjusted EPS is calculated as adjusted net income (loss) divided by diluted shares outstanding; and free cash flow is calculated as cash flow from operations less capital expenditures. Some of the items added back to, or removed from, these non-GAAP measures have occurred in prior periods and may occur in future periods. We report these non-GAAP measures to help investors assess the operating performance of the Company’s business. The financial measures noted above are metrics that have been adjusted from GAAP SG&A, net income (loss), net income (loss) per share, and cash flow from operating activities in an effort to provide readers with normalized metrics in making comparisons more meaningful across the cannabis industry as competitors commonly disclose similar performance measures; however, our calculation of adjusted SG&A, adjusted SG&A margin, EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, EBITDA margin, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income (loss), adjusted net income (loss) per diluted share, and free cash flow may not be comparable to other similarly titled performance measures of other companies, as well as to remove items that may otherwise distort the GAAP SG&A, net income (loss), net income (loss) per share or cash flow from operating activities measure. As noted above, our adjusted SG&A, adjusted SG&A margin, EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, EBITDA margin, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income (loss), adjusted net income (loss) per diluted share, and free cash flow measures are not prepared in accordance with GAAP, and should not be considered in isolation of, or as an alternative to, measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. There are a number of limitations related to the use of these non-GAAP measures rather than SG&A, net income (loss), net income (loss) per share and cash flow from operating activities, which are the most directly comparable financial measures calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Because of these limitations, we consider, and you should consider, these non-GAAP measures together with other operating and financial performance measures presented in accordance with GAAP.
