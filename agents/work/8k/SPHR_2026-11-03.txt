SPHERE ENTERTAINMENT CO. REPORTS
THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
NEW YORK, N.Y., November 5, 2026 - Sphere Entertainment Co. (NYSE: SPHR) (“Sphere Entertainment” or the “Company”) today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Recent highlights for the Company’s Sphere segment include:
•In late September, the Company debuted enhanced and expanded 4D effects for The Wizard of Oz at Sphere, and announced that the production had exceeded half a billion dollars in ticket sales with over four million tickets sold since its debut in August 2025, while the production also surpassed its 1,000th showing in early October;
•Production is underway on The Rocky Horror Picture Show at Sphere, a new Sphere Experience, which the Company expects will debut by the end of March 2027;
•In October, Metallica began a new concert residency at Sphere, with 24 concerts planned through March 2027;
•Backstreet Boys and the Eagles have continued to extend their long-running residencies – now up to 63 and 74 planned nights, respectively – while Oprah Winfrey and John Mayer each announced new residencies for 2027; and
•The Company announced a new multi-year sponsorship agreement with Lyft.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported revenues of $274.3 million, an increase of $11.7 million, or 4%, as compared to the prior year quarter. In addition, the Company reported an operating loss of $240.6 million, an increase of $77.5 million, or 47%, and adjusted operating income of $39.2 million, an increase of $2.8 million, or 8%, both as compared to the prior year quarter.(1)
Executive Chairman and CEO James L. Dolan said, “With today’s results, our Sphere segment remains on track to deliver substantial growth this calendar year. Looking ahead, we continue to make progress on our long-term vision for Sphere, including a global network of venues and a growing slate of original content.”

Sphere
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Sphere segment reported revenues of $198.9 million, an increase of $24.8 million, or 14%, as compared to the prior year quarter.
Revenues related to The Sphere Experience increased $13.1 million as compared to the prior year quarter, which reflected an increase in the number of overall performances and, to a lesser extent, higher per-show revenue. In the current year quarter, The Sphere Experience reflected 241 performances of The Wizard of Oz at Sphere as compared to a combined 220 performances of The Wizard of Oz at Sphere, Postcard from Earth and V-U2 An Immersive Concert Film in the prior year quarter. The Wizard of Oz at Sphere debuted on August 28, 2025 in the prior year quarter.
Revenues from sponsorship, Exosphere advertising and suite license fees increased $6.2 million as compared to the prior year quarter due to higher Exosphere advertising revenues and, to a lesser extent, higher sponsorship revenues and suite license fee revenues.
Event-related revenues increased $6.1 million as compared to the prior year quarter, primarily due to higher revenues from concerts. The increase in revenues from concerts primarily reflects the impact of three additional concert residency shows held at Sphere in Las Vegas during the current year quarter.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Sphere segment had direct operating expenses of $83.8 million, an increase of $5.1 million, or 6%, as compared to the prior year quarter. This increase was primarily due to higher expenses associated with The Sphere Experience of $7.6 million as compared to the prior year quarter, which reflected higher per-show expenses and an increase in the number of overall performances. As mentioned above, in the current year quarter, The Sphere Experience solely reflected performances of The Wizard of Oz at Sphere as compared to showings of The Wizard of Oz at Sphere, Postcard from Earth and V-U2 An Immersive Concert Film in the prior year quarter.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, selling, general and administrative expenses of $98.3 million increased $5.6 million, or 6%, as compared to the prior year quarter, primarily due to (i) higher employee compensation and related benefits and (ii) other cost increases, partially offset by (iii) the impact of mark-to-market adjustments on certain share-based compensation awards as a result of the decrease in the Company’s stock price during the current year quarter.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, operating loss of $67.0 million improved by $17.5 million, or 21%, as compared to the prior year quarter, primarily due to the increase in revenues, partially offset by higher selling, general and administrative expenses and direct operating expenses. Adjusted operating income of $32.4 million increased $15.3 million, or 90%, as compared to the prior year quarter, primarily due to the increase in revenues, partially offset by higher direct operating expenses and selling, general and administrative expenses (excluding share-based compensation).
MSG Networks
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the MSG Networks segment reported total revenues of $75.4 million, a decrease of $13.0 million, or 15%, as compared to the prior year quarter.
Distribution revenue decreased $12.8 million, primarily reflecting a decrease in total subscribers of approximately 15.5%.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, direct operating expenses of $59.3 million increased $1.0 million, or 2%, as compared to the prior year quarter.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, selling, general and administrative expenses of $10.0 million increased $3.0 million, or 43%, as compared to the prior year quarter. This increase was primarily due to (i) higher employee compensation and related benefits of $3.5 million and (ii) other cost increases, partially offset by (iii) lower advertising and marketing costs of $1.6 million.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, operating loss of $173.6 million increased $94.9 million as compared to the prior year quarter, primarily due to an increase in impairments and other losses(3) and, to a lesser extent, the decrease in revenues. Adjusted operating income of $6.8 million decreased $12.5 million as compared to the prior year quarter, primarily due to the decrease in revenues.

About Sphere Entertainment Co.
Sphere Entertainment Co. is a leader in immersive experiences, technology and media. The Company includes Sphere, an experiential medium powered by advanced technologies. The first Sphere opened in Las Vegas, with plans also announced for Sphere venues in Abu Dhabi and National Harbor. In addition, the Company includes MSG Networks, which operates two regional sports and entertainment networks, MSG Network and MSG Sportsnet, as well as a direct-to-consumer and authenticated streaming product, MSG+, delivering a wide range of live sports content and other programming. More information is available at www.sphereentertainmentco.com.
Non-GAAP Financial Measures
We define adjusted operating income (loss), which is a non-GAAP financial measure, as operating income (loss) before (i) depreciation, amortization and impairments of property and equipment, goodwill and intangible assets, (ii) amortization for capitalized cloud computing arrangement costs, (iii) share-based compensation expense, (iv) restructuring charges or credits, (v) merger, debt work-out and acquisition-related costs, including merger-related litigation expenses, net of insurance recoveries, (vi) gains or losses on sales or dispositions of businesses and associated settlements, (vii) the impact of purchase accounting adjustments related to business acquisitions, and (viii) gains and losses related to the remeasurement of liabilities under the Company’s Executive Deferred Compensation Plan. We believe that the exclusion of share-based compensation expense or benefit allows investors to better track the performance of our business without regard to the settlement of an obligation that is not expected to be made in cash. We eliminate merger, debt work-out and acquisition-related costs, including merger related litigation expenses, net of insurance recoveries, when applicable, because the Company does not consider such costs to be indicative of the ongoing operating performance of the Company as they result from an event that is of a non-recurring nature, thereby enhancing comparability. In addition, management believes that the exclusion of gains and losses related to the remeasurement of liabilities under the Company’s Executive Deferred Compensation Plan, provides investors with a clearer picture of the Company’s operating performance given that, in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), gains and losses related to the remeasurement of liabilities under the Company’s Executive Deferred Compensation Plan are recognized in operating income (loss) whereas gains and losses related to the remeasurement of the assets under the Company’s Executive Deferred Compensation Plan, which are equal to and therefore fully offset the gains and losses related to the remeasurement of liabilities, are recognized in other income (expense), net, which is not reflected in operating income (loss).
We believe adjusted operating income (loss) is an appropriate measure for evaluating the operating performance of our business segments and the Company on a consolidated basis. Adjusted operating income (loss) and similar measures with similar titles are common performance measures used by investors and analysts to analyze our performance. Internally, we use revenues and adjusted operating income (loss) as the most important indicators of our business performance, and evaluate management’s effectiveness with specific reference to these indicators. Adjusted operating income (loss) should be viewed as a supplement to and not a substitute for operating income (loss), net income (loss), cash flows from operating activities, and other measures of performance and/or liquidity presented in accordance with GAAP. Since adjusted operating income (loss) is not a measure of performance calculated in accordance with GAAP, this measure may not be comparable to similar measures with similar titles used by other companies. For a reconciliation of operating income (loss) to adjusted operating income (loss), please see page 5 of this release.
