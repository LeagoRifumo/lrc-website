FINANCIAL NEWS
Cirrus Logic Reports Fiscal Second Quarter Revenue of $559.5 Million
AUSTIN, Texas – November 4, 2026 – Cirrus Logic, Inc. (Nasdaq: CRUS) posted on its website at investor.cirrus.com the quarterly Shareholder Letter that contains the complete financial results for the second quarter of fiscal year 2027, which ended September 26, 2026, as well as the company’s current business outlook.
“Cirrus Logic delivered solid results in the September quarter primarily driven by strong demand and increased content shipping into smartphones,” said John Forsyth, Cirrus Logic president and chief executive officer. “During the quarter we were pleased to see our custom components featured in newly launched smartphones, in addition to broader adoption of our third-generation camera controller in these devices. We also made progress on new product development as we began sampling our analog front-end components for smart metering and taped out both our fourth-generation camera controller for smartphones and second-generation smart codec for AI-enabled PCs. With an extensive roadmap and strong pipeline of opportunities, we remain confident in our ability to grow long-term shareholder value.”
Reported Financial Results – Second Quarter FY27
•Revenue of $559.5 million;
•GAAP gross margin of 53.7 percent and non-GAAP gross margin of 53.8 percent;
•GAAP operating expenses of $168.2 million and non-GAAP operating expenses of $141.6 million; and
•GAAP earnings per share of $2.23 and non-GAAP earnings per share of $2.70.
A reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP financial information is included in the tables accompanying this press release.
Business Outlook – Third Quarter FY27
•Revenue is expected to range between $510 million and $570 million;
•GAAP gross margin is forecasted to be between 51 percent and 53 percent; and
•Combined GAAP R&D and SG&A expenses are anticipated to range between $184 million and $190 million, including approximately $21 million in stock-based compensation expense, $9 million in acquisition-related costs, and $2 million in amortization of acquisition intangibles, resulting in a non-GAAP operating expense range between $152 million and $158 million.
Share Repurchase Authorization
The company also announced that in October 2026 its Board of Directors authorized the repurchase of up to an additional $500 million of the company's common stock. The repurchases are expected to be funded from working capital and anticipated cash flow from operations and may occur from time to time depending on a variety of factors, including general market and economic conditions and other corporate considerations. Repurchases may be effected through a variety of means, including open market purchases, privately negotiated transactions, 10b5-1 plans, or other means, in accordance with applicable securities laws. The company is not obligated to repurchase any specific amount of shares, and repurchases may be suspended or discontinued at any time without notice.
Financial Results Conference Call
Cirrus Logic will host a live Q&A session at 5 p.m. ET today to discuss its financial results and business outlook. Participants may listen to the call on the investor relations website at investor.cirrus.com. A replay of the call can be accessed at the same website.

Cirrus Logic is a leader in low-power, high-precision mixed-signal processing solutions that create innovative user experiences for the world’s top mobile and consumer applications. With headquarters in Austin, Texas, Cirrus Logic is recognized globally for its award-winning corporate culture.

In Q2 FY27, Cirrus Logic delivered revenue of $559.5 million and GAAP and non-GAAP earnings per share of $2.23 and $2.70, respectively. Revenue and Gross Margin
Revenue for the September quarter was $559.5 million, up 22 percent quarter over quarter and flat year over year. The increase on a sequential basis reflects higher smartphone unit volumes and increased content shipping into smartphones, and to a lesser extent, growth in custom components in non-smartphone applications. This was partially offset by a decline in PCs, and to a lesser extent, a reduction in revenue from certain general market components that are coming to their end of life. On a year-over-year
Q2 FY27 Letter to Shareholders | 2

basis, revenue was flat as increased smartphone unit volumes and higher content shipping into smartphones were offset by lower general market sales and previously anticipated pricing reductions. In the December quarter, we expect revenue to range from $510 million to $570 million, down three percent sequentially and down seven percent year over year at the midpoint.
In Q2 FY27, revenue derived from our audio and HPMS product lines represented 56 percent and 44 percent of total revenue, respectively. One customer contributed approximately 93 percent of total revenue in Q2 FY27. Our relationship with our largest customer remains outstanding, with continued strong design activity across a wide range of products. While we understand there is intense interest in this customer, in accordance with our policy, we do not discuss specifics about this business.

GAAP gross margin in the September quarter was 53.7 percent, compared to 52.6 percent in Q1 FY27 and 52.5 percent in Q2 FY26. The quarter-over-quarter increase in gross margin reflects lower wafer costs, largely driven by the temporary benefit from wafers purchased under prior agreements with GlobalFoundries, and lower production ramp expenses. On a year-over-year basis, gross margin increased due to a favorable product mix, lower inventory reserves, and a temporary benefit from wafers purchased under prior agreements with GlobalFoundries. These increases were partially offset by higher freight expense and supply chain costs. Non-GAAP gross margin in the September quarter was 53.8 percent, compared to 52.7 percent in Q1 FY27 and 52.5 percent in Q2 FY26. In the December quarter, we expect gross margin to range from 51 percent to 53 percent.

Operating Profit, Tax, and EPS
Operating profit for Q2 FY27 was 23.7 percent on a GAAP basis and 28.5 percent on a non-GAAP basis. GAAP operating expense was $168.2 million and included $18.0 million in stock-based compensation and $1.6 million in amortization of acquisition intangibles. GAAP operating expense also included $6.9 million in acquisition-related costs, including professional fees and other discrete costs incurred in connection with acquisition-related activity, as well as acquisition-specific employee bonus and retention payments.
On a sequential basis, GAAP operating expense increased by $10.8 million, primarily driven by acquisition-related costs and, to a lesser extent, higher product development expenses and variable compensation. This was partially offset by a decrease in stock-based compensation. Within employee-related expenses, an increase in salaries driven by higher headcount was offset by a seasonal reduction in benefits costs. On a year-over-year basis, GAAP operating expense increased by $18.6 million primarily due to higher employee-related expenses, acquisition-related costs, product development expenses, and R&D development expenses. This was partially offset by a decrease in stock-based compensation and variable compensation expense. Non-GAAP operating expense for the quarter was $141.6 million, up $6.2 million sequentially and $13.9 million year over year. The company’s total headcount exiting Q2 was 1,815, up 98 employees from the prior quarter as we continue to increase our investment in R&D to address the growing opportunities across our business.
Combined GAAP R&D and SG&A expenses for Q3 FY27 are expected to range from $184 million to $190 million, including approximately $21 million in stock-based compensation expense, $9 million in acquisition-related costs, and $2 million in amortization of acquisition intangibles, resulting in a non-GAAP operating expense range between $152 million and $158 million.

Company Strategy
We believe that our long-term growth is best served by the successful execution of three core strategic priorities: first, maintaining our leadership position in smartphone audio; second, increasing HPMS content in smartphones; and third, leveraging our strength and intellectual property in audio and HPMS to expand into additional applications and markets with both existing and new components.
In audio, demand for our latest-generation custom boosted amplifier and 22-nanometer smart codec remained strong, and we were delighted to see these components featured once again in our customer's newly launched devices. These products deliver exceptional audio performance and meaningful power and efficiency advantages, while also enabling considerable flexibility in system design. Our custom audio components have historically had long production lifecycles, with the prior generations of our codec and amplifiers shipping for five and six years, respectively. We expect our current generation to follow a similar trajectory, providing the company with solid long-term visibility and sustained revenue contribution. Looking ahead, we remain closely engaged with our customer on future audio innovations as we continue to strengthen our leadership in this core area of our business.
Beyond audio, we continue to expand our smartphone content with HPMS solutions. Since fall 2020, we have introduced three generations of camera controllers, each delivering greater performance and more advanced capabilities than the last. Historically, each new generation has launched in premium models

Consistent with this pattern, we were pleased to see a higher attach rate of our third-generation controller in certain models of recently introduced smartphones. This broader adoption reflects the growing value our technology delivers as our customer extends more advanced capabilities across a wider range of camera functions. Continuing to execute on our camera roadmap, we recently taped out our fourth-generation controller, which we expect to begin production in calendar year 2028 and to further increase our content per device.
Across our entire smartphone business, we are excited about the path of content growth over the next several product cycles. In the near term, we expect the successive launch windows over the next approximately 18 months to see growth in attach rates across a number of our existing ICs. Beyond that, we expect to build on these gains and further increase our content per device with multiple new components that are now in development, including our fourth-generation camera controller, smart power IC for 3D sensing, and other HPMS components. Alongside these gains, we also anticipate continued positive contributions from content expansion in accessories, wearables, and other non-smartphone devices.
Outside of smartphones, we continue to make progress executing on our third strategic priority to expand into new applications and markets. The PC market continues to be affected by memory and other component shortages and the associated pricing pressure, which market research indicates is driving significant declines in unit volumes this year and will further erode shipments in calendar year 2027. These conditions have delayed the introduction of certain new models, most of which are expected to have more of our content per system and higher volumes. While these headwinds are impacting our near-term outlook, the design wins behind those models remain in place, our customer engagement on new programs remains strong, and we believe we are positioned for growth as demand recovers.
Even in this environment, we are seeing tangible evidence of our progress that reinforces our confidence in our PC business. This includes the launch by a tier 1 OEM of a new premium PC based on NVIDIA’s RTX Spark™ platform, which features a six-speaker audio system powered by Cirrus Logic amplifiers and a codec. This design showcases our ability to deliver rich, high-fidelity audio in thin and power-efficient form factors. We are also excited about the opportunity to enable voice as a natural interface for AI-enabled PCs. Our latest smart codec integrates a dedicated DSP and a low-power wake-up signal chain that handles wake-word detection, noise reduction, and audio buffering independently of the CPU or NPU. This enables an always-on, conversational interaction while simultaneously preserving battery life. Customer interest in this product was strong during the quarter, and over time we anticipate that voice capabilities will extend from premium platforms into a broader range of mainstream designs. Further expanding our product portfolio, we recently taped out our second-generation smart codec for AI-enabled PCs. Beyond PCs, we also see a growing opportunity in AI gadgets and smart glasses as AI assistants move beyond the smartphone into new types of wearable and portable devices. These small, battery-powered products need high-quality audio and efficient power management in a very compact design, which plays directly to our strengths. We are engaged with several market leaders and expect end devices utilizing our products to come to market in the next few quarters.
Beyond PCs and wearables, we continue to expand our general market business across a broad base of customers and applications in the professional audio, automotive, industrial, and imaging markets. These components typically have long lifecycles and gross margins above our corporate average, and over the past several years we have invested in new product families designed to broaden our reach across these markets. During the September quarter, we began sampling our analog front-end components for smart metering in electrification applications. We also secured the first production orders for our new industrial imaging components, which are designed for high-precision scanning in applications such as line-scan

Unlike alternative solutions that require separate illumination drivers, sensor-timing circuitry, and signal conditioning, this product family integrates these functions into a scalable imaging front end. Customer engagement with these components has remained strong since we introduced this product family earlier this year, and we expect to begin mass production in the December quarter.
Finally, we are excited to have completed the acquisition of INVENTVM, a fabless semiconductor company based in Pavia, Italy, that specializes in mixed-signal power management and audio integrated circuits. Our teams have collaborated closely for the past five years, giving us firsthand insight into their engineering talent and R&D capabilities. The combined team not only expands our engineering capacity but also strengthens both our competitive position and product portfolio in the emerging consumer wearable and AI gadget markets.
