MIDERA FOOD PROCESSING REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
Completed first quarterly reporting period as an independent company
Delivered fifth consecutive quarter with double-digit year-over-year organic order growth
Completed first acquisition post-spin subsequent to quarter-end
Raises full-year 2026 guidance
ROSEMONT, Ill.— November 17, 2026 — Midera Food Processing, Inc. (Nasdaq: MFP) (“Midera,” the “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), a leading global pure-play food processing technology company, today reported financial results for its fiscal third quarter ended October 3, 2026, its first quarterly reporting period following its separation (the “Spin-off” or “Separation”) from The Middleby Corporation (Nasdaq: MIDD) (“Middleby”) on July 6, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•
Net sales of $229 million increased 13.1% over the prior year period; 11.5% increase on an organic basis1
•
Net earnings of $21 million compared to $14 million in the prior year period
•
Adjusted EBITDA1, 2 of $43 million, an increase of 44.9% compared to $30 million in the prior year period
•
Total debt of $249 million, Net debt1 of $200 million and Net leverage ratio1 of approximately 1.2x as of quarter-end
•
Strong demand with orders up 13% over the prior year period to $229 million, maintaining record backlog of $446 million at quarter-end
•
Raised full-year 2026 guidance. The midpoint of the updated guidance range reflects net sales growth of 13%, organic net sales growth1 of 7%, and Estimated Standalone Adjusted EBITDA1, 3 growth of 24% over the prior year period
“We are pleased to deliver a great first quarter as an independent company,” said Mark Salman, Chief Executive Officer of Midera. “Net sales grew 13 percent, including 12 percent organic growth1, and Adjusted EBITDA margin1, 2 expanded 410 basis points to 18.7 percent as the expected margin improvements we previously outlined are being realized. Across protein, bakery, and snack, our customers continue to invest in automation, yield, and new product formats, and our ability to deliver total line solutions, backed by the aftermarket support that follows, deepens those relationships for years to come.”
Mr. Salman continued, “In October, we welcomed Phoenix Innovations to the Midera family, adding new further processing capabilities that strengthen what we can offer poultry customers. We are executing on our growth strategy, and with a strong balance sheet and a healthy acquisition pipeline, we have the flexibility to keep investing in growth and creating long-term value for our shareholders.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Midera’s third quarter reflects its first quarterly reporting period as an independent public company. Prior to Separation from Middleby on July 6, 2026, Midera operated as the Food Processing segment of Middleby. Financial information for pre-Separation periods has been derived from Middleby’s accounting records and is presented on a carve-out basis as if Midera had operated as a standalone company for all periods presented. Financial information for pre-Separation periods may not necessarily reflect what Midera's financial condition, results of operations or cash flows would have been had Midera been a standalone company during the periods presented.
Net sales were $228.9 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 13.1% compared to $202.3 million in the prior year period. Net sales from the acquisitions of Frigomeccanica and Oka accounted for $4.1 million of the year-over-year increase in the third quarter. Organic net sales growth1 was 11.5%, primarily driven by increases in aftermarket parts and service related to the bakery and protein categories and pricing actions as well as higher equipment sales across the snack, bakery and protein categories.
Net earnings were $20.9 million for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $14.3 million in the prior year period. Adjusted EBITDA1, 2 was $42.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, up $13.3 million as compared to $29.6 million in the prior year period, primarily driven by higher sales of aftermarket parts and services, higher equipment sales, including recent acquisitions, and operating efficiencies, partially offset by the impact of inflationary cost increases.
Financial Position
On June 29, 2026, the Company entered into a five-year, $1.0 billion credit agreement in connection with the Spin-off. At the end of the third quarter, the Company's Net debt1 was $199.8 million, consisting of total debt of $249.0 million and cash and cash equivalents of $49.2 million. As a result, the Company’s Net leverage ratio1 was 1.2x based on last twelve months Estimated Standalone Adjusted EBITDA1,3. Total liquidity was $829.2 million, including cash and cash equivalents and $780.0 million of availability under the Company's revolving credit facility.
During the third quarter, the Company repurchased 14,700 shares for approximately $0.6 million at an average price of $42.64 per share. As of October 3, 2026, there was $49.4 million remaining on the current $50.0 million share repurchase authorization.
As previously announced on October 23, 2026, subsequent to the end of the third quarter, the Company acquired Phoenix Innovations (“Phoenix”), a high-quality designer and builder of poultry processing equipment with extensive experience in the protein industry. Phoenix is currently operating at an annual revenue run rate of approximately $25 million.

About Midera Food Processing
Midera Food Processing provides food processing equipment and automation solutions for industrial protein, bakery, and snack producers, delivering total line solutions from preparation and thermal processing through packaging. With a portfolio of 30+ industry-leading brands reaching customers across six continents, Midera helps food processors produce safer, more consistent products while improving efficiency and reducing waste at scale. Headquartered in Rosemont, Illinois, Midera employs approximately 2,900 people worldwide. For more information about Midera, please visit www.midera.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The Company uses non-GAAP financial measures to supplement the financial measures presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). The non-GAAP financial measures disclosed by the Company should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP, and the financial results prepared in accordance with GAAP. In addition, the non-GAAP financial measures do not have standard meanings and may vary from similarly titled non-GAAP financial measures used by other companies. The Company believes that its presentation of non-GAAP financial measures is useful because it provides investors and securities analysts with the same information that it uses internally for purposes of assessing its core operating and financial performance. The Company has not provided a reconciliation of forward-looking Estimated Standalone Adjusted EBITDA to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure, net earnings, because certain items cannot be reasonably estimated at this time without unreasonable effort. These items include, but are not limited to, restructuring charges and the impact of changes in foreign exchange rates. The timing and magnitude of these items are uncertain and could have a material impact on the Company's results reported in accordance with GAAP.
Definitions of the non-GAAP financial measures presented are as follows:
•
Change in Organic net sales is defined as the change in net sales adjusted to exclude the impact of acquisitions and foreign exchange rates.
•
Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net earnings before interest, income taxes, depreciation and intangible amortization, or EBITDA, adjusted to exclude restructuring, acquisition related adjustments, impairment charges, share-based compensation and other items which management considers to be outside core operating results.
•
Adjusted EBITDA margin is defined as Adjusted EBITDA divided by net sales.
•
Estimated Standalone Adjusted EBITDA is defined as Adjusted EBITDA less estimated incremental recurring costs for the Company to operate certain corporate support functions as a standalone public company (executive management, finance, accounting, tax, treasury, information technology and legal, among others).
•
Estimated Standalone Adjusted EBITDA margin is defined as Estimated Standalone Adjusted EBITDA divided by net sales.
•
Adjusted net earnings is defined as net earnings adjusted to exclude intangible amortization, restructuring, acquisition related adjustments, impairment charges, and other items which management considers to be outside core operating results.
•
Adjusted diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) is defined as Adjusted net earnings divided by diluted weighted average common shares outstanding.
•
Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditures.
•
Net debt is defined as current maturities of long-term debt and long-term debt less cash and cash equivalents.
•
Net leverage ratio is defined as net debt divided by last twelve months Estimated Standalone Adjusted EBITDA.
In the third quarter of 2026, the Company revised its definition of acquisition related adjustments to include transaction costs related to both potential and completed transactions, which management considers to be outside core operating results. Prior period amounts have been recast to conform to the revised definition.
The Company believes change in Organic net sales, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Estimated Standalone Adjusted EBITDA, Estimated Standalone Adjusted EBITDA margin, Adjusted net earnings, and Adjusted diluted EPS are useful as supplements to its GAAP results of operations to evaluate certain aspects of its operations and financial performance, and its management team primarily focuses on non-GAAP items in evaluating performance for business planning purposes. The Company believes free cash flow is useful to investors as a measure of cash generated from operations that can be used for debt repayment, acquisitions, and other strategic investments. The Company believes net debt and net leverage ratio are useful to investors in understanding our overall financial condition.
