Press Release
Q3 2026: double-digit growth in sales and business EPS
FY 2026: guidance upgraded
October 30, 2026
Q3 sales growth of 14.3% at CER1 and business earnings per share (EPS)2 of €3.55
•Pharma launches sales increased by 31.4%, reaching €1.4 billion, driven by ALTUVIIIO, Ayvakit, and Rezurock
•Dupixent sales increased by 34.8% to €5.7 billion, above €5 billion per quarter for the second time
•Vaccines sales decreased by 6.6% to €3.2 billion, impacted by the anticipated phasing in respiratory prevention
•Research and Development expenses reached €1.8 billion, broadly stable
•Selling and general expenses reached €2.3 billion, down by 0.9%, reflecting cost discipline
•Business EPS was €3.55, up by 19.6% at CER; 22.0% at actual exchange rates; IFRS EPS €2.52
Pipeline update
•Two regulatory approvals; in rare diseases and vaccines
•One regulatory submission acceptance and two regulatory designations (priority review and PRIME)
Guidance upgraded
•2026 guidance is upgraded: sales are expected to grow by a low-teens percentage at CER. Business EPS at CER is expected to grow by a mid-teens percentage.3
Belén Garijo, Chief Executive Officer: “Sanofi achieved another strong quarter with double-digit sales growth of 14.3%, mainly driven by our new medicines and Dupixent. Business EPS increased by 19.6%, driven by cost discipline. The pipeline milestones included two regulatory approvals, one regulatory submission, and two additional regulatory designations. Our strong commercial and financial momentum to date leads us to raise our 2026 guidance: sales growth is now expected at a low-teens percentage and business EPS growth at a mid-teens percentage, underscoring our sustainable, profitable growth.
In the third quarter, we accelerated Sanofi’s transformation by expanding our long-standing alliance with Regeneron, which opens up further opportunities in immunology. We also continued to simplify our portfolio through a partnership with Cheplapharm covering a number of legacy medicines4.
We are confident about our ability to pursue our strong commercial and financial performance in the short and medium term. The whole organisation is focused on managing Sanofi’s post-Dupixent trajectory over the next decade. Our priority is to protect earnings through Dupixent’s loss of exclusivity. We have already begun taking action to improve R&D productivity. Business development and M&A will be critical to complement this transformation. We remain committed to our long-term capital allocation policy, including our progressive dividend.”

Q3 and YTD 2026 summary
A conference call and webcast for investors and analysts will begin at 13:30 CET with details on sanofi.com, including the slides.
The performance shown in this press release covers the three-month period to September 30, 2026 (Q3 2026) and the nine-month period to September 30, 2026 (the year-to-date (YTD) 2026) compared to the three-month period to September 30, 2025 (Q3 2025) and the nine-month period to September 30, 2025 (YTD 2025) respectively. All percentage changes in sales in this press release are at CER, unless otherwise stated.
In Q3 2026, sales were €14,444 million and increased by 14.3%. Exchange rate movements had a positive effect of 1.9 percentage points (pp); therefore, at actual exchange rates, sales increased by 16.2%. The divestments of medicines/portfolio streamlining had a negative impact of 0.4pp on sales growth. In YTD 2026, sales were €36,550 million and increased by 15.2%. Exchange rate movements had a negative effect of 2.1pp; therefore, at actual exchange rates, sales increased by 13.1%. The divestments of medicines/portfolio streamlining had a negative impact of 0.4pp on sales growth.

US sales were €8,603 million and increased by 23.9%. The growth was driven by Immunology (Dupixent and Kevzara), pharma launches, and insulins with vaccines decreasing by 6.8% from lower influenza and Beyfortus sales partly offset by newly acquired Heplisav-B.

Business operating income
In Q3 2026, business operating income (BOI) was €5,430 million and increased by 19.7% (22.2% at actual exchange rates) from €4,445 million in Q3 2025. The ratio of BOI to net sales was 37.4% and increased by 1.7pp (37.6% at actual exchange rates, up by 1.9pp). The increase was mainly driven by growth in business gross profit and operating leverage partly offset by the increase in other operating expenses from higher profit sharing to Sanofi’s collaboration partner Regeneron. In YTD 2026, BOI was €11,688 million and increased by 21.1% (19.2% at actual exchange rates) from €9,808 million in YTD 2025. The ratio of BOI to net sales was 31.9% and increased by 1.6pp (32.0% at actual exchange rates, up by 1.7pp).
Business development
In September, Sanofi and Cheplapharm announced the intention to create a new strategic partnership in mature medicines under which Cheplapharm would take over from Sanofi a selection of 20 mature medicines and three manufacturing sites worldwide. In return, Sanofi will receive a 26.4% equity stake in Cheplapharm, building on a collaboration that started in 2014. The commercial transfer of the medicine portfolio is planned to begin in the first quarter of 2027, followed by the transfer of the sites, subject to information and consultation procedures with employee representatives, regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions. The transaction is expected to be fully completed by the third quarter of 2027. The medicines being divested from Sanofi include Lovenox/Clexane (enoxaparin), excluding in the US. Lovenox/Clexane had 2025 sales of €822 million. Additional financial details are expected to be provided at a later stage.
In early October, Sanofi and Sanofi’s collaboration partner Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. announced an expansion of their longstanding antibody collaboration, adding multiple next-generation, long-acting antibodies to the alliance. Under the expanded license and collaboration agreement, the companies intend to co-develop and co-market four new long-acting, Regeneron-invented antibodies targeting interleukin-13 (IL13), interleukin-4 (IL4), interleukin-4 receptor alpha (IL4Rα), and an IL4xIL13 bispecific. The IL13 antibody REGN20423 is currently in a phase 1 clinical study for atopic dermatitis, and the other three molecules are expected to enter clinical studies in 2027. Regeneron will also have the option to include in the collaboration Sanofi’s lunsekimig, exercisable upon completion of its phase 3 studies for chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. Regeneron will receive an upfront payment of $1 billion from Sanofi, with the potential for development, regulatory and commercial milestone payments of up to an additional $7 billion. The companies will share development and commercial costs for the new programmes and will split future profits from these potential new medicines 50:50 on a global basis. Regeneron will lead research and development activities, and Sanofi will lead global commercial efforts. The existing profit-sharing agreement on Dupixent and Kevzara will not change.

Immunology
Tzield/Teizeild (teplizumab)
The European Medicines Agency (EMA) accepted Teizeild for regulatory review to delay the onset of stage 3 type 1 diabetes (T1D) in patients aged one to seven years diagnosed with stage 2 T1D. Teizeild is already approved for this indication in adults and children aged eight years and older.
lunsekimig (IL13xTSLP Nanobody® VHH)
At the European Respiratory Society Congress 2026 in Barcelona, Spain, positive phase 2 data from two separate studies showed that lunsekimig, targeting thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP) and interleukin-13 (IL13), led to clinically meaningful improvements in patients with asthma and CRSwNP.
•The AIRCULES phase 2b dose-ranging study (clinical study identifier: NCT06102005) evaluated the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of lunsekimig in addition to standard of care therapy over 48 weeks in adult patients with moderate-to-severe asthma who had one or more exacerbations in the preceding year, compared with placebo. Rare diseases
Ayvakit (avapritinib)
The US FDA approved a label update for Ayvakit including a 50 mg daily dose option in adults with indolent systemic mastocytosis (ISM), for use as clinically indicated after at least six months of treatment at 25 mg daily, which remains the recommended starting dose. ISM is defined by a broad spectrum of disease activity that varies by patient, highlighting the importance of providing flexibility to healthcare providers to address these diverse medical needs. This label update was based on observed data for Ayvakit 50 mg from the long-term, open-label extension portion of the PIONEER clinical trial (clinical study identifier: NCT03731260).
Vaccines
MenQuadfi (meningococcal disease)
The EU approved an extension of the indication for MenQuadfi (Meningococcal Group A, C, W and Y conjugate vaccine) to include infants from six weeks of age to help protect against invasive meningococcal disease caused by Neisseria meningitidis serogroups A, C, W, and Y. MenQuadfi was previously approved in the EU for use in individuals aged 12 months and older.
Invasive meningococcal disease is a life-threatening bacterial infection that can progress rapidly, with patients potentially deteriorating from the onset of symptoms to death within 24 hours. Infants are among the populations at highest risk for invasive meningococcal disease, facing high rates of mortality and severe long-term complications. Although serogroup A is now rarely reported in the EU, serogroups C, W, and Y remain responsible for a significant share of invasive meningococcal disease cases.
Beyfortus (nirsevimab)
The Chinese National Medical Products Administration granted a priority review to Beyfortus for the regulatory submission to extend the indication to children up to 24 months of age during their second respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) season. Beyfortus is currently approved for the prevention of RSV lower respiratory tract infection in neonates and infants entering or during their first RSV season.
SP0087 (rabies)
The EMA’s Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) issued a positive opinion for SP0087 for pre- and post-exposure prophylaxis against rabies in all age groups. This next-generation vaccine is highly purified, free from human and animal-origin materials, with enhanced thermostability and longer shelf-life.
SP0289 (influenza H5 pandemic)
The CHMP granted PRIME eligibility to the second generation structurally designed mRNA influenza H5 pandemic vaccine. This pandemic preparedness vaccine is intended for prevention of influenza in case of an officially declared pandemic. The CHMP acknowledged the vaccine's potential to address unmet medical need, noting the mRNA platform enables rapid manufacturing and adaptation to emerging virus variants or novel pandemic strains.

Sustainability update
Acting for nature: reducing dependencies, building resilience
Biodiversity is a key focus area in the efforts to limit the impact on nature and reduce to a minimum the use of natural resources.
Sanofi has completed a nature-related risk and dependency analysis across its operations and value chain. The analysis identified four critical nature-based commodities: horseshoe crab blood, palm oil, cattle, and pig derivatives. Although the projected cost impacts are financially immaterial, Sanofi is proactively strengthening its long-term resilience through targeted adaptation measures.
Horseshoe crab blood is essential for testing the safety of injectable medicines and vaccines, as required by regulators. Sanofi is accelerating its transition to synthetic alternatives and has an action plan with the following targets to be achieved by the end of 2030: (1) 70% of endotoxin tests performed without horseshoe crab reagents in all Sanofi quality control laboratories; (2) All new launches submitted with synthetic method.
This plan reinforces Sanofi's commitment to reducing its impact on nature, while safeguarding uninterrupted access to medicines for patients worldwide.

Opella
On April 30, 2025, Sanofi and CD&R closed the Opella transaction, creating an independent global consumer healthcare leader. Sanofi retained a significant shareholding in Opella through a 48.2% equity interest in OPAL JV Co, which indirectly holds 100% of Opella. Bpifrance owns 1.8% and CD&R the remaining 50.0%. To aid the ongoing assessment of the value of Sanofi’s Opella stake, Opella’s summary financials are disclosed at Q2/H1 and Q4/FY financial results.
