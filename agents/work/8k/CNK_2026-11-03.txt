CINEMARK HOLDINGS, INC. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
Grew Net Income 146% year-over-year to $124 million
Achieved highest third quarter and second-best quarterly revenue and Adjusted EBITDA
in Company history of $1.1 billion and $268 million, respectively
Plano, TX, November 3, 2026 – Cinemark Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: CNK), one of the largest and most influential theatrical exhibition companies in the world, today reported results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
“Following our record-breaking second quarter, we once again delivered exceptional Q3 results with worldwide revenue and Adjusted EBITDA reaching third quarter highs, and Adjusted EBITDA margin expanding 460 basis points year-over-year,” stated Sean Gamble, Cinemark’s President and CEO. “Our ability to translate positive box office momentum from a compelling slate of summer releases into consistent, meaningful earnings growth reflects the strength of our operating model, the effectiveness of our team and the sustained impact of our strategic initiatives.” Gamble continued, “Looking ahead, we remain highly confident in our long-term growth prospects and value proposition, supported by favorable consumer trends and content dynamics, as well as the distinct advantages we derive from our high-quality circuit, healthy balance sheet and differentiated competitive position.”
Q3 2026 Earnings Highlights
•
Entertained 64 million moviegoers across our global footprint that spans 14 countries.
•
Sustained our sizable market share gains of more than 150 basis points since the pandemic in both the U.S. and Latin America, representing the most significant gains of all major exhibitors.
•
Achieved record-setting results throughout our business:
o
Generated best-ever third quarter revenue of $1.1 billion, including record results across all major revenue categories.
o
Delivered all-time high premium large format box office across XD, IMAX and ScreenX, which increased 50% year-over-year and surpassed our prior record by 20%.
o
Produced highest quarterly motion seat revenue that grew 62% year-over-year and accounted for 7% of global box office.
o
Delivered a record third quarter worldwide concession per cap of $6.50 through continued execution of strategic initiatives.
o
Reported record third quarter Adjusted EBITDA of $268 million with Adjusted EBITDA margin of 25.3% that expanded 460 basis points year-over-year.
•
Reported Net Income attributable to Cinemark Holdings, Inc. of $123 million, or $1.04 diluted earnings per share.
•
Deployed $70 million in capital expenditures to maintain and enhance our circuit, including investments in new build theaters, recliner remodels, laser projector conversions, premium amenities and other ROI-generating initiatives; year-to-date capital expenditures reached $170 million as we progress toward a full-year expected investment of approximately $250 million.
•
Generated $136 million of cash from operations and $66 million of free cash flow and ended the quarter with a strong balance sheet, including $556 million of cash.

Financial Results
Cinemark Holdings, Inc.’s total revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 increased 23.5% to $1,059.1 million compared with $857.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. For the three months ended September 30, 2026, admissions revenue was $530.8 million while concession revenue was $413.9 million, with attendance of 63.7 million patrons. Worldwide average ticket price was $8.33 and concession revenue per patron was $6.50.
Net income attributable to Cinemark Holdings, Inc. for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $122.8 million compared with $49.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Diluted earnings per share for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $1.04 compared with $0.40 for the three months ended September 30, 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $267.6 million compared with $177.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Reconciliations of non-GAAP financial measures are provided in the financial schedules accompanying this press release and at https://ir.cinemark.com.
Cinemark Holdings, Inc.’s total revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 increased 19.2% to $2,788.6 million compared with $2,338.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, admissions revenue was $1,382.2 million while concession revenue was $1,102.4 million, with attendance of 166.4 million patrons. Worldwide average ticket price was $8.31 and concession revenue per patron was $6.63.
Net income attributable to Cinemark Holdings, Inc. for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $255.8 million compared with $104.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Diluted earnings per share for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $2.17 compared with $0.77 for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $650.1 million compared with $446.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Reconciliations of non-GAAP financial measures are provided in the financial schedules accompanying this press release and at https://ir.cinemark.com.

About Cinemark Holdings, Inc.
Cinemark Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: CNK) provides extraordinary out-of-home entertainment experiences as one of the largest and most influential theatrical exhibition companies in the world. Based in Plano, Texas, Cinemark makes every day cinematic for moviegoers across nearly 500 theaters and more than 5,600 screens, operating in 42 states in the U.S. (301 theaters; 4,211 screens) and 13 South and Central American countries (195 theaters; 1,407 screens). Cinemark offers guests superior sight and sound technology, including Barco laser projection and Cinemark XD, the world’s No. 1 exhibitor-branded premium large format; industry-leading penetration of upscale amenities such as expanded food and beverage offerings, Luxury Lounger recliners and D-BOX motion seats; top-notch guest service; and award-winning loyalty programs such as Cinemark Movie Club. All of this creates an immersive environment for a shared, entertaining escape, underscoring that there is no place more cinematic than Cinemark. For more information go to https://ir.cinemark.com.
