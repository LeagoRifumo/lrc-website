Disc Medicine Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update
•
Pivotal Phase 3 APOLLO trial of bitopertin in erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP) successfully met all primary and secondary endpoints
•
Bitopertin NDA resubmission planned by end of 2026, with an FDA decision for potential approval in EPP anticipated by mid-2027
•
Feedback from the upcoming end of phase 2 meeting with the FDA on selcodebart in anemia of myelofibrosis (MF) and plans for pivotal development in anemia of MF to be announced by the end of the year
•
Presented positive initial results from the Phase 2 RESTORE-PV study of DISC-3405 in polycythemia vera (PV)
•
Updated data from RESTORE-PV and initial data from a Phase 1b study of DISC-3405 in sickle cell disease (SCD) to be presented at the 68th American Society of Hematology (ASH) annual meeting in December 2026
•
Strong financial position ending Q3 with approximately $683 million in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities; completed a public offering in October 2026, with net proceeds of approximately $486 million extending cash runway into 2030
WATERTOWN, Mass., November 5, 2026 – Disc Medicine, Inc. (NASDAQ:IRON), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on the discovery, development, and commercialization of novel treatments for patients suffering from serious hematologic diseases, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a review of recent program and corporate developments.
“In our first pivotal trial readout as a company, APOLLO delivered strong and compelling evidence of the potential clinical benefit of bitopertin for patients with EPP,” said John Quisel, J.D., Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of Disc. “We look forward to engaging with regulators with the goal of bringing an important, innovative medicine to this community as efficiently as possible.”
“We also made significant progress across our broader pipeline, including encouraging initial RESTORE-PV data that reinforce the potential of DISC-3405 to maintain target hematocrit levels and improve symptoms for patients with polycythemia vera,” Dr. Quisel added. “Alongside our anticipated end-of-Phase 2 FDA meeting for selcodebart in anemia of myelofibrosis in the fourth quarter and a strengthened balance sheet, we are well positioned to support the anticipated launch of bitopertin and advance two additional programs toward pivotal-stage development in 2027.”
Recent Highlights and Anticipated Milestones:
Bitopertin: GlyT1 Inhibitor (Heme Synthesis Modulator)
•
Positive topline data from the Phase 3 APOLLO clinical trial of bitopertin in EPP achieved statistical significance on both co-primary endpoints of reduction in whole blood metal-free PPIX from baseline and average monthly total time in sunlight without pain during month 6 of treatment vs. placebo. The data showed:
o
Bitopertin reduced whole-blood metal-free PPIX by 41.4% versus placebo (p<0.0001) and increased average monthly total time in light without pain by 12.2 hours during month 6 (p=0.0010)
o
Positive and statistically significant results from all secondary endpoints, including reductions in phototoxic reactions and improvements on multiple measures of light tolerance. Exploratory endpoints of quality-of-life benefits were also positive and statistically significant.
•
Bitopertin was generally well-tolerated. Consistent with prior studies, dizziness was the most commonly reported treatment emergent adverse event (TEAE) in the bitopertin group (50%) and was mild or moderate and generally transient. There were no TEAEs above Grade 3.
•
Successful APOLLO results add to bitopertin’s robust clinical data package. Based on these results, Disc plans to resubmit a New Drug Application (NDA) to the U.S. FDA by the end of 2026, with a decision from the agency anticipated by mid-2027
Selcodebart (DISC-0974): Anti-Hemojuvelin Antibody (Hepcidin Suppression)
•
Updated data from the RALLY-MF trial to be presented at ASH in December
•
Feedback from an end of phase 2 meeting with the FDA and plans for pivotal development in anemia of MF to be announced by the end of the year
•
Progressing Phase 2 study of selcodebart in patients with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) with initial data expected in 2027
DISC-3405: Anti-TMPRSS6 Antibody (Hepcidin Induction)
•
Presented positive initial results from the RESTORE-PV Phase 2 clinical trial of DISC-3405 in patients with PV at the SOHO Annual Meeting. Efficacy data was presented for the Q2 weekly dosed cohort. Across escalation and maintenance periods, results demonstrated:
o
DISC-3405 increased hepcidin and lowered serum iron, translating to controlled hematocrit, reduced phlebotomy, and improved symptoms in PV patients
o
Generally well-tolerated with adverse events that are consistent with underlying disease and a low rate of injection site reactions which were mild and self-limited
•
Updated data from RESTORE-PV, including initial efficacy data on the Q4 weekly dosed cohort, to be presented at ASH in December
•
Initial data from Phase 1b study of DISC-3405 in patients with sickle cell disease also to be presented at ASH
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results:
•
Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $682.8 million as of September 30, 2026. In October 2026, Disc completed a public offering with net proceeds of approximately $485.9 million. Together with such net proceeds, we believe that our cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities will be sufficient to fund our current operating and capital expenditure plans and our debt service obligations into 2030.
•
Research and Development Expenses: R&D expenses were $44.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $50.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease in R&D expenses was primarily driven by a $10.0 million milestone payment and drug manufacturing in the comparative period that did not recur in the current period. These decreases were partially offset by increased investment across our development portfolio, including higher personnel-related costs driven by increased headcount.
•
Selling, General and Administrative Expenses: SG&A expenses were $20.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $17.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily due to increased headcount.
•
Net Loss: Net loss was $59.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $62.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower research and development expenses resulting from costs incurred in the prior-year period that did not recur in the current period, partially offset by continued investment in advancing our pipeline and higher personnel-related costs.
About Disc Medicine
Disc Medicine (NASDAQ:IRON) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company committed to discovering, developing, and commercializing novel treatments for patients who suffer from serious hematologic diseases. We are building a portfolio of innovative, potentially first-in-class therapeutic candidates that aim to address a wide spectrum
of hematologic diseases by targeting fundamental biological pathways of red blood cell biology, specifically heme biosynthesis and iron homeostasis. For more information, please visit www.discmedicine.com.
