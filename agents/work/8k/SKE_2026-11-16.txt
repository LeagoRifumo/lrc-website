Skeena Gold & Silver Reports Q3 2026 Financial Results;
Announces Management Appointment
​
Vancouver, BC (November 16, 2026) Skeena Resources Limited (TSX: SKE, NYSE: SKE) (“Skeena Gold & Silver”, “Skeena” or the “Company”) reports interim financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The interim financial statements and management’s discussion and analysis (“MD&A”) are available on Skeena’s website, on SEDAR+ at www.sedarplus.ca and on EDGAR at www.sec.gov.
​
Management Appointment
Skeena is pleased to provide the following Management appointment:
​
Sébastien Fortin as Senior Vice President, Operations – Mr. Fortin brings over three decades of global leadership experience to the team, with extensive international experience spanning mine operations, project development, and technical services. He was most recently Vice President & Head of the British Columbia Business Unit for Hudbay Minerals Limited. Prior to his role at Hudbay, Mr. Fortin spent over fifteen years in progressive leadership roles at Teck Resources, where he was recognized with multiple awards for workplace safety, innovation, and production. Sébastien was also part of the development team of the Kupol gold mine in Russia, when it was owned by Bema Gold. Mr. Fortin has a strong background in geological and mining engineering from Laval University, along with an Executive MBA from Simon Fraser University and is a registered Professional Engineer.
​
Randy Reichert, President & Chief Executive Officer, commented: “As we prepare for operations and initial production at Eskay Creek in Q2 2027, we continue to strengthen our management team with leaders who have successfully operated major mining assets. Sébastien’s extensive experience in mine development, commissioning, and operations will be invaluable as we transition from a developer to a mid-tier producer next year.”
​
About Skeena
Skeena is a leading precious metals development company focused on advancing the Eskay Creek Gold-Silver Project in British Columbia’s Golden Triangle. With the Project fully permitted and under construction, the Company is progressing Eskay Creek towards initial production and cash flow in the second quarter of 2027. Once in operation, Eskay Creek is expected to be one of the world’s highest-grade and lowest-cost open-pit precious metals mines, with significant silver by-product production that exceeds the output of many primary silver mines. Skeena is committed to responsible and sustainable mining in partnership with Indigenous communities, while maximizing the value of its mineral resources to generate long-term shareholder returns.

Mineral property, plant and equipment
The cost of property, plant and equipment includes an estimate of future variable consideration associated with its acquisition. A corresponding liability is recognized on the acquisition date and initially measured based on the estimated future variable payments. The liability is subsequently measured at amortized cost, with any changes to future variable payments recognized as an adjustment to the cost of the related asset. Share-based payments
Most share-based payments are issued pursuant to the Company’s Omnibus Equity Incentive Plan, under which it is the Company’s choice whether to settle in cash or by issuing equity instruments. In contrast, for share-based payments in which the terms of the arrangement provide the counterparty with a choice of whether to settle in cash or by issuing equity instruments, the Company accounts for the arrangement as a compound financial instrument, comprising a liability component, which represents the counterparty’s right to demand settlement in cash, and equity component, which represents the counterparty's right to demand settlement in equity instruments rather than cash.

At the date of grant, the Company measures the fair value of the compound instrument. Gold Stream
In exchange for a deposit of US$200,000,000 (the “Deposit”), the Company committed to deliver 3.52% of the payable gold production from the Eskay Creek Project (the “Stream Percentage”) for the life of the mine.
