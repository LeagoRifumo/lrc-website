Kingsoft Cloud Announces Unaudited Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Kingsoft Cloud Holdings Limited (“Kingsoft Cloud” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: KC and HKEX: 3896), a leading cloud service provider in China, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Mr. Tao Zou, Chief Executive Officer of Kingsoft Cloud, commented: “I am excited to report one of the strongest quarterly results, with total revenue up 40% year-over-year, the fastest growth in the past 5 years, and adjusted operating profit margin of 4.4%1, a record high in the Company’s history. The performance reflects a strategic conviction we formed years ago — that AI would be applied across a wide range of industries. Since then, we have kept refining our solutions, investing capital prudently, strengthening our supply chain, and optimizing our day-to-day operations. At a time of rapid changes and significant opportunities, we will build on these strengths and focus on promising areas including intelligent computing, agentic AI and inference platforms, and FDE solutions, to improve monetization and support sustainable growth.”
Ms. Yi Li, Chief Financial Officer of Kingsoft Cloud, added, “We delivered another solid quarter, with total revenue reaching RMB3.5 billion, representing an accelerated year-over-year growth of 40.4%. Gross billings of our AI cloud business reached RMB1.7 billion, representing a year-on-year increase of 115%, accounting for 61% of our public cloud revenue, compared with 45% a year ago. We further expanded our margins quarter-over-quarter, with an adjusted gross margin of 15.7%2 and an adjusted operating profit margin of 4.4%. We remained disciplined in capital deployment – the RMB6.2 billion quarterly cash capital expenditure was nearly 100% covered by our operating cash flows, same as in the previous quarter.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total revenues reached RMB3,478.1 million (US$518.83 million), increased by 40.4% year-over-year from RMB2,478.0 million in the same quarter of 2025, and increased by 13.2% quarter-over-quarter from RMB3,072.0 million in the second quarter of 2026. The increase was mainly due to fast growth in our AI cloud services, supported by continued upgrades to our AI cloud infrastructure and product offerings.
Revenues from public cloud services were RMB2,756.7 million (US$411.2 million), increased by 57.3% year-over-year from RMB1,752.3 million in the same quarter of 2025 and increased by 16.9% quarter-over-quarter from RMB2,357.6 million last quarter. Within public cloud services, our AI cloud gross billings reached RMB1,683 million, while our non-AI public cloud services also recorded decent growth.
Revenues from enterprise cloud services were RMB721.4 million (US$107.6 million), representing a year-over-year decrease of 0.6% from RMB725.7 million in the same quarter of 2025 and a quarter-over-quarter increase of 1.0% from RMB714.3 million last quarter.

Selling and marketing expenses were RMB127.1 million (US$19.0 million), decreased by 16.5% from RMB152.2 million in the same quarter in 2025 and increased by 12.4% from RMB113.1 million last quarter. The year-over-year decrease was mainly due to the decrease of personnel costs and share-based compensation.
General and administrative expenses were RMB179.4 million (US$26.7 million), increased by 2.9% from RMB174.4 million in the same quarter in 2025 and increased by 28.0% from RMB140.2 million last quarter. Research and development expenses were RMB185.8 million (US$27.7 million), decreased by 6.9% from RMB199.6 million in the same quarter in 2025 and decreased by 2.1% from RMB189.9 million last quarter. The year-over-year decrease was mainly due to the decrease of personnel costs and share-based compensation.
Operating profit was RMB47.9 million (US$7.2 million), compared with operating loss of RMB145.3 million in the same quarter in 2025 and operating profit of RMB23.0 million last quarter. The improvement was mainly supported by strong top-line growth, stable gross margin, and enhanced operational leverage. Non-GAAP operating profit5 was RMB152.9 million (US$22.8 million), compared with operating profit of RMB15.4 million in the same quarter last year and operating profit of RMB124.0 million last quarter.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The unaudited condensed consolidated financial information is prepared in conformity with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“U.S. GAAP”). In evaluating our business, we consider and use certain non-GAAP measures, Non-GAAP gross profit, Non-GAAP gross margin, Non-GAAP operating profit (loss), Non-GAAP operating profit (loss) margin, Non-GAAP EBITDA, Non-GAAP EBITDA margin, Non-GAAP net profit (loss) and Non-GAAP net profit (loss) margin, as supplemental measures to review and assess our operating performance. The presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. We define Non-GAAP gross profit as gross profit excluding share-based compensation expenses allocated in the cost of revenues, and we define Non-GAAP gross margin as Non-GAAP gross profit as a percentage of revenues. We define Non-GAAP operating profit (loss) as operating (loss) profit excluding share-based compensation expenses and amortization of intangible assets and we define Non-GAAP operating profit (loss) margin as Non-GAAP operating profit (loss) as a percentage of revenues. We define Non-GAAP net profit (loss) as net loss excluding share-based compensation expenses and foreign exchange (gain) loss, and we define Non-GAAP net profit (loss) margin as Non-GAAP net profit (loss) as a percentage of revenues. We define Non-GAAP EBITDA as Non-GAAP net profit (loss) excluding interest income, interest expense, income tax (benefit) expense and depreciation and amortization, and we define Non-GAAP EBITDA margin as Non-GAAP EBITDA as a percentage of revenues. We present these non-GAAP financial measures because they are used by our management to evaluate our operating performance and formulate business plans. We also believe that the use of these non-GAAP measures facilitates investors’ assessment of our operating performance.
These non-GAAP financial measures are not defined under U.S. GAAP and are not presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. These non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools. One of the key limitations of using these non-GAAP financial measures is that they do not reflect all items of income and expense that affect our operations. Further, these non-GAAP measures may differ from the non-GAAP information used by other companies, including peer companies, and therefore their comparability may be limited.
We compensate for these limitations by reconciling these non-GAAP financial measures to the nearest U.S. GAAP performance measure, all of which should be considered when evaluating our performance. We encourage you to review our financial information in its entirety and not rely on a single financial measure.
