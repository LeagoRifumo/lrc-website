PDF Solutions® Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Santa Clara, CA, November 5, 2026 – PDF Solutions, Inc. (Nasdaq: PDFS), a leading provider of comprehensive data solutions for the semiconductor and electronics ecosystem, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Financial Highlights of Third Quarter 2026
● | Record quarterly total revenues of $68.3 million, up 20% over last year’s comparable quarter
● | GAAP gross margin of 73% and non-GAAP gross margin of 77%
● | GAAP operating margin of 12% and non-GAAP operating margin of 28%
● | GAAP diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $0.16 and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $0.40
● | Ending backlog of $247.2 million

Total revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $68.3 million, compared to $61.5 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $57.1 million for the third quarter of 2025.
GAAP gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 73%, compared to 69% for the second quarter of 2026 and 72% for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 77%, compared to 73% for the second quarter of 2026 and 76% for the third quarter of 2025.
GAAP operating margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 12%, compared to 8% for the second quarter of 2026 and 8% for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP operating margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 28%, compared to 22% for the second quarter of 2026 and 23% for the third quarter of 2025.
GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $6.8 million, or $0.16 per diluted share, compared to net income of $4.3 million, or $0.10 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2026, and net income of $1.3 million, or $0.03 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $17.2 million, or $0.40 per diluted share, compared to non-GAAP net income of $11.4 million, or $0.27 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2026, and non-GAAP net income of $9.8 million, or $0.25 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.
Ending backlog for the third quarter of 2026 was $247.2 million, compared to $270.7 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $292.0 million for the third quarter of 2025.
Financial Outlook
“In the third quarter of 2026, PDF Solutions continued its growth trajectory with total revenues 20% higher compared to the third quarter of 2025,” said John Kibarian, CEO and President of the Company. He continued, “On September 9, 2026, we announced Exensio Aurora, the new, highly-scalable Exensio analytics architecture. Exensio Aurora is purpose-built to handle semiconductor manufacturing data at petabyte scale and to securely deploy agentic AI across manufacturing operations and the supply chain. We believe this new architecture will help semiconductor companies address the challenges associated with the fast growth and transformation of the industry. Based on actual results through the third quarter and the pipeline for the fourth quarter, we reaffirm our 20% annual revenue growth target for 2026.”

Information Regarding Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to providing results that are determined in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“GAAP”), PDF Solutions also provides certain non-GAAP financial measures. Non-GAAP gross profit and margin exclude stock-based compensation expense and the amortization of acquired technology under costs of revenues. Non-GAAP income from operations and operating margin exclude stock-based compensation expense, amortization of acquired intangible assets, and the effects of certain non-recurring items, such as expenses for certain legal proceedings, and acquisition-related and integration costs. Non-GAAP net income excludes stock-based compensation expense, amortization of acquired intangible assets, amortization of debt issuance costs, and the effects of certain non-recurring items, such as expenses for certain legal proceedings, acquisition-related and integration costs, recovery from previously written-off property and equipment, and their related income tax effects, as applicable, as well as adjustments for the valuation allowance for deferred tax assets and reconciling items. PDF Solutions management uses non-GAAP financial measures to evaluate the Company’s operating performance and make strategic decisions because these measures exclude items that management believes are not indicative of ongoing operating results and that can vary significantly between periods. These non-GAAP results should not be considered an alternative to, or a substitute for, GAAP financial information, and may differ from similarly titled non-GAAP measures used by other companies. In particular, these non-GAAP financial measures are not a substitute for GAAP measures of income or loss as a measure of performance, or to cash flows from operating, investing and financing activities as a measure of liquidity. A reconciliation of the comparable GAAP financial measures to the non-GAAP financial measures is provided at the end of the Company’s unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements presented below.

About PDF Solutions
PDF Solutions (Nasdaq: PDFS) provides comprehensive data solutions designed to empower organizations across the semiconductor and electronics industry ecosystems to improve the yield and quality of their products and operational efficiency for increased profitability. The Company’s products and services are used by Fortune 500 companies across the semiconductor ecosystem to achieve smart manufacturing goals by connecting and controlling equipment, collecting data generated during manufacturing and test operations, and performing advanced analytics and machine learning to enable profitable, high-volume manufacturing.
Founded in 1991, PDF Solutions is headquartered in Santa Clara, California, with operations across North America, Europe, and Asia. The Company (directly or through one or more subsidiaries) is an active member of SEMI, INEMI, TPCA, IPC, the OPC Foundation, and DMDII.
