GOOSEHEAD INSURANCE, INC. RELEASES PRELIMINARY THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
WESTLAKE, TEXAS – November 9, 2026 - Goosehead Insurance, Inc. (“Goosehead” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: GSHD), a rapidly growing independent personal lines insurance agency, today announced preliminary results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and reaffirmed its full year 2026 guidance. The Company also announced that it will be delaying its full earnings release and restating certain prior period financial statements to correct departures from GAAP related to those periods. See the “Restatement of Financial Results” section below for additional information.
“We delivered strong third quarter results, with total revenue up approximately 17%, and core revenue1 up 13%," said Mark Miller, Chief Executive Officer. "Years of investment in our agents, service organization and proprietary technology have created a platform that pairs personal guidance with digital convenience and broad product choice. These capabilities are strengthening client relationships, expanding the ways we reach and serve clients, and positioning Goosehead to capture the substantial growth opportunity ahead.”
Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Results*
•Total Revenues grew approximately 17% in the third quarter of 2026 over the prior year period, at the mid-point of the range, based on preliminary estimates of the impact of the restatement*
•Third quarter Core Revenue1 of $94.7 million increased 13% over the prior-year period
•Total written premiums placed for the third quarter increased 11% over the prior-year period to $1.31 billion
•Policies in force grew 14% from the prior-year period to approximately 2.1 million
•Corporate agent headcount of 568 increased 9% compared to the prior-year period
•Total franchise producers of 2,272 increased 7% from the prior-year period
For the third quarter of 2026, total revenues are estimated to be between $108.8 million and $110.8 million, an increase of approximately 17% at the mid-point of the range, compared to an estimated $92.9 million to $94.9 million in the corresponding period in 2025, based on preliminary estimates of the impact of the restatement. Core Revenue, a non-GAAP measure which excludes contingent commissions, initial franchise fees, interest income, franchise operating fees, and other franchise revenues, was $94.7 million, a 13% increase from $83.9

Core Revenue is the most reliable revenue stream for the Company, consisting of New Business Commissions, Agency Fees, New Business Royalty Fees, Renewal Commissions, and Renewal Royalty Fees. Core Revenue growth was driven by continued improvement in Client Retention and strong new business generation, driven by growth in total producers and improved franchise productivity. The Company grew total written premiums, which we consider to be the leading indicator of future revenue growth, by 11% in the third quarter compared to the corresponding period in the prior year. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $32.8 million. We have a line of credit of $75.0 million, of which $26.0 million was drawn as of September 30, 2026. Total outstanding notes payable was $322.3 million as of September 30, 2026. As of September 30, 2026, $144.6 million remained available under the share repurchase authorization.

Restatement of Financial Results
In response to a comment letter received from the SEC as part of its standard review of our annual filings, management determined that its historical accounting for certain costs to obtain customer contracts did not conform to GAAP. As reported in Item 4.02 of Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K filed on November 9, 2026, the Company concluded that the following previously issued financial statements should no longer be relied upon and should be restated (the "Restatement"): (i) the audited consolidated financial statements for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2025, 2024 and 2023 (the “Annual Periods”), included in the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on February 19, 2026; and (ii) the unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements as of and for the quarterly and year-to-date periods ended September 30, June 30 and March 31 of each of 2025, 2024 and 2023, and June 30 and March 31, 2026, included in the Company’s Quarterly Reports on Form 10-Q for those periods (the “Interim Periods” and, together with the Annual Periods, the “Non-Reliance Periods”). Concurrently with this evaluation, management initiated a comprehensive review of our broader accounting policies. Through this process, management identified an additional departure from GAAP in the presentation of certain fees charged to franchisees, consisting of technology-related fees and fees for participation in the Company's errors and omissions ("E&O") insurance program.
During the Non-Reliance Periods, certain qualifying commissions paid to obtain customer contracts were expensed within employee compensation and benefits rather than capitalized and amortized over the period of expected benefit. While our historical accounting reflects the underlying economic realities of the business by expensing these costs as incurred, we have determined it does not conform to the FASB’s standards and must be restated. The Company preliminarily expects employee compensation and benefits to decrease and income from operations to increase by approximately 0.5% to 5.5% of employee compensation and benefits for each of the full year ended December 31, 2025 and the interim periods ended June 30, 2026 and March 31, 2026, with related effects on tax expense and other assets.
During the Non-Reliance Periods, certain fees charged to franchisees, consisting of technology-related fees and fees for participation in the Company's E&O insurance program, were recorded as a reduction of general and administrative expenses rather than recognized as franchise revenues in accordance with Accounting Standards Codification Topic 606, Revenue from
4
Contracts with Customers (“ASC 606”). Correcting this departure from GAAP results in a reclassification that increases both franchise revenues and general and administrative expenses by equal amounts in the same periods. This correction affects only the presentation of these amounts, not the timing of their recognition, and had no effect on operating income, net income or cash flows for the Non-Reliance Periods. We preliminarily expect both total revenue and general and administrative expenses to increase by equivalent amounts in the ranges of approximately $11 million to $16 million for the year ended December 31, 2025 and approximately $5.5 million to $8.5 million for the six months ended June 30, 2026.
The Company intends to file an amendment to its Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 (the “Form 10-K/A”) to restate the financial statements for the Annual Periods, including the comparative financial information for 2024 and 2023, and for the quarterly and year-to-date periods ended September 30, June 30 and March 31 of 2025 and 2024. It also intends to file amendments to its Quarterly Reports on Form 10-Q for the quarterly periods ended June 30, 2026 and March 31, 2026 to restate the affected interim financial statements. The Company intends to file these amendments as soon as practicable.

Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Measures to GAAP
This release includes certain financial performance measures that are not required by, nor presented in accordance with, generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”). The Company refers to these measures as “non-GAAP financial measures.” The Company uses these non-GAAP financial measures when planning, monitoring and evaluating its performance and considers these non-GAAP financial measures to be useful metrics for management and investors to facilitate operating performance comparisons from period to period by excluding potential differences caused by variations in capital structures, tax position, depreciation, amortization and certain other items that the Company believes are not representative of its core business. The Company uses these non-GAAP financial measures for business planning purposes and in measuring its performance relative to that of its competitors.
These non-GAAP financial measures are defined by the Company as follows:
•"Core Revenue" is a supplemental measure of our performance and includes Renewal Commissions, Renewal Royalty Fees, New Business Commissions, New Business Royalty Fees, and Agency Fees. We believe that Core Revenue is an appropriate measure of operating performance because it summarizes all of our revenues from sales of individual insurance policies.
•"Cost Recovery Revenue" is a supplemental measure of our performance and includes Initial Franchise Fees, Franchise Operating Fees and Interest Income. We believe that Cost Recovery Revenue is an appropriate measure of operating performance because it summarizes revenues that are viewed by management as cost recovery mechanisms.
•"Ancillary Revenue" is a supplemental measure of our performance and includes Contingent Commissions and Other Franchise Revenues. We believe that Ancillary Revenue is an appropriate measure of operating performance because it summarizes revenues that are ancillary to our core business.
While the Company believes that these non-GAAP financial measures are useful in evaluating its business, this information should be considered as supplemental in nature and is not meant as a substitute for revenues, net income, or earnings per share, in each case as recognized in accordance with GAAP. In addition, other companies, including companies in the Company’s
industry, may calculate such measures differently, which reduces their usefulness as comparative measures.
