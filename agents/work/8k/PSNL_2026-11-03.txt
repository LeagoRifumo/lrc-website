Personalis Reports Third Quarter Results
Reports Q3 clinical test volume of 13,157, clinical revenue of $4.4 million and overall revenue of $25.6 million
FREMONT, Calif. – November 4, 2026 – Personalis, Inc. (Nasdaq: PSNL), a leader in advanced genomics for precision oncology, today reported financial and operational results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Recent Strategic and Operational Highlights
●
Launched NeXT Personal® Real-Time Variant Tracker™: Commercially launched optional Real-Time Variant Tracker supplemental results in the NeXT Personal molecular residual disease (MRD) test report, which identify clinically relevant resistance and therapeutically targetable variants over time, during NeXT Personal testing.
●
Reported pre-interim results from prospective Triple Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC) B-STRONGER-1 study: Personalis’ study is a multi-site, prospective study evaluating ctDNA dynamics in stage I-III TNBC patients receiving neoadjuvant therapy (NAT). Results showed high sensitivity, achieving a 96% ctDNA detection rate at baseline with 83.3% of positive detections at T1 in the ultrasensitive range below 100 PPM. Also, the results showed an 82% ctDNA clearance rate following two cycles of NAT.
●
Demonstrated the value of ultrasensitive ctDNA for predicting early immunotherapy response: A study conducted with The University of California, San Diego (UCSD) for 55 patients across 8 metastatic cancer types showed that an ultrasensitive assay may provide resolution in cases of heterogeneous response to stratify patients with radiographically stable disease into distinct risk groups, and dynamic ctDNA monitoring during immunotherapy provides lead time for identifying patients at high risk of progression.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results Compared with Third Quarter 2025
●
Quarterly Revenue: Total revenue of $25.6 million compared with $14.5 million, primarily driven by growth in clinical test revenue, as well as core revenue from increased population sequencing and higher pharma testing services.
●
Clinical Revenue: Clinical test revenue of $4.4 million compared with $0.4 million, resulting from the increase in test volume and also a full quarter of Medicare covered IO test volume, in addition to revenue from breast and lung cancer covered tests; delivered 13,157 clinical tests compared with 4,388.
●
Core Revenue Streams: Revenue from pharma testing services and all other customers totaled $17.2 million compared with $13.2 million. Revenue from population sequencing (the VA MVP) totaled $4.0 million compared with $0.2 million.
●
Cash Position: Ended the quarter with approximately $190.6 million in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments.

About Personalis, Inc.
At Personalis, we are transforming the active management of cancer through breakthrough personalized testing. We aim to drive a new paradigm for cancer management, guiding care throughout the patient journey. Our highly sensitive assays combine tumor-and-normal profiling with proprietary algorithms to deliver advanced insights even as cancer evolves over time. Our products are designed to detect minimal residual disease (MRD) and recurrence at the earliest timepoints, enable the selection of targeted therapies based on ultra-comprehensive genomic profiling, and enhance biomarker strategy for drug development. Personalis is based in Fremont, California. To learn more, visit www.personalis.com and connect with us on LinkedIn and X (Twitter).
