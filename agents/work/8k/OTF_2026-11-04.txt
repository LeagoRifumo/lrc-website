Blue Owl Technology Finance Corp. Announces September 30, 2026 Financial Results
NEW YORK — November 4, 2026— Blue Owl Technology Finance Corp. (NYSE: OTF) (“OTF” or the “Company”) today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
THIRD QUARTER 2026 HIGHLIGHTS
•Third quarter GAAP net investment income (“NII”) per share of $0.29
•Third quarter adjusted NII per share(1) of $0.29, as compared to the prior quarter of $0.30
•Dividends totaled $0.40 per share, including a base dividend of $0.35 per share and a special dividend of $0.05 per share that was declared in connection with the listing, representing an annualized dividend yield of 9.6%(2)
•All special dividends declared in connection with OTF's listing last June have now been paid
•Net asset value (“NAV”) per share increased to $16.62, as compared to $16.48 as of June 30, 2026
•New investment commitments were $441 million and sales and repayments were $334 million
•Net debt-to-equity ended at 0.94x, as compared with 0.93x as of June 30, 2026
•Investments on non-accrual represented 1.8% and 1.1% of the portfolio at cost and fair value, respectively, as compared to 0.6% and 0.1% as of June 30, 2026
•Repurchased $55 million of common stock during the quarter, which was accretive to third quarter NAV per share
•Raised $900 million of new debt capital, including $550 million of senior unsecured notes, $250 million through a new SPV facility, and $100 million by upsizing an existing bilateral SPV
“OTF delivered a strong third quarter, with steady earnings and a rebound in NAV driving an 11.7% net income ROE,” said Craig W. Packer, Chief Executive Officer. “We continue to see improving market sentiment toward software and technology as investors take a more balanced view of the potential impacts of AI. This market recovery, together with continued health of our borrowers and accretive share repurchases, contributed to a 0.9% increase in NAV per share.”
“Investment opportunities across software and technology have become increasingly attractive, with wider spreads creating more favorable economics for lenders,” said Erik Bissonnette, President. “With approximately $2.9 billion of liquidity and leverage at the low end of our target range, OTF has significant capacity to selectively deploy capital at attractive terms. We also expect higher base rates to provide an additional earnings tailwind as our floating rate loans reset, supporting ROE expansion over time.”
Dividend Declarations
On November 3, 2026, the Board declared a fourth quarter 2026 base dividend of $0.35 per share for stockholders of record as of December 31, 2026, payable on or before January 15, 2027.
A full schedule of the record and payment dates can be found on the Company’s website at www.blueowltechnologyfinance.com.

RESULTS OF OPERATIONS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026
Investment Income
Investment income modestly increased to $341 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $338 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026, primarily reflecting modestly higher average leverage during the quarter. Other income remained relatively consistent period-over-period. The Company expects that investment income will vary based on a variety of factors including the pace of originations and repayments, spreads of new deployments, and base rate movements.
Expenses
Total operating expenses increased to $209 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $199 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026, primarily due to increased average daily borrowings. As a percentage of total assets, professional fees, directors’ fees and other general and administrative expenses remained relatively consistent period-over-period.
Liquidity and Capital Resources
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $221 million in cash, $7.3 billion in total principal value of debt outstanding, including $3.2 billion of unsecured notes and $2.6 billion of undrawn capacity(1) on the Company’s credit facilities. The funding mix was composed of 56.7% secured and 43.3% unsecured borrowings as of September 30, 2026 on an outstanding basis. The Company was in compliance with all financial covenants under its credit facilities as of September 30, 2026. The Company has analyzed cash and cash equivalents, availability under its credit facilities, the ability to rotate out of certain assets and amounts of unfunded commitments that could be drawn and believes its liquidity and capital resources are sufficient to take advantage of market opportunities.

ABOUT BLUE OWL TECHNOLOGY FINANCE CORP.
Blue Owl Technology Finance Corp. (NYSE: OTF) is a specialty finance company focused on making debt and equity investments to U.S. technology-related companies, with a strategic focus on software. As of September 30, 2026, OTF had investments in 207 portfolio companies with an aggregate fair value of $14.8 billion. OTF has elected to be regulated as a business development company under the Investment Company Act of 1940, as amended. OTF is externally managed by Blue Owl Technology Credit Advisors LLC, an SEC-registered investment adviser that is an indirect affiliate of Blue Owl Capital Inc. (“Blue Owl”) (NYSE: OWL) and part of Blue Owl’s Credit platform.
