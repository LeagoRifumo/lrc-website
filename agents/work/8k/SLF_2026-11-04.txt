Sunlife Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Sunlife's(1) Q3 results reflect growth across Canada, Asia, U.S. stop loss, and GreenOak, with double-digit growth in underlying EPS.
TORONTO, ON - (November 4, 2026) - Sun Life Financial Inc.(1) (TSX: SLF) (NYSE: SLF) announced its results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
•Underlying net income(2) of $1,146 million increased $99 million or 9% from Q3'25.
•Reported net income(3) of $785 million decreased $321 million or 29% from Q3'25.
•Underlying EPS(2)(4) of $2.05 increased 10% from Q3'25; reported EPS(4) of $1.40 decreased 29% from Q3'25.
•Underlying return on equity ("ROE")(2) was 19.0%; reported ROE(2) was 13.0%.
•Assets under management ("AUM")(2) of $1,676 billion increased $53 billion or 3% from Q3'25.
•SLF Inc. LICAT ratio of 142%(5).
•Increase to common share dividend from $0.96 to $1.00 per share.
“Sunlife delivered a solid third quarter, with growth across Canada, Asia, U.S. stop loss, and GreenOak. Our diversified global strategy delivered robust continued momentum in insurance and wealth led by Canada and Asia, and new sources of growth in asset management,” said Kevin Strain, President and CEO of Sunlife.
“Our Purpose remains at the heart of how we create value for Clients. We’re using AI and digital capabilities to create better outcomes and more seamless experiences for our Clients. Our new Sunlife and GreenOak brands align to our Purpose and strategy and reflect how our businesses have evolved over the last decade, positioning us to accelerate into our next chapter.”

Underlying net income(12) of $1,146 million increased $99 million or 9% from prior year, driven by:
•Strong business growth across Canada, the U.S. and Asia, higher fee-related earnings in GreenOak(13), and favourable net investment results; partially offset by
•Less favourable insurance experience in Canada and higher financing costs supporting the acquisition of our remaining interests in GreenOak subsidiaries.
Reported net income of $785 million decreased $321 million or 29% from prior year, driven by:
•Unfavourable interest rate impacts;
•Acquisition and restructuring charges primarily in Sun Life Asset Management related to the integration of GreenOak subsidiaries and the Bell Partners Inc. acquisition(14); and
•A prior year gain from increased ownership interest in Bowtie(15); partially offset by
•The increase in underlying net income.
Foreign exchange translation led to an increase of $7 million in underlying net income and an increase of $8 million in reported net income.
Underlying ROE was 19.0% and reported ROE was 13.0% (Q3'25 - 18.3% and 19.3%, respectively). SLF Inc. ended the quarter with a LICAT ratio of 142%.
Business Group Highlights
Sun Life Asset Management: A global leader in asset management
Sun Life Asset Management underlying net income of US$297 million increased US$16 million or 6% from prior year, driven by:
•MFS(16) down US$1 million, as lower fee income, impacted by average fee rates, was offset by lower operating expenses and higher net investment income. Pre-tax net operating profit margin(2) improved to 40.7% for Q3'26, compared to 39.2% in the prior year, primarily driven by the impact from the sale of closed-end funds(17).
•GreenOak up US$13 million, reflecting higher fee-related earnings(2), which increased 39%, driven by the acquisition of Bell Partners Inc.(14) and lower compensation expenses, partially offset by lower net seed investment income. Fee-related earnings margin(2) was 27.8% for Q3'26, compared to 25.7% in the prior year.
•Solutions & Other up US$4 million, reflecting improved credit experience.
Reported net income of US$209 million decreased US$55 million or 21% from prior year, driven by:
•Acquisition and restructuring charges primarily related to the integration of GreenOak subsidiaries and the Bell Partners Inc. acquisition(14); and
•Unfavourable ACMA(18) impacts in Solutions & Other; partially offset by
•The increase in underlying net income and a gain on sale of MFS’ closed-end funds(17).
Foreign exchange translation led to an increase of $5 million in underlying net income and an increase of $4 million in reported net income.
Sun Life Asset Management gross flows(2) decreased US$7.7 billion or 18% from prior year, reflecting lower gross flows in MFS, partially offset by higher gross flows in GreenOak and Solutions & Other.
Total Sun Life Asset Management AUM(2) at Q3'26 was US$903.7 billion (Q3'25 - US$903.3 billion), consisting of:
•MFS: US$618.7 billion (Q3'25 - US$658.7 billion);
•GreenOak: US$205.2 billion (Q3'25 - US$186.0 billion); and
•Solutions & Other: US$79.8 billion (Q3'25 - US$58.6 billion).
Total Sun Life Asset Management managed assets(2) at Q3'26 of US$1,018.6 billion was in line with prior year.

On August 25, 2026, Sunlife entered into a definitive agreement with Wilton Re(19) to establish a strategic reinsurance and asset management partnership. The arrangement combines Wilton Re’s liability management expertise with Sunlife’s insurance and asset management capabilities. Wilton Re will establish Windsor Life Re, a U.S. and Bermuda-domiciled reinsurer, with GreenOak serving as lead asset manager, leveraging its insurance asset management expertise and multi-asset investment platform. The partnership is expected to increase our scale and presence in insurance asset management while providing strategic access to permanent capital to accelerate the growth of GreenOak. The partnership is expected to launch in the first half of 2027, subject to required regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions.
On October 26, 2026, we launched GreenOak, SLC Management’s new global brand. Our ambition is to become a leading global alternative asset manager, bringing the strength of our five businesses together as one enterprise. GreenOak, our new brand, is the first visible expression of the integrated platform we are becoming.

We are making it seamless for Clients to manage their health, wealth, and insurance needs through our cohesive digital and AI ecosystem. During the quarter, we launched Online Counselling, by Dialogue, providing Clients convenient access to additional mental health support alongside a growing range of health services. By integrating care, coverage, and health expertise into one connected experience, we are contributing to healthier, more resilient workplaces and communities.
Sunlife was recognized with two 2026 Global Insurance Innovation Awards: Best Mobile Insurance App (Canada) and Best Digital Insurance Company (Canada), presented by The Global Insurer. U.S.: A leader in health and benefits
U.S. underlying net income of US$141 million increased US$34 million or 32% from prior year, and includes:
•Strong medical stop-loss results reflecting double-digit premium growth and improved morbidity experience;
•Higher earnings in Dental reflecting improved morbidity experience and lower expenses, partially offset by lower revenue;
•Favourable In-force management results driven by mortality experience and net investment results; and
•Lower earnings in Employee Benefits reflecting unfavourable morbidity experience from higher long-term disability claims severity and volumes.
Reported net income of US$77 million increased US$5 million or 7% from prior year, driven by:
•The increase in underlying net income and ACMA impacts; partially offset by
•Unfavourable interest rate impacts.
Foreign exchange translation led to an increase of $3 million in underlying net income and an increase of $2 million in reported net income.
U.S. sales of US$333 million were up 22% from prior year, primarily driven by:
•Higher medical stop-loss sales reflecting strong close rates, continued pricing discipline supported by our risk selection tools, and favourable market conditions.
•Higher commercial sales in Dental aligned with our strategic growth focus, reflecting higher average case sizes, were offset by lower Medicare Advantage sales.

We are leveraging the strength of our U.S. health businesses and employee benefits capabilities to deliver a more connected and differentiated Client experience. During the quarter, we added Health Navigator to our disability offering to help members get the care they need sooner, which can drive better health outcomes and reduce the cost of care. Members facing potentially disabling health conditions will now have access to our personalized care advisory services. Paired with our return-to-work program, this provides a more holistic path to returning members to health and productivity after a major health event.
We also began working with Claritev, a healthcare technology, data, and insights company, to help self-funded employers deliver a more seamless supplemental health benefits experience. Claritev’s machine-learning technology identifies diagnosis connections between employees' medical claims and supplemental health coverages, enabling covered employees to efficiently access the benefits they are eligible for. This claims technology creates a more cohesive and proactive approach while accelerating employee supplemental health payments to get members care when they need it most.
Asia: A regional leader focused on fast-growing markets
Asia underlying net income of $226 million increased $15 million or 7% from prior year, driven by:
•Continued strong sales momentum and in-force business growth, favourable credit experience and lower expenses; partially offset by
•Lower contributions from joint ventures, less favourable mortality experience in High Net Worth, and lower fee income from the transitioning of the administration business to the centralized eMPF platform in Hong Kong.
Reported net income of $123 million decreased $236 million or 66% from prior year, driven by:
•Unfavourable ACMA impacts and a prior-year gain from increased ownership interest in Bowtie; partially offset by
•Improved interest rate impacts and the increase in underlying net income.
Foreign exchange translation led to a decrease of $1 million in underlying net income and reported net income, respectively.
Asia's sales(22):
•Individual insurance sales of $935 million were up 9%, driven by:
◦Higher sales in Hong Kong across all channels; and
◦Strong growth in India and Malaysia, primarily from the bancassurance channel; partially offset by
◦Lower sales in China primarily from the broker channel.
•Asset management gross flows and wealth sales of $1 billion were up 35%, reflecting higher Mandatory Provident Fund ("MPF") sales in Hong Kong and higher group fund sales in India.
New business CSM of $280 million in Q3'26 was down from $322 million in the prior year, reflecting an increasing competitive environment primarily in Hong Kong.
We continue to strengthen our leadership in serving High-Net-Worth Clients through the launch of Sun Life Private Wealth, an integrated platform that brings together our global High-Net-Worth capabilities. Building on more than three decades of experience in High-Net-Worth insurance, Sun Life Private Wealth combines our financial strength, specialist expertise and deep local knowledge with integrated underwriting and servicing capabilities. This enables a more seamless experience across key global wealth hubs, helping Clients navigate increasingly complex cross-border wealth planning, preservation and intergenerational wealth transfer needs.
We are also enhancing the Client and advisor experience through continued investments in our digital capabilities. In Indonesia, we implemented automated claims adjudication enhancements in the quarter for medical claims, increasing straight through processing by nearly 20% within two months, enabling a faster, more seamless experience for Clients. Additionally, we launched an AI-powered productivity assistant for advisors across the region, enabling more efficient performance of daily activities.
Corporate
Underlying net loss was $115 million compared to underlying net loss of $95 million in the prior year, reflecting higher financing costs supporting the acquisition of our remaining interests in GreenOak subsidiaries.
Reported net loss was $156 million compared to reported net loss of $92 million in the prior year, driven by the estimated impact of changes in interest rates on tax-exempt investments and the change in underlying net loss.
During the quarter, we announced a Commitment to Canadian Infrastructure Initiative, seeking to deploy $5 billion over five years into investments supporting Canada’s economic growth and resilience, while delivering long-term returns. As part of this commitment, we intend to deploy $1.5 billion over five years into Canadian infrastructure equity overseen by GreenOak and originated, executed and managed by InfraRed Capital Partners. This part of the commitment relies on amendments to the Insurance Companies Act which would allow insurers to make equity investments in infrastructure. The investment capacity reflects Sunlife's role as a long-term institutional investor, and our ability to source and mobilize capital in support of Canadian infrastructure.

About Sunlife
Sunlife is a leading international financial services organization providing asset management, wealth, insurance and health solutions to individual and institutional Clients. Sunlife has operations in a number of markets worldwide including Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Hong Kong, the Philippines, Japan, Indonesia, India, China, Australia, Singapore, Vietnam, Malaysia, and Bermuda. As of September 30, 2026, Sunlife had total assets under management ("AUM")(1) of $1.68 trillion. For more information, please visit www.sunlife.com.
Sun Life Financial Inc. trades on the Toronto (TSX), New York (NYSE) and Philippine (PSE) stock exchanges under the ticker symbol SLF.
A. How We Report Our Results
Sun Life Financial Inc. ("SLF Inc."), its subsidiaries and, where applicable, its joint ventures and associates are collectively referred to as "the Company", "Sunlife", "we", "our", and "us". We manage our operations and report our financial results in five business groups/segments: Sun Life Asset Management, Canada, United States ("U.S."), Asia, and Corporate. Information concerning these segments is included in our annual and interim consolidated financial statements and accompanying notes ("Annual Consolidated Financial Statements" and "Interim Consolidated Financial Statements", respectively, and "Consolidated Financial Statements" collectively) and interim and annual management's discussion and analysis ("MD&A"). We prepare our unaudited Interim Consolidated Financial Statements using International Financial Reporting Standards ("IFRS"), the accounting requirements of the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions ("OSFI") and in accordance with the International Accounting Standard ("IAS") 34 Interim Financial Reporting. Reported net income (loss) refers to Common shareholders' net income (loss) determined in accordance with IFRS.
Unless otherwise noted, all amounts are in Canadian dollars. Amounts in this document may be impacted by rounding.

1. Life Insurance Capital Adequacy Test
The Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions has developed the regulatory capital framework referred to as the Life Insurance Capital Adequacy Test for Canada. LICAT measures the capital adequacy of an insurer using a risk-based approach and includes elements that contribute to financial strength through periods when an insurer is under stress as well as elements that contribute to policyholder and creditor protection wind-up.
SLF Inc. is a non-operating insurance company and is subject to the LICAT guideline. Sun Life Assurance, SLF Inc.'s principal operating life insurance subsidiary, is also subject to the LICAT guideline.
SLF Inc.'s LICAT ratio of 142% as at September 30, 2026 decreased fifteen percentage points compared to December 31, 2025, driven by M&A(1) activity, shareholder dividend payments, and market impacts, partially offset by subordinated debt issuance.
Sun Life Assurance's LICAT ratio of 130% as at September 30, 2026 decreased ten percentage points compared to December 31, 2025, driven by dividend payments to SLF Inc. and market impacts.
The Sun Life Assurance LICAT ratios in both periods are well above OSFI's supervisory ratio of 100% and regulatory minimum ratio of 90%.
