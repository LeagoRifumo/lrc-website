Bilibili Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
SHANGHAI, China, November 24, 2026 — Bilibili Inc. (“Bilibili” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: BILI and HKEX: 9626), an iconic brand and a leading video community for young generations in China, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:
• | Average daily active users (DAUs) were 123.5 million, representing an increase of 5% year over year. Average daily time spent was 121 minutes, driving total user time spent up by 14% year over year.

• | Total net revenues were RMB8.32 billion (US$1.24 billion), representing an increase of 8% year over year. Advertising revenues, a key driver of total net revenues, were RMB3.09 billion (US$460.4 million), representing an increase of 20% year over year.

• | Gross profit was RMB3.10 billion (US$462.8 million), representing an increase of 10% year over year. Gross profit margin reached 37.3%, improving from 36.7% in the same period of 2025.

• | Net profit was RMB504.1 million (US$75.2 million), representing an increase of 7% year over year.

• | Adjusted net profit1 was RMB828.2 million (US$123.5 million), representing an increase of 5% year over year.

“We drove healthy growth in the third quarter, with record community engagement alongside steady revenue gains and improving profitability,” said Mr. Rui Chen, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Bilibili. “DAUs climbed to nearly 124 million, monthly active users increased to 394 million, and average daily time spent rose to 121 minutes, each surpassing its previous peak. High-quality content is at the heart of our momentum. It draws users to Bilibili, gives them reason to stay and fosters genuine connections around shared passions. We are using AI to amplify these strengths, helping creators make quality content more easily and connecting it more efficiently with the users who value it most. While AI is reshaping much of the landscape, we believe that great content and vibrant communities remain the key to capturing users’ intentional time and earning their long-term loyalty. In today’s crowded market, this is Bilibili’s irreplaceable edge.”
Mr. Sam Fan, Chief Financial Officer of Bilibili, said, “Our financial performance continued to improve in the third quarter, with steady revenue growth and further margin expansion. Total net revenues grew 8% year over year, with advertising revenues delivering 20% year-over-year growth despite a challenging macro environment. Gross profit rose 10%, and gross margin improved to 37.3% from 36.7% a year ago, while operating profit and net profit grew 32% and 7% year over year, respectively. Continued improvement in profitability gives us greater flexibility to invest for the long term while maintaining financial discipline. We are focused on advancing our AI capabilities across video understanding, distribution and creation. We believe these investments will strengthen our foundation, sharpen our unique edge, and create value for our shareholders over time.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total net revenues. Total net revenues were RMB8.32 billion (US$1.24 billion), representing an increase of 8% from the same period of 2025.
Advertising. Revenues from advertising were RMB3.09 billion (US$460.4 million), representing an increase of 20% from the same period of 2025, mainly attributable to the Company’s improved advertising product offerings and enhanced advertising efficiency.
Value-added services (VAS). Revenues from VAS were RMB3.15 billion (US$469.6 million), representing an increase of 4% from the same period of 2025, mainly attributable to increased revenues from premium memberships and other value-added services.
Mobile games. Revenues from mobile games were RMB1.41 billion (US$210.7 million), representing a 7% year-over-year decrease and a 1% quarter-over-quarter increase. The year-over-year decrease was primarily attributable to a high base in the prior period, with the pace of decline continuing to narrow as San Guo: Mou Ding Tian Xia entered a more stable operating phase.
IP derivatives and others. Revenues from IP derivatives and others were RMB670.1 million (US$100.0 million), representing an increase of 15% from the same period of 2025, mainly attributable to increased revenues from sales of IP derivatives and from offline events.
Cost of revenues. Cost of revenues was RMB5.21 billion (US$777.8 million), representing an increase of 7% from the same period of 2025. Revenue-sharing costs, a key component of cost of revenues, were RMB3.11 billion (US$463.2 million), representing an increase of 2% from the same period of 2025.
Gross profit. Gross profit was RMB3.10 billion (US$462.8 million), representing an increase of 10% from the same period of 2025, primarily driven by the growth in total net revenues, which outpaced the increase in cost of revenues, as the Company enhanced its monetization efficiency.
Total operating expenses. Total operating expenses were RMB2.63 billion (US$392.9 million), representing an increase of 7% from the same period of 2025.
Sales and marketing expenses. Sales and marketing expenses were RMB1.12 billion (US$166.4 million), representing an increase of 6% from the same period of 2025.
General and administrative expenses. General and administrative expenses were RMB510.9 million (US$76.2 million), relatively flat compared with the same period of 2025.
Research and development expenses. Research and development expenses were RMB1.01 billion (US$150.3 million), representing an increase of 11% from the same period of 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher expenses related to server depreciation.
Profit from operations. Profit from operations was RMB468.7 million (US$69.9 million), representing an increase of 32% year over year.
Adjusted profit from operations1. Adjusted profit from operations was RMB807.4 million (US$120.4 million), representing an increase of 17% from the same period of 2025.
Total other income, net. Total other income, net was RMB56.9 million (US$8.5 million), compared with RMB100.7 million in the same period of 2025.
Income tax expense/benefit. Income tax expense was RMB21.5 million (US$3.2 million), compared with income tax benefit of RMB14.8 million in the same period of 2025.
Net profit. Net profit was RMB504.1 million (US$75.2 million), representing an increase of 7% from the same period of 2025.
Adjusted net profit1. Adjusted net profit was RMB828.2 million (US$123.5 million), representing an increase of 5% from the same period of 2025.
Basic and diluted EPS and adjusted basic and diluted EPS1. Basic and diluted net profit per share were RMB1.22 (US$0.18) and RMB1.14 (US$0.17), respectively, compared with basic and diluted net profit per share of RMB1.13 and RMB1.05, respectively, in the same period of 2025. Adjusted basic and diluted net profit per share were RMB2.00 (US$0.30) and RMB1.85 (US$0.28), respectively, compared with RMB1.89 and RMB1.75, respectively, in the same period of 2025.
Cash and cash equivalents, time deposits and short-term investments. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents, time deposits and short-term investments of RMB25.71 billion (US$3.83 billion).
Recent Developments
In September 2026, the Company completed an offering of US$500 million in aggregate principal amount of zero-interest convertible senior notes due 2031 (the “Notes”). The Company used approximately US$100 million of the offering proceeds to concurrently repurchase 6,795,540 Class Z ordinary shares (each represented by one ADS) (the “Concurrent Repurchase”), leaving approximately US$400 million in gross proceeds before offering costs.
In addition, the Company entered into an agreement with subsidiaries of Tencent Holdings Limited for the subscription of an additional US$200 million in aggregate principal amount of the Notes (the “Tencent Notes Subscription”) and a concurrent repurchase of US$200 million of its Class Z ordinary shares (the “Concurrent Tencent Repurchase”). The Tencent Notes Subscription and Concurrent Tencent Repurchase were approved by the Company’s disinterested shareholders on October 28, 2026, and were completed on November 5, 2026.
The Concurrent Repurchase and the Concurrent Tencent Repurchase were made pursuant to the Company’s special share repurchase program (the “Special Share Repurchase Program”), which is separate from the Company’s two-year US$300 million share repurchase program approved by the Board of Directors in June 2026 (the “2026 Program”). Under the Special Share Repurchase Program, the Company repurchased a total of 20,386,630 Class Z ordinary shares for an aggregate amount of US$300 million. All of these shares were cancelled as of the date of this announcement, reducing the Company’s total number of issued shares by approximately 4.9% from the level immediately prior to the repurchases under the Special Share Repurchase Program.
Pursuant to the 2026 Program, a total of 2.9 million of the Company’s listed securities have been purchased for a total cost of approximately US$49.2 million as of the date of this announcement, with the remaining amount under the 2026 Program being approximately US$250.8 million.

About Bilibili Inc.
Bilibili is an iconic brand and a leading video community with a mission to enrich the everyday lives of young generations in China. Bilibili offers a wide array of video-based content with “All the Videos You Like” as its value proposition. Bilibili builds its community around aspiring users, high-quality content, talented content creators and the strong emotional bonds among them. Bilibili pioneered the “bullet chatting” feature, a live comment function that has transformed users’ viewing experience by displaying the thoughts and feelings of audience members viewing the same video. The Company has now become the welcoming home of diverse interests among young generations in China and a frontier for promoting Chinese culture around the world.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The Company uses non-GAAP financial measures such as adjusted profit from operations, adjusted net profit, adjusted net profit margin, adjusted net profit per share and per ADS, basic and diluted and adjusted net profit attributable to Bilibili Inc.’s shareholders in evaluating its operating results and for financial and operational decision-making purposes. The Company believes that the non-GAAP financial measures help identify underlying trends in its business by excluding the impact of share-based compensation expenses, amortization expense related to intangible assets acquired through business acquisitions, income tax related to intangible assets acquired through business acquisitions, gain/loss on fair value change in investments in publicly traded companies and loss on repurchase of convertible senior notes. The Company calculates adjusted net profit margin by dividing the adjusted net profit by revenue for the same period. The Company believes that the non-GAAP financial measures provide useful information about the Company’s results of operations, enhance the overall understanding of the Company’s past performance and future prospects and allow for greater visibility with respect to key metrics used by the Company’s management in its financial and operational decision-making.
The non-GAAP financial measures are not defined under U.S. GAAP and are not presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP and therefore, may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies. The non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools, and when assessing the Company’s operating performance, cash flows or liquidity, investors should not consider them in isolation, or as a substitute for net profit, cash flows provided by operating activities or other consolidated statements of operations and cash flows data prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP.
The Company mitigates these limitations by reconciling the non-GAAP financial measures to the most comparable U.S. GAAP performance measures, all of which should be considered when evaluating the Company’s performance.
