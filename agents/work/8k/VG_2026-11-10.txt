ARLINGTON, Va., November 10, 2026 – Venture Global, Inc. ("Venture Global," "we," or "our") (NYSE: VG) has reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. As a reminder, Venture Global will host a conference call for investors and analysts beginning at 9:00 am Eastern Time (ET), November 10, 2026, to discuss third quarter results.
"Venture Global had an exceptional third quarter, with record sales and earnings, enabled by our team's best-in-class execution and LNG production at the high end of our anticipated range. We are proud to have commenced commercial operations at Plaquemines Phase 1 on October 31st and begun delivering on our long-term contracts at one of the fastest rates for a greenfield LNG project of this scale. Our strong operational performance and outlook on pricing have allowed us to raise the midpoint of our volume and Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA guidance for FY 2026, and we remain confident in our business heading into next year," said Venture Global CEO Mike Sabel.
"We are also excited about a potential new, additive line of business for the Company: expanding our existing and proven capabilities building and operating an island-mode power and infrastructure network to be able to offer an off-grid AI utility to support data centers. Venture Global can provide access to island-mode power, land, closed-loop water systems, American natural gas supply, our in-house EPC, and track record of execution as the market dictates to support compute capacity for the country’s AI race. By leveraging this natural extension of our Company’s core competencies, we can solve for the predominant challenges to the data center build out similar to how we solved the key speed and scale challenges for the LNG market.”

LNG Facility Updates
Calcasieu Pass: In the third quarter, the Calcasieu facility produced 37 cargos in line with prior expectations and surpassing our SPA obligations. With cooler weather and completion of summer maintenance, we expect modestly higher fourth quarter production. For 2027, we are planning incremental investments during the year which we expect should help enhance production volumes from the facility starting late next year.
Plaquemines: We commenced commercial operations at Phase 1 of the Plaquemines Project on October 31, 2026, and as of today have loaded six cargos for our SPA customers. COD for Plaquemines Phase 2 remains on target for mid-2027. We continue to progress early development of the Plaquemines brownfield expansion and have now ordered much of the long lead-time equipment including eight liquefaction trains and gas compressor systems for the Cloud Connector Pipeline to support Phase 1 of the expansion. Subject to regulatory approvals, we are targeting FID in mid 2027 and first LNG in 2029.
CP2: Construction at our CP2 Project continues to advance well, and given the tremendous progress we have made since Phase 1 FID in July 2025, we are narrowing the timeline for first LNG production to mid-year 2027. We now have 24 of the 36 liquefaction modules on site, as well as much of the gas pretreatment and power equipment. This should position the project to begin production approximately two years from FID, which we believe is substantially faster than any other LNG project of this scale to date. We are also making progress on the 10 mtpa CP2 brownfield expansion and are targeting FID in early 2027 with first production in late 2028.

Island-Mode AI Utility Frame Agreement Updates
In October 2026, Venture Global entered into frame agreements with GE Vernova and Baker Hughes Energy Services for up to 16 new, 720 MW power island units and associated power transformer and grid equipment. The agreements support the company's potential expansion of its existing island-mode power and infrastructure network to support the country’s data center buildout.
This marks a defining step in Venture Global's evolution into what we believe will be the first fully vertically integrated AI Utility, combining energy and infrastructure as a single turnkey offering supporting America's data centers. These agreements create a flexible framework to obtain such power generation and transformer equipment, among the scarcest resources in the market today. In this way, we believe Venture Global is able to solve one of the biggest constraints on new compute capacity and can deliver power to customers years ahead of traditional grid-connected timelines. The agreements build directly on the strengths we have developed over more than a decade of delivering large-scale LNG infrastructure: approximately 15,000 acres of controlled land, access to roughly 13 bcf/d of pipeline capacity, about 4 GW of existing power, and a 1,000-person in-house execution and EPC team with deep experience designing, constructing, commissioning, and operating island-mode grids. With two sites in Cameron and Plaquemines Parishes, about 250 miles apart, Venture Global expects to offer reliable, large-scale compute capacity without any reliance on public electricity or water supply.
We believe Venture Global is positioned to begin delivering compute capacity in late 2028 and to scale to approximately 5 GW by the end of 2031. This disciplined approach mirrors our proven LNG model and creates a growth platform that complements our existing business.

About Venture Global
Venture Global is an American producer and exporter of low-cost U.S. LNG with over 100 MTPA of capacity in production, construction, or development. Venture Global began producing LNG from its first facility in 2022 and is now one of the largest LNG exporters in the United States. The company’s vertically integrated business includes assets across the LNG supply chain including LNG production, natural gas transport, shipping and regasification. The company’s first three projects, Calcasieu, Plaquemines, and CP2, are located in Louisiana along the Gulf of America. Venture Global is developing carbon capture and sequestration projects at each of its LNG facilities.

Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Measures
This earnings release contains references to Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA, which is not required by, or presented in accordance with, generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”).
We believe Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA provides investors and other users of our consolidated financial statements with useful supplemental information to evaluate the financial performance of our business on an unleveraged basis, to enable comparison of our operating performance across periods. Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA also allows investors and other users of our financial statements to evaluate our operating performance in a manner that is consistent with management’s evaluation of financial and operating performance.
We define Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA as net income attributable to common stockholders of Venture Global, Inc., as determined in accordance with GAAP, adjusted to exclude net income attributable to non-controlling interests, income taxes, gain/loss on interest rate swaps, gain/loss on financing transactions, interest expense, net of capitalized interest, interest income, depreciation and amortization, stock-based compensation expense, gain/loss from changes in the fair value of forward natural gas supply contracts, and gain/loss from changes in exchange rates on foreign currency transactions. We believe the exclusion of these items enables investors and other users of our consolidated financial statements to assess our sequential and year-over-year performance and operating trends on a more comparable basis.
Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA has material limitations as an analytical tool and should be viewed as a supplement to and not a substitute for measures of performance, financial results and cash flow from operations calculated in accordance with GAAP. For example, Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA excludes certain recurring, non-cash charges such as stock-based compensation expense and gain/loss from changes in the fair value of forward natural gas supply contracts, and does not reflect changes in, or cash requirements for, our working capital needs. In addition, although depreciation and amortization are non-cash charges, the assets being depreciated and amortized may have to be replaced in the future, and Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect cash requirements for such replacements. Other companies, including companies in our industry, may also calculate Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA differently, which may limit its usefulness as a comparative measure.
