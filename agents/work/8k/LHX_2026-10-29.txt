L3Harris Delivers Record Backlog and Strong Third-Quarter Results
Broad-based growth with robust earnings and cash flow; increases full-year guidance
•Orders of $19.3 billion drove record backlog of $53.2 billion; book-to-bill of 3.2x
•Revenue of $6.1 billion, up 7%, and up 9% on an organic1 basis
•Operating income margin of 13.0%, up 200 bps vs. prior year; segment operating margin2 of 15.9%
•Diluted EPS of $3.28, up 33% year-over-year
•Operating cash flow of $1.4 billion; free cash flow3 of $1.3 billion, up 195% compared to prior year
•Guidance Update: Increases bottom end of the revenue guidance range to $23.5 billion to $23.7 billion (prev. $23.2 billion to $23.7 billion), increases GAAP EPS range to $12.25 to $12.35 (prev. $11.80 to $12.00)
MELBOURNE, Fla., October 29, 2026 — L3Harris Technologies (NYSE: LHX) reported its third quarter 2026 results.
“Our team’s exceptional execution delivered solid financial performance in the third quarter. We grew backlog to record levels, underscoring the consistently strong demand for our mission-critical capabilities. Our priority now is to convert that demand into results. We will continue to invest in production capacity and differentiated capabilities across missiles and missile solutions, space, resilient communications and multi-domain sensing, which will enable us to deliver for the warfighter, our customers and our shareholders,” said Sam Mehta, President and CEO, L3Harris.
Third-Quarter Summary
Revenue of $6.1 billion increased $401 million compared to prior year, up 7%, and up 9% on an organic1 basis, driven by double-digit growth in the Space & Mission Systems and Missile Solutions segments. Operating income margin was 13.0%, up 200 bps compared to prior year, driven by higher revenues and continued management of corporate expenses. Segment operating margin2 was 15.9%, flat to prior year. Third quarter GAAP EPS increased 33% to $3.28 driven by higher volumes, a lower level of corporate expense, lower interest expense and a lower effective tax rate.
Third-Quarter Cash Generation
Net cash provided by operating activities in the quarter totaled $1.4 billion. Capital expenditures were $179 million. Free cash flow3 was $1.3 billion, up 195% over prior year. The Company ended the quarter with $11.0 billion in total debt, $2.5 billion in cash and equivalents and $550 million in short-term investments.

Backlog and Orders
Orders received in the quarter totaled $19.3 billion, resulting in backlog of $53.2 billion. Book-to-bill ratio was 3.2x for the quarter. By segment, Space & Mission Systems book-to-bill was 1.0x, Communications & Spectrum Dominance was 1.9x and Missile Solutions was 11.8x.
FY26 Guidance
Management increases the bottom end of the revenue guidance range to $23.5 billion to $23.7 billion (prev. $23.2 billion to $23.7 billion), increases GAAP EPS range to $12.25 to $12.35 (prev. $11.80 to $12.00) and maintains FY26 Free Cash Flow guidance of $3 billion3.
Third Quarter & Recent Business Highlights
•Captured a $6.3 billion (excluding escalations), seven-year undefinitized contract to scale propulsion production for the Terminal High Altitude Area Defense (THAAD) system
•Captured a $4.7 billion (excluding escalations), seven-year undefinitized contract to scale propulsion production for the PAC-3 Missile Segment Enhancement (MSE) interceptor
•Announced a $250 million follow-on contract to manufacture standard-range Guided Multiple Launch Rocket System (GMLRS) propulsion units
•Secured more than $500 million in VAMPIRE C-UAS orders from the United States and allies. Delivered the first VAMPIRE units to the U.S. Army for hostile drone defense just two months after receiving their order
•Awarded an $876 million contract modification to advance the development and production preparation of the U.S. Navy Next Generation Jammer Low Band (NGJ-LB) tactical electronic warfare system
•Opened the ‘Arsenal of Freedom’ solid rocket motor production facility in Camden, Arkansas in October – one of approximately 60 new facilities being built to address rising demand for munitions
•Completed the launch of NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope with L3Harris' 2.4-meter Optical Telescope Assembly (OTA), which serves as Roman’s eye, as well as L3Harris’ radiation-hardened electronics, which keep the telescope in operation approximately 1 million miles from Earth
