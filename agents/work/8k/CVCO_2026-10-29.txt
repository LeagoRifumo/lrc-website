CAVCO INDUSTRIES REPORTS FISCAL 2027 SECOND QUARTER RESULTS
PHOENIX, October 29, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Cavco Industries, Inc. (Nasdaq: CVCO) ("we," "our," the "Company" or "Cavco") today announced financial results for the second fiscal quarter ended September 26, 2026.
Quarterly Highlights
•Net revenue was $656.4 million, up $100 million or 17.9% compared to $556.5 million in the second quarter of the prior year, primarily on home sales volume and average selling price per home growth.
•Home sales volume was up 15.3% and capacity utilization remained consistent year over year at approximately 75%.
•Factory-built housing Gross profit as a percentage of Net revenue was 21.1%, down from 22.9% in the same period in the prior year.
•Financial services Gross profit as a percentage of Net revenue was 60.2%, compared to Gross profit of 55.6% in the same period in the prior year.
•Selling, general and administrative expenses include a one-time $7 million benefit from a return of insurance premium which favorably impacts Net income per diluted share by $0.70.
•Income before income taxes was $73 million, up $6 million, or 9.1% compared to $67 million in the same period in the prior year.
•Net income per diluted share was $7.17, up 9%, compared to $6.55 in the prior year quarter.
•Backlogs totaled $348 million at the end of the quarter representing 8-10 weeks of production, up $50 million sequentially from $298 million.
•Stock repurchases were approximately $30 million in the quarter. At the end of the second quarter, $158 million remains available for repurchases under our previously announced Board authorizations.
Commenting on the quarter, President and Chief Executive Officer Bill Boor said, "This is our second consecutive quarter of record revenue and unit volume. While orders were off the preceding quarter pace, they remained healthy and backlogs continued to grow. This positions us very well going into the seasonally slower winter months with the ability to adjust production depending on order trends. While materials inflation continued and is expected to remain a challenge going forward, during Q2, selling price increases were in excess of cost pressures, resulting in a small sequential Gross Margin improvement. Additionally, we began to see data center workforce housing orders and shipments during the quarter which are contributing to healthy backlogs."
He continued, "A highlight this quarter was showing Cavco’s Grande Arbor, the first off-chassis HUD-Code home, at HUD’s Innovative Housing Showcase in Washington DC. Increasing federal and state-level support through the ROAD to Housing Act and other reforms will be opening exciting opportunities for factory-built housing in the years ahead."

About Cavco
Headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona, Cavco Industries, Inc. (Nasdaq: CVCO) designs and builds manufactured homes and modular homes, distributed nationwide through a network of independent and Company-owned retailers. Cavco is one of the largest manufactured and modular home builders in the United States, based on reported wholesale shipments, and is also a leading producer of park model RVs, vacation cabins, and factory-built commercial structures. Cavco's finance subsidiary, CountryPlace Mortgage, is an approved Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac seller/servicer and a Ginnie Mae mortgage-backed securities issuer, offering manufactured home loans — including conforming, non-conforming, and home-only financing — to buyers of manufactured and modular homes. Cavco's insurance subsidiary, Standard Casualty, provides manufactured home insurance and other property and casualty coverage to manufactured homeowners. Learn more about Cavco's manufactured homes and modular homes at www.cavcohomes.com.
