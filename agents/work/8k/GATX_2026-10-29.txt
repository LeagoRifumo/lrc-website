GATX CORPORATION REPORTS 2026 THIRD-QUARTER RESULTS
•Third-quarter diluted earnings per share increased to $2.92 from $2.25 in the prior-year period
•Company expects 2026 full-year earnings to modestly exceed the high end of its previously announced guidance range
•Rail North America fleet utilization increased to 98.4% at quarter end
•Demand for aircraft spare engines remains strong
•Investment volume was $171.7 million in the third quarter and totaled over $4.8 billion year to date
CHICAGO, Oct. 29, 2026—GATX Corporation (NYSE: GATX) today reported 2026 third-quarter net income attributable to GATX of $105.2 million, or $2.92 per diluted share, compared to net income attributable to GATX of $82.2 million, or $2.25 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Net income attributable to GATX for the first nine months of 2026 was $294.1 million, or $8.11 per diluted share, compared to $236.3 million, or $6.46 per diluted share, in the prior year period.
The 2025 third-quarter and year-to-date results include a net positive impact of $5.3 million, or $0.15 per diluted share, from Tax Adjustments and Other Items. Details related to these items are provided in the attached Supplemental Information under Tax Adjustments and Other Items.
"We delivered another quarter of outstanding performance, supported by favorable leasing fundamentals and strong execution," said Robert C. Lyons, president and chief executive officer of GATX. "The overall dynamics for railcar leasing in North America remain positive. Rail traffic is stable or modestly growing for most commodities, attrition in the industry railcar fleet continues, and the supply of new railcars is limited. This drives favorable leasing dynamics, evidenced by our high renewal success rate (86.1%), solid LPI (12.4%), and increased fleet utilization (98.4%). On top of this, the demand for railcars in the secondary market is extremely strong, underscoring the attractive value of our existing fleet. Net gains on asset dispositions were $50.3 million in the quarter and $167.8 million year-to-date.
"GATX Rail Europe's fleet utilization improved to 96.1% at quarter end, and we achieved renewal rate increases across many car types. While economic uncertainty continues across the region, the future of rail transport is positive, and we are well positioned to capitalize on the strength of our existing platform and growth opportunities. In India, demand for railcars remains robust, supported by continued infrastructure investment and economic growth, and GATX Rail India’s fleet

remained fully utilized. During the quarter, we stayed focused on selectively growing and diversifying our international fleets through disciplined investments."
Mr. Lyons added, "At Engine Leasing, our Rolls-Royce and Partners Finance affiliates ("RRPF") and wholly owned engine portfolio delivered solid results. RRPF continues to invest in attractive opportunities, with investment volume approaching $1.0 billion year to date. Engine demand remains robust and global air travel is at healthy levels. While we continue to monitor geopolitical developments and broader industry conditions, we remain confident in the long-term outlook for the business."
Mr. Lyons concluded, "Based on year-to-date performance and our outlook for the remainder of the year, we now expect our 2026 full-year earnings to modestly exceed the high end of our previously announced guidance range of $9.90 to $10.30 per diluted share. Our outlook reflects positive dynamics and strong execution across our global businesses, benefits from the acquisition of Wells Fargo's rail assets and related portfolio management activities, and strong performance at Engine Leasing. This guidance excludes any impact from Tax Adjustments and Other Items."
RAIL NORTH AMERICA
Rail North America reported segment profit of $121.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $70.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Year to date 2026, Rail North America reported segment profit of $343.7 million, compared to $256.1 million in the same period of 2025. Higher 2026 third-quarter and year-to-date results were driven by higher lease revenues, increased management fee revenue from the Brookfield-owned finance lease portfolio, and higher net gains on asset dispositions, partly offset by higher interest, depreciation and maintenance expenses.
As of Sept. 30, 2026, Rail North America’s fleet totaled approximately 199,100 cars, including approximately 9,000 boxcars. The following fleet statistics and performance discussion exclude the boxcar fleet.
Fleet utilization was 98.4% at the end of the third quarter of 2026, compared to 98.0% at the end of the prior quarter, and 98.9% at the end of the third quarter of 2025. During the third quarter of 2026, the renewal lease rate change of the Lease Price Index (LPI) was positive 12.4%, compared to 16.8% in the prior quarter and 22.8% in the third quarter of 2025. The average lease renewal term for all cars included in the LPI during the third quarter of 2026 was 52 months, compared to 54 months in the prior quarter and 60 months in the third quarter of 2025. The 2026 third-quarter renewal success rate was 86.1%, compared to 82.6% in the prior quarter and 87.1% in the third quarter of 2025. Rail North America’s investment volume during the third quarter of 2026 was $109.3 million.
Additional fleet statistics, including information on the boxcar fleet, and macroeconomic data related to Rail North America’s business are provided in the attached Supplemental Information under Rail North America Statistics.

RAIL INTERNATIONAL
Rail International’s segment profit was $35.9 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $34.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. Year to date 2026, Rail International reported segment profit of $99.1 million, compared to $92.3 million in the same period of 2025. Higher 2026 third-quarter segment profit was driven primarily by more railcars on lease, while year-to-date segment profit was driven primarily by more railcars on lease and changes in foreign currency exchange rates.
As of Sept. 30, 2026, GATX Rail Europe’s (GRE) fleet consisted of approximately 36,700 cars. Fleet utilization was 96.1%, compared to 95.3% at the end of the prior quarter and 93.7% at the end of the third quarter of 2025.
As of Sept. 30, 2026, Rail India's fleet consisted of over 12,900 railcars. Fleet utilization was 100.0%, similar to the end of the prior quarter and the end of the third quarter of 2025.
Rail International’s investment volume during the third quarter of 2026 was $49.3 million.
Additional fleet statistics for GRE and Rail India are provided on the last page of this press release.
ENGINE LEASING
Engine Leasing reported segment profit of $51.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to segment profit of $60.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. Year to date 2026, segment profit was $153.1 million, compared to segment profit of $126.3 million in the same period of 2025.
2025 third-quarter and year-to-date results include a net positive impact of $10.9 million ($8.2 million after tax) from Tax Adjustments and Other Items. Additional details are provided in the attached Supplemental Information under Tax Adjustments and Other Items.
Excluding these impacts, 2026 third-quarter and year-to-date results exceeded the prior-year periods, driven primarily by higher operating and remarketing income at RRPF. Year-to-date results also benefited from a larger wholly owned aircraft spare engine portfolio.
As of Sept. 30, 2026, RRPF's portfolio, 50% of which is owned by GATX, consisted of 475 aircraft engines.
As of Sept. 30, 2026, GATX Engine Leasing, the Company’s wholly owned engine portfolio, consisted of 46 aircraft engines.

COMPANY DESCRIPTION
At GATX Corporation (NYSE:GATX), we empower our customers to propel the world forward. GATX leases transportation assets including railcars, aircraft spare engines and tank containers to customers worldwide. Our mission is to provide innovative, unparalleled service that enables our customers to transport what matters safely and sustainably while championing the well-being of our employees and communities. Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois since its founding in 1898, GATX has paid a quarterly dividend, uninterrupted, since 1919.
