Olin Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
Highlights
•Third quarter 2026 net income of $14.4 million, or $0.13 per diluted share
•Quarterly adjusted EBITDA of $191.4 million
•Expect Olin and Huntsman merger to close first quarter 2027
•Substantial progress made with integration planning
Clayton, MO, October 29, 2026 – Olin Corporation (NYSE: OLN) announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Third quarter 2026 reported net income was $14.4 million, or $0.13 per diluted share, which compares to third quarter 2025 reported net income of $42.8 million, or $0.37 per diluted share. Third quarter 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $191.4 million excludes depreciation and amortization expense of $114.4 million, acquisition-related costs of $13.7 million, and restructuring charges of $1.7 million. Third quarter 2025 adjusted EBITDA was $222.4 million. Sales in the third quarter 2026 were $1,747.7 million, compared to $1,713.2 million in the third quarter 2025.
Ken Lane, President and Chief Executive Officer, said, “During the third quarter, Olin delivered adjusted EBITDA consistent with our expectations and restored normal operations at our vinyl chloride monomer (VCM) assets. Our Chlor Alkali Products and Vinyls business maintained disciplined market participation and stable domestic Electrochemical Unit values despite continued challenging global demand. Epoxy earnings improved as structural cost reductions and volume growth offset persistent demand weakness in Europe. Winchester delivered consistent year-over-year commercial volume growth through the third quarter, while pricing actions were implemented to offset higher metals and raw material costs.
“We also achieved two important milestones related to our proposed merger with Huntsman. Shareholders of both companies overwhelmingly approved the transaction, and the waiting period under the U.S. Hart-Scott-Rodino Act expired, satisfying a key closing condition. We continue to see good progress on the remaining regulatory approvals and now expect the transaction to close in first quarter 2027, subject to customary closing
conditions. We are advancing permitted pre-close integration planning to prepare for an expeditious combination and realization of expected synergies.
“Our fourth-quarter outlook reflects normal seasonality, as well as elevated year-end customer destocking in Chemicals, higher feedstock costs, continued pressure from subsidized Asian exports and significant planned maintenance turnarounds. We remain focused on our value-first commercial approach amid volatile export markets. Fourth quarter 2026 adjusted EBITDA is expected to be in the range of $80 million to $100 million. Our priorities remain operating safely and reliably, delivering Beyond250 structural cost savings and generating cash,” Lane concluded.
SEGMENT REPORTING
Olin defines segment earnings as income (loss) before interest expense, net, other operating income (expense), non-operating pension income, other income, and income taxes.
CHLOR ALKALI PRODUCTS AND VINYLS
Chlor Alkali Products and Vinyls sales for the third quarter 2026 were $850.8 million, compared to $924.0 million in the third quarter 2025. The decrease in sales was due to lower volumes, primarily resulting from lower trading volumes associated with Blue Water Alliance and lower VCM volumes, partially offset by higher pricing. The Blue Water Alliance joint venture concluded operations at the end of 2025. Third quarter 2026 segment earnings were $71.3 million, compared to $127.6 million in the third quarter 2025. Third quarter 2026 segment earnings included a $5.0 million pretax benefit compared to $32.0 million last year, primarily related to the clean hydrogen production tax credit under Section 45V as part of the Inflation Reduction Act of 2022. The remaining $29.3 million decrease in segment earnings was primarily due to previously announced operating issues with the VCM plant at our Freeport, Texas facility resulting in higher costs and reduced profit from lost sales ($32.1 million) partially offset by higher pricing, primarily caustic soda and ethylene dichloride. Chlor Alkali Products and Vinyls third quarter 2026 results included depreciation and amortization expense of $91.1 million compared to $109.0 million in the third quarter 2025.
EPOXY
Epoxy sales for the third quarter 2026 were $412.2 million, compared to $349.6 million in the third quarter 2025. The increase in sales was due to higher volumes and improved pricing. Third quarter 2026 segment earnings were $0.6 million, compared to a segment loss of ($32.2) million in the third quarter 2025. The $32.8 million increase in segment results was primarily due to higher volumes and lower operating costs. Product margins were comparable year-over-year with higher pricing offsetting higher raw material costs, primarily
benzene and propylene. Epoxy third quarter 2026 results included depreciation and amortization expense of $11.1 million compared to $13.2 million in the third quarter 2025.
WINCHESTER
Winchester sales for the third quarter 2026 were $484.7 million, compared to $439.6 million in the third quarter 2025. The increase in sales was primarily due to higher commercial ammunition sales. Third quarter 2026 segment earnings were $33.8 million, compared to $19.3 million in the third quarter 2025. The $14.5 million increase in segment earnings was primarily due to higher commercial ammunition pricing and volume, partially offset by higher raw material costs, primarily commodity metal costs. Winchester third quarter 2026 results included depreciation and amortization expense of $9.0 million compared to $7.8 million in the third quarter 2025.
CORPORATE AND OTHER COSTS
Other corporate and unallocated costs in the third quarter of 2026 increased $0.8 million compared to the third quarter 2025 primarily due to an unfavorable impact from foreign currency, partially offset by lower stock-based compensation, which includes mark-to-market adjustments.
PROPOSED MERGER OF EQUALS
On June 16, 2026, Olin and Huntsman Corporation announced that they have entered into a definitive agreement to combine in an all-stock merger of equals to form a combined company, OlinHuntsman Corporation. Third quarter 2026 results included acquisition-related costs of $13.7 million related to this pending merger.
LIQUIDITY
The cash balance on September 30, 2026, was $214.8 million. Olin ended the third quarter 2026 with net debt of approximately $2.8 billion and a net debt to adjusted EBITDA ratio of 5.2 times. On September 30, 2026, Olin had available liquidity of approximately $1.3 billion, including unrestricted access to the undrawn portion of its revolving credit facility.

COMPANY DESCRIPTION
Olin Corporation is a leading vertically integrated global manufacturer and distributor of chemical products and a leading U.S. manufacturer of ammunition. The chemical products produced include chlorine and caustic soda, vinyls, epoxies, chlorinated organics, bleach, hydrogen, and hydrochloric acid. Winchester’s principal manufacturing facilities produce and distribute sporting ammunition, law enforcement ammunition, reloading components, small caliber military ammunition and components, industrial cartridges, and clay targets, along with contracted U.S. military project revenue.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures - Adjusted EBITDA(a)

Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. Management believes that this measure is meaningful to investors as a supplemental financial measure to assess the financial performance without regard to financing methods, capital structures, taxes or historical cost basis. The use of non-GAAP financial measures is not intended to replace any measures of performance determined in accordance with GAAP and Adjusted EBITDA presented may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. Reconciliation of forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures are omitted from this release because Olin is unable to provide such reconciliations without the use of unreasonable efforts. This inability results from the inherent difficulty in forecasting generally and quantifying certain projected amounts that are necessary for such reconciliations. In particular, sufficient information is not available to calculate certain adjustments required for such reconciliations, including interest expense (income), income tax provision (benefit), other expense (income) and restructuring charges (income). Because of our inability to calculate such adjustments, forward-looking net income guidance is also omitted from this release. We expect these adjustments to have a potentially significant impact on our future GAAP financial results.
