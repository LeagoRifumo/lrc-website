Steve Madden Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
LONG ISLAND CITY, N.Y., November 5, 2026 – Steven Madden, Ltd. (Nasdaq: SHOO) (the “Company”), a leading designer and marketer of fashion-forward footwear, accessories and apparel, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Amounts referred to as “Adjusted” are non-GAAP measures that exclude certain items identified as “Non-GAAP Adjustments” in the “Non-GAAP Reconciliation” section of the press release, which include the corresponding reconciliations of these non-GAAP measures to the most comparable GAAP measure.
Third Quarter 2026 Results
● | Revenue increased 15.7% to $772.6 million, compared to $667.9 million in the same period of 2025.
● | Gross profit as a percentage of revenue was 44.6%, compared to 41.5% in the same period of 2025. Adjusted gross profit as a percentage of revenue was 43.4% in the third quarter of 2025.
● | Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue were 34.9%, compared to 36.8% in the same period of 2025. Adjusted operating expenses as a percentage of revenue were 34.3%, compared to 36.4% in the same period of 2025.
● | Income from operations totaled $73.2 million, or 9.5% of revenue, compared to $31.4 million, or 4.7% of revenue, in the same period of 2025. Adjusted income from operations totaled $78.9 million, or 10.2% of revenue, compared to $46.3 million, or 6.9% of revenue, in the same period of 2025.
● | Net income attributable to Steven Madden, Ltd. was $52.4 million, or $0.72 per diluted share, compared to $20.5 million, or $0.29 per diluted share, in the same period of 2025. Adjusted net income attributable to Steven Madden, Ltd. was $57.3 million, or $0.79 per diluted share, compared to $30.4 million, or $0.43 per diluted share, in the same period of 2025.

Edward Rosenfeld, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, commented, “We delivered an outstanding third quarter, with strong growth across our brands and channels. The Steve Madden brand was a particular highlight, as our trend-right product assortments and compelling marketing continue to resonate with consumers and drive strong momentum. We are also pleased with the performance across the broader brand portfolio, reflecting our team’s disciplined execution of our strategy.
“Based on our strong year-to-date results and the continued momentum we see in the business, we are raising our revenue and Adjusted diluted earnings per share outlook for the year. Looking further ahead, the strength of our brands and the multiple growth opportunities across our diversified portfolio give us confidence in our ability to drive sustainable growth and create long-term value for our stockholders.”
Third Quarter 2026 Channel Results
Revenue for the wholesale business in the third quarter of 2026 was $528.7 million, a 19.4% increase compared to the third quarter of 2025. Wholesale footwear revenue increased 21.0%, and wholesale accessories/apparel revenue increased 17.0%. Gross profit as a percentage of wholesale revenue was 36.8% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 32.7% in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross profit as a percentage of wholesale revenue was 33.6% in the third quarter of 2025. Compared with the prior-year Adjusted gross margin, the increase reflected strong underlying improvement as well as a smaller negative impact from tariffs, partially offset by higher freight expense.
Direct-to-consumer revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was $239.2 million, an 8.0% increase compared to the third quarter of 2025. Gross profit as a percentage of direct-to-consumer revenue was 60.7%, compared to 58.3% in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross profit as a percentage of direct-to-consumer revenue was 61.9% in the third quarter of 2025. Compared with the prior-year Adjusted gross margin, the decrease was primarily due to higher freight expense, a shift in markdown cadence and deeper discounting on seasonal product.
The Company ended the quarter with 381 company-operated brick-and-mortar retail stores, including 92 outlets, as well as eight e-commerce websites and 190 company-operated concessions in international markets.
Balance Sheet Highlights
As of September 30, 2026, total debt outstanding was $120.2 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $71.3 million. Net debt is a non-GAAP financial measure that the Company defines as total debt less cash and cash equivalents. Net debt was $48.8 million as of September 30, 2026.
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company did not repurchase any shares of its common stock in the open market.
Quarterly Cash Dividend
The Company’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.21 per share. The dividend is payable on December 24, 2026 to stockholders of record as of the close of business on December 11, 2026.
Fiscal 2026 Outlook
The Company now expects fiscal 2026 revenue to increase 12% to 13% compared with fiscal 2025, versus its previous guidance range of an 11% to 13% increase. The Company now expects fiscal 2026 diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) to be in the range of $2.58 to $2.63, compared to its previous guidance range of $2.55 to $2.65. The Company now expects Adjusted diluted EPS to be in the range of $2.15 to $2.20, up from its previous guidance range of $2.05 to $2.15.

About Steve Madden
Steve Madden designs, sources and markets fashion-forward footwear, accessories and apparel. In addition to marketing products under its own brands including Steve Madden®, Kurt Geiger London®, Dolce Vita®, Betsey Johnson®, Carvela®, Blondo® and ATM®, Steve Madden licenses footwear, handbags and other accessory categories for the Anne Klein® brand. Steve Madden also designs and sources products under private label brand names for various retailers. Steve Madden’s wholesale distribution includes department stores, mass merchants, off-price retailers, shoe chains, online retailers, national chains, specialty retailers and independent stores. Steve Madden also directly operates brick-and-mortar retail stores and e-commerce websites. In addition, Steve Madden licenses certain of its brands to third parties for the marketing and sale of certain products in the apparel, accessory and home categories.
