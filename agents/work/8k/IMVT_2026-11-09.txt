DURHAM, N.C. November 9, 2026 – Immunovant (Nasdaq: IMVT), a clinical-stage immunology company dedicated to enabling normal lives for people with autoimmune diseases, today reported corporate updates and financial results for its second fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Recent Highlights and Upcoming Milestones:
Following the open-label lead-in in Period 1 of the potentially registrational trial in patients with D2T RA, where a total of 170 participants were enrolled to receive IMVT-1402 600 mg for 16 weeks, study participants meeting the criteria for an ACR20 response at Weeks 14 and 16 were eligible to advance to Period 2 of the trial. A total of 117 participants were included in the Period 2 efficacy analysis and randomized to IMVT-1402 600 mg (n=40), IMVT-1402 300 mg (n=36), or placebo (n=41). IMVT-1402 600 mg met the primary endpoint of ACR20 response at Week 28 (92.5% vs. 75.6% placebo, p=0.0139). Participants randomized to IMVT-1402 300 mg did not show a difference from placebo, with 75.0% maintaining an ACR20 response at Week 28 (p=0.9426). The secondary endpoints, change in Clinical Disease Activity Index (CDAI) and Simplified Disease Activity Index (SDAI) from Period 2 baseline to Week 28, numerically favored IMVT-1402 600 mg over placebo. Least squares mean CDAI changes (a decrease indicates improvement) were -0.3 for IMVT-1402 600 mg and +3.1 for placebo (p=0.1096), and least squares mean SDAI changes (a decrease indicates improvement) were +0.1 for IMVT-1402 600 mg and +3.5 for placebo (p=0.1299). In exploratory analyses, ACR50 response rates at Week 28 were 80.0% for IMVT-1402 600 mg vs. 56.1% for placebo (nominal p=0.0117), and ACR70 response rates were 52.5% vs. 36.6% (nominal p=0.1674); the trial was not powered to detect differences in these measures. IMVT-1402 was generally well tolerated in Period 2, with no serious adverse events, Grade 3 or higher treatment-emergent adverse events, or adverse events leading to treatment discontinuation reported in any arm. FDA discussions are planned for later this year to determine the path forward for the registrational program of IMVT-1402 in D2T RA.
In light of the evolving SjD landscape and recent data in the field, we expect it is unlikely that we will advance IMVT-1402 further in SjD beyond the ongoing, fully enrolled Phase 2 trial.
The Company expects to report topline data across potentially registrational trials of IMVT-1402 in Graves’ disease (GD) and myasthenia gravis (MG) in calendar year 2027. In calendar year 2028, topline data are expected for the potentially registrational trial of IMVT-1402 in chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy (CIDP).
Financial Highlights for Fiscal Second Quarter Ended September 30, 2026:
Cash Position: As of September 30, 2026, Immunovant’s cash and cash equivalents totaled $722.3 million, providing runway to the potential commercial launch of IMVT-1402 in GD, based on our current operating plan.
Research and Development Expenses: Research and development (R&D) expenses were $85.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $114.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily due to the wind-down of activities for our batoclimab clinical trials and lower costs from activities related to our clinical trials of IMVT-1402, driven by the timing of contract manufacturing costs.
Non-GAAP R&D expenses were $78.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $106.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.
General and Administrative Expenses: General and administrative (G&A) expenses were $19.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $17.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily due to higher investments in pre-commercial activities.
Non-GAAP G&A expenses were $12.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $11.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.
Net Loss: Net loss was $97.2 million ($0.47 per common share) for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $126.5 million ($0.73 per common share) for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025 included $13.3 million and $13.4 million, respectively, related to non-cash stock-based compensation expense. Non-GAAP net loss was $84.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $113.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.
Common Stock: As of September 30, 2026, there were 206,785,540 shares of common stock issued and outstanding.
Financial Highlights for Fiscal Six Months Ended September 30, 2026:
Research and Development Expenses: Research and development expenses were $227.6 million for the six months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $215.4 million for the six months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily due to activities related to our clinical trials of IMVT-1402, including contract manufacturing costs, partially offset by lower overall costs as we wind-down our batoclimab clinical trials.
Non-GAAP R&D expenses were $213.8 million for the six months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $199.9 million for the six months ended September 30, 2025.
General and Administrative Expenses: General and administrative expenses were $36.8 million for the six months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $43.5 million for the six months ended September
30, 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower personnel-related expenses and professional fees, partially offset by higher investments in pre-commercial activities.
Non-GAAP G&A expenses were $23.6 million for the six months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $27.2 million for the six months ended September 30, 2025.
Net Loss: Net loss was $250.5 million ($1.22 per common share) for the six months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $247.1 million ($1.43 per common share) for the six months ended September 30, 2025. Net loss for the six months ended September 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025 included $27.1 million and $31.9 million, respectively, related to non-cash stock-based compensation expense. Non-GAAP net loss was $223.4 million for the six months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $215.4 million for the six months ended September 30, 2025.
