CorVel Announces Revenues and Earnings
FORT WORTH, Texas, November 3, 2026 — CorVel Corporation (NASDAQ: CRVL) announced the results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Revenues for the quarter were $267 million, an increase from $240 million in the September quarter of 2025. Earnings per share for the quarter were $0.67, compared to $0.54 in the same quarter of the prior year. Revenues for the six months ended September 30, 2026 were $527 million, an increase from $474 million for the six months ended September 30, 2025. Earnings per share for the six months ended September 30, 2026 were $1.31, compared to $1.06 during the six months ended September 30, 2025.
Second Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Highlights
•
Revenue increased 11% to $267 million, compared to second quarter of fiscal year 2026.
•
Gross profit increased 24% to $72.3 million, at 27% gross margin, compared to second quarter of fiscal year 2026 gross profit of $58.2 million.
•
Diluted earnings per share increased 24% to $0.67, compared to second quarter of fiscal year 2026 diluted earnings per share of $0.54.
•
The Company exited the quarter with $270 million of cash, cash equivalents, and no borrowings.
•
The Company repurchased $18.1 million of common stock during the quarter.
CorVel generated strong momentum in its workers' compensation and liability businesses through new customer wins and deeper relationships with existing clients. As employers and insurers face greater claim complexity, rising medical costs, and increasing administrative demands, the Company's integrated claims management and managed care model brings together clinical expertise, technology, and cost containment solutions to improve patient outcomes and manage total claim costs.
Technology innovation remains central to CorVel's efforts to modernize claims administration and increase operational capacity. The integration of artificial intelligence into core claims workflows is reducing manual processes and providing adjusters with more timely, actionable information. Recent developments within CareMC and MyCare, a digital self-service portal, are improving communication and access to claim information. Within Network Solutions, the introduction of Marketwise RepricingTM provides a more sophisticated approach to out-of-network medical repricing, while investments in pharmacy benefits management strengthen pricing, network access, and clinical oversight.
CERIS gained further traction among the nation's largest healthcare payers with the implementation of an additional Top 10 national health plan and a strategic partnership providing access to the Blue Cross Blue Shield market. Product development efforts include new Hospital Bill Audit and Professional Claims solutions, extending the scope of CERIS' prepayment services to address complex reimbursement challenges. Together, these initiatives position CERIS to deepen customer relationships, reach new markets, and deliver greater value to health plans seeking improved payment accuracy and control over healthcare expenditures.
