ING announces new distribution programme of up to €1.9 billion
ING announced today a shareholder distribution of up to €1.9 billion. The distribution consists of a share buyback programme for a maximum total amount of €1.65 billion and a cash payment of €0.25 billion. The purpose of the distribution is to maintain our CET1 ratio in line with our target of ~13%.
ING Group’s CET1 ratio was 13.3% at the end of the third quarter of 2026, which is well above the prevailing CET1 ratio requirement of 11.10%. In line with ING’s dividend reserving approach, the additional distribution will be financed from dividend reserves. As a result, the implied CET1 impact of 56 bps is already reflected in the reported 13.3% CET1 ratio for 3Q2026. The share buyback programme will commence on 29 October 2026 and is expected to end no later than 26 April 2027. The €0.25 billion in cash will be paid on 14 January 2027.
The ECB has approved the distribution, and the share buyback programme will be executed in compliance with the Market Abuse Regulation and within the limitations of the existing authority to acquire a maximum of 20% of the issued shares as granted by the general meeting of shareholders on 14 April 2026. ING has entered a non-discretionary arrangement with a financial intermediary to conduct the buyback.
We provide weekly updates on the progress of the programme via a press release and on the share buyback programme page on our website.

ING PROFILE
ING is a global financial institution with a strong European base, offering banking services through its operating company ING bank. The purpose of ING Bank is: empowering people to stay a step ahead in life and in business. ING Bank’s more than 60,000 employees offer retail and wholesale banking services to customers in over 100 countries.
ING Group shares are listed on the exchanges of Amsterdam (INGA NA, INGA.AS), Brussels and on the New York Stock Exchange (ADRs: ING US, ING.N).
ING aims to put sustainability at the heart of what we do. Our policies and actions are assessed by independent research and ratings providers, which give updates on them annually. ING's ESG rating by MSCI has been upgraded from 'AA' to 'AAA' in October 2025. As of July 2026, in Sustainalytics’ view, ING’s management of ESG material risk is ‘Strong’ with an ESG risk rating of 16.7 (low risk). ING Group shares are also included in major sustainability and ESG index products of leading providers. Here are some examples: Euronext, STOXX, Morningstar and FTSE Russell.
