Ionis reports third quarter 2026 financial results and highlights progress on key programs
– ZANVASTROTM (zilganersen) first approved medicine for Alexander disease; first independent launch underway from leading neurology pipeline –
– TRYNGOLZA® (olezarsen) 2026 product sales guidance lowered as sHTG launch ramps more gradually than expected reflecting early dynamics of establishing a new treatment paradigm –
– DAWNZERA® (donidalorsen) launch momentum continues; reaffirming 2026 product revenue guidance –
– Reaffirming 2026 total revenue, operating loss and cash guidance –
CARLSBAD, Calif., November 9, 2026 – Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: IONS) (the “Company”) today reported financial results and provided key updates for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“In 2026, we continue to advance innovative medicines for people with serious diseases, underscored by three approvals for groundbreaking first-in-class medicines, two of which are Ionis-owned,” said Brett P. Monia, Ph.D., chief executive officer of Ionis. “TRYNGOLZA, our first launch in a large indication, represents a new treatment paradigm for the underserved sHTG population. While initial sales progress has been more gradual than anticipated, we are encouraged by the underlying launch fundamentals. Importantly, we have identified clear opportunities to improve performance, and we remain confident in TRYNGOLZA's long-term growth potential. ZANVASTRO for people with Alexander disease marks our first independent neurology launch and lays the foundation for future launches from our leading neurology pipeline. In addition, our partnered pipeline continues to advance, with the recent approval of HIBSAGO for chronic hepatitis B and two additional positive Phase 3 outcomes creating near-term opportunities for additional potential approvals and revenue growth.”

●

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
“In the third quarter, we generated higher quarter-over-quarter commercial revenue and substantial R&D revenue from partnered medicines while continuing to invest in our innovative wholly owned pipeline. Our commercial revenue growth was driven by DAWNZERA, which continues to gain momentum, supporting our confidence in achieving full-year DAWNZERA product sales guidance. These results keep us on track to achieve our 2026 total revenue, operating loss and cash guidance,” said Elizabeth L. Hougen, chief financial officer of Ionis. “We are lowering our 2026 TRYNGOLZA product sales guidance to reflect a more gradual ramp than we anticipated. We remain confident in TRYNGOLZA’s potential to deliver sustained, long-term revenue growth and multi-billion-dollar peak sales. As we execute on our independent launches and advance the many additional opportunities across our rich pipeline, we remain committed to achieving our goal of sustained positive cash flow and long-term value creation.”

2026 Financial Guidance
Ionis reaffirms its 2026 total revenue, including DAWNZERA product sales, operating loss and cash guidance based on its year-to-date results and fourth quarter outlook. Following the first full quarter of the TRYNGOLZA launch as the first and only treatment to reduce triglycerides and acute pancreatitis in people living with severe hypertriglyceridemia, Ionis is lowering TRYNGOLZA 2026 product sales guidance to reflect a more gradual-than-anticipated near-term launch ramp, while remaining confident in TRYNGOLZA’s long-term growth potential.

Ionis’ Marketed Medicines
TRYNGOLZA® (olezarsen): TRYNGOLZA was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration as an adjunct to diet to reduce triglycerides (TG) and the risk of acute pancreatitis in adults with severe hypertriglyceridemia (sHTG: TG ≥500 mg/dL) and as an adjunct to diet to reduce TG in adults with familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS). For more information about TRYNGOLZA, including the full U.S. Prescribing Information, visit tryngolza.com.
DAWNZERA® (donidalorsen): DAWNZERA was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration for prophylaxis to prevent attacks of hereditary angioedema (HAE) in adult and pediatric patients 12 years of age and older. For more information about DAWNZERA, including the full U.S. Prescribing Information, visit dawnzera.com.
ZANVASTROTM (zilganersen): ZANVASTRO was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration for the treatment of Alexander disease (AxD) in pediatric and adult patients. For more information about ZANVASTRO, including the full U.S. Prescribing Information, visit zanvastro.com.

About Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Inc.
For more than three decades, Ionis has invented medicines that bring better futures to people with serious diseases. Ionis currently has marketed medicines and a leading pipeline in neurology, cardiometabolic disease and select areas of high patient need.
