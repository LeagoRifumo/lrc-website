Liquidia Corporation Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
· | YUTREPIA® (treprostinil) inhalation powder net product sales of $222.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, up 31% from the second quarter of 2026

· | October 2026 total patient shipments reached their highest monthly level since launch

· | Resolved all litigation with United Therapeutics through a global settlement, securing continuity of care for all patients treated with YUTREPIA

· | Recorded net loss of $236.3 million reflects a $389.7 million litigation settlement charge

· | Fifth consecutive quarter of increasing non-GAAP, adjusted EBITDA to $140.5 million

· | Increased cash and cash equivalents to $396.1 million prior to settlement payments

· | Granted Fast Track designation to YUTREPIA for the treatment of SSc-RP and initiated RE-WARM, a Phase 2 randomized open-label dose-finding study

MORRISVILLE, N.C., November 10, 2026 – Liquidia Corporation (NASDAQ: LQDA), a biopharmaceutical company driven by science and compassion to revolutionize care for patients with challenging respiratory and vascular diseases, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The company will also host a webcast at 8:30 a.m. ET on November 10, 2026, to discuss its financial results and provide a corporate update.
Dr. Roger Jeffs, Liquidia’s Chief Executive Officer, said: “Five quarters after launch, YUTREPIA has rapidly established a preferred product profile because physicians value the control and flexibility it gives them over each patient’s therapy. That affinity and broad adoption have proven durable and our business continues to grow in parallel. With the litigation now fully resolved, and YUTREPIA’S commercial availability secure, we can fully focus on cementing the value of YUTREPIA through Phase 4 studies in PAH that will position it as the best-in-class inhaled therapy and first-in-choice prostacyclin. Simultaneously, we will continue to advance a pipeline that builds on our deep understanding of inhaled treprostinil, from L606 in PAH and PH-ILD to new indications where patients have no approved options, such as SSc-RP and PH-COPD. We are confident that YUTREPIA and L606 can meet the needs of many more patients, both today and in the years ahead, as we look to become the leading provider of inhaled prostacyclin care.”

Global Settlement with United Therapeutics
On October 13, 2026, Liquidia entered into a global settlement agreement with United Therapeutics Corporation (United Therapeutics), which resolved all six pending lawsuits between the companies. The settlement followed a September 30, 2026, ruling by the U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware that certain claims of U.S. Patent No. 11,826,327, related to the treatment of PH-ILD with inhaled treprostinil, were valid and infringed. Under the settlement, YUTREPIA remains available to patients without disruption, and no patients are required to discontinue. Liquidia agreed not to appeal the ruling, has submitted a Prior Approval Supplement to remove the PH-ILD indication from YUTREPIA’s label, and has ceased promoting YUTREPIA for PH-ILD. The settlement agreement includes mutual releases without admission of liability by either party.
Under the settlement agreement, Liquidia agreed to pay net $380.7 million in multiple installments in 2026 and 2027, inclusive of payments to other parties to resolve the antitrust litigation against United Therapeutics related to Treprostinil Injection, which the company expects to fund from current cash and cash equivalents and ongoing sales of YUTREPIA.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $236.3 million, which includes a $389.7 million charge related to the litigation settlement. Excluding that charge, YUTREPIA sales led to Liquidia’s fifth consecutive quarter of increasing non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA, which was $140.5 million in the third quarter of 2026.
Cash and cash equivalents totaled $396.1 million as of September 30, 2026, prior to the payments pursuant to the settlement agreements, compared to $190.7 million as of December 31, 2025.
Product sales, net, were $222.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $51.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. We began shipping YUTREPIA to customers in the United States in June 2025, following receipt of full FDA approval for YUTREPIA in May 2025. The increase of $171.0 million was due to higher volume of YUTREPIA sales.
Service revenue, net and cost of service revenue, were $2.6 million and $0.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to $2.7 million and $0.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, respectively, and related to the promotion agreement with Sandoz, Inc., pursuant to which we shared profits from the sales of Treprostinil Injection in the United States (the “Promotion Agreement”), which was terminated in October 2026. We will no longer generate service revenue.
Cost of product sales was $14.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $2.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Cost of product sales is related to sales of YUTREPIA. The increase of $11.7 million was primarily due to higher volume of YUTREPIA sales.
Research and development expenses were $25.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $9.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase of $16.0 million or 170% was primarily due to an $11.1 million increase in expenses for our L606 program, a $3.2 million increase in expenses related to YUTREPIA research and development activities, and a $1.2 million increase in personnel expenses driven by higher headcount.
Selling, general and administrative expenses were $59.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $40.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase of $18.9 million or 47% was primarily due to a $6.4 million increase in personnel expenses and a $5.5 million increase in stock-based compensation driven by higher headcount, a $4.1 million increase in commercial and consulting expenses to support the commercialization of YUTREPIA.
Litigation settlement, net was $389.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and reflects charges recorded for the settlement of the litigation with United Therapeutics and the parties with interests in the antitrust litigation against United Therapeutics related to Treprostinil Injection, consisting of $330.4 million payable to United Therapeutics, less $10.0 million receivable from United Therapeutics, $60.3 million payable to the parties with interests in the antitrust litigation against United Therapeutics related to Treprostinil Injection, and other non-cash write offs of assets related to the Promotion Agreement.
Interest expense, net was $2.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $5.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease of $2.4 million was primarily attributable to higher money market balances and lower interest accretion under the Company’s revenue interest financing agreement with HealthCare Royalty Partners IV, L.P.
Income tax benefit was $30.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026. We did not recognize any income tax benefit during the three months ended September 30, 2025.
Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $236.3 million, or $2.64 per basic and diluted share, as compared to a net loss of $3.5 million, or $0.04 per basic and diluted share, for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

About YUTREPIA® (treprostinil) Inhalation Powder
YUTREPIA is an inhaled dry-powder formulation of treprostinil delivered through a convenient, low-effort, palm-sized device. YUTREPIA was designed using Liquidia’s PRINT® technology, which enables the development of drug particles that are precise and uniform in size, shape and composition, and that are engineered for enhanced deposition in the lung following oral inhalation. YUTREPIA is approved and marketed for the treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH). YUTREPIA is not approved for the treatment of systemic sclerosis-associated Raynaud’s phenomenon (SSc-RP); it has received Fast Track designation from the FDA for SSc-RP. YUTREPIA was previously referred to as LIQ861 in investigational studies.
About L606 (liposomal treprostinil inhalation suspension)
L606 is an investigational, extended-release formulation of treprostinil administered twice daily with a next-generation nebulizer. The L606 suspension uses a proprietary liposomal formulation to encapsulate treprostinil which can be released slowly at a controlled rate into the lung, enhancing drug exposure over an extended period of time. L606 is currently being evaluated in an open-label study in the United States for treatment of PAH and PH-ILD and is being evaluated in Re-Spire, a global pivotal placebo-controlled efficacy study to evaluate L606 in adults for the treatment of PH-ILD.
About Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH)
PAH is a rare, chronic, progressive disease caused by hardening and narrowing of the pulmonary arteries that can lead to right heart failure and eventually death. Currently, more than 50,000 estimated patients are diagnosed and treated in the United States. There is currently no cure for PAH, so the goals of existing treatments are to alleviate symptoms, maintain or improve functional class, delay disease progression and improve quality of life.
About Pulmonary Hypertension Associated with Interstitial Lung Disease (PH-ILD)
PH-ILD includes a diverse collection of up to 150 different pulmonary diseases, including interstitial pulmonary fibrosis, chronic hypersensitivity pneumonitis, connective tissue disease-related ILD, and chronic pulmonary fibrosis with emphysema (CPFE) among others. Any level of PH in ILD patients is associated with poor 3-year survival. A current estimate of PH-ILD prevalence in the United States is greater than 60,000 patients, though actual prevalence in many of these underlying ILD diseases is not yet known due to factors including underdiagnosis and lack of approved treatments until March 2021 when inhaled treprostinil was first approved for this indication.
About Liquidia Corporation
Liquidia Corporation is a biopharmaceutical company driven by science and compassion to revolutionize care for patients with challenging respiratory and vascular diseases. The company’s current focus spans the development and commercialization of products in pulmonary hypertension and other applications of its proprietary PRINT® Technology. PRINT enabled the creation of YUTREPIA® (treprostinil) inhalation powder, approved for the treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH), and in development for Raynaud’s phenomenon secondary to systemic sclerosis (SSc-RP). The company is also developing L606, an investigational extended-release formulation of treprostinil administered twice daily via a next-generation nebulizer to treat PAH and pulmonary hypertension associated with interstitial lung disease (PH-ILD). To learn more about Liquidia, please visit www.liquidia.com.
