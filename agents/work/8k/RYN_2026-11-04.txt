RAYONIER REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
•Third quarter net income attributable to Rayonier of $123.3 million (or $0.41 per diluted share), pro forma net income of $40.0 million (or $0.13 per diluted share), and Adjusted EBITDA of $144.6 million.
•Closed the previously announced, tax-efficient timberland transactions with RMS, consisting of a $145 million Large Disposition of approximately 36,000 acres in Washington and a $146 million acquisition of approximately 57,000 acres in Texas and Alabama.
•Repurchased $59.8 million of shares at an average price of $21.00 per share.
•Company’s board of directors approved a new incremental $300 million share repurchase program.
•Debt outstanding of $1.86 billion and cash of $361.4 million as of September 30, 2026.
WILDLIGHT, FL — November 4, 2026 — Rayonier Inc. (NYSE:RYN) today reported third quarter net income attributable to Rayonier of $123.3 million, or $0.41 per diluted share, on sales of $565.6 million. This compares to net income attributable to Rayonier of $43.2 million, or $0.28 per diluted share, on sales of $177.5 million in the prior year quarter.
The third quarter results included $90.6 million of income (net of tax) related to a Large Disposition1 in Washington and $6.9 million of costs (net of tax) related to the merger with PotlatchDeltic.2 Excluding these items and adjusting for pro forma net income adjustments attributable to noncontrolling interests,3 third quarter pro forma net income4 was $40.0 million, or $0.13 per diluted share, on pro forma sales4 of $420.6 million. This compares to pro forma net income4 of $50.2 million, or $0.32 per diluted share, in the prior year period.

“We generated Adjusted EBITDA of $144.6 million in the third quarter, reflecting solid operational performance across our businesses,” said Mark McHugh, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Our team continued to execute well amid a challenging market environment, while also making meaningful progress on integration initiatives that position us to create long-term value for our shareholders. We further remained focused on disciplined and opportunistic capital allocation, repurchasing $60 million of our common stock during the third quarter, which brought our total share repurchases through the first nine months of the year to $163 million.”
“In our Southern Timber segment, Adjusted EBITDA increased 23% versus the prior year quarter to $52.4 million, driven primarily by the contribution of approximately 1.4 million tons of harvest volume from the legacy PotlatchDeltic timberlands. In our Northwest Timber segment, Adjusted EBITDA of $32.3 million was roughly five times higher than the prior year quarter, primarily due to 368,000 tons of incremental harvest volume from the PotlatchDeltic timberlands as well as higher sawlog prices in Idaho.”
“In our Wood Products segment, Adjusted EBITDA totaled $27.0 million, reflecting strong lumber price realizations and shipment volumes. Lumber prices improved early in the third quarter before moderating as the quarter progressed, with average lumber price realizations reaching their highest levels since the third quarter of 2022. Shipment volumes were also in line with our targets.”
“In our Real Estate segment, Adjusted EBITDA totaled $48.5 million, exceeding the high end of our prior quarterly guidance, reflecting strong transaction activity and continued momentum across our real estate categories.”
Southern Timber
Third quarter sales of $107.4 million increased $40.6 million, or 61%, versus the prior year period. Harvest volumes increased 56% to 3.02 million tons versus 1.94 million tons in the prior year period, primarily driven by 1.4 million tons of incremental volume from the PotlatchDeltic timberlands. Average delivered pine sawtimber prices decreased to $45.31 per ton versus $47.45 per ton in the prior year period, primarily reflecting a shift in geographic mix from the expansion of our Southern Timber footprint, as well as modestly softer market conditions. Average delivered pine pulpwood prices decreased to $31.14 per ton versus $36.47 per ton in the prior year period, reflecting geographic mix impacts and generally weaker pulpwood market conditions. Weighted-average prices on stumpage sales (including hardwood) decreased to $15.33 per ton versus $20.20 per ton in the prior year period. Operating income of $20.7 million decreased $1.9 million versus the prior year

Northwest Timber
Third quarter sales of $73.6 million increased $53.7 million, or 269%, versus the prior year period. Harvest volumes increased 171% to 571,000 tons versus 210,000 tons in the prior year period, driven by 368,000 tons of incremental volume from the PotlatchDeltic timberlands. Average delivered prices for sawtimber increased to $129.12 per ton versus $100.16 per ton in the prior year period, primarily reflecting geographic mix due to the addition of Idaho sawtimber, which more than offset modestly lower prices in the Pacific Northwest. Operating income of $3.6 million increased $1.9 million versus the prior year period due to higher prices ($13.4 million), higher volumes ($12.2 million), and higher non-timber income ($0.2 million), partially offset by higher depletion expense ($16.7 million) and higher costs ($7.3 million). The elevated depletion expense recognized in the third quarter primarily reflects a cumulative catch-up adjustment related to revisions to the preliminary purchase price allocation for the PotlatchDeltic merger.
Third quarter Adjusted EBITDA4 of $32.3 million was $25.9 million above the prior year period.
Wood Products
Third quarter sales totaled $206.2 million, consisting of $172.5 million of lumber sales and $33.7 million of plywood, residual, and other sales. Lumber price realizations remained strong during the third quarter as import duties and tariffs, mill curtailments, and trucking shortages constricted supply. Lumber shipments totaled 327 MMBF, with average lumber price realizations of $528 per thousand board feet.
Third quarter operating income and Adjusted EBITDA4 were $11.6 million and $27.0 million, respectively.
Real Estate
Third quarter sales of $208.0 million increased $117.2 million versus the prior year period, while operating income of $134.2 million increased $107.8 million versus the prior year period. Third quarter sales and operating income included $145.0 million and $90.6 million, respectively, from Large Dispositions.1 Excluding Large Dispositions,1 pro forma sales4 were $63.0 million and pro forma operating income4 was $43.5 million. Pro forma sales4 decreased $27.8 million versus the prior year period, reflecting fewer acres sold (7,127 acres sold versus 23,348 acres sold in the prior year period), partially offset by higher weighted-average prices ($8,012 per acre versus $3,486 per acre in the prior year period). Pro forma operating income4 increased $10.1 million versus the prior year period.
Improved Development sales of $12.2 million included $6.1 million from the Chenal Valley development project in Little Rock, Arkansas, $5.2 million from the Wildlight development project north of Jacksonville, Florida, and $0.8 million from the Heartwood development project south of Savannah, Georgia.
Unimproved Development sales of $19.3 million consisted of 1,730 acres at an average price of $11,177 per acre, including a 1,720-acre sale in St. Johns County, Florida for $10,809 per acre. There were no Unimproved Development sales in the prior year period.
Rural sales of $25.6 million consisted of 5,135 acres at an average price of $4,984 per acre. This compares to prior year period sales of $7.3 million, which consisted of 1,520 acres at an average price of $4,811 per acre.
There were no Timberland & Non-Strategic sales in the third quarter, while the prior year period included sales of $53.5 million, consisting of 21,601 acres at an average price of $2,477 per acre.
Third quarter Adjusted EBITDA4 of $48.5 million decreased $25.3 million versus the prior year period.

Other Items
Third quarter corporate and other operating expenses of $21.9 million increased $12.9 million versus the prior year period, primarily reflecting the larger scale of the combined company and $7.0 million of costs related to the merger with PotlatchDeltic.2
Third quarter interest expense of $16.7 million increased $9.9 million versus the prior year period, primarily due to incremental debt assumed in the merger with PotlatchDeltic. Third quarter interest income of $4.0 million decreased $5.8 million versus the prior year period, primarily due to a lower average cash balance as a result of share repurchases, debt repayments, and merger-related expenditures.
Third quarter income tax expense of $7.8 million was primarily driven by income generated from the Company’s Wood Products and Real Estate development businesses.
Share Repurchases and 2026 Repurchase Authorization
During the third quarter, the Company repurchased approximately 2.8 million shares at an average price of $21.00 per share, or $59.8 million in total. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $66.2 million remaining on its 2024 share repurchase authorization. On November 3, 2026, the Company’s board of directors approved a new $300 million share repurchase authorization, which is incremental to the remaining capacity under the 2024 authorization.
Outlook
Consistent with the initial 2026 financial guidance we provided in February, the following full-year metrics reflect a pro rata contribution from legacy PotlatchDeltic operations for January 31, 2026 through December 31, 2026.
•Southern Timber: In our Southern Timber segment, we expect to achieve full-year harvest volumes of 12.2 to 12.4 million tons, with anticipated harvest volumes of 3.0 to 3.2 million tons in the fourth quarter. We expect regional sawtimber and pulpwood prices to remain relatively stable for the fourth quarter compared to the third quarter. However, full-year and quarterly average pine prices for the combined company’s Southern Timber segment are expected to be lower than the standalone prices for Rayonier in the prior year based on the geographic mix of the combined company.
•Northwest Timber: In our Northwest Timber segment, we expect to achieve full-year harvest volumes of 2.0 to 2.1 million tons, with anticipated harvest volumes of 500,000 to 550,000 tons in the fourth quarter. We expect overall sawtimber prices to be lower in the fourth quarter compared to the third quarter, primarily due to lower sawlog prices in Idaho. We also continue to expect that full-year 2026 average log pricing for the combined company’s Northwest Timber segment will be higher than the standalone pricing for Rayonier in the prior year.
•Wood Products: In our Wood Products segment, we continue to expect lumber shipments to total approximately 1.1 billion board feet for the 11 months of contribution in 2026, including approximately 295 to 305 million board feet in the fourth quarter. We expect fourth quarter lumber realizations to be lower than the third quarter, reflecting softer market conditions and typical seasonal demand patterns.
•Real Estate: We are pleased by the continued momentum in our Real Estate segment and maintain a strong pipeline of rural and improved development land sales for the remainder of the year. Based on our current transaction pipeline and sales closed quarter-to-date, we expect an Adjusted EBITDA contribution in the fourth quarter of $60 to $70 million.

About Rayonier
Rayonier is a land resources real estate investment trust (REIT) with a portfolio comprising over four million acres in the U.S. South and U.S. Northwest. The company is focused on managing its timberlands on a sustainable basis while optimizing its overall portfolio value by delivering land to its highest and best use. Rayonier also operates six sawmills, an industrial-grade plywood mill, residential and commercial real estate developments, and a rural land sales program. Rayonier is committed to corporate responsibility, third-party forest certification, and supporting climate change mitigation through its land-based solutions business.
