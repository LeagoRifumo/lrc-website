Chicago, Illinois, November 5, 2026 - Madison Air Solutions Corporation (NYSE: MAIR) (the “Company” or “Madison Air”), a global provider of air quality solutions, today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
“Our third quarter results demonstrate the power of disciplined execution against a clear long-term strategy,” said Jill Wyant, President and CEO. “We grew orders 26%, net sales 25% and adjusted EBITDA 19%, and ended the quarter with a record $3.2 billion backlog. Our Return on Air® value proposition, which delivers measurable customer outcomes, continues to drive consistent results. Combined with our focus on performance-driven growth markets where air is mission-critical and our value creation model, it is helping fuel broad-based momentum across the business. As customers increasingly recognize air as a business driver, we are expanding into new residential and commercial applications and unlocking attractive whitespace opportunities.”
Wyant added, “We continue to expect to complete our acquisition of ebm-papst soon after the end of the year. Planning remains on track, with a dedicated transition team in place actively advancing workstreams across key functional areas in preparation for a successful close. Our confidence in the strategic rationale and value creation opportunities continues to grow. The acquisition further strengthens our position in attractive markets with strong secular tailwinds and favorable lifecycle economics. Across the business, we are doing what we said we would do. Our strong year-to-date performance and record backlog give us the confidence to raise our full-year Madison Air outlook.”
Recent highlights include:
•Generated broad-based orders and revenue growth across our diverse portfolio of end markets and mission-critical applications. Within Commercial, strength across the portfolio drove growth, with Big Ass Fans delivering particularly strong performance and continued whitespace penetration. Residential also grew, led by AprilAire and continued adoption of Healthy Air solutions.
•Continued growing our data center cooling business to meet significant customer demand. Company margins reflected success-based mix from strong growth in this strategic market and new-program ramp costs. We made further progress on price/cost actions and ramp execution, and our services business delivered a record quarter.
•Raised full-year net sales and Adjusted EBITDA guidance, reflecting strong third-quarter performance and sustained momentum. Growing backlog, healthy new business pipelines and order activity continue to provide increased visibility into future performance.
•Announced the prospective acquisition of ebm-papst, further expanding our technology portfolio and capabilities. The combination broadens our addressable market and creates additional opportunities across commercial, aftermarket and services, while providing meaningful synergy potential through our operating model, scale and commercial reach. The transaction remains on track to close soon after the end of the year, with financing secured and integration planning underway.

Third Quarter 2026 Results by Segment
Commercial. Commercial segment net sales increased $217.9 million, or 39.3%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025 primarily driven by broad-based growth across the segment, led by air, liquid and hybrid cooling, air movement and heat and commercial dehumidification solutions.
Commercial segment Adjusted EBITDA increased $38.3 million, or 23.6%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The EBITDA growth was driven by higher volumes and pricing, partially offset by success-based data center business mix, production ramp costs and inflation.
Commercial segment net sales increased $460.4 million, or 29.1%, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Organically, Commercial net sales increased by 26.7% driven by broad-based growth across the segment, led by air, liquid and hybrid cooling, air movement, and energy efficiency solutions. Acquisitions contributed $36.1 million, or 2.3%, of additional net sales in the period.
Commercial segment Adjusted EBITDA increased $88.0 million, or 19.7%, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Acquisitions contributed $9.2 million of incremental Commercial segment Adjusted EBITDA. Excluding the AprilAire Acquisition, Commercial segment Adjusted EBITDA growth was mainly driven by higher volumes and pricing, partially offset by production ramp costs and inflation.

Residential. Residential segment net sales increased $11.2 million, or 3.2%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025 primarily driven by pricing and demand for healthy air systems.
Residential segment Adjusted EBITDA increased $11.0 million, or 11.7%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The EBITDA growth was mainly driven by pricing, productivity improvements, and favorable net tariff impacts, partially offset by inflation.
Residential segment net sales increased $175.6 million, or 21.2%, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Organically, Residential net sales decreased by 0.4% driven by modest volume declines for ventilation solutions, net of price increases and strong demand for healthy air systems. Acquisitions contributed to $177.0 million, or 21.3%, of additional net sales in the period.
Residential segment Adjusted EBITDA increased $73.0 million, or 34.8%, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Acquisitions contributed $59.3 million of additional Residential segment Adjusted EBITDA. Excluding the AprilAire Acquisition, Residential segment Adjusted EBITDA increased by $2.7 million.

SELECTED FINANCIAL DATA, NON-GAAP MEASURES AND DEFINITIONS
Following are tables that present the Company's selected financial data.
Madison Air reports its financial results in accordance with United States GAAP. We supplement the reporting of our financial information determined under GAAP with certain non-GAAP financial information. The non-GAAP information presented provides investors with additional useful information, but should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for related GAAP measures. Moreover, other companies may define non-GAAP measures differently, which limits the usefulness of these measures for comparison with such other companies. A reconciliation of the non-GAAP measures to the corresponding amounts prepared in accordance with GAAP appears in the tables. The tables provide additional information as to the items and amounts that have been excluded from the adjusted measurements.
Organic sales and organic sales growth are non-GAAP financial measures. We define organic sales and organic sales growth rate as net sales and net sales growth rate as adjusted for acquisitions and divestitures and currency exchange rates. Sales from acquired businesses are excluded from the organic sales and organic sales growth calculation for the first 12 months following the acquisition date, while sales from divested businesses are excluded for the 12 months preceding the divestiture. Organic sales and organic sales growth are based on continuing operations and exclude sales from discontinued operations.

Free Cash Flow (“FCF”) is a non-GAAP liquidity measure. We define FCF as net cash flows provided by operating activities—continuing operations less purchases of property, plant and equipment plus proceeds from sale of property, plant and equipment.
Free Cash Flow Conversion is a non-GAAP liquidity measure. We define Free Cash Flow Conversion as free cash flow divided by net income (loss) from continuing operations.
Adjusted Gross Profit is a non-GAAP financial measure. We define Adjusted Gross Profit as net sales less cost of goods sold, excluding the purchase accounting impacts of acquisitions such as amortization related to technology-related intangible assets and purchase accounting inventory adjustments.
Adjusted Gross Profit Margin is a non-GAAP financial measure. We define Adjusted Gross Profit Margin as Adjusted Gross Profit divided by net sales.
Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) as adjusted for net (income) loss from discontinued operations, interest and financing expenses, income tax expense (benefit), depreciation and amortization, acquisition and divestiture expenses, restructuring expenses, equity appreciation rights expense, non-operating expenses (income), allocated Madison Industries costs, and non-recurring professional and consulting expenses.
Adjusted EBITDA Margin is a non-GAAP financial measure. We define Adjusted EBITDA Margin as Adjusted EBITDA divided by net sales for the same period.
Adjusted Net Income (Loss) is a non-GAAP financial measure. We define Adjusted Net Income (Loss) as net income (loss) as adjusted for certain items that impact comparability from period to period. These adjustments include net (income) loss from discontinued operations, amortization expense, acquisition and divestiture expenses, restructuring expenses, equity appreciation rights expense, non-operating expenses (income), allocated Madison Industries costs, non-recurring professional and consulting expenses, and the tax effect of net income (loss) adjustments.
Adjusted Earnings Per Share is a non-GAAP financial measure. We define Adjusted Earnings Per Share as Adjusted Net Income (Loss) divided by weighted average diluted shares outstanding. The most directly comparable GAAP financial metric is earnings per share.
Adjusted Net Income Margin is a non-GAAP financial measure. We define Adjusted Net Income Margin as Adjusted Net Income divided by net sales for the same period.
Net debt is a non-GAAP liquidity measure. We define net debt as total debt adjusted for discounts and financing fees, net and cash and cash equivalents.
Net Leverage is a non-GAAP liquidity measure. We define Net Leverage as net debt divided by Adjusted EBITDA on a trailing twelve-month basis (“TTM”).
Adjusted Net Leverage is a non-GAAP liquidity measure. We define Adjusted Net Leverage as net debt calculated without giving effect to the net proceeds from the Private Placement, divided by Adjusted EBITDA on a TTM basis.
Backlog is a key performance indicator used to assist us in evaluating the performance of our business. Backlog represents the total expected future revenue from confirmed customer orders that have been received but not yet shipped or rendered as of a given date. Backlog is applicable to sales of products and systems and services. However, the timing and conversion of backlog is subject to numerous uncertainties and risks and are not necessarily indicative of the amount of revenue to be earned in the upcoming fiscal year.
