NEWS RELEASE
Enbridge Reports Strong Third Quarter Results, Places Over $3 Billion of Gas Transmission Projects Into Service, and Reaffirms 2026 Financial Guidance
CALGARY, AB, November 6, 2026 /CNW/ - Enbridge Inc. (Enbridge or the Company) (TSX:ENB) (NYSE:ENB) today reported third quarter 2026 financial results, reaffirmed its 2026 financial guidance and provided a quarterly business update.
Highlights
(All financial figures are unaudited and in Canadian dollars unless otherwise noted. * identifies non-GAAP financial measures. Please refer to Non-GAAP Reconciliations Appendices.)
•
Third quarter GAAP earnings attributable to common shareholders of $1.3 billion or $0.59 per common share, compared with GAAP earnings attributable to common shareholders of $0.7 billion or $0.30 per common share in 2025
•
Adjusted earnings* of $1.1 billion or $0.49 per common share*, compared with $1.0 billion or $0.46 per common share in 2025
•
Adjusted earnings before interest, income taxes and depreciation and amortization (EBITDA)* of $4.4 billion, compared to $4.3 billion in 2025
•
Cash provided by operating activities of $2.7 billion, compared with $2.9 billion in 2025
•
Distributable cash flow (DCF)* of $2.7 billion, compared with $2.6 billion in 2025
•
Reaffirmed 2026 full year financial guidance and medium-term financial outlook
•
Announced the US$2.6 billion acquisition of Tallgrass Energy LP's crude oil business (Tallgrass), which will expand the Company's Liquids Pipelines footprint to include a competitive Rockies-to-Cushing platform, and the US$0.3 billion Pony Express 2 (PXP2) growth project
•
Announced and closed the US$0.6 billion acquisition of Salt Creek Midstream's crude oil gathering business to establish a new wellhead-to-water export service in the growing Permian basin
•
Completed $3.0 billion common equity offering to partially fund the Tallgrass and Salt Creek Midstream acquisitions and to enhance financial flexibility to fund potential future growth opportunities
•
Closed an initial $0.7 billion investment by KKR and Apollo and secured an additional $2 billion commitment to support the Aspen Point and Sunrise Expansion Programs on Westcoast's B.C. Pipeline system
•
Announced US$0.5 billion agreement to acquire the assets of Laramie Energy, providing additional natural gas supply for Enbridge Gas Utah's customers under an existing cost-of-service framework
•
Sanctioned $0.2 billion Long Lake Expansion, supporting CNOOC Petroleum North America's growing operations in Northern Alberta
•
Placed the Aspen Point expansion of B.C. Pipeline and Tennessee Ridgeline projects into service

"The global focus on energy security, reliability and affordability continues to reinforce the value of Enbridge’s strategically positioned infrastructure. Our scale, connectivity, diversified asset base, and all-of-the-above approach to energy investment position us to serve growing energy demand while delivering predictable, long-term value for customers and shareholders. As energy demand accelerates, driven by AI infrastructure, advanced manufacturing and broader economic growth, our integrated networks are increasingly important to providing the reliable, affordable energy our customers require. That opportunity continues to translate into visible growth across our businesses. Our secured growth backlog stands at $38 billion, even as we placed $3 billion of completed projects into service this quarter. We've consistently added new projects to our backlog this year, positioning us well to deliver on our $10–20 billion target for new project announcements through 2027. Our strong organic opportunity set is complemented by three accretive tuck-in acquisitions in our Liquids Pipelines and Gas Distribution businesses, demonstrating the multiple avenues available to us to deploy capital prudently and create long-term shareholder value.
"In Liquids Pipelines, we entered into two transactions to grow our leading North American footprint. The acquisition of Tallgrass Energy’s crude oil business will expand our presence in the Bakken, Powder River and Denver-Julesburg basins by adding a highly contracted pipeline with connectivity from Guernsey, WY to Cushing, OK. This acquisition is expected to provide us with a new corridor through the U.S. Rockies, complementing our existing assets, including Express-Platte and Seaway. They also demonstrate our ability to selectively deploy capital into high-quality assets that enhance our existing footprint, increase customer optionality, and create long-term opportunities for continued growth.

THIRD QUARTER BUSINESS UPDATES
Liquids Pipelines: Tallgrass Energy Crude Oil Acquisition
On September 9th, Enbridge announced it had entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Tallgrass Energy's crude oil business for cash consideration of US$2.55 billion, comprising a 75% operating interest in the Pony Express Pipeline, a 51% operating interest in the Powder River Gateway system, approximately 8.4 million barrels of crude oil storage capacity, and the Stanchion crude marketing business primarily responsible for optimizing the acquired assets. Pony Express is a 460 kbpd highly contracted crude oil system serving the Bakken, Powder River (PRB) and Denver-Julesburg basin supply zones with connectivity into the Cushing, OK demand hub. The Powder River Gateway system includes two short-haul crude oil pipelines offering approximately 240 kbpd of combined capacity from the PRB basin into Guernsey, WY. The transaction includes the PXP2 growth project, an incremental US$0.3 billion expansion of Pony Express expected to increase capacity to approximately 515 kbpd. PXP2 is underpinned by take-or-pay contracts and is expected to enter service in late 2027. The transaction is subject to regulatory approvals and other closing conditions.
Liquids Pipelines: Salt Creek Midstream Crude Gathering Acquisition
On August 26th, Enbridge announced it had entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Salt Creek Midstream's crude oil gathering business for a total cash consideration of US$0.6 billion. The acquisition includes a 100% interest in the Orla and Wink North gathering systems, and a 50% interest in the Delaware Crossing gathering system, inclusive of each systems' respective crude oil terminals and storage infrastructure. The assets are underpinned by long-term commercial agreements with average remaining contract lives of approximately 10 years. The transaction closed effective October 9th.
Liquids Pipelines: Long Lake Expansion
Enbridge has sanctioned the Long Lake Expansion project, further strengthening connectivity to CNOOC Petroleum North America’s Long Lake oil sands asset in Alberta. The project involves building a new lateral pipeline and storage tank while expanding capacity on the Waupisoo (Line 18) and Wood Buffalo Extension (Line 45) pipelines. The project is supported by long-term take-or-pay agreements and provides additional capacity to support growing production in the region. Enbridge expects the project to cost $0.2 billion and enter service in 2028.
5
Gas Transmission: Westcoast KKR Investment
During the quarter, Enbridge closed the previously announced investment by KKR and Apollo in the Westcoast system. As part of the transaction, KKR and Apollo contributed approximately $0.7 billion at closing to fund capital spend to date on the Aspen Point and Sunrise expansion programs, and committed to contribute an additional approximately $2.0 billion to fund future capital expenditures related to the expansion programs in exchange for a minority ownership interest in the aggregate Westcoast system. As capital is contributed, KKR and Apollo's ownership stake in the Westcoast system will increase until reaching 29% upon Sunrise entering service in 2028. In addition, Enbridge retains the option to repurchase KKR and Apollo's interest in the system at any time between the seventh and fourteenth year following close. Enbridge will continue to majority own and operate the system.
Gas Distribution & Storage: Wexpro Gas Supply Acquisition
During the quarter, Enbridge entered into an agreement to acquire substantially all of the assets of Laramie Energy for US$485 million plus up to US$65 million in contingent payments. The acquisition will expand Wexpro's natural gas production platform and provide stable, cost-of-service natural gas supply for Enbridge Gas Utah. Upon closing, the transaction is expected to increase Wexpro's contribution to Enbridge Gas Utah's supply portfolio and support long-term customer affordability and reliability. Closing is expected in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions.
Gas Distribution & Storage: Enbridge Gas Ohio Rate Stipulation
During the quarter, Enbridge Gas Ohio filed a stipulation with parties to its pending rate proceeding before the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio. The stipulation, if approved, would provide an annual revenue requirement increase of approximately US$145 million, an equity thickness of 52.87%, and an allowed return on equity of 9.59%. The filing supports continued investment in system safety, reliability and modernization, and advance customer affordability programs and support measures. A Commission decision is expected in or before the first quarter of 2027, with new rates expected to become effective on March 1, 2027.

NON-GAAP RECONCILIATIONS APPENDICES
This news release contains references to EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted earnings, adjusted earnings per common share (EPS) and DCF per share. Management believes the presentation of these metrics gives useful information to investors and shareholders, as they provide increased transparency and insight into the performance of the Company.
EBITDA represents earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.
Adjusted EBITDA represents EBITDA adjusted for unusual, infrequent or other non-operating factors on both a consolidated and segmented basis. Management uses EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA to set targets and to assess the performance of the Company and its business units.
Adjusted earnings represent earnings attributable to common shareholders adjusted for unusual, infrequent or other non-operating factors included in adjusted EBITDA, as well as adjustments for unusual, infrequent or other non-operating factors in respect of depreciation and amortization expense, interest expense, income taxes, noncontrolling interests and redeemable noncontrolling interests on a consolidated basis. Management uses adjusted earnings as another measure of the Company’s ability to generate earnings and uses EPS to assess performance of the Company.
DCF is defined as cash flow provided by operating activities before the impact of changes in operating assets and liabilities (including changes in environmental liabilities) less distributions to noncontrolling interests and redeemable noncontrolling interests, preference share dividends and maintenance capital expenditures and further adjusted for unusual, infrequent or other non-operating factors. Management also uses DCF to assess the performance of the Company and to set its dividend payout target.
This news release also contains references to Debt-to-EBITDA, a non-GAAP ratio which utilizes adjusted EBITDA as one of its components. Debt-to-EBITDA is used as a liquidity measure to indicate the amount of adjusted earnings to pay debt, as calculated on the basis of generally accepted accounting principles in the United States of America (U.S. GAAP), before covering interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.
Reconciliations of forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures and non-GAAP ratios to comparable GAAP measures are not available due to the challenges and impracticability of estimating certain items, particularly certain contingent liabilities and non-cash unrealized derivative fair value losses and gains
subject to market variability. Because of those challenges, a reconciliation of forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures and non-GAAP ratios is not available without unreasonable effort.
Our non-GAAP financial measures and non-GAAP ratios described above are not measures that have standardized meaning prescribed by U.S. GAAP and are not U.S. GAAP measures. Therefore, these measures may not be comparable with similar measures presented by other issuers.
