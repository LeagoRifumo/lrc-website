Third Quarter 2026 Overview
Net sales of $4.5 billion, decreasing 2.3% year over year (YoY); up 1.6% organically YoY
Tire unit volume of 38.6 million units, decreasing 3.3% YoY, driven by replacement market weakness partially
offset by higher OE volumes reflecting market share gains
Goodyear net loss of $238 million, or a loss of $31 million as adjusted
Segment operating income of $174 million; organic results improved YoY in Asia Pacific and EMEA offset by
factory utilization and cost absorption pressures in the Americas
Continued progress shifting toward higher-value consumer tires; 18" and above products represent 44% of
sales volumes, up from 39% a year ago
Dedicated transformation office established to accelerate evaluation and execution of opportunities to improve
structural returns and cash generation
AKRON, Ohio, Nov. 4, 2026 – The Goodyear Tire & Rubber Company (NASDAQ:GT) reported third quarter 2026
results today and will host an investor call tomorrow morning, Thursday, Nov. 5, at 8:30 a.m. Eastern time led
by Mark Stewart, Goodyear’s chief executive officer and president, and Scott Deakin, the company’s interim
executive vice president and chief financial officer.
"While our third quarter results reflected meaningful sequential earnings improvement, our financial
performance remains below our expectations. We've made real progress in strengthening our product portfolio
and optimizing our manufacturing footprint. But as challenging industry conditions persist, incremental
improvements alone won't get us to where we need to be," said Stewart. "To get there, we've established a
dedicated transformation office to pursue broader operational and strategic opportunities and to drive
structural changes that will allow the business to generate more consistent free cash flow."

Financial Results
Goodyear's third quarter 2026 net sales were $4.5 billion, with tire unit volumes totaling 38.6 million. After
adjusting for the impact of the sales of its Chemical business and the Dunlop brand of $177 million, organic
net sales increased 1.6% as a result of price/mix benefits, partially offset by lower volume.
Third quarter 2026 Goodyear net loss was $238 million, or $0.82 per share, compared to Goodyear net loss
one year ago of $2.2 billion, or $7.62 per share. Third quarter 2026 included several significant items,
including, on a pre-tax basis, rationalization charges of $148 million. This significant item, and others, are
excluded from adjusted earnings.
Third quarter 2026 adjusted net loss was $31 million, compared to adjusted net income of $82 million in the
prior year's third quarter. Adjusted loss per share was $0.11, compared to adjusted earnings per share of
$0.28 in the prior year's third quarter. Per share amounts are diluted.
Segment Results
The company reported segment operating income of $174 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to
$287 million from one year ago.
After adjusting for the sales of its Chemical business and the Dunlop brand, segment operating income
decreased $57 million. The decrease in segment operating income reflects the impact of lower volume of $77
million, inflation of $69 million, and higher tariffs and other costs of $48 million, partially offset by $90 million
of benefits from Goodyear Forward and favorable price/mix versus raw material costs of $76 million.
Additional earnings materials can be found on Goodyear’s investor relations website at http://
investor.goodyear.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures (unaudited)
This news release presents non-GAAP financial measures, including Total Segment Operating Income and
Margin, Adjusted Net Income (Loss), Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS), and organic earnings
measures, which are important financial measures for the company but are not financial measures defined by
U.S. GAAP, and should not be construed as alternatives to corresponding financial measures presented in
accordance with U.S. GAAP.
Total Segment Operating Income is the sum of the individual strategic business units’ (SBUs’) Segment
Operating Income as determined in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Total Segment Operating Margin is Total
Segment Operating Income divided by Net Sales as determined in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Management
believes that Total Segment Operating Income and Margin are useful because they represent the aggregate
value of income created by the company’s SBUs and exclude items not directly related to the SBUs for
performance evaluation purposes. The most directly comparable U.S. GAAP financial measures to Total
Segment Operating Income and Margin are Goodyear Net Income (Loss) and Return on Net Sales (which is
calculated by dividing Goodyear Net Income (Loss) by Net Sales).
Adjusted Net Income (Loss) is Goodyear Net Income (Loss) as determined in accordance with U.S. GAAP
adjusted for certain significant items. Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS) is the company’s Adjusted
Net Income (Loss) divided by Weighted Average Shares Outstanding-Diluted as determined in accordance
with U.S. GAAP. Management believes that Adjusted Net Income (Loss) and Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per
Share (EPS) are useful because they represent how management reviews the operating results of the company
excluding the impacts of rationalizations, asset write-offs, accelerated depreciation, discrete tax items,
impairments, asset sales and certain other significant items.
Organic earnings measures, including organic Net Sales growth, are non-GAAP financial measures that
exclude the direct impacts of the divestitures of the Dunlop brand and Chemical business from year-over-year
comparisons. We believe these measures provide investors with a supplemental understanding of underlying
earnings trends by providing comparisons on a constant basis. We completed the sale of the Dunlop brand
and our Chemical business in May 2025 and October 2025, respectively.
It should be noted that other companies may calculate similarly-titled non-GAAP financial measures
differently and, as a result, the measures presented herein may not be comparable to such similarly-titled
measures reported by other companies. See the following tables for reconciliations of historical Total
Segment Operating Income and Margin, Adjusted Net Income (Loss) and Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share
to the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP financial measures.
