Performance Food Group Company Reports First-Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results
Strong Case Volume, Net Sales and Profit Growth; Reiterates Full-Year Fiscal 2027 Financial Guidance
First-Quarter Fiscal 2027 Highlights
•
Total case volume increased 4.2%
•
Total independent Foodservice case volume increased 7.8%
•
Organic independent Foodservice case volume increased 5.5%
•
Net sales increased 5.5% to $18.0 billion
•
Gross profit improved 8.5% to $2.2 billion
•
Net income increased 38.4% to $129.5 million

RICHMOND, Va. – November 4, 2026 – Performance Food Group Company (“PFG” or the “Company”) (NYSE: PFGC) today announced its first-quarter fiscal 2027 business results.
“We started fiscal 2027 with solid momentum, delivering strong first-quarter sales and profit growth,” said Scott McPherson, PFG’s President and Chief Executive Officer. “Continued market share gains across our businesses drove strong case volume and revenue performance in the quarter. Our team’s execution on several key efficiency initiatives improved our profitability, driving gross margin expansion and double-digit Adjusted EBITDA and EPS growth in the quarter. We recognize the macroeconomic environment remains challenging, but I am confident in our ability to continue gaining market share, execute our strategic priorities and deliver strong results through the remainder of fiscal 2027.”
First-Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Summary
Total case volume increased 4.2% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period. Total organic case volume increased 2.5% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period, benefiting from a 5.5% increase in organic independent Foodservice cases, including growth in Performance Brands cases. Total independent Foodservice case volume increased 7.8%.
Net sales for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 grew 5.5% compared to the prior year period to $18.0 billion primarily driven by an increase in organic cases sold, incremental sales from recent acquisitions, and an increase in selling price per case as a result of inflation. Overall product cost inflation for the Company was approximately 3.4% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027.

Gross profit for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 grew 8.5% compared to the prior year period to $2.2 billion primarily driven by vendor rebates and promotional incentives, a favorable shift in the mix of cases sold, including growth in the independent channel which generates higher gross profit due to additional services provided, as well as recent acquisitions.
Operating expenses rose 6.9% to $1.9 billion in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period primarily driven by recent acquisitions, an increase in personnel expenses related to salaries, wages, commissions and benefits, and an increase in fuel expenses (excluding the impact of fuel surcharges in net sales) primarily due to higher fuel prices and miles driven as a result of new business, partially offset by realized gains related to fuel hedges. The increase in operating expenses was partially offset by decreases in professional fees and insurance expense primarily related to auto insurance and workers’ compensation.
Net income for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 increased $35.9 million year-over-year to $129.5 million primarily driven by an increase in gross profit and decrease in interest expense, partially offset by increases in operating expenses and income tax expense. The effective tax rate in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 was 25.2% compared to 23.0% in the first quarter of fiscal 2026. The effective tax rate differed from the prior year period primarily due to a decrease in deductible discrete items related to stock-based compensation and a decrease in federal tax credits net of valuation allowance established, partly offset by a decrease in non-deductible compensation and other non-deductible expenses and a decrease in foreign taxes as a percentage of income.
For the quarter, Adjusted EBITDA rose 10.0% compared to the prior year period to $528.0 million.
Diluted EPS increased 36.7% to $0.82 per share in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period. Adjusted Diluted EPS increased 11.9% to $1.32 per share in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period.
Cash Flow and Capital Spending
During the first quarter of fiscal 2027 and the first quarter of fiscal 2026, PFG’s net cash used in operating activities was $89.3 million and $145.2 million, respectively. The improvement in net cash used in operating activities in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the first quarter of fiscal 2026 was largely driven by a change in the timing and volume of advanced purchases of inventory to take advantage of preferred pricing.
In the first quarter of fiscal 2027, PFG invested $53.9 million in capital expenditures, a decrease of $25.0 million compared to the prior year period. In the first quarter of fiscal 2027, PFG delivered negative free cash flow of $143.2 million compared to negative free cash flow of $224.1 million in the comparable prior year period.1
Share Repurchase Program
As of September 26, 2026, $498.5 million remained available for share repurchases under the Company’s $500 million share repurchase program, which expires on May 27, 2029.
First-Quarter Fiscal 2027 Segment Results
Foodservice
First-quarter fiscal 2027 net sales for Foodservice increased 5.0% compared to the prior year period to $9.6 billion primarily driven by recent acquisitions, organic case volume growth, and an increase in selling price per case as a result of inflation. Total case growth for Foodservice was 4.2% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period. Total independent case growth was 7.8% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period. Securing new, and expanding business with, independent customers resulted in organic independent case growth of 5.5% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period. For the first quarter of fiscal 2027, independent sales as a percentage of total Foodservice sales increased to 43.0% from 41.6% in the comparable prior year period.
First-quarter fiscal 2027 Adjusted EBITDA for Foodservice increased 13.4% to $367.9 million compared to the prior year period primarily as a result of an increase in gross profit, partially offset by an increase in operating expenses. Gross profit contributing to Foodservice’s Adjusted EBITDA increased 10.7% primarily driven by growth in cases sold, a favorable shift in the mix of cases sold, including more Performance Brands products sold to independent customers, vendor rebates and promotional incentives, and recent acquisitions. Operating expenses impacting Foodservice’s Adjusted EBITDA increased 9.7% compared to the prior year period primarily as a result of recent acquisitions, an increase in personnel expenses primarily related to salaries, wages, commissions, and benefits, and an increase in fuel expenses (excluding the impact of fuel surcharges in net sales) primarily due to higher fuel prices and miles driven as a result of new business, partially offset by realized gains related to fuel hedges.
Convenience
First-quarter fiscal 2027 net sales for Convenience increased 5.4% compared to the prior year period to $6.9 billion primarily driven by case growth due to the addition of new chain customers and an increase in selling price per case as a result of inflation, partially offset by a mix shift from cigarettes to alternative nicotine products. Total Convenience cases sold increased 4.1% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period.
First-quarter fiscal 2027 Adjusted EBITDA for Convenience increased 3.8% to $125.6 million compared to the prior year period primarily as a result of an increase in gross profit, partially offset by an increase in operating expenses. Gross profit contributing to Convenience’s Adjusted EBITDA increased 3.7% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period primarily due to vendor rebates and promotional incentives and income earned from manufacturers for distribution and related services. Operating expenses impacting Convenience’s Adjusted EBITDA increased 3.5% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period primarily as a result of an increase in personnel expenses primarily related to salaries, wages and benefits and an increase in fuel expenses (excluding the impact of fuel surcharges in net sales) primarily due to higher fuel prices, partially offset by realized gains related to fuel hedges. These increases in operating expenses were partially offset by decreases in fleet maintenance and repair expenses, rent expense primarily related to fleet leases, and insurance expense primarily related to auto insurance and workers’ compensation.
Specialty
During the current fiscal year, Specialty has revised its sales channel classifications to better align with how management evaluates operating performance and market opportunities. As a result, the former value channel is presented as grocery retail, and the theater, concessions and hospitality channels are presented on a combined basis within entertainment. Comparative channel information discussed below has been reclassified to conform to the current presentation.
First-quarter fiscal 2027 net sales for Specialty increased 10.3% compared to the prior year period to $1.4 billion primarily driven by an increase in selling price per case due to favorable channel mix as well as inflation and an increase in cases sold, with growth in the vending, grocery retail, entertainment, and office supply channels, partially offset by declines in retail. Total Specialty cases sold increased 4.3% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period.
First-quarter fiscal 2027 Adjusted EBITDA for Specialty decreased 4.8% to $89.5 million compared to the prior year period primarily as a result of an increase in operating expenses, partially offset by an increase in gross profit. Gross profit contributing to Specialty’s Adjusted EBITDA increased 3.5% for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period primarily driven by vendor rebates and promotional incentives and net sales growth. Operating expenses impacting Specialty’s Adjusted EBITDA increased 9.0% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior year period primarily due to an increase in personnel expenses primarily driven by salaries, wages and benefits, an increase in bad debt expense, an increase in fuel expenses (excluding the impact of fuel surcharges in net sales) primarily due to higher fuel prices and miles driven, partially offset by realized gains related to fuel hedges, and an increase in third-party outbound freight expense.
Fiscal 2027 Outlook
For the second quarter of fiscal 2027, PFG expects net sales to be in a range of approximately $17.0 billion to $17.2 billion and Adjusted EBITDA to be in a range of approximately $490 million to $510 million.
For the full fiscal year 2027, PFG continues to expect net sales to be in a range of approximately $72.5 billion to $73.0 billion and Adjusted EBITDA to be in a range of approximately $2.125 billion to $2.225 billion. The guidance range for the full fiscal year includes the impact of a 53rd week.

About Performance Food Group Company
Performance Food Group is an industry leader and one of the largest food and foodservice distribution companies in North America with more than 150 locations. Founded and headquartered in Richmond, Virginia, PFG and our family of companies market and deliver quality food and food-related products to over 350,000 locations, including independent and chain restaurants, schools, business and industry locations, healthcare facilities, retailers, convenience stores, vending and office coffee service distributors and entertainment venues, and provide small parcel “pick and pack” capabilities utilizing third-party carriers to deliver order sizes too small to be served effectively by our fleet network. PFG’s success as a Fortune 100 company is achieved through our over 44,000 dedicated associates committed to building strong relationships with the valued customers, suppliers and communities we serve. To learn more about PFG, visit pfgc.com.

Statement Regarding Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This earnings release and the accompanying financial statement tables include several financial measures that are not calculated in accordance with GAAP, including Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Diluted EPS, and Free Cash Flow. Such measures are not recognized terms under GAAP, should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for measures prepared in accordance with GAAP, and are not indicative of net income as determined under GAAP. Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Diluted EPS, Free Cash Flow, and other non-GAAP financial measures have limitations that should be considered before using these measures to evaluate PFG’s liquidity or financial performance. Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Diluted EPS, and Free Cash Flow, as presented, may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies because of varying methods of calculation.
PFG uses Adjusted EBITDA to evaluate the performance of its business on a consistent basis over time and for business planning purposes. In addition, targets based on Adjusted EBITDA are among the measures we use to evaluate our management’s performance for purposes of determining their compensation under our incentive plans. PFG believes that the presentation of Adjusted EBITDA enhances an investor’s understanding of PFG’s performance. PFG believes this measure is a useful metric to assess PFG’s operating performance from period to period by excluding certain items that PFG believes are not representative of PFG’s core business.
Management measures operating performance based on our Adjusted EBITDA, defined as net income before interest expense, interest income, income taxes, and depreciation and amortization, further adjusted to exclude certain items we do not consider part of our core operating results. Such adjustments include certain unusual, non-cash, non-recurring, cost reduction and other adjustment items permitted in calculating covenant compliance under PFG’s $5.0 billion secured credit facility (the “ABL Facility”) and indentures governing its outstanding notes (other than certain pro forma adjustments permitted under our ABL Facility and indentures relating to the Adjusted EBITDA contribution of acquired entities or businesses prior to the acquisition date). Under our ABL Facility and indentures, PFG’s ability to engage in certain activities such as incurring certain additional indebtedness, making certain investments, and making restricted payments is tied to ratios based on Adjusted EBITDA (as defined in the ABL Facility and indentures).
Management also uses Adjusted Diluted EPS, which is calculated by adjusting the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure by excluding the same items excluded in PFG’s calculation of Adjusted EBITDA, as well as amortization of intangible assets, to the extent that each such item was included in the applicable GAAP financial measure. For business combinations, the Company generally allocates a portion of the purchase price to intangible assets and such intangible assets contribute to revenue generation. The amount of the allocation is based on estimates and assumptions made by management and is subject to amortization over the useful lives of the intangible assets. The amount of the purchase price from an acquisition allocated to intangible assets and the term of its related amortization can vary significantly and are unique to each acquisition, and thus the Company does not believe it is reflective of ongoing operations. Intangible asset amortization excluded from Adjusted Diluted EPS represents the entire amount recorded within the Company’s GAAP financial statements; whereas, the revenue generated by the associated intangible assets has not been excluded from Adjusted Diluted EPS. Intangible asset amortization is excluded from Adjusted Diluted EPS because the amortization, unlike the related revenue, is not affected by operations of any particular period unless an intangible asset becomes impaired, or the estimated useful life of an intangible asset is revised.
Management also uses Free Cash Flow, which is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditures (purchases of property, plant, and equipment). PFG also believes that the presentation of Free Cash Flow enhances an investor’s understanding of PFG’s ability to make strategic investments and manage debt levels.
PFG believes that the presentation of Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Diluted EPS, and Free Cash Flow is useful to investors because these metrics provide insight into underlying business trends and year-over-year results and are frequently used by securities analysts, investors, and other interested parties in their evaluation of the operating performance of companies in PFG’s industry.

Segment Results
Based on the Company’s organizational structure and how the Company’s management reviews operating results and makes decisions about resource allocation, the Company has three reportable segments: Foodservice, Convenience, and Specialty. Management evaluates the performance of these segments based on various operating and financial metrics, including their respective sales growth and Segment Adjusted EBITDA, which is the Company’s GAAP measure of segment profit. Segment Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income before interest expense, interest income, income taxes, depreciation, and amortization and excludes certain items that the Company does not consider part of its segments’ core operating results, including changes in the LIFO reserve, stock-based compensation expense, and acquisition and business optimization expenses.
Corporate & All Other is comprised of unallocated corporate overhead and certain operations that are not considered separate reportable segments based on their size. Corporate & All Other may also include capital expenditures for certain information technology projects that are transferred to the segments once placed in service.
