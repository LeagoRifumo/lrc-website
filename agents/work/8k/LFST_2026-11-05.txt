LifeStance Reports Strong Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Raises Full Year Outlook
SCOTTSDALE, Ariz. – November 5, 2026 – LifeStance Health Group, Inc. (Nasdaq: LFST), one of the nation’s largest providers of outpatient mental healthcare, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
(All results compared to prior-year comparative period, unless otherwise noted)
Q3 2026 Highlights and FY 2026 Outlook
•
Revenue of $455.9 million increased 25% compared to revenue of $363.8 million
•
Clinician base increased 11% to 8,863 clinicians, a sequential net increase of 321 in the third quarter
•
Third quarter visit volumes increased 17% to 2.7 million
•
Net income of $22.4 million compared to net income of $1.1 million
•
Adjusted EBITDA of $68.4 million compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $40.2 million
•
Net cash provided by operations of $37.0 million in the third quarter
•
Free Cash Flow generation of $22.4 million in the third quarter
•
For full year 2026, raising revenue expectations to $1.741 billion to $1.761 billion, Center Margin expectations to $599 million to $613 million, and Adjusted EBITDA expectations to $242 million to $250 million
“The team delivered a tremendous quarter, with execution across the business driving results that once again exceeded our expectations,” said Dave Bourdon, CEO of LifeStance. “The strong momentum gives us the confidence to further raise our full-year 2026 outlook across all financial metrics, while our positive patient outcomes, most recently across TMS and Spravato, reflect our commitment to clinical excellence. At the same time, the investments we are making in our platform, including the implementation of our new EHR, are positioning LifeStance for continued success and scale over the long term.”

Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of revenue increased in the third quarter as a result of higher total revenue per visit, lower center costs as a percentage of revenue, and improved operating leverage from revenue growing faster than general and administrative expenses.
Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and Capital Allocation
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, LifeStance generated $170.0 million of cash flow from operations, including $37.0 million during the third quarter of 2026. The Company ended the third quarter with cash and cash equivalents of $218.8 million and net long-term debt of $255.5 million.
2026 Guidance
LifeStance is providing the following outlook for 2026:
•
The Company is raising full year revenue to $1.741 billion to $1.761 billion, Center Margin to $599 million to $613 million, and Adjusted EBITDA to $242 million to $250 million.
•
For the fourth quarter of 2026, the Company expects total revenue of $446 million to $466 million, Center Margin of $149 million to $163 million, and Adjusted EBITDA of $56 million to $64 million.

About LifeStance Health Group, Inc.
Founded in 2017, LifeStance (Nasdaq: LFST) is reimagining mental health. We are one of the nation’s largest providers of virtual and in-person outpatient mental healthcare for children, adolescents and adults experiencing a variety of mental health conditions. Our mission is to help people lead healthier, more fulfilling lives by improving access to trusted, affordable, and personalized mental healthcare. LifeStance and its supported practices employ over 8,800 psychiatrists, advanced practice nurses, psychologists and therapists and operates across 35 states and more than 575 centers. To learn more, please visit www.LifeStance.com.
We routinely post information that may be important to investors on the “Investor Relations” section of our website at investor.lifestance.com. We encourage investors and potential investors to consult our website regularly for important information about us.
