Securitize Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Announcing the River One acquisition to expand into tokenized asset-backed finance and a $50 million share repurchase program
MIAMI, November 9, 2026 — Securitize Corp. (“Securitize” or the “Company”) (NYSE: SECZ) today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026, which ended September 30, 2026.
“In the third quarter we listed on the New York Stock Exchange and reached a Company-record $5 billion in Average Tokenized Assets, up 39% from a year ago,” said Carlos Domingo, Chairman and CEO of Securitize. “That growth came from tier one asset managers bringing real products onchain including new ones from ARK Invest, BlackRock and Neuberger, and to me it’s the clearest sign yet that institutions are past the experimenting stage. Our agreement to acquire River One takes us into asset-backed finance, and Securitize Stocks, which we launched after the quarter closed to trade alongside our own tokenized stock, lets eligible investors around the world trade U.S. equities onchain with the same rights they’d have holding the stock(1). Alongside our fully regulated venue, we are also planning on supporting Tokenized Securities Venues under the recently announced SEC Innovation Exemption because more paths onchain are good for the whole market. This quarter showed where capital markets are heading, and we plan to be the infrastructure they run on.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
●Record average Tokenized Assets on Platform(2) in the third quarter of $5.0 billion, up 39%, with total Tokenized Assets on Platform of $4.8 billion as of September 30, 2026, up 9%
●Total Revenue of $18.6 million, up 13% versus the prior-year period, the second-highest quarterly Total Revenue figure in the Company's history
●Net loss of $66.3 million, with a net loss per diluted share of $(0.41), including approximately $60 million (~90% of Net loss) of largely non-cash expenses and accounting charges, primarily associated with our business combination and public listing
●Adjusted EBITDA(3) loss of $2.0 million, versus positive Adjusted EBITDA of $3.6 million in the prior-year period
●Aggregate Transaction Volume(4) of $4.3 billion during the third quarter of 2026, up 20%
●669 active funds(5) serviced by Securitize Fund Services ("SFS") as of September 30, 2026, roughly flat versus the prior year period
●Securitize Fund Services total AUA(6) of $25.2 billion as of September 30, 2026, down 29%
Percentage comparisons throughout this press release are calculated for the third quarter of 2026 versus the third quarter of 2025, unless otherwise specified.
Francisco Flores, Chief Financial Officer, commented, “We are very pleased with our results for the third quarter of 2026, our first reporting period as a public company. Total Revenue was $18.6 million, up 29% from the prior quarter. It was the second-highest quarterly revenue in our history, and our highest first nine months to date. Adjusted EBITDA was a loss of $2.0 million, a 64% improvement from the prior quarter. Approximately 90% of our reported net loss from the quarter was attributable to expenses and
accounting charges, primarily non-cash in nature, including those associated with our business combination and NYSE listing, as well as warrant-related expenses.”
Flores concluded, “Our near-term financial priorities remain focused on driving revenue growth, improving operational efficiency, and making progress toward sustained profitability. We ended the quarter with approximately $300 million in cash and cash equivalents and no debt, providing us with the financial flexibility to continue investing in our growth initiatives while opportunistically managing our capital structure. To this end, our Board authorized a $50 million share repurchase program, reflecting our confidence in Securitize’s long-term prospects and our commitment to disciplined capital management.”
Share Repurchase Program
On November 6, 2026, Securitize's Board of Directors authorized the repurchase of up to $50 million of the Company's common stock. The share repurchase program will remain open through December 31, 2027 and does not obligate the Company to purchase any shares and may be modified, suspended or terminated at any time. The share repurchase program will be funded from available cash on hand.
Agreed to acquire River One, expanding into asset-backed financing
Today, Securitize announced it has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire River One, an institutional asset-backed financing platform drawing from the stablecoin economy. River One was incubated by Framework Ventures, with investment backing from both Framework Ventures and Maple Finance. The acquisition is expected to add warehouse financing capabilities to Securitize's tokenization infrastructure, expanding the Company's addressable market and creating new revenue opportunities from institutional financing activity. The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to customary closing conditions.
Securitize Stocks
Launched global onchain trading of U.S. stocks with full security entitlements: On October 8, 2026, Securitize launched Securitize Stocks, giving eligible investors in the United States, Europe and other permitted jurisdictions access to tokenized interests in U.S.-listed equities through its regulated infrastructure(7). Securitize Stocks launched first on Solana. It offers security entitlements backed one-for-one by underlying shares and preserves applicable shareholder rights and economic benefits, including dividends and voting rights. The initial offering includes Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Tesla and Amazon, among other leading U.S. companies.
Securitize Stocks do not operate under the SEC’s Innovation Exemption(8), the temporary conditional exemptive relief the SEC granted during the third quarter to certain tokenized securities trading venues and liquidity providers. It runs on Securitize’s established regulatory framework, which includes its SEC-registered broker-dealer, SEC-regulated alternative trading system and SEC-registered transfer agent. The Company views the exemption as a welcome additional pathway for onchain securities markets and
believes its regulated infrastructure positions it to support tokenized capital markets as regulatory frameworks evolve.
Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights
Completed NYSE listing and brought SECZ onchain: Securitize completed its business combination with Cantor Equity Partners II on July 1, 2026, and began trading on the NYSE under the ticker symbol SECZ on July 2. Simultaneously with its public listing, Securitize tokenized its own stock as an issuer-sponsored token through Securitize Transfer Agent, and enabled trading of an NMS security through Securitize Markets, its registered broker-dealer, demonstrating how public companies can use regulated blockchain infrastructure to support digital ownership of their shares and enable utility.
Introduced new tokenized investment products with BlackRock, ARK Invest and Neuberger: BlackRock broadened its tokenized cash offerings with BRSRV, a money market fund tokenized by Securitize and designed to support stablecoin reserve management. ARK Invest tokenized its ARK Venture Fund (ARKVX) with Securitize, while Neuberger launched the Neuberger Securitize High Income Tokenized Fund (HINC), bringing additional venture capital and fixed-income strategies onchain.
Secured SEC registration as an investment adviser: Securitize Capital became an SEC-registered investment adviser, strengthening the Company's regulated financial services capabilities and supporting its ability to develop and manage investment products for institutional markets.
Advanced issuer-sponsored tokenization and onchain capital formation: Securitize and Cantor Fitzgerald & Co. announced a collaboration to enable onchain initial public offerings and follow-on offerings for public companies. The collaboration builds on Securitize's broker-dealer capabilities and is designed to extend tokenization into the capital formation process. Separately, Securitize and CURRENC Capital announced a strategic collaboration to advance issuer-sponsored tokenization of publicly traded equities, supporting a model that connects tokenized securities to the rights and benefits of underlying shares.
Expanded Securitize’s reach across blockchains, DeFi markets, international markets, and asset classes: BlackRock’s BUIDL fund expanded to Tempo, and the Neuberger Securitize High Income Tokenized Fund integrated with Loopscale’s credit markets on Solana, giving holders new ways to use tokenized fixed income in decentralized finance. Securitize also announced a collaboration with KB Securities and Optimism to explore institutional investment tokenization in South Korea, a partnership with Socios.com to develop tokenized equity offerings for professional sports teams, and an integration with ADI Chain to support institutional tokenization in global markets.

Securitize Stocks and Regulatory Structure Securitize Stocks are security entitlements under Article 8 of the Uniform Commercial Code backed 1:1 by corresponding underlying shares held through Securitize Markets’ brokerage infrastructure. References to having the “same rights” as the underlying stock mean that applicable economic benefits and voting entitlements associated with the underlying shares are passed through to holders, including dividends and, where applicable, voting instructions. Securitize Stock holders are not registered shareholders of the underlying issuer unless and until a supported conversion into registered shares is completed. The nature and availability of particular benefits and entitlements depend on the underlying share class, applicable arrangements and legal and operational requirements.
The issuers of the underlying securities have not sponsored or endorsed Securitize Stocks. Securitize Stocks are offered and traded through Securitize Markets’ existing broker-dealer infrastructure and do not rely on the Innovation Exemption.
Securities offerings and other activities conducted by Securitize or its affiliates may rely on other exemptions from registration or other relief available under applicable securities laws. Securitize Stocks are available only to eligible investors in permitted jurisdictions and remain subject to onboarding, identity verification, applicable securities laws, product availability and other conditions. Securitize Stocks are offered and traded through Securitize Markets, LLC, an SEC-registered broker-dealer and member of FINRA and SIPC. Securitize Markets also operates Securitize Markets ATS, an alternative trading system. Securitize Transfer Agent, LLC and Securitize Capital LLC are separately registered affiliated entities. Registration with the SEC does not constitute approval or endorsement by the SEC of Securitize, its affiliates, Securitize Stocks or any other product or service. Conversion of a Securitize Stock security entitlement into an issuer-sponsored tokenized share is not available for every issuer or security and requires issuer authorization, transfer-agent support, holder eligibility, operational readiness and satisfaction of applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
