COMPANY PROFILE
ATS is an industry leader in planning, designing, building, commissioning and servicing automated manufacturing and assembly systems - including automation products and test solutions - for a broadly diversified base of customers. ATS' reputation, knowledge, global presence and standard automation technology platforms differentiate the Company and provide competitive advantages in the worldwide manufacturing automation market for life sciences, industrial & consumer, food & beverage, and energy. Founded in 1978, ATS employs over 7,000 people at more than 65 manufacturing facilities and over 85 offices in North America, Europe, Asia and Oceania. The Company's website can be found at www.atsautomation.com. The Company's common shares are traded on the Toronto Stock Exchange ("TSX") and the New York Stock Exchange ("NYSE") under the symbol ATS.

STRATEGY
To create sustainable shareholder value, the Company employs a three-part value creation strategy: Build, Grow and Expand.
Build: To build on the Company's foundation and drive performance improvements, management is focused on the advancement of the ATS Business Model ("ABM"), the pursuit and measurement of value drivers and key performance indicators, a rigorous strategic planning process, succession planning, talent management, employee engagement, and instilling autonomy with accountability.
Grow: To drive organic growth, ATS has developed and implemented growth tools under the ABM, which provide innovation and value to customers and work to grow reoccurring revenues.
Expand: To expand the Company's reach, management is focused on the development of new markets and business platforms, expanding service offerings, investment in innovation and product development, along with strategic and disciplined acquisitions that strengthen ATS.
The Company pursues all of its initiatives by using a strategic capital framework aimed at driving the creation of long-term sustainable shareholder value.
ATS Business Model
The ABM is a business management system that ATS developed with the continuing goal of enabling the Company to pursue its strategies, outpace the growth of its chosen markets, and drive year-over-year continuous improvement. The ABM emphasizes:
•People: developing, engaging and empowering ATS' people to build the best team;
•Process: aligning ATS' people to implement and continuously improve robust and disciplined business processes throughout the organization; and
•Performance: consistently measuring results in order to yield world-class performance for ATS' customers and shareholders.
The ABM is ATS' playbook, serving as the framework to achieve business goals and objectives through disciplined, continuous improvement. The ABM is employed by ATS divisions globally and is supported with extensive training in the use of key problem-solving tools, and applied through various projects to drive continuous improvement. When ATS makes acquisitions, the ABM is quickly introduced to new companies as a means of supporting cultural and business integration.

EXECUTIVE UPDATE
Updated fiscal 2027 performance expectations
Management continues to see healthy activity in the Company's pipelines across its chosen end markets, supported by its differentiated technologies, deep domain expertise, and strong customer relationships. In the second quarter of fiscal 2027, Order Bookings were $827 million, including the highest life sciences quarterly total since the second quarter of fiscal 2025, and the Company's second-highest quarter of life sciences Order Bookings on record. Strength in radiopharmaceuticals and medical devices markets drove Order Bookings, resulting in ATS' book-to-bill ratio being approximately 1.05:1 on a trailing-twelve-month basis. Management also continues to see increasing revenue contribution from services and remains well positioned to participate in the long-term growth of the nuclear and radiopharmaceutical markets, although customer awards in these markets can vary in timing and size.
During the second quarter of fiscal 2027, the Company experienced temporary delays in Order Bookings within its non-life sciences businesses, primarily related to packaging automation equipment. These delays reflected a timing shift in customer orders, which was further exacerbated by tariffs imposed under Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 ("Section 338 tariffs") that came into effect in late August 2026 following the breakdown of trade talks between Canada and the U.S. and affected packaging equipment manufactured in Canada and sold into the U.S. As a result of these delays and the execution time for the Company's Order Backlog to convert to revenues, Management does not expect to achieve

Management's revenue estimates are calculated each quarter based on the assessment of project schedules across all customer contracts in Order Backlog, expectations for faster-turn product and services revenues, expected delivery timing of third-party equipment and operational capacity.
At the end of October the U.S. announced modifications to Section 338 tariffs, which eliminate the impact on a majority of the covered products. This modification is expected to be beneficial to ATS and its affected customers as they digest the various changes over time and advance their capital expenditure plans. The near-term impact has been driven primarily by customer deferral of capital decisions, including as a result of tariff uncertainty, rather than by the Company's ability to serve those customers, or the strength of the Company's pipelines. The Company's global manufacturing footprint provides the flexibility to shift production of tariff-impacted volume to U.S. facilities if needed and the Company has been evaluating such adjustments in consultation with affected customers.
Fixed Cost Transformation Program and margin expansion update
In the second quarter of fiscal 2027, the Company advanced the implementation of its previously announced Fixed Cost Transformation Program, a multi-phase initiative to simplify its operating structure, optimize its global footprint and reduce fixed costs. When completed, the program is designed to deliver $65 million to $75 million of annualized cost savings, equivalent to approximately 250 basis points of adjusted earnings from operations margin. This represents approximately half of the 500 basis points of adjusted earnings from operations margin expansion required to move from ATS' fiscal 2026 base of approximately 10% to the Company's long-term target of 15%. The Company has made initial progress in line with its expectations; however, lower revenues and mix are temporarily offsetting the benefits of the program in the near term. To date, the Fixed Cost Transformation Program has resulted in several announced facility closures, including in Europe, which will result in the reduction of approximately 10% in the Company's overall square footage. As a result of the announced actions from Fixed Cost Transformation and other restructuring activities, the Company expects to reduce its workforce, including subcontractors, by approximately 300 to 400, with additional adjustments expected as further steps in the program are taken.
Of the $21.3 million of restructuring costs recorded in the second quarter of fiscal 2027, approximately half was related to the Fixed Cost Transformation Program. Management continues to expect that payback on restructuring will be less than one year, with some variability depending on jurisdiction.
Given the nature of the facility and reorganization activities involved, implementation is expected to continue over the next 15 months, with benefits accruing progressively as program actions are completed within the original 18-month timeframe.
Management continues to believe that executing on the Company's long-term growth opportunities and operational-improvement initiatives, together with portfolio optimization and cost-reduction activities, including the Fixed Cost Transformation Program, support the Company's achievement of its long-term adjusted earnings from operations margin target of 15%.

ORDER BOOKINGS BY QUARTER
Second quarter of fiscal 2027 Order Bookings were $827 million, a 12.7% year-over-year increase, primarily reflecting a 12.4% increase in organic Order Bookings. By market, Order Bookings in life sciences increased compared to the prior-year period, reflecting continued customer capital investment across a broad range of applications, including large enterprise orders. Order Bookings within life sciences remain well diversified, including orders for radiopharmaceutical applications and for a range of pharmaceutical and medical device automation solutions. Order Bookings decreased in industrial & consumer compared to the prior period a year ago, primarily due to the timing of customer projects and the Company's ongoing portfolio repositioning towards opportunities that support improved profitability and capital efficiency. Order Bookings in food & beverage increased compared to the prior-year period due to the timing of customer orders. Order Bookings in energy decreased compared to the prior-year period primarily due to the timing of customer projects, specifically for nuclear refurbishment projects, and due to strong nuclear refurbishment-related activity in the prior-year period that benefited from several large project awards. Given the nature of the nuclear market, Order Bookings can fluctuate based on the timing of customer investment decisions and project awards. After-sales service revenues are accretive to the Company's overall margin, and growth in this proportion is expected to support the Company's margin expansion targets.

Tariffs
The majority of the Company's shipments from Canada into the U.S. fall within the current terms of the U.S.-Mexico-Canada trade agreement ("USMCA"). In 2026, the U.S. declined to agree to extend the USMCA in its current form, triggering annual joint reviews that will continue until the parties either agree to an extension or the agreement expires on July 1, 2036. Although the USMCA remains in full force and effect, the annual review process, and the ability of any party to withdraw from the agreement on six months' written notice, creates potential long-term uncertainty regarding North American free trade compounded by additional tariffs imposed by the U.S. on certain goods from various jurisdictions globally, including Canada and Europe; and further tariffs and trade agreements continue to be discussed. The uncertainty of Section 338 tariffs has had a more material impact during the quarter than previously anticipated, primarily affecting packaging equipment and customer decision making based on expected tariff impacts. Subsequent changes to tariff policy may reduce the impact of these tariffs on the Company going forward, although the effect of such changes may not translate immediately into Order Bookings and revenues. The Company does not currently expect tariffs imposed under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 ("Section 301 tariffs") to have a material impact and continues to assess the application of these tariffs. Management continues to actively monitor the situation as it evolves and is taking steps to mitigate risks where possible while continuing to offer support to customers based on their needs, which may include onshoring or reshoring production. Supply chain impacts resulting from shifting trade dynamics have been largely mitigated through alternative sourcing, along with pricing strategies. While the Company could see impacts over time arising from unmitigated costs related to the tariffs themselves, potential supplier price increases, and the timing and geographic shifts in customers' capital deployment, ATS' global footprint and decentralized operating model, supported by the ABM, provide some flexibility to address potential disruptions over the long term. On a trailing-twelve-month basis, the Company's equipment and product adjusted revenues from its Canadian and European operations being sold into the U.S. remained consistent with the range previously disclosed of just over 20% of the Company's adjusted revenues.
