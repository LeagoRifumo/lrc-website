Q3 2026 financial highlights
BCE operating revenues grew by 2.2% in Q3 2026, compared to the same period last year, driven by both higher service and product revenues of 1.6% and 6.9%, respectively. The growth in service revenues was attributable to our Bell CTS U.S. segment, as a result of the acquisition of Ziply Fiber on August 1, 2025, which included Ziply Fiber's results for a full quarter compared to only two months in Q3 2025, along with higher Bell Media revenues, led by the success of the broadcast of the 2026 Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) World Cup coupled with increased direct-to-consumer (DTC) streaming revenues, as well as reflecting the continued growth from our AI-powered solutions business (1) in Bell CTS Canada. The year-over-year increase in service revenues was moderated by continued erosion in legacy voice, data and TV services and ongoing lower demand for traditional advertising. Although wireless service revenues declined year over year, pricing on base rate plans in the market was much more disciplined. The growth in product revenues was driven by greater wireline equipment sales to large business customers, partly offset by reduced wireless product revenues mainly from lower device sales to consumers.
Net earnings of $701 million and net earnings attributable to common shareholders of $630 million in the third quarter of 2026 decreased by $3,854 million and $3,872 million, respectively, compared to the same period last year, mainly due to lower gains on investments resulting from the sale of our minority stake in Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment Ltd. (MLSE) in 2025, partly offset by lower impairment of assets, lower income taxes and lower other expense.
BCE adjusted EBITDA increased by 0.9% in Q3 2026, compared to the same period last year, driven by our Bell CTS U.S. segment, mainly due to the acquisition of Ziply Fiber on August 1, 2025, which included Ziply Fiber's results for a full quarter compared to only two months in Q3 2025, and reflected growth from our Bell Media segment, partly offset by a decline in our Bell CTS Canada segment. The year-over-year growth in adjusted EBITDA was driven by higher operating revenues, partly offset by greater operating expenses. This resulted in an adjusted EBITDA margin of 45.1% in Q3 2026, down 0.6 pts over Q3 2025, primarily due to higher operating expenses, reflecting the inclusion of the operating costs of Ziply Fiber as a result of the acquisition and an increased proportion of low margin product sales in our total revenue base, partly offset by ongoing cost containment and operating efficiencies achieved across the company.
BCE’s EPS of $0.67 in Q3 2026 decreased by $4.17 compared to the same period last year.
(1)AI-powered solutions revenues is comprised of revenues from Ateko, Bell Cyber, and Bell AI Fabric.

Agreement with La Caisse for a 49.9% equity interest in Bell's wireless tower infrastructure company
On October 27, 2026, Bell Canada entered into a definitive agreement with global investment group La Caisse, under which La Caisse will acquire a 49.9% stake in a newly formed wireless tower infrastructure company, TowerCo, for a purchase price of $2.45 billion, subject to customary adjustments. TowerCo will own and operate Bell's portfolio of passive wireless infrastructure assets, comprising more than 5,000 sites across Canada.
Bell will retain a 50.1% controlling equity interest in TowerCo, and TowerCo's financial results will be consolidated into Bell’s consolidated financial statements. TowerCo is expected to have no financial debt at closing. TowerCo will lease capacity on the transferred towers to Bell, and Bell will maintain access to existing sites through long-term agreements. TowerCo will develop new sites for Bell as needed. Bell will retain full ownership and control of its spectrum, all active network equipment and owned land.
The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, following receipt of regulatory approvals and satisfaction of other customary closing conditions.
Bell AI Fabric Saskatchewan data centre and memorandum of understanding for potential expansion
On March 16, 2026, Bell announced a partnership with the Government of Saskatchewan to develop a 300 megawatt (MW) AI data centre in the Rural Municipality of Sherwood, Saskatchewan. On October 1, 2026, Bell and the Government of Saskatchewan marked construction milestones at the site, with all four buildings in the first phase under construction. We continue to expect the first data hall to come online in the first half of 2027.
On September 14, 2026, Bell Canada and the Government of Saskatchewan announced a non-binding memorandum of understanding (MOU) to expand Bell AI Fabric in Saskatchewan through the development of up to 900 MW of additional capacity at the Sherwood facility which, together with the previously announced 300 MW, would create a path to 1.2 gigawatts (GW) of capacity in the province.
As at September 30, 2026, capital expenditures and associated cash outflows for the Sherwood facility were approximately $475 million, mainly related to the purchase of equipment, building construction and labour. Project spending is expected to increase in the fourth quarter of 2026 as construction continues and orders of long-lead-time components progress.
We previously expected the construction of the originally announced 300 MW of capacity to require approximately $1.7 billion of incremental capital expenditures. With the anticipated expansion of the Sherwood facility from 300 MW to up to 1.2 GW, we now expect total capital expenditures at the facility over time to exceed $1.7 billion. We expect to provide further updates on Bell AI Fabric, including the Sherwood facility, as development progresses.
Disposition of land mobile radio networks services business
On March 26, 2026, Bell Mobility Inc. entered into an agreement to sell its land mobile radio networks services business to Motorola Solutions Canada Networks Inc., a subsidiary of Motorola Solutions, for $675 million, subject to customary adjustments and a deferred net working capital settlement.
