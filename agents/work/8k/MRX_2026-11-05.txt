Marex Group Limited announces third quarter 2026 results
NEW YORK, November 5, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Marex Group Limited (‘Marex’ or the ‘Group’; Nasdaq: MRX) a diversified global financial services platform providing market access, infrastructure services and essential liquidity to clients across commodities and financial markets, today reported the Group’s preliminary unaudited financial results for the third quarter ('Q3 2026').
Ian Lowitt, Group Chief Executive Officer, stated, “Marex delivered another record performance in the third quarter, with revenue and Adjusted Profit Before Tax1 growing both year on year and sequentially. Adjusted Profit Before Tax1 was $176m for the quarter and $495m for the first nine months of 2026, reflecting the continued successful execution of our strategy through acquisitions, market share gains and deeper institutional client relationships. We continue to see momentum and expect to close the year with a larger, more diversified earnings base, continued opportunities for organic and inorganic growth, and confidence in delivering our 10–20% Adjusted Profit Before Tax1 growth target in 2027."

Performance for Q3 2026
Revenue increased 53% year-on-year to a record $743.2m (Q3 2025: $484.6m), an increase of $47.4m from the previous record quarter in Q2 2026. Year-on-year growth was broad-based across all four business segments, reflecting the continued expansion of the Group's capabilities, increased client activity and contributions from acquisitions.
Net trading income more than doubled to $420.2m (Q3 2025: $197.7m), driven by Agency and Execution, Market Making and Hedging and Investment Solutions. Agency and Execution delivered continued strong growth in Prime and FX. Market Making delivered strong organic growth, particularly in Metals, supported by favorable market conditions and contributions from recent acquisitions, including Winterflood, Webb Traders and Levmet.
Net commission income increased by $20.4m to $252.6m (Q3 2025: $232.2m), reflecting higher income in Clearing and Agency and Execution. Continued client onboarding and increased activity from existing clients drove growth in Clearing, while growth in Securities within Agency and Execution was partly offset by lower revenue in Energy.
Net physical commodities income increased by $18.5m to $34.7m (Q3 2025: $16.2m), primarily reflecting the contribution from Levmet within Market Making.
Net interest income decreased by $2.8m to $35.7m (Q3 2025: $38.5m) despite significantly higher average balances as the benefit of balance growth was more than offset by higher interest expense. Interest income grew, reflecting higher average balances of $25.2bn (Q3 2025: $18.6bn) more than offsetting a 60 bps decline in average Fed Funds rates. Higher interest expense associated with the Group’s $500m senior debt issuance, completed in April 2026, together with increased structured note issuance in Hedging and Investment Solutions, more than offset the increase in interest income earned on balances. The Group’s deliberate decision to maintain substantial liquidity headroom creates a modest near-term headwind to net interest income, while strengthening the balance sheet and preserving capacity to support clients and pursue future growth opportunities.
Total expenses increased by $174.3m to $564.2m (Q3 2025: $389.9m), reflecting higher performance-related compensation tied to strong revenue performance, continued investment in technology and infrastructure, and the impact of acquisitions completed since the prior-year period, principally Winterflood, Levmet and Webb Traders.
Compensation and benefits increased by $137.2m to $429.2m (Q3 2025: $292.0m), reflecting higher performance-related compensation and a larger workforce. Average FTE headcount increased by 19% to 3,544 (Q3 2025: 2,968), reflecting acquisitions and continued investment in front office and control and support capabilities.
Other expenses increased by $35.1m to $123.4m (Q3 2025: $88.3m), reflecting the enlarged operating platform following acquisitions and continued investment in technology and infrastructure.
Net provision for credit losses increased by $6.6m to $6.7m (Q3 2025: $0.1m), primarily reflecting an increase in Stage 1 expected credit loss provisions. This does not reflect a deterioration in underlying credit quality or concerns relating to any individual client exposures, but rather growth in our portfolio.
Profit Before Tax from Continuing Operations increased by $73.9m to $172.7m (Q3 2025: $98.8m), with the margin increasing to 23.2% (Q3 2025: 20.4%). The improvement reflected strong revenue growth across the Group, which more than offset higher performance-related compensation and continued investment in the business.
Profit After Tax increased by $57.1m to $130.3m (Q3 2025: $73.2m), with no contribution from discontinued operations in the quarter.
Adjusting items totaled $3.4m in Q3 2026 (Q3 2025: $1.7m), comprising $2.2m of amortization of acquired brands and customer lists, $0.4m of professional advisory and legal fees in relation to M&A activity and $0.8m of redomiciliation costs.
Adjusted Profit Before Tax¹ increased 75% year-on-year to $176.1m (Q3 2025: $100.5m), while Adjusted Profit Before Tax Margin¹ increased to 23.7% (Q3 2025: 20.7%).

Clearing
Marex provides Clearing services across the full range of commodity and financial markets. We act as principal for our clients and provide direct access to more than 60 exchanges globally.
Performance for Q3 2026
Revenue increased by $36.6m to $170.1m (Q3 2025: $133.5m), with growth across all revenue lines. Performance was supported by continued client onboarding, increased activity and a greater share of business from existing clients, alongside higher average clearing client balances.
Net commission income increased by $10.5m to $76.8m (Q3 2025: $66.3m), reflecting higher volumes of contracts cleared, driven by new client onboarding and increased activity from existing clients.
Net interest income increased by $21.3m to $82.9m (Q3 2025: $61.6m), driven by an increase in average client balances to $18.7bn (Q3 2025: $13.3bn) which more than offset lower average Fed Funds rates year-on-year. Balance growth reflected new client wins, increased activity from existing clients, as well as higher asset prices and associated margin requirements.
Net trading income increased by $4.8m to $10.4m (Q3 2025: $5.6m), reflecting increased client activity across the segment.
Adjusted Profit Before Tax¹ increased by $17.6m to $84.6m (Q3 2025: $67.0m). Adjusted Profit Before Tax Margin¹ was broadly stable at 49.7% (Q3 2025: 50.2%).
Performance for the nine months ended Q3 2026
Revenue increased by $77.1m to $468.6m (Q3 YTD 2025: $391.5m), driven by higher net commission income and net interest income, partially offset by the impact of a client default in the first quarter.
Net commission income increased by $31.7m to $237.3m (Q3 YTD 2025: $205.6m), reflecting heightened client activity across Energy and Metals, continued client onboarding and increased business from existing clients, which supported higher volumes of contracts cleared.
Net interest income increased by $58.8m to $227.9m (Q3 YTD 2025: $169.1m), reflecting higher average client balances from new and existing clients, alongside higher asset prices and margin requirements, more than offsetting lower average Fed Funds rates.
Net trading income decreased by $13.4m to $3.4m (Q3 YTD 2025: $16.8m). This includes the impact of a default from a natural gas client, recognized across both trading income ($28.2m) and credit loss provisions ($5.7m) in Q1 2026. The loss arose from an idiosyncratic event during a period of exceptional volatility. The impact of the default was partly offset by lower performance related compensation across the front office and control and support functions.

Agency and Execution
Agency and Execution provides essential liquidity and execution services to our clients primarily in the energy and financial securities markets.
Our Securities division provides essential liquidity and risk management solutions to clients across global financial markets. Leveraging our international network, we connect buyers and sellers in equities, credit, financing, foreign exchange (FX), and rates, enabling efficient price discovery and tailored hedging strategies. Through our Prime business, we also deliver comprehensive solutions for institutional clients, including clearing, custody, capital introduction, portfolio financing, and outsourced trading.
Our Energy division provides essential liquidity to clients by connecting buyers and sellers in the energy markets. We have leading positions in many of the markets we operate in, including key gas and power markets in Europe; environmental and crude markets in North America; and oil products globally. We achieve this through the breadth and depth of the services we offer to customers, including market intelligence for each product we transact in, based on the extensive knowledge and experience of our teams.
Performance for Q3 2026
Revenue increased by $102.6m to $361.1m (Q3 2025: $258.5m), driven by continued strength across Securities, particularly in Prime, FX and Equities, partially offset by lower Energy revenue.
Securities revenue increased by $104.4m to $285.9m (Q3 2025: $181.5m), reflecting strong growth in Prime, FX and Equities. Prime revenue more than doubled to $114.5m (Q3 2025: $57.2m), reflecting the increased scale of the business and continued growth in institutional client relationships. FX revenue more than doubled, increasing by $33.5m to $59.1m (Q3 2025: $25.6m), reflecting continued growth in Corporate FX, including the scaling of Hamilton Court on the Group’s platform, alongside growth across the wider FX business. Equities revenue increased by $12.4m to $65.8m (Q3 2025: $53.4m), driven by increased client demand for securities lending and equity derivatives. This growth was partially offset by lower Credit revenue, reflecting subdued client activity and transaction volumes in a challenging credit environment, while Rates revenue increased by $4.6m to $33.8m (Q3 2025: $29.2m).
Energy revenue decreased by $2.9m to $72.1m (Q3 2025: $75.0m), reflecting the product mix despite higher overall volumes. Lower activity in some of our larger oil products, amid ongoing geopolitical uncertainty in the Middle East, was partly offset by growth in other products. Activity improved from the subdued levels seen in Q2, despite the seasonally quieter summer period.
Adjusted Profit Before Tax¹ increased by $48.5m to $114.7m (Q3 2025: $66.2m), with Adjusted Profit Before Tax Margin¹ increasing to 31.8% (Q3 2025: 25.6%). The improvement reflected strong revenue growth and a favorable shift in business mix toward higher-margin Securities activities, including FX and Prime.
Performance for the nine months ended Q3 2026
Revenue increased by $275.6m to $1,034.4m (Q3 YTD 2025: $758.8m), driven by strong growth across Securities, partially offset by lower Energy revenue.
Securities revenue increased by $281.0m to $782.1m (Q3 YTD 2025: $501.1m). Non-IFRS Financial Measures and Key Performance Indicators
This press release contains non-IFRS financial measures, including Adjusted Profit Before Tax, Adjusted Profit Before Tax Margin, Adjusted Basic Earnings per Share, Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share, Adjusted Profit After Tax Attributable to Common Equity and Adjusted Return on Equity. These non-IFRS financial measures are presented for supplemental informational purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for profit after tax, profit margin, return on equity or any other financial information presented in accordance with IFRS and may be different from similarly titled non-IFRS financial measures used by other companies.
Adjusted Profit Before Tax
We define Adjusted Profit Before Tax as profit after tax from continuing operations adjusted for (i) tax, (ii) goodwill impairment charges, (iii) merger and acquisition costs (iv) bargain purchase gains, (v) owner fees, (vi) amortization of acquired brands and customer lists, (vii) activities in relation to shareholders, (viii) employer tax on the vesting of Growth Shares, (ix) IPO preparation costs, (x) fair value of the cash settlement option on the Growth Shares, (xi) public offering of ordinary shares and (xii) redomiciliation costs. Items (i) to (xii) are referred to as “Adjusting Items.” Adjusting Items are excluded because they are not reflective of our ongoing underlying trading performance. They typically relate to acquisition accounting, shareholder-related activities and other non-recurring items, which can vary significantly between periods and are not considered part of the Group’s core operations.
The Group has expanded its definition of acquisition costs, relabeling this item as Merger & Acquisition (M&A) activity to better reflect the broader range of transactions now captured. The Group undertakes acquisition activity as part of its business strategy, and M&A-related costs may therefore recur from period to period. Management generally excludes costs arising from transactions outside the Group's normal course of M&A activity in support of its core business, such as the acquisition and disposal of Winterflood Custody Business, which was non-operating in nature and unrelated to the Group's ongoing trading activities. Costs from routine, bolt-on acquisitions are typically not excluded, as these are considered part of the underlying cost base of the business. Each item is assessed by management on its individual facts and circumstances.
Adjusted Profit Before Tax is an important measure used by our management to evaluate and understand our underlying operations and business trends, forecast future results and determine future capital investment allocations. Adjusted Profit Before Tax is the measure used by our executive board to assess the financial performance of our business in relation to our trading performance. The most directly comparable IFRS Accounting Standards measure is profit after tax from continuing operations.
We believe Adjusted Profit Before Tax is a useful measure as it allows management to monitor our ongoing core operations and provides useful information to investors and analysts regarding the net results of the business. The core operations represent the primary trading operations of the business.
Adjusted Profit Before Tax Margin
We define Adjusted Profit Before Tax Margin as Adjusted Profit Before Tax (as defined above) divided by revenue. We believe that Adjusted Profit Before Tax Margin is a useful measure as it allows management to assess the profitability of our business in relation to revenue.
IFRS accounting standards do not define profit margin. Therefore the most directly comparable IFRS measure for profit margin is Profit After Tax divided by revenue.
Adjusted Profit After Tax Attributable to Common Equity
We define Adjusted Profit After Tax Attributable to Common Equity as profit after tax adjusted for the items outlined in the Adjusted Profit Before Tax paragraph above. Additionally, Adjusted Profit After Tax Attributable to Common Equity is also adjusted for (i) tax and the tax effect of the Adjusting Items to calculate Adjusted Profit Before Tax and (ii) profit attributable to AT1 and hybrid perpetual note holders, which is the coupons on the AT1 and hybrid perpetual issuances and accounted for as dividends, adjusted for the tax benefit of the coupons, and (iii) profit attributable to non-controlling interest.
We define Common Equity as being the equity belonging to the holders of the Group’s share capital. We believe Adjusted Profit After Tax Attributable to Common Equity is a useful measure as it allows management to assess the profitability of the equity belonging to the holders of the Group’s share capital.
The most directly comparable IFRS Accounting Standards measure is profit after tax.
Adjusted Return on Equity
We define the Adjusted Return on Equity as the Adjusted Profit After Tax Attributable to Common Equity (as defined above) divided by the average Common Equity for the period.
Common Equity is defined as being the equity belonging to the holders of the Group’s share capital, excluding additional Tier 1 capital, hybrid perpetual instruments and non-controlling interest.
