CENTURI REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS, ACHIEVES RECORD QUARTERLY REVENUE & GROSS PROFIT AND DELIVERS MARGIN EXPANSION
PHOENIX, AZ – November 5, 2026 - Centuri Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: CTRI) ("Centuri" or the "Company") today announced financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 27, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Results and Highlights
•Achieved company record quarterly Revenue of $1.2 billion, a 40% increase versus the third quarter of 2025
•Produced record quarterly Gross Profit of $113.4 million, a 45% increase from the same period last year
•Delivered a Gross Profit margin of 9.5%, up from 9.2% in the prior year period
•Generated incremental gross profit margin of 10.4% on a $342.0 million year-over-year revenue increase
•Reported Net Income of $26.9 million and Adjusted Net Income of $42.5 million, 1,174% and 155% higher than last year's same-period, respectively
•Recorded Adjusted EBIT of $73.0 million, a 79% increase year-over-year, and a 6.1% Adjusted EBIT margin
•Realized Adjusted EBITDA of $108.0 million, a 44% increase year-over-year, and a 9.1% Adjusted EBITDA margin
•Expanded opportunity pipeline to a record $19 billion, highlighting continued end-market strength
Year-to-date 2026 Results Summary
•Revenue of $2.9 billion, 35% higher than last year
•Gross Profit of $218.3 million and Adjusted Gross Profit of $227.2 million, 31% and 37% higher than last year, respectively
Preliminary 2027 Targets
•Initial 2027 targets of mid-teens Revenue percent growth and 8.5% to 9% Gross Profit Margin
•Current 2027 Revenue coverage of over $3.7 billion
"I am very pleased with our third quarter results that clearly demonstrate that investments made to build greater scale and capacity during the first half of the year, including our workforce additions, delivered the benefits we expected as productivity and efficiency improved across our operations," said Centuri President & CEO Christian Brown. "These investments have created a scalable and sustainable operating platform that positions us to support continued growth and profitability in the quarters ahead. In addition to delivering record quarterly revenue, this progress drove gross profit margin to 9.5%, up from 7.2% in the second quarter. In U.S. Gas, where our workforce expansion was most significant, we delivered particularly strong improvement with gross profit margin reaching 8.2%, up from 4.2% in the second quarter. These results reinforce our confidence in our ability to translate strong demand and revenue growth into improved profitability as we continue executing our Vision One Centuri strategy."
"Commercial momentum remains strong across our end markets, supported by robust customer demand and significant opportunities across our core and adjacent end markets. Through the first three quarters of the year, we have secured approximately $2.9 billion of bookings. With nearly $4.8 billion of outstanding bids, including $2 billion of MSA renewals expected by year-end, we anticipate a full-year book-to-bill ratio above our 1.2x target, providing strong future visibility."
"Looking ahead, we have approximately $3.7 billion of revenue coverage for 2027, supporting our early expectations of mid-teens percent revenue growth next year. Additionally, for 2027, we are targeting a gross profit margin range of 8.5% to 9.0%, supported by continued margin expansion through reduced seasonality, improved pricing, increased productivity and operating efficiencies, and the quality of our growing portfolio of bid opportunities."

Management Commentary
Third quarter 2026 revenue increased by $342.0 million, or 40%, to $1.2 billion, and Gross Profit was $113.4 million compared to $78.0 million in the prior year quarter. Gross Profit Margin was 9.5% in the third quarter, up from 9.2% in the same quarter last year and the incremental gross profit margin for the third quarter was 10.4%.
Revenue growth was broad-based across all segments, with Union Electric leading at 65%, followed by U.S. Gas at 40%, Canadian Operations at 33%, and Non-Union Electric at 18%. Revenue growth was primarily driven by new bid work and the inclusion of JJ White in the Union Electric segment, new bid and Master Service Agreement ("MSA") contracts in the U.S. Gas segment, the inclusion of Connect Utility Services in the Canadian Operations segment, and increased storm restoration services and volumes under new and existing MSAs in the Non-Union Electric segment.
Net Income Attributable to Common Stock in the third quarter was $26.9 million compared to $2.1 million in the prior year. Adjusted Net Income for the third quarter was $42.5 million, a 155% increase compared to the same quarter last year. Adjusted EBIT in the third quarter was $73.0 million compared to $40.7 million in the prior year quarter, a 79% year-over-year increase. Adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter was $108.0 million compared to $75.2 million in the prior year quarter, a 44% year-over-year increase.
In the third quarter 2026, revenue and gross profit from storm restoration services totaled $21.3 million and $6.1 million, respectively.
Centuri's Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA Ratio was 2.6x as of September 27, 2026, which compares to 3.8x as of September 28, 2025.
Commercial Update
During the third quarter of 2026, Centuri secured approximately $730 million in total bookings, including nearly $445 million of new bid awards, approximately $185 million of new or expanded MSA awards and approximately $100 million of MSA renewals.
Total bookings year-to-date reached approximately $2.9 billion, representing a book-to-bill ratio of 1.01x. The Company estimates that approximately $2 billion of MSA contract value will be renewed by year-end and forecasts that the full year book-to-bill will exceed its 1.2x target.
As of quarter-end, Centuri had a backlog of approximately $6.2 billion. The quarter-end opportunity pipeline expanded to $19 billion, up 46% from the third quarter last year, driven by continued end-market strength.
Full Year 2026 Financial Guidance
The Company has updated full-year 2026 guidance. The financial guidance assumes no contributions from storm restoration services during the fourth quarter of 2026.

•Adjusted Gross Profit of $305 to $315 million

Backlog
Backlog represents contracted revenue on existing bid agreements as well as estimates of revenue to be realized over the contractual life of existing long-term MSAs. The contractual life of an MSA is defined as the stated length of the contract including any renewal options stated in the contract that we believe our customers are reasonably certain to execute.
Book-to-Bill Ratio
Book-to-bill ratio represents the ratio of total bookings in a period to total revenue recognized in the same period.
Incremental Gross Profit Margin
Incremental gross profit margin represents the change in gross profit from the prior year period divided by the change in revenue from the prior year period.
Opportunity Pipeline
Opportunity pipeline represents our current unweighted bids and opportunities tracked in our sales database.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
We prepare and present our financial statements in accordance with GAAP. However, management believes that EBIT, Adjusted EBIT, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBIT Margin, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Diluted Earnings per share ("Adjusted EPS"), Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA Ratio, Adjusted Revenue, Adjusted Gross Profit, Adjusted Gross Profit Margin, U.S. Gas Adjusted Revenue, U.S. Gas Adjusted Gross Profit, and U.S. Gas Adjusted Gross Profit Margin, all of which are measures not presented in accordance with GAAP, provide investors with additional useful information in evaluating our performance. We use these non-GAAP measures internally to evaluate performance and to make financial, investment and operational decisions. We believe that presentation of these non-GAAP measures provides investors with greater transparency with respect to our results of operations and that these measures are useful for period-to-period comparisons of results. Management also believes that providing these non-GAAP measures helps investors evaluate the Company’s operating performance, profitability and business trends in a way that is consistent with how management evaluates such matters. Because these non-GAAP measures, as defined, exclude some, but not all, items that affect comparable GAAP financial measures, these non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. Management believes that, due to the non-recurring nature of the previously reported City of Chicago reversal, its exclusion from certain non-GAAP financial measures provides investors with a better understanding of the current performance of the business.
Beginning with this press release, the Company no longer presents Base Revenue, Base Gross Profit or Base Gross Profit Margin, which excluded storm restoration services as well as the City of Chicago reversal, and instead presents Adjusted Revenue, Adjusted Gross Profit and Adjusted Gross Profit Margin, which exclude only the City of Chicago reversal.
EBIT is defined as earnings before interest and taxes. Adjusted EBIT is defined as EBIT, adjusted for (i) non-cash stock-based compensation, (ii) acquisition costs, (iii) separation-related costs, (iv) strategy implementation costs, (v) severance costs, (vi) other professional fees and (vii) the City of Chicago reversal. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as Adjusted EBIT, adjusted to remove depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBIT Margin is defined as the percentage derived from dividing Adjusted EBIT by revenue. Adjusted EBITDA Margin is defined as the percentage derived from dividing Adjusted EBITDA by revenue.
Management believes that EBIT, Adjusted EBIT, and Adjusted EBITDA help investors gain an understanding of the factors affecting our ongoing cash earnings from which capital investments are made and debt is serviced, and that Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted EBITDA provide additional insight by removing certain expenses that are non-recurring or non-operational in nature. Management believes that Adjusted EBIT Margin and Adjusted EBITDA Margin are useful for the same reasons as Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted EBITDA, respectively, and also provide an additional understanding of how Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted EBITDA, respectively, are impacted by factors other than changes in revenue.
Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA Ratio is calculated by dividing net debt as of the latest balance sheet date by the trailing twelve months of Adjusted EBITDA. Management believes this ratio helps investors understand our leverage. Net debt is defined as the sum of all bank debt on the balance sheet and finance lease liabilities, net of cash.
Adjusted Net Income is defined as net income (loss) adjusted for (i) separation-related costs, (ii) strategy implementation costs, (iii) severance costs, (iv) amortization of intangible assets, (v) other professional fees, (vi) City of Chicago reversal, (vii) loss on debt modification and extinguishment, (viii) non-cash stock-based compensation, (ix) acquisition costs and (x) the income tax impact of adjustments that are subject to tax, which is determined using the incremental statutory tax rates of the jurisdictions to which each adjustment relates for the respective periods. Management believes that Adjusted Net Income helps investors understand the profitability of our business when excluding certain expenses that are non-recurring and/or non-operational in nature. Adjusted EPS is defined as Adjusted Net Income divided by weighted average diluted shares outstanding.
Adjusted Revenue is defined as total revenue, net adjusted to exclude the impact of the City of Chicago reversal. Adjusted Gross Profit is defined as gross profit adjusted to exclude the impact of the City of Chicago reversal. Adjusted Gross Profit Margin is calculated by dividing Adjusted Gross Profit by Adjusted Revenue. U.S. Gas Adjusted Revenue is defined as U.S. Gas segment revenue, net adjusted to exclude the impact of the City of Chicago reversal. U.S. Gas Adjusted Gross Profit is defined as U.S. Gas segment gross profit adjusted to exclude the City of Chicago reversal. U.S. Gas Adjusted Gross Profit Margin is calculated by dividing U.S. Gas Adjusted Gross Profit by U.S. Gas Adjusted Revenue.

Using EBIT, Adjusted EBIT, and Adjusted EBITDA as performance measures has material limitations as compared to net income (loss), or other financial measures as defined under GAAP, as they exclude certain recurring items, which may be meaningful to investors. These metrics all exclude interest expense net of interest income; however, as we have borrowed money to finance transactions and operations, or invested available cash to generate interest income, interest expense and interest income are elements of our cost structure and can affect our ability to generate revenue and returns for our stockholders. Further, these metrics exclude income taxes; however, as we are organized as a corporation, the payment of taxes is a necessary element of our operations. Adjusted EBITDA also excludes depreciation and amortization; however, as we use capital and intangible assets to generate revenue, depreciation and amortization are necessary elements of our costs and ability to generate revenue. As a result of these exclusions, the metrics from which they are excluded have material limitations compared to net income (loss). When using these metrics as a performance measure, management compensates for these limitations by comparing them to net income (loss) in each period, to allow for the comparison of the performance of the underlying core operations with the overall performance of the Company on a full-cost, after-tax basis.
As to certain of the items related to these non-GAAP measures: (i) non-cash stock-based compensation varies from period to period due to changes in the estimated fair value of performance-based awards, forfeitures and amounts granted; (ii) acquisition costs vary from period to period depending on the level of our acquisition activity; (iii) separation-related costs represent expenses incurred post-IPO in connection with the separation and stand up of Centuri as its own public company, including costs incurred in association with Southwest Gas Holdings’ sale of its holdings of our common stock, which are not reflective of our ongoing operations and will not recur given that Centuri is fully separated from Southwest Gas Holdings; (iv) strategy implementation costs represent non-recurring consulting fees incurred in connection with implementing the Company’s new long-term strategy announced on May 6, 2026; (v) severance costs related to non-recurring leadership restructuring; (vi) other professional fees are non-recurring costs associated with certain one-time events; (vii) the City of Chicago reversal relates to a non-recurring reversal of revenue on a legacy contract; and (viii) loss on debt modification and extinguishment represents non-recurring professional fees expensed as part of our credit facility refinance as well as the non-cash write-off of unamortized debt issuance costs associated with debt extinguishments.
