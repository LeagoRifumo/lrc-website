GLOBAL INDUSTRIAL REPORTS Third QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
Sales Increased 10.2% to $389.5 Million and 8.5% on an Average Daily Sales Basis
Diluted EPS Increased 33.3% to $0.64
Board Declared $0.28 Dividend and Special Dividend of $1.00
PORT WASHINGTON, NY, November 3, 2026 – Global Industrial Company (NYSE: GIC), a value-added distributor and source for industrial equipment and supplies today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

* | Global Industrial Company manages its business and reports using a 52-53 week fiscal year that ends at midnight on the Saturday closest to December 31. For clarity of presentation, fiscal years and quarters are described as if they ended on the last day of the respective calendar month. The actual fiscal quarters ended October 3, 2026 and September 27, 2025, respectively. The third quarters of both 2026 and 2025 included 13 weeks and the first nine months of both 2026 and 2025 included 39 weeks. Average daily sales is calculated based upon the number of selling days in each period, with Canadian sales converted to U.S. dollars using the current year's average exchange rate. There were 64 selling days in the U.S. in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 63 selling days in the third quarter of 2025 and there were 192 selling days in the U.S. for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to 191 selling days for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. There were 63 selling days in Canada in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 62 selling days in the third quarter of 2025 and there were 189 selling days in Canada for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 188 selling days for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Summary:
• | Consolidated sales increased 10.2% to $389.5 million compared to $353.6 million last year and average daily sales increased 8.5% compared to prior year.

• | Consolidated gross margin increased to 35.7% compared to 35.6% last year. Consolidated gross margin benefited by approximately 60 basis points from tariff refunds.

• | Consolidated operating income from continuing operations increased 24.3% to $32.7 million compared to $26.3 million last year. Consolidated operating income benefited by approximately $2.0 million from tariff refunds.

• | Net income per diluted share from continuing operations increased 33.3% to $0.64 compared to $0.48 last year. Net income per diluted share from continuing operations benefited by approximately $0.04 from tariff refunds.

Year to Date Q3 2026 Financial Summary:
• | Consolidated sales increased 9.0% to $1.13 billion compared to $1.03 billion last year and average daily sales increased 8.5% compared to prior year.

• | Consolidated gross margin increased to 36.9% compared to 35.9% last year. Consolidated gross margin benefited by approximately 200 basis points from tariff refunds.

• | Consolidated operating income from continuing operations increased 31.5% to $102.6 million compared to $78.0 million last year. Consolidated operating income benefited by approximately $23.1 million from tariff refunds.

• | Net income per diluted share from continuing operations increased 35.1% to $2.00 compared to $1.48 last year. Net income per diluted share from continuing operations benefited by approximately $0.46 from tariff refunds.

Anesa Chaibi, Chief Executive Officer, said, "Sustained sales momentum, improved profitability and continued execution of our go-to-market strategy drove another strong quarter for Global Industrial. Third quarter revenue increased 10.2% and we delivered our fourth consecutive quarter of high-single digit average daily sales growth, up 8.5%. Revenue growth was led by our industrial and retail customer verticals and strong double-digit performance among our largest strategic accounts.”
“Our go-to-market strategy is strengthening customer relationships, enabling us to win larger opportunities and to capture greater share of wallet. Over the past year, we have continued to advance our customer centric, relationship-driven B2B approach, with a focus on developing a deeper understanding of our customers, enhancing our vertical expertise and better aligning our sales, marketing and merchandising teams. We believe this progress, supported by our strong balance sheet, positions Global Industrial for continued profitable growth and long-term value creation.”
At September 30, 2026, the Company had total working capital of $266.2 million, cash and cash equivalents of $119.2 million, and excess availability under its credit facility of approximately $119.8 million. Operating cash flow provided by continuing operations in the quarter was $44.5 million. The Company’s Board of Directors has declared a cash dividend of $0.28 per share and a special cash dividend of $1.00 per share to common stock shareholders of record at the close of business on November 16, 2026, payable on November 23, 2026.

About Global Industrial Company
Global Industrial Company (NYSE: GIC), is a leading distributor of high-quality, industrial-strength equipment and supplies, serving organizations of all sizes across a wide range of industries. With more than 75 years of experience, customers rely on Global Industrial for its broad portfolio of national and private brands, trusted service and focus on value. We help customers keep their operations running by delivering the right products when they need them, because We Can Supply That®.
