ArcBest Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
​
● | Third-quarter revenue increased 15% year over year to $1.2 billion, driven by stronger performance across both operating segments

● | Disciplined pricing, productivity initiatives, and cost savings from organizational changes contributed to higher earnings and operating margin

​
FORT SMITH, Arkansas, November 4, 2026 — ArcBest® (Nasdaq: ARCB), a leader in supply chain logistics, announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
​
Third quarter 2026 revenue totaled $1.2 billion, compared to $1.0 billion in the third quarter of 2025. Net income was $51.7 million, or $2.30 per diluted share, compared to $39.3 million, or $1.72 per diluted share, in the prior-year period.
​
On a non-GAAP basis, net income was $58.7 million, or $2.62 per diluted share, compared to $33.4 million, or $1.46 per diluted share, in the prior-year period. Non-GAAP results exclude restructuring charges associated with the restructuring plan announced on July 16, 2026, as well as other items described in the attached non-GAAP reconciliation tables.
​
“The third quarter reflected the progress we’ve been making across ArcBest and the commitment of our people,” said Seth Runser, ArcBest President and CEO. “Stronger earnings and profitability were driven by disciplined execution, continued momentum in Asset-Light and ongoing savings from actions to simplify our organization and improve efficiency. We also continued to invest in technology and innovation to better serve our customers and support long-term profitable growth.”
​
Results of Operations Comparisons
Asset-Based
Third Quarter 2026 Versus Third Quarter 2025
● | Revenue of $798.6 million compared to $726.5 million, a per-day increase of 9.9 percent

● | Tonnage per day increase of 9.6 percent

● | Shipments per day decrease of 3.0 percent

● | Billed revenue per shipment increase of 14.2 percent

● | Billed revenue per hundredweight increase of 1.1 percent

● | Weight per shipment increase of 13.0 percent

● | Operating income of $75.6 million and an operating ratio of 90.5 percent, compared to $70.2 million and 90.3 percent

● | On a non-GAAP basis, operating income of $76.3 million and an operating ratio of 90.4 percent, compared to $54.4 million and 92.5 percent

​
Tonnage growth was driven by higher weight per shipment, reflecting a continued shift in freight profile, partially offset by fewer shipments per day. Revenue per shipment benefited from the heavier freight profile and slightly higher revenue per hundredweight, largely reflecting higher fuel surcharge revenue. Excluding fuel surcharge, revenue per hundredweight decreased in the low single digits.
​
Customer contracts and deferred pricing agreements renewed during the third quarter averaged a 5.1 percent increase, and LTL industry pricing remains rational.
1
​
​
Operating expenses increased due to annual union wage and benefit adjustments, as well as higher fuel and purchased transportation costs.
​
Compared to the second quarter of 2026, third quarter daily revenue improved 1.1 percent. Tonnage per day increased 0.6 percent, driven by a 0.6 percent increase in weight per shipment, while daily shipments were flat. Billed revenue per shipment increased 0.9 percent due to the heavier freight profile, while revenue per hundredweight increased less than one percent, both including and excluding fuel surcharge. The non-GAAP operating ratio was in line with second quarter.
​
Asset-Light
Third Quarter 2026 Versus Third Quarter 2025
● | Revenue of $446.3 million compared to $356.0 million, a per-day increase of 25.4 percent

● | Shipments per day increase of 1.2 percent

● | Revenue per shipment increase of 23.8 percent

● | Purchased transportation expense was 85.0 percent of revenue compared to 84.9 percent

● | Operating income of $12.5 million compared to operating loss of $1.6 million

● | On a non-GAAP basis, operating income of $14.4 million compared to $1.6 million

● | Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA”), as defined in the attached non-GAAP reconciliation tables, of $15.6 million compared to $3.1 million

​
Revenue increased primarily due to higher revenue per shipment, reflecting a tightening capacity environment along with modest shipment growth. Revenue growth, combined with disciplined cost management and productivity improvements, drove improved non-GAAP operating income compared to the prior year.
​
Compared to the second quarter of 2026, third quarter daily revenue increased 0.9 percent, reflecting a 3.8 percent increase in revenue per shipment, partially offset by a 2.7 percent decrease in daily shipments. Higher revenue per shipment and disciplined cost management resulted in improved non-GAAP operating income compared to the previous quarter.

About ArcBest
ArcBest® (Nasdaq: ARCB) is a multibillion-dollar integrated logistics company delivering end-to-end supply chain solutions designed around how businesses operate. Partnering with 30,000+ customers across the globe, ArcBest combines the reliability and control of an asset-based LTL network, the reach and flexibility of a brokerage network, and innovative technology to help build stronger, more efficient supply chains. ArcBest View™, the company’s digital logistics platform, gives customers greater control and clarity by bringing quoting, booking, shipment visibility and reporting into one connected experience. With 14,000 employees, approximately 240 campuses and service centers, and over a century of expertise, ArcBest delivers a premium experience built on reliable service, clear visibility and responsive support. For more information, visit arcb.com.
