HELMERICH & PAYNE, INC. ANNOUNCES FISCAL FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL 2026 RESULTS
Operating and Financial Highlights for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
▪H&P announced consolidated revenue of $1.1 billion, reflecting 6% sequential growth.
▪Consolidated net loss attributable to Helmerich & Payne, Inc. of $490 million, or $4.91 loss per share, which includes a $610 million non-cash asset impairment charge for assets primarily located in the Middle East. Adjusted for select items, adjusted net income(1) was $36 million, or $0.36 per share.
▪Consolidated adjusted EBITDA(2) totaled $269 million, increasing 14% sequentially.
▪North America Solutions (NAS) reported operating income of $151 million and achieved direct margin(3) of $259 million, increasing 7% sequentially.
•Announced the expanded deployment of FlexRobotics® to nine rigs working for ExxonMobil in the Lower 48, phased through 2027.
▪International Solutions reported an operating loss of approximately $648 million and achieved direct margin(3) of $42 million, increasing 34% sequentially.
▪Offshore Solutions reported operating income of approximately $17 million and achieved direct margin(3) of $34 million, increasing 18% sequentially.
▪Approximately $25 million returned to shareholders as part of the Company’s ongoing dividend program.
Fiscal Year 2026 Highlights
▪Fiscal year consolidated revenue of $4.1 billion.
▪Fiscal year net loss attributable to Helmerich & Payne, Inc. of $569 million.
▪Fiscal year adjusted EBITDA(2) of $913 million.
▪Completed the sale of Utica Square, generating approximately $100 million in after-tax proceeds and retired $400 million term loan facility ahead of schedule.
▪Gross capital expenditures of $287 million, within the $270 - $310 million guidance range.

Management Commentary
“I am proud of H&P’s fiscal 2026 performance and the strong finish we delivered,” said President and CEO Trey Adams. “In the fourth quarter, direct margins were at or above the high end of our guidance ranges across all segments.
“As we enter fiscal 2027, we maintain a constructive outlook. Our diversified operations and differentiated technology offerings continue to position us well despite ongoing market volatility. We expect numerous growth opportunities across key markets to drive stronger overall direct margins compared to fiscal 2026.
“In North America Solutions, activity continued to increase with an average of 151 rigs operating during the quarter, while daily margins also improved,” Adams said. “Stronger contract economics reflect the competitive strength of our super-spec fleet and our ability to deliver value to customers. Technology remains another key differentiator. Last month, we announced an expanded collaboration with ExxonMobil to deploy FlexRobotics® on a total of nine rigs. These dynamics, coupled with ongoing super-spec rig market tightness, strengthen our competitive position as we enter fiscal 2027.
“In International Solutions, direct margins improved meaningfully on a sequential basis, as strong growth in Argentina complemented improving margins in our Middle East operations,” said Adams. “Looking ahead, we expect momentum in the Vaca Muerta to continue as we mobilize three rigs from the U.S., lifting our rig count to 15, with several opportunities to expand further.
"We expect Middle East activity to remain subdued through 2027. Following recent notifications of conventional rig suspensions in Saudi Arabia, we now expect to operate an average of 19 rigs through the first fiscal quarter, with the potential for further activity reductions over the course of the year. We will continue to right-size our operational footprint and improve our cost structure as we focus on delivering differentiated results drilling unconventional and complex wells globally. Despite these reductions, we remain committed to driving strong year-over-year growth in direct margins.
“Our Offshore Solutions segment continued to deliver strong results, supported by consistent execution and the achievement of several performance-related bonuses. The long-term contract portfolio provides earnings visibility into fiscal 2027 and beyond."
Senior Vice President and CFO Todd Scruggs added, “2026 demonstrated our ability to remain fiscally disciplined and advance our financial priorities in a dynamic operating environment. During the year, we retired our acquisition-related term loan ahead of schedule, completed the sale of Utica Square, and initiated several Enterprise Optimization programs, and ended the year with $250 million of cash and cash equivalents.
“As we enter fiscal 2027, our capital allocation priorities remain focused on strengthening the balance sheet, maintaining our base dividend, and investing selectively in opportunities that support long-term growth,” Scruggs said. “The combination of strong margin growth and low capital spending levels will enable us to achieve our target of approximately 1x net debt to adjusted EBITDA by calendar year-end 2027.”
“Our leading fleet, differentiated technology solutions, and diversified global operations position us well for the year ahead,” Adams concluded. “We remain focused on disciplined execution and delivering long-term value for shareholders.”

Operating Segment Results for the Fourth Quarter of Fiscal Year 2026
North America Solutions: Realized operating income of $151 million, compared with $140 million in the previous quarter. Direct margin(3) increased to $259 million, versus $241 million the previous quarter. On a per-day basis, direct margins averaged $18,657 with 151 rigs active for the fourth fiscal quarter. Results were negatively impacted by a non-recurring inventory write-down charge of approximately $4 million.
International Solutions: Recorded operating loss of approximately $648 million, which included a $606 million asset impairment, compared with a loss of approximately $54 million in the prior quarter. Direct margin(3) improved to $42 million, up from $31 million last quarter. Results included the impact of a non-recurring inventory write-down charge and an accrual related to certain outstanding claims of approximately $2 million. During the fourth quarter we had an average of 66 rigs working.
Offshore Solutions: Reported operating income of approximately $17 million, in line with the previous quarter. Direct margin(3) was $34 million, up from $29 million last quarter, driven by strong performance-related bonuses. We had three active rigs and 29 management contracts in operation during the quarter.

Saudi Arabia Long Lived Asset Impairment
During the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, the Company identified indicators of impairment related to its Saudi Arabia T-Series asset group, including associated equipment, parts and supplies and customer relationship intangible assets. These developments resulted in a one-time non-cash impairment charge of $529.4 million, consisting of $456.8 million related to property, plant and equipment, $58.0 million related to customer relationship intangible assets and $14.6 million related to inventory.
The impairment charge relates to the 30-rig Saudi T-Series asset group and excludes the Company's Saudi FlexRigs, Oman operations, and six T-Series rigs classified as held-for-sale. While the impairment charge did not require a current-period cash outlay, the revised outlook reflects lower expected future cash flows from these operations, which continue to incur maintenance expenditures and costs associated with suspended rigs.

About Helmerich & Payne, Inc.
Founded in 1920, Helmerich & Payne, Inc. (H&P) (NYSE: HP) is committed to delivering industry leading levels of drilling productivity and reliability. H&P operates with the highest level of integrity, safety and innovation to deliver superior results for its customers and returns for shareholders. Through its subsidiaries, the Company designs, fabricates and operates high-performance drilling rigs in conventional and unconventional plays around the world. H&P also develops and implements advanced automation, directional drilling and survey management technologies. As of November 18, 2026, H&P's fleet includes 202 land rigs in the United States, 95 international land rigs and four offshore platform rigs, plus operating 29 offshore management contracts. For more information, see H&P online at www.hpinc.com.

NON-GAAP RECONCILIATION OF ADJUSTED EBITDA
Adjusted EBITDA and 'Select Items' are considered to be non-GAAP metrics. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) before taxes, depreciation and amortization, gains and losses on asset sales, other income and expense - which includes interest income and interest expense, and excludes the impact of 'select items' which management defines as certain items that do not reflect the ongoing performance of our core business operations. These metrics are included as supplemental disclosures as management uses them to assess and understand current operational performance, especially in analyzing historical trends which are used in forecasting future period results. For this reason, we believe this measure will be useful to information to investors. The presence of non-GAAP metrics is not intended to suggest that such measures should be considered as a substitute for certain GAAP metrics and, given that not all companies define Adjusted EBITDA the same way, this financial measure may not be comparable to similarly titled metrics disclosed by other companies.
