For the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, after-tax gains or losses arising from the sale and valuation of shares of Kioxia Holdings Corporation (Head office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Hiroo Ota; “Kioxia”), recognized through TB Investment Limited Partnership, an equity-method affiliate of ORIX Corporation, in connection with transactions by Toshiba Corporation (Head office: Kawasaki City, Kanagawa Prefecture; President and CEO: Taro Shimada; “Toshiba”), are recognized in ORIX Corporation’s consolidated earnings. Although such gains or losses are substantial, such valuation gains or losses are expected to fluctuate with future changes in Kioxia’s share price and therefore remain uncertain. In addition, ORIX Corporation will not directly receive cash inflows corresponding to such gains or losses.
Accordingly, ORIX Corporation’s dividend policy for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027 is that the annual dividend will be the higher of either an amount calculated by applying 39% to adjusted EPS calculated based on adjusted net income attributable to ORIX Corporation Shareholders excluding such gains or losses on an after-tax basis, or ¥156.10 per share. Based on this policy, the dividend for the second quarter-end was calculated based on adjusted EPS for the six months ended September 30, 2026, and the dividend per share was set at ¥113.56 per share, calculated by applying 39% to adjusted EPS based on adjusted net income attributable to ORIX Corporation Shareholders of ¥317,529 million after deducting the after-tax amount of such gains of ¥538,689 million from net income attributable to ORIX Corporation Shareholders of ¥856,218 million for the six months ended September 30, 2026.

Overview of Business Performance (April 1, 2026 to September 30, 2026)
Total revenues for the six months ended September 30, 2026 increased 10% to ¥1,673,875 million compared to ¥1,527,599 million during the same period of the previous fiscal year primarily due to increases in gains on investment securities and dividends, service income, and sales of goods and real estate.
Total expenses increased 11% to ¥1,434,355 million compared to ¥1,297,175 million during the same period of the previous fiscal year primarily due to increases in selling, general and administrative expenses and costs of goods and real estate sold.
Equity in net income of equity method investments was ¥809,610 million, primarily due to increased equity in net income from a certain equity method investee, partially offset by impairment of certain equity method investments. Gains on sales of subsidiaries and equity method investments and liquidation losses, net decreased 30% to ¥68,750 million compared to the same period of the previous fiscal year.
Due to the above results, income from continuing operations before income taxes for the six months ended September 30, 2026 increased 195% to ¥1,117,880 million compared to ¥378,951 million during the same period of the previous fiscal year and net income attributable to ORIX Corporation Shareholders increased 216% to ¥856,218 million compared to ¥271,096 million during the same period of the previous fiscal year.

(7) Segment Information (Unaudited)
The Group CEO, as the Chief Operating Decision Maker (“CODM”), regularly allocates management resources and assesses segment performance by using the amount equivalent to income before income taxes attributable to ORIX Corporation Shareholders of each business segment.
Beginning with the current consolidated fiscal year, the Company has reorganized its reportable segments into Japan & APAC, Infrastructure, USA & Europe, and Insurance, reflecting changes in the segments used by the CODM for resource allocation and performance evaluation purposes. In connection with this change, segment information for the previous interim consolidated period and as of the end of the previous fiscal year has been retrospectively recast.
The principal businesses of each segment are as follows
Japan & APAC:
The Japan & APAC segment provides business solutions to corporate clients and engages in corporate investment activities in Japan, Asia and Australia. Its principal businesses include financial services such as leasing, lending and various types of financing; services such as life insurance sales, real estate brokerage and M&A advisory; automobile leasing and fleet management; rental of electronic measuring instruments, ICT-related equipment, transformers and other equipment; and corporate investments in Japan and overseas.
Infrastructure:
The Infrastructure segment engages in the development and operation of assets that support social and industrial infrastructure, as well as the provision of related services. Its principal businesses include environmental and energy-related businesses such as renewable energy power generation, electricity retailing and waste management; real estate investment and asset management; housing-related businesses; operation of hotels, Japanese inns and other facilities; and operation of airports and other public infrastructure. The segment also engages in investment, asset management and brokerage businesses related to aircraft and ships.
USA & Europe:
The USA & Europe segment engages primarily in investment, financing, asset management and advisory businesses in the United States and Europe.Its principal businesses include investment and financial services such as corporate finance, real estate finance, real estate investment, debt investment, asset valuation and private equity investment, as well as asset management businesses covering equities, fixed income and other asset classes.
Insurance:
The Insurance segment engages in the development and sale of insurance products and asset management activities.

The accounting policies of the segments are almost the same as accounting policies for condensed consolidated financial statements except for the treatment of income tax expenses, net income attributable to noncontrolling interests, and net income attributable to redeemable noncontrolling interests. The CODM evaluates segment performance based on the amount equivalent to income before income taxes attributable to ORIX Corporation Shareholders. Therefore, net income attributable to noncontrolling interests, net income attributable to redeemable noncontrolling interests, and income tax expenses are not included in segment profit or loss. Most of selling, general and administrative expenses, including compensation costs that are directly related to the revenue generating activities of each segment and excluding the expenses that should be borne by ORIX Group as a whole, have been accumulated by and charged to each segment. Gains and losses that management does not consider for evaluating the performance of the segments, such as certain interest expenses and certain foreign exchange gains or losses (included in other (income) and expense) are excluded from the segment profits or losses, and are regarded as corporate items.
Assets attributed to each segment are total assets except for certain cash and head office assets.
Effective from the current fiscal year, the Company has revised the method of allocating interest expense for certain transactions. Accordingly, segment figures for the prior interim period have been recast to conform to the current presentation.

Segment information includes only amounts related to continuing operations and excludes the discontinued operations. Segment information for the previous interim consolidated period and as of the end of the previous fiscal year has also been recast to reflect this change.
