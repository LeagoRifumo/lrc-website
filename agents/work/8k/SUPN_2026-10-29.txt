Indivior Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
• Q3’26 Total Net Revenue of $356 Million, Up 13% YoY
• Record Quarterly Total SUBLOCADE® Net Revenue of $267 Million in Q3’26, Up 22% YoY
• Record Quarterly Net Income of $137 Million and Record Non-GAAP Net Income of $161 Million in Q3’26
• Record Quarterly Adjusted EBITDA of $211 Million in Q3’26, Up 76% YoY
• Net Income Margin of 39%, up 2,500 basis points YoY, Adjusted EBITDA Margin of 59%, Up 2,100 Basis Points YoY
• On Track to Close Merger with Supernus on November 2nd
Richmond, VA, October 29, 2026 – Indivior Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: INDV) today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“In the third quarter, we delivered record SUBLOCADE net revenue and record adjusted EBITDA, reflecting the strength of our business and the disciplined execution of the Indivior Action Agenda,” said Joe Ciaffoni, Chief Executive Officer. “Upon the imminent closing of our merger with Supernus, we will have successfully completed all three phases of the Indivior Action Agenda. I want to thank the Indivior team for their commitment and hard work that generated these results and made the transaction with Supernus possible. I am confident in Supernus’ future and its ability to build on this strong foundation for people living with central nervous system diseases and to create long-term value for shareholders.”
“We achieved record financial performance in the third quarter, including SUBLOCADE net revenue growth of 22% year-over-year and adjusted EBITDA margin of 59%, up 2100 basis points year-over-year,” said Ryan Preblick, Chief Financial Officer. “Our strong results and the execution of our Indivior Action Agenda puts us on track to achieve our previously communicated 2026 financial guidance, excluding the impact of our pending acquisition. Strong cash generation brought cash and investments to $332 million at quarter end, adding to the financial strength we will bring to our merger with Supernus.”
Q3 2026 Business Highlights:
• | As of September 30, 2026, over 583,000 patients in the U.S. have been prescribed SUBLOCADE since launch.

• | Grew total SUBLOCADE net revenue 22% year-over-year to $267 million. U.S. SUBLOCADE net revenue increased 24% year-over-year to $252 million versus the prior year, primarily driven by 17% dispense unit volume growth. Net revenue also benefited from more favorable price/mix and gross-to-net adjustments.

• | In August 2026, Indivior and Supernus Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (“Supernus”) announced a definitive agreement to combine in an all-stock merger of equals transaction to create a leading, diversified, scaled, CNS-focused biopharmaceutical company.

• | In September 2026, Indivior announced that its Board of Directors declared a special cash dividend contingent upon closing of the pending merger transaction with Supernus.

• | In October 2026, Indivior shareholders approved the proposed merger with Supernus. The merger is expected to close on November 2, 2026, at which time the combined company will operate as Supernus, Inc. and will trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol “SUPN” beginning November 3, 2026.

Full-Year 2026 Financial Guidance:
Prior to the closing of the merger, Indivior is on track to achieve its previously communicated full year 2026 guidance as a standalone organization. That guidance assumed no material change in exchange rates for key currencies compared with 2025 average rates, notably USD/GBP and USD/EUR. Assuming the merger is completed November 2, 2026, we will operate as a combined company and the guidance will no longer apply.

Financial Results for Quarter Ended September 30, 2026:
• | Total net revenue was $356 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 (the 2026 quarter), compared to $314 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025 (the 2025 quarter), representing a 13% increase year-over-year.

• | Total SUBLOCADE net revenue was $267 million for the 2026 quarter, compared to $219 million for the 2025 quarter, representing a 22% increase year-over-year.

• | GAAP operating expenses were $125 million for the 2026 quarter, compared to $187 million for the 2025 quarter, representing a 33% decrease year-over-year. Non-GAAP operating expenses, which exclude stock-based compensation expense and other adjustments, were $103 million for the 2026 quarter, compared to $145 million for the 2025 quarter, representing a 29% decrease year-over-year.

• | GAAP net income for the 2026 quarter was $137 million ($1.12 diluted earnings per share), compared to GAAP net income for the 2025 quarter of $42 million ($0.33 diluted earnings per share). Non-GAAP net income for the 2026 quarter was $161 million ($1.32 diluted earnings per share), compared to non-GAAP net income for the 2025 quarter of $93 million ($0.72 diluted earnings per share).

• | Adjusted EBITDA for the 2026 quarter was $211 million, compared to $120 million for the 2025 quarter, representing a 76% increase year-over-year.

• | The Company ended the 2026 quarter with cash and investments of $332 million.

Financial Results for Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026:
• | Total net revenue was $1,016 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 (the 2026 period), compared to $881 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 (the 2025 period), representing a 15% increase year-over-year.

• | Total SUBLOCADE net revenue was $753 million for the 2026 period, compared to $604 million for the 2025 period, representing a 25% increase year-over-year.

• | GAAP operating expenses were $398 million for the 2026 period, compared to $521 million for the 2025 period, representing a 24% decrease year-over-year. Non-GAAP operating expenses, which exclude stock-based compensation expense and other adjustments, were $331 million for the 2026 period, compared to $459 million for the 2025 period, representing a 28% decrease year-over-year.

• | GAAP net income for the 2026 period was $348 million ($2.78 diluted earnings per share), compared to GAAP net income for the 2025 period of $108 million ($0.85 diluted earnings per share). Non-GAAP net income for the 2026 period was $427 million ($3.41 diluted earnings per share), compared to non-GAAP net income for the 2025 period of $213 million ($1.68 diluted earnings per share).

• | Adjusted EBITDA for the 2026 period was $562 million, compared to $286 million for the 2025 period, representing a 97% increase year-over-year.

About Indivior
As the leader in long-acting injectable treatments for opioid use disorder (OUD), Indivior is singularly focused on delivering evidence-based treatment and advancing understanding of OUD as a chronic but treatable brain disease. For more than 25 years, we have revolutionized the science of addiction medicine — developing treatments that help people move toward long-term recovery with independence and dignity. Building on this heritage, we are ushering in a new era, renewing our commitment to individuals living with OUD and carrying forward what matters most: compassion, integrity, and science. Together – with science, people living with OUD, public health champions, and communities, we are powering recovery and renewing hope. Visit www.indivior.com to learn more. Adjusted EBITDA
Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure that represents GAAP net income adjusted to exclude interest expense, interest income, income tax expense or benefit, depreciation and amortization, stock-based compensation, and other adjustments reflecting changes in our business that do not represent ongoing operations. Adjusted EBITDA margin is Adjusted EBITDA divided by Total net revenue. Adjusted EBITDA, as used by us, may be calculated differently from, and therefore may not be comparable to, similarly titled measures used by other companies.
