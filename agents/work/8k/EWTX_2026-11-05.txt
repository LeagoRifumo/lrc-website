News Release
Edgewise Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
– Published the differentiated mechanism of action of EDG-7500 that targets the regulatory light chain –
– Phase 3 trial for EDG-7500 in obstructive and nonobstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) on track to initiate by year-end 2026 –
– Dosed first patient in Phase 2 EQUINOX-HFpEF trial of EDG-15400 in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) –
BOULDER, Colo., Nov. 4, 2026 — Edgewise Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: EWTX), a leading clinical-stage cardiovascular biopharmaceutical company, today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026 and recent business highlights.
“Our third quarter reflects continued execution across both cardiovascular programs,” said Kevin Koch, Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of Edgewise. “In HCM, positive CIRRUS-HCM Part D results and our JCI Insight publication reinforce the differentiated profile of EDG-7500, and we expect to initiate our Phase 3 trial by year-end 2026. In parallel, we dosed our first patient in the Phase 2 EQUINOX-HFpEF trial of EDG-15400, our selective cardiac sarcomere modulator designed to address impaired cardiac relaxation in HFpEF.”
Recent Highlights
EDG-7500 for symptomatic HCM: EDG-7500 is an investigational, oral, first-in-class cardiac sarcomere modulator that is designed to act on the regulatory light chain to slow early contraction velocity and improve impaired cardiac relaxation associated with HCM. In August 2026, the Company shared positive top-line results from the 12-week Phase 2 Part D CIRRUS-HCM open label trial of EDG-7500 in obstructive and nonobstructive HCM in a late-breaking science presentation at the European Society of Cardiology Congress, which can be found here. CIRRUS-HCM was designed to inform a Phase 3 trial, which the Company expects to initiate by the end of 2026.
The Company also published the preclinical pharmacologic profile of EDG-7500, targeting the regulatory light chain, in JCI Insight, which can be found here. The data support EDG-7500's ability to improve cardiac relaxation while preserving overall systolic performance. Furthermore, early clinical observations in patients with symptomatic HCM have been consistent with these preclinical data.
EDG-15400 Phase 2 EQUINOX-HFpEF in heart failure: EDG-15400 is an investigational, oral, selective cardiac sarcomere modulator that has demonstrated improvements in diastolic function in preclinical models and is being developed to address impaired cardiac relaxation, a hallmark of HFpEF and a major contributor to patient symptoms and disease burden. The Company dosed the first patient in the Phase 2 EQUINOX-HFpEF trial evaluating EDG-15400 in adults with symptomatic HFpEF and a left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) of at least 50%. To learn more about this study, go to ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT07795814).
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were approximately $1,743.9 million as of September 30, 2026.
Research and development (R&D) expenses were $31.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $37.5 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily attributable to lower costs associated with sevasemten following its sale to Servier in July 2026, partially offset by higher program spend on EDG-7500, discovery and preclinical programs, and personnel-related costs.
General and administrative (G&A) expenses were $14.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $9.4 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to higher personnel-related costs, including stock-based compensation, as the Company expanded its organizational capabilities to support clinical development, as well as increased professional fees and other administrative costs.
Gain on sevasemten sale was $1.348 billion for the third quarter of 2026 and reflects the completion of the sale of the sevasemten program and muscular dystrophy business to Servier in July 2026. The gain reflects consideration recognized at closing, including amounts associated with the general indemnification arrangement, less the carrying value of assets transferred, transaction costs and consideration allocated to transition services. The Company received $1.396 billion in upfront cash proceeds at closing.
Net income and net income per share were $1,166.2 million, or $10.71 per basic share and $9.65 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a net loss and net loss per share of $40.7 million, or $0.39 per basic and diluted share, for the same period in 2025, primarily reflecting the gain recognized on the sale of sevasemten to Servier, partially offset by income tax expense associated with the transaction.
About Edgewise Therapeutics
Edgewise Therapeutics is a cardiovascular biotechnology company developing novel therapies intended to address cardiac equilibrium, the balance of contraction and relaxation across the cardiac cycle. Drawing on deep expertise in integrated muscle physiology and small molecule drug development, Edgewise is advancing novel therapies intended to fine-tune cardiac muscle function and improve treatment options for people with symptomatic hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and heart failure. To learn more, go to edgewisetx.com or follow Edgewise on LinkedIn, X, Facebook and Instagram.
