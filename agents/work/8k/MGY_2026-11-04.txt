Magnolia Oil & Gas Corporation Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
HOUSTON, TX, November 4, 2026 - Magnolia Oil & Gas Corporation (“Magnolia,” “we,” “our,” or the “Company”) (NYSE: MGY) today announced its financial and operational results for the third quarter of 2026.

•Magnolia reported third quarter 2026 net income of $80.5 million, or $0.35 per diluted share. Third quarter 2026 adjusted net income (1) was $166.3 million, or $0.71 per diluted share. Adjusted net income, which increased by 114% compared to the same prior-year period, excludes special items consisting primarily of transaction and integration costs related to the acquisition of WildFire Energy that closed in the third quarter, and net unrealized losses on derivative contracts.
•Adjusted EBITDAX (1) was $392.0 million during the third quarter of 2026. Total drilling and completions (“D&C”) capital was $147.4 million for the third quarter and below our earlier guidance, representing approximately 38% of adjusted EBITDAX.
•Net cash provided by operating activities was $369.0 million during the third quarter of 2026 with adjusted free cash flow(1) generated by the Company of $223.7 million. Magnolia generated adjusted operating income as a percentage of revenue (1) (pre-tax margins) of 43% during the third quarter.
•Total Company production volumes in the third quarter of 2026 grew by 17% on a year-over-year basis to 117.3 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day (“Mboe/d”) and included 49.1 thousand barrels of oil per day (“Mbbls/d”) which grew by 24% compared to the prior year period. Total production in Giddings increased 23% year-over-year to 97.7 Mboe/d with oil volumes growing by 39%.
•The Company repurchased 2.3 million shares of its Class A Common Stock during the third quarter for $60.2 million. Magnolia’s Board of Directors has increased the existing share repurchase authorization by an additional 15 million shares, bringing the total remaining authorization to 22.6 million shares of Class A Common Stock.
•As previously announced, Magnolia’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.18 per share, payable on December 1, 2026, to shareholders of record as of November 10, 2026.
•Magnolia returned $103.3 million, or 46% of the Company’s adjusted free cash flow(1), to shareholders during the third quarter through a combination of share repurchases and dividends. Magnolia ended the third quarter with $17.5 million of cash on the balance sheet and $435 million in outstanding borrowings on its revolving credit facility.
“Magnolia continues to execute at a high level as we successfully integrate the WildFire assets,” said Magnolia’s Chairman, President and CEO Chris Stavros. “Although our third quarter results include only a partial period of ownership following the September closing, the benefits of the combined asset base are already clearly evident. Our business generated $224 million of adjusted free cash flow during the quarter, with a reinvestment rate of just 38 percent of adjusted EBITDAX. That strong momentum has carried into the fourth quarter – our first full quarter as a combined company.
1
“The integration continues to progress smoothly, and we are meeting all our planned milestones. Synergy capture remains ahead of our expectations, with more than one-third of the anticipated annual synergies on track to be realized by year-end. We are currently running four rigs and two completion crews, and have recently substituted some equipment in an effort to capture further operational and capital efficiencies. Recent Austin Chalk well results on WildFire operated acreage are encouraging and are supportive of our geological model that led to Magnolia’s strong Austin Chalk development plan over the last several years. These early results also suggest greater inventory potential than our earlier baseline acquisition analysis. We plan to continue to delineate the Austin Chalk across our now 1.25 million net acre position in Giddings and our 2027 company-wide drilling program is expected to allocate approximately 65 percent of targeted activity to the Austin Chalk and 35 percent to the Eagle Ford.
“Supported by these strong operational and financial results, Magnolia returned approximately $103 million to shareholders during the quarter through our dividend and share repurchase program, while reducing net debt to approximately $1.9 billion. We achieved our goal of less than 1.0x net debt to forecasted EBITDAX more than a year sooner than originally planned when we announced the WildFire acquisition. The significant free cash flow generation allows us to continue aggressively reducing leverage, while consistently paying and growing our dividend, and maintaining our peer-leading program of repurchasing at least 1 percent of shares outstanding each quarter. Magnolia’s Board has increased our share repurchase authorization by an incremental 15 million shares, bringing the remaining authorization to 22.6 million shares – or nearly 8.5 percent of current shares outstanding – providing us the flexibility to lean into additional repurchases above the 1 percent level opportunistically and when warranted.”
Operational Update
Total Company production volumes in the third quarter of 2026 grew by 17 percent on a year-over-year basis to 117.3 Mboe/d including 49.1 Mbbls/d. Total production in Giddings increased by 23 percent in the third quarter, compared to the prior year period with Giddings oil production growing by 39 percent year-over-year. Giddings production represented 83 percent of total Company volumes during the third quarter. The Company’s third quarter oil production mix represented approximately 42 percent of total volumes, including only a partial period of WildFire ownership, and is expected to rise to 49 to 50 percent of total production in the fourth quarter. Magnolia’s third quarter 2026 capital spending on drilling, completions, and associated facilities was $147.4 million.
Additional Guidance
Fourth quarter 2026 D&C capital spending is estimated to be roughly $235 million. Total production for the fourth quarter is expected to be 159 to 161 Mboe/d (49% to 50% oil).
2027 production is estimated to grow 4 to 5 percent for both oil and total production off a second quarter 2026 pro forma production base of approximately 78 Mbbls/d and 158 Mboe/d after accounting for the production volumes from the non-core asset sale in the third quarter. D&C capital spending is currently estimated to be in the range of $900 to $950 million during 2027 which incorporates a modest amount of oil field service inflation.

About Magnolia Oil & Gas Corporation
Magnolia (MGY) is a publicly traded oil and gas exploration and production company with operations primarily in South Texas in the core of the Eagle Ford Shale and Austin Chalk formations. Magnolia focuses on generating value for shareholders by delivering steady, moderate annual production growth resulting from its disciplined and efficient philosophy toward capital spending. The Company strives to generate high pre‐tax margins and consistent free cash flow allowing for strong cash returns to our shareholders. For more information, visit www.magnoliaoilgas.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Reconciliation of net income to adjusted EBITDAX
In this press release, we refer to adjusted EBITDAX, a supplemental non-GAAP financial measure that is used by management and external users of our consolidated financial statements, such as industry analysts, investors, lenders, and rating agencies. We define adjusted EBITDAX as net income before interest expense, income taxes, depreciation, depletion and amortization, exploration expenses, accretion of asset retirement obligations, transaction and integration costs, and unrealized loss on derivatives, net, adjusted to exclude the effect of certain items included in net income. Adjusted EBITDAX is not a measure of net income in accordance with GAAP.
Our management believes that adjusted EBITDAX is useful because it allows them to more effectively evaluate our operating performance and compare the results of our operations from period to period and against our peers without regard to our financing methods or capital structure. We also believe that securities analysts, investors, and other interested parties may use adjusted EBITDAX in the evaluation of our Company. We exclude the items listed above from net income in arriving at adjusted EBITDAX because these amounts can vary substantially from company to company within our industry depending upon accounting methods and book values of assets, capital structures and the method by which the assets were acquired. Adjusted EBITDAX should not be considered as an alternative to, or more meaningful than, net income as determined in accordance with GAAP or as an indicator of our operating performance or liquidity. Certain items excluded from adjusted EBITDAX are significant components in understanding and assessing a company’s financial performance, such as a company’s cost of capital and tax structure, as well as the historic costs of depreciable assets, none of which are components of adjusted EBITDAX. Our presentation of adjusted EBITDAX should not be construed as an inference that our results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items. Our computations of adjusted EBITDAX may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies.

Management uses adjusted net income to evaluate our operating and financial performance because it eliminates the impact of certain items that management does not consider to be representative of the Company’s on-going business operations. As a performance measure, adjusted net income may be useful to investors in facilitating comparisons to others in the Company’s industry because certain items can vary substantially in the oil and gas industry from company to company depending upon accounting methods, book value of assets, and capital structure, among other factors. Management believes adjusting these items facilitates investors and analysts in evaluating and comparing the underlying operating and financial performance of our business from period to period by eliminating differences caused by the existence and timing of certain expense and income items that would not otherwise be apparent on a GAAP basis. However, our presentation of adjusted net income may not be comparable to similar measures of other companies in our industry.

Free cash flow and adjusted free cash flow are used by management as an additional measure of liquidity.
