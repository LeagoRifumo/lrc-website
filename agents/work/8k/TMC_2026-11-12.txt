TMC Provides Third Quarter 2026 Corporate Update
NEW YORK, November 12, 2026 — TMC the metals company Inc. (Nasdaq: TMC) (“TMC” or “the Company”), a leading developer of the world’s largest resource of critical metals essential to energy, defense, manufacturing and infrastructure, today provided a corporate update and third quarter financial results for the period ended September 30, 2026.
Q3 2026 Financial Highlights
· | Liquidity of approximately $123 million available from our cash on hand and our credit facilities as of September 30, 2026

· | $19.4 million cash used in operations for the quarter ended September 30, 2026

· | Net loss of $28.4 million and net loss per share of 6 cents for the quarter ended September 30, 2026

TMC USA’s Applications Continue Progressing Through NOAA Review
· | In a November 3, 2026 letter, the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (“NOAA”) stated that it now expects to notify our subsidiary, The Metals Company USA (“TMC USA”), of its final determination as to certification of the TMC USA-A consolidated application by mid-December 2026, rather than the end of October

· | The letter references NOAA’s process, specifically the need for adequate time to review over 1,600 public comments received during the public comment period that closed on October 19. The letter does not identify any issue or deficiency with the application

· | Certification would be a major permitting milestone. It would reflect NOAA’s determination that TMC USA has the financial and technical capacity to carry out its proposed nodule collection operations in the USA-A area, and that its commercial recovery plan meets the statutory requirements

· | Based on NOAA guidance, the Company continues to expect issuance of the permit in advance of the targeted Q4 2027 offshore collection system commissioning; the Company believes the rigor of NOAA’s review supports the permit’s long-term durability and legal defensibility and reinforces the high standards applicable to all applicants

Allseas Partnership: Secure First Production, Unlock Full-Scale Growth
· | TMC and Allseas are advancing a capital-light approach intended to enable the start of commercial operations upon potential permit grant, reduce upfront investment and operating costs through a simplified offshore system designed to meet the first two years’ production targets

· | A planned second production vessel is expected to be key to the Company's transition to full-scale operations, enabling Hidden Gem to undergo its full-capacity conversion in Year 3 while maintaining continuous production

· | The phased approach would enable TMC to scale production and investment progressively, aligned with operational performance and available funding, while preserving the original timeline to reach steady-state production of 12 million wet tonnes per annum

U.S. Government Funding Update
· | The Company is actively engaged in funding processes with specific U.S. agencies named in Executive Order 14285 regarding plans to build nodule processing and refining capacity in the United States

· | While these processes continue confidentially, the Company does not currently intend to pursue other capital market transactions until such time as one or more of these processes are completed

Gerard Barron, Chairman & CEO of TMC, commented: “Our Commercial Recovery Permit application for USA-A is going through a thorough review at NOAA. Recent developments give us confidence that we could have the permit in hand by this time next year. Our focus is on being ready to mobilize for production directly after we receive it.
Together with Allseas, we’re working to significantly reduce the capital required to bring Hidden Gem into production. Rather than targeting maximum production capacity from day one, we’re prioritizing speed to first production and a lower upfront investment, which is good news for TMC shareholders. Even at these lower initial volumes, we expect the operation to be EBITDA-positive in its first years.
Our objective remains to build a greenfield processing and refining facility in Brownsville, but having it up and running in time to receive the first nodules from our offshore operations is a challenge. That’s where we may lean on our five-year partnership with PAMCO and its existing processing facility in Japan. PAMCO will need to make some modifications to its plant to prepare for the arrival of the first nodules. Our team and external engineering consultants have been in Hachinohe this week working through those plans to make sure our schedules align.
In parallel, we continue to work closely with Mariana Minerals and are making good progress on the feasibility, permitting, capital requirements and operating costs of a nodule processing plant in Brownsville, leveraging Mariana’s software-first, AI-driven approach to plant design, construction and operations. Due diligence is progressing at full pace, with two U.S. government agencies considering financial support for our Brownsville plant. Encouragingly, the agencies are working together on the file, and I hope to have more substantive news to share on that soon.”
Operational Highlights
NOAA Holds Virtual Public Hearing and Closes First 60-Day Public Comment Period for the USA-A Consolidated Application
On October 19, 2026, NOAA closed the first public comment period for the consolidated application submitted by TMC USA for an exploration license and commercial recovery permit over the USA-A area under the Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources Act (“DSHMRA”) and its implementing regulations. Prior to the closure of the 60-day comment period, NOAA held a virtual public hearing on October 13 to hear from the public as to our application. As part of NOAA’s transparent permitting process, the public will have multiple opportunities to share their views on the substance of the USA-A application.
NOAA Publishes TMC USA’s Consolidated Application for an Exploration License and Commercial Recovery Permit for TMC USA-A in the Federal Register
On August 19, 2026, we announced that NOAA had published in the Federal Register the consolidated application submitted by TMC USA for the USA-A area under DSHMRA and its implementing regulations. Publication of the consolidated application in the Federal Register followed the determination of full compliance by NOAA on April 28, 2026, and provided public notice of TMC USA’s submission. We believe the rigor of NOAA’s review supports the long-term durability and legal defensibility of any licenses or permits issued and reinforces the high standards applicable to all applicants. NOAA’s review will continue under DSHMRA and its implementing regulations and is now expected to proceed through remaining steps, including:
2
- | Certifying the application

- | Conducting the required environmental review and providing a draft Environmental Impact Statement (“EIS”) for public comment

- | Making a final determination on whether to issue the exploration license and commercial recovery permit and associated Terms, Conditions, and Restrictions (“TCRs”)

NOAA Publishes Notice of Intent in the Federal Register to Prepare an Environmental Impact Statement for TMC USA’s USA-B Exploration License Application
On August 17, 2026, we announced that NOAA had published in the Federal Register a Notice of Intent (“NOI”) to prepare an EIS for the exploration license application submitted by the Company’s subsidiary, TMC USA, under DSHMRA and its implementing regulations. The USA-B application area in the Clarion Clipperton Zone of the Pacific Ocean covers approximately 122,000 km2 of seafloor and hosts an estimated 1.02 billion tonnes of polymetallic nodules based on TMC’s Technical Report Summary for the Initial Assessment published in August 2025. The publication of the NOI followed the earlier certification of TMC USA’s application for an exploration license over the USA-B area and represented another key step in a steady, transparent cadence of expected regulatory milestones:
- | NOAA will publish for public comment a draft EIS and draft Terms, Conditions and Restrictions (“TCRs”) for TMC USA’s proposed exploration activities on the USA-B area

- | Following the public comment period, NOAA will consider comments and finalize the EIS

- | NOAA will then make a final determination on the issuance of the license and its associated TCRs

TMC Announces Appointment of Liam Mallon, Former President of ExxonMobil Upstream, to its Board of Directors
On September 29, 2026, we announced that Liam Mallon, former President of ExxonMobil Upstream Company, had joined our Board of Directors. Mr. Mallon brings over 40 years of global energy and offshore operating experience, including leadership of ExxonMobil’s worldwide upstream business, where he oversaw $20–30 billion in annual capital deployment and major U.S. shale and offshore deepwater growth initiatives such as ExxonMobil’s landmark Guyana development and $60 billion Pioneer Natural Resources acquisition.
TMC USA Enters into MSA with Mariana Minerals
On July 21, 2026, TMC USA entered into a Master Services Agreement with Mariana Minerals for a phased program to advance a proposed polymetallic nodule processing and refining industry park at the Port of Brownsville, Texas, beginning with mobilization, concept development and technical design work to establish plant feasibility and design basis, as well as a trade-off and opportunity assessment program for Phase 1, Stage 1 of the proposed onshore nodule processing facility.
3
TMC and Eco Minerals Enter Exclusive Offshore Survey and Exploration Services Agreement
On July 22, 2026, TMC and Eco Minerals entered into a Mutual Master Services Agreement under which Eco Minerals will serve, subject to vessel availability, as TMC’s exclusive provider of vessel charter and marine survey services, including advanced seafloor mapping, sampling and autonomous survey capabilities. TMC will provide Eco Minerals with environmental and regulatory advisory services, drawing on its offshore assessment and permitting experience. The companies may jointly pursue third-party survey and exploration services opportunities. The agreement builds on an existing memorandum of understanding identifying TMC as a potential processing partner for Eco Minerals, subject to a standards-compliant prefeasibility study, due diligence, definitive agreements and required regulatory approvals.
Industry Update
TMCR Royalty Interest Mesabi Metallics Confirms $15 Billion Investment in Largest Steel Complex in U.S. History
On September 28, 2026, Mesabi Metallics announced it would invest $15 billion in a new steel complex in Iowa, and an additional $3 billion for the completion of its Iron Range iron ore mine in Minnesota. The Metals Royalty Co. (Nasdaq: TMCR) has a 2.0% Gross Overriding Royalty on the Mesabi Metallics iron ore project. The plant and supporting mine represent a major step toward rebuilding America’s supply chain for steel production, which also requires large quantities of manganese.
TMC USA Partner Mariana Minerals Raises $310 Million Series B
On August 3, 2026, Mariana Minerals announced a $310 million Series B financing led by Khosla Ventures, with participation from Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Breakthrough Energy Ventures, BHP Ventures, Mitsubishi Corporation and In-Q-Tel, among others. The round brings Mariana’s total parent and project capital raised to approximately $400 million. Mariana’s software-first platform, MarianaOS, is designed to cut traditional project timelines roughly in half. Under the Master Services Agreement it signed with TMC USA in July, Mariana is leading feasibility-level engineering for the first smelting stage at the proposed Brownsville processing site.
Financial Results Overview
At September 30, 2026, we held cash of approximately $79.3 million and had no financial debt. We believe that our cash position will be sufficient to meet our working capital and capital expenditure commitments for at least the next twelve months.
4
We reported a net loss of approximately $28.4 million, or $0.06 per share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $184.5 million, or $0.46 per share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Exploration and evaluation expenses during the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $23.5 million compared to $9.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The third quarter of 2026 included higher costs to recognize the full charge of ongoing development, operating, and vessel costs, higher share-based compensation expense, an increase in feasibility and prefeasibility study costs and an increase in legal and professional costs related to the project.
General and administrative expenses were $9.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $45.7 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, due to a $32.7 million lower share-based compensation expense, and a decrease in legal fees.

About The Metals Company
The Metals Company is a developer of lower-impact critical metals from seafloor polymetallic nodules, on a dual mission: (1) supply metals for energy, defense, manufacturing and infrastructure with net positive impacts compared to conventional production routes and (2) trace, recover and recycle the metals we supply to help create a metal commons that can be used in perpetuity. The Company has conducted more than a decade of research into the environmental and social impacts of offshore nodule collection and onshore processing. More information is available at www.metals.co.
