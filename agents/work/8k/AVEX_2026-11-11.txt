AEVEX Corp. Announces Financial Results for Third Quarter 2026
SOLANA BEACH, Calif., November 11, 2026 – AEVEX Corp. (NYSE: AVEX) (“AEVEX” or the Company) announced results today for the three months ended September 30, 2026 (“Third Quarter 2026”).
• | Total revenue of $189.7 million, up 56.5% from $121.2 million in Q3 2025

• | Net loss of $16.8 million compared to net income of $9.9 million in Q3 2025

• | Adjusted EBITDA* of $30.8 million compared to Adjusted EBITDA* of $17.8 million in Q3 2025

• | Completed the acquisition of BlackSea Technologies on September 8, 2026, establishing Maritime Systems as AEVEX’s third reportable segment

Raised Outlook for Full Year 2026
• | Total revenue of $820.0 million to $840.0 million, compared to our prior range of $700.0 million to $720.0 million

• | Adjusted EBITDA* of $128.0 million to $132.0 million, compared to prior range of $105.0 million to $111.5 million

* | See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below for an explanation of this measure. The Company is unable to provide a reconciliation for forward-looking outlook of Adjusted EBITDA to net income (loss), the most closely comparable GAAP measure without unreasonable effort, because certain material reconciling items cannot be estimated due to factors outside of the Company’s control and could have a material impact on the reported results. However, the Company estimates depreciation and amortization of approximately $30.0 million and net interest expense of approximately $13.0 million for the year ending December 31, 2026.

“AEVEX delivered strong third-quarter results with total revenue and Adjusted EBITDA growth, and related margin expansion,” said Roger Wells, Chief Executive Officer of AEVEX. “Beyond the success in our legacy business, we announced and closed on our first acquisition as a public company, with the purchase of BlackSea Technologies. This combination expands our capabilities across air, surface and subsea domains, strengthens our production infrastructure and is creating new opportunities to deliver collaborative, mission-focused solutions to a broader set of customers.”
“The strength of our third quarter performance, end market demand and the addition of BlackSea gives us the confidence to raise our full-year outlook. We look forward to continuing to advance the strategic priorities we outlined at the IPO earlier this year, as we are scaling production, broadening our mission and customer reach, investing ahead of the threat and building a differentiated multi-domain autonomous systems company.”

Financial Results for Third Quarter 2026
Total revenues increased to $189.7 million from $121.2 million, or by $68.5 million, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the same period in 2025. The increase is primarily due to $59.3 million of higher revenues in our Airborne Systems Segment and $12.4 million of higher revenue in our Maritime Systems Segment driven from the recent acquisition of BlackSea. The increase is partially offset by $3.2 million of lower revenue in our Global Solutions Segment.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, our net loss was $16.8 million with a net loss margin of 8.8% compared to a net income of $9.9 million with a net income margin of 8.2% for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The $26.7 million unfavorable change was primarily driven by a $41.7 million increase in provision for income tax and $21.1 million increase in selling, general and administrative expenses. These unfavorable impacts were partially offset by a $20.1 million increase in products gross profit, a $4.3 million increase in services gross profit, a $6.3 million increase in other income, net, and a $5.9 million decrease in interest expense.
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, Adjusted EBITDA was $30.8 million with Adjusted EBITDA margin of 16.2% compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $17.8 million with Adjusted EBITDA margin of 14.6% for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in Adjusted EBITDA was primarily driven by an $18.2 million increase in Airborne Systems Adjusted EBITDA as a result of the increase in products revenue and decrease in research and development expense for UAS products and services, which was partially offset by the increase in cost of products revenue and the increase in selling, general and administrative expenses. In addition, the increase was also partially offset by a $2.9 million decrease in Global Solutions Adjusted EBITDA, primarily due to lower gross profit from aircraft modifications, higher selling, general, and administrative expenses, and higher unallocated corporate costs.

Airborne Systems
Airborne Systems segment revenue increased to $147.8 million from $88.5 million, or by $59.3 million and 67.1%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase is primarily due to higher revenue from UAS products and support services.
Airborne Systems Adjusted EBITDA increased to $33.5 million from $15.3 million, or by $18.2 million, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase is primarily due to the increase in products revenue and the decrease in research and development expense for UAS products and services discussed above, which is offset by the increase in cost of products revenue and the increase in selling, general and administrative expenses for incentive compensation expense and new employee-related costs discussed above.
Maritime Systems
Maritime Systems segment revenue increased to $14.4 million from $2.0 million, or by $12.4 million and 623.2%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase is primarily due to the acquisition of BlackSea on September 8, 2026.
Maritime Systems Adjusted EBITDA increased to $2.0 million from $0.3 million, or by $1.7 million, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase is primarily due to the acquisition of BlackSea on September 8, 2026.
Global Solutions
Global Solutions segment revenue decreased to $27.5 million from $30.8 million, or by $3.3 million and 10.7%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease is due to lower revenue primarily from aircraft modifications and testing products.
Global Solutions Adjusted EBITDA decreased to $1.5 million from $4.4 million, or by $2.9 million, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease is primarily due to lower gross profit from aircraft modifications and higher selling, general, and administrative expenses.
Business Outlook for the Full Year 2026
The demand environment for affordable, operationally proven autonomous systems remains compelling. Our customers are seeking platforms that can move quickly from development to scaled production and support increasingly collaborative air, surface and subsea missions. The growth and diversification of our core business, together with our funded backlog, total convertible contract ceiling, and expanding pipeline, reinforce our confidence in continued growth while reducing our reliance on any single program or theater.
For the full fiscal year 2026, the Company expects total revenue of between $820.0 million to $840.0 million, and Adjusted EBITDA* between $128.0 million and $132.0 million.

About AEVEX
AEVEX Corp. (NYSE: AVEX) is a leading U.S. defense technology company delivering autonomous unmanned systems, AI-enabled mission software, and advanced ISR and electronic warfare solutions for national security customers. With vertically integrated engineering, rapid prototyping, and high-volume manufacturing across multiple U.S. locations, AEVEX provides affordable, front-line-ready capabilities designed for contested and GPS-denied environments. AEVEX’s mission is to strengthen deterrence, enhance warfighter effectiveness, and help ensure the United States maintains technological and industrial advantage in the era of autonomy.

Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin
We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest income and expense, income tax expense (benefit), depreciation and amortization expense, other income (expense), changes in the fair value of contingent consideration liabilities and derivative liabilities, noncash stock compensation expense, offering costs related to the IPO or Secondary Offering, asset impairments, business acquisition costs, restructuring costs, and gains or losses on debt extinguishments, as well as certain non-recurring items. We define Adjusted EBITDA Margin as Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue. We believe that Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin are important metrics for management and investors as they remove the impact of items that we do not believe are indicative of our core operating results or the overall health of our company and allow for consistent comparison of our operating results over time and relative to our peers.

Free Cash Flow
We consider free cash flow to be a useful, supplemental measure of our ability to generate cash on a normalized basis. We use free cash flow to supplement GAAP measures in evaluating our flexibility to allocate capital and pursue opportunities that may enhance shareholder value and the effectiveness of our strategies, to make budgeting decisions and to compare our performance against that of our peer companies, many of which present similar non-GAAP financial measures.
While expenditures and dispositions of property and equipment will fluctuate on a period-to-period basis, we seek to ensure that we have adequate capital on hand to maintain ongoing operations and enable growth of the business. Additionally, free cash flow is of limited usefulness in that it does not represent residual cash flows available for discretionary expenditures due to the fact the measures do not deduct the payments required for debt service and other contractual obligations or payments.
We define free cash flow as the sum of our net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less our capital expenditures. Our calculation of free cash flow may not be comparable to the calculation of similarly titled measures reported by other companies.
