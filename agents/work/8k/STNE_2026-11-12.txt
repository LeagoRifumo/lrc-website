StoneCo Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
George Town, Grand Cayman, November 12, 2026 – StoneCo Ltd. (Nasdaq: STNE) (“Stone” or the “Company”) today reports its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

• Total Revenue and Income from continuing operations reached R$3,686.1 million in 3Q26, up 3.3% year over year and 2.8% sequentially. Year-over-year growth was primarily driven by the increasing contribution of our credit product. Quarter over quarter, growth reflects our credit product performance and a gradual improvement in payments volumes.
• Adjusted Gross Profit from continuing operations reached R$1,475.9 million in 3Q26, down 8.0% year over year and 5.6% quarter over quarter. The year-over-year decrease reflects higher provisions for loan losses and higher costs to sustain our operations more than offsetting revenue growth. Quarter over quarter, the decline was driven by higher costs to sustain our business and higher financial expenses, which outweighed the revenue increase. Adjusted Gross Profit margin was 40.0% in the quarter, down from 45.0% in 3Q25 and 43.6% in 2Q26. Year over year, the contraction was mainly driven by higher Cost of Services. Sequentially, the margin was also pressured by higher financial expenses, due to a higher number of business days in the quarter.
• Adjusted EBT from continuing operations totaled R$635.3 million in 3Q26, down 16.1% year over-year, mainly driven by lower Gross Profit, while higher investments in Selling were offset by lower Administrative and Other Operating Expenses. Adjusted EBT margin was 17.2%, down 4.0p.p. from 3Q25 and 2.2p.p. from 2Q26. The year-over-year decline mainly reflects the gross margin dynamics described above, partially offset by lower Other Operating Expenses as a percentage of revenue. Quarter over quarter, Adjusted EBT decreased 8.9%, mainly reflecting the lower Gross Profit and higher SG&A, which were partially compensated by lower Other Operating Expenses.
• Adjusted Net Income from continuing operations was R$542.1 million in 3Q26, down 15.5% from 3Q25, as lower EBT was partly offset by a lower effective tax rate. Adjusted net margin from continuing operations was 14.7%, down from 16.2% in 2Q26 and 18.0% in 3Q25. The sequential contraction reflects a lower EBT margin, which was partially cushioned by a more favorable tax rate.
• Adjusted Basic EPS from continuing operations was R$2.33 per share in 3Q26, a decrease of 2.5% year over year and 2.8% quarter over quarter. The annual decrease is mainly explained by the lower net income, partially offset by a lower weighted average share count of 36.9 million, in part due to the R$2.8 billion in share buybacks in the past twelve months.
• ROE was 23.5% in 3Q26, up 1.8p.p. both year over year and quarter over quarter. The sequential increase reflects a lower average equity base following the dividend paid in May 2026, as a result of the Linx sale.

Net Revenue from Transaction Activities and Other Services
Net Revenue from Transaction Activities and Other Services totaled R$425.3 million in 3Q26, down 32.1% year over year. This decrease was primarily driven by three factors. First, ongoing pricing optimizations between card MDRs and prepayment revenues across our bundled offers which negatively impacts Net Revenue from Transaction Activities and Other Services, but positively contributes to Financial Income. Second, a shift in mix, with PIX growing ahead of card volumes. And third, the simplification of our offers, as part of our initiatives to reduce churn, also contributed to the decrease, as new offers are priced focusing on long-term client value.
Quarter over quarter, Net Revenue from Transaction Activities and Other Services was broadly flat.
As our bundled offerings continue to evolve, pricing optimization should be ongoing, underscoring the importance of focusing on gross profit rather than individual revenue lines. This approach provides a more comprehensive view of our business performance.
Net Revenue from Subscription Services and Equipment Rental
Net Revenue from Subscription Services and Equipment Rental increased 3.1% year over year to R$231.2 million in 3Q26. This can be primarily attributed to higher subscription revenues from our remaining software operation.
Quarter over quarter, this revenue line decreased 5.4%, mainly due to lower (i) rental revenue, as a result of bundled offers pricing and (ii) subscription revenues from our remaining software operation.
Financial Income
Financial Income reached R$2,796.9 million in the quarter, up 9.9% year over year. This growth was driven by higher credit and prepayment revenues and by a reclassification of taxes on intragroup revenue to Other Financial Income. These effects were partially offset by lower float income from deposits, a funding source introduced in early 2025 as an alternative for our operations. Higher prepayment revenues are mostly a result of the following factors: (i) ongoing pricing optimizations between card MDRs and prepayment revenues as anticipated in the “Net Revenue from Transaction Activities and Other Services” section, and (ii) higher prepaid volumes.
Quarter over quarter, Financial Income increased 4.9% as a result of the same factors mentioned above for the year over year variation.
Credit revenues were R$395.7 million in 3Q26 compared with R$348.5 million in 2Q26 and R$184.6 million in 3Q25.
Other Financial Income
Other Financial Income totaled R$232.8 million in 3Q26 compared with R$172.7 million in 3Q25. This increase was primarily due to a higher average cash balance, and partially compensated by a lower CDI rate in the period from 14.90% in 3Q25 to 13.96% in 3Q26.
Compared with the previous quarter, Other Financial Income decreased 6.9%, mainly due to (i) taxes on intragroup revenue that were previously recorded in Financial Income, (ii) a lower average cash balance and (iii) a lower average CDI rate, which fell from 14.44% in 2Q26 to 13.96% in 3Q26. These effects were partially offset by a higher number of working days.

Cost of Services
Cost of Services totaled R$1,052.3 million in 3Q26, increasing 28.7% year-over-year.
Loan loss provisions were R$197.4 million, compared with R$86.4 million in 3Q25, and R$187.6 million in 2Q26. The variation in provision levels is explained in the Credit session in "Main Operating Metrics". Excluding these provisions, Cost of Services was R$854.8 million, up 16.9% year-over-year. This increase was mainly driven by higher (i) investments in software, with AI as a key and fast-growing component, (ii) transaction and operational costs as we grow our business, and (iii) risk management costs.
Quarter over quarter, Cost of Services was 11.3% higher. Excluding loan loss provisions, Cost of Services increased 12.8% sequentially mainly reflecting higher risk management costs, as in 2Q26 we benefited from new initiatives in this area, which had a smaller impact this quarter, along with the drivers (i) and (ii) mentioned above for the year-over-year variation.
As a percentage of revenues, Cost of Services excluding loan loss provisions was 23.2%, compared with 20.5% in 3Q25 and 21.1% in 2Q26.
Adjusted Financial Expenses, Net
Financial Expenses, Net were R$1,157.9 million in 3Q26, a 1.2% increase compared with the prior-year period. This growth can be mainly attributed to (i) increased funding requirements to support the growth of our operations and the restructuring of certain legal entities we executed during this quarter, which impacted our short-term liquidity. These effects were partially mitigated by (ii) a lower average CDI rate, from 14.90% in 3Q25 to 13.96% in 3Q26, (iii) improved funding efficiency, mostly as a result of the deployment of client deposits as a funding source and (iv) a
lower number of working days. As a percentage of Total Revenue and Income, Financial Expenses, Net decreased from 32.1% in 3Q25 to 31.4% in 3Q26.
Compared with 2Q26, Financial Expenses, Net increased 7.4% mostly due to a higher number of working days in the quarter. As a percentage of revenues, Financial Expenses, net increased from 30.0% in 2Q26 to 31.4% in 3Q26.
Adjusted Gross Profit
Adjusted Gross Profit from continuing operations reached R$1,475.9 million, a 8.0% contraction year over year, as revenue growth was compensated by higher provisions for loan losses, increased operating costs, and slightly higher financial expenses. Sequentially, Adjusted Gross Profit reduced by 5.6% as higher operating costs and financial expenses more than offset the increase in revenues.

Adjusted Administrative Expenses
Administrative Expenses were R$206.4 million, down 7.6% year over year, mainly due to lower personnel expenses. This reflects our more disciplined approach towards costs and expenses in recent quarters. As a percentage of revenues, Administrative Expenses decreased from 6.3% in 3Q25 to 5.6% in 3Q26.
Compared with the previous quarter, Administrative Expenses increased 2.4%, mostly as a consequence of higher third party services and software amortization expenses, which were partially compensated by lower expenses with personnel. As a percentage of revenues, Administrative expenses remained stable at5.6% in 3Q26.
Selling Expenses
Selling Expenses were R$570.3 million in 3Q26, up 8.2% year over year, mainly due to (i) higher investments in our distribution channels, (ii) higher marketing expenses, which includes our repositioning campaign as the bank of the Brazilian entrepreneur, and (iii) higher expenses with software licenses. As a percentage of revenues, Selling Expenses increased from 14.8% in 3Q25 to 15.5% in 3Q26.
Compared with 2Q26, Selling Expenses increased 4.8%, explained by the same factors mentioned above for the year over year increase. As a percentage of revenues, Selling Expenses were up from 15.2% in 2Q26 to 15.5% in 3Q26.
Adjusted Other Income (Expenses), Net
Other Expenses, Net were R$65.0 million in the quarter, down 32.1% on a year over year basis, mainly due to lower share-based compensation expenses, as we updated our estimate of awards expected to be forfeited by departing employees. This effect was partially offset by (ii) higher contingencies expenses and (iii) higher net provisions for POS losses.
Compared with the previous quarter, Other Expenses, net, were 46.2% down as a result of the share-based compensation expenses refinement mentioned above.

Glossary of Terms
"Active Client Base": Considers a merchant that has generated at least R$0.01 in revenues in the past 30 days;
“Adjusted Net Cash”: is a non-IFRS financial metric and consists of the following items: (i) Adjusted Cash: Cash and cash equivalents, Short-term investments, Accounts receivable from card issuers, Financial assets from banking solution and Derivative financial instrument; minus (ii) Adjusted Debt: Retail deposits, Accounts payable to clients, Institutional deposits and marketable debt securities, Other debt instruments and Derivative financial instrument.
"Adjusted Basic EPS": calculated as Adjusted Net Income from Continuing Operations attributable to controlling shareholders, divided by the basic weighted average number of shares outstanding in the period.
"ARPAC": Average revenue per active client. Considers total revenue and income divided by the average of the total active clients between the current and previous quarter.
"Average Monthly Credit Yield": defined as quarterly credit revenues including credit card interchange revenues, converted to a monthly figure, divided by the average credit portfolio between the current and the previous quarter.
“Banking”: refers to our digital banking solution.
"Consolidated Credit Metrics”: refer to metrics for credit cards and merchants, the latter including the sum of working capital and revolving credit.
"Continuing Operations”: refer to our financial services solutions to clients and to certain software assets that were not included in the scope of the software divestments announced in the July 22, 2025 6-K filing, titled "StoneCo Announces Divestment of Software Assets".
"Cost of risk”: annualized provision for expected credit losses, divided by the average credit portfolio between the current and the previous quarter.
"Coverage ratio”: consists of the accumulated loan loss provision expenses over NPL over 90 days.
“Credit Portfolio”: is gross of provisions for losses, but net of amortizations. Note: the amount recorded in the balance sheet is already net of provisions.
“CTPV”: means Card Total Payment Volume and refers only to transactions settled through cards. Does not include PIX QR Code volumes.
"Discontinued Operations”: refer to the software assets included in the scope of the software divestments announced in the July 22, 2025 6-K filing, titled "StoneCo Announces Divestment of Software Assets."
"Managerial Capital Ratio": Managerial Capital Ratio mirrors the Brazilian Central Bank methodology used for our prudential conglomerate, applied to all StoneCo's legal entities as defined by the Brazilian Central Bank, and including DTAs from fiscal losses and temporary differences.
“MDR or Merchant Discount Rate”: means the fee or commission paid by merchants for the service of capturing, processing, transmitting and settling transactions. The merchant discount rate is applied to the value of each Cardholder’s transaction, including the interchange fee and assessment fees.
”Merchants solution (credit)”: consists of the sum of (i) working capital and (ii) revolving credit.
“NPL (Non-Performing Loans)”: is the total outstanding of the contract whenever the clients default on an installment. More information on the total overdue by aging considering only the individual installments can be found in Note 5.4.2 of the Financial Statements.
“PIX QR Code”: includes the volume of PIX QR Code transactions from dynamic POS QR Code and static QR Code, unless otherwise noted.
“Revenue”: refers to Total Revenue and Income net of taxes, which is net of interchange fees retained by card issuers and assessment fees paid to payment schemes.
“Retail Deposits”: includes time deposits and deposits from retail customers.
"ROE": calculated as the quarter's annualized Adjusted Net Income from continuing operations divided by the average of equity, both attributable to controlling shareholders, based on the current and the previous quarter.
“TPV”: Total Payment Volume. Reported TPV figures consider all card volumes settled by StoneCo, including PIX QR Code transactions from dynamic POS QR Code and static QR Code, unless otherwise noted.
