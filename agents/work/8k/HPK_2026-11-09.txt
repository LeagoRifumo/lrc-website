HighPeak Energy, Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results and Guidance Update
Fort Worth, Texas, November 9, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) - HighPeak Energy, Inc. (“HighPeak” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: HPK) today announced financial and operating results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and updated guidance for the full year 2026.
A statement from our President and CEO, Michael Hollis:
The third quarter was an important one for HighPeak. Operationally, the team continued to execute at a high level, delivering production above expectations and improving efficiency. Financially, we generated meaningful free cash flow, reduced debt, and took a major step toward strengthening our balance sheet with the comprehensive refinancing transaction we recently announced. As drilling efficiencies continue to improve, rather than releasing a rig, we plan to keep activity steady and reinvest those efficiency gains into additional high-return opportunities, resulting in a modest increase in capital investment while maintaining financial discipline.
Over the past two years, we have remained focused on a clear set of priorities: practicing capital discipline, driving corporate efficiencies, strengthening our capital structure, and ensuring every decision we make is guided by the goal of creating long-term shareholder value. With the announcement of our pending refinance, which dramatically improves our capital structure, we can confidently say that we have made meaningful progress on all these objectives. While we are proud of what we have accomplished, we view these achievements not as a finish line, but as a foundation for continued success. We remain committed to these principles and to delivering sustainable value for all our stakeholders, including our employees, royalty owners, vendors, community partners, and shareholders. We believe this new and improved HighPeak enhances our ability to execute our strategy, capitalize on opportunities and build a stronger company for the future.
Recent Events
On October 6, 2026, HighPeak announced a transformational comprehensive refinancing through a strategic $450 million preferred equity investment and a new $800 million credit facility. The Company will pay off its existing $1.2 billion term loan upon closing the refinancing transaction which is expected to close later this month.
The Company provided the following updates to its 2026 Outlook:

Third Quarter 2026 Operational Update
HighPeak’s sales volumes averaged 44.2 MBoe/d during the third quarter of 2026 consisting of approximately 67% crude oil and 84% liquids.
The Company drilled 9 gross (8.6 net) company operated horizontal wells, completed 5 gross (4.8 net) company operated horizontal wells and turned-in-line 13 gross (12.8 net) company operated producing wells. On September 30, 2026, the Company had 18 gross (13.3 net) horizontal wells in progress, including 16 gross (11.4 net) horizontal wells in various stages of completion.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
HighPeak reported a net loss of $55.7 million for the third quarter 2026, or ($0.44) per diluted share and adjusted net income1 of $38.5 million, or $0.32 per diluted share. We also reported EBITDAX1 of $163.8 million, or $1.23 per diluted share. Finally, free cash flow1 was $22.4 million and free cash flow before changes in working capital1 was $58.4 million for the quarter. The Company also reduced debt by $30 million.
Third quarter 2026 average realized prices were $86.86 per Bbl of crude oil, $22.20 per Bbl of NGL and $1.42 per Mcf of natural gas, resulting in an overall realized price of $63.18 per Boe, or 73% of the weighted average of NYMEX crude oil prices, excluding the effects of derivatives. Including the effects of derivatives, third quarter 2026 average realized prices were $77.64 per Bbl of crude oil, $22.20 per Bbl of NGL and $2.37 per Mcf of natural gas, resulting in an overall realized price of $57.94 per Boe. HighPeak’s cash costs for the third quarter 2026 were $17.64 per Boe, including lease operating costs of $6.70 per Boe, expense workovers of $1.91 per Boe, gathering, processing and transportation expenses of $4.30 per Boe, production and ad valorem taxes of $3.24 per Boe and G&A expenses of $1.49 per Boe. As a result, the Company’s unhedged EBITDAX per Boe was $45.54 per Boe.
HighPeak’s total capital expenditures, excluding acquisitions, for the third quarter were $71.1 million.
