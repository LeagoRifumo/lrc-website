Concentra Announces Results For Its Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
and Raises FY 2026 Guidance
DALLAS — November 5, 2026 — Concentra Group Holdings Parent, Inc. (“Concentra”, the “Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) (NYSE: CON), the nation’s largest provider of occupational health services by number of locations, today announced results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, declaration of a cash dividend, and raised its full-year 2026 guidance. The quarter included revenue growth of 8.6% year over year, net income attributable to the Company growth of 36.5% year over year, Adjusted EBITDA growth of 17.9% year over year, and a net leverage ratio of 2.90x.
As previously announced, effective November 1, 2026, (i) Matt DiCanio succeeded Keith Newton as president and chief executive officer, (ii) Tanner Newton became executive vice president and chief financial officer, and (iii) Keith Newton transitioned to executive chairman of the Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”).
“Concentra delivered another strong quarter, reflecting the consistency of our operating model and the strength of our team,” said Keith Newton, executive chairman of the Board. “Over the past 50 years, we have built a market-leading occupational health company with a clear strategy for growth. With Matt assuming the CEO role, Concentra enters its next chapter with strong momentum, an experienced leadership team and significant opportunities to expand our reach and serve more employers and patients.”
“Our third-quarter results demonstrate our ability to translate strong operating performance into profitable growth,” said Matt DiCanio, president and chief executive officer. “We expanded our footprint, strengthened the balance sheet and returned capital to shareholders. The increase in full-year guidance reflects confidence in Concentra’s performance and continued momentum. Our focus remains on disciplined growth, operational excellence and delivering high-quality care while creating long-term value for customers, patients and shareholders.”
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Revenue of $622.1 million, an increase of 8.6% from $572.8 million in Q3 2025
•Net income of $67.8 million, an increase of 36.0% from $49.8 million in Q3 2025
•Net income attributable to the Company of $65.9 million, and Adjusted Net Income Attributable to the Company of $66.1 million, an increase of 36.5% and 32.5% over prior year, respectively
•Earnings per share of $0.52 and Adjusted Earnings per Share of $0.52, an increase of 36.8% and 33.3% over prior year, respectively
•Adjusted EBITDA of $140.2 million an increase of 17.9% from $118.9 million in Q3 2025
•Net income margin increased to 10.9%, an expansion of 220 basis points year over year
•Adjusted EBITDA Margin increased to 22.5%, an expansion of 178 basis points year over year
•Patient visits of 3,731,956, or 58,312 visits per day, an increase of 4.9% from 55,589 visits per day in Q3 2025
•Revenue per visit of $152.14, an increase of 3.3% from $147.31 in Q3 2025
•Net cash provided by operating activities of $51.9 million and Free Cash Flow of $32.2 million
•Repurchased 1.0 million shares of common stock totaling $34.65 million
•Cash balance of $85.8 million and a net leverage ratio of 2.90x
•Repaid $52.4 million on our term loan due 2031
•Received a corporate credit rating upgrade from S&P Global, from BB- to BB, and a revised outlook from Moody’s, from stable to positive
•Total occupational health centers of 639, compared to 628 at the end of Q3 2025
•Opened three de novo occupational health centers in Kansas City, Boise, and Daytona Beach
•Completed the acquisition of a four-center occupational health practice in Minnesota
•Total onsite health clinics of 423, compared to 413 at the end of Q3 2025

Balance Sheet
As of September 30, 2026, our balance sheet reflected cash of $85.8 million, total debt of $1,521.3 million and total assets of $3,011.6 million. Concentra’s net leverage ratio as of September 30, 2026 was 2.90x, which was in compliance with the financial covenant under our credit agreement.
Cash Flow
Cash flows provided by operating activities in the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 totaled $51.9 million compared to $60.6 million for the same quarter of the prior year. During the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, cash flow from investing activities resulted in cash used of $25.9 million, including capital expenditures of $22.5 million and business acquisitions of $6.2 million, partially offset by proceeds from sale of assets of $2.8 million. Concentra had Free Cash Flow of $32.2 million in the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $40.2 million for the same quarter of the prior year. Cash flow from financing activities used $98.2 million for the quarter, driven primarily by $52.4 million in repayments on the term loan, $34.7 million in repurchases of shares of common stock and $8.0 million in dividend payments. This resulted in a net decrease in cash of $72.2 million for the quarter.
Dividend
On November 4, 2026, the Board declared a cash dividend of $0.0625 per share. The dividend will be payable on or about December 4, 2026, to stockholders of record as of the close of business on November 19, 2026.
There is no assurance that future dividends will be declared. The declaration and payment of dividends in the future are at the discretion of the Board after taking various factors into account, including, but not limited to, the Company’s financial condition, operating results, available cash and current and anticipated cash needs, the terms of indebtedness, and other factors the Board may deem to be relevant.
2026 Share Repurchase Program
On November 4, 2026, the Board authorized a new share repurchase program to repurchase up to $250 million of the Company’s outstanding common stock with no expiration date (the “2026 Share Repurchase Program”). The 2026 Share Repurchase Program is in addition to the Company’s existing share repurchase program authorized in November 2025. Stock repurchases under the 2026 Share Repurchase Program may be made in the open market or through privately negotiated transactions, and at times and in such amounts as the Board deems appropriate. The authorization of the 2026 Share Repurchase Program does not obligate the Company to repurchase any shares.

Company Overview
Concentra is the largest provider of occupational health services in the United States by number of locations, with the mission of improving the health of America’s workforce, one patient at a time. Our approximately 13,000 colleagues and affiliated physicians and clinicians support the delivery of an extensive suite of services, including occupational and consumer health services and other direct-to-employer care. We support the care of approximately 55,000(1) patients each business day on average across 46 states and the District of Columbia at our 639 occupational health centers, 423 onsite health clinics at employer worksites, and Concentra Telemed as of September 30, 2026.
(1) As of TTM September 30, 2026.

X. Net Income to Adjusted EBITDA Reconciliation
For the Three and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 and 2025
(In thousands, unaudited)
Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin are non-GAAP measures that we believe provide useful insight into the underlying performance of our business by excluding items that may obscure trends in our core operating results. These metrics are not intended to be substitutes for U.S. GAAP measures such as net income and net income margin, and may differ from similarly titled metrics supported by other companies.
