Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results - Continuing Operations
•Revenue was $167 million, up 5% year over year. Organic revenue, which excludes a nominal impact from foreign exchange and a 3-percentage point impact from the acquisition of UK Biocentre Limited, was up 2% year over year, reflecting organic growth in Sample Management Solutions, partially offset by an organic revenue decline in Multiomics.
•Sample Management Solutions revenue was $94 million, up 9% year over year.
◦Organic revenue, which excludes the impact from foreign exchange and the contribution from the acquisition of UK Biocentre Limited, was up 4%, mainly driven by higher revenue in Sample Repository Services and Consumables and Instruments, partially offset by lower revenue in Automated Stores.
•Multiomics revenue was $73 million, flat year over year.
◦Organic revenue, which excludes the impact from foreign exchange, was down 1% year over year, primarily driven by lower Sanger Sequencing and Next Generation Sequencing revenue, partially offset by growth in Gene Synthesis.
Summary of GAAP Earnings Results - Continuing Operations
•Operating income was $2.5 million. Operating margin was 1.5%, an increase of 30 basis points year over year.
◦Gross margin was 46.0%, an increase of 60 basis points year over year, primarily driven by improved operating leverage, favorable mix and the benefits of ongoing cost initiatives. This improvement was partially offset by unfavorable fixed-cost absorption associated with lower sales volumes in certain areas of the portfolio, as well as quality remediation and rework activities in Automated Stores.
◦Operating expenses in the quarter were $74 million, up 6% year over year, primarily driven by higher research and development expenses, higher selling, general and administrative expenses, as well as higher restructuring charges, partially offset by a reversal of stock-based compensation expense related to our former Chief Executive Officer’s unvested awards.
•Total other income included $5 million of net interest income, consistent with the prior year period.
•Diluted EPS from continuing operations was $0.14 compared to $1.12 in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025. Diluted EPS from discontinued operations was $0.02, compared to ($0.02) a year ago. Total diluted EPS was $0.16, compared to $1.11 a year ago.
Summary of Non-GAAP Earnings Results - Continuing Operations
•Adjusted operating income was $11.5 million. Adjusted operating margin was 6.9%, an increase of 120 basis points year over year.
◦Adjusted gross margin was 47.2%, an increase of 50 basis points year over year, primarily driven by improved operating leverage, favorable mix and the benefits of ongoing cost initiatives. This improvement was partially offset by unfavorable fixed-cost absorption associated with lower sales volumes in certain areas of the portfolio, as well as quality remediation and rework activities in Automated Stores.
◦Adjusted operating expenses in the quarter were $67 million, up 3% year over year, mainly driven by higher research and development expenses, partially offset by a reversal of stock-based compensation expense related to our former Chief Executive Officer’s unvested awards.
•Adjusted EBITDA was $19.4 million, and Adjusted EBITDA margin was 11.6%, a decrease of 140 basis points year over year.
•Adjusted diluted EPS was $0.28, compared to $0.21 one year ago.

Recent Strategic Investments
Elegen Agreements
•On October 28, 2026, the Company entered into a Technology, Equipment and License Agreement and a Master Services and Supply Agreement with Elegen Corp. (“Elegen”) to expand the Company’s gene synthesis capabilities. Under the agreements, the Company obtained an exclusive, royalty-free license to certain Elegen gene synthesis and cell-free cloning technologies, subject to certain restrictions specified in the agreements, and entered into related equipment, supply and service arrangements. The license has an initial five-year term and requires fixed license payments totaling $23.0 million. The Company has an option, exercisable between 12 and 18 months after execution, to convert the license to an irrevocable, perpetual, exclusive, royalty-free license for $25.0 million, plus any unpaid license fees, subject to the license’s terms. Aggregate capital deployment under the agreements is expected to range from approximately $32.0 million to $71.0 million, depending on future equipment purchases and whether the Company exercises the conversion option.
SynPlexity Acquisition
•On October 28, 2026, the Company acquired SynPlexity Inc. (“SynPlexity”), a synthetic biology company, for a base purchase price of $15.0 million in cash, of which approximately $2.5 million was held back, subject to customary closing and post-closing adjustments. The purchase agreement also provides for contingent consideration of up to $10.0 million based on the achievement of a specified revenue milestone through September 30, 2029. SynPlexity is a DNA assembly technology company providing products and services centered on large, multiplexed gene libraries for functional screening, drug discovery, therapeutics, and other applications. The acquisition is intended to expand the Company’s gene synthesis capabilities within its Multiomics business. Due to the timing of the acquisition, the initial accounting for the business combination is incomplete and certain acquisition-related disclosures are not yet available.
Share Repurchase Program Update
•On December 8, 2025, our Board of Directors approved a share repurchase program authorizing the repurchase of up to $250 million of our common stock through December 31, 2028, or the 2025 Repurchase Program. Repurchases under the 2025 Repurchase Program may be made in the open market or through privately negotiated transactions (including under an accelerated share repurchase agreement), or by other means, including through the use of trading plans intended to qualify under Rule 10b5-1 under the Exchange Act, subject to market and business conditions, legal requirements, and other factors. During the fiscal year ended September 30, 2026, the Company repurchased 4.4 million shares of common stock for $118.2 million (excluding fees, commissions, and excise tax) in open market repurchases pursuant to the 2025 Repurchase Program. As of September 30, 2026, $131.8 million remained available for future repurchases under the 2025 Repurchase Program.

Full Year Fiscal 2027 Guidance
The Company expects total reported revenue from continuing operations to range approximately between $648 to $660 million for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2027.
•Total organic revenue growth is expected to range approximately between up 3% to 5% relative to fiscal 2026.

•Organic revenue for Multiomics is expected to grow low-single-digits.
•The high end of the fiscal 2027 revenue outlook includes the potential contributions from Elegen and SynPlexity. Fiscal 2027 is expected to be primarily a year of integration, scale-up, and commercialization of these capabilities.
•Adjusted EBITDA margin expansion of 200 to 250 basis points year-over-year, inclusive of the expected dilution of 100 basis points associated with the Elegen agreements and SynPlexity acquisition.
•Free cash flow (cash flow from operations less capital expenditures) is expected to improve approximately 25%, relative to fiscal 2026.

About Azenta Life Sciences
Azenta, Inc. (Nasdaq: AZTA) is a leading provider of life sciences solutions worldwide, enabling life science organizations around the world to bring impactful breakthroughs and therapies to market faster. Azenta provides a full suite of reliable cold-chain sample management solutions and multiomics services across areas such as drug development, clinical research and advanced cell therapies for the industry's top pharmaceutical, biotech, academic and healthcare institutions globally. Our global team delivers and supports these products and services through our industry-leading brands, including GENEWIZ, FluidX, Ziath, 4titude, Limfinity, Freezer Pro, and Barkey.
