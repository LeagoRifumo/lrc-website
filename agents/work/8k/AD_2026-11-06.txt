Array reports third quarter 2026 results
Affirms guidance for 2026
CHICAGO (November 6, 2026) — Array Digital Infrastructure, Inc. (NYSE:AD) reported third quarter operating results.
“As Array marks its first year as a tower company, we continue to make meaningful progress against our 2026 strategic priorities,” said Anthony Carlson, President and CEO. “Our team remains intensely focused on maximizing the value of our tower portfolio by driving tenant growth and enhancing operational efficiency. These efforts are strengthening the earnings power of our assets and positioning the company for long-term value creation."
Highlights*
•Optimizing tower operations
◦Site rental revenues grew 14% year over year
◦Delivered consecutive quarter-over-quarter colocation growth
•Continuing to close pending sales of wireless spectrum
◦Received regulatory approval for remaining pending transactions in October, closings remain subject to FCC finality and other customary closing conditions
•Reaffirming 2026 financial guidance
* Comparisons are 3Q’25 to 3Q’26 unless otherwise noted.
Array reported total operating revenues from continuing operations of $53.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, versus $47.1 million for the same period one year ago. Net income attributable to Array shareholders and diluted earnings per share from continuing operations were $69.6 million and $0.81, respectively, for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $108.8 million and $1.25, respectively, in the same period one year ago.
Pending transactions
Subsequent to the August 1, 2025 close of the sale of its wireless operations, Array reached additional agreements with T-Mobile for the sale of spectrum. A significant portion of these closed in May 2026 with approximately $30 million related to 600 MHz and 700 MHz licenses remaining. These remaining transactions received regulatory approval on October 7, 2026 and are expected to close in 2026, subject to FCC finality and other customary closing conditions.
Recent Development
On May 7, 2026, TDS delivered to the Array Board of Directors a letter setting forth a non-binding proposal to acquire all of the outstanding Array Common Shares that are not owned by TDS (the “Array Proposal”). On September 1, 2026, TDS announced that it is no longer pursuing the Array Proposal. For additional information, see TDS' Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on September 2, 2026.

About Array
Array leases tower space to tenants and provides ancillary services, holds noncontrolling interests in primarily wireless operating companies and holds certain wireless spectrum licenses. Array owns 4,455 towers in 19 states and enables the deployment of 5G and other wireless technologies throughout the country. As of September 30, 2026, Telephone and Data Systems, Inc. owned approximately 81.9% of Array.
