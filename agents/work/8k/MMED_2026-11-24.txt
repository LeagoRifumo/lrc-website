MiniMed Delivers Strong Fiscal Q2 with Accelerating Pump Demand, Expanding Profitability, and Faster Pipeline Commercialization
NORTHRIDGE, Calif., November 24, 2026 – MiniMed (Nasdaq: MMED) today reported financial results for its second quarter (Q2) of fiscal year 2027 (FY27), ended October 30, 2026.
•Accelerating pump demand supports durable growth: New Pumps Sold (NPS) increased 18% year-over-year, accelerating from 8% in Q1
•Broad-based growth across geographies: U.S. net sales grew 8%, with NPS >30%; International net sales grew 10% as reported and 10% organic, with Western Europe NPS increasing >20%
•Operating rigor driving profitability and cash generation: Net Income at 3% of sales, Adjusted EBITDA margin of 17%; operating cash flow of $116 million; free cash flow of $55 million, positive for the first time as an independent company
•Post-quarter pipeline commercialization advances rapidly: MiniMed Fit™ FDA cleared in under three months, several months early, accelerating U.S. launch from Summer to Spring 2027; MiniMed Flex™ with Simplera™ launched last week in Western Europe; MiniMed Flex™ with Instinct, made by Abbott, received CE Mark with launch planned in Q4 FY27
“MiniMed delivered another strong quarter, with consistent execution across our business, accelerating pump demand, improving profits and cash generation, and continued momentum in our innovation pipeline,” said MiniMed Chief Executive Officer Que Dallara. “New Pumps Sold growth accelerated to 18% worldwide, reinforcing the strength of demand for our products, and our confidence in durable revenue growth. With MiniMed Flex™ expanding internationally and MiniMed Fit™ receiving FDA clearance ahead of expectations, we are adding important new drivers across our portfolio. We believe this progress positions us well for the next phase of MiniMed’s growth.”
Q2 Results
•Worldwide net sales of $822 million grew 9.4% as reported and 9.7% organic, including an approximately $(3) million impact from foreign currency translation
◦International net sales of $579 million grew 9.8% as reported and 10.4% organic, supported by new product introductions, increased sensor availability, and continued AID adoption; Western Europe NPS increased >20%
◦U.S. net sales of $243 million grew 8.3% as reported and organic. NPS increased >30%, driven by the continued expansion of the MiniMed Flex™ launch
◦Worldwide pump net sales of $151 million grew 14.7% as reported and 15.0% organic
◦Worldwide CGM net sales of $423 million grew 10.4% as reported and 10.8% organic
◦Worldwide consumables net sales of $243 million grew 4.0% as reported and 4.6% organic
•GAAP net income was $22 million or 2.6% of sales; Adjusted EBITDA was $140 million or 17.0% margin
•Worldwide NPS of ~38,000, an increase of 18.3% year over year
•Worldwide CGM Attachment Rate of 70%, an increase of ~100 bps quarter over quarter

Recent Highlights
•MiniMed Fit™ FDA clearance creates an important new growth opportunity with a first-of-its-kind tubeless experience. Combines up to seven days of uninterrupted wear without charging or cartridge changes, 300-unit insulin capacity, SmartGuard™ automation, and compatibility with Simplera™ and Instinct
•MiniMed Flex™ international expansion provides another near-term growth catalyst. MiniMed Flex™ with Simplera™ launched in Europe last week; MiniMed Flex™ with Instinct, made by Abbott, received CE Mark and expected to launch in Q4 FY27
•DGK (dual glucose-ketone) sensor, made by Abbott, expected to launch with MiniMed™ Flex and MiniMed™ Fit in Summer 2027
FY27 Outlook
For the fiscal year 2027 ending April 30, 2027, MiniMed expects:
•Organic revenue growth of approximately 10.5%, reaffirming prior guidance. The outlook includes the approximate 1.0 to 1.5% benefit from the extra week in Q1 FY27; and
•Adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 11.3%, reaffirming the guidance updated following FDA clearance of MiniMed Fit™ solely to reflect the accelerated $162 million Blackstone charge. Excluding this expected charge, MiniMed’s underlying adjusted EBITDA margin outlook remains unchanged at approximately 16%, consistent with the outlook in effect immediately before Fit clearance.
MiniMed’s strong Q2 performance provides capacity to fund the manufacturing, sales, and marketing investments being pulled forward to support the accelerated MiniMed Fit™ launch while maintaining the company’s fiscal 2027 outlook.

About MiniMed
MiniMed is a global leader in insulin delivery, constantly advancing therapies that support people with diabetes in more than 80 countries. Our full-stack, integrated ecosystem, including our insulin delivery systems, CGMs, algorithms, and easy-to-use app experience, is designed to work seamlessly together, supported by white-glove, wrap-around service. For over 40 years, we’ve pioneered therapies people can rely on by anticipating needs, reducing burden, and helping make life with diabetes easier. Our mission is to make every day a better day for people with diabetes.
