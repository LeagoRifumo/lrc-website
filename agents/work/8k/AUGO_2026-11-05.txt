Earnings Release Q3 2026 | 9M 2026
Miami, November 5, 2026 – “Production was the highlight of the quarter: a new record of 96k GEO, taking us to 253k GEO year-to-date, the highest nine-month result in Aura’s history and keeping us firmly on track for our 340k–390k full-year guidance. Led by a record quarter at Borborema and the continued turnaround at MSG, this operational strength translated into solid financials, with nine-month Adjusted EBITDA of $677 million — nearly double last year and already ahead of all of 2025 — and Recurring Free Cash Flow of $124 million in the quarter, funding dividends, buybacks and expansion capex with leverage stable at 0.20x. We are also moving ahead on the projects that will drive the next stage of growth: Era Dorada remains on time and on budget, the Almas and Borborema expansions are progressing, and the recent fleet insourcing is set to provide cost savings starting next year. We are delivering strong results today while building a clear path toward more than 600k GEO annually.” Commented Rodrigo Barbosa President and CEO of Aura.

Headlines
· | Record High Production: Q3 2026 total production reached 95,557 GEO, a 27% increase compared to Q2 2026 and 29% higher than Q3 2025 at current metal prices (at constant prices, +26% QoQ and +25% YoY). In 9M 2026, Aura produced 253,131 GEO (255,268 GEO at constant prices), a 28% increase over 9M 2025 at current prices (+26% at constant prices) and the highest nine-month production in the Company’s history and on track to deliver its consolidated guidance of 340k – 390k GEO in 2026. Over the last twelve months, Aura produced 335,198 GEO at current prices, another record high and a 27% increase compared to the twelve-month period ended Q3 2025. Q3 2026 and 9M 2026 highlights:

o | Aranzazu: 24,920 GEO (+39% QoQ; +16% YoY at current prices), mainly reflecting higher grades from mine sequencing, as planned, supported by favorable metal price effects on GEO conversion. The average realized copper price increased 6% QoQ to $6.46/lb, while the average realized gold price decreased 3% to $4,285/oz. At constant prices, production was +35% QoQ and +6% YoY, by higher grades and more copper contained in concentrate. In 9M 2026, total production reached 58,497 GEO (-9% YoY) at current prices. At constant prices, Aranzazu produced 60,634 GEO (-12% YoY), primarily due to lower grades anticipated for the first half of the year.

o | Almas: 14,264 GEO (-12% QoQ; -5% YoY), mainly due to lower grades, reflecting the processing of additional ore following the plant expansion and in line with the mine sequencing plan, which moves into higher-grade areas in Q4. As result of the expansion plan, plant feed increased 31% YoY and 3% QoQ, with recovery stable at approximately 88%. In 9M 2026, production totaled 46,232 GEO (+12% YoY), as the higher plant feed enabled by the expansion more than offset the impact of lower grades. Aura expects higher grades in Q4 2026, keeping Apoena on track to meet its 2026 guidance.

o | Borborema: 22,842 GEO (+60% QoQ; +124% YoY), a quarterly record for the operation, representing approximately 24% of Aura’s total production in Q3 2026. This result already represents 70% of the guidance high range.

o | Minosa: 13,344 GEO (-7% QoQ; -26% YoY), mainly due to lower ore plant feed, still an effect of the increase in stacking level within the leach pad, as seen in the previous quarter. Even with the effects of the higher stacking level, Minosa has reached 71% of the low end of its 2026 guidance range.

o | MSG: 13,321 GEO (+85% QoQ), driven by higher grades (from 0.90 g/t in Q2 2026 to 1.19 g/t in Q3 2026), an increase in ore plant feed (+28% QoQ) and a 1.9 p.p. improvement in recovery rate (89.6%), as the mine turnaround advances, with underground infrastructure and the transition to the bottom-up method progressing according to plan. OTHER UPDATES:
Syndicated Loan Facility: On September 18, 2026, Aura contracted a US$200 million syndicated loan facility. The facility bears interest at SOFR + 2.70% per year, with a two-year grace period and a five-year maturity. The transaction further diversifies Aura’s funding sources and supports its growth strategy, including the construction of Era Dorada, the expansion of production capacity at Almas, the expected expansion of Borborema, and its medium-term target of producing over 600k GEO per year.
Mining Fleet Insourcing: On October 2, 2026, Aura announced that it had signed a definitive agreement to acquire a company (“Newco”) that holds the mining fleet, related equipment leases and dedicated workforce currently operated by a third-party contractor at the Apoena, Almas and Borborema gold mines, bringing fleet operation and maintenance in-house (the “Insourcing”), with the transition targeted for January 2027. The agreed Enterprise Value is R$612 million (approximately US$118 million, based on an exchange rate of R$5.18/US$1.00), of which up to R$250 million will be paid in cash, with the remainder consisting of the assumption of existing equipment lease financing and other contingent payments, a portion of which is subject to performance during the transition period. Based on the Company’s internal estimates, the Insourcing is expected to reduce all-in sustaining costs (AISC) by US$150 to US$200 per ounce of gold sold, on a weighted-average basis across the three operations, beginning in 2027. Closing is subject to customary conditions precedent, including clearance from Brazil’s Administrative Council for Economic Defense (CADE). MSG already operates its fleet mine directly.

Production
In Q3 2026, total production reached a record 95,557 gold equivalent ounces (“GEO”), up 27% from Q2 2026 and 29% from Q3 2025 at current metal prices. At constant metal prices, production grew 26% QoQ and 25% YoY, confirming that the improvement was driven mainly by operational performance rather than by price effects.
QoQ drivers:
· | Aranzazu: mine sequencing reached higher grades, with copper at 1.51% vs. 1.27% in Q2. Favorable metal prices in the copper-to-GEO conversion added to the gain.

· | Borborema: record quarter. Grade rose 42% to 1.65 g/t (vs. 1.16 g/t) and plant feed rose 14% on better filtering.

· | MSG: production up 85% as the turnaround advanced. Plant feed rose 28% to 381,060 t, grade improved to 1.19 g/t (vs. 0.90 g/t) and recovery gained 1.9 p.p. to 89.6%.

Lower grades at Almas and lower production at Minosa partially offset these gains. Both should recover in Q4, while Apoena is also expected to improve: Almas moves into higher-grade zones, and at Apoena, access to the main higher-grade areas of Phase 3 of the Nosde Pit was completed in Q3.
YoY drivers:
· | Addition of MSG and better performance at Borborema, on higher grades and higher ore plant feed.

· | Aranzazu, on favorable metal prices and higher copper content in concentrate.

· | Lower output at Apoena, Almas and Minosa trimmed the gain, with Minosa still affected by the higher stacking level within the leach pad, as seen in the previous quarter and Apoena and Almas impacted mainly by mine sequencing.
