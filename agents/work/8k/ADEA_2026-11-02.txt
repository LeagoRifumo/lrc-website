ADEIA ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
Dipti Vachani appointed as Chief Executive Officer
Signed multi-year license renewals with Charter and TCL
Reiterate confidence in long-term $600 million annual revenue outlook
SAN JOSE, Calif. – November 2, 2026 – Adeia Inc. (Nasdaq: ADEA) (the “Company” or “Adeia”), the technology company pioneering foundational innovations that shape how the world computes, connects and experiences entertainment, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“I am honored to join Adeia at such an exciting time as AI is driving innovation in both media and semiconductors, and Adeia’s foundational technologies are at the heart of these trends,” said Dipti Vachani, chief executive officer of Adeia. “Our third quarter results reflected strong deal activity with eight license agreements signed across Pay-TV, consumer electronics, e-commerce and social media. Those deals included significant renewals with long-time customers Charter and TCL and four new customers during the quarter. We are on target to achieve our long-term annual revenue outlook of $600 million and we remain excited about our future.”
Vachani continued, “I want to thank the Board for their confidence in me and Paul Davis for his support during this transition. Through my nearly 30 years of experience in the semiconductor space, I have a deep understanding of the technology, the ecosystem and the barriers to scale that Adeia customers are experiencing. With my proven track record, coupled with our talented team of innovators and extensive patent portfolios, I am confident that we have all the ingredients to grow and expand our business.”
“We are pleased with the progress that we have made to date as our pipeline remains strong and is growing,” said Keith Jones, chief financial officer. “As we approach the end of the year, our revenue could be impacted by the ultimate timing of executing a select few deals. We remain confident in our ability to achieve the high-end of our revenue guidance range for 2026. However, we felt it prudent to adjust the range to reflect the potential impact to the extent certain of these deals are executed beyond the end of the year.”
Third Quarter Financial Highlights
•
Revenue was $80.2 million as compared to $96.1 million in the second quarter of 2026
•
GAAP diluted earnings per share (EPS) was $0.02 and non-GAAP diluted EPS was $0.21
•
GAAP net income was $2.3 million and adjusted EBITDA was $36.9 million
•
Cash flow from operations was $5.1 million
•
Paid down $6.1 million on our term loan
•
Ended the quarter with $127.7 million in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities
Business Highlights
•
Signed eight license agreements, including with four new customers in the quarter, for access to our media portfolio:
o
A multi-year renewal with Charter, the largest Pay-TV and broadband provider in the U.S.
o
A multi-year renewal with TCL, one of the world’s largest manufacturers of connected TVs
o
A new long-term license agreement with Fanatics, a leading sports apparel e-commerce company
o
New license agreements with a social media company and a consumer electronics company, as well as three other license agreements with Pay-TV providers
Capital Allocation
During the quarter, the Company made $6.1 million in principal payments towards its term loan, bringing the outstanding balance to $386.5 million as of September 30, 2026.
On September 14, 2026, the Company distributed $5.5 million to stockholders of record on August 24, 2026, for a quarterly cash dividend of $0.05 per share of common stock.
The Board of Directors declared a dividend of $0.05 per share, payable on December 14, 2026, to stockholders of record on November 23, 2026.

About Adeia Inc.
Adeia Inc. (Nasdaq: ADEA) develops foundational innovations that shape how the world computes, connects and experiences entertainment. Its inventions span a broad range of semiconductor and media technologies, including 3D interconnect (hybrid bonding) and advanced thermal management for AI datacenters, as well as content discovery, personalization, and streaming. These inventions are licensed to leaders across the chip design and manufacturing, consumer electronics, automotive, digital entertainment, and e-commerce industries. Adeia’s innovations enable more powerful and efficient products, and more immersive, personalized experiences across billions of devices worldwide. Learn more at www.adeia.com.
