Santiago, Chile - November 17, 2026 - Sociedad Química y Minera de Chile S.A. (SQM) (NYSE: SQM; Santiago Stock Exchange: SQM-B, SQM-A) reported today net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, of US$1,569.0 million or US$5.49 per share, an increase of 288.0% compared to US$404.4 million or US$1.42 per share reported for the same period last year.
Gross profit(1) reached US$3,002.3 million (47.4% of revenues) for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, 233.6% higher than US$900.0 million (27.7% of revenues) recorded for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Revenues totaled US$6,340.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, representing an increase of 95.0% compared to US$3,252.4 million reported for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
The Company also announced net income for the third quarter of 2026 of US$544.2 million or US$1.91 per share, an increase of 205.0% compared to US$178.4 million or US$0.62 per share for the third quarter of 2025. Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 reached US$963.8 million, 179.6% higher than the US$344.7 million reported for the third quarter of 2025. Revenues totaled US$2,112.0 million for the second quarter of 2025, an increase of 80.0% compared to US$1,173.0 million for the third quarter of 2025.
SQM’s Chief Executive Officer, Ricardo Ramos, stated, “I am pleased to announce SQM Group’s third-quarter results. We continued to deliver solid performance across our main business lines, with positive year-over-year results. Despite evolving market conditions, we remain confident in the fundamentals of our businesses and in the opportunities we see across our key markets.”
He added, “In lithium, we continue to see encouraging demand trends and remain confident in the long-term growth prospects of the market. Although lithium market prices have significantly declined since the peak observed during the second quarter, we continue to see strong appetite for lithium, supported not only by electric vehicle adoption but also by increasing lithium content in vehicles, the expansion of markets beyond China and the growing contribution of battery energy storage systems. We estimate that global lithium demand could grow by approximately 30% in 2026, and expect the market could surpass 3 million metric tons by 2030. These developments reinforce our confidence in the long-term fundamentals of the lithium market and our ability to participate in its continued growth.”
Mr. Ramos continued, “Moving to iodine, we once again delivered solid commercial results, supported by healthy market demand and favorable pricing conditions. Demand for iodine and its derivatives continues to be driven primarily by X-ray contrast media, and we believe the market could continue growing at an attractive pace in 2027. We remain focused on strengthening our position as a reliable supplier while advancing our operational efficiency and production capabilities.”
The CEO added, “The Specialty Plant Nutrition business also continued to demonstrate good commercial activity during the third quarter. We saw strong sales across our traditional markets, including the United States, Mexico and Spain. However, higher fertilizer prices have led some farmers to moderate their purchasing activity, prompting us to adjust our full-year sales volume growth outlook to approximately 5%. Meanwhile, prices have continued to show a positive trend.

SQM Group
In October, we launched SQM Group, the same company our investors know as SQM, now with a renewed identity and a clear brand architecture for all of our businesses.
As part of SQM Group, we renamed our divisions to better reflect our businesses, organizing our five business lines into three divisions:
•Novandino Division: our lithium operations from the Salar de Atacama, Chile, to the world, carried out through our partnership with Codelco.
•SQM International Lithium Division: our lithium operations and project development in Australia and other countries.
•SQM Division: our iodine, plant nutrition, industrial chemicals businesses and metallic exploration.
SQM Group serves as the corporate umbrella for our divisions and brings them under a single set of corporate values. Excellence, safety, integrity and sustainability are at the core of everything we do, with a long-term view and innovation built into all of our solutions. This new chapter reflects what has always defined us: transforming natural resources into solutions for the human progress.

During the third quarter, we observed a shift in lithium price trends, with our average realized lithium sales price decreasing by 10% quarter-over-quarter, while spodumene prices remained relatively flat. However, in recent weeks, we have observed a significant decline in market prices, which suggests that our average realized lithium sales price in the fourth quarter could be substantially lower than in the third quarter.
We continue to see encouraging underlying demand trends. Although electric vehicle sales in China have experienced some moderation, the total lithium content in EVs has continued to increase, reflecting the evolution of the market toward vehicles with higher lithium requirements. Furthermore, Chinese automotive exports have continued to grow strongly, particularly to markets outside China, including South Korea, Brazil, Australia and India. At the same time, EV sales in Europe continue to demonstrate solid growth. Together, these trends are supporting global EV adoption beyond the Chinese domestic market.
Looking ahead, we expect additional sources of demand to contribute to market growth, including the electrification of heavy-duty trucks and the continued deployment of battery energy storage systems (BESS), particularly for behind-the-meter applications.
Overall, we remain confident in the long-term fundamentals supporting lithium demand and the continued growth of the market.
•Novandino Division:
During the third quarter, sales volumes for Novandino reached approximately 73.3 thousand metric tons of lithium products, representing a 16% increase compared to the same period last year, and a 3% decrease compared to the record levels reported in the second quarter of 2026.
Operations and commercial activity continued as expected during the quarter, with ongoing expansion projects progressing according to schedule.
The average realized lithium sales price for Novandino during the third quarter was approximately US$19.6 per kilogram, representing a 10% decrease compared to the second quarter of 2026 and a 123% increase compared to the average realized price in the same period last year.
•SQM International Lithium Division:
During the third quarter, sales volumes in Australia reached approximately 5.5 thousand metric tons of LCE. Although volumes were lower compared to both the same period last year and the previous quarter, this primarily reflects the timing of sales, with year-to-date sales volumes remaining 47% above the same period in 2025. Looking ahead, we expect sales volumes to increase during the fourth quarter, allowing us to close 2026 with full-year sales volumes approximately 20% above 2025 levels.
Production continues to progress well, with current annual production reaching approximately 175 - 185 thousand metric tons of spodumene concentrate (SC6) attributable to SQM. We have also commenced the expansion phase of the mining and concentrator plant at Mount Holland, which is expected to progressively increase production capacity to approximately 350 thousand metric tons of spodumene concentrate (SC6) attributable to SQM by 2030 - 2031.
Our average realized spodumene sales price during the third quarter reached approximately US$2,036 per metric ton of spodumene concentrate (SC6), remaining relatively flat compared to the second quarter of 2026, and representing an increase of 185% year-over-year.

During the third quarter, fertilizer prices continued to increase, and our main products followed this trend. Higher prices have led some farmers to moderate their purchases of potash-based products, including potassium nitrate. In light of these market dynamics, we are revising our full-year 2026 sales volume growth outlook for this business line to approximately 5%, compared to our previous expectation of approximately 10% growth over 2025.
The increase in sales volumes and prices during the quarter was primarily driven by supply constraints in certain markets, particularly lower product availability from China, as well as supply disruptions associated with the conflict in the Middle East. These conditions allowed us to increase sales volumes in non-traditional markets, such as Eastern Europe and Africa.
We also observed strong commercial activity in the United States, Mexico and Spain.
Looking ahead, we continue to monitor the potential impact of higher-than-expected rainfall associated with the El Niño phenomenon along the Pacific coast. These weather conditions could affect agricultural activity and lead to lower fertilizer purchases by customers in these markets during the coming months.
Our average realized price for this business line reached US$1,119 per metric ton, representing a 20% increase compared to the same period last year and a 5% increase quarter-over-quarter. Based on current market dynamics, we expect prices to remain broadly at current levels through the remainder of the year.

Commercial activity for iodine and its derivatives remained strong during the third quarter. Although sales volumes declined seasonally compared to the first half of the year, they increased by 5% compared to the same period in 2025.
We expect demand to remain robust and estimate that global iodine demand could grow over 3% in 2027 compared to 2026, mainly driven by continued demand for X-ray contrast media.
During the third quarter of 2026, our average realized iodine price reached US$73 per kilogram, representing a 2% increase year-over-year and a slight decrease quarter-over-quarter due to customer mix. Based on current market dynamics, we believe prices could remain broadly stable, with the potential for a slight increase during the fourth quarter.
We continue to advance our expansion plans and improve operational efficiency, optimizing our product mix while maintaining our position as a reliable supplier to our customers. Our current effective annual production is estimated to exceed 16,000 metric tons of iodine this year. Looking ahead, subject to additional investments and market requirements, we believe there could be potential to increase our annual production capacity to approximately 20,000 metric tons in the coming years.
