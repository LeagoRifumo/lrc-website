Pulse Biosciences Reports Business Updates and Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Completed enrollment of NANOPULSE-AF US IDE pivotal study for treatment of AF with nPulse Cardiac Catheter System
Ended the quarter with $189 million of cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities
HAYWARD, California [Business Wire] — November 5, 2026. Pulse Biosciences, Inc. (Nasdaq: PLSE), the company leveraging its ever evolving novel and proprietary Nanosecond Pulsed Field Ablation™ (nano-PFA or nsPFA™) technology, today announced business updates and financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Pulse Biosciences delivered several major milestones since the second quarter, led by completing enrollment in our U.S. pivotal IDE study in just six months, three months ahead of plan,” said Paul LaViolette, CEO and Co Chairman of Pulse Biosciences. “That speed reflects the growing enthusiasm physicians have shown for the nPulse catheter since their very first cases. We also meaningfully strengthened our balance sheet, represented by institutional investor conviction in the opportunities ahead enabled by our technology and our team. With NANOPULSE-AF fully enrolled, and the capital to execute, we are now focused on preparations for launch readiness, additional data readouts, and regulatory submissions. We are also advancing new clinical study initiatives and exciting product development innovations, reflecting the deep application pipeline for our platform technology that gives us the opportunity to bring nsPFA to very large, underserved patient populations.”
Recent Business Highlights
● | Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $189.2 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $101.6 million as of June 30, 2026. Based on current yields on cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities at the current balance, provides over $7.5 million in annual interest income.
● | The Company raised $95.8 million in net proceeds through its ATM programs during the third quarter, and more than $150 million since May representing less than 6% dilution.

Endocardial Catheter AF Ablation
● | Completed enrollment in NANOPULSE-AF, the U.S. IDE pivotal study of the nPulse Cardiac Catheter System for the treatment of atrial fibrillation (AF), with 169 evaluable patients at 15 sites with 36 investigators. Enrollment was completed in under six months, three months faster than the timeline originally communicated.

● | Plans to present 12-month follow up data on 100 patients from the EU feasibility study in Q1 2027.

● | Continued to progress toward a CE Mark submission with anticipated CE mark approval in Q4 2027.

● | Plans to submit an IDE application for a persistent AF study in the fourth quarter of 2026, extending the nPulse catheter platform to larger and more complex patient groups.

Surgical AF Ablation and Soft Tissue Ablation
● | Continued to enroll NANOCLAMP-AF, the U.S. IDE pivotal study for concomitant surgical AF ablation, which remains on track to complete enrollment by the end of the first half of 2027. First patient enrolled in the trial has reached one year follow-up.
● | Recorded $0.4 million of third quarter revenue from Vybrance, compared to $0.1 million in the prior year period, while commercial case volume increased from prior quarters and important market development milestones were achieved focusing on existing account development.

● | Completed enrollment of the PRECISE-Benign Thyroid Nodule (PRECISE-BTN) study in September with 100 patients. Preliminary data are being presented at the American Thyroid Association annual meeting, with further data readouts expected after all patients achieve six-month and twelve-month follow-up.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $0.4 million, compared to $0.1 million in the prior year period. The Company remains in a controlled commercial launch of its Vybrance system, directing efforts toward market development and clinical use within a limited customer base. Results continue to be encouraging, we expect this phase of the Vybrance controlled launch to continue into Q4 2027.
Total GAAP costs and expenses, representing cost of product revenue, research and development, and selling, general and administrative expenses, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $24.7 million, an increase of $4.2 million compared to $20.5 million in the prior year period. The increase was primarily driven by increased investment in clinical program and employee-related spend. Non-GAAP costs and expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $19.1 million, an increase of $4.5 million compared to $14.6 million in the prior year period, driven by increased clinical program and employee-related spend.
GAAP net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was ($23.1) million compared to ($19.4) million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Non-GAAP net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was ($17.5) million compared to ($13.5) million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.
Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $189.2 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $101.6 million in cash and cash equivalents as of June 30, 2026. The sequential increase of $87.6 million reflects net proceeds raised under the Company’s ATM programs. Cash used in operating activities in the third quarter of 2026 totaled $15.3 million, compared to $13.0 million in the same period of the prior year and $18.7 million in the second quarter of 2026.
The Company raised $95.8 million in net proceeds through its ATM programs during the third quarter, bringing the total since May to more than $150 million, representing under 6% dilution. Approximately $64 million of ATM capacity remains available.

About Pulse Biosciences®
Pulse Biosciences is a novel bioelectric medicine company committed to health innovation that has the intention as well as potential to improve the quality of life for patients. The Company’s proprietary nPulse™ technology delivers nanosecond pulses of electrical energy to non-thermally clear cells while sparing adjacent non-cellular tissue. The Company is actively pursuing the development of its nPulse technology for use in the treatment of atrial fibrillation and in a select few other markets where it could have a profound positive impact on healthcare for both patients and providers.

Glossary of Terms
● | Arrhythmia: Any abnormal heart rhythm where the heart beats too fast, too slow, or irregularly due to electrical disturbances.
● | Atrial arrhythmia: Any abnormal heart rhythm originating in the atria, including AF, atrial flutter, and atrial tachycardia.
● | Atrial fibrillation (AF): Irregular, often rapid heartbeat starting in the atria that can cause symptoms and increase stroke risk.
● | Atrial tachycardia (AT): A rapid abnormal heart rhythm caused by electrical activity originating in the atria outside the heart’s normal pacemaker (sinus node).
● | Cavo tricuspid Isthmus (CTI): A region of right atrial tissue between the inferior vena cava and tricuspid valve that forms part of the electrical circuit in typical atrial flutter.
● | Electroanatomic mapping: 3D mapping of heart chambers that combines anatomy and electrical data to guide ablation.
● | Electrophysiology (EP): Cardiology subspecialty focused on the heart’s electrical system and the diagnosis and treatment of arrhythmias.
● | Myocardial Infarction (MI): Death of heart muscle tissue due to prolonged inadequate blood flow and oxygen supply, commonly called a heart attack.
● | Paroxysmal atrial fibrillation: AF episodes that start and stop on their own, typically lasting less than seven days.
● | Persistent Atrial Fibrillation: Continuous AF lasting more than seven days.
● | Pulsed Field Ablation (PFA): Non‑thermal ablation using short, high‑voltage pulses to selectively injure cardiac cells via irreversible electroporation.
● | Posterior Wall Isolation (PWI): Ablation technique that electrically isolates the posterior wall of the left atrium to target electrical activity that may initiate or sustain AT or AF.
● | Premature ventricular contraction (PVC): An early heartbeat originating in the ventricles that interrupts the normal heart rhythm and may feel like a skipped beat.
● | Pulmonary vein isolation (PVI): Ablation technique that electrically isolates the pulmonary veins from the left atrium to prevent AF triggers.
● | Ventricular Tachycardia (VT): A rapid abnormal heart rhythm originating in the ventricles that can impair blood flow and may be life-threatening.
