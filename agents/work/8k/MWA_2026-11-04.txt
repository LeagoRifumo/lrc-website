MUELLER WATER PRODUCTS REPORTS RECORD FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR 2026 RESULTS; INTRODUCES FISCAL 2027 GUIDANCE
Achieved Net Sales of $376.5 million in Quarter, $1.5 billion in Fiscal Year
Earned Net Income per Diluted Share of $0.33 in Quarter, $1.41 in Fiscal Year
Reported Adjusted Net Income per Diluted Share of $0.38 in Quarter, $1.57 in Fiscal Year
Generated Adjusted EBITDA of $95.5 million in Quarter, $372.2 million in Fiscal Year
Announced Agreement to Transition Metering Business to ZENNER USA
ATLANTA, November 4, 2026 - Mueller Water Products, Inc. (NYSE: MWA), a leading manufacturer and marketer of products and solutions used in the transmission, distribution and measurement of water in North America, announced financial results for its fourth quarter and fiscal year ended September 30, 2026.
In the fourth quarter of 2026, the Company:
•Achieved net sales of $376.5 million, a 1.1% decrease as compared with $380.8 million in the prior year quarter
•Reported operating income of $73.1 million as compared with $69.6 million in the prior year quarter, and increased adjusted operating income 4.7% to $82.6 million as compared with $78.9 million in the prior year quarter
•Reported operating margin of 19.4% as compared with 18.3% in the prior year quarter, and increased adjusted operating margin to 21.9% as compared with 20.7% in the prior year quarter
•Generated net income of $52.3 million as compared with $52.6 million in the prior year quarter, with net income margin of 13.9% as compared with 13.8% in the prior year quarter, and reported adjusted net income of $59.2 million as compared with $59.7 million in the prior year quarter
•Reported net income per diluted share of $0.33 as compared with $0.33 in the prior year quarter, and generated adjusted net income per diluted share of $0.38 as compared with $0.38 in the prior year quarter
•Increased adjusted EBITDA 4.0% to $95.5 million as compared with $91.8 million in the prior year quarter, and expanded adjusted EBITDA margin to 25.4% as compared with 24.1% in the prior year quarter
In the 2026 fiscal year, the Company:
•Increased net sales 3.2% to $1,475.0 million as compared with $1,429.7 million in the prior year
•Reported operating income of $290.8 million as compared with $260.6 million in the prior year, and increased adjusted operating income 15.5% to $322.3 million as compared with $279.1 million in the prior year
•Reported operating margin of 19.7% as compared with 18.2% in the prior year, and increased adjusted operating margin to 21.9% as compared with 19.5% in the prior year
•Generated net income of $221.9 million as compared with $191.7 million in the prior year, with net income margin of 15.0% as compared with 13.4% in the prior year, and increased adjusted net income 19.7% to $246.2 million as compared with $205.6 million in the prior year
•Reported net income per diluted share of $1.41 as compared with $1.22 in the prior year, and increased adjusted net income per diluted share 19.8% to $1.57 as compared with $1.31 in the prior year
•Increased adjusted EBITDA 14.1% to $372.2 million as compared with $326.2 million in the prior year, and expanded adjusted EBITDA margin to 25.2% as compared with 22.8% in the prior year
•Increased net cash provided by operating activities $43.6 million, or 19.9%, to $262.9 million as compared with $219.3 million in the prior year
•Increased free cash flow $35.3 million, or 20.5%, to $207.3 million as compared with $172.0 million in the prior year
•Returned $74.3 million to shareholders through dividends and common stock repurchases
“We delivered record profitability to close fiscal 2026 with our fourth quarter adjusted EBITDA increasing 4.0% year-over-year and adjusted EBITDA margin expanding to 25.4%. These results capped another record year for the Company, as we expanded adjusted EBITDA margin by 240 basis points this year and 940 basis points over three years, achieved record net sales with 3.2% year-over-year growth, despite slower new residential construction activity, increased adjusted earnings per share by approximately 20% and generated more than $200 million in free cash flow. That combination of sales growth, stronger profitability and outstanding cash generation underscores the quality of our execution, the stability of our business and the dedication of our employees,” said Paul McAndrew, President and Chief Executive Officer of Mueller Water Products.
“What stands out the most to me is how our teams delivered these results while continuing to improve the way we work. Across Mueller, employees stayed close to our customers, managed through changing demands in uncertain times and continued to make and capitalize on important investments in our foundries and manufacturing network. We are building a more streamlined and accountable organization through portfolio optimization, and the Mueller Operating System, which is giving our people a common framework to solve problems, share best practices and raise performance.”
“Our priorities for fiscal year 2027 are straightforward. We will protect the strength of our core business, improve returns from our operations and invest where we see the best opportunities to create value. Municipal repair and replacement demand remains resilient, and our balance sheet provides capacity to invest as we pursue strategies to drive above-market sales growth and continue margin expansion. While we remain mindful of market uncertainty, we enter the year with a clear agenda and confidence in our ability to deliver another step forward in margin performance,” Mr. McAndrew concluded.
Consolidated Results
Net sales for the 2026 fourth quarter decreased $4.3 million, or 1.1%, to $376.5 million as compared with $380.8 million in the prior year quarter, primarily due to lower volumes, partially offset by higher pricing across most product lines.
Gross profit for the fourth quarter decreased $0.7 million, or 0.5%, to $139.3 million as compared with $140.0 million in the prior year quarter. Gross margin increased 20 basis points to 37.0% as compared with 36.8% in the prior year quarter, primarily due to higher pricing and manufacturing performance, partially offset by inflationary pressures, lower volumes, product mix and tariffs, and $0.6 million of Decatur legacy brass foundry closure costs.
Selling, general and administrative expenses for the fourth quarter decreased $9.4 million, or 14.1%, to $57.3 million as compared with $66.7 million in the prior year quarter, primarily due to lower incentive-based compensation, favorable personnel-related costs and reduced foreign currency headwinds, partially offset by inflationary pressures.
Operating income for the fourth quarter increased $3.5 million, or 5.0%, to $73.1 million as compared with $69.6 million in the prior year quarter. This increase was primarily driven by higher pricing, lower SG&A expenses and manufacturing performance, partially offset by strategic reorganization and other charges, inflationary pressures, lower volumes, product mix and tariffs. Operating margin increased to 19.4% as compared with 18.3% in the prior year quarter.
During the quarter, the Company incurred $8.9 million of strategic reorganization and other charges, primarily related to the closure of our legacy brass foundry in Decatur, Illinois, and severance. Strategic reorganization and other charges and the Decatur legacy brass foundry closure costs in gross profit have been excluded from adjusted results.
Adjusted operating income for the quarter increased $3.7 million, or 4.7%, to $82.6 million as compared with $78.9 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted operating margin improved to 21.9% as compared with 20.7% in the prior year quarter.
Net income decreased $0.3 million, or 0.6%, to $52.3 million as compared with $52.6 million in the prior year quarter. Net income margin improved to 13.9% as compared with 13.8% in the prior year quarter. Adjusted net income decreased $0.5 million, or 0.8%, to $59.2 million as compared with $59.7 million in the prior year quarter.
Adjusted EBITDA of $95.5 million increased $3.7 million, or 4.0%, as compared with $91.8 million in the prior year quarter, and adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 25.4% as compared with 24.1% in the prior year quarter.
Segment Results
Water Flow Solutions
Net sales for the 2026 fourth quarter decreased $6.6 million, or 3.0%, to $210.9 million as compared with $217.5 million in the prior year quarter, primarily due to lower volumes, partially offset by higher pricing across most product lines.
Operating income was $58.8 million and adjusted operating income was $59.4 million for the quarter. Adjusted operating income increased $4.3 million, or 7.8%, compared with the prior year quarter. Benefits from manufacturing performance, higher pricing and lower SG&A expenses more than offset lower volumes, product mix, inflationary pressures and tariffs. Operating margin was 27.9% and adjusted operating margin was 28.2% as compared with 25.3% for both operating and adjusted operating margins in the prior year quarter.
Adjusted EBITDA of $67.1 million increased $4.4 million, or 7.0%, as compared with $62.7 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 31.8% as compared with 28.8% in the prior year quarter.
Water Management Solutions
Net sales for the 2026 fourth quarter increased $2.3 million, or 1.4%, to $165.6 million, as compared with $163.3 million in the prior year quarter, primarily due to higher pricing across most product lines, partially offset by lower volumes.
Operating income was $35.6 million and adjusted operating income was $36.0 million for the quarter. Adjusted operating income decreased $3.8 million, or 9.5%, compared with the prior year quarter. Lower volumes, tariffs and inflationary pressures were partially offset by benefits from higher pricing and lower SG&A expenses, including reduced foreign currency headwinds. Operating margin and adjusted operating margin were 21.5% and 21.7%, respectively, as compared with the prior year quarter operating margin of 20.9% and adjusted operating margin of 24.4%.
Adjusted EBITDA of $41.2 million decreased $3.8 million, or 8.4%, as compared with $45.0 million in the prior year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA margin decreased to 24.9% as compared with 27.6% in the prior year quarter.
Interest Expense, Net
Interest expense, net, for the 2026 fourth quarter and fiscal year decreased to $0.5 million and $3.8 million, respectively, as compared with $1.0 million and $6.6 million in the prior year periods, respectively, primarily as a result of higher interest income.
Income Taxes
For the 2026 fourth quarter, income tax expense was $20.3 million, or 28.0% of income before tax, as compared with $16.1 million in the prior year quarter, or 23.4% of income before tax. For the fiscal year, income tax expense was $65.0 million, or 22.7% of income before tax, as compared with $62.5 million in the prior year, or 24.6% of income before tax. The lower effective tax rate for the fiscal year compared with the prior year was primarily due to a one-time tax benefit resulting from the recognition of a loss on a foreign subsidiary investment.
Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Net cash provided by operating activities for the 2026 fiscal year increased $43.6 million, or 19.9%, to $262.9 million, as compared with $219.3 million in the prior year. The increase was primarily driven by higher net income and lower cash taxes compared with the prior year, partially offset by changes in working capital.
During the fiscal year, the Company invested $55.6 million in capital expenditures as compared with $47.3 million in the prior year, primarily driven by investments in our iron foundries.
Free cash flow (defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditures) for the fiscal year increased $35.3 million, or 20.5%, to $207.3 million, as compared with $172.0 million in the prior year, due to the increase in net cash provided by operating activities partially offset by higher capital expenditures.
As of September 30, 2026, Mueller Water Products had $453.8 million of total debt outstanding and $566.0 million of cash and cash equivalents. We did not have any borrowings under our ABL Agreement at the end of the year, nor did we borrow any amounts under our ABL during the year.
There are no maturities on the Company’s debt financings until June 2029, and our 4.0% Senior Notes have no financial maintenance covenants. At the end of the quarter, the Company had $730.7 million of total liquidity, including $164.7 million in availability under the ABL.
Return of Capital to Shareholders
On October 22, 2026, the Company announced that its Board of Directors approved a $300.0 million increase to the Company’s common stock repurchase authorization, bringing the total authorization to $334.5 million. Repurchases may be made from time to time through open market purchases, privately negotiated transactions or other means in accordance with applicable securities laws. The timing and amount of any repurchases will depend on market conditions and other factors, and the program may be suspended or discontinued at any time.
The Board also declared a quarterly dividend of $0.08 per share, a 14.3% increase compared with the prior quarterly dividend of $0.07 per share. The dividend is payable on November 19, 2026, to stockholders of record at the close of business on November 9, 2026.
During fiscal 2026, the Company returned $74.3 million to shareholders, including $43.8 million in dividends and $30.5 million in common stock repurchases.
Portfolio Optimization to Transition Metering Business
In a separate press release issued today, Mueller Water Products announced that it has entered into an agreement to transition its metering business, which operates via its Mueller Systems subsidiary, to ZENNER USA, part of the Minol-ZENNER Group, a leading global manufacturer and distributor of water meters, heat and cooling energy meters, gas meters, submetering and digital solutions for remote reading. The Company’s press release can be found on the Company’s investor relations website at www.muellerwaterproducts.com.
Fiscal 2027 Outlook
The Company is introducing fiscal 2027 consolidated net sales guidance of $1,440 million to $1,470 million. This guidance excludes all metering-related financial results. In fiscal 2026, the metering-related net sales were approximately $40 million. The Company anticipates its fiscal 2027 adjusted EBITDA to be between $378 million and $388 million. At the midpoint of the guidance range, the Company expects to achieve an adjusted EBITDA margin of 26.3% for the year, reflecting a 110 basis point year-over-year improvement. The Company anticipates the second half of fiscal 2027 adjusted EBITDA margin will be higher than the first half, driven primarily by the seasonality of net sales, benefits from portfolio optimization and operational improvements. The Company expects free cash flow as a percentage of adjusted net income to be more than 80% in fiscal 2027.

•Total SG&A expenses between $228 million and $233 million
•Net interest expense between $3 million and $5 million
•Effective income tax rate between 24% and 26%
•Depreciation and amortization between $48 million and $52 million
•Capital expenditures between $60 million and $70 million

MUELLER WATER PRODUCTS ANNOUNCES AGREEMENT TO TRANSITION METERING BUSINESS TO ZENNER USA
Portfolio Optimization through Execution of Mueller Operating System Supports Future Margin Expansion
ATLANTA, November 4, 2026 -- Mueller Water Products, Inc. (NYSE: MWA), a leading manufacturer and marketer of products and solutions used in the transmission, distribution and measurement of water in North America, today announced it has entered into an agreement to transition its metering business, which operates via its Mueller Systems subsidiary, to ZENNER USA, part of the Minol-ZENNER Group, a leading global manufacturer and distributor of water meters, heat and cooling energy meters, gas meters, submetering and digital solutions for remote reading.
This action follows a comprehensive review by Mueller’s Board of Directors and management of the strategic fit and performance of the metering business in the Company’s portfolio. Under the agreement, Mueller is exiting the metering business and will transition customers to ZENNER USA for replacement software and hardware to continue to fulfill our customer commitments. Transitioning the metering business will simplify Mueller’s portfolio and allow the Company to focus capital, talent and management attention on areas where it has stronger competitive positions and clearer opportunities for profitable growth.
“We are taking this step to sharpen Mueller’s focus and strengthen the Company for the future,” said Paul McAndrew, President and Chief Executive Officer of Mueller Water Products. “It reflects our Mueller Operating System in action: making disciplined, fact-based decisions, putting customers first and executing with clear accountability. By working closely with ZENNER USA, we plan to provide continuity for customers while concentrating our resources on the businesses where Mueller is best positioned to lead, grow and create long-term value.”
McAndrew concluded, “Our priority throughout this process has been and will be protecting customers and ensuring they continue to receive the products, service and support they expect from Mueller. ZENNER USA brings the specialized scale, technology investment, compatible products and global manufacturing capabilities needed to support customers over the long term. We are dedicated to working closely with ZENNER USA to deliver a seamless transition and honor our existing customer commitments.”
Mueller expects the transition to support adjusted EBITDA margin expansion with a 100 to 140 basis point annualized benefit, primarily beginning in the second quarter of fiscal 2027. The Company and ZENNER USA will begin the transition process immediately and work closely with customers and suppliers to provide continuity of service and ongoing support. In connection with the agreement, the Company expects to incur approximately $26 million of one-time expenses in fiscal 2027, primarily related to the outstanding warranty liability, operating and customer
service costs, transaction-related costs, severance, inventory and other asset write-offs. The transition impacts will be adjusted as appropriate in the Company’s key financial metrics. The one-time and ongoing transition activities will be included in net sales, cost of sales and strategic reorganization and other charges. The majority of the expenses will be incurred during the first quarter, with most of the remaining expenses incurred prior to the end of the year. The financial impacts and benefits will occur in the Water Management Solutions segment. Taking the one-time expenses and annualized benefit into account, the Company believes the cash return on investment of completing the transition is very attractive relative to other opportunities.
