Acadia Realty Trust Reports Third Quarter 2026 Operating Results
Key Highlights for the three months ended September 30, 2026 include:
•
Third quarter GAAP net loss of $0.03 per share (compared to net earnings of $0.03 in third quarter 2025) and FFO As Adjusted of $0.31 per share (compared to $0.29 in third quarter 2025)
•
Third quarter REIT Portfolio same-property NOI increased 10.2%, driven by street retail, which increased 15.2% and the Company anticipates full-year 2026 same-property NOI growth of 7.5% to 8.5%
•
Delivered REIT Portfolio GAAP and cash leasing spreads on new leases of 98% and 62%, respectively, driven by street retail
•
Grew its signed-not-open (“SNO”) pipeline 32% to $21.8 million, up from $16.5 million at June 30, 2026, driven by approximately $8.4 million of new leasing, of which approximately $6.2 million was from new street and urban leases
•
Increased leasing at its Henderson Avenue development in Dallas to approximately 90% of retail GLA executed or in advanced stages of negotiation, with openings targeted throughout 2027 and a continued expected yield on cost for the entire project of 8% to 10%
•
Completed approximately $152 million of accretive street acquisitions in the REIT Portfolio
•
The Company has maintained its full-year NAREIT FFO and 2026 FFO As Adjusted Guidance. It revised net earnings per share to $0.29-$0.30 from $0.40-$0.41
RYE, NY (November 2, 2026) - Acadia Realty Trust (NYSE: AKR) (“Acadia” or the “Company”) today reported operating results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. All per share amounts are on a fully-diluted basis, where applicable. Acadia owns and operates a high-quality real estate portfolio of street and open-air retail properties in the nation's most dynamic retail corridors (“REIT Portfolio”), along with an investment management platform that targets opportunistic and value-add investments through its institutional co-investment vehicles (“Investment Management”).
Kenneth F. Bernstein, President and CEO of Acadia, commented:
“We delivered third-quarter same-property NOI growth that ranks among the strongest in our history, led once again by growth of more than 15% in our street retail portfolio. Through the first nine months
1
of 2026, same-property NOI growth exceeded 8%, and that top-line growth is dropping to the bottom line, with FFO As Adjusted up nearly 10% both compared with the same period last year. Leasing momentum continued across our key street retail corridors, and we are now beginning to see demand broaden into our larger-format urban markets, which have been slower to recover. Complementing this momentum, we acquired approximately $150 million of street retail during the quarter, adding properties that are accretive across each of our key metrics and strengthen the scale advantage we have established in these markets. With strong internal growth continuing into 2027 and beyond, along with ample liquidity and meaningful investment capacity, we are well positioned to further accelerate our growth as compelling opportunities emerge.”
Financial Results
A complete reconciliation, in dollars and per share amounts, of (i) net (loss) earnings attributable to Acadia to Funds From Operations (“FFO”) (as defined by the National Association of Real Estate Investment Trusts “NAREIT”) and FFO As Adjusted attributable to common shareholders and Common OP Unit holders and (ii) operating income to net property operating income (“NOI”) and definitions of non-GAAP metrics are included in the financial tables of this release. The amounts discussed below are net of noncontrolling interests (except for the Common OP Unit holders) and all per share amounts are on a fully-diluted basis.
Net (Loss) Earnings
•
Net loss per share for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $0.03. This compares with net earnings per share for the three months ended September 30, 2025 of $0.03. The decrease was primarily due to a non-cash impairment charge of $11.7 million, or $0.08 per share, related to two properties that are in process of being sold.
NAREIT FFO
•
NAREIT Funds From Operations (“NAREIT FFO”) for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $44.5 million, or $0.30 per share, as compared to $38.6 million, or $0.28 per share, for the three months ended September 30, 2025.
FFO As Adjusted
•
FFO As Adjusted for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $45.2 million, or $0.31 per share, as compared to $39.7 million, or $0.29 per share, for the three months ended September 30, 2025.
REIT Portfolio Same-Property NOI
•
Same-property NOI grew 10.2% for the third quarter of 2026 compared with the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by 15.2% growth from the street retail portfolio. Same-property NOI growth for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was 8.3% compared to the same period in 2025, and the Company anticipates full-year 2026 same-property NOI growth of 7.5% to 8.5%. These amounts exclude developments and redevelopments.

REIT Portfolio Occupancy and Leasing Update
•
As of September 30, 2026, economic occupancy and leased occupancy each decreased 20 basis points to 94.2% and 95.5%, respectively, from 94.4% and 95.7% as of June 30, 2026. The declines were entirely attributable to approximately 16,600 square feet of vacancy in street retail properties acquired during the third quarter at 44 Main Street in Westport and 69-73 Greene Street in Soho, which the Company expects to lease in the near term. Excluding this acquired vacancy, economic occupancy would have increased approximately 10 basis points to 94.5%, with street and urban economic occupancy up approximately 30 basis points to 91.7%.
•
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, conforming GAAP and cash leasing spreads on new leases were 98% and 62%, respectively, and 49% and 25%, respectively, inclusive of renewal leases.
•
As part of third quarter activity, the Company executed a lease with a national specialty retailer at 129 Fifth Avenue in Manhattan’s Flatiron District, which was acquired vacant in the second quarter of 2026. Leveraging its tenant relationships, the Company drew competing bids and signed a lease for the entirety of the building within 30 days at a rent well above underwriting, for a projected yield on cost in excess of 9%.

Transactional Activity
During the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company completed approximately $152 million of accretive street retail acquisitions within its REIT Portfolio. Details of the third quarter acquisitions are discussed below. This brings year-to-date acquisition volume to $775 million (approximately $436 million at the Company's share), including $351 million in the REIT Portfolio and $424 million (approximately $85 million at the Company's share) through the Investment Management platform.
REIT Portfolio Acquisitions
•
Westport, Fairfield County, Connecticut. In September 2026, the Company acquired 44 Main Street for a purchase price of $23 million, adding four storefronts on Main Street, Westport’s premier retail corridor,
3
and bringing the Company's total storefronts owned on Main Street to nine. The property includes a mix of in-place retail tenancy, along with an additional vacancy offering near-term leasing upside.
•
Soho, Manhattan, New York. In September 2026, the Company acquired two storefronts at 69-73 Greene Street for a purchase price of $66 million, bringing the Company's total storefronts owned on Greene Street to 11 and its total storefronts owned in Soho to 21. The acquisition includes one storefront leased to Amiri and an adjacent vacancy offering a near-term lease-up opportunity.
•
West Village, Manhattan, New York. In August 2026, the Company acquired 385 Bleecker Street and 371-373 Bleecker Street for an aggregate purchase price of $20 million, adding three storefronts on the West Village's premier retail corridor. These acquisitions bring the Company’s total storefronts owned on Bleecker Street to 10.
•
Greenwich, Fairfield County, Connecticut. In August 2026, the Company acquired 171-173 Greenwich Avenue for a purchase price of $10 million, bringing the Company’s total storefronts owned on Greenwich Avenue to five. The in-place lease provides an embedded mark-to-market opportunity on a supply-constrained corridor with high barriers to entry.
•
West Hollywood, Los Angeles, California. In July 2026, the Company acquired 8800-8804 Melrose Avenue for a purchase price of $30 million. Located in the heart of West Hollywood’s ascendant luxury retail corridor, in close proximity to the Company’s Melrose Place portfolio, the property directly complements and expands the Company’s scale within its West Hollywood corridor. Additionally, the site includes a parking lot that can accommodate additional retail GLA, offering embedded upside that would more than double the building's square footage.
Henderson Avenue Development
•
Henderson Avenue Development, Dallas, Texas. During the third quarter, leases executed or in advanced stages of negotiation at the Company’s Henderson Avenue development reached approximately 90% of retail GLA (95% including the stabilized portfolio). Leasing activity was led by national apparel and lifestyle brands, including Lululemon, Vuori and Jenni Kayne, alongside a curated mix of local restaurants and retailers. The Company is the largest owner on Henderson Avenue, with a portfolio spanning more than a mile. The development is designed to connect and complete the street, extend the Knox-Henderson district and create a walkable street retail destination. The total estimated investment of approximately $210 million to $235 million is fully funded with approximately $144 million invested as of September 30, 2026. The amount invested included the September 2026 acquisition of an additional Henderson Avenue parcel for approximately $3 million, with anticipated future development costs of approximately $7 million, which are included in the amounts above. Adjacent to the Company’s existing holdings, the parcel provides further expansion and lease-up opportunities. Tenant openings are expected to begin in 2027 and continue throughout the year, with NOI contributions expected to be weighted toward the second half of 2027. Strong leasing demand continued to support a projected yield on cost of 8% to 10%.

Investment Management Platform Dispositions
During the third quarter, the Company, through its Investment Management platform, completed the disposition of one property for $8 million, of which the Company’s share was approximately $2 million. Details of the disposition are discussed below. Year-to-date, the Company has disposed of a total of approximately $723 million, including recapitalizations of $504 million, of which the Company’s share was $144 million. These fund dispositions and the Fund V recapitalization (completed in the first quarter) generated a weighted average gross equity multiple of approximately 1.9x. The Company also has approximately $110 million of dispositions ($22 million at the Company's share) under contract for sale, subject to customary closing conditions.
•
New York, New York (Fund IV). During July 2026, the Company completed the disposition of a parking garage condo unit at 1035 Third Avenue, in New York, NY, for $8 million.
Structured Finance (Subsequent Event)
Sunset Park, Brooklyn, Loan Amendment and Extension. In October 2026, the Company received $20 million in connection with an amendment and extension of its existing structured finance loan. As part of the transaction, the Company acquired the property's senior loan, investing approximately $120 million of incremental capital, net of the $20 million payment, and consolidating its investments into a new $200 million financing encompassing the property's entire debt capital stack. The Company is now the sole lender, with the financing bearing interest at SOFR plus 300 basis points for a four-year term. The cash-flowing, mixed-use retail property on the Sunset Park waterfront is anchored by Floor & Decor and Marshalls, with funded reserves available to cover operating expenses and capital expenditures. Additional leasing and redevelopment opportunities could further strengthen the underlying collateral securing the Company's loan.

The Company considers funds from operations (“FFO”) as defined by the National Association of Real Estate Investment Trusts (“NAREIT”) and net property operating income (“NOI”) to be appropriate supplemental disclosures of operating performance for an equity REIT due to their widespread acceptance and use within the REIT and analyst communities. In addition, the Company believes that given the atypical nature of certain unusual items (as further described below), “FFO As Adjusted” is also an appropriate supplemental disclosure of operating performance. FFO, FFO As Adjusted and NOI are presented to assist investors in analyzing the performance of the Company. The Company believes they are helpful as they exclude various items included in net income (loss) that are not indicative of operating performance, such as (i) gains (losses) from sales of real estate properties; (ii) depreciation and amortization; (iii) impairment of depreciable real estate assets related to the Company’s main business and land held for the development of property, and (iv) items that management believes are not reflective of ongoing core operating results, including non-comparable revenues, expenses, gains, and losses. While these adjustments may be subject to fluctuations from period to period, with both positive and negative short-term impacts, management believes that the removal of the impacts of these items enhances our understanding of the operating performance of our properties. The Company’s method of calculating FFO, FFO As Adjusted and NOI may be different from methods used by other REITs and, accordingly, may not be comparable to such other REITs. Neither FFO nor FFO As Adjusted represents cash generated from operations as defined by generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), nor is indicative of cash available to fund all cash needs, including distributions. Such measures should not be considered as an alternative to net income (loss) for the purpose of evaluating the Company’s performance or to cash flows as a measure of liquidity.
a.

b.
Also consistent with NAREIT’s definition of FFO, the Company has elected to include: the impact of the unrealized holding gains (losses) incidental to its main business, including those related to its investments in Albertsons in FFO.
c.
FFO As Adjusted (new metric starting in 2026) begins with the NAREIT definition of FFO and adjusts FFO (or as an adjustment to the numerator within its earnings per share calculations) to take into account FFO without regard to certain unusual items including charges, income and gains that management believes are not comparable or indicative of the results of the Company’s operating real estate portfolio.
