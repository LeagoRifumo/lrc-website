ADVANCED DRAINAGE SYSTEMS ANNOUNCES SECOND QUARTER
FISCAL 2027 RESULTS
•Net sales increased 12.5% year-to-date
•Organic net sales increased 2.3% year-to-date
•Repurchased $375.1 million of common stock
•Generated $458.2 million in cash from operations
•Announced proposed acquisition of StormTrap
HILLIARD, Ohio – (November 5, 2026) – Advanced Drainage Systems, Inc. (NYSE: WMS) (“ADS” or the “Company”), a leading provider of innovative water management solutions in the stormwater and onsite wastewater industries today announced financial results for the fiscal second quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Scott Barbour, President and Chief Executive Officer of ADS commented, "Our second quarter results demonstrate the resilience of our business model and the team's ability to execute in a challenging demand environment. Net sales increased 4.6% to $889.4 million, including the contribution from NDS, and we generated Adjusted EBITDA of $286.8 million, resulting in an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 32.2%. Revenue in the second quarter was impacted by a larger than previously estimated pull forward of revenue into the first quarter. Looking back over the first half of fiscal year 2027, our performance was strong, with net sales up 12.5%, organic net sales up 2.3% and Adjusted EBITDA up 14.0%. The first half Adjusted EBITDA margin of 34.1% represents the highest first half profitability in the Company’s history."
"We continue to see strong performance in areas where innovation and evolving regulations are creating new demand, with advanced wastewater treatment, stormwater capture and water quality products each growing double digits. We also took an important step to expand our addressable market with the proposed acquisition of StormTrap, a leader in large-volume, space-constrained and highly engineered stormwater solutions. StormTrap complements and further diversifies our portfolio, fits ADS’ specification-driven commercial model and creates new cross-selling opportunities to further expand the package in the non-residential and infrastructure markets."
"Favorable price/cost has more than offset unfavorable volume and transportation costs, contributing to robust profitability in the quarter, which we expect to continue into the second half of the year. We are experiencing lower inflationary pressure than initially expected on materials, mitigating the need for corresponding pricing actions that had previously been included in our net sales outlook. The demand environment is softer today than when we initially issued guidance in May, driven by higher interest rates and ongoing geopolitical uncertainty. These changes are reflected in our updated revenue outlook."
"Because of our focus on new product introductions, material conversion, managing price/cost, executing on productivity and deploying capital in a disciplined manner, the Adjusted EBITDA outlook is largely the same despite the change in net sales guidance. This reflects our confidence in the resilient earnings profile of the business and our focus on the factors within our control. We remain confident in the long-term fundamentals of ADS as a pure-play water management company, and we believe the investments we are making today position us to deliver as our end markets recover."
Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results
Net sales increased $39.0 million, or 4.6%, to $889.4 million, as compared to $850.4 million in the prior year quarter. Stormwater sales increased $41.4 million, or 6.2%, to $712.1 million, as compared to $670.7 million in the prior year quarter. Stormwater sales include $77.3 million of revenue from the acquisition of National Diversified Sales (“NDS”). On an organic basis, Stormwater sales decreased 5.3%. Wastewater sales decreased $2.4 million, or 1.3%, to $177.3 million as compared to $179.7 million in the prior year quarter.
Gross profit increased $5.7 million, or 1.7%, to $345.9 million as compared to $340.1 million in the prior year quarter. The increase in gross profit is primarily driven by the acquisition of NDS, as well as favorable price/cost more than offsetting unfavorable volume and transportation costs.
Selling, general and administrative expenses increased $10.4 million, or 8.7% to $129.6 million, as compared to $119.2 million. As a percentage of sales, selling, general and administrative expense was 14.6% as compared to 14.0% in the prior year quarter. The increase was primarily driven by the acquisition of NDS and associated transaction costs.

Net income from continuing operations decreased $22.9 million, or 14.6%, to $133.6 million as compared to $156.5 million in the prior year quarter. Diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) from continuing operations decreased $0.22, or 11.0%, to $1.77 as compared to $1.99 in the prior year quarter.
Adjusted EBITDA (Non-GAAP) decreased $0.7 million, or 0.2%, to $286.8 million, as compared to $287.5 million in the prior year quarter, primarily due to the factors mentioned above. As a percentage of net sales, Adjusted EBITDA was 32.2% as compared to 33.8% in the prior year quarter.
Segment sales results are based on Net sales to external customers. Reconciliations of GAAP to Non-GAAP financial measures for Adjusted EBITDA, Organic Net Sales, Free Cash Flow and Adjusted Earnings per Share have been provided in the financial statement tables included in this press release. An explanation of these measures is also included below under the heading “Non-GAAP Financial Measures.”
Year-to-Date Fiscal 2027 Results
Net sales increased $210.3 million, or 12.5%, to $1,890.5 million, as compared to $1,680.3 million in the prior year period. Stormwater sales increased $199.2 million, or 15.1%, to $1,521.4 million, as compared to $1,322.2 million in the prior year period. Stormwater sales include $172.0 million of revenue from the acquisition of NDS. On an organic basis, Stormwater sales increased 2.1%, driven by growth in the core non-residential construction end market. Wastewater sales increased $11.0 million, or 3.1%, to $369.1 million as compared to $358.1 million in the prior year period.
Gross profit increased $83.3 million, or 12.4%, to $753.9 million as compared to $670.6 million in the prior year period. The increase in gross profit is primarily driven by the acquisition of NDS, as well as favorable volume, price/cost and mix of construction market sales more than offsetting unfavorable transportation costs.
Selling, general and administrative expenses increased $37.3 million, or 16.7%, to $260.4 million, as compared to $223.2 million. As a percentage of sales, selling, general and administrative expense was 13.8% as compared to 13.3% in the prior year. The increase was primarily driven by the acquisition of NDS.
Net income from continuing operations increased $9.6 million, or 3.2%, to $310.2 million as compared to $300.6 million in the prior year period. Diluted EPS from continuing operations increased $0.19, or 5.0%, to $4.02, as compared to $3.83 per share in the prior year, primarily due to the factors mentioned above.
Adjusted EBITDA (Non-GAAP) increased $79.4 million, or 14.0%, to $645.1 million, as compared to $565.7 million in the prior year period, primarily due to the factors mentioned above. As a percentage of net sales, Adjusted EBITDA was 34.1% as compared to 33.7% in the prior year period.
Balance Sheet and Liquidity
Net cash provided by operating activities was $458.2 million, as compared to $509.8 million in the prior year. Free cash flow (Non-GAAP) was $359.5 million, as compared to $398.8 million in the prior year. Net debt (total debt and finance lease obligations net of cash) was $1,619.9 million as of September 30, 2026, an increase of $71.0 million from March 31, 2026.
ADS had total liquidity of $891.8 million, comprised of cash of $153.2 million as of September 30, 2026 and $738.6 million of availability under committed credit facilities. As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s trailing-twelve-month leverage ratio was 1.6 times Adjusted EBITDA.
In the six months ended September 30, 2026, the Company repurchased 2.7 million shares of its common stock for a total cost of $375.1 million. As of September 30, 2026, approximately $677.3 million of common stock may be repurchased under the Company's existing stock repurchase authorization.
Fiscal 2027 Outlook
Based on results to date, current visibility, backlog of existing orders and business trends, the Company updated its financial targets for fiscal 2027. Net sales are now expected to be in the range of $3.300 billion to $3.400 billion and Adjusted EBITDA is now expected to be in the range of $1.000 billion to $1.030 billion. Capital expenditures are expected to be approximately $200 million.

About the Company
Advanced Drainage Systems is a leading manufacturer of innovative stormwater and onsite wastewater solutions that manage the world’s most precious resource: water. ADS, along with NDS and Infiltrator Water Technologies, provides superior stormwater
2
drainage and onsite wastewater products used across commercial, residential, infrastructure, and agricultural applications, while delivering unparalleled customer service. ADS operates the industry’s largest company-owned fleet, an expansive sales team and a vast manufacturing network. As one of the largest plastic recycling companies in North America, ADS keeps millions of pounds of plastic out of landfills each year. Founded in 1966, ADS’ water management solutions are designed to last for decades. To learn more, visit the Company’s website at www.adspipe.com.

ADVANCED DRAINAGE SYSTEMS ANNOUNCES QUARTERLY CASH DIVIDEND
HILLIARD, Ohio – (November 5, 2026) – Advanced Drainage Systems, Inc. (NYSE: WMS) (“ADS” or the “Company”), a leading provider of innovative water management solutions in the stormwater and onsite wastewater industries, today announced that its Board of Directors (the “Board”) has approved a quarterly cash dividend to its shareholders in the amount of $0.20 per share, a 11% increase over the prior year dividend amount.
Scott Barbour, President and Chief Executive Officer of Advanced Drainage Systems commented, “Today’s dividend announcement, is predicated on the strength of our balance sheet, formidable cash generation, and ongoing commitment to returning capital to shareholders. Our strong financial performance and operational excellence initiatives provide us with the confidence and financial flexibility to return excess cash to our shareholders while simultaneously continuing to strategically invest in our business.”
The quarterly cash dividend of $0.20 per share will be paid on December 15, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 1, 2026.
About the Company
Advanced Drainage Systems is a leading manufacturer of innovative stormwater and onsite wastewater solutions that manage the world’s most precious resource: water. ADS, along with NDS and Infiltrator Water Technologies, provides superior stormwater drainage and onsite wastewater products used across commercial, residential, infrastructure, and agricultural applications, while delivering unparalleled customer service. ADS operates the industry’s largest company-owned fleet, an expansive sales team and a vast manufacturing network. As one of the largest plastic recycling companies in North America, ADS keeps millions of pounds of plastic out of landfills each year. Founded in 1966, ADS’ water management solutions are designed to last for decades. To learn more, visit the Company’s website at www.adspipe.com.
