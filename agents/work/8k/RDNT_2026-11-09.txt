RadNet Reports Third Quarter Financial Results with Record Quarterly Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA(1) and Revises Upwards 2026 Financial Guidance Ranges

LOS ANGELES, California, November 8, 2026 – RadNet, Inc. (NASDAQ: RDNT), a national leader in providing high-quality, cost-effective outpatient diagnostic imaging services and a premier global developer of digital health solutions, today reported financial results for its third quarter of 2026.
Dr. Howard Berger, President and Chief Executive Officer of RadNet, commented, “We delivered record quarterly Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA(1) in the third quarter. Total Company Revenue grew 22.1% and Adjusted EBITDA(1) grew 23.8% compared with last year’s third quarter, and Adjusted EBITDA(1) margin expanded 23 basis points. These results were driven by continued initiatives to create and fill capacity at existing imaging centers, new center openings, the continued shift in the business mix towards advanced imaging, tuck-in acquisitions, improved reimbursement from commercial payors, the expansion of our health system joint ventures and 32.6% Revenue growth in the Digital Health segment.”
1
Dr. Berger continued, “Advanced imaging growth continues to be strong, both in aggregate and on a same-center basis. Compared with last year’s third quarter, aggregate advanced imaging procedural volumes increased 19.9% and same-center advanced imaging procedural volumes increased 8.9%. As a result, RadNet’s advanced imaging business mix increased 232 basis points, from 28.2% of all procedures in the third quarter of 2025 to 30.6% in the third quarter of 2026.”
“With strong performance across nearly every part of the core imaging center business, we are again raising certain guidance ranges in anticipation that full-year results will exceed both our original expectations and the revised ranges issued in conjunction with first and second quarter 2026 results. We are raising 2026 Imaging Center guidance ranges for Revenue, Adjusted EBITDA(1) and Free Cash Flow(2),” added Dr. Berger.
Dr. Berger continued, “We are particularly excited about the opportunities for growth and expansion that will be made possible as a result of the recently announced pending acquisition of ONRAD. The inbound interest from current joint venture partners and potential new partners and customers has exceeded our expectations. The strategic fit that ONRAD’s capabilities have with both RadNet’s core imaging center business and Digital Health solutions is already being recognized in the imaging marketplace, and we look forward to the opportunities that could result once the acquisition is closed.”
“RadNet’s balance sheet remains healthy and continues to provide the operating flexibility necessary to achieve further growth. We ended the third quarter with $750.6 million in cash and a Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA(1) leverage ratio of 1.66x. This liquidity enables us to keep investing in organic growth and to pursue attractive strategic acquisitions in the coming quarters,” concluded Dr. Berger.

Third Quarter Financial Results
For the third quarter of 2026, RadNet reported Total Company Revenue of $638.3 million and Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $105.0 million. Revenue increased $115.4 million (or 22.1%) and Adjusted EBITDA(1) increased $20.2 million (or 23.8%) as compared with the third quarter of 2025.
For the third quarter of 2026, RadNet reported Digital Health Revenue (inclusive of intersegment revenue) of $32.9 million and Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $3.0 million. Revenue increased $8.1 million (or 32.6%) and Adjusted EBITDA(1) decreased $0.4 million (or -11.8%) as compared with the third quarter of 2025.
Unadjusted for unusual or one-time items impacting the third quarter, Total Company Net Income for the third quarter of 2026 was $4.2 million as compared with a Total Company Net Income of $5.4 million for the third quarter of 2025. Fully diluted Net Income Per Share for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.05, compared with a fully diluted Net Income Per Share of $0.07 in the third quarter of 2025, based upon a weighted average number of diluted shares outstanding of 79.2 million shares in the third quarter of 2026 and 77.4 million shares in the third quarter of 2025.
There were a number of unusual, one-time and noteworthy items impacting the third quarter including: $1.2 million in severance expense related to cost-savings initiatives; $7.5 million gain on the contribution of imaging centers into joint ventures; $0.6 million expense related to leases for de novo facilities under construction that have yet to open their operations; $5.4 million loss on the disposal of equipment; $7.3 million intangibles amortization expense related to the Digital Health operating segment; $5.6 million of non-capitalized research and development expenses related to the DeepHealth Cloud OS and AI solutions; $2.8 million in legal settlements; $2.7 million of acquisition transaction costs; $8.3 million in non-cash change in contingent consideration; and $2.1 million of lease abandonment charges. Adjusting for the above items, Total Company Adjusted Earnings(3) was $23.8 million and diluted Adjusted Earnings Per Share(3) was $0.30 during the third quarter of 2026. This compares with Total Company Adjusted Earnings(3) of $18.8 million and diluted Adjusted Earnings Per Share(3) of $0.24 during the third quarter of 2025.
For the third quarter of 2026, as compared with the prior year’s third quarter, MRI volume increased 18.3%, CT volume increased 22.0% and PET/CT volume increased 30.4%. Overall volume, taking into account routine imaging exams, inclusive of x-ray, ultrasound, mammography and other exams, increased 10.8% over the prior year’s third quarter. On a same-center basis, including only those centers which were part of RadNet for both the third quarters of 2026 and 2025, MRI volume increased 8.2%, CT volume increased 10.2% and PET/CT volume increased 8.2%. Overall same-center volume, taking into account routine imaging exams, inclusive of x-ray, ultrasound, mammography and other exams, increased 3.2% over the prior year’s same quarter
Nine Month Financial Results
For the first nine months of 2026, RadNet reported Total Company Revenue of $1,836.6 million and Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $268.0 million. Revenue increased $344.1 million (or 23.1%) and Adjusted EBITDA(1) increased $55.4 million (or 26.1%) as compared with the first nine months of 2025.
For the first nine months of 2026, RadNet reported Digital Health Revenue (inclusive of intersegment revenue) of $94.5 million and Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $6.8 million. Revenue increased $29.7 million (or 45.8%) and Adjusted EBITDA(1) decreased $3.7 million (or -35.4%) as compared with the first nine months of 2025.
Unadjusted for one-time or unusual items, Total Company Net Loss for the first nine months of 2026 was $21.8 million as compared with a Total Company Net Loss of $18.1 million for the first nine months of 2025. Fully diluted Net Loss Per Share for the nine month period of 2026 was $(0.28), compared with a fully diluted Net Loss Per Share of $(0.24) in the nine month period of 2025, based upon a weighted average number of diluted shares outstanding of 77.6 million shares for the nine month period in 2026 and 74.7 million shares for the nine month period in 2025.

About RadNet, Inc.
RadNet, Inc. is a leading national provider of freestanding, fixed-site diagnostic imaging services in the United States based on the number of locations and annual imaging revenue. RadNet has a network of owned and/or operated outpatient imaging centers. RadNet’s imaging center markets include Arizona, California, Delaware, Florida, Idaho, Indiana, Maryland, New Jersey, New York, Texas and Virginia. In addition, RadNet provides radiology information technology and artificial intelligence solutions marketed under the DeepHealth brand, teleradiology professional services and other related products and services to customers in the diagnostic imaging industry globally. Together with contracted radiologists, and inclusive of full-time and per diem employees and technologists, RadNet has over 12,000 team members. Learn more at www.radnet.com.
