LIQUIDITY SERVICES ANNOUNCES FISCAL YEAR 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
Full Year 2026 GAAP Net Income Up 33% and Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA Up 30%
Fiscal Year 2027 GMV to Exceed $2 Billion Following Invaluable Acquisition and Continued Secular Growth
Bethesda, MD - November 19, 2026 - Liquidity Services (NASDAQ:LQDT; www.liquidityservices.com), the leading global provider of e-commerce marketplaces and software solutions powering the circular economy, today announced its financial results for its fiscal quarter and year ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the corresponding prior period:
Fiscal Year 2026 Results:
•
GMV of $1.6 billion, up 5%, Revenue of $477.9 million, in line with prior year
•
GAAP Net Income of $37.3 million, up 33%, GAAP Diluted EPS of $1.14, up 31%
•
Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA of $79.1 million, up 30%, Non-GAAP Adjusted Diluted EPS of $1.63, up 27%
“Fiscal 2026 showcased Liquidity Services’ global circular economy leadership highlighted by strong marketplace economics, increased direct profit across all of our segments and record buyer participation. Together, this drove a 33% increase in GAAP Net Income and a 30% increase in our Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA, our fourth consecutive year of Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA growth. Revenue was essentially unchanged as our business mix continued to shift toward asset-light consignment transactions, while our segment direct profit margins improved 6% on average from the prior year. Liquidity Services continues to thrive from our ability to provide exceptional service and unmatched liquidity to buyers and sellers through our marketplace platform which connects government agencies, corporations, retailers and auction houses with millions of buyers. Auction participants reached approximately 1.25 million in Q4, up 24% year over year and completed transactions increased by 36%, reflecting how greater buyer and seller participation reinforced network effects and supported higher recovery for clients.
We enter Fiscal 2027 with an outstanding, diversified marketplace platform trusted by a global network of buyers and sellers and powered by our investments in embedded AI, automation and analytics which are fueling improvements in every area of our business. Our recent acquisition of Invaluable extends our reach into fine art, antiques, and collectibles and sets us on a course to reach $2.5 Billion of annual GMV in the near term. These strategic investments will allow us to maximize value for our sellers, buyers, and shareholders,” said Bill Angrick, CEO of Liquidity Services.
Fourth Quarter Financial Highlights
Fourth Quarter Fiscal Year 2026 Results:
•
Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV) of $404.0 million, in line with the prior year quarter, with Revenue of $106.4 million, down 10% with pricing model shift to consignment
•
GAAP Net Income of $11.9 million, up 52%, GAAP Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS) of $0.36, up 50%
•
Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA of $22.3 million up 20%, Non-GAAP Adjusted Diluted EPS of $0.46, up 24%
•
Cash balance of $225.2 million1 with zero financial debt
GMV for the fiscal fourth quarter of 2026 was $404.0 million, compared with $404.5 million in the fiscal fourth quarter of 2025.
•
GMV in our CAG segment increased 2%, reflecting growth across our asset categories, including heavy equipment, in North America and a rebound in APAC, partially offset by lower volumes in EMEA where several large projects shifted timing into the first quarter of fiscal 2027.
•
GMV in our GovDeals segment increased 1%, reflecting continued seller acquisition and higher vehicle volumes, partially offset by lower average vehicle values and a shift in real estate towards listing-fee service engagements.
•
GMV in our RSCG segment decreased 4%, reflecting the planned transition towards higher-margin inventory and our response to elevated supply late in the fiscal fourth quarter, partially offset by continued growth in our consignment and direct-to-consumer programs. Selected sellers entrusted us with significant volumes under certain programs, and with that supply weighing on near-term pricing, sales were paced to reduce margin pressure while increasing inventory available for sale in future periods.
•
Consignment sales represented 86% of consolidated GMV for the fiscal fourth quarter of 2026.
Revenue for the fourth quarter of 2026 was $106.4 million, a 10% decrease from $118.1 million in the fourth fiscal quarter of 2025.
•
Revenue in our CAG segment increased 18% and segment direct profit increased 16%, outpacing GMV growth, reflecting a higher commission rate that offset costs from several large projects whose size and complexity required greater coordination and project management.
•
Revenue in our RSCG segment decreased 18%, reflecting the planned transition towards lower-volume, higher-margin purchased inventory. Segment direct profit increased 11% and segment direct profit margin expanded 910 basis points, driven by this transition and growth in consignment flows, partially offset by lower near-term recovery rates and higher transportation costs associated with storing some of the elevated late-quarter volumes.
•
Revenue in our GovDeals segment increased 3%, and segment direct profit increased 5%, reflecting pricing initiatives, the expansion of our full-service offerings and higher vehicle volumes.
•
Revenue in our Machinio & Software Solutions segments was flat, as growth in Machinio System subscriptions was offset by lower Machinio Advertising revenue, while our Software Solutions business continued to invest in its Auction.io platform.
Our marketplaces continued to demonstrate operating leverage during the fiscal fourth quarter, as the shift towards higher-margin flows continued, along with lower transaction processing costs and disciplined cost management which drove profit growth despite lower revenues from the changes in product mix.
•
GAAP Net Income of $11.9 million, or $0.36 per share, for the fiscal fourth quarter of 2026, an increase from $7.8 million, or $0.24 per share, for the same quarter last year. GAAP Net Income grew at a higher rate than Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income primarily because stock compensation expense, which is excluded from Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income, decreased $2.1 million from the fiscal fourth quarter of 2025, which included accelerated expense for performance-based awards.
•
Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income for the fiscal fourth quarter of 2026 was $15.1 million, or $0.46 per share, an increase from $11.9 million, or $0.37 per share, for the same quarter last year.
•
Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA for the fiscal fourth quarter of 2026 was $22.3 million, a $3.8 million or 20% increase from $18.5 million in the same quarter last year.

Fourth Quarter Operational Metrics
•
Registered Buyers — At the end of Q4-FY26, registered buyers, defined as the aggregate number of persons or entities who have registered on one of our marketplaces, totaled approximately 6.5 million, representing an 8% increase over the approximately 6.0 million registered buyers at the end of Q4-FY25.

•
Completed Transactions — Completed transactions, defined as the number of auctions in a given period, were approximately 365,000 in Q4-FY26, a 36% increase from the approximately 269,000 completed transactions in Q4-FY25.

First Quarter Business Outlook
Coming off a strong fiscal 2026 performance that highlighted the strength of our diversification, scale and ability to create operating leverage across market segments and economic cycles, we enter fiscal 2027 with the addition of Invaluable to our market reach and potential for leveraging our now 10 million combined buyer base and our technology and operating capabilities. The acquisition of Invaluable expands our markets and technology enabled solutions, while continuing to focus on bringing buyers and sellers together for a superior experience.
We expect Invaluable to be accretive to Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income and Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA in fiscal 2027, and to GAAP Net Income on a full-year basis, although the timing of integration-related costs and amortization of acquired intangibles may result in GAAP Net Income dilution in certain quarters. We expect integration-related costs to be modest and incurred primarily in the first half of fiscal 2027.
On a consolidated basis, including Invaluable, consignment GMV for the fiscal first quarter is expected to be in the low-nineties as a percentage of total GMV. Consolidated revenue as a percentage of GMV is expected to be in the high-teens, and total segment direct profit as a percentage of consolidated revenue is expected to be in the low-to-mid sixty percent range. These ratios can vary based on our overall business mix, including asset categories and seasonality, in any given period.
The outlook for the fiscal first quarter reflects an expected strong performance in CAG, where several large projects contracted during fiscal 2026, including projects that shifted from the fiscal fourth quarter, are scheduled for completion, supported by a growing base of recurring corporate clients and expanded sales capacity in heavy equipment. We expect GovDeals to continue to grow compared to last year, driven by seller acquisition and expanded service offerings, partially offset by softer vehicle pricing during the quarter compared to last year. Guidance reflects elevated inventory levels from selected client flows in our RSCG segment that began during the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 which will dampen recovery levels in the market temporarily. With our scale, operational capabilities and leading position in the market we are able to address the needs of our sellers and currently expect this to normalize over the next few quarters. RSCG’s fiscal first quarter GMV and revenue are expected to decline year-over-year reflecting previously planned changes in mix of inventory inflows, combined with the anticipated higher levels of supply we’ve received.

Non-GAAP Measures
To supplement our consolidated financial statements presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), we use certain non-GAAP measures of certain components of financial performance. These non-GAAP measures include earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA), Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Income (Loss) and Adjusted Diluted Earnings (Loss) per Share. These non-GAAP measures are provided to enhance investors’ overall understanding of our current financial performance and prospects for the future. We use EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA: (a) as measurements of operating performance because they assist us in comparing our operating performance on a consistent basis as they do not reflect the impact of items not directly resulting from our core operations; (b) for planning purposes, including the preparation of our internal annual operating budget; (c) to allocate resources to enhance the financial performance of our business; (d) to evaluate the effectiveness of our operational strategies; and (e) to evaluate our capacity to fund capital expenditures and expand our business. Adjusted Diluted Earnings (Loss) per Share is the result of our Adjusted Net Income (Loss) and diluted shares outstanding.
We prepare Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA by eliminating from Non-GAAP EBITDA the impact of items that we do not consider indicative of our core operating performance. You are encouraged to evaluate these adjustments and the reasons we consider them appropriate for supplemental analysis. As an analytical tool, Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA is subject to all of the limitations applicable to Non-GAAP EBITDA. Our presentation of Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as an implication that our future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items.
We believe these non-GAAP measures provide useful information to both management and investors by excluding certain expenses that may not be indicative of our core operating measures. In addition, because we have historically reported certain non-GAAP measures to investors, we believe the inclusion of non-GAAP measures provides consistency in our financial reporting. These measures should be considered in addition to financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP, but should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, GAAP results. A reconciliation of all historical non-GAAP measures included in this press release, to the most directly comparable GAAP measures, may be found in the financial tables included in this press release.
We do not quantitatively reconcile our guidance ranges for our non-GAAP measures to their most comparable GAAP measures in the Business Outlook section of this press release. The guidance ranges for our GAAP and non-GAAP financial measures reflect our assessment of potential sources of variability in our financial results and are informed by our evaluation of multiple scenarios, many of which have interactive effects across several financial statement line items. Providing guidance for individual reconciling items between our non-GAAP financial measures and the comparable GAAP measures would imply a degree of precision and certainty in those reconciling items that is not a consistent reflection of our scenario-based process to prepare our guidance ranges. To the extent that a material change affecting the individual reconciling items between the Company’s forward-looking non-GAAP and comparable GAAP financial measures is anticipated, the Company has provided qualitative commentary in the Business Outlook section of this press release for your consideration. However, as the impact of such factors cannot be predicted with a reasonable degree of certainty or precision, a quantitative reconciliation is not available without unreasonable effort.
Supplemental Operating Data
To supplement our consolidated financial statements presented in accordance with GAAP, we use certain supplemental operating data as a measure of certain components of operating performance. GMV is the total sales value of all transactions for which we earned compensation upon their completion through our marketplaces or other channels during a given period of time. We review GMV because it provides a measure of the volume of goods being sold in our marketplaces and thus the activity of those marketplaces. GMV and our other supplemental operating data, including registered buyers, auction participants and completed transactions, also provide a means to evaluate the effectiveness of investments that we have made and continue to make in the areas of seller and buyer support, value-added services, product development, sales and marketing and operations. Therefore, we believe this supplemental operating data provides useful information to both management and investors. In addition, because we have historically reported certain supplemental operating data to investors, we believe the inclusion of this supplemental operating data provides consistency in our financial reporting. This data should be considered in addition to financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP, but should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, GAAP results.
