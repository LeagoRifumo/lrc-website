TEXAS PACIFIC LAND CORPORATION ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER RESULTS
–Achieved Record Consolidated Revenue, Net Income and Free Cash Flow(1)
–Achieved Record Quarterly Oil and Gas Royalty Revenue and Production
–Earnings Call to be Held Thursday, November 5, 2026 at 9:30 am CT
DALLAS, TX (November 4, 2026) – Texas Pacific Land Corporation (NYSE: TPL) (the “Company,” “TPL,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), one of the largest land and royalty owners in the State of Texas with surface and royalty ownership that provides revenue opportunities through the support of energy production and related industries, today announced its financial and operating results for the third quarter of 2026.
“Our third quarter results were the strongest in TPL’s history with new records set across our business,” said Tyler Glover, Chief Executive Officer of the Company. “TPL again generated record revenue, net income, and free cash flow. Our royalty acreage benefited from robust development activity, elevated oil prices, and improved gas realizations, driving record oil and gas royalty revenue. Pipeline easement revenue was strong as operators expanded development of midstream infrastructure. The continued trend of growing Permian produced water supported record produced water royalty volumes and revenues. Finally, our efforts towards data centers, power generation, and produced water desalination and beneficial reuse continue to progress, and we are steadfastly working to advance those into meaningful commercial outcomes.”

Financial Results for the Third Quarter of 2026 - Sequential
The Company reported net income of $167.4 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to net income of $153.9 million for the second quarter of 2026.
Total revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $273.4 million compared to $246.1 million for the second quarter of 2026. The increase in total revenues was primarily due to a $21.7 million increase in oil and gas royalty revenue, a $12.5 million increase in easements and other surface-related income, and a $4.3 million increase in produced water royalties, partially offset by an $11.3 million decrease in water sales compared to the second quarter of 2026. For oil and gas royalty revenue, the Company’s average realized price was $44.97 per Boe in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $42.17 per Boe in the second quarter of 2026, and the Company’s share of production was 42.3 thousand Boe per day for the third quarter of 2026 compared to 39.7 thousand Boe per day for the second quarter of 2026. Water sales decreased in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the second quarter of 2026 due to a decrease in average realized pricing, partially offset by an increase in water sales volumes. TPL’s revenue streams are directly impacted by commodity prices and development and operating decisions made by its customers.
Total operating expenses were $65.6 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $54.2 million for the second quarter of 2026. The increase in operating expenses was principally related to a $6.2 million increase in depreciation, depletion and amortization expense, a $2.6 million increase in general and administrative expenses, and a $2.2 million increase in water service-related expenses during the third quarter of 2026 compared to the second quarter of 2026.

Financial Results for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 - Year Over Year
The Company reported net income of $464.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to net income of $358.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
Total revenues for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $756.3 million compared to $586.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in total revenues was primarily due to a $116.1 million increase in oil and gas royalty revenue, a $21.3 million increase in produced water royalties, a $20.2 million increase in land sales, a $6.1 million increase in water sales, and a $6.0 million increase in easements and other surface-related income during the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the same period of 2025. The Company’s share of oil and gas production was 39.7 thousand Boe per day for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to 33.6 thousand Boe per day for the same period of 2025, and the Company’s average realized price was $41.60 per Boe for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $36.01 per Boe for the same period of 2025. Produced water royalties increased due to increased produced water volumes, and water sales increased primarily due to increased water sales volumes. TPL’s revenue streams are directly impacted by commodity prices and development and operating decisions made by its customers.
Total operating expenses were $174.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $143.7 million for the same period of 2025. The increase in operating expenses was principally related to a $12.9 million increase in depreciation, depletion and amortization, a $9.9 million increase in general and administrative expenses, a $4.7 million increase in salaries and related employee expenses, and a $3.7 million increase in water service-related expenses during the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the same period of 2025.

About Texas Pacific Land Corporation
Texas Pacific Land Corporation is one of the largest land and royalty owners in the State of Texas, with the majority of its ownership concentrated in the Permian Basin. The Company is not an oil and gas producer, but its land and royalty ownership provides revenue opportunities throughout the life cycle of a well. These revenue opportunities include providing pipeline, utility, wellbore, and commercial easements/leases; monetizing caliche and materials resources; supplying source and recycled/treated produced water for well completions; allowing access to subsurface pore space; granting produced water crossing rights; and monetizing oil and gas royalty and mineral interests.

EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, and Free Cash Flow
EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measurement of earnings before interest expense, taxes, depreciation, depletion and amortization. The purpose of presenting EBITDA is to highlight earnings without finance, taxes, and depreciation, depletion and amortization expense, and its use is limited to specialized analysis.
The purpose of presenting Adjusted EBITDA is to highlight earnings without non-cash activity such as share-based compensation and other non-recurring or unusual items, if applicable. Additionally, Adjusted EBITDA is a metric used by the compensation committee of our Board to evaluate the Company’s performance in determining the short-term and long-term incentive compensation of our executive officers on an annual basis. We calculate Adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA plus employee share-based compensation, less land sale with financing arrangement and pension curtailment and settlement gain, as applicable to the periods presented.
The purpose of presenting free cash flow is to provide investors a metric to measure the funds available for investing in future acquisitions and returning capital to our stockholders through dividends and share repurchases after current income tax expense and purchases of fixed assets. Additionally, free cash flow is a metric used by the compensation committee of our Board to evaluate the Company’s performance in determining the short-term and long-term incentive compensation of our executive officers. To calculate free cash flow, net income is adjusted by adding back income tax expense, depreciation, depletion and amortization and employee share-based compensation, less current income tax expenses, land sale with financing arrangement, purchases of fixed assets and pension curtailment and settlement gain, as applicable to the periods presented.
We have presented EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, and free cash flow because we believe that these metrics are useful supplements to net income in analyzing the Company’s operating performance, ability to fund future acquisitions, ability to return capital to our stockholders and explaining how our executive officers are compensated. Our definitions of EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, and free cash flow may differ from computations of similarly titled measures of other companies.
