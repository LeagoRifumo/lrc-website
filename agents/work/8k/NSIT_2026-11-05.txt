INSIGHT ENTERPRISES, INC. REPORTS
THIRD QUARTER RESULTS
Continued strong financial performance and sustained broad-based growth
Insight raises full-year 2026 outlook
CHANDLER, AZ – November 5, 2026 – Insight Enterprises, Inc. (NASDAQ: NSIT) (the “Company”) today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Highlights include:
•Consolidated net sales increased 22% year over year
•Gross profit increased 20% year over year to $523.2 million and gross margin contracted 30 basis points to 21.4%
•Consolidated net earnings decreased 57% year to year to $22.2 million
•Adjusted consolidated net earnings increased 28% year over year to $107.3 million
•Adjusted earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization (“EBITDA”) increased 22% year over year to $177.4 million
•Diluted earnings per share of $0.74 decreased 54% year to year
•Adjusted diluted earnings per share of $3.59 increased 34% year over year
•Cash flows provided by operating activities were $128.4 million
In the third quarter of 2026, net sales increased 22%, year over year, to $2.4 billion, and gross profit increased 20%, year over year, to $523.2 million. Gross margin contracted 30 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025 to 21.4%. Selling and administrative expenses increased 29%, year over year, while Adjusted selling and administrative expenses increased 19%, year over year. The increase in selling and administrative expenses primarily reflects higher earnout valuation adjustments, increased variable compensation expense associated with stronger performance of the business, and increased expenses associated with acquisitions completed in the fourth quarter of 2025, which were not present in the comparable prior-year period. Earnings from operations of $68.7 million, or 2.8% of net sales, decreased 26% compared to $93.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease in earnings from operations was primarily attributable to higher earnout valuation adjustments compared to the prior-year period, as well as higher severance and restructuring costs in the current period, including costs associated with the Company’s voluntary separation program initiated in the third quarter of 2026. Adjusted earnings from operations of $166.9 million, or 6.8% of net sales, increased 23% year over year compared to $135.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. Consolidated net earnings were $22.2 million, or 0.9% of net sales, in the third quarter of 2026, down 57% year to year compared to $50.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted consolidated net earnings were $107.3 million, or 4.4% of net sales, in the third quarter of 2026, up 28% year over year compared to $84.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. Diluted earnings per share for the quarter was $0.74, down 54% year to year, and Adjusted diluted earnings per share was $3.59, up 34% year over year.
“We delivered another strong quarter, with broad-based momentum across our business. Gross profit increased 20%, adjusted earnings from operations grew 23%, and adjusted diluted earnings per share increased 34%,” stated Jack Azagury, President and Chief Executive Officer. “We continue to see strong performance in our strategic growth priorities, with infrastructure hardware gross profit increasing more than 50% and more than 20% gross profit growth across Cloud and Insight Core Services,” Azagury added.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
Results for the Quarter:
•Consolidated net sales for the third quarter of 2026 of $2.4 billion increased 22%, year over year, when compared to the third quarter of 2025. Product net sales increased 22%, year over year, and services net sales increased 20%, year over year. Software product net sales decreased 3%, year to year, while hardware product net sales increased 32%, year over year.
•Net sales in North America increased 22%, year over year, to $2.0 billion;
◦Product net sales increased 24%, year over year, to $1.6 billion;
◦Services net sales increased 15%, year over year, to $364.7 million;
•Net sales in EMEA increased 6%, year over year, to $338.0 million; and
•Net sales in APAC increased 104%, year over year, to $120.0 million.
•Excluding the effects of fluctuating foreign currency exchange rates, consolidated net sales increased 22%, year over year, with increases in net sales in APAC, North America and EMEA of 93%, 22% and 7%, respectively, year over year.
•Consolidated gross profit increased 20% compared to the third quarter of 2025 to $523.2 million, with consolidated gross margin contracting 30 basis points to 21.4% of net sales. Product gross profit increased 17%, year over year, and services gross profit increased 23%, year over year. Cloud gross profit increased 26%, year over year, and Insight Core services gross profit increased 21%, year over year. By segment, gross profit:
•Increased 15% in North America, year over year, to $393.6 million (19.8% gross margin);
•Increased 22% in EMEA, year over year, to $90.5 million (26.8% gross margin); and
•Increased 121% in APAC, year over year, to $39.0 million (32.5% gross margin).
•Excluding the effects of fluctuating foreign currency exchange rates, consolidated gross profit increased 20%, year over year, with gross profit growth in APAC, EMEA and North America of 109%, 23% and 15%, respectively, year over year.
•Consolidated earnings from operations decreased 26% compared to the third quarter of 2025 to $68.7 million, or 2.8% of net sales. By segment, earnings from operations:
•Increased 1% in North America, year over year, to $84.1 million, or 4.2% of net sales;
•Increased 53% in EMEA, year over year, to $6.9 million, or 2.0% of net sales; and
•Decreased by more than 100% in APAC, year to year, to a loss of $22.3 million, or (18.6)% of net sales.
•Excluding the effects of fluctuating foreign currency exchange rates, consolidated earnings from operations decreased 26%, year to year, with increases in earnings from operations in EMEA and North America of 55% and 1%, respectively, year over year, partially offset by a decrease in APAC of more than 100%, year to year.
•Adjusted earnings from operations increased 23% compared to the third quarter of 2025 to $166.9 million, or 6.8% of net sales. By segment, Adjusted earnings from operations:
•Increased 13% in North America, year over year, to $132.0 million, or 6.7% of net sales;
•Increased 52% in EMEA, year over year, to $18.4 million, or 5.5% of net sales; and
•Increased 156% in APAC, year over year, to $16.5 million, or 13.7% of net sales.
•Excluding the effects of fluctuating foreign currency exchange rates, Adjusted consolidated earnings from operations increased 23%, with increases in Adjusted earnings from operations in APAC, EMEA and North America of 148%, 53% and 13%, respectively, year over year.
•Consolidated net earnings and diluted earnings per share for the third quarter of 2026 were $22.2 million and $0.74, respectively, at an effective tax rate of 49.9%.
•Adjusted consolidated net earnings and Adjusted diluted earnings per share for the third quarter of 2026 were $107.3 million and $3.59, respectively. Excluding the effects of fluctuating foreign currency exchange rates, Adjusted diluted earnings per share increased 36%, year over year.

GUIDANCE
For the full year 2026, we are raising our gross profit growth expectations to be between 12.5% to 14.0% and expect gross margin to be approximately 21.5%. We now expect our Adjusted diluted earnings per share to be between $13.55 and $13.75. This represents approximately 27% growth at the midpoint of $13.65 compared to our full year 2025 Adjusted diluted earnings per share of $10.75.
This outlook assumes:
•interest and other expenses of approximately $95 million;
•an effective tax rate of approximately 25.5% for the full year;
•capital expenditures between approximately $20 million and $30 million;
•an average share count for the full year of approximately 30.0 million shares.
This outlook excludes acquisition-related intangibles amortization expense of approximately $83.4 million, excludes non-cash stock-based compensation expense and assumes no acquisition or integration related expenses, transformation or severance and restructuring expenses, net, no significant change in our debt instruments, and no significant change in the macroeconomic environment, whether due to tariffs or otherwise. Due to the inherent difficulty of forecasting some of these types of expenses, which impact net earnings, diluted earnings per share and selling and administrative expenses, the Company is unable to reasonably estimate the impact of such expenses, if any, to net earnings, diluted earnings per share and selling and administrative expenses. Accordingly, the Company is unable to provide a reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP Adjusted diluted earnings per share for the full year 2026 forecast.
