Carlyle Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Washington, D.C. and New York, NY – November 5, 2026 – The Carlyle Group Inc. (NASDAQ: CG) today
reported its unaudited results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The full detailed presentation of
Carlyle’s third quarter 2026 results can be viewed at ir.carlyle.com.
U.S. GAAP results for Q3 2026 included loss before provision for income taxes of $77 million and a margin on loss
before provision for income taxes of 23.0%.
Carlyle Chief Executive Officer Harvey M. Schwartz said, “Our third quarter results reflect the strength of Carlyle’s
diversified global platform and the significant momentum we continue to build across the firm. We delivered one of
our strongest quarters of Distributable Earnings per share on record, while distinguishing ourselves as an outlier in
returning capital to our investors, approaching $100 billion in distributions since the beginning of 2024. With near-
record levels of Pending Fee-earning AUM and the early stages of a powerful fundraising super-cycle, we are
exceptionally well positioned to accelerate growth in the years ahead.”
Dividend
The Board of Directors has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.35 per common share to holders of record at the
close of business on November 16, 2026, payable on November 23, 2026.
Conference Call
Carlyle will host a conference call at 8:30 a.m. EST on Thursday, November 5, 2026, to discuss its third quarter
2026 financial results. The conference call will be available via public webcast from the Events & Presentations
section of ir.carlyle.com and a replay will also be available on our website soon after the call’s completion.
About Carlyle
Carlyle (NASDAQ: CG) is a global investment firm with deep industry expertise that deploys private capital across
three business segments: Global Private Equity, Global Credit, and Carlyle AlpInvest. With $479 billion of assets
under management as of September 30, 2026, Carlyle’s purpose is to connect people, ideas, and capital to fuel
growth for companies and performance for investors. Carlyle employs more than 2,600 people in 28 offices across
four continents. Further information is available at www.carlyle.com. Follow Carlyle on X @OneCarlyle and
LinkedIn at The Carlyle Group.
