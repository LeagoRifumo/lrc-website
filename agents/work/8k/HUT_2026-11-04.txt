Hut 8 Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Disciplined execution broadens Hut 8’s origination platform, advances contracted projects toward delivery, and expands committed, non-dilutive funding capacity
Hut 8 fully commercializes Beacon Point AI data center campus and advances 1,300 MW of energy capacity to exclusivity
Company closes $1.07 billion revolving credit facility, returns $250.0 million to shareholders through share repurchases, and increases total repurchase authorization to $1.0 billion subsequent to quarter-end
Earnings Release Highlights
- | Fully commercialized the Beacon Point campus, signing a second 352 MW IT lease with the Phase 1 tenant (the “Phase 2 lease”) bringing expected aggregate base-term contract value across the Company’s AI data center portfolio to approximately $26.6 billion.
- | Broadened the scope of Hut 8’s power-first origination model across geographies and channels, increasing Energy Capacity Under Exclusivity by 1,300 MW to 3,180 MW across multiple regions, and entered into definitive agreements to acquire two energized sites in Texas with up to 230 MW of utility capacity at full energization, subject to final power approvals and infrastructure development.
- | Increased utility capacity under construction to 1,330 MW across the Company’s AI data center portfolio following the execution of the Beacon Point Phase 2 lease, with initial data hall delivery targeted for Q2 2027 at River Bend, Q3 2027 at Beacon Point Phase 1, and Q2 2028 at Beacon Point Phase 2.
- | Advanced the Company’s capital strategy, closing a $1.07 billion senior secured revolving credit facility that provides low-cost, non-dilutive capital, increases financing flexibility, and supports the Company’s liquidity as it pursues an investment-grade corporate credit profile, and repurchasing $74.7 million of shares during the quarter and a further $175.3 million subsequent to quarter-end, funded with proceeds from Bitcoin sales.

MIAMI, November 4, 2026 – Hut 8 Corp. (Nasdaq, TSX: HUT) (“Hut 8” or the “Company”), an energy infrastructure platform integrating power, digital infrastructure, and compute at scale to fuel next-generation, energy-intensive technologies, today reported its financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
Asher Genoot, CEO of Hut 8, said: “In the third quarter, we executed our power-first strategy at greater scale, broadening our origination program across geographies, entering into definitive agreements to acquire two energized sites in Texas, fully commercializing our Beacon Point campus, advancing the construction of our two AI data center campuses, and expanding our funding capacity in a manner designed to minimize recourse to the parent and dilution to shareholders.
“Today, our pipeline is more advanced, with more than a gigawatt of additional capacity under exclusivity, and more diversified, with less concentration in any single region. Construction at both River Bend and Beacon Point remains on track to meet our delivery targets, and we expect these projects to be funded entirely at the project level, without requiring equity issuance or recourse debt at the parent. Partner conviction has deepened, with our tenant at Beacon Point doubling its contracted capacity at the campus, and with a syndicate of lenders committing more than $1 billion of credit for future growth. And our shareholders now own a larger share of a growing platform following share repurchases funded with Bitcoin from our balance sheet.
“We view each project we undertake as an obligation to the tenants, lenders, communities, and shareholders to whom we have committed. The first of those obligations comes due with the delivery of the first data hall at River Bend in the second quarter of 2027, and meeting each of them across our development pipeline is how we intend to build an enduring, generational business at the intersection of energy and technology.”
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
Power
- | Generated $1.3 million in third quarter revenue from Managed Services.

- | Advanced 500 MW of utility capacity at Beacon Point into Energy Capacity Under Construction following execution of the Phase 2 lease, increasing total Energy Capacity Under Construction to 1,330 MW, comprising 330 MW at the River Bend campus and 1,000 MW at the Beacon Point campus.

- | Entered into agreements to acquire two energized data center sites in West Texas with up to 230 MW of utility capacity at full energization, subject to final power approvals and infrastructure development, from affiliates of Poolin Technology Pte. Ltd. through a Chapter 11 sale process, for consideration of up to $180.0 million. Closing is expected as early as Q4 2026.

Digital Infrastructure
- | Generated $1.4 million in third quarter revenue from Colocation services. The King Mountain Joint Venture, in which the Company holds a 50% interest, generated $26.9 million of Colocation revenue, including reimbursements, in the third quarter. The Company’s $13.5 million share of that revenue is recognized in the “Equity in earnings of unconsolidated joint venture” line item.

- | Commercialized the Company’s first gigawatt-scale AI data center campus through the execution of the Beacon Point Phase 2 lease, representing approximately $9.8 billion in expected base-term contract value and approximately $655.0 million of expected average annual NOI on a triple-net, take-or-pay basis and bringing expected base-term contract value across the campus to approximately $19.6 billion and expected average annual NOI to approximately $1.3 billion.

- | Advanced the buildout of River Bend, with initial data hall delivery targeted for Q2 2027. Progress during the quarter included completion of structural steel and building foundations across all four data halls and initial energization of the campus substation.

- | Advanced the buildout of Beacon Point, with initial data hall delivery targeted for Q3 2027 at Phase 1 and Q2 2028 at Phase 2. Progress during the quarter included commencement of construction of the campus substation, foundation work and underground utility work at Phase 1, and civil design for Phase 2.

Compute
- | Generated $68.4 million in third quarter revenue from ASIC Compute, AI Cloud, and Traditional Cloud solutions.

- | Completed the sale of the Company’s Traditional Cloud business as part of its planned exit from businesses no longer central to its strategy. As part of the transaction, the buyer entered into a multi-year colocation agreement with the Company, under which the cloud infrastructure remains hosted at the Company’s Canadian data centers.

Capital Strategy and Balance Sheet
- | Maintained a strong liquidity position, supported by approximately $6.6 billion in unrestricted cash, restricted cash and cash equivalents, and restricted short-term investments, in addition to Bitcoin holdings of $697.4 million attributable to Hut 8 and $702.9 million attributable to American Bitcoin, as of September 30, 2026.

- | Closed a four-year, $1.07 billion senior secured revolving credit facility that provides low-cost, non-dilutive bank liquidity at the parent level and increases financing flexibility. No borrowings were outstanding under

the facility as of September 30, 2026. Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company issued $3.9 million of letters of credit under the facility.
- | Repurchased and retired 953,802 shares of common stock during the quarter for an aggregate purchase price of $74.7 million under the Company's $250.0 million stock repurchase program, funded with net proceeds of $74.8 million from the sale of approximately 954 Bitcoin. Subsequent to quarter-end, repurchased and retired an additional 2,384,677 shares for an aggregate purchase price of $175.3 million, funded with net proceeds of $175.4 million from the sale of approximately 2,148 Bitcoin, bringing cumulative repurchases under the program to 3,338,479 shares for approximately $250.0 million.

- | Increased, subsequent to quarter-end, the Company's stock repurchase authorization to $1.0 billion.

- | Simplified the Company’s debt profile, terminating the undrawn $200.0 million Two Prime credit facility and, subsequent to quarter-end, repaying in full the $26.5 million TZRC Secured Promissory Note.

- | Advanced financing plans for Beacon Point Phase 2, evaluating a range of structures consistent with the Company's disciplined approach to funding campus development.

Select Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $71.1 million, compared to $83.5 million in the prior-year period, and consisted of $1.3 million in Power revenue, $1.4 million in Digital Infrastructure revenue, and $68.4 million in Compute revenue.
Net income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $189.5 million, compared to net income of $50.6 million in the prior-year period. Net income for the period included $413.8 million of primarily unrealized gains on digital assets, compared to $76.6 million of primarily unrealized gains on digital assets in the prior-year period.
Adjusted EBITDA for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $33.0 million, compared to $30.5 million in the prior-year period. Beginning with the three months ended June 30, 2026, the Company revised
its definition of Adjusted EBITDA to exclude mark-to-market gains and losses on digital assets attributable to Hut 8 Corp. and presents Adjusted EBITDA inclusive of digital assets mark-to-market as a separate measure. Prior-period amounts have been recast to conform to the current presentation. Adjusted EBITDA inclusive of digital assets mark-to-market for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $350.3 million, compared to $109.0 million in the prior-year period. Stock Repurchase Program
On December 4, 2024, as part of its capital management strategy, the Company launched a $250.0 million stock repurchase program (the “Stock Repurchase Program”) with respect to its common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the “Common Stock”) with no expiration date. On November 2, 2026, the Company’s board of directors approved an additional $750.0 million under the Stock Repurchase Program, increasing the total authorization to $1.0 billion. All other terms of the program remain unchanged.
Under applicable Canadian securities regulations, the number of shares that the Company can repurchase at any time may not exceed 5.0% of the Company’s issued and outstanding Common Stock during the preceding 12 month period. As of November 2, 2026, the maximum number of shares of Common Stock available to repurchase under this limit (before reducing for any prior repurchases within 12 months) is 6,001,877. The Company expects that any repurchases will be made through the facilities of Nasdaq at prevailing market prices, in accordance with applicable securities laws.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to its results determined in accordance with GAAP, the Company relies on Adjusted EBITDA, inclusive of digital assets mark-to-market, and Adjusted EBITDA, which are non-GAAP financial measures, to evaluate the business, measure the Company’s performance, and make strategic decisions.
Adjusted EBITDA, Inclusive of Digital Assets Mark-to-Market
The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA, inclusive of digital assets mark-to-market, as net income or loss adjusted for interest expense, interest income, income tax provision or benefit, depreciation and amortization, our share of unconsolidated joint venture depreciation and amortization, foreign exchange gain or loss, gain or loss on the sale of property, equipment, and other assets, loss or gain on derivatives and other financial liabilities, gain on other investments, non-recurring transactions, income or loss attributable to non-controlling interest, and stock-based compensation expense.
Adjusted EBITDA
The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as Adjusted EBITDA, inclusive of digital assets mark-to-market, further adjusted to exclude gain or loss on digital assets attributable to Hut 8 Corp., which removes the effect of mark-to-market fluctuations of digital assets held on the Company’s balance sheet. Periodic appreciation or depreciation in the fair value of such digital assets is primarily driven by changes in market prices and does not reflect the results from the Company’s core operations.
Expected Net Operating Income (NOI) Contribution
The Company defines expected net operating income (NOI) contribution as expected lease revenue attributable to a particular lease, less any non-reimbursable operating expenses attributable to the leased property.
How the Company Uses These Measures
The Company’s board of directors and management team use Adjusted EBITDA, inclusive of digital assets mark-to-market, and Adjusted EBITDA to assess the Company’s financial performance, as these measures allow for the comparison of operating performance on a consistent basis across periods by removing the effects of the Company’s capital structure (such as varying levels of interest expense and income), asset base (such as depreciation and amortization), and other items (such as non-recurring transactions mentioned above). The Company excludes gains or losses on the sale of assets and gains or losses on other investments because these items arise from transactions or valuation changes outside the Company’s core operating activities and, in management’s view, excluding them facilitates comparisons of the Company’s operating performance across periods. Adjusted EBITDA further excludes the impact of changes in the fair value of the Company’s digital asset holdings, which may otherwise affect the comparability of the Company’s financial results across periods. Investors are encouraged to evaluate each adjustment and the reasons the Company’s board of directors and management believe these measures provide useful supplemental information.
Limitations
Net income (loss) is the GAAP measure most directly comparable to Adjusted EBITDA, inclusive of digital assets mark-to-market, and Adjusted EBITDA. In evaluating these measures, you should be aware that the Company may incur expenses in the future that are the same as, or similar to, certain adjustments reflected in the calculation of these measures. Accordingly, the presentation of these measures should not be construed as an inference that the Company’s future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items.
Operating income is the GAAP measure most directly comparable to expected NOI contribution. In evaluating this measure, you should be aware that the Company may incur non-reimbursable lease operating expenses that are not currently known or quantifiable. Accordingly, the Company's presentation of expected NOI contribution should not be construed as an inference that the Company’s future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items. Expected NOI contribution also excludes the impact of selling, general and administrative expenses and depreciation and amortization, each of which has a real economic effect and could materially impact the Company's consolidated financial results. No reconciliation of expected NOI contribution to its most directly comparable GAAP measure is included in this press release because the Company is unable to quantify certain amounts that would be required to be included in operating income without unreasonable effort, and any such quantification would imply a degree of precision that could be confusing or misleading to investors.
The Company may modify the calculation or presentation of these measures in the future, and any such modification could be material. These measures have important limitations as analytical tools and should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for analysis of the Company’s results as reported in accordance with GAAP. Because other companies, including companies in the Company’s industry, may calculate similarly titled measures differently, the Company’s non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to those reported by other companies, which limits their usefulness for comparative purposes.
