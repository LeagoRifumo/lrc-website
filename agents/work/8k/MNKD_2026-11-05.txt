MannKind Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

Rapid adoption of Furoscix ReadyFlow, making up approximately 75% of the current weekly Furoscix shipments

Afrezza® pediatric launch accounts for an approximately 80% increase in average monthly new-to-brand prescriptions in Q3 vs. Q2 2026

o

Conference call and webcast today at 4:30 p.m. ET
DANBURY, Conn. and WESTLAKE VILLAGE, Calif., November 5, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- MannKind Corporation (Nasdaq: MNKD)
a biopharmaceutical company dedicated to transforming chronic disease care through innovative, patient-centric solutions for cardiometabolic and orphan lung diseases, today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026, and provided a business update.
“The third quarter marked an important transition for MannKind as we shifted from delivering on three major catalysts to executing against them,” said Michael Castagna, Chief Executive Officer of MannKind. “We are focused on scaling two commercial launches while supporting three programs in IPF. Furoscix ReadyFlow is off to an exceptionally strong start, and we believe Furoscix will generate more revenue in the fourth quarter than it did in the first half of 2026. ReadyFlow significantly expands the opportunity ahead and reinforces our conviction that Furoscix can be transformational for MannKind and the patients we serve.”
Business Update
Commercial Products
Furoscix
•
Furoscix® generated $22.4 million in net revenue for the third quarter of 2026, with Furoscix ReadyFlow shipments commencing in mid-September
•
Approximately 75% of Furoscix shipments transitioned to ReadyFlow by late October, demonstrating rapid adoption following launch
•
Achieved record monthly prescribers and prescriptions in September, reflecting continued growth in Furoscix utilization
Afrezza
•
Afrezza® generated $19.3 million in net sales for the third quarter of 2026
•
Total prescriptions increased approximately 17% compared with Q2 2026, driven by strong early uptake following the pediatric launch
•
All 60 priority pediatric institutions have prescribed Afrezza, with approximately 2,000 pediatric patients treated since launch
Development
Nintedanib DPI (MNKD-201)
•
Positive Phase 1b INFLO-1 results demonstrated tolerability of nintedanib DPI, validating the Technosphere® DPI platform in IPF
•
Advancing MNKD-201 into global Phase 2 trial
Ralinepag DPI (MNKD-1501)
•
Formulation work completed; United Therapeutics plans to pursue clinical development
•
Anticipate earning $15 million in milestone payments in 2027
Inhaled GLP-1 Program
•
Entered into a licensing and collaboration agreement with Rose Pharma to develop an inhaled rapid-acting, short-duration GLP-1 for weight management
Corporate Update
•
Cash, cash equivalents and investments as of September 30, 2026, totaled $96.6 million
•
Closed $50 million private placement on July 24, 2026; proceeds funded the $45 million CVR payment triggered by the FDA approval of Furoscix ReadyFlow

Total revenues for the third quarter of 2026 increased compared to the same period in the prior year primarily due to the addition of Furoscix to our product portfolio following the October 2025 acquisition of scPharma, as well as an increase in revenue from royalties. The increase in royalties was due to UT’s increase in net revenue from sales of Tyvaso DPI. The increase in total revenue was partially offset by a decrease in revenue from collaborations and services which was primarily attributable to decreased product sold to UT. UT provides notice of their subsequent year purchase quantity on or prior to October 15th of each year and quarterly revenue fluctuations reflect delivery timing rather than changes in annual order volume.
Operating Expenses and Other Financial Highlights

Cost of goods sold – commercial, excluding amortization of acquired intangible assets, was $11.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $4.5 million for the same period in 2025.

The increase is primarily attributable to the inclusion of Furoscix into our product portfolio following the acquisition of scPharma in October 2025. The Furoscix on-body infusor has a lower gross margin percentage than Afrezza which resulted in a decrease in gross margin in the current period.

Research and development expenses were $14.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $14.1 million for the same period in 2025, an increase of 2%.

The increase was primarily attributable to the development of Furoscix ReadyFlow, higher personnel costs following the acquisition of scPharma, and increased clinical development costs for MNKD-201. These increases were partially offset by lower clinical development expenses resulting from the discontinuation of the ICoN-1 clinical study for MNKD-101 and the completion of the Afrezza pediatric study.

Selling, general and administrative expenses were $61.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $29.1 million for the same period in 2025, an increase of 111%.

The increase was primarily attributable to costs associated with the promotion and support of Furoscix, which was acquired from scPharma in October 2025, expansion of our field-based teams, and launch activities related to Furoscix ReadyFlow and the pediatric indication for Afrezza.

About MannKind
MannKind Corporation (Nasdaq: MNKD) is a biopharmaceutical company dedicated to transforming chronic disease care through innovative, patient-centric solutions. Focused on cardiometabolic and orphan lung diseases, we develop and commercialize treatments that address serious unmet medical needs, including diabetes, pulmonary hypertension, and fluid overload in heart failure and chronic kidney disease.
With deep expertise in drug-device combinations, MannKind aims to deliver therapies designed to fit seamlessly into daily life.
