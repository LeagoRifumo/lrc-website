Columbia Financial, Inc. Announces
Q3 2026 Financial Results
and
Quarterly Cash Dividend
Fair Lawn, New Jersey (October 29, 2026): Columbia Financial, Inc. (the “Company”) (NASDAQ: CLBK), the holding company for Columbia Bank ("Columbia"), reported net income of $5.2 million, or $0.02 per basic and diluted share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $14.9 million, or $0.07 per basic and diluted share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported net income of $32.8 million, or $0.14 per basic and diluted share, as compared to $36.1 million, or $0.16 per basic and diluted share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported core net income (a non-GAAP financial measure) of $40.6 million, or $0.16 per basic and diluted share, as compared to $15.4 million, or $0.07 per basic and diluted share for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported core net income of $70.6 million, or $0.30 per basic and diluted share, as compared to $37.1 million, or $0.17 per basic and diluted share for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. See the "Reconciliation of GAAP to Non-GAAP Financial Measures" included in this release, detailing non-core items, related core net income, per share amounts and select performance ratios.
Mr. Thomas J. Kemly, President and Chief Executive Officer commented: “During the quarter, our team successfully completed the second-step conversion offering and simultaneous acquisition of Northfield Bancorp, Inc. (“Northfield”), and executed a series of transactions to improve earnings, reduce interest rate and credit risk on the balance sheet by selling securities and delinquent, nonperforming and criticized and classified assets, as well as, materially reducing our exposure to New York City rent regulated multifamily loans.”
Mr. Kemly noted, “In addition, we continue to attract experienced commercial bankers and other professionals to grow our Company and deliver premier lending, deposit and treasury solutions to deploy our capital and to capture current market share in the markets we serve. We continue to believe that our approach to community relationship banking, and our disciplined focus on capital deployment will drive strong earnings growth and stockholder returns."
Financial Highlights
•Total assets increased $5.4 billion to $17.6 billion at September 30, 2026 from $12.2 billion at June 30, 2026, primarily due to the acquisition of Northfield, and the second-step conversion stock offering.
•Return on average assets for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 decreased to 0.13% as compared to 0.55% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, while core return on average assets for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 increased to 0.97% as compared to 0.56% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
•Net interest margin of 2.96% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 increased by 67 basis points compared to 2.29% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, and increased 52 basis points compared to 2.44% for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
•Total gross loans increased $3.1 billion, or 37.2%, for September 30, 2026, as compared to June 30, 2026, due primarily to $3.2 billion in loans acquired in the Northfield acquisition. Commercial real estate, construction and commercial business loans, gross, increased $899.8 million, or 23.0% for September 30, 2026, as compared to June 30, 2026, primarily due to the Northfield acquisition.
•Total deposits increased $3.1 billion, or 32.2%, for September 30, 2026, as compared to June 30, 2026, mainly due to $3.8 billion in deposits acquired in the Northfield acquisition, partially offset by $970.1 million in stock subscription proceeds that were transferred to stockholders' equity. Non-interest-bearing deposits increased $771.6 million, or 49.6%, as compared to June 30, 2026, mainly due to $747.9 million in deposits acquired in the Northfield acquisition.
•The Company's Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.05 per share to be paid on November 25, 2026, to stockholders of record as of November 10, 2026.
Significant Transactions Impacting the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026
The Company's growth, financial condition and earnings for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were materially impacted by a series of transactions including:
•Completion of its second-step conversion stock offering and raising approximately $1.6 billion of common stockholders' equity net of underwriting and transactional expenses. Total shares outstanding following completion of the stock offering and Northfield acquisition were 269,539,984.
•The acquisition of Northfield on July 20, 2026, added approximately $5.2 billion in total assets, $3.2 billion in loans receivable and $202.3 million in loans held-for-sale, $1.2 billion in securities, $646.4 million in borrowings, and $3.8 billion in deposits, inclusive of purchase accounting adjustments. The Company recorded a bargain purchase gain of $25.7 million, net of tax, and a core deposit intangible $76.2 million during the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Merger-related expenses recognized for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $30.6 million. The Company currently plans to complete the core system conversion during the first quarter of 2027.
•Purchase of $1.1 billion of debt securities designated as both available for sale and held to maturity, with a weighted average yield of 4.80% and a duration of approximately 2.0 years. Approximately one-third of these debt securities purchased have coupons that adjust monthly. Furthermore, the Company repaid and prepaid $353.5 million of borrowings with a weighted average cost of 3.97%.
•The sale of $650.6 million of Northfield debt securities available for sale resulting in no gain or loss, as the securities were marked-to-market as part of purchase accounting adjustments. The proceeds from the sales were reinvested in debt securities with a similar yield, but with improved cash flow stability in various rate scenarios.
•The sale of $254.7 million of debt securities available for sale at a net loss of $40.8 million. With the proceeds, the Company purchased $221.2 million of debt securities. The yield on the securities purchased was 445 basis points higher than the securities sold, with lower average lives and duration, and the Company currently expects to earn approximately $9.3 million in additional interest income over the next twelve months as a result of this transaction.
•Sold $55.6 million of commercial real estate and multifamily loans originated by Northfield that would be criticized or classified assets under the Company’s risk rating system. This transaction resulted in a loss on sale of loans of $1.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
•Entered into a letter of intent to sell loans of $246.2 million of unpaid principal, collateralized by New York City rent regulated multifamily properties originated by Northfield. The loans selected for sale were deemed to have the highest credit risk based upon the percentage of units that were rent regulated, the estimated loan to value or the debt service coverage. This transaction resulted in a loss on loans of $5.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The sale closed on October 27, 2026. The Company’s remaining exposure to New York City rent regulated multifamily loans was $164.6 million, or less than 1.0% of total assets at October 27, 2026.
•Entered into a letter of intent to sell $9.1 million of delinquent and nonperforming residential real estate loans originated by Columbia Bank. The Company recorded a loss of approximately $900,000 related to the transaction. This sale closed in October 2026.

Balance Sheet Summary
Total assets increased $6.5 billion, or 59.4%, to $17.6 billion at September 30, 2026 from $11.0 billion at December 31, 2025. The increase in total assets was primarily attributable to increases in cash and cash equivalents of $118.0 million, debt securities available for sale of $1.7 billion, debt securities held to maturity of $516.0 million, loans held-for-sale of $211.4 million, loans receivable, net, of $3.4 billion, bank-owned life insurance of $191.8 million and other assets of $157.6 million, mainly due to assets acquired in the Northfield merger.
Cash and cash equivalents increased $118.0 million, or 34.6%, to $458.8 million at September 30, 2026 from $340.8 million at December 31, 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to proceeds raised through the offering and cash and cash equivalents of $95.2 million acquired in the Northfield acquisition.
Debt securities available for sale increased $1.7 billion, or 151.0%, to $2.8 billion at September 30, 2026 from $1.1 billion at December 31, 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to $1.2 billion of securities acquired in the Northfield acquisition, and purchases of $1.7 billion of securities, consisting primarily of U.S. government obligations and mortgage-backed securities which were purchased with proceeds from the second-step conversion offering. These increases were partially offset by sales of securities with an amortized cost of $643.3 million which were acquired in the Northfield acquisition and the sale of an additional $213.9 million of seasoned securities sold as part of a balance sheet repositioning transaction, which resulted in a realized loss of $40.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company sold predominantly fixed rate, low-yielding debt securities and used the proceeds to repay high costing borrowings acquired from Northfield and purchase higher-yielding debt securities to improve the net interest rate margin in the future. The increase in debt securities available for sale also included a decrease of $33.9 million in the unrealized loss on securities available for sale.
Debt securities held to maturity increased $516.0 million, or 130.2%, to $912.2 million at September 30, 2026 from $396.2 million at December 31, 2025. The increase was attributable to purchases of securities of $542.8 million, consisting primarily of U.S. government obligations and mortgage-backed securities, partially offset by maturities on securities of $14.9 million, and repayments on securities of $13.1 million.
Loans held-for-sale increased to $211.4 million, at September 30, 2026 from zero at December 31, 2025. About Columbia Financial, Inc.
The consolidated financial results include the accounts of Columbia Financial, Inc., its wholly-owned subsidiary Columbia Bank (the "Bank") and the Bank's wholly-owned subsidiaries. Columbia Financial, Inc. is a Maryland corporation organized as Columbia Bank's parent stock holding company. Columbia Bank is a federally chartered savings bank headquartered in Fair Lawn, New Jersey that operates more than 100 full-service banking offices and offers traditional financial services to consumers and businesses in its market area, including select counties in New Jersey, and Brooklyn and Staten Island New York.
