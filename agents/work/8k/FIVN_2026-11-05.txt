Five9 Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Q3 Revenue Grew 13% year-over-year
Q3 Subscription Revenue Grew 16% year-over-year
Five9 Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Level AI
SAN RAMON, Calif. - November 5, 2026 - Five9, Inc. (NASDAQ:FIVN), the Genius CX Platform provider, today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 increased 13% to $323.3 million, compared to $285.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•GAAP gross margin was 53.4% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 55.0% for the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted gross margin was 61.7% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 62.8% for the third quarter of 2025.
•GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $14.5 million, or $0.19 per diluted share, and 4.5% of revenue, compared to GAAP net income of $18.0 million, or $0.21 per diluted share, and 6.3% of revenue, for the third quarter of 2025.
•Non-GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $63.7 million, or $0.82 per diluted share, and 19.7% of revenue, compared to non-GAAP net income of $60.6 million, or $0.78 per diluted share, and 21.2% of revenue, for the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was $81.2 million, or 25.1% of revenue, compared to $71.7 million, or 25.1% of revenue, for the third quarter of 2025.
•GAAP operating cash flow for the third quarter of 2026 was $58.4 million, compared to GAAP operating cash flow of $59.2 million for the third quarter of 2025.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to disclosing financial measures prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), this press release and the accompanying tables contain certain non-GAAP financial measures. We calculate adjusted gross profit and adjusted gross margin by adding back the following items to gross profit: stock-based compensation, depreciation, intangibles amortization, acquisition and related transaction costs and one-time integration costs, costs related to reduction in force plans, and lease amortization for finance leases. We calculate adjusted EBITDA by adding back or removing the following items to or from GAAP net income: stock-based compensation, depreciation and amortization, interest expense, interest income and other, acquisition and related transaction costs and one-time integration costs, costs related to reduction in force plans, office closure lease termination costs, impairment charge related to consolidation of corporate headquarters, lease amortization for finance leases, one-time expenses related to strategic consulting services for operational review, other cost-reduction and productivity initiatives, one-time expenses related to advisory services for long-term strategy and growth, one-time expenses related to advisory services for research and development transformation, legal fees related to the securities class action, and provision for income taxes. We calculate non-GAAP operating income by adding back or removing the following items to or from GAAP income from operations: stock-based compensation, intangibles amortization, acquisition and related transaction costs and one-time integration costs, costs related to reduction in force plans, office closure lease termination costs, impairment charge related to consolidation of corporate headquarters, one-time expenses related to strategic consulting services for operational review, other cost-reduction and productivity initiatives, one-time expenses related to advisory services for long-term strategy and growth, one-time expenses related to advisory services for research and development transformation, and legal fees related to the securities class action. We calculate non-GAAP net income by adding back or removing the following items to or from GAAP net income: stock-based compensation, intangibles amortization, amortization of discount and issuance costs on convertible senior notes, exit costs related to closure and relocation of Russian operations, acquisition and related transaction costs and one-time integration costs, costs related to reduction in force plans, office closure lease termination costs, impairment charge related to consolidation of corporate headquarters, one-time expenses related to strategic consulting services for operational review, other cost-reduction and productivity initiatives, one-time expenses related to advisory services for long-term strategy and growth, one-time expenses related to advisory services for research and development transformation, legal fees related to the securities class action, and deferred taxes - tax adjustments from an acquisition. For the periods presented, these adjustments from GAAP net income to non-GAAP net income do not include any presentation of the net tax effect of such adjustments given our significant net operating loss carryforwards.
