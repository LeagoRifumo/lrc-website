ZIM Reports Industry Leading Third Quarter 2026 Profitability, Driven
by Favorable Market Conditions and Focused Strategic Execution
Q3 Revenues up +53% to $2.71bn, and net income up +573% to $830m, y-o-y
Adjusted Q3 EBITDA and net income, up +124% to $1.33bn and
up +478% to $717m, y-o-y, respectively
Achieved industry leading Q3 freight rate
per TEU, Adjusted EBIT per TEU and Adjusted EBIT margin
of $2,480, $1,032, and 37%, respectively

$3.3bn to $3.5bn and Adjusted EBIT of $2.0bn to $2.2bn

representing 30% of 9-month aggregate 2026 net income
Haifa, Israel, November 18, 2026 – ZIM Integrated Shipping Services Ltd. (NYSE: ZIM) (“ZIM” or the “Company”) announced today its consolidated results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
· | Net income was $830m (compared to $123m in 2025), or diluted earnings per share of $6.88 (compared to $1.02 in 2025).

· | Adjusted net income was $717m (compared to $124m in 2025).

· | Adjusted EBITDA was $1.33bn, a year-over-year increase of 124%.

· | Revenues were $2.71bn, a year-over-year increase of 53%.

· | Carried volume was 962 thousand TEUs, a year-over-year increase of 4%.

· | Average freight rate per TEU was $2,480, a year-over-year increase of 55%.

· | Free cash flow of $1.22bn generated during the quarter.

· | Net debt, comprised predominantly of lease liabilities minus total cash position, of $1.86bn as of September 30, 2026, compared to $2.77bn as of June 30, 2026, and $2.92bn as of December 31, 2025.

· | Net leverage ratio of 0.8x as of September 30, 2026, compared to 1.6x net leverage ratio as of June 30, 2026, and 1.3x net leverage ratio as of December 31, 2025.

· | Net cash position (total cash position minus financial debt; i.e., excluding lease liabilities) of $3.29bn as of September 30, 2026.

· | Launched ZX3, ZIM’s 3rd premium expedited service Asia to USWC; and Falcon, offering faster transit time from China to Argentina and South Brazil.

Financial and Operating Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
Total revenues were $2.71 billion for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.78 billion for the third quarter of 2025, mainly driven by the increase in freight rates as well as in carried volume.
ZIM carried 962 thousand TEUs in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 926 thousand TEUs in the third quarter of 2025. The average freight rate per TEU was $2,480 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1,602 for the third quarter of 2025.
Operating income (EBIT) for the third quarter of 2026 was $1.15 billion, compared to $259 million for the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by the above-mentioned increase in total revenues.
Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $830 million, compared to $123 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $717 million, compared to $124 million for the third quarter of 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was $1.33 billion, compared to $593 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBIT for the third quarter of 2026 was $993 million, compared to $260 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBIT margins for the third quarter of 2026 were 49% and 37%, respectively. This compares to 33% and 15% for the third quarter of 2025, respectively.
Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted Net income exclude a non-cash impairment reversal of $155 million ($197 million, pre-tax) and acquisition related costs of $41 million ($41 million, pre-tax).
Net cash generated from operating activities was $1.24 billion for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $628 million for the third quarter of 2025.
Financial and Operating Results for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026
Total revenues were $5.89 billion for the first nine months of 2026, compared to $5.42 billion for the first nine months of 2025, primarily driven by the increase in freight rates.
ZIM carried 2,750 thousand TEUs in the first nine months of 2026, compared to 2,765 thousand TEUs in the first nine months of 2025. The average freight rate per TEU was $1,813 for the first nine months of 2026, compared to $1,622 for the first nine months of 2025.
Operating income (EBIT) for the first nine months of 2026 was $1.27 billion, compared to $873 million for the first nine months of 2025. The increase in operating income for the nine months of 2026 was primarily driven by the above-mentioned increase in total revenues.
Net income for the first nine months of 2026 was $808 million, compared to net income of $443 million for the first nine months of 2025, mainly driven by the above-mentioned increase in total revenues. Adjusted net income for the first nine months of 2026 was $721 million, compared to $442 million for the first nine months of 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA for the first nine months of 2026 was $2.13 billion, compared to $1.84 billion for the first nine months of 2025. Adjusted EBIT for the first nine months of 2026 was $1.16 billion, compared to $872 million for the first nine months of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBIT margins for the first nine months of 2026 were 36% and 20%, respectively. This compares to 34% and 16%, respectively, for the first nine months of 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted Net income exclude a non-cash impairment reversal recorded in the third quarter of $155 million ($197 million, pre-tax) and acquisition related costs of $68 million ($80 million, pre-tax).
Net cash generated from operating activities for the first nine months of 2026 was $1.90 billion, compared to $1.92 billion for the first nine months of 2025.
Liquidity, Cash Flows and Capital Allocation
ZIM’s total cash position (which includes cash and cash equivalents and investments in bank deposits and other investment instruments) was $3.36 billion as of September 30, 2026, compared to $2.53 billion as of June 30, 2026 and $2.80 billion as of December 31, 2025.
Capital expenditures totaled $19 million and $61 million for the third quarter of 2026 and for the first nine months of 2026, respectively, compared to $67 million for the third quarter of 2025 and $170 million for the first nine months of 2025. Other cash flow items in the first nine months of 2026 include a dividend payment of $106 million and $1.17 billion of debt service, mostly related to charter vessel and equipment lease liability repayments.
Net debt position as of September 30, 2026, was $1.86 billion compared to $2.77 billion as of June 30, 2026, and $2.92 billion as of December 31, 2025.
Net cash position (total cash of $3.36 billion minus financial debt of $70 million, i.e., excluding lease liabilities) was $3.29 billion as of September 30, 2026, compared to $2.46 billion as of June 30, 2026, and compared to $2.72 billion as of December 31, 2025. ZIM’s net leverage ratio as of September 30, 2026, was 0.8x, compared to 1.6x net leverage ratio as of June 30, 2026 and 1.3x as of December 31, 2025.
Third Quarter 2026 Dividend
In accordance with the Company's dividend policy, the Company's Board of Directors declared a regular cash dividend of approximately $243 million, or $2.01 per ordinary share, reflecting approximately 30% of nine-month aggregate 2026 net income. The dividend will be paid on December 8, 2026, to holders of record of ZIM ordinary shares as of November 30, 2026.
All dividends are subject to the discretion of Company's Board of Directors and to the restrictions provided by law. In addition, distribution of special dividends is restricted under the merger agreement between the Company and Hapag-Lloyd.
New Charter Agreements and Fleet Update
ZIM currently operates 112 containerships with a total capacity of 703 thousand TEUs, as well as 13 car carriers, compared to 116 containerships with total capacity of 711 thousand TEUs and 14 car carriers as of our Q3 2025 earnings release (November 20, 2025). During the second half of 2026, ZIM took delivery of two 8,000 TEU newbuild containerships, with charter durations of 5-7 years.
ZIM's charter orderbook comprises of 56 vessels, including 52 containerships ranging from 3,000 to 12,000 TEUs each, with an aggregate capacity of approximately 418 thousand TEUs and four car carriers. Of these, 52 are newbuilds, including 18 x 11,500 TEU dual-fuel LNG vessels, two x 12,000 TEU scrubber-fitted vessels and two x 7,000 CEU dual-fuel LNG, on which ZIM holds purchase options. Additional information can be found in ZIM’s Interim Financial Statements for the period ended September 30, 2026.
In addition, the Company has seven vessels scheduled for charter expiration during the remainder of 2026, representing an aggregate capacity of approximately 22 thousand TEUs. In 2027, 14 vessels are scheduled for charter expiration, representing an aggregate capacity of approximately 26 thousand TEUs. Upon expiration, ZIM has the option to either renew the charters or redeliver the vessels to their owners.

Transaction with Hapag-Lloyd
On February 16, 2026, ZIM announced that it entered into a merger agreement with Hapag-Lloyd, under which Hapag-Lloyd will acquire ZIM for $35.00 per share in cash. The transaction was unanimously approved by ZIM’s Board of Directors and approved by shareholders at a special meeting held on April 30, 2026. The transaction remains subject to satisfaction of customary closing conditions, including approvals by various regulatory authorities among them the State of Israel pursuant to the requirements of the Special State Share (the “Golden Share”). The original application to the State of Israel was terminated and Hapag-Lloyd intends to soon submit a new application. The transaction is targeted to close in the first quarter of 2027.
Until the closing of the transaction, Hapag-Lloyd and ZIM remain separate independent companies, and ZIM continues to operate in the ordinary course.

About ZIM
Founded in Israel in 1945, ZIM (NYSE: ZIM) is a leading global container liner shipping company with operations in more than 90 countries, serving over 30,000 customers across more than 300 ports worldwide. ZIM leverages digital strategies and a commitment to ESG values to provide customers innovative seaborne transportation and logistics services and exceptional customer experience. ZIM’s differentiated global-niche strategy, based on agile fleet management and deployment, covers major trade routes with a focus on select markets where the Company holds competitive advantages. Additional information about ZIM is available at www.ZIM.com.

Use of Non-IFRS Financial Measures
The Company presents non-IFRS measures as additional performance measures as the Company believes that it enables the comparison of operating performance between periods on a consistent basis. These measures should not be considered in isolation, or as a substitute for operating income, any other performance measures, or cash flow data, which were prepared in accordance with IFRS as measures of profitability or liquidity. Please note that Adjusted EBITDA does not take into account debt service requirements or other commitments, as well as capital expenditures, and therefore, does not necessarily indicate the amounts that may be available for the Company's use. In addition, the non-IFRS financial measures presented by the Company may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies due to differences in the way these measures are calculated.
Adjusted EBITDA is a non-IFRS financial measure which we define as net income (loss) adjusted to exclude financial expenses (income), net, income taxes, depreciation and amortization in order to reach EBITDA, and further adjusted, as applicable, to exclude impairment of assets (or the reversal of which), capital gains (losses) beyond the ordinary course of business, expenses related to legal contingencies and acquisition related expenses (compensation costs and professional fees).
Adjusted EBIT is a non-IFRS financial measure which we define as net income (loss) adjusted to exclude financial expenses (income), net and income taxes, in order to reach our results from operating activities, or EBIT, and further adjusted, as applicable, to exclude impairment of assets (or the reversal of which), capital gains (losses) beyond the ordinary course of business, expenses related to legal contingencies and acquisition related expenses (compensation costs and professional fees).
Adjusted net income is a non-IFRS financial measure which we define as net income (loss) adjusted, as applicable, to exclude impairment of assets (or the reversal of which), capital gains (losses) beyond the ordinary course of business, expenses related to legal contingencies and acquisition related expenses (compensation costs and professional fees), all of which net of their respective income tax effect.
Free cash flow is a non-IFRS financial measure which we define as net cash generated from operating activities minus capital expenditures, net.
Net debt is a non-IFRS financial measure which we define as face value of short- and long-term debt, minus cash and cash equivalents, bank deposits and other investment instruments.
Net cash position is a non-IFRS financial measure which we define as the total cash position (which includes cash and cash equivalents, bank deposits and other investment instruments) minus financial debt (i.e., excluding lease liabilities).
Net leverage ratio is a non-IFRS financial measure which we define as net debt (see above) divided by Adjusted EBITDA for the last twelve-month period. When our net debt is less than zero, we report the net leverage ratio as zero.

1 | Reporting entity

ZIM Integrated Shipping Services Ltd. (hereinafter - the "Company" or "ZIM") and its subsidiaries (hereinafter – "the Group" or "the Companies") and the Group’s interests in associates, operate in the field of cargo shipping and related services.
ZIM is a company incorporated in Israel, with limited liability. ZIM’s ordinary shares have been listed on the New York Stock Exchange (the “NYSE”) under the symbol “ZIM” on January 28, 2021. The address of the Company’s registered office is 9 Andrei Sakharov Street, Haifa, Israel.
Entry Into Agreement and Plan of Merger
On February 16, 2026, the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger, by and among the Company, Hapag-Lloyd AG, a shipping company incorporated under the laws of Germany (“Parent”), and Norazia (Israel) Ltd., a company organized under the laws of the State of Israel and a direct or indirect wholly owned Subsidiary of Parent (“Merger Sub”). Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, and upon the terms and subject to the conditions therein, Merger Sub will merge with and into the Company (the “Merger”), with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation in the Merger and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. In connection with the Merger Agreement, Parent entered into a binding memorandum of understanding with FIMI Opportunity 7, L.P. and FIMI Israel Opportunity 7, Limited Partnership (together, “FIMI”), pursuant to which certain activities and related assets will be transferred to a new entity established by FIMI, that would assume the responsibilities related to the Special State Share, subject to the approval of the State of Israel (see also Note 12(b) to the Company’s 2025 annual financial statements).
In a Special Shareholders’ Meeting held on April 30, 2026, among several topics on the agenda, the proposed Merger transaction was approved. The completion of the Merger is subject to certain conditions, including, among others, obtaining the approval in connection with the Special State Share, and other regulatory approvals required.
During the recent months, several governmental offices of the State of Israel, including the Ministry of Defense, the Ministry of Finance and the Prime Minister office, provided professional opinions expressing their objections to the proposed merger in its original structure, based on concerns regarding potential risks to the state’s national security, economic independence and long-term commercial prospects. In September 2026, the Israeli Government Companies Authority has communicated its decision to terminate its review of the proposed transaction with Hapag-Lloyd, as Hapag-Lloyd conveyed its intention to submit a proposal of a revised transaction structure.
Upon the completion of the merger, if completed, each issued and outstanding ordinary share of the Company, excluding the Special State Share, will automatically be converted into the right to receive $35.00 per share in cash, without interest (the “Merger Consideration”). At that time, the Company Shares will be delisted from the New York Stock Exchange and deregistered under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”).

4 | Financial position

(a) | The container shipping industry continues to be impacted by the supply and demand dynamics, as well as by uncertainties in the global trade, including the implications of the ongoing armed conflicts in the Middle-East (including the recent developments in Yemen) and between Russia and Ukraine, the recent disruption in the strait of Hormuz and the continuing disruption in the Red Sea, the changing, and sometimes escalating, trade barriers between the US and China and other countries, and other geopolitical challenges. These factors contribute to the continuing volatility in freight rates, charter rates and bunker prices (including the recent volatility in bunker prices, due to the implications of the above-mentioned developments in the Middle-East and in the strait of Hormuz). In addition, regulators in certain jurisdictions continue to enforce enhanced regulatory oversight activities over our industry.
