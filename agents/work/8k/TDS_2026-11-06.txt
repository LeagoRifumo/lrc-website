TDS reports third quarter 2026 results
TDS Board authorizes incremental $250 million share repurchase plan
TDS Telecom updates guidance for 2026
CHICAGO (November 6, 2026) — Telephone and Data Systems, Inc. (NYSE: TDS) reported third quarter 2026 operating results.
“We continue to make progress as our teams execute our strategic objectives,” said Walter Carlson, TDS President and CEO. “During the quarter, TDS Telecom expanded its marketable fiber service footprint by approximately 76,000 addresses and grew fiber net adds by approximately 17,000. Array increased the number of colocations sequentially, highlighting the value of our assets, and continues to improve profitability. Additionally, I'm pleased to report that following TDS' withdrawal of the offer to acquire the minority interest in Array, TDS was in the market, and has repurchased nearly 15 million shares in 2026, returning over $520 million to shareholders.”
Highlights*
TDS
•Returning value to shareholders
◦Repurchased approximately 6 million TDS shares in the quarter at an average price per share of $36.65
◦Continued to be in the market following the end of the quarter, virtually exhausting the existing authorization
•TDS Board approved incremental $250 million share repurchase authorization
◦Provides future flexibility for the organization
TDS Telecom
•Executing on fiber broadband strategy
◦Delivered approximately 76,000 marketable fiber service addresses
◦Grew fiber connections with approximately 17,000 residential fiber net additions
•Service revenue down 3%
◦Residential fiber revenue growth of 14% more than offset by impacts of divestitures and continued legacy declines
•Updated 2026 Financial Guidance
◦Revenues of $990 million to $1,010 million
◦Adjusted OIBDA of $280 million to $300 million
◦Adjusted EBITDA of $290 million to $310 million
◦Capital expenditures of approximately $750 million
Array
•Optimizing tower operations
◦Site rental revenues grew 14% year over year
◦Delivered consecutive quarter over quarter colocation growth
•Continuing to close pending sales of wireless spectrum
◦Received regulatory approval for remaining pending transactions in October, closings remain subject to FCC finality and other customary closing conditions
•Reaffirming 2026 financial guidance
* Comparisons are 3Q’25 to 3Q’26 unless otherwise noted.

About TDS
Telephone and Data Systems, Inc. (TDS) provides broadband, video, voice and wireless services through its TDS Telecom business. Array leases tower space to tenants and provides ancillary services, holds noncontrolling interests in primarily wireless operating companies and holds certain wireless spectrum licenses. Founded in 1969, TDS is headquartered in Chicago.
