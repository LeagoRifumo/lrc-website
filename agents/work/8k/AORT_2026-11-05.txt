Artivion Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Third Quarter & Recent Business Highlights:
•Achieved revenue of $123.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 versus $113.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 9% on a GAAP basis and 9% on a non-GAAP constant currency basis
•Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $(7.9) million, or $(0.16) per fully diluted share, and non-GAAP net income was $3.8 million, or $0.08 per fully diluted share
•Adjusted EBITDA declined 8% to $22.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $24.6 million in the third quarter of 2025
ATLANTA, GA – (November 5, 2026) – Artivion, Inc. (NYSE: AORT), a leading cardiac and vascular surgery company focused on aortic disease, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“In the third quarter of 2026, we delivered 9% constant currency revenue growth, reflecting continued execution of our strategy to drive long-term, profitable growth through an expanding and clinically differentiated product portfolio. Revenue growth was led by On-X, which grew 13% on a constant currency basis compared to the third quarter of 2025. Stent grafts revenue grew 12% for the second consecutive quarter, despite a tougher AMDS prior year comparison,” said Pat Mackin, Chairman, President, and Chief Executive Officer.
Mr. Mackin continued, “The number of U.S. AMDS implants increased from the second quarter, which we believe reflects the impact of our recent PMA approval and our efforts to drive adoption of this new technology. Preparations for the U.S. commercial launch of NEXUS in January 2027 are progressing well, and we look forward to our first surgeon training event in December. We also exited the quarter in a strong supply position for On-X following our investments in our Austin manufacturing facility, and ARTIZEN, our clinical trial for ARCEVO LSA, continues to enroll on schedule.”

Mr. Mackin concluded, “Overall, we are encouraged by our third quarter performance and the progress we have made so far in 2026. With the recent PMAs of AMDS and NEXUS in the U.S., and ARCEVO LSA on track with enrollment in the ARTIZEN IDE trial, we believe we are on track to being the only company with a comprehensive aortic arch portfolio, which represents an approximately $330 million annual U.S. market opportunity.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $123.8 million, an increase of 9% on a GAAP basis and 9% on a non-GAAP constant currency basis, both compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $(7.9) million, or $(0.16) per fully diluted common share, compared to net income of $6.5 million, or $0.13 per fully diluted common share for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $3.8 million, or $0.08 per fully diluted common share, compared to non-GAAP net income of $7.9 million, or $0.16 per fully diluted common share, for the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 includes pretax losses related to foreign currency revaluation of $0.6 million.
2026 Financial Outlook
Artivion has narrowed its expectations for the full year 2026 and now expects revenue to be in the range of $484 to $492 million, representing growth of 8% to 10% on an adjusted constant currency basis compared to 2025 adjusted revenue1. This compares to the previous range of $480 to $496 million, representing growth of 7% to 11% on an adjusted constant currency basis compared to 2025 adjusted revenue1. This guidance contemplates a continued expectation for currency to represent an approximate one percentage point tailwind for the full year.
Artivion is narrowing its full year 2026 adjusted EBITDA to be in the range of $93 to $98 million. This compares to the previous range of $92 to $99 million. This guidance includes the previously articulated expectation to incur approximately $8 million of expense through the full year 2026 associated with the acquisition of Endospan, which closed in May 2026.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

Investors should consider this non-GAAP information in addition to, and not as a substitute for, financial measures prepared in accordance with US GAAP. In addition, this non-GAAP financial information may not be the same as similar measures presented by other companies. The Company’s non-GAAP adjusted constant currency growth rates compare current year revenues to prior period revenues adjusted for the impact of changes in currency exchange. The Company’s non-GAAP net income, EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, general, administrative, and marketing, and free cash flows results primarily exclude (as applicable) depreciation and amortization expense, interest income and expense, non-cash compensation expense, loss or gain on foreign currency revaluation, income tax expense or benefit, expense/(income) for business development, integration, and severance, losses on inducement/extinguishment of debt, non-cash interest expense, capital expenditures, and other non-recurring items.

About Artivion, Inc.
Headquartered in suburban Atlanta, Georgia, Artivion, Inc. is a medical device company focused on developing simple, elegant solutions that address cardiac and vascular surgeons’ most difficult challenges in treating patients with aortic diseases. Artivion’s four major groups of products include: aortic stent grafts, surgical sealants, On-X mechanical heart valves, and implantable cardiac and vascular human tissues. Artivion markets and sells products in more than 100 countries worldwide. For additional information about Artivion, visit our website, www.Artivion.com.
