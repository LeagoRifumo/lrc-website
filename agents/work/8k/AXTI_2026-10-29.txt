AXT, Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
FREMONT, Calif., October 29, 2026 – AXT, Inc. (NasdaqGS: AXTI), a leading manufacturer of compound semiconductor wafer substrates, today reported financial results for the third quarter, ended September 30, 2026.
Management Qualitative Comments
“This is a pivotal and exciting period for AXT,” said Morris Young, chief executive officer. “Our revenue in Q3 reached a record $73.4 million, up 54 percent from the prior quarter and up 162 percent year over year, driven by exceptionally strong customer demand for our indium phosphide material. We entered 2026 with a commitment to double our indium phosphide capacity and I am pleased to report that not only have we surpassed that goal but we have more than quadrupled our 2026 indium phosphide revenue opportunity through additional productivity gains and the expansion of our capabilities for high-value, large diameter products. We remain laser-focused on execution and supporting our customers as they scale, and we believe we are well positioned for transformational growth as our market continues on an extraordinary trajectory.”
Third Quarter 2026 Results
In order to provide better clarity on its operational and financial results, AXT reports its financial results on both a GAAP and non-GAAP basis. Non-GAAP results exclude stock-based compensation expense. Investors can find GAAP to non-GAAP reconciliation tables in the financial statements in this earnings release.
● | Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $73.4 million, compared with $47.6 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $28.0 million for the third quarter of 2025.

● | GAAP gross margin was 56.0 percent of revenue for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 44.9 percent of revenue for the second quarter of 2026 and 22.3 percent for the third quarter of 2025.

● | Non-GAAP gross margin, after excluding charges for stock-based compensation, was 56.1 percent of revenue for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 45.0 percent of revenue for the second quarter of 2026 and 22.4 percent for the third quarter of 2025.

● | GAAP net income, after minority interests, for the third quarter of 2026 was a net income of $25.7 million, or $0.39 diluted income per share, compared with a net income of $11.1 million, or $0.17 per share, for the second quarter of 2026 and a net loss of $1.9 million, or $0.04 per share, for the third quarter of 2025.

● | Non-GAAP net income for the third quarter of 2026 was a net income of $26.8 million, or $0.41 per share, compared with a net income of $11.9 million, or $0.19 per share, for the second quarter of 2026 and a net loss of $1.2 million, or $0.03 per share, for the third quarter of 2025.

About AXT, Inc.
AXT is a material science company that develops and manufactures high-performance compound and single element semiconductor wafer substrates comprising indium phosphide (InP), gallium arsenide (GaAs) and germanium (Ge). The company’s wafer substrates are used when a typical silicon wafer substrate cannot meet the performance requirements of a semiconductor or optoelectronic device. End markets include 5G infrastructure, data center connectivity (silicon photonics), passive optical networks, LED lighting, lasers, sensors, power amplifiers for wireless devices and satellite solar cells. AXT’s worldwide headquarters are in Fremont, California where the company maintains sales, administration and customer service functions. AXT has its Asia headquarters in Beijing, China and manufacturing facilities in three separate locations in China. In addition, as part of its supply chain strategy, the company has partial ownership in more than ten companies in China producing raw materials and consumables for its manufacturing process. For more information, see AXT’s website at http://www.axt.com.
