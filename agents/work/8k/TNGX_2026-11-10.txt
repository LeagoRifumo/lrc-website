Tango Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
and Provides Business Highlights
Promising Phase 1/2 data from combination study of vopimetostat plus daraxonrasib presented at 2026 ESMO Congress demonstrated 82% objective response rate (ORR) and 88% 9-month progression-free survival (PFS) rate in patients with 2L/3L pancreatic cancer; combination was generally well-tolerated at go-forward dose
Registrational Phase 3 study of vopimetostat plus daraxonrasib as front-line treatment for patients with MTAP-deleted pancreatic cancer expected to be initiated in 1H 2027
Data from vopimetostat monotherapy in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and brain-penetrant TNG456 in NSCLC, glioblastoma and other cancers and development strategy in lung cancer expected to be disclosed in January 2027
Cash position of $977.5 million as of September 30, 2026
BOSTON, November 9, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Tango Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: TNGX) a clinical-stage biotechnology company committed to delivering breakthrough targeted therapies that extend and improve the lives of patients with cancer, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided business highlights.
“In 2026, Tango has made significant strides towards bringing vopimetostat to patients with pancreatic cancer,” said Malte Peters, MD, President and Chief Executive Officer of Tango. “This progress is rooted first in the data. Last month at ESMO, we shared promising data from the combination of vopimetostat plus daraxonrasib that reinforced the potential of this program to raise the bar for patients with MTAP-deleted and RAS-mutant pancreatic cancer. Based on those data, we have engaged with the FDA on our plans for a registrational study in the front-line setting and plan to initiate that study in the first half of next year. In addition, we plan to engage with regulators on the possibility of seeking accelerated approval of this combination in second line pancreatic cancer. Beyond pancreatic cancer, we look forward to sharing our strategy in lung cancer, supported by data from vopimetostat and TNG456, in January of next year. I am grateful to the dedicated Tango team for the progress we have made in 2026 thus far and look forward to advancing our mission of bringing these important therapies to patients with cancer.”
Clinical Pipeline Updates
•
ESMO DATA. In October 2026, Tango reported promising updated data from vopimetostat in combination with Revolution Medicines' RAS(ON) inhibitors daraxonrasib or zoldonrasib in
patients with MTAP-deleted, RAS-mutant pancreatic cancer. Data demonstrated that vopimetostat in combination with daraxonrasib achieved an 82% objective response rate (ORR) and 88% nine-month progression-free survival (PFS) rate in efficacy evaluable patients with second and third line pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), and median PFS was not yet reached. Efficacy-evaluable patients are those patients who received their first dose of treatment at least 14 weeks prior to the data cutoff date of August 20, 2026. As of the data cutoff date, the combination had a generally well-tolerated safety profile at dose level one (vopimetostat 200 mg/daraxonrasib 100 mg QD).
•
PIVOTAL STUDY PLANS. Based upon the encouraging Phase 1/2 data and discussions with the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA), Tango intends to initiate a pivotal Phase 3 study of the combination of vopimetostat plus daraxonrasib in front-line MTAP-deleted pancreatic cancer. The Company anticipates that this will be a global, randomized study of vopimetostat 200 mg plus daraxonrasib 100 mg, once-daily vs. standard-of-care chemotherapy. The planned study has dual primary endpoints of ORR and PFS, with overall survival as a key secondary endpoint. The Company plans to initiate the study in the first half of 2027. In addition, Tango intends to discuss the possibility of pursuing approval of this combination in patients with second line PDAC with regulatory authorities.
•
ERASCA STUDY START. In November 2026, the Company announced that the Phase 1/2 study of oral PRMT5 inhibitor vopimetostat in combination with pan-RAS molecular glue ERAS-0015 in patients with MTAP-deleted PDAC and MTAP-deleted/RAS-mutant non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) has initiated enrollment.
•
LEADERS IN PRMT5 INHIBITION. In October 2026, the Company announced that it plans to initiate a Phase 1/2 combination study of vopimetostat plus panRAS inhibitors in patients with MTAP-wild type pancreatic cancer in the first half of 2027. This study of approximately 25 patients will explore the Company’s hypothesis, based on a strong biological rationale and emerging clinical and preclinical data, that the synergy observed in MTAP-deleted cancers has the potential to extend to MTAP-wild type cancers.
Corporate Updates
•
FOCUS ON PRMT5. In October 2026, Tango disclosed a strategic reprioritization of the Company’s research and development activities to focus on the advancement of PRMT5-targeted therapies.

Financial Results
As of September 30, 2026, the Company held $977.5 million in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities, which the Company expects to fund its current operating plan.
Collaboration revenue was $0 for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $53.8 million for the same period in 2025, and $0 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $62.4 million for the same period in 2025. All remaining deferred revenue from the upfront and research option-extension payments under the Gilead collaboration was recognized as collaboration revenue during the year ended December 31, 2025 as a result of the truncation of the collaboration agreement which concluded all research activities in August 2025.
Research and development expenses were $48.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $30.8 million for the same period in 2025, and $119.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $100.1 million for the same period in 2025. The change was primarily due to increased spend related to the advancement of the vopimetostat and TNG456 clinical programs. This increase was partially offset by decreased spend due to the Company's portfolio prioritization efforts.
General and administrative expenses were $29.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $8.9 million for the same period in 2025, and $67.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $31.7 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily due to increased spend on personnel-related costs, including share-based compensation expense which included increases derived from the modification of previously issued equity awards for former executive officers.
Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $68.4 million, or $0.39 per share (basic and diluted), compared to a net income of $15.9 million, or $0.14 per share (basic) and $0.13 per share (diluted), in the same period in 2025. Net loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $169.2 million, or $1.08 per share (basic and diluted), compared to a net loss of $62.8 million, or $0.57 per share(basic and diluted), in the same period in 2025.
About Tango Therapeutics
Tango Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company dedicated to delivering breakthrough targeted therapies that extend and improve the lives of patients with cancer. Using an approach that focuses on genetic vulnerabilities in cancer, Tango is pursuing PRMT5-targeted therapies designed to address pancreatic and other forms of cancer while sparing healthy normal tissues. For more information, please visit www.tangotx.com.
