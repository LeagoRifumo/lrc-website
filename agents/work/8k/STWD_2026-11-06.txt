MIAMI BEACH, FL, November 6, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Starwood Property Trust, Inc. (NYSE: STWD) today announced operating results for the fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company reported a third quarter GAAP net loss of $389.7 million, inclusive of a $336.6 million credit loss provision and $32.2 million of non-cash property impairments, and Distributable Earnings (a non-GAAP financial measure) was $116.8 million. Distributable Earnings prior to realized commercial lending gains and losses (also a non-GAAP financial measure) was $155.2 million. See definitions and reconciliation tables below.
“The third quarter of the year was somewhat historic. Interest rates skyrocketed and the forward yield curve moved sharply higher. Though the real estate markets continued their healing, bolstered by a meaningful collapse in most all major asset classes, the rise in rates sent a bit of a shock wave through the property sector and related rate sensitive sectors. We held steady and continued to make meaningful investments in the quarter while continuing to resolve our underperforming assets and loans. Through the first 9 months of the year, we have deployed $8.2 billion into our various business lines at very attractive risk adjusted returns. In addition, we still expect to resolve almost half of the underperforming assets we started the year with. Although our borrowers are feeling the pain of higher interest costs, our balance sheet and liquidity remain very strong,” said Barry Sternlicht, Chairman and CEO of Starwood Property Trust.
“We took advantage of favorable market conditions earlier this year, before the significant rate rise, to proactively refinance and extend our corporate debt. We completed approximately $2.1 billion of financing transactions at rates and spreads below those available today. We continue to be even more vigilant in the deployment of capital across our diversified businesses, including investing in our own shares, purchasing a record $108 million in the quarter, as we focus on the most attractive risk-adjusted returns,” added Jeffrey DiModica, President of Starwood Property Trust.

Definitions of Distributable Earnings and Distributable Earnings Prior to Realized Commercial Lending Gains and Losses
Distributable Earnings, a non-GAAP financial measure, is used to compute the Company’s incentive fees to its external manager and is an appropriate supplemental disclosure for a mortgage REIT. For the Company’s purposes, Distributable Earnings is defined as GAAP net income (loss) excluding non-cash equity compensation expense, the incentive fee due to the Company’s external manager, acquisition costs for successful acquisitions, depreciation and amortization of real estate and associated intangibles, any unrealized gains, losses or other non-cash items recorded in net income (loss) for the period and, to the extent deducted from net income (loss), distributions payable with respect to equity securities of subsidiaries issued in exchange for properties or interests therein. The amount is adjusted to exclude one-time events pursuant to changes in GAAP and certain other non-cash adjustments as determined by the Company’s external manager and approved by a majority of the Company’s independent directors.
The Company believes it is also useful to present another non-GAAP financial measure, Distributable Earnings prior to realized commercial lending gains and losses. This measure adjusts Distributable Earnings to exclude realized gains and losses resulting from the disposition or resolution of commercial loans that were on nonaccrual or properties acquired through foreclosure held within the Company’s Commercial and Residential Lending Segment. The Company believes this supplemental measure helps investors assess the earnings capacity of its existing investment portfolio and inform their assessment of future earnings and potential dividends by separately identifying the impact of realized commercial lending gains and losses arising from loan resolutions and dispositions of foreclosed real estate. Because these gains and losses remain an important component of the Company’s overall economic performance and dividend coverage, they are included in Distributable Earnings. The Company’s management and Board of Directors consider both measures when evaluating its performance and determining its dividend.

About Starwood Property Trust, Inc.
Starwood Property Trust (NYSE: STWD), an affiliate of global private investment firm Starwood Capital Group, is a leading diversified finance company with a core focus on the real estate and infrastructure sectors. As of September 30, 2026, the Company has successfully deployed $123 billion of capital since inception and manages a portfolio of $32 billion across debt and equity investments. Starwood Property Trust’s investment objective is to generate attractive and stable returns for shareholders, primarily through dividends, by leveraging a premiere global organization to identify and execute on the best risk adjusted returning investments across its target assets.
