JERSEY CITY, NJ - November 3, 2026 - Amdocs Limited (NASDAQ:DOX today reported operating results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
“We delivered solid fourth quarter and full year fiscal 2026 results, consistent with our guidance, and I want to thank our employees around the world for their focus and commitment. I took over as CEO two quarters ago, and since then we have moved fast to develop a new growth strategy and are busy executing it. The core pillar of that strategy is aOS, our Agentic Operating System for telco, which is quickly gaining market traction. Today we announced a significant, eight-year strategic agreement with AT&T, our largest customer. It expands our longstanding partnership by pairing aOS with AT&T’s AI-enabled delivery framework to modernize its business and customer operations. On top of AT&T, we are also excited to announce another three major strategic aOS deals with VodafoneThree in UK, VEON in Eurasia and Globe in Philippines. Together, these deals are great examples of how aOS is enabling Amdocs to expand its activities and becoming the trusted partner of choice for our customers’ agentic journeys,” said Shimie Hortig, president and chief executive officer of Amdocs Management Limited.
Hortig continued, “We are also excited to announce our entry into a new vertical with the acquisition of EIS, a leading provider of an agentic multi-line insurance platform. This is our first step into the insurance industry. It significantly expands our addressable market and establishes insurance as a strategic medium- to long-term growth engine for Amdocs. The rationale is compelling. Insurance shares many of the characteristics that have defined our success in telecommunications: highly regulated environments, complex and mission-critical platforms, and data-intensive workflows. EIS brings an AI-native core platform, an established global customer base, and deep knowledge of both Life & Annuities and Property & Casualty. By combining EIS’ assets and deep vertical expertise, with Amdocs aOS, our scale and our accountability-driven business model, we aim to provide a differentiated offering and become their partner of choice to lead their agentic journey.”
Hortig concluded, “We believe our growing strategic momentum, expansion into the insurance market and internal transformation will position us for long-term growth and improved profitability. As part of the focus of our new strategy, we also decided to exit certain businesses and activities that aren’t strategically relevant to our agentic future, including the recently announced sale of our media business and the exit of non-strategic business activities relating to the resale of hardware activities. Looking into fiscal 2027, our guidance reflects major headwinds from T-Mobile due to a recent dispute, the completion of the integration project of the USCellular customers and reduction in T-Mobile’s discretionary spending. That said, the rest of our business remains robust and continues showing growth in fiscal 2027. Moreover, with positive market traction for aOS and an expanding pipeline of significant customer opportunities, our fiscal 2028 expectations position us for mid-single digit revenue growth, high-single digit earnings growth and a steadily rising dividend.”

About Amdocs
Amdocs develops, transforms and operates the mission-critical systems that businesses depend on every day. With more than 40 years of experience serving the world’s leading communications companies, we combine proprietary technology, deep industry expertise and end-to-end accountability, from transformation through ongoing operations and evolution. Powered by aOS, our agentic operating system, we bring industry-specific AI into core business processes, advancing customers toward intelligent, increasingly autonomous operations across business, IT and network domains. We enable customers to modernize complex systems in highly regulated environments while maintaining the reliability, security and scale their businesses demand, driving measurable improvements in customer experience, operational efficiency and revenue growth.
Amdocs runs billions of transactions daily, and our technology is relied on every day. Together, we help those who shape the future to make it amazing.
