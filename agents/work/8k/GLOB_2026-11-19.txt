Globant Reports 2026 Third Quarter Financial Results
LUXEMBOURG / November 19, 2026 - Globant (NYSE: GLOB) today announced results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
“As an AI-native company, we are seeing our momentum accelerate, with AI Pods reaching an annualized run rate of $106.6 million in the third quarter, more than doubling quarter-over-quarter and positioning us to exceed $130 million by year end. This quarter, we completed the rollout of Glob.AI, our AI-native services platform comprising our AI Pods and Glob.AI OS, an operating system that provides governance and traceability. We are seeing growing enterprise adoption as clients shift from hourly billing toward outcome- and consumption-based delivery. Supported by an expanding partner ecosystem and two decades of engineering playbooks, we believe we are well positioned to help our clients accelerate their AI transformation”, explained Martín Migoya, Globant’s CEO and co-founder.
“In Q3 2026, Globant delivered $611.1 million in revenue, in line with the midpoint of our guidance, and expanded adjusted operating margin sequentially to 14.1%. Core client expansion remained strong, highlighted by a 5.7% year-over-year increase in top 50 accounts and record revenue per head of $99.8 thousand. Supported by streamlined operations, debt maturities extended to 2031, and $25.0 million in Q3 share repurchases, we believe our strong balance sheet and robust cash conversion will enable us to navigate regional headwinds while advancing our business model transformation. Given current market valuations, we have upsized our share repurchase program from $125 million to $250 million through December 2027,” stated Juan Urthiague, Globant’s CFO.
Please see highlights below. Note that reconciliations between IFRS and Non-IFRS financial measures are disclosed at the end of this press release.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
· | Revenues were $611.1 million, representing a 1.0% year-over-year decline
· | IFRS Gross Profit Margin was 34.1% compared to 34.8% in the third quarter of 2025.
· | Non-IFRS Adjusted Gross Profit Margin was 36.8% compared to 38.1% in the third quarter of 2025.
· | IFRS Profit from Operations Margin was 4.4% compared to 9.1% in the third quarter of 2025.
· | Non-IFRS Adjusted Profit from Operations Margin was 14.1% compared to 15.5% in the third quarter of 2025.
· | IFRS Diluted EPS was $0.32 compared to $0.73 in the third quarter of 2025.
· | Non-IFRS Adjusted Diluted EPS was $1.48 compared to $1.53 in the third quarter of 2025.

Other Financial Highlights as of and for the quarter ended September 30, 2026
· | Cash and cash equivalents and Short-term investments were $153.6 million as of September 30, 2026.
· | The Company invested $25.0 million during the third quarter under its share repurchase program.
· | Globant completed the third quarter of 2026 with 26,164 Globers, 24,486 of whom were technology, design and innovation professionals.

· | The geographic revenue breakdown for the third quarter of 2026 was as follows: 54.2% from North America (top country: US), 21.8% from Latin America (top country: Argentina), 19.5% from Europe (top country: Spain) and 4.5% from New Markets1 (top country: Saudi Arabia).
· | Globant’s top customer, top five customers and top ten customers for the third quarter of 2026 represented 9.8%, 21.7% and 31.2% of revenues, respectively.
· | During the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, Globant served a total of 882 customers (with revenues over $100,000 in the last twelve months), with 321 accounts generating more than $1 million of annual revenues, compared to 339 for the same period one year ago.
· | In terms of currencies, 65.5% of Globant’s revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were denominated in US dollars.

2026 Fourth Quarter and Full Year Outlook
Based on current market conditions, Globant is providing the following estimates for the fourth quarter and the full year of 2026:
· | Fourth quarter 2026 Revenues are estimated to be in the range of $596 million to $604 million, representing a 2.7% to 1.4% year-over-year decline. This expected decline includes a neutral FX outlook.
· | Fourth quarter 2026 Non-IFRS Adjusted Profit from Operations Margin is estimated to be in the range of 13.5% to 14.5%.
· | Fourth quarter 2026 Non-IFRS Adjusted Diluted EPS is estimated to be in the range of $1.43 to $1.53 (assuming an average of 43.3 million diluted shares outstanding during the fourth quarter).
· | Fiscal year 2026 Revenues are estimated to be in the range of $2,429 million to $2,437 million, implying a 1.1% to 0.7% year-over-year decline. This expected decline includes a positive FX impact of 70 basis points.
· | Fiscal year 2026 Non-IFRS Adjusted Profit from Operations Margin is estimated to be in the range of 13.5% to 14.5%.
· | Fiscal year 2026 Non-IFRS Adjusted Diluted EPS is estimated to be in the range of $5.77 to $5.87 (assuming an average of 43.5 million diluted shares outstanding during 2026).

About Globant (NYSE:GLOB)
Globant (NYSE: GLOB) is a global AI-native services company that helps organizations reinvent themselves and drive measurable business results by combining technology, creativity and innovation with AI at its core. Through Glob.AI, its AI-native technology services model and its signature AI Pods, agentic service units supervised by experts, Globant delivers enterprise-grade technology services tied directly to core business metrics.
Present across 30+ countries, Globant operates a network of industry-specific AI Studios and partners with global brands including FIFA, Google, Riot Games, the LA Clippers and Santander. Recognized by IDC MarketScape as a Worldwide Leader in Experience Design Services (2025) and a Leader in AI Services (2023), Globant’s work is featured as business case studies at Harvard, MIT and Stanford. The company collaborates with leading technology platforms including Anthropic, NVIDIA, AWS and Unity.
For more information, please visit www.globant.com
