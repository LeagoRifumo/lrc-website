Consolidated Results
Mexico City, October 29, 2026 — Grupo Televisa, S.A.B. (NYSE: TV; BMV: TLEVISA CPO; “Televisa” or “the Company”), today announced results for the third quarter of 2026. The results have been prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (“IFRS Accounting Standards”).
Beginning in the fourth quarter of 2025, we present the operating results of our Cable and Sky businesses as a single reportable segment, Telecom, with three revenue categories: Residential, Satellite, and Enterprise. This change in segment reporting is a result of organizational changes that integrated the operations of our Cable and Sky businesses into a single business, and our senior management now analyzes the results of our operations, makes decisions, and assigns resources to it as a single business. Through September 30, 2025, the operating results of our Cable and Sky businesses were presented as separate reportable segments. As a result of this change in our segment reporting, the operations previously reported under our former Cable and Sky segments are now classified into a single reportable segment for any comparative period presented.

Revenues and Operating Segment Income
Third quarter segment revenues decreased by 3.8% to Ps.14,077.7 million compared with Ps.14,627.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Our Residential Services revenues remained essentially flat year-on-year partly due to the phase out of revenue coming from World Cup subscription packages. Our Enterprise Services revenues increased by 6.8% mainly due to the signing of new projects with the public and private sectors. Finally, our Satellite Services revenues declined by 20.5%, driven by a year-on-year decrease in RGUs.
Third quarter operating segment income increased by 5.6% to Ps.5,996.0 million compared with Ps.5,677.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. The margin reached 42.6%, increasing by around 380 basis points year-on-year due to the efficiency measures and Opex reductions that have been implemented over the last several quarters.

Corporate Expense
Corporate expense increased by Ps.65.2 million, to Ps.122.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, from Ps.57.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase primarily reflected a higher share-based compensation expense, as well as an increase in other corporate expenses.
Share-based compensation expense in the third quarter of 2026 and 2025 amounted to Ps.99.1 million and Ps.72.3 million, respectively, and was accounted for as corporate expense. Share-based compensation expense is measured at fair value at the time the equity benefits are conditionally sold to officers and employees and is recognized over the vesting period.
Other Expense, Net
Other expense, net, increased by Ps.925.8 million, to Ps.969.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, from Ps.43.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. This increase reflected primarily (i) a non-recurring non-cash impairment loss in the carrying amount of certain technical equipment in our Enterprise operations; (ii) an increase in expenses related to legal and financial advisory services; and (iii) the absence in the third quarter of 2026 of a non-recurrent income from an insurance claim recovery in the third quarter of 2025. These unfavorable variances were partially offset by (i) a favorable change in gain or loss on disposition of equipment; and (ii) a lower non-recurring severance expense in connection with headcount reductions.

Transaction with AT&T
On October 13, 2026, we announced that a consortium owned 50% by each of the Company and Fintech Holdings Inc. (“Fintech” and, together with the Company, the “Consortium”) reached an agreement with AT&T, Inc. (“AT&T”) to acquire AT&T’s wireless telecommunications business in Mexico (“AT&T Mexico”) for a purchase price of U.S.$2 billion, subject to certain adjustments. The Consortium expects to finance the acquisition through a combination of cash on hand and non-recourse secured debt financing. We anticipate accounting for this acquisition as an investment in a joint venture by using the equity method, based on applicable IFRS Accounting Standards. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including receipt of regulatory approvals, and is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027. By the closing date of this transaction, we expect to make a cash contribution for an estimated amount of U.S.$400.0 million in connection with the acquisition of a 50% interest in AT&T México.

Sustainability
In 2026, we continued integrating sustainability into our business strategy and management, with a focus on the identification, assessment, and management of financially material risks and opportunities.
During the third quarter, we published our sustainability and climate-related disclosures aligned with IFRS S1 and IFRS S2 standards. As part of a phased adoption approach, and in accordance with applicable transitional relief provisions, our disclosures focused on climate-related risks, opportunities, and metrics that we consider material to users of the financial statements. This exercise strengthened our governance, risk management, and climate metrics monitoring processes, while further integrating the effects of climate change into our enterprise risk management framework and reinforcing our commitment to transparency with investors and other stakeholders. In addition, we published our voluntary sustainability report, “One network across generations”. This report expands the information provided on our environmental, social, and governance initiatives, performance, and progress.
Finally, as part of our commitment to transparency and continuous improvement in environmental disclosure, during the year we completed our participation in the Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP) questionnaire. CDP is an international non-profit organization that operates one of the most widely recognized global environmental disclosure systems, enabling investors, companies, cities, states, and regions to manage and communicate their environmental impacts, risks, and opportunities.

About Televisa
Grupo Televisa S.A.B. (“Televisa”) is a major telecommunications company that owns and operates one of the most significant cable network groups as well as a leading direct-to-home satellite pay television system in Mexico. Televisa’s cable networks offer integrated services, including high-speed data, video, mobile, and voice to residential and commercial customers as well as telecommunications managed services to domestic and international enterprises. Televisa also offers pay television and broadband services through its direct-to-home satellite system. Televisa holds a number of concessions by the Mexican government that authorizes it to broadcast programming over television stations for the signals of TelevisaUnivision, Inc. (“TelevisaUnivision”), and Televisa’s cable networks and satellite system. In addition, Televisa is the largest shareholder of TelevisaUnivision, a leading media company producing, creating, and distributing Spanish-speaking content through several broadcast channels in Mexico, the U.S. and, over 50 countries through television networks, cable operators, and over-the-top or OTT services.
