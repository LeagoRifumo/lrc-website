Otter Tail Corporation Announces Third Quarter Results and Affirms the Midpoint of Annual Earnings Guidance
FERGUS FALLS, Minnesota - Otter Tail Corporation (Nasdaq: OTTR) today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
SUMMARY
•Produced quarterly diluted earnings per share of $1.41.
•Affirmed the midpoint of our annual earnings guidance and narrowed the range.
CEO OVERVIEW
“Our team members continue to deliver on our strategic growth plan and near-term priorities amidst a challenging operating environment,” said CEO Chuck MacFarlane.
“Otter Tail Power continues to perform well, as our team is executing on our regulatory priorities and robust rate base growth plan. We are making progress on our ongoing Minnesota rate case and our integrated resource plan filed with the Minnesota Public Utilities Commission earlier this year. Our team is advancing our ongoing renewable generation and transmission capital investment projects despite increasing challenges in transmission development. We are focused on strong project execution for the benefit of our customers and shareholders.
“We are encouraged by increasing sales volumes within our Manufacturing segment. End market demand continues to improve and our team is effectively executing on our strategy of providing value-added service to our customers. Our businesses are being impacted by inflationary cost pressures and the challenge of responding to increasing customer demand.
“As expected, Plastics segment financial results for the quarter were negatively impacted by lower sales volumes and a continued decline in the average sales price of our PVC pipe. Sales volumes decreased as our customers advanced their purchases of our PVC pipe earlier this year in response to announced resin price increases. In addition, a softening housing market also contributed to the decline in volumes for the period. Despite the quarterly decrease, our team continues to effectively leverage the large diameter capacity added at our PVC pipe facility in Arizona, helping to offset the decrease.
“We remain well positioned to deliver on our financial objectives for the year. We are maintaining the midpoint and narrowing the range of our 2026 annual earnings guidance.
“The fundamentals of our diversified portfolio remain strong, and we are confident in our ability to deliver on our strategic growth plan over the long-term. We continue to target a long-term earnings per share growth rate of 7 to 9 percent and a total shareholder return of 10 to 12 percent.”
QUARTERLY DIVIDEND
On November 2, 2026, the corporation’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly common stock dividend of $0.5775 per share. This dividend is payable on December 10, 2026 to shareholders of record on November 13, 2026.
CASH FLOWS AND LIQUIDITY
Consolidated cash provided by operating activities was $307.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $288.9 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily driven by lower working capital requirements, largely reflecting the timing of the recovery of fuel costs and rider revenues from our utility customers.
Investing activities for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 included $451.6 million of capital expenditures. Our capital investments were largely within our Electric segment and primarily related to our Abercrombie and Solway solar projects, wind repowering initiative, and transmission and distribution system investments.
Financing activities for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 included the issuance of $170.0 million of long-term debt by Otter Tail Power, the proceeds of which were used to repay short-term borrowings, fund capital investments, and support operating activities. Financing activities for the period also included $72.8 million of dividend payments and $31.5 million of net repayments of short-terms borrowings.
As of September 30, 2026 available liquidity totaled $601.8 million, consisting of $170.0 million and $178.5 million available under our Otter Tail Corporation and Otter Tail Power credit facilities, respectively, and $253.3 million of cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities.

Operating Revenues decreased $3.6 million, primarily due to an $11.9 million aggregate decrease in fuel recovery revenues and the impact of increased production tax credits, neither of which affects net income. These decreases were partially offset by a $5.9 million increase from rate increases in Minnesota and South Dakota, a $2.7 million increase from the recovery of additional rate base investments, and a $2.3 million increase from favorable weather.
Net Income increased $0.1 million as the benefits of interim rate increases, favorable weather, the recovery of additional rate base investments, and higher allowance for funds used during construction associated with continued investments in our large solar projects were largely offset by higher operating and maintenance expenses, including increased labor, vegetation management, and plant outage-related costs, as well as higher depreciation and interest expense associated with rate base growth.

Operating Revenues increased $12.8 million primarily due to higher sales volumes and the pass through of increased steel costs. Sales volumes increased 13%, reflecting improved demand in the construction, recreational vehicle, lawn and garden, and industrial markets after experiencing softer market conditions and customer inventory reduction efforts during the same period last year.
Net Income decreased $1.3 million driven by higher selling, general, and administrative expenses and lower gross profit margins. Gross margins were negatively impacted by inflationary cost pressures and lower productivity as we added headcount to meet growing demand. These impacts were partially offset by higher sales volumes and increased scrap sales.

Operating Revenues decreased $14.2 million compared to the same period last year, reflecting a 9% decrease in average sales prices and a 5% decrease in sales volumes. Average sales prices continued to moderate from the peak level reached in late 2022. Lower sales volumes were primarily driven by the timing of customer purchases, including purchases made earlier in the year ahead of announced PVC resin cost increases, and softer demand for our products in certain regional markets due, in part, to softness in the housing market. Increased production capacity at our Phoenix facility partially offset the volume declines.
Net Income decreased $14.5 million, primarily due to lower sales prices and volumes, as discussed above, and a 10% increase in PVC resin and other input material costs, primarily driven by global PVC resin supply and demand dynamics.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES
This press release includes certain adjusted financial measures (non-GAAP financial measures). The Company believes these non-GAAP financial measures, when reviewed in conjunction with GAAP financial measures, can provide more information to assist investors in evaluating current period performance and in assessing future performance. For these reasons, internal management reporting also includes non-GAAP financial measures.
We use Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Earnings per Share, and Adjusted Return on Equity in evaluating the operating performance and profitability of our business. Management believes that these measures provide useful information to investors by facilitating period-to-period comparisons of operating results excluding the effects of the legal settlement expense and related income tax benefit. We define Adjusted Net Income as net income excluding legal settlement expenses and the related income tax benefit. We define Adjusted Earnings per Share as diluted net income per share excluding the per share impact of legal settlement expenses and the related income tax benefit. We define Adjusted Return on Equity as annual Adjusted Net Income divided by the average of total consolidated shareholders’ equity excluding the impact of legal settlement expenses and the related income tax benefit.
These non-GAAP financial measures should be considered in addition to, and not superior to or as a substitute for, the GAAP financial measures presented in this release and the Company's financial statements and other publicly filed reports. Non-GAAP financial measures presented in this release may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies. Investors are encouraged to review the reconciliations of adjusted financial measures used in this release to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures. Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share are reconciled to their most directly comparable GAAP measures in the non-GAAP Reconciliations section. Guidance for Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share and Adjusted Return on Equity are forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures that are reconciled to their respective most directly comparable GAAP financial measures in footnote (1) under 2026 Outlook.
