WOLVERINE WORLDWIDE REPORTS
THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
ROCKFORD, Mich., November 12, 2026 – Wolverine World Wide, Inc. (NYSE: WWW) today reported financial results for the third quarter ended October 3, 2026.
"We delivered a strong third quarter performance, led by our two largest brands and disciplined execution across the business," said Chris Hufnagel, President and Chief Executive Officer of Wolverine Worldwide. "This quarter, now our eighth consecutive quarter of growth, is further evidence that our transformation story is also a growth story – with continued earnings improvement and a healthier balance sheet providing us greater capacity to invest behind our brands and the opportunities we see ahead. While we continue to navigate a dynamic environment – based on our year-to-date results and our expectations for the final quarter of the year – we're raising our outlook for 2026."

During the third quarter of 2026, the Company received refunds of $35.3 million, including interest, related to International Emergency Economic Powers Act ("IEEPA") tariffs paid by the Company. As of October 3, 2026, $32.6 million was recorded as a reduction to cost of goods sold, $1.3 million was recorded as a reduction to interest expense, and $1.4 million was recorded as a reduction to inventory. In the third quarter of 2026, the IEEPA tariff refunds benefited gross margin, operating margin, and adjusted operating margin by approximately 640 basis points each and, net of tax, diluted earnings per share and adjusted diluted earnings per share by approximately $0.31 each.
Gross margin was 54.6% compared to 47.5% in the prior year.
Operating margin was 17.7% compared to 8.4% in the prior year, and adjusted operating margin was 17.8% compared to 9.1% in the prior year.
Diluted earnings per share was $0.78 compared to $0.30 in the prior year, and adjusted diluted earnings per share was $0.79 compared to $0.36 in the prior year.

FULL-YEAR 2026 OUTLOOK
The Company's outlook reflects the impact of foreign currency and includes the benefit of IEEPA tariff refunds received in the third quarter. The Company's previous outlook did not include any benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds. Additionally, fiscal 2026 is a 52-week year and fiscal 2025 was a 53-week year, which will affect annual comparisons.
For fiscal year 2026, the Company currently expects:
•Revenue to be approximately $2.000 billion to $2.010 billion, representing growth of approximately 6.7% to 7.2% compared to 2025, constant currency growth of approximately 5.9% to 6.4%, and constant currency growth of approximately 6.6% to 7.2% excluding the impact of the 53rd week in 2025. This compares to the previous outlook for revenue of approximately $1.980 billion to $2.000 billion.
•Gross margin to be approximately 48.7%, up 140 basis points compared to 2025, including an approximate 170 basis point benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds. This compares to the previous outlook for gross margin of approximately 46.9%.
•Operating margin to be approximately 11.5%, up 350 basis points compared to 2025, and adjusted operating margin to be approximately 11.8%, up 280 basis points compared to 2025, each including an approximate 170 basis point benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds. This compares to the previous outlook for operating margin of approximately 9.5% and adjusted operating margin of approximately 9.9%.
•Effective tax rate to be approximately 19.0%. This compares to the previous outlook for effective tax rate of approximately 18.0%.
•Diluted earnings per share in the range of $1.88 to $1.92 and adjusted diluted earnings per share in the range of $1.95 to $1.99, each including an approximate $0.32 benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds. This compares to the previous outlook for diluted earnings per share in the range of $1.48 to $1.58 and adjusted diluted earnings per share in the range of $1.55 to $1.65.
•Diluted weighted average shares of approximately 82.5 million. This compares to the previous outlook for diluted weighted average shares of approximately 82.0 million.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES
Measures referred to in this release as “adjusted” financial results are non-GAAP measures. Adjusted financial results exclude legal settlement costs, environmental and other related costs net of recoveries, reorganization costs and financing transaction costs. The Company also presents constant currency information, which is a non-GAAP measure that excludes the impact of fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates. The Company calculates constant currency basis by converting the current-period local currency financial results using the prior period exchange rates and comparing these adjusted amounts to the Company's prior period reported results. The Company believes providing each of these non-GAAP measures provides valuable supplemental information regarding its results of operations, consistent with how the Company evaluates performance. The Company also presents constant currency revenue growth excluding the impact of the 53rd week of fiscal 2025, which further excludes revenue recognized in the 53rd week of fiscal 2025, because the Company believes it improves the comparability of a 52-week year to a 53-week year.
The Company has provided a reconciliation of each of the above non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure. The Company believes these non-GAAP measures provide useful information to both management and investors because they increase the comparability of current period results to prior period results by adjusting for certain items that may not be indicative of core operating results and enable better identification of trends in our business. The adjusted financial results are used by management to, and allow investors to, evaluate the operating performance of the Company on a comparable basis. Management does not, nor should investors, consider such non-GAAP financial measures in isolation from, or as a substitute for, financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP.

ABOUT WOLVERINE WORLDWIDE
Wolverine World Wide, Inc. (NYSE: WWW) is a global footwear and apparel company driven by a vision to Make. Every Day. Better. Founded in 1883, the Company builds and grows iconic brands including Merrell®, Saucony®, Wolverine®, Sweaty Betty®, Chaco®, Hush Puppies®, Stride Rite®, HYTEST®, and Bates®, along with footwear licenses for Cat® and Harley-Davidson®. Headquartered in Rockford, Michigan, the Company’s products are sold in approximately 170 countries and territories worldwide. For more information, visit www.wolverineworldwide.com.
