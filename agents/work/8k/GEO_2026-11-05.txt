THE GEO GROUP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
AND UPDATES FULL YEAR 2026 GUIDANCE
• | 3Q26 Revenues Increased 12% to $761.1 Million

• | 3Q26 Net Income Attributable to GEO Operations of $49.2 Million

• | 3Q26 Adjusted Net Income Increased 48% to $51.8 Million

• | 3Q26 Adjusted EBITDA Increased 21% to $145.3 Million

• | Guidance for FY26 Revenues of $2.975-$3.025 Billion

• | Guidance for FY26 Net Income Attributable to GEO Operations Increased to $726.3-$730.5 Million, Including Gain on Sale of Adelanto, Calif. ICE Complex

• | Guidance for FY26 Adjusted Net Income Increased to $185-$190 Million, or $1.40-$1.44 Per Diluted Share

• | Guidance for FY26 Adjusted EBITDA Increased to $568-$573 Million

Boca Raton, Fla. – November 5, 2026 — The GEO Group, Inc. (NYSE: GEO) (“GEO”, “we” or the “Company”), a leading provider of contracted support services for secure facilities, processing centers, and reentry centers, as well as enhanced in-custody rehabilitation, post-release support, and electronic monitoring programs, reported its financial results for the third quarter 2026 and updated fourth quarter 2026 and full year 2026 financial guidance.
For the third quarter 2026, we reported total revenues of $761.1 million compared to $682.3 million for the third quarter 2025, reflecting a 12 percent increase. We reported third quarter 2026 net income attributable to GEO Operations of $49.2 million, or $0.37 per diluted share, compared to net income attributable to GEO Operations of $173.9 million, or $1.24 per diluted share, for the third quarter 2025, which included a gain on asset divestitures and other unusual or non-recurring items.
Third quarter 2026 results reflect $3.5 million, pre-tax, in combined non-cash contingent liability and litigation and settlement costs, start-up expenses, transaction fees, employee restructuring expenses, and close-out expenses. Excluding these items, we reported adjusted net income for the third quarter 2026 of $51.8 million, or $0.39 per diluted share, compared to $35.0 million, or $0.25 per diluted share, for the third quarter 2025, reflecting a 48 percent increase.
We reported third quarter 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of $145.3 million, compared to $120.1 million for the third quarter 2025, reflecting a 21 percent increase.

Results for the First Nine Months of 2026
For the first nine months of 2026, we reported total revenues of $2.2 billion compared to $1.9 billion for the first nine months of 2025, reflecting a 14 percent increase. We reported net income attributable to GEO Operations for the first nine months of 2026 of $135.1 million, or $1.02 per diluted share, compared to net income attributable to GEO Operations of $222.6 million, or $1.58 per diluted share, for the first nine months of 2025, which included a gain on asset divestitures and other unusual or non-recurring items.
Results for the first nine months of 2026 reflect $5.6 million, pre-tax, in combined loss on asset divestitures/impairment, non-cash contingent liability and litigation and settlement costs, start-up expenses, transaction fees, employee restructuring expenses, and close-out expenses. Excluding these items, we reported adjusted net income for the first nine months of 2026 of $139.2 million, or $1.05 per diluted share, compared to $85.3 million, or $0.61 per diluted share, for the first nine months of 2025, reflecting a 63 percent increase.
We reported Adjusted EBITDA for the first nine months of 2026 of $418.7 million, compared to $338.5 million for the first nine months of 2025, reflecting a 24 percent increase.

Operational Highlights
We entered into a five-year support services contract, effective July 9, 2026, with ICE for the activation of a federal immigration processing center at the 1,188-bed Big Horn Facility in Hudson, Colorado, while also entering into a lease agreement with the facility owner. The Big Horn Facility support services contract is expected to generate approximately $85 million in annual revenues in the first full year of operations.
We entered into a five-year support services contract, effective August 1, 2026, with ICE for the activation of a federal immigration processing center at our GEO-owned, 1,320-bed Rivers Facility in Winton, North Carolina. The Rivers Facility support services contract is expected to generate approximately $80 million in annual revenues in the first full year of operations.
The City of Big Spring, Texas entered into a 10-year intergovernmental service agreement with ICE, effective November 1, 2026, for the activation of a federal immigration processing center at the 2,329-bed Big Spring Complex in Texas (the “Big Spring Complex”), consisting of the 1,404-bed Flightline Facility and the 925-bed Cedar Hill Facility. GEO will provide support services at the Big Spring Complex, which is expected to generate approximately $200 million in annual revenues for GEO in the first full year of operations.
Under all three contracts, ICE will reimburse GEO for the capital expenditures needed to reactivate these four facilities, as well as provide funding for start-up expenses during the activation period. We expect the activation of the Big Horn Facility and Rivers Facility to be completed by the end of 2026, with both facilities expected to achieve normalized operations and earnings contribution in early 2027, while the activation of the Big Spring Complex is expected to be completed by the end of the first quarter of 2027, with normalized operations and earnings contribution expected in the second quarter of 2027.
We entered into a new 10-year management contract, effective October 28, 2026, with ICE for the continued provision of support services at the GEO-owned 1,635-bed Northwest ICE Processing Center located in Tacoma, Washington. The new 10-year contract is expected to generate approximately $140 million in annual revenues for GEO and continue through October 27, 2036.

Facility Sales
On October 2, 2026, we completed the sales of the 1,280-bed Adelanto West ICE Processing Center, 660-bed Adelanto East ICE Processing Center, and 704-bed Desert View Annex located in Adelanto, California (the “Adelanto Complex”) to the United States of America and its assigns, by and through the Department of Homeland Security, for an aggregate gross sales price of $950 million, with net proceeds of approximately $705 million after federal and state taxes and transaction fees and expenses. We expect to continue providing support services under our existing contract with ICE for the Adelanto Complex, which has a full term effective through December 19, 2034.
In addition to these completed sales, we remain engaged in an active process for the sale of multiple other company-owned facilities to ICE, subject to mutual agreement on price and GEO’s continued management of those facilities under long-term support services contracts. At this time, there is no definitive agreement in place and no precise timeline for the closing of any additional transactions, and we can provide no assurance that any additional transactions will occur.
Financial Guidance
Today, we updated our financial guidance for the fourth quarter and full year 2026. For the fourth quarter 2026, we increased our Net Income Attributable to GEO Operations guidance to a range of $591 million to $595.5 million, which includes the gain on sale of the Adelanto Complex. We increased our fourth quarter 2026 Adjusted Net Income guidance to a range of $46 million to $51 million, or a range of $0.35 to $0.39 per diluted share, on quarterly revenues of $777 million to $827 million. We increased our fourth quarter 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance to a range of $149 million to $154 million.
For the full year 2026, we increased Net Income Attributable to GEO Operations guidance to a range of $726.3 million to $730.5 million, which includes the gain on sale of the Adelanto Complex in the fourth quarter of 2026. We increased full year 2026 Adjusted Net Income guidance to a range of $185 million to $190 million, or a range of $1.40 to $1.44 per diluted share, on annual revenues of $2.975 billion to $3.025 billion and based on an effective tax rate of approximately 28 percent, inclusive of known discrete items. We increased our full year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance to a range of $568 million to $573 million. We expect total unreimbursed capital expenditures for the full year 2026 to be between $140 million and $150 million.

Balance Sheet
On October 5, 2026, we entered into an Amended and Restated Credit Agreement to extend the maturity of our $550 million Revolving Credit Facility to July 14, 2031 and to increase our restricted payments capacity, including share repurchases.
On October 15, 2026, we used the proceeds from the sale of the Adelanto Complex to complete the redemption of our $650 million, 8.625% Senior Secured Notes due 2029 (the “2029 Senior Secured Notes”). Following the redemption of the 2029 Senior Secured Notes, our outstanding corporate debt totals $625 million, consisting of our 10.250% Senior Unsecured Notes due 2031 (the “2031 Senior Unsecured Notes”).
Our available liquidity is approximately $600 million, including cash on hand and Revolving Credit Facility availability.
We are permitted to make unlimited restricted payments, including share repurchases, under our Credit Agreement, to the extent the Company’s total leverage ratio (as defined therein) calculated on a pro forma basis after giving effect to such restricted payment would be equal to or less than 2.25 to 1.00 and no default exists thereunder.
Additionally, under the Indenture governing the 2031 Senior Unsecured Notes, we are permitted to make unlimited restricted payments, including share repurchases, to the extent the Company’s consolidated total leverage ratio (as defined therein) calculated on a pro forma basis after giving effect to such restricted payment would be equal to or less than 2.00 to 1.00 and no default exists thereunder.
Share Repurchase Program
On October 5, 2026, we announced that our Board of Directors approved a $750 million increase to our share repurchase program, from $500 million to $1.25 billion, which is effective through December 31, 2029.
As of November 5, 2026, we had repurchased approximately 10.1 million shares of GEO common stock at an aggregate cost of approximately $177 million under our $1.25 billion share repurchase authorization, bringing our current outstanding share count to approximately 132 million and leaving approximately $1.07 billion of repurchase authorization available under the share repurchase program.

About The GEO Group
The GEO Group, Inc. (NYSE: GEO) is a leading diversified government service provider, specializing in design, financing, development, and support services for secure facilities, processing centers, and community reentry centers in the United States, Australia, South Africa, and the United Kingdom. GEO’s diversified services include enhanced in-custody rehabilitation and post-release support through the award-winning GEO Continuum of Care®, secure transportation, electronic monitoring, community-based programs, and correctional health and mental health care. GEO’s worldwide operations include the ownership and/or delivery of support services for 97 facilities totaling approximately 76,000 beds, including idle facilities and projects under development, with a workforce of up to approximately 20,000 employees.

EBITDA is defined as net income adjusted by adding provisions for income tax, interest expense, net of interest income, and depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as EBITDA adjusted for (gain)/loss on asset divestitures/impairment, pre-tax, net loss attributable to noncontrolling interests, stock-based compensation expenses, pre-tax, non-cash contingent liability and litigation settlement costs, pre-tax, start-up expenses, pre-tax, transaction fees, pre-tax, employee restructuring expenses, pre-tax, close-out expenses, pre-tax, other non-cash revenue and expenses, pre-tax, and certain other adjustments as defined from time to time.
Given the nature of our business as a real estate owner and support services provider, we believe that EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are helpful to investors as measures of our operational performance because they provide an indication of our ability to incur and service debt, to satisfy general operating expenses, to make capital expenditures, and to fund other cash needs or reinvest cash into our business.
We believe that by removing the impact of our asset base (primarily depreciation and amortization) and excluding certain non-cash charges, amounts spent on interest and taxes, and certain other charges that are highly variable from year to year, EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA provide our investors with performance measures that reflect the impact to operations from trends in occupancy rates, per diem rates and operating costs, providing a perspective not immediately apparent from net income.
The adjustments we make to derive the non-GAAP measures of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA exclude items which may cause short-term fluctuations in income from continuing operations and which we do not consider to be the fundamental attributes or primary drivers of our business plan and they do not affect our overall long-term operating performance. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA provide disclosure on the same basis as that used by our management and provide consistency in our financial reporting, facilitate internal and external comparisons of our historical operating performance and our business units and provide continuity to investors for comparability purposes.
Adjusted Net Income is defined as net income attributable to GEO operations adjusted for certain items which by their nature are not comparable from period to period or that tend to obscure GEO’s actual operating performance, including for the periods presented (gain)/loss on asset divestitures/impairment, pre-tax, loss on extinguishment of debt, pre-tax, non-cash contingent liability and litigation and settlement costs, pre-tax, start-up expenses, pre-tax, transaction fees, pre-tax, employee restructuring expenses, pre-tax, close-out expenses, pre-tax, and the tax effect of adjustments to net income attributable to GEO operations.
