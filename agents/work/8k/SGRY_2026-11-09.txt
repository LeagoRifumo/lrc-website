SURGERY PARTNERS, INC. ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
BRENTWOOD, Tenn., November 9, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) - Surgery Partners, Inc. (NASDAQ:SGRY) (“Surgery Partners” or the “Company”), a leading short-stay surgical facility owner and operator, today announced results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
(All comparisons are year-over-year unless otherwise noted; results reflect the ownership of the Idaho Falls facilities through September 13, 2026)
•Revenue decreased (0.8)% for the third quarter
◦Same-facility revenues increased 6.0% for the third quarter
◦Same-facility cases increased 0.2% for the third quarter
•Net loss attributable to Surgery Partners, Inc. was $21.1 million for the third quarter
◦Adjusted EBITDA was $132.7 million for the third quarter
Eric Evans, Chief Executive Officer, stated, “Our financial results in the third quarter reflect the impact of the successful Idaho Falls divestiture and sustained momentum in our core business, underscoring the value of our short-stay surgical platform. Additionally, the recently announced amended merger agreement with HealthSpan Partners serves as further validation of our unique offering, while representing a compelling outcome for our patients, physicians, stockholders, and other stakeholders. We are excited to move forward with this next chapter as we continue to deliver high-quality care to all our patients and support our physicians, colleagues and health system partners.”
Transaction with HealthSpan Partners
On November 9, 2026, Surgery Partners announced an amendment to its previously announced merger agreement with HealthSpan Partners, following Surgery Partners’ receipt of an unsolicited competing proposal from a third party. The amendment increases the merger consideration to $25.00 per share in cash, representing an enterprise value of up to approximately $6.0 billion. It further provides that the Company’s stockholders (other than affiliates of Bain Capital Private Equity) will receive additional monthly cash payments (the “Ticking Fee”) in the event the requisite Company stockholder approval has been obtained but the merger between HealthSpan Partners and Surgery Partners has not occurred by March 1, 2027, which payments will be paid in equal monthly installments in arrears at a rate equal to 10% per annum on the $25.00 per share merger consideration, among other updates. The Merger is still expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to the approval of Surgery Partners stockholders, the receipt of regulatory approvals and satisfaction of other customary closing conditions.
In light of the pending transaction with HealthSpan Partners, and as is customary during the pendency of such transactions, Surgery Partners will not host an earnings conference call and will no longer provide forward-looking guidance.
Third Quarter 2026 Results
Revenues for the third quarter of 2026 decreased (0.8)% to $815.3 million compared to $821.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. Same-facility revenues for the third quarter of 2026 increased 6.0% compared to the same period in the prior year, with a 5.8% increase in revenue per case and a 0.2% increase in same-facility cases. For the third quarter of 2026, the Company’s Adjusted EBITDA was $132.7 million, compared to $136.4 million for the same period in 2025.
Year-to-Date 2026 Results
Revenues year-to-date 2026 increased 2.1% to $2,475.1 million compared to $2,423.7 million for the 2025 period. Same-facility revenues for year-to-date 2026 increased 4.6% compared to the prior year period, with a 4.2% increase in revenue per case and a 0.3% increase in same-facility cases. For year-to-date 2026, the Company’s Adjusted EBITDA was $360.2 million, compared to $369.3 million for the same period last year.

Liquidity
Surgery Partners had cash and cash equivalents of $354.5 million and $692.8 million of borrowing capacity under its revolving credit facility as of September 30, 2026. Cash flows from operating activities were $103.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $83.6 million for the same period in 2025. Year-to-date, operating cash flows were $174.5 million compared to $170.9 million in the prior year period.
The Company’s ratio of total net debt to EBITDA, as calculated under the Company’s credit agreement, was approximately 4.4x at the end of the third quarter of 2026.
About Surgery Partners
Headquartered in Brentwood, Tennessee, Surgery Partners is a leading healthcare services company with a differentiated outpatient delivery model focused on providing high quality, cost effective solutions for surgical and related ancillary care in support of both patients and physicians. Founded in 2004, Surgery Partners is one of the largest and fastest growing surgical services businesses in the country, with more than 200 locations in 30 states, including ambulatory surgery centers, surgical hospitals, multi-specialty physician practices and urgent care facilities.
To learn more about Surgery Partners, please visit the Company's website at www.surgerypartners.com. Surgery Partners uses its website as a channel of distribution for material Company information. Financial and other material information regarding Surgery Partners is routinely posted on the Company's website and is readily accessible.
