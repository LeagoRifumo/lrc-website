dLocal Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
TPV reached US$18 billion (+77% year-over-year), marking the 8th consecutive quarter of 50%+ year-over-year growth. Revenue reached US$398 million (+41% year-over-year), with gross profit of US$132 million (+28% year-over-year).
Operating profit reached US$76 million (+37% year-over-year), with the Operating Profit/Gross Profit ratio at 58% (+7 p.p. vs. 2Q26), its highest level since 4Q23. Gross profit growth exceeded OPEX growth (+17% year-over-year), reflecting continued cost discipline and automation efforts.
Record net income of US$68 million (+31% year-over-year).
Adjusted Free Cash Flow reached US$182 million in 9M26, with conversion to net income of 111%, reflecting the reversal of temporary 1Q26 working-capital effects.
Montevideo, Uruguay, November 12, 2026 — DLocal Limited (“dLocal”, “we”, “us”, and “our”) (NASDAQ:DLO), a leading cross-border financial infrastructure platform connecting global merchants to emerging markets, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
dLocal’s management team will host a conference call and audio webcast on November 12, 2026 at 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time. Please click here to pre-register for the conference call and obtain your dial in number and passcode.
The live conference call can be accessed via audio webcast at the investor relations section of dLocal’s website, at https://investor.dlocal.com/. An archive of the webcast will be available for a year following the conclusion of the conference call. The investor presentation will also be filed on EDGAR at www.sec.gov.
“We delivered another quarter of consistent execution, supported by deepening merchant relationships, improving operating leverage and solid cash generation. TPV reached $18.3 billion, up 77% year-over-year, marking our 8th consecutive quarter above 50% growth, and our 4th consecutive at or above 70%. Net revenue retention was 140%, reflecting the continued strength of our business with the existing merchant base. Gross profit grew 28% year-over-year to $132 million, while operating profit increased 37% to $76 million. The strong operating leverage we expected in the second half of the year is materializing,” said Pedro Arnt, CEO of dLocal.
Third quarter 2026 financial highlights
dLocal reports in US dollars and in accordance with IFRS as issued by the IASB
1.Total Payment Volume (“TPV”) reached US$18.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 77% year-over-year compared to US$10.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and up 4% compared to US$17.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026. In constant currency, TPV growth for the period would have been 68% year-over-year.
2.Revenues amounted to US$398.0 million, up 41% year-over-year compared to US$282.5 million in the third quarter of 2025 and almost flat compared to US$399.7 million in the second quarter of 2026. In constant currency, revenue growth for the period would have been 36% year-over-year. The quarterly comparison was impacted by a higher share of local-to-local and pay-out volumes, and softer volumes in Argentina, following the seasonal Hot Sale peak in the second quarter. Excluding Argentina, revenue would have grown approx. 1% quarter-over- quarter.
3.Gross profit was US$132.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, a new record, up 28% compared to US$103.2 million in the third quarter of 2025 and up 4% compared to US$127.2 million in the second quarter of 2026. In constant currency, gross profit growth for the period would have been 24% year-over-year. Quarterly performance was driven by lower advancement costs in Argentina, and broad-based volume growth across Africa and Asia combined with one-off cost recovery in Nigeria and FX hedge gains. These gains were partly offset by (i) shift in payment method mix in Brazil; (ii) higher processing costs in Mexico, including cost catch-ups from prior periods; and (iii) mix shift towards local-to-local flows in Other LatAm markets and lower volumes in Bolivia.
4.As a result, gross profit margin was 33% in this quarter, compared to 37% in the third quarter of 2025 and 32% in the second quarter of 2026.
5.Gross profit over TPV was at 0.72%, decreasing from 0.99% in the third quarter of 2025 and flat compared to the second quarter of 2026, reflecting mix shifts (as described previously), and the natural margin dynamics of scaling volume with merchants, offset mostly by lower advancement costs in Argentina and cost recovery in Nigeria.
6.Operating expenses reached US$55.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, up 17% year-over-year and down 11% quarter-over-quarter. The year-over-year increase reflects the annualization of investments made in the second half of 2025. Sequentially, disciplined cost management, with lower third-party spend, lower headcount, and continued benefits from our automation agenda supported the decline.
7.As a result, Operating profit was US$76.2 million, up 37% year-over-year and 19% quarter-over-quarter. The Operating Profit to Gross Profit ratio was 58%, up 7 p.p. quarter-over-quarter compared to 50% as reported in the second quarter of 2026, and up 4 p.p. year-over-year compared to 54% as reported in the third quarter of 2025.
8.Net financial result (Finance income minus finance costs) was a US$5.3 million gain, compared to a net financial gain of US$6.4 million in the third quarter of 2025 and US$2.3 million in the second quarter of 2026.
9.Our effective income tax rate for the period was approximately 15%, in line with the third quarter of 2025 and close to the 16% for the second quarter of 2026.
10.Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was US$68.0 million, or US$0.23 per diluted share, up 31% compared to a profit of US$51.8 million, or US$0.17 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025, and up 24% compared to a profit of US$54.8 million, or US$0.18 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2026. The quarterly comparison is explained by higher operating profit and higher finance income, given higher interest income on our cash balances, despite higher interest expense related to the US$150 million loan signed in August.
11.Adjusted free cash flow for the third quarter of 2026 amounted to US$99.1 million, up 163% year-over-year compared to US$37.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, and up 44% compared to US$68.5 million in the second quarter of 2026. The increase reflects higher operating profit and the reversal of temporary working-capital effects from the first quarter of 2026.
12.As of September 30, 2026, dLocal had US$1,033.9 million in total cash and cash equivalents, which includes US$536.9 million of Corporate cash and cash equivalents. The Corporate cash and cash equivalents increased by US$203.8 million from US$333.1 million as of September 30, 2025. When compared to the US$369.1 million Corporate cash and cash equivalents position as of June 30, 2026, it increased by US$167.8 million quarter-over- quarter, explained by the US$150 million loan signed in August. Under the US$300 million program authorized in March 2026, the Company has repurchased approximately 9.1 million Class A shares for US$120.1 million through the end of the third quarter.

Adjusted Free Cash Flow reconciliation
We calculate “Adjusted Free Cash Flow” as net cash (used in) / generated from cash flows from operating activities, less (i) changes in working capital (merchant), and (ii) capital expenditures. The working capital (merchant) is defined as (i) changes in Trade receivables net (disclosed in Note 17 to our consolidated financial statements for the period ended September 30, 2026), plus (ii) changes in Trade payables (disclosed in Note 20 to our consolidated financial statements for the period ended September 30, 2026), plus (iii) changes in Other tax liabilities (disclosed in note 21 to our consolidated financial statements for the period ended September 30, 2026). Capital expenditures consist of acquisitions of property, plant and equipment and additions of intangible assets.
Management uses Adjusted Free Cash Flow as a measure for evaluating the Company's cash generation and the cash available for distribution to our shareholders as dividends pursuant to our dividend policy. Adjusted Free Cash Flow is not a financial measure recognized under IFRS and does not purport to be an alternative to cash generated from operating activities or as a measure of liquidity. Our presentation of Adjusted Free Cash Flow has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider it in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under IFRS. See below for a reconciliation of our Adjusted Free Cash Flow to the nearest IFRS measure.

About dLocal
dLocal builds financial infrastructure for markets of the future, connecting global enterprises with billions of emerging market consumers in more than 60 countries across high-growth markets in Africa, Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America. Through the "One dLocal" concept (one direct API, one platform, and one contract), global companies can accept payments, send payouts, and settle funds globally without the need to manage multiple local entities and integrations. For more information, visit www.dlocal.com
