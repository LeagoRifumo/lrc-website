PROCEPT BioRobotics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Reaffirms 2026 Financial Guidance
SAN JOSE, Calif., November 4, 2026 -- PROCEPT BioRobotics® Corporation (Nasdaq: PRCT) (the “Company”), a surgical robotics company focused on advancing patient care by developing transformative solutions in urology, today reported unaudited financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“As we enter the fourth quarter, we remain focused on executing against our full-year revenue guidance and profitability plan,” said Larry Wood, Chief Executive Officer. “Further, we remain excited to present data from WATER IV, our randomized trial comparing Aquablation to radical prostatectomy in prostate cancer, on track for the first half of 2027. We see significant opportunity and a clear path to achieving durable growth and profitability ahead.”
Wood continued, “This quarter, we continued expanding our installed base and drove strong utilization in HYDROS® accounts. We delivered approximately 18% revenue growth and 22% U.S. procedure growth while accelerating our replacement cycle to transition AquaBeam systems to HYDROS.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Total revenue of $98.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 18% compared to the prior year period in 2025
•U.S. procedures over 13,400 for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of approximately 22% compared to the prior year period
•Delivered 62 U.S. HYDROS systems, which included 20 replacement systems. U.S. average selling price was approximately $495,000 for new HYDROS systems
•U.S. handpieces sold as percent of U.S. procedures in the third quarter of 2026 was approximately 106%
•Third quarter of 2026 handpiece average selling price of approximately $3,570 increased 12% compared to the third quarter of 2025
•International revenue of $12.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 32% compared to the prior year period
•Gross margin of 66% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 65% in the prior year period.
Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $98.3 million, an increase of 18% compared to the prior year period. The increase was driven by increased U.S. handpiece and system revenue and international revenue. U.S. revenue was $85.8 million, representing growth of 16% compared to the prior year period. U.S. handpiece and consumable revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $53.9 million, an increase of 21% compared to the prior year period. U.S. system revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $24.7 million, flat compared to the prior year period. International revenue was $12.5 million for the quarter, an increase of 32% compared to the prior year period.
Gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 66% compared to 65% in the prior year period.
Operating expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $92.5 million, compared with $77.2 million in the prior year period. The increase in operating expenses reflects continued investment in our people, product development, clinical studies and sales and marketing programs.
Net loss was $26.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a loss of $21.4 million in the prior year period. Adjusted EBITDA* was a loss of $9.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a loss of $7.4 million in the prior year period.
Cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash balances as of September 30, 2026, totaled approximately $221.4 million.
Full Year 2026 Financial Guidance
•The Company reiterates revenue for the full year 2026 to be in the range of $390 million to $410 million, which represents growth of 27% to 33% compared to the prior year period
•The Company reiterates U.S. procedure volume for the full year 2026 to be in the range of 54,000-56,000 which represents growth of 25% to 29% compared to the prior year period
•The Company reiterates gross margin for the full year 2026 to be approximately 65%
•The Company reiterates adjusted EBITDA* loss for the full year 2026 to be in the range of $35 million to $30 million

About PROCEPT BioRobotics Corporation
PROCEPT BioRobotics is a surgical robotics company focused on advancing patient care by developing transformative solutions in urology. PROCEPT BioRobotics manufactures the AQUABEAM® and HYDROS Robotic Systems. The HYDROS Robotic System is the only AI-powered, robotic technology that delivers Aquablation therapy. PROCEPT BioRobotics designed Aquablation therapy to deliver effective, safe, and durable outcomes for males suffering from lower urinary tract symptoms or LUTS, due to BPH that are independent of prostate size and shape or surgeon experience. BPH is the most common prostate disease and impacts approximately 40 million men in the United States. The Company has developed a significant and growing body of clinical evidence with approximately 250 peer-reviewed publications, supporting the benefits and clinical advantages of Aquablation therapy.
