Avalyn Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Recent Business Highlights
Initiated Phase 1 study of AP03, a fixed dose combination of nebulized pirfenidone and nintedanib, in healthy volunteers
Presented new AP01 data at ERS Congress and published detailed results from AP02 Phase 1 trials in Respiratory Research; data support Avalyn’s approach of delivering antifibrotic medicines directly to the lungs
Ended the third quarter of 2026 with $386.6 million in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities; cash runway projected into 2029
BOSTON, MA, November 12, 2026 -- Avalyn Pharma Inc. (Avalyn or the Company, Nasdaq: AVLN), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company pioneering inhaled therapies to transform the treatment paradigm of serious, rare respiratory diseases today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and recent business highlights.
“As we approach year end, I’m proud of the progress we’ve made across our portfolio and the strong foundation we’ve built for what we expect to be a transformational 2027, with data anticipated across all three of our programs,” said Lyn Baranowski, Chief Executive Officer of Avalyn. “We remain confident in the potential of inhaled therapies to transform the standard of care for pulmonary fibrosis by delivering effective, well-tolerated treatments both as monotherapies and in combination. With our monotherapy programs advancing through later stages of development, the initiation of our first-in-human study of our combination program, AP03, marks the next step in our portfolio expansion. Backed by a strong balance sheet and dedicated team, we are well-positioned to deliver on our key milestones and continue advancing our mission to address the significant unmet needs of people living with rare respiratory diseases.”
Recent Pipeline Progress
AP01 (inhaled pirfenidone)
•
European Respiratory Society (ERS) International Congress 2026. In September, the Company presented two posters at the ERS Congress in Barcelona, Spain. The first poster highlighted data modeling AP01 distribution throughout the target regions in both healthy and idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) lungs, demonstrating robust delivery to sites of fibrotic remodeling and active disease, even in the case of advanced fibrosis. The second poster described patient perspectives on IPF-focused clinical trial design and demonstrated a strong willingness of to participate in clinical trials among survey respondents. The presentations and related distribution modeling videos are accessible on the Company’s publications section of the website.
•
Results from the MIST study of AP01, a global Phase 2b clinical trial in patients with progressive pulmonary fibrosis (PPF) are anticipated in the second half of 2027.
AP02 (inhaled nintedanib)
•
Phase 1 Data Published in Respiratory Research. In September, Phase 1 data were published in Respiratory Research highlighting AP02’s lung-targeted delivery and encouraging tolerability profile. The findings demonstrated AP02’s potential to achieve substantially higher lung exposure and markedly lower systemic exposure as compared to oral nintedanib. AP02 was generally well-tolerated across both Phase 1 studies, with no serious adverse events reported. The paper, titled, “Nebulized nintedanib (AP02) for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis demonstrates favorable safety and lung lining fluid exposures: Results from two Phase 1 safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetic studies,” was published on September 21, 2026, and can be found on the Company’s publications section of the website.
•
Results from the AURA study of AP02, a global Phase 2 clinical trial in patients with IPF, are anticipated in late 2027.
AP03 (inhaled combination of pirfenidone and nintedanib)
•
Initiated Phase 1 Trial of AP03 in Healthy Volunteers. The Phase 1 trial is a randomized, double-blinded, placebo controlled, single-center study designed to assess the safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics of AP03 in healthy volunteers. It is expected to enroll at least 32 participants and will evaluate single ascending doses (SAD) and multiple ascending doses (MAD). The SAD cohort will begin with a combination dose of 50 mg pirfenidone plus 2 mg nintedanib, with additional doses to follow. Primary endpoints include safety and tolerability. Secondary endpoints will evaluate the pharmacokinetic profile of inhaled pirfenidone and nintedanib following single and multiple-dose administration.
•
The study is cleared to proceed in Australia following Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) and institutional approval, and notification to the Therapeutic Goods Administration under the Clinical Trial Notification (CTN) pathway. More information about the Phase 1 clinical trial ACTRN12626001341314 can be found here.
•
Results from the Phase 1 healthy volunteer study of AP03 are anticipated in the second half of 2027.

Robert J. Meyer, MD, Appointed to Avalyn’s Board of Directors. In September, we appointed Robert (Bob) J. Meyer, MD, a distinguished pulmonary physician and former senior U.S. Food and Drug Administration leader, to the Company’s Board of Directors.
Financial Results for Third Quarter 2026
Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $386.6 million
as of September 30, 2026. The Company’s current cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities are projected to be sufficient to fund operations into 2029.
Research & Development (R&D) Expenses: R&D expenses were $25.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $22.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in R&D expenses was primarily driven by the ongoing AP01 and AP02 clinical trials and the AP01 open-label extension study, SAIL.
General & Administrative (G&A) Expenses: G&A expenses were $7.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $3.2 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in G&A expenses was primarily driven by the increase of G&A headcount and personnel-related expenses, including stock-based compensation.
Net loss: Net loss was $29.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $23.6 million for the third quarter of 2025.
About Avalyn Pharma
Avalyn aims to transform the treatment paradigm for pulmonary fibrosis and other serious, rare respiratory diseases. The Company is advancing optimized inhaled formulations of established antifibrotic medicines designed to deliver drug directly to the lungs, enhance local efficacy, and reduce systemic side effects. Avalyn’s AP01 program is an optimized inhaled formulation of pirfenidone currently being evaluated in MIST, a global Phase 2b clinical trial in patients with progressive pulmonary fibrosis (PPF). AP01 has indicated encouraging safety and clinical activity across Phase 1b and multi-year open-label extension trials, with long-term data supporting the potential to preserve lung function while improving tolerability relative to historical oral pirfenidone. Avalyn’s AP02 program is an optimized inhaled formulation of nintedanib currently being evaluated in AURA, a global Phase 2 clinical trial in patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF). Avalyn is also advancing AP03, an inhaled fixed-dose combination of pirfenidone and nintedanib, designed to deliver multiple antifibrotic mechanisms through a single lung-targeted platform. By leveraging its proprietary drug-device approach and deep expertise in rare respiratory disease development, Avalyn aims to establish a new standard of care in pulmonary fibrosis through inhaled, lung-targeted therapies. For more information, please visit avalynpharma.com and follow the Company on LinkedIn.
