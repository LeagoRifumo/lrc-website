Primoris Services Corporation Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
​
Dallas, TX – November 3, 2026 – Primoris Services Corporation (NYSE: PRIM) (“Primoris” or the “Company”) today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided comments on the Company’s operational performance and outlook for the remainder of 2026.
​
For the third quarter of 2026, Primoris reported the following highlights (1):
● | Revenue of $2.0 billion, down $0.2 billion, or 10.4%, compared to the third quarter of 2025;

● | Net income of $51.3 million, or $0.95 per diluted share, a decrease of $43.3 million, or $0.78 per diluted share, from the third quarter of 2025;

● | Adjusted net income of $64.9 million, or $1.20 per diluted share, a decrease of $38.2 million, or $0.68 per diluted share, from the third quarter of 2025;

● | Adjusted earnings before interest, income taxes, depreciation, and amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA”) of $134.4 million, down $34.3 million, or 20.3%, from the third quarter of 2025;

● | Record total backlog of $14.1 billion, including $8.5 billion of total master service agreement (“MSA”) backlog;

● | Updating net income and Adjusted EBITDA guidance ranges to $65.2 million to $73.2 million and $290.0 million to $310.0 million, respectively, for the full year 2026; and

● | Updating diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) and Adjusted EPS guidance ranges to $1.20 to $1.35 and $2.10 to $2.25 per diluted share, respectively, for the full year 2026;

● | Announced share purchase authorization of up to $250 million of common shares.

(1) | Please refer to “Non-GAAP Measures” and Schedules 1, 2, 3 and 4 for the definitions and reconciliations of our Non-GAAP financial measures, including “Adjusted Net Income,” “Adjusted EPS” and “Adjusted EBITDA.”

​
“Our third quarter results reflect meaningful progress in completing the renewable energy projects that have weighed on our margins this year, while highlighting the continued strength and resilience of the broader Primoris platform,” said Koti Vadlamudi, President and Chief Executive Officer of Primoris. “As the last of our six previously discussed challenged projects moves closer to completion, we are increasingly confident that these impacts are soon to be behind us and that Primoris is well positioned to capitalize on the significant opportunities ahead.”
​
“We remain confident in the long-term fundamentals of the renewables market and Primoris’ position as a leading provider of utility-scale solar and battery storage solutions. Demand for reliable and affordable energy infrastructure remains robust, and we continue to see attractive opportunities across our end markets.”
​
“As we approach the end of a challenging year, we believe the foundation is in place for a return to growth in 2027. Supported by a record backlog, improved project execution, and sustained demand for power generation and utility infrastructure services, we are well positioned to return to profitable growth, expand margins, and create sustainable value for our customers, employees and shareholders,” he added.
​
Third Quarter 2026 Results Overview
Revenue was $2.0 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026, a decrease of $0.2 billion, or 10.4%, compared to the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower renewables revenue in the Energy segment, partially offset by growth in our Utilities segment. Operating income was $94.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, a decrease of $42.1 million, or 30.7%, compared to the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower revenue and margin in the Energy segment, partially offset by an increase in margin in the
​
Utilities segment. Gross profit as a percentage of revenue decreased to 10.5% for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to 10.8% for the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily a result of lower margins in the Energy segment.
​
During the third quarter of 2026, net income was $51.3 million compared to net income of $94.6 million in the prior year. Diluted EPS was $0.95 for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $1.73 for the same period in 2025. The decrease in net income and EPS can be largely attributed to lower operating income, from lower revenue and gross profit, and higher interest expense. Adjusted net income was $64.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $103.1 million for the same period in 2025. Adjusted diluted EPS was $1.20 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.88 for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA was $134.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $168.7 million for the same period in 2025.

Utilities Segment (“Utilities”): Revenue increased by $33.4 million, or 4.5%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the same period in 2025, primarily due to increased activity in our gas operations and power delivery businesses, partially offset by decreased activity in our communications business. Operating income for the three months ended September 30, 2026, increased by $15.0 million, or 27.2% compared to the same period in 2025 due to improved performance in our power delivery operations in 2026. Gross profit as a percentage of revenue was 13.0% for the three months ended September 30, 2026, up from 11.7% for the same period in 2025.
​
Energy Segment (“Energy”): Revenue decreased by $261.9 million, or 17.6%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily attributable to decreased renewable energy activity, due to slower than anticipated start of new projects and release of new work. The decrease was partially offset by the incremental impact from the May 1, 2026, acquisition of PayneCrest Electric, Inc. (“PayneCrest”), and an increase in power generation projects compared to 2025. Operating income for the three months ended September 30, 2026, decreased by $57.6 million, or 53.0%, compared to the same period in 2025, primarily due to lower revenue and gross margins.
Gross profit as a percentage of revenue decreased to 8.5% during the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to 10.1% in the same period in 2025, primarily due to the lagging effect of completing renewables projects that experienced operational challenges. In addition, we have had lower than anticipated volumes in 2026, which led to higher relative carrying costs for equipment and personnel.

Other Income Statement Information
Selling, general and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses were $109.0 million during the quarter ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $11.3 million compared to 2025. SG&A expense as a percentage of revenue increased to 5.6% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 4.5% in the third quarter of 2025 primarily driven by lower revenue and additional SG&A from the PayneCrest acquisition.
​
Interest expense, net for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, was $14.3 million compared to $7.0 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase of $7.3 million was primarily due to higher average debt and accounts receivable securitization facility balances, partially offset by a lower average interest rate. Interest expense, net for the full year 2026 is expected to be between $43 million and $47 million.
The effective tax rate on income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, of 30.3% differs from the U.S. federal statutory rate of 21.0% primarily due to state income taxes and nondeductible components of per diem expenses, partially offset by discrete tax benefits for equity compensation in the first nine months. We recorded income tax expense for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, of $19.3 million compared to $88.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The $69.6 million decrease in income tax expense is primarily driven by lower pre-tax income.
Outlook
The Company is updating its estimates for the year ending December 31, 2026. Net income is expected to be between $65.2 million and $73.2 million, or $1.20 and $1.35 per diluted share. Adjusted EPS is estimated in the range of $2.10 to $2.25 per diluted share. Adjusted EBITDA for the full year 2026 is expected to range from $290.0 million to $310.0 million.
​
The Company is targeting SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue in the low 6.0% range for the full year 2026. The Company’s targeted gross margins by segment are 10.0% to 12.0% for the Utilities segment and 6.0% to 8.0% for the Energy segment for the full year 2026. The Company expects its effective tax rate for 2026 to be approximately 33.0%, but it may vary depending on the mix of states in which the Company operates.

About Primoris
Primoris Services Corporation is a leading provider of critical infrastructure services to the utility, energy, and renewables markets throughout the United States and Canada. Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted EPS
Primoris defines Adjusted Net Income as net income (loss) adjusted for certain items including, (i) non‐cash stock‐based compensation expense; (ii) transaction/integration and related costs; (iii) asset impairment charges; (iv) changes in fair value of the Company’s interest rate swap; (v) change in fair value of contingent consideration liabilities; (vi) amortization of intangible assets; (vii) amortization of debt discounts and debt issuance costs; (viii) losses on extinguishment of debt; (ix) severance and restructuring charges; (x) selected (gains) charges that are unusual or non-recurring; and (xi) impact of changes in statutory tax rates.
