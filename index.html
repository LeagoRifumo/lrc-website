How a rating is made

Evidence first. Opinion second.

Every company goes through the same five steps. If the numbers don't tie or the story contradicts the filings, we say so.

1

Understand the business

What it sells, who pays, why customers stay and what could break it.

2

Check the numbers

Four years of financials: growth, margins, cash flow, debt and returns.

3

Value it three ways

Forward P/E, discounted cash flow and EV/EBITDA against listed peers.

4

Score & rate

A 50-point checklist plus the valuation upside decide BUY, HOLD or SELL.

5

Time & manage risk

Technical review, review levels and position sizing before any trade.
